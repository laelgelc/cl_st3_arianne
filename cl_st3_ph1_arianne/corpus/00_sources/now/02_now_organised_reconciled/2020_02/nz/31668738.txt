<h> Mike 's Minute : Child poverty is the KiwiBuild of social failure <p> If you 're looking to vote on accountability and delivery this election , Labour is in increasing trouble . <p> Another social indicator lays bare yet another promise not met . Child poverty is up , the stats department released the cold hard truth yesterday . <p> This was the Prime Minister 's calling card . This was the one dear to her heart . Unlike climate change , which was her generation 's nuclear moment , where you can fudge your way out of any real sort of accountability . <p> Although a zero carbon bill has been passed , the fact it will achieve nothing and has exemptions in it you can drive a bus through , you can at least fill time during the campaign wittering on about your intent and hope , and of course using her favourite line : there is more work to do .
