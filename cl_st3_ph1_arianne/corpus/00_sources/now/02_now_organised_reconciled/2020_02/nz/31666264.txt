<h> Launch Of Parliament Petition To Remove Aluminium Dross <p> This afternoon to a crowd of over 100 people in Mataura -- Mark Patterson , New Zealand First List MP based in Clutha-Southland launched a parliamentary petition regarding the aluminium dross issue in Mataura , Southland . <p> The petition asks that the House Of Representatives urge the Government to take any means necessary to remove the aluminium dross from the Mataura paper mill site and relocate it to Tiwai Point . <p> The Mataura paper mill site stores almost 10,000 tonnes of toxic aluminium dross in the town of Mataura .
