By Andrea Germanos <p> As global governments gathered at a conference in Rome Monday to advance a framework for protecting the planet 's biodiversity , environmental and human rights advocates warned that the draft text that has emerged from meetings so far is " hopelessly weak and inadequate . " <p> The draft document for " living in harmony with nature , " first unveiled in January , is being considered at the Feb. 24 -- 29 meeting of the Working Group on the Post-2020 Global Biodiversity Framework .
