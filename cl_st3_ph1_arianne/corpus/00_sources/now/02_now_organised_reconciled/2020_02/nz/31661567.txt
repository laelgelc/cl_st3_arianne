<h> Submission Period For Whitebait Consultation Extended <p> Conservation Minister Eugenie Sage has extended the date for people to have their say on proposed changes to improve management of whitebait across New Zealand . <p> Submissions were due to close on 2 March 2020 but will now remain open until 9am on Monday 16 March 2020 . <p> " I have received feedback from the public and iwi that they need more time to submit on proposals in the Improving Whitebait Management discussion document . I want to ensure we hear from as many New Zealanders as possible and have made the decision to extend the consultation period , " Eugenie Sage says . <p> " Whitebait are a cherished part of Kiwi culture . We want to ensure that whitebait numbers flourish , while maintaining a healthy fishery long-term . It really is important that as many people as possible submit on the proposals I have outlined . <p> " I want to thank everyone who has engaged on the issue to date -- we have received more than 2,000 submissions already .
