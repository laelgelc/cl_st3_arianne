But in this workplace the lives of tens of thousands of people are at stake . <p> Today The Detail goes inside the office to follow the visa process and gets a glimpse of the types of fraud it is grappling with , from fake passports to " diploma mills " issuing fake qualifications . <p> Getting this kind of access to an immigration office is unusual for a journalist .
