<h> Tourism firm bears pressure of bushfires and coronavirus outbreak <p> The Australian bushfires and the novel coronavirus outbreak are set to put further pressure on the profit of Tourism Holdings . <p> The campervan rental and tourism company now expects a full year profit of around $24 million compared with last year 's $29.8m . <p> The company warned last year that weak campervan sales in the US and its investment in a new travel technology business , Togo , were already going to cause a lower profit . <p> " There are Australian bushfires cancellations and forward booking reduction , and a forecast reduction in Chinese inbound customers over the next two months due to the coronavirus containment measures , " the company said in a statement . <p> " We continue to assess the impact of these factors and our response . " <p> However , it said there were positive signs in its business , which should lead to a better result in the new financial year . <p> " New Zealand and Australia have had solid performances in vehicle sales from both a volume and margin perspective .
