<h> $19.9 Million From PGF For Kawerau <p> A $19.9 million investment from the Provincial Growth Fund will help develop essential infrastructure for an industrial hub in the Bay of Plenty town of Kawerau , Regional Economic Development Minister Shane Jones says . <p> " The funding will go to three projects to further develop the Putauaki Trust Industrial Hub , an industrial development which will act as a catalyst for much-needed economic growth the Eastern Bay of Plenty . " <p> Putauaki Trust Industrial Hub needs an efficient rail link to transport goods and connect heavy industry in the Bay of Plenty to global markets . A reliable road network is also essential for the hub to attract new businesses , " Shane Jones said . <p> A total of $9.6 million from the PGF is funding the construction of rail siding at the Kawerau Container Terminal to allow for the loading and unloading of containers . This will take pressure off roads by removing an estimated 40 daily truck trips from local roads and reduce Co2 emissions . <p> The Putauaki Trust industrial roading extension will connect around 50ha of vacant plots within the Hub to existing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ SH34 . <p> The Kawerau Off-Highway Road will be a new 1.6km stretch direct to the Kawerau Container Terminal . This will be used by large vehicles that can not use the existing highway . <p> In 2018 , the Putauaki Trust Industrial Hub received $2 million for the first stage of construction . <p> " The $19.9 million I 'm announcing today will create significant employment opportunities and attract further private sector investment . This is an exceptional example of the Government partnering with Maori , local council , businesses and the community to create a landmark change for the future of Kawerau . <p> " We back communities such as Kawerau by funding projects that will make a difference to the lives of local people for generations to come , while boosting industries vital to New Zealand 's economic success , " Shane Jones said . <p> The project is the result of more than a decade of planning work from Putauaki Trust , council and businesses , and is expected to create at least 150 jobs in Kawerau . <h> Scoop Citizen Members and ScoopPro Organisations are @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ publishing is a milestone , but your support is essential to keep Scoop thriving .
