<h> Cultural shift needed to tackle Pacific nutrition issues <p> Pacific culture and tradition revolves around food , but the prominence of processed food has led to a nutrition crisis . Laura Walters travelled to the Pacific to find out about the complicated relationship between malnutrition and obesity . <p> Small shops - the Pacific equivalent of local dairies - are scattered along the roadside in the Kiribati capital of South Tarawa . <p> The corrugated iron stalls have a few shelves holding easy-to-make meals like ramen noodles , along with soft drinks and other assorted goods . <p> Alongside the stalls sit pallets piled high with sacks of white rice and refined sugar . <p> For many of the country 's 110,000 people , this is the basis of their daily diet . <p> In the local supermarket , an entire side of an aisle is dedicated to different sized cans of corned beef .
