<h> Govt to miss yet another electric vehicle target <p> In the latest blow to its efforts to reduce transport emissions , the Government has admitted it will not meet a target set under National to have 64,000 electric vehicles on the road by the end of 2021 , Marc Daalder reports <p> New Zealand will fail to meet a National-era target to have 64,000 electric vehicles on the road by the end of 2021 , according to the Ministry of Transport . <p> There are currently more than 19,000 electric vehicles in the country and that number is expected to double over the next 22 months , but we will still fall far short of the target . <p> The news comes after Stuff reported on Friday that the Government 's controversial feebate scheme , which would have subsidised electric vehicles by placing fees on high-emitting cars , will not come into effect . <p> Since the feebate scheme would only have started in 2021 , its demise has had little impact on the failure to reach the target . <p> The target was set according to " base case " projections , meaning it was thought in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ end of 2021 without any policy intervention . <p> Even with an uptick in EV use , a target for 2021 set by the last government appears well out of reach .
