<h> FTA with EU a top trade priority <p> New Zealand diplomats are working to ensure Gisborne cheese and manuka honey are not left out of a potential multibillion-dollar free trade agreement with the European Union , and are urging other businesses and Maori interests here to have a say in what they want to see . <p> Speaking to the Gisborne Chamber of Commerce , New Zealand Foreign Affairs and Trade principal adviser Mark Trainor said it was one of the Government 's " top trade priorities " to conclude a free trade agreement ( FTA ) with the European Union . <p> " That reflects not only because it 's an important economic trading relationship for New Zealand , but also the wider breadth and depth of relationships we have with all the EU member countries . " <p> Mr Trainor pointed out the EU was New Zealand 's third largest two-way trading partner , worth $23 billion last year , which made up about 14 percent of the nation 's total trade . <p> " Even if you take out the UK , you are still looking at $15 billion of two-way trade , so it @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ world 's biggest single market . <p> " What 's important for us , is New Zealand is one of only six WTO members that does not have an existing FTA with the EU , or until very recently , a negotiation launched to get an FTA .
