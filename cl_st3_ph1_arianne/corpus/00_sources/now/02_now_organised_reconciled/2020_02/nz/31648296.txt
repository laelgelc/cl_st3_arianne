Klobuchar was third with 19.8% , according to the Associated Press , which called the race . <p> The tight finish allows multiple candidates to claim success , even though Sanders came out on top . Voters looking for a moderate alternative to Sanders ' democratic socialism were left without a clear choice . <p> Buttigieg gains credibility from his strong second-place finish coming on the heels of his top showing in Iowa .
