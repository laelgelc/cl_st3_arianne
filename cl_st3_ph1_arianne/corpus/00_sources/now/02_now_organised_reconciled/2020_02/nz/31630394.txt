I came across The Making of an American Capitalist in a second-hand bookstore . It 's a well-regarded account of the legendary investor , though , despite running nearly 500 pages , is generally considered more glib than Alice Schroeder 's definitive The Snowball ( 832 pages ) . But as someone finishing uni , both fascinated and intimidated by the world of money , it was more than enough -- I was enthralled by the story of a kid from Omaha who seemed to understand investing in a completely different level and on a different timeline , to all the financiers of Wall Street . <p> I remember feeling like a door was opening up to me , like this forbidden world was somehow accessible now .
