<h> Elizabeth Knox arrives in America <p> ReadingRoom literary editor Steve Braunias reports on the amazing news that Elizabeth Knox 's latest novel has won a lucrative deal with a US publisher . <p> A bidding war in the fierce US book trade for the rights to publish the new novel by acclaimed Wellington writer Elizabeth Knox has resulted in a six-figure deal -- and a " flood " of interest from US film and TV studios . <p> It 's an unprecedented level of excitement in the US for a New Zealand novel . It 's also very sudden . The hype for The Absolute Book , published by Victoria University Press in September last year , took off with a hiss and a roar in late January , after Dan Kois 's extraordinary rave reviewappeared on the US site Slate . <p> The headline to his review read , in seething caps , " This New Zealand Fantasy Masterpiece Needs to Be Published in America , Like , Now . " It happened , like , fast . His review appeared on January 29 . I emailed Knox 's husband , Victoria University publisher Fergus Barrowman , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ if any US publishers were sniffing around the book . He replied , " It is a rapidly evolving situation . " <p> I inquired again , on Wednesday February 12 : " Any news on that which was rapidly evolving ? " <p> He replied , " Elizabeth 's agent is fielding offers . There might be some news on Friday morning . Picture Elizabeth on the beach with the shags , waving her phone around . " <p> I duly pictured the scene -- the couple were on holiday , swanning around Tasman/Golden Bay -- and two days later , on Friday morning , he emailed from the Collingwood Tavern with a very happy announcement : " North American rights to The Absolute Book have gone to Viking Penguin , agented by Scott Miller at Trident Media Group . No date yet . UK and other rights are going to the London Book Fair in March .
