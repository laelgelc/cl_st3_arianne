<h> Future of Masterton 's Henley Lake up for debate <p> The shape and future of Masterton 's Henley Lake will be put to ratepayers after Wednesday 's district council meeting . <p> Henley Lake . Photo : 123RF <p> District councillors will weigh up two options on the lake 's future at the extraordinary meeting . <p> Henley Lake , which was conceived as a recreational area in the 1960s and constructed in the 1980s , is currently considered off limits because of toxic algae . <p> The lake is fed water through a water race from the Ruamahanga River , which runs adjacent to the park . <p> This is allowed through a resource consent granted by Greater Wellington District Council . <p> Three small streams also enter the lake but the primary water source is necessary in order to maintain lake health . <p> This consent has now lapsed , and changes to Regional Plan rules have made getting a new one a harder task . <p> The complexity means continuing the existing resource consent application would mean " a positive outcome would be extremely uncertain " , a report by David Hopman of Masterton District @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ two options to put to ratepayers as part of MDC 's Annual Plan consultation . <p> One option recommends only taking water from the river when flows are above the minimum , without making any changes to the lake . The second approach would be a wetland area within the lake margins . <p> Councillors Gary Caffell and Chris Petersen sat on the working party set up on the matter . <p> Caffell said he was prepared to fight to keep the lake as it is . <p> " I 'd like to see the council fight hard to make it happen . I 'm certainly not giving up that fight at this stage . <p> " It does seem because of the consent processes from the GWRC that it 's going to be a battle .
