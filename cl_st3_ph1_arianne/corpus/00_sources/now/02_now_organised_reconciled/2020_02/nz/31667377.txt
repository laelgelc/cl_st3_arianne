<h> Extra $25m for dam not stopping progress <p> The Tasman District mayor is vowing to press on with a large irrigation project , despite a 25 percent jump in its cost . <p> When finished in 2022 , the Waimea Dam will be 53 metres high and hold 13 billion litres of water - that 's more than the contents of 5000 Olympic swimming pools . <p> Mayor Tim King said the dam was vital to provide water to a parched farming region west of Nelson currently enduring yet another drought . <p> It would contribute to town water supplies but would mainly provide irrigation to rich orchard and market garden land outside Richmond . <p> Construction of the dam was approved in 2018 after almost two decades of bureaucratic argument and protests by some environmentalists and citizen groups . <p> It was seen as a victory by the irrigation industry that had suffered a big defeat in Hawke 's Bay and was further thwarted by a crackdown just after the current government was elected . <p> The cost of the Waimea project was projected to be $104.4 million . <p> The majority of this would be split @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , either as equity or loans . <p> But late last week , the company building the dam announced that the cost would jump by $25m , due to what it called " unforeseen geological conditions " . <p> " The projected new cost is $129.4 million and there is now a two to four-month delay , largely attributable to problems identified with the rock-fill during testing , " the company said in a statement . <p> The problem was that rock near the site was found to be fractured and prone to breaking up and so was unsuitable for use in a key part of the dam . <p> Alternative rock would have to be found , and this raised the prospect of streams of trucks carrying rock quarried elsewhere up the road to the dam site . <p> This would account for most of the extra $25m . <p> The company 's chief executive , Mike Scott , said " as always we are looking for opportunities to review components of the dam 's design to save on costs wherever we can . <p> " But this must @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of this significant infrastructure project .
