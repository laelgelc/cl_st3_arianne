<h> Govt to help forestry sector affected by delays in China <p> The Ministry for Primary Industries is assuring businesses that Chinese borders are not closed to New Zealand logs , but that there are problems with oversupply , and logs backing up at Chinese ports , which is leading to the demand for new logs to drop . <p> Forestry contractors warn 30 percent of New Zealand 's logging crews are not harvesting as a result . <p> Government agencies are working on a support plan for the East Coast , which is being hit particularly hard . <p> Forestry New Zealand Te Uru Rakau Deputy Director-General Julie Collins told Morning Report there had been some good news in the last 24 hours . <p> " One of the key log exporters that stopped exporting logs last week have indicated that they are going to resume log exports later on this week .
