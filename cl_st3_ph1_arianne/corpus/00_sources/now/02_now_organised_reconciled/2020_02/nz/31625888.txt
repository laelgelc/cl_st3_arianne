<h> Fast , scattered science fights coronavirus <p> Who is organising the world 's scientific community in the fight against the new coronavirus ? No one it seems , but even without coordination , scientists are collaborating and rapidly sharing research . <p> While borders snap shut in an attempt to curb the spread of the new coronavirus , scientists are busy working on other angles such as faster detection , possible treatments and prevention . <p> Nine days after Chinese officials notified the World Health Organisation of a string of pneumonia-like illnesses , Wuhan local researchers released the genetic code of the new coronavirus . <p> It 's not the first time this has been done , but the speed at which it was released has helped scientists from around the world delve into trying to understand where the virus may have come from , what it 's similar to , how to rapidly diagnose new cases and to work on vaccines . <p> It 's resulted in some very fast science being done to tackle the new virus without a great deal of global coordination or work occurring outside of academic science 's normal way . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ following the outbreak and the scientific effort closely . <p> " The short answer is there is no coordination .
