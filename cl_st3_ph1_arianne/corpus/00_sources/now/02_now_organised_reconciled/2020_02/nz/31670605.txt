<h> ORC proposes 9.1% general rates increase in draft Annual Plan <p> ORC is stepping up work across its priority areas , but has proposed to reduce the impact on ratepayers by increasing Port Otago dividends and the use of general reserves . <p> The Otago Regional Council ( ORC ) today approved proposed expenditure and funding requirements in the draft Annual Plan for the coming financial year , and an ' informal consultation ' on the proposals to begin in March . <p> The estimated total expenditure of ORC activities in the 2020-21 financial year is $75.494m , up from $67.702m in the current financial year , and up against the Long-Term Plan ( LTP ) forecast expenditure of $64.728m . <p> While the predicted expenditure increase is $7.792m higher than the current financial year , the proposed increases to general rates ( $1.017m ) and targeted rates ( $437,000 ) are relatively low , owing to an increase in the Port Otago dividend ( from $7.9m to $10.1m ) and the use of $4m from general reserves . <p> Total targeted rates are proposed to increase by $437,000 ( 2.9% ) in the 2020-21 financial year . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ increase , subject to consultation , would not be significant in dollar terms . <p> " We know rates are increasing for a lot of people around the region , and we understand that another increase may cause some anxiety .
