<h> The Local Hero saving food from landfill to fight poverty and climate change <p> Last week Nick Loosley , founder of food waste and food poverty charity Everybody Eats , was named the 2020 Kiwibank New Zealand Local Hero of the Year . He talked to Alex Braae about how a more sustainable approach to our food system can help feed those in need . <p> There 's a theme of reconnection that runs through the work Nick Loosley does . Reconnecting with the community , with an understanding of where our food comes from , and even reconnecting with our senses . <p> Everybody Eats is a fascinating concept that brings all that together . At two sites in central Auckland , it offers " pay what you feel " meals , at which a millionaire might eat alongside someone who sleeps on the streets . The tables are large and communal , and everyone eats the same meal . <p> And what meals they are , with a range of Auckland 's top chefs doing volunteer stints in the kitchen .
