The future of Broadcasting Minister Kris Faafoi might also depend on the outcome , Mark Jennings writes . <p> When Labour 's golden boy , Kris Faafoi , was handed the broadcasting portfolio following the demise of the clumsy Clare Curran , a degree of optimism surged through the media industry . <p> Here , at last , was someone who really understood the industry . <p> In the past , the broadcasting portfolio has been the preserve of junior ministers -- think Marian Hobbs , Craig Foss , and Clare Curran . Or minsters who had other , more important tasks -- think Amy Adams and Simon Bridges .
