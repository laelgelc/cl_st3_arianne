<h> Flats an under-utilised resource <p> This is the third column in a series I have been writing trying to stimulate discussion about our defensive planning in preparation for the onslaught of climate change . <p> The response to the first two -- fresh water delivery and economic restructuring of the Northern Tairawhiti -- has been more encouraging than I could have hoped for ; at least , judging by the positive reaction from members of the public to my suggestions . <p> I had hoped the council would welcome the opportunity to engage with ratepayers on such serious matters as well , but apparently they remain comfortable with their decision to do nothing . . . so I guess we will just have to carry on without their support , until overwhelming pressure forces them to pull their heads out of the sand . <p> We live in hope , but time is running out . <p> Today 's column focuses on the need to restructure the economic model we have been using for earning our living during the past 150 years .
