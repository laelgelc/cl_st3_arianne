<p> News from Greater Wellington Regional Council The Regional Transport Committee today agreed to a vision for a strategic framework that will see safer roads , lower transport generated emissions and an increase in active travel and public transport mode share , over the next 10 years . <p> " These headline targets aim to see a 40 per cent reduction in deaths and serious injuries on our roads , 30 per cent reduction in transport generated carbon emissions and 40 per cent increase in active travel and public transport mode share , all by 2030 . <p> " These goals aim to reflect the mix of urban , suburban and rural transport settings across our diverse region . They are ambitious targets that aim to shift the region towards a more sustainable future . <p> " The RLTP 2021 will include a monitoring framework which will observe progress towards each of these objectives and ensure we are on track to meeting the targets set out , " Cr Staples says . <p> The deaths and serious injuries target aligns with the target in Central Government 's ' Road to Zero ' , New Zealand road safety strategy . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with national targets on this .
