<h> Uncertainty swirls over Mackenzie dairy plan <p> The legal battle over a large dairy farm planned for the Mackenzie Basin is heading to the High Court . David Williams reports . <p> The future of the Mackenzie Basin 's Simons Pass Station -- a lightning rod for national environmental opposition -- remains as unclear as a swirling effluent pond . <p> Dunedin businessman Murray Valentine has spent 16 years and millions of dollars gathering approvals , court settlements , and building infrastructure for a $100-million-plus dairy development at Simons Pass , near Lake Pukaki . Valentine told Newsroom last year he plans to irrigate 4500 hectares at the property -- some of which is Crown lease land -- and stock more than 15,000 animals , including 5500 cows .
