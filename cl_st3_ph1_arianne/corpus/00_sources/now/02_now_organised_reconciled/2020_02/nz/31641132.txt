The response to Radio New Zealand 's proposal to automate Concert FM and shift it off the FM frequency to make way for a youth-oriented station has been passionate , polarising , and predictable . <p> I say predictable because I have seen all of these arguments before . It suggests a deficit in information , dialogue , commentary , and knowledge . And look , I am as guilty of it as anyone . To suggest there 's a gap in understanding is not to call everyone who 's tweeted about the issue a philistine . <p> Commentary about the proposed changes , now partially put to bed after government intervention , has drawn out the time-honoured set of jabs and jibes seen and heard whenever the issue of arts funding hits the headlines . This issue has had plenty of coverage , but most has been approached using a political or media industry lens . It 's news , I get it . This is n't a hit on journalists doing their jobs .
