<h> Coronavirus - it 's a misinfodemic <p> The global health emergency that is the Coronavirus - or Covid-19 - has become a game-changer for scientists around the world in terms of the speed of the response . But at the same time it 's been termed a ' misinfodemic ' <p> Covid-19 -- or coronavirus -- is a game changer for scientists around the world . <p> For the first time they are sharing their research even before it is peer reviewed , in the rush to find treatment and vaccines . <p> The response of health experts around the world to the rapidly evolving global health emergency has led to a " knowledge explosion " . <p> Vaccinologist Helen Petousis-Harris from the University of Auckland calls it an unprecedented triumph in human research collaboration . Researchers tend to be precious about their life 's work , she says ; they guard it closely in case someone else beats them to it .
