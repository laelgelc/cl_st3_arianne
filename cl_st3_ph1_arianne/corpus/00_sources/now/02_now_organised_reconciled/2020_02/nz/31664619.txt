<h> From Northland to Bluff on two wheels <p> Rod Oram is cycling the length of New Zealand , and sharing some economic and environmental insights along the way . <p> On Monday I set off from Cape Reinga to cycle to Bluff . Over the next month I 'll ride 3,000km through the complete kaleidoscope of Kiwi countryside of beach , bush , mountains and farmland , with lots of settlements , a few small towns and two cities along the way . <p> All that wonderful variety of New Zealand , plus the companionship of fellow riders on the biennial Tour Aotearoa cycle event will be a real treat . Just as rewarding , though , is the chance to chat to lots of people and see plenty of examples of economic life down the country . <p> So , this and the next three columns will be about some of things I learn along the way . This week I 've been in the Far North and Northland and I 'm writing this column about them on the Kewpie Too boat of Kaipara Cruises . Given the scientific analysis , we know the local climate will only get hot and drier . <p> That 's why the big new horticultural development is so controversial . The council has granted rights for extracting massive quantities of water from the ancient aquifer for new , extensive , irrigated avocado orchards . Some scientists and locals fought hard to block the consents arguing the depletion of the aquifer will drastically harm local ecosystems , particularly with the climate changing . <p> Recent vandalism of some of the new plantings is a sign of the continuing conflict . <p> Since the local soil is so sandy , the irrigation required for the orchards is another example of a grave mismatch of crop and soils . It takes much mayhem before growers figure out how to develop enduring businesses . <p> The best example of this is kiwifruit . It took another big step two weeks ago by announcing a significant improvement in its sustainability programme . <p> Tourism is another area of fast growth for the far north , with plenty of signs of investment in new accommodation and attractions along my cycling route .
