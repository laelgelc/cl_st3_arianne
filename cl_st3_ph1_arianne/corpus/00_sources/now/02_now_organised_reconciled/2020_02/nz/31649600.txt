<h> Housing sales were particularly strong in Auckland in January although prices remained around recent levels <p> Auckland 's housing market started the year on a strong note with a surge of sales in January . <p> The Real Estate Institute of NZ says 1295 properties were sold in Auckland in January , up +9.7% compared to January last year and the highest number of sales in the month of January since 2016 . <p> Within the Auckland region sales were particularly strong on the North Shore , up +29.0% , in Waitakere up +28.6% and Rodney up +21.1% <p> Around the rest of the country excluding Auckland 3308 properties were sold in January , up +0.9% compared to January last year . This was also the highest level of sales for the month of January since 2016 , with 10 of the country 's 16 regions posting higher sales levels than a year ago . <p> Regions with particularly high sales levels compared to January last year were Nelson +42.6% , Manawatu/Whanganui +15.3% , Bay of Plenty +11.5% and Marlborough +11.3% . <p> However there were also some regions where there was a significant decline in sales compared @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ -27.2% and Otago -17.1% ( the interactive chart below plots the monthly sales trends in all regions ) . <p> Prices remained firm and mostly at the top end of recent ranges , with the national median selling price dropping back to $615,000 in January from $629,000 in December , but that was still up 11.8% compared to January last year . <p> In Auckland the median dropped back to $875,000 in January from $890,000 in December and remained below the record of $900,000 set in March 2017 . <p> Canterbury 's median price of $465,000 equalled the record set in October last year . <p> Going against the trend there was a particularly large drop in Wellington 's median price , which declined from $684,000 in December to $620,000 in January . <p> Other regions to record declines in the median price in January compared to December were Northland , Gisborne , Tasman and Nelson ( see the interactive chart below which tracks median prices in all regions ) . <p> We welcome your help to improve our coverage of this issue .
