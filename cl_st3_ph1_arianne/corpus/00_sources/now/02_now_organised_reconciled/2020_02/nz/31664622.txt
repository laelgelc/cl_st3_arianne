Its boss , UK-based Clive Cowdery has made millions over the past 15 years via three different zombie-buying companies all called ( rather confusingly ) Resolution Life . <p> No problem with that . In fact Cowdery was knighted back in 2015 for philanthropically giving some of his millions away . <p> The risk for NZ policyholders <p> But third parties making money from owning zombie funds comes with risks for existing policyholders in those funds - in theory at least . Because Resolution Life is based overseas and the insurance book is closed , the company does n't necessarily need to worry about branding or reputational damage if customers get grumpy or vociferous about any perceived poor treatment . <p> And because of the long-term nature of life insurance policies , many customers ca n't just up sticks and change providers if they do n't like what 's happening at AMP/Resolution . <p> They are stuck .
