<p> One of seven artistically inclined children - Catholic of course - Ruth Buchanan was always destined to be an artist . <p> It said so on a business card she made for herself at eight years old , in fact . " Hilariously it said Ruth Buchanan , part time artist . I wonder what I imagined what I was doing for the rest of my time then . I ca n't remember . " <p> " I 've got quite a creative family and a lot of my childhood was spent in art galleries . I grew up thinking art was actually an option . Art was part of my language for understanding the world , I guess . " <p> At some point it dawned on her that maybe she could n't survive financially as an artist . <p> " I thought becoming a designer would be a whole lot smarter so I decided to enrol in interior architecture design . But I realised working to a brief for clients was n't going to work for me really either . " <p> And so it was .
