<p> The huge majority of New Zealanders think fishing industry donations to Members of Parliament at the last election have the potential to " strongly influence " decisions the MPs make , according to a poll conducted by Horizon Research . <p> The poll , which asked more than 1,000 people how much they felt donations by companies with large fishing investments would influence MPs and policy , found 73% saying that any donations by companies with large fishing investments have the potential to strongly influence MPs ' decisions . <p> Of the remainder surveyed , 11% felt the donations would probably have no influence while 2% only said it would definitely have no influence and 14% were not sure . <p> The poll was conducted for the non-profit LegaSea organisation , which is advocating for a review of fisheries management to help return abundance to fish stocks . <p> Some quota stocks are now depleted to as little as 17% of their original levels . <p> LegaSea 's release of the poll comes after RadioNew Zealand reported the concerns over secret donations to the New Zealand First Foundation .
