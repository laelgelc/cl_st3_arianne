<h> Draft Annual Plan before council <p> The council meeting this week is the first full council meeting for 2020 . There have been committee meetings of the new triennium and apart from wastewater and regional transport , each of those are now all of council elected representatives . <p> The meeting this Thursday has committee recommendations where there is only delegated authority to recommend to council . The meeting seeks to approve the purchase of a multi-purpose boat for the use of the Harbourmaster , to better facilitate their legislated role . The cost , up to $200,000 , is subject to better definition of the operating costs of such a venture ! There was much debate in the committee on this proposal , with a real concern that maintainenance costs may escalate beyond the $4000 a year reported . <p> The one-way proposal for Titirangi ( with a speed reduction to 30kmh to accommodate the many walkers and cyclists ) is also up for confirmation .
