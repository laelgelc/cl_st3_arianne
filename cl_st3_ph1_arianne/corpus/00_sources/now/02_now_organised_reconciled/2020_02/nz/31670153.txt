<h> Do n't mess with farmers ! <p> Policymakers in Ireland have learned the lesson about demonising farmers -- just do n't do it . <p> That 's the word from a leading Irish scientist , Dr Karl Richards from Teagasc , that country 's semi-state organisation that is responsible for R&amp;D , training and advisory services to farmers . <p> Richards told Rural News , at recent seminar at Massey University , that policy makers in Ireland have realised that farmers will react badly to being constantly demonised and are less likely to react positively to improving the environment . <p> He says policymakers and farmers in Ireland are now working collaboratively and policy makers better understand the challenges that farmers are facing and the constraints that they are working under . <p> Richards says there are many initiatives in Ireland to look at ways to reduce greenhouse gas ( GHG ) emissions and improve water quality . <p> He says while Ireland only has 1.6 million dairy cows it conversely has about five million head of beef , all of which contribute to GHG emissions .
