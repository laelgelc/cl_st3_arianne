<h> Crayfish to be returned to ocean after China cancels orders <p> A huge haul of crayfish could be released back into the ocean after China cancelled orders amid the novel coronavirus outbreak . <p> The crayfish had been caught for sale to China but the orders were cancelled because of trade and travel disruption caused by the novel coronavirus outbreak . <p> The virus is thought to have first appeared in the central China city of Wuhan . The Chinese government has virtually locked down the province of Hubei and its capital , Wuhan . China is facing mounting isolation as airlines suspend flights to its mainland cities . <p> Between 150 and 180 tonnes of live rock lobster are held in New Zealand in pots and tanks , at sea and on land . <p> The government has ruled that a limited number can be released back into the ocean . <p> Live crayfish in holding pots at sea could be released immediately , subject to sign off by an official from the Ministry for Primary Industries ( MPI ) . <p> Lobster held together at processing facilities would be considered on a case-by-case basis due to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ strict rules around the return of rock lobsters to the sea , " Fisheries Minister Stuart Nash said . <p> " Officials want to make sure it is done in a way which ensures they are likely to survive , and also protects the environment where they are being released . <p> " Anyone who wishes to release live crayfish will need to provide information to Fisheries New Zealand , and fishery officers will monitor the return of crayfish to the sea .
