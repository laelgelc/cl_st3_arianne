<h> NZ stock market 's prospects lift <p> New Zealand 's capital markets are showing signs of revival , although the stock exchange looks set to lose more companies . <p> Law firm Chapman Tripp 's annual review has found a revamp of the the NZX 's rules , fees , and the move to one trading board over the past year have lifted the stock market 's prospects . <p> Corporate partner Rachel Dunne , who sat on a committee reviewing capital markets said the improvements had paid off , despite just two initial public offerings ( IPO ) in the last year . <p> She said the listing by the Napier Port had received strong Australian institutional investor demand even though the company opted against a dual listing on Australia 's ASX . <p> " This indicates a confidence in the NZX not seen in previous years , " she said . <p> " We think we will see more than two IPOs this year but we wo n't see a return to 2013 and 2014 levels .
