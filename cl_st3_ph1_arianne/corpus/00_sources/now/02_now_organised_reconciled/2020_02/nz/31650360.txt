<h> Native trees at risk of developer chainsaws <p> Described in a pamphlet funded by a local board as an ' urban forest ' , the land home to 46 mature native trees is on the market as a goldmine for development <p> Mature native trees in a leafy pocket of Avondale are up for sale and at risk of being felled . <p> The trees were planted almost 100 years ago and some stand up to 20 metres high . Among the 46 trees are 17 different species , including black maire titoki , totara , rimu , puriri and whau . <p> Only one of the trees , a pohutukawa , is in Auckland Council 's schedule of notable trees . The rest could be cut down by a developer . <p> The Avondale property on Canal Road has been owned by the same family for around 100 years .
