At the same time , rising inequality has resulted in populist politicians , from the US to Europe , either surging to power or having their policies adopted by mainstream politicians . The intergenerational divide appears wider than ever . Worryingly , faith in our institutions ( political , economic , cultural ) is dwindling at a time when climate change threatens us all . <p> Dramatic stuff . However , as investors , our job is to look through short-term noise to try and identify long-term trends .
