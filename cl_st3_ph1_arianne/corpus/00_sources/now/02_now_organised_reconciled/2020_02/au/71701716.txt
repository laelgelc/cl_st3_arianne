<p> After the United Kingdom voted in 2016 to leave the European Union , policymakers and political leaders across Europe feared that they too would soon face a similar crisis . They worried about a domino effect in which populist movements and politicians would lead other member states out of the EU one by one , effectively reversing a decades-long process of European integration . <p> But , at least until recently , Brexit had the opposite effect . Much to everyone 's surprise , the EU in the years after the UK 's referendum enjoyed a Brexit dividend . Europeans watched the UK descend into political chaos , with Britons literally stockpiling food and medicine for fear of what the future might hold . EU member states that typically agree on very little suddenly found themselves united behind the European Commission 's negotiating strategy . Support for the EU among Europeans soared .
