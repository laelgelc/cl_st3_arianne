<p> FOR me , tipping points are one of the scariest aspects of climate change . To get an idea of tipping points , imagine yourself sitting in a chair which you slowly push backwards . At one point you will be just balanced ; if you push further you will end up on your back on the floor . The tipping point is the point of no return - up until then , beforehand , you can right yourself , but afterwards , you 're definitely going to fall . So it is with climate change .
