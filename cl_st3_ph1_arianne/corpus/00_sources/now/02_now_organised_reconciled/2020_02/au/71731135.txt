Not many people would disagree with that . <p> What the renewable energy industry wants from Taylor , however , is not more subsidies . It wants a plan , and a target , and some effort from the government to smooth the transition to a zero carbon grid by removing the increasing number of regulatory and government hurdles Taylor seems determined to keep in their way . <p> Investment in wind and solar is falling away rapidly .
