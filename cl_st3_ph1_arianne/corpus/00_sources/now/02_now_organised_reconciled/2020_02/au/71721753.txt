Turing is widely considered to be the father of artificial intelligence and I remember studying one of his concepts at University . It was known as the Turing test . This is a test of a machine 's ability to exhibit intelligent behaviour indistinguishable from that of a human . The test came from his paper that posed the simple question : " Can machines think ? " Turing went on to explain that ' thinking ' is difficult to define so posed the solution to the problem with the idea of a game called the ' imitation game . ' If a person has a text only conversation between either a computer or a person and ca n't reliably tell the machine from the human , then the machine has passed the test .
