<p> Lord Mayor Sandy Verschoor this morning announced the council had entered into an agreement with Melbourne-based energy retailer Flow Power to source all its electricity from solar and wind power . <p> The contract means all council-owned infrastructure and buildings -- including street lights , libraries , community centres and Town Hall -- will be powered by 100 per cent renewable energy from July . <p> The switch is expected to slash the council 's current carbon emissions -- more than half of which are tied to electricity use -- by 11,000 tonnes each year . <h> Advertisement <p> Verschoor told reporters this morning the agreement would result in a 20 per cent reduction to the council 's electricity costs , however she said she was " not allowed to talk dollars " due to commercial confidentiality . <p> She said the agreement would mean the council would become carbon neutral-certified by the end of this year , joining the already-certified Brisbane , Sydney and Melbourne city councils . <p> Carbon neutrality , or having a net-zero carbon footprint , is achieved when the amount of greenhouse gas emissions emitted into the atmosphere is balanced by the amount @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ save our ratepayers money , it helps cement Adelaide 's international clean and green reputation , " Verschoor said . <p> " It 's a really big step in Adelaide 's journey to take on climate change action .
