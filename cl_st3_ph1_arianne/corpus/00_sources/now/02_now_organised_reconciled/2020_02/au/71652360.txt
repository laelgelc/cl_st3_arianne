<p> Tasmanian senator Nick McKim has been elected co-deputy of the Australian Greens . The party held a leadership contest on Tuesday following the resignation from the leadership of Richard Di Natale . Mr Di Natale plans to quit the Senate later this year to spend more time with his family . Melbourne MHR Adam Bandt was elected as leader with Queensland senator Larissa Waters retaining her position as co-deputy . Senator McKim said he was honoured to have the support of his colleagues in being elected as co-deputy . " I 'll be working as hard as I can to support the rest of the leadership team and the whole party room as we campaign for stronger action on climate change the the implementation of a Green New Deal for Australia , " Senator McKim said . " I want to make sure that we continue our focus on respecting nature , getting strong action to address the breakdown of our climate and making sure that wealth is distributed more fairly in our country . Senator McKim said Tasmania had always been pivotal to the Green movement , with himself and Launceston-based Greens senator Peter Whish-Wilson advocating for the state . " A Green New Deal is really relevant to Tasmania , " he said . " We 've got high unemployment , we 've got forests and ecosystems that need restoring and rewilding and we 've got really great expertise in renewable energy so there 's real jobs and opportunities there for Tasmania . " Senator McKim paid tribute to outgoing leader Senator Di Natale . " Richard did an amazing job as leader and in the same vein I have no doubt Adam is going to be an awesome leader for our party , " he said . Mr Bandt said on Twitter he was honoured to be elected as parliamentary leader of the Greens .
