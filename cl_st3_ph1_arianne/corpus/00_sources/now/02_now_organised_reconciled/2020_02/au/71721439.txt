<p> The way Ballarat gets rid of its waste could be changing in a big way , after a major state government policy announcement focusing on encouraging recycling . The 10-year , $300 million circular economy strategy will be paid for by almost doubling the landfill levy over three years - meaning it will eventually cost $125.90 per tonne to dump waste . Importantly for residents , this may not mean a sharp increase in rates .
