<h> OPINION : Brand Australia gets burned <p> The impact of the devastating bushfire crisis and the country 's response to climate change will be felt on Brand Australia for years to come , says Tim Riches . And that extends well beyond the image we represent to potential holidaymakers . <p> Australia 's bushfire disaster , its impact on our environment and our government 's stance on climate has put us in the spotlight for all the wrong reasons . And while the effect on tourism has been well documented , it 's not just our tourism value proposition that 's been damaged . <p> The connected bushfire and climate crises demonstrate there 's only one Brand Australia . It 's not ring-fenced . There 's no distinct business-to-consumer brand for the sectors of tourism , export and migration . Nor is there a business-to-business or business-to-government brand that applies to trade , foreign direct investment or diplomatic endeavours . <p> We 've only got one brand and it 's in crisis . <p> Australia is famous for its unique , clean , beautiful and natural environment . Our " place " provides reasons to visit as a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . People pay a premium for our clean , green food and we are universally recognised for unique natural wonders and fauna . Our cities are famous for their liveability . And some of our mining companies are even seen as leaders in responsible development of natural resources . <p> This creates an expectation , at least an implication , that Australians should be capable and innovative when it comes to the management of the environment particularly given it is one of the defining dimensions of Brand Australia . <p> Right now , our approach to climate change looks ignorant , incompetent or uncaring and this is damaging our nation brand and its family of value propositions ; all while eroding our collective reputation . Being seen as a laggard diminishes our ability to compete for visitors , consumers , talent , capital and influence . <p> The international coverage of our bushfire crisis , and the associated focus on our response to climate change , has been jarring for the world as they discover Australia lacks a plan and is n't leading in this crucial area . <p> The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Australia and its delivery , and the fallout is being felt in every market our brand competes in . <p> A potential tourist may fear whether their Great Barrier Reef trip of a lifetime will be a depressing disappointment . Migrants will question whether cities that experience the worst air quality in the world are really all that liveable . Consumers of imported Australian produce will question its integrity . Multinational companies may associate Australia with climate risk and governance failure risk , out of touch with fast-evolving Environmental , Social and corporate Governance ( ESG ) standards . <p> The damage to Brand Australia from this tragic period will be felt for years to come . And what 's disappointing is that we have the potential for it to be otherwise . We are home to both the oldest continuous human culture in the world and one of the world 's great science agencies in the CSIRO . Our university sector is world-class . We have innate advantages in terms of resources and thrive on a challenging continent . We are attractive to talent from all over the world @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in the research and industries that will underpin prosperity and quality of life in an era of changing climate . And by doing so , this would only serve to strengthen the many facets of Brand Australia . <p> As a brand strategist , I can see the challenge and the opportunity . We have the proof points but there needs to be a coherent policy framework and tangible , impactful initiatives to assert and sustain our nation brand advantage . <p> It 's no small cultural and policy thinking shift for Canberra and Australia 's business leaders , and like any change , it needs a sense of urgency -- a reason to change . As Port Arthur was on gun policy , let 's hope this can be a wake-up call to political courage and longer-term thinking .
