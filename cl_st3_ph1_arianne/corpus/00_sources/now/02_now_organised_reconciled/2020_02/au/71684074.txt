<p> Australia should join fully in global efforts to reduce greenhouse gases and commit to having zero net carbon emissions by 2050 , leading economist Ross Garnaut has said . The author of the landmark 2008 climate change review has expressed support for the carbon reduction target set in independent MP Zali Steggall 's climate change bill . Australia would transform itself from a " laggard " to a full participant in the global effort to minimise temperature rises if it committed to zero emissions by 2050 , Professor Garnaut said . " We can be quite sure that the world wo n't get to zero by 2050 if Australia does n't get there , " he said . " At the moment we 're a laggard and so we 're making it less likely that the world will get there . " Australians should not underestimate the effect it would have on the global effort from moving from being a laggard to being a full or leading participant in the global effort .
