<h> Let 's clear the air and create the climate action plan that Australians want <p> In response to the growing desire amongst all Australians for real action on climate change , the Business Council for Sustainable Development Australia ( BCSD Australia ) is convening a summit in our nation 's capital on Tuesday 11 February to accelerate the national conversation on how to transition Australia to a prosperous net-zero economy by no later than 2050 . <p> In response to the growing desire amongst all Australians for real action on climate change , the Business Council for Sustainable Development Australia ( BCSD Australia ) is convening a summit in our nation 's capital on Tuesday 11 February to accelerate the national conversation on how to transition Australia to a prosperous net-zero economy by no later than 2050 . <p> We know that building and implementing a robust and practical roadmap to achieve net-zero emissions by 2050 is not just a challenge for just one parliament , one political party , or for that matter one generation . All Australians and all parts of the community must be a part of a process of shaping our future national direction @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ intent is not to be something brand new , but to amplify and cross-fertilise and document the extraordinary work in Australia that has already been done by so many . <p> There will be four sessions during the Summit which will articulate and discuss possibilities and challenges in achieving a net-zero economy for the following sectors : <p> Power ( grids , microgrids , PPAs , renewables ) <p> Industry ( including manufacturing and materials management ) <p> Cities and Transport ( including infrastructure ) , and <p> Land Use ( food , agriculture and forestry ) <p> In each session expert speakers and interactive audience feedback will chart how we build an aspirational national roadmap net-zero greenhouse gas emissions , as called for by the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) . Specifically , Summit attendees will start a year-long initiative to frame an ambitious Nationally Determined Contribution for Australia 's emission reductions . <p> BCSD Australia invites representatives from all parts of the community -- business , not-for-profits , academia , city and state government policymakers , professionals and the wider population -- to join us @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ stories of action . <p> Powerful solutions have already been proposed by so many and so we want to invite feedback through our website www.bcsda.org.au to record key national information and opportunities on Action , Ambition and ways to address Transition and opportunities for people across Australia and around the globe to understand Australia 's effort toward and plans to realise a net-zero carbon future . <p> At the Summit , the Independent Member for Warringah , Zali Steggall OAM , will also share with the Summit delegates her vision for policy action . <p> Other confirmed speakers include : <p> Brooke Donnelly , CEO , Australian Packaging Covenant Organisation <p> Jan Gardberg , CEO &amp; CSO , IKEA Australia / New Zealand <p> Dr John Hewson , Chair , Business Council for Sustainable Development Australia <p> Shane Rattenbury , Minister for Climate Change , ACT Government <p> Behyad Jafari , CEO , Electric Vehicle Council <p> Prof. Frank Jotzo , Crawford School of Public Policy , Australian National University
