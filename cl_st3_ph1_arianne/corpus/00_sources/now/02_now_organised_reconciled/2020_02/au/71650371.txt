<h> The International Security Program <h> About the project <p> The International Security Program looks at strategic dynamics and security risks globally , with an emphasis on Australia 's region of Indo-Pacific Asia . Its research spans strategic competition and the risks of conflict in Asia , security implications of the rise of China and India , maritime security , nuclear arms control , Australian defence policy and the changing character of conflict . The Program draws on a network of experts in Australia , Asia and globally , and is supported by diverse funding sources including grants from the MacArthur Foundation and the Nuclear Threat Initiative .
