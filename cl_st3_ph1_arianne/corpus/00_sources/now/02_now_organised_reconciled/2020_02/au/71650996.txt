<h> Climate change ' defining challenge ' of decade : Telstra chief <p> Telstra chief executive Andy Penn says climate change will be the " defining challenge " of the decade , though he will not be drawn on government policy on the issue . <p> " We need more urgent action on climate as changing weather patterns deliver more frequent bushfires , floods , droughts and storms , " the telco boss said in an address to an American Chamber of Commerce lunch in Melbourne on Thursday . <p> Telstra CEO Andy Penn used a speech at an American Chamber of Commerce event in Melbourne on Thursday to champion climate change as the defining business challenge of 2020 . Credit:Steven Siewert <p> Mr Penn 's focus on climate was part of a wide-ranging speech that argued Australian businesses must operate with fairness top of mind in an increasingly complicated consumer environment . <p> He said Telstra was one of the largest energy users in the country and that as volumes of data increased by as much as 50 per cent each year on the Telstra network , the company 's energy consumption was " considerable " . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the politics of climate change . <p> " I 'm not going to get into a political conversation around government 's role , " he said . <p> He instead said individuals and businesses must become more aware of their own carbon footprints . <p> " Every single one of us actually have a role to play ... all businesses today , especially large businesses publish their emissions . How many of us individually know what our carbon footprint is as an individual ? It 's capable of being known , " he said . <p> The social responsibilities of big business was also front of mind in the address , with Mr Penn reflecting that he agreed in principle with Australian Competition and Consumer Commission chairman Rod Sims ' suggestion that there should be a simplified law against unfair conduct . <p> " I think I agree with Rod 's point about fairness because when you think about it , that is what responsible business is all about , " he said .
