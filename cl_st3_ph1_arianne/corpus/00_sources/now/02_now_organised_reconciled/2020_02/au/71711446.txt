<h> Climate protesters shutdown Melbourne streets in national day of action <p> They gathered at the State Library from Extinction Rebellion , unions , vegans and children all with the Prime Minister firmly in their sights . <p> " He 's the one going to be the one dying of old age , but we 're the ones going to be dying of climate change , " a young protester told 9News . <p> The group used the rally to slam Scott Morrison for his handling of the bushfire crisis , labelling his government arsonists and climate criminals . " I think his leadership has been despicable , " another protester shared . <p> " We 're pumping billions of dollars into fossil fuels when we should be shutting them down and putting it into a national renewable system , " another said . <p> CFA members also joined the rally worried the burnt landscape may never recover . " Praise this living earth and living languages that totally surround me - we do not what to lose those things , " one CFA member told the rally .
