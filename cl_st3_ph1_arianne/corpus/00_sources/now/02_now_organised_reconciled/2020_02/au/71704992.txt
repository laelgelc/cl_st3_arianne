<h> Forests , logging and climate change <p> THE INTERGOVERNMENTAL PANEL for Climate Change ( IPCC ) recommends that " natural solutions " are employed to deal with climate change emergency . The immediate protection and restoration of natural systems for carbon dioxide removal ( CDR ) are preferred to geo-engineering and B.E.C.C.S -- burning biomass as a fossil fuel substitute while using some sort of carbon capture and storage . <p> It is clear that the resilience of natural systems must be enhanced to withstand climate change impact , lest they falter and collapse , inhibiting their capacity for CDR . <p> The fairytale that burning wood instead of coal is carbon neutral continues to wreak havoc on the world 's extant forests . But that fairytale could soon end , taking with it the myth that the industrial logging of the world 's native forests has been and is now " sustainable " . <p> For a long time , the falsity of carbon emission accounting for forest bioenergy has been apparently invisible to many policymakers .
