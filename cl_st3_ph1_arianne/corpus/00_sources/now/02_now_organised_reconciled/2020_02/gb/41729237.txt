Holocaust Remembrance Alliance definition of anti-Semitism and the All Party Parliamentary Group definition of Islamphobia -- but not before some debate and some councillors expressing reservations . council , he said . <p> The definitions were perfectly compatible with the council 's Vision 2024 aims , said Coun Swift . <p> In Calderdale in 2024 -- the authority 's 50th anniversary -- these are
