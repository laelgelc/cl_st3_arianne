<h> Minor changes made to council 's plan after consultation <h> Minor changes made to Dorset Council Plan after consultation <p> Minor changes have been made to the Dorset Council Plan after a consultation . <p> More than 1,800 responses were made to the draft plan from residents , town and parish councils , partners , businesses and council staff . <p> The over-arching strategic plan sets out the principles for each council department which , in time , will each have their own plans for the coming years . <p> The overall council plan will also shape the authority 's medium-term financial strategy where there has already been a warning that it could end up ? 40m in the red with reserve funds , inherited from previous councils , dwindling . <p> Cabinet member Cllr Peter Wharf , who has led the process , says the main overall themes have remained unaltered as most of those making comments on the draft supported them .
