<h> Saltaire eco shop saves 65,000 pieces of plastic from landfill in six months <h> Saltaire eco shop saves 65k pieces of plastic from landfill <p> AN eco shop owner in Saltaire has saved 65,000 pieces of plastic from landfill and our oceans since opening six months ago . <p> Joel Harratt from Giddy Arts says he has seen a rise in customers using his refill stations for household products , cupboard essentials and recycling scheme as awareness of climate change rises . <p> Joel worked out the figures based on the estimated swaps to more sustainable items instead of plastic bottles , wipes , cling film and cotton buds as well as people switching to shampoo bars or eco food wraps . <p> He also sent 13kg of crisp packets - estimated to be between 2500 - 3000 - and 8kg of biscuit wrappers for recycling , all donated by loyal customers . <p> This website and associated newspapers adhere to the Independent Press Standards Organisation 's Editors ' Code of Practice .
