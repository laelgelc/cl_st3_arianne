<h> Heathrow expansion to dramatically increase Scottish emissions <h> Heathrow expansion will increase Scottish emissions <p> THE expansion of Heathrow Airport will lead to an extra 600,000 tonnes of climate pollution from Scotland 's airports over a 15-year period , according to an analysis by the Scottish Government . <p> The planned construction of a third runway at London 's biggest airport will result in more than 5000 extra flights every year from Edinburgh , Glasgow , Aberdeen and other Scottish airports , the analysis says . This will significantly increase climate-disrupting carbon dioxide emissions from planes . <p> The revelation has prompted campaigners to criticise the Scottish Government 's backing for the expansion at Heathrow as " indefensible " in the face of the climate emergency . But the Government argues that the expansion will boost jobs and investment in Scotland . <p> The UK Government and Parliament backed a third runway at Heathrow in June 2018 , but the expansion remains deeply controversial because of concerns over pollution and noise . Heathrow 's chief executive John Holland-Kaye promised that " an expanded Heathrow would create up to 16,000 jobs in Scotland " . <p> The climate impact of the expansion has been revealed by an internal " emission modelling " analysis released by the Scottish Government to The Ferret under Freedom of Information law .
