<h> The oil giant has said it 's aiming for net-zero carbon emissions by 2050 <p> BP 's new chief executive Bernard Looney outlined an ambitious new objective on Wednesday to make the oil giant carbon-neutral by 2050 - the most exacting sustainability promise made by any major oil company . <p> Looney , a week into his tenure , was sparing on how exactly the 111-year-old company intends to achieve its new goal , but specified a transition away from fossil fuels amid shifting consumer and investor demands , plus growing government legislation . <p> " We are aiming to earn back the trust of society , " said Looney , in front of industry investors and analysts in London . " We have got to change , and change profoundly . " <p> Some commentators were cynical , saying the news conference was little more than a PR stunt to announce the new CEO .
