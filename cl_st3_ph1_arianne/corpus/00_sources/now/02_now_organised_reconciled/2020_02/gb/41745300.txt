You eat some sugary cereal , or a slice of cake , or a simple elevenses snack of a fool 's gold loaf , and it has zero immediate impact on your appearance . You eat the thing every day for a few weeks or months , and the consequences , as with climate change , will only very gradually appear . <p> It 's interesting to think how human behaviour would change if our inbuilt short-sightedness could somehow be circumvented . We 'd probably make better decisions , but by shifting our vision to the future , perhaps we 'd take less pleasure from the present . Neurologically , we 're a long way from reaching this quandary , but nutritionally , we 're getting closer . <p> This is where the Keyto device comes in . It 's one of several devices to have recently come to market that measure , or putatively measure , acetones in your breath .
