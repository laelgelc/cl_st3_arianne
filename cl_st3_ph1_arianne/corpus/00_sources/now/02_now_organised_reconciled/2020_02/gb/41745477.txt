<h> " The Brexit deal is not good and we need a people 's vote " -- EU political hopefuls quizzed by Islanders <p> SIX political hopefuls were quizzed by Islanders about what they would achieve if they were elected as Members of the European Parliament last night ( Wednesday ) . <p> The candidates covered the entire political spectrum , from UKIP and the Brexit Party to the Socialists and Greens . <p> Hosted by Joe Plumb , known to many for his Vectis Radio show ' Political Soapbox ' , candidates were given three minutes to explain why residents of the Isle of Wight should vote for their party , before fielding questions from the audience . <p> In the election , candidates will vote for a party rather than individual candidates .
