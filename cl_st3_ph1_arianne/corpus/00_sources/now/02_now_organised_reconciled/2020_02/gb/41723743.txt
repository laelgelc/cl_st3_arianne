<h> Series of events to address climate change planned in Aberdeen <p> The Aberdeen Student Climate Strike on Friday . The students in Aberdeen are gathered in front of the Marischal College at 11am to show their support in the global movement against the climate change . Pictured - Isabella Maria Engberg in front of the protesters . Picture by Kami Thomson 24-05-19 <p> Aberdeen activists are calling on people to get behind a series of events raising awareness of climate change . <p> Climate Week North East will take the form of 10 days of events across the city and Aberdeenshire , running from March 20-29 . <p> Designed to show people how they can reduce their carbon footprint and live in a way which helps the planet , the programme aims to provide knowledge and inspiration . <p> Alison Stuart , of the Aberdeen Climate Action campaign group said : " There are so many great initiatives taking place in the north-east that concentrate on really living sustainably . <p> " It is inspiring and heartening to see how everyone is coming together to create community ' eco ' projects , grow our own food , repair @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and really promote a sustainable lifestyle . <p> " Climate Week is a great way of highlighting this and the actions we all can take . It shows that taking action can be fun , and demonstrates the real benefits of reducing our carbon emissions and living more sustainably . " <p> There will be a variety of events and workshops running over the course of the campaign . <p> People can choose between green family fun days , guided walks and bike rides , craft and upcycling workshops , talks and conferences . <p> There will also be the opportunity to make wild meadows or plant trees . <p> The full programme of events can be seen on **27;20465;TOOLONG or the CWNE Facebook page .
