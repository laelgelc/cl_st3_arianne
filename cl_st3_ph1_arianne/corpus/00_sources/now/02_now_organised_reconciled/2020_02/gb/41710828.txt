<h> Scientists to dive into ' Midnight Zone ' of ocean to study wildlife <h> Scientists to dive into ' Midnight Zone ' of ocean to study wildlife <p> Scientists are preparing to dive deep into the depths of the Indian Ocean . <p> The scientists from the British-led Nekton Mission plan to survey wildlife and gauge the effects of climate change in the unexplored " Midnight Zone " where light barely reaches but life still thrives . <p> Working with the Seychelles and Maldives governments , the five-week expedition is targeting seamounts -- vast underwater mountains that rise thousands of metres from the sea floor . <p> To explore such inhospitable depths , the Nekton scientists will board one of the world 's most advanced submersibles , called Limiting Factor . <p> " What we do know is that beneath 1,000 metres ( 3,280 feet ) , there 's no light down there , but a lot of animals ... are bioluminescent .
