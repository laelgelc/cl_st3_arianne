<h> Extinction Rebellion to target Barclays , PwC and News Corp in new wave of protests <p> Climate change activist group Extinction Rebellion said today it would target the financial industry in a new wave of protests this year . <p> The group 's co-founder said Barclays Bank , Big Four accountancy firm PwC and media group News Corp would be among the targets of direction action . <p> Gail Bradbrook told Reuters that " the economic system is acting like a cancer on humanity . " and said : " The regulatory system , the accountants , the legal firms support the metastasizing of this cancer . " <p> Last year the group targeted the City in two waves of protests in April and October , shutting down streets and glueing themselves to the London Stock Exchange . <p> The group said its new tactics would be more targeted , moving away from its previous strategy of blockading roads . <p> A spokesperson said : " One of the things we are trying to do this year is move away from disrupting the public and disrupting roads .
