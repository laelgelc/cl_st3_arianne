A world in which present generations make human well-being and equity impossible for future generations is grossly unjust : the Christian Churches need to be clear in naming this for what it is , and courageous in doing what can be done to set it right . " <p> The open letter to Synod members , which invites them to the vigil , outlines three " asks " of the Church of England . The first is to be prophetic and tell the truth of the climate emergency ; the second is to divest immediately from fossil fuels ; and the third is to lead by example and pledge to reduce net zero emissions by 2025 . The group points out that the National Farmers ' Union has set a net zero date of 2040 , and the UK water industry of 2030 . <p> The Revd Sue Parfitt , a priest in Bristol , is a member of Christian @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ will not wait for us .
