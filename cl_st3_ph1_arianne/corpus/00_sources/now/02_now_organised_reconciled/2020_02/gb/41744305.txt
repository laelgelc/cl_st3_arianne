The number of wildcats is too small , the hybridisation too far advanced and the population too fragmented . <p> " We therefore conclude it is too late to conserve the wildcat in Scotland as a stand-alone population . " <p> Estimates of the remaining population vary from 30 to 430 , a wide enough discrepancy to confirm what many of us have known for a long time -- nobody has a clue what the numbers are , only that there are not enough . <p> If you watched the Europe episode of the BBC 's Seven Worlds , One Planet series , you would have seen a cameo about the Iberian lynx . As things stand , we can not do that in Scotland because unlike almost every other country in western Europe our national parks are not owned by the nation .
