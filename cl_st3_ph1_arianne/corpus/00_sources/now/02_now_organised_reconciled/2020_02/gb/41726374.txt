There was even a rumour circulating that he wanted to move the event to a different location across the Border . <p> It seems Johnson does n't care one whit about having a positive relationship with Scotland in terms of making this vital summit a success or engaging in reassuring diplomacy for the 200 delegates attending . <p> If Nicola Sturgeon is n't at the COP26 gathering , people will be asking questions .
