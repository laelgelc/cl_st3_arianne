<h> From toothpaste to climate change : a timely history tracks the rise of the world economy <p> Philip Coggan has written a big and timely book , about a big topic . The subject is the long rise of the world economy , of human development and growing prosperity . It is timely because there are those who would wish to put a spanner in the works of rising global prosperity , and indeed are doing so . <p> Coggan 's account of the rise of the world economy is accessible and mercifully free of jargon . To illustrate the importance of trade in economic development , he begins with the humble toothpaste tube , pointing out that " its journey to your bathroom involves thousands of people and hundreds of processes " .
