<h> Suffering is Church Mission Society focus for Lent <p> IN RESPONSE to the impact of climate change and growing unrest and instability around the world , the Church Mission Society has launched a campaign , Lament for Lent . <p> The campaign urges Christians to take the time to lament properly the climate crisis , persecutions , war , and racism , so that they can learn to lean more deeply on God . <p> It was inspired by words from the Roman Catholic Archbishop Christophe Munzihirwa , of Bukavu , Congo , who was assassinated in 1996 . He said : " There are things that can be seen only with eyes that have cried . " <p> The free six-week resource , " Only with Eyes that have Cried " , draws on theologians such as Emmanuel Katongole and Walter Brueggemann , and also on experiences from CMS mission partners such as Ruth Radley , in South Sudan .
