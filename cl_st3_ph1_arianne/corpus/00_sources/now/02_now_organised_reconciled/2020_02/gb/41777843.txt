<h> Who is Clive Lewis ? <p> Lewis has had a varied career , working as a journalist and soldier before becoming an MP in 2015 . Born on a council estate in Northampton , he was raised by a single Afro-Caribbean father who encouraged him to become engaged in politics . <p> He was the first person in his family to go university , became vice-president of the NUS , and spent a decade working as a journalist for regional broadcasters before becoming the main presenter on the BBC 's Politics Show . He became an army reservist and completed a three-month tour of duty in Afghanistan . <p> After he was elected MP for Norwich South in 2015 , he declared New Labour to be " dead and buried " and promised to protect the most vulnerable from the Conservative Party 's " onslaught of cuts " . <p> Early in his political career Lewis was dubbed " one to watch " and a potential future leader of the Labour Party . He was appointed to the Labour frontbench as shadow minister of energy and climate change .
