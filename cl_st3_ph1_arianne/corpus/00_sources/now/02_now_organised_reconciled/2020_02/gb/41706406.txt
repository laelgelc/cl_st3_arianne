<h> Imdaad reaffirms commitment to UAE Vision 2021 National Agenda with launch of FARZ <p> Nation 's most advanced fully-automated material recovery facility has capacity to recycle 1,200 tonnes of waste per day <p> Press Release <p> Dubai , UAE : Underlining its commitment to promoting sustainability and recycling , Imdaad , a Dubai-based group of companies that provides integrated , sustainable facilities management services that enhance operational efficiencies of physical assets , today marked the official launch of FARZ , a state-of-the-art material recovery facility ( MRF ) situated in the National Industries Park in Jebel Ali . His Excellency Dr Thani bin Ahmed Al Zeyoudi , Minister of Climate Change and Environment of the UAE , was the chief guest at the event . <p> One of the largest fully-automated MRFs in the region , FARZ is also the most advanced facility of its kind in the UAE .
