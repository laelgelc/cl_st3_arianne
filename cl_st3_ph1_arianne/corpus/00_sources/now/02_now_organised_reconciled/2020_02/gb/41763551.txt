<p> There are estimated to be more than 3,000 " lost " settlements in Britain , including deserted medieval towns , abandoned hamlets and disappearing villages - with around 60 of them recorded in Derbyshire . <p> Some are remembered in current place or road names such as Chatsworth , Kedleston , Mercaston Lane or Ashe Lane , even though the original settlements have long vanished . <p> Why were so many places abandoned and left to revert back to nature ? <p> Reasons for desertion are varied across the county . They include more recent abandonment with the construction of reservoirs such as at Derwent and Ashopton , which both disappeared under millions of gallons of water in the mid-20th century . <p> Or some villages moved or vanished such as Chatsworth and Kedleston because the local land owners wanted to make aesthetic changes .
