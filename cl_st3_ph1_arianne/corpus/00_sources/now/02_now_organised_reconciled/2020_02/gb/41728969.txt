<h> A seismic political moment for the UK and Europe <p> We reflect on the day the UK formally left the EU with Professor Simon Hix and Professor Tony Travers . <p> 31 January 2020 marked the moment the UK formally left the EU . It was a day that some thought would never come and others hoped would never come . However , following three and a half years of political turmoil since the EU referendum in June 2016 it was the point at which Britain 's Brexit fate was finally and irrevocably sealed even if the arguments and practical realities surrounding it remain far from resolved . We spoke to two of our leading experts on British and EU politics to get their thoughts on this most seismic of political moments . <p> We may be leaving the EU but we have many common interests and reasons to work together <p> " Leaving the EU is a sad moment for some British people " , said Professor Simon Hix when we asked him to reflect on the significance of this day .
