Back to the future : Why the lure of the past could jeopardize the future for Industrials shows that at the beginning of a new decade , the sector is experiencing disruption at scale , from technology and trade to skills and sustainability . While many of these market shifts are having a positive impact placing Industrial companies at the forefront of dynamic developments and exciting innovations , leaders of the largest Industrial companies are concerned about maintaining and protecting business performance . <p> Baker McKenzie worked with an independent market research partner to compile this research . They conducted 700 interviews with Chief Executives , Chief Strategy Officers and Company Directors leading multinationals in the Automotive , Aviation &amp; Aerospace , Chemicals , Construction &amp; Engineering , Manufacturing and Transportation sectors .
