<p> Hundreds of local schoolchildren and university students are expected to go on strike in Plymouth City Centre this Friday . <p> As part of a campaign against " the continued and woeful lack of action on Climate Change " , campaign groups from across the region have planned to come together for a protest , with some children as young as seven expected to take part . <p> Organisers say Fridays for Future , Climate Strike 4 Schools , Climate Action and University of Plymouth Extinction Rebellion Society have planned the protest , while Plymouth Extinction Rebellion will also have a presence and will act as " stewards " . <p> The march will begin at Jigsaw Park at about midday and the group will walk down Cornwall Street to Place Du Brest , before walking along Armada Way and New George Street . <p> The march will then proceed through Drake Circus shopping centre and back to Jigsaw Park , before a Citizens Assembly at Methodist Central Hall . <p> Protest organisers say someone at each " station " ( main stop on the protest ) will speak on one of the four themes of the protest @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Organisers said : " As with all events Climate Change is too an important issue sic to be left to politicians who may have divided loyalty 's sic and need to be reminded that we live in a democracy where they take instruction from the public and not from big corporate lobbyists . <p> " Some Plymouth 's MP 's are well off the curve in this matter and need to start representing the concerns of their constituents properly on this very important matter .
