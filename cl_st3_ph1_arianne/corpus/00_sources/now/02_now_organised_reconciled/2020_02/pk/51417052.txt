<h> Electric Rickshaws will be launched in 3 Major Cities of Pakistan <p> ISLAMABAD , Pakistan : Electric Rickshaws with innovative swappable batteries will be launched in three Major Cities of Pakistan including Lahore , Sialkot and Peshawar with the collaboration of Asian Development Bank ( ADB ) and local manufacturers . <p> In this regard , the Prime Minister 's Advisor on Climate Change Malik Amin Aslam met a Korean delegation at his Office in Islamabad on Tuesday , and discussed the launch of Electric Rickshaws . <p> The advisor was briefed about the Pilot Project of Launching the Electric Rickshaws with innovative swappable batteries in Lahore , Sialkot and Peshawar . <p> It was told that there would be 600 Electric Rickshaws ready in approximately one month period . <p> Malik Amin Aslam said that this would be a good initiative to create awareness regarding climate change .
