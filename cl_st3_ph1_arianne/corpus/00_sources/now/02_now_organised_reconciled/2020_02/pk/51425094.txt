<h> Electric Rickshaws to be Introduced in 3 Major Cities <p> The adviser to the PM on Climate Change Malik Amin Aslam said that electric rickshaws would be introduced in 3 major cities , with creative swappable batteries . <p> While talking to a Korean delegation , who called on him in the federal capital he said that the three cities include , Peshwar , Lahore and Sialkot . <p> The adviser also added that this would be a good step towards building awareness about climate change , and also mentioned that this project would help in the measurement of the feasibility and the testing grounds for the experts in the future . <p> The Korean delegation also gave a briefing to the adviser regarding the project and said that there would be 600 electric rickshaws all set to be released in nearly one month period . <p> While displaying the suggested models of the e-rickshaws , the Korean delegation explained that the swappable batteries of the e-rickshaws would be recharged in an hour and is t best technology that is environmentally friendly too . <p> The three-wheeler vehicle would be introduced with the collaboration of the Asian Development @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on the 27th of January , the first produced 3-wheeler electric rickshaw had been launched out in a special ceremony that was held in Lahore . <p> The event was arranged by the Climate Change Ministry which had been attended among others by the adviser to the PM on Climate Change Malik Amin Aslam .
