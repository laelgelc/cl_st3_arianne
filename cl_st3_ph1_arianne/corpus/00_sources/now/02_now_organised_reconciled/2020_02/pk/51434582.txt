<h> UN Secretary-General to visit Pakistan from Sunday <h> Share <p> According to Foreign Office Spokesperson Aisha Farooqui , during the visit , the UN Secretary General will hold talks with Pakistani leadership and he will also address an International Conference on 40 Years of Hosting Afghan Refugees in Pakistan . <p> The two-day conference , which will be inaugurated by Prime Minister Imran Khan , is being organized in Islamabad by Pakistan and UN Refugee Agency . <p> The Secretary-General will also call on the President and the Prime Minister , and hold a meeting with Foreign Minister Makhdoom Shah Mahmood Qureshi . <p> The Secretary-General 's other engagements include interactions with parliamentarians , media and the youth . He will deliver special talks on themes of sustainable development , climate change and peacekeeping . <p> He will also travel to the Gurdwara Kartarpur Saheb . The Secretary-General will also pay a visit to Lahore .
