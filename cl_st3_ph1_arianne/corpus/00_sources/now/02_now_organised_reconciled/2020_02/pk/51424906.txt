<h> Import of Garlic and Ginger from China is Getting Costlier <p> The garlic and ginger being sold in the Pakistani markets in Lahore is getting costlier . <p> These items were being imported from China but the trade had ceased in mid of the coronavirus outbreak . <p> As per a local merchant , the price of the commodities has increased up to Rs.400 per kilogram . He further said that there is a prediction that it would be increasing to Rs.1,000 per kg if the trade is not resumed . <p> The other supplier of garlic and ginger besides China is Thailand but it can not meet the requirements of the Pakistani market alone , as informed by the local merchant .
