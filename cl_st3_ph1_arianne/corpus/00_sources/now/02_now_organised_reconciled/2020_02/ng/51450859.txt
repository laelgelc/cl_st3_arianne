<h> BUK 's Centre for Dryland Agriculture board inaugurated <p> The Vice-Chancellor , Bayero University Kano , BUK , Prof. Muhammad Bello on Monday inaugurated the Industry/Sectoral Advisory Board of the Centre for Dryland Agriculture , CDA . <p> The V-C , represented by the Deputy Vice-Chancellor , Academics , Prof. Adamu Tanko , charged the board members to bring their wealth of experience to bear in the Centre 's activities . <p> According to him , doing so would make the centre more relevant to the local and regional environments . <p> He expressed optimism that the contribution of the board would make positive impact the university . <p> In her remarks , the Chairperson of the board , Karimatu Babangida , a Director in the Federal Department of Agriculture , said the board would give priority to agricultural entrepreneurship , " where students would be prepared for industries before their graduation " . <p> According to her , climate change has affected agricultural activities negatively , especially in the dryland areas . <p> She noted that the areas could turn this challenge into opportunities and become food baskets of the world . <p> Mrs Babangida emphasized that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the CDA to achieve its mandate . <p> Speaking earlier , the Director , CDA , Prof. Jibrin Jibrin said inaugurating the board was part of the World Bank 's requirement so that the activities of the Africa Centres of Excellence , ACEs , would be aligned with the industries . <p> The director noted that since the establishment of CDA in 2012 , the University management had wisdom of designing a strategy of linking the activities of the Centre with the industries . <p> He said the CDA was lucky to have such technocrats to be at the helm of its industry board .
