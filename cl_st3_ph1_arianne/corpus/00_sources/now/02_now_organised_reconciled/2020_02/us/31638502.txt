<p> A little more than a year ago , Transportation Secretary Stephanie Pollack announced an ambitious reconfiguration of a tight stretch of land alongside the Charles River , to bring the Massachusetts Turnpike down to ground level through Allston and to elevate Soldiers Field Road above it . <p> It was a decisive call after five years of debate about the next major Boston-area highway project . The compromise plan managed to please most of the legions of neighborhood activists and advocacy organizations that had been working to shape it . <p> Until , that is , it became clear what the construction might actually entail : up to a decade of major disruptions for commuters and a temporary structure in the Charles to carry Soldiers Field Road . <p> Advertisement <p> " I think it 's very fair to say there are second thoughts , " said Emily Norton , executive director of the Charles River Watershed Association , which advocates for a clean river .
