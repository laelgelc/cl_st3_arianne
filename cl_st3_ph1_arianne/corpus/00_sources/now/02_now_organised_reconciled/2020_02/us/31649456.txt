<p> The University of Arizona 's Humanities Seminars Program is offering a climate change course open to the public to show impacts to Southern Arizona and the world . <p> Starting next month , professors from the university 's atmospheric sciences , hydrology and geosciences will give a " comprehensive overview " of climate change in six lectures from March 17 to April 28 . <p> The UA has opened registration to more than 500 people . Registration is $200 . The course will be on Tuesdays from 6 p.m. to 8 p.m. in the Environment and Natural Resources 2 building , 1064 E. Lowell St. <p> Some of the topics will touch on the evolution of earth systems and the role of the ocean and climate . <p> " This course is about climate change , which is the biggest challenge of the 21st century , " said Jessica E. Tierney , associate professor of geosciences . " The only way that we can understand where we 're headed in the future , this warm climate , is to study warm climates into the past .
