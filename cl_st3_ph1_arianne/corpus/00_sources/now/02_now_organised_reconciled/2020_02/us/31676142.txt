<h> Duffy Opens Up About Assault <p> " I was raped and drugged and held captive over some days . Of course I survived . The recovery took time . " <p> Duffy , July 2009 ( Mark Venema/Getty Images ) <p> Duffy , the Welsh singer best known for her 2008 hit " Mercy , " has opened up about her absence from the public eye in an Instagram post today ( February 25 ) . " Many of you wonder what happened to me , where did I disappear to and why , " Duffy wrote , continuing : <p> The truth is , and please trust me I am OK and safe now , I was raped and drugged and held captive over some days . Of course I survived . The recovery took time . There 's no light way to say it . But I can tell you in the last decade , the thousands and thousands of days I committed to wanting to feel the sunshine in my heart again , the sun does now shine . <p> Find Duffy 's note below .
