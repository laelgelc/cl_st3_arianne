<p> Two lawmakers in Sweden have nominated Swedish teenage climate activist Greta Thunberg for the 2020 Nobel Peace Prize . They say " action for reducing our emissions is therefore also an act of making peace . " <p> According to the Associated Press , Thunberg was nominated by Jens Holm and Kaham Svenelling , two members of Sweden 's Left Party . <p> Thunberg , 17 , has encouraged students to skip school to join protests demanding faster action on climate change . She was also nominated for the Nobel Peace Prize last year , won a Right Livelihood Award and was the youngest person ever named Time magazine 's " Person of the Year . " <p> " For sounding the alarm about humanity 's predatory relationship with the only home we have , for bringing to a fragmented world a voice that transcends backgrounds and borders , for showing us all what it might look like when a new generation leads , Greta Thunberg is TIME 's 2019 Person of the Year , " TIME editor-in-chief wrote . <p> Any national lawmaker is able to nominate someone for the Nobel Peace Prize .
