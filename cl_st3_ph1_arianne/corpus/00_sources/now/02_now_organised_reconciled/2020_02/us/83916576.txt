<h> Somerset Water Dept. to apply for $4 million to fix dam at reservoir <h> Friday <p> SOMERSET -- The Somerset Water Department will be applying for a grant for $4 million to rehabilitate the dam at the town 's reservoir that supplies the public drinking water supply . <p> The work on the dam would make it resistant to climate change , Water and Sewer Superintendent Robert Bozikowksi said . The work would include raising the crest of the dam and enlarging the overflow swale and culvert that is under North Street , based on a hydraulic model developed by the Water Department 's consulting engineer . <p> Bozikowski said he is seeking support from town officials , as well as people who live in Somerset Village and on Lake Street , for the grant because those are the most vulnerable areas . <p> Bozikowski will apply for a Massachusetts Municipal Vulnerability Preparedness Grant for the project .
