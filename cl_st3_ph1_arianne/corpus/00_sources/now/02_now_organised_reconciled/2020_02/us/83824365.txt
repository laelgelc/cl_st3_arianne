<h> HNN Introduces Classroom Activity Kits <p> Chelsea Connolly is an intern with the History News Network and an undergraduate at the George Washington University studying history . <p> Kyla Sommers is the editor-in-chief of the History News Network . <p> As part of a new initiative to merge the worlds of education and journalism , HNN is introducing a series of Classroom Activity Kits . <p> These kits , all crafted by undergraduate students at George Washington University , are designed to illustrate the relationship between current events and history . Each kit consists of a complete 45-60 minute lesson plan which requires little-to-no preparation on part of the instructor . These lessons are designed for high school and college students , but they can be altered to suit other education levels . <p> During these lessons , students will be use news articles as a tool for understanding a specific history . In doing so , students will engage in critical thinking , group-work , text analysis , research and more .
