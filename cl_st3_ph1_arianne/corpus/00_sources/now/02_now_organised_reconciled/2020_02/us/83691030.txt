Geneva time ( 0500 GMT ) there were 31,211 confirmed coronavirus cases in China and 637 deaths , as well as 270 cases in 24 other countries with 1 death , Tedros said . <p> " For the last two days there had been fewer reported infections in China , which is good news , but we @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ could go up again , " he said . <p> ( Reporting by Stephanie Nebehay in Geneva ; Writing by Nick Macfie ; editing by John Stonestreet and Giles Elgood ) <p> Our global editorial team of more than 50 journalists and about 250 freelancers covers the world 's under-reported stories at the heart of aid , development , women 's and LGBT+ rights , human trafficking , property rights , and climate change .
