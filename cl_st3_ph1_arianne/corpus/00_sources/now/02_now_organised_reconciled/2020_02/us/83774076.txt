<h> Updated : Thursday , February 13th , 2020 at 10:27am <p> PHOENIX -- Arizona cities and counties would not be allowed to ban new natural gas hookups under legislation moving through the Arizona Legislature , an effort to prevent the spread here of gas bans being adopted by California cities to help combat climate change . <p> The measure cleared the House in a 35-25 vote on Wednesday , and similar legislation is advancing in the Senate . All Republicans were joined by a handful of Democrats in support . <p> The bill 's supporters say homeowners should be able to decide for themselves what kind of energy they want to use in their homes . They also say restaurants would suffer if they could n't cook on gas stoves . <p> " It 's ridiculous that any government will get down to that level that they ' d tell someone how they can heat their house , " said Rep. Bob Thorpe , a Republican from Flagstaff .
