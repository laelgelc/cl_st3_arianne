<h> Polis to address Net Zero Cities next week <p> LOVELAND -- Only a few days remain for ticket sales to Net Zero Cities , a BizWest event that will delve into aspects of climate change . <p> Gov . Jared Polis will speak at the event , scheduled for 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. , Thursday , March 5 , at The Ranch event complex in Loveland . Admission is $79.49 , with tickets available here . <h> Sponsored Content <p> Net Zero Cities will unpack what climate change means for cities and counties and the reason behind the movement , as well as address implications for the private sector and the regional population at large . <p> Sessions at the BizWest-sponsored Net Zero Cities will be : <p> The circular economy -- recycling and reuse of products and materials . <p> Net Zero Colorado -- providing a snapshot of what cities , counties and the state are doing to combat climate change . <p> Climate change , business &amp; industry -- identifying what businesses are doing to address the issue .
