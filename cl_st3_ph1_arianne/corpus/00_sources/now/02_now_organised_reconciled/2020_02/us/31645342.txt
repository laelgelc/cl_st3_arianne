In a series of more than 400 airplane rides in 2017 , researchers with NASA 's Arctic Boreal Vulnerability Experiment discovered 2 million methane hotspots . <p> The findings point to one of the more disturbing aspects of climate change in the Arctic as frozen soil thaws , releasing greenhouse gases into the atmosphere . The Arctic is warming at an accelerated rate , and it 's causing frozen layers of soil -- called permafrost -- to melt . And methane is a powerful greenhouse gas : It carries a global warming potential that is some 30 times the potential of carbon dioxide over 100 years . <p> As more gets released into the atmosphere , the world will warm even further .
