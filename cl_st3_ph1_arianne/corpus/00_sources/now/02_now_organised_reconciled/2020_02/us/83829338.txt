<h> Pope cautions against ' unfair ' Middle East peace plans <p> ROME ( AP ) -- Pope Francis has cautioned against " unfair " solutions aimed at ending the conflict between Israelis and Palestinians . <p> In a speech Sunday during a visit to the Italian southern port city of Bari to reflect on peace in countries bordering the Mediterranean Sea , Francis lamented the many areas of war and conflict , including in the Middle East and Northern Africa . <p> Francis spoke of " the still unresolved conflict between Israelis and Palestinians , with the danger of not fair solutions , and , thus , presaging new crises . " <p> The pope did n't cite any specific proposals . <p> A new U.S. peace plan would let Israel annex all of its settlements along with the strategic Jordan Valley . It would give the Palestinians limited autonomy in several chunks of territory with a capital on the outskirts of Jerusalem , but only if they meet stringent conditions . <p> In the same speech , Francis took a swipe at populist politics .
