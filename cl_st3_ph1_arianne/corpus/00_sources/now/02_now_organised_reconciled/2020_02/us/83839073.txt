<p> BERLIN , Feb 23 ( Reuters ) - Voters in the German city of Hamburg punished Angela Merkel 's conservatives on Sunday , pushing them into third place in their first electoral test since the chancellor 's protegee gave up her hopes of taking the top job . <p> Exit polls also indicated that four days after a racist gunman killed 11 people in the western town of Hanau , the far-right Alternative for Germany ( AfD ) would be ejected from the Hamburg parliament , falling just short of the 5% threshold needed . <p> " Nazis out , " shouted supporters of the victorious Social Democrats ( SPD ) and Greens as they celebrated in the north German city . <p> Exit polls for public broadcaster ZDF put the SPD , who share power with the conservatives at the federal level , at 38% , down about 7 points from 2015 but still the strongest party in Hamburg , traditionally a centre-left stronghold . <p> Facing a backlash over a leadership crisis and an outcry over a local branch of the CDU in eastern Germany siding with the AfD , Merkel 's conservative Christian Democrats ( @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in Hamburg . <p> " For us at the CDU , this is a bitter election result , " said the CDU premier of the state of Schleswig Holstein , Daniel Guenther . <p> Two weeks ago , CDU leader Annegret Kramp-Karrenbauer plunged the party into chaos by saying she would stand aside , blowing open the race to succeed the chancellor . <p> Merkel , chancellor for almost 15 years , has said she will not run again in the next federal election , due by October 2021 . <p> The Greens were the biggest winners , reflecting their strength at the national level , due mainly to growing fears about climate change .
