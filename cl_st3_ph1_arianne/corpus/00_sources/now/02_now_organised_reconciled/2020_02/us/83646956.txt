<h> Siemens Energy ready to be at center of global climate debate - CEO <p> Published Wed , Feb 5 20205:03 AM EST <p> MUNICH , Feb 5 ( Reuters ) - Siemens Chief Executive Joe Kaeser is ready for Siemens Energy - an energy business it plans to spin off by September - to be at the center of the global debate on climate change , he told the company 's AGM on Wednesday . <p> " That is why we are setting up a sustainability committee , where independent leadership people will be represented , " he said . <p> " This committee will report on sensitive projects , and be able to escalate them , " Kaeser said . <p> The trains to factory software maker has come under fire after it agreed to honor a contract to supply signaling equipment to a massive coal mine in Australia .
