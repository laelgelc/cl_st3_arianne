<p> ( Corrects name of mall to Terminal 21 , not Century 21 , in third paragraph ) <p> BANGKOK , Feb 8 ( Reuters ) - Dozens of people were seen running from the shopping mall where a soldier has been on a shooting ramapage in northeastern Thailand , live pictures on the local Amarin television network showed . <p> The people came out in groups , some carrying children . Others supported elderly people , carrying them with one person on each side . <p> Police said at least 17 people were killed and 21 wounded in the shooting at the Terminal 21 mall in Nakhon Ratchasima .
