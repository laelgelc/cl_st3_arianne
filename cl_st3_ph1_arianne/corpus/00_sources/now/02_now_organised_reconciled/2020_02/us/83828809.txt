<h> Greta Thunberg 's mother speaks out on daughter 's autism <p> Greta Thunberg 's mother is opening up about her daughter 's struggles , including an eating disorder that almost required the young girl to be hospitalized . <p> In the family 's new book , Our House is on Fire : Scenes of a Family and a Planet in Crisis , the Swedish environmental activist 's mother , Swedish opera singer Malena Ernman , details everything from her daughter 's autism and eating disorder to panic attacks and bullying at school , the Observer reports . <p> " She was slowly disappearing into some kind of darkness , " Ernman says . " She stopped playing the piano . She stopped laughing . She stopped talking . And she stopped eating .
