<p> LONDON , Feb 2 ( Reuters ) - It is difficult to see how there will not be any new checks between Britain and Northern Ireland if London pursues a trade agreement which is not aligned with the European Union , Arlene Foster , leader of Northern Ireland 's DUP , said on Sunday . <p> Asked whether she believed Prime Minister Boris Johnson 's assertions that there will be no new checks , Foster told Sky News : " It 's difficult to see how that is the case given that we 've heard from other members of the cabinet that they intend to diverge from single market regulations , whilst Northern Ireland remains within the single market . It 's difficult to see how that 's going to work .
