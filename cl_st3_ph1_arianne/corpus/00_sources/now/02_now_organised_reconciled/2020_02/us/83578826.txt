<h> Despite plan , South Bend disciplines more black students <p> SOUTH BEND , Ind . ( AP ) -- In 2013 , the South Bend school district created a department to tackle the chronic problem of black students being disciplined at higher rates than others . <p> Seven years later -- after the district has spent more than $1 million on the African American Student and Parent Services department -- a greater percentage of black students are suspended and expelled than before the department was created . <p> Black students , who today make up 37% of the district 's enrollment , are still nearly five times as likely to be expelled and nearly four times as likely to be suspended out of school as their white classmates , state data show . <p> Out-of-school suspensions totaled 61% of the black student population in 2018 , compared to 39% in 2013 .
