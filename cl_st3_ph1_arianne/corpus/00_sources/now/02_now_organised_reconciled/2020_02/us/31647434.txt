<h> Gov . Lamont elected chair of Northeastern Governors Coalition , will host Canadian premiers in September <p> Gov . Ned Lamont meets with the governors of Maine , Massachusetts , and Vermont during a meeting of the Coalition of Northeastern Governors on February 9 , 2020 . Courtesy of Gov . Lamont 's office . <p> Gov . Ned Lamont was elected to serve as chairman of the Coalition of Northeastern Governors Friday . He will serve in the position for one year . <p> The nonpartisan group includes the governors of Connecticut , Maine , Massachusetts , New Hampshire , New York , Rhode Island , and Vermont . Founded in 1976 , it aims to enhance communication and cooperation among the states in the region . <p> Advertisement <p> " I 've long believed , particularly among the smaller states , that regional cooperation works best when implementing policies that work best for our residents , " said Lamont in a statement . " This is particularly true when it comes to thing like the creation of a modernized transportation system , energy policies that deliver fair prices for consumers in the cleanest ways possible , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ can have a much more powerful voice working collectively as a region . " <p> The association 's current policy priorities include integrating regional transportation systems and environmental measures to address climate change , such as promoting energy efficiency and clean energy . The group also supports the Northeast Committee on Environment , an informal association of the seven states ' environmental commissioners . <p> Lamont also said Connecticut is expected to host the annual Conference of New England Governors and Eastern Canadian Premiers in Montville on September 20 to 22 , 2020 . The conference includes the six New England governors and the premiers of New Brunswick , Newfoundland &amp; Labrador , Nova Scotia , Prince Edwards Island , and Quebec . The conference began in 1973 , with the purpose of enhancing cooperation on shared interests across the border . Current priorities include transportation , air quality , and climate change .
