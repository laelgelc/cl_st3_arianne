<h> GOP House votes to move energy office now run by Democrat <p> Updated 3:15 pm PST , Thursday , February 13 , 2020 <p> TALLAHASSEE , Fla . ( AP ) -- Florida 's Office of Energy would be moved out of the agency run by the only statewide elected Democrat and into an agency controlled by Republican Gov . Ron DeSantis under a bill passed by the House on Thursday . <p> The GOP-dominated House voted 76-40 to move the Office of Energy from the Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services to the Department of Environmental Protection in what Democrats are calling a power grab . Democrat Nikki Fried is the state 's agriculture commissioner . <p> " Under our Department , the Office of Energy is finally working , so let 's call it what it is -- a power grab to benefit the Governor ( and ) slow down our progress on energy and climate change . I 'm hopeful that cooler heads will prevail in the Florida Senate , " Fried said in a statement emailed to the media . <p> House Democrats also suggested the bill was politically motivated . <p> " I @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ progression of the state of Florida , " said Democratic Rep. Kimberly Daniels . " It 's a scary thing to do things just because we can . " <p> But Republican Rep. Rick Roth pointed out that the Office of Energy was once part of the Department of Environmental Protection and said it 's a good decision to return it to the agency . <p> " It 's not politics . It 's an effort to try to consolidate agencies that are doing like functions , " he said . " This is good policy . "
