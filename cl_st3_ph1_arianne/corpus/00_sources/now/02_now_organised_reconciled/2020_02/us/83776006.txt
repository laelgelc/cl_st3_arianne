<p> MOSCOW , Feb 12 ( Reuters ) - The Kremlin said on Wednesday that Turkey was not upholding agreements it made with Moscow to " neutralise " militants in the Syrian province of Idlib and that militant attacks on Syrian and Russian forces were continuing in the region . <p> Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said Moscow remained committed to the agreements it struck with Ankara , but that Russia considered the attacks in Idlib to be unacceptable and in contravention of Moscow 's deal with Ankara .
