<h> MEDICINE Environment not only worry <h> The OBSERVER 's View <p> We are told frequently that we ought to be worried about the seas rising , because of climate change . But another type of sea -- of viruses and bacteria -- also is rising . Ought we to be as complacent as we clearly are about it ? <p> At this writing , Chinese officials had said the death toll in a viral epidemic there had risen to more than 550 . Nearly 28,000 cases of the disease had been confirmed worldwide . <p> Given China 's past reluctance to admit the seriousness of outbreaks , it is entirely possible the numbers are far in excess of those reported . <p> A new type of coronavirus is being blamed for the outbreak in China . The name comes from the appearance of that type of virus under powerful microscopes . A kind of halo appears to surround the virus . <p> Chinese scientists have said the virus was transmitted somehow from animals to people . But , they add , it also can be caught from contact with an infected person .
