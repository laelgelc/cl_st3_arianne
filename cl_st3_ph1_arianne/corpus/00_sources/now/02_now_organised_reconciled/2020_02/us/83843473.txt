<h> Post <h> Can We Address Emissions Using The Pareto Principal ? <p> Finding solutions to Planet Earth 's emission problem seems to have forgotten the Pareto principle , i.e. the 80/20 rule . While China and India are two of those countries in the 20% causing part of the 80% of global emissions , the world appears to focus on solutions from the 80% of countries causing 20% of the problem . <p> The Pareto principle ( also known as the 80/20 rule , the law of the vital few , or the principle of factor sparsity ) states that , for many events , roughly 80% of the effects come from 20% of the causes . This is also true in the case of global CO2 emissions . <p> Chart 1 : The Pareto Principle <p> Where in business and science 's efforts to solve problems has a similar strategy to focus 80% of the effort in CO2 reduction on the 20% result , ever been smart , effective , or efficient ? <p> Let 's first take a look at the share of coal production by key countries in Graph 1 where China and India are @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the world coal production projected by 2040 . <p> Graph 1 : Share Of Coal Production By Key Country <p> Twelve of the 15 most polluted cities in the world are in India .
