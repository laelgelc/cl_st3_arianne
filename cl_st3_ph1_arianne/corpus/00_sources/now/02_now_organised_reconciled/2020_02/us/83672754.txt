<h> Fact sheet focuses on building climate resiliency <p> LYONS - The Center for Rural Affairs has released a fact sheet focused on building resiliency in the face of climate change . <p> Policy Associate Kayla Bergman says they created it because of increased concerns their constituents are bringing up . <p> Bergman says this guide addresses climate resiliency in four different lifestyles ; educators , rural entrepreneurs , homeowners , and farmers and landowners . <p> " For educators it outlines ways to reduce carbon footprints in the classroom . The rural entrepreneurs fact sheet outlines four different sections on how to lead on climate in your business . The third fact sheet is on a homeowners guide for climate leadership and it kind of outlines ways for you in home to reduce your carbon footprint . " <p> Bergman says for farmers and landowners , it talks about investing in soil health and the impact that has on the climate .
