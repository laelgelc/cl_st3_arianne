<p> Last week , an elaborate procession led King Felipe VI through the Spanish capital to Congress , where he gave a speech officially inaugurating a new government . The ceremony was an awkward reunion . The governing coalition is the most left-wing administration since the Spanish Civil War , and many of its members are very open about the fact that they want to abolish the monarchy . The last time he was here , in 2016 , members of the radical Podemos Party -- now in a fragile partnership with the moderate Spanish Socialist Workers ' Party ( PSOE ) -- refused to applaud him , and others wore shirts with anti-monarchy slogans .
