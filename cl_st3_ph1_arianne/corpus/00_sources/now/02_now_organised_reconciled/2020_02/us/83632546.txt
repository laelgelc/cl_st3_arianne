<p> I attended the Jan. 22 special Planning &amp; Zoning Commission meeting regarding the proposed Oenoke facility . One important point was missed : Climate change was not considered <p> Should the overlay zone succeed , flooding is a condition the Planning &amp; Zoning Commission can and must consider and plan for . I live in one of the 165 Oenoke condos below the proposed facility . Engineers warn the Oenoke drainage system may experience back flow and the drainage pond/marsh may overflow . The Planning &amp; Zoning office told me builders must equal present conditions but need not improve those conditions . The proposed facility does not address climate change -- perhaps because state and local statutes do not address climate change -- yet climate change is very much with us today . This is an important consideration . <p> A reminder : The proposed Oenoke facility is not a continuing care retirement community . It does not offer assisted living .
