<h> News <h> Global energy-related carbon emissions flat in 2019 <p> BERLIN -- Global energy-related emissions of heat-trapping carbon dioxide remained steady last year , with declines in rich countries balancing out a rise in poor nations , according to data published Tuesday . <p> The International Energy Agency said emissions of the main man-made greenhouse gas stayed at 33 gigatons in 2019 , even as the world economy grew by 2.9% . <p> " This was primarily due to declining emissions from electricity generation in advanced economies , thanks to the expanding role of renewable sources ( mainly wind and solar ) , fuel switching from coal to natural gas , and higher nuclear power generation , " the Paris-based agency said . " Other factors included milder weather in several countries , and slower economic growth in some emerging markets . " <p> The country with the biggest drop in energy-related CO2 emissions was the United States , which recorded a fall of 2.9% to 4.8 gigatons on the back of coal-fired power plant closures and lower demand for electricity .
