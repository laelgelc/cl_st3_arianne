<p> BANGKOK , Feb 8 ( Reuters ) - Police and soldiers have stormed into the Thai shopping mall where a soldier has been on a shooting rampage and helped hundreds of people to escape , defence ministry spokesman Kongcheep Tantrawanit told Reuters . <p> " Police officers and soldiers are joining forces and have helped evacuate hundreds of people from inside the mall , " he said . " It 's not known how many are still inside . " <p> He said the suspected gunman was still inside the Terminal 21 mall in Nakhon Ratchasima .
