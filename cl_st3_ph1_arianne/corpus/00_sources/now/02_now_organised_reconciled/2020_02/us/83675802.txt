<h> Nature Watch : Decline of butterflies in Erie area alarming <h> Saturday <p> Feb 8 , 2020 at 12:01 AM <p> Presque Isle State Park alone has lost nearly half of its species in just the past couple of decades . <p> Whether it 's climate change , loss of habitat or the use of herbicides and pesticides , butterflies are disappearing . I have been monitoring butterflies in Erie County for more than 45 years and have seen drastic changes . Some species have become rare or are completely gone like coral hairstreak , dreamy duskywing , Leonard 's skipper , Indian skipper and tawny-edged skipper . Presque Isle State Park has lost nearly half of its species in just the past couple of decades mainly because of changes in habitat .
