The specific potential impact of extreme events on energy systems has been difficult to quantify due to the unpredictability of future weather events . Here we develop a stochastic-robust optimization method to consider both low impact variations and extreme events . Applications of the method to 30 cities in Sweden , by considering 13 climate change scenarios , reveal that uncertainties in renewable energy potential and demand can lead to a significant performance gap ( up to 34% for grid integration ) brought by future climate variations and a drop in power supply reliability ( up to 16% ) due to extreme weather events .
