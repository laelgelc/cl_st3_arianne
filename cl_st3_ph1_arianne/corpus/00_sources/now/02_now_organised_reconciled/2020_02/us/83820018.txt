<h> Grimes Personifies The End Of The World In New Album , ' Miss Anthropocene ' <p> The fifth studio album from experimental indie pop artist , Grimes , is here and it 's a soundtrack fit for the end of the world . Miss Anthropocene , out via 4AD Records , is 15 tracks of hypnotic insanity that stretch and pull at your emotions but never let you leave . Throughout the album the soundscapes vary greatly , featuring drawn out angel-like vocals , to heavy nu-metal punk-like grooves . It is quite a departure from her last album , Art Angels , almost 5 years ago which house more upbeat and dancey qualities . <p> Loosely a concept album , the tracks are about personifying climate change in hopes that it would be easier to look at . By taking something abstract and turning it something more tangible , she hopes to make climate change more fun instead of something to be guilty about . <p> You can listen to Miss Anthropocene now , below .
