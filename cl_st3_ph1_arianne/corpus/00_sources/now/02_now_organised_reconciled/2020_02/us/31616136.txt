Similar process will continue till a preference for all the cadres is indicated by the candidate . <p> If a candidate does not give any preference for any of the Zones or Cadres , it will be presumed that the candidate has no specific preference for those Zones or cadres . <p> However , a candidate claiming benefit of reservation under Physically Disabled quota will have an additional option to indicate his preference ( at the time of indicating preferences for various Zones and Cadres thereunder ) for any one state or cadre ( other than Home cadre ) of his first preferred zone . <p> A candidate will be allotted to his Home cadre , on the basis of the candidate 's merit , preference and vacancy available at his turn in his category . <p> For allocation to Home cadre against an Insider vacancy , a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Zone in which his Home cadre falls as well as first preference to the Home cadre within that relevant Zone , otherwise the candidate will not be considered for his Home cadre at all . <p> The cadre allocation will consist of three steps as follows : <p> First , all those candidates who can he allocated against the Insider vacancies available in their category would be so allocated . <p> Second , the candidates who are eligible for allocation to their Home cadre will be allocated .
