<p> Bernie Sanders , at seventy-eight , three months clear of a heart attack , has outlived obscurity to become the co-front-runner for the Democratic nomination for the Presidency of the United States . He is still thin and intent ; to my eye , the hunch in his back has deepened . On a tour of Iowa last weekend , he wore a suit with an open-necked shirt , and his hair was on the tame side of its range .
