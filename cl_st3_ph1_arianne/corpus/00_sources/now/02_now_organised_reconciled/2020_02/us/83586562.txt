<h> Punxsutawney Phil Says Winter Is Canceled <p> Put your hats and scarves in storage , it is going to be a short winter . <p> This is n't just wishful thinking , this is an empirical , measurable truth discovered Sunday in Punxsutawney , Pennsylvania . <p> Local weather prognosticator Punxsutawney Phil , the shaggy Nostradamus of Gobbler 's Knob , emerged from his woodland home and failed to see his shadow . Therefore , so says this marmota monax Jeane Dixon , it 's as clear as a gorgeous spring day ( like the ones coming sooner than usual ) that the icy blasts of winter shall be truncated this year . <p> Punxsutawney Phil has seen his shadow 103 times since his climate-predicting powers were first exploited by top hatted Pennsylvanians in 1887 . Seeing his shadow means six more weeks of winter . This year is only the 20th time he has not done so . There are nine years of Phil 's predictions that have been lost to time , and one year ( 1943 ) when no prediction was given at all .
