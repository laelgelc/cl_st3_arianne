<p> President Trump is proposing a $4.8 trillion spending plan for the next fiscal year , called " A Budget for America 's Future . " Unfortunately , it would mortgage that future by slashing funding for scientific and technological research . <p> The White House 's budget is a wish list , a statement of priorities . Both Democrats and Republicans in Congress will push back on many of President Trump 's alarming proposals , including huge cuts in Medicaid , education , environmental protection , and foreign aid . But lawmakers ought to handle these budget negotiations as more than the latest round of annual haggling over the operations of individual government agencies . They ought to respond to the provocation in the budget 's title .
