<p> ALBANY -- A town hall will be held at Albany Public Library , 161 Washington Ave. , to discuss the governor 's proposed state budget among community members Saturday . <p> The event is hosted by Assemblywoman Patricia Fahy , a Democrat representing the 109th district , and fellow Democratic Assemblyman John McDonald , who represents the 108th district . It will take place in the Community Room at 10:30 a.m. until noon . <p> The governor 's address also included several progressive plans previewed in his State of the State address earlier this month , such as a proposal to legalize the recreational use of marijuana . <p> Additional legislative initiatives include removing guns from domestic abusers , passing a hate-crime anti-terrorism act , closing prisons , consolidating local jails , and legalizing and regulating e-bikes . Cuomo highlighted action against climate change as a top priority at his address , proposing $33 billion in environmental and energy spending over the next five years .
