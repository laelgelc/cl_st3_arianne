<h> TMC NEWS <h> TMCNET eNEWSLETTER SIGNUP <p> MEDIA ADVISORY : Stanford Graduate School of Business 2nd Annual Climate , Business and Innovation Conference to Convene on February 12 <p> February 10 , 2020 <h> MEDIA ADVISORY : Stanford Graduate School of Business 2nd Annual Climate , Business and Innovation Conference to Convene on February 12 <p> Stanford Graduate School of Business <p> WHAT : <p> The second annual Climate , Business and Innovation conference at Stanford Graduate School of Business ( GSB ) will inform the Stanford community of the risks and opportunities presented by climate change . <p> More than 200 students and alumni from across Stanford University will join industry leaders in finance , food , energy , technology , policy and sustainable cities to discuss how climate change is shaping the future of business with the goal of inspiring a dialogue on the relevance and importance of these issues to tomorrow 's business leaders .
