<p> ( Adds comments from Taiwan president , first Taiwan patient released from hospital , paragraphs 5-6 and 16 , byline ) <p> By Fabian Hamacher <p> TAIPEI , Feb 7 ( Reuters ) - Taiwan and China are embroiled in a new dispute over the fate of Taiwanese stranded in the Chinese city of Wuhan , the epicentre of a coronavirus outbreak , after Taiwan said a citizen sent back on the first flight was infected with the disease . <p> Only one flight from China , which claims Taiwan as its own territory , has evacuated 247 of the estimated 500 Taiwanese caught up in China 's preventative lockdown of Wuhan . <p> Beijing has permitted countries from the United States to Japan to send flights to Wuhan , some of them several times , to collect their nationals . <p> But Taiwan and China have been unable to agree on more flights to take out the Taiwanese . <p> Taiwan 's China policy-making Mainland Affairs Council said the first flight back , on Monday , had one passenger confirmed to be infected with the virus and three others who had fevers . <p> All the passengers @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the sick passenger " created a tear in virus prevention " , the council said in a statement . <p> " This also caused an overlap infection risk for the people on the same flight , and if there are even more infected people caused by this then the serious consequences do n't bear thinking about .
