<p> LONDON , Feb 6 ( Reuters ) - Britain is to help protect Australia 's biodiversity in the emergency collection of plant seeds following the country 's devastating wildfires . <p> Scientists from the Royal Botanic Gardens at Kew in London will help with emergency seed-collecting in areas devastated by the bushfires and store specimens at its Millennium Seed Bank ( MSB ) in Britain . <p> Kew 's MSB works as an insurance policy against plant extinction so they can be protected for the future . <p> Seeds are stored in air-tight glass containers stacked in huge -20 ? C freezers and can be used to grow a new generation of plants in years to come . <p> For the collecting , Kew scientists will work alongside colleagues in the Australian Seed Bank Partnership . <p> " We ... are pleased to be able to support their efforts , as part of our ongoing partnership to address biodiversity loss through seed-banking in Australia , " said the MSB 's Elinor Breman in a statement . <p> She added : " Kew 's scientists will work with the ASBP to conduct emergency seed-collecting in areas devastated by the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the international effort to restore habitats more quickly in this precious and biodiverse region . " <p> Kew has worked with Australian seed banks since 2000 , sharing expertise on seed collection processes , conservation and research so that the seeds of plant species considered rare or threatened can be banked and conserved for the future . <p> So far , 12,450 seed collections representing 8,900 Australian species , all of which are saved in local seed banks , have been duplicated and stored in Kew 's MSB . <p> Australia 's wildfires have burned through an area the size of Greece since September , in what the government there has called an ecological disaster . <p> The protected species of Wollemi Pines - prehistoric trees which outlived the dinosaurs - survived the wildfires . <p> Others were not so lucky : wood-chopping company Kangaroo Island Plantation Timbers suspended trading in its shares after severe fire-damage meant 90% of its tree crop was no longer productive . <p> The collaboration with Kew was announced by visiting British Foreign Secretary Dominic Raab . <p> " This further collaboration between the Australian @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , will help protect Australia 's precious biodiversity following the terrible bushfires , " Raab said .
