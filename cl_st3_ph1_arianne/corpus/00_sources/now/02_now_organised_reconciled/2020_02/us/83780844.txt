<h> Tompkins County receives grant to reduce food waste and help hungry New Yorkers <p> Wednesday , February 12th 2020 , 8:55 AM EST by Olivia Jaquith <p> TOMPKINS COUNTY , N.Y . ( WENY ) -- Friendship Donations Network could soon be receiving an influx of food in Tompkins County , with new investments from the state to reduce waste and cut greenhouse gas emissions . <p> Governor Cuomo yesterday announced grants totaling more than $4.3 million for 111 projects across the state . <p> " Wasted food hurts needy families facing the terrible challenges of food insecurity and harms the environment by growing landfills and contributing to climate change , " Cuomo said . " These awards are the latest step New York is taking to help local governments and community organizations support smart investments that prevent food waste , reduce greenhouse gas emissions caused by food disposal , and provide nutritious and healthy food to combat hunger across New York State .
