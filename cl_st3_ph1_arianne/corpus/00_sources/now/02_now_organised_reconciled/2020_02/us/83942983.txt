<h> New Mexico Forestry Division to plant 1,200 trees <p> Updated 8:53 pm PST , Friday , February 28 , 2020 <p> SANTA FE , N.M . ( AP ) -- The New Mexico Forestry Division will be planting 1,200 trees across the state in celebration of Arbor Month . <p> Officials with the agency say the month of March often has the best conditions for tree planting as snow melt and soil moisture vary from south to north . The work is being organized through the Urban and Community Forestry Program in support of the New Mexico Climate Strategy and the National Association of State Foresters ' centennial celebration . <p> State Forester Laura McCarthy said planting trees is an important part of New Mexico 's response to climate change . <p> " Trees provide shade , help cool our communities and reduce our energy use , " McCarthy said . " Trees provide an especially significant benefit in areas that are dense with heat-absorbing roads and buildings where shade trees can provide as much as 10 degrees of cooling . " <p> Gov .
