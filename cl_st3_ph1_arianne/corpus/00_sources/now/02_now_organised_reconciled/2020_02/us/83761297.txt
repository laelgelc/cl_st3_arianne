<p> Christmas came and went - and so did the cookies you left for Santa ! But what do reindeer eat offseason ? <p> This is Sandra Tsing Loh with the Loh Down on Science . <p> Climate change in the far north freezes Rudolph 's favorite plants . It also melts sea-ice bridges , preventing reindeer from foraging elsewhere ! Yet , they seem to be doing OK . What gives ? <p> Brage Hansen of Norway University of Science and Technology and an international research team investigated . <p> By collecting poop from over one-hundred Norwegian reindeer . They analyzed carbon in the dung to see what reindeer eat during tough times . Different carbon types and amounts imply different plant-based diets . <p> Result ? Hansen 's team found that the reindeer gave up their usual candy cane diets ... for SEAWEED ! Seaweed snacks help to top off reindeer tummies when land plants get too icy . Too much seems to make digestion ... move a little faster , if you catch my snow-drift . <p> Looks like climate change is altering reindeer diets , and moving their herds towards the shores !
