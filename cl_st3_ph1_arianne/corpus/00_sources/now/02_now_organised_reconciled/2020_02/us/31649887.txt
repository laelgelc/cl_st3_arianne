While a chill remains on new construction in the United States , the golf market has been boiling in many far-away regions . <p> As has been the case throughout his career on and off the golf course , Greg Norman has placed himself at the center of the action . Later in February , Greg Norman Golf Course Design will open its latest creation , Rancho San Lucas in Cabo San Lucas , Mexico . The course spans several distinct environments on the southwestern tip of Baja California , beginning in upland terrain marked by arroyos , giant saguaro and other desert vegetation , transitioning into large intermediary sand dunes and finally stretching onto the breezy Pacific Ocean beachfront . Norman says he has built golf holes on each type of land , but never on a site that encompassed all three . <p> In the years following the financial recession of 2009 , when nearly all golf development was temporarily snuffed out , the opening of an upscale resort like Rancho San Lucas , with 14 ocean-view holes and several more @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ one of the year 's golf highlights .
