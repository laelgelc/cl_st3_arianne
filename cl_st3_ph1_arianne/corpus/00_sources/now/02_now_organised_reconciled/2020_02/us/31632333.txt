It 's certainly better than I 've thought . <p> You may not have noticed it , amid the flood of bad news about the " Emissions Gap " and the collapse of the COP25 climate conference in Madrid , but over the last few weeks a new narrative about the climate future has emerged , on balance encouraging , at least to an alarmist like me .
