<p> HONG KONG , Feb 13 ( Reuters ) - The Hong Kong and Singapore legs of the World Rugby Sevens Series will be postponed until October , due to the coronavirus outbreak , World Rugby said in a statement on Thursday . <p> The Singapore leg will take place in Oct. 10-11 , and Hong Kong 's tournament is scheduled for Oct. 16-18 . <p> " This prudent decision has been taken in order to help protect the global rugby community and the wider public , " it said , adding the move was " fully supported by stakeholders , including unions and commercial partners .
