<h> Column : The State of the Union address was a parade of Trump lies <p> President Donald Trump delivers his State of the Union address to a joint session of Congress in the House Chamber on Capitol Hill in Washington on Feb. 4 , 2020 , as House Speaker Nancy Pelosi listens . ( LEAH MILLIS/AP ) <p> More than once , back in the 1990s , I found opportunities to quote Sen. Bob Kerrey , a Nebraska Democrat , on the Democrat then in the White House : " Clinton 's an unusually good liar . Unusually good . Do you realize that ? " <p> By the standards of his time , that was true . But today , watching Bill Clinton 's presidential prevarications is like watching gold medal figure skaters from the 1960s . The jumps that were impressive then would be embarrassingly puny today . Donald Trump lies with an extravagance , shamelessness and frequency that put Clinton in the shade . <p> Advertisement <p> Clinton saw the truth as something he could sometimes depart from when he thought it necessary . He lied selectively . Trump sees the truth as his greatest @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ lies profusely . <p> No , undocumented immigrants are not making us less safe ; their incarceration rates are below that of native-born Americans . No , he is not protecting patients with pre-existing conditions ; his administration is in court trying to dismantle the Affordable Care Act , which includes that safeguard . <p> He said , " I want people to come into our country in the largest numbers ever , but they have to come in legally , " but he has slashed refugee admissions and cut legal immigration overall . He boasted of raising blue-collar wages , but much of the credit for that improvement goes to minimum wage increases enacted by states and cities . The Washington Post 's Fact Checker tabulated 31 dubious claims . <p> Flagrant deception is at the heart of Trump 's approach to virtually everything . In his view , climate change is a myth ; the economy is booming beyond belief ; North Korea is denuclearizing ; tax cuts pay for themselves ; Russia did n't meddle in the 2016 election to help him . His method is to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that they stop trying to tell what 's actually true . <p> The reason he does that is because the truth does not serve him well . In the real world , the climate is changing : The planet 's five hottest years on record were the last five years . Economic growth is only middling . North Korea has not given up a single nuclear weapon . The tax cuts helped boost the deficit to more than $1 trillion a year . Even Republican senators admit that Putin intervened to defeat Hillary Clinton . <p> For the most part , Trump , his party and his followers are determined to live in a fake world that fits their preconceptions rather than face simple , unpleasant facts . They refuse to confront the consequences of a warming planet , exploding government debt and more . <p> But eventually , it will all catch up with us with a vengeance . You can try to avoid reality , but reality will not avoid you .
