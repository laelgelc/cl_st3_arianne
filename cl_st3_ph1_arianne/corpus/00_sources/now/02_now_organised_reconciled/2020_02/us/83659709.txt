<p> MILAN , Feb 6 ( Reuters ) - A high-speed train derailed in the northern Italian province of Lodi in the early hours of Thursday , killing its two drivers and interrupting traffic on the busy line between Milan and Bologna , officials said . <p> Video from the scene showed that the front engine and at least one passenger wagon had skipped the tracks , flipping onto their side . Photographs suggested the engine had broken clear of the rest of the train and smashed into an adjacent building . <p> The head of Italy 's civil protection unit said the two dead were the train drivers . A further 27 people were hurt , but none had life-threatening injuries . <p> " There should n't be any more deaths , " the government representative in Lodi , Marcello Cardona , told reporters at the scene , adding it was lucky the toll had n't been higher . <p> Officials said only 33 people had been aboard the early morning train , which had left Italy 's financial capital Milan at 5.10 a.m. ( 0410 GMT ) and was heading to the southern city of Salerno . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , some 40 km ( 25 miles ) from Milan . <p> Italy 's state railways said it was investigating the accident .
