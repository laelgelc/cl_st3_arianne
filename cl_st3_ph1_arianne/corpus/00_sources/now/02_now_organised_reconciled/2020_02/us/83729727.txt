<p> HONG KONG , Feb 11 ( Reuters ) - Hong Kong is evacuating people from a residential building in the New Territories district of Tsing Yi , where two people confirmed with coronavirus live though on different floors , authorities said early on Tuesday . <p> The director of the Centre for Health Protection , Dr Wong Ka-hing , said the government was investigating the suspected environmental transmission of two cases in the building . <p> Hong Kong has confirmed 42 cases of coronavirus , which has killed more than 900 people , all but two in mainland China .
