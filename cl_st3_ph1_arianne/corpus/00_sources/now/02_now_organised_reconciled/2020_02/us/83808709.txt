<h> Delta Air Lines to invest $1 bn to reduce emissions <p> Delta Air Lines has also pledged to voluntarily cap its emissions at 2012 levels and stepped up its recycling efforts <p> Delta Air Lines said Friday it plans to invest $1 billion over the next decade to reduce its emissions , the first major airline to make such a commitment . <p> " There 's no challenge we face that is in greater need of innovation than environmental sustainability , and we know there is no single solution , " Delta CEO Ed Bastian said in announcing the move . <p> The investment aims to reduce emissions across all of the US airline 's operations worldwide , starting in March and lasting for 10 years . <p> Delta says it will invest in technologies to reduce airline CO2 emissions and waste , though it does not specify if technologies like carbon capture and removal are among the innovations it will targeting . <p> As concern grows over climate change , air carriers are aiming to drastically reduce their capping its own emissions at 2012 levels , while recycling the aluminum cans , plastic bottles and cups and paper waste it generates . <p> The United Nations recently announced that the decade between
