<h> Florida lawmaker switches allegiance from Joe Biden to Mike Bloomberg <h> Share this : <p> Former state Rep. Robert Asencio on Tuesday switched his endorsement for president from Joe Biden to Mike Bloomberg . <p> The Miami Democrat switched his vote weeks ahead of Florida 's March 17 Presidential Preference Primary . He cited the former New York Mayor 's connections with the Puerto Rican community as motivation for the switch . <p> " While many leaders offer empty words and meaningless rhetoric on Puerto Rico , Mike Bloomberg is offering a substantive plan that would ensure long term security for the Island , " Asencio said . <p> " As a career public servant I am inspired by Mike 's plan on Puerto Rico and his bold initiatives that place the interests of our nation and its people first . Given his record on climate change , job creation , and his commitment to public safety in addressing gun violence , I am proud to endorse him .
