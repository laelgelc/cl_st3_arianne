<p> Years and millions of dollars in the making , a draft federal report on hydroelectric dam operations in the Columbia Basin will not settle the decadeslong fight over saving imperiled salmon in the Columbia and Snake rivers . <p> Federal agencies found that taking out the dams would " provide a long-term benefit to species that spawn or rear in the mainstem Snake River habitats , " but also would have adverse impacts , including increased power costs , a rise in greenhouse gases and reduced reliability of the electric grid . <p> The report rejects the idea of removing the dams to save endangered or threatened salmon .
