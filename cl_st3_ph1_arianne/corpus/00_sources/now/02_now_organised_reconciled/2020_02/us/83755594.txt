<h> Council makes recommendations to address climate change <p> Updated 3:05 pm PST , Tuesday , February 11 , 2020 <p> HELENA , Mont . ( AP ) -- A council appointed by Montana Gov . Steve Bullock has released a list of draft recommendations for the state to address climate change , reduce greenhouse gases and take advantage of new technology and innovations that will come from the effort . <p> The public has until March 31 to comment on the proposals announced Tuesday by the Montana Climate Solutions Council . <p> The council recommends public institutions , business and labor organizations , philanthropic groups and community leaders collaborate to implement the recommendations . Those efforts start with preparation and planning for climate effects ; reducing emissions of greenhouse gases and increasing carbon storage ; and identifying ways the economy can be strengthened and jobs created through efforts to mitigate the effect of carbon emissions . <p> Montana 's average annual temperature is increasing . Early spring runoff causes flooding , making less water available later in the year , the council said . One suggestion is to replace pre-1976 mobile homes because of their lack of energy efficiency . <p> The council suggests the state create tax incentives for low- and zero-emission vehicles and develop and expand electric vehicle-charging stations . That effort would include building charging stations at rest stops operated by the Montana Department of Transportation , the council said . <p> Montana should also use tax incentives to recruit and attract new industries that reduce carbon emissions or sequester carbon while providing well-paying jobs and increasing the tax base , the council said . <p> Comments may be submitted by email to ClimateCouncil@mt.gov . All comments received will be made available for public review .
