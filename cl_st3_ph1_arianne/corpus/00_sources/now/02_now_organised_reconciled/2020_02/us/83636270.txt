<p> WASHINGTON , Feb 4 ( Reuters ) - U.S. Secretary of State Mike Pompeo on Tuesday condemned the Syrian government 's attacks on Idlib province , and stated his country 's support for Turkey in light of Syria 's mortar attacks on a Turkish observation post on Monday . <p> " The United States once again condemns the continued , unjustifiable , and ruthless assaults on the people of Idlib , " Pompeo said in a statement . " We stand by our NATO ally Turkey in the aftermath of the attack , which resulted in the death of multiple Turkish personnel serving at an observation post used for coordination and de-escalation , and fully support Turkey 's justified self-defense actions in response .
