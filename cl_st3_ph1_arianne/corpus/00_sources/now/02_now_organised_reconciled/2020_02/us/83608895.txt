<h> Central Students Participate in Des Moines Climate March <p> A group of Central College students have been active this academic year in advocating for the climate . Students Elizabeth Sheldon and Savanna Henning were among Central students attending the climate march in Des Moines Saturday . Both say they wanted to participate in the march because of their passion to stop different aspects of climate change . <p> " I 'm at the climate march because I care about my future being livable , breathable , and peaceful , because there 's not going to be peaceful results from it , " Sheldon says . " I want to be able to have a livable future for me and if my friends want to have children , I want their children to be okay . It 's my major -- environmental sustainability , so it 's like my whole life . " <p> " I 'm here as a student , but also as a person of faith , " Henning says .
