There 's A Lot At Stake <p> The Feb. 29 South Carolina primary is all that stands between the Democratic candidates and Super Tuesday , one of the biggest days in the presidential election cycle . Why is Super Tuesday important ? Bernie Sanders is the Democratic frontrunner , with 45 pledged delegates from the Iowa and Nevada caucuses and the New Hampshire primary .
