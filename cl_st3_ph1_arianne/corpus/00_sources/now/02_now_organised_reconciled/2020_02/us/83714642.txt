<p> CAIRO , Feb 9 ( Reuters ) - Iraq 's Basra Oil Co will cut crude oil output at the Nahr Bin Omar field to a minimum because of pollution and gas emissions , state news agency reported on Sunday . <p> The company 's Director General Ihsan Abduljabbar said : " Nahr Bin Omar oilfield is considered one of the most controversial fields because of the pollution and gas emissions " . <p> Abduljabbar added " it has been decided to cut crude oil production to minimum and to stop operating all oil wells to provide environmental protection " .
