<p> LONDON , Feb 2 ( Reuters ) - Britain expects the EU to " live up to " its undertakings to offer London a Canada-style free-trade deal , foreign minister Dominic Raab said on Sunday , reiterating that any deal will not require " high alignment " with the bloc 's rules . <p> After Britain officially left the EU on Friday , the two sides are expected to start negotiating a future trade deal in March , with Prime Minister Boris Johnson setting out his stall in a speech on Monday .
