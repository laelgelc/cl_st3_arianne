<p> The Quebec government had a $4.5-billion surplus at the end of November , a sum that should go down considerably over the last four months of the fiscal year , the Legault government says . <p> The surplus in the monthly financial report published on Friday is slightly higher than a year ago and takes into account $1.6 billion given to the Generations Fund . <p> Excluding that contribution , the surplus is $6.1 billion and essentially the same as the corresponding period of the previous year . <p> " The results at Nov. 30 confirm the good economic performance of Quebec , " said Finance Minister Eric Girard , noting his budget plan being unveiled March 10 will focus on the fight against climate change . <p> Girard anticipated GDP growth of 2.4 per cent in 2019 , 0.6 percentage points higher than the forecast of the last budget and higher than the Canadian average for the second straight year . <p> Despite that , Quebec 's commitments should bring the surplus down to $1.7 billion by the end of the fiscal year , according to the budget .
