<p> In the first act of " Jagged Little Pill , " the new Broadway musical featuring Alanis Morissette songs , a teenager named Bella sits timidly on a sofa trying to piece together what little she can remember from a party the night before . She tries to shrug it off and calls herself an idiot . <p> But a classmate , Frankie , shakes her head . " That 's rape , " she says . " It could happen to any of us .
