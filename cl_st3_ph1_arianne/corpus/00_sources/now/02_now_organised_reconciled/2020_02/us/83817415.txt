Being carbon-neutral means that the amount of U.S. emissions of carbon -- primarily from burning fossil fuels like coal , oil and gas -- is fully offset by the amount of carbon removed from the atmosphere . <p> Navigating a realistic path to carbon neutrality is tricky , though , with many scientific , economic and political uncertainties surrounding the available technologies @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , the report envisions that the United States could ramp up to removing up to 2 metric gigatons of CO2 a year from the atmosphere by 2050 . <p> This roadmap to carbon neutrality would devote about two-thirds of that initial decade of funding , or $4 billion a year , to support tree restoration projects across the United States .
