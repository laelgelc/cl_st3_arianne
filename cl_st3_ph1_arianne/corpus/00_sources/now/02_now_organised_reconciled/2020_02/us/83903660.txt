They have 2.4-liter direct fuel injection or 2-liter direct injection turbocharged engines . <p> Kia says a fuel hose can deteriorate and crack due to engine heat . The hoses can leak and cause fires . <p> A fix is still being developed . The recall is expected to start April 16 . <p> The second recall covers about 51,000 2011 and 2012 Sedona minivans . The fuel injector rail can crack from exposure to heat , causing a gas leak . <p> Dealers will replace the injector part starting April 16 . <p> No fires or injuries have been reported in either recall .
