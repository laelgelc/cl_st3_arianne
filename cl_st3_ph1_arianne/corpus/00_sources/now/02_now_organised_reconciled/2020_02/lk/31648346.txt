The essence of blue ocean strategy is to open up new market opportunities and create new demand where the competition will be irrelevant . It is about creating and capturing uncontested market opportunities where competition does not exist . In the blue ocean strategy , the demand is created rather than fought over with other competitors . The strategy focuses on identifying opportunities for rapid growth and profitability . The competition is rather irrelevant as the company is targeting at unexplored market space . The strategy also speaks on " Red Ocean " or current market space where the boundaries or the limits and competition are known . The companies that are in red ocean try to out-perform the competitors to get a share of the current demand .
