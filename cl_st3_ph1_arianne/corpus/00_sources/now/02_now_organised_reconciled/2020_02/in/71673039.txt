The Amur falcon migrates from China , comes to Nagaland and then goes to Somalia using this flyway . At the CoP , we will have some discussions to conserve this flyway , " he said . <p> Representatives from 130 member nations will arrive to discuss strategies and actions for the conservation of migratory species for the CoP . <p> The minister talked about the conservation efforts for vultures and the Great Indian Bustard .
