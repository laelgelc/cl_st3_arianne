The Budget is no longer insured from public opinion , favourable or otherwise -- many people took to social media to voice their criticism . While not all of them might be completely informed about all the policies , it is unfair to keep the common person in the dark about policies that directly affect them . The sentiment clouding the Budget seems to be mostly negative . <p> The new income tax regime has been one of the things widely talked about .
