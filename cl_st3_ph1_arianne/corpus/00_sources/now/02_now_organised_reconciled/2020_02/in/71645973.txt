<h> Fair director Jagdip Jagpal says the fair , instead of going international , is " opening people 's minds " about the diversity and dynamics of South Asian art . <p> The India Art Fair is not trying to be an international fair , and regional fairs should not be looked down upon , says Jagdip Jagpal , fair director . She says it is important to have depth instead of being " a mediocre , average kind of everything " . <p> The fair , which began in 2008 , has been promoting South Asian artists and designates 70% of the fair space to Indian galleries exhibiting South Asian art . The fair , which exhibits works by artists from India , Pakistan , Bangladesh , and Sri Lanka besides Europe and the Americas , is " opening people 's minds " about the diversity and dynamics of South Asian art , says Jagpal .
