Simultaneous reduction of urban pollution at the local level and greenhouse gas ( GHG ) emissions at the global level are urgent challenges that both countries face . This involves balancing short-term economic costs of reducing GHG emissions ( e.g. the cost of new technologies ) with long-term benefits , such as the stimulation of the economy and better health outcomes .
