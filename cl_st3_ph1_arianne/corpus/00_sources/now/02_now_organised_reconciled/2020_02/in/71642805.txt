<h> Locust attack : Plague a grim reminder of climate change 's reality <h> Last Updated : Monday 03 February 2020 <p> Change in cyclonic patterns over the Arabian Sea is behind the locust invasions in east Africa , west and south Asia , experts have said . <p> The plague of locusts that have been harrowing the three regions since last year , have now caused Pakistan and Somalia to declare ' national emergencies ' . <p> Pakistan declared emergency on February 2 , 2020 , after locust swarms put its food security at risk . Its premier , Imran Khan , held a meeting that day and directed the formation of a high level committee headed by National Food Security Minister Makhdoom Khusro Bakhtiar . <p> The country lost 40 per cent of its food crops by December 2019 , according to media reports . Its immediate priority is to save its ripe cotton crops from the insects . In October 2018 , the Arabian Peninsula was hit by Cyclone Luban , which created more favourable conditions for locusts to breed . <p> The floodgates finally opened in January and February of 2019 .
