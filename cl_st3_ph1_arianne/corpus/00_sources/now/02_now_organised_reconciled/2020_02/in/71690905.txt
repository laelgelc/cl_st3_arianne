<h> ' GIP ' may cause extinction of GIB <p> The irony of India hosting 13th edition of Conference of the Parties ( COP ) to the Convention of Migratory Species and Wild Animals ( CMS ) at Gandhinagar with the Great Indian Bustard as the event 's mascot . The bird is on the verge of extinction and global experts are in Gandhinagar to discuss ways to bring it back from the brink . <p> Great Indian Bustard is a carnivore large flying bird whose natural habitat lies in Kutch , Gujarat . It is found not only in Kutch but also some parts of Saurashtra . However , with time its number has dwindled and today it has dropped to five females . The only male in the flock disappeared about 2 years ago , never to return . <p> It is a known fact that Rajasthan , presently governed by Congress , refused to hand over sole male GIB in its possession for breeding with female GIBs in Gujarat , under BJP rule for two decades , unless high tension power lines are put underground in Kutch .
