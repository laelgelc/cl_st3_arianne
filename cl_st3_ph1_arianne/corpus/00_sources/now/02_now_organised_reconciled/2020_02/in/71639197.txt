<p> New Delhi/Bengaluru : Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman 's budget announcement on February 1 , 2020 made a push for solar energy and clean air and signalled the end for old and polluting thermal power plants . <p> Climate change has changed rainfall patterns in India , affected communities and caused deaths . Air pollution too has been an increasingly visible problem with India home to 15 of the 20 most polluted cities in the world . With increasing domestic and global pressure to act , India 's budget for 2020-21 makes it a frontrunner among countries driving change with a policy-level push . <p> The environment ministry 's budget is proposed to be increased by nearly 5% to Rs 3,100 crore over the Rs 2,955 crore in the preceding year . <p> The budget 2020-21 speech announced the expansion of the government 's Kisan Urja Suraksha Evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan ( KUSUM ) scheme to enable about 2 million new farmers to install standalone solar pumps .
