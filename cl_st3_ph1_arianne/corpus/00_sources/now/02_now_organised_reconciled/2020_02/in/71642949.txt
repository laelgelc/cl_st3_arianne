<p> With Delhi elections just around the corner , political parties have been busy preparing their manifestoes for the upcoming polls . A few parties have also been consulting city residents to prepare an inclusive manifesto . As a result of such campaigns , people have started expressing their sense of urgency on matters ranging from women 's safety to environment conservation . <p> With increasing environmental degradation and its immediate consequences , the environment has become central to public discourse . Air quality in the capital has already been shuffling between indices suggesting ' poor ' and ' very poor ' in the last few months . But it is still difficult to find among the top priorities for political parties in the forthcoming election . <p> Some believe the environment is not an election issue for parties because only a handful of people in the huge population understand it as a critical existential issue . <p> Jyoti Pande Lavakare , co-founder of Care for Air India says " There is a lack of awareness of how the environment affects our health and existence , the connection has not yet been made ...
