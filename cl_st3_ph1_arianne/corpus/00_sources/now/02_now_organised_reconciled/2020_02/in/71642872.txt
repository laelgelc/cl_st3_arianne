<h> India 's clean air plan overlooks 231 polluted cities : Report <h> Last Updated : Tuesday 21 January 2020 <p> The National Clean Air Programme ( NCAP ) leaves out at least 231 cities or towns where people breathe air as polluted as the 102 cities notified under the plan , a report released on January 21 , 2020 , showed . <p> The study profiled 287 cities where air pollution stations had more than 52 days ' monitoring data . In more than 80 per cent , PM ( Particulate Matter ) exceeded 60 microgram per cubic metre ( ? g/m3 ) prescribed under National Ambient Air Quality Standards ( NAAQS ) by the Central Pollution Control Board ( CPCB ) . <p> This implies that all these cities / towns belong to the non-attainment list but have not been added . The National Green tribunal ( NGT ) too had asked the CPCB to expand its list of such cities last year . <p> The cities identified under the NCAP are expected to reduce air pollution levels by 20-30 per cent by 2024 from 2017 levels .
