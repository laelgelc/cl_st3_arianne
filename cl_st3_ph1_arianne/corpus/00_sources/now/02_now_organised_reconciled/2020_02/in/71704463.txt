<p> India ranked 77th on a sustainability index that takes into account per capita carbon emissions and ability of children in a nation to live healthy lives and was placed 131st on a ranking that measures the best chance at survival and well-being for children , according to a UN-backed report . <p> The report was released on Wednesday by a commission of over 40 child and adolescent health experts from around the world . It was commissioned by the World Health Organization ( WHO ) , UN Children 's Fund ( UNICEF ) and The Lancet medical journal . <p> In the report assessing the capacity of 180 countries to ensure that their youngsters can survive and thrive , India ranks 77th on the Sustainability Index and 131 on the Flourishing Index , it said . <p> Flourishing is the geometric mean of Surviving and Thriving .
