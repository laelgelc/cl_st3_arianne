( UNIVERSITY OF GUAM ) <p> The University of Guam Center for Island Sustainability , the Micronesia Climate Change Alliance , and the UOG Green Army seek motivated youth ages 11 to 25 to participate in a first-time youth and young adults leadership program titled " Kulo ' : Emerging Climate Leaders . " <p> In CHamoru , kulo ' is the trumpet shell used to gather the community together for a purpose . The Kulo ' cohort will work to amplify the call to climate action for their generation and throughout Micronesia , raising awareness and driving change . <p> The yearlong program will prepare youth to be leaders in the face of climate change based on their collective skills and interests .
