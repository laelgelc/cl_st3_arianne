<h> Small , marginal farmers hit hard by poor paddy procurement in Bihar <h> Last Updated : Wednesday 05 February 2020 <p> Small and marginal farmers in Bihar are unhappy and worried by the state government 's poor procurement of paddy . <p> The slow speed of paddy procurement that started in Bihar on November 15 , 2019 , has forced farmers to sell their crop to traders and agents of rice mills , according to reports from districts . <p> The government 's own data reflects this . Agencies have procured 350,518 metric tonnes of paddy in the 80 days since procurement began , against the target of 30 lakh metric tonnes . <p> Till date , government agencies have procured paddy from nearly 46,000 of the 1.16 crore registered farmers in the state . <p> " Farmers in Bihar are being forced to sell paddy at Rs 1,350-Rs 1,400 per quintal to traders , who pay them immediately , without any of the paperwork that government agencies do , " Mahesh Tiwary , a farmer from Buxar district , said . <p> He pointed out that selling in the open market was not a lucrative option for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ This is because the market rates are lower than the price given by the government . Moreover , such a practice leaves farmers at the mercy of local traders and middlemen , who exploit them . <p> Tiwary 's views are echoed by Harendar Kumar , a marginal farmer from Mohan Chak village of Vikram block , 35 kilometres from Patna . <p> " I waited for over a month after the harvest to get a rate of Rs 1,815 per quintal as the Minimum Support Price announced by the government but was forced to sell paddy to a trader at much lower rate of Rs 1,350 , " he said . <p> " Traders and middlemen are taking advantage of the delay on the part of government agencies to procure paddy and exploiting farmers , " Kumar , who also works as a construction labourer in Patna for the sake of additional income , added . <p> Sambhu Sen Kumar , nodal officer for paddy procurement in the state cooperative department refuted allegations of negligence and apathy in paddy procurement . <p> He admitted that due to some reasons @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as January .
