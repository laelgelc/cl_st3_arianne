<p> There is a fundamental shift occurring in our world that will affect every aspect of our lives . Climate change , in conjunction with anthropomorphic drivers , is changing the way we live , work and plan for the future . <p> With the extreme effects now visible through the likes of the Californian and Australian wildfires , as well as extended droughts elsewhere , and more frequent , higher intensity storms on our own doorstep , we are all coming to the realisation that something must be done . <p> That dawning has been some time in coming , but ever since about 2003 , after the science became incontrovertible , many began to change the way they did things , with carbon control and reduction as a key plank of those efforts . Things were going swimmingly too , with many companies and industries , not least of which was the tech sector , making all sorts of pledges and promises . <p> Then 2008 happened and all those good intentions went pretty much out the window as the focus became survival over pretty much anything else .
