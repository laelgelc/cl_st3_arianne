He has extensive experience in the integration of knowledge across neuroscience , psychophysics , and social sciences to understand and predict human experience and decision-making . He will speak on the topic of predicting human behaviour and decision-making . <p> Professor Tony Day , Executive Director of the IERC said : " The IERC Conference 2020 will take place against a backdrop of key issues for the energy sector .
