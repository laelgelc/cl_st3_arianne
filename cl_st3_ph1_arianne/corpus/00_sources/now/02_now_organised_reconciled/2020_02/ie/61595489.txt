<p> Of course , threatened by the potentials of socialism , those in influential places , along with much of the media , and encouraged by the right wing of the Labour Party , worked " unpaid overtime " in their attempts to undermine and destabilise the Labour leadership ; and yes the contradictions of Brexit turned out to be a major implement in their overall armoury . <p> Jeremy Corbyn withstood four relentless years of a craven and unprecedented witch-hunt conducted to destroy his reputation and credibility . What it did n't and could n't destroy however was his dignity or his principles . <p> The party manifesto was and remains visionary : those of us who hoped and longed for something better were left heartbroken by the election results ; but the party still has the potential to build on its great achievements and its honourable intentions . <p> The crises of climate change are not just going to go away . Tragically , neither will genocide , and I wholeheartedly echo the cri de coeur : Never again -- for anyone .
