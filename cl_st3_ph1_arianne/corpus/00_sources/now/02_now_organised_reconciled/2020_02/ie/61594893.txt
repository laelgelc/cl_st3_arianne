establishing exactly how much carbon dioxide ( CO2 ) is emitted by each of the earth 's individual nations into the atmosphere every year . <p> Official figures are self-reported by countries to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change , and activities measured for CO2 emissions include electricity and heat production , road transportation , and domestic aviation . <p> At the
