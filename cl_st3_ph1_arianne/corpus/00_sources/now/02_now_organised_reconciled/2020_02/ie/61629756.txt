<p> The Farm to Fork strategy forms part of the EU 's Green Deal , which was revealed last month . <p> On the feedback page of its website , the commission said : " A sustainable food strategy is key to achieving the goals of the EU 's Green Deal . <p> " This strategy sets out the regulatory and non-regulatory measures needed to create more efficient , climate-smart systems that provide healthy food , while securing a decent living for EU farmers and fishermen , " it adds . <p> The webpage explains : " Feedback will be taken into account for further development and fine tuning of the initiative . The commission will summarise the input received in a synopsis report explaining how the input will be taken on board and , if applicable , why certain suggestions ca n't be taken up . <p> Feedback received will be published on this site and therefore must adhere to the feedback rules . <p> The new strategy is aimed at reducing the use of pesticides , fertilisers and antibiotics as part of the Green Deal . <p> Advertisement <p> According to the commission , it is hoped @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ tackle climate change , protect the environment and preserve biodiversity " . <p> Among the main points of the strategy is increasing the " level of ambition " to significantly reduce the " use and risk of chemical pesticides , fertilisers and antibiotics " . <p> The commission says that it will identify measures needed to bring about these reductions , based on stakeholder dialogue . <p> Improving information for consumers about food is also cited as a goal of the strategy .
