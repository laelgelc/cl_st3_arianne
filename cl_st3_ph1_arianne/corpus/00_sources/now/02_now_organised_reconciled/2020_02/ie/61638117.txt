<h> Cemex states CO2 reductions ' not enough ' <p> Mexican cement producer Cemex has announced that , despite reducing its Carbon Dioxide ( CO2 ) emissions by 22% , this achievement is not good enough . <p> To combat this problem , which it calls one of the biggest challenges of our time , the company has created a new Climate Action Strategy in which it envisions a carbon-neutral economy . <p> A new target of a 35% reduction in its CO2 emissions by 2030 has been set by Cemex to fall in line with the 2016 Paris Agreement -- an agreement within the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change , dealing with greenhouse-gas-emissions mitigation . <p> Cemex said that its new targets have been guided by science and also has its sights set on delivering net-zero CO2 concrete by 2050 . <p> Fernando Gonzalez , CEO of Cemex , said , " Climate change has been a priority for Cemex for many years .
