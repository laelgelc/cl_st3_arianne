Kevin O'Keeffe is the artist and his ' Meandering Inkings ' opens at 7pm on Friday February 28 for a couple of hours . On then to Saturday 29 from 11am to 5pm , Sunday March 1 from 2pm to 4pm and Monday March 2 from 11am to 3pm . <p> Walk right in .
