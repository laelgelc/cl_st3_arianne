Number 8860726 . <h> Seattle ups environmental elements in Cruise Terminal 46 RFP <p> The Port of Seattle released a revised request for proposals for a new cruise facility at Terminal 46 with additional environmental elements . <p> The updated RFP went to shortlisted proposers being evaluated as potential investment and operating partners . The estimated $200m facility would entail a $100m investment from the port and the balance from a partner . <h> Shore power requirement <p> The updated RFP includes new language requiring cruise ships to be equipped with shore power and connect while at berth .
