Currently I lead the ' Nature for Climate ' Branch at UN Environment Programme ( UNEP ) . My team 's focus this year ( which we call the ' Super Year for Nature ' because of all the global meetings and growing political momentum on the topic ) , is to work with Governments and the private sector to redirect large public and private investments into the restoration and conservation of nature . <p> Current public subsides and private investment patterns often cause or accelerate the degradation of nature , such as tropical deforestation that is fueled by agricultural subsidies . Basically , we are drawing down Earth 's natural capital faster than nature can replenish it . They work in remarkably similar ways .
