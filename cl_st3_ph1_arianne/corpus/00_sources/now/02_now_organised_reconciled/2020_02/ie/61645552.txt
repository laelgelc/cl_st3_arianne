<h> Skibbereen ready to put its best feet forward in carbon debate <h> Could a town in West Cork become a centre of excellence for climate change projects ? Two local businessmen think so and are determined to make it happen -- with a little help from others <p> ENVIRONMENTAL campaigners have declared their intention to make ' a centre of excellence for action on climate change and renewable energy . ' <p> Top environmental consultant , Noel Casserly , who is also the managing director of SmartEarth , and well-known local environmental campaigner , Jim O'Donnell , first mooted the idea of creating a ' Green Skibbereen ' in September of 2019 . <p> Since then , they have conducted meetings with local planners as well as hosting a meeting at the West Cork Hotel that attracted a large crowd of like-minded individuals and business people . <p> One such individual was Alicia O'Sullivan , who is still a student at Skibbereen Community School but was invited to be Ireland 's representative at the 2019 United Nationals Youth Climate Action Summit . <p> Jim O'Donnell opened the meeting with a call to arms saying everyone -- regardless of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ world 's climate emergency . <p> He said it was one of his five grandchildren that prompted him to take immediate action when she asked him the very direct question : ' What are you going to do about it ? ' <p> Being a practical person , and the former head of a large family furniture making business in the area , Jim said : ' The first and obvious course of action would be to put a significant number of solar panels on businesses in Skibbereen . ' He said this would generate green electricity and ' take a major amount of carbon out of the equation . ' <p> Jim 's friend , and willing ally in the project , is Noel Casserly , who previously worked with the Irish Government on the international climate change agenda . <p> Both men are convinced that ' now is the time ' to focus on local projects and to encourage all local residents , as well as local business owners , to join their drive for cleaner , greener energy . <p> Noel Casserly told The Southern Star it @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ there has never been greater awareness and understanding about the role that civil society and young people can play in affecting positive change . <p> Both he and Jim believe that if the community comes together it can take real action on renewable energy and energy efficiency . <p> The use of solar panels , for example , to generate green electricity would require a considerable investment on the part of homeowners and participating businesses but it would also prove cost effective in the long term . <p> Jim explained that the cost of solar panels is coming down and their efficiency is increasing all the time . He said there are also government packages and subsidies to incentivise the switch , as well as a government commitment to generate 70% of electricity from renewable sources by 2030 . <p> ' Our vision for Skibbereen , ' he said , ' is that it would become a test case , and a focal point , for this .
