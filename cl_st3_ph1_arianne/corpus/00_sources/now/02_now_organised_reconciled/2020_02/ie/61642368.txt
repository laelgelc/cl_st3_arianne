<h> Achill 's ' ticking time bomb ' <h> Share This <p> Coastal erosion needs ' urgent ' action <p> Edwin McGreal <p> Three storms so far this year reveal the need for much swifter responses to the problem of coastal erosion . That 's according to Achill-based Cllr Paul McNamara , who described coastal erosion as ' Achill 's ticking time bomb ' . Cllr McNamara was speaking after yesterday 's ( Monday ) monthly meeting of the West Mayo Municipal District where he called for an emergency fund for speedy repairs to flood defences . He also called for more ' decisiveness ' at national level to tackle the impact of ever increasing storms . He referred to the damage caused by Storms Brendan , Ciara and Dennis in recent weeks . Cllr McNamara told the meeting that two beaches in Achill -- Keel and Dooagh -- are in danger of being washed away after the recent storms . Sand and stone barriers at the back of Keel beach have been ' virtually flattened ' , he said , and the wider village was now at a greater risk of flooding .
