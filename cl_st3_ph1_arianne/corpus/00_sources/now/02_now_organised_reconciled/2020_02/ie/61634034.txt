<h> Funny T-shirts The Last Man in Ireland <p> As part of the Smock Alley Scene and Heard Festival 2020 , Funny Tshirts and The Last Man in Ireland team up for a double bill performance in Smock Alley Theatre on Tuesday 18th and Wednesday 19th February . Funny T-shirts starring Colin Chadwick : The evolution of one man 's funny t-shirt collection . The highs , the lows , from ascendancy to dependency and ultimately , what happens when the laughter stops . The Last Man in Ireland starring Monkey Backstage : Due to climate change , Ireland now sits beneath the Atlantic Ocean . Except for one man and his farm . With returning siblings , revelations from the past collide with pints in the present leaving a trail of devastation ensuring none of them " will ever be the same again . "
