<h> ' Common sense needed ' on CAP funding -- Irish MEP <p> An Irish MEP has called for " common sense " to prevail when it comes to the budget for the Common Agricultural Policy ( CAP ) , arguing against a cut to farmer payments . <p> Ireland South MEP Billy Kelleher was speaking following a debate in the European Parliament 's Committee on the Environment , Public Health and Food Safety on the topic of the new ' Farm to Fork ' Strategy . <p> Farmers are the custodians of the land . It is , and always has been , in their interests to protect and enhance the land they own . <p> Kelleher argued that farmers " are not the enemy " when it comes to climate change , and could be " part of the solution " if the correct supports are in place . <p> " No farmer I know wants to see the land go to rack and ruin , " the MEP highlighted . <p> Kelleher , a member of the parliament 's environment committee , urged against a cut in funding , pointing out the proportion of farm incomes @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ want to be part of the solution , but common sense is needed .
