<h> UK schemes unfit to report on ESG policies , says CACEIS <p> A research study by custody and asset servicer CACEIS has shown that more than two fifths ( 43% ) of UK-based trustees and pension fund managers do not feel properly equipped to monitor and report on their pension schemes ' environmental , social and governance ( ESG ) policies to a high standard . <p> The research follows the UK 's new legislation of 1 October 2019 , that requires trustees to outline how they approach financially material factors , including ESG and climate change considerations , into the investment decision making within their Statement of Investment Principles . <p> Pat Sharman , managing director at CACEIS , said : " While 2019 saw ESG , and with it the improved standards of governance , creep higher on the corporate agenda , now is the year ESG becomes front and centre for UK pension schemes .
