Researchers from the Energy and Climate Intelligence Unit conclude that 41 of 59 climate change and extreme weather studies demonstrate that climate change has made extreme weather events more intense and long-lived . <p> Not only this , but the number of weather-related natural disasters is increasing . Since 1970 , the number of natural catastrophes worldwide has more than quadrupled to around 400 a year .
