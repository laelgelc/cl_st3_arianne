In order to be able to concentrate on things outside of keeping us alive , we need to feel well fed , healthy , and have a roof over our heads . <p> Arguably those were the concerns of voters in the election just gone . People were struggling to find a home , to see a doctor , and to be able to afford to put food on the table . So was the environment and climate change totally absent from voters concerns ? <p> Not really . Insider now wonders if the climate has slipped in the hierarchy of needs ? Maybe we now feel that the destruction of the environment is impacting our ability to feed ourselves , keep ourselves healthy , or keep a roof over our head ( or stop ourselves from being flooded out of our homes ) ? While politicians play , the planet burns . <h> Is there light at the end of the tunnel ? <p> As an eternal optimist , Insider believes there is cause for hope .
