<h> Joe Gill : Wall of money ready to drive climate change projects <p> A wave of green change is sweeping across the corporate landscape and they have profound consequences for the way in which all of us will be employed and how we will consume , writes Joe Gill . <p> No one should underestimate the power of large institutional investment firms as they react to the mega theme of environmental change . <p> Over the past two years , in the investment business in which I work , we have noted a sharp step-up in the focus being given to all things linked to sustainability and climate change . <p> This has come from the top as board directors and senior executives have chosen to shift gears in how they deploy capital . <p> Increasingly , if as a company you want to attract debt or equity finance , you will have to prove at a minimum that you have started a journey towards sustainability . <p> The power of money will have a greater effect on behaviour and investment decisions than any amount of protesting . <p> Companies worldwide are recognising the need to change tack @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ need for change . <p> The corporate response is multifaceted . <p> Individual companies can start by converting their own internal business operations . <p> A switch to 100% electric cars , the abolition of single-use plastics , full recycling of all waste and a reliance on renewable energy are ways in which industry can move the dial . <p> At another level , companies can change their product suite to incorporate environmental priorities . <p> Packing products in fully reusable boxes instead of plastic is one option . <p> Another is to change whatever is being produced in to a 100% recyclable product . <p> Other companies are being created to fully exploit the sustainability agenda . <p> Power companies that rely completely on renewable energy are a good example of that . <p> Food companies that manufacture food in ways that do not damage the environment are another . <p> The trends are only going to grow and expand in the months and years ahead . <p> The investment community does not expect companies to convert overnight . <p> Instead , they look for boards and management teams @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a detailed plan is put in place , and authority is given at a senior level to an accountable post holder who reports to the board , investment firms are supportive . <p> This explains , for example , why large asset managers are staying supportive of a fossil fuel company like BP because it has laid out concrete plans to move to a zero-emission footprint within a determined number of years . <p> Every private company and public sector employer will have to respond in kind . <p> Politicians and regulators will add pressure too but it is those who supply finance that has the greatest influence on how businesses and civil servants react .
