<h> S&amp;P Dow Jones Indices <h> Conceptualizing a Paris-Aligned Climate Index for the Eurozone <p> On the brink of irreversible climate change , a combination of ground-breaking datasets and index innovation is emerging , through which investors will have the choice to align their investments to a future climate scenario compatible with mitigating catastrophic global warming to the planet . <p> This new breed of sustainable climate indices will not only offer solutions that intend to be impactful , but equally aim to provide investors with reduced risks from transitioning to a low-carbon economy and the consequences of physical , environmental events while capturing financial opportunities that arise . <p> An increasing number of investors require indices that are aligned with their investment objectives and their personal or institutional values . The S&amp;P ESG Indices were designed with both of these needs in mind .
