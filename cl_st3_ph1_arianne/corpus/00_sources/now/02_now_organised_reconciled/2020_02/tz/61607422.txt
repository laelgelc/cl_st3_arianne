<h> Clean energy the way to go in a progressive world <h> Wednesday February 12 2020 <p> Environmental degradation can no longer continue to be swept under what is rapidly becoming a tattered rug in this day and age of climate change . Climate change in all its forms is adversely compounded by human activity , resulting in deterioration of the natural environment . <p> The deterioration is generally through depletion of resources such as clean air and water , as well as the destruction of ecosystems/habitats and the living organisms that depend on them . <p> Among the human activities which are not environment-friendly is the use of fossil fuels in industries , in households and in other daily activities involving transportation by vehicles , vessels , trains and aircraft . <p> Fossil fuels include coal , petroleum and natural gas . In fact : any of a class of hydrocarbon-containing materials of biological origin occurring within the Earth 's crust -- and which are used as a source of energy . At the end of the day , all these are heavy environmental pollutants . <p> It , therefore , comes as a relief that leaders in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the development and production of fossil fuels -- and turn to clean energy sources which do not as adversely impact the environment . <p> These include solar power , as well as power from water ( rivers , tides ) and wind-propelled generator turbines . <p> The call to " go clean energy " -- dubbed the " deCOALonize Campaign " as a Kenya-based twist on the decolonisation of Africa to free the continent from colonial subjugation -- is intended to free Africa from the shackles of environment-unfriendly fossil fuels . <p> We agree with the call made at the Africa Energy Leaders Summit on Climate Change , Energy and Energy Finance held in Addis Ababa on January 28-29 , 2020 . <p> We also hail the African Hub for Accountability and Development Initiatives ( AHADI ) for supporting the deCOALonize Campaign . AHADI is a Tanzania-based pan-African social enterprise that co-creates solutions for Africa 's development , focusing on economic empowerment , education , good governance and -- last but not least : environmental justice .
