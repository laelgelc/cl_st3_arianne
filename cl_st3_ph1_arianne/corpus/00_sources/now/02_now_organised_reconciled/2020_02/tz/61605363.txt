At least 25 African NGOs , networks and community resistance groups have called on African governments to tap into the opportunities offered by renewable energies and reduce the use of fossil fuels . <p> This comes following the recent Africa Energy Leaders Summit on Climate Change , Energy , and Energy Finance in Addis Ababa . <p> A statement sent to the media over the weekend ahead of the African Union Summit ( yesterday and today ) indicated the meeting in Ethiopia criticised the deliberate proliferation of coal , oil , and natural gas in Africa , contrary to scientific evidence that highlighted the contradiction between planned fossil fuel expansion and globally agreed climate targets .
