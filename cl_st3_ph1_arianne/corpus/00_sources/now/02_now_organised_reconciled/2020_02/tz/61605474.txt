<h> WMO appeals for more investments in weather services <p> ? ? ? ? ? ? ? THE World Meteorological Organisation ( WMO ) has urged the World Bank ( WB ) and other development partners to capacitate member countries to invest on Public-Private Partnerships ( PPP ) for best provision of weather services . <p> WMO vice president Dr Agness Kijazi <p> WMO vice president Dr Agness Kijazi who is also the Director General of the Tanzania Metrological Authority ( TMA ) and the WMO Capacity Building Pannel chairperson made the appeal during the World Weather Services Workshop held recently in Boston , USA . <p> " The WB is a great partner of climate and weather issues ; it is high time now for it and other stakeholders to invest in educating governments in members countries on the importance of partnerships , this will improve weather service provision for sustainable development , " she noted . <p> Organised by the WMO in collaborations with the World Bank 's Disaster Management Fund ( DMF ) and the American Meteorological Society ( AMS ) , the forum brought together weather stakeholders from various parts as part of implementation of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 's general meeting held in June , 2019 . <p> The symposium aimed to discuss areas of cooperation to strengthen weather service provision . <p> The event was opened by the assistant director of AMS , Bill Hooke , who said that due to increased demand for climate and weather services caused by the climate change impacts and weather patterns , it is essential to discuss the key areas of collaborations with private sector . <p> Hooke said engaging private sector is key in the production and provision of high-quality and reliable climate and weather services .
