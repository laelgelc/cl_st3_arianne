<h> African countries propose concerted action to conserve giraffes <p> SEVEN African countries proposed a plan of ' concerted action ' for conserving giraffes to be considered at the ongoing 13th Conference of Parties to the United Nations Environment Programme 's Convention on Conservation of Migratory Species ( CMS CoP 13 ) . <p> The proposal , prepared in coordination with non-profit Giraffe Conservation Foundation called for the development of Africa-wide , national and regional plans . <p> The actions include an online international database to collect and share information and overcome data gaps , understanding the movements of giraffes and their habitats to protect giraffe corridors , inclusion of communities for managing conflicts and ensuring co-existence , assessing feasibility of establishing a giraffe conservation fund to secure funds for giraffe conservation initiatives and Africa-wide giraffe translocation guidelines for individual giraffe range states <p> The population of giraffes has declined by nearly 28 per cent over the past four decades , leaving an estimated 110,000 giraffes in the world , according to most recent estimates of the Giraffe Conservation Foundation . <p> Of the 28 countries where it used to be found , the giraffe has become extinct in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Mauritania , Nigeria and Senegal . <p> The absence of giraffes from their historical geographic ranges in Africa was concerning , the most recent and accurate geographical range map for giraffe in sub-Saharan Africa , said . <p> Loss , fragmentation and degradation of their habitat , illegal harvesting and trade , disease , and civil unrest are major threats to giraffes . Loss of habitat led by infrastructural developments , along with climate change , was a major factor behind the declining population of the northern Giraffe ( Giraffa camelopardalis ) , claimed a recent study . <p> Giraffes have also been found to migrate due to over-population and drought . Giraffes in Niger started migrating recently to neighbouring Mali due to the growing population of humans as well as their own increasing numbers . <p> They had migrated to Niger due to drought and war in Mali four decades ago . Now , they are returning to Mali again . <p> This is not the first time that countries have called for an action plan to save the giraffe .
