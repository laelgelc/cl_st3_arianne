North Canterbury farmers have faced one of their worst ever droughts . <p> Globally , the impact of climate change is clear . Record high temperatures have been recorded in the last 14 months and the 17 warmest years on record have occurred in the last 18 years . <p> What is important now is that we recognise the need to limit climate change as much as we can and to adapt to the changes we ca n't avoid .
