<h> News feed <h> City 's green award baffles residents <p> The winners receive R3.5-million to fund infrastructure projects aimed at the protection of the environment . <p> 22 hours ago <p> Residents say they were surprised when the city was awarded the greenest municipality award . <p> THE eThekwini Municipality has been named the greenest municipalities in South Africa . The money will be given through funding of infrastructure projects aimed at the protection of the environment .
