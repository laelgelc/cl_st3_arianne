<h> Mexican residents use LED lights to grow vegetables <p> GUADALAJARA -- Mexico City resident Leonardo Moreno grows his own vegetables , but not in a rooftop garden warmed by sunlight , as one might imagine , nor in small pots arranged on a kitchen windowsill . <p> Moreno uses LED lights to grow spinach , lettuce and other leafy greens in small fish tanks inside his apartment . <p> " The idea is not to shut ourselves off ( to possibilities ) and find alternatives to ( traditional ) food production , " Moreno told Xinhua . <p> A member of Biohackers Mexico , which brings together people interested in biotechnology , Moreno presented his experiment in small-scale indoor urban farming at Campus Party , a five-day technology fair taking place until Sunday in Guadalajara , the capital of western Jalisco state . <p> " The creation of smart crops at home represents an alternative for people ( in cities ) .
