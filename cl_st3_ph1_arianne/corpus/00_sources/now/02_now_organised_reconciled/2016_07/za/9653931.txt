<h> Think-tank predicts low regional economic growth <p> Africa Growth Initiative , an international economic think-tank based in United States , has projected a decline in economic prospects for Africa in 2016 due to falling prices in oil and metals , China 's economic slowdown , increase in external borrowing costs as the US Federal Reserve further raises with options for borrowing becoming limited . <p> Besides the external factors that are likely to impinge the continent 's economic growth , another threat is climate change as East and Southern Africa expect to bear the likely brutal effects of El Nino-induced drought . <p> In its recent Foresight Africa report of 2016 , Africa Growth Initiative , which conducts independent research that establishes long-term strategies for economic growth and strong policies for development in Africa , says Sub-Saharan Africa in 2015 experienced its lowest economic growth rate since the 1998 global financial crisis . <p> " According to IMF , the region 's real GDP growth fell from 5.0 percent in 2014 to 3.7 percent in 2015 and will rebound to 4.3 percent in 2016 .
