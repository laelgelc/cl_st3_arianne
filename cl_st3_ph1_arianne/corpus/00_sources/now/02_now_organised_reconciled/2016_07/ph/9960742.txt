<h> DA pushes for free farm inputs <p> GENERAL SANTOS CITY ( MindaNews/19 July ) -- The Department of Agriculture ( DA ) is pushing for the implementation of a new support program that would provide free seeds and other inputs to palay or rice farmers for two cropping seasons starting next year . <p> Agriculture Secretary Emmanuel Pinol said they are currently working on the details and arrangements for the initiative , which he described as a top priority of the DA and the national government 's rice self-sufficiency program . <p> He said President Rodrigo R. Duterte gave the go-signal for the program 's implementation during their cabinet meeting last week . <p> " I was able to convince the President that over the next three years , DA will support farmers for two planting seasons by providing them with free seeds and fertilizers , " said Pinol , who held a consultation with farmers and fisherfolk here on Tuesday . <p> He also graced the 50th foundation anniversary celebration of South Cotabato and 17th T'nalak Festival in nearby Koronadal City . <p> Under the scheme @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ seeds and fertilizers are required to deposit in a bank the equivalent amount of the inputs after the first harvest . <p> " This is to ensure that they will have money to buy seeds and other inputs that they would need for the next planting , " he said . <p> Pinol said the program is similar to the old practice of rice farmers wherein they store a portion of their harvests to serve as buffer supplies for the next planting season . <p> He said such practice needs to be revived to also help local farmers cope with the impact of climate change , specifically the onset of El Nino and La Nina phenomena . <p> " Climate change has made everything unpredictable for our agriculture sector . We need to be really prepared and make it must for us achieve food or rice sufficiency in the process , " he said . <p> Pinol urged local government units to identify and report to the agency the coverage of their existing rice areas for the proper allocation of the free seeds and fertilizers . <p> He said they should @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and the needed interventions , especially on irrigation . <p> The official said DA is ready to provide communal irrigation projects and other related machineries to facilitate the opening of new rice areas . <p> During the consultation here , Pinol led the turnover of around P190 million worth of farm machineries , fishing gears and projects to farmers ' and fisher folk groups as well as local government units here and nearby Sarangani Province . <p> He said DA will deliver more support projects and machineries to local farmers in the coming months as part of the continuing farm modernization program .
