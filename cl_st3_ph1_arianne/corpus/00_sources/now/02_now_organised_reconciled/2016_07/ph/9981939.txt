To formalize the partnership between BSU and Sunstar Baguio , a Memorandum of Agreement was forged on July 18 , 2016 . The MOA which was earlier approved by the Board of Regents will focus on research and extension activities in the University . Articles related to instruction , publication and governance will likewise be included . <p> Now on its centennial year , the Benguet State University continues to carry out its programs along instruction , research , extension and production . It is the first state university in the CAR created by law to implement these four-fold functions . <p> BSU envisions itself to be a premier University delivering world-class education that promotes sustainable development amidst climate change . Along with this , the university is committed to contribute to the development of the larger community it belongs to . <p> The University is also mandated to carry out Research and Extension ( R&amp;E ) programs . This area which carries two of the University 's four-fold functions , envisions to create a dynamic and responsive sector that generates and promotes relevant technologies through leading a strong and excellent research and extension culture . Research outputs are used to enrich and enhance the University 's academic programs as well as to develop extension activities that empower and sustain various communities . <p> In particular , the R&amp;E sector is commissioned to generate , develop and verify technologies that are responsive to the needs of its target communities .
