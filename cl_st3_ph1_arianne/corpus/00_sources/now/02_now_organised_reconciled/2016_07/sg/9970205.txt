<h> Number of cases top 200 a week for four weeks as Singapore enters peak season <p> Dengue fever cases are creeping up again as Singapore enters the traditional peak dengue season , with the number of cases exceeding 200 a week for the fourth week in a row . <p> There were 223 reported cases of dengue last week , slightly down from 246 the week before . <p> On June 25 , a 72-year-old woman in Simei became the sixth victim here to succumb to dengue this year . In the whole of last year , four people died from the virus . <p> The National Environment Agency ( NEA ) and Ministry of Health ( MOH ) are " anticipating an upward trend in the number of dengue cases in the coming months " . <p> Since April , the NEA has found 50 per cent more Aedes mosquitoes , which spread the virus , caught in its Gravitraps islandwide . <p> A total of 9,641 cases have already been reported this year .
