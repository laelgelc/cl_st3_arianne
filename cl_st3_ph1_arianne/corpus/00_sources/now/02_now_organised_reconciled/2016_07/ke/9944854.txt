<p> The Himachal Pradesh state government has launched a Rs 11.15 billion horticulture development project funded by the World Bank , to protect the state 's Rs 35 billion apple economy which is being threatened by global warming and climate change . <p> This project , spread over a period of seven years , will focus on providing new technologies to the horticulturists for increasing their crop productivity and capacities . Moreover , to protect fruit crops , especially , apples from hailstorms , the state government has enhanced the subsidy on anti-hail nets to 80% . <p> Apple is the mainstay of 170,000 families across the seven districts of the state and constitutes 49% of the total area under fruit crops and about 85% of the total fruit production . Now , even in the tribal Lahaul Spiti district , people are taking to apple plantation on a large scale . <p> A senior official from horticulture department said that in order to safeguard the interests of the apple cultivators during adverse climatic conditions , the state government has introduced several schemes for their welfare .
