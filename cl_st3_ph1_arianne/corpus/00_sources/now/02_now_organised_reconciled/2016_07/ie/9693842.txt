<h> Hedgehogs have adapted into city slickers <p> After 15 million years of evolution , the humble hedgehog has learned to cope with all kinds of environmental changes , including urbanisation . <p> A study by German scientists has found that hedgehogs are highly adaptable and , if they find a suitable garden or park , can thrive in cities . <p> ( Ben Birchall/PA ) <p> To investigate how well street-wise hedgehogs were coping , the German team fitted a number of the prickly creatures with temperature-sensitive transmitters . <p> The devices allowed the researchers to monitor hibernation patterns , winter nest-site use , and the territory over which the animals roamed -- and it turns out the little creatures are pretty flexible . <p> " We found that urban hedgehogs had much smaller nightly ranging areas than their rural counterparts -- five hectares verses 50 -- and that they adjusted their activity to levels of human disturbance , " said lead scientist Dr Lisa Warnecke , from the University of Hamburg . <p> ( Kirsty Wigglesworth/PA ) <p> The hedgehogs mainly stayed in private gardens during @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ night to forage and look for mates . <p> Unexpectedly , their hibernation patterns were no different from those of their country counterparts . Over winter , both urban and rural hedgehogs entered a " torpor " state during which they dramatically lowered their metabolic rate and body temperature to save energy . <p> This was despite good winter eating opportunities in the city , such as leftovers from human meals or pet food placed outside . <p> Unlike those in rural areas , city hedgehogs often chose to nest next to busy roads . <p> ( David Jones/PA ) <p> The findings , presented at the Society for Experimental Biology 's annual meeting in Brighton , highlighted the importance of private gardens and public parks to the urban hedgehog . <p> Dr Warnecke said : " Gardens and public parks are very important for city hedgehogs . They need gardens with natural vegetation and public parks less immaculately pruned , with plenty of natural , bushy areas .
