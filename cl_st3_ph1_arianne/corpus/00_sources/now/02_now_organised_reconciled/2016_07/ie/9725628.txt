<h> This Spice Girls remake is everything a female equality campaign should be <p> Tuesday , July 05 , 2016 <p> I 'll tell you what I want , what I really really want ... <p> A new video released by campaign group The Global Goals is encouraging people across the globe to tell the world what they want on the 20th anniversary of the release of the Spice Girls ' Wannabe single . <p> The aim is to grasp the attention of world leaders at the UN and remind them of the obligations they signed up to when they subscribed to the Sustainable Development Goals in 2015 . The 17 goals include putting an end to poverty and climate change and tackling inequalities . <p> The video , which features women and girls from different parts of the globe dancing along to the 1996 hit single , encourages people to use the hashtag #WhatIReallyReallyWant to share photos of themselves holding up paper with messages saying what they ' really really ' want for girls and women globally .
