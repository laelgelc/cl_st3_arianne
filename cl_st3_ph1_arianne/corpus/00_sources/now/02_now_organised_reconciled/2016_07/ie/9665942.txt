Growing along with the saxifrages are rare northern mosses that served as foundation mattress for the growth of sphagnum , the moss that raised bogs on the limestone fens of the midlands . <p> The " tundra " theme picks up again in the EU 's protection of marsh saxifrage . It blooms in late summer in erratic abundance , and the PhD botany student who once enjoyed the array of 1,000 golden blossoms was unusually lucky . <p> But , to quote an incorrigibly provocative friend , what good is the marsh saxifrage ? A fifth of Europe 's flowering plants , conifers and ferns are classified as threatened , and 64 have already become extinct . Climate change adds new uncertainties of drought and deluge , not least on peatland . What is the special worth of marsh saxifrage among the countless species now at risk ? <p> " Ecosystem services " to humans are supposed to sell support for biodiversity .
