<p> President John Dramani Mahama has appealed to leaders of faith-based organisations to fully participate in new developmental challenges in order to make positive impact on society . <p> He said issues such as terrorism , climate change , tidal waves and interconnectivity were paramount in growth and development and , therefore , needed full participation from all especially the religious leaders due to their huge following . <p> President Mahama , who was having a breakfast meeting with religious leaders at Wa as part of his two-day " Accounting to the People " tour of the Upper West Region , said the world had become a global village and it was only full participation of all that could salvage the world of calamities . <p> " Some of our farmers for example need to know much about climate change in order to re-adjust to maximise production and we need all hands on deck to achieve this , " President Mahama said . <p> He said religious leaders and faith-based organisations had ,
