<h> ECG privatisation long overdue -- Energy Institute <p> Think tank , Institute of Energy and Climate Change Policy ( IECP ) has said government 's move to privatise the Electricity Company of Ghana ( ECG ) is long overdue . <p> According to the think tank , private sector is essential for the growth of Ghana 's economy hence allowing private participation in the affairs of ECG is in the right direction . <p> " A vibrant private sector is essential for Ghana 's economic growth . Thus , the participation of private industries in the energy sector could serve as an engine of economic growth and propel Ghana to high economic freedom which is one of the aims of the MCC Millennium Challenge Corporation , " a statement signed by Executive Director of the IECP , Dr. Mutaka A Alolo , stated . <p> Government in its bid to reform ECG is leasing part of the company to the private sector under a concessionary arrangement within a period of 25 years dubbed " ECG Financial and Operational Turnaround Project .
