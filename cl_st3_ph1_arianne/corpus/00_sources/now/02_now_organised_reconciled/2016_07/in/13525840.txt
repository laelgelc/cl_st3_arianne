<h> State Editions <h> Yoga centres in all areas of Haryana civic bodies <p> To promote yoga , Haryana Government would open yoga centres in jurisdictions of each Municipal Committees , Municipal Councils and Municipal Corporations in the entire State . <p> " In its first phase , one yoga centre would be opened in the area of each Municipal Committee , two within jurisdiction of each Municipal Council and four in each Municipal Corporation , " said Haryana Sports and Youth Affairs Minister , Anil Vij on Tuesday . <p> Vij said through these yoga centres , senior citizens , women , youth and children of urban areas would be encouraged to practice yoga . <p> He said in those areas where adequate space would be made available , yoga centres would be made operational with immediate effect and where both space or building are not available , arrangements would be made for the same . <p> The construction of 800 Vyayam and Yogashalas in various districts would begin during this month and all the 21 districts would be covered so as to achieve the goal of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of 1,000 yoga trainers for these vyayam and yogshalas has been initiated . Further , to adopt the slogan of ' Sports for all ' in letter and spirit , 662 coaches of various disciplines of sports would be recruited on contract basis , he informed . <p> Vij added that those coaches , who are at present posted in Sports Department , would also be given yoga training .
