Photo ) <p> The Mumbai suburban collectorate and state mangrove cell asked the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation ( BMC ) to explain the alleged destruction of mangroves at Evershine Nagar , Malad ( West ) by municipal workers . <p> HT had reported that Malad resident Sharique Raza filed a complaint with government agencies , stating that municipal workers destroyed a quarter-of-an-acre patch of mangroves adjacent to the creek , opposite Neelkanth apartments , by dumping debris at the site . BMC officials denied destroying mangroves and levelling the area to construct a chowki to park vehicles . <p> " Last week , officials from the state mangrove cell sent a notice to the BMC P-North ward office asking them to clarify whether mangrove trees had been destroyed at the site .
