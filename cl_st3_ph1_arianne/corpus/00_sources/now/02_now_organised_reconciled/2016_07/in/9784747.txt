Do the storm water drains constructed in the city have enough capacity to handle rainwater during every monsoon ? <p> These are some of the questions that will get answered through an international collaborative project funded by the Information Technology Research Academy ( ITRA ) , an independent body that has taken up a study on integrated urban flood management in India . <p> As part of the project , urban flood management of four Indian cities including Vadodara , Bangalore , Hyderabad and Chennai will be studied . <p> Flooding problems faced by all these four cities are different . Along with the increase in average rainfall , there is also an increase in the rainfall that occurs in a single day . <p> The average of single day maximum rainfall between 1961 and 2000 stood anywhere between 50 and 60 mm . But between 2001 and 2011 , the single day maximum rainfall increased to 125 mm . <p> Similarly , the average yearly rainfall between 1961 and 2000 was 850 mm .
