<h> Making sense of a world in flux <p> KOCHI : Terms like ' Environmental degradation ' , ' climate change ' and ' global warming ' might be cliche subjects to speak of , but it is a subject that continues to inspire artists of every ilk . Having been an art director in the advertising industry for over three decades , Ramu Kochattil has lived to witness the changes urbanisation and ' development ' has wrought on the landscape , and the effects of which have begun to manifest itself . <p> " What we see around us is the wanton exploitation of natural resources . And over the past few years , we have been witnessing the effects of such exploitation in the form of global warming , " says the artist , whose first exhibition of artworks titled ' Warming Earth ' concluded on Sunday at Durbar Hall . <p> The 20 works under this very self-explanatory title grapples with the total phenomenon of climate change .
