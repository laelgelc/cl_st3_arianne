<p> I am currently reading Rana Ayyub 's self-published book Gujarat Files . While the book could do with a dose of editing in terms of language , it is a remarkably courageous book in its ambition and theme . For a young woman to have kept up appearances for an eight-month-long sting operation is very brave . The content of the book , the taped conversations that she manages to get from top officials there have revealed , to me at least , facets of the Gujarat incidents that I did n't know before .
