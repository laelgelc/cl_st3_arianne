A space mission like this stretches the limits of human engineering , as any astro-enthusiast will be quick to point out . More than that , if you 're not easily given to enthusiasm , it always highlights the fact that a military-corporate complex controls outer space . An ordinary terrestrial citizen has no access to God 's infinite vistas -- the very idea is considered outrageous . <p> Thus , it was not humanity that landed on the moon in 1969 . That romantic view has been replaced by the bare facts -- only a certain elite has access to big science and particularly space .
