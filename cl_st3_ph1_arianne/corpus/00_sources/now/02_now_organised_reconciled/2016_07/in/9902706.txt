<h> Darjeeling growers seek halt to Nepal tea imports <h> Almost all the region 's estates posted losses <p> Imports of ' deceptively similar-looking ' teasfrom neighbouring Nepal are threatening Darjeeling 's tea industry and growers have urged the Centre to take steps to stop low-cost imports . <p> The commodity imported under the Indo-Nepal Free Trade Agreement is sold in the domestic market affecting both demand and the price of Darjeeling tea , which is reeling under the impact of climate change and high labour costs . <p> " Teas , imported from our neighbouring country under the Agreement are sold to unsuspecting Indian customers in the retail markets , " chairman of the Darjeeling Tea Association Sheo Shankar Bagaria said . " These teas were low-priced as tea is cultivated mostly in the unorganised sector and wages are 50 per cent lower .
