<h> State 's reserve forests turn easy fodder for encroachers <p> KOZHIKODE : A total of 7289.33 hectares of forest area is under encroachment in the state . According to the information provided by the Kerala Forest Department , the highest encroachment is reported in Eastern circle comprising of Palakkad and Malappuram districts with a total of 3,684.70 hectares of forest under illegal possession . <p> Forests to the tune of 1,727.16 hectares in High range circle and 1,384.44 hectares in Northern circle are also under the clutches of encroachers . Further , in the central circle the encroachment is reported in an area of 276.05 hectares and that in Southern Circle the figure stands at 18.95 hectares .
