<p> Imagine sitting by the babbling river , a hot cup of tea and a loved one by your side . All of a sudden , it starts to pour . As you watch , the river rises , hitting rocks , destroying everything in sight . And you are waiting to snap out of what you can only explain as a bad dream--even though you are witnessing it right in front of you . This is the kind of stupidity that Amitav Ghosh warns us against in his latest work , The Great Derangement : Climate Change and the Unthinkable ( Penguin ; INR 399 ) --seeing but a relentless reluctance to believe . <p> His arguments on how literature , politics and art ignore the clear signs of climate change , are a wake up call .
