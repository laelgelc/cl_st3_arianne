of fossil fuels , metal ores and non-metallic minerals has accelerated rapidly since 2000 as emerging economies such as China transform their industries and cities , requiring unprecedented amounts of iron , steel , cement , energy and construction materials . <p> The global economy now needs more material per unit of gross domestic product than it did at the turn of the century because economic in 1970 , the report said . <p> If the world continues to provide housing , food , energy , water and mobility in the same way as currently , it will require 180 billion tonnes of material every year to meet demand by 2050 . <p> " This is almost three times today 's amount and will likely raise the acidification and eutrophication of the world 's soils and water bodies , increase soil erosion and lead to greater amounts of waste and pollution , " the report said . <p> It will also intensify climate change , result in shortages of critical materials and heighten the risk of local conflicts .
