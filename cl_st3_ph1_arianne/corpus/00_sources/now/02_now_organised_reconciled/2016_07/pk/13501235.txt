" <p> The minister said , Parliament of Pakistan has established the SDG Secretariat which is now serving as a Resource Center . <p> Federal and provincial governments , he said , have committed a total of 15 million US dollars for SDG localization in Pakistan for 5 years . <p> " This is indicative of our steadfast commitment to SDGs and places Pakistan ahead of other countries " he added . <p> The minister said , " It is great privilege for us that Pakistan being @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in 2015 is being quoted time and again .
