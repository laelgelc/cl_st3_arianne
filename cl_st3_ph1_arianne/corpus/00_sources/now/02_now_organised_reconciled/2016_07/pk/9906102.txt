<p> UNITED NATIONS : Another candidate running for the position of the next United Nations Secretary-General seat stood before the General Assembly on Friday , and answered questions with regard to how she would promote sustainable development , improve efforts to establish peace , protect human rights , and deal with the significant humanitarian catastrophes , if she gets selected to lead the 193-member Organization . <p> Christiana Figueres , a Costa Rican candidate for the post , presented her vision statement before the General Assembly.In her address , she noted the challenges and opportunities facing the UN and the next Secretary-General , and also answered questions from the audience . Since 2010 , Figueres has been acting as the Executive Secretary of the UN Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) . <p> The current Secretary-General , Ban Ki-moon , is the eighth occupant of the Organization 's 70-year history . He took his office in January 2007 and will be ending his 10-year tenure on December 31 , 2016 . <p> The informal briefings for the UN Secretary-General race had started in April , as @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ their views to the global community in open dialogues . Two candidates , who were later put forward by their respective governments , were heard in early June . The present informal hearings were held only days after the UN had organised its first-ever globally televised and webcast townhall-style debate in the General Assembly Hall , on Tuesday , where 10 of the 12 confirmed candidates , including Figueres , were seen taking questions from both diplomats and the public , at large . The two candidates unable to attend the event in New York were also invited to send their video messages to be used in the debate .
