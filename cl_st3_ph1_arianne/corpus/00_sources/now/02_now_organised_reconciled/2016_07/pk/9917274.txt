<h> Pakistan taking up issue of Kashmir killings on world forums : minister <p> Federal Minister for Law and Climate Change Zahid Hamid has said that Pakistan is taking up the nasty matter of brutal killings of innocent Kashmiri people by Indian army in occupied Kashmir to all the relevant international forums . <p> He expressed these views while taking to the chairmen , vice chairmen and councilors of Pasrur district 's union councils at his political Dera in Pasrur city on Saturday . <p> He said Pakistan has strongly condemned the unabated human rights violations , brutal killings and genocide of the innocent Kashmiri people by Indian army in the occupied valley , adding that this nasty situation was enough to shake the conscience of the world . <p> The minister asked the UC chairmen , vice chairmen and councilors to utilise their full energies for the victory of Pakistan Muslim League-N ( PML-N ) on July 21 , 2016 in AJK general elections , and added that the day of July 21 will dawn with the news of big victory for PML-N in the AJK polls @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the AJK polls with the majority of votes and that heavy mandate will be given to the PML-N by the Kashmiri people , saying that only the PML-N has the capability to get the Kashmiri people rid of corrupt rulers in Azad Jammu and Kashmir ( AJK ) . <p> On the occasion , Zahid Hamid strongly condemned the mounting atrocities , larger scale human rights violations , brutal killings and genocide of the innocent Kashmir people by the Indian army in the Occupied Jammu and Kashmir . <p> He urged the world to use its complete influence to globally pressurise India for the urgent peaceful solution to the burning Kashmir issue as per the aspirations of the people of the Held Valley . <p> Zahid Hamid said that the plebiscite was the basic right of the Kashmiri people , adding that the sun of freedom of Kashmir will rise soon and the oppressed people of Occupied Jammu and Kashmir will soon get freedom from Indian yoke .
