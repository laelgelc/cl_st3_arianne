<p> ISLAMABAD : Former president Asif Ali Zardari has expressed grief over the death of over 30 persons and damages to property in flash floods in Chitral . <p> " The havoc caused by floods in Chitral has saddened the nation , " he said in a statement issued by the Pakistan People 's Party ( PPP ) Secretariat here on Monday . He asked the authorities concerned to rush out in providing immediate relief to the victims . <p> Zardari also condoled with the bereaved families over the death of their loved ones and prayed for eternal rest to those who lost their lives in the calamity . The former president also directed provincial chapter of the party to reach out to the flood victims and assist them in rehabilitation . <p> Zardari said flash floods , torrential rains , drought and heavy monsoons were the result of global warming and climate change and asked the government to prepare a long term strategy for combating the vagaries of climate change . <p> " The entire region is beset with the problem of climate change and we need @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , " he added .
