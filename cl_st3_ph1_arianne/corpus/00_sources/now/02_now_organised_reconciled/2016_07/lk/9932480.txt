But the analysis of atmospheric pollution in the Environmental Impact Assessment ( EIA ) for the coal burning power plant ( CBPP ) proposed at Sampur in Trincomalee is based on wind blowing from the East . On this discrepancy rides not only the credibility of the EIA but unexamined impacts of profound consequence . <p> Coal burning power plants release dust and pollutants ( Sulphur Dioxide ( SO2 ) , Nitrous Oxide ( N2O ) , Carbon Dioxide ) , and toxins such as Mercury into the atmosphere .
