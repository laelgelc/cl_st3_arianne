<h> President inaugurates 10,000 villages programme in Kegalle <p> The national event to mark the commencement of the construction of ten thousand " Nila Haritha Villages " was held under the auspices of President Maithripala Sirisena.The event was held at the Sanasa Education Campus in Paragammana , Kegalle . <p> A total of ten thousand eo-friendly villages are to be constructed around the country under the " Nila Haritha Lassana Lanka " programme.At the last climate change summit held in Paris , a decision was arrived upon by world leaders at the summit to restrict the increase in global temperatures to below 2 degrees Celsius . <p> Based on the decision which was arrived at the summit , the creation of these ten thousand eco-friendly villages stands out in the contribution of curtailing the rise of global warming . <p> Meanwhile , a cheque was presented as compensation by the President , to the wife of an officer of the Sanasa Education Campus who passed away in the Aranayake Landslide
