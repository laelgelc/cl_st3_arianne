<p> A national study conducted by Climate Central and The Weather Channel found that over a span of 50 years , cities in Nevada are among the " fastest warming " cities in the United States . <p> The study ranked Reno as #1 and Las Vegas as #3 in terms of the fastest warming cities in the country . <p> The study showed Las Vegas ' annual average temperature rose by roughly five degrees Fahrenheit since 1965 . <p> Climate Central 's Senior Scientist Alyson Kenward says climate change plus urban development are some of the main reasons behind why Las Vegas is heating up ... and heating up fast . <p> " A city like Las Vegas is seeing more days above 90 degrees and more days above 95 much more now than they did 30 or 40 years ago , so we 're really looking at much more extreme heat that 's also on the rise , " said Kenward . <p> To view the national study and see what other cities made the list , CLICK HERE .
