This took place about 8 million years ago , in the Miocene epoch , as Earth began to warm , the Mediterranean Sea dried up and Africa 's thick forests transitioned to grasslands and savannah . At the same time , numerous species of apes , which had lived across Africa and southern Europe , began to disappear , and monkeys evolved more lineages .
