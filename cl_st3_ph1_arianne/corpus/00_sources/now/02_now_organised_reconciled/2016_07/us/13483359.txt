<p> One of the most rapidly warming places on Earth in the past half century actually cooled in the past 20 years . <p> Temperatures on the Antarctic Peninsula have reversed course , dropping by an average of about 0.5 degree Celsius per decade since the late 1990s while the rest of the world experienced record heat , 10 researchers from the British Antarctic Survey concluded in an article published in the journal Nature . <p> The report 's authors did n't make a conclusion about what their findings mean for the debate about global warming , saying the changes they noticed could fit at the extreme end of natural climate variations . That suggests it may take years and further research to determine the direction of temperatures in the Antarctic and what they mean for the world .
