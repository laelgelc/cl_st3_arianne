<h> Australian State Victoria to generate 25 per cent of electricity from renewable energy by 2025 : : <p> Victoria has become the latest Australian state to announce ambitious new energy targets , aiming to have 25 per cent of its electricity to come from renewable energy by 2020 , and 40 per cent by 2025 . <p> According to a report released by Ernst and Young and the Climate Council , 50 per cent of renewable energy by 2030 would boost employment by 50 per cent more than the current trajectory , which is 34 per cent renewables by 2030 . <p> Currently around 14 per cent of Victoria 's power grid is serviced by renewable energy . <p> Victorian Minister for Energy , Environment and Climate Change Lily D'Ambrosio said that by 2025 up to 5400 megawatts of new large-scale renewable energy capacity is anticipated to be built in Victoria , which would represent an estimated A$2.5 billion of investment . <p> She said about 4,000 jobs would be created in the renewable energy sector during the expected peak of construction , and there would be @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ emissions by 2034-35 . <p> Victorian Premier , Daniel Andrews , said that the world was shifting towards renewable energy , and Victorians expected their government to do the same . <p> He also announced an auction scheme where project developers would compete to be the lowest cost provider , and successful bidders would be given long-term contracts to support their projects , a move that will provide certainty to investors . <p> Separate auctions for large-scale solar projects will commence in 2017 . <p> The State Government of Victoria has already signed contracts that will help two windfarms be built in the state over the next 12 months .
