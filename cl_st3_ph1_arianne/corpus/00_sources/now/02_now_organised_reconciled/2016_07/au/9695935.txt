" <p> While this is true more generally of the Coalition , it is even more applicable to Turnbull . <p> If this election was about anything , it was about securing Turnbull 's authority ; not only a mandate to pass his more contentious policies , but also to legitimise Turnbull 's replacement of Tony Abbott as Liberal leader . <p> Now Turnbull must do what he can without either .
