<h> Eight shades of Blue Room to end 2016 - The West Australian <p> A latter-day Macbeth set by Greg Fleet and Roz Hammond in the blood-curdling world of WA Liberal politics is a highlight of the Blue Room 's season to round out 2016 . <p> The mix of established artists , bold ideas and emerging talent is again a feature of the season , which runs from next month to December , with eight new works at Perth 's independent theatre hub . <p> The experienced , well-credentialled Red Ryder Ryder Productions returns to the Blue Room in September for the first time in three years with the WA premiere of Grounded , George Brant 's gripping monologue about a woman piloting a deadly drone from the other side of the world . <p> Directed by Emily McLean , Grounded stars Alison van Reeken as the top gun balancing her job as a mother and a remote-control killing machine .
