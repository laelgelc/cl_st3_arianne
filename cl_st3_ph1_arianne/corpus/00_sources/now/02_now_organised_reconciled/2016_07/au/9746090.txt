<h> The election limbo must n't stifle urgently needed action . <p> While Australia reels from an unsettled election , one thing remains clear : the next government will need to act quickly on climate change if we are to honour Australia 's Paris Climate commitments . In fact , without a change of policy from our federal government , we currently stand little chance of meeting our agreed targets to reduce climate pollution 26-28 percent by 2030 based on 2005 levels . <p> The Paris Climate agreement is designed to cut global carbon emissions as fast as possible and to achieve net zero climate pollution well before the end of the century . It includes a goal to keep global warming below 2 degrees , and to pursue a 1.5 degree limit -- critical if we are to avoid the most catastrophic impacts of climate change and to keep the world 's coral reefs alive . <p> So , what does this mean for Australia 's next government ?
