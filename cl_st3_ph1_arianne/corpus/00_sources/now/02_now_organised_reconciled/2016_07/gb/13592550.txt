<h> Saved in the ' Nick ' of time from the world 's most expensive power station ? <p> In the nick of time , it seems , a glimmer of sanity is at last breaking in on what I have long been describing as the most insane single project a British government has ever put its hand to . We can credit the decision to put on hold our agreement for EDF to build the most expensive power station in the world at Hinkley Point to Theresa May 's joint chief of staff , Nick Timothy : the man who , having back in April described the Climate Change Act as " a monstrous act of self-harm " , was also behind the abolition of the Department for Energy and Climate Change ( Decc ) . <p> Until now , the absurd story of Hinkley has been as vivid an example of the self-deluding power of groupthink as could be imagined .
