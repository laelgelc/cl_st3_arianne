<h> Govt clears draft law for research , better technology in agriculture <h> The Bangladesh government has approved a draft law to invigorate agriculture and pave the way for research on ways to deal with risks posed by climate change . <p> A Cabinet meeting , chaired by Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina , approved in principle the draft of the ' Bangladesh Agricultural Institute Act , 2016 ' on Monday . <p> The ordinance that led to the formation of the institute in 1976 was twice amended , once in 1984 and again in 1996 , said Cabinet Secretary Md Shafiul Islam said . <p> After the High Court annulled the 7th Constitutional Amendment , the ordinances from the past military regimes became invalid . <p> The Awami League government then initiated the drafting of new laws . The draft of ' Bangladesh Agricultural Institute Act , 2016 ' is a part of those measures . <p> " Agricultural research is not the same as it was in 1976 , " said the cabinet secretary . " <p> The new aspects proposed in the draft are -- starting of research on climate risk management , production and distribution of new types of seeds , use of technology in agriculture and the use of a new almanac for yields in the backdrop of climate change . <p> " The institute will work to ensure local , foreign training and higher studies . Its board members or director can not be charged under criminal of civil laws for something he or she does with innocent intent , " the cabinet secretary said quoting the draft . <p> The past ordinance had 18 Sections but the draft has added four more , he said .
