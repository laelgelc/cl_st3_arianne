<h> Human trafficking on group 's radar <p> The National Council of Jewish Women has made it a habit to keep Winnipeggers aware of and up to date on a variety of social justice and global issues . <p> This has included sponsoring programs to raise awareness about missing and murdered indigenous women , the need for universal daycare , the importance of literacy and the plague of human trafficking . <p> The issue of human trafficking , in fact , has become one of the major areas of concern for the women 's service organization . <p> " Human-trafficking awareness is an important subject ( the council ) brings to the community every year , " explains board member Yelena Maleyev , " and this year , we were inspired to provide a different perspective and focus on the human trafficking and slavery in the supply chain . " <p> That inspiration was turned into a formal program , billed as a Fair Trade Awareness Day to Help End Child Slavery .
