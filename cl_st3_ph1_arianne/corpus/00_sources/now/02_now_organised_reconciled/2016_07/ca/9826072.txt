<h> Why did thousands of naked blue people march through northern England ? <p> A horde of naked people painted multiple lush shades of blue stormed through Hull City in northern England over the weekend , but not because they 'd all caught a communal cold . <p> No , the mass of blue people was part of an art project by New York artist Spencer Tunick , who is known for creating nude human installations all around the world , reports The Associated Press . <p> For the work , titled " Sea of Hull , " 3,200 volunteers were painted in four different shades of blue and gathered together to raise awareness of rising sea levels due to climate change -- because how else does one spark a conversation on weather ? <p> The project was commissioned by the Ferens Art Gallery in honour of the city 's celebration next year as the U.K. City of Culture . No word yet on whether Tobias Bluth participated .
