<h> Alberta ' crippling itself ' by ending the use of ' inexpensive ' coal <h> Poll <p> The Medicine Hat News is to be congratulated for pointing out what few media in Canada dare : " Climate change is real but the cause and what we can or should do to correct it is clearly still a debatable issue that should be encouraged or we all suffer as a result . " <p> University of Western Ontario applied mathematician Dr. Chris Essex , an expert in the mathematical models that are the basis of the climate scare , explains how uncertain all this is : " Climate is one of the most challenging open problems in modern science . Some knowledgeable scientists believe that the climate problem can never be solved . " <p> The Nongovernmental International Panel on Climate Change ( **33;238;TOOLONG ) lists thousands of peer-reviewed science papers that show that much of what we thought we knew about climate is wrong or highly debatable . <p> The lack of global warming for the past 18 years while hydrocarbon fuel use is soaring in China @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ wrong with the human-caused warming theory . <p> As I showed the 1,500 students I taught at Carleton University here in Ottawa , the science is becoming more unsettled as the field advances . Not only can we not control global climate , we do n't even know if warming or cooling lie ahead . <p> Yet , to supposedly ' stop climate change ' , Ontario ended our use of inexpensive coal to generate electricity . This was the single , more important cause of the 318 per cent rise in our power rates since 2002 . It is worrisome for us here in Ontario when Alberta , the province which helps bail out our province from its crippling debt , is bent on crippling itself in this way as well . <p> Tom Harris , B. Eng. , M. Eng . ( Mech . ) <p> Ottawa <p> ( The writer is executive director of the International Climate Science Coalition ( ICSC )
