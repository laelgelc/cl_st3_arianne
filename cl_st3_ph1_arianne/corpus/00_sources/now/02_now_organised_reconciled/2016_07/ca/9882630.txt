<h> CARP Halton Chapter launches fundraiser for Highway of Heroes project <h> CARP Halton Chapter launches fundraiser for Highway of Heroes project <p> The Canadian Association of Retired Persons ( CARP ) Halton Chapter and its partners announced recently they would spearhead a fundraising drive for the Highway of Heroes Living Tribute Project . <p> The project , which began earlier this year , will see the planting of 117,000 trees along and near the section of Hwy. 401 between Trenton and Toronto designated as the Highway of Heroes . <p> One tree will be planted for every one of Canada 's war dead . <p> CARP representatives said this living , breathing memorial offers a wonderful opportunity to tell the story of Canadian veterans . <p> It is intended to remind those who pass by of the great debt Canadians owe these departed soldiers . <p> It is also seen as a great way to green North America 's busiest transportation corridor- re-naturalizing areas by planting trees that can help mitigate the impacts of climate change and support the local environment . <p> The project is expected @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ around $20 million .
