<p> The Allan Brooks Nature Centre has a new offering this year to one-up last year 's movie nights . Family Fun Nights offer a different theme each week until mid-August and are fun for any age . <p> The decision to switch from family movie nights to activity nights was an obvious success based on the enjoyment that was had by more than 70 attendees for the launch of Family Camp Fun Night July 12 . Happy faces full of marshmallow and smeared chocolate were aglow around the campfire . The crafts tables were pressed at points , even the eastern sky lit up with a show of the most brilliant double rainbow . <p> Parents made friends with strangers , shared names of their favourite places to camp with their kids and more than one family was interested in camping the night right there at the viewpoint . The July 9 Family Games Night was stormed out , but guests still had a great time inside playing games and seeing the Nature Centre after hours , some for the first time .
