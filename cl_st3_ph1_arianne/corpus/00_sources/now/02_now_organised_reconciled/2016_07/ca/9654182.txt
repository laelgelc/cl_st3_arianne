Or look at the shifts in weather patterns bringing massive wildfires across North America . <p> Increasingly we are seeing shifts in weather , that are indicative of shifting climate . Yet still there are many who doubt the impact , or doubt the science . <p> For Northwestern Ontario , change in recent years is impacting communities across the far north . Winter roads -- a vital life line for getting materials into the north have seen increasingly shorter seasons , and in many cases the need for smaller loads on the trucks .
