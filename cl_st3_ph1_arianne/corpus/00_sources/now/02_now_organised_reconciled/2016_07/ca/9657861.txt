<p> The Cape Spear Lighthouse was awarded with a heritage designation during a ceremony on Thursday . <p> It was formally designated as a heritage lighthouse under the Heritage Lighthouse Protection Act . <p> The Heritage Lighthouse Protection Act provides an opportunity for Canadians to actively engage in the recognition and protection of historic lighthouses that have been such important symbols to Canada 's culture , heritage and identity . <p> " Governments at all levels , community organizations , and individual Canadians have worked passionately to designate and protect historic lighthouses under the Heritage Lighthouse Protection Act . The Historic Sites and Monuments Board of Canada is honoured to join them in making the implementation of the Act a success .
