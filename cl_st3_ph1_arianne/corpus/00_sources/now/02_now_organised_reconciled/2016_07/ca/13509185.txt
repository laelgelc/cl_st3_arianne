<h> RCMP come to the aid of dehydrated hiker in West Kelowna park <p> WEST KELOWNA -- A sick and dehydrated hiker was helped out of Okanagan Mountain Park by police over the weekend . <p> The Calgary man was hiking with a companion when he became ill and dehydrated overnight , Const . Jesse O'Donaghey says in a media release . <p> Because the man was physically unable to hike out of the park , an RCMP boat was deployed to pick him up around 1 p.m . Sunday , July 24 . <p> " The RCMP boat , which was on the waters of Okanagan Lake at that time , located both hikers south of Rattlesnake Island , " O'Donaghey says . <p> The men had hiked about 15 km into the park , and stopped near Commando Bay on Okanagan Lake . <p> They were transported across the lake to Emergency Health Services , which were waiting at Antlers Beach in Peachland .
