<h> Zibi condo development protesters disappointed by McKenna 's response <p> Protesters in opposition to the Zibi condo development arrived at the doorstep of Catherine McKenna on Monday afternoon to get a response to the open letter they posted on the outside wall of her constituency office three weeks ago . <p> The letter , written by Stop Windmill : Student and Labour Allies for Akikodjiwan , a group of community activists , requested that the MP for Ottawa Centre and minister of the environment and climate change act to ensure that there is no illegal land transfer and halt the development pending " appropriate " consultation and consent . The group also asked her to respond to the Algonquin chiefs who called for consultation on the project last December . <p> The Zibi project faces opposition from nine of 10 federally recognized Algonquin First Nations .
