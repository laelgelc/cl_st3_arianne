<h> News Local <h> Air quality concerns at Our Lady of Fatima <p> Tim Miller/The Intelligencer Recent air sampling at Our Lady of Fatima Catholic School have indicated some " exceedances " of the Ontario Ministry of Environment and Climate Change standards for the volatile organic compound Trichloroethylene ( TCE ) . <p> The Algonquin and Lakeshore Catholic District School Board ( ALCDSB ) is keeping a close eye on the air quality situation in one of its local schools . " The board has received some data from the recent air sampling investigations conducted by Golder Associates inside Our Lady of Fatima Catholic School , data which indicates some exceedances when measured against the MOECC 's ( Ontario Ministry of Environment and Climate Change ) most stringent standards for the volatile organic compound Trichloroethylene ( TCE ) , " stated Doug Campbell , controller of plant and planning services for ALCDSB , in a June 28 letter to parents . The letter went on to state the data has been reviewed by both the MOECC and Public Health Ontario with the MOECC providing reassurance the low concentration @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is not expected to be associated with any immediate health effects and is of low concern " . The annual air quality testing is the result of historic operations at the former Nortel Networks manufacturing facility which borders the Sidney Street school . Those operations had in the past resulted in groundwater contamination in the area with samples containing evidence of volatile organic compounds ( VOCs ) . When ALCDSB originally became aware of the groundwater contamination issues in 2001 it retained XCG Consultants to complete environmental site assessments and a site specific risk assessment for the Our Lady of Fatima Catholic School property . The investigations by XCG included the installation of eight groundwater monitoring wells on the school property during the summer of 2001 . The assessments conducted by XCG Consultants between 2001 and 2004 concluded , while there was some evidence of groundwater contamination beneath some areas of the school property , there was no evidence they posed a significant health risk as the school is connected to the municipal drinking water supply and does not use the groundwater .
