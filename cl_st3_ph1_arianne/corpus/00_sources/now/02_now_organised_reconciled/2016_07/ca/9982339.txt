<p> Energy required by data centers is on constant rise which represents a challenge for the climate change . RoboNodes ? is an innovative solution to reduce and reuse the energy , while eliminating the on-site support staff . <p> In today 's data centers , cold air is pumped into the server room and thousands of small fans in the servers are moving the air thought the servers . The hot air coming out of the server is lost by mixing with the cold air . In better cases there are cold&amp;hot isles , but it is still far from being efficient . <p> " The server rack is more than 50 years old . " said Szabo . " There is no other piece of technology in data centers that has survived for so long . " <p> RoboNodes consist of custom server racks and servers . The air used for cooling is completely separated from the air in the server room , which means we capture all of the resulting heat that we can reuse for heating the building or recovering back to electricity . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ valves , which guarantees that no server is being cooled more than necessary . <p> In case of a server failure , the whole server is replaced by an autonomous robotized vehicle as shown in the video . This removes the need of 24/7 staff on site and allows for small autonomous data centers to be spread to many locations . RoboNodes data centers could be also deployed in cold remote areas and managed remotely . <p> " We are the global leader in pay-as-you-go web hosting , which means we need a lot of supporting IT architecture " said Szabo .
