<p> The recession gripping Alberta is likely to go down in history as one of the most severe the province has ever endured , according to a report . <p> The report released Monday by TD Economics says by the end of this year , Alberta 's GDP is anticipated to have posted a three per cent contraction in 2016 , adding up to a 6.5 per cent loss since the downturn started in 2014 . <p> That 's twice the magnitude of the average of the past four recessions to hit Alberta . <p> " Alberta 's economy ca n't catch a break , " says the report , which was written by deputy chief economist Derek Burleton and economists Diana Petramala and Warren Kirkland . <p> " The economic fallout from last year 's oil price plunge has continued to reverberate through the province so far this year . And just as the global oil market has provided a decent whiff of recovery , the northern part of the province was hit by the outbreak of wildfires in May . two recessions ... was part of a global economic crisis whereas the second ... was more specifically attributable to weakness in the global oil market that delivered a nasty blow to the Alberta economy , " the report says . <p> The current recession is also expected to last roughly two years , which would match the 1982-83 downturn as the longest in the post-war period . <h> Labour market
