<h> Disaster team meets to take stock of Mombasa flood situation <p> Motorists and pedestrians wade through a flooded section of Bamburi area in Mombasa County on Thursday May 10 , 2018 . Photo by Mohamed Hassan/KNA . <p> The Mombasa County Disaster Management Committee on Thursday held an emergency meeting to assess the extent of destruction caused by the on-going heavy rains in the region and other parts of the country . <p> Reports presented in the meeting chaired by the County Commissioner ( CC ) , Evans Achoki shows 20 villages have been affected by floods and over 500 people displaced . <p> Besides human suffering , the rains have also adversely affected key facilities such as Baricho Water Works and treatment plant in Malindi , Moi International Airport , schools and several roads within the county . <p> Two boreholes at the Baricho water plant have been destroyed which has resulted to acute water shortage in Kilifi and Mombasa counties . <p> The two are among eight boreholes at the water plant that normally supplies 90,000 m3 per day to Malindi , Kilifi and Mombasa . <p> Following the destruction of the two boreholes , the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ has forced the Coast Water Services Board ( CWSB ) to ration water in the two counties . <p> According to the CWSB Procurement Manager , Stanslus Jira , the water body requires Sh.1.5 billion to restore the two damaged boreholes . <p> " We have approached the Ministry of Devolution and Arid and Semi-Arid Land to help us with the funds to repair the two boreholes , ' said Jira during the meeting which brought together officials from government departments , parastatals , Red Cross and other stakeholders . <p> The New Hope Secondary School at Tudor has been closed due to floods and 300 students have been sent home . <p> Parts of Mombasa and its environs have been rendered inaccessible following days of heavy rains in the county causing massive floods in both residential areas and the Central Business District ( CBD ) . <p> The situation has been compounded by the lack of working drainage system as the floods left scores of residents in low-lying areas inundated and marooned in their homes . <p> The Mombasa County Director of Meteorological Department , Lydiah Inoti told the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of May . <p> She described the on-going heavy rains in the coastal region and other parts of the country as above normal and attributed this to climate change . <p> Achoki advised residents living in slum areas to move to safer grounds to avoid being swept away by mudslides and floods . <p> He mentioned Kalahari , Mikindani and Jomvu as among more vulnerable areas during heavy rains . <p> " Persistent nature of the rain could result in dangerous flooding in some low lying areas on the seaside city , " added Achoki . <p> The CC assured all those affected that the government will assist them with relief food , non-food items , medicine and other supplies .
