<p> On a day trade unions organised nationwide strike to protest against the economic policies of the government and privatisation , the Union cabinet cleared disinvestment in four central public sector companies and two Odisha companies . <p> The strategic disinvestment of equity shareholding to the tune of 49.7 per cent will be taken up in MMTC , 10.10 per cent in NMDC , and 0.68 per cent each in MECON and BHEL , Union minister Prakash Javadekar said . <p> In the case of NINL , an equity disinvestment is done by finding a strategic buyer identified through a two-stage auction procedure . NINL is a joint venture company , in which four CPSEs -- MMTC , NMDC , BHEL and MECON -- and two state PSUs of Odisha government , namely IPICOL and OMC , are shareholders <p> " The proposed strategic disinvestment of NINL would unlock resources to be used to finance the social sector and developmental programmes of the government . It is also expected that the successful strategic buyer may bring in new management , technology and investment for the growth of the company and may use innovative methods for the development of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ employment opportunities , " the cabinet note said . <p> In another cabinet decision , the Union cabinet approved promulgation of Mineral Laws ( Amendment ) Ordinance 2020 which seeks to amend the Mines and Minerals ( Development and Regulation ) Act , 1957 , and Coal Mines ( Special Provisions ) Act , 2015 , to allow auction of coal blocks to all by easing rules . This will help in easing end-user restrictions on coal and mining projects . The move will open up the coal sector and bring in more FDI into the sector , Union minister Prahlad Joshi said . <p> The minister added that after the Supreme Court cancelled about 200 coal blocks , only 29 could be auctioned due to end-user restrictions . Under the new amendments approved , these end restrictions will be removed and even private companies will be allowed commercial mining of coal . <p> Another key decision approved was 60 per cent viability gap funding of the Rs 9,265 crore northeast Gas Grid project in the northeast India .
