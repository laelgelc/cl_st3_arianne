Global debt , which comprises borrowings from households , governments and companies , grew by $9 trillion to nearly $253 trillion during that period , according to the Institute of International Finance . That puts the global debt-to-GDP ratio at 322% , narrowly surpassing 2016 as the highest level on record . <p> More than half of this enormous number was accumulated in developed markets , such as the United States and Europe , bringing their debt-to-GDP ratio to 383% overall . <p> There are plenty of culprits .
