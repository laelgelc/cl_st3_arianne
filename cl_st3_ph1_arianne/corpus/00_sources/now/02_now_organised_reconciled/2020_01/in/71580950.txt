<h> AMR , flu pandemic again top WHO 's list of top health challenges for 2020 <h> Last Updated : Tuesday 14 January 2020 <p> Antimicrobial resistance ( AMR ) , climate crisis , eventuality of an influenza epidemic and spread of infectious diseases such as malaria , HIV and tuberculosis have topped the list of top global health challenges for 2020 released by the World Health Organization ( WHO ) on January 13 , 2020 . <p> This is the second straight year that these challenges have topped the WHO 's list . <p> Rather than talk about just diseases as ' threats ' , the list also takes into account infrastructure , social media , new tech and human resources in health . <p> A new entrant to the list of 13 potential threats is the lack of access . About a third of the world 's people lack access to medicines , vaccines , diagnostic tools and other essential health products , the report says . <p> " Medicines and other health products are the second-largest expenditure for most health systems ( after health workers ) and the largest component of private health expenditure in low- @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ healthcare in conflict-ridden areas has remained one of the biggest challenges just not for the WHO but the global health fraternity . The WHO recorded 978 attacks on healthcare personnel in 11 countries last year , with 193 deaths of health workers . <p> This has been more pronounced in the ongoing outbreak of Ebola in the Democratic Republic of Congo ( DRC ) , which started in August 2018 ( killing 2,000 people ) as well as the measles outbreak since January 2019 ( killing 6,000 people ) . According to this report , as many as 390 attacks have taken place in the DRC from August 2018 . <p> The shortage of healthcare workers has been flagged as a major concern . " Chronic under-investment in the education and employment of health workers , coupled with a failure to ensure decent pay , has led to health worker shortages all over the world , " the report says . <p> It notes that the world will need 18 million additional health workers by 2030 , primarily in low- and middle-income countries , including nine million nurses and midwives @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a mention as threat for 2020 : <p> Lack of food , unsafe food and unhealthy diets are responsible for almost one-third of today 's global disease burden .
