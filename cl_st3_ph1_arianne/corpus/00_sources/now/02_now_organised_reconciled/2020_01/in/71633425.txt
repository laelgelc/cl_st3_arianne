<h> Economic Survey 2019-20 : Need was to get serious about climate crisis <h> Last Updated : Friday 31 January 2020 <p> The Economic Survey is supposed to provide critical context framing the political choices in the Union Budget . On the climate front , the 2019-20 Survey failed to provide that context . <p> There are three elements that climate advocates look for in the survey : <p> A breakdown of India 's greenhouse gas emissions trend <p> An assessment of the ' climate relevance ' of India 's past budget expenditure <p> An economic analysis of the climate risks facing India <p> On the first point , Chapter 6 ( Sustainable Development and Climate Change ) provides a figure for " India 's national GHG inventory ( 2014 ) " . <p> There is no trend , no attempt to provide at least an initial estimate for more recent years , and no breakdown by sector , region or urban/rural . In the Statistical Annex , there seems to be no mention of greenhouse emissions at all . <p> This kind of context is needed to make equitable policy .
