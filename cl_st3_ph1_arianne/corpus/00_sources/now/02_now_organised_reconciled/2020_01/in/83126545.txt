<h> Punjab govt approves waste-to-energy power plant project at Mohali <p> New Delhi : The Punjab Government on Monday decided to build a 7 megawatt ( MW ) waste to energy plant at Simgauli village in Mohali district , an official statement said . Chief Minister Amarinder Singh gave the go-ahead for the project of building the plant in over 50 acre area under the Build Own Operate ( BOO ) model , it said . <p> The project will be completed within the next two years and the waste to energy plant would generate power from 600 tonnes per day ( TPD ) waste collected from Mohali and Patiala , the statement said . <p> Singh chaired a meeting here and also gave approval for an MoU between the National Thermal Power Corporation ( NTPC ) and Municipal Corporation Mohali , the nodal agency for executing the project , it said . <p> The chief minister said the project would contribute to the implementation of the State Action Plan on Climate Change ( SAPCC ) and also the Swachh Bharat Abhiyan . <p> He said the Municipal Solid Waste ( MSW ) had always posed a major challenge @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ could now be effectively used as a source of renewable energy . <p> He pointed out that MSW had not been harnessed systematically on a significant scale and hoped that the project would pave the way for more such initiatives . <p> During the meeting , General Manager , Waste to Energy , NTPC , Amit Kulshreshtha apprised the chief minister that the new state-of-the-art plant , complying with the latest environmental norms , would be installed as a pilot project in the state .
