<h> Will The Paris Agreement Still Be Able To Deliver After The US Withdrawal ? <p> In the short term , the United States ' withdrawal from the Paris Agreement will certainly have ripple effects globally . But rather than fatally undermine the Paris Agreement , it will likely cause other countries to reaffirm their firm commitment to the full implementation of the climate deal . <p> In the White House Rose Garden , US President Donald Trump said he wanted to start to renegotiate to see " if there 's a better deal " . <p> " If we can , great .
