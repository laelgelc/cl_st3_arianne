<h> Why agri-business management should be on your radar <h> With the sharp focus on raising farm productivity , the need for agri-biz managers is increasing <p> It is well known that the farm sector plays an important role in the country 's economy . Agriculture and allied sectors contribute 16 per cent of the country 's total GDP and are the source of income for a significant chunk ( almost 50 per cent ) of the working population . <p> But the sector has a host of issues that need to be dealt with -- extreme weather events linked to climate change , unfair trade practices , deficient policies , unavailability of proper sales channels leading to the dominance of middlemen , and the disturbing and growing incidence of farmer suicides , primarily due to indebtedness . Unfortunately , the sector has been neglected and not given the attention it deserves.It has neither been considered a desirable alternative for job-seekers nor an option for new business ventures . Though the Centre has started taking corrective measures and initiated several remedial policies , there is a long way to go . <p> Need for skilled workers <p> The biggest @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to the lack of adequate training and proper education , there is a huge demand-supply gap . Inventory management , efficient sales channels , marketing , and promotion -- almost every branch needs skilled and trained professionals who can help revive and flourish the sector with their innovative and technologically advanced ideas . <p> This is where education comes into play . The right guidance and knowledge about the industry can create a competent workforce . Given the growing concern with boosting the efficiency of farming practices and assuring farmers of the right markets for their produce , agri-business management will , in the coming years , become one of the most popular management courses among students , perhaps even topping the list of options in the management stream . <p> The steady demand for management graduates in agri-business has encouraged many prominent educational institutes to introduce courses catering to this sector . <p> Some of the topics that are covered in such courses are agricultural and food policy , agricultural finance , value chain management , social entrepreneurship , economics of food quality , international agri-food trade , and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ sales and distribution management are also covered . . <p> However , the number of institutes that offer these courses is still very low , and can barely accommodate a fraction of the students who need specialised training to address the sector 's immediate requirements . <p> Technological innovation <p> Recently , the Centre announced the setting up of a dedicated cell to promote agri start-ups to help farmers cut costs and engance their incomes .
