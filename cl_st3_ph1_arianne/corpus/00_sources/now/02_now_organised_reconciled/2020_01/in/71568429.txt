<p> Four scientists from the city are set to join the two-month long Indian Scientific Expedition to the Southern Ocean also called the Antarctic Ocean , that is to set sail from Mauritius on Tuesday , on the oceanographic research vessel SA Agulhas , with 30 other scientists on board . <p> The programme is part of the Ministry of Earth Sciences ( MoES ) initiative kicked off in 2004 to pursue multi-disciplinary , multi-institutional research activities addressing various key scientific components , including hydrodynamics , biogeochemistry , biodiversity ( plankton and higher marine organisms ) , air-sea interactions , lower atmospheric processes , palaeoclimatology and other factors . <p> Anoop Mahajan from the Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology ( IITM ) , Pune , will be leading a team of researchers drawn from 18 research organisations and universities pan-India . " The research would include studying how biodiversity is affected due to climate change and vice versa .
