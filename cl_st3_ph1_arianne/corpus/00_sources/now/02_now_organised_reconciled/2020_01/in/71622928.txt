<h> Last Updated : Monday 20 January 2020 <p> The Centre 's decision on January 16 , 2020 , to waive off environmental clearance ( EC ) for onshore and offshore gas as well as oil exploration projects could have serious consequences , according to an expert from New Delhi-based non-profit Centre for Science and Environment ( CSE ) . <p> Islands , fishing areas , breeding grounds and migratory routes , could risk serious damage because of the decision . <p> " Oil and gas exploration is considered to be the most complex among infrastructure development projects . Its consequences are not just restricted to the local environment but a global one , " Digvijay Singh Bisht , deputy programme manager for compliance monitoring at CSE , said . <p> " In addition , such projects pose a huge risk to islands , major fishing areas , nearest coastal towns , breeding grounds as well as migratory routes . All of this highlights their potency for a global catastrophe . The spilled oil can also settle on the seabed and destroy the ecosystem there , " he added . <p> The move could have been geared to counter @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ especially in view of the United States-Iran conflict .
