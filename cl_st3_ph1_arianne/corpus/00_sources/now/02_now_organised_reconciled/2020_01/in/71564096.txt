<p> One of the lesser known culprits of accelerated climate change today are architects . How , you ask ? They are largely responsible for the built-up environment around us today . <p> Promotion <p> " With 36 per cent of global energy devoted to buildings and 8 per cent of global emissions caused by cement alone , the architectural community is deeply entwined with the flows of materials , energy , and ideas that relate to climate change , both causes and solutions , " says this report . <p> In the past three decades , many have spoken of sustainability , but it is time to act . Everything we build from now onwards must be eco-sensitive , energy efficient , cost efficient , people-centered and value based .
