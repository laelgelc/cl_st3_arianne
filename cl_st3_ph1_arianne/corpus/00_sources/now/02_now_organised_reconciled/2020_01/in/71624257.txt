It aims to reduce PM2.5 and PM10 concentration by 20-30% by 2024 . <p> " Overall objective of the NCAP is comprehensive mitigation actions for prevention , control and abatement of air pollution besides augmenting the air quality monitoring network across the country and strengthening the awareness and capacity building activities , " Harsh Vardhan said when the initiative was launched . <p> But climate activists say that the plan is unlikely to make much difference to the abysmal air quality in many Indian cities .
