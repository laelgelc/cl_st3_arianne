<p> To mark the start of 2020 , Mint has been inviting policy makers , thought leaders and heads of corporate to share five ideas that will impact the decade ahead . K. Srinath Reddy is President , Public Health Foundation of India tells Neetu Chandra that development should not only focus on pathways that will deliver the Sustainable Development Goals for India as short term returns but lay firm foundations for a strong , stable and steadily progressing society where human development becomes both the lever for and the desired product of economic development . <p> EXPAND HORIZONS IN EDUCATION <p> Development demands trans-disciplinary solutions to challenges posed by complex systems . Linear solutions from siloed single disciplines will not suffice . Both school and college education must become crucibles for creating scientific minds guided by a social mission . For this the artificial separation of sciences and humanities must cease . Education must produce individuals who develop the depth of expertise in a chosen area but possess the breadth of familiarity with other relevant disciplines to enable collaboration . Prevention and early care of common ailments preserve individual health as an asset , under the broad umbrella of primary health services . While central and state governments must raise health budgets and earnestly deliver universal health coverage at all levels of care , primary health services must be at the vanguard of health for development . A convergence of multi-sectoral actions for health and active community engagement are best accomplished in primary healthcare settings . Primary healthcare democratises the health system and democracy is good for development . <p> ALIGN FOOD SYSTEMS TO NUTRITION NEEDS <p> Malnutrition , in myriad forms , damages health , impairs cognitive development , diminishes productivity and derails development . Agriculture and food systems must offer crop and dietary diversity which enable people to regularly consume nutrient-rich foods without financial barriers . Fiscal and regulatory measures must curb the production , marketing and promotion of unhealthy foods and beverages @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ just cereals and commercial crops but pulses , millets , fruits , vegetables and healthy edible oils must be promoted and positioned in the public distribution system . Ecological sustainability and climate resilience of agriculture must become key drivers of change in agricultural investments and practices . Child , adolescent and maternal nutrition must receive special attention through the National Nutrition Mission . <p> ENVIRONMENT AS A SHIELD FOR DEVELOPMENT <p> As the damage to our physical environment threatens to steer distorted development to a dystopian future , we must proactively protect the quality of air , water , soil and food from pollutants and act resolutely to put the brakes on climate change . Environmental harm has immediate as well as inter-generational consequences which may do irreversible harm to economic and human development . Even if India is not a major contributor to global climate change , we will be one of its major victims . We must act nationally and globally to protect our development . <p> FOCUS ON INCLUSIVE DEVELOPMENT <p> Unless the educational , occupational and social status of women improves , India 's developmental aspirations will remain unfulfilled . Without gender justice , economic development will be fundamentally flawed . Women must now lead our march to accelerated , inclusive and sustainable development . Gender respect and gender equity must become the basis of healthy gender relations at personal and societal levels . The seeds of gender violence need to be eliminated by changing regressive mindsets and challenging flawed social traditions that confine girls and women to subsidiary and subservient roles , even as policing and criminal justice reforms strengthen provisions for protection of innocents and punishment of offenders .
