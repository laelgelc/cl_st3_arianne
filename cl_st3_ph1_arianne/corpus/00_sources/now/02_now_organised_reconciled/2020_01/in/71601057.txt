<p> Destruction caused by the Kerala floods would have been less severe if the government followed the recommendations of the Gadgil Committee report on preservation of the Western Ghats . Kochi : The recent occurrence of extreme rainfall events and associated floods in Kerala that killed many and rendered thousands homeless were due to climate change , a noted climate expert said today . Chandra Bhushan , Deputy Director General of New Delhi-based advocacy group Centre for Science and Environment ( CSE ) , said the dam management system in the country will have to be " re-evaluated " taking into account the repeat occurrence of extreme rainfall events and associated flash floods . " The floods in Kerala were caused by climate change and the disaster was exacerbated by bad development practices , particularly on Western Ghats and other ecologically sensitive regions of Kerala , " Bhushan told PTI .
