<h> Extreme weather : 2019 broke records ; do n't expect 2020 to be better <h> Last Updated : Thursday 16 January 2020 <p> Expect a slew of extreme weather events in 2020 , continuing the trend from past two years . <p> " 2020 has started out where 2019 left off -- with high impact weather and climate related events , " World Meteorological Organisation ( WMO ) Secretary-General Petteri Taalas said . <p> Australia had its hottest , driest year on record in 2019 . This set the scene for massive bushfires that devastated people and property , wildlife , ecosystems and the environment , he pointed out . <p> Record levels of heat-trapping gases in the atmosphere will make the weather more extreme in the coming decades , he warned . <p> The World Meteorological Organization ( WMO ) has said 2019 was the second-warmest year ever recorded . <p> After analysing five different datasets , scientists declared that average temperature for 2019 was 1.1 degree Celsius above the average for pe-industrial 1850-1900 .
