<h> New MoEF guidelines for elephant deaths from anthrax <h> Last Updated : Friday 17 January 2020 <p> An official team to oversee safe disposal of elephant carcasses infected with anthrax as well as a map of anthrax hotspots are some of the steps recommended in a new standard operating procedure ( SOP ) formulated by the Union Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change ( MoEF&amp;CC ) . <p> The overall authority responsible at the state level under the SOP 's provisions will be the chief wildlife warden , in accordance with the Wildlife ( Protection ) Act , 1972 . <p> Under him , field directors in case of tiger reserves , protected area managers in case of national parks or wildlife sanctuaries and wildlife wardens or divisional forest officers / deputy conservators of forests in case of revenue lands /conservation revenue / community reserves / townships ) would be responsible . <p> The SOP also lays down rules for the constitution of a team to oversee assessment and disposal of elephant carcasses suspected to be infected with anthrax . <p> Apart from the officials mentioned previously , this team would also include a nominee of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the National Tiger Conservation Authority if the incident pertains to a tiger reserve , a representative from the local non-governmental organisation , district veterinary officer or his representative and a representative of the local Panchayati Raj institution among others . <p> The procedure will also have steps to assess whether the elephant died due to anthrax and the collection of biological samples . <p> A map of anthrax hotspots is to be created as well . <p> " It is advised to prepare a histogram of anthrax-related deaths using retrospective data overtime to forest disease occurrence . In addition , spatial distribution should be plotted to identify anthrax zones in the given area , " the SOP document , uploaded on the MoEF&amp;CC 's website on January 3 , 2020 , said . <p> " The deaths of elephants due to anthrax are not widespread . But they occur sporadically , like the last one in Odisha 's eastern range . That was the trigger to form this SOP . One can only know if an elephant has died from anthrax after proper post-mortem is done . It is primarily a disease of herbivorous animals particularly cattle , sheep , goats , horses and mules . <p> The disease occurs naturally among herbivores through contaminated soil and feed . It occurs among omnivores and carnivores through contaminated meat , bone meals or other feeds ; and among wild animals from feeding on anthrax-infected carcasses . <p> Infected animals shed the bacilli in terminal haemorrhage or spilt blood at death .
