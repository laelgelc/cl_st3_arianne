Two-thirds of all coal-fired electricity needs to be phased out by 2030 . <p> Keeping the global economic cost of this transition to a minimum would see the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development ( OECD ) and the European Union ( EU ) phase out coal power by around 2030 , with China and the rest of the world phasing out by around 2040 and 2050 respectively . <p> One proposal , illustrated below , is to phase out the oldest plants first . There are variations in how these general types of impacts are felt in different economies . <p> Livelihood impacts : In India , around one million livelihoods depend directly or indirectly on coal power . Income from coal royalties constitutes almost 50 per cent of total earnings of significant coal-producing states like Jharkhand and Odisha , which are also some of the least-developed Indian states . <p> China employs about five million in coal industries .
