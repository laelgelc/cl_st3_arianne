terminal and the gas-fired plant , which is well below the cost of building an import terminal for coal and a new power plant . <p> For this reason , several Asian countries that had been considering new coal-fired plants are now assessing gas-fired units . <p> The argument for LNG becomes more favorable if climate change and environmental factors come into play , especially if developed nations ,
