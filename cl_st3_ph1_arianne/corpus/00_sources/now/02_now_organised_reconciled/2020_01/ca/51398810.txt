Jason Blanch said . He said the program offers the best way to ensure Amherst realizes energy efficiencies in its facilities and operations and reduce its carbon footprint . <p> " There may be an opportunity to offset the salary by , for example , partnering with a company in the industrial park or with Oxford or Cumberland County for specific facilities , but establishing such an agreement will first depend on the specific skill set of the OEM , " Blanch said . <p> In aiming to reduce its carbon footprint , Blanch said the town is doing so in a financially sustainable way by withdrawing the $100,000 per year from operating reserves so it will not impact tax rates . <p> He feels it will also lead to energy savings for the town . <p> " In 2018 , Amherst spent over $880,000 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ which will continue to increase as costs of energy increases , " he said .
