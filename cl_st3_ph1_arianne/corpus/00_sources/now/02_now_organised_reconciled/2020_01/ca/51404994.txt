An oft-cited report from the International Monetary Fund found that removing the remaining barriers to interprovincial trade could boost Canada 's gross domestic product as much as four per cent per capita . It noted non-tariff trade barriers and regulatory distortions hinder labour mobility , productivity , competitiveness , internal trade , and our economic growth . <p> Recently , Bank of Canada Governor Stephen Poloz echoed these sentiments when he characterized interprovincial trade @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . " <p> The Trudeau government did take steps to liberalize interprovincial commerce during its first term in office , including the signing of the Canadian Free Trade Agreement in 2017 -- an important first step towards freer intranational trade . The second , and harder , step is to address the myriad non-tariff barriers which continue to disrupt interprovincial economic integration . <p> Enter Chrystia Freeland . When Prime Minister Trudeau appointed Freeland as his first Deputy Prime Minister , he did so with a specific purpose in mind : to help unify the country .
