Our country is surrounded by great ( nuclear ) powers -- America to the south , China to the west , Russia across the Arctic , and Europe to the east . We will either be crushed or vassalized by the pressures and pulls of these border powers , or we will emerge as one of this century 's leading countries . To be great , we must develop the national assets , capabilities and mindset to resist and build our own future on our own terms . There is no other path . <p> Rule 2 : The keys to both Wexit and climate change run through the Arctic . These territories and the Arctic waters abutting them are transforming by melt .
