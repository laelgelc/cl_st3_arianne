<h> Frigid cold to linger this week <p> According to a weather alert issued by Environment and Climate Change Canada , the " multi-day episode " of very cold wind chills is expected to continue . <p> The alert said that a cold , arctic air mass is " entrenched " over southern Saskatchewan . <p> Extreme cold values of -40 to -45 will roll in overnight , they will moderate somewhat during the day but will return again tomorrow night . <p> The trend is expected to continue as the cold air mass will remain anchored over the prairies into the weekend . <p> The extreme cold warnings will spread eastward throughout the week . <p> The weather alert reminded residents to dress warmly in layers that can be removed if you get too warm . The outer layer should be wind resistant . <p> The alert also told residents they should keep emergency supplies in their vehicles such as extra blankets and jumper cables . <p> It advised residents
