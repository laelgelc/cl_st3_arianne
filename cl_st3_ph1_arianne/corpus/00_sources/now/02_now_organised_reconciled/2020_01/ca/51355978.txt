<p> All 176 people on board Ukraine International Airlines Flight PS752 died when it crashed after takeoff near Tehran , Iran . Prime Minister Justin Trudeau has said that intelligence sources indicate an Iranian missile downed the plane , though it might have been unintentional . There were 57 Canadian citizens on board and 138 were headed to Canada . Postmedia papers across the country worked together to gather the stories of the victims with ties to Canada . They included students , professors , teachers , newlyweds and children . Their loss leaves a trail of heartbroken family members , classmates and colleagues here in Canada and in their homeland .
