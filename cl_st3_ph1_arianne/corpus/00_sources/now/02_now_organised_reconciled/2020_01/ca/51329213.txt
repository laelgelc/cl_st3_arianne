<p> The Annapolis Valley town of Wolfville has become popular for vacationers , wine-drinkers , and those looking for an escape from the city . <p> But the town is starting to think about its future as a tourist destination , as the tides of the Minas Basin loom ever closer . <p> " Sometimes a little too close , " says Jeff Cantwell , Mayor of Wolfville . " We are at sea level for the most part , protected by a dyke system built several hundred years ago by the Acadians and currently maintained by the Department of Agriculture . " <p> With the Bay of Fundy having the highest tides in the world , Cantwell tells NEWS 95.7 that his staff are starting to plan on how to mitigate climate change . <p> " When we had those higher tides in the fall and when the lunar pull is so strong , we get some water in and around our main street and downtown area , " he explains . <p> Cantwell explains that most of the downtown area , where Acadia University is located , is at sea level .
