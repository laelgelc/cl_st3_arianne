For more information , visit the Sustainable Orillia website . <p> Canadian winters are legendary -- frigid mornings feature the collective groans of Canadians and their cars as they get their January days underway . But those groans are becoming fewer as technological improvements make newer vehicles more and more ' ready to roll ' even on the most brutal mornings . <p> According to Car Help Canada , most recent models need less than 30 seconds to warm up and will do so more efficiently when in motion . ' Warming up the car ' by leaving it idling in the driveway is simply no longer a necessity . <p> Idling our vehicles less will save us money on fuel , improve air quality in our community , and reduce emissions contributing to climate change . <p> We know asking readers to eliminate vehicle-idling in the midst of winter is a big ASK -- but how about limiting your vehicle-idling ? Consciously cutting it in half or reducing it @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ yes , eliminating it entirely ? ) <p> Let 's take a look at a couple of the reasons why these actions make a difference . <p> It 's calculated that over 10 billion gallons of gasoline are wasted each year world-wide from vehicle-idling . Idling our vehicle for 10 seconds or more uses more fuel than restarting the engine , a main reason many new vehicle models are equipped with an automatic engine stop-and-start feature , reducing emissions at every red light or traffic back-up . <p> Reducing our personal vehicle-idling by just 10 minutes each day translates into an additional eight or more kilometres of travel , saving both gas and money . <p> Vehicle emissions are the largest contributor of greenhouse gases ( GHG ) world-wide . Idling a vehicle for just 10 minutes generates the equivalent of a pound of greenhouse gas . <p> When we multiply that by the number of vehicles we see idling everyday , it 's clear that reducing vehicle-idling is a positive step to reduce GHG emissions . <p> And it 's not just the health of our planet that is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , too . <p> Many studies identify the negative effects of exhaust fumes on our health -- in particular , our brain cells . School boards have become leaders in restricting vehicle-idling in school zones to protect the health of their young constituents -- our children and grandchildren . <p> Another sure-fire way to reduce vehicle-idling is to avoid or reduce our use of ' drive-throughs . " Drive-throughs ( or " drive-thrus " -- take your pick ) are a concentrated source of vehicle-idling -- so much so that an increasing number of North American cities have taken steps to curtail development of additional drive-through operations within their areas . <p> And again , consider the additional health benefit of parking , getting out of your car , and walking to and from the coffee counter ! Exercise , people , exercise . <p> Small , individual steps matter . Doing our personal best to preserve our planet and quality of life is the only way we can make a difference and provide role models for our families and friends . Reducing our vehicle-idling is one of these small @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ other ways we can reduce GHG emissions .
