Despite this , foresters are n't legally obligated to consider what impacts their harvest will have downstream . <p> The convergence of extremely heavy snowpacks , sudden downpours and unseasonably warm temperatures were all factors in the historic flood that swept through Grand Forks and other parts of British Columbia 's southern Interior in 2018 . <p> But some argue that the deforested landscape , where melting snow is exposed to direct sunlight , likely exacerbated river overflows . <p> Logging companies in B.C. are n't legally required to consider downstream flooding when they harvest trees in watersheds .
