What most people do n't know about Berman is that the very first cause for which she advocated involved her hometown Jewish community . <p> " I grew up in a very religious household . Thanks in part to her efforts , her synagogue in London , Ont. , became one of the first Conservative shuls in Canada to permit women in its minyanim . <p> For Berman , environmental activism is a natural extension of her Jewish values .
