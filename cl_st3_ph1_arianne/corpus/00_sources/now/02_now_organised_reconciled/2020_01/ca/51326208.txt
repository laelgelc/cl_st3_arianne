<h> New poll suggests most Canadians think low-carbon energy transition beneficial in the long-term <p> Most Canadians think the transition to less carbon intensive energy sources is a necessary global trend that will reap benefits for the country in the long-term even if it makes life more expensive , according to a new Abacus Data poll for Clean Energy Canada . <p> In the online survey of 1,848 Canadian adults , conducted between Dec. 12 and Dec. 19 , 2019 , 75 per cent of respondents said the conversation about an energy transition is a global one , not isolated to Canada . The exact same percentage of respondents said this energy transition would be beneficial for Canada in the long-term , while 70 per cent also said it was necessary , whether it 's preferable or not . <p> Also , 61 per cent said it would be beneficial to Alberta , in particular , in the long-term and 59 per cent said it would make life more expensive , though the same percentage said it needs to be done at a faster pace .
