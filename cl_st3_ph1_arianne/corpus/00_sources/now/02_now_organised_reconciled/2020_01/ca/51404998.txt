<h> The Drilldown : Two new LNG projects win approval in B.C. <p> The development of a natural gas liquefaction facility was approved by the B.C. Oil and Gas Commission earlier this week . The project will be run by GasNorth Energy Ltd. in an equity partnership with the Fort Nelson First Nation . Its main focus will be shipping LNG to northern communities whose primary source of energy are diesel power generators , reported CBC News . <p> The provincial government also approved a permit for the Fort Nelson First Nation that will allow them to assess old natural gas fields with the possibility of repurposing them for geothermal electricity . <p> Internationally <p> The U.K. government is being sued following their approval of a new gas-fired power plant run by Drax Power Station .
