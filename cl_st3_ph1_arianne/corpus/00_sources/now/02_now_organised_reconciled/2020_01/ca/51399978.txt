Hypothermia occurs when the body 's core temperature drops below 35 degrees Celsius and can have severe consequences , including organ failure and death . Frostbite can also occur in cold weather when skin freezes and , in severe cases , can lead to amputation when deeper tissues freeze . <p> Those most at risk of cold-related illness are people experiencing homelessness or those under-housed , those who work outdoors , people with a pre-existing heart condition or respiratory illness , elderly people , infants and young children .
