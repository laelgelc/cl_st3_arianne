I think Noam Chomsky coined it , with collaborator John Halle . I ca n't find other sources . <p> Chomsky has a puckish side . In the 1970s , writing on the " political economy of human rights , " he delineated mainstream types of terrorism : nefarious ( done by the bad guys ) , constructive ( by our side ) and benign ( irrelevant to Western interests , so who cares ) . LEV basically means voting for Clinton since she 's slightly less abominable than Trump . Chomsky , who leans toward anarchism , supports this . <p> Even before the coinage , it was subject to passionate denunciation in the U.S. The Des Moines Register warned : Do n't vote your fears by voting for the lesser of two evils . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " Lesser of Two Evils Vote is Counterproductive and Morally Corrupt . The rebuttals -- too mild a word -- divide along two lines . <p> There 's the Not a Dime 's Worth of Difference argument , which shades into Hillary Might Even Be Worse .
