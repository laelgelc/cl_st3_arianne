Under those policies , suppliers like FortisBC will be required to increase the renewable content of its natural gas to 15% . <p> Renewable natural gas is currently produced primarily from landfills and dairy farms ( biogas ) , and from wood waste ( syngas ) . But hydrogen made from wind and water and then injected into the natural gas stream could also count as renewable . <p> Using electrolysis , with the electricity supplied from wind and hydro power , the hydrogen atoms are split off from water molecules to produce pure streams of hydrogen and oxygen . <p> This process for producing " green hydrogen " is much more expensive than producing " blue hydrogen " from natural gas , but it requires no fossil fuels , so various climate change policies -- from carbon taxes to renewable content requirements , both @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ expected to eventually make green hydrogen competitive with blue hydrogen . <p> Puetter said the cost of wind power has come down to the point where a dedicated wind farm could produce power at a cost that is lower than what BC Hydro currently charges .
