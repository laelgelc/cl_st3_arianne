" These stakeholders want us to listen and I 'm up to the challenge . " <p> Named in 2016 as chair of the Commons standing committee on veterans ' affairs , Ellis said he is taking that experience to a Canada-wide constituency of farmers to render unwavering support from Ottawa to compete on a global scale . <p> Part of his responsibilities will include taking queries from 337 MPs on the floor of the House in Minister Bibeau 's absence and acting as @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ from ridings across the country . <p> He will also tour the country meeting with stakeholders in the industry and has already met with the Ontario Grain Farmers and the Dairy Farmers of Ontario . <p> It 's Quinte 's first connection to the agriculture ministry since former Prince Edward-Hastings MP Lyle Vanclief served as the federal agriculture minister under former Prime Minister Jean Chretien from 1997 to 2003 becoming one of the longest serving politicians in that post in Canadian history . <p> Bibeau was given 10 key targets for her ministry to achieve this session of parliament by Trudeau in one of 37 mandate letters sent to his ministers outlining goals in each ministry . <p> " As Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food , you will continue to support the agricultural sector so that it is a leader in job creation and innovation .
