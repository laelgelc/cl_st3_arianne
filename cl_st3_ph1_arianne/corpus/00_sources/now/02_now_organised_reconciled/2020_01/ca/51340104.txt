The world has been gathering to discuss the imperiled environment since 1988 in Toronto , when politicians and scientists concluded that " humanity is conducting an unintended , uncontrolled , globally pervasive experiment whose ultimate consequences could be second only to a global nuclear war . " <p> Over the next 26 years , what Time Magazine called " the annual exercise in futility that is the U.N . climate summit " has been staged around the world , consistently producing daily dramatic walkouts @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Almost 200 nations met in Doha , Qatar in late November 2012 in yet another high-profile attempt to hammer out a U.N . deal to curb global emissions of greenhouse gases by 2020 . Opinions were mixed about the wisdom of having an OPEC member like Qatar host a summit on fossil fuels , and there was a furious controversy over the choice of summit president . Not only was Abdullah bin Hamad al-Attiyah Qatar 's oil minister and a former president of OPEC , but he had been named the best petroleum executive in the world in 2007 . <p> These concerns appear to have been borne from the fact that interventions from non-governmental organisations groups during the talks were limited to a farcical 30 seconds .
