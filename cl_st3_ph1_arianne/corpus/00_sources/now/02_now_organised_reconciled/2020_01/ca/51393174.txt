<p> The Supreme Court of Canada Thursday declined to hear an appeal from the city of Victoria about whether its 2018 ban on plastic bags is valid . <p> In 2018 , Victoria passed a bylaw prohibiting businesses from providing or selling plastic bags to customers . Instead paper bags were to be offered to customers for a fee of no less than 15 cents . Fines to businesses for providing a plastic bag were $100 . <p> Victoria , in a statement announcing the bylaw , said Victoria residents used 200 plastic bags a year each , adding up to 17 million bags a year . <p> " Plastic bags are made from a limited supply of non-renewable petroleum sources which contribute to greenhouse gases , air quality issues , natural resource depletion and chemical , waste and litter accumulation , " the statement said . <p> The Canadian Plastic Bag Association asked for a judicial review of the bylaw .
