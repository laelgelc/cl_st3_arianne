<p> Canada elected 91 rookie MPs to parliament in the 2019 federal election . The National Post spoke with a selection to discover what was behind their political drive . Today , Maura Forrest 's interview with NDP MP Leah Gazan . <p> OTTAWA -- Leah Gazan comes by her socialist credentials honestly . Raised in Winnipeg , her parents were organizers with the Co-operative Commonwealth Federation , a social-democratic party founded by farmers , labourers and socialists that was a predecessor of the NDP . <p> Gazan , the newly elected NDP MP for Winnipeg Centre , says she grew up steeped in politics . " I believe in democratic values , I believe in our socialist roots , " she told the National Post in a recent interview . " I 'm a proud socialist and I 'm looking forward to pushing that in the House of Commons and in caucus . " <p> Gazan 's focus on a grassroots style of politics has earned her comparisons to Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez , the U.S. congresswoman who 's part of a crop of young , social media-savvy politicians pushing the Democrats to the left . <p> I believe in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ But for the most part , Gazan 's chief concerns align closely with the NDP 's under leader Jagmeet Singh , who has steered his party away from the more centrist tendencies of former leader Thomas Mulcair and has made climate change , inequality and Indigenous rights top priorities . <p> " I think being born Indigenous in Canada is a political act in itself , " she said .
