It has focused on responsibilities , roles , and policy options for Australia to contribute to this reform process at the national , regional and global levels . <p> Australia should have the political courage to assume these responsibilities and take on these roles . The current lull in asylum flows to Australia can not be taken for granted , and Australia is likely to be more directly and quickly affected by environmental migration than most other industrialised nations .
