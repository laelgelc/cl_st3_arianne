<p> More mechanical clearing of land could be part of the answer to Tasmania 's bushfire threat , new Premier Peter Gutwein believes . Mr Gutwein , who said during the week the government needed to do more on climate change , floated the mechanical clearing idea during a wide-ranging interview on Tuesday . " We know that we 've got a changeable climate , " he said . " We know that at this time of year there is the possibility of greater fire action , so I think we need to look more at what we 're doing in terms of fuel mitigation . " How do we protect settlements ? " Is mechanical clearing something that we should be looking at in terms of working hand in glove with fuel reduction ? " Mechanical clearing involves slashing , brushcutting and/or machinery which uproots trees and can be used to manage the density of regrowth .
