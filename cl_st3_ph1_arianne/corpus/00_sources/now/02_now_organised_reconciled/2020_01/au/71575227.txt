<h> How the US , EU could clip the wings of ambitious post-Brexit Britain <p> London In the early 1960s , former US secretary of state Dean Acheson famously said that " Great Britain has lost an empire and has not yet found a role " . More than half a century later , it 's still looking . <p> With Brexit just weeks away , the search is on in earnest . No longer clutching at the hem of the European Union , but no longer able to rest comfortably in the bosom of the trans-Atlantic " special relationship " , what kind of a country does Britain want to be ? <p> UK foreign minister Dominic Raab had a go at answering that question in parliament on Monday ( Tuesday AEDT ) . <p> Unshackled Britannia would be ambitious , self-confident , globalist and " a force for good in the world " , he declared . <p> " Our guiding lights will remain the values of free trade , democracy , human rights and the international rule of law . <p> Advertisement <p> " We 're proud of our ability to make a difference to the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ world , and we will continue that effort every day of the week .
