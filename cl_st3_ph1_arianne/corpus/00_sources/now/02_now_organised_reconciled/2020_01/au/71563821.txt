<p> Jakarta welcomed the new year with a new record -- the heaviest rains in decades . The deluge left parts of the Indonesian capital under 5 metres of water , which triggered landslides and cut off whole neighbourhoods . Before the water finally receded this week , at least 60 people were dead and hundreds of thousands forced from their homes . <p> Jakarta is sinking quickly . Experts say that by 2050 , North Jakarta will be nearly entirely undersea .
