<p> Christmas and New Year are traditionally a time of forbearance and fellowship , where the old year is forsaken and old grievances forgotten . Except in politics , where it 's traditional to pass judgment on winners and losers of the 12 months past - and scope out the 12 to come . Last year 's biggest winner was Scott Morrison . At the start of the year , you would n't have wanted your last 10 bucks riding on him winning the 2019 election . Indeed , Sportsbet went so far as to pay out those who bet on Labor before the election was held . Given Australia 's traditional affiliation with punting , you could hardly script a more Australian version of the Chicago Tribune 's infamous " Dewey defeats Truman " front page . Of course , in retrospect , it 's possible to identify many of the factors that led to his victory . Moreover , a number of the policies that Labor took to the election were a mistake , not only politically but from a policy perspective . Income tax rates should be lower , not higher , at the top end . Negative gearing is a legitimate feature of the tax system , as are refundable franking credits .
