<p> Ahead of the 2020 World Economic Forum in Davos , Switzerland , it is staggering to note that the wealthiest 1 per cent of Australians , just 250,000 people , have more than double the wealth of the bottom 50 per cent of the rest of the population - or more than 12.5 million people . Oxfam is committed to tackling poverty and inequality , but figures such as these reveal that we have a broken economic system that is concentrating more wealth in the hands of the rich and powerful , while ordinary people struggle to scrape by . Although there was a slight decrease in the number of billionaires in Australia to 36 in 2019 , the number has more than tripled over the past 10 years and their wealth is still increasing . The wealth of Australian billionaires , who are mostly men , grew by an average of USD $460 million from 2018 to 2019 .
