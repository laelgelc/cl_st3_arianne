<p> At the dawn of 2020 , the world is waking up on a pillow of uncertainty . The trade war between the United States and China has weakened the global economy , instabilities in the Middle East , Asia , and Latin America intensify public anxiety , and the impact of climate change is becoming ever more pronounced . Global economic growth shrank from 3.7% in 2018 to 3.2% in 2019 , reflecting the general gloominess linked to trade and geopolitical tensions . <p> In contrast , 2019 was a relatively successful year for the Pacific 's regional economy , where growth estimates have been revised upwards by the Asian Development Bank , from 3.5% to 4.0% .
