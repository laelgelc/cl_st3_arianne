<p> With the second decade of the 21st Century just ended , is the world getting better or worse ? Alan Austin reviews the year and the decade . <p> THERE WAS PLENTY to be dismayed about in 2019 , but plenty to celebrate also . Let 's start with the positives . <h> Global wealth and poverty <p> Wealth is increasing globally , faster in some regions than others . Average wealth per person this year reached a record high of US$70,850 ( AU$102,110 ) . Since the turn of the century , total net worth -- assets minus debt -- declined in 2001 , 2007 , 2013 , 2014 and 2017 , but increased in the other 14 years . <h> Agriculture <p> There is more than enough food to feed the world . Local famines are the result of distribution issues , not supply . <p> Land under cultivation in poor countries has increased from 36.4% in 2000 to 38.8% today .
