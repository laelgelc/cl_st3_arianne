<h> Fire victims join ANZ climate complaint <p> An environmental group and three people affected by bushfires have lodged a complaint against ANZ <p> For Jack Egan , wanting to make big corporations act in line with international climate agreements is deeply personal . <p> Less than a month ago on New Year 's Eve , he lost his home at North Rosedale , south of Bateman 's Bay in NSW when bushfires ripped through the area . <p> Mr Egan had stayed to defend his home , but was forced to flee to a bunker-type laundry on his neighbour 's property after watching his fire hose melt and his water pump catch alight . <p> " I saw our front deck catch on fire ... the flames of the deck were licking into the window spaces and around the doors , " he told AAP . <p> While he acknowledges Australia has always had droughts and bushfires , he is convinced global warming played a role in the severity of the drought and fierceness of the blazes . <p> " Many scientists are saying this is well-predicted and it 's a consequence of the global heating , " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the more keen to urge everyone , including governments and businesses , to do their bit to help keep climate change at bay . <p> That 's why he is one of three people affected by bushfires to join environmental group Friends of the Earth in lodging a complaint against ANZ . <p> They hope the bank will improve its climate policies . <p> ANZ said it is reviewing their claims . <p> " Since the Paris Agreement was reached in 2015 , our exposure to thermal coal has decreased by 50 per cent and we expect this trend to continue , " an ANZ spokesman told AAP . <p> " ANZ has been clear that the bank 's thermal coal exposure has , and will continue to , significantly reduce over time . " <p> The complaint was made on Thursday to the Australian National Contact Point ( ANCP ) , a section of the federal treasury which deals with guidelines for multi-national companies . <p> The document alleges the bank has not adhered to the OECD 's Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises , including a failure to be fully @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ it contributes to by financing the fossil fuel industry . <p> " It remains the biggest financer of fossil fuels among the big four Australian banks and it has neglected a number of opportunities to improve its direct and indirect environmental impact , " the complaint reads . <p> " ANZ 's lack of full disclosure about its climate change impacts prevents consumers from making informed decisions about whether or not to engage with the bank . " <p> The complaint is based on a similar one brought by Friends of the Earth Netherlands against ING Bank , which the environmental group says has resulted in the bank committing to stronger climate action . <p> About 30 people linked to the group delivered the complaint to ANZ 's sustainability officer at their Melbourne head quarters on Thursday .
