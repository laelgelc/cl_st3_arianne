<h> Boost for Canberra 's growing renewable sector <p> Canberra 's growing renewable sector will receive a boost with the second round of Renewable Energy Innovation Fund ( REIF ) Direct Grants and a new REIF co-funding scheme for clean tech , announced by the Minister for Climate Change and Sustainability Shane Rattenbury . <p> The ACT Government 's $12 million REIF is providing up to $2.5 million of funding to support innovation grant programs , including the successful Direct Grants program and a new Cleantech Co-funding scheme .
