<p> Scott Morrison 's $2 billion bushfires and climate fund set the Government up against a mountain of knowledge and expertise demanding much harder action , writes Lee Duffield . <p> IT BECOMES A CHOICE between a government PR campaign with $2 billion to spend and serious decision-making about the dangerous and endangered environment . <p> Scott Morrison as Prime Minister is acting the frontman for a huge relief and reconstruction program and , make no mistake , the nation-wide disaster response has become exemplary worldwide with all branches of society demonstrating dedication , ability , courage , humanity -- making the best of a very bad situation . <p> However , the Government 's rhetoric and politicking must be raising doubts about its competence and sincerity , as it rejects urgent demands for a national attack on the menaces of climate change . <h> DECLARATION ON THE RADIO <p> Morrison himself is a tarnished brand due to his past efforts to get votes out of climate change denial : taking the mining industry 's lump of coal into Parliament ; pretending electric cars would compulsorily replace 4WDs and spoil your weekend ; refusing to see the former fire @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Hawaii in disregard of their explicit warnings . <p> On Monday , after some weeks of over-talking , Scott Morrison distilled it into a few clear sentences on what needs to be done on ABC 's AM : first level , emergency action on a big scale ; second level , " hazard reduction preparedness " ; and third , the " longer-term climate adaptation issues " . That list would be understood and accepted widely across Australia , but he immediately backed away from the full commitment . <h> CAMPAIGNING ALL THE TIME <p> Consider the first level , the full-scale rescue and relief , compensation payments schemes and the huge benefit of site clean-ups by volunteers and soldiers . His first response was going to Hawaii and letting them carry it , no different governments " climbing over one another " .
