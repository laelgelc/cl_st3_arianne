<p> How do you support people forever attached to a landscape after an inferno tears through their homelands , decimating native food sources , burning through ancient scarred trees and destroying ancestral and totemic plants and animals ? <p> The fact is , the experience of Aboriginal people in the fire crisis engulfing much of Australia is vastly different to non-Indigenous people . <p> Colonial legacies of eradication , dispossession , assimilation and racism continue to impact the lived realities of Aboriginal people . Added to this is the widespread exclusion of our people from accessing and managing traditional homelands . These factors compound the trauma of these unprecedented fires . <p> As Australia picks up the pieces from these fires , it 's more important than ever to understand the unique grief Aboriginal people experience . Only through this understanding can effective strategies be put in place to support our communities ' recoveries . <h> Perpetual grief <p> Aboriginal people have been ignored as their homelands have been neglected and mismanaged .
