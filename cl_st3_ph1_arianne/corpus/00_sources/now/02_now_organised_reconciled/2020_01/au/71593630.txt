<h> The consequences of the strengthening US-India partnership are still uncertain <p> Over the past month , Hugh White and I have exchanged opposing views on the meaning of the US-India relationship on The Interpreter . <p> Hugh first argued that President Barack Obama 's January trip to New Delhi failed , ' because India is not willing to make the preservation of US primacy its principal strategic aim in Asia . ' I replied that this was an unfairly high bar to set in terms of judging India 's role in the pivot . Hugh then clarified : <p> India 's new alignment with the US will only make a real difference if it is credibly willing to support America militarily against China if and when US primacy is at stake . Diffuse and politically acceptable diplomatic support wo n't cut it at a time like this . So the test of the US-India alignment is simple : does anyone think India would send forces to help America defend Japan 's claim to the Senkakus , or the Philippines ' claims in the South China Sea , or Taiwan ?
