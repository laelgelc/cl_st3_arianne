<h> Archie wins tough 2019 ORCV Melbourne to Hobart Yacht Race <h> Hobart , December 31 , 2019 : Jeff Sloan 's Archambault A35 Archie has been declared the provisional winner of a very challenging ORCV Melbourne to Hobart Yacht Race , an event that takes the boats via the west coast of Tasmania 's world heritage wilderness . <p> Archie arrived in Hobart at 11.12.59 on New Year 's Eve during a night of rain and squalls . <p> " We knew it would be a tough race , which it was . " Jeff Sloan said , " But not because of big waves or strong winds -- it was due to no breeze ! <p> " It was the quietest crossing ( of Bass Strait ) we have ever done , followed by a good run down the coast with some storms at the end . " <p> Asked whether he would recommend the race to others , he pointed out that for all but two of the crew it was their first time in the Westcoaster . <p> " My advice is , do n't fear it . Yes , it 's a challenge but @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ my crew are graduates of the ORCV Beyond the Bay program and I think that set them up to be able handle whatever was thrown at us . " <p> Line honours winner was Guillaume Leroux 's Class 40 Lord Jiminy , the first French entrant in the race . After arriving in Hobart at 3.30am , Leroux commented , " They were very challenging conditions after we rounded Maatsuyer Island at about 4pm yesterday . When the thunderstorms came through we were down to a staysail ( with no mainsail up ) but still had a top speed of 22.5 knots . It was tough , but we enjoyed the challenge . " <p> The boat was entered in the four-plus-autohelm division , introduced by the Ocean Racing Club of Victoria to encourage participation in offshore races .
