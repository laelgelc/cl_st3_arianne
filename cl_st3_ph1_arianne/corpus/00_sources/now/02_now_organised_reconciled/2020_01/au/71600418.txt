<h> ' Prophets of doom ' : Trump , Thunberg spar over climate outlook <p> US President Donald Trump and teenage climate activist Greta Thunberg have renewed their tit-for-tat about the environment at the World Economic Forum in Davos . <p> Mr Trump used a speech to the forum , which has sustainability as its main theme , to decry climate " prophets of doom " . <p> Without actually naming Ms Thunberg -- who was in the audience at the Swiss ski resort -- he called for a rejection of " predictions of the apocalypse " and said the US would defend its economy . <p> Less than an hour later , the young Swede returned fire in her own speech , telling political leaders the world " in case you had n't noticed , is currently on fire " . <p> Environmental destruction is at the top of the agenda at the annual summit of the world 's decision-makers , which takes place at a Swiss ski resort . <p> " Our house is still on fire , " Ms Thunberg said , repeating her remarks at the annual meeting of the World Economic Forum a year @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , " the teenage activist added , in her latest to-and-fro with the 73-year-old President . <p> Donald Trump leaves the forum on Tuesday .
