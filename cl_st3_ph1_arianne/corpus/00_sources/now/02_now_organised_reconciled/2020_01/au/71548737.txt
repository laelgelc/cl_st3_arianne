This is a question we tried to answer in our recent study published in Nature Climate Change . <p> We compared two groups of global climate model simulations : one assuming rapid warming through the 21st Century , and another with slower warming that eventually stabilises in the 23rd Century . By comparing these two different groups of climate simulations we could examine the effect of different rates of global temperature increases on the temperature patterns across the world . <p> And we found that the rate of temperature increase , irrespective of the absolute temperature rises , has significant implications for local climates . <p> In a rapidly-warming climate @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ if the climate is given time to stabilise then the oceans start to catch up .
