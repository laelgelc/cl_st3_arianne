Sixty per cent said either China receives more respect than it deserves or about the right amount of respect . Of nine possible threats , it was non-traditional threats that most worried the Chinese people . ' Environmental issues like climate change ' and ' water and food shortages ' topped the list with 76% and 67% respectively saying they were a threat . Fifty per cent of Chinese people said the United States posed a threat to China 's security , while only a slim majority ( 51% ) said Japan did not pose a threat . Most other reasons tested also received similar but lower levels of agreement . <p> Younger Chinese adults tended to be more likely than their elders to say China was receiving less respect than it deserved and that Japan and the United States posed a threat .
