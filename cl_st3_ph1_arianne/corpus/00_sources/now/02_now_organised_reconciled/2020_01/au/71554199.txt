<h> Down to Earth : A Fire and Climate Relief Concert <p> Gang of Youths , Tash Sultana , Angus and Julia Stone -- and many more -- are banding together . <p> Some of Australia 's most-loved musicians are doing their part to support the bushfire relief effort , with a huge concert at the Sidney Myer Music Bowl . <p> And the line-up is a real corker . It includes Gang of Youths , Tash Sultana , Angus and Julia Stone , Thelma Plum , Jack River , Ruby Fields , Briggs , plus special guest appearances . All artists have waived their fees and commissions to be part of this event . <p> One hundred per cent of profits ( including those from bar and merchandise sales ) will be donated to the Red Cross , as well as a number of other charities and groups .
