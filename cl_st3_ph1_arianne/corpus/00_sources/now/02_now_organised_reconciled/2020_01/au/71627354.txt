<h> Potters in Protest at Piccolina <p> Trade in your ice-cream cup or cone for a beautiful ceramic bowl ( that you can keep ) . All proceeds will support climate change action . <p> Melbourne-based ceramicist Britt Neech ( of Clae Studio ) has teamed up with Collingwood 's Piccolina Gelateria for a one-off ceramics-and-gelati event in response to the worsening climate emergency . <p> Neech put a call-out to her creative network , which resulted in more than 300 ceramic bowls being donated from makers all over the country ( and some from Australian potters living abroad ) . <p> Here 's how it will work : you 'll choose your favourite bowl , and for $30 it will be filled with Piccolina gelati . Then you 'll be able to take it home . <p> One hundred per cent of proceeds will be donated to the Australian Conservation Foundation who are , among other incredible initiatives , putting pressure on the Australian Government to make urgent decisions to transition to clean energy .
