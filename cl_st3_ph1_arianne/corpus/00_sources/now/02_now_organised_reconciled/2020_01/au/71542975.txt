<h> Opinion <h> Fires rage and burn while Sydney , oblivious , parties on <p> I 'm a big Clover supporter , as you know , but this was a bad call . " 2020 arrives in sparkling Sydney style , " crowed the City of Sydney 's New Year 's Day media release of its fireworks extravaganza the evening before . But from where I sat , in thick , acrid smoke , it felt downright insulting . <p> The Southern Tablelands forecast said " mostly sunny " but the new decade dawned so smoke-shrouded that the neighbour 's sheep , metres away , appeared like atmospheric thickenings . Even inside , the air made your eyes water . Sparkling ? There was nothing sparkling about this . <p> We 'd long planned New Year 's Eve at our tiny off-grid farmlet . It should have been fun . Not fireworks , exactly , but fun . Yet it started spooky and got spookier . At noon , NYE , I was underground in the National Library , a sanctuary of cool against the crackling heat outside .
