<h> Tiffany &amp; Co uses ads to tell the PM to fight climate change <p> Tiffany &amp; Co has taken out print ads urging Prime Minister Scott Morrison to take action on climate change . <p> The jewellery company , which mines for diamonds , printed full-page ads across the weekend in Nine 's The Sydney Morning Herald and The Age acknowledging Australians battling the bushfires , and addressing the prime minister directly . <p> " Dear Prime Minister Morrison , As the brave people of Australia continue to battle bushfires that are devastating communities and wildlife , now is the time for bold and decisive action , " the ad states . <p> " The disaster of climate change is too real , and the threat to our planet and to our children is too great . " <p> Glen Schlehuber , Tiffany &amp; Co 's vice president and managing director for Australia and New Zealand says the company has also donated to support firefighters and wildlife recovery . <p> " We ran this message today in two of Australia 's largest newspapers as an open letter to our Prime Minister , " Schlehuber says . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is willing to stand up and take action on climate change .
