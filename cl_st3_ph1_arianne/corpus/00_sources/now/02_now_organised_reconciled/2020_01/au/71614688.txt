<p> Sustainable investing is an umbrella term for different forms of socially responsible investment . The movement is best known for ethical managed funds that exclude tobacco , weapons and other harmful sectors , even though that is the smaller part of sustainable investing . <p> Most sustainable investing is done by active managed funds that have ESG overlays in their investment process . Their ESG analysis screens companies for non-financial risks . As part of their responsible-investing approach , more funds are engaging actively with companies and voting at annual general meetings against resolutions which could undermine ESG principles . <p> Climate-change concerns are a key driver of the ESG and sustainable-investing boom . Australians ranked climate change at the top of 10 possible threats in the next 10 years , the 2019 Lowy Institute Poll found .
