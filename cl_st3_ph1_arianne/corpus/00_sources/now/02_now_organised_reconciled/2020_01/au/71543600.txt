Sadly , the lessons of Black Saturday are being ignored in far too many places . <p> We know what to do when extreme fire threatens . We save lives , not properties . We also know the difference between typical summer fire risk and what we have now . They are not the same , never have been the same and it is plain dangerous to confuse the two . <p> The bushfire has destroyed Mallacoota in Victoria.Credit:Getty Images <p> There have been bad fires ever since history started being recorded . They were small compared to what we face right now . <p> The drought has primed the bush to explode . On Black Saturday , many died in their cars fleeing in chaos and confusion . <p> Firefighters are being asked to risk their lives to save yours . If you are in an extreme risk fire zone and ignore the warnings to evacuate or decide to take your chances , are you entitled to ask someone to risk their life to help you ? <p> Footage from the Fire Rescue NSW truck racing through flames in Currowan on the NSW South Coast is enough to make even the most hardened heart skip a beat . The room heating came from an oil-burning space heater in the hall . Burning oil . That is correct , you did not misread it . <p> Once a month , a truck would pull up out the front in our suburban street , a large man with heavy gloves would unfurl a hose from a reel on the back of the truck , drape it over the fence , pull it up the side of the house and fill the rusty slab-sided metal green tank with oil . <p> Then he would go along to the next house and do the same . And the next one . When it was cold , we would prime the wick , fill the heater with oil and set it alight for warmth . <p> Loading <p> That was not unusual . And it was not that long ago . We were paying other countries to solve our rubbish problems for us . <p> We are among the most prosperous people on Earth . We are also per capita the most wasteful . We create more pollution of all kinds than anyone else . We are world leaders at consuming stuff . That is how we live . <p> We can not ask the rest of the world to change unless we are prepared to change , too . It is plain wrong to ask poor countries to take complex and expensive measures that we ourselves refuse . <p> We export coal . We burn coal . We have for a long time . Entire communities rely on it for their livelihoods . Not surprisingly , they are worried about losing everything they have . But coal is like the gas-burning heater in the hall or the briquette hot-water system in 1968 . <p> We must change again . <p> The gradual shift will be a challenge but it must be done . It will require careful planning , looking after families and towns that have known no other industry , and co-operation by governments - federal , state and local . <p> We saw the transition plan after the closure of our car factories achieve incredible outcomes . There were people who missed out but most found new jobs , suburbs did not become ghost towns . The factories are being re-purposed . No one was thrown on the scrap heap . <p> These fires are terrible . But we have had terrible fires not long ago on the outskirts of metropolitan Melbourne .
