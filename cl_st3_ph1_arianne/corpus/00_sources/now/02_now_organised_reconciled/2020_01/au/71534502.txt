Normally I 'd reach for my wallet -- I 'd done so a few weeks earlier when they were collecting for an actors ' charity -- but this time I declined . <p> Like Victoria 's Country Fire Authority , the RFS is staffed by volunteers . Why did they need donations ? Presumably , to help cover the cost of needed equipment or incidental expenses . Really ? What 's happened to the state government 's cheque book ? And do n't I remember hearing that the RFS had had its funding cut ? <p> No one believes every worthy cause should be funded by the government so that private charity becomes redundant .
