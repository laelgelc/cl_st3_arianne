<h> YOUR SAY : Bushfires not first sign of warming climate change <p> THE bushfires have been a tragic sign that climate change has hit Australia . <p> But the fires are not the first sign . In 2016-2017 , the Great Barrier Reef suffered unprecedented back to back coral bleaching events which destroyed nearly 50 per cent of the hard coral cover . <p> A coral bleaching event is the equivalent of a bushfire underwater , a heatwave of such intensity that the corals stress and die . <p> These heatwaves , like the fires , are driven by global warming . <p> Of course , more than just trees are destroyed by bushfires and more than just corals are destroyed by bleaching events . <p> Research completed after the 2016-2017 bleaching events has shown extensive collateral damage , eroding the very values that make the Great Barrier Reef great . <p> Seagrass meadows suffered a significant decline .
