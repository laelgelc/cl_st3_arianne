Rather than taking the ' doom and gloom ' approach to the brief , the team at TBWA wanted to show how creativity will be the key to success for the future of agencies . Working with a 3D and retouching artist at Limehouse , the agency set itself a mission to create something that provoked and demanded people 's attention . AdNews called on LIDA Australia , part of M&amp;C Saatchi Group , to not only create the cover but also present data-driven findings for the feature . Using its AI-powered bot Minnie , LIDA looked at how women have been represented in advertising in The Australian Women 's Weekly from the 1930s through to today . Reflecting those findings , the cover shows how representation of the female gender has transformed from servitude to self-actualisation . <p> AprilMental health is one of the biggest issues faced by adland today .
