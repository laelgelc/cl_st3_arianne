One opponent was so peeved after losing a boardroom battle with Hohn that he titled a book about the experience Invasion of the Locusts . That approach made Hohn 's TCI Fund Management the world 's best-performing , large hedge fund last year . <p> Now Hohn is bringing his hardball tactics to the fight against global warming . The money manager , with $US30 billion ( $44 billion ) in assets , is pushing portfolio companies to dramatically reduce greenhouse gas emissions and disclose their carbon footprint . If they do n't , he says he 'll oust their boards or dump their shares .
