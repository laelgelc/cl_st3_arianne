The scenario paints what , in typically understated fashion , scientists have called a " rocky road , " one where the world ca n't be bothered to stop climate change or adapt to it , creating a worsening cycle that could send humanity and the climate to a very dark place . <p> Scientists have modelled the future climate for more than a century .
