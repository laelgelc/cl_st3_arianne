<h> Trump administration approves Keystone pipeline on US land <h> ( MGN ) <p> BILLINGS , Mont . ( AP ) - The Trump administration has approved a right-of-way allowing the Keystone XL oil sands pipeline to be built across U.S. land . <p> Federal officials told The Associated Press that Interior Secretary David Bernhardt signed the approval Wednesday for 46 miles of the line 's route . <p> The move pushes the controversial $8 billion project closer to construction , but it still faces court challenges . <p> The 1,200-mile pipeline would transport up to 830,000 barrels of crude oil daily from western Canada to U.S. refineries . It 's become emblematic of the struggle between economic development and curbing the fossil fuel emissions that are causing climate change . <p> ( Copyright 2020 The Associated Press .
