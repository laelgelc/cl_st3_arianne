<p> Home/News from NPR/Australian Prime Minister To Launch Inquiry Into Handling Of Wildfires <h> Australian Prime Minister To Launch Inquiry Into Handling Of Wildfires <p> By Laurel Wamsley <p> January 12 , 2020 <p> Facing criticism for his handling of the ferocious wildfires that have swept across Australia , Prime Minister Scott Morrison says he will call for a government inquiry into the response to the blazes . <p> In an interview with the Australian Broadcasting Corporation , Morrison said that the " unprecedented " fire season has " created an environment where people for the first time I think , arguably , have wanted to see a more direct involvement of the federal government in responding to that natural disasters . " <p> The crisis has been blamed for at least 27 deaths . The most recent fatality occurred on Saturday , when a falling tree in eastern Victoria state killed a firefighter with 40 years of experience . He is the fourth firefighter to die in the blazes . <p> Many have pointed to climate change as a major cause of the country 's devastating fires , and have blamed successive conservative governments in Australia for inaction @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ champion of the coal industry . <p> The prime minister acknowledged the role of climate change . " We 're living in longer , hotter , drier summers . This is obviously affected by the broader changes in climate , " he said . " You 'll find that in all the government 's policy statements . " <p> He signaled the possibility of reducing Australia 's climate emissions target , saying that the government and cabinet " will continue to evolve our policies to meet our targets and to beat them . " <p> Morrison said that " there are things that I could have handled on the ground much better . " He demurred on the specifics , but seemed to be referencing his visits to fire-affected communities where people refused to shake his hand . <p> He has also been criticized for vacationing in Hawaii while Australia burned . <p> Morrison said the country will set aside about $52 million for psychological counseling for firefighters and communities traumatized by the fires .
