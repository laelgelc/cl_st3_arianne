" <p> " Recently , it seemed like everyone was talking about burping cows , and how the methane they emit is a powerful greenhouse gas , " Mason said . " Today , the message seems to be that natural grazing methods can lock up carbon in the soil -- or is it that we have to eat less meat ? As climate-conscious consumers , do we all need to go vegan , or can grass-fed beef and dairy save the world ? Is industrial beef production surprisingly green ? Or should we all eat meat @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , a scientist specializing in the links between agriculture and climate change , will explain how and why raising cattle contributes to climate change , what can be done to reduce those emissions , and why food choices matter . <p> In her most recent research , she simulated how a changing climate will alter how dairy farms affect the environment .
