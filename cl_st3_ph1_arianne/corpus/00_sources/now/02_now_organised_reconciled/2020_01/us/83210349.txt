<h> Abu Dhabi-based agency calls for clean energy investments to double <p> Abu Dhabi ( AFP ) - An international agency promoting clean energy called Sunday for investments in renewable energy projects to be more than doubled , redirecting funds from fossil fuels . <p> The Abu Dhabi-based International Renewable Energy Agency ( IRENA ) also said that electricity output from renewable sources would more than double over the next decade . <p> " The agency 's data shows that annual renewable energy investment needs to double from around $330 billion today to close to $750 billion to deploy renewable energy at the speed required , " it said in a report . <p> " Much of the needed investment can be met by redirecting planned fossil fuel investment , " IRENA said . <p> It estimated as much as $10 trillion worth of investments are planned in non-renewables-related energy projects by 2030 , risking failure to meet the goals of fighting global warming . <p> Some $2.6 trillion had been invested in renewable energy projects by end 2019 , according to the United Nations Environment Programme . <p> IRENA director general Francesco La Camera told reporters on the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ energy programmes must be accelerated . <p> If we want to deal " with the consequences of climate change , we have to accelerate our path . It means we have to go and double our investments in renewables " , he said . <p> IRENA 's report said additional investment in renewables can achieve substantial savings , including minimising losses caused by climate change as a result of inaction . <p> " Savings could amount to between $1.6 trillion and $3.7 trillion annually by 2030 , three to seven times higher than investment costs for the energy transformation , " the report said . <p> Electricity from renewable sources , mainly solar and wind , will supply 57 percent of global power by the end of the current decade , up from just 26 percent currently , IRENA said . <p> The falling costs of renewable energy has greatly enhanced its contribution . <p> Over the past 10 years , solar costs have dived by 90 percent and wind turbine prices have halved in the same period , IRENA said .
