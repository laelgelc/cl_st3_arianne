<p> MONTPELIER , Vt . ( AP ) -- Gov . Phil Scott reiterated his long-standing call Thursday to rescue Vermont , with its stagnant , aging population , from a demographic crisis that is preventing much of the state from growing while the tax base shrinks . <p> During his 40-minute speech to a joint assembly of the Vermont House and Senate , Scott said that compared with 2000 , there are today 55,000 fewer people in Vermont under the age of 45 and 44,000 more over the age of 65 . <p> In different parts of the state there is a large gap between median home prices , median household income , average wage and more , he said . <p> " My friends , this is what a demographic crisis looks like , " Scott said . " In too many places , and in the lives of too many Vermonters , I see and feel the emotional and financial toll of policies built for a few areas in the state that can afford them when the rest of the state can not .
