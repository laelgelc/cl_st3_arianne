<h> Readers respond : Preserving forests makes perfect sense <p> OSU researchers recently released a report that reaffirmed what some of us already believe : Preserving forests and wildlife habitat in the western United States is the most effective and cost-efficient way to protect against climate change . It makes perfect sense that leaving our forests alone with minimal management and low-impact recreation is the common-sense way toward reducing greenhouse gases in the atmosphere and helping to ensure a high quality of life for people , wildlife and ecosystems . The efficacy of this strategy is obvious and inexpensive compared to other management-intensive efforts . The Oregonian reported that preserving Northwest forests " would be equivalent to halting eight years of carbon dioxide emissions in the western contiguous United States , according to the study , " ( " Preserving western forests is crucial in fight against climate change , OSU researchers find , " Dec. 10 ) .
