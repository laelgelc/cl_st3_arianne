<h> Actions <h> Firefighter dies as Australia works on long-term battle plan <p> BY NICK PERRY , AP -- A firefighter has been killed by a falling tree while battling the Australian wildfire crisis and the prime minister says his government is adapting and building resilience to the fire danger posed by climate change . <p> The tragedy brings the death toll to at least 27 people in a crisis that has destroyed more than 2,000 homes and scorched an area larger than the U.S. state of Indiana since September . <p> Four of the casualties are firefighters . <p> The crisis has brought accusations that Prime Minister Scott Morrison 's conservative government needs to take more action to counter climate change . <p> Morrison says his government is developing a disaster risk reduction framework dealing with wildfires , cyclones , floods and drought . <p> Copyright 2020 Scripps Media , Inc .
