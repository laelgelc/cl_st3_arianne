<h> LETTER : No war with Iran -- Congress must act <h> Tuesday <p> Jan 21 , 2020 at 12:24 PM <p> To the editor : <p> We are outraged at the U.S. airstrike that killed Iranian General Soleimani . Granted , he is responsible for many deaths in the Middle East . But it 's against international law to assassinate other countries ' leaders . A war with Iran would be an utter disaster , and would gravely aggravate all the complicated conflicts in the region . <p> It 's great that hundreds of Cantabrigians have joined in anti-war demonstrations since Jan. 3 . But congress must also take decisive action . <p> Last month congress approved the defense budget , which included rejecting an amendment to repeal the 2001 and 2002 Authorization for the Use of Military Force acts . For 17 years , AUMF has been used by presidents to justify military actions in the Middle East , Afghanistan and other places , without consulting Congress . So AUMF must be repealed immediately . Unfortunately , last month Congresswoman Katherine Clark -- who represents part of Cambridge -- voted for the defense budget , thereby allowing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ pass whatever measures are needed to stop a new war with Iran . <p> Two crucial bills are House Resolution 5543 and Senate Resolution 3159 , which would prevent the Pentagon from spending a dime on attacking Iran or Iranian officials . In the House , the lead sponsor is Rep. Ro Khanna . We urge all Chronicle readers to contact their congresspeople and senators , and urge them to support these bills . <p> Beyond this , broader radical policy changes must be made . <p> First , sanctions against Iran must be lifted . They are quietly killing thousands of ordinary Iranians . <p> Second , the U.S. should immediately start withdrawing troops from the Middle East . <p> Third , the only solution to the complex conflicts in the region is patient and creative diplomacy . This can work -- a key example is the 2015 agreement limiting Iran 's nuclear programs . <p> Fourth , the $738 billion military budget must be greatly reduced . Because so many of our tax dollars go to wars and war preparations , cities like Cambridge are deprived of millions @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ like climate change , housing , education and poverty . <p> Finally , all countries must take urgent steps to address climate change . US involvement in the Middle East is largely driven by our government 's need to protect oil supplies coming from the region . There will be no need for Mideast oil if we make a radical shift to renewable energy and energy efficiency .
