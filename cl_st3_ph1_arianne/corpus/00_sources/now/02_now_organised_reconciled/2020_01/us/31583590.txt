<h> Last Word : confessions of a San Francisco car owner <p> Mercede Travis directs traffic in front of the West Portal Muni station in San Francisco . <p> Photo : Paul Chinn / The Chronicle <p> Confessions of a San Francisco car owner <p> I do n't wear a fur coat or own a handgun , but I drive a car in San Francisco . That last part makes me a flawed citizen of the city . Day by day , it 's harder to pull out on the street without wondering what pavement bump , bike lane or curb widening has sprouted overnight to remind me of my place . <p> The reasons for this discomfort all make sense . There are more people than ever -- 800,000-plus -- living in a very compact place . The bus system needs to speed up . Bikes , e-scooters , skateboards and those inane single-wheel things all have their place alongside my car window . <p> But cars have a claim , too . Notice the rainy weather ? How about carting a family around or taking a supermarket trip .
