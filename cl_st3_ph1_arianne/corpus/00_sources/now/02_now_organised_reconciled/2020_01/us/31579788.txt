<p> Some 160 readers took me up on a request in last week 's column to share ideas for the kind of coverage they 'd like to see from MLive 's journalists across eight local markets , as well as statewide . Some were broad , a thirst for more coverage on a certain topic . <p> Some readers , like a high school student named Ethan , wrote with specific story tips : " With the recent legalization of marijuana in Michigan , what will happen to those currently serving time in prison for possession of marijuana prior to the passing of the law ? " <p> Great question , Ethan , and worth looking into ! <p> The response was so strong that I was unable to respond individually to each submission .
