Despite the tunnel-vision of the legislature 's leaders , it 's worth taking a broader look at what North Carolina should do in 2020 . <p> No. 1 : Expand Medicaid . When the history of this era in state politics is written , the Republican lawmakers ' refusal to take the federal government 's offer to pay 90 percent of the cost of expanding Medicaid may be remembered as their cruelest and most financially reckless act .
