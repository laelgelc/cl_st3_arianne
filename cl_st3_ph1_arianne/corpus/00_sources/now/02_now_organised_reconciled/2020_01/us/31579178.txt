<p> BROOKLINE -- Seeking to reduce a major source of carbon emissions , Brookline residents have voted to ban the installation of oil and gas pipes in new buildings as well as in extensive renovations of existing buildings -- the first such prohibition in Massachusetts . <p> The controversial bylaw would require homeowners and developers to install heat , hot water , and appliances that use electricity . Only three of more than 200 Town Meeting members voted against the measure Wednesday . <p> " We need to do something about climate change , " said Werner Lohe , one of the measure 's sponsors and cochair of the town 's climate action committee . " We need to stop burning fossil fuels inside our buildings . . . . This is the first step in Brookline toward an all-electric , all-renewable-energy world .
