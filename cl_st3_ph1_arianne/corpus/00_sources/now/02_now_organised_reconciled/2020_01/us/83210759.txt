<p> We have had the good fortune to work with an incredible person in Wellesley named Phyllis Theermann . Phyllis co-founded Wellesley Green Schools 10 years ago with a mission to " inspire students to create a healthy , sustainable world " . Since then , both through her efforts with Wellesley Green Schools and Sustainable Wellesley , Phyllis has worked tirelessly on many endeavors to create a healthy and more sustainable town and world . She has organized initiatives to encourage the reduction of disposable water bottles and single use plastics , encourage waste reduction , encourage energy use reduction , promote recycling , increase idling awareness , work to reduce gas leaks , promote clean energy , solar and beneficial electrification , implement food composting at schools , and so much more . She has organized Green Teams at Wellesley schools and her leadership has proven very effective in accomplishing many sustainable goals . She organizes an annual Green Summit where she gets different groups from around town to exchange ideas and collaborate on ways to make Wellesley greener . Phyllis was instrumental in helping Wellesley Public Schools win a national Green Ribbon award .
