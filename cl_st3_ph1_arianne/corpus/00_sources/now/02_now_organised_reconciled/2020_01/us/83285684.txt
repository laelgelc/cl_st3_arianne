<p> Qumsiyeh , an expert in biodiversity and climate change , will give the Shafer Lecture of Promoting Enduring Peace . He is director of the Palestinian Museum of Natural History and Institute for Biodiversity and Sustainability in Bethlehem on the occupied West Bank , which he founded in 2017 . He is former director of the Cytogenetics Department at Yale University . <p> Author of several books , including " Sharing the Land of Canaan " and " Popular Resistance in Palestine , " Qumsiyeh has spoken widely about Palestinian rights , biodiversity and climate change <p> Yousef Aljamal , a Palestinian writer from Gaza , also will speak . He is co-author of " Gaza Writes Back : Short Stories of Young Palestinian Writers . " <p> The Palestine Museum US is located at 1764 Litchfield Turnpike . Admission for the lecture will be at a reduced price of $4.
