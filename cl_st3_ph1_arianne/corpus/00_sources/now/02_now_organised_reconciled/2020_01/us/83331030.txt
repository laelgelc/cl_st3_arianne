<p> BEIJING , Jan 20 ( Reuters ) - China will release 20,000 tonnes of frozen pork from its state reserves on Jan. 21 , according to a notice on the website for the China Merchandise Reserve Management Center on Monday . <p> The notice comes just ahead of the week-long Lunar New Year holiday , kicking off on Friday , when pork consumption typically spikes . <p> Beijing has released more than 200,000 tonnes of frozen pork from state reserves since early December to make up for a hit to market supply after African swine fever knocked out 21% of pork production in the world 's top consumer last year .
