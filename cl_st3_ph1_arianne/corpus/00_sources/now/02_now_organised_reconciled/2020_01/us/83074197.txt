<p> DUBAI , Jan 5 ( Reuters ) - Iran will decide on Sunday about its next step to further roll back its commitments to a 2015 nuclear deal with major powers , Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesman was quoted as saying , days after the U.S. killing of top Iranian military commander Qassem Soleimani . <p> President Donald Trump pulled the United States out of the 2015 agreement between Tehran and six major powers in 2018 and reimpoised sanctions on Iran that have crippled its economy . Hostilities between the countries escalated sharply after Friday 's U.S. drone attack in Baghdad that killed Soleimani . <p> " Tonight , there will be a very important meeting to decide about our next nuclear step and the implementation of the deal ... considering the recent threats ( by America ) it should be underlined that in politics , all developments and threats are linked to each other , " spokesman Abbas Mousavi was quoted as saying by state news agency IRNA . <p> In reaction to the U.S. policy of " maximum pressure " since Trump pulled out of the nuclear agreement , Iran has gradually distanced itself from the deal @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in exchange for a lifting of many international sanctions . <p> Iran has warned that it will further decrease its commitments if European parties to the agreement fail to shield Tehran 's economy from U.S. penalties . <p> In November , Iran gave Britain , France and Germany a third 60-day deadline to salvage the deal or face a further decrease of Iran 's commitments to nuclear pact .
