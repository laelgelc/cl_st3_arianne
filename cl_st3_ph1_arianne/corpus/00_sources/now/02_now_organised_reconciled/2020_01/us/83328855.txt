<h> Boat protesters in Venice take on damage from big ships <p> 2 Min Read <p> Scores of boats take to the Saint Mark 's Basin , as Venetians protest against the damage caused by big ships , in Venice , Italy January 19 , 2020 . REUTERS/Manuel Silvestri <p> VENICE , Italy ( Reuters ) - Hundreds of people in small rowing boats protested in front of Venice 's St Mark 's Square on Sunday to denounce what they say is a proliferation of motor boats that have crowded the waters of the lagoon city . <p> The protesters also took aim at the giant cruise ships that regularly sail close to Venice , a Unesco World Heritage site that has come under increasing strain from rising waters . <p> Critics say waves created by cruise ships and other large , motorized boats on the canal are eroding the foundations of the city , which regularly suffers from flooding . <p> The protesters arrived on a flotilla of small , traditional boats , where the rowers stand as they plow through the water . <p> " Stop the waves , stop the smog , " said one banner @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ waves , " read another in Venetian dialect . " Look out for the waves , they are drowning us , " said a third . <p> Beloved around the world for its canals , historic architecture and art , Venice suffered a record string of floods in November , which left much of the city underwater and threatened the famed Saint Mark 's Basilica . <p> The high tides were blamed on climate change , but protesters say local authorities could be doing much more to protect the fragile city by banning big boats . <p> A decision was taken in 2017 to shoo away the cruise liners but it has yet to be enacted as alternative mooring sites are being developed .
