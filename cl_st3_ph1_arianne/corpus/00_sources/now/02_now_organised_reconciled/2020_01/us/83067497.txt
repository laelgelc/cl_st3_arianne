<p> PARIS , Jan 4 ( Reuters ) - French Foreign Minister Jean-Yves Le Drian said he had discussed the situation in the Middle East on Saturday with his German and Chinese counterparts , and added that all three had agreed on the need to avoid any escalation in tensions . <p> Le Drian said he had held a telephone conversation on Saturday with German foreign minister Heiko Maas and senior Chinese diplomat Wang Yi . <p> " We all noted in particular our agreement in the importance of preserving the stability and sovereignty of Iraq , and the whole of the region in general , as well as the need for Iran to avoid any new violation of the Vienna Agreement , " added Le Drian .
