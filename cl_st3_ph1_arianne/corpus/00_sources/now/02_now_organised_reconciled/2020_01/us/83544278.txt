<p> ROME , Jan 30 ( Reuters ) - Italian Prime Minister Giuseppe Conte on Thursday said there were two confirmed cases of coronavirus in Italy , the first two in the country since the emergency exploded . <p> Conte said Italy had closed air traffic to and from China . <p> " We have two confirmed cases of coronavirus in Italy , " the prime minister told a news conference , adding two Chinese tourist from China had contracted the virus . <p> On Thursday , the World Health Organization declared that the coronavirus epidemic in China now constitutes a global public health emergency .
