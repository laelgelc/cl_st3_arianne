<p> WASHINGTON , Jan 7 ( Reuters ) - Republicans in the U.S. Senate have enough votes to proceed on rules for the impeachment trial of President Donald Trump that would delay a decision on whether to allow witness testimony , Senate Majority Leader Mitch McConnell said on Tuesday . <p> McConnell , speaking to reporters at a news conference , said the chamber first needed to receive the articles of impeachment from the House of Representatives , and he hoped House Democrats ' withholding of articles would end this week .
