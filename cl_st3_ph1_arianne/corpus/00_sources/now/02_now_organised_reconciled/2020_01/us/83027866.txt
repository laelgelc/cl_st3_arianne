<h> When Central Banks Go Green <p> On a challenge as large as climate change , there are financial risks associated with making the shift to a low-carbon economy , and there are risks tied to inaction . Central banks thus have no choice but to focus more closely on the issue , with or without an expansion of their traditional policy mandates . <p> NEW YORK -- Central banks confronted with the issue of climate change face a number of questions . Should monetary policymakers ( and other financial regulators and supervisors ) focus on the implications of climate change for financial stability ? Should they treat climate change as a potential threat to their ability to pursue their macroeconomic mandates of full employment and/or price stability ?
