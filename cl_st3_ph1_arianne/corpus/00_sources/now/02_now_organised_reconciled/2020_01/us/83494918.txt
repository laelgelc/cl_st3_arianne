<p> BEIJING , Jan 29 ( Reuters ) - A drugstore in the Chinese capital will be fined 3 million yuan ( $434,530 ) for hiking the price of masks by almost six times the online rate amid a spreading virus outbreak , the Beijing municipal market regulator said on Wednesday . <p> The coronavirus outbreak , which started in the central city of Wuhan late last year , has killed 132 people , with nearly 6,000 infected in China . <p> An administrative penalty notice has been issued to the Beijing Jimin Kangtai Pharmacy for sharply raising the price of N95 masks , the regulator said in a statement on its website . <p> The store raised the price of a box of 3M brand masks to 850 yuan while the online price was just 143 yuan , state television said . <p> Since Thursday , the regulator has investigated 31 price violation cases , it said , as it boosted supervision of prices of protective gear and punished illegal activities , such as hoarding or fabricating information about price hikes . <p> In the commercial capital of Shanghai , the municipal market regulator has ordered the closure @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ city government said . <p> The regulator has asked the store to refund buyers and dispose of unsold ones , it said on its official account on the Weibo app. ( $1=6.9040 yuan ) ( Reporting by Judy Hua , Leng Cheng and Tony Munroe ; Editing by Michael Perry and Clarence Fernandez ) <p> Our global editorial team of more than 50 journalists and about 250 freelancers covers the world 's under-reported stories at the heart of aid , development , women 's and LGBT+ rights , human trafficking , property rights , and climate change .
