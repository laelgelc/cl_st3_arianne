<p> WASHINGTON , Jan 3 ( Reuters ) - The United States is sending nearly 3,000 additional troops to the Middle East from the 82nd Airborne Division as a precaution amid rising threats to American forces in the region , U.S. officials said on Friday . <p> The officials , speaking on condition of anonymity , said the troops would be joining the roughly 750 forces that were sent to Kuwait earlier this week . <p> U.S. officials told Reuters earlier this week that thousands of additional troops could be sent to the region and had been told to prepare to deploy .
