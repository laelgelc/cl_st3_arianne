<p> JERUSALEM , Jan 6 ( Reuters ) - The United States warned its citizens in Israel and the Palestinian territories on Monday to be vigilant , citing the risk of rocket fire , three days after a U.S. drone strike killed an Iranian military commander in Iraq . <p> The alert issued on the websites of the U.S. State Department and the U.S. embassy in Israel did not specifically mention the killing of Qassem Soleimani but referred to heightened tensions in the Middle East . <p> Israel has not issued a similar warning to its own citizens , though Army Radio reported the military had been put on alert . <p> Israel 's security cabinet , led by Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu , met on Monday for the first time since Soleimani was killed . <p> Rare leaks from inside the forum to Israeli media quoted unidentified Israeli intelligence officials as saying the probability of an imminent Iranian attack on Israel was low . <p> The U.S. message said it " strongly encourages U.S. citizens to remain vigilant and take appropriate steps to increase their security awareness , as security incidents , including rocket fire , often take @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " red alert " siren system may be activated in the event of " mortar or rocket fire " .
