On Wednesday , Colorado Senator Michael Bennet echoed Delaney 's remarks , pointing to the plan California Senator Kamala Harris published on Monday . " We need to be honest about what 's in the plan , " Bennet told Harris . " It bans employer-based insurance and taxes the middle class to the tune of $30 trillion .
