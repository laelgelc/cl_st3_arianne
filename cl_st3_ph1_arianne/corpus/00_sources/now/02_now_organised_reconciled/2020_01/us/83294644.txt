<p> DUBAI , Jan 17 ( Reuters ) - Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei said in a Friday sermon that Iran 's missile strikes on U.S. targets in Iraq this month delivered a " slap on the face " to the United States , showing the Islamic Republic had divine support . <p> During a spike in tension , Iran launched missiles at U.S. targets on Jan. 8 in response to a U.S. drone strike on Jan. 3 that killed Qassem Soleimeni , a powerful Iranian general who was close to Khamenei . <p> " The fact that Iran has the power to give such a slap to a world power shows the hand of God , " Khamenei said , after referring to the launch of missiles against U.S. targets as a " day of God " .
