That honor usually goes to concrete and steel -- steel embedded in concrete provides support and a foundation for tall buildings , while concrete increases tensile strength and can be formed into a variety of shapes with the help of rebar . Wood , on the other hand , decays and is vulnerable to moisture damage and fire . <p> That 's not necessarily the case anymore , thanks to the development of advanced timber .
