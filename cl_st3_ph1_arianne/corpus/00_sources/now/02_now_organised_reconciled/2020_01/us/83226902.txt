Though we do n't keep count , we do strive for balance -- ideologically and geographically -- in our fact checks . <h> How do we report a fact check ? <p> In almost all cases , our fact checks are reported by reporters who are subject matter experts . The reporting could include : <p> Asking the person who made the claim for evidence to back up the assertion , then researching that material . <p> Interviewing topic experts , including those with varying perspectives . <p> Reviewing primary source documents related to the claim . <p> Seeking official , nonpartisan sources such as city halls , statehouses and Congress for bills , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for corporate information , and agencies such as the IRS and FEC for relevant data . <p> All sources used will be hyperlinked and/or listed at the bottom of every fact check . <h> How do we edit our fact checks ? <p> First , before a fact check is undertaken , a reporter reviews the claim with an editor and discusses the approach . <p> Once the story is written , an editor carefully reviews the fact check for tone and to ensure the sourcing is transparent , relevant and trustworthy . The editor and reporter work together to confirm that every statement in the story is accurate and the wording used is fair . They then choose a rating . <p> After that , another fact check editor reviews their work . If there are any concerns , they will enlist the guidance of a senior editor . <p> A copy editor will read the story before it is published . <h> What are our ratings ? <p> We 've tried to keep our story format and ratings system as straightforward as possible . <p> You 'll generally @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ A short explanation of what we 're checking . <p> A section describing the context , where we 'll explain the circumstances around the topic as well as what we 've discovered in our research . <p> A summary of our findings . <p> Those findings will explain how and why we arrived at one of these ratings : <p> True -- the content in the item we fact-checked was supported by our research . <p> Partly False -- some of the content in the item was not supported by our research . <p> False headline -- the headline was false or misleading , though the content in the item was supported by research . <p> False -- the content in the item was not supported by our research . <h> What if we need to correct something ? <p> We recognize that mistakes happen -- or that new information can emerge after a story is published -- and we pledge to address all concerns quickly , fairly and transparently . <p> If a correction , clarification or update to a fact check is warranted , we will note that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ was made .
