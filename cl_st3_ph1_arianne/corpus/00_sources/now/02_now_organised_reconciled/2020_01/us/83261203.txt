<p> ATHENS , Jan 15 ( Reuters ) - Greece said on Wednesday that migration policy will be implemented faster from now on by a new ministry as the country struggles with a resurgence of migrant and refugee arrivals . <p> Until now , migration issues were managed by a department of the country 's Civil Protection Ministry . <p> The new conservative government , elected in July , announced plans to shut overcrowded refugee camps on Greek islands and replace them with more restrictive holding centres , mostly on the mainland . <p> It has promised to deploy more border guards to " shut the door " to migrants not entitled to stay . <p> " What is needed is a faster implementation of this plan , " government spokesman Stelios Petsas told state TV ERT . <p> The government appointed deputy Labour Social Affairs Minister Notis Mitarachi as head of the new Ministry of Migration and Asylum .
