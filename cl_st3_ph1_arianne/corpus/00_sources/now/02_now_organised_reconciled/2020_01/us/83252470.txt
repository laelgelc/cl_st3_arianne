Last week , the CCA announced it would serve a fully plant-based menu to its celebrity guests which included vegan burritos from Baja Fresh and Impossible Burgers from The Counter . " First , I 'd like to thank the awards for going plant-based and trying to offset our carbon footprint . It 's a really amazing message , " Phoenix said before thanking his mother for his success .
