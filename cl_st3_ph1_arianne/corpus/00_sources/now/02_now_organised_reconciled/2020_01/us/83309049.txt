<h> Actions <h> Scientists seek rare species survivors amid Australia flames <p> BY CHRISTINA LARSON AND MATTHEW BROWN , AP -- Much of Australia is still ablaze in an unprecedented wildfire season that has already charred an area the size of Kentucky . <p> Scientists fear that some of Australia 's unique and colorful endangered species may not recover . <p> Now they are looking for surviving representatives of rare species including the kangaroo-like brush-tailed rock wallabies , and helping threatened creatures get enough food and water in recently scorched forests . <p> Koalas are not now in imminent danger of extinction , but scientists worry that the iconic marsupial 's habitat has been greatly reduced by wildfires . <p> Scientists say climate change is making massive wildfires more common . <p> Copyright 2020 Scripps Media , Inc .
