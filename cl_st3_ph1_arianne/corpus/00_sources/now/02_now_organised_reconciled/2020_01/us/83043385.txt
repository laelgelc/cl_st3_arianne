<p> LONDON , Jan 3 ( Reuters ) - The British government said on Friday it will implement an action plan to help prevent future power disruptions after an outage in August last year that affected more than 1 million customers . <p> A government-backed report was commissioned into the outage and has recommended 10 actions to reduce the likelihood of a similar event happening again . <p> These include assessing the need for improvements to the governance , monitoring and enforcement processes for large and smaller generators ; and reviewing the pros and cons of the National Grid Electricity System Operator ( ESO ) holding additional back-up generation . <p> Business and Energy Secretary Andrea Leadsom confirmed that the action plan would be implemented in full .
