Ecologists have watched as coastal forests steadily declined , unable to keep pace . A June study published in the journal Nature Climate found the Gulf Coast has lost about 57 square miles of forest over the last 120 years . With just over three feet of sea rise , the report found between 4,600 and 19,000 square miles of dry land up and down the U.S. coast will become tidal zones filled with wetlands . <p> But the story in the Keys is different . The underground freshwater lenses that once fed the pineland are shrinking .
