Building on the organization 's mission to create a generation of conservationists , the Earth Rangers App empowers kids and their families in the face of growing anxiety over the state of the planet . <p> " We hear more about large-scale environmental challenges now than we ever have before , from climate change to plastics in our oceans to species extinction . " <p> The app houses over two dozen fun and impactful missions that take the app experience offline by engaging kids in activities like shoreline clean-ups , battery recycling drives , conserving energy and creating butterfly gardens . The app also offers localized content , with missions that are customized by province orregion so that members can learn about environmental issues relevant to where they live . Local events , such as tree planting and other on-the ground opportunities will be promoted to members in different parts of the country . <p> " Research shows that eco-anxiety is a real thing .
