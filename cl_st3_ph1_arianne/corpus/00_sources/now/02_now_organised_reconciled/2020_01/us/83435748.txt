<p> The Anchorage Museum invites creative practitioners , changemakers , and those interested in positive change and climate change to gather at the Critical Futures Creative Conference to share , imagine , envision , invent , and create possible futures on April 13-17 , 2020 . This five-day conference is part of North x North , the Anchorage Museum 's celebration of connection , creativity , imagination , and innovation across the Circumpolar North , convening people worldwide for a discussion about potential futures . <p> The Critical Futures Creative Conference looks at three actions : <p> Prototype . Creating examples of future solutions . <p> Respond . Responding through language , visuals , voices , and action . <p> Connect . Making connections between people , places , and landscapes as a way to understand , build awareness , and respond to climate and global change . <p> Working together , participants will develop shared platforms for collaboration and ideas . The schedule includes excursions , hands-on workshops , discussions , films , conversations , and live music .
