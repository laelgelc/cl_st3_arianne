<h> EU 's Barnier says a year not enough to agree full deal with UK <p> The European Commission Brexit chief negotiator Michel Barnier speaks during the Web Summit in Lisbon <p> STOCKHOLM ( Reuters ) - A comprehensive agreement on the future relationship between the European Union and Britain will take longer than the 11-month transition period that begins when the UK leaves the EU on Jan. 31 , the EU 's chief negotiator said on Thursday . <p> After Brexit , the two sides will have until the end of the year to negotiate a new trade relationship -- a short period given the complexity of the discussions . <p> The negotiations are likely to be tough , especially as the EU says that access to the single market will be proportional to how closely Britain sticks to EU rules . <p> Prime Minister Boris Johnson has said Britain will not extend the transition period beyond December . <p> " We are ready to do our best and to do the maximum in the 11 months to secure a basic agreement with the UK , but we will need more time to agree on each and every @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Brexit negotiator Michel Barnier said in a speech in Stockholm . <p> He said the extremely tight timetable meant the EU would have to prioritise . <p> First will be building a new capacity to work together with Britain both bilaterally and in global institutions to address issues such as climate change and peace in the Middle East . <p> " The second point is that we need to build a very close security relationship , " Barnier said , although he added that cooperation would necessarily be less than currently . <p> Finally , he said the EU would insist that a future economic partnership is based on a level playing field in regulations , tax and other matters . <p> " Competing on social and environmental standards can only lead to a race to the bottom that puts workers , consumers and the planet on the losing side , " Barnier said . <p> Access to the EU 's vast single market would depend on how tightly the UK aligns itself with the bloc , including in matters such as state aid . <p> " If the UK @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ zero tariffs , zero quota , we need to be careful about what we call zero " <p> Johnson has said London is not seeking a relationship based on alignment with existing rules . <p> In the longer term , Barnier held out the possibility of an unprecedentedly close relationship with Britain . <p> " We will strive for a partnership that goes well beyond trade ... covering everything from services and fisheries to climate action energy transport , space , security and defence , " he said . " But that is a very huge agenda and we simply can not expect to agree on every single aspect of this new partnership in under one year .
