<p> TOKYO , Jan 28 ( Reuters ) - A person in Japan who had not visited the Chinese city of Wuhan has contracted the new coronavirus , Japan 's health ministry said Tuesday . <p> The infected man is a tour bus driver in his 60s in the city of Nara , who had contact with visitors from Wuhan between Jan. 8-16 , the ministry said on its website . He began showing symptoms on Jan. 14 and was hospitalized on Jan. 25 before being diagnosed , according to the statement . <p> Another man in his 40s who lives in Wuhan was also confirmed to have contracted the virus , the ministry said . <p> The new infections bring the total number of confirmed Japanese cases to six .
