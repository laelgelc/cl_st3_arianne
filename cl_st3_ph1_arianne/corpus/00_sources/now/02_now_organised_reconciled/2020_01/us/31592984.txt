<h> Coffee , cork and car seat clothes all the rage at Berlin Fashion Week <p> The sustainable fashion trade fair Neonyt has opened on the sidelines of Berlin Fashion Week . <p> The event has moved to the abandoned Tempelhof Airport , where jackets made from ocean plastics , shoes made out of recycled materials and t-shirts made with coffee grounds are some of the products on display . <p> A sensational fashion show draws the attention to multi-label looks put together from over 80 sustainable designer collections .
