<p> KIEV , Jan 11 ( Reuters ) - Ukrainian Prime Minister Oleksiy Honcharuk said on Saturday that Ukraine would pay 200,000 hryvnia ( $8,350 ) each to the families of those who died in a plane crash in Iran this week . <p> He said Ukraine International Airlines would make payments to the families of the crew members who died in the crash . The airline 's spokeswoman was not immediately available for comment . Honcharuk also said Ukrainian diplomats were working on how to receive compensation from the Iranian authorities . <p> Iran said earlier its military had shot down the Ukrainian plane , killing all 176 aboard in a " disastrous mistake " .
