On Dec. 13 , the California Air Resources Board ( CARB ) selected the eastern Coachella Valley -- which includes the cities of Indio and Coachella , and the unincorporated communities of Thermal , Oasis , Mecca , and North Shore -- as an AB 617 community . This came at the recommendation of South Coast Air Quality management District , but only after more than two years of advocacy by ECV residents . <p> The Community Air Protection Program , otherwise known as AB 617 , will help the community develop an air monitoring plan and an emission reduction plan .
