<h> Portland Commissioner Nick Fish dies of cancer <p> Nick Fish , the longest tenured member of the current Portland City Council , died Thursday from cancer . He was 61 . <p> Fish died at home surrounded by his loved ones , according to Sonia Schmanski , his chief of staff . He announced on Tuesday that he planned to resign in 2020 to continue to focus on his health while battling abdominal cancer , which he was diagnosed with in 2017 . <p> " The family wanted me to convey publicly their thanks for all the words of love and encouragement sent to Nick since his resignation , " Schmanski said . " Nick called his 11 years of service on the Portland City Council ' the great honor of my life . ' " <p> He is survived by his wife , Patricia Schechter , and their two children , Maria and Chapin . <p> A beloved figure in and out of City Hall known for his steady hand and self deprecating sense of humor , Fish was a strong advocate for affordable housing , environmental protections and services to aid the houseless . <p> A @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in politics since the 1800s .
