<p> MANILA , Jan 28 ( Reuters ) - The Philippines stopped issuing visas on arrival to Chinese nationals on Tuesday , in a bid to keep the southeast Asian nation free of a new virus that has killed 106 and infected more than 4,500 since emerging in China in December . <p> There have been no confirmed cases in the Philippines since the coronavirus outbreak began in China 's central city of Wuhan , but infections have been confirmed in Japan , Nepal , Singapore , South Korea , Taiwan , Thailand , the United States and Vietnam . <p> " We are taking this proactive measure to slow down travel , and possibly help prevent the entry of the 2019-nCov , " Jaime Morente , the commissioner of the Philippines ' Bureau of Immigration , said in a statement , referring to the virus . <p> There is no order barring Chinese nationals from entering the Philippines , however , Morente added . <p> He did not say when the facility would be resumed . Chinese nationals can still apply for visas at any Philippine embassy or consulate at their places of residence . <p> Health authorities @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ coronavirus , they said on Tuesday . <p> The " visa upon arrival " facility has been offered to Chinese nationals since 2017 , in an effort to boost tourism . Those eligible included investors and businessmen , athletes , delegates to international conventions and tour groups . <p> Chinese tourists accounted for 22% of the 7.5 million visitors to the Philippines between January and November last year , making China the second top nation of origin for international travellers to the country .
