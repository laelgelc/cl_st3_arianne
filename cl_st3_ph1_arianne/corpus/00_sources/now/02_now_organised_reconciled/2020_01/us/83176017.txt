<h> City of Duluth Seeks Million in Bonding Money for Coastal Repair <h> City officials say the bonding money will be used to put focus on community gems such as the Lakewalk , Baywalk , and Brighton Beach . <p> DULUTH , Minn. -- The city of Duluth announced a proposal seeking $13.5 million in state bonding money this upcoming legislative session for coastal repair . <p> City officials say the bonding money will be used to put focus on community gems such as the Lakewalk , Baywalk , and Brighton Beach . <p> The money will contribute to an ongoing $62 million project aimed at reconstructing the storm-damaged coastal infrastructure including behind the DECC and make the green space there more usable . <p> It also would move the road at Brighton Beach away from the lake to prevent it from getting thrashed by waves . <p> Officials say the plan is to protect Duluth 's landmarks . <p> " In light of climate change we need to renew them to a more resilient or durable standard that can endure these kinds of storms for the next 50 years , " said Jim Filby Williams , the director @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Duluth . <p> This coming summer , city , state , and federal funds are already in place to fix the Lakewalk behind the hotels in Canal Park . <p> The city will not know , however , if it has secured $13.5 million for the next year until the end of the state legislative session in May .
