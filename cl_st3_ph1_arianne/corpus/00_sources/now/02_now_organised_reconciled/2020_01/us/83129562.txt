<p> MOGADISHU , Jan 8 ( Reuters ) - A bomb exploded at a busy junction in Mogadishu on Wednesday , wounding at least 11 people , the head of the rescue service in the Somali capital said . <p> There was no immediate claim of responsibility for the blast , which happened near buildings housing government offices . <p> Abdikadir Abdirahman , head of Aamin ambulance services , told Reuters that three of those wounded were women . <p> There was no immediate comment from the police . <p> At least 90 people were killed in Mogadishu last week in a bomb attack by Islamist militant group al Shabaab , the deadliest in the country in more than two years .
