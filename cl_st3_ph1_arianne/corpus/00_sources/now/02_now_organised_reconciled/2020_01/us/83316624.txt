<h> HB 1100 : Subsidizing an unlivable future PennLive letters <p> Scientists have made it abundantly clear that we need to limit our emissions and energy use in order to have a livable future . As a resident of Pennsylvania , I am particularly concerned about House Bill 1100 , which passed the State House last fall and would provide an $800 million subsidy for fracking and petrochemical related infrastructure . <p> This allows yet another way for fossil fuel infrastructure to be locked-in to the Pennsylvania economy at a time when we need to be transitioning to clean , renewable energy sources . We are already seeing the impacts of climate change in Pennsylvania , from an increasing number of bad air days to extreme weather events , which negatively impact public health and the ecosystems of PA 's beautiful natural environment .
