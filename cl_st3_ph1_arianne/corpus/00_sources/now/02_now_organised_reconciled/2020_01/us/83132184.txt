( Michael McAndrews / Hartford Courant ) <p> The Yale School of Public Health has launched a center for climate change and health to study the effects of climate change on populations around the globe and work toward climate stability . <p> The climate change center grew out of the Yale Climate Change and Health Initiative , which was formed in 2015 to address the effects of climate change on populations around the globe , according to Yale . The center was approved in December 2019 , with the High Tide Foundation as its main funder . <p> Advertisement <p> " I am excited to see what important contributions our new research center will make to our understanding of climate change and how we might mitigate human suffering caused by weather , " Dr. Sten H. The center will also expand a partnership with the Yale School of Forestry and Environmental Sciences in which students carry out applied research and public health practice projects .
