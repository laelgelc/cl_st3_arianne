<p> MADRID , Jan 27 ( Reuters ) - The Spanish government is working with China and the European Union to repatriate Spanish nationals from Wuhan , China , over coronavirus concerns , Spain 's Foreign Minister Arancha Gonzalez Laya said on Monday . <p> " We 're working ... with our consulate in Beijing , and officials in China and the European Union to repatriate around 20 Spaniards in Wuhan , Hubei , the epicentre of the coronavirus . We will continue to update on any advances , " she wrote on Twitter .
