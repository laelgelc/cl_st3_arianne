<p> Mark Charles Poloncarz left no doubt about his political philosophy or his future course Tuesday after taking the oath of office for a third term as Erie County executive . <p> His administration will be progressive , it will welcome immigrants , it will confront climate change , and it will invest " in our neighborhoods , our communities , our people , through our government . " <p> In a ringing re-commitment to not only his Democratic Party but the progressive principles he increasingly embraces , Poloncarz pledged that government will play a key role in continuing the progress that he says stemmed from his first two stints as leader of upstate New York 's largest county . <p> " I believe that government , when run effectively , can be a force of good , " he said . " It must lead when others will not . We have proven that a government built on the principles of compassion , inclusion , service to others , and fiscal stability can succeed .
