<h> Share <h> Consortium To Develop Road Map For Hybrid-Electric Airliners <h> Share <p> A European consortium of industry and academia will develop a road map for furthering hybrid-electric propulsion for commercial aircraft under a project funded by the European Commission ( EC ) . <p> Led by French aerospace research agency Onera , the IMOTHEP project ( for Investigation and Maturation of Technologies for Hybrid-Electric Propulsion ) has received ? 10.4 million ( $11.5 million ) in funding under the EC 's Horizon 2020 research program . <p> IMOTHEP will perform a detailed investigation of technologies for aircraft with unconventional configurations and innovate propulsion architectures that provide synergistic benefits through the close integration of propulsion and airframe . <p> " Analyzing potential technologies and technical issues at the relevant scale is fundamental for hybrid-electric propulsion and addressing the challenge of climate change requires exploring the technology for commercial aircraft that represent the bulk of current emissions , " Onera said . <p> The project involves airframers Airbus and Leonardo , engine manufacturers Safran , GE Avio , MTU , ITP and GKN , as well as research organizations and universities in Belgium , France , Germany , Ireland @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Scotland , Sweden and the UK . <p> IMOTHEP will collaborate with five organizations in Russia , including engine research institute CIAM and aerohydrodynamic institute TsAGI , as well as the National Research Council of Canada . <p> European industry and academia are already studying unconventional configurations for ultra-efficient airliners . Under the Clean Sky 2 civil aeronautics research program , teams lead by Onera , German aerospace center DLR , Netherlands aerospace center NLR and Rolls-Royce are studying " novel and radical " designs for an Airbus A320-size aircraft that could enter service around 2035 . <p> Onera , for example , has developed a research configuration , called Dragon , for an aircraft with distributed turbo-electric propulsion . This has two turboshaft engines mounted on the aft fuselage driving four generators that power 40 electric ducted fans arrayed under the wing trailing edge . <p> The research agency 's analysis suggests that , with moderate technology assumptions , the Dragon configuration could reduce fuel burn by 7% compared with an equivalent 2035-technology conventional turbofan-powered aircraft .
