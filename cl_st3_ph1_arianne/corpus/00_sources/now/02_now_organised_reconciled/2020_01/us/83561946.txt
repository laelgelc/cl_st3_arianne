<h> Weatherwatch : how repeated flooding can shift levees <p> A new study from North Carolina University suggests that repeated flooding has a cumulative impact on flood defences , damaging them in ways that may not be picked up by current inspection techniques . <p> Earth embankments , or levees , are widely used for flood protection in the US , and are typically more than fifty years old . The study , in Engineering Geology , looked at the local Princetown levee and the effect of recent hurricanes Matthew and Floyd . <p> Repeated storm cycles of high and low water meant that the levee experienced a huge difference in forces from the weight of the water . These forces shift the levee fractionally each time , and the rate of shifting increases with the number of storm cycles . The cumulative damage may not be noticed by visual inspections , but is still significant .
