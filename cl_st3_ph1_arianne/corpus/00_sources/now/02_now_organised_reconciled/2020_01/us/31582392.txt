<h> You did Veganuary for the environment , but does your food fly further ? <p> Those taking part in Veganuary are more than half way through now . Supermarkets are now able to offer a host of options from dairy-free alternatives to cheese , milk and ice cream in addition to meat alternatives ranging from Quorn or soy mince and chicken style pieces to ' next generation ' meat imitating burgers and steaks . <p> The more adventurous might attempt to create their meals from scratch . Perhaps substituting mince for lentils , creating alternatives to cream from cashews or building burgers from chickpeas . <p> But where do the ingredients for these new options come from ?
