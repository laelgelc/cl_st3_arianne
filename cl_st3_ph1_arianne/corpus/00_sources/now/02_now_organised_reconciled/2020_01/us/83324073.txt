<h> Actions <h> Thousands gather for Women 's March rallies across the US <p> Posted : 8:16 PM , Jan 18 , 2020 <p> Updated:2020-01-18 20:16:15-05 <p> By : AP Wire <p> WASHINGTON -- Thousands gathered in cities across the country Saturday as part of the nationwide Women 's March rallies focused on issues such as climate change , pay equity , reproductive rights and immigration . <p> The fourth annual marches were aimed to harness the political power of women , although crowds were noticeably smaller than in previous years . <p> The first marches in 2017 drew hundreds of thousands of people to rallies on the day after President Trump was inaugurated . <p> The protesters planned to march around the White House on Saturday , but Trump was n't there .
