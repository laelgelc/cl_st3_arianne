<h> EU : the more UK distance from bloc rules , the less market access <p> BRUSSELS , Jan 31 ( Reuters ) - The more Britain diverges from EU rules and regulations after Brexit , the less access to the bloc 's single market it will have , European Council President Charles Michel said on Friday . <p> " The more the UK will diverge from the EU standards , the less the access to the single market they will have , " Michel said . <p> Michel , European Parliament President David Sassoli and the head of the bloc 's executive arm , Ursula von der Leyen of the European Commission , also said the EU would now focus on fighting climate change and advancing digitalisation . <p> " We want to have the best possible relationship with the United Kingdom but it will never replicate the benefits of membership , " von der Leyen told reporters in a joint statement . ( Reporting by Gabriela Baczynska , Marine Strauss Editing by John Chalmers )
