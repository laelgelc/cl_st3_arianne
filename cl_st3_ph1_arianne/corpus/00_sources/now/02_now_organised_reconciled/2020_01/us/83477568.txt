<p> SINGAPORE , Jan 28 ( Reuters ) - Sasseur Real Estate Investment Trust said on Tuesday it has temporarily closed its four outlet malls in China to help prevent the spread of a coronavirus that has killed over 100 people . <p> The REIT 's units were down 8% on Tuesday morning , while the broader market was down more than 2% . Its malls in China are in Chongqing , Bishan , Hefei and Kunming . <p> While local authorities have not imposed any regulations to direct the closures of malls , the manager of the REIT said Sasseur decided the outlets should be temporarily closed due to the severity of virus . <p> Sasseur said that while it could not ascertain the financial hit of the crisis on its financial performance , the impact should not be material if it was resolved in the short term . <p> It said it will aim to re-open the malls as soon as it is appropriate .
