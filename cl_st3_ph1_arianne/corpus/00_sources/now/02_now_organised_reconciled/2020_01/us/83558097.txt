<h> The five-time Wimbledon champion explains that while a plant-based diet helps him win matches , his dedication to a vegan lifestyle is an ethical choice related to " being conscious of what is happening in the animal world . People around me did n't really think that I could do that . There was a lot of doubt and a lot of question marks . " Last year , while following a fully plant-based diet , the Serbian-born tennis player won his fifth Wimbledon title , beating former champion Roger Federer in the four-hour-long match -- the longest single 's final game in Wimbledon history .
