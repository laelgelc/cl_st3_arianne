<p> DUBAI , Jan 20 ( Reuters ) - Iranian Foreign Minister Mohammad Javad Zarif will not attend the World Economic Forum 's annual meeting in Davos this week because its organisers had " abruptly changed its agenda " , its foreign ministry spokesman said on Monday . <p> " He was scheduled to attend ... but they abruptly changed the schedule and it was not the schedule that we agreed upon . So he will not attend Davos , " Abbas Mousavi told a televised weekly conference . <p> Reuters last week reported that Zarif was no longer on the list of nearly 3,000 people due at the event , which is being held under the banner " Stakeholders for a Sustainable and Cohesive World " .
