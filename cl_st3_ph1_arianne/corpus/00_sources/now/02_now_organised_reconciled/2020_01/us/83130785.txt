<p> HANOI , Jan 9 ( Reuters ) - At least four people , including three policemen , were killed on Thursday in Vietnam when protesters attacked authorities trying to build a wall near a military airport , the Ministry of Public Security said . <p> The protesters attacked the authorities with hand-grenades , petrol bombs and knives in Dong Tam village , near the capital , Hanoi , the ministry said in a statement on its website . <p> Three policemen and one of the protesters were killed , while one protester was injured , the ministry said . <p> It did not elaborate on the protesters ' objections . <p> " The authorities have launched an investigation into the case and have arrested the lawbreakers , " the ministry said . <p> Disputes over land do occur in Vietnam though this is the first time in years policemen were killed in a such a dispute .
