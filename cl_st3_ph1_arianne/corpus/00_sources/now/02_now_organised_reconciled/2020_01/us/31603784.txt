<p> We have now learned that 11 American servicemen have been injured by the Iranian rocket attack . They were concussed by the blast waves emanating from the explosions . The Army has responded appropriately and has sent them out of theater to be evaluated . <p> My unit in Vietnam fired 508,653 155mm howitzer rounds from March 1967 to October 1971 . Each one of those muzzle blasts caused a " mild traumatic brain injury " for the gun crew and anyone else in the radius of the blast wave . We often fired six rounds per minute for extended periods of time ; combat is like that . The repetitive concussions generated a condition called post-concussion syndrome ( PCS ) , which can lead to early-onset dementia and Alzheimer 's , and ends with chronic traumatic encephalopathy . We are all affected . <p> The Department of Defense and the VA do not recognize PCS as a battlefield injury , even though it has existed since WWI . We will decline cognitively before our noncombat peers . We will die before them , too .
