<p> First , it was the nene goose , Hawaii 's state bird . Now it 's the Hawaiian hawk 's turn to mark a milestone of returning from the brink of extinction . <p> The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service has removed the Hawaiian hawk , or io , from its federal list of endangered and threatened wildlife after 53 years . On Jan. 2 the USFWS issued a " final rule , " the required procedure for any addition or removal from the list . Its removal is effective Feb. 3 . <p> The USFWS said studies have shown rangewide population estimates for the io have been stable for at least 30 years . <p> " We found that their population has been stable , and so we have determined that they no longer need the protections of ( the Endangered Species Act ) and that they are an adaptable species able to use multiple habitats , " said Megan Laut , USFWS conservation and restoration team manager , Pacific islands .
