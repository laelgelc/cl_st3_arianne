<h> 500 Rabbis Vow Action on Climate Crisis <p> Over 500 rabbis and Jewish leaders have signed a call to action to fight climate change . The list includes rabbis from across the Jewish spectrum , including social justice leaders like Rabbi Sharon Kleinbaum of Congregation Beit Simchat Torah in Manhattan , Rabbi Sharon Brous of Ikar in Los Angeles and Rabbi Jill Jacobs of the rabbinic human rights organization T'ruah . <p> The statement was initiated by Rabbi Arthur Waskow of the Shalom Center in Philadelphia , a longtime leader in the Jewish Renewal movement and a human rights advocate . <p> " I think it 's fair to say that many people in the Jewish community have been concerned about the environment for a really long time , " said Ruth Messinger , who served as president of the American Jewish World Service for 20 years and was one of the initiating signatories of the call to action . " But in the last few years it 's become sort of a dramatic crisis that 's visible to everyone , that requires the community to be that much more concerned and focused . " There will unfortunately be millions of people who are climate refugees who are not able to continue to live in their own countries , " said Messinger . <p> The statement , notable for the large number of signatories , calls for wide-reaching actions on personal , communal and national scales .
