<p> PARIS , Jan 15 ( Reuters ) - Romania has reported an outbreak of the highly pathogenic H5N8 bird flu virus on a farm in the northern part of the country , the first such outbreak in nearly three years , the World Organisation for Animal Health ( OIE ) said on Wednesday . <p> The virus killed 11,190 birds out of a flock of 18,699 in the Maramures region near the borders with Ukraine and Hungary , the Paris-based OIE said , citing a report from Romania 's veterinary services . <p> The rest of the flock was slaughtered , it said .
