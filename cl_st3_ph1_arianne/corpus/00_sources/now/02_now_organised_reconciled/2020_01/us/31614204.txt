<p> Regulations for electronic vehicle parking spaces and fees for carbon emissions could impose an ambiguous cost on taxpayers under a new climate policy that passed in the state Senate this week . <p> " During the debate , Senators repeatedly rejected requests to determine the cost of this new carbon policy , which is particularly concerning , " Chris Carlozzi , state director of the National Federation of Independent Business , told the Herald . " The Senate seems more fixated on passing a ' radical ' carbon policy rather than taking into consideration the fiscal impact on residents ' and small businesses ' operating costs . " <p> A legislative package put forward by the Senate to combat climate change and reach " net-zero " greenhouse gas emissions by 2050 would establish carbon pricing as law , essentially forcing Gov . Charlie Baker to find an alternative if the Transportation Climate Initiative falls through .
