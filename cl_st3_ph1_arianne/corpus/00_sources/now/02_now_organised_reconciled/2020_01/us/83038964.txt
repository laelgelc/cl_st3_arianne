<h> OPDEnergy connects 50-MWp plant in Spain <p> January 3 ( Renewables Now ) - Spain-based renewables developer OPDEnergy announced Thursday it has connected to the local grid its 50-MWp Miramundo solar photovoltaic ( PV ) plant . <p> Together with the European infrastructure fund Marguerite , OPDEnergy has invested more than EUR 32 million ( USD 35.7m ) in the construction of the solar park and structured the debt financing . <p> Located in a 111-hectare ( 274.3 acres ) area in the town of Puerto Real , Cadiz province , the facility consists of 133,290 PV modules capable of generating enough power to meet the demand of nearly 30,000 homes . UK energy company Centrica plc ( LON:CNA ) signed a power purchase agreement ( PPA ) to act as the purchaser of Miramundo 's output . <p> Miramundo will also be able to offset around 40,000 tonnes of carbon dioxide ( CO2 ) per year , according to the emission factors released by the Spanish Office of Climate Change
