<p> DUBAI , Jan 11 ( Reuters ) - A leader of Iran 's opposition Green Movement , Mehdi Karroubi , called on Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei , the top authority in the Islamic Republic , to step down over the handling of the shooting down of the Ukrainian airliner . <p> In a statement posted online , Karroubi asked when Khamenei was told about the shooting down of the plane after it took off from Tehran on Wednesday , and why there had been a delay in informing the public about the real reasons for the crash .
