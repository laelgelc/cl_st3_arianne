<p> BEIRUT , Jan 21 ( Reuters ) - Lebanon 's new government will be announced on Tuesday , political sources and local media said , after the powerful Shi'ite Hezbollah group and its allies clinched an agreement on a cabinet that must tackle a deep economic crisis . <p> Two senior political sources told Reuters the cabinet would be made up of 20 ministers with economist Ghazi Wazni as finance minister . In a Twitter post earlier on Tuesday , caretaker Finance Minister Ali Hassan Khalil of the Hezbollah-allied Amal Movement said a new government was " hours away " . <p> Heavily indebted Lebanon has been without effective government since Saad al-Hariri resigned as premier in October due to protests against state corruption and waste - the root causes of the country 's worst financial and economic crisis since its 1975-90 civil war . <p> The issue has become more pressing since hundreds of people were injured in clashes between protesters and security forces at the weekend .
