<h> Florida Legislature to focus on teacher raises , health <h> Climate change , immigration among hot issues <p> Sen. Tom Lee , R-Brandon , and his wife Secretary of State Laura are recognized by the Senate at the start of session on Tuesday , Jan. 14 , 2020 , in Tallahassee , Fla . ( AP Photo/Steve Cannon ) ( Associated Press ) <p> TALLAHASSEE , Fla. -- The Florida Legislature began its election-year work of considering hundreds of bills , including teacher pay raises , climate change and immigration . <p> During the session that started Tuesday morning , legislators also will consider outlaying the billions of dollars required to keep the country 's third-most populous state operating . <p> The Senate opened its session commemorating the Dec. 6 shooting at the Pensacola naval air station that killed three sailors . <p> Gov . Ron DeSantis is hoping the congeniality and applause that greeted his State of the State address will extend into the session as lawmakers delve into his proposals .
