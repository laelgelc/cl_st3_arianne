<p> CAIRO , Jan 15 ( Reuters ) - Sudan 's airspace is now open , the head of the country 's sovereign council , Lieutenant General Abdel Fattah al-Burhan , said in a speech early on Wednesday . <p> He also vowed never to allow any coup to take place , and confirmed that all intelligence buildings were under the control of the army . <p> Sudan witnessed violence on Wednesday that was the biggest confrontation yet between its old guard and supporters of the new administration that helped topple former ruler Omar al-Bashir . <p> In a protest over severance packages , the former employees of the National Intelligence and Security Service also shut two small oilfields in Darfur , a government source told Reuters . The fields had an output of around 5,000 barrels a day .
