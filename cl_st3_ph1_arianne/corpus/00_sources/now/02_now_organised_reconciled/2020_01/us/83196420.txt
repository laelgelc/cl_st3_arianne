<h> Firefighter dies as Australia plans to adapt to wildfires <p> BURRAGATE , Australia ( AP ) - A firefighter has died battling the Australian wildfire crisis and the prime minister has said his government is adapting and building resilience to the fire danger posed by climate change . <p> Officials say the firefighter died on Saturday near Omeo in eastern Victoria state . <p> No details of the circumstances were released . <p> The tragedy brings the death toll to at least 27 people in a crisis that has destroyed more than 2,000 homes and scorched an area larger than the U.S. state of Indiana since September . <p> Four of the casualties are firefighters . <p> The crisis has brought accusations that Prime Minister Scott Morrison 's conservative government needs to take more action to counter climate change . <p> Conditions are milder and forecast to remain relatively benign for the next week .
