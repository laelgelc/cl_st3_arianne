<h> French campaign groups ask court to order Total to act on global warming <p> 2 Min Read <p> FILE PHOTO : The logo of French oil giant Total is pictured at a petrol station in Laplume , France January 16 , 2020 . REUTERS/Regis Duvignau/File Photo <p> PARIS ( Reuters ) - Fourteen French local authorities and six campaign groups have asked a French court to order oil major Total ( TOTF.PA ) to sharply reduce its greenhouse gas emissions , campaign groups said on Tuesday . <p> The lawsuit filed against Total comes as oil majors face increasing pressure from activists to act on climate change . <p> The French campaign groups said in a statement the lawsuit was the first of its kind filed in France . <p> Total said it regretted the legal action taken , adding it was working in compliance with national legal standards . <p> " Total remains open to constructive talks , " it said . <p> Under French law , large French companies are required to publish annual plans that address any adverse impact of their activities , and those of subsidiaries and suppliers , on people and the environment @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ groups , that included Les Eco Maires , a group of local authorities acting for sustainable development , alleged that Total 's activities failed to comply with the goal of limiting a rise in global warming to 1.5 degrees Celsius . <p> Reporting by Bate Felix ; Writing by Matthieu Protard ; editing by David Evans
