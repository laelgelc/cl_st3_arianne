<p> The managing director of an international financial institution has warned countries not to let the next decade lead to a financial disaster . <p> The new International Monetary Fund ( IMF ) chief , Kristalina Georgieva , compared the new decade starting in 2020 to the 1920s , stating that accelerated growth in the " roaring 20s " ultimately ended in " a financial disaster " otherwise known as the Great Depression . <p> Georgieva said that although inequality between countries has decreased , there is growing inequality within countries that is " not a good thing . " <p> Speaking to an audience at the Peterson Institute for International Economics in Washington DC on Friday , she called out the United Kingdom where she said the " top 10% control nearly as much wealth as the bottom 50% " .
