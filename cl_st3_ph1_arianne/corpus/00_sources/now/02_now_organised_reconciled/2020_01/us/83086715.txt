<h> Readers respond : Demise of comments sad but inevitable <p> I am sad to see the comments on OregonLive discontinued ( " OregonLive eliminates comments , joining other media organizations , " Dec. 29 ) . But , given the degree to which the commentary had degenerated , it is hardly surprising . One big reason it devolved is that posters could be anonymous . I expect the tenor of the conversation would have been more civil had real names been required . <p> That said , having to identify yourself would be a double-edged sword , opening the author to retribution by misguided fellow citizens . For example , I had a Guest Opinion posted several years ago , essentially making the case that burning fossil fuel is causing global warming and thus climate change . Shortly thereafter , a local anthropogenic global warming denier wrote a letter to my superiors in an apparent attempt to get me in trouble or otherwise intimidate me . <p> The dramatic decline in the level of public discourse in the last several years , led by our president and his political party , bodes ill for our society .
