They 've countered the utility 's proposal with their own bill , which aims to further accelerate the replacement of diesel buses . The measure , championed by an array of green groups , would allow school districts to tap into a new block grant program administered by the Department of Education to pay for the difference between a diesel and a more expensive electric bus , and the necessary affiliated charging infrastructure . <p> As well , Keam 's bill prioritizes bus replacements in school districts with high rates of air pollution and asthma .
