<p> BEIRUT , Jan 23 ( Reuters ) - Militants in Syria set off car bombs and used heavy gunfire to storm army positions in Idlib on Thursday , state news agency SANA reported . <p> The militant attack forced the army to redeploy and clashes were ongoing , SANA added . <p> Idlib is the last rebel-held swathe of territory in the country and hundreds of thousands of people in the area have fled in recent weeks amid heavy airstrikes by Russian and Syrian forces .
