<p> BERLIN , Jan 19 ( Reuters ) - U.S. Secretary of State Mike Pompeo expressed outrage during a meeting with Egyptian President Abdel Fattah al-Sisi over the " pointless and tragic death " of detained U.S. citizen Moustafa Kassem in Egypt , a U.S. official said on Sunday . <p> The United States on Monday confirmed the death of Egyptian-American Kassem in a prison in Egypt , where he had been in custody since 2013 , and vowed to continue to raise concerns over Cairo 's human rights record . <p> A Egyptian presidency statement on the meeting made no mention of Kassem 's case .
