<p> BEIRUT , Jan 22 ( Reuters ) - U.N . Secretary General Antonio Guterres welcomed the formation of a new Lebanese government on Tuesday and will work with the new premier to support reforms in the heavily indebted country grappling with an urgent economic crisis . <p> A statement issued by Guterres ' spokesperson also said the United Nations was committed to supporting " Lebanon 's strengthening of its sovereignty , stability and political independence " . <p> Lebanon formed a new government under Prime Minister Hassan Diab after the Shi'ite Hezbollah movement and its allies agreed on a cabinet after weeks of wrangling over portfolios .
