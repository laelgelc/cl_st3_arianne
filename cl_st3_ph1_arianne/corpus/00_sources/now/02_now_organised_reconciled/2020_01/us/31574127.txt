<p> The next frontier in California 's battle against pollution : lawn equipment . <p> State air regulators are laying long-term plans to phase out gasoline-powered devices like leaf blowers and lawn mowers , saying they can produce more noxious emissions than cars . <p> Plenty of Bay Area cities are already acting : At least eight have banned gas-powered blowers , and more restrict their use during times of day or up to a certain noise level . Novato may soon join the list . <p> " What I think we need to realize is that we have to do something different for climate change in the world , " said Novato Mayor Pro Tem Pat Eklund , who proposed a ban on gas lawn mowers and leaf blowers in December . " If not , we are going to see a different world than we do today . Every little bit is going to help . " <p> Such restrictions force people to use cleaner , quieter electric machinery instead .
