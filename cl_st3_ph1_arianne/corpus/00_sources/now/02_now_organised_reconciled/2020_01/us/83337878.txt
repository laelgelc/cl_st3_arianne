<h> Provincetown considers climate change committee <h> Sunday <p> PROVINCETOWN -- Climate change is one of the biggest concerns on the Cape 's tip , and the town is pondering formation of a group to make sure it can keep Provincetown afloat . <p> " I know when we talk about creating another committee it becomes an issue because we have so many , " Select Board member Lise King said . " My recommendation , however , is we strongly consider the creation of a climate action committee because of the urgent nature of the conversation . " <p> King brought the idea to the board Jan. 13 and said the new committee could be fashioned after Truro 's Climate Action Committee , which formed last spring . <p> The Center for Climate Integrity released a report last year estimating that , at around $7 billion , Barnstable County would have the third-highest price tag of coastal communities nationwide for protecting public infrastructure from sea level rise over the next 20 years . <p> The committee could be a repository for all of the town 's past climate studies and work to keep sea level rise at bay @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with issues related to climate change would likely make up the committee . The Select Board voted to have town staff look at the composition of the committee and its purview . <p> Assistant Town Manager David Gardner suggested having representatives from the Cape Cod National Seashore and the Center for Coastal Studies be ex officio committee members . <p> " Having them at the table , I think in some respects , could certainly be helpful for that conversation , " he said . <p> The board also wanted the new committee to interact with the other neighboring Outer Cape towns .
