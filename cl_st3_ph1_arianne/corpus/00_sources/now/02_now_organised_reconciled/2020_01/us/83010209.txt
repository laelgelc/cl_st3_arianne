<h> Featured Tracks : <p> In the public eye , Pittsburgh may forever be the " home of the Steelers , " the embodiment of a bygone blue-collar economy weaponized in Bruce Springsteen songs and MAGA agitprop alike . If that 's the case , its reputation has n't caught up to reality : It 's a meds-and-ed mecca where the gentrification rate compares to San Francisco , Denver , or Austin -- fast enough that a young band like Short Fictions can look back on 2014 with nostalgia .
