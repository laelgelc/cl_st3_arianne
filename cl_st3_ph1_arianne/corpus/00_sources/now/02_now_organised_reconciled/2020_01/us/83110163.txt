<h> Wed 8:30 Can More Food Offset Climate Effects On Salmon ? <p> Scientists and fishery managers were already concerned about fish stocks , before the effects of climate change became apparent . Salmonids in our region can be very sensitive to changes in temperature ; they often need colder water to survive . <p> But recent research out of the University of California-Davis shows that the effects of warm water can be mitigated somewhat by an abundant food supply . So let the water warm , but feed the fish more ? <p> The salmon of our region are very adaptable ; they 've survived millions of years in both the ocean and freshwater streams . But humans have presented some challenges to the fish , and climate change compounds the situation . <p> A recent study looked at climate pressures on salmon and steelhead populations up and down the West Coast , and it gives some indication of which species will require more attention and assistance to survive .
