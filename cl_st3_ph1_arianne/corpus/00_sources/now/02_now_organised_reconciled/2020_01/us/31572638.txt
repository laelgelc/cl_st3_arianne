He tried to return Britain to the path of collective welfare for all , not one of dog eat dog or every man for himself and God for us all . <p> The 105-page Labour Manifesto was aimed at transforming the country including the renationalization of rail , mail , water and energy , and in this digital age , making access to the internet , part of the right to information and free speech . <p> The Corbyn-led Labour Party offers included raising ? 75bn to build 150,000 new council and social homes a year over five years , a living wage of ? 10 an hour , a five per cent pay raise for public sector workers with follow-up annual raises based on inflation rate . Its programmes included reinstating 3,000 bus routes , reducing all primary school classes to fewer than 30 children and giving EU nationals living in UK the automatic right to stay . <p> Corbyn whose leadership also worked out funding for the change promised , warned during the campaigns : " Over the next three weeks , the most powerful people in Britain and their supporters are going to tell you that everything in this manifesto is impossible . That it 's too much for you . Because they do n't want real change . <p> Why would they ? The system is working just fine for them . It 's rigged in their favour . He was also accused of turning the party to : " a welcoming refuge for anti-Semites . " <p> The fact that he and his parents have a long history of pro-Jewish struggles including his personal efforts of getting Britain to resettle Yemeni Jews and saving the Jewish cemetery in his constituency from being flattened , did not blunt such false accusations .
