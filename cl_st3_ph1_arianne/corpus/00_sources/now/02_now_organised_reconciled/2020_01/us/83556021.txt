Mr. Biden led in all previous Journal/NBC News polls of the 2020 primary race . <p> Sen. Elizabeth Warren slipped to a distant third place , with 15% support . In the prior two surveys , she was about even with Mr. Sanders for second place . Mr. Sanders has gained support by drawing voters from Ms. Warren , the poll found .
