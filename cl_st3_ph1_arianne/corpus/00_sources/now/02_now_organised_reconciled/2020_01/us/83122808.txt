<h> Photos show the eerie devastation of Australia 's fires <p> Editor 's note : This story was originally published by Mother Jones . It appears here as part of the Climate Deskcollaboration . <p> Australia launched its largest military operation since World War II this weekend , not against a human enemy but to combat the bushfire crisis sweeping the country . Thus far , 23 people have died as 140 blazes have devoured more over 12 million acres . <p> To combat the fires , which are only expected to get worse as Australia endures high temperatures and windy conditions , the government is mobilizing 3,000 army reservists , aircraft , and naval ships to help evacuations and fight fires , the New York Timesreported Saturday . " It is the first time that reserves have been called out in this way in living memory , " Defense Minister Linda Reynolds said . The conflagrations now and in the future are expected to worsen due to climate change .
