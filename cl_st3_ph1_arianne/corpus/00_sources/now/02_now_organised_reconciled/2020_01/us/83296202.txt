<h> Children , young adults can not sue U.S. government over climate change : ruling <p> ( Reuters ) - A U.S. federal appeals court on Friday threw out a lawsuit by children and young adults who claimed they had a constitutional right to be protected from climate change , in a major setback to efforts to spur the U.S. government to address the issue . <p> In a 2-1 decision , the 9th U.S. Circuit Court of Appeals said the plaintiffs , who were between the ages of 8 and 19 when the lawsuit began in 2015 , lacked legal standing to sue the United States . <p> Circuit Judge Andrew Hurwitz said the majority reached its conclusion " reluctantly , " given " compelling " evidence the government had long promoted fossil fuels despite knowing they could cause catastrophic climate change , and that failing to change policies could hasten an " environmental apocalypse . " <p> Our Children 's Trust , an Oregon-based non-profit behind the lawsuit , said in a statement that it would seek an 11-judge panel of the court to review the decision .
