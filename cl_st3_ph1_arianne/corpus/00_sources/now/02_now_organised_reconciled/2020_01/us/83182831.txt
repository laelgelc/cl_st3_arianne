<p> Actors Joaquin Phoenix and Martin Sheen were the latest stars to be arrested at Jane Fonda 's weekly Washington environmental protests , known as " Fire Drill Fridays . " <p> The actors joined Fonda and a throng of other protesters Friday and marched up the steps of the capitol . They were arrested when they refused to disperse after cops ordered them to do so , NBC News reported . <p> Phoenix , who won a best actor Golden Globe for his role in " Joker , " addressed the crowd and the protest , urging people to be mindful of what they buy . <p> " The meat and dairy industry is the third leading cause of climate change , " he said . <p> " There is something that you can do today , right now and tomorrow by making your choice about what you consume , " he added .
