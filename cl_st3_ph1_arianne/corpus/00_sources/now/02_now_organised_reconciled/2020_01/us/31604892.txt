<p> American car insurance rates are going up up up . In the past decade , they climbed 29.6 percent , to an average of $1,548 in 2019 from $1,194 in 2011 . The surge , detailed in a new report from insurance shopping site The Zebra , outpaced both inflation ( by far ) and the increase in average car prices ( more narrowly ) . And it came even as the rate of crashes has fallen year over year . <p> Aggrieved drivers have plenty of directions to point their fingers . Vehicle theft is on the rise , and extreme weather fueled by climate change can destroy swaths of vehicles in short order . Hurricane Harvey wrecked up to 1 million cars in the Houston area in 2017 . And while crash rates have dropped , they 've been buoyed by increasing urbanization and a strong economy , which put more drivers -- many of them distracted by smartphones -- in tighter spaces . <p> A more surprising , counterintuitive culprit is n't the wider world or the person behind the wheel but the car itself .
