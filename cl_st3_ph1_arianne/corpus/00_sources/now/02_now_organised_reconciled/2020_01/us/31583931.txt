The ride is far from over . <p> When the campaign season opened early last year , the party 's progressive wing appeared ascendant . Sens . Bernie Sanders ( I-Vt. ) and Elizabeth Warren ( D-Mass. ) were seen as two of three or four top-tier candidates . Medicare-for-all , a proposal first championed by Sanders , was drawing support from other candidates . A costly Green New Deal to combat climate change was on every candidate 's list of must-do policies . <p> As the year progressed , both Warren and Sanders had ups and downs . She struggled early but caught fire over the summer and led the Iowa Poll in September . Then she hit a rough patch and slipped back . Sanders showed his fundraising prowess in the early months of 2019 but appeared to be fading as the year progressed .
