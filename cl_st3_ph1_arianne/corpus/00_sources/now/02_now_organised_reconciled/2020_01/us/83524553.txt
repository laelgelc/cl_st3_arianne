<p> KUALA LUMPUR , Jan 29 ( Reuters ) - The world 's largest glovemaker , Top Glove Corporation , could beat its 2020 sales growth target of 10-15% due to the spread of a new coronavirus in China , the Malaysian firm 's executive chairman said on Wednesday . <p> " Over the past few days , the total sales orders received are equivalent to more than double our usual sales to China , " Lim Wee Chai told Reuters via email . <p> " We are expecting more sales orders to come in depending on the severity of the outbreak and how long it lasts , " he said . <p> The company has seen a spike in orders from China , Hong Kong and Taiwan , particularly for latex and nitrile examination gloves , Lim said . <p> More than half of its 33 factories across the region , including one in China , will be ramping up capacity , he said . <p> It is currently running at 85% of its output capacity but is prepared to operate at up to full capacity if the need arises , he added . <p> On Wednesday confirmed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ deaths from the flu-like virus rose to 132 .
