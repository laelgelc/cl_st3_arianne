<h> Symposium to honor 50th Anniversary of Rubisco Oxygenase Discovery <p> In honor of the work of Bill Ogren and his research directly linking photosynthesis and photorespiration via one enzyme , the Carl R. Woese Institute for Genomic Biology ( IGB ) is hosting the symposium " Rubisco Oxygenase : 50 Years of Progress and Looking Into the Future " from March 27-29 , 2020 . The symposium will feature a public lecture and a series of plenary talks . <p> The international panel of speakers represents diverse disciplines , such as plant biology and physiology , molecular biology , evolutionary biology , and computational biology , and will discuss the profound foundational impact of Ogren 's discovery on current and future scientific research . <p> The symposium , to be held at the IGB building on of the University of Illinois campus , will start with a public lecture on Friday , the evening of March 27 .
