<p> LUCKNOW , India , Jan 31 ( Reuters ) - Police shot dead a man holding around 20 women and children hostage at his house in northern India after a 10-hour standoff , state officials said on Friday . <p> The hostages who were held at gunpoint were safe , principal secretary home Awanish Kumar Awasthi said after the raid at the house in a village in Farrukhabad in Uttar Pradesh state . <p> The hostage taker was serving a life sentence for murder and was out on parole , he added . <p> Two policemen and a villager were injured in the rescue operation . <p> After the siege , a group of incensed villagers stormed the house where the children had been kept and attacked the hostage-taker 's wife , Awasthi said . The woman died from her injuries early on Friday , he said . <p> The abduction took place after the man had invited some children and women from the village to his house , saying he was throwing a birthday party for his daughter . <p> Police said his motive for holding the children was not clear .
