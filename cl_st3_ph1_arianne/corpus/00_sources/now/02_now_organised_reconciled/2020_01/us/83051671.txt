<h> Climate change is coming home to roost Sheneman <p> The 2020s are going to be the decade of climate change . So are the 2030s and 40s and so on . Climate change is very real , it is here now and there are consequences we will not be able to avoid . Radical and swift action may still be able to fend off some of the catastrophic predictions , but when was the last time we did anything radical or swiftly ? The 2010s were the hottest decade on record , Australia is on fire and species are going extinct at an alarming rate . Happy new year . <p> Climate change , its effects and how we deal with them are going to be the defining story of the next century . The how we deal with them part may well decide whether we 're around much longer . The fight against climate change is often framed as a battle for the fate of the planet , but it 's not . The planet will be just fine . It will continue orbiting the mid-sized star at the center of our solar system until that same @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ planet whole about 5 billion years from now . The real fight against climate change is about how we want to spend our time on the planet before it shakes us off and ushers in whatever comes next . Do we want to spend our time making this rock as hard as possible to live on for future generations or will we finally take steps to make sure the planet does n't become an easy bake oven ?
