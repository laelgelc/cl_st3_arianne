<p> Florida is a good decade overdue in addressing climate change , which already is starting to affect the state with flooding and storms . Rather than begin measures to protect our vulnerable coastlines , Tallahassee has focused on increasing short-term economic and employment growth with an eye toward bolstering the state 's wealth and boosting our quality of life . But will that really add to Florida 's wealth in the long run if increasing amounts of our resources will be devoted to preventing , adapting to and replacing property damaged because of climate change ? <p> Probably not . Instead , you and I and every other Florida taxpayer will be asked to foot the bill for merely maintaining the state 's current level of wealth and quality of life .
