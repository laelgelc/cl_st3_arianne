<p> Jan 23 ( Reuters ) - An earthquake of magnitude 6.2 struck 81 km ( 50 miles ) west of the city of Adak in Alaska at a depth of 10 km , the U.S. Geological Survey said on Thursday . <p> There was no danger of a tsunami , the National Tsunami Warning Center based in Palmer , Alaska , said . <p> The quake was followed by more than 30 aftershocks of magnitudes in the range of 2.1 and 3.9 within two hours in the same Aleutian Island region , according to the Alaska Earthquake Center at the University of Alaska Fairbanks . <p> Adak is the westernmost city not only in Alaska but in the United States . It is about 1,300 miles southwest of Anchorage and has a population of about 300 . <p> It is a very seismically active area , with many earthquakes and active volcanoes .
