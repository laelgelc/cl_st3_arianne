<h> A dangerous form of climate denialism <h> Sunday <p> Jan 5 , 2020 at 12:01 AM <p> The Register-Guard expressed a dangerous form of climate denialism in its Dec. 30 editorial , which supported managing the Elliott State Forest in a manner that would " be a win for everyone in 2020 . " It first highlighted laudable goals -- conservation of habitat , recreation and educational research -- and then endorsed logging on much of the land to raise money to achieve them . <p> This endorsement ignores research that shows logging is Oregon 's largest source of carbon dioxide emissions that will intensify climate disasters for decades to come . For logging on state-owned lands , the resulting climate-disaster costs total at least seven times -- and perhaps more than 50 times -- the state 's logging revenue . So , contrary to the assertion , only a few people will win , but most will lose much more . <p> I believe the RG knows that climate change is not a hoax .
