<h> Futurist David Houle to Speak at Sarasota Chamber 's Annual Breakfast <h> Houle will discuss his predictions for the next 10 years and how they will affect businesses of all sizes . <p> The Greater Sarasota Chamber of Commerce has announced the futurist David Houle will be the guest speaker at chamber 's 2020 annual breakfast on Friday , Feb. 21 , at the Hyatt Regency Sarasota . Houle will present a talk called " The 2020s : The Future of Business . " In it , he 'll discuss the dynamics of the next 10 years and how they will affect businesses of all sizes , including transportation and energy , global finance , climate change , generational change , 21st-century manufacturing , and medical and health discoveries . Houle 's new book , The 2020s : The Most Disruptive Decade in History , is the first in a series that will examine the ascendent trends and disruptions of the 2020s . <p> At the meeting , the chamber will also present its 2020 strategic goals for its membership .
