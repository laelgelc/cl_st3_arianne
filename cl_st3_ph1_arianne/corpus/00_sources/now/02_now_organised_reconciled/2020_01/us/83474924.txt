<p> TAIPEI , Jan 27 ( Reuters ) - Taiwan will inspect people leaving the island to ensure they are not breaking a temporary ban on exporting face masks amid an outbreak of the new coronavirus which started in China , but people carrying them for personal use will be exempted . <p> Taiwan , which has close economic and cultural links with China even though political ties are strained , has reported five cases of the coronavirus and has stepped up inspections at airports and stopped most Chinese visitors from coming . <p> A total of 81 people have died , all in China . <p> Last week Taiwan 's government announced a one-month ban on the export of specialist masks designed to be used for medical personnel , saying it had to look after the needs of its own people first . <p> Many pharmacies in Taiwan have reported shortages of face masks since the island reported its first case a week ago . <p> In a statement on Monday , Taiwan 's customs administration said people could take masks out with them if they were for personal use . <p> " Customs will judge the reasonable @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ number of accompanying people , the number of travel days , and the tourist area , " it said in a statement . <p> The inspections appear aimed at stopping travellers from Taiwan , whether Taiwanese citizens or others , from taking the masks to China for resale , as China is facing tight supplies due to the rapid rise in coronavirus case numbers . <p> Taiwan 's official Central News Agency said that over the Lunar New Year period that means each person can only take out five boxes . <p> Taiwan returns to work on Thursday after the week-long holiday .
