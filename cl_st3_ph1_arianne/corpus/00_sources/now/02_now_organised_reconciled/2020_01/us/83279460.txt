<p> Jan 16 ( Reuters ) - The FBI arrested on Thursday three men suspected of being members of a neo-Nazi hate group who had weapons and discussed attending a gun rally in Virginia next week in hopes of helping to start a race war , the New York Times reported . <p> The men were arrested in Maryland and were expected to make an appearance in federal court later Thursday , the newspaper reported , citing unnamed law enforcement sources . Gun rights supporters are planning a large rally in Richmond , Virginia , on Monday in response to the newly Democratic-controlled state legislature 's push to stiffen gun laws .
