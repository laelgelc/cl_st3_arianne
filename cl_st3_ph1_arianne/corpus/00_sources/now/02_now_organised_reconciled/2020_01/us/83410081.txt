<h> Broto : Art-Climate-Science conference in Provincetown announced <h> Thursday <p> Jan 23 , 2020 at 7:00 AMJan 23 , 2020 at 7:00 AM <p> PROVINCETOWN -- Broto : Art-Climate-Science , a conference dedicated to climate change innovation , will host its third annual conference May 16-17 in Provincetown . The program will examine time as a tool in understanding how we affect nature and how nature affects us -- into the far future . Speakers include Nicholas Paul Brysiewicz from The Long Now Foundation , Don McKay , award-winning poet inspired by geology , Hilairy Hartnett , co-director of PlanetWorks at Arizona State University , Jonathon Keats , the conceptual artist and experimental philosopher behind Millennium Camera , and students from Provincetown Schools , who will outline their visions for a future made sustainable , along with other experts from the Cape Cod region . Featured speakers , interactive panels and social events round out the creative , brainstorm-style weekend program . The venue is Provincetown Commons at 46 Bradford St. Conference tickets start at $100 with a discount through Jan. 31
