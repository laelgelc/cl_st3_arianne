So it 's important to count every person to ensure our fair share of representation and federal dollars . Florida surpassed New York back in 2014 to become the third-most populous state , but that status wo n't be reflected in congressional seats until the results of the 2020 census are in . No. 1 California understands the importance of counting every one and is spending $187 million on outreach and education , according to the New York Times . No. 2 Texas and Florida are spending nothing . The Urban Institute predicts as many as 320,000 Floridians could go uncounted , including many people of color and those with lower incomes .
