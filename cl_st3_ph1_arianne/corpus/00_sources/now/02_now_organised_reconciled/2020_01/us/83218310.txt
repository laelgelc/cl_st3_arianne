<p> OTTAWA , Jan 12 ( Reuters ) - Iran has issued eight more visas to a team of Canadian officials in the wake of a fatal plane crash near Tehran and most members of the group should be in Tehran on Monday , Canadian Foreign Minister Francois-Philippe Champagne said on Sunday . <p> Champagne said on Twitter that three officials from the rapid deployment team had flown to Iran on Saturday to set up a base of operations and a further eight would travel on Monday . The last member will arrive in Ankara on Monday . <p> " We expect the ( team ) to be fully in place to do their important work by Jan 14 , " Champagne said . <p> Canada says it wants to take part in the crash investigation and help the families of the 57 Canadians who died . The team includes consular officials and members of Canada 's Transportation Safety Board . <p> Ottawa does not have diplomatic relations with Iran , which says its forces shot down the plane by mistake . <p> Prime Minister Justin Trudeau spoke on Saturday to Iranian President Hassan Rouhani .
