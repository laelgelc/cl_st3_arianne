<h> Wonders if Amy " can even claim to be a Democrat " <h> January 31 , 2020 <p> Amy Klobuchar claims to be the best Democratic candidate to win the Presidential race because she has so often succeeded by " reaching across the aisle " in her home state . <p> Let 's set the record straight . The Minnesota Wildlife Organization reports that Amy has sided WITH fracking , drilling , pipelines , and taking wolves off the endangered species list so they can be shot and killed , amongst other important issues . <p> And the Environmental Center Action Fund in Washington D.C. has compiled a graph of how candidates rank according to their concerns and actions regarding environmental issues and climate change ( see **39;3559;TOOLONG ) . <p> Bernie Sanders received the highest rank with a ten out of ten score with Tom Steyer close behind . The others were lower and Amy ranked the lowest of all .
