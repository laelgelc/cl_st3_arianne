<p> ROME , Jan 26 ( Reuters ) - Italy 's centre-left Democratic Party ( PD ) looked set for a resounding victory against Matteo Salvini 's rightist League party in a closely watched election in the northern region of Emilia-Romagna , an initial vote forecast said on Sunday . <p> The forecast released by La7 television based on early returns predicted that the PD incumbent had won 51.8% of the vote , while the League candidate , who was backed by an alliance of rightist parties , was seen trailing on 41.5% . <p> If confirmed , such a result would be a major relief for the national government , which includes the PD , and a blow for Salvini , who has campaigned relentlessly in Emilia-Romagna , looking to end decades of leftist rule in the wealthy region .
