<p> New electric car sales in Norway rose by a third last year amid soaring demand for Tesla Inc 's vehicles , but the pioneering U.S. company will face a more competitive market in 2020 as rivals prepare to launch new electric models . <p> Fully electric cars made up 42.4% of sales in the Nordic country last year , a global record , rising from a 31.2% market share in 2018 and just 5.5% in 2013 , the Norwegian Road Federation said on Friday . <p> Norway , which is Europe 's biggest oil and gas producer , is seeking to become the first country to end the sale of fossil-fueled cars by 2025 . The country has exempted battery-powered vehicles from the taxes imposed on petrol and diesel engines and after just a few years the streets of Oslo have become quieter and have less air pollution . <p> New car sales in the country last year were 142,381 , of which 60,316 were fully electric .
