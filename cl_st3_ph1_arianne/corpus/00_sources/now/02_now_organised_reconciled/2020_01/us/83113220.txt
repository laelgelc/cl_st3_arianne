Then , in 2016 , Fiji was devastated by Winston , an even stronger cyclone which again reduced her reef to rubble . <p> Roberta understood that she 'd made a mistake by replanting in the same spot where the destruction from Winston had originally occurred . She began to study how the reef was coming back and analyzed the wave action in order to replant more strategically . Through trial and error she determined where best to put her efforts . <p> She also realized she would need a coral " bank account " that could withstand storm surge .
