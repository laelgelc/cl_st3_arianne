<p> BERLIN , Jan 19 ( Reuters ) - Russian President Vladimir Putin on Sunday said he hoped that the Berlin summit on Libya could bring further progress and that Moscow 's peace efforts had yielded first results . <p> " We do n't lose hope that dialogue will continue and the conflict will be solved , " Putin said before meeting Turkish President Tayyip Erdogan on the sidelines of the conference . <p> Putin said that Russia and Turkey had taken a " very good step " by urging Libyan factions to stop fighting , adding that large military actions had been stopped in Libya .
