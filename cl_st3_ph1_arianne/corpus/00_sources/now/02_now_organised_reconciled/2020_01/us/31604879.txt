<h> Earth 's most biodiverse ecosystems face a perfect storm <p> A combination of climate change , extreme weather and pressure from local human activity is causing a collapse in global biodiversity and ecosystems across the tropics , new research shows . <p> The study , published today , mapped over 100 locations where tropical forests and coral reefs have been affected by climate extremes such as hurricanes , floods , heatwaves , droughts and fires .
