<p> TALLAHASSEE , Fla. -- A House Republican on Friday filed a proposal that would create a Statewide Office of Resiliency in Gov . Ron DeSantis ' office as part of efforts to address the impacts of climate change . <p> The proposal ( HB 1073 ) , filed by Rep. Cyndi Stevenson , R-St . Johns , is identical to a measure ( SB 7016 ) that has started moving forward in the Senate . In addition to creating the Statewide Office of Resiliency , the proposal calls for creating a Statewide Sea-Level Rise Task Force that would " recommend consensus projections of the anticipated sea-level rise and flooding impacts along this state 's coastline . " <p> The task force would face a Jan. 1 , 2021 , deadline to submit the projections to the state Environmental Regulation Commission for consideration . If adopted , the projections would serve as the state 's official estimates for sea-level rise and flooding impacts and would be used in developing future state plans and projects . <p> The bills come after DeSantis in August appointed Julie Nesheiwat as the state 's first chief resilience officer .
