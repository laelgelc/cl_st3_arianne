<p> WASHINGTON , Jan 14 ( Reuters ) - U.S. Senate Democratic leader Chuck Schumer said on Tuesday he believed a majority of 51 U.S. senators will approve a war powers resolution to block President Donald Trump from further military action , which would be a rebuke of the president 's policy in the Republican-controlled chamber . <p> " We believe ( it ) will get 51 votes that is needed to pass . And so , we will work out the timing , " Schumer told a weekly news conference . If passed by the House of Representatives and Senate , the measure does not need Trump 's signature to go into effect , although Democrats and Republicans disagreed over whether it is binding .
