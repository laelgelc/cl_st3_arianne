<p> FRANKFURT , Jan 17 ( Reuters ) - Germany 's Lufthansa on Friday said that flights to Teheran were suspended until March 28 , extending a ban of flights over Iran after a Ukranian airliner was shot down last week . <p> Lufthansa , the only major European carrier flying to Iran since 2018 , had initially suspended flights until the end of January . The company 's Austrian Airlines business is also adhering to the suspension , it said . <p> All 176 passengers and crew aboard the Ukraine International Airlines plane were killed after being shot down by a missile soon after take-off from Tehran . <p> ( Reporting by Ilona Wissenbach Writing by Edward Taylor Editing by David Goodman ) <p> Our global editorial team of more than 50 journalists and about 250 freelancers covers the world 's under-reported stories at the heart of aid , development , women 's and LGBT+ rights , human trafficking , property rights , and climate change .
