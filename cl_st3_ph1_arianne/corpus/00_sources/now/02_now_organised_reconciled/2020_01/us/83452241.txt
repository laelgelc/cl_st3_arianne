The locusts " reproduce rapidly and , if left unchecked , their current numbers could grow 500 times by June , " the United Nations says . <p> Is this another effect of a changing climate ? " Swarms migrate with the wind and can cover 100 to 150 kilometers ( 62 to 93 miles ) in a day . An average swarm can destroy as much food crops in a day as is sufficient to feed 2,500 people .
