<h> ' Navigating Life 's Currents ' six-week course <p> " Navigating Life 's Currents : Finding balance , resilience , surrender , ease , and connection " will be presented on six Wednesdays from 1 -- 2:30 p.m. , Jan. 29 -- March 4 , in the Orcas Island Community Foundation Volk Conference Room . The program is free of charge but donations appreciated and all proceeds go to OICF . <p> There are so many elements of life that can cause us to experience disconnection from self and others : grief ; loss ; aging ; physical or mental pain ; anxiety ; fear ; shame ; loneliness ; illness ; climate change and so much more . Together , we can offer each other courage and meaning in our often-challenging human condition.This group will be a six-week conversation based on thoughtful and reflective inquiry , providing support for integration of new possibilities . It is not intended to be a therapy group or mental health intervention . <p> Facilitators <p> Bonnie Burg , LICSW , BCD , is a clinical social worker who sees individual and couples in her psychotherapy practice in Eastsound .
