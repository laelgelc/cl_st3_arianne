<p> HONG KONG , Jan 3 ( Reuters ) - HSBC will suspend overnight services at 19 ATM clusters in Hong Kong on weekends and public holidays , the bank said on Friday , two days after its branches and ATMs were targeted during anti-government protests . <p> Hong Kong is the bank 's single most important market , accounting for just over half of its $12.5 billion pre-tax profits in the first half of 2019 . <p> Until mid-December , HSBC had largely escaped direct involvement in the often violent anti-government protests that have shaken Hong Kong for more than six months even as other companies with perceived links to Beijing saw premises vandalised . <p> Since then , HSBC has drawn the ire of some protesters who accuse it of being complicit in action by the authorities against activists trying to raise money to support their campaign . <p> HSBC strongly denies any connection . <p> The 19 groups of ATM machines are located in common protest hotspots . As a precautionary measure they will be closed from 8 p.m. to 6 a.m. on Fridays , Saturdays , Sundays , the days before public holidays and on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a statement . <p> HSBC on Thursday closed two branches and seven indoor ATM clusters after they were vandalised during anti-government protests on New Year 's Day and Christmas Eve . <p> The famous pair of lions that guard its city centre headquarters were daubed with graffiti and briefly set alight after being doused in a flammable liquid .
