<p> YouTube has been recommending and serving ads against videos that feature denial of or misinformation about climate change , according to a new report . The identified videos had 21.1 million views between them and might be suggested to users after a search for " climate change , " " global warming , " or the conspiracy theory " climate manipulation . " <p> The study , from the nonprofit activist group Avaaz , found ads from 108 brands running on videos that contained climate change misinformation . Those brands included major companies like Samsung , Uber , Nintendo , Showtime , Harley Davidson , and Warner Bros . Ads for environmental groups , including Greenpeace and the World Wildlife Fund , appeared on the videos as well . Avaaz says 10 advertisers , including Samsung and the environmental groups , said they were unaware their ads appeared on these videos ( the others did n't respond ) . <p> The report also states that YouTube promotes these videos in its " up next " box , but the evidence is less clear-cut .
