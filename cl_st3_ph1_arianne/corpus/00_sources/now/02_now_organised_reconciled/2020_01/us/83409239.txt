" The lower Oslo District Court ruled in January 2019 . <p> Fredrik Sejersted , the lawyer representing the Norwegian government , argued that the allotments of licenses in the Barents Sea have been dealt with several times in the Storting , or Parliament -- where it was backed by a large majority .
