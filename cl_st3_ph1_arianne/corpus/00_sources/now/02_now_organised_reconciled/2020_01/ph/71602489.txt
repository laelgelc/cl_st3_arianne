<h> Duck-billed platypus on brink of extinction due to climate change , scientists warn <p> The duck-billed platypus is close to being wiped out permanently partly due to climate change , scientists have warned . <p> Increasing numbers of the unique animal are becoming stranded as rivers dry up , according to new research . <p> Australia 's devastating drought is currently having a critical impact on the venomous egg-laying mammal , which is listed as " endangered " in the country . <p> Lead author of the research Dr Gilad Bino , of New South Wales University ( UNSW ) , says action must be taken to save the platypus . <p> The platypus is being threatened by climate change ( Picture : SWNS ) <p> The conservation biologist said : " There is an urgent need for a national risk assessment for the platypus to assess its conservation status , evaluate risks and impacts and prioritise management in order to minimise any risk of extinction .
