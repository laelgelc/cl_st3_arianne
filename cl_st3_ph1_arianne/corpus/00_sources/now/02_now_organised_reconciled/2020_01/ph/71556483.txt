<h> Business groups urge Duterte to sign Rice Tariffication bill <p> Business groups yesterday urged President Rodrigo Duterte to sign into law the rice tariffication measure , which will open up importation of this staple food , to ensure food security in the country . <p> The petition was signed by 13 business and professional groups led by the American Chamber of Commerce of the Philippines , Management Association of the Philippines , Bankers Association of the Philippines , Makati Business Club , Investment House Association of the Philippines , Financial Executives Institute of the Philippines , Foundation for Economic Freedom , Judicial Reform Initiative , Philippine Investment Funds Association , Organization of Socialized Housing Developers of the Philippines , Philippine Institute of Certified Accountants , Semiconductor and Electronics Industries in the Philippines , Inc. , and Shareholders ' Association of the Philippines . <p> The groups said they strongly support ongoing efforts and measures of the administration to liberalize the economy to unleash its full potential to ensure ? sustainable , robust and inclusive economic growth , while ensuring better quality of life for Filipinos through affordable food . <p> The groups believe that upon enactment of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and the Senate , the financial resources , management expertise , logistics support and extensive nationwide distribution system of the private sector will be harnessed to ensure food security , particularly of the most important food stable -- rice . <p> They said that stability in supply and prices of rice is ensured because the law protects both the producers and consumers . <p> The measure imposes 35 percent on imported rice . The high tariff is going to discourage imports , especially during harvest season , but will facilitate faster importation of this commodity during tight supply situation . The high tariff wall will protect farmers from the onslaught of unabated imports . <p> This also means that consumers will be assured of enough supply and protected from high prices of rice . ? The groups emphasized the need to balance the interest of both producers and consumers . <p> It could be recalled that insufficient supply of rice has led to the skyrocketing prices of this staple food middle of last year. ? " <p> On the part of consumers , maintaining a strategic reserve of rice stock is essential to guard against supply dislocation that may arise in case of natural calamities , climate change and disruption in the world market of rice . ? Once the measure is signed into law by President Duterte , the government is expected to utilize the revenue collection from the rice tariffs to provide sustained essential support services and facilities -- irrigation , better seedlings , ? modern growing and efficient harvesting technology , safe agricultural chemicals and post-harvest facilities to further assist the farm sector to be more productive and increase rural income . <p> Earmarking and judicious utilization of the rice import tariff revenue for these purposes will greatly help attain these objectives , the groups concluded .
