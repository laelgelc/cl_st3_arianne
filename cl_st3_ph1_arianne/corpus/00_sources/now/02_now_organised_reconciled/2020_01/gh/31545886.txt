<h> Make Spiritual And Physical Care A Priority In 2020 - Campbell <p> Reverend Father Andrew Campbell , Parish Priest of Christ the King Catholic Church has asked Ghanaians especially Christians to make spiritual and physical care a priority in 2020 . <p> He described the two elements as critical due to their role in propelling productivity and enhancing Christian lives in the next decade . <p> In a sermon during a Eucharistic Celebration to usher parishioners into the New Year , Rev Campbell counselled Christians to be truthful in all their deeds as they were the light of the world . <p> " Give up the old toxic habits such as smoking , poor eating , alcoholism , self-centeredness , fornication , greed , envy and watching of pornography . All these will work against your physical health , " he said . <p> " Rather focus on maintaining a constant relationship with God and your fellow human being , make good friends , exercise regularly , constantly forgive others , cultivate the habit of sharing with the poor and needy , and love your neighbours as Jesus Christ loved the world " . <p> Touching on the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to reduce the adverse effects of climate change by planting trees and use natural resources judiciously . <p> " Environmental issues are dear to the heart of our Holy Father Pope Francis .
