<h> ' Agriculture will always result in GHG emissions ' -- Creed <p> It has been highlighted by the Minister for Agriculture , Food and the Marine , Michael Creed , that the EU 's long-term strategy ' A Clean Planet for All ' acknowledges that agricultural production " will always result in non-CO2 greenhouse gas ( GHG ) emissions " , such as methane and nitrous oxide . <p> In making the comment , Minister Creed clarified : " These gases can be reduced by 2050 thanks to efficient and sustainable production methods .
