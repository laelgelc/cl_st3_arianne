<p> Today President of Ireland Michael D Higgins planted a Native Sessile Oak tree in the Phoenix Park to commemorate Ireland 's launch of International Year of Plant Health 2020 . The UN 's Food and Agriculture Organisation ( FAO ) has chosen the year 2020 to raise awareness on how protecting plant health can help end hunger , reduce poverty , protect the environment , and boost economic development . Plants make up 80 per cent of the food we eat , and produce 98 per cent of the oxygen we breathe . Yet , they are under constant and increasing threat from pests and diseases . Climate change and human activities are altering ecosystems , reducing biodiversity and creating conditions where pests can thrive . At the same time , international travel and trade has tripled in volume in the last decade and can quickly spread pests and diseases around the world causing great damage to native plants and the environment . <p> The importance of protecting plants , wildlife and our natural environment has been a constant theme throughout the Presidency of Michael D Higgins .
