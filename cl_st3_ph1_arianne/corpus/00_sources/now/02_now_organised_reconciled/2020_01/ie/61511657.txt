<h> JetBlue to be first major US airline to offset all emissions from domestic flights <p> US low-cost carrier JetBlue Airways says that it will offset all of its emissions from domestic flights beginning in July 2020 , becoming the first major US airline to do so in an effort to reduce its carbon footprint from flight operations . <p> In addition , the New-York-based airline said on 6 January that it will begin to use sustainable aviation fuel , made from waste and residue raw materials , on flights departing from San Francisco beginning in mid-2020 .
