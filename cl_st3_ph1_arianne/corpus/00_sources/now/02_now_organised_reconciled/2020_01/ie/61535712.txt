<h> Nicholls Transport opens LNG station with Gasrec <p> Nicholls Transport has opened an LNG refuelling station at its Kent , UK base . <p> Opened together with Gasrec , the station will be used to support an initial order for 10 Stralis NP 460 6x2 tractor units from local dealer Haynes Trucks . <p> Nicholls follows a steady line of major own-account and 3PL fleets in embracing a cleaner and lower cost fuel . <p> The LNG station is the first of its kind in Kent and perfectly situated for freight traffic bound for the continent , being less than five miles from Junction 5 of the M2 -- with Nicholls making the site available to third parties by arrangement . <p> Paul Nicholls , Managing Director of Nicholls Transport , says , " Moving to alternative fuels has been high on our agenda -- but gas is currently the only technology that 's ready for 44-tonne operation .
