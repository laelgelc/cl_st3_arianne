Geothermal only works in a few areas . Hydroelectric generally requires large bodies of water and the right environmental circumstances and has since ( in some cases ) been found to cause wildlife problems . A global strategy whereby every region generates " green " electricity in the way that works best for itself , and whereby we pool our resources would be the ideal way forward .
