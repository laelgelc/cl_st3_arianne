<p> Although packed with geopolitical surprises 2019 turned out to be better than expected for financial assets . Equities and bonds rallied together reversing last year 's ' unusual occurrence ' of both performing badly . <p> While global growth is picking up , forecasts for 2020 indicate growth rather than a boom . After the US Federal Reserve 's pivot away from tightening , central banks are in dovish mode , a reassuring stance for risk markets , hungry for liquidity . <p> Although economic activity may not be booming , what has really helped is that two of the biggest risks facing markets -- trade tensions and a no-deal Brexit -- have both receded . <p> As well as the lessening of those risks , the resilience of US consumption , while the rest of the world shuddered to a virtual stop , can not be overstated . Worries that weakness in the US manufacturing sector would spill into consumption have so far not materialised . <p> China 's economic outlook remains critical for the emerging market universe , especially those geared towards China 's fixed investment cycle .
