<h> Any attempt at charging for the Heath ponds will be resisted <p> ' The City can not be allowed to ' manipulate health and safety ' to enclose the Heath bathing ponds ' <p> * THE Corporation of London unwisely promoted a private film last summer about those strictly limited and marvellous scarce resources , the swimming ponds of Hampstead Heath . <p> First they assisted and encouraged the making of this film . Then they staged showings of it at cinemas ; it was shown across north London and beyond . What they did not do was to budget for additional staffing resources to cope with the sudden , predictable and inevitable spike in demand for them . <p> Unfortunately all of this has been entangled with the regrettable death of a swimmer last summer , who suffered a heart attack , and the subsequent comments of the Health and Safety Executive about the number of lifeguards to swimmers . The corporation offer no statistical evidence that the rise in numbers is part of a trend . <p> Moreover , these types of days occur only a few times in a year . The City can not @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ enclose the Heath bathing ponds by charges and or other means . <p> The admirable rescue staff at the ponds were overwhelmed last summer by this sudden unbudgeted increase in numbers as angry and frustrated people , brought there by this film , sought admission to the ponds on the hottest , sunniest days -- always peaks in seasonal demand . The staff have had to carry the load of this shortsighted bit of unbudgeted marketing and promotion . <p> Now the City corporation seeks to deal with this problem , of its own making , by portraying it as some ineluctable and mysterious expression of social and climate change -- and ( you guessed it ) entertaining , yet again , the notion of charging for use of these bathing ponds , which like the rest of the Heath , have been free since the Hampstead Heath Act of 1872 made them so . <p> Understandably , and inevitably , any attempt to use this as justification for charging Heath users will be reasonably resisted as they always have been . <p> The corporation -- certainly short-term -- has @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . There is no proper statistical basis for deducing a change in the trend of usage . <p> Any attempt to impose charges would be unscientific and self-serving opportunism by the Corporation of London in an enclosure of the Heath , contrary to the intention of the Hampstead Heath Act .
