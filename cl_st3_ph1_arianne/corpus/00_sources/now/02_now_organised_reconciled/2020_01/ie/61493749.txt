<h> Tipperary historical group plan a busy start to new year <h> CAHIR SOCIAL AND HISTORICAL SOCIETY <p> Now that we are in a New Year , Cahir Social and Historical Society announce that they have organised a diverse range of lectures for early 2020 . <p> Starting on January 23 , a well known son of Cahir , Dr. Kieran Hickey will give a talk on " The Vulnerability of Ireland to Extreme Climate Change " . <p> Kieran will be followed on February 13 by Pat Holland 's lecture , " The History of Mountain Rescue in Ireland " , which will be based on his recently launched publication . <p> Pat lives locally and has given lectures hosted by the Society in the past .
