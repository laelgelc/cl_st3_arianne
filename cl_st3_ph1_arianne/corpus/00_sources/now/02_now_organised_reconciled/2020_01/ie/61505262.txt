<h> A drive-through , not as we know it , to reinvent fast food <p> It 's a drive-through but not as we know it . Billed as Ireland 's first organic drive-through fast food restaurant , a new dining experience will open in Carrick-on-Shannon this summer . <p> Behind the vision of fast food from the farm via an innovative drive-through are Liam and Justina Gavin of Drumanilra organic farm . Transforming a former KFC premises in Carrick , the family will be reinventing the fast food concept with their drive-through . <p> The Drumanilra Farm Kitchen will feature ethically and locally produced meats as well as organic vegetarian , vegan and ' free-from ' options . The signature Dexter burger , made from organic pedigree Dexter beef , is raised on the Drumanilra organic farm , a 15-minute drive away . <p> Vegetarian and vegan food will feature prominently on the menu , using organic salads and vegetables grown in the farm 's market garden and by other local growers , said the couple behind the drive-through . <p> Liam and Justina moved back to the family farm in 2012 .
