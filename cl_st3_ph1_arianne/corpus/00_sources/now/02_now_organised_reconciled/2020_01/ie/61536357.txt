" With brutal candour he warns , " Do not expect any idealism . This book is about ruthless pragmatism . " Such is the influence of the book on the Finnish MEP he even makes his advisers and assistants " know the text by heart . " <p> Split into four main chapters ; gaining power , get re-nominated , steer the legislative process and gain public support . At only 89 pages , the book could be described more like a pamphlet . But its brevity has the advantage of making it short enough for most MEP 's attention span . Apart from MEPs , " How to run the European Parliament " is also useful to anyone else who has an interest in the European parliament or wants to influence its politics . It makes a useful addition to the must-have reference book ' The European Parliament ' by Richard Corbett MEP , Francis Jacobs and Michael Shackleton .
