<h> Loftus throws his hat in election race <p> Founder of Ireland 's Future , Gerry Loftus , will contest the upcoming general election as an independent candidate . <p> The Mayo farmer , who was born in Cloughbrack , at the foot of Nephin Mountain , views this as a " critical election " for the people of Mayo and Ireland . <p> Announcing his intention to run he said : " We , in the west , and indeed across rural Ireland , are not being represented , politically , socially or agriculturally . We have been underfunded by successive Governments the past 25 years , with all investment going to the east and south .
