In keeping with a directive from Croke Park , Cavan county board voted to change its underage football competitions from even ( U14 , U16 , U18 ) to odd ( U13 , U15 , 17 ) age groups two years ago . The Minor Championships of 2018 and 2019 were played as U17 competitions .
