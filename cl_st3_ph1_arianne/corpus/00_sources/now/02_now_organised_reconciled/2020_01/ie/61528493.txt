The Mediterranean diet in other words . The diet of people for whom meat was a luxury , forcing them to get creative with plant-based foods available to them . First written about in the ' 60s , it 's tried and tested and as old as the hills , and it is associated with a reduction in all-cause mortality ( any cause of death ) in observational studies .
