They conducted workshops with 376 5-7 year olds from a range of school settings with a number of different tasks including : choosing between gender-specific and gender-neutral toys ; drawing and naming an engineer , and ; rating male and female competency at a number of gender-specific roles . One of the most striking findings emerging from the research was that 96% of boys drew a male engineer while just over 50% of girls drew a female engineer . <p> This , along with the other data , indicates that gender stereotypes emerge in young children and that they are particularly strong among boys .
