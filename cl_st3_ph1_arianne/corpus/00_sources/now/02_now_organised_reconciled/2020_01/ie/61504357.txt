were confirmed on the Fine Gael party ticket for the next election . <p> It came as the party prepared for the very real possibility of an election that Christmas over the Garda Whistleblower controversy . <p> Now , the pair will hope to regain the two seats which Fine Gael held between 2007 and 2016 , with former Finance Minister and party leader Michael Noonan stepping down at this Indeed , it 's entirely possible many voters from these two tribes could plump for the councillor on this basis . <p> The entry of the Greens into local politics in 2019 is comparable with the emergence of Sinn Fein in the 2014 local election , something which was crowned by the party securing a Dail seat for Maurice Quinlivan , the first time the party had a local Oireachtas seat since the 1920s . <p> But six months ago , Sinn Fein saw its local vote decimated , plunging to just two seats at council . <p> However , Mr Quinlivan will take comfort in the fact that his party 's vote at least held out in his power base @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> " I would be confident enough .
