Whichever way one looks at it , there is now no stopping Singapore 's astutely , calibrated moves to be Asia 's premier bunkering hub . <p> Pavilion Energy , a subsidiary of Singapore 's state-owned company Temasek , and Total Marine Fuels Global Solutions have together signed a 10-year deal to help develop LNG bunkering in Singapore . <p> The agreement is binding , and follows an earlier non-binding deal signed in June 2018 . <p> Key to the agreement is the shared long-term use of a newly-built 12,000 cubic meters GTT Mark III LNG bunker vessel .
