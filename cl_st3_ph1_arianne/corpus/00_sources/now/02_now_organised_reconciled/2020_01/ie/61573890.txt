<h> What 's the point when the unelected are given the power ? <p> * I HAVE received postal vote documents for the Referendum on the Neighbourhood Plan for Dartmouth Park . <p> All looks very impressive , identical to the recent general election postal voting ballot paper and envelopes . Must have cost Camden -- that is to say , its residents -- a pretty penny to organise . <p> Shame no one at the council thought to include or to communicate at any stage beforehand details of what the plan is that it is now inviting us to vote on . <p> What a complete waste of money ! I presume we were all expected to search for the details on the council website , as we are all supposed to be online these days . <p> I shall not be bothering . Camden 's consultations are all notoriously cosmetic and bogus .
