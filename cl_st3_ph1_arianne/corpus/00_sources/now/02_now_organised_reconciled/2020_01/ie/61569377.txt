<h> Abu Dhabi to host the largest youth engagement <h> ABU DHABI , 27 days ago <p> The largest youth engagement event in the Middle East , The Middle East Youth Expo 2020 , will take place at Mubadala Arena located in Zayed Sports City , Abu Dhabi from February 3 to 5 , 2020 . <p> The three-day festival style event is the first of its kind in the region bringing together thousands of youth delegates to collaborate and explore sustainable solutions for both people and planet aligned to the UN Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) and the UAE Vision 2021 . <p> Over 70 international and local speakers will present their own stories of success and challenges to inspire and empower the youth delegates to achieve their life goals as young leaders of tomorrow and future ' responsible global citizens of the world ' . <p> The Middle East Youth Expo 2020 will have over 10 immersive experiences including the ' Youth Now ' hub where youth delegates will be able to join in debates and discussions to work together to solve some of the world 's most important challenges . <p> Over 30 workshops and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ allowing the youth delegates access to all the speakers on everything from the impact of plastic pollution in our oceans , sustainable fashion , social entrepreneurship , developing renewable energy solutions , the power of technology &amp; AI to localising the SDGs and the empowerment of youth as change makers . <p> Developed in strategic partnership with the UAE Ministry of Education , UAE Ministry of Climate Change &amp; Environment , UAE Ministry of Economy , UAE Ministry of Community Development , alongside local and international organisations including Department of Education &amp; Knowledge Abu Dhabi ( ADEK ) , UN Water , Dubai Cares , Emirates Nature - WWF , Central Bank of the UAE , PwC and attendance from representatives from the UN Sustainable Development Goals , UNHCR , and EXPO 2020 , the event is a ground breaking platform to support youth in the UAE . <p> Jaber Saeed Al Lamki , Executive Director of Media and Strategic Communications at the National Media Council , said : " Positive youth engagement is fundamental in society and the world today .
