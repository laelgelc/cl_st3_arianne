<h> Labour Party to run candidate in Mayo constituency <p> The Labour Party is to run a candidate in the four-seat Mayo constituency in next month 's general election . <p> Kamal Uddin will be contesting his fourth election in Mayo , having run in the 2014 and 2019 local elections and the 2016 general election . <p> He contested the 2014 and 2016 elections as an independent before joining the Labour Party ahead of last year 's local elections in the Castlebar Municipal District . <p> Mr. Uddin is originally from Bangladesh but moved to Mayo 20 years ago and lives in Castlebar with his family . He runs a small family business in Kiltimagh . <p> Ahead of the 2019 local elections , he said his priorities included giving a voice to the retail sector and doing more to help combat climate change . <p> He also stated he wanted to see the delivery of a strong public housing model in the area along with the continued delivery of improved community infrastructure , and to see improvements in services across rail , road and cycleways.
