<h> What is the IFA looking for from the next government ? <p> With the 2020 General Election fast approaching , a number of farm organisations have now revealed their election submissions and manifestos , outlining what they see as the main issues to be tackled by any future government . <p> Below are some of the key points from the election document from the Irish Farmers ' Association ( IFA ) -- whose annual general meeting ( AGM ) is being held today , Monday , January 27 and tomorrow , Tuesday , January 28 . <p> The IFA 's submission is divided into several sections , covering a number of areas , including : the Common Agricultural Policy ( CAP ) ; Brexit ; EU trade policy ; the environment and climate action ; and retailers . Some of the main points of the document are summarised below . <h> CAP <p> The association is formally calling for the CAP Budget to be increased to take account of inflation , and to compensate farmers for environmental requirements .
