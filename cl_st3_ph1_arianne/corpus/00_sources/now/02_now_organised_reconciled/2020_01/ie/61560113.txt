<h> IFA to host ' Climate Action in Agriculture ' event tomorrow <p> The Irish Farmers ' Association ( IFA ) is set to host an event tomorrow , Tuesday , January 21 , in an effort to " rebalance the debate around climate action " . <p> The event is titled ' Climate Action in Agriculture -- A Balanced Approach ' and will feature " one of the foremost experts in the world on the important role of farming in the climate debate " , according to the IFA . <p> Dr. Frank Mitloehner from UC Davis , California , will be the keynote speaker at the event and will " bring balance , with scientific evidence , to the discussion about the contribution of agriculture to the climate challenge " . <p> Other speakers at the event will include Prof. John Fitzgerald , chair of the Climate Change Advisory Council ( CCAC ) , and Teagasc 's Dr. Frank O'Mara . <p> Speaking in advance of the event , outgoing IFA president , Joe Healy , said : " Farmers are tired of being kicked around and scapegoated when it comes to climate action . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for Irish farmers , given the high standards we adhere to and the quality of the food we produce . <p> For example , 90% of Ireland 's beef exports are now in an audit and carbon footprinting programme and 100% of milk production is completing a carbon auditing cycle . <p> " This is unique -- we are the only country in the world that measures , monitors and manages carbon from farm to fork .
