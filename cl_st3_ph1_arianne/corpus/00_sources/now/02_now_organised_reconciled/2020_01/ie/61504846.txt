The Programme will be developed in the context of the proposed EU Regulation on a European Maritime , Fisheries and Aquaculture Fund , which is presently being negotiated by the EU co-legislators with a view to enactment in the Autumn of 2020 . The new operational programme will be co-funded by the Government of Ireland and the European Union . <p> Speaking at the launch of the public consultation , Minister Creed said : " My Department 's present EMFF Operational Programme for the seafood sector is nearing its end and will be wound up in 2021 .
