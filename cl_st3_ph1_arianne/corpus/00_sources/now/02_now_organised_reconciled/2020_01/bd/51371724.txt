This environmentally friendly traditional cultivation technique utilizes the natural resources of wetlands to grow vegetables and other crops almost all year round , providing numerous social , economic , agricultural , and ecological benefits to the local communities . <p> These pictures of floating gardens shot in Barisal 's Banaripara is a testament that how farmers in Bangladesh , through various adaptive and coping mechanisms , are staying productive and facing the challenges of climate change effectively . Banaripara municipal area stands on the River Shandhya while the entire upazila is crisscrossed by many rivers . As land gets scarce at many places , ingenious farmers grow their seedbeds and plants on floating gardens .
