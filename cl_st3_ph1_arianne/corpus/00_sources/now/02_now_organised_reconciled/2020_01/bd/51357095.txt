<h> We really must stop starting wars <p> Let us not go again to war . Let us not go to war because we can not afford to " win " another war . There would be no winning this war . There is only losing , at a cost that staggers every dimension on all sides -- in lives , in money , and in the squandered opportunity to do anything better with our collective time and resources on this already beset planet . <p> I 'm speaking here of war with Iran , our current precipice . But I issue the warning more generally , too . Bookmark this page and pull it up as prophylaxis anytime the drums of war begin rattling anew : War is n't just tragic . It is increasingly dumb and pointless , too . <p> War is becoming an outdated means of human conflict resolution . But there should be no debate that many of America 's recent wars have ended in misery for all . Our politicians talk of war as a last resort , but that is only to keep up appearances , because the truth is too terrible to admit : that our ever-more-expensive war machine ( a cost of nearly $740 billion in 2020 ) can buy us little peace . Rather than a last resort , war now offers no resort . War can no longer be defended as the thing to do after everything else has failed . War must instead be seen as failure itself . <p> I had thought the futility of war might be grasped by most American politicians by now .
