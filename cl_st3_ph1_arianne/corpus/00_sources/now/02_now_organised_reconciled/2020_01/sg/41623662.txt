Instead , you 've ordered your ride to come to you . <p> The car that arrives has no driver or steering wheel . As you climb in , the electric motor silently comes to life , and the car whisks you into an aerodynamic peloton of vehicles , slipping through cross-traffic at intersections without stopping . <p> The prevailing belief is that a system of self-driving cars will solve several environmental and social problems without us needing to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ our travel habits . <p> Unfortunately , this future will almost certainly never come to pass . Self-driving cars , left to their own devices , will likely do more harm than good . To avoid that outcome , we 'll have to turn off autopilot and shape the system of autonomous mobility so that it best serves both our needs and the needs of the planet . <p> Cars might also become less energy-efficient as they 're modified to meet the demands of users . Passengers may run them at higher speeds because they 're safer , which consumes more energy due to aerodynamic resistance . Car manufacturers may also begin to design larger vehicles to accommodate mobile offices and bedrooms . <p> This might be mitigated somewhat by electric vehicles , but that electricity may still come from fossil fuels . Plus , bigger vehicles with bigger batteries will produce more carbon emissions as a byproduct of their construction . <p> These processes could , theoretically , be carbon-neutral , but that may not occur quickly enough . This would be a more sustainable option . <p> Unfortunately , people are attached to their cars .
