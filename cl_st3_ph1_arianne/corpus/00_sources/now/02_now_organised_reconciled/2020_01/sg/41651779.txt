<h> Meet the Eco-Business A-Listers : Janice Lao , hospitality trailblazer <h> Janice Lao , who steered the Hongkong and Shanghai Hotels Group 's shift away from single-use plastics last year , talks about being a sustainability leader , supporting her daughter 's burgeoning awareness of climate change , and her unsustainable guilty pleasure . <p> Janice Lao has earned a reputation for being a pioneer in every sector she 's worked in . In her current role as director of corporate responsibility of the Hongkong &amp; Shanghai Hotels Group , she is communicating sustainability in luxury .
