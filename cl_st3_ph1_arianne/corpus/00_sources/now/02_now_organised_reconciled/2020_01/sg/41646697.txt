<h> Explainer : What is carbon neutrality , and can it really be achieved ? <h> From banks to builders , more companies are going carbon neutral . Carbon offsetting is not without controversy , but in cases where sustainability can only be approached and not achieved , it may be the appropriate way to balance out emissions . <h> Share this story <p> The term " carbon neutral " is being increasingly slung around alongside phrases like " net zero emissions " . <p> From paint manufacturers and the livestock industry , to banks and developers , more corporations in diverse industries are announcing plans to go carbon neutral . <p> It 's a simple enough idea : the sum of all the greenhouse gases that you put into and take out of the atmosphere should balance out to zero . But in practice , it 's proven to be a confusing and divisive concept .
