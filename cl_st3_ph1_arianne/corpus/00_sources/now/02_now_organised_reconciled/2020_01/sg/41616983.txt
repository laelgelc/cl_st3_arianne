<p> Electronica Finance created a business model out of providing machine loans to MSMEs . It says it opened 55 branches across India , served more than 7,500 MSME customers , and has over Rs 1,150 crore worth Assets Under Management ( AUM ) . <p> The MSME sector in India accounts for 45 percent of manufacturing output and more than 40 per cent of the country 's exports . These numbers show how critical the sector is to India 's manufacturing sector and its economy . <p> However , it is not easy for manufacturing MSMEs to sustain their momentum . <p> Expensive machinery and equipment needs to be upgraded at regular intervals to maintain and increase production . MSMEs need to also purchase new machinery to meet demand .
