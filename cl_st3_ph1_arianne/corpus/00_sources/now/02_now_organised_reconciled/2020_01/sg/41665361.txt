The final phase-out is likely to be finalised at the treaty meet in Rwanda this October . <p> Besides charting out a more aggressive Montreal Protocol , Modi and Obama announced a slew of measures to boost the use of solar energy in India , building on the initiatives relating to energy security and clean energy they discussed at the 2014 summit in Washington . Modi said the two nations would continue working " shoulder to shoulder " . <p> After China , the US and India are the world 's largest emitters of greenhouse gases ( GHG ) .
