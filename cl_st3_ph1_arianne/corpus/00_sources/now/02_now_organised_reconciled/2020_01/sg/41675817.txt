<h> Karachi 's green buses to be powered by dung <h> In Karachi , a plan for a natural gas powered bus transport system may bring unexpected benefits to a cattle colony , promising sanitation , energy , and enhanced livelihood prospects . <p> Dung from cattle could help run the 213 buses of the Karachi Breeze Red Line , one of the five bus rapid transit ( BRT ) lines that Pakistan 's most populous cities will be getting in the next two years .
