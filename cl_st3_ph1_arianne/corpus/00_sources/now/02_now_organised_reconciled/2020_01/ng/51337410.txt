The challenge , however , is that most of them have said more than they have done in reining in the monster . Some have , by their inept , visionless and corrupt leadership driven by greed sent many more citizens into the ignoble pit of poverty . Worse still , by starting or stoking the fires of preventable wars and political crises , including terrorism not a few of them have exacerbated the pangs of poverty , claiming millions of innocent lives every year . <p> Add that to natural disasters such as drought , floods , tsunamis , tornados , hurricanes heightened by climate change and the picture of the increasing negative impact of poverty gets clearer .
