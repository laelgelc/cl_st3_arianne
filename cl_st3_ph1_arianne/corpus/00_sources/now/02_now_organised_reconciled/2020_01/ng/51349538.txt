<h> How Nigerian women fared in 2019 <p> In this report , ENE OSANG looks at how Nigerian women fared in 2019 ; their progress , challenges , achievements with gender equality and the empowerment of women as it relates to the Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) . <p> Nigeria is one of the 189 countries that adopted the Beijing Declaration and Platform for Action ( BDPfA ) in 1995 . Not only did this provide institutional legitimacy around the demand for accountability on women 's human rights , it also enables the emergence and coalescing of women 's groups and gender equality advocates around a broad set of shared aspirations . <p> Since that time , new laws and policies that affirm the BDPfA commitments have emerged at national level and in the states ; institutions responsible for promoting gender justice have been created ; the experiences and challenges of women and girls have reflected in some critical policy discussions ; and funding support to improve the situation of women and girls in Nigeria , has been made available through government budgets and by several international organizations . <p> However , there are still several challenges . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ gender equality and the empowerment of women are still as strong as they were twenty five years ago .
