<h> NEWS <h> Soka Gakkai President Calls for Support on Nuclear Disarmament and Youth Climate Action <p> By Dipen Barua <p> Buddhistdoor Global 2020-01-31 <p> In an annual peace proposal dated 26 January and titled " Toward Our Shared Future : Constructing an Era of Human Solidarity , " Daisaku Ikeda , president of the Soka Gakkai International ( SGI ) Buddhist network , urged world leaders to increase their efforts to rid the world of nuclear weapons and to take stronger measures to address the growing threat of climate change . <p> Each year since 1983 , SGI has issued a peace proposal highlighting global challenges and suggesting solutions and responses grounded in the Buddhist teaching . This year 's proposal in English will be published in full on the SGI website on 14 February . <p> SGI is the world 's largest lay Buddhist organization , claiming some 12 million members around the globe , who practice Nichiren Buddhism and are involved in promoting peace , cultural exchange , and education . <p> SGI president Daisaku Ikeda .
