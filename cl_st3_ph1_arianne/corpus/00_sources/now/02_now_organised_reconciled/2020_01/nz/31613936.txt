<h> Nod to ' Tairawhiti we want by 2050 ' <p> Gisborne district councillors have adopted Tairawhiti 2050 but not before making some last-minute changes . <p> The district 's first spatial plan has been more than a year in the making and sets aspirations for Gisborne 's development over the next 30 years . <p> At a Sustainable Tairawhiti committee meeting yesterday , councillors insisted on a higher target for getting Gisborne city residents out of cars and walking or cycling to work or school by 2050 -- from 20 percent to targets of 40 percent of adults and 80 percent of schoolchildren . <p> There was also debate over the target for 100 percent of vehicles to be powered by renewable energy in 30 years ' time . <p> Councillor Sandra Faulkner said that may not be a realistic target for business and industrial transport , which still ran on fossil fuels . <p> Councillor Shannon Dowsing said the disposal by 2050 of all fossil fuel vehicles currently in the district would cause its own environmental problems , and councillor Pat Seymour agreed . <p> But Bill Burdett believed the document needed to be treated as aspirational @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ how realistic the targets were , as no
