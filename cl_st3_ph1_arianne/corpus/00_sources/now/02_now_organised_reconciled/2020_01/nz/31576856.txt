<p> Small businesses affected by delays to Auckland 's City Rail Link ( CRL ) project say they have little faith that a hardship fund set up for them will be of much help . <p> The details of the fund were announced just before Christmas and businesses that meet certain criteria are being told to apply for rent assistance . <p> CRL said they were running a workshop on Thursday briefing people about the fund which offers rent assistance . <p> The owner of a florist shop , who did not want to be named , said the assistance , which caps at $75,000 , would not make up for loss of business and the rates they have to pay . <p> He said the people setting up the hardship fund failed to consult the businesses appropriately . <p> " They did n't even consult us about what they were going to do for us .
