<h> How to open a bookstore in Martinborough <p> In the second of our occasional series on bookstores around New Zealand , former Act MP and eternal provocateur Deborah Coddington writes about opening her bookstore in the Wairarapa . <p> I could have enjoyed a retired life among the vines , escaping in winter to suck up champagne on luxurious cruises , but I cashed in my term investment and poured it into a bookstore in Martinborough . What the hell made me choose a so-called dying industry to compete with Amazon ? My restless energy has never left me in peace . <p> I should have been driven insane back in the 80s , running a licensed restaurant in Russell with four school-age children living on the premises . Or perhaps it did , because I left a subsequent career as a feature writer at North &amp; South magazine to compile The 1996 Paedophile and Sex Offender Index , and notched up death threats in the process . <p> Then ( clearly certifiable at this stage ) I jumped at the chance to be a list MP for the Act Party .
