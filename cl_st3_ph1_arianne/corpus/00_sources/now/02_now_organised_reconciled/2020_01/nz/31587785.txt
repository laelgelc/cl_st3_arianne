<h> Fletcher Building takes down fences at Ihumatao <p> Fletcher Building is pulling down fences at Ihumatao , but the future of the disputed site is still to be settled . <p> Fletcher said its development at Ihumatao continues to be on hold at the Prime Minister 's request . <p> Construction of 480 homes has been on hold since July after hundreds of protesters occupied the land to stop the development . <p> Fletcher contractors have begun pulling down fences at the site . <p> But a spokesperson for Fletcher Building said it was just making minor changes to its traffic management plan because of reduced activity on the site . <p> The road to the maunga is being reopened . <p> Fletcher said discussions with all parties on the future of the site were " progressing " . <p> Pania Newton of protest group SOUL said she was pleased Fletchers had removed fencing from the whenua and she was hopeful the details of a resolution could be confirmed and announced in the coming days . <p> There will be a hui with whanau tonight . <p> " It 's amazing that we 're able to tell them @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ equipment on this whenua and to us that indicates that a resolution is just around the corner , " Newton said . <p> " We know that Kiingi Tuheitia wants this resolved through Ratana Pa , and last year Fletchers and the government advised us that there would be a resolution before the incoming year .
