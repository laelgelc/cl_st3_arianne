<h> Vodafone 's early finish initiative ' will have a big impact on morale ' <p> Telecommunications company Vodafone is letting some of its employees clock off early on a Friday afternoon , and it 's a win-win situation , says an organisational psychologist . <p> More than 2000 workers have been given an extra incentive to finish the summer weeks early , at 2pm on Fridays . <p> Vodafone acting HR director Katie Williams said it 's about making sure employees have the best work-home balance . <p> Organisational psychologist John Eatwell told Morning Report employees value time and work flexibility is one of the highest valued rewards a workplace can offer . <p> " Extra time , particularly during summer , is a great reward and will have a big impact on morale .
