<h> NZ youth call for greater ambition from the govt at UN climate talks <p> Climate Change Minister Paula Bennett arrived at the United Nations COP22 climate change conference on Monday to New Zealand youth calling out the government 's half-hearted approach to reducing its greenhouse gas emissions . <p> As New Zealand government negotiators looked on , youth from across the globe - coordinated by the New Zealand Youth Delegation - called for greater ambition to tackle climate change , asking New Zealand to play its part in global climate efforts to keep temperature increase within 1.5 degrees celsius . <p> " If the government takes the words of the Paris Agreement seriously , it needs to increase ambition and set a path for zero emissions by 2050 , " Alex Johnston , member of the New Zealand Youth Delegation , explains . <p> " By ratifying the Paris Agreement , but failing to act on global ambitions of keeping global warming to 1.5 degrees , we are saying ' yeah , nah ' to meaningful climate action . " <p> " Our current emissions reduction targets are simply inadequate . In the assessment , the government said that it would be relying on international carbon credits to meet its 2020 target , rather than a sole focus on reducing domestic emissions . <p> " To meet the enormous global challenge of climate change , we must have domestic policy that meaningfully reduces emissions .
