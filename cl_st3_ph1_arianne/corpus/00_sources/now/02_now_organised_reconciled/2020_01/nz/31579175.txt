<h> Battlelines drawn as council backs latest dam project <p> Greater Wellington Regional Council is heralding a controversial dam project for Wairarapa as necessary protection against the threat of climate change . <p> But environmentalists warn it is likely to increase CO2 emissions , drain natural waterways and create more water dependence , not less . <p> The council 's deputy chairperson Adrienne Staples told Summer Report scientists have predicted conditions were set to become even hotter and drier and a sustainable water supply was desperately needed . <p> She is backing plans by Wairarapa Water to build a dam at Wakamoekau to build capacity to deal with climate change challenges of diminishing water supply . <p> " Twenty years ago it was about irrigation for farms , now we are talking about water security for Wairarapa and that is in the face of the fact our weather over here is even hotter and drier . We are getting more and more low flows in our rivers .
