<h> Auckland 's buses to become fully electrified ? <p> Words : Nile Bijoux <p> 22 Jan 2020 <p> Auckland Transport and Vector have announced an agreement to explore the possibility of electrifying the entire 1360-strong bus fleet . <p> The feasibility study will begin immediately to assess the impact of an electric fleet on Auckland 's electricity grid . It falls in line with AT 's Low Emission Bus roadmap from 2018 , which detailed a commitment to completely electrify all new buses coming into Auckland from 2025 . By 2040 , the entire bus fleet will be electric . <p> " Electrifying the bus fleet would stop around 70,000 tonnes of CO2 from entering the atmosphere every year and address the problem of pollution from black carbon , which is at high levels in areas such as the city centre , " Auckland mayor Phil Goff said . Not to mention eliminating the noise pollution of the current diesel engines . <p> " In 2018 I committed Auckland to ensuring that all replacement buses purchased from 2025 would be non-carbon emitting .
