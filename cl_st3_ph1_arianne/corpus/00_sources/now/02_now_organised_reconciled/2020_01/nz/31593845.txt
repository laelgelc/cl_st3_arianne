<h> Rental prices rise fastest in Wellington city <p> People renting in Wellington city are facing record-breaking prices , with median weekly rent cracking the $600-per-week mark for the first time , according to Trade Me . <p> Its latest rental price index shows median prices in the capital had jumped $35 a week by December , a 6.2 percent increase on the same month a year before . <p> The most popular rental property in New Zealand last month was a two-bedroom house in the Wellington suburb of Mt Victoria for $550 a week , which had 95 enquiries in the first two days . <p> Trade Me Property 's Aaron Clancy said rents in Wellington city had been rising the fastest of the main centres . <p> " Tenants are rushing to find a rental in the capital and the number of enquiries for Wellington city rentals rose 8 percent on the year prior , as many tenants spent their summer holiday looking for a new place to live .
