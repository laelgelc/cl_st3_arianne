Photo : Lynn Grieveson <h> Food prices fall overall but fruit and vegetable prices spike <p> Food prices fell for the third consecutive month in December as cheaper groceries and drinks offset a sharp rise in fresh fruit and vegetables . <p> Official numbers show prices fell 0.2 percent on the previous month . <p> Grocery prices fell one percent as a group , driven by such items as chips , biscuits , some dairy and meat products , and soft drinks . <p> The biggest price rises were fruit and vegetables , especially kiwifruit , which hit a record high average price of more than $8.20 a kilogram , nearly double the price the year before . <p> Annual food inflation remained at 2.4 percent . <p> Food prices make up about a fifth of the overall inflation rate . <p> This article was originally published on RNZ and re-published with permission . <p> We value fearless , independent journalism .
