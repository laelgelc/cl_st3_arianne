<h> Ex tycoon 's nurse fights on for millions <p> A rugby and business legend with longstanding links to New Zealand is still being pursued in US courts by his former personal nurse for millions of dollars . <p> Former British &amp; Irish Lions rugby star and billionaire Sir Tony O'Reilly now has to give evidence in a US court fight with his longtime nurse and assistant over her claim he reneged on a promise of US$40m in stock for her as compensation . <p> Ex Heinz boss O'Reilly , whose Independent News &amp; Media once owned the New Zealand Herald and papers on four continents , declared bankruptcy in 2015 in the Bahamas and his trustee wants the US courts to stop the action by his former nurse of 16 years , Sabina Vidunas , which alleges a breach of his contract with her for the stock compensation . He argues the Bahamas bankruptcy applies also in America . <p> O'Reilly , aged 83 , retired from heading H J Heinz worldwide as the highest paid CEO in America , became a billionaire and was Ireland 's richest man .
