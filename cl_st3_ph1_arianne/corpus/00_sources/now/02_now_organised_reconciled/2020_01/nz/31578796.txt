<h> Britain lacks means to be truly independent of EU <p> Brexit is a Tory invention and pro-Europeans must still fight the prospect of EU exile , writes Will Hutton . <p> In two weeks , Britain leaves the EU . Those vast marches , the crowded public meetings , the indefatigable Remain campaigners , the great speeches , the parliamentary wheeler-dealing and principled resignations were all for nothing . The " get Brexit done " Tories , exploiting the least electable Labour leader ever , won the election and were handed an 80-seat majority . The die is cast . <p> For the political class , the issue has become toxic . Boris Johnson wants Brexit expunged from the lexicon so the new normal is for Britain to be wholly outside the EU . Labour , flattened by its epic defeat , is agreed that to be pro-EU is political death . Remain Britain -- half the population -- has no champion . <p> It is a first-order political miscalculation .
