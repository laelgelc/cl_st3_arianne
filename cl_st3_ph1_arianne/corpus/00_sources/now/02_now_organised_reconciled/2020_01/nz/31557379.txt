<h> Changing South : Stargazing <p> WATCH the video above <p> They ca n't see stars at home so they 're flocking to see them here . Astro-tourism is putting the twinkle into Tekapo . <p> Pull up into Tekapo 's Church of the Good Shepherd car park after the sun goes down and you 'll find a swarm of international visitors , many capturing , on even the smallest digital camera , incredible images of a galaxy they 've never seen with the naked eye . <p> The Mackenzie night sky is recognised internationally as a Dark Sky Reserve and there have been attempts in the past to gain UNESCO World Heritage status . Mackenzie District Mayor , Graham Smith , wonders how the town would cope , amid his estimate that visitor numbers are doubling year-on-year . <p> " If we got World Heritage status ... we would seriously have to contemplate how we were going to manage the tourists .
