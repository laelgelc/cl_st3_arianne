<p> Ioane Teitiota had claimed his right to life was infringed by New Zealand authorities deporting him . <p> Mr Teitiota had sought refugee status in New Zealand , claiming climate change , sea level rise , the scarcity of land and water shortages meant it would be impossible for him and his family to survive on Kiribati . <p> He first raised the issue in 2012 , appealing to the New Zealand Immigration and Protection Tribunal in 2013 , before his eventual deportation in 2015 . <p> His case was taken to the Committee of the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights , which this month released a split decision rejecting Mr Teitiota 's claim that his rights were violated by his deportation . <p> However , the Committee does note that New Zealand , in future deportation cases , has a responsibility to take into account the impact of climate change on Kiribati .
