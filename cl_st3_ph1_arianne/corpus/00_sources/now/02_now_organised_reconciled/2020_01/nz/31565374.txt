<h> Tenure review to be scrapped <p> It 's the year of delivery , Jacinda Ardern says . David Williams reports . <p> The Government is about to scrap tenure review . <p> Land Information Minister Eugenie Sage wo n't confirm the move , but Newsroom has been told by multiple sources that the controversial process , dubbed " organised corruption " by opponents , is finished .
