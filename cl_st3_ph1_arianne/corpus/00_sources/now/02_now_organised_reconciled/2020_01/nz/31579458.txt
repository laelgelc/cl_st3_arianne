<h> Westpac centralising 250 jobs in Auckland <p> Westpac plans to shift 250 jobs from Wellington to Auckland to consolidate its technology and product marketing team . <p> The bank has acquired more floor space at its headquarters in Auckland and will base the team there from next year . <p> The move was taken after a new opportunity arose to acquire more floor space at the bank 's headquarters in Auckland 's Britomart precinct . <p> It says all affected Wellington employees have 12 months to decide whether they want to be reimbursed for moving north or take redundancy . <p> Westpac says there wo n't be an overall fall in job numbers and the shift does n't affect the number of bank branches in Wellington . <p> Westpac NZ experience hub general manager , Karen Silk , said to centralise teams in Auckland offered an opportunity to more efficiently deliver services . <p> " Westpac NZ will continue to remain strongly committed to Wellington with 12 branches and over 300 banking staff in the region , " she said . <p> " We look forward to maintaining a corporate presence at two offices in Wellington , with @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ banking .
