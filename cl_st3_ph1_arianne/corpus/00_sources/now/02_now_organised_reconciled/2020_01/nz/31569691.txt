<h> New Zealand is one of the best countries to own an electric vehicle in , professor says <p> Electric cars are up to four times better for the environment when being driven in New Zealand than in Australia . <p> Charging an electric vehicle . " <p> An electric vehicle run in New Zealand emits between 30 and 40 grams of carbon dioxide per passenger kilometre travelled . <p> By comparison , a comparative petrol car emits between 130g and 170g , and a diesel car emits between 120g and 150g of carbon dioxide per passenger kilometre travelled . <p> In Australia , that same electric car would emit between 80g and 130g of carbon dioxide per passenger kilometre travelled . <p> Ralph Sims .
