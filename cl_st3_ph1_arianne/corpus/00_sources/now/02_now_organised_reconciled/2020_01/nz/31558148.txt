<p> Please Do Touch , an exhibition by Raglan artist Yaniv Janson , previously displayed in Paris and New York , will be a featured exhibition at ArtsPost , part of Waikato Museum , in Hamilton from Friday 10 January.Paris , New York -- and now Hamilton -- for Raglan artist 's exhibition <p> Please Do Touch , an exhibition by Raglan artist Yaniv Janson , previously displayed in Paris and New York , will be a featured exhibition at ArtsPost , part of Waikato Museum , in Hamilton from Friday 10 January . <p> The exhibition features a series of paintings that explore United Nations Sustainable Development Goals such as climate change , water quality , oceanic ecological systems , poverty and sustainable cities . They examine what could happen if we act , and what will happen if we do n't . <p> " This exhibition represents what is most important in life : the water we drink , the seas around us , the cities we live in , the people we do n't always see and the delicate balance of our world , " Mr Janson says . <p> Please Do Touch was exhibited in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and travelled to New York as a solo exhibition at the UN Headquarters in 2018 . <p> Mr Janson , who is an award-winning artist with autism and epilepsy , says " It 's important for me to show that disability is not a barrier to achieving .
