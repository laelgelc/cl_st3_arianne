<h> NZ 's new average asking price of $703k <p> The national asking price for houses has surpassed $700,000 for the first time since Realestate.co.nz 's records began 13 years ago . <p> Its figures for December 2019 show the national average asking price hit $703,780 - a 3.4 percent increase from November . <p> There are also record lows in new listings and total homes available for sale . <p> Realestate.co.nz spokesperson Vanessa Taylor said it was the first time since records began in 2007 that there were three national records in the same month . <p> " There have been years where we have seen a combination of two , but never all three . <p> " Low stock , teamed with high asking prices sees us close out the decade as a strong sellers ' market , " she said . <p> But what exactly will $703,000 get you differs around the country . <p> In Auckland , where the average asking price is $940,761 , you could get a two-bedroom apartment in Mount Wellington , with joint facilities such as swimming pool , gymnasium , sauna and tennis court . <p> In Manukau , you @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ In Northland asking prices decreased by 4.5 percent to $619,775 and for $685,000 you could get 4.9 hectare property and four bedroom , two bathroom home near Whangarei . <p> In the Wellington region , where the average asking price is $706 , 000 , you could get a two-bedroom apartment in Roseneath . <p> Or if you want to live in Upper Hutt you could get a three-bedroom newly built home , with two bathrooms with underfloor heating . <p> Further south in Canterbury the average asking price is a more affordable at $508,153 . <p> A three bedroom house with a swimming pool in Burnside , Christchurch will set you back $695,000 . <p> For the real bargain hunter , moving to Southland where the average asking price is $374,636 , in Invercargill you could get a five bedroom , two bathroom house , sitting on 2.2 hectares for $685,000 . <p> This article was originally published on RNZ and re-published with permission . <p> We value fearless , independent journalism .
