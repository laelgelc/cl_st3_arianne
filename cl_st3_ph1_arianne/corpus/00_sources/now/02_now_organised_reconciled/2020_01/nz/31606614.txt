<h> Govt announces massive $12b infrastructure package <p> Words : Nile Bijoux <p> 29 Jan 2020 <p> Prime Minister Jacinda Ardern has announced details of a huge $12b injection into New Zealand 's transport infrastructure . <p> Details include $6.8 billion for transport projects in Auckland , Waikato , Bay of Plenty , Wellington , Canterbury , Queenstown ; $1.1b for rail , including more than $900m for projects in Auckland ; $2.2b for new roads in Auckland , including Penlink , widening SH1 from Papakura to Drury , Mill Rd four-lane highway ; and the Auckland Harbour Bridge ' SkyPath ' for pedestrians and cyclists will be built . <p> The Government is calling the package of projects the ' New Zealand Upgrade Programme ' , which also includes funding for schools and hospitals . Finance Minister Grant Robertson said the last government " announced a number of projects but did not commit any money to them . Some of those ghost roads we have improved , brought forward and funded .
