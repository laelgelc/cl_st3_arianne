<h> Time for a mature conversation on water <p> Kiwis need to grow up when it comes to conversations on water quality , says an opposition MP . <p> OPINION : New Zealanders have a natural affinity with our water . Whether that be swimming at the beach in the beautiful Bay of Plenty , kayaking on a West Coast river , or pulling in a snapper on the Hauraki Gulf to take home for the family dinner . <p> There is no argument that kiwis want clean and healthy waterways where we can swim , surf and fish . Nobody wants to see plastic in our oceans , polluted rivers or septic beaches that are unable to be used -- yet these issues continue to prevail and in some cases are getting worse . The challenge is that while we all agree on the ultimate goal of improving water quality , there is disagreement on what needs to be done to achieve it , the pace and scale of change required , and whether we are open to storing more of it to assist . <p> The simple truth is that while everyone says they want clean water @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that needs to change their behaviour or spend the money to achieve the goal . <p> Our country 's reality is that we all need to play a role in the solution . There is an ongoing lament that our rural communities are largely to blame for the degradation of our waterways and if only they would do something about it , the issue would be sorted .
