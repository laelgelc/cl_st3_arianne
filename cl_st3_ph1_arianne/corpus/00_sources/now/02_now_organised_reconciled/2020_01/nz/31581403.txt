<h> Originair brings back Nelson to Wellington route <p> A Nelson-based commuter airline is re-introducing a Cook Strait service to its schedule . <p> Originair has announced today it is adding the Nelson-Wellington route to its schedule in response to passenger demand . <p> The airline , established in 2015 , once flew the route but pulled out with the arrival of Jetstar , which no longer flies into Nelson . <p> Originair founder and chief executive Robert Inglis said the new service will initially be limited to 10 flights per week , concentrating on peak demand times . <p> Inglis is a veteran of the commuter airline industry and is the co-founder of Air Nelson , which became an Air New Zealand link subsidiary . <p> He said customers had been asking the airline to consider a Nelson-Wellington route and Originair was pleased to be able to respond to that interest . <p> " We 're not offering an extensive schedule at the outset but we hope that it will provide extra capacity at times that are most useful to travellers on that route . " <p> Inglis said it will offer the service using its current two @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 30-seat aeroplane to its fleet . <p> " We 've only had the two Jetstreams available to us and we 're looking at options , both in terms of the current type and a larger guage . " <p> He said that at the moment the airline 's focus was on the British Aerospace Jetstream type . <p> " We have n't made that decision yet but it 's part of an investigation we 're doing and we 'll come to a view on it shortly . " <p> The announcement followed consistent and encouraging demand for seats on the airline 's newly-resumed Nelson-Palmerston North service . <p> " We are very happy with the way that the reintroduction of our Palmerston North service has been going .
