<h> 48% of Kiwis plan to eat less meat in 2020 <p> Nearly half ( 48% ) of all Kiwis are planning to reduce their meat consumption in 2020 , according to a new independent survey released by plant-based food brand , MorningStar Farms . <p> Asked why they planned to change , the most popular response given by 69% of people was personal health , however environmental impact and climate change was also a popular answer , particularly with those under 40 ( 61% ) . <p> The shift in attitudes reflects the growing ' flexitarian ' trend that sees consumers adjust their diets based on personal choice at a particular time .
