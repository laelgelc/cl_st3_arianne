<h> Manufacturing sector index falls <p> The manufacturing sector is back in the doldrums after a short-lived improvement . <p> The BNZ-Business New Zealand Performance of Manufacturing Index ( PMI ) fell to 49.3 in December from 51.2 the month before . <p> A reading above 50 indicated expansion . <p> Three of the index 's components fell , with a drop-off in new orders and a slowdown in production weighing heavily on the sector . <p> BNZ senior economist Craig Ebert said the result was disappointing . <p> " After a couple of months flirting with positivity , New Zealand 's PMI dipped back just below the breakeven line again in December . " <p> The industry showed signs of improvement late last year , moving back into expansion after three months of contraction . <p> The December reading was at odds with a business confidence survey released earlier this week , which showed a rebound in manufacturers ' sentiment . <p> Ebert said that survey might suggest the industry was through the worst , and output and profitability would soon pick up . <p> This article was originally published on RNZ and re-published with permission . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ do too . <p> Newsroom has repeatedly broken big , important national news stories and established a platform for quality journalism on issues ranging from climate change , sexual harassment and bullying through to science , foreign affairs , women 's sports and politics . <p> But we need your support to continue , whether it is great , small , ongoing or a one-off donation .
