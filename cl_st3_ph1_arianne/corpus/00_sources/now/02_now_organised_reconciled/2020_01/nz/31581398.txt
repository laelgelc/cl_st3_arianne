<h> Yesterdaze : Logging back in to reality without a password <p> Have n't been paying attention to the news over the holidays ? Fret not , James Elliott has your water-cooler chat royally covered with his take on the news of the summer . <p> January in New Zealand sees most of us spread out all over the country enjoying diverse summer holiday pursuits , from fishing to music festivals to doing sweet Fanny Adams . It 's a time when we pay little attention to the news , often because there is little news of substance to pay attention to . <p> But this week as most of us started back at work , there was a strong surge of national unification at about 9.03am on Monday morning when we were bound together as one to ask the same critical question ' What the hell is my login password again ? ' . <p> Having scoured our memories for the name of our first pet and/or car and/or mother 's maiden name we logged on to find out that there had in fact been quite a lot of news while we were busy not reading the books @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ cataclysmic bush fires , Donald Trump had nearly started World War III , and there was a rupture in the British Royal family . <p> For those of you still logged out and waiting for the IT Helpdesk to start back next Monday here 's a quick summary of some of the summer news . <p> While series 3 of ' The Crown ' was available on Netflix , series 6 of the reality TV version was screening on just about every other media platform .
