<h> Black Friday takes bite out of Christmas holiday sales <p> Jewellery retailer Michael Hill International said strong Black Friday weekend trading in November appeared to bring forward holiday sales , with a " meaningful " lift in overall November foot traffic and sales . <p> November was a big shopping month for retailers in all sectors , with China 's 11 November Singles ' Day , Black Friday and Cyber Monday . <p> " While the shift in consumer behaviour saw a significant uplift in November sales in all markets , our sales in early December were somewhat disappointing , " Chief executive Daniel Bracken said . <p> " Pressure on gross margin continued in all markets , with deep-discounting from many competitors . " <p> The Australian-based retailer , which also operated in New Zealand and Canada , said all countries delivered positive sales growth in the first half and second quarter ended 31 December . <p> Australia 's same store sales increased by 1.6 percent for the quarter , but the company said Christmas trade was challenging with deep and enduring discounting . <p> New Zealand 's same store sales increased by 5 percent for the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> Canada 's same store sales rose 1.4 percent for the quarter , following a strong performance the year earlier . <p> The company said the Canadian business presented further opportunity for growth , with investment in training , personnel and logistics . <p> " There is a real air of enthusiasm across the business with a focus on delivery of initiatives that continue into the second half , " Bracken said . <p> Official numbers show sales using electronic cards fell a seasonally-adjusted 0.8 percent compared with a 2.9 pct rise in November . <p> The largest fall was for sales of durable goods , such as furniture , hardware , appliances , and sports and recreational goods , down 2.5 percent . <p> " The dip in durables comes on the back of a very strong November month , " Stats NZ retail manager Sue Chapman said . <p> They were the types of goods heavily discounted and promoted in the Black Friday and Cyber Monday sales in November . <p> There were slight rises in spending on food , drink and groceries . <p> Actual sales hit @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ percent ahead of last year . <p> Rising house values , wages , and low interest rates have been credited with lifting consumer confidence and retail spending . <p> This article was originally published on RNZ and re-published with permission . <p> We value fearless , independent journalism .
