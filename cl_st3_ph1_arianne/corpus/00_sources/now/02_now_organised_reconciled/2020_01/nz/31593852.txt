<h> Scammed Chinese workers promised fairness instead told to go <p> Construction workers from China caught in an employment scam were promised fair treatment by the Immigration Minister but instead have been asked to leave the country . <p> Construction workers from China caught in an employment scam were promised fair treatment by the Immigration Minister , but instead have been asked to leave the country , a union representing them says . <p> About 50 workers paid roughly $50,000 dollars each for visas and jobs in New Zealand , after working with a recruitment agent from a New Zealand firm . <p> Unite Union national director Mike Treen said they arrived in mid-2018 , and Immigration New Zealand ( INZ ) launched an investigation within months . <p> About half had their visas extended by INZ while an employment case was taken by the union against the hire company involved , National Personnel Limited . <p> But a settlement has now been made out of court .
