<h> Mediocrity hangs over long-term outlook for economy <p> Mediocrity hangs over the long-term outlook for the economy , according to economic consultancy Infometrics . <p> It said in its latest look ahead there was little reason to feel more upbeat about New Zealand 's economic prospects , despite some improvement in confidence surveys over the past few months . <p> The company said the economy was likely to regain some momentum over the next year , but not enough to help the economy avoid mediocre results beyond 2021 , with annual economic growth expected to average less than 2 percent between 2021 and 2024 . <p> Infometrics chief forecaster Gareth Kiernan said there had not been a fundamental change in real growth prospects , with improved property price rises symptomatic of a sustained housing shortage . <p> " A stuttering global economy , slowing population growth , and falling house prices all shape as real concerns hanging over the medium-term outlook , " he said . <p> He said slowing employment growth meant there was no assurance workers ' income growth would continue . <p> While the government planned to spend another $12 billion on infrastructure , he @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ timely manner raised serious doubts about how quickly the stimulus might filter through to the economy . <p> " In our view , New Zealand 's potential for growth will also be hampered by more difficult conditions for exporters and an inability across the wider economy to achieve productivity growth of any magnitude , " Kiernan said . <p> Meanwhile , ANZ 's look ahead said the economic outlook was modest , but the economy had found a floor after last year 's slow down . <p> It said the hit from the coronavirus should be short term and limited , while longer term high commodity prices , a stronger housing market , government spending , and low interest rates would all back growth . <p> " The economy is at a crossroads and the political and international context will be crucial .
