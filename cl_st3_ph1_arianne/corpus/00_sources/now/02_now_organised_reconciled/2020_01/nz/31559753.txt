<h> Climate Change Expert Visits New Zealand <p> One of the foremost experts on climate change visits New Zealand as a guest of government next week . <p> Dr Robert Watson is the Chairperson of the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) <p> He is also senior spokesperson for the World Bank on global warming and climate change and was the Associate Director for Environment in the Executive Office of the US President in the Clinton administration . <p> During his visit he will address business , politicians , policy makers and other interested parties on issues related to climate change , including domestic policy initiatives undertaken by other countries . <p> Dr Watson arrives in the country on Sunday , 17 February , and will be involved in a whirlwind schedule of meetings and seminars , starting in Invercargill and moving to Christchurch , Auckland and Wellington , before flying out on Friday 22 February . <p> His schedule includes attending the Auckland Regional Council Transport meeting on Tuesday 19 February , addressing the Employers and Manufacturing Association ( EMA ) in Auckland on Wednesday as their keynote speaker , and speaking at a workshop for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ school children in Invercargill and the Hutt Valley , and join a special schools video-link to discuss climate change issues -- with those who will live longest with the consequences of global warming . <p> Dr Watson is expected to draw from his extensive knowledge of overseas initiatives to address climate change . He will also emphasise the opportunities available to New Zealand through ratifying the Kyoto Protocol on climate change . <p> The Government has stated its intention to ratify the Kyoto Protocol , an international agreement to reduce greenhouse gases , by this September at the World Summit on Sustainable Development in Johannesburg . <p> Dr Watson visited the country previously in April 2001 .
