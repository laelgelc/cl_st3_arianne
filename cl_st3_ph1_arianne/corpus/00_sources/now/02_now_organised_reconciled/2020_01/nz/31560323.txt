The long-standing economic theory , according to which firms or people deliver the best outcomes for society by acting individually to maximize profits or " utility , " has never been valid . If it were , businesses would see no advantage in becoming much larger , and advertisers would never use social pressure to manipulate consumers . But in the digital world , it is simply impossible to ignore our interdependence . <p> Consider today 's pervasive digital platforms .
