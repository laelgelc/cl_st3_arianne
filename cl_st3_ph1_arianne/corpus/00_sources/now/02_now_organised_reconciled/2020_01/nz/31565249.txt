century if greenhouse gas emissions go unchecked , a new report examining the projected impact of climate change on the region shows . <p> Gavin Palmer . <p> The 136-page report , commissioned from Niwa by the Otago Regional Council ( ORC ) , also shows worst-case scenarios that include average temperatures in Otago increasing by up to 3.5degC , an up to 40% increase in winter rain , up to 35% hazards analyst Ellyse Gore said the report was commissioned to help the council with its climate change risk assessment , now under way . <p> As part of the risk assessment project , the council was
