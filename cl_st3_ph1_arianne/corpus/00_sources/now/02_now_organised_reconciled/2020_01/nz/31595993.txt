Jenny Shipley is prime minister , we win zero medals at the Olympics , and an actress named Lucy Lawless is the first New Zealander to host American TV sketch comedy show Saturday Night Live . We 're at the height of Xenamania , and Lawless is not just the most famous person in New Zealand , but the most famous New Zealander in the world . <p> Looking back at the episode feels more like looking into some strange funhouse mirror rather than into a part of television history .
