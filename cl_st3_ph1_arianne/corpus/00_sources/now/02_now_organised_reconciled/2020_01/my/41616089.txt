<p> This video by Sahabat Alam Malaysia presents voices from around Friends of the Earth Asia Pacific and International in solidarity with fishermen affected by existing reclamation projects and those who will be affected by the 4,500-acre Penang South Reclamation project . Activists from Friends of the Earth International share their worries about the impacts on the environment , climate change , the livelihoods of fishermen and the coastal marine ecosystem . They call for the cancellation of the project . <p> ( Visited 62 times , 1 visits today ) <p> Thanks for dropping by !
