That was a tsunami of a change ! For BN had been in power for 60 years before that . <p> Since then seven by-elections have been held : PH has won four , including Anwar Ibrahim 's Port Dickson parliamentary seat . <p> After Port Dickson , PH suffered three successive electoral defeats -- in Semenyih , in Cameron Highlands and in the Negri Sembilan state-seat of Rantau , where the PKR candidate Dr S Streram lost to acting Umno chief Mohamad Hasan . <p> Indications are that PH will win the Sandakan contest on 11 May and reverse the trend of electoral losses . <p> Support the struggle to build a New Malaysia based on Justice , Freedom , Solidarity : <p> Even if that comes to pass , not all is well for the PH government . This must continue . <p> But CSOs also need to engage with the state in a positive manner . By this is meant , CSOs must penetrate and negotiate the state and perhaps , over the longer term , take over and steer the state . <p> Accordingly , some CSO activists must endeavour to become members of the civil service ! Those who can not adapt to the demands of reforming the bureaucracy should step down and step aside . Of course , this will not happen overnight . <p> But the point is that we need to recruit a new generation of action-oriented and reformist-minded civil servants . This is also the time to stress that henceforth we want to transfer to a B40 needs-based plan from the race-based one which the majority of the civil servants have previously adhered to and prioritised . <p> There is an urgent need to re-programme and steer the educational system , which has almost half a million teachers and administrators under the Ministry of Education . The public health services needs emergency treatment so that the standard of services provided is upgraded . Urgent re-thinking on how to retain our specialist doctors must be conducted . <p> No doubt , throughout the world , governments have resorted @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ provision of public utilities in order to upgrade the delivery of these services . <p> But that is not necessarily the way out .
