<h> Ministry to restructure DOE <p> KUALA LUMPUR : The Ministry of Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change ( MESTECC ) will give extra attention on the restructuring of the Department of Environment ( DOE ) to strengthen its organisational structure , streamline its enforcement operations and enhance the integrity of its personnel . <p> Minister Yeo Bee Yin said the restructuring would be made through the DOE Road Map in the first quarter of this year . <p> Yeo Bee Yin <p> " These are the things that we have been doing since 2019 and will continue to do this year . In fact , these are among the main focus areas that will be given extra attention , ' ' she said in RTM 's Bicara Naratif programme discussing on MESTECC 's achievements 2019 , on Friday . <p> The minister also said she would ensure that the 1,400 posts at all DOE offices nationwide would be properly filled so that the department could function better . <p> " I was shocked when I first found out that only 1,200 posts were filled .
