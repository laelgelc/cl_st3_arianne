<h> No justification to waste millions on expensive underpass <p> The Penang Island City Council plans to spend RM55m on an underpass connecting Mount Erskine Road to Burmah Road ( crossing below Gottlieb Road ) . <p> The latest hike in assessment rates would rake in RM54m per year for the council while burdening ratepayers . But much of this additional revenue raised from the rate hikes would be blown away just to pay for land acquisition ( over RM20m ) and the construction of this underpass . <p> This is a huge waste of ratepayers ' funds as the average travelling time to cross from the junction of Jalan Fettes/Lebuhraya Halia to Mount Erskine Road/Gottlieb Road junction ( a distance of 2.1km ) is less than 10 minutes even during morning peak hours . During off-peak hours , the travel time is under five minutes . This is hardly a long time -- and certainly no justification for wasting millions of ringgit on an underpass . <p> Data in Appendix 1 ( below ) is based on Google Traffic historical data for two random days -- 28 August and 24 September 2019 . The longest time to clear the traffic was 12 minutes , which shows up only once . <p> Data in Appendix 2 ( below ) is based on a daily commuter 's own experience in crossing the same junctions at around 7.30am from Monday to Friday ( 18-22 November 2019 ) . The average time to travel the distance was seven minutes , with the longest at 10 minutes ( once ) . <p> The millions spent on the proposed short underpass would be better spent upgrading the bus service , introducing feeder buses and dedicated bus lanes , providing incentives for the greater use of school buses , and more efficient traffic management .
