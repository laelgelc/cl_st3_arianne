<h> NST Leader : State of the world <p> TWENTY-TWENTY . You ca n't get any more perfect than this . Sadly , that may be the only perfect thing about 2020 . We will be glad to be perfectly wrong . But we are terribly worried that we will be right . <p> Truth be told , we have not left the dark decades behind . Old habits die hard indeed . <p> A snapshot of the world as it is in this early days of 2020 will be explanatory . Australia is burning , and for the first time , continent-wide . Rocketman Kim Jong-un is back to calling United States President Donald Trump a dotard . <p> As if to prove Jong-un right , Trump is sending his troops into Iraq after a war of words with Iran . Not to be left out , China ambitiously wants everything to come with Chinese characteristics . <p> Neighbour India is up to its own discriminatory ways , excluding when it should include . <p> The world ca n't get any more depressing . This is what we must celebrate . <p> Their lives may be at stake but they fight against earth , wind and fire . Yes , more than a million hectares of forest have been burned . And with it homes and orchards . <p> And some lives - human and animal - have been lost . Who would forget the heart-rending picture of a badly burned koala screaming along the road only to be saved by a heroic grandma , Toni Doherty ? Lewis , so named by Doherty , <p> was last seen hooked up to an oxygen mask at Macquarie Koala Hospital.Stories like this give us hope that despite the dim decade that lies ahead there are some bright sparks . When humans rush to save our kind and other life forms , we know our human spirit is very much alive . <p> Thankfully , it is not only the old who are so spirited .
