<h> Australia bolsters defences as cost of huge bushfires rises <p> Australian firefighters used the cool weather of today to try to strengthen containment lines around almost 200 wildfires burning in the country 's southeast , as Prime Minister Scott Morrison prepared to meet with insurance and bank executives to discuss the crisis . <p> Fires have razed more than eight million hectares ( 19.8 million acres ) of land across Australia , an area nearly the size of Austria , killing 25 people and destroying or damaging thousands of houses . <p> The Insurance Council of Australia today increased its estimate for damages claims from the fires to more than A$700 million ( RM1.99 billion ) , with claims expected to jump further when more fire-hit areas are accessible . <p> Thousands of people have been left homeless , while many in rural towns have spent days without electricity , telecommunications and , in some cases , drinking water .
