<h> Pakistan starts production of first fully-electric vehicles <h> Share <p> LAHORE - Pakistan has started commercial production of environment-friendly and fuel-efficient electric rickshaws . <p> The first indigenously produced electric rickshaw was rolled out in a special ceremony held in Lahore on Saturday . The event was attended among others by Advisor to the Prime Minister on Climate Change Malik Amin Aslam . <p> Talking on the occasion , Malik Amin Aslam said the success of the project would depend on the financial benefit of its owner . If it was found suitable for its owner , more rickshaws could be converted to electric technology , he added . <p> Experts say the indigenous production of electric rickshaws will not only save fuel but also put a positive impact on the environment . <p> Its manufacturers told that the production of electric rickshaws could be enhanced through patronage and encouragement of the government . <p> It is eco-friendly , low-price and the money which is used for oil importation will be reduced , thus , it will serve the country in one way or the other way , Malik added . <p> The battery is designated as EcoRik @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 6kwh . It will be charged in 5 hours , and once the Rickshaw gets charged it can drive for 125 Km , the advisor maintained . <p> Moreover , the vehicles will be locally manufactured except for the electric kit . <p> The advancement comes after the cabinet approved in November 2019 -- to put at least 30pc of vehicles on electricity by 2030 . Among which , 0.5 million will be three-wheeler vehicles , alongside 100,000 cars and 1,000 buses .
