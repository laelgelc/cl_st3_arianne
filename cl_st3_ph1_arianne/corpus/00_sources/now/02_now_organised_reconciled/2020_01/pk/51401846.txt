<h> World 's Wine Supply threatened by Climate Change <p> Over half of the areas around the world suitable for growing grapes used for wine are threatened by climate change . These areas will not be suitable for growing grapes in 80 years ' time due to climate change . <p> In the National Academy of Sciences ' online journal , a study was published that said that 56% of wine-growing regions will be inapt by 2100 should the climate of Earth warm 3.6 degrees Fahrenheit at that time . <p> Also , the study revealed that these losses will be cut in half if the wine growers diversify their crops and move various species of grapes to different regions .
