<h> News and insight from the Office for National Statistics <h> Informing the election : 2019 <p> The ONS does n't just produce the UK 's most important numbers . Making sense of data is central to our mission too . Miles Fletcher looks back at how impartial ONS expertise again helped the UK 's major news broadcasters to find and interpret statistics in the 2019 General Election . <p> Most people 's exposure to statistics and data still comes via the media and when general elections come around we at the ONS have a special responsibility to make sure the coverage rests on sound numbers . <p> In 2015 we took a proactive approach and sent a senior statistician to BBC News to lend his expertise to producers for the duration of the campaign . <p> After positive feedback we expanded the programme in 2017 , sending three colleagues to work with news teams at the BBC , Channel Four and ITV . <p> The news teams benefitted from our impartial advice and support . The ONS people experienced the rigour of working to tight deadlines in a demanding media environment . <p> With another election called in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ staff with the BBC , Channel Four and ITV once again .
