<h> Fed up with poor performance of the FTSE 100 's big dividend payers ? Try these stocks instead <p> Consider BT , HSBC , Royal Dutch Shell , or Vodafone . For many investors they are reliable stocks , providing a backbone to an investment portfolio . After all , they are low risk and provide good income . Then again , consider their share price performance . <p> BT shares have fallen 30% over the last year , while today 's share price is barely more than a third of the price five years ago . Sure yield is handsome -- almost 10% , but such an appalling share price performance is enough to shake any investor 's confidence . <p> Or take HSBC , whose yield on the shares is close to 7% . While the share price has performed better than BT 's it is still 15% down over the last year , and flat over the last five . <p> Then there 's Royal Dutch Shell , whose shares have fallen by a fifth over one year , and are flat over five . Take BT as an example . The dividend may be somewhere off in the stratosphere , but an investor who bought into the company five years ago would now be receiving a yield that is the equivalent of 3% of the original investment . <p> Royal Dutch Shell has a fundamental problem .
