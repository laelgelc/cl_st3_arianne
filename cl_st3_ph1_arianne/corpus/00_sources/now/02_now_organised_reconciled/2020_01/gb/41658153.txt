Let alone " staring at my phone and ignoring my parents " . Now the fusty old DofE Awards programme -- founded by HRH Prince Philip back in 1956 -- has moved with the times , publishing a list of 25 experiences that every 21st-century adolescent should experience as part of their personal development . These include learning about climate change , cooking a vegan meal , going on a " digital detox " , and walking barefoot in a forest . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ It all comes off the back of worrying research conducted by the youth charity that showed that more than half of 14 to 18-year-olds have never volunteered for their community or worked a part-time job , while 43 per cent never campaigned for something they believed in and 20 per cent have never been for a walk in the countryside . In turn , 72 per cent of employers surveyed think school leavers are n't equipped with the right skills to succeed in employment . <p> The new list of recommendations is backed by leading employers including Heathrow Airport , Barclays LifeSkills and RSM .
