<h> Dorset Council is considering ways of cutting down on single use plastic <p> DORSET Council is considering ways of cutting down on single use plastic in its offices and services . <p> A draft policy comes before councillors this week , nine months after the council declared a climate and ecological emergency . <p> It is the first policy to be presented in public around climate change but is not likely to be agreed until the council 's Cabinet next meets in March . <p> Today 's ( Thursday ) Place Scrutiny committee is being asked to comment on the draft proposals which come in two phases -- the first to reduce single use plastic in all 14 Dorset Council offices and the second to reduce single use plastic in council services and operations . <p> A report says that , at the moment , a group has only been set up to look at the first phase and has yet to start work on phase two . <p> An audit being reported to councillors has found small plastic milk pots in use at County Hall and Princes House , Dorchester with plastic milk containers in 13 out @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ offices ; plastic cutlery and plastic water bottles at County Hall and single use plastic bottles for cleaning products in six council offices . In almost all of these cases alternatives are being investigated . <p> The report , by executive director for place , John Sellgren , says that a communications plan has been prepared which will be delivered by the council 's communications team , once approved . It will send out key messages and will encourage staff to bring in their own re-usable cutlery and crockery . <p> In his conclusion Mr Sellgren says : " The Single Use Plastic ( SUP ) policy presents a joint and consistent approach to managing Dorset Council 's use of SUPs . The policy and action plan fit well with the Council 's emerging Corporate Plan and our ongoing work around the climate change emergency .
