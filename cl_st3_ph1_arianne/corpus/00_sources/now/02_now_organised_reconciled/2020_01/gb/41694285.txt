<h> Crematorium operators accused of ' burying their heads in the sand ' over harmful gas emissions <h> Crematorium operators accused of ' burying their heads in the sand ' over harmful gas emissions <p> AN ESTABLISHED funeral director has urged crematorium operators to stop " burying their heads in the sand " over harmful gases being released into the air . <p> The call from Tapper Funeral Service director Steven Tapper comes as it is revealed almost all council-run crematoria have not invested in technology to limit the release of nitrogen oxides ( NOx ) . <p> Around 95 per cent of coffins used in cremations nationally are made from chipboard , also known as MDF .
