<h> Amid the gloom of 2019 there was also plenty to celebrate <h> Amid the gloom of 2019 there was also plenty to celebrate <h> Letters <p> FOR many of us 2019 will be viewed more than a little negatively , driven by a combination of Brexit , terrorism , climate change and increasing global instability . <p> However , while it may not have made the front pages , there is much for us to rejoice in and be thankful for . For example , children born today have the best chance of growing up healthy , safe and educated . There are now 4.4 million fewer child deaths per year than in 2000 , according to Save The Children 's Global Childhood Report 2019 . In addition , there are now 115 million fewer children out of school than at the turn of the century , and 94 million fewer child labourers . <p> Figures from the World Health Organisation ( WHO ) also reveal that child-killing diseases have significantly declined since 2000 . Measles , for example , declined 86% and there was also an 82% decrease in the incidence of tetanus .
