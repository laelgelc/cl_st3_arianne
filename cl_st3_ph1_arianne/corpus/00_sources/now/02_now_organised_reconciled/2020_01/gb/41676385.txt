Are there more efficient systems than using air conditioning or gas radiators ? <p> Logicor certainly thinks so . The company designs infrared heating systems , which work by passing electricity through panels that emit energy into a room . These panels can generate heat up to 27 feet away . <p> " It 's a unique type of heat , " explains David Bowen , chief technology officer and founder of Logicor . <p> " In a normal room , heated by air conditioning or radiators , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ turn off the heating and you 're no longer heating the air , you 've got to trap that air for an awfully long time and hope none gets out .
