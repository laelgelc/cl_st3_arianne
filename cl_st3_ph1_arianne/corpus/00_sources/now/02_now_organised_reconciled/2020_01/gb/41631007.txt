<p> A group of Barclays shareholders have taken formal action against the bank , demanding it stops financing fossil fuel companies that are not aligned with the Paris Agreement 's climate change goals . <p> A group of 11 institutional investors managing over ? 130bn of assets , coupled with over 100 individual investors have launched the shareholder resolution -- thought to be the first ever climate change resolution at a European bank . <p> Barclays is the largest financer of fossil fuels in Europe , and the sixth largest globally . Since the Paris Agreement was signed in 2015 , the bank has provided over $85bn ( ? 65bn ) of finance to fossil fuel firms and high-carbon projects , according to investment charity Share Action , which coordinated the resolution . <p> The Paris Agreement outlines a goal of keeping global warming well below two degrees celsius compared to pre-industrial levels , and ideally limit it to 1.5 degrees . <p> The resolution calls on Barclays to publish a plan to phase out the provision of financial services to companies in the energy sector not aligned with the Agreement . <p> " Piecemeal changes in energy policy @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ campaign manager Jeanne Martin . " <p> Natasha Landell-Mills , head of stewardship at Sarasin , told City A.M. the resolution represented an " escalation " in pressure on Barclays from shareholders that was " absolutely appropriate in light of the increasing urgency of the threats that we see from climate change " . <p> " The urgency of the problem we face is such that we do n't have lots of years to waste debating this , " she added . <p> A spokesperson for Barclays said : " We are working to help tackle climate change , and we meet with Share Action and other shareholders regularly to update them on our progress . " <p> European banks including BNP Paribas , Credit Agricole and Standard Chartered have all recently taken steps towards aligning their energy financing with the Paris Agreement 's goals .
