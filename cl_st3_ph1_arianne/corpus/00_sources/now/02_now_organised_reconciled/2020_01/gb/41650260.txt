<p> In a year where the news veered consistently between two poles -- " awful " and " very awful " -- one recurring spark kept hope alive : the UK 's electricity network . Last year , the UK totted up 83 coal-free days , including a record-breaking 18-day stretch in May and June . For the year as a whole , 43 per cent of the UK 's electricity came from fossil fuels , with coal making up just two per cent -- both of them record-breaking stats in their own right . <p> The decade overall yielded some encouraging stats , too -- the amount of UK electricity coming from renewable sources jumped from seven per cent in 2010 to 37 per cent in 2019 . Since 1990 , the country has cut its emissions by around two-fifths .
