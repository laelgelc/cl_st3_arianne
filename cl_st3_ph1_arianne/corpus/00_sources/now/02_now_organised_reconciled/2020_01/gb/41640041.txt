<p> The charity has donated the tree to the City Council-run park to make it even greener and remind people about the work they are carrying out in Ethiopia . <p> 2020 will be the Year of the Tree for Exeter Ethiopia Link . They will be turning their attention to tree planting in Nekempte , the town in Ethiopia they first made links with 32 years ago . <p> The charity was set up in 1987 by Exeter people who responded to the tragic pictures of famine shown on our on TV screens . It followed hot on the heels of the famous Band Aid initiative and the charity is still going strong . <p> These days the charity focuses on a positive improvement each year . 2019 was the Year of the Library , with the group paying librarian salaries and setting up libraries in Nekempte schools .
