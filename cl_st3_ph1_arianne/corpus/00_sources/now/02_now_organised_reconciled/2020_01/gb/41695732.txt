" <p> The research also assessed whether presenting a more balanced picture that highlights similarities instead could improve social cohesion . <p> The team found support for the idea that accentuating such similarities can encourage Brexiteers and Remainers to get along better . <p> Co-author Dr Lukas Wolf , from the University of Bath , said : " Many people seem to be worried about an increasingly fractious society and therefore it is encouraging to see that people are actually quite similar . <p> " Realising that fact seems to have a positive effect on bringing people together again .
