<h> What is climate change ? The definition , causes and effects <p> Climate change is the catch-all term for the shift in worldwide weather phenomena associated with an increase in global average temperatures . It 's real and temperatures have been going up around the world for many decades . <p> Reliable temperature records began in 1850 and our world is now about one degree Celcius hotter than it was in the period between 1850 and 1900 -- commonly referred to as the " pre-industrial " average . <p> While this temperature increase is more specifically referred to as global warming , climate change is the term currently favoured by science communicators , as it explicitly includes not only Earth 's increasing global average temperature , but also the climate effects caused by this increase . <h> Read next <h> Tropical forests are dying .
