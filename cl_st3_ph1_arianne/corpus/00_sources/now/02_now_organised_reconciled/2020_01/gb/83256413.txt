<h> Pakistan hit by cold weather , over 100 die <p> Local residents search for the avalanche victims in the snow in Neelum Valley , in Pakistan-administered Kashmir on January 15 , 2020 . ( Photo by AFP ) <p> Javed Rana Press TV , Islamabad <p> The lingering cold weather conditions have turned out to be a recipe for disaster when the snowfall triggered avalanches . Over 200 houses either collapsed or damaged . Scores of people have died and injured during the past several days while many more are still unaccounted for . The worst-hit region is Pakistani administrated Kashmir . With rain and snowfall continuing in many parts of the country , officials fear a surge in the death tolls . <p> How to address worsening climate changes is one of the biggest challenges for Pakistan . <p> Pakistan has over 72-hundred known glaciers which produce more glacial ice in the country than anywhere else on the earth outside the polar regions .
