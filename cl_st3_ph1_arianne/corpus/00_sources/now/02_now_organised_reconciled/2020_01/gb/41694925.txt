although it is yet to reveal where in the centre it will be based . <p> Bradford Council 's Executive , when it meets on Tuesday will be asked to approve an outline business case for the ambitious scheme , which has been in the pipeline for years . <p> It had previously been announced that the system would use greener @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for nearby buildings . <p> The process would release less carbon into the city 's atmosphere than existing energy provisions -
