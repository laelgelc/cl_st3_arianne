<h> U.S. coal-fired power plants closing fast despite Trump 's pledge of support for industry <p> FILE PHOTO : A fence closes off the closed coal-fired Somerset power plant in Somerset <p> By Scott DiSavino <p> ( Reuters ) - U.S. coal-fired power plants shut down at the second-fastest pace on record in 2019 , despite President Donald Trump 's efforts to prop up the industry , according to data from the federal government and Thomson Reuters . <p> Power companies retired or converted roughly 15,100 megawatts ( MW ) of coal-fired electricity generation , enough to power about 15 million homes , according to the data , which included preliminary statistics from the Energy Information Administration and Reuters reporting . <p> That was second only to the record 19,300 MW shut in 2015 during President Barack Obama 's administration . <p> The replacement of coal with power generation from natural gas and renewables has cut total U.S. carbon emissions in four of the past five years .
