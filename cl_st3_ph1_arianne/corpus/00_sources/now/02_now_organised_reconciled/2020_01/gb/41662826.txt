<h> Wildfires may permanently alter Australian landscape , scientists warn <h> Wildfires may permanently alter Australian landscape , scientists warn <p> Scientists say the Australian landscape is being permanently altered by the nation 's wildfire crisis as a warming climate brings profound changes to the island continent . <p> Heat waves and drought have fuelled bigger and more frequent fires in parts of Australia , with some 40,000 square miles ( 104,000 square kilometres ) scorched so far this fire season . <p> While blazes continue to rage in the country 's south-east , government officials are drawing up plans to re-seed burned areas to speed up forest recovery that could otherwise take decades or even centuries . <p> But some scientists and forestry experts doubt that reseeding and other intervention efforts can match the scope of the destruction . Since September , the fires have killed 28 people and burned more than 2,600 houses . <p> Before the recent wildfires , ecologists divided up Australia 's native vegetation into two categories : fire-adapted landscapes that burn periodically , and those that do n't burn .
