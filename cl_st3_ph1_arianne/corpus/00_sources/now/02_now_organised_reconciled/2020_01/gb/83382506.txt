<h> Sustainable light art festival i Light Singapore is postponed <p> UPDATE , February 11 : Amidst the 2019 novel coronavirus situation , this event is postponed to a later date , which will be announced in due course . According to a statement by i Light , the move is in line with the advisory to cancel or defer non-essential large-scale events . The artworks and complementary activities lined up for the upcoming festival also require visitors to come into close contact with the artworks or other individuals . It joins other events that have been postponed or cancelled due to the novel coronavirus such as the NUS Arts Festival , Chingay 2020 @ Heartlands and Checkpoint Theatre 's The Nuclear Family . If you were looking forward to seeing a specific installation at i Light Singapore , do n't fret -- artists whose artworks have been selected for this year 's edition will be invited to present their works again . <p> January 22 : The Marina Bay area is set to be illuminated with artworks and light installations . An iconic event on the arts calendar , i Light will be returning from March 6 to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ For this year 's edition , local and international artists are invited to create artworks that seek to encourage conversations about our lifestyles and decision-making to achieve a more sustainable future . <p> Presented by URA , the Festival 's line-up of artworks and programming will inspire visitors to make small but consistent changes to their daily habits that can impact broader issues like climate change . Many artists have responded with creative ideas that explore and reimagine the endless potential of everyday objects and waste , and repurpose them into stunning light art installations at Marina Bay . <p> i Light Singapore will take place from 7.30pm to 11pm daily and extends to midnight on Fridays and Saturdays .
