<h> New protections for threatened seabirds announced <h> New protections for threatened seabirds announced <p> New protections for the breeding and feeding grounds of tens of thousands of threatened seabirds are being announced by the Government . <p> Seabirds including little and common terns , avocets , ruffs and migratory knots could benefit from new and extended special protected areas ( SPA ) in the Solent and near Middlesbrough . <p> The conservation areas aim to protect rare and vulnerable seabirds from human activity , such as fishing or recreation . <p> It is hoped the designations will benefit common terns ( Natural England/PA ) <p> Environment minister Rebecca Pow is also announcing plans for a seabird conservation strategy which will assess the vulnerability of each species in the light of pressures they face and propose actions to address them . <p> A new Solent and Dorset Coast special protection area will cover more than 891 square kilometres ( 344 square miles ) , an area equivalent to more than 125,000 football pitches . <p> It aims to protect almost 1,000 pairs of three species of tern , as the area is the fifth most important foraging site in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for common terns during the breeding season . <p> The Teesmouth and Cleveland Coast SPA is being extended by 109 square kilometres ( 42 square miles ) , bringing the total size of the protected area to more than 122 square kilometres ( 47 square miles ) . <p> Sandwich terns are among the birds the new protections aim to help ( Natural England/PA ) <p> The Environment Department said with the extension in place , more than 35,000 birds including avocets , ruffs and knots will be protected . <p> The new and extended areas join 47 existing sites in English waters . <p> Ms Pow said : " As the devastating impacts of climate change are only too visible , it is vital that we take decisive steps now that make a real difference to help protect our wildlife and allow vulnerable species to recover . <p> " We have already protected important nesting sites for seabirds , such as the little tern , and these new and additional protections to their feeding grounds , together with the development of a new strategy to protect our seabirds , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ importantly , thrive . " <p> The avocet is the symbol of the RSPB , which welcomed the designations ( Natural England/PA ) <p> Tony Juniper , chairman of government conservation agency Natural England , said : " Many of Britain 's sea and shorebird populations are globally important and for that reason we have a particular responsibility to protect and enhance them . <p> " I am delighted that , following an extensive evidence-based assessment by Natural England , these new areas , confirmed today by Government , will help to do that . <p> " They will ensure that species of conservation concern , such as terns and waders , have access to secure food sources , including during their critical annual breeding seasons . " <p> The move was also welcomed by the RSPB , which warned that many seabirds were declining at an alarming rate globally and across the UK . <p> Martin Harper , director of global conservation at the charity , said the announcement " will be a first step in tackling this growing problem and is something that the RSPB has been calling @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with the UK Government to make sure that these islands remain globally important for seabirds , " he said . <p> The UK plays host to around a quarter of Europe 's breeding seabirds and the SPA network conserves breeding sites and foraging grounds for an estimated 70% of UK breeding seabirds and 37% of UK non-breeding waterbirds . <p> Special Protection Areas are a designation under EU law but officials said the protected areas would be maintained after Brexit . <p> This website and associated newspapers adhere to the Independent Press Standards Organisation 's Editors ' Code of Practice .
