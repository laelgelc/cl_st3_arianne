<h> Gold and oil prices surge , but investors need to be creative in how they react <p> Maybe the recent rise in global tensions between America and Iran could be the catalyst to set off the next shift in the oil and gold cycle . Before you dive into gold and oil , however , consider another perspective . <p> The oil cycle has been oscillating for more than 100 years . When the oil price is high , onlookers start talking about the price of oil being permanently high and refer to such concepts as ' peak oil ' , suggesting we are running out of the black stuff . But in such times , consumers slowly change behaviour , replacing existing cars with more fuel-efficient models , for example , or insulating their lofts . Simultaneously , oil companies invest more in exploration . <p> It can take several years , but eventually oil demand falls while supply rises -- the price can then collapse . <p> When oil is cheap , we often see the opposite effect . <p> In recent years , fracking has added a new dimension .
