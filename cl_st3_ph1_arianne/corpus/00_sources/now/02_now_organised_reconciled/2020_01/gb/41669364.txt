Because for the first time ever there is a political consensus that we are facing a climate emergency which threatens the future of our planet ... and something must be done to address it . <p> Scientific evidence demonstrating the scale of the crisis and the need to cut greenhouse gas emissions quickly has never been clearer . We can no longer afford a " business as usual " approach to managing the climate emergency . Radical changes are needed . <p> The UK will host COP26 , the United Nations climate change summit , in Glasgow in November 2020 . Euro VI buses are up to 95 per cent cleaner than earlier models and cleaner than equivalent cars . Not only that , buses offer up to 20 times the carrying capacity of standard cars .
