<h> New marine zone will help protect seabirds from fishing and coastal explorers <h> New Solent and Dorset Coast Special Protection Area ( SPA ) <p> Moves to help protect Dorset 's rare and vulnerable seabirds have been announced . <p> The creation of a special zone will protect birds from human activity , such as fishing or outdoor recreation . <p> Close to 1,000 pairs of three species of tern will benefit from a new Solent and Dorset Coast Special Protection Area ( SPA ) which will span more than 891 km2 . <p> The area , which includes the Purbeck coast east of Lulworth Cove , is the fifth most important foraging site in the UK for little terns and the seventh most important for common terns during their breeding season . <p> Environment Minister Rebecca Pow also confirmed a comprehensive Seabird Conservation Strategy , which will be published in December .
