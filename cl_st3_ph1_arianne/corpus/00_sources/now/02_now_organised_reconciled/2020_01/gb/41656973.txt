<h> Emergency response is a core activity of the Red Cross movement across the world , and its famous inverted Swiss Cross emblem is recognised globally as a symbol of help for people in crisis . <p> For example , Red Cross is supporting people left homeless having lost their entire communities as a result of the ongoing bush fires in Eastern Australia . <p> In Northern Ireland , the British Red Cross also responds to emergencies , where it provides humanitarian response in support of 999 organisations and multi-agency statutory responders including local councils and government departments .
