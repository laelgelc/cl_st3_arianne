<h> Boeing Suppliers Shift the Balance of Power , One Deal at a Time <h> Boeing Suppliers Shift the Balance of Power , One Deal at a Time <p> ( Bloomberg Opinion ) -- A would-be Boeing Co. takeover target has found its own dance partner . <p> Woodward Inc. , a maker of cockpit controls and engine-actuation systems , announced late Sunday that it 's merging with fellow aerospace supplier Hexcel Corp . The all-stock deal values Hexcel , a maker of lightweight composite materials , at about $7.5 billion including debt , with Woodward shareholders set to own about 55% of the combined company . While both suppliers have felt the sting of Boeing 's 737 Max grounding , executives said Sunday that the deal was n't a response to that crisis but rather an effort to position the combined company to better compete in the pursuit of more fuel-efficient engines . <p> The effort to sell this merger as a play on climate change is interesting , and it shows the degree to which companies are taking seriously the increasing criticism of greenhouse-gas emissions . With new technologies requiring ever-higher levels of spending , it also just helps to have a bigger balance sheet . Woodward and Hexcel expect to spend $250 million on R&amp;D in the first year after the deal closes , or about 5% of combined sales .
