EPA/MALDIVES PRESIDENCY / HO EDITORIAL USE ONLY <p> The archipelago 's former president Mohamed Nasheed famously held a cabinet meeting underwater to draw attention to submerging land and global warming a decade ago . <p> Yet the Maldives , best known for its white sands and palm-fringed atolls that draw luxury holiday-makers , has struggled to find money to build critical infrastructure like sea-walls . <p> " For small states , it is not easy , " Foreign Minister Abdulla Shahid told Reuters in New Delhi . " By the time the financing is obtained , we may be underwater . " <p> At the U.N . climate talks in Madrid in December , the Maldives and other vulnerable countries pushed for concrete progress on fresh funding to help them deal with disasters and longer-term damage linked to climate change -- but failed . <p> Shahid was hopeful the next round of talks , slated to take place in Glasgow in November , would yield better results . <p> One of the world 's lowest-lying countries , more than 80% of the Maldives ' @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , making its population of around 530,000 people extremely vulnerable to storm surges , sea swells and severe weather . <p> In 2004 , the Indian Ocean tsunami ravaged the Muslim-majority state , causing financial losses of around $470 million -- 62% of GDP -- and hitting infrastructure , including its only international airport that was shut for several days . <p> ' WE NEED IT ' <p> Two of the country 's main industries -- tourism and fishing -- are heavily dependent on coastal resources , and most settlements and critical infrastructure is concentrated along the coast . <p> In 2014 , more than 100 of the archipelago 's inhabited islands were already reporting erosion , and around 30 islands are identified as severely eroded . <p> The Maldives spends around $10 million annually for coastal protection works , but will need up to $8.8 billion in total to to shield all of its inhabited islands , according to a 2016 estimate by its environment ministry . <p> " In order to protect the islands , we need to start building sea walls , " Shahid said .
