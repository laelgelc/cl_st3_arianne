<p> Spekboom has been lauded as a wonderplant for its hardy and resilient nature . The City 's Recreation and Parks Department have announced they will participate in the Spekboom Challenge alongside Newlands Nursery . It plans to grow 5 000 spekboom trees to be distributed for planting during Climate Change Month in April this year . <p> The purpose of this initiative is to increase awareness about the importance of trees in the urban environment . Also known as Pork Bush or Elephant 's Food , spekboom has the ability to absorb more carbon dioxide than any other plant . <p> The Spekboom Challenge is a social media drive , motivating all South Africans to plant at least 10 spekboom plants each in 2020 . <p> " Planting trees is a vital part of the efforts by the City 's Recreation and Parks Department for Cape Town to be recognised as an Urban Forest . Our participation in the Spekboom Challenge will not only motivate communities to participate , but will also be a catalyst in our drive to Urban Forest recognition . The spekboom shoots will be planted at area-based events across the city on World Earth @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ will encourage residents to also get involved in tree planting and maintenance , " said the Mayco Member for Community Services and Health , Councillor Zahid Badroodien . <p> Apart from being a strong grower and a water-wise plant , Spekboom can live up to 200 years and has the ability to adapt to environmental changes . It can be planted to beautify a garden or open space , or act as windbreaks when planted in a lane . <p> It is also edible , with very high nutritional value and can be used in a variety of ways . A healthy thicket of spekboom is ten times more effective at removing carbon dioxide from the air than the Amazon , the ' lungs of the earth ' . This is based on its contribution to cleaning the air and removing carbon dioxide .
