<h> Global megatrends pose defence and security challenges : PwC report <p> Press Release issued by PWC - <p> 1st Dec 2016 <p> 2475 <p> The global megatrends are set to have a significant and disruptive effect on defence and security environments around the world , says a new PwC report . <p> This impact poses a need for more agility and accountability from governments and greater collaboration across the whole of society in order to combat risk , warn PwC global defence and security leaders . <p> The five megatrends that are widely believed to be shaping the future of our world are : the shift in global economic power , demographic shifts , accelerating urbanisation , the rise of technology , and climate change and resource scarcity . <p> Each of these key trends and their potential impact is analysed in a new report from PwC , entitled : " Five megatrends and their implications for global defense and security " . <p> The shift in global economic power will create more powerful national economies in different regions with greater resources to protect , and more resources to invest in defence and security . This could create both internal and external security issues that will require greater investment and innovative strategies to combat . <p> Accelerating urbanisation could mean the aggregate power of the growing megacities will rival that of the national governments due to the sheer size of their constituencies . The explosion in urbanisation will present significant challenges for law enforcement , intelligence and internal security agencies , as well as traditional defence organisations . Providing adequate police and security for these areas will be costly and will require a higher level of inter-agency information-sharing and collaboration . <p> The rise of technology offers exciting new technological @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . The challenge for defence and security organisations will be to develop and adapt these tools at the speed of business -- not the traditional speed of government . <p> Meanwhile , climate change and resource scarcity will increase tensions between nations over access to natural resources .
