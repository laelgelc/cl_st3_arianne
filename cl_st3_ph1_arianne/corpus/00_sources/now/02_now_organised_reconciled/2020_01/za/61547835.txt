<h> Youth group donates to primary schools <p> In a bid to help achieve Sustainable Development Goal ( SDG ) 4 on quality education for all , a youth group dubbed as Youth for Change ( Y4C ) has embarked on an initiative of donating assorted items to learners in different primary schools across the country . <p> On Friday , the group donated exercise books , ball pens and pencils approximately to 200,000 to over 200 students at Mzamba Catholic Primary School in Blantyre . <p> Speaking after the donation , the Organising Director for the group , Chisomo Munthali said they made the donation in order to motivate the students to excel in their studies . <p> " We believe that primary education is the basic of all what we do in life , so that 's why we came here and made this donation as well as to motivate the children to work hard in class so that they can do well in future , " Munthali said . <p> He added that the group is geared to assist youth in the country in different ways more especially in the education sector . <p> Apart from @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ renowned Hip-Hop artist Noel Chikoleka also known as Phyzix and Ivy Akim Chisambo who is an accountant at the Department of Climate Change and Metrological services . <p> The two offered a motivation talk to the students . <p> " This was a deliberate move to bring these two people here to motivate them , since we knew that a lot learners already know the two and they admire their work and talents , so this might push them to work hard in class to be like them one day , " Munthali added . <p> On his part , the headmaster for the school , Hasting Mulenga hailed Y4C for the gesture and he further appealed to other well-wishers to do the same since the school is facing a number of challenges . <p> He said the main challenge affecting the school is lack of desks and a deficit in number of Houses for the teachers . <p> " We have a problem with desks , it 's only in Standard 8 where students sit on desks and we only have 4 houses for teachers which are also in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ due to lack of houses here , " he admitted . <p> A 16-year old boy , Yankho Banda who is a Standard 8 learner , also thanked Y4C group for the donation and motivation , saying it will help him to work hard in his studies in order to achieve his goal . <p> Mzamba Catholic primary school which falls under Bangwe zone in Blantyre urban , has over 1 , 800 students . <p> Recently , the group also donated assorted items at Dolota Primary School in Mzuzu and had a sweeping exercise at Kamba Market in Blantyre .
