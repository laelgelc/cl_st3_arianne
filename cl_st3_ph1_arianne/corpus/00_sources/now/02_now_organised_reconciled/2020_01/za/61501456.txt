Globally , several countries from Australia to Poland saw temperature records broken . <p> The United Nations said last decade was the hottest in history , and all four of the hottest year 's worldwide have come in the last four years . <p> Earth has already warmed 1.1C since pre-industrial times due to greenhouse gas emissions from burning coal , oil and gas . <p> In order to avert climate disaster , the UN says global carbon emissions need to fall 7.6 percent annually to 2030 .
