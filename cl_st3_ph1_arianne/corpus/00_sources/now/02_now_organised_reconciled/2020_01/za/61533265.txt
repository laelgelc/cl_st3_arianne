<h> Thousands of students still fail to take up Nsfas funding <p> Lack of knowledge and misconceptions about the National Student Financial Aid Scheme ( Nsfas ) has meant that thousands of young people who are eligible for funding and who qualify to study at a higher education institution have failed to take up the opportunity . <p> This , despite the scheme being in existence since 1996 , and information about it contained in the life orientation subject content in schools . <p> For many learners , the scheme is the only way that they can afford tertiary education . This year , Nsfas received 543 ? 268 applications . Half of those were from students who are also grant beneficiaries . <p> But the number of applicants should be higher . <p> In an interview with the Mail &amp; Guardian this week , Nsfas administrator Randall Carolissen said misconceptions about the scheme have meant that people who qualify for funding -- especially so-called ' coloured ' people -- did not apply because they believed that the scheme was only for black African people . <p> Carolissen said he visited towns in the Northern Cape where schools had @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the learners had applied for funding at Nsfas because they did not know they qualified .
