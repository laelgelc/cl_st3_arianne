The result was a massive transfer of wealth to the financial , insurance and real estate ( or FIRE ) sector . <p> In South Africa , finance contributes 23% to GDP ( in the US it is 85% ) . It makes little contribution to the economy as it involves the re-trading of already-existing assets at ever-inflated prices .
