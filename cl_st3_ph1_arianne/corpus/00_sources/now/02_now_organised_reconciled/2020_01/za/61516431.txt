The Constitution gives municipalities exclusive power over " potable water supply systems and domestic waste-water and sewage disposal systems " , but higher tiers of government must monitor and support development of local government capacity . And , what is more , they must " see to the effective performance " of municipalities ' water functions . <p> The Constitution says everyone has the right to access sufficient water . So all spheres of government have a role to play , and constitutional duties in realising the right to water . <p> The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the " public trustee " of the nation 's water resources and must ensure that water is " protected , used , developed , conserved , managed and controlled in a sustainable and equitable manner , for the benefit of all persons " . " <p> The day-to-day management of the city 's water is the job of the municipality ( including cleaning and reticulation ) , but national government is responsible for oversight and bulk water supply .
