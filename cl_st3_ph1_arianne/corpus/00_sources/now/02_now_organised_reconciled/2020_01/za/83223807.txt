<h> UPDATE 2-Panama Canal reduces slots for ships due to droughts <p> 2 Min Read <p> ( Updates with details , context ) <p> PANAMA CITY , Jan 13 ( Reuters ) - The Panama Canal said on Monday it will cut its daily slot reservations due to droughts , and impose a " freshwater " charge on ships to maintain the thoroughfare 's levels , tightening access to one of the world 's most important trading routes . <p> Canal Administrator Ricaurte Vasquez told a news conference that from Feb. 15 , a fixed charge would be set at $10,000 for any vessel over 125 feet long , as well as a variable surcharge based on the level of Gatun Lake at time of transit . <p> " It 's not an easy decision , " Vasquez told reporters , adding that the short-term measure was needed to tackle the impact of climate change . <p> Daily reservations would be reduced to 27 from 32 , he said . <p> The canal authority said the measures were being imposed due to a lack of rainfall , which has negatively affected the supply of water from Gatun Lake , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ levels on the lake improve , the charge could be lowered and the reservation slots could rise , the canal said . <p> Millions of dollars need to be spent to guarantee the water supply to the canal , Vasquez said . <p> Local rainfall was 20% below the historical average last year , making 2019 the fifth driest year in seven decades . <p> The canal , which opened a new set of locks in 2016 , has historically handled about 5% of world trade .
