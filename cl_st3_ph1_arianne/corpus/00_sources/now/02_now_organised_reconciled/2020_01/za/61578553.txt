( Image : supplied ) <h> Following the lead of Swedish climate activist Greta Thunberg , thousands of school learners across the world are boycotting their classes every Friday to protest against inaction over the climate crisis . In 2020 , learners in South Africa are starting to take up the cudgels . One of the first will be Raeesah Noor-Mohamed who last week released a video on Instagram explaining her intentions . Maverick Citizen spoke to her during her school break on Wednesday . <p> Raeesah Noor-Mahomed is an ordinary 17-year-old schoolgirl . She 's worked hard throughout her school life , done well in her exams , and has dreams and ambitions for her life . But last week , she deliberately steered her life into uncharted territory .
