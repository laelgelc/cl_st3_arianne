<h> Climate Change : Paris agreement is possible , necessary and urgent <p> Climate change has increasingly become a global concern that needs global efforts to tackle and is a global challenge to both sustainable livelihood and economic development . <p> The adverse impacts of climate change are now evident in many parts of the world , including Tanzania . <p> It is a serious risk to poverty reduction and threatens to undo decades of development efforts . This is especially true where changes in the pattern of rainfall and temperature threaten sustainable development goals related to poverty reduction , water , food security , health , education and biodiversity loss . <p> Scientists have proved that for over 200 years , there has been a rapid burning of fossil fuel by the industrialised countries releasing enormous amount of CO2 in the atmosphere leading to changes in the climate system , making the globe warmer , leading to sea level rise , increased frequencies and intensity of extreme weather events such as droughts , heat waves , heavy rainfall and the associated impacts . <p> Unfortunately , although @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the adverse impacts are more pronounced in such countries due to their least adaptive capacity and weak early warning systems . <p> Addressing climate change is therefore an urgent challenge for Africa and for Tanzania in particular , given its vulnerability to the adverse impacts . <p> For Tanzania , the adverse impacts of climate change are already vivid in almost all sectors of the economy . <p> The twenty-first session of the Conference of the Parties ( COP ) and the eleventh session of the Conference of the Parties serving as the meeting of the Parties to the Kyoto Protocol which began on Monday will go on until December 11 , 2015 , in Paris , France to address the challenges of the Climate Change . <p> Put bluntly , the meetings in Paris this week are about whether we want to save this earth from overheating or become live subjects for grill . <p> The Paris UN Climate Conference therefore represents an historic opportunity to put the world on course to meet the climate change challenge . <p> The world needs a new model of growth that is safe @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ deliver a clear pathway with short and long term milestones , and a system to help us measure and increase progress over time until we get the job done . <p> Yet that seems a pipe dream .
