<h> Govt , stakeholders urged to mitigate climate change impacts <p> he government and environment stakeholders have been asked to help the public fight climate change impacts through education and provision of financial support . <p> Director of Tanzania Civil Society Forum on Climate Change Rebecca Muna said a study conducted by her organization in Njombe and Singida Regions to track government fund allocations on climate change impact on agriculture and livestock sectors for the past five years found that the amounts were not enough . <p> Muna said among areas affected by climate change impacts in the country included Panza Island Mwanyageni in Pemba South region with a population of about 600 people , which is about to be submerged into the sea . <p> She said their current studies aimed to find ways of minimizing climate change impacts in agriculture-related sectors , including farming and livestock keeping . <p> " Farmers and livestock keepers are victims of the impacts of climate change because of a limited budget of only 2 per cent to the agriculture sector , according to our study , which is not enough @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> " Ideally the government should reserve 10 per cent of the development budget for the agriculture sector , as the percentage was the consensus reached by the Malabo Summit states which met in 2014 , " says Muna <p> She added that between 2009 and 2014 a larger part of the budget on climate change impact came from donors and not the government . <p> She said climate change impact needed to be controlled as it could lead to drought , floods and even El Nino rains .
