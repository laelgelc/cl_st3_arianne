<h> Last Saturday of every month now Environment Day <p> The government has ordered that every Saturday of end of the month will be an Environment Day and everyone is to conduct cleanness as was done on December 9 , this year . <p> The announcement was made by Deputy Minister in the Vice President 's Office , Union Affairs and Environment , Luhaga Mpina mid this week in Dar es Salaam . <p> He also directed all districts , council directors and regional commissioners to oversee the planting of 1.5 million trees every year in their regions . <p> Mpina said Tanzania is under threat of becoming a desert if actions are not taken to conserve the environment . <p> " Planting trees is the only solution at this time where the world is experiencing huge impacts of climate changes and struggling to overcome them , " he said and warned that failure to implement the directions would result to stern measures . <p> " We are committed to address the impacts of climate change so if it will occur that some few people restrain the exercise @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ again warned . <p> The deputy minister cited that almost 61 per cent of Tanzania is at risk of becoming a desert because of human activities such as firewood gathering , cutting trees for industrial needs and charcoal burning all of which are converting semiarid regions into deserts . <p> He directed the officials to ensure that environment and natural resources are protected ; " they have to prohibit irregular use of forests in the areas , " he ordered . <p> He noted that over 400,000 acres of forests are destroyed annually by among other reasons , charcoal burning and industrial uses . <p> " Climate change has become a critical challenge likely to undermine global and country efforts to achieve sustainable development ... but planting trees will at large extent help curb the challenges , " he said .
