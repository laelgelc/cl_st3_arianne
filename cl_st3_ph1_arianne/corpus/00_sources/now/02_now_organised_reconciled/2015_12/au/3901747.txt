<h> Winners of the 2015 CRCSI Awards <p> Hosted at the iconic Melbourne Cricket Ground , the Cooperative Research Centre for Spatial Information 's annual awards ceremony is in its second year and celebrates and recognises the contributions to the successes of CRCSI across four award categories . This year 's awards took place during the Cooperative Research Centre for Spatial Information 's recent Annual Conference . <p> Prof David Lamb is the winner of the Research Excellence Award . As leader of the Precision Agriculture Research Group at the University of New England and CRCSI Science Director for Agriculture , Natural Resources and Climate Change , Prof Lamb conceived , designed and led the Biomass Business project . Through spatial information , Biomass Business empowers Australia 's response to climate change by transforming the way public and private land managers balance agricultural productivity and sustainability .
