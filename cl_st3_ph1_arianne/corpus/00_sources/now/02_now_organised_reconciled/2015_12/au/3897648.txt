<p> The study was based on data collected over eight years of research into ways to reduce emissions in livestock as part of Meat and Livestock Australia 's methane abatement program . <p> The methodology also brings the National Greenhouse Gas Inventory in line with the estimates of the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change , the leading international body on the assessment of climate change established by the United Nations and the World Meteorological Organisation in 1988 . <p> CSIRO 's Ed Charmley says the work was conducted because of concerns and doubt surrounding the accuracy of previous calculations . <p> " Different methods used to calculate emissions from livestock in temperate and tropical regions were based on studies done in the 1960s and 1990s , mainly with dairy cattle , " says Dr Charmley . <p> " Both of these past methods were found to be likely over-estimating the emissions from cattle . " <p> The analysis of Australian cattle research data was conducted by CSIRO , the Victorian Department of Economic Development , Jobs , Transport and Resources , the New South Wales Department of Primary @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Department of Agriculture and Fisheries , with technical input from the Australian Government Department of Environment .
