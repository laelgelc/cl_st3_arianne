The economic case for battery storage is further augmented by electricity providers typically charging higher rates during peak demand . Batteries could save costs by providing stored electricity in more expensive periods . With sufficiently powerful and cost-effective battery storage , it 's theoretically possible that one day homes and business may go off the electricity grid . <p> From a technological perspective , battery storage is a mature industry and there are hundreds of suppliers providing reliable systems . However , there are significant barriers to overcome before battery storage can be fully integrated as a mainstream option in the power sector ; these barriers include cost , performance and safety issues , general functionality , regulatory barriers and utility acceptance . Nevertheless , recent developments indicate that these barriers can be beaten . One notable high-profile example is the recent launch of Tesla Motor 's Powerwall for residential-use battery packs and Powerpack for commercial-use battery packs . In this respect , even Musk has conceded that battery storage is uneconomic in most cases . This view is echoed in the consensus of academic and sell-side analyst opinion that suggests that it will likely be several years before battery costs drop sufficiently to allow widespread adoption . According to energy storage expert Professor Peter Bruce of Oxford University , average battery costs will need to at least halve from today 's US$300 per kilowatt hour to about the US$100 to US$150 per kilowatt hour to become commercially viable. 7 The good news , however , given the fast rate of technical progress , is that it can only be matter of time before this happens . <p> Wind boost <p> Wind power , with its limited applicability for consumers , attracts less interest than solar even though it leads on most measures of global renewables adoption .
