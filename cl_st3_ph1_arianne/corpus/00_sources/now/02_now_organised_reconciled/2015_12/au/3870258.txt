The legacy provided by Governor Macquarie and successive caretakers is Sydney 's most important heritage asset , where nature and the harbour coalesce . Combined , the garden and adjoining Domain provide serenity , recreation and inspiration to more than seven million people every year . <p> However , there can be a tendency for the public to take this green infrastructure for granted @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ cultural institutions . Imagine Sydney without the " lungs of the city " -- and then think about its important ongoing contribution to the quality of human life in a densely populated city . The botanic gardens , the Australian Native Garden at Mt Annan , the Blue Mountains Botanic Garden at Mt Tomah and the Domain play an increasingly important role in the liveability and sustainability of greater Sydney . These are priorities for the government and the Greater Sydney Commission . <p> It would be hard to find many Sydney residents who do n't have their own personal memories and connections with the botanic gardens . However , the role of the Royal Botanic Gardens goes well beyond recreation . We connect people with plants through imaginative horticulture , beautiful landscapes and transformational learning experiences . <p> The key question now is the role and responsibilities of the garden in the future . It was created 200 years ago to enrich the quality of people 's lives -- and that objective remains relevant today .
