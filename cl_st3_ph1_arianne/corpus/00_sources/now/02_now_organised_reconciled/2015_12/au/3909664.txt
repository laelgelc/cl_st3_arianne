Land Services of Coast and Country objected to this , on grounds including potential climate change impacts , and impacts on endangered species . <p> Following a five-week hearing earlier this year , the Land Court yesterday recommended that the Carmichael Mine be approved , subject to additional conditions . <p> The Land Court recommendation is not binding on government ; under the respective Acts , the Environment Minister must " have regard to " the recommendation , and the Mines Minister must " consider " it . <h> The federal approval process <p> Under the federal approval process , the federal environment minister may approve an action .
