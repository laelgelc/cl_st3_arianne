<h> Pacific Beat <h> Australia 's warming back flip ' nothing short of a miracle ' : Tony deBrum <p> Updated December 07 , 2015 09:29:17 <p> Australia 's Foreign Minister Julie Bishop has flown to Paris to lead Australia 's negotiating efforts for the final week of the climate change summit . <p> Over the weekend Australia agreed to support a goal of limiting global warming to 1.5 degrees Celsius , in exchange for more favourable carbon emissions rules . <p> 1.5 degrees Celsius is the level demanded by low-lying Pacific island nations most at risk from climate change , but Pacific leaders say they also need stronger curbs on carbon emissions to protect their countries from disappearing under rising sea levels . <p> From Paris , the Marshall Islands foreign minister Tony de Brum spoke about what the Marshall Islands wants from the final week of negotiations .
