In Paris , Arctic indigenous people have been strongly lobbying to have their rights mentioned in the final climate agreement . <p> As Aili Keskitalo , President of the Saami Parliament of Norway , told a conference side event in Paris , Saami reindeer pastures are suffering from worsening snow conditions , but mining the minerals needed for wind farms , as well as the wind farms themselves , is also destroying their pastures . <h> Mitigation and adaptation <p> Given the existing greenhouse emission levels in the atmosphere , climate change can not be completely avoided any more .
