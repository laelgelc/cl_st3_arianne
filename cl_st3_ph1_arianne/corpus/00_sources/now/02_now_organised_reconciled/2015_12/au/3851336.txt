<p> The 202020 Vision is hosting a workshop programmed specifically to address the unique opportunities and challenges facing Western Australia in urban greening . <p> To be attended by several WA Councils , the one-day 202020 Vision workshop being held on Wednesday 2 December in Perth will present a platform for all participants to share knowledge and discuss the mitigation of various issues arising out of climate change . <p> A new Roy Morgan poll reveals that almost one in five people in Western Australia has named climate change as the biggest issue facing Australia , outstripping the level of concern in all other states by 10 per cent or more . This could be attributed to WA 's longstanding vulnerability to the impacts of climate change and in particular to water scarcity issues .
