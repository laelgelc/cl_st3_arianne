<p> The Paris Agreement is a truly landmark climate accord , and checks all the boxes in my five-point scorecard for a potentially effective deal . It provides a broad foundation for meaningful progress on climate change , and represents a dramatic departure from the Kyoto Protocol and the past 20 years of climate negotiations . <p> In Paris , representatives of 195 countries ( plus the European Union ) adopted a new hybrid international climate policy architecture that includes : bottom-up elements in the form of " Intended Nationally Determined Contributions " ( INDCs ) , which are national targets and actions that arise from national policies ; and top-down elements for oversight , guidance , and coordination . Now , all countries will be involved in taking actions to reduce emissions . <p> Remarkably , 186 of the 196 parties to the agreement submitted INDCs by the end of the Paris talks , representing some 96% of global emissions .
