<h> The best books for Christmas gifts in Canberra : Colin Steele 's suggestions <h> Colin Steele <p> Shaun Usher 's Letters of Note was an international bestseller in 2014 . He has now followed up with More Letters of Note , ( Canongate , $59.99 ) , each accompanied by full page illustrations , covering " the heartfelt , the historically significant , the tragic , the comic and the unexpected " . Amongst the 122 letters are Jane Austen complaining in November 1800 to her sister Cassandra that she drank too much at a ball and Richard Burton 's moving letter to Elizabeth Taylor after she left him in 1973 . <p> Beatrix Potter 's September 1893 letter , with illustrations of four rabbits , marks the first ever appearance of Peter Rabbit , while Katherine Mansfield , in March 1923 , tells her husband 's mistress , Princess Elizabeth Bibesco , to stop sending love letters to him , " I do not like scolding people and I simply hate having to teach them manners " . Usher concludes his delightful potpourri of correspondence that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . . . whichever letter you land on will offer you a snapshot of history " . <p> Tom Adams is a noted English artist , whose work over 70 years includes advertising , portraits , posters , film work and , most notably , book covers . Tom Adams Uncovered , ( Harper Collins , $54.99 ) , is " designed to reflect my life through my pictures " . Although " it is not a written autobiography " , there are many personal insights in his comments accompanying the numerous full-page colour illustrations . Adams first came to notice with his famous 1963 cover of John Fowles ' The Collector . Other iconic book covers , mixing fantasy and realism , were undertaken for Eric Ambler , Desmond Morris and not least Agatha Christie . Adams ' most famous corpus of work is undoubtedly the 150 Agatha Christie paperback covers from the1960s and 1970s . All the covers have captions by Adams , with commentaries by Christie historian John Curran . Tom Adams Uncovered is an ideal Christmas present for both Christie and graphic art lovers . <p> Another @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ? illustrated version of Lewis Carroll 's Alice In Wonderland , ( Hodder , $29.99 ) issued to mark its 150th anniversary . Writers and Artists Under English Skies , ( Thames and Hudson , $55 ) , is sure to generate interest . Harris traces through literature and art , " the weather as it is daily recreated in the human imagination " . Harris ' comprehensive survey begins with Roman legionaries feeling the cold on Hadrian 's Wall and ends with contemporary concerns on climate change . Literary figures cited over the centuries include Chaucer , Shakespeare , the Romantic poets , Jane Austen , Benjamin Britten and Ted Hughes . <p> Harris supplements her text with 60 carefully chosen illustrations . His young adult novel , The World According to Anna , ( Weidenfeld &amp; Nicolson , $24.99 ) , follows 15-year-old Anna , through her dreams in 2012 , to a climate-change ravaged world of 2082 .
