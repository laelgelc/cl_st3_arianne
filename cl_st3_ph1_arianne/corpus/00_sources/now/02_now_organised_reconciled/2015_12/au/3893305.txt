<h> The goal <p> The purpose is to hold global warming to " well below " 2 degrees Celsius over pre-Industrial Revolution levels , and to strive for 1.5C if possible . <h> Getting there <p> The world will aim for climate-altering greenhouse gas emissions to peak " as soon as possible " , with " rapid reductions " thereafter . <p> By the second half of this century , there must be a balance between the emissions from human activity such as energy production and farming , and the amount that can be captured by carbon-absorbing " sinks " such as forests or carbon storage technology . <h> Burden-sharing <p> Developed countries , which have polluted for longer , should take " the lead " by taking on absolute emissions cuts . <p> Developing nations which still need to burn coal and oil to power growing populations , are encouraged to enhance their efforts and " move over time " to cuts . <p> Rich countries are required to provide support for developing nations ' emissions cuts . <h> countries make the costly shift to green energy and shore up their defences against climate change impacts like drought and storms . <p> Funding must be scaled up , and the agreement says rich nations must report every two years on their finance levels -- current and intended . <p> Moved from the legally binding core agreement to a separate non-binding " decision section " , the document refers to
