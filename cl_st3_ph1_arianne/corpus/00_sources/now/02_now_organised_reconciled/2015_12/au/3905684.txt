The islands are made up of coral atolls , making diving the biggest draw for tourists . <p> But the islands are threatened by shifting conditions that are the result of climate change , including rising sea levels and storm surges . Tour guide Haraoki Ueda also said that he has noticed extensive coral bleaching , which happens when water temperatures rise .
