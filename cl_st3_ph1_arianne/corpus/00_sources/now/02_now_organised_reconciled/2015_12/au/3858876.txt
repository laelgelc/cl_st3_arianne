Hence , in Paris , they are seeking to limit global warming to less than two degrees . Why two degrees ? For cold countries , surely there will be some benefits from a warmer climate . After considering the benefits and damages from global warming , governments decided that the risks and damages of global warming far outweigh any possible benefits . These risks grow rapidly for greater levels of global warming . So they chose to try to limit global warming to only two degrees .
