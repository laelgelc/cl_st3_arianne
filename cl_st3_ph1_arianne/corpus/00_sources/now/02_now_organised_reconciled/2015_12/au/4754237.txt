Well , that was the first stage in a project that examines the sedimentary record of the lake bed . <p> Advertisement <p> " The lake is an outstanding natural archive -- it contains the longest continuous sedimentary record ( around 4 million years ) of any Australian lake and has a long , unresolved human occupation history , " explains Papp , who adds " the core samples taken extended to up to 77 metres below the lake bed and represent around 2 million years of sedimentary history .
