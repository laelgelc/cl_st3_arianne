<p> The Paris climate agreement , which commits countries to " pursue efforts " to limit global warming to no more than 1.5 ? above pre-industrial temperatures , sends a much-needed political signal that the world is ready to take serious action on climate change . <p> But how do we actually go about limiting warming to 1.5 ? ? The Paris Agreement acknowledges that developed countries need to lead on reducing greenhouse gas emissions , but action in developing countries will also need to be swift , with poorer countries requiring support for a rapid transition to a clean energy future . <p> While the Paris Agreement has been interpreted as heralding the end of coal and ushering in a new age of renewable energy , it does n't explicitly say those things .
