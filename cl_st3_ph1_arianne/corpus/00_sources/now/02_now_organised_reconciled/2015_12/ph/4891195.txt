<p> PARIS -- The head of the Philippine delegation has said the country will speak up on behalf of vulnerable countries during the ministerial level meetings of the climate change negotiations in Paris . <p> Climate Change Commission ( CCC ) Secretary Manny de Guzman on Saturday told Filipino journalists that the Philippines , which is the incumbent president of the Climate Vulnerable Forum ( CVF ) , has vowed to represent the cause of at least 43 countries before the ongoing 21st Conference of Parties ( COP21 ) . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> The CVF now has 43 members , from only 20 country participants last year . <p> " Since we have a common ground and shared aspiration as regards to the Paris climate change agreement , we met yesterday and we reaffirmed our solidarity as a group , as a leadership and advocacy group of vulnerable countries , " De Guzman , who heads the Philippine delegation , said at the sidelines of the COP21 . <p> " We made that reassurance and we also expressed and conveyed to them as incumbent president of the CVF @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ next week , " he added . <p> De Guzman explained that the group wanted a " strong voice . " <p> " The voiceless should have a strong voice through the incumbent presidency of the CVF . We may not be hearing them articulate in the negotiation but as one group of advocacy leaders for a meaningful Paris climate change agreement , we reaffirmed that at the ministerial level we will actively be participating as one group , " he said . <p> De Guzman said there are still contentious issues regarding the draft COP21 agreement , which has recently been reduced to 21 pages . <p> " We will present our position echoing the provisions of the Manila-Paris declaration of the CVF on the contentious issues . There are still contentious issues , " he said . <p> The CVF on November 30 adopted a Manila-Paris Declaration of the Climate Vulnerable Forum , which includes its views on the draft Paris agreement . <p> The CVF is not a negotiating bloc in the COP21 but it has been pushing for the 1.5C goal or the goal to limit global @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ The current goal of COP21 is two degrees Celsius . <p> On Friday , the Philippines convened an " extraordinary ministerial session " of the CVF after a collapse in the Review of the 2 degrees Long-Term Goal under the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) . <p> De Guzman , in a statement , earlier said the resolve of the group became stronger after the meeting . <p> " The movement for a safe future is unstoppable . Countries denying science and reason will have to face the growing number of countries and citizens calling for the 1.5 degree target to be reflected in the Paris agreement , " he had said . <p> The Philippines has been one of the poster boys of climate change after it was devastated by Super Typhoon Yolanda ( international name Haiyan ) in 2013 .
