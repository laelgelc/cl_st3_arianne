<h> Senators hope for unity in 2016 <h> Senators hope for unity in 2016 <p> A DAY before 2015 ends , some senators hoped that Filipinos learn from the challenges they faced this year and that they would be united despite their differences . <p> " Good or bad , these challenges allowed us to prove once again the resilience of the Filipino people and our oneness , " Senator Grace Poe said in a statement . <p> " The New Year is always a hopeful time , as we celebrate the conclusion of one year and the beginning of another . In this spirit , let us continue to hope , pray and work for glad tidings , good health and abundant graces for our family and the nation , " she added . <p> As the people welcome 2016 , Senator Fedinand Marcos Jr . said they could have another chance to " set things right , for reaffirmations with dreams afresh embracing new joys .
