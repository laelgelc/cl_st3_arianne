<h> Photographers Focus to Save the Land <p> It may take a showing of photographs to convince some in the public and private sectors that the Khmer environment is at risk because of illegal acts on the forested lands of the Kingdom . <p> This photo exhibition is the third of its kind organized by the Building Community Voices Organization in cooperation with forest and land communities of Cambodia . <p> Pry Phally Phuong , the executive director of the organization , said yesterday at the Bophana Center in Phnom Penh , where the photos are on display , that the show is to help communities advocate and find solutions to environmental problems . <p> " We encourage them to work by themselves to advocate with policy makers to solve their issues , " she said . <p> Penh Art , a photographer of the Steung Khsaach Sar Forest Community of the Bribo district in Kampong Chhnang province displayed her photographs taken of an illegal coal furnace located in the community forest .
