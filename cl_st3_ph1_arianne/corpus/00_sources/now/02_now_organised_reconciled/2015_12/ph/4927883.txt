<h> Monsanto vows to be carbon-neutral by 2021 <p> Agrochemical and agricultural biotechnology firm Monsanto Company vowed to be carbon-neutral by 2021 with the production of carbon-neutral crops and seeds and in its crop protection operations in close collaboration with the farmers . <p> Hugh Grant , Monsanto chairman and CEO , said climate change is one of the biggest challenges facing the agriculture sector and one of the most pressing concerns of humanity , which is why the company is doing its part within its business to help support farmers and others . <p> " While progress has been made to reduce agriculture 's carbon footprint , we must work collectively to do even more if we are going to sustainably feed 9.6 billion by 2050 , " Grant said in a statement . <p> In the Philippines , the production of carbon-neutral crops would be pushed hard as the country is the 13th most climate-vulnerable in the world , said Monsanto Philippines country head and CEO Sandro Rissi . <p> Monsanto Philippines said the production of carbon-neutral crops would contribute to mitigating the serious impact of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Filipino farmers . <p> The production of carbon-neutral crops will be implemented across Monsanto 's business units in the Philippines focusing on : a ) seed production by leveraging diverse products and agronomic approaches such as breeding , plant biotechnology , data science , conservation tillage and cover cropping ; b ) crop protection by developing a program to provide incentives to farmer-customers who adopt carbon-neutral crop production methods and c ) sharing data , increasing adoption of best practices based on the models developed by the US corn belt , where the most accurate data on yields , soil types , crop rotations and best management practices are publicly available . <p> The US models on carbon-neutral corn and soybeans have the potential to reduce emissions from crop production equivalent to 100 million metric tons of carbon dioxide , which is equal to reducing 233 million barrels of oil consumption per year . <p> The company has a division called Climate Corporation , where advancements of data science in agriculture , innovations and other data scientists allow farmers to plant and harvest crops more precisely than ever .
