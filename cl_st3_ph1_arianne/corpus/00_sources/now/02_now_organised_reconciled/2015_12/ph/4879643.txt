<h> #TINDOGTAYO2016 Mar highlights need to prepare for disasters <p> Many people say that Filipinos are used to typhoons . But being used to them is not enough , according to Liberal Party standard-bearer Mar Roxas . Rather , Filipinos must be prepared . <p> In his behalf , Akbayan Representative Barry Gutierrez presented Roxas ' plans to make the Philippines more resilient to disasters brought by climate change , as well as the programs that the former Interior Secretary had been able to put in place during the present administration . <p> Representatives of Senators Miriam Defensor-Santiago and Grace Poe were also present at the Tindog Tayo 2016 forum held at the GT-Toyota Asian Center Auditorium in Quezon City . <p> According to Gutierrez , Roxas was able to accomplish the following . <p> Respond to major calamities like the Supertyphoon Yolanda and Typhoon Ruby , as well as the Zamboanga siege and the Bohol quake . Roxas was on the ground to see what needed to be done and work towards that . <p> Prepare communities for disasters , through the Department of the Interior and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ government leaders , but ordinary citizens . They tried their best to boost the knowledge and ability of the people so that they would know what to do before , during , and after disasters . <p> Implement Project Listo , a program which provides LGUs with a checklist of what needs to be done before , during , and after a disaster . These guidelines were first used during Typhoon Ruby , and helped minimize casualties . Evacuations were conducted effectively , relief operations sped up , and roads cleared . It was an offshoot of the Yolanda experience , a way of making up for the deficiencies then . <p> Ensured greater coordination between LGUs and national government agencies . Also a product of the Yolanda experience , this was the means to clarify the role of the national government and the LGUs during disasters . <p> Made the level of preparedness in terms of disaster risk reduction and management a basis for awarding the Seal of Good Local Governance . <p> On the other hand , these are Roxas ' plans should he become president . <p> Continuously @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . Fisherfolk can not be moved out of coastal zones because it is a source of livelihood .
