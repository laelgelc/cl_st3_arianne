( AP Photo/Petr David Josek ) <p> CSA MINE -- While world leaders try to reach a deal to limit climate change , one of the most polluting fossil fuels , brown coal , is enjoying a revival in the Czech Republic , where entire villages are threatened by new plans for mining . <p> The Czech Republic is one of a group of countries that is turning to coal , a cheap but dirty energy source , as its economy slows . Last year , the Czech Republic mined 38.8 million metric tons , down 5.5 percent from the previous year . <p> " We will need this coal for the heating industry , power plants and households , " Prime Minister Bohuslav Sobotka said . <p> World ( Article MRec ) , pagematch : 1 , sectionmatch : 1 <p> It is the first time the country has eased the limits on brown coal mining that it put in place in 1991 , two years after the collapse of the communist regime , which relied heavily on industry and mining .
