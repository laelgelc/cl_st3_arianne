<h> A slow road to recovery <h> A slow road to recovery <p> Dried up crops . In this photo taken last June , a man walks through a farm in San Fernando . Climate change and unpredictable weather have been blamed for the poor performance of the region 's agriculture sector . Crops and fisheries continued to decline , dragging down total agriculture production for the first nine months of 2015 to a drop of 4.8 percent . ( Sun.Star File ) <p> IT WAS another negative year for agriculture in Central Visayas , although there was some recovery noted in 2015 . <p> Total production dropped by 4.8 percent from January to September 2015 , the latest data available showed . <p> Among the following subsectors--crops , fisheries , livestock and poultry--the last two saw significant recovery when total production grew by 3.4 percent and 12.6 percent , respectively . <p> The rest are still in the red . Crops declined 7.6 percent , and fisheries went down 2.4 percent .
