<h> December 7th , 2015 04:38 PM <p> PARIS -- The Singapore government said on Saturday its climate action plan submitted ahead of the Paris climate talks is unconditional on external support . <p> But the government also said that if the talks fail to clinch a global deal to fight climate change , then Singapore would have to review its plans . <p> " Singapore submitted its INDC ahead of the 21st Session of the Conference of Parties to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) to demonstrate our commitment to the anticipated agreement at the meeting , " a spokesman for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs said in response to media queries . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> " Singapore 's INDC ( Intended Nationally Determined Contribution ) is not conditional on external support , unlike the INDCs submitted by many non-Annex I parties , " the spokesman said . <p> Non-Annex 1 countries are developing countries under the UNFCCC . <p> Under Singapore 's INDC , the government intends to reduce the nation 's emissions intensity by 36 per cent from 2005 levels @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ peaking around 2030 . <p> The Ministry emphasized that climate change required a global solution . <p> " If the negotiations in Paris do not succeed , not only will the INDCs submitted by countries become nullified , but climate change would worsen , " the spokesman said in a statement . <p> " Some actions which we and other countries pledged to undertake would only be meaningful if all countries undertook to do the same . As a city state on a low-lying island , Singapore would have to review our plans at that point . While we would still proceed with measures to improve energy efficiency and economic resilience , we would have to reallocate our limited resources to deal with increased threats , including sea level rise , floods and droughts .
