She said the United States had shown a " high level of political commitment " to ASEAN , further evidenced by top level visits from President Obama , Secretary of State John Kerry and Secretary of Defence Ashton Carter this year . <p> It is expected that the future summit talks will again centre on territorial disputes in the South China Sea , a dominant theme during the discussions in Manila and Kuala Lumpur .
