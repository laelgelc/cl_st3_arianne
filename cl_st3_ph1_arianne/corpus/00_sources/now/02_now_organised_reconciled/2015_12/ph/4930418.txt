<h> LNG shippers brace for wave of consolidation as freight rates sink <p> Singapore -- Shippers who ply the seas to deliver liquefied natural gas ( LNG ) in massive tankers are likely to face a wave of consolidation and asset sales , with freight rates plunging as a growing fleet clashes with tepid demand . <p> Companies that manage to weather the shakeout in one of the key sectors in the global shipping industry should be in a prime position , however , to benefit from a string of new LNG projects expected to start trickling online by the end of next year . <p> " I see more mergers and acquisitions , more consolidation and a lot more joint ventures as things get tougher , " said Andrew Bridson , business development manager at maritime consultancy BMT Asia Pacific . <p> " That is not a bad thing .
