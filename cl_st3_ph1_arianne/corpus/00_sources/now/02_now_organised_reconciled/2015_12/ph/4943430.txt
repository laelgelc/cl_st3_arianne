<h> Russia warming 2.5 times quicker than global average <p> Moscow - Russia is warming more than twice as fast as the average for the rest of the world , the environment ministry said Friday , sounding an alarm on the rise in floods and wildfires nationwide . <p> A government report on environmental protection said temperatures in Russia had warmed by 0.42 degrees Celsius per decade since 1976 , or 2.5 times quicker than the global warming trend of 0.17 degrees . <p> " Climate change leads to growth of dangerous meteorological phenomena , " the ministry said in a comment to the report published Friday . <p> There were 569 such phenomena in Russia in 2014 , " the most since monitoring began , " the ministry said , specifically mentioning last year 's ravaging floods and this year 's " water deficit " east of Lake Baikal , which led to a " catastrophic rise in fires .
