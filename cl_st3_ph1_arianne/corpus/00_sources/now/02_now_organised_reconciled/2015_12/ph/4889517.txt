<h> Aquino hopes COP21 to yield positive result vs climate change <h> Aquino hopes COP21 to yield positive result vs climate change <p> PRESIDENT Benigno Aquino III has expressed hope that the 21st Conference of the Parties ( COP 21 ) that he attended in Paris will yield enforceable commitments from countries to address climate change . <p> Aquino , who attended the Paris climate conference on November 30 , said the good thing about the debate on climate issue is that countries now start to recognize that climate change is real . <p> Super Typhoon Haiyan , or Yolanda , which hit the Philippines in 2013 , painted a very grim picture of exactly what climate change is all about , he said . <p> " Not too long ago , if everybody recalls , there was even a debate as to whether or not there was climate change .
