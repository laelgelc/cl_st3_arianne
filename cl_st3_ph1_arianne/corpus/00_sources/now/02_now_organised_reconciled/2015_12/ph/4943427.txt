<h> Jarales : Non-negotiables <h> Jarales : Non-negotiables <p> CHRISTMAS Day has come and gone , but we have yet to see the conclusion of the recent word war between presidential aspirants Mar Roxas and Rodrigo Duterte . <p> Of course , the end of their spat may only come once the May 2016 elections are held -- and so the end is not too far . In a few days will come the new year . With it comes the ever-growing need to encourage discourse on the future of our country 's politics . <p> I never followed the word war closely ; it looked to me too congratulatory of typical Filipino politician behavior , too resemblant to an MMFF entry to be even remotely mentally stimulating . It has come to the point that if you ask people how they will fill up their ballots , they will most likely say that they will choose the least evil , rather than discern who comes closest to the ideal . <p> What makes the ideal Filipino president , after all ?
