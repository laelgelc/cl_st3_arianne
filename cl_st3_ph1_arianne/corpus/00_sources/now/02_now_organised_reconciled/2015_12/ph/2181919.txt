<h> Erap allots P600-M for Manila barangays ' disaster readiness in 2016 <p> MANILA , Philippines -- Manila Mayor Joseph Estrada on Tuesday allotted P600 million for the disaster preparedness of the city 's 896 barangays next year . <p> Johnny Yu , officer-in-charge of the Manila Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office , said the allocation would make the city 's barangays substantially capable of mitigating the impacts of climate change in 2016 . <p> " With the incremental and year-round preparations of Manila , the city , especially at the barangay level , is now relatively ready to face any disaster or calamity in 2016 , " Yu said . <p> He said the allocation was " additional support " for " trainings , tools and equipment , and other DRRM concerns . " <p> " Rescue vans , amphibious trucks and jeeps and fiber boats were already purchased for the barangays , " he added . <p> Yu said the allocation was made possible by high revenues from enhanced tax collection . <p> The United Nations Office for Disaster Risk Reduction ranked the Philippines fourth @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ over the past 20 years . <p> The country experienced 274 disasters from 1995 to 2015 . <p> The Department of Finance estimated that calamity-caused economic losses amount to at least an initial 1.1 percent of annual gross domestic product , or around P130 billion in 2014 . But the lingering effects of calamities could easily bring the total to 2.0-2.5 percent of GDP , according to the Climate Change Commission . <p> Early into his term , Estrada , a former president , said : " Calamities cause damages to properties , infrastructure and facilities ; paralyze the flow of people and products ; hassle businesses , communication and supply of water and electricity ; and impact heavily on the economy and the people 's lives in general . With a good disaster program , we can lessen these impacts caused by calamities . " <p> Yu said Manila 's DRRM program has earned the city the Seal of Disaster Preparedness and the Seal of Good Housekeeping from the Department of Interior and Local Government .
