<h> Pacete : 2016 : Hopes , wishes and aspirations <h> Pacete : 2016 : Hopes , wishes and aspirations <p> HOPE is a confident expectation that a desire will be fulfilled . Wish is a kind of feeling having unfulfilled satisfaction with respect to something or someone . Aspiration is our ambition to be fulfilled . For the New Year , our dream is loaded with hopes , wishes , and aspirations . <p> We hope that there will be peace in the world ... that terrorists will become extinct . ( They could simply shoot each other or retire without asking pension . ) We wish that world leaders will find a way to find solutions to problems brought about by climate change by stopping the proliferation of coal-fired power plants . We aspire that all churches will join this crusade if they do n't want body and souls to get burned while still on earth . <p> We hope that powerful countries will stop their greed for military and economic conquest at the expense of third world countries . ( We also aspire that ... that only genuine God will appear . ) <p> We hope that the United Nations Organization will find a 24-carat solution to human trafficking . We do n't want to see our young women become whores in drug dens operated by the mighty drug lords ( also gambling lords ) who financed political warlords . We wish that our children will not become easy preys to pedophiles that do witch hunting and crave for young human flesh . We aspire that our agents of authority have enough balls to fight the obvious . <p> We hope that education will cast out poverty ( not poverty to eat education ) . We can only wish that our government will continue financing serious research that will elevate the wisdom-level of human beings . ( We do n't want to believe that we come from the planet of the apes . ) We aspire that educated men will become effective leaders . Ceremonies bore God . God wants emphatic prayer coupled with proofs . We can always aspire that religions will start looking for God in our midst . <p> We hope that in the Philippines , Filipinos will elect a president who will lead us to peace , unity and prosperity . We wish that we will stop killing each other ( no more rebels with a cause ) . We can only do that when our leaders will stop dwelling in their political casket . We wish to see the Philippines managed by political leaders who have risen above their greed and immaturity . We aspire that no more mothers will grieve for the loss of their children in the battlefield or in the political arena . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ us make the farmers our heroes by giving them the best opportunity . Politicians can not give us our daily rice . We wish that our fishermen will also receive a preference from the pedestal of government . Just like the farmers , they need support to feed the people of God . We aspire that the government will run after those who engage in illegal fishing . <p> We hope that our hacienda and factory workers will enjoy profit sharing . Capital is nothing without labor . Teachers , nurses , engineers , doctors and other government workers wish for salary increase . Workers in the private firms aspire also to level off with government employees when it comes to salary and other fringe benefits . <p> Without the fulfillment of our hopes , wishes and aspirations , 2016 will just become the unpleasant part of our history . History is made by people who create memorable events . Together , let us make significant actions . Together , let us make excellent event for 2016 .
