<h> His Majesty to Visit Laos <p> As requested by the head of state , the Cambodian Royal King will spend official three days visiting the Lao People 's Democratic Republic , starting from Monday . <p> In a Royal Letter , dated December 19 , His Majesty Preah Bat Samdach King Norodom Sihamoni , King of Cambodia said , he would be absent from the country from today to Wednesday in order to make an official visit as requested by Choummaly Sayasone , head of state of the Lao People 's Democratic Republic . <p> According to the letter , during his absence , King Norodom Sihamoni will grant his status to Say Chhum , president of the Senate , making him acting head of the kingdom of Cambodia . <p> King Norodom Sihamoni was earlier absent from the country to attend the opening celebration of the Paris Climate Change Conference from November 22 until December 6 . <p> Earlier this year , he also visited the Socialist Republic of Vietnam for three days from September 24 to 26 , as requested by the Vietnam state government @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ two countries .
