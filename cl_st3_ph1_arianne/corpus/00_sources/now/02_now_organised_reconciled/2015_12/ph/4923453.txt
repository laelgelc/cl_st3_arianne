<p> THE Cebu Provincial Disaster Risk Reduction Management Office ( PDRRMO ) will conduct more orientations and seminars on disaster management for all the local government units , schools , and other private groups next year . <p> PDRRMO head Baltazar Tribunalo said that for this year , they conducted disaster management seminars to 44 municipalities and seven cities in the province . The office was also tapped by the local governments of Masbate City , Zamboanga City , Davao City and Surigao City to hold orientations and seminars in their areas . <p> Tribunalo said the PDRRMO has also engaged the 2,700 youths , 1,250 priests and lay ministers , 925 teachers , and 4,650 cooperative members in disaster management seminar this year . <p> Tribunalo said they also conducted the first water summit in the province . <p> " We have also provided typhoon assistance to some municipalities in the province .
