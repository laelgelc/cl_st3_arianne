<p> PARIS -- In another move supporting the call for a greener world , the Vatican on Dec. 8 , 2015 , projected on the facade of St. Peter 's Basilica an hourlong light show aimed at inspiring change around the climate change crisis . <p> The show , called Fiat Lux , which roughly translates to " Let there be light , " included images of various wildlife -- among them a tiger , an elephant and a stingray -- and time lapses of glaciers melting and international communities protesting the degradation of the environment . <p> Fiat Lux was broadcast live on YouTube at 7 p.m . Paris time ( 2 a.m. in Manila ) . Paris and Rome share the same timezone . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> Earlier in the day , Cardinal Peter K. A. Turkson , papal envoy to the 21st Conference of Parties ( COP21 ) supported the climate goal of below 2 degrees Celsius at the eighth meeting between ministers of the 195 parties .
