<p> Gov . Araceli Wong encouraged participants of the 5-Day Training Seminar on Disaster Preparedness , Rescue and Relief Operations on November 23 at Camp Francisco Camacho Social Hall in Virac to reecho their learnings to barangay levels . She believes that residents ' and barangay captains ' involvement in disaster management will ensure that problems of climate change or calamities will be given immediate action . <p> Wong also urges multi-sectoral participation of the different stakeholders in disaster preparedness and rescue management like the Philippine Atmospheric Geophysical Services Administration ( PAGASA ) , Department of Science and Technology ( DOST ) , Provincial Disaster Risk and Rescue Management Council ( PDRRMC ) and Local Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council ( LDRRMC ) . <p> The training was attended by 53 personnel from Provincial Police Safety Company ( PPSC ) and Municipal Police Stations of the province is aimed at developing skills for disaster mitigation before , during and after a large scale calamity .
