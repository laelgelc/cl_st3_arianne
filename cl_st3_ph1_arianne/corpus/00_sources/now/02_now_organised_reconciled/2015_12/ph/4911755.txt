<h> PH committed to climate pact <p> A long-term temperature goal of 1.5 degrees Celsius in the Paris climate pact may be bold and difficult but the international community has committed to exert efforts to attain such target , Ma-laca ? ang declared yesterday . <p> Presidential Communications Operations Secretary Herminio Coloma Jr . recognized that the limiting global warming to 1.5C to pre-industrial level was listed as an " aspirational goal " in the Paris pact . <p> After almost two weeks of diplomacy and negotiations , a historic agreement to tackle climate change was adopted by 195 countries . The pact commits every nation to reduce the greenhouse gas emissions that cause climate change . The global cap of 1.5C target was vigorously pushed by a Philippine-led coalition of countries most vulnerable to the worst effects of climate change . <p> " We recognize that this is a difficult goal but countries such as the Philippines are determined to fulfill their commitments and do everything possible to combat climate change , " Coloma said in Filipino . <p> Echoing the words of President Aquino at the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Philippines has taken " proactive steps " to mitigate the impact of climate change such as the national greening program . <p> The administration has also pursued the renewable fuels program and implemented Project NOAH ( National Operational Assessment of Hazards ) in response to the impact of climate change . <p> " In the long term , we hope that with the collective effort of countries , we can reduce the level of global warming to the target of 1.5 degree Celsius , " Coloma said . <p> Presidential Spokesman Edwin Lacierda said they welcome the approval of the " breakthrough agreement " to halt global warming during the 21st Conference of the Parties ( COP21 ) to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change . <p> " By signing the pact -- the world 's first comprehensive climate agreement -- 195 nations made the commitment to limit the rise of global temperatures to well below 2 degrees Celsius , with the bolder aim of 1.5 degrees Celsius , " Lacierda said .
