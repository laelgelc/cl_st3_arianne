<h> Troopers engage communities in pre-Christmas activities <p> December 29 , 2015 <p> Maria Teresa B. benas <p> LAGANGILANG , Abra , Dec . ( PIA ) -- The 24th Infantry ( Wildcat ) Battalion of the 7th Infantry Division concluded its first pre-Christmas activities for a Cause on December 14 and 15 at its headquarters in Sitio Barbarit , Brgy . Tagodtod this town with support and participation of stakeholders . <p> On the first day was the Basketball Friendship Games joined by Lagangilang Municipal Police Station , Tagodtod Youth and 77thCivilian Armed Forces Geographical Unit ( CAFGU ) . <p> Aside from engaging community dynamics , the troopers also led the Road Run and tree planting to intensify their role in mitigating and combating the adverse effects of climate change . <p> Another featured activity was pinning of rank insignias to battalion personnel in recognition of their meritorious service for the country . Tineg Mayor Corinthia Crisologo accompanied Lt.Col .
