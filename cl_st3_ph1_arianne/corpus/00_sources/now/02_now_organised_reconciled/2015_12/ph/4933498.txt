<p> MANILA - After the Supreme Court dismissed their petition , a nun and a priest have asked high tribunal to reconsider its decision and appealed to Chief Justice Maria Lourdes Sereno to help implement the results of the climate talks in Paris and issue a temporary environmental protection order ( TEPO ) against the 200-megawatt Sarangani coal-fired power plant . <p> According to a Aksyon Klima Pilipinas statement , petitioners Sr. Susan O. Bolanio , OND and Fr . Rey Carvyn Ondap , CP noted that among the decisions made at the 21st Conference of Parties ( COP21 ) in Paris was the phase-out of fossil fuels . <p> " As a result of the Paris Agreement , the world is on its path toward the phase-out of fossil fuels and coal-fired power plants will become an anomalous way of generating energy , " said Bolanio . <p> Last December 12 , the Parties to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change adopted the Paris Agreement , a new international agreement that consolidates international action against climate change . Among its key actions is to enhance @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ fossil fuel-free economy on all countries , including the Philippines . <p> " The judiciary should play its role in phasing out fossil fuel-based forms of energy generation and the Supreme Court , through the leadership of Chief Justice Sereno , can play a key role by issuing a TEPO against the Sarangani coal-fired power plant now , " said the feisty nun from the Mindanao-based Oblates of Notre Dame who has amassed a significant track record fighting progressive causes dating back from the Martial Law era . <p> Last May 2014 , Bolanio and Ondap , of the Passionist-Center for Justice , Peace and Integrity of Creation Inc. based in General Santos City , filed a petition against the 200MW coal-fired power plant in Kamanga , Maasim , Sarangani Province operated by the Sarangani Energy Corporation . <p> The court that received the case , the Justice on Wheels of Sarangani , took some time to act on the petition , and refused to issue a temporary environmental protection order ( TEPO ) . This led the two to go to the Supreme Court , which summarily dismissed their petition @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ December 2015 . <p> The organization handling the petition is Biodiversity , Innovation , Trade and Society ( BITS ) Policy Center Inc. , a member of Aksyon Klima Pilipinas , a national network of civil society groups engaged on issues relating to climate change .
