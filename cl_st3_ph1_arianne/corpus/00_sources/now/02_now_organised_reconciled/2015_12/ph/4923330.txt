<h> Pangilinan : Giant lanterns , rain and shine <h> Pangilinan : Giant lanterns , rain and shine <p> BY THE time this column comes out , everyone , at least festival goers and festival organizers alike , will be a little bit worried about the weather and whether or not drizzles or thunderstorms will mar the Giant Lantern Festival 2015 . <p> The last time it rained during the giant lantern competition a few years back , there was a slight delay in the program but the event pushed through as planned . <p> The past few days had us busy with typhoons and low pressure areas , adding more cold showers to the already chilly months . With the rapid changes in our weather and climate these days , one of the realities that the festival organizers and giant lantern makers have to deal with climate change adaptation as part of its sustainability . <p> For one , while the festival organizers are doing marvels in the form of makeshift structural adaptations to waterproof the rotor systems and provide a roofed stage for the VIP guests @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for next year 's staging which will address the present challenges being faced . <p> On the other hand , giant lantern makers will have to test their innovative prowess and think of waterproofing the rotor system and make it fit for any weather condition . I am pretty sure that the young and daring innovators among our lantern makers will be up to the challenge . <p> What has greatly impressed me the past few days in the face of these seeming threats to organizing an event of this magnitude is the synergy of the public and private partnership of the Giant Lantern Festival Executive Committee and City Government of San Fernando who have pulled their efforts and committed their resources together . For me , this really is good governance in action . It perfectly illustrates how a deep sense of pride of place can empower the citizenry in actively participating in the governance process . <p> Small and great things can really be accomplished if we know how to work together . From offering eggs to Saint Claire and asking for the public 's support as prayer warriors @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ protect the rotor systems , whether cling wraps or hairdressers will be put to good use , the Giant Lantern Festival committees just keep on moving forward and facing challenges head on . <p> Moreover , our Giant Lantern Festival organizers never waver in their faith and belief that the festival will push through , come rain or shine . They draw their strength from the resilient and indomitable spirit of the Kapampangans , and above all , from the penultimate source that has made all things possible . <p> The Giant Lantern Festival endures , rain and shine ! <p> Published in the Sun.Star Pampanga newspaper on December 19 , 2015 . <p> Latest issues of Sun.Star Pampanga also available on your mobile phones , laptops , and tablets .
