<h> Slight Decline in Rice Production in 2015 <p> Khmer Times/Sum Manet <p> Sunday , 27 December 2015 <p> 1280 views <p> Rice production declined slightly in 2015 due to drought in some areas , according to estimates from the Ministry of Agriculture , Forestry , and Fisheries , but officials say this will not affect domestic supply or exports . <p> Cambodia produced 9.2 million tons of rice this year compared with 9.3 million tons of rice in 2014 , according to preliminary data . <p> Hean Vanhan , deputy general director of general department of agriculture , said rice production declined slightly because of drought in some areas . However , there remains about 3 million tons of rice in stock , he added . <p> " It does not affect food security or exports , " Mr. Vanhan said .
