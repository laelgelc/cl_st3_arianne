<p> COTABATO CITY - Environment officials have started planting one million trees in the Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao ( ARMM ) in line with the National Greening Program ( NGP ) , officials said . <p> The one million trees are to be planted in the province of Maguindanao and Lanao del Sur this year , according to Kahal Kedtag , regional secretary of Department of Environment and Natural Resources ( DENR-ARMM ) . <p> Another one million trees will also be planted in the provinces in the region . ARMM is also composed of Basilan , Sulu , and Tawi-Tawi . <p> Kedtag told reporters that the objective of this project is to reduce the effects of climate change in the region , starting with Maguindanao followed by Lanao del Sur and three other provinces . <p> " Our objective is to help in climate change mitigation , " Kedtag added , adding that the seedlings for the project can reforest up to 50,000 hectares of balding lands . <p> Kedtag said the watersheds in Dimapatoy , South Upi , and Kabulnan in Ampatuan - all @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to be planted with the seedlings . <p> In 2011 , ARMM Governor Mujiv Hataman had implemented a total log ban in the region through Executive Order 001 . <p> The region has a total of 585,000 hectares of forest land . <p> ARMM now has 301,000 hectares of forest cover , an increase of 6.7 percent compared to 282,640 hectares in 2003 after a " precise geo-mapping , tagging , and monitoring , " Kedtag said . <p> To ensure the success of the program , Kedtag said his office has commissioned a local community , mostly of Teduray ethnicity , in Datu Odin Sinsuat town to raise the seedlings at the department 's central nursery at Sitio Lomboy , Barangay Awang also in Datu Odin Sinsuat , Maguindanao . <p> Green jobs <p> More than 400 residents joined in the bagging of the seedlings and availed themselves of jobs under the cash-for-work program of DENR-ARMM .
