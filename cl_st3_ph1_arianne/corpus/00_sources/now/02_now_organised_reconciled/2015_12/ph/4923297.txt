<h> Agusan Marsh threatened by conversion , human activity <p> Bunawan , Agusan del Sur -- The conversion of a vast track of the 14,836-hectare Agusan Marsh Wildlife Sanctuary ( AMWS ) into palm oil tree plantations , as well as its re-purpose for agriculture and other forms of land use , has threatened the natural haven located in the landlocked province of Agusan del Sur . <p> This was disclosed by environmentalists who warned about the alarming rate at which the Agusan Marsh is being converted from a rich haven of flora and fauna . <p> The AMWS is home to 135 flora species , 64 non-peatland bird species from 32 families , 92 bird species who thrive in peatlant that include 36 new species , 44 endemic animal species , 11 mammal species that include four newly-discovered species . <p> Agusan Marsh is also the site of thousands of migrant birds coming from European and Asian countries during summer .
