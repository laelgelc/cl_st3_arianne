<p> PARIS -- A legally binding agreement to curb global carbon emissions may have already been signed , but the fight for climate justice is far from over for the thousands of environmental activists who painted Paris red in protest of the lack of action against climate change . <p> Hours before the 21st Conference of Parties ( COP21 ) officially adopted the Paris Agreement on Dec. 12 , 2015 , an estimated 2,000 demonstrators converged at the Arc de Triomphe and Eiffel Tower -- both historic landmarks in the City of Lights -- at 12 p.m. and 2 p.m. , respectively . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> Protesters weaved a long red banner in front of and around the protest venues , dancing and chanting to the tune of , " What do we want ?
