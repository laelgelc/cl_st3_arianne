It is a bank holiday . The Oxford English Dictionary gives the earliest attestations of the term as originating from England in the 1830s , defining it as " the first week-day after Christmas Day , observed as a holiday on which post-men , errand-boys , and servants of various kinds expect to receive a Christmas-box . " <p> The term Christmas-box dates back to the seventeenth century , meaning a present or gratuity given at Christmas given to those who are supposed to have a vague claim upon the donor for services rendered . The box may include leftover food from last night 's Christmas festivity to share with the poor .
