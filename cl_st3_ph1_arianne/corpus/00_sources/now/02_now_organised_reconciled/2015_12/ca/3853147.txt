" <p> " As a senator , he said , he was expected to hold informed opinions under as many headings as were listed in the Federal Directory -- on weapons and civil rights as well as on nuclear energy , education , Indians ( American and Miskito ) , communications theory , military intelligence , Arizona ground water and the volume of barge traffic on the Mississippi river , " Lapham writes . <p> " It 's a joke , of course , " he Moynihan said . A dangerous joke , maybe , but still a joke .
