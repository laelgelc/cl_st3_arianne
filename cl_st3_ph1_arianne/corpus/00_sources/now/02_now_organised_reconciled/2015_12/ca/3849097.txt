<h> Selinger must put our energy interests ahead of Hydro 's <p> The sun sets not he western horizon silhouetting the Manitoba Hydro generation station in Brandon . <p> Premier Greg Selinger is about to release Manitoba 's climate change strategy , an overview of how this province can ease up on its greenhouse gas emissions . One element in the plan will be conserving energy -- and Manitoba Hydro is central to the goal . <p> How Manitobans can be pushed to cut back on consuming energy -- specifically electricity and natural gas -- is a heated debate . Manitoba Hydro , the monopoly seller of hydroelectricity , has come under fire for doing a poor job on conservation and efficiency . There 's little surprise in that , given the inherent conflict for a Crown corporation created to build dams and sell power . <p> That conflict was highlighted by the Public Utilities Board in its report last year on Hydro 's multibillion-dollar capital plans , which include new dams and transmission lines to beef up its capacity to send power to customers , initially in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ once a conservation leader , but now behind many jurisdictions -- does not give what is called " demand-side management " the respect it deserves . It has underspent its DSM budget by more than $35 million since 2012-13 , and the take-up on its Power Smart programs has fallen noticeably short of goals . Going into the PUB hearings , Hydro hiked its conservation targets , but only for the near future . <p> That 's not good enough , said the PUB . <p> Demand-side management is n't just about conservation . It includes looking at alternate sources of energy , such as solar and wind , to meet consumer demand and public policy goals . <p> The PUB concluded only an external , independent agency could set reliable , hard targets to cut energy use . <p> Such bodies exist in the U.S. , where they also aim for greenhouse gas emissions cuts , watch the shifting ground of energy markets and alternative sources . In Vermont , the agency cut two million tonnes of CO2 emissions and saved 297,000 megawatt/hours of energy in 2009-11 . The technology of alternative energies , especially solar power , is changing fast and may become a real option for homeowners . It 's the kind of guidance an external , independent authority could produce . <p> It is also the kind of advice that could mess with Manitoba Hydro 's capital plans , aggressively pushed by the Selinger government and backed by the utility 's own forecasts of how domestic demand for energy will rise . The plan is predicated on annual rate hikes of 3.95 per cent that will double electricity bills by 2032 . With spot export prices so low , why would Hydro want ratepayers to cut back or switch to natural gas for heat ? <p> What 's the NDP government to do ? Tell the PUB thanks , but no thanks , for its advice . It may allow Mr. Selinger to dress up Hydro as an environmental leader poised to cut energy demand by 1.5 per cent annually for the next 15 years or so , along with GHG emissions . <p> But a subsidiary will never jeopardize the mothership 's course , so it ca n't put Manitobans ' interests first .
