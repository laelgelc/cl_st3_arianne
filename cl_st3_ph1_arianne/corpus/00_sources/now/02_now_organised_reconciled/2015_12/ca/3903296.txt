<p> On the very same day the UN climate summit kicked off in Paris , Vancouver 's port authority approved a cost-saving amendment allowing for the proposed Fraser Surrey Docks terminal to export massive amounts of thermal coal to Southeast Asia on ships rather than barges . The irony has n't been lost on environmental activists . <p> " It was just such a stark contradiction in the timing around this most recent approval where the port authority is improving a new thermal coal port on day one of global climate talks , " says Laura Benson , Dogwood Initiative 's Beyond Coal campaign director . " One foot 's going backwards into the 19th century and one foot 's trying to move ahead into a brighter future where we can fight climate change . " <p> Fraser Surrey Docks , owned by a Macquarie Group-managed investment company , currently exports lumber , steel and containers .
