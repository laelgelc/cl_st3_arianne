<p> The Alberta government will spend $1.2 million demolishing 17 flood-damaged homes that it purchased in three Calgary neighbourhoods after the 2013 flood , but residents will get a chance to suggest uses for the properties , Municipal Affairs Minister Danielle Larivee said Wednesday . <p> Larivee said the homes in Elbow Park , Rideau Park and Roxboro must be removed , but residents will be invited to provide input in January about what they would like to see in those public spaces . <p> " Decisions by the previous government have left a patchwork of vacant homes and residents have questions and concerns about the impacts of this program on the quality of life in their neighbourhoods , " she said in a tele-conference Wednesday .
