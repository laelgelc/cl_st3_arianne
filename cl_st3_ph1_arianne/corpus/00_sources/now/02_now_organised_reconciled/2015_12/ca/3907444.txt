<h> Threatened North York mural saved by outpouring of community support <h> ' Scary ' parts of mural had elicited a few complaints <h> Mural art <p> The artists who created a controversial mural in the Caledonia Road and Lawrence Avenue area said they are " deeply moved " by community support , which ultimately allowed the piece to remain as is . <p> " The Essencia Art Collective is deeply moved by the overwhelming community support that our most recent mural on Lawrence West has received , " Shalak Attack , Fiya Bruxa , and Bruno Smoky wrote in an email to The Mirror . " The positive outcome of the public forum is a reflection that this mural is something greater than ourselves , and after witnessing an overwhelming amount of support in the past four weeks where we have received thousands of written testimonials of support , and again at the public forum where over 100 people came in support of the mural , we feel this mural belongs to the community . " <p> In a statement sent by Councillor Frank Di Giorgio 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Dec. 8 to discuss the piece deemed " scary " by some residents , which virtually all attendees showed support for , his office sent word the mural will remain as is . <p> " Dear friends , thank you very much for your emails and phone calls to my office on the mural at Lawrence Avenue West and Caledonia Road , " Di Giorgio 's statement read . " On Tuesday , December 8 , 2015 , I held a public consultation at Amesbury Community Centre to discuss the new mural installed along Lawrence Avenue West near Caledonia Road . <p> " The mural was funded through a grant awarded by the City of Toronto 's StreetARToronto ( StART ) program . However , due to unforeseen circumstances , the original location of the mural had to be changed at the last minute , and a fulsome consultation was not held with the residents of Ward 12 . <p> " Prior to the meeting , some members of the Ward 12 community expressed concern over a few images in the mural . Others contacted me to express their support @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ program . Therefore , to address these differing opinions , I convened a community meeting so that residents could hear City staff describe the overall StreetARToronto program , and so that the artists could present their vision , inspiration , and background information about the mural . <p> " After the presentations , community members in attendance were invited to comment and provide feedback to the artists . Close to 125 people attended the consultation . Through that discussion it was clear that the community considered the mural to be a welcome addition to the neighbourhood . Therefore , it will remain as installed . " <p> The 300-metre-long mural , created by artists from Essencia Arts Collective , was completed in mid-November . But shortly after the piece , which tells the story of global warming and the environment , was finished , Essencia Arts Collective received word from the city that Di Giorgio 's office had gotten several complaints about the work . <p> The piece is made up of imagery of glaciers , deserts , forests , water , animals , and birds , along with magical scenes @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ pollution - all an ode to Mother Earth , the common theme in Essencia Arts Collective 's works . <p> Di Giorgio said concerns focused on the west part of the piece , specifically images of a deer skull and a person wearing a gas mask . <p> The artists , who call themselves visual storytellers , explained during the meeting the piece deals with climate change and the fossil fuel dependent generation , and how species will disappear due to society 's current lifestyle . They also said removing even one image would be detrimental to the entire piece .
