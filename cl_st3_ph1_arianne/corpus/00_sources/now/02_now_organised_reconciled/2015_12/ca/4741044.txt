<p> Female ministers in Prime Minister Justin Trudeau 's cabinet fielded fewer than half as many questions as their male colleagues in the House of Commons in December . <p> Though Trudeau achieved a perfect gender balance by appointing 15 men and 15 women to his cabinet last month , a Citizen analysis of House transcripts shows that the men got far more talk-time during the few days of question period that took place . <p> Women in cabinet gave 46 responses to opposition queries during Parliament 's first full week , while men in the ministry responded to 98 -- not including the 40 responses that came from Trudeau himself . <p> Which minister responds to a question is mostly determined by the opposition MP who asks it , although the government side may put forward another minister to respond where appropriate . <p> That men in Trudeau 's cabinet got much more talk time is partly a function of their higher-profile positions in cabinet , as they hold the key jobs of finance ,
