<h> Dissent the cornerstone of the scientific process <p> DUBAI , United Arab Emirates Dec. 8 , 2015/ Troy Media/ -- The climate change debate has become so polarizing that legitimate dissenting science is being buried . <p> For many people , climate change is a defining issue of our time . But for others , the idea that man-made greenhouse gases cause climate change is a distraction from more significant issues . <p> Certainly the Earth 's climate is changing and part of the reason is human activity -- farming , transport , energy systems and population growth . <p> But the science of climate change is not nearly settled . The climate data from satellites and other physical measurement systems do not match the predictions derived from computer models . The Earth is not warming as fast as predicted , despite the significant rise in CO2 . Extreme weather events , according to the International Panel on Climate Change , are complex and not caused by human activity . However , the level of damage they cause is very much linked to where people live ( @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and in vulnerable coastal and island regions ) . <p> Many climate scientists will tell you that estimating climate sensitivity to CO2 is problematic . In addition , the role of other factors -- the sun , clouds , winds , ocean currents , El Nino and La Nina , land and sea sequestration of CO2 -- is not fully understood . Judith Curry , a leading climatologist at Georgia Tech , points out the climate is a " wicked " problem . It is also messy as a science and a significant challenge for public policy . <p> Reducing human emissions of CO2 may not have a significant impact on the rate of warming -- it may be that natural factors play a much bigger role than some scientists think . Danish author and environmentalist Bjorn Lomborg estimates the full impact of the Paris COP21 commitments to reduce CO2 emissions will be less than 0.05C by 2100 . <p> The idea that the sun is a key factor driving Earth surface temperatures is not new . And the scientific process must encourage differences of opinion based on a hypothesis supported by data , evidence and analysis . Advancement is made through debate , challenge and dispute . A hypothesis , over time , can change or be replaced based on new data . <p> Some scientists suggest that those who disagree with them are " deniers " of an established science . The word is used to discredit those whose views differ from their own .
