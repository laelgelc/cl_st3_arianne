<h> Rising levels of nitrogen dioxide in India affecting air quality <p> Using new , high-resolution global satellite maps of air quality indicators , NASA scientists have tracked air pollution trends over the past decade . The latest maps show emissions of NO2 have gone up significantly over South Asia . <p> Nitrogen dioxide ( NO2 ) is a yellowish-brown gas . Nitrogen Dioxide is formed when nitric oxide ( NO ) , which is emitted by motor vehicles or other combustion processes , such as power plants and industrial activity , combines with oxygen in the atmosphere . <p> The United States and Europe are the biggest emitters of NO2 , however , there has been a significant reduction in levels recorded between 2004 and 2014 .
