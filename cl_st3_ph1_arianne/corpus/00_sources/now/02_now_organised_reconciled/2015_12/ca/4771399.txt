<h> Scott Simon : A beacon of hope for sustainable environmental practices in the South China Sea <p> Since the South China Sea is widely regarded as one of the world 's potential flashpoints in the year to come , it is reassuring to see a number of regional peace initiatives . <p> Recently , Taiwanese Minister of the Interior Chen Wei-zen led a group of officials to Itu Aba Island ( Taiping ) , the largest of the disputed Spratly Islands . Chen presided over the opening ceremonies for a renovated wharf and new lighthouse , and the unveiling of a memorial tablet . This naturally formed island , inhabited by a few hundred coast guards , includes a farm , a hospital , a Buddhist temple and facilities that can be used for humanitarian rescue efforts at sea . Historically , the island was used as a weather station by the French and as a naval base by Japan . It has been under the jurisdiction of the Republic of China ( Taiwan ) since troops landed there in 1956 . The Philippines has tried to characterize Itu Aba as a " rock , " which is clearly misguided when one considers the past and present use of the territory . According to Article 121 of the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea ( UNCLOS ) , it is an " island " because it has proven to sustain human habitation and economic life . Taiwan has been more helpful by proposing pragmatic cooperation , as it has done recently in fishing agreements with both Japan and the Philippines in disputed waters . Over the past 10 years , Taiwan has used Itu Aba as a base for rendering humanitarian assistance in 20 cases , including the rescue of Philippine nationals . <p> Taiwan 's government has promised to deal with the islands of the South China Sea by " setting aside sovereignty issues " and " jointly developing resources , " but Itu Aba is clearly more important than the oil resources that may exist below it and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ avian and marine species . Since 2007 , the Taiwanese municipality of Kaohsiung has managed a turtle reserve on the island and research has been done on the preservation of other species . <p> The island is also an important monitoring station for numerous climate change initiatives . Since 2009 , Taiwan 's Environmental Protection Agency has been cooperating with the United States to test water quality and monitor aerosol in the atmosphere . The U.S. National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration has been using it to jointly monitor greenhouse gases . Taiwan 's Ministry of Science and Technology hopes to establish a scientific research station there for research projects on climate , oceanography and marine conservation . As scientific activities on the island intensify , it will also be a strategic site for advance-warning of natural disasters resulting from climate change and rising sea levels . <p> Taiwan promises to transform Itu Abu into an island of peace , ecological preservation and low carbon emissions , as part of President Ma Ying-Jeou 's South China Sea peace initiative .
