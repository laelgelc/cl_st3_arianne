<h> In Shanxi , China , where residents live in the shadow of nearby coal fired plants . <p> A history of heavy dependence on burning coal for energy has made China the source of nearly a third of the world 's total carbon dioxide ( CO2 ) emissions , the toxic pollutants widely cited by scientists and environmentalists as the primary cause of global warming . China 's government has publicly set 2030 as a deadline to reach the country 's emissions peak , and data suggest the country 's coal consumption is already in decline . The governments of more than 190 countries are expected to sign an agreement in Paris to set targets on reducing carbon emissions in an attempt to forge a new global agreement on climate change .
