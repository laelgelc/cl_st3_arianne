<h> Gary Lamphier : Numbers tell the story on the state of our planet , good , bad and ugly <p> Hey Donald Trump , I 've got news for you : more Mexicans are actually leaving the U.S. these days than are trekking north to the land of opportunity . It 's true . <p> In fact , since 2009 , 140,000 more Mexican migrants have headed south than north , as a sluggish U.S. economy hindered job growth and Mexico 's economy expanded . That 's the first time that 's happened since the 1940s , according to the Pew Research Center . <p> Take the threat of climate change .
