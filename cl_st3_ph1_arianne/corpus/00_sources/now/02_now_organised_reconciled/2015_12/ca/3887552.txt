<h> Two songs you need to hear : Sean Michaels 's playlist of the week Add to ... <p> I 'm composing this from a room with tall glass windows , mountains and conifers all around , everything streaked in snow . Winter in Canada has so many different magnificences , but one of the most acute is the way the landscape will at times simply wear the weather . When the blizzard 's long past , when the winds die down , the snow-streaked land just lies before you -- serene and awake , painted by the day . <p> These are good times for listening to music . Every song seems to compliment the wide-open December . It almost does n't matter what it is , Sade or Bartok or Ornette Coleman or DIANA or Pusha T -- any of it soundtracks the tranquil cold . But today I 'm going to recommend Steve Gunn 's song Way Out Weather , released last year . Like Kurt Vile and The War On Drugs , who are former bandmates , Gunn is interested in a particular glimmering @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ reverb , drums that shamble and jog . Way Out Weather 's a folk-country that seems to sway on its feet -- that weird waver when you bend back too far , stare up too high , stargazing . " Way out weather is a common song , " Gunn sings in a soft drawl , " pullin ' tides show what 's wrong . " Maybe it 's a tune about climate change , maybe just the spirit 's high and low pressure systems . Whichever , whatever ; one of art 's dirty secrets is that effect outweighs intent . I do n't know where Gunn is , I do n't know what he 's thinking . But Way Out Weather is playing right now , as I write this , and it will go on playing for as long as daylight lasts . <p> The Evaporators -- What If I Care About the People Who Live in the Seas ? ( 2007 ) <p> This week brought the extremely upsetting news that Nardwuar the Human Serviette has suffered a stroke . The Evaporators bandleader is one of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ irreplaceable punk-rocker , radio DJ , archivist , tam-wearer , interviewer and ambassador for " Vancouver , British Columbia , Canada . " A fixture of UBC 's CITR radio , MuchMusic and , more recently , YouTube , Nardwuar has built a career from encyclopedic knowledge , shameless enthusiasm , and a loopy sense of humour . He has dumbfounded the likes of Ed Sheeran and Pharrell Williams and " hip-flipped " with two prime ministers ; joining him on the airwaves , last winter , was one of the indisputable highlights of my career . <p> But Nardwuar 's foremost love is probably his band . The giddy , garagey Evaporators occupy an essential place in the CanCon canon and there 's no better way to wish Nardwuar well than to rock out with a song like this . It 's silly ; it 's proud ; it 's punk rock at its brazen best . Get well soon , Nardwuar . We need you .
