<p> CONCORD , Ont. -- An access ladder leads through a hatch to the roof of the Ozz Group of Companies in Concord , just north of Toronto . On the roof spread 750 south-facing solar panels , built at the Silfab Solar Inc. factory in nearby Mississauga . Gauges on the Canadian-made Satcon inverters in the basement show that even on this short , cloudy day , this 170-kilowatt array has by 2 p.m. produced 67 kilowatt-hours of electricity . <p> The landlord , who owns the solar panels , signed a deal with Ontario in 2010 , by which the province buys the electricity for 71.3 cents per kWh .
