<p> YELLOWKNIFE -- The Northwest Territories has its first two-term premier . <p> Incumbent Bob McLeod will return to the job after winning a majority vote in the territorial legislature Wednesday under the rules of the territory 's consensus government . <p> " It makes me feel really good , " said McLeod , 63 , settling back into the chair in his legislature office . <p> " It took a lot of soul-searching , because I recognized that none of the premiers before had even bothered to seek a second term . " <p> Premiers in the Northwest Territories are chosen after general elections by members of the legislature . Most of the 19 legislature members who won seats Nov. 23 are rookies , which had led to some speculation that there might be a mood for a change in premier . <p> But McLeod -- a longtime veteran of northern government -- was successful over challenger Glen Abernethy . <p> The straight-talking , good-humoured McLeod was a bureaucrat in both the federal and territorial governments before he was first elected in 2007 .
