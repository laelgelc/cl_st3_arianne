Our members are young , old , retired , heads of family ... and even people who have taken off work just for the day to come and volunteer here . " <p> " We have faith-based members , as well , " he said , citing two French Catholic-based member organizations -- Secours Catholique , the French branch of Caritas , and CCFD-Terre Solidaire , a French Catholic development agency -- as well as Catholic " pilgrims from the Philippines who walked all the way to Paris . " <p> Loret noted that Coalition Climate 21 had been one of the main organizers of the Nov. 29 rally to combat global warming ; the rally was canceled by French authorities after the Nov. 13 terror attacks in Paris . " <p> Many attending the coalition 's events at the public cultural center Dec. 8 projected that the climate conference in Paris -- or COP21 , as it is known -- was similarly doomed . <p> They claimed COP21 was controlled by big industries and corporate interests and speculated a climate change agreement friendly to poor nations and indigenous peoples was impossible . <p> " Time and time again you guys have failed to show up with real solutions , " said Xiuhtezcatl Martinez , a 15-year-old Aztec Indian , environmental activist and hip-hop artist from Boulder , Colorado . <p> " It 's up to the people to change the world .
