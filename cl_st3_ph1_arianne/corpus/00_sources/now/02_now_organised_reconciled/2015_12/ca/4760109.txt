Hot cup of kindness <p> The concept of paying it forward is brought to life in Fort Simpson . At the Northern Store , Manager John Dempsey keeps a little slip of paper at the till . When folks pay for their gas or groceries , they often also pay forward a cup of coffee or a soup . The store then makes the coffee or soup available for an elder without much money , or someone else who is struggling . <p> A sewing club that sprung up in Gameti caught the attention of CBC North producer Rachel Zelnicker .
