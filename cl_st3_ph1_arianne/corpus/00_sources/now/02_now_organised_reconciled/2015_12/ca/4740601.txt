Warm weather so far is not great news for natural gas producers . ( RYAN TAPLIN / Staff / file ) <p> Warmer than usual winter weather is great for Nova Scotia energy consumers but not so much for natural gas producers offshore . <p> " We 're looking at one of the warmest starts to winter in 50 years if not of all time since they started recording ( temperatures ) in the 1800s , " said Halifax energy expert Todd McDonald . <p> " Everybody around here would be able to relate to that , " said McDonald , president of Energy Atlantica Inc. , which manages oil and natural gas assets of large commercial and industrial customers in Atlantic Canada and the northeastern United States . <p> " There 's a combination of factors that 's absolutely crushing prices throughout North America and definitely New England .
