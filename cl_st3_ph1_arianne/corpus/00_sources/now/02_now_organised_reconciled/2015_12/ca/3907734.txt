<p> Every level of government seems to agree that a new footbridge over the Rideau Canal between Fifth Avenue and Clegg Street is a good idea , but so far only the City of Ottawa is committing cash to the project . <p> " It 's a priority of mine , it 's a priority of the city 's , the province is also interested , so now the question is finding the funding so we can make it go forward , " Ottawa Centre MP Catherine McKenna said Wednesday after emerging from a meeting with Mayor Jim Watson and three other local Liberal MPs . <p> McKenna , who 's also the Minister of the Environment and Climate Change , said the $17.5-million bridge would be a " huge addition " to the Old Ottawa East , Glebe and Old Ottawa South neighbourhoods , and could encourage more people to walk or cycle to events at Lansdowne Park . <p> Ottawa Centre MPP Yasir Naqvi pledged in a statement that he would continue advocating for the bridge and work with McKenna and Watson " to make it @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ comes after the city diverted $2.1 million from another project to the Fifth-Clegg bridge , which the mayor described as " our No. 1 priority " for a foot bridge . <p> The cash had been earmarked for a multi-use pathway on the Prince of Wales Bridge , a decommissioned rail bridge spanning the Ottawa River just east of Lemieux Island . The project , with an estimated price tag of $10.5 million , was more expensive than anticipated and the city never reached an agreement with the National Capital Commission or the City of Gatineau to split the costs of construction . <p> In the 2016 budget , the city chipped in an additional $2.7 million for the Fifth-Clegg bridge , for a total of $4.8 million earmarked for the canal crossing .
