It 's great for the environment and the people of Nobleton . If they 'd selected ( route ) 7F that would have been really detrimental so , in general , we 're all excited , " he said . <p> However , the longstanding problem of an east-west bypass to drag traffic away from King Road has also been sought by the township . It 's been thought the GTA West Highway might be a solution for that problem as it would link up with Hwy. 400 . <p> " We in King were also looking at it as a way to alleviate the flow-through traffic that happens from the east and the west @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ news announcement ) , " said Pellegrini . <p> " The concern I also have , later on ... " he said . <p> News the provincial Ministry of Transportation has suspended work on the environmental assessment of the Greater Toronto Area West Highway Corridor ( GTA West ) was roundly welcomed by environmentalists . <p> Minister of Transportation Steven Del Duca said the project will be reviewed and an update provided in spring 2016 . <p> The announcement comes just as the new federal government has promised a decade-long cash injection of $20 billion for transit and infrastructure projects across the country . <p> In his announcement Del Duca said , " Having the right transportation network in place that builds on our climate change commitments , our ability to embrace new and emerging technologies and the sharing economy , will help us plan for a flexible transportation system that adapts @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ success for generations to come . " <p> Susan Swail of Environmental Defence reacted to the announcement of the suspension by calling it , " welcome news . " <p> Noting that the GTA West Highway would cut through farms , forests and rivers as well as a portion of the Greenbelt , Swail said the proposed highway would , " encourage more car use , enable further sprawl and contribute to traffic congestion in the GTHA that damages the economy , our health and quality of life . " <p> Swail called for " investing the estimated $4 billion cost of this highway in public transit will get more people moving across the region and is the healthier choice for the environment . " <p> Sony Rai , director of Sustainable Vaughan , called Del Duca 's announcement , " very good news , very positive , in terms of not just the suspension of the EA but ... sustainable transportation .
