<h> Sendzik joins Compact of Mayors working to address climate change <h> Mayor says City has ' joined on to be part of the global solution to climate change ' <p> ST . CATHARINES -- Mayor Walter Sendzik wants to see St. Catharines become a leader when it comes to joining the " global solution " to climate change . <p> During a recent council meeting , city councillors endorsed a motion by the mayor to join the Compact of Mayors , pledging the City 's intent to be part of the global solution to address climate change . The Compact of Mayors is a coalition of progressive city leaders working to address climate change by pledging to reduce greenhouse gas emissions , tracking progress and preparing for the impacts of climate change . <p> Sendzik describes climate change as " one of the greatest challenges of our time " and said he wants the City to take a leadership role when it comes to looking at ways to reduce greenhouse gas emissions and addressing climate change . <p> " It is widely recognized that cities have an @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of climate change , " said Sendzik of council 's decision to join the global initiative . <p> Launched at the 2014 United Nations Climate Summit and led by former Mayor of New York City Michael Bloomberg and Vancouver Mayor Gregor Robertson , the Compact of Mayors sends a strong signal that cities around the world are committed to lead in the response to climate change . <p> The City has made its own commitment to corporate and environmental sustainability in its new Strategic Plan with specific goals and objectives to lead in environmental protection and to be prepared for the impacts of climate change . City council also adopted a corporate sustainability strategy called Tending our Garden City in 2011 , with detailed goals and strategies for St. Catharines to become a sustainable community . <p> " We can look at the targets and how we may be able to implement things at the City level , " Sendzik told councillors . <p> Council voted at the Nov. 30 meeting to support membership in the compact , but a request was made to City staff to prepare a report on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for future consideration in line with the Strategic Plan and Sustainability Strategy . <p> Coun.
