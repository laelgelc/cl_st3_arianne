<h> COLUMN : Heartwarming tales of Christmas in King Township <h> Tim Kelly <p> At a time when so much misery makes headlines -- mass shootings , the ravages of climate change , missing and murdered Aboriginal women and children to name just a few -- it 's heartwarming to see what some in King are doing this Christmas season . And you do n't have to look far . I attended a King Township Chamber of Commerce lunch just last week overflowing with Christmas spirit . Everybody showed up with either a toy or toys in hand or chequebook at the ready to support the Nobleton Christmas Drive put on annually by the indomitable Mimi Buchanan/Lyons . A success each and every year , there are too many people to mention by name who put forth a tremendous effort for the Nobleton Christmas Drive which helps at least a dozen families with toys , food and other needed goodies . Mimi organizes it all and puts her heart and soul into it and she gets a big assist from Fire Chief Jim Wall and his crew at @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ forward in a huge way at Christmas time -- and all through the year frankly -- is the King Township Food Bank . The KTFB , whose point person is Carol Ann Trabert , does a tremendous job of helping people who need it most in King Township but it could n't do it without the support of contributions from individuals and businesses throughout the township . Service clubs also do a great job of helping out and , at the risk of singling out one , I will give the Nobleton Lions a big pat on the back for the $6,315 they raised recently to buy the Township three defibrillators . The idea came from Nobleton Councillor David Boyd who saw a need for the devices in township vehicles and challenged Nobleton Lions to come through with the funds . They met that challenge and now want to see if another service club -- or clubs -- can do the same . It 's always clear when the Township has its annual awards night that King is blessed with more than its fair share of wonderful volunteers .
