<p> As it turns out , support for whether you want to save the planet by ending global warming is all a matter of degree ... or maybe two . <p> The deadline for a deal at COP21 -- the Internet shorthand for the Paris Climate Summit -- was pushed back until today because of the complexity of the negotiations , including whether one or two more degrees of global warming is acceptable . <p> Yes , negotiations . Decades after Al Gore and others declared the science ofglobal warming to be setttled , the supposed fate of the planet is still coming down to last-second negotiations . <p> Oh well , debating or " negotiating " such science does seem an advance from 500 years ago when Galileo was tried by the church on suspicion of heresy , forced to recant his theories that the Earth orbited the sun and ordered to spend the rest of his life under house arrest .
