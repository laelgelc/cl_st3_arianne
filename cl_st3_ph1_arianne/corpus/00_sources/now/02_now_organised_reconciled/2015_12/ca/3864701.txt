<h> Paris , parking , and popular politics <p> Premier Wade MacLauchlan is scheduled to be in Paris this weekend for COP21 , the Conference of Parties , where 190 countries are gathered to talk about climate change , carbon footprints , et cetera . No doubt he will hold conference delegates spell bound as he tells the story of our 60 or 70 windmills . <p> It 's a good story , but from a world perspective , it 's pretty small potatoes . Denmark or Germany would have as many windmills in one small county as we do in the whole province . Along the shores of Lake Erie in Ontario there are likely five times as many as on the Island . <p> But , for a small jurisdiction , it is a significant contribution to climate change . What 's next ?
