<h> Halton Hills calls on the Province to work with all levels of government on climate change policies <h> Solar panels on Mold-Masters SportsPlex <p> Photo courtesy of Town of Halton Hills <p> Halton Hills Hydro Inc . President and CEO Art Skidmore ( left ) is congratulated after the completion of a solar panel project- with 1,762 panels- on the roof of the Mold-Masters SportsPlex earlier this year . It was a partnership between the Town and the local utility company . <p> The Town of Halton Hills is calling on Ontario Premier Kathleen Wynne to work with local governments to reduce greenhouse gas emissions . Halton Hills Council passed a motion to that effect Monday , joining other towns and cities across the country that have been asked to endorse such a resolution by the Association of Municipalities of Ontario ( AMO ) . Local and Regional Councillor Jane Fogal presented the motion , which details a variety of measures the Town wants Wynne to take on the environmental front , including : * Giving municipalities adequate , stable and long-term funding to invest in public @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ efficiency and other programs * Recognizing municipal projects that reduce greenhouse gases for offset credits in cap and trade programs * Allocating funds for climate change adaptation to help municipalities provide resilient infrastructure . With municipalities either directly or indirectly controlling about half of Canada 's greenhouse gas emissions , it 's vital to take steps such as these , said Fogal . " This is an extremely important area for investment , " she said , noting the Town of Halton Hills has already taken the issue seriously through a long list of initiatives . These include measures such as the Mayor 's Community Energy Plan , active transportation projects and construction of efficient Town facilities . With the Climate Change Conference underway in Paris , Fogal said she expects that funds for green initiatives could soon be made available to municipalities to help the Province achieve its commitments . Fellow Local and Regional Councillor Clark Somerville asked for the motion to be circulated to AMO , Wellington-Halton Hills MP Michael Chong and the federal minister of the environment and climate change . " With the amount of work done @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ past 10 years , I think they should know what we 're doing , " he said . Somerville is currently in Paris attending the Climate Change Conference in his capacity as 1st-Vice President of FCM ( Federation of Canadian Municipalities ) . The motion will also be sent to several provincial ministers , Wellington-Halton Hills MPP Ted Arnott and all local municipalities . Mayor Rick Bonnette noted that Halton Hills ' climate change efforts " are on the map , " with delegates from China coming to town seeking assistance on sustainability and environmental programs . " That 's a tribute to everyone here , " he said . " For a community of our size , we 've taken this very seriously . "
