<h> U of A expert urges continued coal technology investment <p> The plan Premier Rachel Notley took to the Paris Climate Change conference includes the phasing out of Alberta 's coal-fired power plants . But an expert on coal says we still need to keep investing in the technology . <p> U of A Professor Dr Rajender Gupta tells the Alberta Morning News that making coal burn more cleanly is critical to stopping climate change , since China has reached it 's peak coal-consumption , India is approaching theirs , and both Germany and Japan are adding coal-fuelled power plants as well . And those are just four examples of hundreds . <p> " That is a hypocrisy I will call it . That you do n't want to burn coal , but you want to sell coal so other people can burn it . " he says . " If you are so philosophically against coal , why do n't you shut down coal mines , also ? " <p> Alberta 's plan is to shift our energy production to natural gas or other renewables .
