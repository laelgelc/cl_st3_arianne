<h> Canada is back , at a cost of billions <p> It was positively uplifting to see commentaries and letters providing reason to the debate regarding climate change . <p> Contrast that with the shocking statement by Green Party Leader Elizabeth May , in which she applauds U.S. President Barack Obama for his decision to reject the Keystone pipeline . Such a non-scientific decision will cost this country billions of dollars in lost revenue , and tens of thousands of jobs . <p> Alberta has already lost 70,000 jobs , with a current deficit of more than $6 billion . Saskatchewan is now dealing with a deficit budget . Ontario 's auditor general suggests that province 's green energy plan has cost taxpayers $37 billion over eight years and provided that province with the highest electricity rates in North America . <p> These are examples of how May 's suggestions as to how to deal with climate change , led by Canada , of course , would seriously deplete provinces financially .
