<h> Montreal 's DSP assures Pointe Claire of total Reliance Site clean-up <p> Since the end of summer two years ago upon the discovery of illegally stored PCBs at the Reliance Power Equipment site in Pointe Claire , the city has been proactive in ridding the dangerous polychlorinated biphenyls from the closed company and surrounding area . <p> Pointe Claire Mayor Morris Trudeau explained that " every possible step to ensure that contaminants are eliminated as quickly as possible , and we are still making every effort to achieve this goal . " <p> Trudeau recently reached out to neighbours and other businesses in the area following the release of a soil contamination report completed by Ministry of Sustainable Development , the Environment and the Fight Against Climate Change denoting " that the site is contaminated and that contaminants may have migrated to immediately adjacent lots . " <p> And while the PCBs have transferred to neighbours , the city of Montreal 's DSP ( Direction de la sant ? publique ) " has confirmed that the site does not represent any kind of hazard for the population @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ unit ( Division des rejets industriels ) states that there is no contamination of the water supply , " said city spokesperson Marie-Pier Paquette-Sequin . <p> Reliance Power Equipment , located at 86 and 88 Hymus Boulevard , and its neighbours will have further soil studies over the course of the next few months and if need be , additional decontamination of the area will be concluded by the provincial government . <p> " You may rest assured that the City of Pointe Claire is determined to reach a full and definitive solution to this situation , " Trudeau said .
