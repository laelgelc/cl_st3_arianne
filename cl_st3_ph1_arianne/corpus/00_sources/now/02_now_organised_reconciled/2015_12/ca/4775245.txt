<h> Cover dark side of warming <p> This holiday season I have felt profoundly saddened . What saddens me is knowing that we are in a fight for human survival on planet Earth and most of us do n't even know it . <p> I looked in vain for some serious coverage of the very dark side of this unseasonably warm December . I have yet to see it . The planet is warming quickly and urgent action is needed by all of us to change that . <p> Despite the determination of the Paris Accord that we will try to keep temperature rise under 1.5 C in order to avoid the worst of runaway global warming , there is no global plan in place that will get us there , and every day counts . <p> We are in overtime -- with the Arctic already in full-blown melting , and the oceans acidifying , and CO2 count of 400 ( safe level might be 350 ) -- and we need to act with full urgency .
