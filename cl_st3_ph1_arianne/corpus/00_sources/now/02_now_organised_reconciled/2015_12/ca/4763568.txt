<h> Tracking the pace of ice-sheet melting in Kangerlussuaq , where one misstep means a guaranteed death <p> Never in scientific history has so much depended on the labours of so few . As the world comes together to structure its political and technological future around the threat of catastrophic climate change , billions are relying on the work of scientists who have the remarkably unsexy task of crunching climate data . <p> One of the most remote outposts for this research is just outside Kangerlussuaq , Greenland . The ice sheet near this Western Greenland town of 512 has been the home base for a NASA-funded team led by University of California geographer Laurence Smith . <p> As the ice caps melt , Greenland is seeing vast lakes of meltwater forming on the ice before being sped to the ocean by an ever-growing system of rivers slicing through the ice .
