<p> The provincial government is billing CN Rail $350,000 for clean-up assistance at the sites of two train derailments near Gogama . <p> This past winter , two different CN oil tanker trains near Gogama derailed within a three week period . More than one million litres of crude oil were spilled and caught on fire . No one was injured . <p> Sudbury MPP Glen Thibeault , the parliamentary assistant to the Ministry of the Environment and Climate Change , said the charges includes ministry staff salaries and laboratory costs associated with the spill . <p> " It 's really does take us away from our core business that we need to do . We 're going to ensure that we recoup these costs when these types of spills occur , " Thibeault said . <p> Thibeault called the bill to the railway company the government 's " line in the sand .
