<p> Oilsands junior Laricina Energy Ltd. has settled debt obligations with the credit arm of the Canada Pension Plan Investment Board , with the result that its equity is now about 89 per cent owned by the board and its subsidiaries , it announced Tuesday . <p> The private company entered Companies ' Creditors Arrangement Act protection in March after it defaulted on a production covenant related to its CPP Credit Investments Inc. secured debt . It said it has settled $118.5 million of that debt , including expenses of CPP Credit , and paid $13.7 million of principal on outstanding notes from cash on hand at closing . <p> Notes totalling $44.1 million remain outstanding at an annual interest rate of 13.5 per cent , with the principal repayable at maturity in March 2018 , Laricina said . <p> The company said it has obtained an extension of CCAA protection until Dec. 19 and plans to apply to the court for termination before the end of the year . <p> It said a new five-person board is to be struck with three members nominated by CPP Credit @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ business plan by the new board of directors once it is in place , Laricina is proceeding with very limited operations under a revised scaled-back business plan with a view to maximizing long-term value for its shareholders , " the news release stated . <p> President and chief executive Glen Schmidt said in an interview the company 's activities going forward will depend on direction from the board but it will likely remain relatively inactive until there is clarity on oil prices , as well as Alberta 's climate change and royalty policies under the NDP government . <p> " CPP is the major shareholder and , having the majority interest in the board , will make the determination if they want to augment or support an expanded budget , just given the capacity of CPP , " he said . <p> Schmidt said the company has reduced staff to about 20 in the Calgary office and one employee in northern Alberta overseeing its two demonstration thermal oilsands projects closed earlier this year to save money . <p> Following the CPP Credit settlement , Laricina had cash of $46.3 million -- @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ said . <p> Under the settlement , $60 million of CPP Credit 's original notes were replaced by 500 million shares at 12 cents each .
