<p> Soil can play a lead role in filtering carbon dioxide from the atmosphere , a University of Saskatchewan professor told United Nations delegates . <p> Most people do n't realize soil can hold more carbon than the atmosphere and all vegetation on earth , Dan Pennock said . That 's why the first , vital step is to get a better idea of soil health around the world . <p> " It 's a pretty sad state of affairs . We just have no idea , " he said . " It 's an international problem . " <p> Pennock presented a 700-page report about soil health to the United Nations in New York last week .
