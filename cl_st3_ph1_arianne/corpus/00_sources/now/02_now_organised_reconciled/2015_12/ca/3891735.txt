<h> Greenpeace cautiously optimistic about COP21 deal <p> Greenpeace gave a lukewarm welcome to today 's milestone climate deal inked in Paris , warning that the 1.5 degree target could yet be derailed by a continued addiction to fossil fuels . <p> " The wheel of climate action turns slowly , but in Paris it has turned . This deal puts the fossil fuel industry on the wrong side of history , " said Greenpeace International executive director Kumi Naidoo . <p> However , he said that more needed to be done to help developing countries , many of them island nations facing a real risk of sinking as a result of rising sea levels . <p> " There 's not enough in this deal for the nations and people on the frontlines of climate change . It contains an inherent , ingrained injustice . The nations which caused this problem have promised too little help to the people who are already losing their lives and livelihoods , " said Naidoo . <p> In addition , the present emissions targets are not enough to keep the Earth from @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ view .
