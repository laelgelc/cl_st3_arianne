<p> WASHINGTON - Vermont Sen. Bernie Sanders is rolling out a plan to slow down the impact of climate change , vowing to cut U.S. carbon emissions by 40 per cent by 2030 . <p> The Democratic presidential candidate said in a proposal released Monday that he would seek to impose a tax on carbon dioxide pollution , long a non-starter with majority Republicans in Congress , and aim to slash carbon pollution in the U.S. by 80 per cent by 2050 compared to levels in 1990 . The proposal , he said , would put " people before the profits of polluters .
