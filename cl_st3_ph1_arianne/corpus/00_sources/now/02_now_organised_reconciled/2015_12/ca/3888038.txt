In her words , " We are going to do our part to address one of the world 's greatest problems . " It was not a bad month for refugees either , with John McCallum announcing that Canada will resettle 25,000 Syrians in this country by the end of February . But as the world 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ might miss is that these two policy developments are closely tied . <p> The glue that binds is food security coupled with the changing climate in the fertile crescent region of the Middle East that includes Syria . Greenhouse gas emissions and associated global warming have been driving up temperatures and driving down precipitation in the region for some years , leading to widespread agricultural failures . Between 2007 and 2010 , Syria experienced the most intense three-year drought in its recorded history . Crop yields in the north of the country plummeted while livestock mortality soared . By 2009 , the drought coupled with unsustainable exploitation of depleting groundwater reservoirs forced some 1.5 million people to abandon farms and rural villages and move to the margins of Damascus , Aleppo and other large cities . Fold in exploding population growth , from nine million in 1980 to an estimated 23 million today , the brutality of the Assad regime , governmental neglect of growing social tensions and human suffering in the cities , and you have a perfect recipe for societal collapse . Climate models project continued drying in the fertile crescent region should greenhouse gas emissions keep rising . Syria is not the sole victim of climatic extremes : Iraq and Iran too are being hard hit by climate change and neither is food secure . In 2013 , Iran 's Forest , Range and Watershed Management Organization reported that more than two-thirds of the country 's land is rapidly turning into desert , adding that environmental challenges " ... are now more perilous to the country 's long-term stability than either foreign adversaries or domestic political struggles . " <p> No country can exist in any civilized state when the rains stop falling , the wells run dry , and its citizens get hungry . " There are only nine meals between mankind and anarchy " , wrote the journalist Alfred Lewis a century ago . <p> While we can argue about the number of missing meals that leads to unrest , there can be no doubt that when food vanishes , social order fails . Syria is not alone in facing recent chaos driven @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ riots in Cairo in January 2011 were a direct consequence of the record heat wave that sat atop western Russia in the summer of 2010 . As wheat withered on its stalks , a ban on exports from the country at the end of July led to a doubling of the world price in a matter of weeks . The ripple effect was severe : the Mubarak government in Egypt could no longer maintain bread subsidies and the population took to the streets . And despite the protesters waving baguettes and not pitchforks , Mubarak 's regime fell shortly thereafter . <p> It is events like these -- -- and the prospect that global warming will drive more of them -- -- that make Alberta 's intent to tighten limits on greenhouse gas emissions so important . While the new policies represent just a modest first step , they have set an example that all jurisdictions must follow . If global society does n't begin to reduce emissions quickly , climatic instability will bite harder and food insecurity will spread . More human tsunamis will spill across more borders . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ arrival to this country of 25,000 Syrian refugees in coming months , but it needs to recognize that a direct line can be drawn between our moral obligation to accept desperate migrants fleeing a drought-stricken , failing state and the companion obligation that greenhouse gas emissions be reduced , as quickly and sharply as possible . The line that connects those two dots is real . <p> Prime Minister Trudeau and Canada 's provincial premiers can make a difference in Paris by continuing to set an example , as Premier Notley did last month . But should they and the other leaders fail to reach a strong and binding agreement , the environmental refugee crisis will deteriorate further .
