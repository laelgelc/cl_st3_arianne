The lesson : appearing to blame voters for fiscal woes when the same government has been in power for four decades does n't go over well . <p> Election debate and ' math is difficult ' <p> Despite the importance heaped on them , election debates have rarely been game changers in Alberta politics . Fresh off a stunning electoral victory , Notley 's NDP invited everyone to the legislature grounds for what amounted to a carnival-like swearing-in party , complete with Popsicles and food trucks . Thousands crammed the legislature grounds , and dozens waded barefoot in the reflecting pools , as they watched a team of rookies become Alberta 's first NDP government . <p> Royalty review and climate change panels <p> Stacked with experts and announced to much fanfare , both the climate and royalty panels are cornerstones of the NDP government 's push to transform Alberta . The climate panel urged a $3-billion economy-wide carbon tax and a list of ambitious environmental goals , such as phasing out coal in 15 years . Albertans will have to wait a bit longer for the results of the royalty panel , but the real question is how Notley 's government weathers the political reaction to its proposals . Only time will tell if the government 's amendment was enough to reassure rural Alberta or if Bill 6 will come back to bite government in 2016 as the real regulatory work gets going . <p> LGBTQ rights <p> Advocates say this year marked a major step forward for gay and trans rights in Alberta , beginning early this year when the former Tory government made gay-straight alliances mandatory in all schools where students request them . Voters sent their first three openly gay MLAs to the legislature in the spring election , and just this month MLAs voted unanimously to add gender identity and expression as prohibited grounds for discrimination under Alberta 's Human Rights Act , safeguarding the rights of trans people in the province .
