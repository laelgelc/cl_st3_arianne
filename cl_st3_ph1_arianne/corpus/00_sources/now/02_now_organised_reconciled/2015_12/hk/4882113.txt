<p> " Our aim is clear , our mission is possible and our destination is on our sights . What counts now is translating promises on paper into change on the ground " said Ban Ki-moon , Secretary-General of the United Nations at the opening of the General Assembly of the United Nations , when the Sustainable Development Goals were officially launched in September . At the press conference he added " We will need all partners to make this a success . " <p> The private sector , ranging from micro-enterprises to cooperatives to multinationals , is explicitly mentioned in the 2030 agenda for sustainable development . There is an expectation that businesses will use their resources , capabilities and creativity in delivering real solutions to these global challenges . Private business activity , investment and innovation are seen as the major drivers of productivity , inclusive economic growth and job creation .
