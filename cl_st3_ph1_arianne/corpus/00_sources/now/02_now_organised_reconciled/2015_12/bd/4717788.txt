<p> With rising sea level due to climate change , salinity is increasing in the coastal areas , creating a scarcity of drinking water there , said Khulna WASA 's Project Director Khan Selim Ahmed . <p> This climate compatible project has been undertaken to find a sustainable solution to this problem in Khulna , he added . <p> Once completed , the project will come as a blessing for the 1.5 million people of the city , says Anisur Rahman Biswas , acting mayor of Khulna City Corporation . <p> " Salinity in groundwater is a major problem for us . We are soon going to get rid of this , " he told bdnews24.com . <p> An ADB delegation went to Khulna to visit the project sites on Dec 17 . <p> " We are happy with the progress .
