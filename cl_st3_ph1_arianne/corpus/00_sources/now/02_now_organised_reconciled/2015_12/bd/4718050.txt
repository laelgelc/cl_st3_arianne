It did pretty well relative to other LDCs . Net enrolment ratio reached nearly 99 per cent , dropouts rate declined , and now more girls attend primary and secondary schools vis-a-vis boys - a real reversal . In 2013 , she nationalised 26,193 primary schools and jobs of 104,776 teachers were nationalised also . Under her leadership , nearly 332 million books were distributed to students free of charge on the first day of the year 2015 , education up to 12th grade has been made free and varieties of scholarships and stipends are being given , especially for girl students .
