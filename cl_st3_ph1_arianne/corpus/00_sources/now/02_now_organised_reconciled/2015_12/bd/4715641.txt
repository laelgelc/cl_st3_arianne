<h> Climate negotiators release new , shorter draft of accord <p> LE BOURGET : Raising hopes of a landmark climate deal , negotiators today unveiled a new shorter draft incorporating major progress as well as the differences , two days ahead of the deadline for the UN accord to curb greenhouse gas emissions . <p> The first draft of the Paris Outcome , prepared after two days of high-level ministerial deliberations , was released by French Foreign Minister Laurent Fabius , which will be discussed by 196 nations further to reach a final agreement to tackle the challenge of climate change . The draft negotiating text , which is " shorter " from the previous version is now of 29 pages and was circulated to all the negotiating countries . Elaborating on the draft text , Fabius , who is the chair of the current round of climate negotiations , said the aim of the text is to enable nations to have an overall view of the progress that has been made so far .
