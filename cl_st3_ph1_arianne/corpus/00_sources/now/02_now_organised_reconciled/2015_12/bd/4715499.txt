<h> Tripura Governor Tathagata Ray has expressed his concern over the impact of global warming on India 's north-eastern region and Bangladesh . <p> He was speaking at the inaugural session of a national seminar on " Sustaining Hill Agriculture in Changing Climate " , held at the Pragnya Bhavan in Agartala on Saturday . <p> " I am specifically referring to Shillong ( capital of Meghalaya ) because I have seen the impact of the climate change there with my own eyes . When we were children we had never seen a fan in Shillong . Now you need air conditioners there ..... It 's quite alarming , " said Ray . <p> " Global warming is happening before our very eyes . So afforestation to increase the forest cover is extremely necessary . " <p> According to the governor , in Northeast India , a critical factor adding to deforestation has been hill agriculture ( jhum cultivation ) . <p> He asked scientists to find solutions which are environmentally suitable as well as productive to feed the growing population . <p> Ray said , " Forestry @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ you have a forest you can not have agriculture , vice versa . Agricultural land is created by clearing forests and what effect that can have on the environment can be well imagined . " <p> In the governor 's view , the consequences of global warming in the region in the coming days will be more disastrous if proper measures are not taken to revive the lost forests . <p> " As a matter of fact , the most densely populated country of the world is Bangladesh . And if Bangladesh gets flooded the rivers that flow from India to Bangladesh into the sea will overflow . They in turn are going to flood parts of India , particularly West Bengal and Assam valley . <p> " This is an aspect which will have to be given due importance while analysing the global warming that we are going to see as a result of deforestation , " said Ray . <p> According to some reports , India 's northeast has lost a forest cover of 628 sq km , with 55 sq km of forests disappearing in Tripura . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ cover in the northeast is 171,964 sq km , which is 65.59 percent of its total geographical area , in comparison to the national forest cover of 21.34 percent .
