<h> President Maithripala Sirisena returns from Malta , Paris visits <p> President Maithripala Sirisena who visited Malta to attend the Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting and later traveled to Paris , France to attend the United Nations Conference on Climate Change returned to the country on Tuesday . <p> President Maithripala Sirisena attended the 24th edition of the Commonwealth Heads of Government meeting on November 27 and delivered a special statement to the assembly . The president then traveled to Paris to attend the United Nations Conference on Climate Change which took place with the participation of 150 World Leaders . <p> Today , is the second day of the United Nations Conference on Climate Change . <p> Parliamentarian , the Venerable Athuraliye Rathana Thero who also visited Paris on the sidelines of the conference convened a special media briefing there .
