<h> Politics <h> World <h> Multimedia <h> More <h> Sri Lanka small business to get disaster risk support <h> Dec 24 , 2015 06:21 AM GMT+0530 0 Comment(s) <p> ECONOMYNEXT -- The United Nations Development Programme is to provide technical assistance to help Sri Lankan small businesses better cope with disasters . <p> The UNDP and the Federation of Chambers of Commerce and Industry of Sri Lanka ( FCCISL ) have entered into a partnership to promote greater private sector engagement in the field of Disaster Risk Reduction . <p> A statement said both organisations have signed a Memorandum of Understanding on the partnership which will help reach out to Sri Lankan businesses . <p> Under this partnership , UNDP will provide technical assistance to the FCCISL to establish a Disaster Risk Reduction and Climate Change Adaptation Unit at FCCISL.
