Another in Brazil has started managing its fish stocks . And one in Peru set up an indigenous local government to protect its environment from oil , mining and logging companies . <p> The Waorani barely seem to notice the stifling tropical heat in Gareno , a hamlet of wooden huts in the middle of the Ecuadoran jungle .
