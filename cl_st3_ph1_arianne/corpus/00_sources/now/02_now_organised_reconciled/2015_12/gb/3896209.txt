<p> A strategy to significantly increase the amount of land in community ownership has been published by the Scottish Government . <p> The report by the one million acre short life working group recommends a number of proposals to deliver the one million acre target by 2020 and spread the benefits of community ownership , including : <p> -- stimulate demand for community owned land through raising awareness -- build capacity of communities to take land ownership projects forward -- improve access to support services and develop a network of support providers -- address issues around the availability of land and barriers to the supply of land <p> The report identified 480,000 acres of land already in community ownership , which has recently been increased to over 500,000 acres by the acquisition of land by The Pairc Trust since the report was finalised . <p> Minister for environment , climate change and land reform , Aileen McLeod , said : " I welcome this report from the one million acre short life working group , which looks at ways to reach our target of one million acres of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the potential to transform the fortunes of communities across the country . <p> " I would like to thank the group for all their hard work and I look forward to considering the recommendations in this report alongside current land reform legislation to help ensure we empower communities with practical powers as well as support sustainability . "
