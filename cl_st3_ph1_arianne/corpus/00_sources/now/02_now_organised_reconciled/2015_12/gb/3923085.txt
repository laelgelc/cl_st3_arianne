<p> A short summary of yourself : I 'm originally from Peterborough , but have been living in Shrewsbury for the last ten years . I 've become far too attached to this part of the country to want to be anywhere else . My professional interests are in environmental technology , sustainability and climate change , and I currently work in transport planning with a focus on cycling , walking , public transport and the ongoing challenges of combining 21st century traffic with a medieval street network ! Photography is more an obsession than an interest now and I always have a camera with me . I spend so much time looking for those combinations of objects and light that work together it has gone beyond a habit . I also like drawing , painting , walking , cycling and sitting in the pub . I enjoy interesting and wild weather and music festivals - but not both at the same time ...
