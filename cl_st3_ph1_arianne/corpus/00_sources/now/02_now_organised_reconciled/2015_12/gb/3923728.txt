<p> The climate agreement reached in Paris is provoking a flurry of caveats , criticisms and cautions . Many of those criticisms are warranted and there 's a lot of work ahead to make sure countries live up to their promises . But we should not miss a chance to celebrate a historic turning point . <p> World leaders finally made commitments to clean , renewable energy that will help to ensure a safer , healthier and more prosperous future for us all . The agreement signals that the age of fossil fuels is coming to a close , and the age of renewable energy is dawning . <p> In many ways , the Paris deal is the mother of all market signals . To deliver on the promises world leaders made , we will need to leave coal and oil in the ground and move toward a complete reliance on clean energy . And major companies are pledging to go 100% renewable , too . <p> Much of that growth in clean , renewable energy has come from the subnational movement , in which cities , states and regions are banding together and leading even if their national governments are lagging .
