But the removal of all reference to human rights in the final draft of the Paris climate agreement has dismayed leaders of nearly all the myriad civil society groups pressing for a strong outcome . <p> According to observers , Britain , Norway , the US and a small group of developed countries actively blocked inclusion of any mention of human rights in the text in retaliation for developing countries ' refusal to give ground on a part of the text called " loss and damage " .
