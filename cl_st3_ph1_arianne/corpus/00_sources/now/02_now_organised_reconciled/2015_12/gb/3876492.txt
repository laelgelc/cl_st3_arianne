<p> Until it was eclipsed by the US , the Saudi kingdom was the world 's largest oil producer and currently ranks as the 10th largest polluter , according to Enerdata . <p> Saudi Arabia has long played a high-profile presence at annual climate summits operating from the luxuriously appointed pavillions of the Gulf Co-operation Council -- and over the years has regularly been accused of blocking action on climate change . <p> In the run-up to the Paris summit , however , the kingdom adopted a more amenable posture . Last month it delivered a plan to fight climate change , pledging a " significant deviation " in emissions , but was the last G20 country to submit its offer to the United Nations , and analysts described the targets as opaque . <p> Last May , Ali al-Naimi acknowledged the global economy was moving away from fossil fuels -- and said that Saudi Arabia was prepared to move with it . <p> " In Saudi Arabia , we recognise that eventually , one of these days , we are not going to need fossil fuels . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ or thereafter , " he said . <p> Oil analysts note the kingdom also faces enormous domestic pressure to diversity its electricity supply .
