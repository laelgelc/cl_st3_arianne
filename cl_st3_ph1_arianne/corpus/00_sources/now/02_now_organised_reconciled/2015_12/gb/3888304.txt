<p> Laurence Tubiana was working as a visiting professor at New York 's Columbia University last year , far from her home in Paris , when the phone rang . France had been confirmed as host of a huge UN conference that was supposed to deliver the first new global climate change accord in 18 years . Would she be willing to be his ambassador in the fraught negotiations needed to make it a success ? <p> " I did n't hesitate of course , " says Tubiana .
