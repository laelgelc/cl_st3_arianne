<h> Historic moment at Paris climate change talks , as agreement made to pass on a sustainable world to the generations to come . But progress must continue <p> Global politicians , their teams and advisers in Paris had very good reason to pack for home in a positive frame of mind because they avoided the failure of Copenhagen six years earlier and successfully reached a legally binding agreement to fight climate change . <p> They made this historic moment possible after extensive wrangling over two weeks . Chinese President Xi Jinping and US President Barack Obama , as the leaders of the two largest emitters , paved the way for success with their earlier climate commitments . They gave the day-and-night talks impetus with their common desire to see an agreement reached . <p> The deal aims to hold global temperatures to rise to below 2C above pre-industrial levels , which if achieved would stave off the worst effects of catastrophic global warming .
