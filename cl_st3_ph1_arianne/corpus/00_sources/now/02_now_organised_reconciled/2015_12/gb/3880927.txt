<p> In June , the CCC 's statutory report on the UK 's progress on climate change highlighted dealing with floods from extreme weather as the government 's most serious failing in preparing for the impacts of global warming . It stated : " Plans and policies , or progress in addressing vulnerabilities , are lacking " . <p> The CCC said " residual " flood risk -- the flooding resulting from extreme weather events that can not be prevented by normal flood defences -- was increasing . On Monday , environment secretary Liz Truss said the Storm Desmond floods had resulted from " extreme weather conditions " and " unprecedented amount of rainfall . " <p> The CCC recommended that the government should " develop a strategy to address the increasing number of homes in areas of high flood risk " .
