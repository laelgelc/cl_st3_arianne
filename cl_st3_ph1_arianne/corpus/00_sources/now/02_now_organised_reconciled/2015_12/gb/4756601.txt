As a motivating force , hope is more fragile , harder to inspire , easier to lose sight of . Fear is a powerful motivator and easy to conjure -- but only hope can lead us into a better world . <p> In 2015 , the brutal violence of the Syrian civil war , which gave rise to a wave of refugees on Europe 's doorstep , metastasized into bloody terrorist attacks in Paris and San Bernardino . Donald Trump led the way , openly appealing to bigotry in his call to bar all Muslims from the US , but the other candidates were n't far behind . <p> It 's not just war and bloodshed that stirred up anxiety in the past 12 months .
