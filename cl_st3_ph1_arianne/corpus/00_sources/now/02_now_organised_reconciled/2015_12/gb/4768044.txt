Here are just a few examples . <h> Art <p> Posters , banners , music , puppetry and performance have long supported demonstrations . Now art is at the centre . <p> The Brandalism campaign caught the attention of the press and public during COP21 in Paris when 600 bus shelters were plastered with satirical posters critiquing the corporate takeover of the climate talks . The artworks , installed in advertising spaces across Paris , challenged the environmental credentials of the car manufacturers , airlines , banks and fossil fuel energy companies involved in the climate discussions . <p> Beyond Paris , other examples of art bringing social and environmental commentary to public spaces include Helen Storey 's Dress for our Time and Jason deCaires Taylor 's sculpture , The Rising Tide , featuring four horses which are submerged as the Thames tide comes in .
