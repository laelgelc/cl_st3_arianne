<h> How Art Conveys The Message Of Climate Change <p> We talk about sustainability and climate change , about how to fight it , how to live green and how to raise awareness . Often , we wonder what it will take for people to understand the serious situation our planet is in . Do we need more numbers ? Why is n't it enough to say entire islands will disappear soon ? Sometimes , we need to see it with our own eyes . These are examples of artists who have found a way to convey very powerful messages : This is what we have done to our planet . The threat is real .
