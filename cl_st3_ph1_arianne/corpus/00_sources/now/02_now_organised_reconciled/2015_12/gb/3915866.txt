<h> Bullied by teachers , many LGBT Cambodians drop out of school <p> BANGKOK , Dec 18 ( Thomson Reuters Foundation ) - Three out of five LGBT people in Cambodia say they have been bullied at school , with many being tormented by teachers and dropping out as a result , rights advocates have found . <p> The Cambodian Center for Human Rights surveyed 245 lesbian , gay , bisexual and transgender ( LGBT ) people , aged 15 to 72 , around the country and found that 63 percent had been bullied as students . <p> Of those who were bullied , 42 percent suffered often or on a daily basis , while 17 percent said their teachers were the perpetrators , the survey found . <p> " I raised the issue with my own teacher regarding his inappropriate speech , " a transgender man studying for a bachelor 's degree in the capital Phnom Penh was quoted as saying in the report . <p> " My teacher said , ' You are totally different than others in the class . ' " <p> Another transgender man , a 19-year-old student in Kampong Chhnang province northwest of Phnom Penh , told researchers he was a good student , but was frequently punished by his teacher . <p> " My teacher often scolds or blames me . I am not comfortable in school as a transgender man . I am not discriminated much by my friends , but my teacher bullies me , " he said . <p> Although same-sex activities are not illegal in Cambodia and there is little overt persecution of LGBT people , they are not protected by laws or policies , experts say . <p> Some respondents ended up avoiding or dropping out of school . In second phase of the survey , of those who had not completed high school , 13 percent said they dropped out because of the bullying . <p> The authors noted the findings were limited by the fact that the people surveyed were openly LGBT , and the research did not reach LGBT people who were not known to community leaders .
