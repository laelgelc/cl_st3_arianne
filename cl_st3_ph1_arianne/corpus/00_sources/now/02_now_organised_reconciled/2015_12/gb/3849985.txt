<h> UK farmers at the forefront of climate change challenges <p> Last updated : 01 Dec 2015 <p> British farmers are on the frontlines of climate change challenges , showing leadership as an industry by reducing the environmental impacts of food production while finding solutions to adapt to the negative impacts of an increasingly volatile climate . <p> But volatile commodity prices , volatile energy prices and volatile weather add up to a ' perfect storm ' for agriculture so at the Conference of Parties in Paris ( known as COP21 ) , farmers are highlighting the important work already being done and asking for support to do more in years to come . <p> Did you know ? <p> agriculture is unique in that it supplies food , stores carbon and generates renewable energy <p> farmers feed a growing population and provide ecosystem services such as habitat creation and food for wildlife <p> farmers use energy from the sun and wind as well as bioenergy to tackle climate change <p> At COP21 , 190 countries will negotiate climate change policies , and The World Farmers ' Organisation @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ where farming groups , researchers , civil society and other advocates will share perspectives on climate change and agriculture . <p> On behalf of British farmers , NFU President Meurig Raymond will champion British food and farming and the important role farmers are playing to prepare the future of food production in a volatile climate .
