Banking , animal rights , business , farming , climate change , nuclear power , hunting , mental health , the arms trade , fossil fuels , fur in fashion and many more merit intense scrutiny . <p> There 's a drive towards creating a fairer society for all and a better deal for everyone and this means that ethical issues are on the agenda more than ever .
