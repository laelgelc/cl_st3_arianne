No longer a star diplomat , he came on foot after completing a 58-day , 1,500km pilgrimage from Rome . He is now an official adviser to the archbishop of Manila . <p> The politics of climate change are limiting , he says . " I am happy not to be stuck inside the negotiating rooms all night .
