It is presented as first-person journalism , a mode not yet born . <p> Did Aphra Behn go to Surinam ? Did she come to know there a " royal slave " , a prince captured in Africa , whose story so moved her she needed the world to know about it ? That 's what she tells us . The details of colonial government , including names , are historically precise ; the outcome -- a slave rebellion , horrific punishment -- convincing .
