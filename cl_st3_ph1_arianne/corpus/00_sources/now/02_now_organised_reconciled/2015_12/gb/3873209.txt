<h> BIR calls for recognition of recycling at Paris climate talks <p> Recycling and waste management provides environmental benefits and huge potential in the fights against climate change in the framework of the Paris climate change conference , a recycling association president has said . <p> The Paris climate change conference is taking place today and tomorrow ( 7-8 December ) <p> This was the message promoted during an event organised by the French recycling association FEDEREC last week ( 4 December 2015 ) by Ranjit Baxi , world president of the Bureau of International Recycling ( BIR ) . <p> In his speech at the event held in the Grand Palais , Mr Baxi explained that the early result of BIR 's updated report on ' Environmental Benefits of Recycling ' concluded that the CO2 savings achieved through recycling are on the rise . <p> For aluminium , savings amount to 92% compared to primary production , for copper 65% and for ferrous 58% , the report showed .
