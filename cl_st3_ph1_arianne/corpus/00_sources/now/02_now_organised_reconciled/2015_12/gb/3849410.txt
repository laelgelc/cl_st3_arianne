<p> In huddles in corners , sitting on the floor in corridors , crowded around smartphones at cafe tables around the sprawling conference centre in Paris where crunch climate change talks are ongoing , a new form of meeting is taking place . <p> These are the " informal informals " - groups of negotiators meeting outside the conventional apparatus of plenary sessions in large halls in order to facilitate a deal . <p> These meetings are intended to tease out and resolve the remaining differences over the detailed wording of the text of a possible Paris agreement . Often , they will work on a single paragraph at a time , reworking the wording and attempting to forge consensus in small groups that they can then feed into the larger process of negotiations . <p> The informal informals are seen as a key - and novel - strand of the talks . Negotiators met in back rooms while an unprecedented 150 world leaders took to the stage to emphasise their commitment to tackling global warming .
