<h> Bernard Ingham : Kellingley closure is last chapter in tale of suicidal stupidity <h> Picture by Simon Hulme <p> REMEMBER Kellingley . The closure of the last deep mine in Britain is symbolic . The pit near Selby that represented the future of coal now speaks volumes for the long-term stupidity of British politicians . <p> Kellingley began producing coal as I left Leeds for Fleet Street in 1965 . Its Beeston seam was given an economic life of precisely 50 years and seems to have delivered just that in spite of all that politicians from Arthur Scargill to Green lunatics like Ed Davey , the former Liberal Democrat Energy Secretary , could throw at it . <p> It is not , however , the end of coal production in Britain . Open cast mining produces about 10m tonnes a year , so indigenous coal is not yet as dead as the environmentalists would have it . <p> By the way , I wonder , how the Leftie " Greens " would have coped with coal if King Arthur were still on his Barnsley throne ?
