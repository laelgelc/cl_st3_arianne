<p> British farmers are adding to the pressure on world leaders to agree a new climate treaty in Paris this month , as the industry reports a rise in severe weather events impacting production . <p> Members of the National Farmers Union are calling for more investment in sustainable farming methods , in response to the growing challenge posed by global warming . <p> The organisation claims that two thirds of its farmers have seen an increase in flooding and other extreme weather in recent years , highlighting the urgent need to boost the UK 's agricultural resilience . <p> Vice President Guy Smith said a number of members have already adapted their practices in response to climate change .
