<h> News <h> Pickersgill : Let 's protect the ' lungs of the Earth ' <p> SANTA CRUZ , St Elizabeth -- Minister of Water , Land , Environment , and Climate Change Robert Pickersgill made a startling admission at a land-titling ceremony in this south-central town last month . <p> " I must make a confession : When the prime minister ( Portia Simpson Miller ) appointed me as the minister of climate change I was wondering what was happening because , I must confess , I knew very little about it , " Pickersgill told his audience , which included a smiling Simpson Miller . <p> Four years on , Pickersgill has seen -- at close quarters -- the dire consequences of rapid climate change . Hitting home hardest of all has been extreme drought in Jamaica and the wider Caribbean over the last two years , which environmentalists say is largely the result of global warming . <p> Pickersgill told his listeners at the St Matthew 's Church Hall in Santa Cruz that the records showed the last " two years to be the hottest @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ record-keeping begun . <p> For Pickersgill , Jamaica 's experience -- particularly in relation to drought and other extreme weather conditions -- underline the importance of the 2015 United Nations Climate Change Conference which is now taking place in Paris . <p> Scientists say the growing level of carbons and other harmful emissions in the atmosphere -- as a result of rapid industrialisation over the last 200 years , -- particularly in northern hemisphere countries -- is leading to global warming and other forms of environmental degradation . <p> Among the dangers , a warmer globe will likely lead to melting of the polar ice caps resulting in rising sea levels and imminent danger to land masses , not least small island states such as Jamaica , experts say . Scientists and environmental experts say if global warming is not arrested , natural ecosystems will be endangered and human health and well-being will come under threat . <p> Diplomats , delegates and political leaders now gathered in Paris are expected to find a way to an agreement that will reduce harmful gas and industrial emissions . <p> A target of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 21st century to no more than two degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels . Beyond that , the globe will be in " uncharted " territory , scientists say . <p> Some scientific sources suggest that global temperatures have risen by more than 0.7 degrees Celsius over the last 100 years , and by more than 0.4 degree Celsius since the late 1970s . <p> Pickersgill told his Santa Cruz audience that part of the drive to contain emissions at the Paris conference will be an effort to set up a " green climate fund of US$100 billion " to benefit countries , including Jamaica , which have committed to environmental recovery . <p> While Jamaica 's role in degrading the planetary environment has been minuscule , Pickersgill told land-titling beneficiaries in Santa Cruz that they and their neighbours also had a role to play in environmental recovery through proper land use . <p> More to the point , the droughts of the last two years should have brought home to Jamaicans the need to protect the forests , which in turn protect the nation 's watersheds , Pickersgill said . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Earth " and must be planted , nurtured and protected . <p> " If our watersheds are degraded : If trees are being cut down and our forests are utilised in an unsustainable manner ... if our air and water are being polluted by human activities , then it goes without question that our environment is dying and , by extension , our communities are also dying , " Pickersgill said . <p> The minister told farmers that , " while you need to earn a living , I also encourage you also to preserve the environment through environmentally friendly farming techniques and practices . Economic development can not and must not come at a cost to the environment . " <p> " One method of ensuring that your land and your community are healthy is by planting , and where necessary replanting trees .
