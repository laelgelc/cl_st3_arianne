<h> Trying to beat the heat <p> Jetty jumpers find the fastest way to to get relief at Henley Beach , west of Adelaide , Australia , as temperatures rose above 42 deg C. <p> If temperatures do not fall below 40 deg C today , the hot spell in Adelaide will officially become a heatwave . That is defined by the country 's Bureau of Meteorology as three consecutive days with a temperature at or above 40 deg C , or five consecutive days where the maximum temperature is at or higher than 35 deg C. <p> Commuters riding the subway have been informed that trains will be travelling more slowly than normal as some parts of the tracks might be affected by the heat . <p> The bureau has no doubt about what is behind these unusually high temperatures : climate change .
