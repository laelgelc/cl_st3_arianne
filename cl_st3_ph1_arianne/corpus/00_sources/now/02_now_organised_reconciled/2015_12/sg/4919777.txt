<p> SINGAPORE -- Visitors to Snow City will get to experience snowfall indoors from Dec 29 , following its first major revamp since opening 15 years ago . <p> And in the meanwhile , as part of the revamp that was launched today ( Dec 17 ) , visitors can experience " realistic , fine snow " with the introduction of a new snow machine , said Snow City in a press release today .
