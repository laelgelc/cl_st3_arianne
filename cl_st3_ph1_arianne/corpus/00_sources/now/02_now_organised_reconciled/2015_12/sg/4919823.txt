<h> Head of UN refugee agency deplores lack of capacity , political will to end conflicts <p> GENEVA * Humanitarian organisations attempting to help the soaring numbers of people fleeing conflicts and natural disasters around the world are at " a breaking point " , UN refugee chief Antonio Guterres has warned . <p> " In the last few years , we have seen a staggering escalation of displacement caused both by conflict and by natural hazards , " he told a conference in Geneva on Wednesday on the root causes of displacement . <p> Conflicts and serious crises are raging in 27 countries , and six of them - in the Central African Republic , Burundi , Nigeria , South Sudan , Syria and Yemen - have spilled over to surrounding regions , bringing the total number of countries currently in peril to a staggering 37 , the UN said earlier this month . <p> The conflicts have forced more than 60 million people to flee their homes worldwide , with those escaping violence in Syria , Iraq and
