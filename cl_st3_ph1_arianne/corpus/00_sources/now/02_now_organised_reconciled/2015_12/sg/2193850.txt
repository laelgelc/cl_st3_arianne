<h> Bring fresh meaning to ' independence ' post-SG50 <p> With Singapore having crossed the 50-year milestone of independence and the start of a new year , it is time for Singaporeans to reflect anew and bring fresh meaning to the word " independence " . <p> We should aim to be an action-oriented people rather than a people who often rely on state-initiated campaigns or paternalistic prodding or nudging from our leaders before making a change . <p> The nation 's No. 1 position in many areas in the international arena is , after all , largely due to strong government institutions , capable leaders and administrative excellence . <p> But at home , the public can do more . <p> It is said that a person is the sum of his actions , and this makes for a strong guiding principle on how we can move forward . <p> For a start , wishing for society to become more gracious is not going to get us anywhere . <p> We should just make it so by putting kindness into action . <p> Highlighting commuters ' bad @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of time and energy , and achieves nothing . <p> Preaching online about how one should act , mixed with ugly vitriol against those who act " ungraciously " , adds up to zero on the graciousness index . <p> At the recent climate change conference in Paris , world governments agreed on ambitious targets in a bid to mitigate the effects of climate change ( " Historic deal may signal end of fossil fuel era " ; Dec 14 , 2015 ) . <p> But on the ground , we must translate this into action right now , independent of what new local green campaigns might kick in . <p> Declining a plastic bag when we shop is not going to be very helpful if we continue to support products with excessive packaging . <p> If the issue is not well understood and we are misguided in our actions , good intentions can result in a deficit in terms of benefit to the environment we want to save . <p> Last but not least , let us banish forever the notion that there is such a thing as a season @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ way .
