<h> Early Bird <p> To curb the massive pollution caused by the textile industry , the government has directed it to adhere to the ' Zero Liquid Discharge ' ( ZLD ) norm . ZLD essentially means that a factory recycles all effluents and does n't release even a drop into the environment . <p> The draft notification issued in this regard appears strict , but a closer study reveals that it could do more harm than good , to both the industry and the environment . Perhaps , this is a consequence of the environment ministry 's zeal to get quick results . <p> Water recycled by these plants will be reused . The units wo n't be allowed to draw groundwater except to make up for the shortfall and for drinking , as assessed by the respective State Pollution Control Board or the Pollution Control Committee . <p> After the notification is approved , the units will have 30 months to set up the treatment plants .
