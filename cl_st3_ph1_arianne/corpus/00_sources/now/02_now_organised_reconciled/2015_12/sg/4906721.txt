Photo : Reuters <p> PARIS -- French President Francois Hollande was expected to attend today 's ( Dec 12 ) unveiling of a planned final draft of a UN pact to tame global warming , following marathon negotiations in the northern outskirts of Paris . <p> " After this last night of negotiations , the president is set to come to Le Bourget in the late morning when he may speak to the plenary session , " during which the draft accord will be presented , a source at the president 's office said . <p> The document is due to be presented to ministers at the conference being attended by ministers from 195 nations at 11.30am ( 6.30pm , Singapore time ) .
