<p> While continuing to warn Japanese Prime Minister Shinzo Abe over what he calls " sensitive issues " between the two countries , the Chinese leader is now trying to woo his neighbour out of fear that China could be internationally isolated due to its actions in the South China Sea . <p> Xi and Abe on Nov. 30 in Paris had a brief meeting without sitting down on the sidelines of the 21st session of the Conference of the Parties to the United Nations . Framework Convention on Climate Change , or COP21 . The meeting marked the two leaders ' third exchange of views since each took office . <p> The first came during the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation , or APEC , summit in Beijing in November 2014 ; the second one was during a meeting of Asian and African leaders in Indonesia this past April . <p> The Indonesian conference was held to commemorate the 60th anniversary of the original Asian-African Conference , also known as the Bandung Conference , which was held in Bandung , Indonesia in 1955.
