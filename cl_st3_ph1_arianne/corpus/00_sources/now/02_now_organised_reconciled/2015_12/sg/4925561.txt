The PM has asked the close aides and cabinet members to promote peace , " The Nation quoted a Pakistani official as saying . <p> As per the statement , Sharif is optimistic about Indo-Pak relations . The PM as well as the military leadership of Pakistan want to go forward with the peace talks . <p> PM Narendra Modi and Nawaz Sharif had a brief interaction in Paris during the Climate Change Conference .
