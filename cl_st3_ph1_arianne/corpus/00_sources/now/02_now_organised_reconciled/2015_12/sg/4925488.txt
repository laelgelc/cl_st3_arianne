<h> IBN7 to kick off year-end special programming on 20 Dec <p> The 11-day series titled ' Saal Kamaal 2015 ' features an array of shows that will take the viewers on a journey through all the big developments of 2015 . <p> It will range from controversies , serious takes on climate change , shocking crime stories , as well as the spectacular rise and fall of the inimitable Radhey Maa . Viewers will also get a glimpse of some of the gala wedding events of the year gone by . <p> The channel will also offer special shows that will focus on the performances and plans of nine key ministers from the Modi government . Horror devotees will be treated to a segment on the supernatural . <p> For viewers who also want a window into 2016 , a tarot , numerology and astrology show will air on the final three days of the telecast.
