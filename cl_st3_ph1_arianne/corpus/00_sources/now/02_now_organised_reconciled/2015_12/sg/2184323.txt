<h> Potential life-saving drug has now received green light from regulators in three countries <p> BRASIILIA * Brazil has become the first South American nation to authorise the world 's first dengue vaccine , which its French manufacturer says has the potential to save hundreds of lives in the country . <p> The tropical disease , a flu-like illness carried by mosquitoes , killed 839 people this year in Brazil and infected more than 1.5 million . <p> Sprawling Brazil 's decision on Monday to allow the new vaccine , called Dengvaxia , is a coup for French pharmaceutical giant Sanofi Pasteur , which has now received the green light from regulators in three countries . <p> Mexico became the first country to allow the vaccine on Dec 9 , and the Philippines followed suit last week .
