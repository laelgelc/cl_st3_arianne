<p> KUALA LUMPUR -- Malaysia , the world 's second-largest producer of palm oil , pledged to cut projected greenhouse gas emissions 45 per cent by 2030 after world leaders met in Paris to open talks on combating climate change . <p> The South-east Asian nation will lower its emissions relative to 2005 levels , Prime Minister Najib Razak said today ( Dec 2 ) in a statement posted on his personal blog . <p> The target for 2030 consists of a 35 per cent cut on an unconditional basis and further 10 per cent reduction based on climate finance and technology transfer . <p> " This new target shall not and should not be seen as an impediment to our economic development , " Mr Najib said .
