" <p> Manuel Garcia Corpus said the CESVO was operating 4,591,000 pesos for the campaign against the Huanglongbing ( HLB ) , which helps protecting 22,613 hectares of citrus against this disease , its vector , the Asian citrus psyllid , and controlling outbreaks . <p> According to the CESVO , the HLB disease , which is caused by the Candidatus Liberibacter spp. bacterium , " is considered the most devastating disease for citrus worldwide , because there is no known cure for the infected trees . In Mexico , the HLB has been detected in 15 citrus states : Baja California Sur , Campeche , Colima , Chiapas , Guerrero , Hidalgo , Jalisco , Michoacan , Nayarit , Puebla , Quintana Roo , Sinaloa , Tabasco , Yucatan , Zacatecas , and Oaxaca .
