<p> More than 200,000 youth in Nakuru will benefit from the Ministry of Agriculture 's training on better methods of farming mitigating the impacts of climate change . <p> Moses Njogu , coordinator of the Nakuru County Youth in Modern Agriculture organisation told journalists on Friday that the training currently ongoing aims at equipping the youth with information on farming activities applicable in the wake of unpredictable weather patterns . <p> " We are doing the training in collaboration with Agricultural Sector Programme , " said Njogu . <p> He said intercropping a variety of crops with trees in the farms and discouraging burning of crop produce are among the practices they are training the youth to adopt to maintain sustainable food production . <p> Njogu said they are making efforts to reach more than 200,000 youth across the county while they encourage them to venture into agribusiness . <p> " We want to ensure that more youth are engaged in productive farming in the county because that is one way they can raise an income for themselves , " he said . <p> Nakuru government is currently @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ one of the Kenya 's food baskets . <p> The Agricultural Development Fund Act of 2014 provides for the provision of credit from the government for individuals interested in investing in agriculture .
