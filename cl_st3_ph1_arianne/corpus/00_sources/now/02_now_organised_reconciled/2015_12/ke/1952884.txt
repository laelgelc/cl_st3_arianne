Matiangi said at Senior Experts Dialogue ( SED ) meeting on science , technology and innovation in Nairobi . <p> He called on the countries to collaborate in initiating innovation hubs , clusters and parks as a link between academia , research , government , community and industry for the commercialization of innovations and intellectual assets . <p> " Kenya recognizes the power of partnerships
