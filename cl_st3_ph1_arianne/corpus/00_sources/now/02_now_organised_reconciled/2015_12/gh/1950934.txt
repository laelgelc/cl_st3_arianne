<p> Rocky Dawuni produced his first studio album , The Movement in 1996 . The industrious son of Ghana had his song , In Ghana featured on Putumayo New York City based record label collection , Reggae Around the World in 1998 . His second studio album , Crusade , was released in the same year . The eleven-track album had songs like : Inside Your Head , Sweet Bright Day , My Love , This Is Reggae and Jammin Nation .
