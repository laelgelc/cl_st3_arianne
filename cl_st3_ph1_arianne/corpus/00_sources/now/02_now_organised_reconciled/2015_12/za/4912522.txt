<h> Paris climate deal exceeded expectations -- World Bank 's Kim <p> The international deal on climate change signed in Paris over the weekend sends a strong signal to financial markets and companies about the importance of tackling climate change , World Bank President Jim Yong Kim said . <p> It 's rare to see that so many countries " can come together and agree on something , " said Kim in a Bloomberg Television interview on Monday . " We went in really with our fingers crossed , hoping for some kind of agreement and this agreement went far beyond what any of us had hoped . " <p> The landmark deal to limit greenhouse gas emissions called for nations to work toward capping global temperature increases since pre-industrial times to 2 degrees Celsius ( 3.6 degrees Fahrenheit ) . Rich countries underlined their $100 billion annual commitment to fund climate action projects in the developing world , with a higher target to be negotiated for 2025 . The financing will be done largely through development institutions such as the World Bank .
