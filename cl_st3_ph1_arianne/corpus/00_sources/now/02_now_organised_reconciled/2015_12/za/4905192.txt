" <p> Over 80% of South Africa 's electricity is generated from coal , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> Over 80 percent of South Africa 's electricity is generated from coal , making Eskom one of the biggest polluters in the world . <p> Some of the people who participated in the march believe renewable energy will help communities save a lot of money that they are currently paying as a result of Eskom 's high tariffs . <p> Meanwhile , this Friday marks the end of COP21 Climate change summit in Paris . <p> Doyle has little faith that the conference will come up with a binding agreement . <p> " We believe that COP is a failing process .
