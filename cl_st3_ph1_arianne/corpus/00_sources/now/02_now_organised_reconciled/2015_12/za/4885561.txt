<h> Africa to double electricity production after launching Renewable Energy Initiative <p> Africa has launched an ambitious Africa Renewable Energy Initiative ( AREI ) as the continent 's major contribution to Conference of Parties ( COP21 ) taking place in Paris , France . <p> The launch of the initiative which aims to produce 300 gigawatts ( GW ) of electricity for the continent by 2030 is a demonstration of Africa 's leadership in the UN climate negotiations . <p> The initiative 's goals are to help achieve sustainable development , enhance well-being and sound economic development by ensuring universal access to sufficient amounts of clean , appropriate and affordable energy . <p> The project also aims to help African countries leapfrog towards renewable energy systems that support their low-carbon development strategies while enhancing economic and energy security . <p> The initiative is expected to deliver 10 GW of new and additional electrical installed capacity by 2020 and 300 GW by 2030 . <p> AREI is an outcome of African leadership in Workstream II of the Durban Platform including their May 2014 proposal for a global renewable energy @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ African Heads of State ( AU Assembly and Committee of African Heads of State and Government on Climate Change ) and Ministers of Environment ( AMCEN ) the G7 ( Elmau Summit ) the G20 ( Energy Summit ) . <p> Speaking during the launch of the project at the Africa Pavillion in Paris , Akinwumi Adesina , the President of the African Development Bank ( AfDB ) , one of the major sponsors of the initiative , said the institution has tripled its financing to climate change initiatives . <p> " We are aware that Africa needs massive financing for climate change mitigation and adaptation efforts .
