" We are severely punishing those responsible for this tragedy , " she said . <p> While they are going to court , Brazilian authorities are looking for a settlement similar to the $20.8 billion agreement reached by the US government with oil firm BP following the 2010 Deepwater Horizon spill in the Gulf of Mexico . <p> However , in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a judge in 2014 ruled that BP was primarily responsible for the spill and had to pay up to $18 billion in penalties on top of the previous payments made . <p> The November 5 damburst in Minas Gerais , Brazil 's main mining state , unleashed 60 million cubic metres of mud and mine waste that demolished a nearby village , killed at least 13 people and polluted a major river valley , killing fish and reaching the Atlantic Ocean . <p> The lawsuit filed in federal court in Brasilia required Samarco to take immediate action to contain and reduce the environmental impact of the damburst , the chief prosecutor of the coastal state of Esp ?
