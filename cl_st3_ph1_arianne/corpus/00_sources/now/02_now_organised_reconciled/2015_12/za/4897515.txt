<h> Experts say climate change affecting Mount Everest <p> A team of experts have warned that they have found evidence that climate change is have an impact on the world 's highest peak , Mount Everest and is resulting in melting of the glaciers . <p> The team of researchers from China said that the Mount Everest is becoming warmer from the previous five decades and glaciers around the peak are shrinking . The researcher showed that the glaciers in the mountain are shrinking at a rapid pace and swelling glacial lakes and rivers downstream . <p> Chinese Academy of Sciences , Hunan University of Science and Technology and Mount Qomolangma Snow Leopard Conservation Centre said in a report that the climate change is impacting the glaciers around the highest peak . The researchers also said that the ecological environment in the area is improving with the increased forest cover around the peak . <p> A report by the Institute of Tibetan Plateau Research showed that Tibet is facing difficult scenario as its glaciers were getting affected .
