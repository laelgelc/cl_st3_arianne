<h> Eco-innovation is critical to solving Africa 's climate challenges <p> Researchers and academics from institutions of higher learning in Africa have been challenged to step out of their comfort zones and provide practical solutions to climate change . <p> Speakers at the Eco-Innovation Strategies for Tackling Climate Change in Africa meeting on Monday , December 9 at the ongoing Paris climate conference ( COP21 ) noted that for Africa to tackle climate change challenges , collaborative efforts among universities , the community and business owners is crucial . <p> Tumusiime Rhoda Peace , Commissioner for Rural Economy and Agriculture at the African Union Commission ( AUC ) , called on all stakeholders to work together to find scientifically proven measures to address climate change in the region . <p> " We have to tow a clean path to protect our environment .
