<h> 2015 End of Year Statement By President Jacob Zuma <p> The year 2015 was full of activities in advancing government 's programme of action to move South Africa forward . A lot of progress has been made in various areas in the implementation of our Medium Term Strategic Framework derived from the National Development Plan . <p> This abridged report touches on only a few areas of the work that has been done to transform the country and attack poverty , inequality and unemployment , and to further deepen relations with Africa and the world . <h> SOCIAL TRANSFORMATION <p> Much has changed in the education landscape since 1994 , and government has sought to build on these gains since 2009 . Our focus has been on getting the fundamentals right in basic education , while strengthening and improving higher education . <p> This has translated into a focussed approach that is beginning to pay dividends , yet we are aware that more still needs to be done . <p> A number of achievements have been scored . <p> Government has increased investment in early childhood development @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ To date government spends R1.8 billion on ECD benefitting 948768 children nationwide . Government pays a subsidy of R15 per child per day for 264 days of the year . <p> The National School Nutrition Programme has reached more than nine million learners daily over the past few years , offering nutritious meals to over 20 000 primary , secondary and special schools nationally . <p> To improve the learning environment , the Accelerated Schools Infrastructure Delivery Initiative , ( ASIDI ) has demonstrated the capability of the state in dealing head on with the infrastructure backlogs . To date , the ASIDI programme has delivered 123 state-of-the-art schools . A further 520 schools were provided with water , 395 were given decent sanitation and 293 were connected to the power grid .
