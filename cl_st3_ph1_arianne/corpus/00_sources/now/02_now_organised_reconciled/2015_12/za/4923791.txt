<h> Gungubele signs greenhouse pledge <p> Johannesburg - Ekurhuleni has pledged to help reduce greenhouse gas emissions after executive mayor Mondli Gungubele committed to a global coalition designed to reduce environmental deterioration . <p> On Thursday , Gungubele signed up with the Compact of Mayors to help make communities in Ekurhuleni more resilient to climate change , meaning the area will have to regularly report its progress in reducing its greenhouse gas emissions publicly . <p> According to the organisation 's website , the Compact of Mayors is a global coalition of mayors and city officials pledging to reduce local greenhouse gas emissions , enhance resilience to climate change and to track their progress transparently . <p> The Compact was launched in September 2014 by UN secretary-general Ban Ki-moon and his special envoy for cities and climate change , Michael Bloomberg . <p> " The Compact of Mayors reinforces our city 's commitment to its people and its businesses , " said Gungubele . <p> By committing to the Compact of Mayors , we are continuing in our work to make Ekurhuleni a place where people want to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ The actions we take at a local level will have a global impact ; and by improving our city , we will be helping to create a better world for today 's urban citizens and generations to come . " <p> Mayoral spokesman Zweli Dlamini added : " For its part , Ekurhuleni will conduct an inventory of greenhouse gas emissions in the city , develop emissions targets and draw up an action plan to mitigate climate change . " <p> According to the mayoral team , recent research has shown that if every local government in the world took action in accordance with the Compact of Mayors - particularly by targeting their building , energy , transportation and waste sectors - they could reduce global greenhouse gas emissions by the equivalent of cutting the world 's annual coal use by more than half . <p> " The Compact of Mayors reflects the commitment that cities are making to reduce carbon emissions and the crucial role they play in confronting climate change , " said Bloomberg . <p> " The more cities that take part in the Compact , the bigger @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ mayors who have joined , and look forward to welcoming more cities .
