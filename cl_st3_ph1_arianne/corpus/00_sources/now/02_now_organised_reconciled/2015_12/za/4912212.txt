<h> Join forces against crop pests , SADC countries urged <p> Sifelani Tsiko There is need for effective coordination at regional level to control plant pests and diseases that threaten food security for countries in the Southern African region , experts say . Plant-disease scientists who gathered in Harare for a two -week Africa Solidarity Trust Fund ( ASTF ) regional plant health sanitary and phytosanitary ( relating to the health of plants , especially with respect to the requirements of international trade ) management ( SPS ) workshop said the region harboured hundreds of unreported crop pests and diseases which posed a significant threat to agricultural productivity . Dr Joyce Mulila-Mitti , a Food and Agriculture Organisation ( FAO ) plant disease expert , said closer collaboration is critical for the region because efforts to control a plethora of new diseases and insect pests that hamper crop production and productivity are fragmented and actions limited to only some countries , allowing the epidemics to persist . Other plant disease experts cited poor adoption of control measures , limited capacity for early and accurate detection , as well as inadequate regulatory and phytosanitary management measures across the SADC region as factors inhibiting control and prevention . " The sub region lacks an organised and effective pest and diseases monitoring system and would benefit immensely from surveillance facilities at strategic points to monitor spread of pests and diseases , " said Dr David Phiri , coordinator for FAO Sub-regional Office for Southern Africa . " This regional training is aimed at enhancing the regional SPS mechanisms and technical capacities with increased interaction and coordination of all sectors involved in the countries . " Insects , birds and plant diseases account for between 30 and 40 percent of crop losses in the SADC region and across Africa , affecting major food and nutritional security crops . Trans-boundary plant and animal pests and diseases ( TPAPDs ) inclusive of forestry and fisheries threats pose a major constraints to the livelihoods of about 70 percent of an estimated 267 million in the SADC region who depend on agriculture for food , intra and inter-regional trade , income and employment . Plant disease experts say ( TPAPDs ) jeopardise food security , especially in highly vulnerable rural communities , and have major economic , social , environmental and public health impacts . Examples food crop pests that affect SADC countries include fruit flies , armyworm , brown locust , migratory locust and red locusts , nematodes and grain borers , noxious weeds , banana Wilts ( including Fusarium wilt TR4 race ) and Bunchy Top Diseases , Cassava Mosaic Virus and Brown Streak Disease and Maize Viral Diseases . Whenever outbreaks occur , these leave a trail of destruction , leaving the poor exposed .
