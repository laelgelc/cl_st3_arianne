<h> KZN health department digs borehole for hospital <p> Durban -- A provincial health facility in one of the areas in the country hardest hit by drought will soon use boreholes to alleviate water shortages . <p> The KwaZulu-Natal department of health said on Tuesday that it had " joined the call to urge the community , particularly health workers , to use water sparingly at all its health facilities " . <p> The department is trying to minimise water shortages at the Stanger Hospital in the iLembe District . <p> " The fact that some of these hospitals were built decades ago and were meant to serve only a few members of the populace has not helped matters . Health facilities like Stanger Hospital use water-cooled air-conditioning chillers , which consume about 20 000 litres of water per day , " the department said in a statement . <p> The department added that the shortage of water " compels the department to , at certain instances , shut down these units " . <p> " To mitigate the impact of this water situation , as a short-term @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a borehole to add to the available one at the Stanger Hospital . <p> " As a long-term measure , the department is engaged in a process aimed at the replacement of the old water-cooled air-conditioning chillers , which consume a lot of water , with new air-cooled air-conditioning chillers that do not consume water .
