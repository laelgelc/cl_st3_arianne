<h> House Revisits Sale of NITEL/MTEL by BPE <p> The House of Representatives on Tuesday directed its Committees on Telecommunications and Privatisation to conduct a thorough investigation into the liquidation and sale of Nigeria Telecommunications ( NITEL ) and Mobile Telecommunications ( MTEL ) by the Bureau of Public Enterprise ( BPE ) . <p> NITEL and MTEL were purchased in December 2014 by NATCOM for the sum of $242.3million . The House expressed concern that since the sale of the companies , a lot of controversies have been raised about the processes leading to their sales , particularly allegations that the companies were under-valued before they were sold . <p> Hon. Henry Nwawuba ( Imo PDP ) in a motion said the revenue potential of the companies are huge , and they also have capacity to bridge the unemployment gap among young Nigerians . <p> There is a need to resolve the issues surrounding the sales and takeover , he said .
