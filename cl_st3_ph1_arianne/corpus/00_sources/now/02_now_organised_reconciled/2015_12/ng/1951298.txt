<h> The Commonwealth as Scotland Assumes Office of Secretary-General <p> Of the many decisions and resolutions adopted at the recently concluded Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting ( CHOGM ) in Malta last week , the election of Rt . Hon.Patricia Janet Scotland QC , as the Sixth Secretary-General of the organization was perhaps the most remarkable in many respects . <p> Rt . Hon. Scotland assumes office as Secretary - General at a time the Commonwealth Secretariat is marking its golden jubilee anniversary having been established in 1965 . <p> The dynamics of international politics requires of the Commonwealth to reform its methods , structure and functions . Such transformation can only be initiated and managed by a virile , focused and technically sound Secretariat driven by the consciousness to truly deepen the meaning , purpose and character of the organization whose membership consists mainly of former British colonies . <p> From her pedigree , Rt . Hon Scotland might just be the right Secretary-General the Commonwealth needs at this point in time .
