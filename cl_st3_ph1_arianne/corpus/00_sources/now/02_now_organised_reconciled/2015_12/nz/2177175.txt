<h> Welcome to the prestigious basil of the year awards <p> OPINION : Kia ora koutou . Yes folks , it 's that time of year where we cross live to the basil patch deep in Newtown for the annual end-of-year Basil Awards . <p> Drum roll please ... <p> Fresh basil politician of the year : John Key kept his party ahead in the polls but created a few train wrecks . Andrew Little did n't make the headway he wanted yet was far from the disaster that some predicted . Winston Peters pulled off a stunning win in Northland but what else ? Politician of the year is Murray McCully for proving that New Zealand is truly the land of opportunity . <p> Bruised basil worst multinational : Aluminium , mining , internet and banking companies were just some of the overseas corporations who managed to avoid paying their fair share of tax . But the stand-out performer was Serco . As well as running Mt Eden prison appallingly they kept Corrections officials and the minister in the dark . Will reinstated Corrections Minister Judith Collins @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ private prisons came in under her watch . <p> Bruised Silver Fern tiresome national issue of the year : The flag , though it did help me to understand why so many New Zealanders do n't vote at election time . And for those predicting the demise of John Key if the flag referendum does n't go his way , remember that in 2011 he urged the country to get rid of MMP . They studiously ignored him yet he romped home in the election . <p> Squashed basil award for tallest poppy : Like most Lefties I was critical of the appointment of Susan Devoy as Race Relations Commissioner , yet I have been pleasantly surprised . Sometimes the difference between a redneck and a progressive is just a bit of education . Yet for daring to suggest that not every single person in the country celebrates the birth of Jesus , Ms Devoy has been subject to a volley of unfair criticism . <p> Lemon basil for leaving a sour taste : Over many months New Zealand Police failed miserably to deal with the serious sexual violence alleged in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ about an award-winning journalist , quick as a flash they spent ten hours illegally searching Nicky Hager 's records . Must do better . <p> Greenhouse basil for biggest political shift of the year : Though scientists are in almost unanimous agreement , many politicians and journalists have been sceptical about climate change . This year , however , has seen the focus change from ' is it happening ? ' to ' what are we doing about it ? ' About time , too . <p> Replanted basil for comeback of the year : After his Australian fiasco Paul Henry looked dead and buried . But with a smart co-host in Hilary Barry and some excellent panel guests his show is an often intelligent alternative to the bores , ditz-rains and 12-year-olds on TVNZ 's Breakfast . <p> Bruised Island Bay basil tiresome local issue of the year : A simple cycleway not yet completed seems to have turned into a massive local issue . <p> Cold basil-infused chips for best game at the Cake Tin : The Super 15 Final was a great game -- if you were from @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Black Caps ' demolition of West Indies back in March . And you were even able to get a cold drink and something hot to eat . <p> Purple basil most ridiculous local issue : In a packed field , our city councillors argued about endangered traffic lights that were n't endangered , free lunches for themselves and in whose backyard a camping ground should be placed . But this award became a no-contest thanks to those who thought pandas at the zoo would be a brilliant idea . <p> Sweet as basil award for community event : Following the tragic death of Jerry Collins the community of Porirua showed the world how to mourn a favourite son in dignified style . Malo lava . <p> Hope you all have a fantastic New Year moving forward , that is full of excellence and innovative new learnings.
