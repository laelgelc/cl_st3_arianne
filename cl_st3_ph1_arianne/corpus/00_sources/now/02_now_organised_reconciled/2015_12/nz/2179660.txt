Can we not get anything done ? Not quite -- the sod was finally turned on Transmission Gully in 2015 , a road that has been preceded by eons of talking . And the prospect of a new flag that erased the Union Jack was greeted with widespread , if slightly perplexing , anger . <p> New Zealand prison inmates assumed a new prominence this year , whether in fights filmed at a privately-run Auckland prison , or on cellphone calls from Christmas Island , where Australia has shipped its unwanted inhabitants .
