One was about how much shopping we as a nation have apparently done over the Christmas period . The other was about how happy members of the Federated Farmers are with the National government . <p> As we head into a new year with indications looking decidedly shaky in the foreseeable future , it 's worth looking at both stories more closely . <p> The news about Christmas spending was that we have surpassed previous spending records , up several per cent on last year . Apparently this is cause for joy . <p> I guess we 're meant to think with all this spending , things must be pretty good , right ? <p> But wait a minute . We 've all seen the empty shop fronts on Devon Street and Broadway in Stratford , McLean Street , Waitara and High Street in Hawera . <p> So , what explains this burst of Christmas largesse ? Have we all become secretly wealthy in spite of our economic conditions ? <p> I doubt it . The lesson of the story of growing Christmas spending is to take it all with a grain of salt . There 's stuff that the spin merchants do n't say . Like how much of the spending is on credit .
