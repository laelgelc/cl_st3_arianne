<h> Climate change deal step towards stability - BusinessNZ <p> The climate change agreement reached today is a step towards a more stable policy environment for business and communities , BusinessNZ says . <p> Chief Executive Phil O'Reilly said it would take some time to consider the implications of the agreement for New Zealand 's policy settings , but international agreements covering all parties were good for certainty . <p> " Business welcomes clear targets for emissions reduction set with regard for growth and competitiveness . <p> " Cooperation by New Zealand businesses in emissions reduction work such as through the Sustainable Business Council is in line with the direction of today 's agreement . " <p> He said there was an ongoing need for government and business to engage on polices to get the best environmental and growth outcomes .
