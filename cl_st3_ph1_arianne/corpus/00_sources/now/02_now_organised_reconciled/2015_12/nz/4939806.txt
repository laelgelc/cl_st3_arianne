<h> Significant trade deals benefit NZ <p> New Zealand is again set to benefit from two significant trade announcements made recently , giving hope to the agriculture sector , in particular , for more open access to previously closed markets . <p> The World Trade Organisation reached deals on agricultural export subsides , food aid and other issues last weekend . The weekend of negotiations capped a ministerial conference in the Kenyan capital of Nairobi where rich and poor countries were split over the path of trade reform . <p> While the deal is said to end years of stalemate , concern is now arising about the fate of the poor in countries like India , where the removal of some tariffs and barriers could impose more misery on people scraping a living in factories making products for the Indian domestic market . <p> Newly-appointed Trade Minister Todd McClay is understandably positive about the ongoing effects for New Zealand from the Nairobi agreement and also , the implementation of the New Zealand-Korea free-trade agreement signed by his predecessor Tim Groser . <p> From last Sunday , current duty-free @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ' ' and existing tariffs will be eliminated on $793 million , or 48% , of New Zealand exports . <p> The wine industry benefits immediately from a full elimination of the 30% tariff on their exports to Korea - a significant win for wineries in Central Otago , and other wine-growing regions .
