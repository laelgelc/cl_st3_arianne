<h> NZ joins Global Geothermal Alliance <p> New Zealand is the newest member of the Global Geothermal Alliance ( GGA ) , Energy and Resources Minister Simon Bridges announced overnight in Paris . <p> The GGA is an initiative led by the International Renewable Energy Agency ( IRENA ) to increase geothermal electricity generation and the direct use of geothermal heat . The announcement of New Zealand 's membership of the GGA took place in Paris as part of the Climate Change Conference . <p> " Joining the Alliance will position New Zealand firms to benefit from geothermal projects and partnerships around the world , " Mr Bridges says . <p> New Zealand currently has geothermal experts operating around the world including the Philippines , Indonesia , the Caribbean and the Pacific . <p> " We have a very unique value proposition and advantage in geothermal .
