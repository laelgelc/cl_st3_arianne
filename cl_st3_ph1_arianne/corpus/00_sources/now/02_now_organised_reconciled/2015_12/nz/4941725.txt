<h> Expat Tales : Happy hours in Hamburg <p> Feilidh O'Dwyer-Strang moved to Hamburg to complete his Master 's in Journalism and Globalisation . <p> Feilidh O'Dwyer-Strang says Hamburg is a compact and affordable option compared to other European cities . <p> What inspired your move , and how long have you been there ? I came to Hamburg to complete my thesis for an Erasmus Mundus Master 's in Journalism and Globalisation . I 've been here for three months . <p> What do you do there ? I 'm a student at the University of Hamburg but I also write for and edit a climate change blog for the university and occasionally represent Otago University at international student recruitment events . <h> Kiwi expats : What 's your tale ? <p> Share your stories , photos and videos . <p> What are the greatest advantages to living there ? It 's a spectacular city . It has amazing architecture , the Alster , cool areas to party and the public transport is excellent . The weather is n't too flash but one could say that about most of Northern Europe . My German is still in the early stages and although most people here speak very good English , there have been times when I was navigating Germany 's bureaucratic madness and I wished I was a bit more fluent with Deutsch . <p> How expensive is it compared to New Zealand ? How much is a beer ? Food and going out is far cheaper than NZ . You can buy a litre of milk for about 55 Euro cents ( less than $1 ) and a pint at a bar wo n't be more than ? 3.50 . Rents are similar to Auckland but it depends on what suburb you live in . <p> What do you do in your spare time ? Practise my German , socialise , read and watch a lot of politics and news , exercise and travel as much as I can . <p> What 's the local delicacy and would you recommend eating it ? The city is quite cycle-friendly with designated biking lanes on most streets . Public transport is great - you can go basically anywhere at any time . <p> What 's the shopping like ? I 've hit a couple of nice clothing shops around town but I tend to shop only when I need something rather than just perusing . <p> Best after-dark activity ? After-dark you should take a stroll along the Reeperbahn . To my knowledge , it 's one of the biggest party streets in Europe . You have a kilometre or so of bars , clubs , outlandish sex shops and general debauchery .
