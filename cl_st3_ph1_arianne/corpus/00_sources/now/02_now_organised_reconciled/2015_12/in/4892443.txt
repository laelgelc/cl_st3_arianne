<h> Highlights of Question Hour in Parliament Houses <p> From monuments being encroached to the discovery of new plant and animal species , questions were asked and answered in both houses of parliament on Monday . Highlights ... <p> ' Integrate CVC , CBI under Lokpal ' <p> The Standing Committee on Personnel , Public Grievances , Law and Justice , presented its report on the Lokpal in both Houses , recommending the CVC and CBI be integrated with the Lokpal . It suggested inclusion of leader of single largest opposition party in Lok Sabha in the absence of a recognised leader of Opposition , as member of Lokpal selection panel . <p> Troubled Monuments <p> Uttar Pradesh accounts for the largest number of encroachments on historical monuments in the country . Out of 278 centrally-protected monuments and sites , which are encroached upon , UP accounts for nearly 100 , Union Culture Minister Mahesh Sharma told the Lok Sabha . The Modi government has taken several steps to protect such sites . <p> ' Postpone UPSC Exams ' <p> Expressing concerns over Chennai-based aspirants of UPSC exams @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in Rajya Sabha sought postponement of the UPSC main exam scheduled for December 18 . Their views were supported by other members . Minister of State for Parliamentary Affairs Mukhtar Abbas Naqvi said the issue would be conveyed to the Ministry . <p> UGC Action Against Erring Deemed Universities <p> UGC had found some institutions deemed to be universities were running off-campus centres without permission . HRD Minister Smriti Irani told the Lok Sabha it has asked them to close such centres . <p> Incensed Over Imported Incense Sticks <p> RS members expressed concern over import of allegedly hazardous incense sticks from China and Vietnam . Congress ' Hussain Dalwai asked for a ban on their sale as millions were affected . <p> Sachin Opens Account <p> The legendary Sachin Tendulkar took three years in the Rajya Sabha to ask a question . His maiden query , related to the Kolkata Metro , was answered by MoS for Railways Manoj Sinha on Friday . The second was on driving license procedures .
