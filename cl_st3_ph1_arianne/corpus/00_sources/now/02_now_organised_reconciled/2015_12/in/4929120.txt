This will be their contribution for a research project of the Space Applications Centre ( SAC ) on climate change and impact of global warming . <p> TNN Dec 22 , 2015 , 11.39 AM IST <p> AHMEDABAD : Two alumni of Ahmedabad University and Gujarat University are studying cryosphere at the south pole . This will be their contribution for a research project of the Space Applications Centre ( SAC ) on climate change and impact of global warming . And , they are beacons of hope for other pure science students . <p> RD Shah , head of department , Geology , at MG Institute of Science , said that the general perception is that only the students from top institutions get such chance . " This could be a life-changing experience for the girls as they would contribute to the researches done on this topic in India and also would understand international research practices .
