ANDHRA PRADESH <h> Call to promote use of FaL-G <p> Setting up more FaL-G brick units in the country would lead to better carbon emission reduction , according to Director-General and Director of Institute for Solid Waste Research and Ecological Balance ( INSWAREB ) Bhanumathi Kalidas and N. Kalidas respectively . INSWAREB is one of the NGOs appointed as observers for Conference of Parties on Climate Change . <p> Mr. Kalidas said Prime Minister Narendra Modi announced at the COP 21 that the country would add 175 gigawatts of renewable energy that would result in abatement of emission of 157,500 tonnes of carbon dioxide . <p> The same abatement could be obtained by setting up 220 FaL-G brick plants each with a capacity of three million bricks even as the renewable energy goals were pursued , he told a press conference here . <p> Mr. Kalidas said as of now 18000 FaL-G brick units were making 54 billion bricks saving 189 million tonnes of top soil . " <p> Mr. Kalidas said fly ash should be made freely available to fly ash brick manufacturers . Besides , taxes and duties were levied on fly ash units and as a result several units stopped short of making one crore bricks .
