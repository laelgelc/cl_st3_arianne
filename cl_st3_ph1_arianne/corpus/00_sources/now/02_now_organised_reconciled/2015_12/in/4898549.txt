<h> 31 Dead , 20,000 Families Homeless In Torrential Rain In Kinshasa <p> 31 people dead and 20,000 families homeless in less than three weeks of Torrential rains and flooding in the Democratic Republic of Congo 's capital , Kinshasa . <p> Kinshasa : Torrential rains and flooding have left 31 people dead and 20,000 families homeless in less than three weeks in the Democratic Republic of Congo 's capital , Kinshasa , city authorities said today . <p> Since November 19 , " we have recorded 31 deaths due to the collapse of homes " in repeated downpours hitting the teeming city of 10 million people , Kinshasa 's Interior Minister Emmanuel Akweti told AFP . <p> Most of the deaths occurred in the city 's particularly impoverished southern and eastern districts , notably along the banks of the giant Congo river . <p> Saying more heavy rain was forecast from now until the end of December , Akweti urged people in flooded areas " to leave their homes without delay . " <p> Kinshasa 's wet season normally runs from October to May , but this @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ third of the average annual rainfall -- or 450 mm ( 17.72 inches ) of water -- falling in less than three weeks , Akweti said . <p> In the southeastern district of Ndanu today , people were wearing boots and in places fecal matter could be seen floating in stagnant water as sewerage overflowed . <p> " We are convinced the unusual rainfall is connected to climate change , " Akweti said . <p> But one resident in Ndanu brushed off the claim , saying " Every year there 's flooding , the authorities do n't do anything about it , they do n't care . " <p> The city 's main pumping facility has been seriously damaged by the flooding , with water supplies cut in some areas . City authorities say taps should be running by day 's end .
