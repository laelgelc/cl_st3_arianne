<p> Pune : Increasing livability index and happiness index is the biggest challenge in front of budding engineers and planes , said Mahesh Zagade , CEO , Pune Metropolitan Region Development Authority , on Thursday . He was addressing the students in the concluding ceremony of Organisation of Students of Planning ( NOSP ) held at College of Engineering Pune . <p> " As in the future 70 percent population will be in Urban Area , increasing livability index and happiness index is challenge before budding engineers and planners .
