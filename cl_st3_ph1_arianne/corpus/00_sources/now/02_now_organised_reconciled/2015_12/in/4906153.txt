<p> Kolkata : In a first for a south Asian city , Kolkata today became part of a worldwide network of C40 cities which collectively work on addressing urban sustainability challenges . <p> West Bengal government today signed a Memorandum of Understanding with C40 Cities Climate Leadership Group which has over 80 cities as members including New York , London , Shanghai and Singapore . <p> " It is a partnership to share experience and expertise on urban development issues , " state Urban Development Minister Firhad Hakim said , adding that the city incorporates environment sustainability features into urban planning . <p> Sanjay Sridhar of the C40 group said , " Kolkata is the fist city in south Asia to have signed the MoU . " <p> He said they would work with Kolkata on issues related to transport , solid waste management , air pollution , water , energy and integrated planning . <p> " We will find cities from our network from whose models and expertise Kolkata can learn from . The idea is to work collectively on sustainability of cities , " Sridhar said @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in the next month.Set up in 2006 , C40 is a network of the world 's mega-cities committed to addressing climate change . It supports cities to collaborate effectively , share knowledge and drive meaningful , measurable and sustainable action on climate change .
