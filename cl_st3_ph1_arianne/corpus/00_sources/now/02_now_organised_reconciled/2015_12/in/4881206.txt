<h> BSRIA looks forward to outcome of Paris Summit on Climate Change <p> BSRIA is looking forward to the outcome of the 21st Session of the Conference of the Parties ( COP21 ) to the UN Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) that began on 30 November in Paris and runs until 11 December , bringing together world leaders to hammer out an agreement aimed at stabilising the climate and avoiding the worst impacts of climate change . <p> In the run-up to Paris , industry business and investor leaders are demonstrating unprecedented support for robust action on climate . The conference is expected to be a turning point in the fight against climate change . C. <p> For the first time at any UN climate conference , renewable energy solutions will be showcased in a series of high-profile events .
