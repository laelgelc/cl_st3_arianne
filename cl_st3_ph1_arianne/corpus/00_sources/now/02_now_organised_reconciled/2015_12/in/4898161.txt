<h> See : Stunning photos of endangered species projected on to the St Basilica in Vatican city <p> New Delhi : As part of the ongoing efforts to tackle the alarming issue of global climate change , a series of animal photographs by some of the world 's best photographers were projected on to the St Peter 's Basilica in Vatican City . <p> Some of the most astounding work on display was from National Geographic 's Joel Sartore 's Photo Ark - a project aimed at bringing the world 's attention to help stop the animal extinction crisis . <p> The ' Fiat Lux : Illuminating our common home ' light show is a public art projection to inspire change around the climate crisis . <p> The facade and the dome of St. Peter 's Basilica were lit on the opening day of the Jubilee where screenings of works that synthesize the cultures of five continents are presented .
