<p> A coalition of African countries aim at restoring 100 million hectares of degraded and deforested land by the year 2030 . The initiative , called as the African Restoration Initiative ( AFR100 ) plans to nurture and re-grow the forests that would lead to carbon sink and improve the livelihood of rural people . <p> The initiative was unveiled at United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCC ) COP-21 in Paris on December 6 , 2015 . The coalition consists of 10 African countries , 9 Financial partners and 10 technical support partners . <p> 10 countries committed about 42 million hectares of land for restoration , namely , Congo , Ethiopia , Kenya , Liberia , Madagascar , Malawi , Niger , Rwanda , Togo and Uganda . The countries , partnering with the World Bank and others have set aside more than $1 billion in development finance and $540 million in private sector impact investment . <p> African forests and wildlife contribute to African landscape and beauty and afforestion and reforestation will contribute in economic growth and development .
