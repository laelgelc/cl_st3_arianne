<h> Teenagers find their feet amidst innovative experiential workshops that ensure thrill and learning at once <p> On the outskirts of the city , switched off from the city 's pace , you see a bunch of enthusiastic teenagers cutting plywood according to a measurement suggested by an Australian mentor . Markings are being done on a big chunk of wood and the way it 's being done , it does n't appear to be rocket-science .
