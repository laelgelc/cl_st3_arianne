<h> Chittur turns hub of synthetic toddy <h> No dip in supply of toddy despite drop in production in coconut groves <p> Synthetic alternatives to toddy , having toxic chemical composition and high alcoholic content , are flowing out of Chittur , the traditional hub of toddy production in the State , to various parts of Kerala . <p> With the end of rainy season , coconut groves in Chittur taluk are witnessing a fall in daily production of toddy , but that has not in any way affected the requirement of more than 4,000 licensed toddy outlets across the State . <p> 1,300 permits <p> As per the data available with the Excise Department , the State government has issued 1,300 permits to transport 3,00,000 litres of toddy a day from Chittur to other parts of the State . <p> Though coconut growers and the Excise Department officials confirm that the actual toddy production in Chittur was way below the permitted amount , the supply had not been disrupted so far .
