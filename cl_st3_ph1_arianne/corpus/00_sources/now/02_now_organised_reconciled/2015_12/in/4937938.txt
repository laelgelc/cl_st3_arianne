Road transport policy including urban transport is mainly made by the States and has remained neglected . Decarbonising this sector and increasing its efficiency needs strong mandates for State governments , requiring them to comply with standards of minimum performance . This would raise standards of bus and urban rail travel , encouraging suitable investments , both public and private , and fast-track sustainable urban development . Providing a cross-subsidy for capital expenditure and operations through a dedicated fee on fossil fuels would be the way forward . <p> Without such measures , carbon emissions from passenger transport are bound to rise steadily .
