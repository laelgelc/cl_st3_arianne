Harris said the capital 's noxious air aggravated his eight-year-old son 's asthma , which became worse with every passing day . Then one day , he wrote , he decided to pack his bags and leave for Washington . <p> While the editorial sparked a conversation about Delhi 's debilitating pollution , not much was done to rein it in . That is , until its severity hit home . As winter set in , smog engulfed the city , covering it in a grim greyness .
