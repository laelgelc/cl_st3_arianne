IN SCHOOL <h> Running out of water ? <h> Climate change warming world 's lakes at alarming rate <p> Climate change is warming lakes around the world at an alarming rate , threatening freshwater supplies and ecosystems , says the largest study of its kind led by an Indian-origin researcher . <p> For the study spanning six continents , a total of 236 lakes , representing more than half of the world 's freshwater supply , were monitored for at last 25 years . <p> " We found that lakes are warming at an average of 0.34 degrees Celsius each decade all around the world , threatening freshwater supplies and ecosystems , " said study lead author Sapna Sharma from York University in Toronto , Canada . <p> " This can have profound effects on drinking water and the habitat of fish and other animals , " Sharma said . <p> At the current rate , algal blooms , which can ultimately rob water of oxygen , will increase by 20 per cent in lakes over the next century , the study @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and animals would increase by five per cent . These rates also imply that emissions of methane , a greenhouse gas 25 times more powerful than carbon dioxide , will increase by four per cent over the next decade . <p> " We found that ice-covered lakes , including Canadian lakes , are warming twice as fast as air temperatures and the North American Great Lakes are among the fastest warming lakes in the world , " Sharma noted . <p> It is also the first to combine manual lake measurements -- made by thousands of scientists over a century -- with satellite measurements of lake temperatures collected by NASA over a quarter century , the researchers said . The study results , published in the journal Geophysical Research Letters , were announced at the American Geophysical Union meeting in San Francisco , U.S.IANS
