<p> NEW DELHI : Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Thursday said that his book ' Convenient Action- Continuity for Change ' talks about India 's efforts to mitigate climate change and importance of climate justice . <p> " At #COP21 Summit in Paris , my book " Convenient Action- Continuity for Change " was released . My book " Convenient Action- Continuity for Change " talks about India 's efforts to mitigate climate change &amp; importance of climate justice , " Prime Minister Modi said in a series of tweets . <p> He also said that the book was released in the august presence of the President of France , Francois Hollande , UN Secretary General Ban Ki-moon and other world leaders at the Climate Summit in Paris . <p> The book offers a rich insight and perspective on the efforts of the Government of India towards mitigating the menace of climate change and towards improving our environment . <p> The book outlines the Prime Minister 's thoughts on climate ethics and climate justice .
