Slow Food began with a pasta party in 1986 when founder Carlo Petrini and his friends gathered to protest Rome 's first McDonalds . A gourmet who inadvertently triggered a revolution , he vociferously opposed , and continues to oppose , what he describes as the " homogenisation of food , " and thereby the homogenisation of taste . The truth is that much of the mass produced food flooding the world today has no history , tradition or cultural identity . More importantly , in an attempt to hook customers , it 's all sassy artificial colours and unabashedly big flavours , high on salt and sugar . And this is not just the type of fast food that comes to mind immediately , such as chips , cola and cookies . This also includes produce like fruit , vegetables and rice . Apples , for example , are selectively bred for sweetness , easy transport and pushy advertising . The result ?
