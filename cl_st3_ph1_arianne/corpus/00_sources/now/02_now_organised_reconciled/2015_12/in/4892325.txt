<h> Young scientists study effect of climate change on dengue <p> A team of students from BCM Arya Model Senior Secondary School , stood first in the state-level Children 's Science Congress held in Moga.The team defeated 88 participating teams to bag the first position . <p> TNN Dec 8 , 2015 , 10.28 AM IST <p> Ludhiana : A team of students from BCM Arya Model Senior Secondary School , stood first in the state-level Children 's Science Congress held in Moga . The team defeated 88 participating teams to bag the first position . They also qualified for the National Children 's Science Congress , 2015 . <p> The budding scientists presented a project on the theme , " Effect of weather and climate on Dengue . " They also worked on ways to mitigate the effects of climate change . The students conducted a number of experiments to lower the pH level and increase salinity in stagnant water to kill the dengue larva . They came up with innovative solutions to address the issue .
