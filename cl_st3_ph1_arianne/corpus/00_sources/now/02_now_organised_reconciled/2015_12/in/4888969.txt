<h> ' World Soil Health Day ' observed in Pakyong <p> 05 Dec , Pakyong ( IPR ) : On the occasion of " World Soil Health Day " , the Food Security and Agricultural Development Department ( FS&amp;ADD ) organised a program that was hosted by the Hon'ble Minister FS&amp;ADD , Shri Somnath Poudal at the Community Hall in Pakyong , East Sikkim . The Hon'ble Speaker of Sikkim State Legislative Assembly , Shri K.N Rai was present as the Chief Guest and area M.L.A , Shri.D.T Lepcha was the Guest of Honour . Also in attendance were Shri.B.B Rai , Shri.Bikram Pradhan , Shri.Bimal Dawari , Shri.Mechung Bhutia , Shri . Hemendra Adhikari and Shri.Gopal Baraily and Shri.Khorlo Bhutia , Secretary FS&amp;ADD . Officials and Staff of the concerned Departments and Pakyong Sub-Division , Press and the Progressive Farmers from the area were present on this occasion . The Director of FS&amp;ADD , MR.A.K Thapa delivered the welcome address after the ceremonial lighting of lamps and offering of silk scarves and flower bouquets .
