<h> Fresh norms to gauge water quality of rivers <p> PUNE : The Union government has come out with fresh guidelines to determine quality of water in rivers and other water bodies . <p> The notification , which was issued by the ministry of environment forests and climate change ( MoEF ) , contains 14 points and states the desired levels in each parameter that will be used to determine quality . The notification aims to check if water bodies are fit for bathing . In the drafting stage , it was made open to public on Monday for their inputs which can be sent over a month . <p> The criteria include points such as fecal coliform , pH value , dissolved oxygen and floating matter among others . The notification stated that " among various types of uses , there is one use that demands the highest level of quality in that stretch . Based on this , water quality requirements have been specified for different uses in terms of ' primary water quality criteria ' .
