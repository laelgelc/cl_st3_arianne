<p> London : President Barack Obama on Saturday vetoed two resolutions passed by the US Congress that sought to block the administration 's power-plant rules designed to cut emissions tied to climate change . <p> The measures targeted the Environmental Protection Agency rule limiting emissions from new power plants and a second standard that covers existing facilities . The regulations are a signature part of the president 's drive to combat global warming . <p> " The Clean Power Plan is essential in addressing the largest source of greenhouse gas pollution in our country , " Obama said in his message to lawmakers . " It is past time to act to mitigate climate impacts on American communities . " <p> The so-called resolutions of disapproval passed the House on 1 December and the Senate on 17 November with majorities well short of what 's needed to override a veto . Bloomberg
