<p> Home &gt;&gt; International &gt;&gt; Foreign secretaries of India and Pakistan to meet on January 15 <h> Foreign secretaries of India and Pakistan to meet on January 15 <p> December 27,2015 , 09.49 AM IST Agencies <p> After Prime Minister Narendra Modi paid a surprise visit to Pakistan on December 25 , the foreign secretaries of India and Pakistan will meet on January 15 in Islamabad . <p> According to agency reports , though there was no official announcement to this effect , sources said that foreign secretary S. Jaishankar will travel to Islamabad to hold talks with his counterpart , Mr Aizaz Ahmad Chaudhry . <p> The meeting will discuss the modalities of the bilateral comprehensive dialogue which was announced during the visit of external affairs minister Sushma Swaraj to Islamabad earlier this month . <p> On December 23 , the neighbouring country had proposed talks between the foreign secretaries to discuss all outstanding issues in the middle of January . According to sources , India had accepted the proposal . <p> The peace process was set in motion when the Prime Ministers of the two countries @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Climate Change Conference , which was followed by the national security advisers ' meeting in Bangkok a week later .
