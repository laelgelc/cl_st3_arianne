<p> LE BOURGET : Dismissing Western media 's criticism over its coal consumption to meet energy needs , India on Monday said that such reports will not deter the country from its stand at the crucial climate change conference here , in Paris . <p> " Certain criticism without attributing any motive to them , I take it as compliment . It does not deter me from my positions , " Environment Minister Prakash Javadekar said . <p> His comments came after India was targeted by the Western media for its expansion plans for usage of coal to meet its energy needs . <p> India has said it was ready to reduce its reliance on coal if the developed nations provide it with finance and technology to switch to clean energy sources . <p> India in its climate action plan has announced its ambitious plans to have 40 per cent of its electricity from renewables by 2030 . <p> " When we are increasing our renewable by seven fold , definitely our coal consumption comes down . India is burning 1/7th of coal that the first two ( emitters ) are doing , " he said on the first day of ministerial-level talks at the summit . <p> When asked about a US proposal to expand the donor base for climate financing , Javadekar said that the $100 billion as committed by certain parties should be fulfilled first . <p> " 100 billions dollars is a commitment of certain parties ...
