<h> Green Guidelines to Cut Delay for Road Projects <p> NEW DELHI : As road and highway projects get delayed seeking green clearance , the National Highways Authority of India ( NHAI ) has now approached the Union Environment Ministry seeking creation of standard guidelines for environment clearances for projects passing through protected areas like national parks and wildlife sanctuaries . <p> The move is expected to also avoid confrontation between the Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change ( MoEFCC ) and the Ministry of Road , Transport and Highways ( MoRTH ) as in the past such differences have landed them in court , thus further delaying the projects . <p> The environment ministry will now come out with standard procedures to be followed while any road and highway project comes for green clearance . This is expected to cut down the time taken for project clearances by many months and would also save cost overrun . <p> This comes after the environment ministry has taken several measures including online environment and forest clearance to cut down the delay in clearance of important infrastructure project @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ various stages so that delay in green clearance is not blamed for hampering country 's growth . <p> Having standard guidelines is also important for NHAI as road connectivity in remote areas is one of the key focus areas for Narendra Modi led government . It will help the MoRTH achieving its goal of building 100 km of highway per day in next few years against around 15 km per day right now . <p> " There have been cases in the past where both the ministries were at loggerheads over green clearances . Now , NHAI has approached us that we provide them with a template like set of rules which they need to meet for getting environment , forest and wildlife clearance for projects passing through tiger reserves , elephant corridor and other protected sites , " said a senior environment ministry official . <p> The official , however , said that it does n't mean that projects would be cleared without any assessment or inspection . The guidelines will have general details about compensatory afforestation , rehabilitation and resettlement of displaced people and others . <p> " These @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ after assessment more conditions can be put based on the requirement .
