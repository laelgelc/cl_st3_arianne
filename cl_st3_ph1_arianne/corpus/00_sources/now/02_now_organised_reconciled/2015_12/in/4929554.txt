<h> Extinction of large animals could make climate change worse <p> The extinction of large animals from tropical forests could make climate change worse ? according to researchers at the University of East Anglia . <p> New research published today in Science Advances reveals that a decline in fruit-eating animals such as large primates , tapirs and toucans could have a knock-on effect for tree species . <p> This is because large animals disperse large seeded plant species often associated with large trees and high wood density - which are more effective at capturing and storing carbon dioxide from the atmosphere than smaller trees . <p> Seed dispersal by large-bodied vertebrates is via the ingestion of viable seeds that pass through the digestive tract intact . <p> Removing large animals from the ecosystem upsets the natural balance and leads to a loss of heavy-wooded large trees , which means that less CO2 can be locked away . <p> The study was led by researchers from S ? o Paulo State University in Brazil , in collaboration with UEA , the Spanish National Research Council ( CSIC ) and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Peres , from UEA 's School of Environmental Sciences , says , ' ' Large birds and mammals provide almost all the seed dispersal services for large-seeded plants .
