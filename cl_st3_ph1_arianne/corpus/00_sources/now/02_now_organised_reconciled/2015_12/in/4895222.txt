<h> Inter-faith prayer meet held for success of tackling climate change <p> While the UN Climate Summit is deciding the future of earth in Paris , US-based NGO EcoSikh organized an inter-faith prayer event on ' Punjab Calling for Climate Justice ' at Bhai Vir Singh Niwas Sthan in Amritsar on Tuesday . <p> TNN Dec 9 , 2015 , 12.59 PM IST <p> Amritsar : While the UN Climate Summit is deciding the future of earth in Paris , US-based NGO EcoSikh organized an inter-faith prayer event on ' Punjab Calling for Climate Justice ' at Bhai Vir Singh Niwas Sthan in Amritsar on Tuesday . People from different spheres , including leaders of all faiths , intellectuals , environmentalists , industrialists , young artistes and school students , actively voiced their concern for earth 's sustainability . <p> The event saw youth delegates from other parts of Punjab and Chandigarh , such as Young Flares of Ludhiana , Farz , MUN ( Model United Nations ) take part . <p> The venue , a green oasis in the city , was vibrant with Buddhist chants , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , verses from Quran , gospels from the Bible and Jain prayers . <p> A common inter-faith prayer was adopted and read by religious leaders together , which said , " We the faith leaders , congregated in the holy city of Amritsar , offer our prayers in unison to Almighty , seeking inspiration for humanity across continents to respect nature and its innumerable gifts to living beings , and blessings for mankind to learn to live in harmony with God 's creation . " <p> Talking about the issue , project manager of EcoSikh Ravneet Singh said , " It 's high time that people and government take meaningful steps to mitigate the effects of climate change . Punjab and rest of India are vulnerable to the effects of climate change . There has to be greater emphasis on finding substitutes for fossil fuels to cut carbon and find alternative sources for India 's energy needs . Punjab , with a mere 4% forest cover , lack of land use policy plus liberal use of water resources , along with climate change , is facing rapid desertification of its land @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and worrisome .
