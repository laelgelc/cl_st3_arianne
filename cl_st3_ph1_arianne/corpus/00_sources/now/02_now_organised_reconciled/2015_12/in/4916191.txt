<h> Indian agency partner with premier organizations for better prediction of weather and climate <p> The Union cabinet on Wednesday approved signing of a ' Consortium Agreement ' by the India 's Earth System Science Organization-Ministry of Earth Sciences ( ESSO-MoES ) with the three other premier international meteorological organizations for the seamless prediction of weather and climate . <p> TNN Dec 16 , 2015 , 10.06 PM IST <p> NEW DELHI : The Union cabinet on Wednesday approved signing of a ' Consortium Agreement ' by the India 's Earth System Science Organization-Ministry of Earth Sciences ( ESSO-MoES ) with the three other premier international meteorological organizations for the seamless prediction of weather and climate . <p> Indian agency will , accordingly , sign the Consortium Agreement with the UK Met Office ( UKMO ) , Korea Meteorological Administration ( KMA ) and the Commonwealth of Australia through its Bureau of Meteorology and the Commonwealth Scientific Industrial and Research Organization ( CSIRO ) for a coordinated effort in the development of a state-of-art Unified Model ( UM ) Earth System . <p> The MOU is likely to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ become the fourth core UM partner and contribute to its technical and scientific development . <p> Under the agreement , core partners will work under a common governance structure , deciding on strategy for planning , designing of joint development programs with prioritized tasks and mobilization of resources . <p> India 's ministry of earth science ( MoES ) will make an annual member contribution of ? 100,000 to the Unified Model ( UM ) Consortium . <p> " Indian scientific and academic community will benefit from this MoU as it is an enabling mechanism to jointly work on scientific challenges in the field of earth system modeling and weather and climate prediction " , said an official statement . <p> It said , " The MoU will allow the exchange of scientific resources as well as knowledge to support the improvement and development of earth system modeling for weather and climate prediction over Indian monsoon region in particular " . <p> The ESSO-MoES provides weather and climate forecasts in various spatial and temporal scales .
