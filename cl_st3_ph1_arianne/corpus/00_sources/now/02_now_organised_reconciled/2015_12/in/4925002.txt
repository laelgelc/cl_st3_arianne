<p> Jammu , December 19 ( Scoop News ) -A gala progrrame jointly organized by the Departments of Botany and Environmental Sciences , University of Jammu marked the successful completion of the green campaign " Tree Talk " . B. R. Sharma said the initiative becomes more important when the whole world is seriously deliberating on the climate change .
