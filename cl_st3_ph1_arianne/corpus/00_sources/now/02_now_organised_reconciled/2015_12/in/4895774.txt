If , however , India does not agree with the rest of the world to consider the outcome to be worthwhile , the possibility of a global deal will quickly collapse . <p> This was observed at a conference here on the sidelines of COP 21 on Monday evening . <p> Three NGOS based in Brussels -- South Asia Democratic Forum ( SADF ) , Europe India Chamber of Commerce ( EICC ) , and Eurochambres -- brought
