<h> Govt proposes to set up water regulatory authority <p> PANAJI The government has proposed setting up of an independent water regulatory authority and amendments to the Goa Ground Water Regulation Act , 2002 and Goa Irrigation Act , 1973 to manage and regulate utilisation of water resources in the state . In an earnest attempt to conserve , protect and regulate the most precious natural resource , the state government on Wednesday put the draft of the state water policy in the public domain for suggestions and recommendations . The draft policy has stressed on the need for strong regulatory mechanisms and proposed numerous systems towards judicious and monitored supply and utilisation of the available water resources . Groundwater being the most essential component for supply of potable water , the draft policy has laid emphasis and sought amendments to the Goa Ground Water Regulation Act , 2002 for regulation of groundwater resources and Goa Irrigation Act , 1973 for regulation of surface water resources . Over-withdrawal of groundwater should be minimised by regulating the use of electricity . Separate electric feeders for irrigation and commercial ventures maybe resorted to . It has also called for stringent action against violators of the groundwater act , mandating registration of all transportation carriers and issuance of pass to tankers by groundwater officers . The officers should carry out surprise inspections periodically and take immediate cognisance of the complaints and carry out raids on illegal transportations of groundwater . The police and the RTO will be instructed to confiscate tankers carrying water which do not carry the valid pass . Amongst the other major measures , the draft policy has proposed to set up state water informatics centre under the water resources department to collect , collate and process all the hydrological data from the various sources . And for the purpose of research an autonomous centre has been proposed to evolve policy directives for changing the water resources scenario . The water resources department in the draft policy has sought regulation of urban settlements , encroachments and developmental activities in the protected upstream areas of reservoirs and water bodies that pose a potential threat of contamination , pollution and reduced recharge . Encroachments on all water bodies , including rivers , lakes , nallahs , etc should be removed and discouraged . Addressing concerns of ' water pollution ' , the policy has envisaged immediate action against contamination of groundwater as per Goa Groundwater Regulation Act . The WRD has set its priority on providing potable water to all the citizens of the state , within easy reach of each household . Further , the policy has proposed to sensitise and encourage communities to utilise water as per local availability along with rejuvenation of village wells . Regarding permissions for septic tanks , the policy has proposed that all cases with a well , pond or nallah within 100 metre of a building should be referred to groundwater officer for comments , who will have to respond within a period of 30 days . The policy , which has proposed discouraging reclamation of water bodies , has said that leakages in distribution lines will be reduced . Emphasis will be laid on rooftop and other rainwater harvesting schemes and recycling of water for non-consumptive uses will be encouraged , it has assured .
