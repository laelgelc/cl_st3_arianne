My 2015 Predictions . <p> In preparation for my predictions for 2016 , I thought I should honor my loyal readers by going back and doing what critics say pundits never do : seeing how well I did on my predictions for 2015 , published in this space on Dec. 31 of last year . To my surprise , some of them came true . Alas , not necessarily the happy ones . <p> Here are those predictions from a year ago and my evaluations of them a year later . <p> 1 .
