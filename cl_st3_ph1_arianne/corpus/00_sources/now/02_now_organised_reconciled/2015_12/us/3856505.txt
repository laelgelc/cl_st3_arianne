<p> As Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi exhorts global leaders to embrace cleaner energy , he 's facing his own challenges in doing so at home . <p> Hydropower projects that would boost the country 's capacity by almost half are held up because of local opposition and concern that they damage the environment , according to the power ministry 's Central Electricity Authority , the agency reviewing technical parameters of such projects . <p> The delays threaten Modi 's plan to shrink India 's dependence on fossil fuels , provide electricity to its 1.3 billion people -- about a fifth of whom lack access to power -- and clean up some of the world 's worst air .
