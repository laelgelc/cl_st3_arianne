<h> Charleston at the center of year 's top news events <p> For a small city , Charleston was at the center of the nation 's attention for much of 2015 . <p> Three of the year 's top 10 news events , according to an annual poll by The Associated Press , took place in part in Charleston or nearby : mass shootings , black deaths in encounters with police and the Emanuel AME Church killings . <p> Several of the others -- gay marriage , the 2016 presidential campaign and climate change -- were debated in the Holy City , as they were elsewhere . <p> The Emanuel AME shooting , in which nine black parishioners were killed at a Bible study , was a part of the year 's No. 4 story on mass shootings across the country . It also was singled out as the No. 9 story . <p> No. 5 included the killing of Walter Scott , a black man killed by a white North Charleston police officer .
