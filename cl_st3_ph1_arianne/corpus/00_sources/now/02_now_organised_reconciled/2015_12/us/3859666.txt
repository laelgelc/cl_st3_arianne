The Soviets had the bomb ; Europe was riddled with tripwires . Yugoslavia was a major concern . Its Communist government had broken with the Soviet bloc three years earlier and relations were worsening . Would the Soviets invade ? In March 1951 , information generated by American intelligence agencies was gathered and distilled . The result was National Intelligence Estimate 29-51 -- " Probability of an Invasion of Yugoslavia in 1951 " -- which concluded that a Soviet assault was " a serious possibility .
