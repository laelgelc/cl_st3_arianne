Samples collected by the Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution and citizen scientists were found to contain traces of cesium-134 in some coastal areas of Alaska , Oregon , Washington and California during recent months . It was also detected in samples taken farther offshore and off Canada 's Vancouver Island .
