<h> The Unsung Heroes of Climate Change <p> State and regional governments will be going to the COP21 negotiations in the French capital determined , ambitious and optimistic . <p> Determined as they know they can , and must , continue to drive climate action post Paris . This determination grows from many years of early climate action that is focused around the needs of local citizens and business . The Climate Group 's States &amp; Regions Alliance is an example of this . <p> From Ontario in Canada to Gujarat in India - we 've been convening state and regional government leaders from around the world in a powerful , high-profile network for over 10 years , with more determined leaders continuing to join us . <p> They are ambitious because they understand that bold leadership is necessary to tackle our collective climate challenge . These governments have been pioneering climate ambition and pushing national governments to go further long before this COP .
