<h> IOC Latest News <h> Climate Summit Puts Spotlight on Sustainable Future of Olympic Cities <p> IOC President Thomas Bach highlighted that the Olympic Games can be a catalyst for the sustainable development of cities . <p> Addressing 1000 mayors from around the globe attending the 21st United Nations Climate Change Conference ( COP 21 ) in Paris , he highlighted the sustainability reforms of Olympic Agenda 2020 and stressed that the experience of past Olympic host cities demonstrate that it is possible to deliver successful Games while minimising environmental impact . <p> The IOC President told the conference that for the Olympic Games 2024 the host city will have to implement a carbon management and reduction plan . <p> The plan comes in top of sustainable work already done by previous host cities . <p> " The Olympic Games London 2012 saved an equivalent of 400,000 tonnes of carbon dioxide , mainly in construction and operational activities , " he said .
