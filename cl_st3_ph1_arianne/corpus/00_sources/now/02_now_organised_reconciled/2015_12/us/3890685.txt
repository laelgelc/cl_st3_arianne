And the biologically rich Monterey Bay is more at risk than most bodies of water . <p> The reason is that the California Current , spanning the west coast of North America , is acidifying twice as fast as the rest of the world 's oceans . Globally , the oceans are already 30 percent more acidic than they were 200 years ago . By the end of the century , scientists say , they are expected to be 150 percent more acidic .
