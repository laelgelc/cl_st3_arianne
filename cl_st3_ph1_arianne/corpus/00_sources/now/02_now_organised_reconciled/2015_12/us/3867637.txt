<p> WASHINGTON -- Rising sea levels due to climate change threaten the Statue of Liberty and it would cost $1.5 billion to replace the landmark , according to the U.S. Interior Department . <p> Reports by the agency 's inspector general and the National Park Service said that $40 billion in national parks , historic sites , and monuments are endangered as water levels rise along with increased temperatures . <p> " Coastal communities in New Jersey and around the country and world are in danger due to climate change , " said U.S. Rep. Frank Pallone Jr . ( D-6th Dist. ) , the ranking Democrat on the House Energy and Commerce Committee . " The specific threat to Sandy Hook in the Interior Department 's report brings home the issue of damage to our climate and sea level rise . "
