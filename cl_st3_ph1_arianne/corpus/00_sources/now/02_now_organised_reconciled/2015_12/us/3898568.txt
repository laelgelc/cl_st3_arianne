<p> In the wake of the Paris Climate Agreement , it is pretty shocking to see these photographs taken in the Shanxi province in Northern China . Shanxi is the leading producer of coal in the most populated country in the world , with about 260 billion metric tons of coal deposits , a third of China 's total . The region produces more than 300 million metric tones of coal annually , and heavily depends on coal mining and burning coal for energy . That makes Shanxi is one of the most polluted areas in China . <p> Over the weekend 195 countries approved an agreement to set targets on reducing carbon emissions -- the primary cause of global warming -- in an attempt to forge a new global agreement on climate change . China is the source of nearly a third of the world 's total carbon dioxide emissions . China 's government has publicly set 2030 as a deadline to reach the country 's emissions peak , and data suggest its coal consumption is already in decline .
