It 's still teeming with life . Its dense forests supports abundant prey species , and it contains the last viable populations of Sumatran tigers . In the Leuser , orangutans number in the thousands . Great herds of Sumatran elephants can still be found within its lowland forests , alongside rhinos , sun bears , clouded leopards , hundreds of bird species , and many more . It is among the most biodiverse and ancient ecosystems ever documented by science .
