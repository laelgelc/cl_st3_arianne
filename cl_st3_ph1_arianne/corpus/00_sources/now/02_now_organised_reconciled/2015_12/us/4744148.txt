Ocean conservation is one of the UN 's new sustainable development goals . These goals set the UN 's agenda for the next 15 years , and it was n't clear the ocean would make the cut , but ( voila ! ) Goal 14 is to " Conserve and sustainably use the oceans , seas and marine resources . " Specific targets include , by 2020 , conserving 10% of the ocean ( but see #1 above for how far we have to go and whether 10% is even enough ) , halting overfishing and illegal fishing , and ending the subsidies that encourage them .
