<h> Research finds long term effect of natural gas leakage <h> December 2 , 2015 <p> As natural gas production increases around the nation , new research from the University of Colorado Denver shows that it can have a similar impact on climate change as coal if it 's allowed to leak while producing electricity . <p> " Reducing greenhouse gas emissions is one of the grand challenges of our time , " said David Mays , PE , PhD , associate professor of civil engineering at CU Denver , the premier research institution in Denver . " A large fraction of greenhouse gas emissions come from electrical generating plants , which are largely fueled by coal . Natural gas has the potential to produce electricity with about half the greenhouse gas emissions of coal . " <p> Recent advances in hydraulic fracturing and drilling have increased natural gas production . But between 70 and 90 percent of that gas is methane , a potent greenhouse gas .
