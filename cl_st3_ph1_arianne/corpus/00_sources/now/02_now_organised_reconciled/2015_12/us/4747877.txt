<p> Perhaps the most popular pet on Earth , the family cat is a beloved member of countless households . Millions of others abandoned or strayed are flourishing independently outdoors , where they may pose serious threats to birds and other small animals . But as familiar as the house cat is , not many people know it has 38 truly wild relatives , distinct species that include not only the iconic " King of the Jungle , " the lion , and the world 's largest predator , the tiger , but also obscure felids like the flat-headed cat , fishing cat , and oncilla .
