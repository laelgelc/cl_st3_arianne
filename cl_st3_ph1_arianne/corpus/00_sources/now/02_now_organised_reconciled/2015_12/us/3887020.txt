<h> The Worst of Times <p> In some ways , these are among the worst of times for President Barack Obama . <p> Known for being unflappable and upbeat , he has recently seemed sour , disgruntled and short-tempered with his political adversaries and the news media . His mood has worsened , advisers say , since the lethal rampage by two people with ties to Islamic extremism in San Bernardino , Calif. earlier this month . <p> Obama is facing the ultimate frustration for any president : seeing serious problems recur again and again , and being unable to do much about them . That 's what Obama faces in dealing with gun violence , which has grown as a national embarrassment and which the president has been powerless to fix , partly because he ca n't get agreement or compromise on Capitol Hill .
