<h> The universal agenda of climate justice , from Seychelles to the US <p> As the United Nations climate summit begins in Paris , it 's an important moment for all of us to reflect on and consider the roles we must play in the negotiations that will foster progress , sustainable growth and development towards a globally sustainable economy . <p> The threat of climate change has never been more urgent and we need strong U.S. leadership if we want to achieve ambitious global action in Paris . Genuine sustainable action on climate change requires a partnership between all people and our planet -- it 's crucial for our social , economic and environmental future . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> As a representative of a Small Island Developing State , the Republic of Seychelles , I know too well the threats and impacts of climate change and climate variability on our infrastructure , agriculture , energy and water security , biodiversity , and human well-being .
