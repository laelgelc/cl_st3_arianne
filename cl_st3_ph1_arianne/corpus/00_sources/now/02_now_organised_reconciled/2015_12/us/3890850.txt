" <p> The agreement also adopts a more ambitious target for limiting global warming : " Holding the increase in the global average temperature to well below 2 degrees C above pre-industrial levels and to pursue efforts to limit the temperature increase to 1.5 degrees C above pre-industrial levels ... " <p> The accord also calls for a regular gathering every five years of nations to assess the carbon contribution of all parties .
