<h> Majority of Americans want strong leadership on climate change <p> Most Americans believe climate change is real and want the U.S. to take the lead in fighting it , according to a set of HuffPost/YouGov surveys taken during the run-up to the Paris climate summit , a meeting of more than 100 world leaders to discuss taking action against climate change . <p> A 52 percent majority of Americans think the U.S. should take a global leadership role in trying to prevent climate change , while 26 percent think it should not , with the rest unsure . <p> Just 28 percent , though , believe the U.S. has so far done more than most other countries to address climate change , with a third saying the country 's record is about average , and 19 percent that it 's done less than other nations . Nearly half -- 46 percent -- want the next president to do more than President Barack Obama has to combat global warming , while just 19 percent want the next president to do less . <p> Of those who believe the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ think efforts to deal with the issue are most effective at the international level . Sixty-three percent of adults under 30 think the next president should do more to cut planet-warming emissions , compared with just 31 percent of those over age 65 .
