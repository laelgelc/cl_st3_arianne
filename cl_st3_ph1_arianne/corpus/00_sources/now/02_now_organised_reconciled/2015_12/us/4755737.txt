<h> Marin Voice : Some resolutions for the start of a new year <p> * Candidates for U.S. president -- Democrats and Republicans -- will make a concerted effort to focus on policy rather than personality as we approach what some believe to be the make-or-break Iowa and New Hampshire primaries . One leading GOP contender in particular has set an all-time low in ethical conduct , which has debased the entire purpose for having campaigns , disowned all responsibility for his false and inflammatory accusations , demeaned entire religions and cultures , appeals to the basest elements in human nature , and has insulted the intelligence of voters across the country . His name is Donald Trump . <p> * Sensible leaders of the GOP -- one of our two great political parties -- who have the nation 's best interests at heart , should move to denounce such behavior if it continues which makes a mockery of all the values for which the country historically stands .
