<p> On this weekend 's broadcast of " The McLaughlin Group , " the show 's panelist gave offered who they thought were the " Most Boring " and " Most Charismatic " of 2015 in the first installment of the program 's annual year-end awards . <p> Among the recipients for " Most Boring " were the Paris climate change summit held earlier this month according to conservative commentator Pat Buchanan and cat videos according to show host John McLaughlin , who argued the time had past for the novelty of cat videos . <p> That led The Daily Beast 's Eleanor Clift to voice her protest over those two mentions for " Most Boring . " <p> " For the record , I have to say climate change and cats are two of the most important things in my life , " Clift said . " So guys , tread carefully . "
