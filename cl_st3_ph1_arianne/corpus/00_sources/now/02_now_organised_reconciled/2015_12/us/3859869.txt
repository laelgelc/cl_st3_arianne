<p> The state has stopped giving the public any information about rail shipments of crude oil through the state , citing a law that went into effect in October that prohibits public disclosure of items transported by a railroad . <p> The law adds rail commodities to the list of state records that are exempt from the Freedom of Access Act . The Legislature passed the bill , LD 484 , in June after overturning a veto by Gov . Paul LePage . <p> But the impact of the law is uncertain since virtually all oil shipments by rail have ceased over the last year because of changing market forces . Refineries in Canada -- the destination of oil transported via rail through Maine -- are getting their crude shipments elsewhere and no longer need Maine rail connections . <p> The Maine Department of Environmental Protection tracks oil shipments by rail through the state and for years has reported the data monthly .
