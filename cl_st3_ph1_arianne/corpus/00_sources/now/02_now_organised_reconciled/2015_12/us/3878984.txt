For the medical sector , the main unit is the sievert , a measure of the probability of cancer or genetic damage resulting from a quantity of ionizing radiation . One sievert is equal to 1 joule per kilogram of absorbed energy , though a millisievert ( mSv ) , or one one-thousandth of a sievert , is the more practical unit . <p> More than 100 mSv of radiation exposure each year leads to a statistically significant increase in cancer risk . Around 500 mSv , you start to see symptoms of radiation poisoning . <p> On average , an American receives a radiation dose of 3.1 mSv each year from natural sources , according to the NRC . A chest CT scan results in a radiation dose of 7 mSv . <p> NRC requires its licensees to limit radiation emissions to 1 mSv above background radiation each year , and the annual limit for nuclear workers is 50 mSv .
