<h> Community members rally against the use of fossil fuels <p> Dozens of people in San Luis Obispo are speaking out against the use of fossil fuels , in honor of the Global Day for Climate Action . <p> Close to 100 San Luis Obispo community members rallied at the city 's train station on Saturday , as the climate change deal was made in Paris . <p> The purpose of the rally was to oppose several proposed crude oil projects in the county such as the Phillips 66 rail project . <p> The company wants to build a rail line that can deliver crude to its refinery in Santa Maria . <p> " We want clean , renewable energy . And that is what our county , city , state and our world need to focus on . It 's our responsibility , we owe it to the future , " Natalie Risner , Arroyo Grande resident , said during the rally . <p> In an effort to the combat climate issue , San Luis Obispo has taken several measures .
