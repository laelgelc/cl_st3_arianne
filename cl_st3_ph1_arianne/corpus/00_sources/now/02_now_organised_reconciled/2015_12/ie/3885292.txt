Green your commute . <p> Transport still consumes more energy than that used for either heat ( space and process ) or electricity generation and it is almost exclusively dependent on imported oil . <p> The transport sector is responsible for the largest share of energy-related CO2 emissions of any sector of the economy , accounting for 35 per cent ( 12.6 MtCO2 - metric tonnes of carbon dioxide ) in 2013 .
