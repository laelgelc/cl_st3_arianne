The E-Mehari offers a wide range of possibilities for personalisation with four body colours : blue , as an invitation to get away from it all , orange to convey a sense of energy and optimism , an invigorating yellow and an elegant beige , two roof colours : black and orange-red and two interior trim colours : a natural-looking beige and a high-tech orange-red with a central pattern inspired by water sports .
