<h> Climate change plan ' historic turning point ' if adopted <p> Organisers of the climate talks in Paris have released details of a proposed landmark deal to curb climate change after two weeks of talks . <p> Nearly 200 countries are attempting to strike the first climate deal to commit all countries to cut emissions . <p> France 's Foreign Minister Laurent Fabius has said the final draft of the deal is fair , legally binding and would limit warming to well below 2 degrees celcius . <p> The accord would come into being in 2020 . <p> French foreign minister Laurent Fabius , who 's leading the talks , has said , if adopted , it would be an " historic turning point " : " It confirms our key objective , the objective which is vital , that continuing to have a mean temperature well below two degrees and to endeavour to limit that increase to 1.5 degrees . "
