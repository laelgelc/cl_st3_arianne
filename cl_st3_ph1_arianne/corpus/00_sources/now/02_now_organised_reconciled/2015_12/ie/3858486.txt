<h> China aims to cut emissions in the power sector by 60 per cent within five years <p> China 's special representative on climate change Xie Zhenhua has described the country 's commitments to cut emissions as " specific , powerful and pragmatic " . <p> Just days after air pollution in Chinese cities went off the charts and enveloped the capital Beijing in foul smog , China 's cabinet , the State Council , made a number of wide-ranging commitments to cut emissions of major pollutants in the power sector by 60 per cent by 2020 . <p> Those include a pledge to upgrade coal-fired power plants to save around 100 million tons of raw coal and reduce carbon dioxide emissions by 180 million tons annually . <p> The cuts will be made through improved efficiency . <p> By 2020 , China will
