<h> Leaders hail Paris climate change deal <p> German Chancellor Angela Merkel has applauded the global pact to fight climate change that was adopted by nearly 200 nations in Paris . <p> Merkel said in a statement that the climate agreement marks " the first time that the entire world community has obligated itself to act , to act in the battle against global climate change " . <p> The German chancellor said while there was still a lot of work ahead , the deal is a " sign of hope that we will manage to secure the life conditions of billions of people for the future . " <p> The Paris agreement aims to keep global warming from rising another degree Celsius between now and 2100 , a key demand of poor countries ravaged by rising sea levels and other effects of climate change . <p> Israel 's prime minister also welcomed the agreement . <p> At his weekly Cabinet meeting Benjamin Netanyahu called the deal " important " . <p> He said Israel has an interest , like other countries , in slowing down global warming @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Protection Minister Avi Gabbay said a budget of about $200m has been devoted to meet climate targets . <p> He said Israel would move toward renewable energy sources , cleaner technology and more public transport .
