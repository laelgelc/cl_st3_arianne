<p> Barely half of Irish people ( 49 per cent ) believe that climate change is a serious problem according to a Sustainable Energy Authority of Ireland ( SEAI ) survey . <p> But over half of Irish citizens ( 53 per cent ) believe that they personally have a role to play in tackling climate change , putting themselves ahead of businesses , environmental groups and local authorities . <p> The survey finds that the general public significantly underestimates the contribution of the residential sector to energy-related emissions , while overestimating the contribution of industry . <p> Seven out of 10 respondents say energy is an important consideration when buying a house or car while six out of 10 say it is important for kitchen appliances and lights . <p> There was a clear preference to develop the use of renewables ( 77 per cent ) followed by promoting advanced research in new energy technologies ( 53 per cent ) . <p> Speaking today Dr Eimear Cotter , Head of Low Carbon Technologies at SEAI said , " What this research suggests however is that we still @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of climate change . <p> " There has been a massive jump in children 's perceived knowledge of saving energy ( 68 per cent ) and the influence which they have on family attitudes ( 61 per cent ) .
