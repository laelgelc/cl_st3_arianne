We all know chaos is no fun , yet stability is n't exactly a laugh a minute either . Firstly , the State is not really a stable system -- it is just a permanent state of crisis management , a perpetual-motion fire engine always rushing from blaze to blaze . It 's not that we do n't have chaos , just that when chaos is chronic everybody gets used to it . <p> In the past three weeks alone we 've had authoritative statements that three of the biggest parts of the State machinery are chronically dysfunctional . The most immediate form of stability in anyone 's life is a home , and it is just not available to growing numbers of people .
