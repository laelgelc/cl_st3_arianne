<h> Council plea on electric car costs <p> CORK County Council is calling on the ESB to reverse a decision to charge motorists for ' topping up ' electric cars . <p> The local authority passed a motion condemning the decision by the ESB to introduce charges for electric cars from next month . Electric car owners can currently charge their vehicles for free , but the ESB is to introduce a pricing structure from January 1 . The Irish Electric Vehicles Owners Association said that once all the planned charges are introduced , electric cars could be as expensive to run as diesel ones . From next month , all new electric car owners will be charged a monthly fee of ? 16.99 to get access to 800 standard charging points . The charges will be introduced for existing electric car owners in April . On top of the standard rates , electric car owners will be charged 30 cent per minute for accessing the 70 ' fast chargers ' across the country .
