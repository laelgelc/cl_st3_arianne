<h> Glas II application deadline extended by a week <p> The Department of Agriculture has extended the GLAS II deadline to 14 December 2015 . <p> More time to apply for GLAS . <p> The Department of Agriculture has extended the GLAS II deadline to 14 December 2015 . <p> Pressure had been growing on the Department to extend the GLAS II deadline past the original deadline of 7 December to due to reports of technical difficulties with the Department 's online planning system . This left planners struggling to cope with the volume of applications . Therefore the Department decided to extend the application deadline by a week to 14 December . <p> Some 14,000 applications have been made to the second tranche of the Green Low-carbon Agri Environment Scheme ( GLAS II ) . About 35% of those applicants have been tier three farmers . <p> Tier three is the lowest priority level of the scheme and includes farmers who take general actions to enhance the climate change , water quality and biodiversity benefits of their farm .
