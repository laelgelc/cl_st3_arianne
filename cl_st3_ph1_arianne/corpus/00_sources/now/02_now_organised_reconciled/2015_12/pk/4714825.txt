<h> UN climate talks answer to ' call of history ' <p> PARIS -- World leaders from around the planet on Monday launched a five-day scramble in Paris to answer " the call of history " and strike a deal to spare mankind from climate disasters . <p> The UN climate change conference has been billed as the last chance to avert the worst consequences of global warming , deadly drought , floods and storms , and rising sea-level that will obliterate islands and densely populated coastlines . <p> The talks kicked off at the Paris-Le Bourget site on last Monday in the north-east of the French capital and today the second week of negotiations begins with the aim of reaching a new universal climate agreement to limit global temperature rise to below two degree Celsius . <p> Speaking on the occasion , the UN Secretary General Ban Ki-moon said that a week ago , 150 world leaders stood here and pledged their full support for a robust global climate agreement that was equal to the test we were facing . <p> " Never before has so many @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with one common purpose , " he said . <p> " The clock is ticking towards a climate catastrophe , " he said , adding that the world was expecting more from them than half-measures and incremental approaches .
