Moreover , Army Chief Gen Raheel Sharif and Advisor to the PM on Foreign Affairs Sartaj Aziz are also likely to visit Kabul soon . <p> Arguably , Pakistan and Afghanistan mostly share common economic interests and are facing the same security challenges in the region .
