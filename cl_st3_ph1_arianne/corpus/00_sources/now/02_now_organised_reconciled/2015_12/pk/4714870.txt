<h> Kabul must formally request Islambad for second round of peace talks : Pakistan <p> NEW YORK : Permanent Representative of Pakistan to the United Nations Dr Maleeha Lodhi said Thursday that Pakistan was ready to host another round of Afghan peace talks . <p> " But we would like to see the anti-Pakistan rhetoric coming from Kabul to cease , " she told reporters , and added that for the second round of peace talks , Afghan government should formally request Pakistan . <p> Maleeha said there was also a need to create an atmosphere to build a greater trust between the two countries . This was the intent behind the Prime Minister Nawaz Sharif 's meeting with Afghan President Ashraf Ghani on the sidelines of United Nations Climate Change Summit in Paris , she added . <p> Maleeha said Pakistan desired to see peace in the neighbourhood , which was reiterated by Nawaz Sharif to Ashraf Ghani in their meeting . <p> Dr Lodhi said the annual resolution adopted by the United Nations General Assembly was an opportunity to debate the revival of peace talks and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ General Assembly , she had presented Pakistan 's viewpoint emphasizing the need for revival of peace talks that began in Murree , where Pakistan hosted first round of the talks . <p> However , she said the importance of the revival of peace talks was underscored by the deteriorating security situation in Afghanistan . She said both Afghan government as well as Afghan Taliban should follow the international consensus . " The international consensus today is in favour of the negotiated peace , " she remarked . <p> Dr Lodhi said the point she had made at the UNGA was that there were two parts to Afghan peace ; one is imposition of military solution and the other is negotiated peace . Pakistan also condemned the recent wave of violence in Afghanistan killing innocent people including women and children . Therefore , Dr Lodhi said , Pakistan desired to see both Afghan government and Afghan Taliban to negotiate . She reiterated that Pakistan was ready to extend its assistance in this regard .
