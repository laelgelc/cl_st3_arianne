AFP <p> British musician Sting has been awarded a Global Citizen Prize for working to protect the Amazon rainforest and its much-threatened indigenous inhabitants . <p> The award went to the 17-time Grammy-award singer-songwriter to salute his efforts with the Rainforest Fund , a charitable foundation that Sting , 68 , co-founded with his wife , Trudie Styler , in 1989 to support indigenous people . <p> And it followed two members of Brazil 's Guajajara tribe being shot dead at the weekend . <p> The attack took place not far from where Paulo Paulino Guajajara , a prominent tribesman defending the forest , was shot in another confrontation with illegal loggers last month . <p> " Climate change and deforestation has pushed parts of the world 's rainforests dangerously close to a point of no return , but the work of organizations like the Rainforest Fund are actively pushing back , " international advocacy organization Global Citizen said . <p> The Rainforest Fund , the US-based Global Citizen noted , has protected more than 13 million hectares of rainforest . <p> Indigenous tribes in Brazil are facing escalating violence under the presidency of Jair Bolsonaro , who has encouraged the commercial exploitation of protected lands and in particular the destruction of the Amazon rainforest . <p> Following the latest deaths , Brazilian authorities claimed that an emergency security force would be sent to the indigenous reserve of Cana Brava in the northeastern state of Maran . <p> Global Citizen , which works to end extreme poverty , also awarded British film director Richard Curtis - known for romantic comedies Four Weddings and a Funeral and Notting Hill - for his charitable work and championing the United Nations ' global goals to end poverty . <p> " We are honored to celebrate and recognize these incredible individuals who place the world 's poor at the forefront of their work , " said Global Citizens chief executive Hugh Evans . <p> " Their efforts are a testament to how the worlds of policy , business , the arts and entertainment can make a positive impact to ending extreme poverty , tackling climate change and fighting inequality . " <p> Other award winners include Nigerian diplomat Amina Mohammed , deputy secretary general of the United Nations , and Hamdi Ulukaya , founder of Greek yoghurt brand Chobani for his work on improving refugee rights .
