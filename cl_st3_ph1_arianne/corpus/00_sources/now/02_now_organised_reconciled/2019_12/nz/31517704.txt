<h> Aggravated burglary of bottle store in Christchurch <p> Police were alerted to a ram-raid-style burglary of Henry 's Liquor store on Yaldhurst Road this morning , 29 May , at around 2.20am . <p> The offenders , driving a silver Nissan Primera , reversed through the glass front doors into the store , leaving the car running . <p> Two people got out of the Nissan and proceeded to take alcohol and remove it from the store . <p> A second dark-coloured sedan pulled up into the forecourt and the alchohol was loaded into the vehicle . <p> Three offenders left the scene in the dark-coloured sedan heading east along Yaldhurt Road . <p> When police arrived at the scene , the Nissan was still running , parked in the store . Police enquiries revealed that the Nissan was a stolen vehicle taken from a Riccarton address , around 4pm yesterday , 28 May . <p> The scene examination is currently in progress and Police are making further inquiries into the matter . <p> If anyone witnessed the burglary at around 2.20am this morning , or has any knowledge of the incident , they are encouraged to phone Christchurch Police on 03 363 7400 . <p> Alternatively , information may be provided anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 . <p> ENDS <p> JOURNALISTS TO NOTE : The CCTV from within Henry 's shows very limited grainy footage , due to lack of light so this wo n't be be released by Police . <h> Scoop Citizen Members and ScoopPro Organisations are the lifeblood of Scoop . <p> 20 years of independent publishing is a milestone , but your support is essential to keep Scoop thriving .
