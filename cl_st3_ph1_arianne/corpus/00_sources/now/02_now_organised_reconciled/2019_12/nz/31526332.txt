<p> New Zealand should be lauded for its climate leadership , but needs closer alignment with the Mori world view to build a sustainable path forward says Mori Climate Commissioner Donna Awatere Huata . <p> New Zealand should be lauded for its climate leadership , but needs closer alignment with the Maori world view to build a sustainable path forward says Maori Climate Commissioner Donna Awatere Huata . <p> Despite the failure of attending nations to reach agreement at COP 25 , just concluded in Madrid , Commissioner Awatere Huata says New Zealand 's leadership , and James Shaw 's role in particular , should be recognised . <p> " Unfortunately , New Zealand 's strong leadership role was overshadowed by the obstructive actions of several nations , whose contribution to the failure to reach agreement on Article 6 meant the talks were left in disarray , " says Commissioner Awatere Huata . <p> Article 6 of the Paris Agreement would create a global mechanism for trading carbon credits and provide incentives for carbon reduction activities . Countries including Australia and Brazil were seen as blocking progress on Article 6 as they sought to gain advantages for their own emissions accounting , while the US opposed efforts to compensate developing nations . <p> " While the world burns , indigenous peoples are being hardest hit by climate change , with the loss of traditional lands and resources -- often in the very countries which are working against the international community 's attempts to deal constructively with the climate crisis , " says Commissioner Awatere Huata . <p> " However , through our strong representation at COP 25 and the passage of the Zero Carbon Act , Aotearoa New Zealand is highlighting on the international stage what can be achieved in addressing the current climate crisis . " <p> Commissioner Awatere Huata says that leadership globally , must be backed by local action , and in particular with the Government 's commitment to working closely with Maori to develop genuine solutions for Aotearoa . <p> " As a country -- and as a leader on the world stage -- we can go further . And that way forward is to be found in the Maori world view . " <p> " We have long understood the importance of living and working in harmony with the natural world , and the concepts of whakapapa , mauri and utu should underpin the way all New Zealanders approach our environment . From these principles we can build an inclusive , all-encompassing approach to climate change that supports and empower everyone . "
