<h> Climates of interest <p> Over 5000 households have responded to the survey sent out by Tauranga MP Simon Bridges . " At over 5400 surveys returned in the Tauranga electorate , I 'm really humbled by the extremely high level of reply . I 'm told that this level of return in an electorate is one of the highest ever . <p> This is great because it means the results are more reflective of our community 's views and I will be able to place more weight on them , " says Simon . The survey reveals 48 per cent of respondents think the government is doing enough to help New Zealander 's in the current economic climate and 93 per cent support tougher sentences for violent and drug offenders . Around 59 per cent of respondents also believe human activity is contributing in some way to climate change and 69 per cent are happy with proposed changes to Resource Management Act . " Probably the most interesting result to me is on climate change . I am happy to say I agree with both these views .
