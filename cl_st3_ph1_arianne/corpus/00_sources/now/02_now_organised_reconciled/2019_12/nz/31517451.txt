<h> Breadcrumb navigation <h> Sustainable summer holiday waste tips <p> Ask yourself : how was it made , how long will it last , how much power will it use , what will I do with it once I 'm done ? <p> The holiday season can be overwhelming , so let us make it easy for you to minimise waste . Whether you 're headed to the beach or bach , or having the family round to your place , use these simple tips to avoid excess waste over the holidays . <p> Councillor Richard Hills , Chair of the Environment and Climate Change Committee , offers his support for thinking sustainably this holiday season . <p> " Over summer and the Christmas period , many of us will be at the beach , in the bush or down at our local park appreciating our natural environment . The best gift we can give is a healthy environment for all generations to enjoy . <p> " We can all do our bit over the holidays to protect our environment , by doing things like reducing our waste , recycling that Christmas packaging , reporting illegal dumping , stop flushing wet wipes and taking our rubbish home at the end of the day out . " <h> Last-minute zero-waste gift ideas <p> It can be so tempting to buy that new appliance or gadget , but it 's worth pausing a moment to question first whether you really need it , whether an existing item could do the job already , or whether second-hand is a better option . <p> Ask yourself : how was it made , how long will it last , how much power will it use , what will I do with it once I 'm done ? This is one of the easiest ways to save some serious money and prevent the waste of so many usable or fixable things you already have . <p> Councillor Pippa Coom recommends the Grey Lynn car boot market for the best unique holiday gifts . It 's on the Sunday before Christmas and the last Sunday of every month . Everything is second-hand , so you shop without growing your carbon footprint . <p> If you do go shopping , remember to bring your reusable bags , just like you would for groceries . You 'll be much happier carrying them than trying to juggle single-use ones . <p> Here are four sources of inspiration for your holiday gift-giving that do n't require you to make or bake anything . <p> Visit a community recycling centre <p> These local centres divert waste from landfill and sell these quality items , so they can find a new life in your home and community . The inventory is always changing but it can be a great place to get outdoor and camping equipment , BBQs , bicycles , furniture , books , jewellery , and so many more one-of-a-kind treasures . Most people simply drop by to find a surprise gem ; that 's half the fun ! Look for one in your neighbourhood and check their opening hours . <p> Join a toy library <p> Rather than buy a toy once , why not give your child a membership to one of Auckland 's many toy libraries . Toy Libraries offer a range of quality toys , puzzles , games and learning activities for members to hire for a set period of time . Toy Libraries are registered charitable organisations that are run by a group of volunteers . <p> Give a tree <p> The Kaip ? ? atiki project will plant a native tree for you in a reserve for only $10 . Your gift is helping the biodiversity of our local spaces and will continue to grow for many years to come . This way of gifting also eliminates any unnecessary wrapping , packaging and transportation . A win for you and our climate . <p> Give your time <p> Offer a night of free babysitting , some gardening , or another special service that helps reduce the summer stress . Or , offer an experience you can do together to create new memories and try something you might not otherwise have done . <h> Zero waste holiday parties <p> Ditch the balloons ! Put a big ribbon with a bow on your mailbox instead . Unlike a balloon it wo n't pop , and you can use it year after year . <p> Better party favours . Do n't waste time and money on goodie bags or extra stocking stuffers . Most of the " goodies " are plastic bits that end up in landfill . Instead , send your guests home with baked goods or leftovers . Or , if you need a party favour , consider giving everyone a tomato plant . <p> Avoid themed decorations . A lot of festive merchandise is single use , adds up to more spend than you planned for , and ends up in a landfill . Not to mention the labour and resources in the whole process . If you want a party theme , focus on a colour scheme and use materials you already have or can use again easily . <p> For kids games , consider re-usable options like fabric and ribbon for pass the parcel , instead of single-use paper that will be in the recycling bin faster than the kids have finished their lollies . You can also think about a simple activity that uses recycled materials , like making your own fidget spinners . <p> Love your leftovers . Here are seven simple ways to get a quick new meal everyone will like , using your holiday leftovers . Sausages and steaks from the barbeque make great tacos , pizza toppings , fried rice , or salad rolls . <h> Waste-free beach trips <p> To keep our public spaces beautiful , Auckland Council is sharing these litter-busting tips to encourage everyone to put litter in its place while out and about . <p> " It 's great when people think about their waste ahead of time whilst they 're planning their holiday trips to the beach or park . We encourage people to ' pack in and pack out ' , " says Parul Sood , General Manager for Waste Solutions . <p> " When you 're packing in , try to pack up your food and other items in reusable containers , wherever possible , and then remove any excess packaging before you leave the house . For the other items , take a couple of extra bags with you and make sure you take the sorted leftover waste home . " <p> If you do spot an overflowing public place litter bin , while you 're out and about around Auckland this summer , please call the Council on 09 301 0101 to report it , and arrangements will be made for a contractor to clear the bin . <h> Be responsible for your waste <p> There are a number of things you can do to reduce the amount of rubbish your household produces . <p> Dumping rubbish is illegal and may result in a fine or prosecution , which would really put a damper on the holiday cheer . If you see items left on the kerb or cul-de-sac , give us a call at 0800 NO DUMP ( 0800 663 867 ) .
