<h> It was the second-deadliest Atlantic hurricane of 2019 , but you probably ca n't name it <p> Hurricane Lorenzo was an unlikely killer forged in landless waters so far east it broke records for its remoteness , yet it was the second-deadliest tropical cyclone this year , stealing lives from the wide-open Atlantic to Florida 's east coast . <p> The National Hurricane Center 's reanalysis of Lorenzo released this month counted 19 direct deaths attributed to the storm , including 11 crew members of the French oceanic tugboat Bourbon Rhode , and eight people along the US East Coast . <p> Lorenzo was the lesser-known of the season 's two Category 5 hurricanes , bubbling to life less than a month after Dorian ravaged the Northern Bahamas with 185-mph winds . A November 29 update from the island nation 's National Emergency Management Agency said Dorian 's death toll stood at 70 with " more expected . " <p> It 's routine for NHC meteorologists to review every storm after the season ends November 30 with reports issued as the reviews are complete . <p> The opening summary in Lorenzo 's 22-page report highlights its Cat 5 muscle and the number of lives lost in a storm that never made landfall as a tropical cyclone . <p> But Lorenzo also was a climatological phenomenon unmatched in the eastern or central Atlantic - and one that raised climate change and forecasting concerns . <p> " Lorenzo 's 140-knot intensity is the highest for any hurricane east of 50-degrees West in the Atlantic hurricane database back to 1851 , " wrote NHC hurricane specialist David Zelinsky in the report . " Records for the eastern and central Atlantic are less reliable prior to the satellite era , but it is noteworthy that there are no hurricanes on record comparable to Lorenzo . " <p> Lorenzo put the 2019 hurricane season in the history books as the second Cat 5 of the year . Only six previous Atlantic hurricane seasons have ended with two or more Cat 5s - including 1932 , 1933 , 1961 , 2005 , 2007 and 2017 . <p> " The bottom line is that Category 5 storms are already rare , and Hurricane Lorenzo is unprecedented in the record-keeping era , " said Marshall Shepherd , a University of Georgia atmospheric sciences professor , in a post he wrote for Forbes . " We just do n't see hurricanes at this intensity so far east and north because water temperatures are typically too cold and wind shear conditions can also be restrictive . " <p> Its sudden strengthening surprised even veteran storm forecasters . <p> Weather models were predicting Lorenzo would form even before it left Africa as a tropical wave . It was just south of the Cabo Verde Islands when it became a tropical storm on Sept. 23 . <p> But errors in the intensity forecasts for Lorenzo were higher than the previous five-year average , the NHC report notes , especially in predicting its rapid intensification and a swift weakening . <p> At 10:10 pm on September 28 , the National Hurricane Center issued a special advisory noting Lorenzo was an " extremely dangerous Category 5 hurricane with winds reaching 160 mph . " <p> That was just 11 hours after forecasters had predicted a slow weakening of what was then a 115-mph , Category 3 storm . <p> " Lorenzo 's sudden strengthening took even veteran hurricane forecasters by surprise , " said Weather Underground meteorologist and writer Bob Henson in a column . <p> The NHC report says no models predicted that Lorenzo would strengthen more than 5 knots from its Cat 3 strength - a trend that continued with each subsequent model even as Lorenzo deepened . <p> Henson said Lorenzo took advantage of unusually warm water temperatures that were 1.8 degrees above normal . He called the rapid boost in wind speeds " remarkable " . <p> Lorenzo was also a large hurricane for much of its life , with tropical-storm-force winds extending 440km from its center and hurricane-force-winds reaching 80km from its center . <p> While the Bourbon Rhode sank near the eyewall of the storm on September 26 , Lorenzo 's eye was about 3380km east of Florida when it reached Cat 5 strength and 3060km from New York . <p> Still , a Vero Beach man drowned October 1 in high waves generated by Lorenzo ; a fisherman in Middletown , R.I. , died after falling into rough surf along the shore ; two teenagers died at Rockaway Beach , N.Y ; and four people drowned in North Carolina . <p> Lorenzo swept west of the Azores and reached Ireland as a post-tropical cyclone . No deaths were reported in Ireland but the Irish Coast Guard responded to several incidents , including a kite surfer who broke his leg , according to the NHC report . <p> " Lorenzo was impressive but since it did n't have that many impacts , people did n't pay much attention , " said Colorado State University researcher Phil Klotzbach in a November interview . " 2019 will be remembered as the year of Dorian . "
