<h> Global watchlist failure causes mega delays at Auckland Airport <p> A failure of boarding systems at Auckland Airport was caused by a global IT failure , Dileepa Fonseka reports <p> Queues at airports across the globe , including Auckland , were caused by the failure of an IT system used by countries to check passengers against terrorist watch-lists . <p> Passengers at airports in Australia and New Zealand were reported by Newshub as being unable to board or experiencing long check-in delays . <p> Nicola Hogg , general manager for Border and Visa operations at Immigration New Zealand said the delays were caused by a failure of an IT system run by France-based SITA . <p> " INZ has been advised by SITA that this is a global outage , affecting a number of countries . " <p> The system checks passenger names against watch-lists drawn up by intelligence agencies and law enforcement authorities like Interpol . <p> New Zealand Immigration uses the service as part of its Advance Passenger Processing system ( APP ) which checks passenger data at check-in counters to see if they are allowed to fly . <p> Without the system in place airlines around the world are unable to issue boarding passes via their electronic systems at check-in . <p> Auckland Airport said in a tweet that passengers could still be checked in manually but that process would cause " delays " . <p> In a statement , SITA 's media relations manager Brenda Flinter said a technical issue had led to its interactive passenger processing systems being disrupted in a number of countries , including Australia and New Zealand . <p> Flinter said the company was treating the problem with " the utmost urgency " , and had now been able to fully restore all services and have operations running as normal . <p> " We regret any impact this incident has had on our customers and passengers . Our priority remains , as always , to ensure a stable system so that our customers can conduct their business efficiently and effectively . " <p> SITA was carrying out a full investigation to discover the root cause of the outage , she said . <p> Two years ago , another outage of the system caused passenger delays across airports in Australia and New Zealand . <p> News website The Blue Swan reported delays at Wellington and Auckland airports at the time . <p> We value fearless , independent journalism .
