<h> Otago institutions work to create centre for rural health education <p> Three Otago institutions are teaming up to improve the future of rural health care . <p> The University of Otago , Otago Polytechnic and Central Otago Health Services have signed a memorandum of understanding on rural health care practice , service , education and research . <p> The organisations want to create a virtual centre for rural health education . <p> Central Otago Health Services chief executive , Kathryn de Luc , hopes exposing trainees to the rural environment for longer , will make it easier to recruit in future . <p> " So we are really trying to look at how the training is delivered and the education is delivered to see if we can make it more attractive . " <p> De Luc said attracting staff is very hard - with one position in a hospital 's radiology department sitting vacant for more than a year . <p> University of Otago Division of Health Sciences Pro-Vice-Chancellor , Professor Paul Brunton , said in a statement that the new relationship is exciting on its merits , but the future prospects it promises are of even greater interest . <p> " We believe that a lot more health professional education and research should be undertaken in rural areas and we see this memorandum as a logical step towards that greater goal . By its very nature this is a deeply collaborative exercise and our three organisations are formalising that collaboration now . " <p> The partners are looking to work in collaboration with other key stakeholders in rural health throughout the country undertaking education and research in the sector , Prof Brunton said . <p> There is already collaboration in the area of rural health in terms of clinical placements , rural health research , postgraduate education and vocational training and professional development activities . <p> It 's expected a newly-established task force made up of representatives from each organisation will identify future collaborations . <p> Otago Polytechnic Head of College for Nursing , Midwifery , Occupational Therapy and Sports Institute , Ian Crabtree , said the long-term goal of having a virtual health entity with wider connections across the rural health sector is both ambitious and forward-thinking . <p> Southern District Health Board chief executive Chris Fleming said it 's essential to have a workforce that can enable the Southern DHB , which covers the largest area of all DHBs , to serve its rural and remote communities . <p> " This is an excellent and very promising step in this direction that draws upon the great strengths in education and rural health care in our district , " he said . <p> This article was originally published on RNZ and re-published with permission . <p> We value fearless , independent journalism .
