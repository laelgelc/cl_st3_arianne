<h> Health and skincare firms head into NZX <p> Two companies involved in health , wellness , and skincare products businesses are headed on to the stock exchange through the backdoor . <p> Me Today and Good Brand Company will list on the local market through what is known as a reverse listing . <p> They are being bought by a listed shell company , China Scrap Metal ( CSM ) , for a total of $5.5 million . <p> The two businesses are controlled by entrepreneurs Grant Baker , Stephen Sinclair , and Michael Kerr , who were involved in the start-up of vodka maker 42 Below , and Ecoya 's acquisition of Trilogy . They also have a significant stake in the car sales and finance company , Turners . <p> " This will give us a really good opportunity to grow the brand and give the brands a profile and obtain early stage capital to expand what we 've got , " Sinclair said . <p> He said they already had sales arrangements with the listed pharmacy owning company , Green Cross Health , which had been generating sales . <p> Sinclair said the company would look to expand sales into Australia and Asia in time . <p> A backdoor listing on the local exchange was the best and logical place for them to get backing for what was a New Zealand brand and products , he said . <p> Shareholders in CSM will have a chance to buy shares in the enlarged company , which along with an issue to other new investors is expected to raise another $1m-$1.5m . <p> The deal is expected to be completed by April next year , after various approvals . The enlarged company will be worth more than $5.5m , and have about $4m available for expansion . <p> CSM will be renamed Me Today and the three backers of the deal will hold 60.8 percent of the shares . <p> This article was originally published on RNZ and re-published with permission . <p> We value fearless , independent journalism .
