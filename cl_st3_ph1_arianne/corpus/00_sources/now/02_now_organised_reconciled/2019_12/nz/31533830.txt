<h> 10 of 26 Central Library to Close <p> Ongoing assessment means Palmerston North 's Central Library will have to close its doors again . <p> While the assessment of the library building is nearing completion , the structural complexities of the project mean the Central Library needs to close on Sunday , August 26 for an uninterrupted day of work . <p> Debbie Duncan , General Manager - Community for the Palmerston North City Council says the assessment has been complicated by the design and age of the Central Library building . <p> " To give the assessment crew more time and a safe space to carry out further work , we 've decided to close the Central Library for a day , " Mrs Duncan says . <p> Assessment work closed the building for three days at the end of July . <p> Mrs Duncan says in addressing health and safety considerations , choosing a Sunday to carry out the work was the best option for the assessment crew , while minimising inconvenience for library visitors . <p> " It 's all part of the assessment process , but we are offsetting the impact on the community as much as possible . " <p> Alternative arrangements have been made for the day of closure . The Mobile Library will be parked up by the library 's entrance ramp on The Square , cancelling its normal Sunday round . <p> Youth Space on Coleman Place will be open to everyone from 10am -- 4pm , and there will be extra computers available for the day . <p> People will be able to pick up their holds at Youth Space and return their books there , as well as through the usual returns slots . <p> " This is an opportunity for our Central Library visitors to familiarise themselves with Youth Space and the Mobile Library , " Mrs Duncan says . <p> " Public reaction to the three-day closure in July was generally understanding and appreciative of the reasons behind it . We hope they understand the need for us to have a further day of closure to assist with the assessment .
