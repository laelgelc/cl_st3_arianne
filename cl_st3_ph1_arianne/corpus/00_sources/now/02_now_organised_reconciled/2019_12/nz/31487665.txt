<p> People are more confident about the housing market with increasing numbers thinking it 's a good time to buy , according to ASB 's Housing Confidence Survey for the September quarter .. <p> Of the survey 's 2864 respondents , 27% said it was a good time to buy compared to 22% in the second quarter of this year , 13% said it was a bad time ( down from 14% ) , 49% said it was neither good nor bad ( barely changed from 50% ) , and 10% did n't know . <p> That was a seven year high in terms of the percentage of people who thought it was a good time to buy . <p> Record low mortgage interest rates appear to be a major factor in those confidence levels , with 43% of respondents expecting lower interest rates over the coming year compared with 31% in the second quarter , while just 12% expect interest rates to rise , down from 14% while 28% expect them to remain the same . <p> However ASB 's chief economist Nick Tuffley said the lift in housing confidence did not mean a return to boom times . <p> " Housing turnover is finally showing some signs of life after a long period of stagnation , " Tuffley said . <p> " The survey is bang on the long-run average , highlighting a point we 've been making in our housing commentaries . <p> " While housing indicators have rebounded strongly over the past three months , this should only really be characterised as a return to normality . <p> "
