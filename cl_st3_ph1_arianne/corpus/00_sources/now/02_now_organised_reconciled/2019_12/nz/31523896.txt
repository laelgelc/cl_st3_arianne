<h> Shocked workers learn of immediate Bunnings shutdowns <p> Workers at two Bunnings retail stores ; Waikanae and Te Aroha , learned today that their stores are closing forever on 27 December 2019 . <p> On 18 December at 6pm , Bunnings informed employees at the stores that they we considering redundancies . Bunnings ' apparent basis for the closures was that " we are concerned that we are no longer able to operate the store to a standard we feel meets Bunnings operational requirements , for you , our Team and of course , our customers " . <p> FIRST Union wrote to the company and requested that due to the inadequate notice to workers and lack of information about the redundancies , the consultation period be suspended until the new year . This request was denied and just two days before Christmas , the company has announced that it will be closing the stores before the new year . <p> " This is just cruel . It 's completely unnecessary to blindside employees with terrible news like this just before Christmas , " said Kirstin Miller , FIRST Union spokesperson . <p> " Bunnings has failed to provide specific details as to why the stores need to close , and why so suddenly . " <p> " We have written to the company again today to try and ascertain what planning has been made for workers here , and what details lie behind the decision . " <p> One worker who has been employed at the Waikanae store for 2 years is shocked and angry at the closure : " We have lives and friendships here , and our store is doing a roaring trade . " <p> " We were told just three weeks ago that the plan was to move to a nearby location in March 2020 . Today we hear this vague statement that ' the numbers do n't stack up ' . " <p> " They have lied to us and the timing is appalling . At this time of year especially the thought of being out of work is very stressful . The way they gone about this is disrespectful . " <p> Kapiti Coast District Councillor Rob McCann said that the closure of Bunnings stores with such short notice is acting without care for the community . <p> " Employees at the Kapiti cheese factory were at least given six months to find new employment . " <p> " Four days notice over Christmas is unacceptable . It 's my hope that any further decision from the company takes into account the workers and their families . " <p> Formed in 2011 after the merger between the National Distribution Union and Finsec , FIRST Union represents 27,000 workers in the Finance , Industrial ( Textile and Wood ) , Retail , Stores , Transport and Logistics sectors . <p> FIRST Union is also affiliated to the New Zealand Council of Trade Unions and runs several networks including the Union Network of Migrants , the Runanga , Fono and Womens ' Committee .
