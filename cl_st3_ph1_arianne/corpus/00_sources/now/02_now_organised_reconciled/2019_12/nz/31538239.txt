<h> TradeMe 's most viewed auctions of the year revealed <p> A hat that looks like one associated with a group of rowdy tourists is TradeMe 's most viewed auction of 2019 . <p> The hat joined a phallic-shaped kumara and the cockpit from a private jet at the top of the e-commerce site 's list of most-viewed auctions , which was released this morning . <p> Originally put up for sale in January 2019 , the auction for the hat capitalised on a media furore at the beginning of the year surrounding a group of overseas tourists after they caused chaos across the North Island . <p> The group initially featured in a video posted on social media showing them abusing a woman who told them off for littering at Takapuna Beach . <p> In the video a small boy wearing a Bunnings hat can be seen shouting threats at the woman . The auction 's description references the incident and the boy 's actions . <p> " I can not be responsible for any obscenities that may flow from your mouth or rude hand gestures while wearing the hat . User beware . Not recommended for children even under adult supervision , " it said . <p> The auction received 192,006 views and sold for $1000 . All proceeds were donated to Mercy Hospice . <p> TradeMe spokesperson Logan Mudge said some auctions get a lot of views because they 're weird , topical or the seller writes a good description on the item . One thing all the listings had in common was a seller that did n't take the whole thing too seriously . <p> " These sellers were all prepared to have a bit of fun with their listing and that really resonates , " he said . <p> 1 .
