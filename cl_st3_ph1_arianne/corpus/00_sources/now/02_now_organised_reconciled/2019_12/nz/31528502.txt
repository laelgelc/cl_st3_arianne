<h> Under-fire PM sorry for Hawaii holiday <p> Prime Minister Scott Morrison has apologised for holidaying in Hawaii while much of Australia burned but also stressed now is not the time for point-scoring but rather " a time to be kind to each other " . <p> The country has been fighting wildfires since September , with blazes destroying more than 700 homes and 1.2 million hectares of bushland . At least nine people have died , including two firefighters . <p> The Liberal leader returned to Sydney on Saturday night and on Sunday morning confirmed he had been in Hawaii with his family on a trip that was planned six weeks ago . <p> " If we had our time over again and with the benefit of hindsight we would have made different decisions , " Morrison told reporters in Sydney . <p> " ( But ) I am sure Australians are fair-minded and understand that when you make a promise to your children you try and keep it . " <p> Morrison said to the people he 'd upset by being away during the latest bushfire emergency : " I apologise for that . " <p> He acknowledged his holiday had caused anxiety . <p> " I get it that people would have been upset to know that I was holidaying with my family while their families were under great stress . " <p> He extended sympathy to the families of NSW Rural Fire Service volunteers Geoffrey Keaton and Andrew O'Dwyer who were killed on Thursday night fighting fires . <p> It was their deaths that prompted Mr Morrison on Friday morning to say he planned to rush home . <p> " I deeply regret any offence caused to any of the many Australians affected by the terrible bushfires by my taking leave with family at this time , " Mr Morrison said in a statement then . <p> " I have been receiving regular updates on the bushfires disaster as well as the status of the search for and treatment of the victims of the White Island ( volcano eruption ) tragedy . " <p> Morrison 's wife and daughters will remain in Hawaii . He was originally scheduled to return to prime ministerial duties on Monday . <p> CLIMATE POLICIES DEFENDED <p> The Prime Minister on Sunday also addressed his conservative Liberal-National coalition 's climate policies , which his government has been forced to defend following the severity of this year 's bushfires . <p> Morrison said there was no argument that there is a link between climate change and weather events around the world . <p> " But I 'm sure people would equally acknowledge the direct connection to any single fire event is not a credible suggestion to make that link , " he old journalists . <p> Earlier this month , Australia drew criticism at a United Nations climate summit in Madrid for its climate-change policy of using old carbon credits to count towards future emissions targets . <p> Australia is one of the world 's largest carbon emitters per capita because of its reliance on coal-fired power plants . It has pledged to cut carbon emissions by 26% from 2005 levels by 2030 , but critics accuse Morrison of paying lip service to that commitment . <p> Mr Morrison recommitted to those policies , which he took to a general election in May , on Sunday . <p> A bushfire burns near the Lithgow Correctional Centre compound in Marrangaroo , New South Wales . Photo : via Reuters <p> FORECAST FOR FIRES <p> The intensity of fires eased overnight in New South Wales , Victoria and South Australia states , where fires had been burning out of control on Friday and Saturday as a combination of extreme heat and strong winds created " catastrophic " conditions in some areas . <p> " We have still got an enormous amount of fire burning in the landscape , " NSW RFS Commissioner Shane Fitzsimmons said on Sunday , adding that the spreading fires in the Blue Mountains area around 100km west of Sydney would be a significant focus for crews . <p> More than 105 fires were still burning across NSW on Sunday , with 59 considered uncontained and one burning at an emergency level . <p> Dozens of properties were reported damaged or destroyed . A man who had been unaccounted for early Sunday after staying on Saturday to protect his property was later found safe . <p> " Today is thankfully expected to be much cooler for large sections of NSW , which will be a welcome reprieve . However , many communities away from the coast will still experience significant heat , " the Bureau of Meteorology said in a tweet . <p> Conditions were expected to remain favourable over coming days and firefighters will work to contain some of the firefronts near communities , particularly in the Blue Mountains region to the west of Sydney .
