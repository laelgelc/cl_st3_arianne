<h> Space fantasies have become real <p> It is 80 years since Orson Welles ' War of the Worlds story of a Martian invasion hit the air waves of the world . <p> For me back then it was the Flash Gordon Trip to Mars serials and brother Dave 's Buck Rogers in the 25th Century comics that stirred my imagination . Rocket-powered space ships , anti-gravity belts , and the weaponry , Armageddon-capable death rays etc . All fantasy . <p> Finally the two atomic bombs were dropped on Japan , killing about 200,000 people . Compared to that horror , the futuristic disintegrator weapons of the Airlords of Han in the fictional Buck Rogers comics were reduced to toys . <p> When peace came , propeller aircraft were gradually replaced by rocket engines . Before long jet aircraft ruled the skies . The British de Havilland Comet was the first commercial jet airliner in 1952 . <p> Then came the space race , the US versus the USSR . Yuri Gagarin was the first man in outer space , when he orbited the Earth in the Soviet Vostok spacecraft in 1961 . <p> Within a decade the USA had successfully landed men on the moon and returned them . <p> Space exploration began . Unmanned space craft have been probing and examining the solar system 's planets and moons to far off Pluto . A comet has been chased down and had a monitoring device attached . <p> Eighty years on from my introduction to space fiction , science and technology has brought reality to much of it . <p> Billionaire Elon Musk is about to cash in on space advances . He and his team plan a 21st century trip to Mars , with a plan to colonise that desolate sphere . <p> Luckily he wo n't have to face Tiger Men with flash guns like Buck Rogers did , or Flash Gordon 's arch enemy Ming the Merciless . <p> Nevertheless , I 'll draw a parallel . <p> The 1930 story theme was that evil alien Ming was out to destroy Earth 's atmosphere with his Radon Ray machine , and it was Flash Gordon who foiled his attempt . <p> That may be fantasy , but the realty exists that the extra carbon we spew out is truly a threat to our existence . <p> Two years ago in the USA , Republican Donald Trump won the White House , despite his open extreme anti-environment climate change denial agenda . <p> Since then nationalist , far-right political parties have made significant electoral gains all across Europe and other continents . None of them rate environmental issues as priorities . <p> Last weekend in South America , far-right candidate Jair Bolsonaro won Brazil 's presidential election . Called Brazil 's Trump by many , he too is extremely anti-environment and has said global warming is nothing more than " greenhouse fables " . <p> I ca n't help thinking of Flash Gordon 's arch enemy Ming the Merciless , with his Radon Ray 's bombarding Earth 's atmosphere . <p> The way things are going , we wo n't need him . We already have enough mad leaders to achieve the same effect with greenhouse gas emissions . <p> Imagine Buck Rogers waking up in 2419 AD to find an uninhabited planet . The Armageddon he was meant to save the world from had already happened . <p> All this because the people of the 21st century refused to make the changes they knew were necessary to save ourselves . <p> Hopefully there will be an awakening from our hypnotic Rip Van Winkle trance in time to prevent this fictional outcome becoming the reality .
