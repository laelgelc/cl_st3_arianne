<h> We look back on an even bigger , more successful year and reveal our most popular stories <p> 2019 has been another very good year for us . Thank you to all our loyal readers . <p> In fact we are ending the decade in great shape . <p> We were visited by a record 2.6 million " unique readers " ( a remarkable +27% rise in just one year ) who dialed up 8.2 million sessions reading 17.5 million pages of content . Almost all of these metrics were new all-time records for us . <p> ( Actually our ' unique reader ' data is by IP address so it probably double-counts some readers who access our content using multiple devices . ) <p> But it is still a good proportion of New Zealand 's adult population , and well more than half of them came at least once in the year to use our content and resources . <p> We aim to be an intelligent read for people who want to understand what is going on in our economy , and the outside forces that also shape it . Although we are still a desktop read to take full advantage of the tables , charts and other embedded resources we offer readers , there was a sharp shift to mobile in the year . In fact , now only just over 48% of our sessions were delivered on desktop browsers , 8% on tablets , and 44% on smartphones . That is quite a change from 2018 . <p> What is remarkable about the growth of the use of our service on mobile is that engagement time of the small screen is high - in fact , it is slightly higher than for desktop showing that intelligent analysis does have a place on a smartphone . <p> And here 's something you might find surprising - more than half of our readers are 45 year old or younger .
