<h> Jacinda Ardern found new heights of power in 2019 - and hit hard limits <p> Prime Minister Jacinda Ardern reviews the year that was , and gives some insight into her Christmas traditions . <p> Jacinda Ardern 's power and popularity reached dizzying heights this year in the weeks after the March 15 tragedy . <p> Over half of the country wanted her to be prime minister in early April according to one poll , and another had her party at more than 50 per cent support . <p> But the harsh limit of Ardern 's actual power in Parliament was brought into sharp relief not long afterwards , when Labour lost its decade-long battle to introduce a capital gains tax , a key tool in the party 's planned assault on high house prices . <p> TOM LEE/STUFF <p> Jacinda Ardern found huge international fame in 2019 , but ran into hard limits back home . <p> It is housing which Ardern pointed to as the biggest problem in her " year of delivery " when talking to Stuff for an end-of-year interview . <p> " We 've been really open that while our housing programme has been huge -- we 're building more houses than any government since the 1970s , particularly state houses ... We 've also openly acknowledged that KiwiBuild has n't achieved what we would 've liked . <p> " That 's an area where we will keep going , keep working to improve . " <p> Ironically , the year-of-delivery line was actually unveiled just as the questions about KiwiBuild really started to ramp up . Ardern was in Martinborough at a summer caucus meeting and then-housing minister Phil Twyford had just admitted that the July 1 target of 1000 homes was not going to be possible . <p> The " year-of-delivery " line has become a bit of a joke for the Opposition throughout the year , rolled out whenever the Government 's actions fall short of its ambitions , or its timeframes . As a bit of rhetoric it has also led to an extremely busy December for the Government as all the things that " had to be done this year " come due , leading to a well-worn joke about it being a month of delivery . <p> Attention is a precious commodity for prime ministers , but when they lavish an area with it things often start moving a lot faster . Part of this is just the gravitas the leader of a country brings , part of it is the hundreds of public servants Ardern has behind her at the Department of Prime Minister and Cabinet . This year her attention has felt laser-focused on two things : the follow-up from Christchurch and climate change . <p> In climate change the Government has passed a Zero Carbon Act , pledged hundreds of millions to decarbonise the public estate , and is now beefing up the Emissions Trading Scheme so net emissions peak at 2020 levels . <p> " If we do n't get ahead of the challenge , we run the risk of having to make changes very quickly , " Ardern said of climate change . <p> " That will be jarring for everyone in New Zealand and across the economy . So we need to move , we need to bring people with us and we need to make sure that we have a plan . That means we do n't have that hard jarring effect . " <p> Ardern said climate change would be a key part of trade talks -- particularly as the EU shares a lot of New Zealand 's ambitions . <p> But the attention Ardern has paid to subjects like climate change has often just gone into getting her coalition partners to agree . This can lead to a feeling that she is much more the glue between three parties than the leader of the Labour Party . But next year people wo n't have the option of voting for the coalition itself -- and Ardern will have to campaign for her actual party without alienating her partners . <p> " We will campaign for Labour , " Ardern said . " That will mean demonstrating the difference that we 've made , that our agenda has made . We 'll be very much focused on us as a team and the New Zealanders will then deliver what they believe we need to do in order to form a government . But it very much will be focused on us . " <p> That will likely mean investment in two of Labour 's core areas of strength : health and education , which have already received big dollops of cash from the Government but could well receive more . <p> It will also mean doing their best to move on from the one story that has attached itself to Labour the party this year like no other -- a serious sexual assault allegation against a staffer . <p> It remains very unclear if Ardern and Labour have the ambition to increase core benefits , despite the Government 's working group on welfare recommending it as the best possible way to alleviate child poverty , which Ardern has said is her No 1 reason for being in politics . <p> " The working group report told us that we had income adequacy issues , that actually people who are on government support are really struggling . Now that 's something that we openly already knew and acknowledged . In fact , when we first came into government , we cancelled the tax cuts and we focused on low and middle income earners . <p> " All of the things that we 've done so far have had an impact on benefits . What we 've happened to do though is focusing on benefits with children are involved and so that 's where our focus has really been . " <p> Ardern 's year was extremely international , with trips to New York and Paris making headlines everywhere . But with an election in 2020 things will be a lot more domestic , a lot more focused on whether Kiwis feel her Government is helping them get ahead or holding them back . But before then she will get something roughly resembling a break , as her briefings thin out over Christmas and she gets time to do other stuff . <p> " The briefcase follows me but not as regularly . So it slows down a bit over Christmas . But I do read over Christmas . I find it hard to get into fiction sometimes . I have to kind of ease myself into it , " Ardern said of her summer plans . <p> " Lots of people work over Christmas and you know , for them I just hope they get a little bit of time out . But I hope that over Christmas this year that we all manage to , as much as we can leave the phone , try and forget all those troubles and everything that keeps us at a distance from our family and friends . Just have some good quality time . "
