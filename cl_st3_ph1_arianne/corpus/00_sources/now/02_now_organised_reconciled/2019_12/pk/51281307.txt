<h> Comeback of plastic bags <p> On August 14 this year , the Islamabad administration with the active collaboration of the ministry of climate change banned single used plastic bags . Yet after only four months , the non-degradable monster is back . It appears the August 14 ban was only a photo-op to boost the government 's image , and not for environmental concerns . Following in the footstep of the capital administration , Punjab and Sindh governments also announced similar steps for Lahore and Karachi , and both cities have made no difference on the plastic front either . In the initial days of the ban , shops and grocery stores would wrap items in paper or in paper bags . With the passage of time , both sellers and buyers are back to the plastic carrier bags . It seems the government was banking on public support to make the campaign successful and urged them to refuse to buy things in plastic packages and to use bags of paper or cloth for shopping , but sadly the public has yet to realise that plastic bag was the worst invention of technology . People are not only accepting single used plastic bags while shopping , they are also disposing garbage using plastic bags . <p> The comeback of the plastic bag points to the failure of enforcement of law by federal and provincial governments . The only exception to this is cantonment areas where the ban is being followed strictly . Governments can learn from the experience of the cantonment inspection models where heavy fines are slapped on violators . Similarly , the public at large will have to wage a war on these harmful bags . In developed countries , cities have got rid of them and life is much healthier . Pakistan must enter that phase now with a strong resolve to live without harmful bags . Now is the time for citizens to play their part as well and make all necessary efforts . If they are able to implement the ban in the bigger cities , other areas and towns of the country will also fall in line and we all will have a healthier place to live in. *
