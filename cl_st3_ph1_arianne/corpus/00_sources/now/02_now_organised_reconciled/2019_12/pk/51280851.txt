<h> November 2019 Second Hottest , As per Records <p> As per a US government agency , last month that was November was the second hottest November in the 140-year global climate record . It also said that the polar sea ice also shrank to near record lows . <p> During the month the average global land and ocean surface temperature was 1.66 degrees Fahrenheit which is above the 20th century average and the second highest November temperature on record , as per the US National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration . <p> More than normal hot temperatures were experienced in most parts of the world except for North America and parts of the Central and Western Asia . <p> The US data has come days after the Copernicus Climate Change Service of the European Union , making use of various methodologies , determined that November 2019 was the joint hottest month , along with the same months in the year 2015 and 2016 . <p> The United Nations has said that 2019 is to be one of the hottest years on record , while the World Meteorological Organization said that it was practically certain that 2010s had been the hottest decade in the history .
