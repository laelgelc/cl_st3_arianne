<h> Letter : Gideon a pro-green candidate for Senate <p> Share <p> Politicians have a vested interest in remaining on the good side of the public . Staying on the good side of the planet , however , seems to present a greater challenge . This is especially true for Sen. Susan Collins . Collins ' website offers an impressive list of unwavering environmental undertakings . But I see Collins more as an imposter . Strong history of cozying up to big fossil fuel ogres , supporting artic drilling , receiving campaign donations from oil and gas corporations , confirming a climate change denier as our nation 's top energy regulator . Sounds like double-dealing to me infused with a flip-flopping beat that seems so Susan Collins of late . Could this be why The League of Conservation Voters denied Collins their endorsement for 2020 ? <p> I not only want fresher air not in my environment , but in the political arena . Sara Gideon will to be my pro-green candidate for 2020 . Her record looks promising , not stained with incongruity and deception -- two ingredients our planet and I wo n't tolerate anymore .
