<p> MANILA , Dec 23 ( Reuters ) - At least 11 people have been killed and more than 300 treated in hospital after drinking coconut wine in the Philippines , including some who were celebrating at a Christmas party , health and local authorities said on Monday . <p> The poisoning occurred in Laguna and Quezon , two provinces south of Manila , and all had consumed lambanog , a drink popular in provinces and consumed widely during holidays and celebrations . <p> Many were admitted to hospitals on the urging of mayor Vener Munoz in Rizal , Laguna , where the deaths occurred between Thursday and Sunday . <p> Two people who had been in critical condition were improving , he told local radio . The coconut wine that was consumed had been made in his town , he added . <p> Blood tests and samples of leftover lambanog would be collected and analysed on Monday , the Department of Health said . <p> " All had a sad history of lambanog ingestion , " the department said , referring to those poisoned . <p> " Some bought for leisure drinking and birthday party , while others were donated by local officials during their Christmas party . " <p> Unregulated production and sales of lambanog are common in the Philippines , and it is often made illegally with dangerous additives . <p> The country 's Food and Drug Administration ( FDA ) has previously warned about the dangerous and prohibited use of methanol as an additive in home brews . <p> A year ago , the FDA and police were deployed to locate and confiscate unregistered lambanog that was being openly sold to the public , and threatened to prosecute sellers . <p> Twenty-one people died after consuming lambanog last year , media reported .
