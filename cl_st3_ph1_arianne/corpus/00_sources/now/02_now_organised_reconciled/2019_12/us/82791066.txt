<h> Reuters joins climate coverage coalition <p> In an effort to push its climate change coverage , Reuters has joined the ' Covering Climate Now , ' a project co-founded by Columbia Journalism Review and the Nation . <p> The initiative sets out to strengthen the media 's focus on climate change . <p> The coalition spans 350 organizations and its sole purpose is to advocate for fair coverage and more stories about the climate crisis . The topic impacts coverage on markets , business , politics , society and nature . <p> " Reuters is committed to providing the most accurate and insightful coverage of the climate crisis , as it threatens the health , safety and economic well-being of people world-wide , " said Reuters editor-in-chief Stephen J.
