<h> Letter : AEP solar project would be good for the future <h> Sunday <p> As a lover of clean air , clean energy and a healthy climate , I 'm very saddened by the Public Utilities Commission of Ohio 's decision to deny AEP 's plan to build two new solar projects . <p> In their decision , the PUCO said that AEP had n't demonstrated the need for the project , but the need could n't be more obvious . In a state where we generate only 2.5% of our energy from renewables , where we have to import power from Canada , where we have about a decade left to avert 1.5 degrees of climate change ( which will irreparably harm countless lives and disproportionately affect vulnerable communities ) , adding more solar capacity is common sense . <p> The proposed projects would have saved customers $200 million over 20 years , improved air quality , increased local taxes to go to schools and other public functions , and produced hundreds of good-paying jobs for an economically depressed region . <p> I 'm heartened to hear the news that the projects could very well go along on their own . Still , Ohio 's leaders should be looking out for us and providing for a future where everyone can prosper . Go solar ! <p> Kevin Hengehold , Cincinnati <h> Never miss a story <p> Choose the plan that 's right for you . Digital access or digital and print delivery .
