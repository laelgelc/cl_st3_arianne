They also recommend coordinating with local transportation agencies to promote public transit to games and other popular events , include trolley and bus shuttles . <p> The proposal diverts 75% of its food and other waste , but the city asks for zero-waste infrastructure to be built onsite.Staff also recommend that a renewable natural gas source be found for the project 's energy needs , and want dust suppression and storm water management plans . <p> A rendering of the upcoming downtown Palm Springs AHL hockey arena due to open in 2021 .
