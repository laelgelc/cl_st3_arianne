<h> States and utilities grapple with electric vehicle charging rules <p> Across the country , states and utilities experimented with policies surrounding electric vehicle charging stations . Among the questions , as reported by David Thill : who should own them and how to manage their toll on the grid ? State regulators in Iowa and North Carolina made notable decisions that made it easier for private businesses to sell electricity to electric car drivers . <h> More big legislation -- and political scandal -- in Illinois <p> As implementation of Illinois ' 2016 Future Energy Jobs Acts winds down , clean energy stakeholders sought to pass another big bill to propel the next phase of renewable development in the state , Kari Lyderson reported . The Clean Energy Jobs Act aims to expand on and address shortcomings in FEJA , while the Path to 100 bill would set a goal of 40% renewables by 2030 .
