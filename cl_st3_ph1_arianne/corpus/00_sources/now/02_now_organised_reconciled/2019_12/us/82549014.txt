( Photo : Family handout ) <p> Former Iowa congressman and businessman Berkley Bedell died Saturday after suffering a stroke earlier in the week , according to a social media post by his son . He was 98 . <p> Bedell was born in Spirit Lake on March 5 , 1921 , and attended Iowa State University . He served in the U.S. Army from 1942 to 1945 and represented Iowa 's 6th District in the U.S. House of Representatives from 1975 to 1987 . <p> Bedell is well known for his contributions to the fishing tackle industry . He was the founder of Spirit Lake-based Berkley &amp; Co. , later renamed Pure Fishing , and inducted into the Bass Fishing Hall of Fame in 2018 . <p> Bedell received the Small Businessman of the Year award in 1964 from President Lyndon B. Johnson . <p> " We feel sadness that Dad 's inspiration of unrelenting determination to make the world and humanity better will have a quieter voice , " son Tom Bedell said in a Facebook post Saturday . <p> Bedell fought hard to protect farm families in times of crisis and boost the middle class , Iowa Democratic Party Chair Troy Price and Fourth District Chair Penny Rosfjord said in a statement Saturday night . <p> " Berkley Bedell was the embodiment of a dedicated public servant . As a stalwart of progressive politics in Northwest Iowa , his work to uphold farm families during the Farm Crisis , boost the middle class , and provide a fair shot for all has impacted countless Iowans , " the statement read . <p> " A true prairie populist , his value-driven policies united Iowans and inspired generations of future Democrats . Our state is a better place because of his leadership , and his dedication to community will be missed . " <p> In 2014 , Bedell worked to increase young voter turnout in order to push presidential candidates to tackle climate change , money in politics , and student costs and debt . <p> " If you are a young person , your life will be affected by whether we do something about climate change , " he told the Des Moines Register in 2014 . <p> Bedell endorsed U.S. Sen. Elizabeth Warren , a Massachusetts Democrat , in the race for president earlier this year . <p> " Congressman Berkley Bedell always fought hard for the people of Iowa . My thoughts are with his family , friends , and loved ones -- and with every person his transformational leadership touched or inspired , " Warren said in a tweet Saturday night . <p> Bedell 's wife , Elinor Bedell , died in 2017 . He is survived by three children .
