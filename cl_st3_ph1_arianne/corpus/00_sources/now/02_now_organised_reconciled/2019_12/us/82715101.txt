<p> WASHINGTON , Dec 16 ( Reuters ) - The U.S. Senate voted overwhelmingly on Monday to advance a $738 billion defense policy bill , clearing the way for a vote on final passage on Tuesday , which will send it to the White House for President Donald Trump to sign into law . <p> The Republican-controlled Senate voted 76-6 on a procedural motion to cut off debate on the National Defense Authorization Act , or NDAA . <p> The Democratic-led House approved the measure by 377-48 last week . <p> Because it is one of the few pieces of major legislation Congress passes every year , the NDAA becomes a vehicle for a range of policy measures as well as setting everything from military pay levels to which ships or aircraft will be modernized , purchased or discontinued . <p> This year 's legislation included a 3.1% pay increase for the troops , the first paid family leave for all federal workers and the creation of a Space Force , a top military priority for Trump .
