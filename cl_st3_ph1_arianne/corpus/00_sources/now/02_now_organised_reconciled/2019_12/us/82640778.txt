<h> ECB 's Lagarde says strategic review to conclude by end of 2020 <p> 2 Min Read <p> FRANKFURT ( Reuters ) - The European Central Bank expects to begin in January a strategic review of how it does business and conclude it by the end of 2020 , new ECB President Christine Lagarde said on Thursday . <p> Lagarde told a news conference following the ECB 's first policy meeting of her term that there was " no preconceived landing zone " for the review , the ECB 's first in 16 years , which she said was " a little bit overdue " . <p> The review " needs to be comprehensive , needs to look at all and every issue , needs to turn every stone and will take its time - but will not take too much time " , Lagarde said . <p> She said the ECB would consult with members of the European Parliament , the academic community and civil society representatives to help determine its objectives . <p> " It will aim at not just preaching the gospel that we think we mastered but also at listening to the views of those to whom we reach out , " she added , promising that it would also include the " immense challenges " posed by inequality and climate change .
