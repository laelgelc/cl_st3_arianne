<p> While parts of the country are still fretting over the recent federal election 's implications for national unity , the Liberal minority government presents a great opportunity for the capital -- if city leaders can seize the moment . <p> It is not only because Ottawa painted the region Liberal red and got two cabinet ministers in return , but particularly because the disposition and interests of this minority government align with the city 's key priorities , which council has been discussing these last few weeks as it set its 2020 budget and worked on the Official Plan . <p> Having Catherine McKenna as infrastructure minister , with billions of dollars to dispense , should count as a big win for Ottawa . Of course , McKenna wo n't just be cutting cheques for us , but if your regional minister is the one holding the purse strings , you are in a good place to advance your interests . " This is a Flora Footbridge , it is LRT , it is fixing up the Boys and Girls Club , it is the renovation to the National Arts Centre , it 's the new central library , " McKenna told the Citizen . In essence , it is bricks and mortar , and she 's holding the trowel . There remain questions about exactly what a minister of middle class prosperity will do , but Mona Fortier has been quite visible , and represents another local champion at the cabinet table . <p> Having Catherine McKenna as infrastructure minister , with billions of dollars to dispense , should count as a big win for Ottawa . <p> Most important perhaps , is the fact that the Liberal government has made the fight against climate change the cornerstone of its policy in this minority Parliament . " Canada 's children and grandchildren will judge this generation by its action -- or inaction -- on the defining challenge of the time : climate change , " Governor General Judy Payette said in the Throne Speech . <p> This " defining challenge " may be Ottawa 's golden opportunity to get Stage 3 LRT and other key projects done . As Ontario Premier Doug Ford told the National Post , public transit is key in the fight against climate change . " He ( Trudeau ) is focused on the environment ; well , there 's no better way to help the environment than take tens of thousands of people out of their cars into rapid transit , " the premier said , as he seeks billions for transit projects in the GTA . <p> Beyond the opportunity to get substantial funding for Stage 3 LRT , is also commuter rail to Gatineau . Time is of the essence , however . Minority governments do n't last full-term but this Liberal government is determined to achieve success , and the city must take advantage . <p> The problem is that the failures of the Confederation Line have sidetracked the city , and with preliminary work on Stage 2 LRT just beginning , it may be difficult to focus on Stage 3 . But the city must walk and chew gum at the same time . Environmental assessments on the extensions to Barrhaven , Kanata and Stittsville must be speeded up so the project is shovel-ready when the money starts to flow . The city ca n't just say Stage 3 will cost $4.6 billion without a clear case and plan for the project . <p> Finally , let 's abandon the ridiculous idea of asking for a pitiful $10 million to turn the Prince of Wales Bridge into a bicycle path . Consider some traffic figures from the City of Ottawa : In March , 189,015 vehicles , on average , crossed the river in both directions everyday . In July , the daily average was 191,081 ; and in September , 191,467 . Imagine the thousands of people we could get out of gas-guzzling vehicles into transit by using PoW for commuter rail . How myopic of our local leaders to think of using this wonderful asset merely as some kind of footbridge . <p> With about a dozen federal politicians from the Ottawa area , including two cabinet ministers , things have never looked so good for the city . We 've had local champions such as Conservative John Baird and Liberal Dalton McGuinty who got big things done for us . Right now , we seem to have not just inside power , but the right environment for success .
