If you see comments in violation of our community guidelines , please report them . <h> Greta Thunberg and Chernobyl among the most mispronounced words and names of 2019 <p> It looks like just about everyone had trouble getting a few choice words correct this year . <p> Language-learning app Babbel commissioned the U.S. Captioning Company " to identify the prominent and topical words of 2019 that commonly proved challenging for newsreaders and people on television to pronounce correctly . " <p> The list of words contains names of some of the most popular people of 2019 . U.S. women 's national team superstar and World Cup champion Megan Rapinoe is included , as is Time magazine 's person of the year , teenage climate change activist Greta Thunberg . South Bend , Indiana , Mayor Pete Buttigieg , a Democratic presidential candidate , is on the list , too . <p> " Our world grows more global with each passing year and our mispronounced words reflect this , " Babbel 's English Editor in Didactics Ted Mentele said in a statement . <p> " In 2019 , America 's news anchors and TV hosts have been grappling with Japanese lifestyle trends , Scandinavian activists , and French cathedrals , all of which are bound to lead to some tricky pronunciations -- foreign proper names always have a lot of nuance ( hence the fact they 're also often mispronounced ) . " <p> In fact , of the 10 words on the lists , only two are just words , as opposed to hard-to-pronounce names or places . The first is " deter . " The second is " tokimeku , " which , according to Babbel , is Japanese for " to flutter . " <p> Here 's Babbel 's list of 2019 's hardest words to pronounce and their pronunciations from Babbel , with pronunciation notes from Mentele : <p> Pronunciation notes : " The final ' g ' in ' Thunberg ' is typically softened by Swedish speakers into something like ' yeh ' in English : ' tOOn-bairyeh ' " <p> Megan Rapinoe ( ruh-PEE-noh ) - American soccer star who helped lead the U.S women 's national team to victory during July 's FIFA Women 's World Cup . She earned the Golden Boot for most goals scored and the Golden Ball as the competition 's best player .
