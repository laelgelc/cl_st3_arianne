<h> Climate Change Activists March Through Denver Streets <p> DENVER ( CBS4 ) -- Climate activists marched through Denver on Friday morning , demanding action on climate change . The crowd paraded from downtown Denver to the state Capitol . <p> ( credit : CBS ) <p> A large part of the group was made up of the younger generation . They urged people to walk out of work to join them in spreading their message . <p> ( credit : CBS ) <p> The last time a large rally about climate change occurred in Denver was in October when climate change activist Greta Thunberg was in Colorado . <p> Greta Thunberg ( credit : CBS ) <p> On Friday , the group focused on the 2020 election and urged Congress to pass the Green New Deal . They want to end all fossil fuel expansion , hold fossil fuel companies responsible for damage , and transition to 100% renewable energy .
