<p> LONDON , Dec 8 ( Reuters ) - British Prime Minister Boris Johnson said on Sunday that the naughtiest thing he was prepared to admit to was breaking the law by cycling on the pavement . <p> When asked by Sky what the naughtiest thing he was ready to admit to was , Johnson initially asked advisers for suggestions before saying : " I think , I , you know , I may sometimes , when I was riding a bicycle every day , which I used to do , I may sometimes have not always have obeyed the law about cycling on the pavement . " <p> " I want you know how firmly and how strongly I disapprove of people who cycle on the pavement and I think it is wrong and I feel bad about it . But I might sometimes have scooted up onto the pavement rather than dismounting before . " <p> Asked if he was nervous about narrowing polls , Johnson said : " Of course , we are fighting for every vote . I think that this is a critical moment for this country .
