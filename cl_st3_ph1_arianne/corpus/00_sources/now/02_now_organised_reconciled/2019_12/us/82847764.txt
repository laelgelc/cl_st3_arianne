<h> Actions <h> Decade in Review : How social media changed our lives <p> The end of a year and a decade is about a week away . We 've come a long way in the past 10 years . PIX11 's Shirley Chan looks back at the social media and movements the defined the decade . <p> The 2010s will be remembered for many things , including the growth of social media and how it impacted change . <p> Leading the way #METOO . It is a movement that has empowered women of all ages to speak out and speak up against sexual assault and harassment , particularly in the workplace . <p> " I think it 's given people space and language to come forward and talk about it and that 's what our movement is really about , " said Tarana Burke.The phrase " MeToo " was first used by sexual harassment survivor and activist Burke . It exploded in late 2017 , when dozens of women accused film producer Harvey Weinstein of rape and other offenses . The strength of the MeToo movement took down powerful men from Weinstein to TODAY host Matt Lauer to Fox News chairman Roger Ailes . <p> From giving women a voice ... to bringing justice and healing to the African American community . Several national incidents gave rise to the battle cry " Black Lives Matter . " First , the 2013 acquittal of George Zimmerman in the Florida shooting death of unarmed black teen Trayvon Martin . Then in 2014 , the police-involved deaths of Eric Garner in Staten Island and Michael Brown in Ferguson , Missouri . The events triggered widespread protests . Soon , the use of #BlackLivesMatter lit up social media and continues to do so . <p> The decade also gave the LGBTQ community a platform to advocate for full acceptance . Social media provided a space , particularly for younger members of the community , to be out and proud . Advocacy groups organized and formed and the term PRIDE becomes symbolic of the movement . <p> A blunt talking 16-year-old Swedish girl becomes the face of the fight against climate change . Greta Thunberg has taken to the world stage , urging political leaders to take action to address the crisis . The outspoken teen is using her 3.7 million followers on Twitter and 9 million on Instagram to help her do so . <p> But Thunberg is just one of many using social media to push their platform . Enter the " First Twitter President , " Donald J. Trump . Shucking traditional media news conferences , Trump takes to Twitter instead , using the 280 character limit to speak directly to his audience . He 's gotten into Tweet wars with countless others , including just recently , Thunberg . <p> Then there 's Instagram . Launched in 2010 , it has become the favored social networking entity to brand yourself . Love ' em or hate ' em , the Kardashian-Jenner clan sits on the Insta throne . At last check , the six women in the family have a combined 643 million followers and have transformed that success into cold hard cash . They 've used Instagram to promote and further their many businesses . <p> Perhaps the most surprising social media star of the decade is Pope Francis . Since his papacy began in 2013 , the pontiff has embraced posting online . His 2015 visit to New York City garnered him rock star status , Francis even stopped to take countless selfies . <p> The selfie made it to the mainstream in the past ten years . Along with it , an obsession with taking self-portraits and sharing them . The phenomenon was given life by front-facing cameras on cell phones . A group selfie taken by Ellen Degeneres at the 2014 Academy Awards nearly broke the internet ! <p> Arguably , the company that had the most effect on all aspects of society in the past decade is AMAZON . Not only has the e-commerce giant changed the way we shop , it gave birth to a new gadget - the home assistant , Alexa . The " Amazon Effect " also lead to the decline of brick and mortar stores , as well as the rise of Amazon Prime video . <p> Amazon is certainly not alone when it comes to video on demand . The past ten years has seen an onslaught of streaming services from Netflix to Hulu to Apple TV , to name a few . Just last month , Disney Plus launched , hitting a whopping 10 million subscribers in its first day . Paying for new content on video streaming has forever changed the way we watch television . <p> Finally , we can call it the decade of the MEME . A meme is the combining of an image or video with a catchphrase and then it 's spread on the internet or even by texting from person to person . Some of the most popular memes in the past decade are the ones with Kermit the Frog sipping tea , the white cat meme and the toddler clenching a fist full of sand . If a popular meme gets licensed , it could be a big money maker for the meme 's creator . <p> So as we say so long to the 2010s , let 's remember the cultural journey of the last ten years . <p> Copyright 2019 Scripps Media , Inc .
