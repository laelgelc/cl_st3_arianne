If you see comments in violation of our community guidelines , please report them . <h> Climate change is real <p> Writing from Madrid , Spain at the United Nations Climate Change Conference ( COP25 ) I am struck with the global passion for reversing the crisis that has the planet in a death grip . What is overwhelmingly striking to me as a man of deep Christian faith is the profound impact that climate change is having on those whom Jesus would call " the least of these . " <p> Across the globe indigenous people , economically disadvantaged populations , marginalized cultures , and politically disenfranchised communities are being displaced by the environmental realities of global climate change . As sea levels gradually rise , catastrophic weather extremes such as flooding rains and debilitating droughts , and catastrophic ecological events such as hurricanes and wildfires decimate whole regions of the planet , it is often the most vulnerable of the global population that suffers the greatest due to climate change . <p> Facing the issue in Carlsbad reminds me a lot of the potential sink hole at the South " Y. " For years scientists and engineers have been warning that the collapse of that cavity is not a matter of if , but rather a matter of when . Yet , due to partisan political wrangling and a pervasive belief that it would never happen or that if it did , the impact would be minimal . In that case , many of the governmental leaders who had the power to make a difference , played down the crisis for many years . Fortunately , thanks to the activism of many from Carlsbad reaching out in Santa Fe and the willingness of local government to have some skin in the game , our State leadership realized that it was not a political problem or a local Carlsbad problem , but a human issue that deserved a genuine sacrifice to make right . <p> Climate change is no different . While here in Madrid I have had the opportunity to learn more about the catastrophic scope of the environmental damage that humanity is doing to this earth -- the only home humanity truly has . This , in spite of the fact that all of the World 's great religious faiths , including Christianity , rightly understand that care of the earth is part of one 's fidelity to our God . Sadly , in many cases , Christians have abandoned their sacred call for Creation Care and embraced the exploitation of the planet for personal gain . <p> Worse yet , many people are quick to down play the science , the global evidence , and the clear warnings coming from the environment . They say it is not their problem or , worse yet , that Climate change is just a hoax . If COP underscored anything for this pastor , it is that climate change is not only real , it is really deadly . It is killing our home . <p> Yet , getting mad at those who pollute the earth or downplaying the severity of climate change and getting angry at those who call for greater environmental responsibility will never solve the problem . Like the potential sink hole in Carlsbad , the solution lies in everyone coming to the table and facing the human catastrophe at hand across our planetary home . <p> Climate change is not an economic issue . It is a human survival issue . All the money in the world will be worthless if there is no world worth inhabiting . Climate change is not a partisan issue . We all breathe the same air and drink the same water that will be permanently tainted if things do not change course . Climate change is not some other nation 's problem . It is a global problem -- one that as Christians and citizens of one of the most influential nations on the planet , we have a sacred obligation to address . <p> Climate Change is real and it is really happening . It is destroying the one home that all humans share . There is work to be done , there are sacrifices to be made , and there are difficult discussions that need to happen . The future of Creation depends on it . <p> Read or Share this story :
