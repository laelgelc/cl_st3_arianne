( Scott Takushi / Pioneer Press ) <p> Not long ago , Roger Hess began toying with the idea that maybe it was time to part with 100 years of family history . <p> The fourth-generation business owner is still considering putting up for sale the lakeside commercial building that for generations has housed the Hess Hair Milk barber supply company at 1911 Rice St. His hope is that a federally designated " Opportunity Zone " around Lake McCarrons , intended to boost private investment in low-income areas , will do exactly that . <p> " As far as I know , it 's another reason for someone to buy your property , " said Hess on Tuesday , acknowledging that what he knows about the federal " Opportunity Zone " effort is limited . <p> Proponents believe that those with a few thousand dollars coming their way from the sale of stocks , bonds or real estate , Ramsey County 's urban and industrial areas might just be the investment opportunity they 're looking for . <p> In fact , there are 18 census tracts across St. Paul and one over the border in Roseville , where Hess Hair Milk is , that would allow investors to defer paying federal capital gains taxes on their newfound wealth for up to a decade . <p> When President Donald Trump signed the 2017 tax-cut bill into law , a piece of the legislation known as the Investing in Opportunity Act created tax incentives for private-sector investors to take their capital gains profits and funnel them to distressed areas . <p> In other words , by year 's end , a bumper crop of new housing , hotels , office buildings and even solar farms could get underway in 8,762 designated low-to-moderate income census tracts across the country -- including 18 such " Opportunity Zones " in St. Paul . <p> In many cases , returns on investment in low-income areas might not be enticing enough to generate much capital spending at all . <p> But proponents see major upsides . Real estate developer Jamie Stolpestad encourages cities to think of the Opportunity Zones as one part of a broader mix of anti-blight and anti-poverty solutions . " Communities are still grappling with the suitability of this tool , and it 's clear to us that this tool is not a silver bullet , " he said . <p> " I 've gotten a lot of calls about the Sears site , " said Stolpestad , a founding partner with MN-Oza , referring to the newly vacant property on Rice Street . " The whole project is in a zone . " <p> Mike Sturdivant , a director of real estate development with Paster Properties , said an Opportunity Zone on St. Paul 's East Side has already helped draw capital investment to Phalen Village . Paster plans to install an Aldi grocery and a medical clinic . <p> St. Paul and Ramsey County have n't been quite as public in their courtship of investors , but they 've recently collaborated to create materials highlighting their 19 Opportunity Zones , which will be shared online later this month . <h> RAMSEY COUNTY OPPORTUNITY ZONES <p> Here 's a closer look at 12 of Ramsey County 's " O-Zones , " based on their latest marketing materials : <p> Hamm's/Beacon Bluff -- Seventh and Arcade . St. Paul 's Housing and Redevelopment Authority owns 7 acres of land at the former Hamm 's Brewery , and the St. Paul Port Authority has assembled the Beacon Bluff business area at the former 3M headquarters , of which 2.2 vacant acres sit within this tract . The census tract contains part of the Dayton 's Bluff Heritage Preservation District , where income-producing properties qualify for preservation tax credits . A future Rush Line Bus Rapid Transit station is planned on Phalen Boulevard at Arcade Street . <p> Phalen Village . The St. Paul HRA owns more than 16 acres of land around Cub Foods and in the Ames Lake area to the south , where new housing has been proposed . A future Rush Line station is planned near Phalen Village . <p> East Frogtown . Key landmarks include Hmongtown Marketplace and the Frogtown Family Artist Lofts , which is seeking to refinance to fund capital improvements ; the Little Mekong cultural district and proximity to the Rondo cultural district offer ethnic festivals , shops and restaurants . The site of a former Saxon Ford car dealership between Rice and Marion streets offers potential for a full-block redevelopment . Former Saxon Ford properties between Galtier and Farrington streets are owned by the city HRA . The city hopes to someday extend Pierce Butler Route , improving industrial access . Access to Metro Transit 's Green Line light rail at Western Avenue and Rice Street stations . <p> Southwest Frogtown . Key Landmarks include Little Mekong cultural district and proximity to Rondo . The city 's HRA owns nearly 3 acres of land , including a former Hmong funeral home and a site owned in partnership with the Neighborhood Development Center at the northwest corner of Dale Street and University Avenue . The vacant Victoria Theater , a historic property , has drawn community partners interested in reopening it . There is Green Line access at Lexington Parkway , Victoria Street and Dale Street . <p> West Side Flats and District del Sol . Hunt Associates and Weidner Apartment Homes plan to build hundreds of housing units on a large site between Plato , Fillmore , Robert and Wabasha streets . The Latin-themed District del Sol cultural and commercial corridor follows Cesar Chavez Street . The city HRA owns roughly 8 acres of land , including sites at Cesar Chavez and Congress streets , and along Stryker Avenue . <p> Allianz Field soccer stadium and commercial heart of Midway . The Midway Shopping Center provides a 35-acre housing-hotel-and-retail opportunity surrounding the new 19,400-seat Allianz Field , home of Major League Soccer 's Minnesota United . Development opportunities include a Target store outlot along University Avenue and developer-owned land near the Wilder Foundation on Lexington Parkway . New housing is planned along South Snelling Avenue . Green Line access at Snelling Avenue , Hamline Avenue and Lexington Parkway stations . A-Line bus rapid transit access along Snelling . <p> Rice and Larpenteur to L'Orient . If Opportunity Zones are intended to provide a social good , the North End would be ripe for it . The area has the highest concentration of poverty in the city , the second highest unemployment rate and the most cost-burdened housing . Large percentages of foreign-born residents from Southeast Asia also make it a prime spot for a cultural corridor . The city 's 2019 city budget sets aside $1.8 million for " cultural destination areas " throughout the city , including street improvements , signage and other " place-making " activities , as well as general business growth . The St. Paul Port Authority has considered expanding the Arlington Jackson Business Center . <p> Rice and Larpenteur in Roseville . St. Paul , Roseville , Maplewood and Ramsey County have all contemplated development improvements near Rice Street and Larpenteur Avenue , where shared jurisdiction has brought added attention to a faded corner . There 's also the potential for reinvestment in retail between Larpenteur and Lake McCarrons .
