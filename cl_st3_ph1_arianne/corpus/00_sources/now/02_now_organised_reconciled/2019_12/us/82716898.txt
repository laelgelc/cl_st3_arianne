<h> Free Beacon names wrong Time Person of the Year <p> Time Person of the Year cover for 2019 ( Credit : Time/Photograph by Evgenia Arbugaeva for TIME ) <p> The Washington Free Beacon , a conservative news site , wrongly reported who Time magazine 's Person of the Year would be . <p> The Dec. 10 article by executive editor Brent Scher claimed that the whistleblower and impeachment witnesses would be named , but actually they ended up being Time 's Guardians of the Year . Greta Thunberg , the Swedish teen climate change activist , was named Person of the Year . <p> According to Mediaite , the Free Beacon 's article with the wrong announcement was " released just hours before " the actual Time announcement . <p> Scher told iMediaEthics , " We did reach out to Time , as we always do when reporting a piece , and did n't publish until after it declined to comment . " He declined to comment further . <p> iMediaEthics has written to Time to confirm the Free Beacon asked for confirmation before publication and if it responded with any information . <p> " Editor 's Note : This piece incorrectly stated that the whistleblower and civil servants who testified during impeachment were to be named TIME 's Person of the Year .
