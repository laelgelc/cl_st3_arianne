Bacon 's popularity is unwavering : Even with a focus on healthier eating nowadays , bacon remains one of the most popular food items in the U.S. , generating billions of dollars in annual sales . Alternatives to pork bacon : Turkey bacon is a popular alternative to pork-based bacon . Consumers may also find bacon made of beef , duck , lamb and venison .
