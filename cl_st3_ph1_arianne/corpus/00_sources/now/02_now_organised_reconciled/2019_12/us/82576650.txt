<p> One of the few things the current generation of government leadership excels at is passing vexing problems to future generations . <p> The latest example is further delay in shoring up fuel storage tanks at the Navy 's Red Hill facility , which caused alarm in 2014 when 27,000 gallons of fuel leaked just 100 feet above the aquifer that provides drinking water to residents from Moanalua to Hawaii Kai . <p> Though the aquifer was n't contaminated then , it raised fears of future leaks of the rusting World War II-era tanks that could spoil much of Oahu 's drinking water for decades . <p> Ernest Lau , chief engineer of the Honolulu Board of Water Supply , has repeatedly called for urgent action to either upgrade underground tanks to double-wall containment -- a tank within a tank -- or move the fuel to secure above-ground tanks . <p> " The potential for catastrophic environmental and economic damages caused by fuel releases from one or more of the 20 field-constructed tanks at Red Hill is quite high , " the BWS has warned . <p> But in 2015 the state Health Department and U.S. Environmental Protection Agency agreed to give the Navy until 2038 to upgrade its tanks , with promises the improvements could actually occur much sooner . <p> Instead , the Health Department and EPA now appear poised to give the Navy more time , until 2045 , to provide secondary containment or remove the fuel , despite public pleas for faster action at recent hearings on the new rules . <p> Until then the Navy promises only better cleaning , inspecting and repairing of tanks that hold up to 250 million gallons of fuel . <p> The slow walk has enjoyed support from top local officials so far . Gov . David Ige praised the controversial 2015 agreement as " the start of long-overdue action to make Hawaii safer , " while U.S. Sen. Brian Schatz called it a " pragmatic step forward to protect Oahu 's drinking water . " <p> The Navy is right that the tanks ca n't be reinforced or moved overnight , but it does n't take 25 years , either , when the potential risk is so high . <p> In Washington state last year , the Navy got approval to replace a smaller array of troublesome underground tanks at Naval Base Kitsap-Manchester just a year after the issue arose , with above-ground tanks to be phased in over six years . A similar upgrade of the Navy 's Point Loma fuel tanks in San Diego was completed in 2013 . <p> Who knows what competing priorities we 'll be facing in 2045 as climate change and other challenges bear down on us ? <p> The Red Hill solutions are relatively straightforward and need n't be a can kicked down the road . <p> Comments on the proposed rules can still be sent to the Health Department until Dec. 16 by email to **40;1311;TOOLONG or postal mail to UST Rules , 2827 Waimano Home Road No. 100 , Pearl City , HI 96782.
