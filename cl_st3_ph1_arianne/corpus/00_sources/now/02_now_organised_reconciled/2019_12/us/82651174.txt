And to reach those , the Iowa City Manager 's office released a report offering some details on how the plan will be put into action . <p> Initially , the goal was to reduce community-wide greenhouse gas by 26 to 28% by 2025 and 80% by 2050 . But in accordance with the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change or IPCC report , in August 2019 , the reduction goal was increased to 45% from 2010 levels by 2030 and net-zero carbon emissions by 2050 . The so-called 100-day report was released to show how the city would meet these new carbon emission goals . <p> " The important thing is that these climate actions are not things the city can do on its own , " said Brenda Nations , sustainability coordinator for Iowa City . " We do n't have jurisdiction over what people do in their buildings or choose for their transportation . " <p> The plan is divided into five sections : Buildings , Transportation , Waste , Adaptation , and Sustainable Lifestyle . Joseph Cress/Iowa City Press-Citizen <p> Iowa City 's buildings account for approximately 82% of its emissions . In order to reach net-zero , significant reductions and offsets will be necessary . A recent back-and-forth with the state 's Department of Public Safety resulted in city leaders being told mandating developers to use a more stringent energy code was not permitted . In addition , city staff wrote that because of MidAmerican Energy 's switch to more renewable sources of fuel , efforts would be focused on electrification and reducing natural gas use . <p> To get more residential buildings to turn towards electrification , the city is looking at offering financial incentives for offsetting the cost of converting appliances and heating equipment from natural gas to electric . Despite representing many fewer users , industrial buildings make up 27% of carbon dioxide emissions ( residential accounts for 22% ) . The plan would create a benchmarking system , requiring larger buildings to report annually their energy performance . This would be similar to an ordinance in Des Moines requiring 800 buildings to report their energy and water usage to the city . To help improve that performance , the city would also have an incentive program to target specific improvements at those properties . <p> " The idea is that by focusing on some of the greatest generators of emissions , we can make greater progress towards reducing emissions , " said Ashley Monroe , assistant city manager for Iowa City . <p> In addition , the city is looking to adjust height and density bonus policies to include stricter energy standards and tie in energy efficiency and tree planting requirements for all existing and future rental permits . <p> While not in stone , the plan does mention a potential project adding a utility-scale solar partnership between Iowa City and MidAmerican Energy . This would be in addition to solar projects in the works at Terry Trueblood Park and the Public Works Building . <p> A major driver of emissions is the number tailpipes driving around town . The City Council has established a goal to double transit ridership by 2028 . In addition , the plan looks at ways to ensure renewable fuels are used to power the fleet . While the transportation study is n't due until next year , the plan still offers some ways for the city to decrease emissions . <p> Buy Photo <p> People hold up homemade signs during the " Town-Gown Climate Accord " hosted by the Iowa City Climate Strikers , Friday , Oct. , 4 , 2019 , at the intersection of Dubuque Street and Iowa Avenue in downtown Iowa City , Iowa . ( Photo : Joseph Cress/Iowa City Press-Citizen ) <p> Fare free services or discounted passes could attract new riders to use buses . Adjusting parking standards could decrease the number of people using cars . In addition to increasing the electric vehicle charging sites , the city will also work to upgrade its fleet to electric . <p> The city 's goal is to reduce the amount of waste reaching the landfill 50% by 2025 and 80% by 2050 . This will range from educating users on how to sort compost and recycling from the rest of the garbage to offering incentives to construction teams that divert material from the landfill . Another addition to the 100-day plan is a Methane Feasibility Study . The city would hire a consultant to look at the potential for energy generation at its Landfill and Wastewater Treatment Facility . <p> Jane Wilch , recycling coordinator for Iowa City , said even with the city 's population growing , they saw a 10% reduction in the amount of waste sent to the landfill . In addition , the 16,125 households that have curbside collection increased the tonnage recycled by 25% and the amount of yard waste composted by 72% . <p> The " adaptation " part of the Climate Action and Adaptation Plan is an important piece . Implied in the city 's climate crisis declaration is the notion that we can not go on living as we have . This part of the plan establishes the creation of " resilience hubs , " which will serve as gathering spaces during extreme weather events . The city will also increase its tree planting by requiring private developers to include trees in site plans and pursuing it in city right-of-way and parks . <p> To create more avenues for sustainable living , the city is looking for behavioral changes that will have an impact on emissions . These range from incentives for local food production , developing local green procurement policies and expanding community gardens . <p> Part of enacting this plan includes the creation of a Division of Climate Action and Outreach in the City Manager 's office . Brenda Nations will continue in her role as coordinator inside this new office . She will be joined by a Climate Action Engagement Specialist who will work in outreach . They will also hire an analyst position for the office who will be in charge of data collection , reporting and grant writing . <p> Finally , the report recommended the city fund the bulk of its climate action activities through the emergency levy . This source could raise approximately $1 million for the city 's climate effort . According to the report , the continued reduction of the debt service levy should mean the increase in the emergency levy will not result in a tax rate increase . <p> " We were tasked with this . We created these proposals . It 's not perfect , " Monroe said . " Now again , we really tried to tailor it for Iowa City , our community , our region to best serve the things that happen here .
