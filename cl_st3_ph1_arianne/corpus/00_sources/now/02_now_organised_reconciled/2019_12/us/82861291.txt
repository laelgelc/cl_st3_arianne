<p> BEIJING , Dec 24 ( Reuters ) - China plans to promote investment in repairing environmental damage caused by mining , and wants a mixture of public and private entities to contribute , the Ministry of Natural Resources said on Tuesday . <p> More than 3.6 million hectares of land in China was used and damaged by mining activities at the end of 2018 , the ministry said in a statement on its website . <p> Until now restoration has been held up by a lack of effective policies to stimulate investment , according to the ministry . <p> The ministry 's " market-oriented way " for the restoration process aims to encourage the repair and utilisation of mine land , including suitable use of abandoned soil and stones left over from mining . <p> It also aims to encourage social capital investment by granting land usage rights for abandoned state-owned construction land after the restoration is completed . <p> The plans , which will be in force for five years , will include all entities including private firms , according to the ministry .
