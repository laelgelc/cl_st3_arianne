<p> DUBAI , Dec 7 ( Reuters ) - A Chinese-American detained in Iran for three years on spying charges was freed and an imprisoned Iranian was released by Washington in return , President Donald Trump and an Iranian official said on Saturday . <p> The prisoner swap took place at a time of particularly strained relations between the longtime foes . <p> Trump said Chinese-American Xiyue Wang was returning to the United States . <p> An Iranian official said that Iranian Massoud Soleimani had been freed from detention in the United States . <p> In a statement issued by the White House , Trump made no mention of the freeing of Soleimani but thanked the Swiss government for its help in negotiating Wang 's release . <p> " Freeing Americans held captive is of vital importance to my Administration , and we will continue to work hard to bring home all our citizens wrongfully held captive overseas , " he said . <p> " Glad that Professor Massoud Soleimani and Mr. Xiyue Wang will be joining their families shortly . Many thanks to all engaged , particularly the Swiss government , " he tweeted . <p> Switzerland represents U.S. diplomatic interests in Iran , since Washington and Tehran cut diplomatic ties shortly after the 1979 Islamic Revolution in Iran . <p> Hua Qu , Wang 's wife , welcomed her husband 's release . <p> " Our family is complete once again . Our son Shaofan and I have waited three long years for this day and it 's hard to express in words how excited we are to be reunited with Xiyue , " she said in a statement . <p> " We are thankful to everyone who helped make this happen . " <p> Wang , a U.S. citizen and Princeton University graduate student , was conducting dissertation research in Iran in 2016 when he was detained and accused by Iran of " spying under the cover of research , " an allegation his family and the university deny . <p> He was subsequently convicted on espionage charges and sentenced to 10 years in prison in 2017 . <p> Soleimani , a stem cell expert , was arrested at Chicago airport in October 2018 for allegedly attempting to export biological materials to Iran in violation of U.S. sanctions on Iran over its nuclear programme . <p> Washington has demanded Iran release the Americans it is holding , including father and son Siamak and Baquer Namazi ; Michael R. White , a Navy veteran imprisoned last year , and Robert Levinson , a former FBI agent missing since 2007 . <p> Several dozen Iranians are being held in U.S. prisons , many of them for breaking sanctions . <p> Tensions have heightened between Iran and the United States since Trump last year pulled Washington out of a 2015 nuclear deal between Iran and six major powers and reimposed sanctions that have crippled Tehran 's economy . <p> In return , Iran has gradually scaled back its commitments to the agreement .
