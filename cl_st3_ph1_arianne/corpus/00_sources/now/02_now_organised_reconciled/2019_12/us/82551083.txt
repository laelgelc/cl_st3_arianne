<p> The National Audubon Society recently announced the dates for the 120th annual Christmas Bird Count -- December 14 through January 5 . <p> The Christmas Bird Count is the longest-running wildlife survey in the world , employing tens of thousands of bird-loving volunteers to gather data on the number and type of bird species found during the peak of migration . <p> Individual counts take place in a 15-mile-wide circle and are led by a compiler responsible for organizing volunteers and submitting observations to Audubon . Within each circle , participants tally all birds seen or heard that day -- not just the species , but total numbers to provide a clear idea of the health of that particular population . <p> Data from Christmas Bird Counts have been used in more than 200 peer-reviewed , scientific articles , including Audubon 's landmark , " Birds and Climate Change Report . " <p> There is no fee to participate and the quarterly report , " American Birds , " is available online . Counts are open to birders of all skill levels .
