<h> News <h> Pellegrini promised new strategy for climate protection <p> Slovakia decided to reduce the generation of electricity from coal . Slovakia understands the need to take steps related to climate changes and is ready for the necessary transformation to protect the climate . <p> These were the words of PM Peter Pellegrini ( Smer ) at the UN Climate Change Conference COP25 held in Madrid.This is why the country increased its ambitions and support to accelerate changes to achieve climate neutrality by 2050 , he added . <p> " Slovakia is already projecting these ambitions into strategic planning , especially in our Energy and Climate Plan by 2030 and the Low-Carbon Technology Development Strategy by 2050 , " said Pellegrini , as quoted by the TASR newswire.Reducing electricity production from coalThis strategy should be completed early next year , he said , adding that Slovakia supports the plan to make Europe the first carbon-neutral continent . <p> Pellegrini pointed out that Slovakia has already decided to reduce electricity generation from coal , leading to a gradual cancellation of the brown coal mining in some regions . At the same time , he stressed the need for social security in the region after the mines are shut down . <p> The government 's plan to help the Upper Nitra region after the local mines are closed has already been approved by the European Commission.Pellegrini also announced a plan to increase the emission reduction targets from 12 to 20 percent by 2023 . He pointed to an increase in energy efficiency and renewable energy targets by 2030 . Change of thinkingSlovakia has decided to set aside another Pound 2 . <p> 5 billion for investments in climate change and the environment , Pellegrini continued . " Slovakia has contributed for the first time to the Green Climate Fund this year , and plans to continue in the coming years , " Pellegrini noted , as quoted by TASR . <p> He called for joint action in this respect . " The fight against climate change will require strong partnerships from global to local levels and across all sectors , " he stressed , as quoted by TASR . <p> " We must not allow fear of losing political popularity or profit from the business to be greater than all rational arguments .
