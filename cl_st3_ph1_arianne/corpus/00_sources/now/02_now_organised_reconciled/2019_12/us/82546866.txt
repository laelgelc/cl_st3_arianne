<h> Buttigieg Releases Details About His Work at McKinsey <h> Rival candidate Elizabeth Warren called for disclosure of his client list at the consulting firm <p> Democratic presidential candidate Pete Buttigieg said much of his work at McKinsey &amp; Co. , including the names of clients served , is covered under a confidentiality agreement . Photo : John Locher/Associated Press <p> WATERLOO , Iowa -- Democratic presidential candidate Pete Buttigieg , responding to questions about his past employment , released a detailed timeline and lengthy statement Friday evening about his nearly three years at McKinsey &amp; Co . <p> The new information comes after Massachusetts Sen. Elizabeth Warren , a top nomination rival , this week called on Mr. Buttigieg , the mayor of South Bend , Ind. , to disclose the names of his clients while he was at the consulting powerhouse . <p> In his statement , Mr. Buttigieg said much of his work at McKinsey , including the names of clients served , is covered under a confidentiality agreement he signed when he started working at the firm . <p> " I am today reiterating my request that McKinsey release me from this agreement , and I again make clear that I authorize them to release the full list of clients I was assigned , " Mr. Buttigieg wrote in the statement . " This company must recognize the importance of transparency in the exceptional case of a former employee becoming a competitive candidate for the U.S. presidency . " <p> Buttigieg 's campaign said it inquired about the scope of the confidentiality agreement in June and did so again last month , including a request to be released from the confidentiality agreement in full . " To date , the company has not agreed , " the statement said . <p> " I understand why some are calling on me to break the agreement , " Mr. Buttigieg wrote . " But it 's important to me to keep my word and commitments . " <p> At a candidate forum here focused on city issues , Mr. Buttigieg was asked about McKinsey during an interview with Chicago Mayor Lori Lightfoot , a Democrat . <p> " This was my first job out of school , " Mr. Buttigieg said . " It 's not like I was the CEO . I was making a lot of spreadsheets and PowerPoints . " <p> Mr. Buttigieg said he has released as much information as he can " without breaking the promise I made in writing . " <p> When Ms. Lightfoot encouraged him to violate his nondisclosure agreement , Mr. Buttigieg responded , " I 'm going to give them the chance to do the right thing , and then we 'll take it from there . " <p> Asked later by reporters how long he 'd give the firm to release him from the agreement before potentially following Ms. Lightfoot 's advice , Mr. Buttigieg declined to provide a timeline . He also said he would consider releasing a copy of the agreement as well as the names of his fundraisers . <p> On the campaign trail , Mr. Buttigieg has distanced himself from what is the main private-sector job on his r ? sum ? . <p> McKinsey has come under scrutiny from some liberals who are critical of big corporations and elite consultants . In addition , as part of legal battles related to the nation 's opioid epidemic , McKinsey consultants helped the maker of OxyContin , Purdue Pharma , boost sales and counter Drug Enforcement Administration efforts to reduce opioid use , according to a civil complaint by the Massachusetts attorney general . <p> In May , the firm said it was no longer helping Purdue Pharma and would stop all other work related to the sale of the pain pills that have been blamed in the epidemic . <p> In the past , Mr. Buttigieg has suggested that McKinsey , which also has ties to the Enron Corp . scandal , needs to be more careful in picking its clients . <p> Mr. Buttigieg worked at the firm after attending Oxford on a Rhodes scholarship and before military service . In his book , he described the time as critical to both his real-world learning and a discovery that business was n't fulfilling enough for him to build a career helping to boost profits . <p> In outlining his work , Mr. Buttigieg said that he was assigned to " months-long stints on ' teams ' of typically three or four people working on a study " for a client . <p> " The bulk of my work on these teams consisted of doing mathematical analysis , conducting research , and preparing presentations , " he wrote . " I never worked on a project inconsistent with my values , and if asked to do so , I would have left the firm rather than participate . " <p> In 2007 , Mr. Buttigieg said he worked in Michigan on a project for a " nonprofit health insurance provider " for approximately three months , doing on-the-job training and performing analytical work as part of a team identifying savings in administrative and overhead costs . <p> The following year , he said , he worked in the Toronto area for about six months to help a grocery and retail chain analyze the " effects of price cuts on various combinations of items across their hundreds of stores . " <p> That same year , in Chicago , he said , he helped " a division of a consumer goods retail chain on a project to investigate opportunities for selling more energy-efficient home products . " <p> Mr. Buttigieg said he was in Connecticut for parts of 2008 and 2009 . " I worked on a project co-sponsored by a group that included the Environmental Protection Agency , the Energy Department , the Natural Resources Defense Council , other nonprofit environmental groups , and several utility companies , to research opportunities to combat climate change through energy efficiency . " <p> Also in 2009 , he said , he worked in California to help " an environmental nonprofit group " on a study to research opportunities in energy efficiency and renewable energy . That same year , he said , he also worked in Washington , with visits to Iraq and Afghanistan . " I served a U.S. Government department in a project focused on increasing employment and entrepreneurship in those countries ' economies , " he wrote . <p> Next , in 2009 and 2010 , Mr. Buttigieg said , he worked in Washington to help a " logistics and shipping provider working to identify and analyze " potential new sources of revenue . <p> " To the best of my recollection , these are all of my client engagements during my time with the firm , but a full release from McKinsey will allow the American public to see the full scope of my work , " Mr. Buttigieg wrote . <p> WSJ opens select articles to reader conversation to promote thoughtful dialogue .
