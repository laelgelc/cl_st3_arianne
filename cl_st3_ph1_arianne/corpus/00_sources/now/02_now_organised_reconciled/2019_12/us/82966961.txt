<p> With climate change being one of the world 's most pressing concerns , the US is certain to head down the path of 100 percent renewable energy . But to reach the end goal of a carbon-neutral environment , everybody has to be on board , specifically homeowners . And according to recent studies , it seems that homeowners are more on board than ever . <p> An article published on Pew Research Center written by Brian Kennedy and Carly Lynn Thigpen states , " ... most U.S. adults prioritize developing alternative energy sources for the country such as solar or wind power rather than increasing U.S. exploration and production of fossil fuels ( 77% vs. 22% ) . " <p> Nicki Zvik , founder of Green Solar Technologies , comments , " At GST , we hear countless reasons why homeowners choose to go solar , and we seem to get an even mix of people who are concerned about the environment and people who want to lower their electricity bills . So it 's not shocking that more homeowners are deciding to make the switch to solar everyday , which is good news for the solar industry and for the green energy movement . " <p> This roughly even mix of people who are concerned about the environment and people who want to lower their electricity bills seems to be a common finding with the article also mentioning , " Homeowners point to a variety of reasons for considering solar panels . A large majority of homeowners who have already installed or have given serious thought to installing solar panels say they want to help the environment ( 87% ) . And almost all ( 96% ) say they want to save money on utility bills . A recent report also found that installing solar panels can increase a home 's value . " <p> The same Pew Research Center article goes on to note , " About half of homeowners in the South Atlantic states ( 51% ) say they are considering installing solar panels , up 20 percentage points from 2016 . Homeowners in other Southern states ( the East and West South Central region ) tend to express more interest in installing solar panels ( 45% in 2019 compared with 34% in 2016 ) ... Western homeowners are particularly likely to say they have already installed solar panels at home . Some 14% of homeowners in Pacific states and 17% of homeowners in the Mountain region have done so . " <p> Zvik comments , " At GST , we definitely notice the eagerness of our West Coast customers to go solar , and it 's one of the reasons we love being based in Southern California . There 's an energy here in Los Angeles that seems to go above and beyond when it comes to being environmentally conscious , and it 's good to see that energy spreading throughout the rest of the country as more and more homeowners decide to go solar .
