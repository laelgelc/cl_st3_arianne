<h> City leaders working tomaintain quality of life <p> Monte Sereno residents enjoy living in a beautiful hillside community with a high quality of life . Monte Sereno City Council members and valued professional city staff work hard together as a team to deliver essential services to residents to maintain that quality of life . <p> Through extensive citizen outreach , resident feedback and an open public process , the current Monte Sereno City Council has implemented increased fiscal transparency , invested in quality public infrastructure , and adopted new building standards to help combat climate change . <p> The Monte Sereno City Council will continue working hard in 2020 to ensure Monte Sereno is operating at optimum service levels . Council is committed to working together in a positive and collaborative fashion to tackle important issues like state housing mandates , emergency preparedness , and long-term fiscal stability of the city . <h> More in Letters to the Editor <p> We encourage you to submit letters that share your opinions on issues of interest to residents in our community . Letters may be edited for length and clarity , and we 'll let you know if your letter is submitted for publication .
