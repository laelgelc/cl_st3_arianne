If you see comments in violation of our community guidelines , please report them . <p> OPINION <h> Letter : The truth about energy <p> Renewable energy or nonrenewable energy ? That is the question . Recently , there has been a lot of uproar surrounding the dangers of renewable energy sources , specifically wind power . Let 's take a look at the facts . <p> First off , there is a wild conspiracy that the renewable energy industry has concocted a climate change conspiracy in order to make loads of profits . If this is the case , the renewable energy industry is failing miserably as they only account for 17% of total energy output in the U.S. No only that the fossil fuel industry ( according to Forbes ) has outspent the entire environmental sector 10 to 1 , in lobby and total political donations . in 2018 alone the fossil fuel industry spent a total of $200 million to block climate motivated policy . A little research will clearly show the influence that the fossil fuel industry has had on elections and policy that solely benefit themselves , while putting the renewables industry in a severe disadvantage . <p> Secondly , wind turbines do n't cause cancer , but pollution from coal powered power plant do . USA Today recently published an article highlighting how Southern Indiana has some of the worst air quality in the world , due solely to the fact that there are 7 coal powered power plants within a 30-mile radius of Evansville . Could this possibly explain our high cancer rate as well ? Also , natural gas ( widely considered the safest of all fossil fuels ) has its drawbacks as well including : groundwater contamination , added methane pollution , gas explosions , fracking induced earthquakes , and large volume water usage in water deficient areas . Not to mention the fact that the increase in greenhouse gases leads to more volatile weather patterns . Coal is the least efficient energy source that is being used , and unfortunately the most widely used . If you disagree look up the cost of equipment and fuel it takes to operate a coal mine . <p> Do n't get me wrong there are issues with renewable energy sources too . Wind turbines may bring added noise pollution and as we are finding out we are going to have to address the Doppler radar issue . If we keep adding to the climate crises by polluting with fossil fuels the radar system wo n't do us much good anyhow . <p> We can address these issues or continue to depend on the dying fossil fuel industry , all while poisoning ourselves and putting our children 's future at risk . Or we can support clean energy sources . The choice is yours ! <p> - John Blankenberger <p> Read or Share this story : https : **39;1397;TOOLONG ...
