<h> Calpers : One-Fifth of Public Holdings Exposed to Climate Risk <p> About one-fifth of the California Public Employees ' Retirement System public markets portfolio is exposed to potential losses from climate change , the $381 billion pension fund manager reported this week in its first ever climate-risk disclosure report . <p> The report , mandated by a state law passed last year , will be presented at an investment committee meeting next week . <p> About 8% of Calpers ' holdings are in the energy sector , the report said , where " policy , market and technology changes may result in the loss of value and/or " stranding " of long-lived and carbon intense energy assets in our portfolio . " <p> Another 3% of the portfolio is in transportation , where government mandates and consumer preference , among other forces , " may result in the loss of value for fossil fuel-dependent transportation , " according to the report . <p> About 6% is in materials and buildings , and 3% in agricultural industries , both of which could lose value as a warming globe creates sea level rise and increases the frequency and intensity of drought , wildfire , hurricanes and other extreme weather . <p> The report showcased how Calpers and other large asset managers are using their holdings as leverage to pressure large polluters to reduce their carbon dioxide emissions , the main source of global temperature increases . <p> But the report also suggested much more needs to be done . Calpers in September was among insurers and pension funds managing $2.3 trillion globally that pledged to make their portfolios carbon neutral by 2050.
