( AP Photo/Nati Harnik ) <h> Is climate ' dereliction of duty ' an impeachable act ? <p> Hiding in the dazzle of impeachment is a tragedy of incomprehensible scale . It is the tragedy of delayed action on climate change . <p> During the three years of Donald Trump 's presidency , alarming scientific reports have indicated that the timescale on which climate changes are occurring is shorter than expected , and that previously underappreciated effects , such as loss of oxygen in the ocean , are rapidly becoming more severe . <p> President Trump has been an enemy of climate action from the start . His animosity toward established science has left the Unted States unable to exercise leadership in emissions mitigation or in supporting climate adaptation in threatened lands . <p> We will , no doubt , pay for this lapse in the not-so-long run .
