<p> SHANGHAI , Dec 30 ( Reuters ) - A Chinese court has sentenced the pastor of the Early Rain Covenant Church , Wang Yi , to nine years in prison on charges of inciting subversion of state power . <p> He has also been deprived of his political rights for three years , and 50,000 yuan of his personal property was confiscated as part of his sentencing , according to a statement on the website of the Chengdu court in the western province of Sichuan . <p> Wang was among dozens of churchgoers and leaders of the Early Rain Covenant Church detained by police in December a year ago . The church is one of China 's best known unregistered " house " churches .
