<h> Biden Tells NH Voters He Can Unite the Country <h> Former Vice President Joe Biden is trying to position himself as a candidate who can bridge the partisan divide . <p> With just six weeks until the presidential nominating contests begin , Joe Biden is fine-tuning what will soon be his final pitch to voters : that he is uniquely qualified to unite . <p> " I refuse to accept the proposition that we will be in a state of perpetual war with Republicans , because you ca n't govern the country if that 's the case , " the former vice president said . " We are a democracy . And a democracy depends upon consensus . We have to be able , we have to be able to pull the county together . " <p> Biden says most of his opponents do n't believe consensus is realistic . <h> Politics <p> It is a key theme of his remarks to an audience in Exeter , New Hampshire , as is his experience . <p> " With experience , hopefully , comes some judgment , and hopefully comes some wisdom , " he said . <p> On matters ranging from foreign policy to health care to climate change , Biden says no other candidate has his depth of knowledge . <p> " I 've passed more major legislation than everybody , combined , that 's running , " he said . <p> And there was a swipe at the most progressive candidates , Bernie Sanders and Elizabeth Warren , who promote free college and Medicare for all . <p> " Who among us do you think can implement what we say we want done ? " he asked . <p> Biden has maintained his national lead among Democrats despite the Ukraine controversy and his share of mishaps -- most recently his shift over the weekend from saying he would not comply with a subpoena in the president 's impeachment trial to saying he would comply . <p> He did not address that topic Monday . <p> With all this talk of unity , Biden was asked if he would consider a Republican for a running mate . He said he would , he just could n't think of one .
