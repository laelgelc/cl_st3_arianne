<p> President Trump said teenage climate activist Greta Thunberg should " work on her Anger Management problem , " reacting to a tweet congratulating Ms. Thunberg for being named Time magazine 's Person of the Year this week . <p> Mr. Trump said Thursday on Twitter that Ms. Thunberg should also " go to a good old fashioned movie with a friend ! Chill Greta , Chill ! " <p> Following Mr. Trump 's tweet , Ms. Thunberg changed her Twitter bio Thursday to read : " A teenager working on her anger management problem . Currently chilling and watching a good old fashioned movie with a friend . " <p> The 16-year-old Ms. Thunberg has sought to raise awareness about reducing carbon footprints by sailing across the Atlantic in a racing yacht . She has implored world leaders to do more to combat climate change , accusing them of " empty words " at a United Nations summit this year . <p> Time magazine Wednesday named Ms. Thunberg its 2019 Person of the Year , making her the youngest individual to receive the accolade . Mr. Trump , 2016 's Person of the Year , was a finalist this year . <p> In a statement announcing the choice , Time Editor-in-Chief Edward Felsenthal wrote that the magazine was honoring Ms. Thunberg " for sounding the alarm about humanity 's predatory relationship with the only home we have , for bringing to a fragmented world a voice that transcends backgrounds and borders , for showing us all what it might look like when a new generation leads . " <p> Mr. Trump has long expressed skepticism of global-warming trends , as a candidate calling them a hoax created by the Chinese , and has questioned the validity of climate science . He is also removing the U.S. from the Paris climate agreement , which aims to cap carbon-dioxide emissions and curb the global rise in temperatures . <p> The president has taken note of Ms. Thunberg before . After she spoke at the United Nations , he retweeted a video of her emotional remarks , adding : " She seems like a very happy young girl looking forward to a bright and wonderful future .
