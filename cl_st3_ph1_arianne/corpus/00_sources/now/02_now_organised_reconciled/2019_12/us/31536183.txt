<h> Bay Area 's top 10 stories of 2019 <h> Blackouts , fire , plunging crime and a crisis on the streets <h> Bay Area 's top 10 stories of 2019 <h> Blackouts , fire , plunging crime and a crisis on the streets <p> For the third year in a row , the biggest Bay Area news story emerged from California 's wildfire emergency . <p> But there was a twist : After the Wine Country fires grabbed headlines in 2017 and the Camp Fire in Butte County followed in 2018 , the No. 1 story in 2019 was not a deadly inferno -- but rather the effort to avoid one . <p> Pacific Gas and Electric Co. ' s precautionary move to black out millions of customers across Northern California brought a new and very personal dimension to the wildfire crisis , underscoring the dangers of climate change and the failures of the utility company that is central to our lives . <p> PG&amp;E 's goal was to make sure electrical lines did n't spark in high winds . Nonetheless , on Oct. 23 , a Sonoma County inferno named the Kincade Fire kicked up under lines that had n't been part of the shut-offs . <p> That was the second biggest local story of the year , according to a vote by our staff . <p> What other stories captured the imagination and defined the year ? <p> In reverse order , here are the top 10 Bay Area stories of 2019 : <p> The hazards of vaping became clearer this year . <p> ( Jessica Christian / The Chronicle 2018 San Francisco Chronicle ) <p> 10 . Teen vaping fuels e-cig backlash and bans <p> The popularity of e-cigarettes and market leader Juul of San Francisco had parents of high schoolers , and even middle schoolers , worried about a new path to addiction -- as if parenting were n't hard enough already . <p> San Francisco voters rejected a ballot measure that would have overturned a city-approved prohibition on the sale of vapes . The legislation goes into effect next month and will suspend the sale of e-cigs that have not passed a Food and Drug Administration review . The backlash comes as new research shows that vaping increases the risk of chronic lung illnesses . <p> The controversy also took a toll on Juul , which announced layoffs and saw its valuation slashed . <p> A boy studies in the school annex room at juvenile hall in San Francisco . <p> ( Scott Strazzante / The Chronicle San Francisco Chronicle ) <p> 9 . Chronicle reporting prompts planned closure of S.F. juvenile hall <p> The Chronicle 's series on the dramatic fall of juvenile crime in California , called Vanishing Violence , investigated the often inadequate response to the historic trend by public officials . Juvenile halls that were once expanded across the state emptied out , we found , while the costs per youth skyrocketed . <p> A Contra Costa County sheriff 's assists the investigation of a shooting during a Halloween party at an Orinda Airbnb rental . <p> ( Carlos Avila Gonzalez / The Chronicle San Francisco Chronicle ) <p> 8 . Five slain at Halloween party at Orinda Airbnb <p> The national toll of gun violence grew on a cool October evening , when shots rang out at a Halloween event billed as a " mansion party " at an Airbnb rental in a wooded Orinda neighborhood . Five people were killed , and the mass shooting shocked neighbors in a town where homicides of any kind are rare . <p> Two people face weapons-related allegations , but the scene was described as a chaotic shootout , and nobody has been charged by prosecutors with homicide -- despite a series of raids and police arrests . Under pressure over its role in the incident , Airbnb restricted house parties . <p> Paul Osaki ( left ) , Teresa Ono and Marjorie Fletcher pay their respects at a memorial for public defender Jeff Adachi in February . <p> ( Amy Osborne / Special To The Chronicle San Francisco Chronicle ) <p> 7 . Jeff Adachi 's death leads to illegal search of journalist <p> San Francisco Public Defender Jeff Adachi , one of the city 's most powerful leaders , died Feb. 22 at age 59 . One of the only elected public defenders in the nation , Adachi had expanded the traditional role of the office , fighting for legal reform and police accountability . <p> Three months later , it was the leak of a police report on Adachi 's death to local journalist Bryan Carmody that roiled San Francisco politics . Police with a signed search warrant stormed into Carmody 's home on May 10 , even though California 's shield law protects journalists from being forced to reveal sources . The raid sparked a national controversy amid larger concerns about attacks on the press . Police Chief Bill Scott defended the raid for two weeks before conceding it was wrong and apologizing . <p> At the center of some of the issues is rampant fare evasion . BART 's effort to crack down on fare cheats , though , had questionable results , and by the end of the year the agency was more focused on making its gates and fences more difficult to breach . <p> For reasons that remain unknown , a 19-year-old man went on a shooting rampage at the beloved Gilroy Garlic Festival in July , killing three people , including two children ages 6 and 13 , and wounding 17 others before killing himself . <p> The killer , who cut through a perimeter fence , carried a semiautomatic weapon that he had bought legally in Nevada three weeks earlier . The purchase would have been impossible in California , highlighting the impact of less stringent laws across the state 's borders . <p> Ronnie Dixson , who says he 's been homeless for 30 years , sleeps by a steam vent in front of the Asian Art Museum in June . <p> ( Noah Berger / Special To The Chronicle San Francisco Chronicle ) <p> 4 . Homelessness crisis expands across Bay Area and beyond <p> Homelessness spiked all over Northern California in 2019 , with Oakland seeing a 47% jump since 2017 and San Francisco 's biennial count rising 17% . City leaders pushed plans for affordable housing , shelter space and better mental health and addiction treatment , but there was no end in sight to the misery on the streets . <p> In October , a row of boulders placed on a street in San Francisco came to symbolize the tension over what to do about the growing number of tents . In Santa Rosa , the flash point was an encampment a mile long that took over a popular walking and biking trail . In Oakland , a seemingly endless string of large encampments confounded city officials as they tried to address their unhoused population with ideas including tiny homes . <p> On a statewide level , Gov . Gavin Newsom added $1 billion in new funding for homelessness initiatives , and his principal advisers on the issue suggested ramping up shelters throughout the state , but President Trump appeared to be ready to force some kind of action for clearing the streets . A showdown seems probable in the new year . <p> House Speaker Nancy Pelosi presides over the impeachment of President Trump this month . <p> ( Saul Loeb / AFP / Getty Images San Francisco Chronicle ) <p> 3 . Nancy Pelosi leads House Democrats in Trump impeachment <p> Pelosi had helped President Barack Obama enact landmark legislation , including the Affordable Care Act . Now , a big part of her legacy will be the impeachment of President Trump , shaping how she is remembered after more than 30 years in Congress . After the House voted mostly along party lines , Trump became only the third president impeached in American history . <p> Motorist Claire Miller waits for evacuation orders to be lifted during power shut-offs amid an October fire in Moraga . <p> ( Nick Otto / Special To The Chronicle San Francisco Chronicle ) <p> 1 . PG&amp;E 's preemptive shut-offs cut power to millions <p> The story of California 's blackouts captured attention for weeks . Everyone wanted to know whether they would lose power , when it would happen , and how long it would last . <p> Most agreed with the embattled utility company 's claim that it had no choice but to de-energize millions of customers to head off possible wildfires . But PG&amp;E 's handling of the shut-offs was poor at times , as was its communication . The ordeal left customers with a range of questions , some practical and others a bit extreme : Should I buy a generator ? Is California now an unlivable hellscape ?
