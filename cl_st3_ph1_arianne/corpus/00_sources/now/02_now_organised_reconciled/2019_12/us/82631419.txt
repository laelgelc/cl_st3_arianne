<h> Municipal vulnerability preparedness to be discussed <h> Wednesday <p> Dec 11 , 2019 at 3:01 AM <p> SOMERSET -- The Town of Somerset will hold a public listening session on municipal vulnerability preparedness on Dec. 16 at the Somerset Council on Aging . The session will be held from 6 p.m. to 7 p.m. at the Somerset Council on Aging , located at 115 Wood St. in Somerset . <p> The Town of Somerset is participating in the Commonwealth 's Municipal Vulnerability Preparedness ( MVP ) program . This program provides support for towns in Massachusetts to plan for and implement projects that mitigate the impacts of climate change and natural hazards . <p> Communities that complete the MVP program become certified as an MVP community and are eligible for state grant funding and other opportunities . <p> The Town of Somerset will continue the MVP certification process by presenting the results of a draft of the Municipal Vulnerability Preparedness Report to the public at a formal public information and listening session scheduled for Dec. 16 at 6 p.m. at the Somerset Council on Aging . This session will provide an opportunity for any member of the interested public to learn about the MVP process and provide feedback about the MVP workshop and recommended highest priority actions resulting from the workshop . <p> The goal of the MVP planning process is to raise awareness about the dangers posed from natural hazards and identify priority actions to improve resilience to the significant natural hazards that the community faces . On Oct. 15 , community members participated in a workshop with local and regional leaders to characterize the hazards facing the community , identify community vulnerabilities and hazards , identify and prioritize community actions , determine overall priority actions for the community .
