<h> Saying what we will do , and doing what we say : Implementing a sustainable and resilient grid <p> During last week #COP25 , high-emitting countries did not send a clear enough signal that they are ready to improve their climate strategies and ramp up ambition through the Nationally Determined Contributions they will submit next year . On the lack of political leaders commitment , the Executive Secretary of UN Climate Change Secretariat , Patricia Espinosa , said on the outcome : <p> " We need to be clear that the conference did not result in agreement on the guidelines for a much-needed carbon market -- an essential part of the toolkit to raise ambition that can harness the potential of the private sector and generate finance for adaptation . Developed countries have yet to fully address the calls from developing countries for enhanced support in finance , technology and capacity building , without which they can not green their economies and build adequate resilience to climate change . " <p> Energy has always represented a challenge for the development of countries and economies , but it 's even more important for islands , isolated and/or remote communities . This challenge is now bigger in many regions of the World because of the stress caused by Climate Change to the infrastructure with even more important storms such as the monstrous Category 5 Hurricane Dorian in the Atlantic Ocean . We should not wait for other catastrophic failures that , we know , will happen more often and much bigger . Let 's first look at two recent examples : the PG&amp;E failure in Calfornia and to the struggle in the Bahamas . <p> Despite the abundance of natural resources in both cases . They have been unable to turn efficiently to renewable forms of energy . " Power actually went out earlier than expected and caught people a little off guard , " said Amy Ricard , a spokeswoman for Sonoma County in California . " It 's been a rolling effect , " she said . In the Bahamas , some are saying that it is due to multinational companies ' control over the islands ' electrical grid . The government 's use of legal roadblocks bars Bahamians from utilizing alternative energy to bypass the " failing " electrical grid , explains Professor Erica Moiah James of the University of Miami . <p> The fallout after Hurricane Dorian and the fires and severe weather conditions that have heightened intense wildfires across California ( where some of the most destructive blazes were caused by PG&amp;E ) highlights the many ways in which the islands have been shortchanged in the global climate crisis as organizations scramble to locate hundreds of missing people . Infrastructure shortfalls such as the weak electrical grid directly affect rescue operations . The director-general of the Bahamas Ministry of Tourism and Aviation , Joy Jibrilu , details the damage left in the aftermath from Hurricane Dorian and what the Bahamas will need to move forward especially on the infrastructures . <p> Executive Secretary Espinosa added , on the COp25 outcomes : " At the same time , in the final decision texts , governments did express the need for more ambition by Parties and non-State actors alike , and they agreed to improve the ability of the most vulnerable to adapt to climate change . ( ... ) A large group of countries , regions , cities , businesses and investors signaled their intention to achieve #netzero CO2 emissions by 2050 , as part of the Climate Ambition Alliance led by Chile . " <h> Integrating AI with other technology can be the solution <p> The increasing demand in power represents a challenge for existing electrical networks and future smart grids in remote communities . It contributes to electricity peak demand , which is also increasing substantially . In this context , renewables ( RE ) can play an important role in shaving the peak demand burdening the electrical grid as soon as it is well integrated into the electric supply . The solution here , combining RE with a mix of energy storage technologies using Machine Learning tools that will allow for improved remote or isolated communities infrastructure and operation , and responsible energy supply that benefits these communities . This approach improves economic development and delivers environmental sustainability for future generations as well as local grid stability and resiliency . <p> Indeed , Artificial Intelligence and integrated energy storage can be used to develop demand-side management ( DSM ) strategies able to shift the load from peak to off-peak hours ( thus exploiting the potential for price arbitrage ) even in presence of renewable energy production . Demand-side management is a means to increase the overall efficiency of the entire electricity network -- from generation to the end-use -- which consists of optimizing the allocation of resources , limiting the peak demand and shaping the demand profile depending on the necessity of the grid . Many solutions are possible . But doing nothing , waiting for the next storm is not . I want to present one option : Hybrid storage integrating Electric Energy Storage ( EES ) and Thermal Energy Storage ( TES ) using a Synthetic Phase Change Material ( SPCM ) to optimize energy efficiency and load levelling for Renewable Energy and Critical Processes . <p> What we generally see in the energy storage field are different storage technologies acting as competitors and not as potential collaborators . This is the paradigm . In fact , no single technology could easily compete with the energy density of cheap fossil fuel . It is important to find ways for these technologies to work together . That would not only help to increase system efficiency but also lead to better energy management . <h> A " non-choice " today will impact our lives for years , decades <p> In addition , investing in an energy asset is often a mid-term or a long-term choice , with longer depreciation period . A choice or awaiting to chose a solution today will have consequences for the next 5 , 10 , 15 years or more . Choosing to invest in one or more renewable or recovery assets today may be a long-term paying choice because it allows to diversify the energy mix and decrease the dependence on traditional energies , whose prices may be particularly erratic . Moreover , such a strategy allows also , at least in part , to overcome the increase in the price of fuel , electricity or even to a lesser extent the CO2 credit market . <p> The integrated hybrid energy storage systems merit a closer look as part of comprehensive Renewable and/or energy storage deployment strategies . The technology has demonstrated the potential to achieve double-digit percentage decreases in CAPEX and OPEX , increase system operating life , and boost revenues by simultaneously providing multiple services , this hybrid solution may be a change in the ' single storage ' paradigm . Of course , the energy management hardware and software needed to manage two different storage technologies for multiple use cases are not trivial , and development will definitely continue with the applications . The ultimate objective , with this solution , is to help the many remote communities that have peak electricity demand rapidly approaching or have already reached their generation capacity . <p> Anyways , the cost of inaction is rapidly becoming significantly higher than the cost of action , said Paul Polman , Co-Vice Chair of the UN Global Compact Board and former Unilever CEO . He made a passionate call-to-action earlier this fall , at the 2019 UN SDG Business Forum . So , let 's start to put solutions in place and make sure our communities become more sustainable , more autonomous and get ready before the storms . P.S.
