<p> DOHA , Dec 14 ( Reuters ) - U.S. Treasury Secretary Steven Mnuchin said on Saturday a factsheet on " phase one " of a trade deal between the United States and China would come out on Saturday or Sunday . <p> Mnuchin told the Doha Forum conference in Qatar that the accord with China aims to create more reciprocal trade relations for many years , and that it would be " very good " for global growth . <p> Mnuchin also rejected accusations that Washington has been weaponising the U.S. dollar , saying that if it was not careful with sanctions , people would start using other currencies .
