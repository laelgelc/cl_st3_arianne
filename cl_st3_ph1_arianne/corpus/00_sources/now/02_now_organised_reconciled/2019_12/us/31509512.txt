<h> Trammell Crow : Trump has veered dangerously from the conservative legacy of protecting our air , water and natural resources <h> Conservation conservatives like me can no longer remain quiet . <p> For most of its history , the Republican Party has made it part of its mission to protect our natural places and resources . From President Abraham Lincoln , who first protected Yosemite , to George H.W. Bush , who strengthened the Clean Air Act , Republicans have worked to create a cleaner , healthier world . <p> I was raised Republican and have been a staunch and proud conservative my whole life . I voted for President Donald Trump in 2016 . I even donated to his campaign . <p> But on environmental issues , the president has veered dangerously from the conservative legacy of protecting our air , water and natural resources , to the point where " conservation conservatives " like me can not remain quiet . <p> There are many things I appreciate about Trump . Under his leadership , the economy has grown , unemployment has fallen , and long-stagnant institutions have been reformed . Difficult issues like immigration and border security are now being addressed . Trump and his administration are working to build a country where free enterprise and businesses can thrive . It 's a vision I support . <p> That is why the president 's consistent and persistent attacks on the environment are so bewildering and wrongheaded . A clean environment and free enterprise are not opposites -- each supports the other . <p> Cleaner air has prevented hundreds of thousands of premature deaths , avoided billions in health care costs , and reduced the amount of school and work missed due to respiratory illnesses . The Clean Water Act and other rules to improve water quality have helped spur economic growth from Boston Harbor to the Mississippi Delta , and led to more efficient uses of our water resources . <p> When it comes to the climate , if left unchecked climate change could cost our country hundreds of billions of dollars annually and our economy could contract by up to 10% by the end of the century . For conservatives who care about the long-term stewardship of our land and resources , and who endeavor to pass down a better country than the one we inherited , failing to act on climate change is not just bad for business , it is also morally irresponsible . <p> Solving environmental problems also creates tremendous business opportunities . Trump has repeatedly denigrated renewable energy , which has already created economic opportunity in Texas and around the country . Wind energy alone is now a multibillion-dollar industry and has thrived in Texas , which " generates more wind energy than the next three states combined , " according to Luke Metzger of Environment Texas . <p> We have an opportunity in Texas and around the country to build an economy that is healthy and clean , with appropriate , limited government regulation creating space and incentives for private entrepreneurs and businesses to compete to solve our environmental crises . <p> hidden ad unit <p> " unitName " : " dfpPosition1 " , " display " : " mobile " ... Yet , in the last three years , the Trump administration has attacked or wiped out over 80 different environmental safeguards , ranging from policies that help keep our air and water clean to ones that aim to curtail dangerous climate pollution . Some of these moves have bordered on the absurd : The administration 's latest action was to cut regulations on ponds holding coal ash , a toxic substance that can destroy waterways and harm human health . <p> Some of the administration 's actions have even been directly contrary to free enterprise , such as its efforts to prop up coal-fired power plants that can no longer compete with cleaner forms of energy such as cheap renewables . <p> In the 50 years since the first Earth Day , we 've made incredible strides in protecting our environment , even as the economy has continued to grow . Rivers no longer catch on fire , our air is cleaner and more of our natural spaces are protected . During this same time , real gross domestic product per capita has more than doubled . <p> Conservatives have played an important role in achieving those gains . Many of us are ready to go to work again to protect our land , water and climate for the next generation . <p> But with the nation 's most visible and powerful conservative , Trump , actively attacking our environment , that legacy is now at risk -- along with our air , water and climate . <p> It 's time for the president to join the ranks of Abraham Lincoln and Teddy Roosevelt as Republicans who have made America great by protecting our environment . If he refuses to do so , it will soon be time for conservation conservatives to hold him to account . <p> Trammell S. Crow is the founder of EarthX , formerly known as Earth Day Texas , and president of the Crow Family Foundation . He wrote this column for The Dallas Morning News .
