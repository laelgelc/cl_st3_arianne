<h> Cherokee Students Advance To Regional Social Studies Fair <h> More than 20 Cherokee County students placed first in competitions , advancing them to the regional social studies fair . <h> Dec 16 , 2019 10:35 am ET <p> A total of 121 students participated in the Cherokee Schools event recently held at Woodstock High School with 89 projects entered in the competition . ( Cherokee County School District ) <p> WOODSTOCK , GA -- The Cherokee County School District 's Social Studies Fair saw impressive participation , with 26 first-place winners advancing to the Northwest Regional Social Studies Fair in February . <p> Through the annual event , students explore social studies in the areas of sociology/psychology , history , anthropology , economics , geography and political science and complete a research project . A total of 121 students participated in the Cherokee Schools event recently held at Woodstock High School with 89 projects entered the competition . <p> " Social studies is a favorite subject of mine , and , having taught it for many years , it 's so exciting to see students engaged and excelling in this field , " Superintendent of Schools Brian Hightower said . " The topics they select always are fascinating , and their research and writing equally impressive . Congratulations and best wishes as you advance to the next level of competition !
