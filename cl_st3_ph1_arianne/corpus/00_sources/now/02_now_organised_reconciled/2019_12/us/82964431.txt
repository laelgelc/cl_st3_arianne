<p> DUBAI , Dec 29 ( Reuters ) - A blast struck a military graduation parade in Yemen 's southern town of al-Dhalea , Yemen 's Security Belt forces said in a tweet on Sunday , in an attack witnesses said caused dozens of injuries or deaths . <p> No claim of responsibility was made . <p> The Security Belt forces are part of a southern separatist front in south Yemen , and are backed by the United Arab Emirates in a fight against Yemen 's Iran-aligned Houthi group . <p> Witnesses told Reuters that a blast occurred near a guest platform during the parade and that dozens were injured or killed . They reported seeing bodies at the scene . <p> Yemen has suffered from almost five years of conflict since the Houthi movement ousted President Abd-Rabbu Mansour Hadi 's government in late 2014 . The coup drew military intervention in 2015 by a Saudi-led coalition , which includes the UAE , trying to restore Hadi 's internationally recognized government . <p> The town of al-Dhalea is controlled by southern separatist forces . It lies on the main south-to-north road linking the southern port of Aden -- controlled by Hadi 's government -- to the Houthi-controlled capital , Sanaa .
