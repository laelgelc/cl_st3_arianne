<p> UM 's Climate Action Movement group was joined by 31 other university student organizations in an open letter to UM 's Presidential Commission on Carbon Neutrality expressing " serious concerns " regarding the scope , pace and efficacy of progress the commission has made since forming nine months ago . <p> " Climate change presents an existential threat to the future of the students at this university and we are invested in pushing for a just transition to carbon neutrality by 2030 , " the letter reads . " However , given the structural issues plaguing the commission , the refusal to address those issues , and the demonstrated lack of progress , it is reasonable to interpret the process thus far as largely performative . " <p> The groups , which account for more than 3,700 students , were critical of UM 's pace of progress , noting it had not yet adopted a working or target date for carbon neutrality , refused to discuss divesting from fossil fuels in its endowment and its overall lack of accomplishments since launching in February , among various concerns . <p> UM spokesman Rick Fitzgerald said the university shares the sense of urgency on climate action issues , but differs with student advocates on approach to progressing toward carbon neutrality . <p> In a response to concerns previously expressed by the Climate Action Movement and One University Coalition student groups , UM President Mark Schlissel said he believes the university must rely on the research and ideas of its experts to determine how UM will become carbon neutral before committing to a date . <p> " A target date must be accompanied by an actionable plan for how ( to ) get to carbon neutrality , " Schlissel wrote . " Many organizations have struggled with this after setting a date in part because of the real-world challenges that must be overcome . We are committed to the hard work - up front - that will make our target meaningful , with actions that are spelled out and hold us to account . " <p> After members of the One University campaign and Climate Action Movement pressed the university on its progress during the regents ' Dec. 5 meeting , they emphasized their point by joining forces to block the main exit of the Richard Postma Clubhouse for nearly 45 minutes , preventing regents and other UM executives from leaving . <p> Activists at the meeting also criticized a proposed $50-million investment in Vendera Resources , a Dallas , Texas-based manager that invests in properties that produce oil and gas . The proposal went before the board and was rejected by the regents with what appeared to be a unanimous denial . <p> In addition to the call for committing to carbon neutrality by 2030 , the majority of the two groups requests were climate action-related , with the addition of extending the Go Blue Guarantee to each of UM 's three campuses . <p> In its first interim report released Dec. 2 , UM 's Presidential Commission on Carbon Neutrality outlined prospective work to explore carbon intensity of methane leakage in the natural gas supply chain and quantify indirect carbon emissions . <p> After accounting for a 2.3% methane leakage rate throughout the entire supply chain , the group estimated that expansion of UM 's Central Power Plant should still result in a cumulative reduction of more than 400,000 metric tons of carbon dioxide equivalent within the first 10 years of operation . <p> Schlissel said the university is not waiting idly to make concrete commitments to step toward carbon neutrality or announcing a timeline . <p> He said UM has completed actions necessary to get to a 25% reduction in its carbon footprint compared to 2006 , ahead of its 2025 target year , and will continue to pursue enhanced energy efficiency standards in construction projects and with campus transportation plans . <p> " To best address the complexity of the real-world challenges we face , we must align the science with the real-world needs of a world-class university , " Schlissel said . " We need to thoroughly explore our options and make smart plans that we know we can implement . "
