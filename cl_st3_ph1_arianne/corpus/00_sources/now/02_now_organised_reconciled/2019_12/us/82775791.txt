<p> Editor 's note : This story has been updated to clarify Bob Yates ' stance on the municipal utility and his position on the council . <p> Boulder late Thursday announced it had re-filed litigation to condemn Xcel Energy assets within the city in the latest move to form a municipal electric utility . <p> It is the second time this year the city has sought to condemn the company 's infrastructure in Boulder to obtain a price from a court for the city to buy the power system . But Boulder 's earlier attempt was dismissed in September by Boulder District Court because the city had yet to finish a state regulatory process meant to finalize a list of assets to be transferred in the condemnation proceedings . <p> The state gave the final green light to the city to proceed to court in the weeks following the court 's dismissal , giving Boulder hope it could complete its lengthy project to form a city-owned utility . However , the dismissal dashed hopes that a referendum including a price tag would be on the 2020 ballot for Boulder residents . <p> The city 's action Thursday signals Xcel did not respond positively to Boulder 's latest offer of $93.96 million for company assets outside substations , the infrastructure where high voltage is converted to electricity for consumption . That offer from the city followed an initial offer of $68.5 million in spring this year , and then a June increase to $82 million . <p> Both parties met to discuss the most recent offer but did not reach an agreement , a city news release said . <p> The city will not be required to pay the cost determined by the court until voters approve moving forward with creating the utility after the condemnation amount and other key project costs are publicized , and construction to separate Boulder 's electric distribution system from Xcel 's is complete . <p> Boulder 's latest litigation seeks to acquire Xcel assets outside substations . The city has committed to building three new substations as part of its project , and has shared a general idea of where each will be -- one on the far east side of the city , a second on the far west side and the third in a northeast area of the city -- officials have not shared specific locations . <p> City staff has also committed to analyzing the value of the municipal utility -- which Boulder initially promoted as a way to dramatically cut carbon emissions by fueling its grid with fully renewable energy -- in light of the new green power standards passed in the state Legislature , along with an Xcel promise to be carbon-free by 2050 . <p> State Rep. Edie Hooton , D-Boulder , also has plans to introduce a bill in the upcoming legislative session that would commission a study of a community choice energy model , by which local governments served by private utilities could choose alternative power providers , while paying the utility to run energy through its infrastructure to cut out carbon-reliant providers . <p> " I think there is an increasing democratization of the energy industry at the Legislature , " said Mayor Pro Tem Bob Yates , who has grown increasingly skeptical of the municipalization effort , told the Camera . <p> Mayor Sam Weaver at a City Council discussion earlier this month argued there are benefits aside from carbon reduction to the municipal utility project , which is running at a cost to the city in excess of $23 million since 2012 , with officials requiring more funding to continue the work . <p> " This is a typical muni conversation , where we focus on costs and talk a lot about emissions reduction , but there are two other values which are really important here , which is democratize and decentralize , " Weaver said . " The democratize part is the one I really think people undervalue . ... <p> " I 'll give you an example of why I think we care as a city a lot : because distribution system planning is going to be a big part of a resilient future in the face of climate change , " he continued . " What that means practically is burying the distribution lines , the most straight-forward thing so the tree branches do n't take them down . But it means microgrids , the ability for neighborhoods to island themselves from the grid and provide their own power .
