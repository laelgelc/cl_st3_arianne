<h> The Year in Diplo Photos <h> 365 days of vicarious living via Thomas Wesley 's Instagram . <p> Happy new year to everyone , but especially Diplo . The Random White Dude Be Everywhere is busier than anyone you know . Actually , we 're tired from compiling his year in pictures . Here it is , the look-back at Diplo you never asked for and did n't realize you needed . What will Diplo do in 2020 ?
