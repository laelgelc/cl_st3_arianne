In the coming weeks , the presidential search committee will be picking finalists to succeed Barchi , following an announcement over the summer that his contract will not be renewed once it ends in June . <p> Rutgers University faculty union members and students brought several demands to the Board of Governors meeting on Dec. 17 , 2019 , including higher pay and divestment from fossil fuel industry . ( Photo : Catherine Carrera ) <p> At the last Board of Governors meeting of the year , Barchi faced a familiar scene , representative of one of the themes from his presidency : union members approached the microphone one by one and urged the president to grant them better wages , saying they feel left in the shadows of the athletics department . <p> " About 600 postdoctoral researchers have been working under expired contracts since July 1 , " read a flyer the union members distributed . " Meanwhile , Rutgers Board of Governors approved $32 million for Greg Schiano -- more than the salaries of all 600 postdocs combined . " <p> Earlier this month , the board approved an eight-year contract for football coach Schiano , with an annual base pay of $4 million , plus several perks including a country club membership , access to a private jet for recruiting and thousands of dollars in bonuses if the team is successful . <p> About 1,400 union members who work as clinical and research physicians at Rutgers Biological and Health Sciences have also been negotiating a new contract for more than a year . <p> Postdoctoral union members say they 're tired of struggling to cover their needs with the $42,000 salaries they earn , while the medical school faculty union members say they want the university to meet them on pay equity , transparency with pay practices and other proposals . <p> Rutgers President Robert Barchi talks before Greg Schiano is introduced for his second tour as Rutgers head football coach on December 4 , 2019 at the Hale Center on Rutgers campus in Piscataway , NJ . ( Photo : Chris Pedota , NorthJersey.com-USA Today Network ) <p> The university 's largest cohort of union members -- 8,000 full-time faculty and graduate assistants -- won what they called a " historic " contract after threatening to strike before finals in the spring 2019 semester . <p> " I hope you do n't need to be convinced of the essential work postdocs contribute to Rutgers through their research , publications and teaching , " said Anne Kirkner , a postdoctoral research associate at the School of Social Work . She said her peers struggle to pay rent , loans , health care and child care with their current salaries . They 're asking for a minimum salary of $60,000 . <p> " Is it fair to keep asking us to postpone our lives while we contribute so much to this institution ? We 're tired of it , " Kirkner said . <p> University spokesperson Dory Devlin said the school has reached agreements with 16 of its unions , representing more than 18,239 employees . <p> " The university has worked diligently to resolve open issues and reach agreements as quickly as possible with the additional bargaining units , " Devlin said . " Negotiations are ongoing . " <h> ' Listen to the youth ' <p> Over the last few months , student activists have pressured the university to take action on climate change initiatives by creating a plan to decarbonize by 2030 and divest from the fossil fuel industry . <p> After a climate change rally that drew hundreds on campus at the start of the semester , Barchi announced on Sept. 24 that he would create a task force on carbon neutrality and climate resilience . Members of the task force , including the university 's top researchers on climate change who contributed to the United Nations reports on the topic released earlier this year , will be developing a climate action plan to identify pathways to carbon neutrality . <p> The group 's first findings will be due during the spring 2020 semester . <p> But some activists say a plan is not enough , and that the school should also divest from corporations that contribute to the climate crisis . <h> Posted ! <p> A link has been posted to your Facebook feed . <p> Robert Barchi on Sept. 4 , 2012 , a few days after becoming the 20th president of Rutgers University .
