<p> She succeeds another former president of Ireland , Mary Robinson , who was elected as first woman chancellor of TCD in 1998 and has held the post until now . <p> Prof McAleese 's functions will include chairing meetings of the university 's senate and overseeing ceremonial events such as commencement ceremonies . <p> At a ceremony at the university on Friday , Prof McAleese said her appointment was " a very proud moment " and paid tribute to the college as a " jewel in the academic crown in the heart of the capital city . " <p> She said she walked through the door for the first time in 1975 to attend an interview for the Reid professorship and recalled coming through the door feeling a " sense of awe and wonder " . <p> Prof McAleese presided over her first honorary degree ceremony as chancellor on Friday afternoon . <p> She was elected to her position as chancellor unopposed by members of the university senate . <p> Among the recipients were Irish Times columnist Fintan O'Toole ; mental health campaigner Joan Freeman ; former t ? naiste Mary Harney ; distinguished professor of psychology and neuroscience at University of California , Michael Gazzaniga ; and champion of climate change and marine scientist at James Cook University , Queensland , Prof Terry Hughes . Prof McAleese graduated in law from Queen 's University Belfast and was as appointed Reid Professor of Criminal Law , Criminology and Penology at TCD in 1975 . Aged just 24 , she was the youngest person to hold the post . <p> While in that role she was , along with Senator David Norris , a founding member of the Campaign for Homosexual Law Reform , as well as its legal advisor . <p> In January 1988 , she became the first Catholic and the first woman to be appointed director at the Institute of Professional Legal Studies at Queens , and in 1994 became the university 's first female pro-vice-chancellor <p> She was president of Ireland for two terms from 1997 to 2011 . She has also worked as a barrister and journalist .
