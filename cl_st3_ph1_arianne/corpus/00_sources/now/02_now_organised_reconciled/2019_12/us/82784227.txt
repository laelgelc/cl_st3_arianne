<h> Maquoketa high school students play flag football with a cow tongue to talk about climate change <p> MAQUOKETA , Iowa -- Students at Maquoketa High School played " cow tongue football , " as part of an Intro to Agriculture lesson on world population growth , hunger and climate change . <p> " ( These issues ) are going to hit these teenagers when they 're in the prime of their lives , their forties , " their teacher Dale Gruis says . <p> Gruis says the point of the lesson is to teach students that with a growing population , there 'll be less food to go around , so people will need to change their diets in order to eat what they can , like a cow tongue . <p> This lesson was also an opportunity for students to have a fun , interactive class . <p> " The bottom side is really smooth and wet , but the actual tongue part is gritty and pokey , " Keelan Hoover , freshman , says . <p> Students in his class say since their future is uncertain , it 's important to learn how to prepare for climate change . <p> " We 'll grow up to be adults , then it 'll be our responsibility to survive , " Hoover says . <p> Freshman Allyson Heister says she hopes after this lesson , her classmates will be inspired . <p> " If a lot of kids hear about world hunger with climate change and poverty , they could step up , " she says . <p> The lesson is part of students ' websites for class on world hunger , climate change and the growing population .
