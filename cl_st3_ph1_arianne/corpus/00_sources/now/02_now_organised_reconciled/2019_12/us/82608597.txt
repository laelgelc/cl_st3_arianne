<p> LONDON , Dec 10 ( Reuters ) - Britain is looking at whether or not to decriminalise the penalty for non-payment of the 154.50-pound ( $198 ) annual BBC " licence fee " tax on all television-watching households , Justice Secretary Robert Buckland said on Tuesday . <p> British Prime Minister Boris Johnson on Monday questioned why the BBC should continue to be supported by the annual fee , one of the biggest hints to date that the funding of Britain 's main news provider could be upended . <p> " What we are talking about as a first step is the decriminalisation of failing to pay the TV licence , " Buckland told BBC radio . <p> He said people during election campaigning had said they worried about the cost of the licence fee . <p> " Is it right to criminalise and target a vulnerable section of society for what really is an issue of civil liability ? We would consult on that to work out whether criminalisation is the right way to approach this issue , " he said . <p> It is a criminal offence to watch TV or use BBC iplayer in the United Kingdom without a valid TV licence .
