Those private properties are then stitched together with vast tracts of neighboring public lands to create one giant , rewilded prairie . The organization has purchased close to 30 properties so far , but it needs at least 50 more . <h> Environment <p> " I see them coming in with big money , buying up ranches and walking over the top of the people who are already here , " says ranch owner Conni French .
