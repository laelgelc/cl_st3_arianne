<h> Climate Migration Frightens ... Climate Poverty Is Frightening <p> Climate change could lead to increased inequality and poverty , researchers write in a VoxEU post . Michal Burzynski , Christoph Deuster , Frederic Docquier and Jaime de Melo anticipate a significant dip in productivity , forced displacement from a rising sea level and income loss as possible consequences of a warming climate . " Considering plausible climate scenarios , we predict forced and voluntary movements of 210 million to 320 million climate migrants over the 21st century , " they write , but most of these migrants will relocate within their home countries .
