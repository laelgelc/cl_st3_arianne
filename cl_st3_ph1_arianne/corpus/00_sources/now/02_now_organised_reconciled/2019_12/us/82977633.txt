( Photo by Mark Wilson/Getty Images ) <p> Melania Trump and her " Be Best " initiative are once again facing accusations that she 's hypocritical in her concerns about people being unkind to children online or using them in political debates after President Trump mocked teen climate activist Greta Thunberg for being named Time magazine 's " Person of the Year . " <p> Last week , Melania Trump , along with the White House and Republicans , excoriated Stanford Law Professor Pamela Karlan for making a comment about Melania and Donald 's 13-year-old son , Barron , during her impeachment hearing testimony . <p> " A minor child deserves privacy and should be kept out of politics , " Melania Trump wrote on her @FLOTUS Twitter account last week . " Pamela Karlan , you should be ashamed of your very angry and obviously biased public pandering , and using a child to do it . " <p> On Thursday , President Trump tweeted that it was " so ridiculous " that Thunberg , who is 16 and has Asperberger syndrome , had received the Time magazine honor . He said , " Greta must work on her Anger Management problem , then go to a good old fashioned movie with a friend ! Chill Greta , Chill ! " <p> Following Trump 's statement , Twitter users hit out at Melania Trump , asking whether she would similarly upbraid her husband for engaging in an " angry " attack on a minor . <p> On Friday , she responded with a statement from White House press secretary Stephanie Grisham that sought to justify her husband 's attack on Thunberg . The statement also essentially argued that it 's OK to attack a minor , as long as it 's not her child . <p> " It is no secret that the President and First Lady often communicate differently -- as most married couples do , " tweeted Grisham , who is also Melania Trump 's spokesperson . <p> " Their son is not an activist who travels the globe giving speeches . He is a 13-year-old who wants and deserves privacy , " Grisham said . <p> Critics had noted Melania Trump 's " hypocrisy " in saying that Karlan should be " ashamed " of herself , while her husband is often referred to as the " biggest cyberbully on the planet . " <p> " Melania , we agree . Children are off limits , " U.S. Rep. Maxine Waters tweeted . " Tell your husband to #bebest and go enroll himself in anger management instead of attacking such a brilliant and courageous teenager fighting for climate change . " <p> Bette Midler thanked a tweet by activist Travis Akers that addressed " the hypocrisy " of Melania Trump 's Be Best message . The actress tweeted : " Her husband 's bullying of Ms. Thunberg , a child , seems to have gone unnoticed by her ' team , ' and by extension , by Melania herself . " <p> Former first lady Michelle Obama more or less told Thunberg to ignore the haters , though she more generously called them " doubters . " She tweeted : " @GretaThunberg , do n't let anyone dim your light . ... you have so much to offer us all . Ignore the doubters and know that millions of people are cheering you on . " <p> But it remains to be seen whether Melania Trump will be much affected by people 's criticisms . The thing is , the contradictory , " hypocritical " anti-cyberbullying message of the first lady 's Be Best initiative has long been known to her -- and she does n't care . <p> " She knew that she would get blasted for including cyberbullying in her overall Be Best program ... but she did it anyway , " writes Bennett , who covers the first lady on a daily basis . <p> " Her husband even told her it would be a bad idea , " Bennett continued . " Trump was not unaware that his personal habit of trashing people on Twitter would open his wife up to a ton of backlash for taking up the cause . He warned her against doing it . She did it anyway and has faced the consequences . " <p> Indeed , Melania Trump 's decision to double-down and forge ahead with a message that even her husband sees as problematic is characteristic of her " stubborn , " " independent " nature , Bennett wrote . <p> " That she picked something to rally around that brought with it added complications because of her husband , thus making the fight against it more difficult , says a lot about who she is , " Bennett said . " Because , like Trump , Melania relishes a good ' Come at me ' challenge . Go after her , if you will . She does n't really seem to care . " <p> Prior to Grisham 's statement , Melania Trump was silent on the brewing controversy . Her only tweet involved a more traditional first lady project : Helping her husband host the annual Congressional Ball at the White House . <p> This tweet inspired critics to fill her feed reminding her of her husband 's attack on Thunberg . " Be Better . Be Greta , " wrote one person . <p> Shortly after Trump was elected , his Twitter habits were identified as the " Achilles heel " in Melania Trump 's inchoate idea about making cyberbullying her cause as first lady , Bennett wrote . <p> " Her husband is the biggest cyberbully on the plant , " Bennett wrote . " How can you tell kids to be nice online when your husband is such a jerk ? " <p> Bennett described how " 60 Minutes " correspondent Lesley Stahl pressed Melania Trump on this issue when she interviewed her and the then-president-elect in November 2016 . <p> " So you never say to him , ' Come on ? ' " Stahl asked . She also asked , " If he does something that you think crosses the line , will you tell him ? " <p> Melania Trump insisted , " Yes , I tell him all the time . " <p> Things also were " awkward " when Melania Trump formally announced the launch of her Be Best campaign during a Rose Garden ceremony at the White House in May 2018 . <p> " I feel strongly that as adults we can and should be best at educating our children about the importance of a healthy and balanced life , " Melania Trump said in her short speech . <p> Be Best , by the way , is said to have three facets : Children 's overall well-being , fighting opioid abuse and focusing on kids ' being positive on social media . But , of course , the cyberbullying component of Be Best -- after the term 's odd grammatical phrasing -- garnered the most attention . <p> Bennett said : " Admittedly , vast parts of her remarks were awkward because they addressed the importance of being nice and kind online and setting a good example for children -- while Trump , who lets fly Twitter name-calling on a regular basis , sat feet from her in the front row . " <p> Bennett also reported that Be Best , though supposedly 16 months in the making , does n't resemble a fully established program . <p> " To this day it has no publicly stated framework , timeline , or markers for progress , " Bennett said . " Melania has done events to promote her platform , but she has mainly done so in conjunction with other government entities that already have programs that dovetail with Melania 's Be Best initiative . " <p> Bennett said that Melania Trump does n't see being first lady as a full-time job , unlike her recent predecessors . One reason is that the first priority is being a hands-on mother to Barron . <p> " She does the ceremonial parts of the gig , but there 's also the sense with her that it feels almost part-time , " Bennett wrote . " She has events , but only when she wants to have them , usually one to three a week , none lasting more than an hour , though they typically they are far shorter . " <p> As for Be Best , Melania Trump might only participate in one or two events per month , Bennett said . Be Best also piggybacks on existing programs within the Department of Health and Human Services and other federal departments . Some might say that this is a " smarter " and more " fiscally " responsible way to run the initiative , but it also means it wo n't have the impact of those her predecessors , including Laura Bush and Michelle Obama . <p> " Not that Melania cares all that much -- she 's just fine with keeping the initiative as it is , " Bennett wrote . <p> " Maybe she 's learned the frustration of being a first lady : no matter what you do or how you do it , you will inevitably be belittled , " Bennett wrote . <p> Martha Ross is a features writer who covers everything and anything related to popular culture , society , health , women 's issues and families .
