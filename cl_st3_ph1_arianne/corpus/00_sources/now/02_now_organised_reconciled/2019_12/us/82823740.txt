If you see comments in violation of our community guidelines , please report them . <h> NM House Dems set sights on thriving future <p> It 's been a busy several months for Democrats in the New Mexico House of Representatives . <p> Since the close of the 2019 legislative session , we 've been busy meeting and listening to business , industry , education , and community leaders throughout the state and through the interim legislative committee process . Collectively , we have visited communities in Portales , Dulce , Deming , Taos , Albuquerque , and points in between . <p> A common theme we 've heard throughout our travel is the urgent need to provide our children with the tools to succeed . This means providing access to quality early childhood education , meaningful and nurturing K-16 education , and diversifying our economy so that our children can look forward to working in the industries of tomorrow including technology , green energy , and value-added agriculture . It 's why my colleagues and I in the New Mexico House of Representatives do the work that we do , and we 're proud of the important leaps we 've taken in the past year . The Energy Transition Act has put us on an aggressive path toward carbon-free energy . Meaningful increases in funding for K-16 are the foundation of our education moonshot , and comprehensive tax reforms will result in a tax cut for 70% of New Mexico 's families , while creating more stable revenue sources for state government . <p> And we 've only just begun . <p> As we approach the 2020 legislative session , New Mexico House Democrats have a clear set of goals ; build upon last year 's accomplishments and continue on our path to diversify New Mexico 's economy and lift a generation of New Mexicans out of poverty . To do this , we 'll push forward with the second phase of our education moonshot and invest further in K-12 public education . We 'll make our institutions of higher education more affordable and accessible for people across the state , and we 'll continue our eight-year push to fully fund early childhood education by investing 1% from our nearly $19 billion Land Grant Permanent Fund . <p> Stabilizing our revenue sources will also be a priority for NM House Democrats . With targeted , gross receipts tax reforms that close existing loopholes while reducing the current rate , the tax burden will be lifted off of NM 's small businesses . These efforts will make our tax system fairer for middle class and working families and leave more money in their pockets . In addition , through these targeted tax reforms we 'll bring revenue resiliency to the state budget in the face of a volatile oil and gas industry . <p> In terms of public safety , we 'll continue our push to get to the root the causes of crime by reforming our criminal justice system . This means rebuilding our behavioral health system , prioritizing treatment over incarceration for nonviolent offenders , and providing law enforcement with the necessary tools to keep violent offenders off our streets . We 'll also continue to support law enforcement by providing the tools and support required to address crime in all its forms . <p> Lastly , we will continue to solidify our state 's transition to clean energy by incentivizing solar , wind , and other forms of renewables . Climate change is the most urgent issue of our time and it 's our mission that New Mexico becomes energy independent , both for our economic resiliency , and for the long-term sustainability of our planet . <p> New Mexico is at a critical crossroads . For the first time in a very long time we have the resources to make the types of investments to transform our state . The people of New Mexico can count on us to govern responsibly and with a vision toward a prosperous future . <p> New Mexico Rep. Javier Mart ? nez , D-Bernalillo , is co-chair of the House Taxation and Revenue Committee
