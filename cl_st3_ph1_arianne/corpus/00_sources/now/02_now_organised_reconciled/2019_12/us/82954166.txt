<p> In 2019 , the Sun Sentinel 's coverage of the Parkland tragedy was recognized with the prestigious Pulitzer Prize for Public Service and as a Pulitzer finalist in Breaking News . The newsroom 's remarkable breaking news , investigative and multimedia work on Parkland won First Place in Breaking News from the Scripps Howard Foundation and the O'Brien Award for Impact in Public Service Journalism from the News Leaders Association . These awards reflect our talented journalists and our commitment to the kind of investigative journalism that can tackle our biggest challenges . We continue to work to tell the stories that improve our community . Building on the success of The Invading Sea , The Sun Sentinel co-founded a broader news initiative covering all aspects of climate change . The Florida Climate Reporting Network has grown to 17 news organizations and has earned two Pulitzer Center grants to undertake reporting projects . <p> Your support , along with that of our valuable advertisers , truly matters .
