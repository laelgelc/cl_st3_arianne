If you see comments in violation of our community guidelines , please report them . <h> Days after U.S. House votes to impeach Donald Trump , Joe Biden preaches unity in Iowa <p> OTTUMWA , Ia. -- Campaigning in Iowa this weekend , Joe Biden said the impeachment of President Donald Trump this week makes it even more important for the next president to unify the country . <p> Biden 's two-day swing through Iowa came days after the U.S. House of Representatives voted to impeach Trump for abuse of power and obstruction of Congress . A Senate trial is the next step in the process . <p> The former vice president has talked throughout his presidential campaign about uniting the country . And while his rivals on the debate stage and the campaign trail have emphasized Trump 's wrongdoing , Biden said impeachment shows more than ever why it 's important to bring the country together . <p> " No one 's taken as much heat and as many lies thrown at them as I have , but again this is not about me . It 's not about my family . It 's about the nation . And we have to reach out and unify this country , " Biden said in Ottumwa on Saturday . <p> People wait in line to see former Vice-President and 2020 Democratic presidential candidate Joe Biden speak during a campaign event at Hotel Ottumwa on Saturday , Dec. 21 , 2019 , in Ottumwa . " And do n't get me wrong , it 's not that I think it 's unfair that Donald Trump has been impeached and has to face the charges that were made , but the idea that it 's worth celebrating I find counterproductive because there 's nothing to celebrate . " <p> Biden 's rivals have questioned whether it 's naive to expect Republicans to work with Democrats after years of obstruction during President Barack Obama 's administration , when Biden was vice president . But Biden stuck to his approach as he campaigned in rural Iowa on Saturday and Sunday , less than a week before Christmas . He said it 's the only way Democrats will be able to achieve progress on priorities like education , climate change and health care . <p> " All the things we talk about , they ca n't happen unless we can bring the country together , " Biden said Sunday in Atlantic . <p> That message of unity is working for some potential caucusgoers . Wendy Thompson , 55 , of Bloomfield came to Biden 's Ottumwa event undecided , but left leaning toward Biden because of what he said about morals and how people need to care for each other . <p> " We have to come together and we have to take care of one another , and if Mr. Biden is looking towards that same view , then I 'm going to look a lot harder at Mr. Biden , " she said . <p> Lori Ryner , a 56-year-old software tester from Winterset , said her first choice is U.S. Sen. Amy Klobuchar of Minnesota . But she also likes Biden , and she 's making him her second choice if Klobuchar does n't reach the 15% viability threshold on caucus night . <p> Her husband , 61-year-old Dan Ryner , said Biden is his first choice while Klobuchar is his second pick . <p> " I think he 's the guy , " he said of Biden . " He knows the world leaders on a first-name basis and he can fix all this Trump chaos . " <p> Still , caucusgoers like 58-year-old Denise Jago of Clarinda are glad the House voted to impeach Trump . The retired investigator with the Iowa Department of Corrections attended Biden 's event in Atlantic on Sunday and said the House had to act to show that there would be consequences for the types of actions Trump is accused of . <p> " We watch our three-year-old grandson during the week and if we do n't set consequences for his behavior , our house would be up in smoke , " she said . " And it 's the same thing . "
