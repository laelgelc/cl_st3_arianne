<p> WASHINGTON , Dec 10 ( Reuters ) - Arms control , election security and national security could be raised when U.S. President Donald Trump and Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov meet at the White House later on Tuesday , a White House spokesman said . <p> " It 's incumbent on any American president to try and build relationships across the world . We 're absolutely expected to talk about arms control but also election security , for example , and national security , " White House spokesman Hogan Gidley said in an interview on Fox Business Network .
