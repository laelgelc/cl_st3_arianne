( Marnie Isaacs/Kaktovik Community Foundation via AP ) <h> Disaster relief more costlythan sustainable energy <p> While Silicon Valley is making strides toward fighting climate change , this needs to be a national endeavor . <p> The carbon dioxide emissions that the United States is producing are affecting people around the world , and it 's not just Silicon Valley 's job to fix it . <p> It is understandable that fossil fuels seem more economically advantageous at the moment , but looking at the economic impact in the years to come should prompt us as a society to reach for more sustainable options . <p> The amount of money that will be spent on natural disaster relief and the effects of unclean air are much greater than the costs of using sustainable energy sources right now . <p> Corporations and cities need to allocate money to environmental sustainability because in the long run , the money they are supposedly saving is going to be gone in a heartbeat after climate change has made its mark on our society . <h> More in Letters to the Editor <p> We encourage you to submit letters that share your opinions on issues of interest to residents in our community .
