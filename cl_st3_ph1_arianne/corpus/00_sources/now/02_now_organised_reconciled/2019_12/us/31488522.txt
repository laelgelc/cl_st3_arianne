In the US , hashtags like #MeToo and #BlackLivesMatter became rallying cries for social justice ; internationally , protesters in the Arab Spring and Hong Kong demonstrations used social media to spread information and work against the governments trying to silence them . <p> While the internet has helped shed light on these causes , it 's also provided a new platform for hate and bigotry in the form of ultranationalism , tribalism , and white supremacy . As we enter the next decade , issues like climate change , internet privacy , and gun violence have gained traction among a new generation of activists who are sure to shift the discourse in the coming years . <p> To highlight the tremendous moments in activism that have defined this past decade , BuzzFeed News combed through thousands of pictures to highlight the ones that best represent the events that have shaped the world as we know it today . While this list could not include every major protest of the last decade , we attempted to collect pictures that spoke to larger social shifts .
