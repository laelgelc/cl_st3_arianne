It was an intense , intensely frustrating two weeks -- from the urgent science and high community expectations leading into the negotiations , to the fraught complexity of Article 6 , to the more than 500,000 marchers who gathered in downtown Madrid to demand real action on the climate crisis , to the criminal misbehaviour of a few big emitters whose obstruction led some observers to describe this year 's COP as the most unproductive ever . <p> One interesting and hopeful takeaway -- during and after the conference , there were so many voices talking about next steps to amp up climate action and build momentum for more ambitious national commitments next year at COP 26 in Glasgow . The basic message : If the COP is n't going to get this done , it 's up to the rest of us , same as it always was .
