You may also want to view these photo galleries : <h> Objective of the clean energy initiatives <p> The proposed initiatives would require all retail electricity sold in Oregon to come from carbon-free sources by Jan. 1 , 2045 , up from about half today . Washington , California and Nevada already have similar targets . <p> One of the two proposed initiatives went further , requiring utilities to more aggressively invest in beneficial electrification programs , such as electric car charging infrastructure . <p> A third initiative was not rejected by the secretary of state 's office . It would accelerate pollution reduction targets already in Oregon law . <p> " As Oregonians and Americans , we have a fundamental civil right to petition our government . It 's a cornerstone of our democracy , " Lisa Adatto , a co-chief petitioner on the measures , said in a statement . " This secretary of state is violating our rights , stepping outside the bounds of her office to block that right . " <p> In the previous rejection of three forestry-related initiative petitions in September , the Department of Justice declined to defend Clarno 's decision , leading to the Secretary of State 's Office retaining private legal counsel .
