<p> STOCKHOLM , Dec 10 ( Reuters ) - Singer Marie Fredriksson of Swedish pop duo Roxette died on Monday at the age of 61 after having spent years battling cancer and the lingering effects of its treatment , her manager said . <p> Roxette produced several international hits including four U.S. number ones with " It Must Have Been Love " , from the film " Pretty Woman " , " The Look " , " Listen to Your Heart " and " Joyride " . <p> " It is with great sadness that we must announce that one of our biggest and most loved artists is gone , " Fredriksson 's manager said in a statement . <p> Roxette was formed in 1986 by Fredriksson and singer and song-writer Per Gessle . The group released its breakthrough album " Look Sharp " two years later . <p> " You painted my black and white songs in the most beautiful colours , " Gessle said of the singer in Monday 's statement . <p> Fredriksson was diagnosed with a brain tumour in 2002 .
