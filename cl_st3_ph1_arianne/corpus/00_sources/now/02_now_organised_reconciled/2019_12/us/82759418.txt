<p> NEW DELHI , Dec 18 ( Reuters ) - India 's top court on Wednesday dismissed an appeal against the death penalty by one of four men convicted of gang-raping and murdering a woman on a bus in New Delhi , saying it found no reason to review the sentence . <p> Four men were convicted of the rape and murder of the young physiotherapy student , who was tortured on a bus in New Delhi in 2012 , an attack that triggered shockwaves across India and abroad . <p> The victim came from a lower-middle class family and worked in a call centre while she studied . <p> Her mother told reporters she welcomed the ruling and hoped all four men would be executed soon . <p> " This is one step closer to justice , " she said .
