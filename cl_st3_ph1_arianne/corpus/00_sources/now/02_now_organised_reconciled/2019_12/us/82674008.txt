<p> WELLINGTON , Dec 14 ( Reuters ) - New Zealand police said on Saturday they would search in the waters near the volcanic White Island in their attempts to retrieve two remaining bodies , following a fatal eruption earlier this week . <p> The remains of six people were successfully retrieved on Friday after a military team in gas masks and hazmat suits went on to the volcano and removed the bodies in a high-risk operation . <p> Police said they could not retrieve the remains of two more people , but were able to spot at least one body in the waters not too far from the shore . <p> " The Police Dive Squad and Navy divers this morning resumed their search for a body seen in the water on Tuesday , " police said in a statement . <p> The police said they will not be returning to the island for a land-based search on Saturday , adding that they were analysing all information available and assessing possible next steps . <p> " Today 's planning will allow us to return to the island to conduct further land-based searches for the remaining deceased , as the environment on and around the island allows , " police said . <p> " There will be no return to the island today , " it added in the statement . <p> The volcano , a popular tourist destination for day-trippers , erupted on Monday , spewing ash , steam and gases over the island . Among the 47 people on the island at the time were Australian , U.S. , German , Chinese , British and Malaysian tourists . <p> The death toll from the eruption is now at 14 , with two bodies on the island still classified as missing . <p> More than two dozen people are in hospitals across New Zealand and Australia , most with severe burn injuries . <p> Authorities have faced growing pressure in recent days from families of some victims to recover the bodies as soon as possible . <p> There has also been criticism that tourists were allowed on the island at all , given the risks of an active volcano .
