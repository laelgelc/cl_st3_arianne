<p> GUATEMALA CITY , Dec 21 ( Reuters ) - A collision between a passenger bus and a trailer truck in eastern Guatemala early on Saturday killed at least 20 people , including a 7-year-old boy , doctors and firefighters said . <p> Guatemalan President-elect Alejandro Giammattei expressed his " profound lament " for the deaths in a post on Twitter . <p> The accident took place near the town of Gualan , near Guatemala 's Atlantic coast , as the Pullman passenger bus was traveling to the city of Santa Elena , near the border with Mexico . <p> It was not immediately clear what caused the crash .
