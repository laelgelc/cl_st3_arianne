A global U.N.sponsored climate change conference is taking place in Madrid . <p> MADRID ( AP ) -- U.N . climate talks in Madrid are kicking into high gear Tuesday , with ministers arriving to tackle some of the tough issues that negotiators have been unable to resolve over the past week . <p> Officials from almost 200 nations have n't managed to finalize the rules for international carbon markets that economists say could help drive down emissions . <p> Another contentious issue is poor countries ' demand for aid to help them cope with the damage and destruction wrought by natural disasters blamed on climate change . <p> Unlike at many past climate summits , few heads of government will join the talks .
