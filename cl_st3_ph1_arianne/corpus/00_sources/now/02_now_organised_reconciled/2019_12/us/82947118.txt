<p> ( Corrects last paragraph to clarify that the cost of restoration applies to entire parliament ) <p> LONDON , Dec 29 ( Reuters ) - Britain 's Big Ben bell in parliament 's landmark clock tower will ring at midnight on New Year 's Eve , marking the start of a year for the first time since its new face was revealed from under scaffolding halfway through restoration work . <p> The work has seen the 96-metre-tall Elizabeth Tower , one of the most photographed buildings in Britain , enveloped in scaffolding for the last two years as the four clock dials are reglazed , ironwork repainted and intricately carved stonework cleaned and repaired . <p> In March , part of the scaffolding was removed , showing that the clock 's once black numerals and hands have been repainted blue , in line with what scientists say was its original colour . <p> Since restoration work began in 2017 , Big Ben has been largely silenced , sounding only for important events . It last tolled on Remembrance Day on Nov. 11 . <p> The bell will be tested several times in the run-up to New Year 's Eve , parliament said in a statement . <p> The restoration work is due to be completed in 2021 and will be followed by a 4-billion-pound ( $5.2 billion ) restoration programme of the entire parliament building .
