Senturia did not seek a third term in November . <p> SHAKER HEIGHTS , Ohio -- In the midst of a lengthy year-end legislative agenda , one matter of official business did not get lost in the shuffle at City Council 's Dec. 16 meeting . <p> Leading off the session was a resolution of appreciation along with a standing ovation for Councilwoman Julianna Johnston Senturia , who did not seek a third term this year . <p> A strong advocate for young families , seniors and city workers alike , as well as transparency in local government -- especially in the realm of municipal finances -- Senturia pulled in more than 5,100 votes ( 39 percent ) in a bid for " interim " mayor in 2018 , running against David Weiss , who made it unopposed and official this November . <p> The resolution , prepared and read by Vice Mayor Anne Williams , noted that Senturia " demonstrated skillful leadership and thoughtful guidance on issues ranging from infrastructure investment in sewers and streets to housing inspections to anti-discrimination legislation with observant and independent commentary , a quick wit , and good sense of humor . " <p> Councilman Rob Zimmerman said the most important word for him was " thoughtful , " noting that Senturia " definitely does her homework , " contributing to her " balanced and clear " approach . <p> Zimmerman told Senturia that the true measure of service on council is " did you leave the community better off ? And in your case , I think the answer is ' absolutely yes . ' " <p> Councilman Sean Malone called Senturia an " indispensable member of this council and a pleasure to work with , putting Shaker residents first with insightful questions and concern for the entire community . " <p> Malone added that Senturia has already offered suggestions about future policy issues in the city . <p> Councilman Earl Williams felt that the only significant omission from the resolution is the fact that Senturia is a " fellow Bobcat , " from Ohio University , where she earned bachelor 's and master 's degrees . <p> " That 's why I chose to sit next to her , " he noted , adding " I know she will be back to see us from time to time . " <p> Senturia will keep busy as the executive director of the Heights Schools Foundation , a nonprofit supporting her other alma mater , the Cleveland Heights-University Heights City School District , as well as numerous civic organizations . <p> Also an award-winning educator at Anshe Chesed Fairmount Temple 's religious school , Senturia described service on City Council as a " team sport , " for which she preferred not to be called out on an individual basis . <p> " It 's about how we work together , which for the most part I think we have done , " Senturia said . <p> She also renewed her call for continued transparency from the city in conducting municipal business " in the light of day , " making access to meetings " even simpler , " with better recording equipment and further electronic distribution . <p> Also on hand were her husband and two of their three children , whom she thanked for their assistance on the campaign trail , as well as hitting up innumerable block parties . <p> She alluded to her family 's ongoing cooperation with " free-for-all nights for dinner " when duty called her away . <p> The resolution of appreciation further cited Senturia 's early involvement in the city 's Climate Change Task Force , which evolved into the Sustainability Task Force and what is now a full-fledged council committee . <p> Senturia also got the honors of moving or seconding legislation providing **26;426;TOOLONG ( COLA ) to non-union city employees , as well as a new contract for the International Association of Machinists Local 1363 , which represents some workers in the city Recreation Department . <p> She noted that she was also a former " I.M. " member , although a different local <p> Last year , she cast the sole dissenting vote on the 2019 budget because it did not include COLA raises for city employees , many of whom could make more money working in the private sector . <p> For them and for her , " it 's an honor to serve this city , " Johnston said . <p> Succeeding her on the at-large council will be Carmella Williams , who will be sworn in after the first of the year .
