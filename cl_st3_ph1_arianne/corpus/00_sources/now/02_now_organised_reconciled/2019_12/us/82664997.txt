<h> Letter : Global warming calls for responsible legislation <h> Friday <p> Dec 13 , 2019 at 4:11 AM <p> In his Wednesday letter , Sen. Rob Portman defended his " effective environmental stewardship and common-sense climate change solutions ... " detailing his many bipartisan initiatives which most certainly are to be applauded . <p> My concern is , are we doing enough , quickly enough ? The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change warned a year ago that we had 12 years for global warming to be kept to a maximum 1.5 degrees Celsius . <p> In January , The Wall Street Journal published a public statement by 3,508 esteemed U.S. economists , former chairs of the Federal Reserve , 27 Nobel laureates , many Ohio State economists and more , all endorsing a carbon fee and dividend as " the most cost-effective lever to reduce carbon emissions at the scale and speed necessary . " They further state that a carbon tax will " send a powerful price signal that harnesses the invisible hand of the marketplace to steer economic actors toward a low-carbon future . " <p> I hope that Portman will work to enact responsible legislation that is effective as well as good for people and the economy . <p> Sheila Fox , Columbus <h> Never miss a story <p> Choose the plan that 's right for you .
