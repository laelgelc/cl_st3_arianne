If you see comments in violation of our community guidelines , please report them . <h> Lower Westchester communities look to cut down on food waste <p> With the holiday season underway , more pies will be baked in households than mouths to eat them . At a local level , officials and stakeholders in three neighboring municipalities are working to reduce that food waste . <p> The Town of Mamaroneck was awarded a grant from the New York State Department of Environmental Conservation that will fund a public outreach campaign encouraging residents to eat all the food they purchase , give excess to people in need and compost whatever else is left . <p> The program , dubbed " Love Your Food , " has stakeholders within the Town of Mamaroneck working with Village of Mamaroneck , Village of Larchmont and Mamaroneck school district concerned citizens in hopes of making an impact locally on climate change and helping the less fortunate . <p> The program is expected to last at least three years , according to Mamaroneck officials . <p> About 40 percent of the food available in the United States ends up in the garbage while one in eight Americans go hungry daily , according to the United States Department of Agriculture ( USDA ) . <p> Tossed food that ends up in landfills and incinerators contribute to greenhouse gases that harm the environment , according to the Love Your Food website . <p> The grant is a first-of-its-kind from the state which only awarded funds to eight municipalities in New York , Karen Khor , who is a Mamaroneck Sustainability Collaborative volunteer , said . The Town of Ossining was also awarded the pioneering grant . <p> Food scrap recycling is already occurring inside Mamaroneck and Larchmont schools since at least September , and about 1,000 households are re-purposing food scraps and bringing them to Maxwell Avenue and Fayette Avenue recycling facilities . <p> " What makes this really amazing is it involves so many different groups of people , " Khor said . <p> Excess food in Mamaroneck school system cafeterias , like fruit or milk cartons , go to the St. Peter 's Episcopal Church food pantry in Port Chester and HOPE Community Services in New Rochelle , Khor said . <p> Mamaroneck Supervisor Nancy Seligson said she looks forward to residents being engaged in cutting down on food waste . <p> " It has the potential to accomplish important results by enabling every member of our community to take an active role in advancing the well-being and quality of life for residents and neighbors of our Tri-Municipal area , " Seligson said in a statement . <p> There was a kickoff event earlier this month at the Down to Earth Farmers Market in Larchmont . Residents with more questions can go to the Love Your Food table Dec. 14 for more information . The farmers market is located at the corner of Chatsworth Avenue and Myrtle Boulevard .
