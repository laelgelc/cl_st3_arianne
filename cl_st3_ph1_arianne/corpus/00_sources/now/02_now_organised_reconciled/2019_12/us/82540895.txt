<h> Community members rally for action on climate change at Eau Claire City Hall <p> EAU CLAIRE , Wis . ( WEAU ) -- Community members and UW-Eau Claire students were outside City Hall Friday morning to bring attention to climate change . <p> " It 's bringing awareness to the climate issues that are happening , " says Lance Miller of Eau Claire . " Regardless of your age or gender or sex you can come to protest and we all care . It affects us all . " <p> The protestors hope to get the attention on Representative Ron Kind and Senator Tammy Baldwin to support climate change legislation . <p> The same group held a Climate March in September that drew hundreds of supporters . <p> Organizers say they hope to continue having events like this to raise awareness in the community and make change . <h> Online Public Information File <p> Viewers with disabilities can get assistance accessing this station 's FCC Public Inspection File by contacting the station with the information listed below .
