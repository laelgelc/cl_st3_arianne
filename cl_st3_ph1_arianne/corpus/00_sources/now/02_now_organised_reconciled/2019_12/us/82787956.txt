<h> Dutch court tells government to step up climate change fight <p> AMSTERDAM ( Reuters ) - The Dutch High Court on Friday ordered the government of the Netherlands to step up its fight against climate change and cut greenhouse gas emissions faster than planned . <p> FILE PHOTO : People take part in a protest claiming for urgent measures to combat climate change during a demonstration in central Amsterdam , Netherlands March 10 , 2019 . REUTERS/Eva Plevier/File Photo <p> The court said the government had not done enough to protect its citizens from the dangerous effects of climate change , which can " threaten their lives and wellbeing " . <p> Home to many large industries , Europe 's main seaport and an abundant supply of cheap natural gas , the Netherlands is among the largest polluters in Europe . <p> To live up to its obligations , the government needs to ensure that CO2 emissions are at least 25% below 1990 levels by the end of 2020 , the court said in a final verdict , upholding a 2015 decision by a lower court . <p> The ruling meant a final defeat for the government in the case brought by environmental group Urgenda Foundation , on behalf of nearly 900 Dutch citizens .
