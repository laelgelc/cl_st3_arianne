<h> Kim Daugherty withdraws from 5th District race <p> Correction : John Lesinski , Claire Good and Shadi Ayyas are also running for the Democratic nomination . Their names were omitted from the earlier story . <p> Fauquier County resident Kim Daugherty announced Monday that she 's withdrawing from the raceto be the Democratic nominee for the 5th District seat in Congress in 2020 . The seat is currently held by Republican Denver Riggleman . <p> " I wanted to run for Congress to help bring real , progressive change . However , the path to victory required forsaking the most important commitments in my life : to my children , my husband and the people I fight for in court every day , " ? she said . <p> Daugherty is an attorney in Manassas . She lives with her family in the Brookside subdivision . Her withdrawal leaves Dr. Cameron Webb and Roger Dean Huffstetler as candidates for the Democratic nomination . Bob Good , a Campbell County supervisor , recently announced he will challenge Riggleman for the Republican nomination . <p> Daugherty called Webb " a talented and thoughtful candidate committed to working class folks . " She said he is " the only candidate in the primary field who has the ability to not only appeal to every voter but to inspire , motivate and activate them to vote Democrat . " <p> Daugherty said she 'll continue to be an advocate for issues . <p> " I will push for Medicare for all or any plan to bring 100 percent coverage to all Americans , women 's rights , criminal justice reform including legalization of cannabis , real steps to reduce inequality , effective gun safety , climate change leadership and to restore respect for constitutional norms in Washington , D.C. " <p> The 5th District covers all or part of 21 counties , plus the cites of Charlottesville and Danville . Areas of Fauquier County not in the 5th District are Midland , Goldvein and Bealeton .
