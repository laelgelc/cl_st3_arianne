( Photo by Drew Angerer/Getty Images ) <h> Washington governor wants to spend $300 million to help homeless <p> Former Democratic presidential candidate and Governor of Washington Jay Inslee speaks about climate change at the Council on Foreign Relations , June 5 , 2019 in New York City . ( Photo by Drew Angerer/Getty Images ) <p> Washington Gov . Jay Inslee wants to spend more than $300 million from the state 's emergency budget reserve to add 2,100 shelter beds and provide other help to combat homelessness . <p> The Democrat on Wednesday unveiled his supplemental spending plan Wednesday to the state 's current $52.4 billion two-year budget . <p> Inslee says Washington must do more to find housing for people who are homeless . He says Washington has the fifth-highest per-capita rate of homelessness in the U.S. <p> The state currently has about $2.5 billion in its emergency fund . The Legislature convenes next month and will consider Inslee 's proposal .
