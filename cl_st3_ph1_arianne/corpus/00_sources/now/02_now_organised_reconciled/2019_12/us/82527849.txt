<h> Fridays for Future to hold Annapolis rally <p> Annapolis high school student Amelia Farrell , who earlier this year organized the March Annapolis climate strike , speaks at a forest conservation rally in front of the Arundel Center before the County Council meeting Monday in Annapolis . ( Joshua McKerrow/Capital Gazette ) <p> The Annapolis chapter of the student-led movement Fridays for Future will hold a rally and march downtown Dec. 6 , the first Friday in the last month of 2019 . <p> This year the same students demonstrated outside the State House urging the passage of the Clean Energy Jobs Act . It passed . A report on the state 's renewable energy portfolio standard is in its final stage of review , a Department of Natural Resources spokesman said , and will be presented to the legislature and posted publicly in the coming weeks . <p> The latest march falls in the middle of the Madrid Climate Change Conference , where leaders in the United Nations will discuss ways to strengthen implementation of the Paris Climate Agreement . The United States is withdrawing from that agreement . <p> The demonstration Friday is n't limited to Annapolis ; strikes will happen around the world , including Chile , Madrid , Stockholm and New York City . <p> The students will be missing school again to protest -- just as the movement 's originator Greta Thunberg did outside Swedish parliament , calling it a school strike for climate .
