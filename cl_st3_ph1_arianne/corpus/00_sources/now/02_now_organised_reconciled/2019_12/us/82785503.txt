While in Ohio , he stopped to discuss the opioid crisis in a roundtable called , " Getting it done : tackling the opioid epidemic . " At the event , Bloomberg was joined by community members who have worked on the frontlines of the epidemic over the past 10 years like former corner Dr. John Gabis , Adena Director of Community Health Advancement Kim Jones , Detective Bud Lyttle , Fire Chief Jeffrey Creed , Adena doctors and individuals who have been personally affected by substance abuse disorder . <p> Buy Photo <p> Bloomberg listened to various stories opioid related stories from different members of the Chillicothe community during a roundtable discussion held at the Carlisle building in Downtown Chillicothe , Ohio , on Dec. 20 , 2019 . " We need leaders at all levels standing alongside us in this battle against addiction , to save lives and get people on the path to recovery . I 'm grateful that Mike Bloomberg understands it is critical that we work together to stop this disease which plagues so many Ohio families . " <p> While the roundtable discussion was closed to the public , Bloomberg spent time discussing his plan to address the opioid epidemic if elected and heard from community leaders who work directly with those affected . Bloomberg 's plan would : provide medications to treat opioid use disorder and provide counseling when people enter hospitals or the criminal justice system , support the Medication Access and Training Expansion ( MATE ) Act , enforce federal laws mandating insurance coverage for mental health and substance use disorders , require insurers to remove obstacles to cover medication for the disorder , remove special training requirements and limits on prescribing buprenorphine -- an opioid medication used to treat opioid dependency -- and develop national standards for states to gather information on opioid use and overdoses . <p> Bloomberg believes his opioid epidemic policy differs from what 's currently in place as it builds on his proposed health care plan which he hopes will put health insurance within reach of all Americans . His policy differs from Medicare for all as it allows those with insurance from their employers to keep their plans . <p> " This is a deadly thing going through society and it does n't seem to spare anyone , " Bloomberg said . <p> If elected , Bloomberg 's four main focuses will be combating the drug problem nationwide , finding a solution to gun violence , working to stop climate change and fixing the public education system . <p> Buy Photo <p> Bloomberg high-fives Brenig Lloyd-Prys during his coffee meeting with Mayor Luke Feeney at Paper City Coffee during the presidential candidate 's visit to Chillicothe on Dec. 20 , 2019 . Bloomberg also highlighted the importance of the public deciding who their leaders are . <p> A spokesperson for Trump Victory issued a statement in response to Bloomberg 's Ohio visit . <p> " Michael Bloomberg is an out-of-touch coastal billionaire who believes he can buy his way into Ohio and push the same radical policy ideas that plagued New York City during his tenure as mayor , " said Dan Lusheck , spokesperson and Ohio press secretary for Trump Victory . " Bloomberg was not good for New York , and certainly will not look out for the best interests of Ohioans . " <p> Yet Bloomberg says that cities and communities in Ohio like Chillicothe and Columbus are looking for solutions to the opioid crisis , automation and ensuring their children have a future -- issues he plans to address . If elected , he says he will find ways to bring people of all political parties back together through leadership that 's currently lacking in Washington . <p> It is unclear if Bloomberg will ever appear on the debate stage as the billionaire is funding his own campaign . Rules established by the Democratic National Convention state that to appear on the January debate stage , candidates must earn at least 5% in four qualifying polls from Nov. 14 and Jan. 10 , must receive donations from 225,000 individuals and need a minimum of 1,000 donors in at least 20 states . Despite criticism from Democratic rivals on how his campaign is funded , Bloomberg says he is happy to appear on the stage if the DNC changes its rules .
