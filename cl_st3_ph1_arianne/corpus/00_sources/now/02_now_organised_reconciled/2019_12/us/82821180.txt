Australian Prime Minister Scott Morrison said he regrets being away on vacation with his family as fires raged across South Australia , resulting in the deaths of three people earlier this week . <p> Australian Prime Minister Scott Morrison said he regrets being away on vacation with his family as fires raged across South Australia , resulting in the deaths of three people earlier this week . <p> " I have obviously returned from leave and I know that has caused some great anxiety in Australia and ( my wife ) Jenny and I acknowledge that , " Morrison said in a news conference on Sunday . " If you had your time over again and you had the benefit of hindsight then ( you 'd ) have made different decisions . " <p> Morrison was heavily criticized for taking a pre-Christmas trip to Hawaii as thousands of firefighters worked to contain the wildfires . <p> " I 'm sure Australians ... understand that when you make a promise to your kids you try and keep it . But as prime minister you have other responsibilities , " he said at the news conference . " I accept the criticism . " <p> More than 105 bush and grass fires continued to burn across New South Wales -- a southeastern state of Australia --- early Sunday , according to the New South Wales Rural Fire Service . About 59 of them were still not contained , the service said . <p> A state of emergency was declared in the state for the second time in two months . <h> The connection to climate change <p> The bushfires have been burning for two months now , exacerbated by strong winds that stoke the flames and spread dangerous embers , and by rising temperatures-- including a record-breaking heat wave that began earlier this week . <p> The prime minister acknowledged there is a connection between weather events , fire events and global climate change . <p> At the news conference Sunday , Morrison said there is " no argument " about the links between " broader issues of global climate change and weather events around the world . " <p> " But I 'm sure people equally would acknowledge that the direct connection to any single fire event , it 's not a credible suggestion to make that link , " he said . <p> " We must take action on climate change , we are taking action on climate change , " he said Sunday . <p> Sunday was expected to be cooler , the Bureau of Meteorology for New South Wales wrote , but many communities " away from the coast will still experience significant heat . " <h> ' Some have lost everything ' <p> Since September , the death toll from the wildfires has jumped to nine , with nearly 800 homes destroyed in the country . <p> " Some have lost everything , some have received news their properties have been saved , &amp; others are still waiting to find out , " New South Wales Premier Gladys Berejiklian said on Twitter . " Their common message was deep gratitude for our amazing firefighters . " <p> Volunteer firefighters Geoffrey Keaton , 32 , and Andrew O'Dwyer , 36 , died Thursday near the town of Buxton , southwest of Sydney . It 's believed their vehicle hit a tree before rolling off the road , said the New South Wales Rural Fire Service in a statement on Friday . Three other firefighters were also injured . <p> Keaton joined the RFS as a volunteer firefighter in 2006 and was deputy captain of the Horsley Park Rural Fire Brigade in western Sydney . O'Dwyer joined the service in 2003 and was also a member of the Horsley Park brigade . Both men were fathers to young children , according to CNN affiliate Nine News .
