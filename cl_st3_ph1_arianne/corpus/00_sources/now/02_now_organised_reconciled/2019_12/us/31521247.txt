A minor riot ensued when Faust employees dumped a huge bucket of water on a KSTP-TV cameraman , and other members of the media entered the melee . <p> " Two employees were hitting the Channel 11 man , including one with a billy club , so at that point I jumped on the man with the club , " O'Rourke told the Pioneer Press at the time . <p> The TV footage was shown for weeks afterward , and O'Rourke acquired a new moniker : " Fighting Molly O'Rourke . " <p> O'Rourke , 61 , of St. Paul , will retire Jan. 28 after nearly 25 years with Washington County , including seven as county administrator . She previously was the county 's deputy administrator , among other roles . <p> As county administrator , she oversees some 1,300 employees and an annual budget of $288 million . The county 's population is more than 265,000 . <p> " Her dedication to staff and the mission of the county was just remarkable , " said Washington County Commissioner Lisa Weik . " She has always led from a position of knowledge and strength , but she had that light touch . Everyone really liked working for her . " <p> Weik said one of O'Rourke 's strengths was finding and hiring talented employees . " She had a really good eye , " Weik said . " She hired department heads who are just outstanding in their field , and they want to stay . " <p> O'Rourke " understands civic engagement and service in a way that most people do n't , and she 's just focused on that all the time , " said Tom Adkins , the county 's interim community-services director . " She leads from the heart and mind , and you 've got to have them both together . She is so easy to follow ... because she 's got that compass pointed in the right direction all the time . " <p> Washington County Attorney Pete Orput said Thursday that he was " devastated " to hear of her upcoming retirement . " I 've come to really admire her work , " he said . " She 's an incredible listener . She has a vision about the future that she sticks with and guides us with , so that we see it like she does . She thinks way ahead . " <p> O'Rourke said she is especially proud of the county 's marks from resident and employee surveys . " They go hand in hand , " she said . " Employees who are highly engaged are more productive and provide better service -- you ca n't have one without the other . " <p> Before coming to work for Washington County in 1995 , O'Rourke worked for St. Paul for 10 years as a legislative aide , city clerk , director of the citizen service office and policy analyst for the city 's mayor . She was a community organizer/social worker at the Merriam Park Community Center for several years in the early 1980s . <p> O'Rourke , who grew up in Duluth , said she has no post-retirement plans other than a family downhill-ski trip to Utah in February and a Florida family vacation in March . She and her husband , Dennis , have two daughters and four grandchildren . <p> " My focus has been on the organization , and I just want to take some time and step back and spend time with my family , " she said .
