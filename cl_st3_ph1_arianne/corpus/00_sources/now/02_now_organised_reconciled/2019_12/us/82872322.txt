<p> BAGHDAD , Dec 24 ( Reuters ) - Iraq 's parliament approved on Tuesday a new electoral law , a key demand of protesters to make elections fairer , but political deadlock is still holding up the selection of an interim prime minister . <p> The new election law passed by parliament will allow voters to elect individual lawmakers instead of choosing from party lists , and have each member of parliament represent a specific electoral district instead of groups of legislators representing entire provinces . <p> Protesters have demand not just a new electoral law , but also the removal of the entire political class and an independent prime minister with no party affiliation .
