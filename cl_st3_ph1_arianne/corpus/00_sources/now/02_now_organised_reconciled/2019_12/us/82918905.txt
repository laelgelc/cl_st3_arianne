<h> Towns to collaborate on shared coastal management plan <h> Friday <p> Information to be gathered on 35 miles of coastline in Wellfleet , Eastham , Truro and Provincetown <p> Coastal resiliency through consistency and cooperation . <p> That 's the goal of an intermunicipal shoreline management plan being developed among the towns of Wellfleet , Eastham , Truro and Provincetown . <p> Eastham Conservation Agent Shana Brogan said the plan , which is being developed with $115,000 from the Massachusetts Coastal Resilience Grant Program , will help the towns adapt to and mitigate damages from sea level rise . <p> " What we do now and how we adapt are crucial , " Brogan said . <p> All four towns have seen the erosion of beaches , shorelines , and parking lots due to flooding from coastal storms . Conservation agents from the four towns want to develop a consistent approach to conserving and managing the shoreline that connects them . <p> That means gathering information on about 35 miles of shoreline from Rock Harbor in Eastham to Race Point in Provincetown . Information about human uses and alterations , natural resources , obstructions and rates of shoreline change will be considered , as well as information about jetties , harbors , structures and shoreline vulnerabilities . <p> The Center for Coastal Studies in Provincetown will gather information from each of the four towns . <p> The center will then evaluate each town 's approach to shoreline management for its strengths and weaknesses . <p> The goal is to develop a plan that makes sense of the consistencies , conflicts and overlaps among the four towns ' methods for shoreline management , Brogan said . <p> " It 's one consistent shoreline , irrespective of the town boundaries , " Brogan said . " What we do in one town can impact another . " <p> When complete , the project should provide direction on how the four towns might develop a proactive , regional approach to address climate change . <p> Truro 's Health and Conservation Agent Emily Beebe said the matrix would be a science-based tool to help the towns adapt to sea level rise . <p> A team made up of the conservation and health agents from each town plans to meet regularly and hold a public information session in late winter or spring . <p> " We want to update the public , educate them on what we 're doing with the grant , what our progress is , and get their feedback , " Brogan said . <p> Hillary Greenberg-Lemos , Wellfleet 's health and conservation agent , will lead the project .
