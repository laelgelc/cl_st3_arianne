If you see comments in violation of our community guidelines , please report them . <p> OPINION <h> Letter : Why climate change should be an ' us for us ' issue not ' us vs. them ' <p> Perhaps the biggest contributing factor to climate change is n't human pollution , wastefulness or ignorance . It 's our fear of one another . Though the desire to end political deadlock is good , climatologists and advocates often unknowingly sow seeds of panic to spur political action . Yet this is having the opposite effect . <p> Scared humans immediately start looking for their aggressor , and too often we find ours across the aisle . This " fear of the other " has changed the climate debate from one of observational science to one of infighting and fear . <p> If we want to end the constant bickering that blockades important , planet-protecting action , we need to rethink the apocalyptic , disuniting messaging about our climate . <p> Climate change should not be an " us vs. them " issue ( insert red , blue , United States or United Nations ) . Rather , climate change is an " us FOR us " issue . If we want to see quick , powerful action on climate change , let 's stop fighting against one another and start fighting for one another . " Bipartisan " is n't just possible ; it 's essential . We all share this planet .
