<p> MANILA , Dec 26 ( Reuters ) - A typhoon that struck the central Philippines over Christmas has killed at least 13 people , disaster agency officials said on Thursday . <p> Typhoon Phanfone hit the Philippines late on Tuesday with winds of up to 120 kph ( 75 mph ) and gusts of 150 kph , heavy rain and flooding . <p> More than 58,000 people were evacuated from their homes before the storm , which caused widespread property damage , and more than 15,000 were stranded at ports when ferries were suspended . Scores of flights were cancelled . <p> The fatalities were in the central provinces of Capiz , Iloilo and Leyte , including a 13-year-old boy who was electrocuted , a man killed by a tree branch and another killed in a car accident , the disaster agency said . <p> The typhoon left the Philippines on Wednesday night and was out over the South China Sea , moving west . <p> An average of 20 typhoons hit the Philippines each year , with storms becoming fiercer in recent years .
