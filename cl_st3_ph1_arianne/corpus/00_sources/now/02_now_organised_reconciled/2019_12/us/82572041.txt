<h> Thousands form human chain in Brussels in climate change demo <p> BRUSSELS ( Reuters ) - Thousands of people holding hands formed a human chain in central Brussels on Sunday to draw public attention to the need for urgent , joint action against climate change . <p> Some 2,400 people took part in the peaceful demonstration , police said , which encircled the Belgian federal parliament and the Royal Palace . <p> The two-hour demonstration took place as policy-makers from around the world gather in Madrid for the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change . <p> " The main purpose is to ask for more climate ambition and for the federal government to tackle the climate crisis , " said Julie Van Houtryve , spokeswoman for one of the organizers , Climate Coalition in Belgium . " We need solidarity and cooperation between governments and politicians in Belgium . "
