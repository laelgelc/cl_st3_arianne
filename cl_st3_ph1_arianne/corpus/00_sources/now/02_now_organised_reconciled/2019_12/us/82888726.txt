<h> TRENDING : <h> SPONSORED : <h> Jane Fonda to be joined by Lily Tomlin at weekly climate protest <p> Actress and activist Jane Fonda will be joined by her " Grace and Frankie " and " 9 to 5 " co-star Lily Tomlin at a climate protest outside the U.S. Capitol on Friday . <p> The rally will mark the 12th consecutive Friday that Fonda has led protests in Washington , D.C. <p> The protests , known as " fire drill Fridays , " have seen a slew of activists and fellow actors take part . They include Brooklyn Decker , Sally Field , Taylor Schilling , Kyra Sedgwick and Sam Waterston , among others . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> The protests call for " fast action " on the Green New Deal and " an end to all new fossil fuel exploration/extraction &amp; taxpayer subsidies to oil companies . " This week 's demonstration specifically is set to address how climate change affects the world 's forests , according to a statement shared with The Hill . <p> The protests typically end with a civil disobedience demonstration on the steps of the Capitol Building . Fonda has had to avoid putting herself in a position to be arrested during certain weeks to avoid a 30-day jail sentence in Washington , although she has been arrested during other rallies . <p> Fonda credits teen climate activist Greta Thunberg with inspiring her to protest , after Thunberg launched her own " Fridays for Future " protests in her native Sweden . <p> She told The Washington Post ahead of the first " fire drill Friday " that " I 'm going to take my body , which is kind of famous and popular right now because of the series , and I 'm going to go to D.C. and I 'm going to have a rally every Friday , " <p> " Greta said we have to behave like it 's a crisis , " She continued . " We have to behave like our houses are on fire . "
