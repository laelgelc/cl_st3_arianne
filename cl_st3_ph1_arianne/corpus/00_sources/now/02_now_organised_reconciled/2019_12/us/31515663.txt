<p> While addressing a panel during the World Youth Forum , United Nations Food and Agriculture Organisation Chief Qu Dongyu called for using the technology , formation of better policies and encouraging the youth to participate more in the initiative to end hunger <p> Rome : Young people face huge challenges , among them growing food scarcity , malnutrition , poverty and climate shocks , making it essential to harness their potential to achieve zero hunger , the United Nations Food and Agriculture Organisation chief Qu Dongyu has said . " We live in a challenging time , " the FAO Director-General told a panel on food security in Africa at the World Youth Forum on Sunday , according to an FAO statement . He said , " But we must not feel discouraged by these challenges . There are more solutions than challenges . " <p> Mr. Qu highlighted the challenges young people in Africa and Middle East are grappling with -- rising hunger and malnutrition , uptake of increasingly unhealthy diets , lack of stable jobs and income opportunities , climate change , water scarcity , and in some countries , conflict . Referring to the goal of ending hunger in all its forms by 2030 , he said , <p> We need the energy and potential of young people to achieve rural transformation , and achieve the Sustainable Development Goal ( SDG ) 2 . <p> Failing to achieve SDG2 places reaching all SDGs at risk , stressed Mr. Qu . Making the most of innovation , digital technology , investments , encouraging youth 's greater appreciation of sustainable and healthy food systems and better policies are some of the solutions , he said . <p> To this end , Mr. Qu also presented FAO 's recently announced Hand-in-Hand initiative , which seeks to match donors and recipients in a more targeted way to assist the most vulnerable people and help to achieve the SDGs . <p> At the forum , the FAO and Egypt agreed to deepen their collaboration in support of the country 's rural youth and women . The FAO Director-General and Egypt 's Minister of Foreign Affairs Sameh Shoukry signed the accord on the margins of the World Youth Forum on Sunday . <p> Under the accord , FAO and the Ministry will seek to roll out programmes promoting employment on and off farm and in different value chains , and providing training and capacity development for youth and women , said the statement .
