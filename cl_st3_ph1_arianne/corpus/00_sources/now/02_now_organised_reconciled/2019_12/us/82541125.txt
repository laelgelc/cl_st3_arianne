<h> Activists Call For Declaration Of Climate Emergency At Bloomington City Hall <h> By Bente Bouthier <h> Posted December 6 , 2019 <p> Various musical groups performed at the rally . ( Seth Tackett , WFIU/WTIU News ) <p> Activists at Bloomington City Hall are calling for more progress from the city on climate change . <p> Friday afternoon 's event was put together by local environmental organizations , with no official permission from the city . <p> Beverley Thompson is a sophomore at Indiana University who attended the City Hall sit-in . She is a member of the environmental group , Students For A Green New World . <p> Thompson says as local activism continues , the relationship between the city is becoming more collaborative . <p> " It used to be very , like one group was doing something , but now , we 're all doing stuff , " she says . " And even though it may not be the same thing for everyone , we all want this climate awareness , we want climate action . "
