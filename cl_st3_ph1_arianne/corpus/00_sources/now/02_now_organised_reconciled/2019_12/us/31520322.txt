<p> STOCKTON -- Many of the Californians who encountered Mike Bloomberg on his first campaign stop in the state Wednesday were wary of the billionaire media tycoon , either for his wealth or the policies he supported as New York mayor . <p> It did n't matter to Laura Plummer , a 69-year-old retiree , that Bloomberg 's first campaign stop was in a design studio called Most Modest . She was wondering if Bloomberg could relate to someone like her , a woman who moved into a Stockton motel after her apartment burned down . <p> " I 'm a little nervous about it because I do n't know if he could understand our needs , " said Plummer , one of a dozen people handpicked by Stockton Mayor Michael Tubbs to meet Bloomberg in the former auto repair shop in a grittier part of town . <p> Plummer softened a bit after the hour-long meeting . The Bloomberg campaign did not allow reporters inside to watch the interactions . <p> " He listened with an open mind , " said Plummer . She said she has n't picked a candidate in the March 3 California primary , but added , " Yeah , I 'd consider " voting for Bloomberg . <p> It was the first of many steps Bloomberg needs to take to change California voters ' early view of him . <p> Bloomberg is the first choice of only 2% of the state 's likely Democratic voters , according to a poll released last week by UC Berkeley 's Institute of Governmental Studies . Only 8% of respondents said they were even considering voting for him , and 40% said they had a negative impression of him . <p> Winning California is key to Bloomberg 's unorthodox campaign plan to skip the first four primaries and caucuses and focus on the large states holding contests March 3 , including California , Texas and Georgia . The 77-year-old plans to spend $14 million through the end of the year on TV commercials in California to introduce himself to voters . <h> Campaign 2020 <p> Tubbs tried to smooth the way for him Wednesday with his endorsement -- which The Chronicle first reported Tuesday -- and by publicly accepting Bloomberg 's apology for his support as mayor for stop-and-frisk police policies that had officers detain people they thought might be engaged in crime and search them for weapons or other contraband . <p> Tubbs said that as a 29-year-old African American man whose father is serving a life sentence in prison for robbery and kidnapping , " issues about about criminal justice are deeply personal to me . " <p> " I think we all recognize in 2019 that stop-and-frisk is not a good policy , " Tubbs told 50 supporters and reporters at the Trail Coffee Roasters cafe , during a second stop Wednesday with Bloomberg at his side . <p> Bloomberg said of stop-and-frisk , " Maybe I should have paid more attention , but it got out of hand . " <p> Bobby Bivens , president of the Stockton NAACP , was unconvinced . <p> " That 's BS , " Bivens said . " You ca n't be the mayor and do n't know what the policies of your police department are . " <p> But Bivens , who says he has not picked a candidate , added , " I think that when you own up to the fact that you made a major error , then we have to accept that at its face value and then look at what 's transpired since that point . " <p> Bloomberg spent most of his time in Stockton outlining his policies designed to lift people out of poverty and improve housing . Like several other Democrats in the race , he has proposed increasing the federal earned income tax credit that goes to low-income people . He also called for raising the federal minimum wage from $7.25 an hour to $15 by 2025 and for tying further increases to the growth in median salaries . <p> On housing , he proposed setting aside $10 billion in federal funds for a competition that would reward cities that " remove obstacles " to building more affordable housing , including " changing zoning rules to allow property owners to build additional housing units . " Some such proposals have been controversial in California cities and suburbs , including a stalled bill by state Sen. Scott Wiener , D-San Francisco , that would allow denser housing near transit stops and multi-unit construction in some neighborhoods now zoned for single-family housing . <p> Later Wednesday , Bloomberg got praise -- but not an endorsement -- from former Gov . Jerry Brown when they shared the stage for a climate change discussion at the American Geophysical Union 's convention in San Francisco . <p> " Hey , he was managing New York and that 's pretty good . That 's very good , " Brown said of Bloomberg . " It 's not as tough as California , but its pretty damn important . " <p> Bloomberg and Brown co-founded America 's Pledge , under which governments and businesses commit to reduce their carbon output . Bloomberg said agreements have been made to shut down 299 coal-fired power plants out of 530 in America . <p> " This administration has its head in the sand , " Bloomberg said , " but the rest of us are putting our heads together and getting things done .
