<p> BEIJING , Dec 6 ( Reuters ) - China 's Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs issued a three-year plan to speed up the recovery of pig production in the country after an epidemic of African swine fever ravaged the world 's largest hog herd . <p> The ministry reiterated earlier targets for pig stocks to stop falling and start picking up by the end of this year , and for production to recover to close to normal levels by end-2020 . <p> It also said populous southeast coastal provinces Jiangsu , Zhejiang , Guangdong and Fujian as well as the city of Tianjin must achieve a self-sufficiency rate of around 70% for pork .
