If you see comments in violation of our community guidelines , please report them . <p> OPINION <h> Why I support Tom Brier for Congress <p> The 10th Congressional District race has been closely watched all over the nation and is predicted to be one of the closest , if not the closest , congressional election in the nation . Most presently see this as an inevitable showdown between incumbent Rep. Scott Perry and Auditor General Eugene DePasquale . Bobby Jeffries has barely made a dent in Perry 's support base . On the Democrat side , however , a funny thing happened on the way to DePasquale 's " inevitable " nomination . Tom Brier , a 27-year-old attorney , author and student of political philosophy from Hershey has kept neck-in-neck with DePasquale in terms of both fundraising and grassroots support through the second and third quarters of 2019 . At very least , his candidacy has proven viable and worthy of some consideration . In my opinion , he is the most viable candidate in the race . <p> I know what most readers must be thinking : why would a moderate or progressive voter throw their support behind a 27-year-old candidate with no political experience ? DePasquale , after all , is a remarkably qualified politician with a track record of success in state government . He seems to have all the right credentials to make a competitive run at flipping the 10th District blue . While Brier is ... well , he is 27 . <p> A candidate 's youthfulness or lack of political experience has never been a good indicator of their viability as a candidate ( just ask Richard Nixon or Joe Crowley ) . A better indicator of a candidate 's qualities are whether the beliefs and convictions of the candidate align with those of the constituents . According to a 2018 US Census Bureau survey , the 10th District will have approximately 600,000 residents of legal voting age in 2020. 90,000 of those will be under 25 . Another 95,000 will be between 25 and 35 . That is roughly equal to the entire eligible population over 55 . Numerous national studies have shown that members of the " Millennial " or " GenZ " age groups tend to be far more likely to support progressive reforms , and far less likely to strongly identify with any political party , than any of the immediately preceding generations . <p> All of this is to say that , if the 10th District is going blue , the path to doing so will require substantial voter turnout rates among the younger voters . This is where Tom Brier exceeds the Auditor General in virtue and competence . Brier 's campaign has , from the outset , targeted issues that matter to younger people . Enormously challenging issues such as climate change , rampant political corruption , a pending collapse of the entire healthcare and retirement systems , the rapidly declining need for labor in an increasingly automated economy , drug abuse and mental health crises . Most experts predict that only around 30 years separates us from these issues becoming catastrophic . The 25 year old of 2020 is the 55 year old of 2050 . The burden of inhabiting this brave new world falls upon the youth of today . <p> The political establishment is not working . The Democratic party is incoherent and self-serving at best and bunglingly incompetent at worst . The GOP appears , at least from the outside perspective , to be suffering from some form of mass insanity . It is time for the stakeholders of the future to shatter once and for all this incessant belief that the status quo will ultimately lead to anything other than complete catastrophe . It is time to put the antiquated notions of capitalism vs. socialism in the past where they belong . <p> It is time for something new . Tom 's compassion , intelligence , sincerity , and devotion to progress have solidified my support of this remarkable young man . I encourage each and every reader , regardless of political affiliation , to come meet Tom on December 19 , 2019 at the Central Market York from 6 PM -- 8PM.
