<h> Letter : Overdevelopment of land is wasteful , dangerous <h> Monday <p> Dec 16 , 2019 at 4:11 AM <p> Recently , as I was driving east on Route 161 near New Albany and Hamilton Road , I noticed a large new sign erected on what was likely former farmland , now cleared and being readied for development . The sign advertises " Hamilton Quarter , " yet another addition to the seemingly endless sprawling development pattern that characterizes Columbus . <p> Apparently there are no limits to how much land -- previously forests and farms -- can be consumed by this wasteful and dangerous habit of land use . <p> Despite well-intentioned efforts by the Mid-Ohio Regional Planning Commission and the Insight 2050 study , little is being done to address what is becoming a serious quality-of-life issue . <p> Worse , this wasteful use of land , a precious nonrenewable resource , is exacerbating climate change , which itself remains wholly unaddressed by any of the powers that be in Ohio . <p> Until coordinated land-use planning and the mechanisms to implement and enforce it are made a priority , the costly impacts of uncontrolled growth will only worsen , much to the detriment of all Ohioans . <p> David Lipstreu , Newark <h> Never miss a story <p> Choose the plan that 's right for you .
