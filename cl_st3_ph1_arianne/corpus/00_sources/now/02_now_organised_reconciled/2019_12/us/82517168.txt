<h> UW Climate Strike <p> press release : On December 6 , a day of global climate action , students and community members will be striking to demand UW-Madison take steps to take action against climate change . There will be a rally at Library Mall to collect petitions and hear from speakers . Speakers will include : <p> ? James Mills , a journalist and adjunct faculty at the University of Wisconsin Nelson Institute For Environmental Studies <p> ? Cara Nastali , an Executive Board member of CLEAN and undergraduate student at UW-Madison <p> ? And other student representatives <p> Following the rally and petition collection , attendees will be marching up Bascom Hill and placing signs on the hill to make a visible statement about support for a clean energy transition . The petition includes demands that Chancellor Blank and the administration make a commitment to : <p> ? Power the university exclusively with renewable energy by 2050 and derive all electrical power from renewable resources by 2030 <p> ? Open up meetings of the Sustainability Advisory Council to students of the university to allow more student involvement and representation in meeting these goals <p> ? Have a concrete plan of action from the Sustainability Advisory Council by the end of January 2021 to reach these energy goals <p> CLEAN and co-sponsoring organization leaders will meet to deliver the signed petitions to Chancellor Blank during the week following the strike .
