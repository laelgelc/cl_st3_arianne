" <p> In a speech peppered with French , Thunberg joked about the similarities between her home country and Canada -- the cold winters , the moose , the hockey . But then her words became serious . <p> " You are a nation that is allegedly a climate leader ; Sweden is also allegedly a climate leader , " she said . " In both cases , it means absolutely nothing . In both cases , it 's just empty words . " <p> Thunberg , who began striking for climate action outside the Swedish parliament in 2018 , said the fight against climate change will not stop until world leaders take the problem seriously . <p> And she dismissed naysayers who say young people ca n't make a difference . <p> " I think we have proven that to be wrong by now , " Thunberg said . " The people have spoken . We are the change and change is coming . " <p> Some of those who marched behind Thunberg said they were inspired by the activist , who has become a global icon . <p> " A few years ago , climate change was n't on my radar at all but -- just as Greta said -- I started listening to the scientists and I 've changed the way I live , " said H ? l ? ne Audrit , a PhD student in neuropsychology . <p> " I 'm a vegetarian now , I no longer own a car , it 's hard not to take action when you realize how urgent the situation is . " <p> Kahawi Jacobs , who came from Kahnawake , said she felt the power of the crowd . <p> " Respecting and preserving the Earth is just part of who we are , " Jacobs said . " Just to be on our territory with so many people behind us , it 's phenomenal . " <p> After the march , Montreal Mayor Val ? rie Plante welcomed Thunberg to city hall , where she was given the keys to the city . <p> " Greta represents all the youth pushing us , the adults , the leaders , the companies , to take our responsibilities , " said Plante , who was among those who took to the streets on Friday . <p> Thunberg told the mayor she was " very overwhelmed " by the reception she received in Montreal . <p> Earlier , Thunberg met Liberal Leader Justin Trudeau privately .
