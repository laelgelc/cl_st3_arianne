If you see comments in violation of our community guidelines , please report them . <h> Two arrested during protest in support of Green New Deal at Sen. Gary Peters ' office <p> LANSING -- Two people were arrested Tuesday at Sen. Gary Peters ' Lansing office as they and about two dozen others demanded he support the Green New Deal . <p> The Sunrise Movement 's Lansing hub leader Jessica Diaz , 20 , and state coordinator Nicholas Jansen , 25 , were arrested Tuesday at Peters ' office in the Boji Tower after they and two others refused to leave until he expressed his support for the Green New Deal , according to a news release from Sunrise . <p> Both Jansen and Diaz were in custody at the Lansing Police Department 's jail Tuesday evening on trespass allegations , then each posted a $250 cash bond . No charges had been filed as of Wednesday morning . <p> Peters ' spokesperson Nirmeen Fahmy said the building where Peters ' Lansing office is located was closing to visitors for the day . When the group refused to leave after multiple requests , building officials contacted Lansing police , she said . <p> The Sunrise Movement is a national organization of young people fighting to stop climate change . <p> Tuesday 's sit-in mirrored nationwide protests Dec. 6 when young people demanded their local representatives back the Green New Deal and proposed legislation to combat climate change . <p> " Senator Peters believes that climate change is an urgent threat to Michigan 's economy , our Great Lakes and our national security -- and it 's a challenge that must be addressed through commonsense solutions , " Fahmy said in a statement . " Our staff works to serve and listen to Michiganders across our state , and constituents are welcome to share their concerns and views with any of our offices . " <p> The Green New Deal is a congressional resolution that lays out a ground plan for fighting climate change . It calls on the federal government to dramatically decrease greenhouse gas emissions and use of fossil fuels across the nation , as well as well as guarantee new jobs in clean energy industries . <p> It was introduced to the House in February by Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez , D-New York , and referred to several committees . Peters was one of 43 senators who voted " present " on the resolution in March . <p> Peters , as part of the Senate Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs Committee , investigated and released a report on how climate change can cost taxpayers money and hurt the state , Fahmy said . <p> He also recently secured $9 million for the Great Lakes Restoration Initiative to protect the lakes , and has led efforts to improve oversight of pipeline operation in the Great Lakes and prevent a spill . <p> Diaz said in a news release that she and other Sunrise Movement members have asked Peters for more than nine months to co-sponsor the Green New Deal . <p> " It 's insane to keep doing what we 've always done , we need a different approach " she said in a news release . " We need a Green New Deal , and I 'm not afraid to put my body on the line and be arrested . "
