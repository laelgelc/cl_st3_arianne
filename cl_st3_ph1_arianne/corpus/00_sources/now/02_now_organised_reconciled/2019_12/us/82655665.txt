<h> Texas A&amp;M student selected to present NASA research at AMS conference <p> A Texas A&amp;M student is presenting her abstract research at a AMS conference . <p> Author : Kacey Bowen <p> Published : 1:27 PM CST December 12 , 2019 <p> Updated : 10:31 PM CST December 12 , 2019 <p> COLLEGE STATION , Texas -- A Texas A&amp;M student has been selected to present her abstract research at a AMS conference . <p> Ariana Castillo is a senior at A&amp;M University and is expected to graduate in December with a B.S. in Atmospheric Science . <p> She interned with NASA over the summer for the second time at the Langley Research Center where she studied smoke plumes from biomass burning and how it affects our environment , our atmosphere and our ecosystems , according to a bio on her GoFundMe . She wrote that it is important to understand modeling where smoke goes in order to forecast for future pollution events so people can be safe and healthy , but also to know how it affects other environments downstream and its implications on climate change . <p> She created a GoFundMe in early December to help raise funds so she could attend the conference . Since then , she has reached her goal and is planning on attending . <p> Also on her GoFundMe she wrote that the AMS conference has been a great opportunity for her to network and establish connections , but also to lay down foundations for her career and a possible path for graduate school . Adding that she wants this to be an opportunity to show other minority women that they also have a place in the atmospheric science community .
