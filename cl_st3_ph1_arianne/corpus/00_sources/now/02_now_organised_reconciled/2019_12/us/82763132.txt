<p> WASHINGTON , Dec 18 ( Reuters ) - The U.S. House of Representatives passed the second of two articles of impeachment against President Donald Trump on Wednesday , voting that Trump obstructed Congress by refusing to cooperate with a Democratic-led impeachment investigation . <p> The 229 to 198 vote fell almost entirely along party lines . All of Trump 's fellow Republicans opposed it , and all but three Democrats supported it . <p> The House has already voted on the first article of impeachment , which charges Trump with abusing his power by pressuring Ukraine to investigate a political rival , Democratic former Vice President Joe Biden .
