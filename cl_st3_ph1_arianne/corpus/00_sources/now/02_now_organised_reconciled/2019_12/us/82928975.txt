Our government proposed a solution of conscription and war that needlessly killed 60,000 American young men . <p> Next was Russia , so we spent our money on the solution of nuclear bombs and quickly outdated weapons systems . Then came the emergency of terrorism and the Bush administration 's public fearmongering of terrorists ' obtaining a nuclear weapon - a self-concocted , bogus lie promulgated by war profiteers . The solution was the baseless invasion of Iraq . More blood and treasure . Now we have the most dire emergency of all : the threat of human extinction within 12 years as a result of CO2 forced climate change . <p> In 1968 , Dr. Paul Ehrlich , a Stanford biologist , stated in his book " The Population Bomb " that humanity would soon be doomed due to critical shortages of food and water , even in the United States . He publicly advocated the solution of spiking water and food in Africa and Asia with sterility drugs as a way of forced population control , which , he said , if not accomplished by 1980 , would guarantee humanity 's demise by 1990 . Instead , vastly increased crop yields of various staple grains have enabled ecosystems to healthfully sustain far more people . <p> Failure of local crop production and water scarcity are part of what is driving the huge sub-Saharan African population migration to Europe . Though blamed on CO2 , other causes are more likely . <p> Solving the problem requires effective land reclamation and rehabilitation , as was successfully accomplished in this country to end the Dust Bowl , with its vast loss of topsoil . This requires the implementation of proven techniques of agricultural sustainability . Unfortunately , " sustainable development " has been conflated with the U.N . ' s Agenda 21 and Agenda 2030 which , though they ostensibly deal with these problems , are far-reaching in their proposals , and much more ambitious in their goals . <p> Some " alarmist " evolutionary biologists and the politicians who echo them have given humanity itself no more than 10 more years before the level of required adaptation will outstrip that amount necessary for our own survival , as well as that of many other species . The current megafauna extinction is largely due to the elimination of habitat due to human overpopulation and its consequences , not climate change . <p> Nevertheless , hysterical voices have become the centerpiece of the current climate change movements , which voice an urgent need to deal with this " climate emergency " by quickly eliminating not only all carbon-based emissions , but hydroelectric and nuclear sources of power as well . Unfortunately , large-scale replacement methods of energy procurement are not yet viable . Some alarmists have gone so far as to say climate change deniers should be prosecuted and thrown in jail , as their beliefs are a threat to humanity ! <p> This exemplifies Voltaire 's adage that if someone can get you to believe absurdities , they can get you to perform atrocities . <p> The current hysteria is absolutely not based on any real , believable science that shows that humanity or other forms of life are doomed within a decade - or a millennium for that matter . The sky is not falling . Our more immediate ecological crisis is not CO2 , but things we can actually change - such as the production and use of vast quantities of toxic chemicals and pesticides . <p> Climate science is still evolving - earth 's climate is cyclic , and forcing models are n't set in stone . Yet , the more strident the voices , and the more dire , immediate and universal the threat , the louder the call is for us to quickly surrender to antidemocratic solutions that are ill considered and likely ineffective . <p> Now we have the immutable correctness of consensus science - a fallible variation of the types of bogus political consensus that brought us Vietnam , Iraq and Afghanistan . The so-called solution to this threat to human existence would be overseen by a nonelected , multinational group of individuals , and which , as the Commonwealth Expert Group 's Secretary General openly states , would be implemented by " governments yielding their national sovereignty to multilateral authorities able to enforce laws across environmentally invisible frontiers . " <p> To me this sounds like an Orwellian communist totalitarian dictatorship of the worst kind . It is a so-called solution that will be far worse than the other miserably concocted ones I 've seen implemented in my lifetime already .
