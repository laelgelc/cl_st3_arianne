<p> DUBAI , Dec 29 ( Reuters ) - Two Saudi men shot dead last week in the eastern city of Dammam were driving a car loaded with explosives and were planning an " imminent terrorist operation " , the SPA state news agency said on Sunday , citing security forces . <p> The two were found with explosive paste in the car , SPA said . They started shooting at security forces when they were approached on Wednesday and were shot dead . <p> Five kg of paste was taken from the car and found later to contain the explosive RDX , SPA said . <p> Police named the two men and said a third unnamed man had been arrested .
