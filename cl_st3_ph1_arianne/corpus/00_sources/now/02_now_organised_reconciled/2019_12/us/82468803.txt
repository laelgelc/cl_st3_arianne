<p> MADRID ( Reuters ) - Spain 's Repsol ( REP.MC ) pledged on Monday to reduce net carbon emissions from its operations and most of its products to zero by 2050 and absorb a 4.8 billion euro ( $5.3 billion ) hit to the value of its oil and gas assets in the process . <p> FILE PHOTO : The logo of Spanish energy giant Repsol SA is seen during the opening ceremony of its first gas station in Mexico City , Mexico March 12 , 2018 . REUTERS/Carlos Jasso/File Photo <p> Describing the move as an industry first , Repsol said it wanted to lead a wider transition to renewable energy , in line with the goals of the 2015 Paris Agreement to avert catastrophic climate change . <p> Repsol announced its target as delegates opened a United Nations climate summit in Madrid aimed at injecting fresh impetus into the accord , which enters a crucial implementation phase next year . <p> Oil and gas producers are coming under increasing pressure from shareholders and environmental activists to reduce their role in fuelling climate change , and investors have poured money into funds that take account of companies ' green credentials . <p> Repsol 's pledge makes it an outlier relative to larger energy companies , which are still investing in developing oil and gas fields that are incompatible with the Paris goals , according to financial think-tank Carbon Tracker . <p> A Repsol spokesman said the new target covers 95% of the emissions released by the use of products it sells , which are complex to account for and have become a key issue for shareholders . <p> These emissions - known as Scope 3 - are typically around six times larger than the combined emissions produced by oil companies ' own operations and power supply needs , according to Reuters calculations . <p> Basing asset values on prospects for oil and gas prices in line with the Paris Agreement would bring a post-tax impairment charge of 4.8 billion euros in 2019 , but that will not affect cash flow or shareholder remuneration , the company said . <h> " SET MORE AMBITIOUS OBJECTIVES " <p> Companies aiming to achieve " net-zero " usually balance emissions from their operations by investing in technology that can store carbon , or in natural sinks such as forests . <p> Repsol said it could reach at least 70% of its goal using technology that was already developed or nearly mature , and then would implement carbon capture , use and storage to raise that figure , and turn to natural sinks if necessary . <p> " We are convinced that we must set more ambitious objectives to fight climate change , " Chief Executive Josu Jon Imaz said in a statement . <p> Giving extra impetus to its new goals , Repsol will link at least 40% of managers ' long-term variable pay to its emissions reduction targets . <p> It will also increase its targets for low-carbon generation capacity to a total 7.5 gigawatts in Spain and beyond by 2025 , double its production of biofuels from vegetable oils , and start producing green hydrogen in its refining business . <p> Reporting by Ashifa Kassam and Isla Binnie , Additional reporting by James Mackenzie ; Editing by Matthew Green and Rosalba O'Brien
