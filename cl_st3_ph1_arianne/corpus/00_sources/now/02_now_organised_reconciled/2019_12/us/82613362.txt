<p> One person was killed and two others were seriously injured when a speeding car slammed into a home in Simi Valley and flipped early Tuesday , police said . <p> Simi Valley police and the Ventura County Fire Department were dispatched to the home at Sycamore Drive and Morley Street shortly after 1:30 a.m . When they arrived , they found a Nissan 350Z on its roof on the home 's front lawn . The vehicle had slammed into a bedroom , but no one inside the home was injured , Cmdr. Steve Shorts said . <p> A woman inside the car was pronounced dead at the scene . Another woman in the car was severely injured . She and a man who was ejected from the vehicle during the crash were taken to a hospital . None of the car 's occupants were identified . <p> Investigators have determined that speed was a factor in the crash , Shorts said , though it is not clear what caused the car to land upside down . Authorities will be looking into whether alcohol was also a factor , he said . <p> Advertisement <p> " What we know right now is that the car was a two-seater sports coupe , so it was impossible for everybody to be seat-belted in , " Shorts said . <p> There were three people inside the home at the time of the crash , but no one was in the bedroom when the car slammed into it .
