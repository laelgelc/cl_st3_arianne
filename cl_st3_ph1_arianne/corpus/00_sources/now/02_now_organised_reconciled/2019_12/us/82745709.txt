( Photo : Pierre-Philippe Marcou , AFP via Getty Images ) <p> It is Saturday morning in the bustling city of Madrid , host city of the UN climate change conference ( COP25 ) . As we made our way through throngs of people on our way back from the COP last evening , it became quite clear that holiday shopping is in full swing . The holiday mood of the city is in stark contrast to the tenor of the negotiating halls at the conference center . <p> Ministers continue to wrangle over who should take responsibility for all of the CO2 pollution that we have pumped into the atmosphere , and whether rich nations should help developing countries pay for all of the loss and damage that is the result of the changing climate . As Ministers fight over how to count emission reduction ( Should earlier emission accomplishments carry over ? What about those countries that exceed their goals ? Should polluting nations be allowed to include those that they " sell " , or is that double counting ? ) , Youth are shouting , protesting , singing , taking actions , and occupying the negotiating hall demanding that Ministers act ambitiously and urgently to save the planet from destruction so that they have the same world to live in that we enjoy today . <p> The science is quite clear , the International Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) scientists phrase it this way - " Limiting global warming to 1.5 ? C would require rapid , far-reaching and unprecedented changes in all aspects of society . " Yet , Ministers continue to discuss emission reduction targets that will be included in their Nationally Determined Commitments ( NDCs ) due NEXT year that do n't even come close to what science shows is needed . <p> From the top down , the COP President to Ministers , negotiators , and staff , all acknowledge through words what is needed , but as of this writing there is no indication that this understanding will lead to the required action . The COP was supposed to close last evening , but through mid-day Saturday , there is still no indication that it will close . <p> There remains a sharp disconnect between what non-governmental organizations ( NGOs ) are calling for and what the negotiators appear to be headed towards . The Climate Action Network ( CAN ) is a network of over 1,300 NGOs working on climate change from around the world in more than 120 countries . The NGO members are autonomous and independent , and CAN produces daily press briefings throughout the COP . The overwhelming message from these NGOs is that not enough is being done to solve what UN Secretary General Antonio Gutterez and nearly all others at COP25 now call " the Climate Emergency " . There is nowhere near the urgency required to turn things around in the coming decade . <p> Alden Meyer , Director , Strategy and Policy , Union of Concerned Scientists described the progress this way : " I 've been attending these climate negotiations since they first started in 1991 . But never have I seen the almost total disconnect we 're seeing here in Madrid between what the science requires and the people of the world demand , and what the climate negotiations are delivering in terms of meaningful action . This is true both when it comes to raising the ambition of countries ' emissions reduction commitments under the Paris Agreement and to mobilizing much greater support for the vulnerable countries and communities who are facing ever-more-devastating impacts of climate change . " <p> Last Friday , the day before we arrived at these negotiations , there was a significant protest started by youth , and followed by an estimated 500,000 people streaming through the streets of Madrid calling for immediate and significant action on the climate emergency . Fridays for Future , the youth movement sparked by Time Magazine 's Person of the Year Greta Thunberg , staged multiple actions and press briefings throughout the COP , with a final action yesterday where the crowd lamented the lack of real action through songs , speeches , and chants . <p> We even witnessed a live call from the International Space Station where Spanish Astronaut Luca Parmitano called for climate action with the famous phrase " Houston ... we have a problem ! " He then went on to stress the urgent need for action to save the planet . Luca described the physical changes that can be directly observed from space , such as polar ice cap reduction and desertification . <p> There are many different groups of countries represented through various organizations . One such group is called the " High Ambition Coalition " that includes countries like the Marshall Islands , Belize , Bataan , and the European Union . These countries are all imploring the world to bring urgency and ambition to these negotiations . The EU is one of the organizations that is stepping up by announcing their Green Deal to achieve carbon neutrality by 2050 . But , other developed countries are not doing enough , and this Coalition is demanding significant emission reductions . <p> The world and her inhabitants are begging , calling , demanding , screaming , imploring , and pleading for action as government Ministers negotiate with an apparent understanding of the urgency and magnitude of this emergency , but is anyone REALLY listening ? <p> If these words reach YOU and you wonder what you can do , try these suggestions . <p> 1 .
