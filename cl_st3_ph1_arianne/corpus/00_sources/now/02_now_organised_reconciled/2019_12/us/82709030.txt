Trump recognized that the climate accord is a costly non-solution , regardless of one 's position on climate change . <p> It 's no surprise that many countries and people are talking the climate talk , but not walking the walk . Once wishful platitudes and ambitious goals meet the realities of an energy-dependent world , commitment evaporates . <p> The stark reality is that healthier economies mean more energy use , and rising emissions , though not always . When global economies were slumping , it was easier to commit to emissions reductions . When global economies began to grow , their commitment changed . <p> Overall , global coal demand has risen largely due to Asian countries building more coal-fired plants . China is planning 350 new coal-fired power plants . Turkey and Indonesia have expanded their use of coal . Pakistan , bluntly stated , " Given the future economic growth ... an exponential increase of emissions for many decades is likely to occur before any decrease in emissions can be expected . " <p> Even some of the most climate progressive nations have struggled . Germany , a world leader on climate action , has failed to cut emissions and has actually seen emissions rise . Japan , rejecting nuclear energy , will expand coal use for energy . Poland and South Korea also have plans for coal expansions . <p> The signatories compliance with the Paris Agreement 2030 targets is dismal .
