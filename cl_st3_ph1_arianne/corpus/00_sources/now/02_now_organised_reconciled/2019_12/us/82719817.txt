<p> BERLIN , Dec 16 ( Reuters ) - The German government and states have agreed to a carbon price of 25 euros ( $27.56 ) per tonne in 2021 that will then increase annually and hit 55 euros in 2025 , sources said on Monday . <p> In November , the lower house of parliament approved a major climate protection package to help Germany meet its 2030 target for reducing greenhouse gas emissions . An initial price of 10 euros a tonne for carbon dioxide emissions from heating and transport , was criticised by the opposition Greens as too low .
