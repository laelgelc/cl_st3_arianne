<h> Pro-Impeachment rally held in Lexington <p> By WKYT News Staff&amp;nbsp&amp;nbsp <p> Posted : Tue 10:35 PM , Dec 17 , 2019&amp;nbsp&amp;nbsp <p> Updated : Tue 11:22 PM , Dec 17 , 2019 <p> LEXINGTON , Ky . ( WKYT ) - While the House of Representatives prepares to vote on two articles of impeachment against President Trump , organizers across the country have planned a number of rallies in favor of removing the president from office . One rally was held Tuesday evening in Lexington . <p> The events are called " Nobody is above the Law " protests . <p> Chuck Eddy was in attendance . He is a Republican candidate for Congress who supports the president 's removal from office . <p> " It 's clear that Trump violated his oath of office by asking Ukraine for aid in investigating Joe Biden , that 's interference for our election , and he 's also refused to acknowledge any of his subpoenas , " Eddy said . <p> 18-year old McKayla Weaver said Trump 's stance on environmental issues is her main concern . <p> " His belief that climate change is a hoax is really failing my future , " she said . <p> Fiorella Salazar , who moved to Lexington from Peru when she was 10 , said Trump 's immigration policies personally affect her . <p> " Trump got elected and all of a sudden my papers froze and I had to become a dreamer to stay here , " she said . <p> A student at UK , who did n't want to be identified , said he was studying for finals in his downtown apartment and could n't concentrate over the noise of the rally . He parked on Main Street and played a pro-Trump song while protesters marched . <p> " As an American , you should be able to voice your opinion one way or the other , I 'm glad there 's no violence here , " he said . <p> The Lexington rally was held at Robert F. Stephens Courthouse Plaza . <p> State House Representative Kelly Flood ( D-75 ) was one of several guests that spoke at the event . <p> People are now marching thru downtown . I 've seen several people drive by and voice support for the president .
