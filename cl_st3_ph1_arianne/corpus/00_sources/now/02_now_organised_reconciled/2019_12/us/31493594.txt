<p> ExxonMobil was cleared Tuesday of claims it hid the financial costs of climate change from investors . <p> The New York Attorney General filed suit against the oil giant in 2018 alleging that the Texas-based company deceived the public and investors for years by using two sets of books to account for the rising costs of environmental regulations around the world -- and the negative impact it would have on the company 's worth . <p> But Tuesday Manhattan Supreme Court Justice Barry Ostrager ruled in favor of Exxon , clearing them of the allegations in the " hyperbolic " lawsuit and declining to make the energy company pay any damages . <p> " The Office of the Attorney General failed to prove , by a preponderance of the evidence , that ExxonMobil made any material misstatements or omissions about its practices and procedures that misled any reasonable investor , " Ostrager 's decision says . <p> Though the AG 's office dropped two fraud claims against Exxon unexpectedly at the end of trial , Ostrager said if he had ruled on those claims Exxon , " would not have been held liable . " <p> " Nothing in this opinion is intended to absolve ExxonMobil from responsibly for contributing to climate change through the emission of greenhouse gases in the production of its fossil fuel products , " Ostrager wrote . " But ExxonMobil is in the business of producing energy , and this is a securities fraud case , not a climate change case . " <p> Environmental activists rally for accountability for fossil fuel companies outside of New York Supreme Court.Getty Images <p> Exxon spokesman Casey Norton said , " Today 's ruling affirms the position ExxonMobil has held throughout the New York Attorney General 's baseless investigation . We provided our investors with accurate information on the risks of climate change . " <p> " Lawsuits that waste millions of dollars of taxpayer money do nothing to advance meaningful actions that reduce the risks of climate change , " Norton said adding the company " will continue to invest in researching breakthrough technologies to reduce emissions while meeting society 's growing demand for energy . " <p> " Throughout this case , we laid out how Exxon made materially false , misleading , and confusing representations to the American people about the company 's response to climate change regulations , " AG Letitia James said in a statement . <p> " Exxon 's inability to tell the truth , further underscores the lies that have been sold to the American public for decades . Despite this decision , we will continue to fight to ensure companies are held responsible for actions that undermine and jeopardize the financial health and safety of Americans across our country , and we will continue to fight to end climate change . "
