<h> Greta Thunberg : Time magazine 's ' Person of the Year ' 2019 , since 1927 <p> 16-year-old Swedish schoolgirl , has been named Time magazine 's ' Person of the Year ' for 2019 . <p> The teenager , who inspired a global movement to fight climate change , becomes the youngest laureate of this distinction , which has been awarded by the magazine in a tradition that started in 1927 . <p> Thunberg kick-started her global movement for climate change in August 2018 , by boycotting school . <p> To register her message , she camped outside the country 's parliament for days wielding a sign painted in black letters on a white background that read Skolstrejk f ? r klimatet : " School Strike for Climate . " <p> The campaign soon attracted global attention birthing the now popular #FridaysForFuture hashtag . <p> " I felt everything was meaningless and there was no point going to school if there was no future , " she was quoted to have said . <p> " I promised myself I was going to do everything I could do to make a difference . " <p> The magazine added that " in the 16 months since , she has addressed heads of state at the UN , met with the pope , sparred with the president of the United States and inspired 4 million people to join the global climate strike on September 20 , 2019 , in what was the largest climate demonstration in human history . " <p> She was recently at the COP25 climate talks in Madrid , where she gave a speech , criticising world leaders for " negotiating loopholes " : <p> " Greta Thunberg is an ordinary teenage girl who , in summoning the courage to speak truth to power , became the icon of a generation , " Time posted on its social media on Thursday <p> She was earlier named among TIME 's 2019 next generation leaders series , which celebrates young minds influencing the world positively .
