We hope that you continue to enjoy our free content . <h> Roxbury joins fight for Lake Hopatcong funding <p> And that 's exactly what Township Councilman Mark Crowley fears is about to happen again regarding funding for Lake Hopatcong . <p> At the Dec. 3 meeting of the council , Crowley said that the Lake Hopatcong Commission had requested $489,000 from the state Department of Environmental Protection ( DEP ) . The funds , he said , would be used in large part to put protections and programs in place to avoid the harmful algae bloom breakout that plagued the lake for most of this past year . <p> The breakout left the lake unusable for swimming for the entire past summer season . Boating was still an option , but overall , use of the lake , including boating , was down . Area businesses , which are dependent on a successful summer recreational season , suffered as a result . <p> " At first , the DEP said yes . Now , they are saying no . Instead , they want to take $13 million and spread it across the state . That does n't help us at all immediately , " Crowley said . <p> Both the commission and the non-profit Lake Hopatcong Foundation , he said , are requesting that letters be sent from the four mayors of the four towns surrounding the lake , including Roxbury , Mount Arlington , Jefferson Township and the Borough of Hopatcong . <p> Previously , Crowley said the state said it would give Lake Hopatcong specifically $600,000 for maintenance programs , educational programs and remedial programs to prevent the algae outbreak again next year . <p> " We were supposed to get $200,000 a year . They asked us to put together a plan when we asked for the money . They said yes , then they took it away . We are looking for a joint letter signed by the four mayors , and the foundation and the commission , " said Crowley , who serves as Roxbury 's representative to the commission . <p> In November , the four towns surrounding Lake Hopatcong asked Trenton to put its money where its proverbial mouth is . <p> Roxbury joined Mount Arlington , Jefferson Township and the Borough of Hopatcong when it passed a resolution requesting $489,000 for the Lake Hopatcong Commission to help prevent another " harmful algal bloom " summer . <p> " All four towns are putting up a unified front here , " Mayor Robert DeFillippo said when the Township Council met on Tuesday , Oct. 29 . " We all share the lake . It 's in all of our best interests , " he said . <p> Specifically , the resolution states that the harmful algae blooms " are enhanced and fed by phosphorous and other nutrients , and the nutrients entering the lake come largely from septic issues an stormwater runoff . " <p> The money is being requested , according to the resolution , " because the Lake Hopatcong Commission , working with its scientific advisors , has determined that there are certain projects that can be conducted in spring 2020 that would demonstrate technologies to treat and prevent harmful algal blooms in Lake Hopatcong and in order to implement these projects , funding in the amount of $489,000 , is needed before the end of calendar year 2019 . " <p> Gov . Phil Murphy unveiled the $13.5 million initiative Monday , Nov. 18 , during a meeting hosted by the Lake Hopatcong Foundation at the group 's headquarters in Landing . Joining him were members of the state Department of Environmental Protection , U.S. Reps . Mikie Sherrill , D-11 , Tom Malinwoski , D-7 , and Josh Gottheimer , D-5 , and other county and local officials . <p> While details have yet to be fleshed out , the aim is to ensure partners on all levels of government , from the federal to local level , partner to prevent or mitigate future blooms attributed to both climate change and improper stormwater management practices . The plan , said Murphy , also includes improving communications between all parties , and possibly creating a bloom warning system similar to that used to determine hurricane categories . <p> Local officials in attendance expressed cautious optimism , as more details must be worked out . <p> " Look , I 'm encouraged that there 's $13.5 million , but we got ta make sure that that money gets back to this area , " said State Sen. Anthony M. Bucco , R-Morris . " We 've been through this before where the state allocates money and it goes to other places . That 's gon na be where the rubber meets the road . " <p> Marty Kane , of the Lake Hopatcong Foundation , was present at the Township Council meeting last month and was asked by a resident why there are septic leeching problems when both the Landing section of Roxbury and Mount Arlington are sewered . <p> The problem , Kane said , is that both Jefferson and Hopatcong are not . <p> " I would say some 60 percent of the area surrounding the lake are still on septic systems , " Kane said . <p> EPA Grant <p> Township Manager John Shepherd said on Dec. 3 that a letter of support is also needed for a potential grant up to $1 million from the federal Environmental Protection Agency . The deadline to get the letter submitted was Dec. 10 . <p> " The Lake Commission wants to start incorporating technology into addressing the harmful algae bloom , " Shepherd said . " This would not be a matching grant , and it could be up to $1 million , " he said , before recommending that the letter of support be sent . <p> " It wo n't cost us anything to apply , and this could bring in a substantial amount of money for the algae bloom , " he said . <p> He also said it would not be matching funds , meaning the township would not have to come up with funding to match what the grant amounts to . <p> " The EPA funding is different from the NJ DEP funding of $489,000 . Councilman Crowley mentioned that DEP initially promised this funding to assist the Lake but now , it appears that funding for HAB 's is Statewide and not ear-marked for Lake Hopatcong which means we might not see any state funding to address this issue , " Shepherd explained Tuesday . <p> Mayor Robert DeFillippo said that the governor had no answer as to how much of the proposed $13 million would actually make it to Lake Hopatcong specifically . <h> Watch this discussion.Stop watching this discussion . <h> ( 0 ) comments <h> Welcome to the discussion . <p> Be Yourself .
