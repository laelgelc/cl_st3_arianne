<h> 30-year goals not enough to fight climate change <p> Mayor Jesse Arreguin is completely correct in describing the urgency needed for the state and cities to immediately reduce greenhouse gas emissions . But he needs to suggest more than just setting distant goals to be achieved 15 and 30 years in the future . <p> It will take more to achieve such goals than just refusing to issue permits for new fossil fuel projects or building 2,500-foot safety buffers around fossil fuel production . <p> How about suggesting stronger encouragement for roof-top solar so it is a win for the environment , the power company and property owners ? <p> How about ways to encourage private industry to build more infrastructure for recharging electric vehicles ? <p> How about reducing sales taxes and increasing fuel taxes in a revenue-neutral way to encourage less use of fossil fuel in transportation ? Or make the rail system more effective in moving cargo , since rail is more fuel-efficient per ton of cargo than trucks . <h> More in Letters to the Editor <p> We encourage you to submit letters that share your opinions on issues of interest to residents in our community .
