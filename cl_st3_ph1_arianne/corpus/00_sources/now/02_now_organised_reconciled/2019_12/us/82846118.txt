<h> Letter to the editor : Parents should listen to children on climate <p> Share <p> Congratulations to Greta Thunberg for her latest recognition as Time Magazine 's Person of the Year . She is a remarkable teenager who has helped to open the world 's eyes to the tragedy of climate change . May she serve as an inspiration to all of us , especially the youngest generations <p> It is our children and their children who will be inheriting the many messes that will be left to them . It will be our children and their children who will suffer the consequences . And , it will be our children who will need to figure out a way to fix those messes . <p> Our children do not need to , nor should they , aspire to be the next Greta Thunberg . But to clean up the planet , or help ensure all Americans have access to healthcare , or to fix our broken political system , they will need to become engaged . And voting is the simplest and easiest way to become engaged . <p> So parents and teachers , encourage your children or students to register to vote and then encourage them to vote . And , remind them that . in Maine , any 17 year old can vote in the presidential primary this March 3 if they turn 18 before Nov. 4 , 2020 . <p> Their lives , as well as our lives , quite literally , depend on their engagement . <p> Joshua Gear , MD <p> York <p> Invalid username/password . <p> Success .
