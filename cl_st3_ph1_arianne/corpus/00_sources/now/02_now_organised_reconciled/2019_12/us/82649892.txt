<p> BRUSSELS , Dec 12 ( Reuters ) - A Brussels court ordered the Belgian government on Thursday to help bring to Belgium 10 children who were born in Syria to Islamic State fighters of Belgian nationality . <p> The children , aged between seven months and seven years , must be brought to Belgium within six weeks , the court said . They are now at the Al-Hol refugee camp in northeastern Syria which is under Kurdish control . <p> If the government does not comply by providing consular assistance and administrative documents for the children , it will be fined 5,000 euros ( $5,511 ) per child per day , the court said . <p> Belgium 's justice minister , Koen Geens , told public radio the government was ready to take back the children as long as it did not have to take in their mothers as well . <p> Last month the court requested that the government take back within 75 days a woman whose husband fought for Islamic State , and her two children .
