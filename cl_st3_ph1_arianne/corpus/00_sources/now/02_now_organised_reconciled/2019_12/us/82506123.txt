<p> ANKARA , Dec 4 ( Reuters ) - Turkey will switch to plain packaging for cigarettes and tobacco products from Dec. 5 , the Agriculture and Forestry Ministry said on Tuesday , in a move aimed at curbing tobacco sales and discouraging smokers . <p> In a statement , the ministry said the production of plain cigarette packages would begin on Thursday and that sales of the older , branded packages would stop on Jan. 5 , 2020 . <p> Turkey will join countries like France , the United Kingdom , Canada , and Australia in selling cigarettes in plain packaging . <p> According to Turkish authorities , diseases attributable to tobacco use cause more than 100,000 deaths in Turkey yearly . <p> According to the amendment , tobacco products shall be marketed in plain and standard packaging with warning messages and other mandatory texts , phrases and images . <p> The brand name will be written only on one side of the pack , covering no more than 5% of the side . No brand logo or symbol will be allowed in the new designs .
