With China joining the effort with its own scheme next year , one-fifth of the total carbon emissions will be covered by some kind of pricing . Among the top industrialized countries , the U.S. is cruelly missing in these efforts , even though it was among the first to know about the global warming problem ( thanks to Exxon ) . <p> Other countries have stepped up their legislative efforts while the U.S. is now lagging behind . But there are now eight different carbon pricing bills already in Congress , and 10 states with pricing programs already in place . <p> It 's now time to accelerate the national debate about how much the carbon fee would be and how to redistribute fairly the revenue .
