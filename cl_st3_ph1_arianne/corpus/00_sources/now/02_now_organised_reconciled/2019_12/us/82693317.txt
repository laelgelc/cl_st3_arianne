<p> A program called " Cli-Fi : The New Genre of Climate Change Fiction " will be held from 5:30-8 p.m . Wednesday , Dec. 18 , at the Folkmoot Center , 112 Virginia Ave. , Waynesville . <p> The lecture is part of Folkmoot 's Cultural Crash series , featuring talks on a variety of current cultural issues . <p> This Crash Course will explore the new fiction genre coined " cli-fi , " which grapples with issues related to climate change and the role that humans are playing in creating an increasingly unstable and volatile environment . <p> The emergence and popularity of works of fiction with a focus on human-made climate change is unsurprising given our growing awareness of the ways that our actions are impacting the planet and given the increase in speculative fiction about the possible end results of unchecked human activity . <p> In this talk , Dr. Laura Wright , a professor with the Western Carolina University English Department , will discuss the ways that some of these works of fiction engage with climate science , possible worst case scenarios , and potential modes of salvation . <h> Watch this discussion.Stop watching this discussion . <h> ( 0 ) comments <h> Welcome to the discussion . <p> Keep it Clean .
