<h> From the devastation of Sandy Hook to the dominance of UConn basketball , these are the stories that shaped Connecticut this decade <p> Hartford Courant <p> Dec 22 , 2019 6:00 AM <p> When the Hartford Courant newsroom began thinking about sharing stories that best captured the past decade , a few immediately came to mind . <p> There were moments of devastation , such as Dec. 14 , 2012 , when the massacre at Sandy Hook Elementary School left the state and the nation in mourning . Or Superstorm Sandy , which just a few months earlier had rocked Connecticut 's shoreline , leaving homes and businesses destroyed in its wake . <p> Advertisement <p> There were also moments of celebration and moments that brought us all together -- like April 2014 when both UConn basketball teams brought home national championship victories . And then there were the moments that divided us . <p> There were protests , corruption , shake-ups in our major industries and developments that have changed the face of both our capital city and our state . These stories have changed our laws , the way we live and the way we think . <p> These are the stories that have shaped Connecticut over the past 10 years . <p> Photos of Sandy Hook Elementary School victims sit at a small memorial near the school on Jan. 14 , 2013 , in Newtown , a month after the horrific massacre that claimed 20 children and six women at the school . ( Getty Images ) <p> The Sandy Hook Elementary School shootings dominated the news for much of the decade , from the massacre in 2012 to the push for expanded gun control and the unending grief of the families . The shootings , and the intense debate over gun control in the years after , are among the stories that shaped the decade . <p> The impact of climate change in Connecticut has gone far beyond simple weather . It has become a national political issue because of the scientific consensus that global warming is being caused by humans , and it appears likely to increasingly become a state political issue because of the environmental and financial issues involved . <p> Dr. William Petit and Johanna Chapman talk with the media ourtside the New Haven Courthouse after jurors decided on the death penalty for Cheshire home invasion killer Joshua Komisarjevsky , 31 . ( Michael McAndrews , Hartford Courant ) <p> The aftermath of the Cheshire home invasion murders and the subsequent repeal of the death penalty dominated the political and criminal justice landscapes in Connecticut for the first half of the decade . <p> Connecticut has long had a reputation as a place where political corruption thrives . <p> But following a bid-rigging scandal that sent former Gov . John G. Rowland to prison in 2005 , the state established a voluntary system designed to foster good government by reducing the influence of big money on the electoral process . The landmark program , considered a model for other states , bars state contractors from contributing to campaigns , limits the role of lobbyists and allows candidates to access public money . <p> Despite those reforms , politicians continued to get themselves in trouble . <p> The shock waves from the Great Recession continued to reverberate across Connecticut over the past decade , leaving the state with a struggling housing market and fewer highly paid jobs , but with notable opportunity in the booming aerospace and defense industries . <p> At the close of the decade , the state was still struggling to regain jobs lost from the recession , with the state workforce 15% smaller and state income tax revenues declining thanks to an exodus of six-figure salaries . They notched a record-setting 90-game win streak before besting it a few years later with 111 straight . ( Mark Mirko / Hartford Courant ) <p> Throughout the political chaos of the past decade , Connecticut remained a progressive state for LGBTQ rights . By 2009 , Connecticut had already legalized same-sex marriage . It was the second state to do so and reached the civil rights milestone under a Republican governor . <p> But while the past 10 years included much legislative progress , gay and transgender people still experience discrimination . Debates surrounding gender and sexual identity continue to shift as Connecticut 's younger residents become a part of the conversation . <p> Transportation issues dominated political discourse in Connecticut over the past decade , from the completion of the $567 million CTfastrak busway that became a punching bag for conservatives and critics of former Gov . Dannel P. Malloy , to the ongoing debate about highway tolls . <p> UConn sophomore Aziz Sandhu , center , cheers for a speaker at Women 's March on Hartford rally at the Connecticut State Capitol . ( Brad Horrigan / Hartford Courant ) <p> Trump 's stunning victory in the 2016 presidential election electrified Connecticut Republicans . Yet his election also ushered in a new era of deep political division , sowing fear in marginalized groups across the state . The ensuing years were marked by intense anti-Trump activism in Connecticut , as protesters rallied for women 's rights , protections for immigrants and refugees , and climate justice .
