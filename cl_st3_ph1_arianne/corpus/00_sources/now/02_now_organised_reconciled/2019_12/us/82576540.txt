<p> Many U.S. sustainable funds are set to beat the broad market this year , easing some long-held concerns that values-based investing comes at the expense of returns . <p> Through November , 48% of large-cap sustainable funds have outperformed the S&amp;P 500 , compared with only 26% of all large-cap funds , according to fund tracker Morningstar Inc . Fund managers usually use data from index and research firm MSCI Inc. or Sustainalytics to rank companies on environmental , social and governance , or ESG , performance . <p> Sustainable funds are often overweight tech stocks and less invested in energy , says Todd Rosenbluth , director of mutual fund and exchange-traded fund research at CFRA Research . " That has contributed favorably " to their performance , he says . Google parent Alphabet Inc. , Apple Inc. and Microsoft Corp . are regularly among the top holdings of sustainable funds . <p> In 2017 , around half of individual investors in a Morgan Stanley survey said they believe there is a financial trade-off for investing around environmental or social issues . Still , the ESG investing trend has gained steam this year , with net inflows into sustainable funds reaching a record of $17.7 billion through November , compared with $5.5 billion for all of last year , according to Morningstar . There is now at least $129 billion in these funds , though that is still just a fraction of the roughly $35 trillion of fund assets in the U.S. <p> Recent research from the International Monetary Fund found that the performance of sustainable funds is at least comparable to that of conventional equity funds . Some ESG funds have done even better . <h> One ETF 's success <p> One of the top-performing exchange-traded funds in the sector is Nuveen ESG Large-Cap Growth ETF ( NULG ) , which has posted gains of 36% year-to-date compared with the S&amp;P 500 's rise of 26% , FactSet market data show . <p> Launched in December 2016 , the ETF surpassed $100 million in assets this year and now has about $110 million under management . It charges an expense ratio of 0.35% , or $35 annually for every $10,000 invested . <p> It invests in companies in the TIAA ESG USA Large-Cap Growth Index , which ranks companies in the MSCI USA Large Cap Growth Index based on factors like climate change , natural-resource use , waste and emissions management , safety , ethics , and compliance with national and international regulations , among other things . It then includes the top 50% of these ESG-ranked companies by industry . <p> The ETF also screens out companies involved in weapons , gambling , alcohol and tobacco . On the environmental side , it removes fossil-fuel owners and companies with higher-than-average carbon-dioxide emissions . <p> The fund skews toward the information-technology sector , which accounts for 30.6% of the holdings . Among the top 10 holdings , Microsoft is the No. 1 stock at 9.19% , followed by Alphabet at 7.2% and Visa Inc. at 3.48% . <p> Jordan Farris , head of ETF product management at Nuveen , says the market is currently at a " key inflection point " when it comes to sustainable investing . <p> " Advisers and investors can now see that performance does live up to the claims , " he says . " It takes a little time for people to fully understand what they are getting . " <h> Mutual fund 's rise <p> On the mutual-fund side , ClearBridge Sustainability Leaders Fund I ( LCISX ) is a top performer and up 33% for the year , FactSet data show . The actively managed fund holds about 40 handpicked stocks that are set to benefit from environmental and social trends . It charges an expense ratio of 0.85% , or $85 annually for every $10,000 invested . <p> " Companies with strong ESG attributes have a lower cost of capital and better performance , " says Derek Deutsch , portfolio manager at ClearBridge Investments , a unit of investment firm Legg Mason Inc. with around $147 billion under management as of September . ClearBridge has some 14 analysts who help identify sustainable companies for the firm . <p> While the fund itself has just over $12 million in assets , ClearBridge says it manages a total of about $406 million in assets using the same strategy the fund uses . <p> Driving the fund 's performance are technology stocks , including its top three holdings , Microsoft , Apple and Alphabet , alongside smaller companies that investors might not be familiar with , Mr. Deutsch says . <p> One high-performing stock is SolarEdge Technologies Inc. , which develops equipment for solar generation and is up 140% for the year , according to FactSet .
