A spending bill approved by the House includes $320 million for the Great Lakes Restoration Initiative . <p> NASA <p> A cleanup program for the Great Lakes would receive a funding boost under a spending bill moving toward enactment in Congress . <p> The House approved the measure Tuesday and sent it to the Senate , where a vote is expected later this week . It includes $320 million for the Great Lakes Restoration Initiative .
