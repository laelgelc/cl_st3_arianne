<p> BEIJING , Dec 2 ( Reuters ) - China 's foreign ministry said on Monday that a Chinese-born Australian writer detained by Beijing for almost a year is in good condition and has not been tortured , rejecting criticism by the Australian government about his treatment . <p> Chinese foreign ministry spokeswoman Hua Chunying made the remarks during a daily news briefing in Beijing . <p> Australian Foreign Minister Marise Payne said Australian consular officials who recently visited Yang Hengjun had reported " unacceptable " conditions , including isolation and daily interrogations , sometimes while he was shackled .
