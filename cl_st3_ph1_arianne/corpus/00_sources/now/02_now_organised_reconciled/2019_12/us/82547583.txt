<h> Protesters march in Houghton to raise awareness on climate change <p> HOUGHTON , Mich . ( WLUC ) - A group of protesters got together in Houghton Friday afternoon to march and raise awareness about climate change . <p> The march was part of a much larger nationwide climate protest on Friday . The march was put together by the Keweenaw Youth for Climate Action , a Copper Country climate change awareness group . The protesters spent an hour and a half walking up and down Sheldon Avenue to spread awareness through the community . <p> " That 's why we 're out here today , we do n't think we 'll grab the attention of the federal government , but we do hope that the community will think twice and say ' Oh yeah maybe we can make some changes here and help the rest of the world out , ' " said Elise Rosky , a member of the Keweenaw Youth for Climate Action . <p> The Keweenaw Youth for Climate Action is also planning a panel discussion for January or February on climate change at Michigan Tech , where experts on the subject will be invited to speak . <h> Online Public Information File <p> Viewers with disabilities can get assistance accessing this station 's FCC Public Inspection File by contacting the station with the information listed below .
