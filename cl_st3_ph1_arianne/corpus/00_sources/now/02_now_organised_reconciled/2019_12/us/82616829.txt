<h> Letter : Making transit free would have several benefits <h> Tuesday <p> Dec 10 , 2019 at 4:11 AM <p> Kansas City has done for its bus system what Bob Weiler has proposed for the Central Ohio Transit Authority : It will now be free of charge . Denver and Salt Lake City may soon follow . With the leadership of a new mayor , Kansas City decided that such a development would be transformative for that city , especially for low-income people who rely on the bus . <p> They also saw it as an important step in their moral responsibility to lower their fossil fuel footprint and fight climate change . Other studies of free transit have pointed to a health benefit for senior citizens , increasing their mobility and alleviating their isolation . In Columbus , free transit also would benefit high-school students who are required to have an internship , enabling them to travel to and from a business location . <p> Even though there would be a public cost to free transit , the public benefits would be worth it . Weiler estimated the cost to implement would be $20 million , which could be raised by diverting 0.1% of the county sales tax for free COTA or by raising the sales tax by 1 cent per $10 purchase . Instead of throwing local , state and federal funds at driverless buses and cars , we could build a bigger public transit ridership , which in turn could attract more grant funding for other mass transit projects like light rail , or provide incentives for more bus service . <p> Unlike driverless cars , mass transit would get cars off the road , alleviating pollution and congestion . Moreover , free transit would be in line with the county commissioners ' goal of reducing poverty in Franklin County . I am grateful that Bob Weiler thinks big and I hope our elected leaders and the COTA board will embark on this legacy-making project , showing concern for our planet , for people in poverty and for the air we breathe , for all of us . <p> Mary Jo Kilroy , Columbus <h> Never miss a story <p> Choose the plan that 's right for you .
