<h> Take That 2019 ! <p> Not long ago , lawyers representing Donald Trump actually argued in open court that the president could not be charged with a crime or even investigated should he follow through on one of his campaign musings and murder someone in cold blood on Manhattan 's Fifth Avenue . They have to jam the word " Christ " in there to ensure that religious freedom in this country is not dead yet . <p> In response , we must jest , if we are to go on . Certain acts of commission and omission require public notice and rebuke . " He brought to life the spirit and wonder of the vast African culture and made it instantly meaningful to all of us , " recalled one UW official .
