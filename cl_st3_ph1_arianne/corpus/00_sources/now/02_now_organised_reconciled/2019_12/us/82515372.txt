<h> BP increases stake in solar energy firm Lightsource BP <p> FILE PHOTO : The logo of BP is seen at a petrol station in Kloten , Switzerland October 3 , 2017 . REUTERS/Arnd Wiegmann/File Photo <p> LONDON ( Reuters ) - BP ( BP.L ) is increasing its stake in solar energy developer Lightsource BP to 50% from 43% and is aiming for rapid growth over the next few years , the two companies said on Thursday . <p> European oil companies like BP have been stepping up investment in renewable power to diversify their businesses in the face of global moves toward a lower carbon economy to combat climate change . <p> Dev Sanyal , chief executive at BP 's Alternative Energy division said , renewables were attractive even though they usually offered lower returns than the company 's traditional oil and gas business . <p> " You do n't actually have the volatility of the oil and gas commodity cycle , what you 've got is a stable returns structure , " he said in an interview . <p> Lightsource BP usually seeks corporate power purchase agreements for its renewable assets , which can provide years of visibility and stability of revenue . <p> BP 's investment will also simplify the joint venture structure as Lightsource BP seeks to accelerate its drive toward 10 gigawatts ( GW ) of developed assets by the end of 2023 , the companies said . <p> Lightsource BP currently has around 2 GW of renewable capacity globally . <p> BP invested $200 million in Lightsource when it took a 43% stake in 2017 . <p> No financial details relating to the stake increase on Thursday were disclosed .
