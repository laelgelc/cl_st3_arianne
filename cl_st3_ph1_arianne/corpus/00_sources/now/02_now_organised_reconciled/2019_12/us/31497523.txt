<p> Greta Thunberg , the soft-spoken Swedish teenager who became a global conscience for climate change and environmental activism , has been named Time magazine 's Person of the Year for 2019 . <p> The magazine announced the 16-year-old as its choice Wednesday exclusively on the " TODAY " show . <p> " She became the biggest voice on the biggest issue facing the planet this year , coming from essentially nowhere to lead a worldwide movement , " Time editor-in-chief Edward Felsenthal told the show , adding that Thunberg is the magazine 's youngest choice ever to be named Person of the Year . <p> Greta Thunberg is Time 's 2019 Person of the Year.TIME <p> Thunberg quickly bloomed into one of the world 's most notable climate change activists , sparking a collective movement to fight the issue after protesting alone outside the Swedish Parliament during school hours on Fridays when she was 15 . The teen held up a now universally recognized hand-painted sign that read " skolstrejk f ? r klimatet , " which translates to " School strike for the climate . " <p> Her initiative to strike galvanized students to protest against climate change throughout Europe and that momentum quickly fanned across the globe , becoming the " Fridays For Future " movement . <p> Her solo protest , Fensenthal noted , eventually prompted millions of people in 150 countries " to act on behalf of the planet . " <p> He also said that Thunberg " represents a broader generational shift in the culture " -- with youth standing up for what they believe in -- from Hong Kong to Chile . <p> Thunberg 's signature no-nonsense blunt style of speaking made her a force that could not be ignored by world leaders and she was asked to speak in front of several high-profile entities , including the United Nations and the U.S. Congress . <p> In October , Thunberg was the recipient of another honor -- an environmental award at a Stockholm ceremony held by the Nordic Council . But she declined it , explaining in an Instagram post , " The climate movement does not need any more awards . " <p> " What we need is for our politicians and the people in power start to listen to the current , best available science , " she wrote . <p> When asked what he thought Thunberg 's response would be after learning that she is Time 's Person of the Year , Felsenthal said , " I do n't know , but I think what she has done , her rise in influence , has been really extraordinary . " <p> The young activist sailed for just over two weeks on a zero-emission boat with her father in August to New York City . <p> One of her most notable appearances occurred at the U.N . Climate Action Summit in September when she excoriated global leaders , including U.N . Secretary-General Antonio Guterres by telling them they had " stolen her dreams and childhood " with their " empty words . " <p> " People are suffering . People are dying . Entire ecosystems are collapsing , " she said . " We are in the beginning of a mass extinction , and all you can talk about is money and fairy tales of eternal economic growth . How dare you ! " <p> Despite being a frequent target of criticism , Thunberg has trudged forward on her quest for environmental justice . <p> Earlier this year , Thunberg , along with 15 other young climate activists , filed a legal complaint with the United Nations against five countries under the U.N . Convention on the Rights of the Child alleging the nations are not doing enough to combat climate change and that their inaction is affecting their right to thrive . <p> Her efforts also earned her a nomination for the 2019 Nobel Peace Prize . While the teen lost the award to Ethiopian Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed Ali , she remains one of the youngest nominees for the illustrious prize . <p> The other 2019 finalists for Time 's Person of the Year were Democratic House Speaker Nancy Pelosi , President Donald Trump , the Hong Kong protesters , and the anonymous whistleblower whose memo on Trump 's dealings with Ukraine triggered the impeachment inquiry . <p> Instead of doing runners-up this year , the magazine gave awards in different categories , including the U.S. women 's soccer team as athlete of the year , Lizzo as entertainer of the year , and Disney CEO Bob Iger as businessperson of the year . <p> The magazine has selected a Person of the Year annually since the 1920s . Last year , Time chose " The Guardians and the War on Truth , " which included four journalists and one news organization who paid a hefty price , either with their lives or freedoms , to be journalists at time when the profession has been under attack on several fronts .
