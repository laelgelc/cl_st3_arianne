( Photo by Massimiliano Ferraro/NurPhoto via Getty Images ) <p> NurPhoto via Getty Images <p> They may be kids , but Generation Z is rocking the world with their activism around gun control , immigration reform , and other causes . Now , 16-year-old Greta Thunberg of Sweden is the youngest individual ever named Time 's 2019 Person of the Year -- an earthshaking nod to her worldwide climate-change youth movement . <p> With a do-or-die dedication to her mission , Thunberg is not alone among her generation . She has inspired hundreds of thousands of young people to participate in " climate strikes , " demonstrably influencing world leaders -- like no one before them -- in their call for transformative and urgent change . <p> Whether you support Thunberg 's views and tactics or not , there 's no denying that she and her Gen Z peers are a force to be reckoned with . They 're not trying to change the world ; they 're already doing it and , in many cases , they 're leading the way . Here are three key reasons why . <p> Technology . The web and social media are amplifying young people 's voices like never before . And as digital natives , Gen Z embodies 24/7 connectivity and navigates the online universe with unmatched ease and fluidity . This , among other things , opens up unprecedented opportunities to create compelling messages that go viral , gain traction , and grow into movements . <p> Education . For Gen Z , the adage " Knowledge is power " has never been truer . They are on track to be the most educated generation the world has ever seen . They have access to unequaled amounts of information and , likewise , know how to exploit it in their quest to make a difference . <p> Passion . Gen-Zers are virtually insatiable in their pursuit of purpose -- something bigger than themselves to ardently take part in . And when they find a cause that genuinely moves them , they pursue it with unrivaled , unapologetic passion . Technology may provide the platform , and education may supply the impetus , but it is Gen Z 's passion that makes them so precociously prominent and powerful on the world stage . <p> Bottom line : do n't underestimate Generation Z and the passionate commitment they bring to the problems our world faces . They are prepared to stand up for what they believe in , regardless of the cost . And meeting these global-scale challenges head on , as Thunberg and her generation are already doing , will call out every ounce of their tremendous potential .
