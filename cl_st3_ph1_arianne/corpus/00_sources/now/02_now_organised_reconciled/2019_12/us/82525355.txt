In penultimate paragraph , deletes " former " in describing Gates as company co-founder ) <p> By Kate Kelland <p> Dec 5 ( Reuters ) - The multi-billion-dollar philanthropic Bill &amp; Melinda Gates Foundation said on Thursday that its chief executive officer , Sue Desmond-Hellmann , is to step down after more than five years , to be replaced by Mark Suzman . <p> Desmond-Hellmann , who until October was also on the board of the social networking company Facebook Inc , said she was stepping down from the Gates Foundation as of February 2020 to be able to " care properly for myself and my family . " <p> Desmond-Hellmann was previously Chancellor of the University of California , San Francisco , the first woman to hold that position , and had worked at the pharmaceutical firm Genentech - now owned by Roche - where she helped develop some of the first gene-targeted cancer drugs , Avastin and Herceptin . <p> " This was without a doubt the toughest decision of my career . But ... one of my mantras is : take your own pulse first . Over the last few weeks and months , I have done just that and concluded that I need to slow down , " Desmond-Hellmann said in a letter to Foundation employees , a copy of which was shared with Reuters . <p> The Gates Foundation , led by Microsoft co-founder Bill Gates and his wife , Melinda , is one of the world 's largest funders of global health programmes to combat disease and poverty . It has around $50 billion in assets . <p> Desmond-Hellmann 's successor , Mark Suzman , joined the Gates Foundation in 2007 and is currently its chief strategy officer .
