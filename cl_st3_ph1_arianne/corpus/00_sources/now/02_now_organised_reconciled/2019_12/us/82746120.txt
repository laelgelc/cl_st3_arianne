If you see comments in violation of our community guidelines , please report them . <h> COP25 : Chile/Madrid <p> COP25 was originally destined for Santiago , Chile ; however , civil unrest in that country prompted by a rise in subway fares forced Chilean President Sebastian Pinera to disinvite the UN climate conference on October 30 . Soon thereafter , Spain stepped in and offered to host COP25 in Madrid . Even though Chile resigned as location host , it maintained the presidency . At an early plenary session I attended in Madrid , COP25 President Carolina Schmidt commented on Chilean civil unrest , " It 's not about 30 pesos , it 's about 30 years of injustice . " She segued from this comment to the nearly 30 years of climate injustice and inaction since the global community adopted the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) at the 1992 Earth Summit in Rio . <p> Two central themes of COP25 -- dubbed ' The Blue COP ' -- were to focus attention on the oceans , which serve as our planet 's most important climate regulator , and Article 6 of the Paris Agreement . I will address the theme surrounding Article 6 because the health of the oceans -- as well as the survival of all humanity -- directly depend on the outcome of this most contentious issue in COP negotiations . Article 6 was the single outstanding element of the Rulebook on the Paris Agreement developed at COP24 last year in Katowice , Poland . The Rulebook set out basic procedures and mechanisms underlying implementation of the 2015 Paris Agreement . All of humanity faces an ambitious task if we have any hope of meeting the Paris Agreement goal to hold below a +2oC rise of preindustrial revolution temperatures , and ultimately , to a +1.5oC limit to prevent the submersion of small island nations and low-lying coastal states . Current commitments put the world on a +3oC path by the end of the century . <h> The Paris Agreement <p> The Paris Agreement has universal membership with every UNFCCC Party -- 196 states and the European Union -- a signatory to the Paris Agreement . This treaty unites all nations in a common cause to undertake ambitious efforts to combat climate change . <p> The actual text of the Paris Agreement is a mere 16 pages long including a preamble and 29 Articles that provide a framework for action . Negotiators at COP25 aimed to clarify national responsibilities under Article 6 , including ramping up voluntary " Nationally Determined Contributions " ( NDCs ) to lower greenhouse gas emissions and market based mechanisms for carbon trading . Article 6 allows for the transfer of carbon credits from a nation that is exceeding its goals to nations in need of those credits ( the big polluters ) . However , robust accounting is needed to ensure " the avoidance of double counting . " This means that a developed country can not transfer its carbon credits , then turn around , and claim those same credits for itself . There is also the issue that some countries want historical credits achieved under the Kyoto Protocol to be traded . And , some wealthy developed nations want to invoke paragraph 52 under Article 8 into the Article 6 negotiations . Whew ! This appears to be getting complicated , but it 's not . It really is a conflict between greed versus lives ; i.e. present-day financial interests versus climate justice . <h> Loss and Damage <p> Article 8 of the Paris Agreement acknowledges the 2013 Warsaw International Mechanism for Loss and Damage . Paragraph 52 " Agrees that Article 8 of the Agreement does not involve or provide a basis for any liability or compensation . " Invoking paragraph 52 essentially ensures that wealthy developed nations who contributed most to emissions can not be held legally liable to compensate the vulnerable countries experiencing losses and damages due to adverse effects of climate change from those emissions . Herein lies the great divide in negotiations . <p> As negotiations closed at the end of the week , discussions around Article 6 were not optimistic . One delegate summed it up , " Given the choice between a burnt cake and no dessert , perhaps it 's better to have no outcome at all -- at least for now . " Carbon trading where a polluting country can purchase the right to pollute from countries that have not reached their emissions limits -- some factions refer to this as ' Climate Colonialism ' -- are a controversial concept dominating negotiations . China , Brazil , and India favor carbon markets while the green economy leading Nordic countries , the EU , and justice organizations consider it a false solution . <h> High Ambition Coalition <p> The High Ambition Coalition is a group of ministers within the climate negotiations pursuing stepped-up ambition on climate action . They appeared as a unified coalition at a press briefing declaring their message , " This is a fight for our present , not just a fight for the future . " The EU Minister emphasized the " Need to act now ! Let me be crystal clear . There is no way we can accept a compromise that jeopardizes environmental integrity . " Ministers from the Marshall Islands , Belize , Bhutan , Costa Rica , and others rejected a text offered early Thursday morning ( December 12 ) on the next-to-last day of COP25 negotiations stating the ambition is not even close to what science informs is required to the reality of not having the world temp go above 1.5oC . <p> This is an existential crisis for small islands and disproportionate to all developing countries . They stated the text must include a call for new and ambitious NDCs for 2020 <p> Stating , " This may be called Article 6 , 6 , 6 " , the ministers drew three non-negotiable red lines : <p> 1 . No double accounting of carbon credits . <p> 2 . No to carryover of Kyoto Protocol carbon credits for trading . <p> 3 . The safeguard of human rights , meaning no invoking of paragraph 52 . <p> When asked who were the culprits thwarting ambitious actions , their response was " Australia , Brazil , and the US " , referred to by some as " the mean-hearted few " who are controlling the agenda . But , this about " People 's lives ... lives are at risk . " <p> US Government is out , but " We are Still In " <p> The US has pledged to withdraw from the Paris Agreement , but can not legally do so until exactly one day after the next presidential election . A sign on the US State Department Delegation door reads " Please send all press inquiries regarding the U.S. delegation at COP25 to ClimateComms@state.gov " . Other than negotiators meeting behind closed doors , this sign was the singular presence of the US government here at COP25 in Madrid . <p> Just a few paces across the hall from the US delegation office stands the U.S. Climate Action Center " We Are Still In " . This center hosted multiple presentations and panel discussions throughout the COP . One I attended was a panel of union leaders and corporate CEOs who released their joint statement declaring their intent " to rally behind the US ' participation in the Paris Agreement . " Their statement reads , " Addressing climate change is what 's best for economic health , jobs , and competitiveness of our companies and country . " <p> Corporations , states , cities , universities &amp; colleges , and others representing 70% of the US economy ( and growing ) have affirmed " We Are Still In " the Paris Agreement . The dwindling 30% -- including York County , York City , York College , and some York businesses -- remain anchored in the gray economy . <h> Fridays for Future <p> A growing global social movement led by youth has declared a climate emergency and demands immediate action . Before 500,000 demonstrators gathered in Madrid last week , teenage environmental activist Greta Thunberg said , " I hope world leaders grasp the urgency of the climate crisis . " I attended a press briefing by Greta and several other youth activists . She is tiny in stature , but her stoic character and clear message has galvanized a global movement . The day after the briefing , she was named Time magazine 's ' Person of the Year ' . <p> Speaking for the first generation to feel the impacts of climate change , Greta has declared , " Our house is on fire . " <p> It 's time to act . <p> Keith E. Peterman is a professor of chemistry at York College of Pennsylvania .
