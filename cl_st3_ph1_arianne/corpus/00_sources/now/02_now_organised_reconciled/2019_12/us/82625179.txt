" <p> SHOW TRANSCRIPT <p> TIME Magazine has named 16-year-old Swedish climate activist Greta Thunberg as its person of the year for 2019 . <p> Thunberg is the magazine 's youngest choice ever for Person of the Year . Her selection was announced on the " TODAY " show Wednesday , with TIME 's editor-in-chief calling her , " the biggest voice on the biggest issue facing the planet this year . " <p> Thunberg 's rise to prominence began in August 2018 , when she started skipping school to protest climate change outside of the Swedish Parliament in Stockholm . Her protest soon gained international recognition as Thunberg took her activism to the world stage . <p> Her perhaps most notable appearance came at the U.N . Climate Action Summit in September , when she delivered a withering takedown of global leaders over their lack of action on the climate crisis . <p> " People are suffering . People are dying . Entire ecosystems are collapsing . We are in the beginning of a mass extinction , and all you can talk about is money and fairy tales of eternal economic growth . How dare you ! " Thunberg said . <p> Thunberg , who has Asperger 's Syndrome , also began the " Fridays for Future " movement and helped inspire nearly 4 million to take part in a global climate strike on September 20 .
