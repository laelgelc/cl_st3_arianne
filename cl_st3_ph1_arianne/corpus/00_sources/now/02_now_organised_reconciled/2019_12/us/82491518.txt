<p> BERLIN , Dec 3 ( Reuters ) - German federal prosecutors believe Russian intelligence was involved in the August killing of a Georgian citizen in Berlin and want to take over the investigation , a legal source told Reuters on Tuesday , confirming a report by public broadcaster ARD . <p> The measure could raise tensions between Germany -- and other western countries -- and Russia , after strains over the poisoning of a former Russian spy , Sergei Skripal , and his daughter on British soil last year . <p> Zelimkhan Khangoshvili , 40 , who had previously fought alongside anti-Moscow separatists in the Russian region of Chechnya , was shot twice in the head in a central Berlin park in August as he was heading to a mosque . <p> " We have evidence that a foreign intelligence agency was behind it and therefore the case is going to be taken over by the federal prosecutor this week , " said the source , who declined to be named . <p> A suspect was detained shortly after the killing and Der Spiegel weekly has reported that his passport number links him to Russian security services . <p> Berlin prosecutors , who have so far led the case , said the suspect had been caught as he tried to dispose of the presumptive murder weapon in the nearby Spree River , along with a bicycle he had been riding . <p> Russia fought major campaigns in 1994-96 and 1999-2000 against separatists in Chechnya , situated in the Caucasus Mountains on Russia 's border with Georgia . Many anti-Moscow fighters from those wars now live in exile and are at odds with the pro-Russian authorities in Chechnya .
