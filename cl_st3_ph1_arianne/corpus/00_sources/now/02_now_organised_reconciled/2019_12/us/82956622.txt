<h> Slippery salvation : Could seaweed as cow feed help climate ? <p> By Associated Press&amp;nbsp&amp;nbsp <p> Posted : Sun 7:44 PM , Dec 29 , 2019 <p> FREEPORT , Maine ( AP ) - A group of scientists and farmers in northern New England is working on a plan to feed seaweed to cows to gauge whether it can help reduce greenhouse gas emissions that contribute to climate change . <p> The concept has gained traction in recent years after some studies showed the potential of seaweed feeds to cut back on methane . <p> About a quarter of the methane in the country comes from cows , which produce the gas when they belch or flatulate . <p> One of the big questions is which kinds of seaweed offer the highest benefit to farmers looking to cut methane .
