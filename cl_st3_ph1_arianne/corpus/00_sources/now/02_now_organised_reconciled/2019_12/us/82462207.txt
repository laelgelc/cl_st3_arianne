<p> BENGALURU , Dec 2 ( Reuters ) - Heavy rains from the retreating monsoon brought down a compound wall in southern Indian state of Tamil Nadu , killing 17 , said police officials from Mettupalayam town , about 500 kilometres from its capital city Chennai . <p> Efforts to rescue any people who may be trapped , and clear the debris from the compound wall , which fell on houses close by , were still in progress , a senior police officer from the area told Reuters by phone on Monday . <p> India saw its wettest monsoon in a quarter of a century and rains have lasted longer this year , triggering floods and killing hundreds of people . <p> The monsoon 's withdrawal , which begins early October and is the main source of rain for the south-eastern states , was delayed . <p> Weather officials in Chennai issued a red alert to six districts on Sunday and warned fishermen against going to sea due to cyclonic storms . <p> Meanwhile , schools in some parts of the state declared a holiday on Monday , local media reported .
