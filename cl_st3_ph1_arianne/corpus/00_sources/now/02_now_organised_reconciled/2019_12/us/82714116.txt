Citizens should be able to trust their governments . If there was no collusion or corruption , an ethics investigation should establish that and clear everyone 's name . If there were actions that were unethical or illegal all Delawareans would benefit from knowing that as well . <p> -- Dick Dahn , Wilmington <h> Pollution by China , India still far less than that by U.S. <p> Recently Tom Koval expressed his views on climate change in " Who 's going to battle China , India over their contributions to global warming ? " He wrote , " Will the global warming progressives be willing to go to war with China and India over their overwhelming contribution to alleged man-made global warming ? " <p> He goes on , " The Paris climate accord is a farce without substance . " Then , " ( Europeans ) were helpless against the Nazis . Now they want , once again , America to bail them out using the climate change global warming scam . " <p> As a PhD scientist who has studied , taught and written about climate change for decades , I can assure you that the cumulative contributions of carbon pollution to the atmosphere by China and India since 1750 are much less than ours ; we started much earlier . China and India have much larger populations , so their per capita emissions are still much less than ours . <p> I have never heard anyone before now suggest war against nuclear-armed China and India to persuade them to reduce emissions . <p> The Paris Climate Agreement is important -- the first time in history that 193 nations agreed to work together to reduce carbon emissions . Only Trump wants to pull out of the agreement . <p> -- Chad Tolman , Wilmington <h> An open letter to our children and grandchildren <p> As children who grew up during World War II , we never thought such horrific things could ever happen again . But frighteningly , history is repeating itself right here in America . <p> Political rallies resembling those in Germany , Italy and Spain the 1930s and ' 40s make us shudder . People are again taught it is okay to hate , hurt and even kill others of a different color , a different accent or who worship in a synagogue , mosque or different church . <p> The concentration of immigrants in detention camps reminds us that your grandfathers , great grandfathers and great uncles fought and died to liberate Europe from dictators and free survivors from such camps . The deafening silence in response to hate speech and increasing numbness to massacres in Walmarts , synagogues and now a kosher grocery in Jersey City speak volumes about the present dangers we face . <p> Learn the lessons of history . See clearly the difference between right and wrong , love and hate . Speak out against violence and injustices done to friends and strangers alike . Use all aspects of our precious democracy to make America better in our own eyes and the eyes of the world . Join your peers around the world in working for the common good . Help bring about the peace on earth you too want for your children and grandchildren .
