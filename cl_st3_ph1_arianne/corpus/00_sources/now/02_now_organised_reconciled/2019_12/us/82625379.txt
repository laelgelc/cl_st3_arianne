( AP ) -- A Democratic congressman from Oregon has announced his opposition to the proposed Jordan Cove liquefied natural gas terminal and its 230-mile ( 370-kilometer ) feeder pipeline in southern Oregon . <p> The World reports U.S. Rep. Peter DeFazio of Springfield said in a letter to campaign supporters last week that he does n't support the project because the federal approval process is run by " unelected political appointees , the project tramples on private property rights , and it will exacerbate the urgent and immediate threat of climate change . " <p> While he had n't taken a hard stance on Jordan Cove previously , DeFazio has spoken out against the 2005 Bush-Cheney Energy Policy Act , which allowed the Federal Energy and Regulatory Commission to claim eminent domain for energy projects . He has introduced legislation to reverse the commission 's eminent domain powers , DeFazio said . <p> DeFazio also said his office ca n't stop the Jordan Cove project . <p> Commission staff in November issued their final environmental analysis of the project , saying it would have some adverse and significant impacts . A final order is expected from commissioners in February . Even if it wins regulatory commission approval , construction would be contingent on the project obtaining a other state , federal and local approvals . <p> Jordan Cove spokesman Paul Vogel has pointed to the jobs and tax benefits the project would deliver and said backers would undertake mitigation to preserve old growth forests , wetlands and riparian habitat .
