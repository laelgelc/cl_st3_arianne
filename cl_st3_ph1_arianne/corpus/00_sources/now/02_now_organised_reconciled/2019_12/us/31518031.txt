<h> L.A. County serial killer dies in prison 40 years after slayings of 5 teenage girls <p> A serial killer convicted in the rape and torture murders of five teenage girls in Los Angeles County 40 years ago has died in custody at San Quentin State Prison , according to prison officials . <p> Lawrence Sigmond Bittaker , 79 , died at San Quentin State Prison on Friday . <p> ( California Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation ) <p> Lawrence Sigmond Bittaker , 79 , died at 4 p.m . Friday of natural causes , the California Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation said in a news release Monday . <p> He was convicted in Los Angeles County of 26 charges , including five counts of murder , five counts of kidnapping , criminal conspiracy , rape , oral copulation , sodomy and being an ex-felon in possession of a firearm . He was sentenced to death in March 1981 . <p> Bittaker and his partner , Roy Lewis Norris , were known as the " Tool Box Killers . " Together , they were responsible for the 1979 murders of Lucinda Lynn Schaefer , 16 ; Andrea Joy Hall , 18 ; Jacqueline Doris Gilliam , 15 ; Jacqueline Leah Lamp , 13 ; and Shirley Lynette Ledford , 16 . Prosecutors said the two lured young hitchhikers into a van and then attacked them with a sledgehammer , ice pick , vice grips and other weapons . <p> Advertisement <p> Norris , 71 , avoided the death penalty by pleading guilty to all charges and testifying against Bittaker , according to prison officials . He was sentenced to 45 years to life in prison in April 1981 . <p> Marin County coroner 's officials will conduct an autopsy to determine Bittaker 's specific cause of death . <p> Alejandra Reyes-Velarde is a Metro reporter for the Los Angeles Times .
