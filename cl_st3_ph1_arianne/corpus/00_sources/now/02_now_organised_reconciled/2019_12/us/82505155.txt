We have failed to safeguard our water supply for future generations , choosing to pollute anytime it 's cost effective , future be damned . All on our watch . <p> Worst of all , boomers have supported Donald Trump . He has lowered the standard of decency . Rather than debate , he calls people names . The president of the United States , resorts to name calling instead of dialog . Trump represents the very worst of the Boomers , he surrounds himself with white supremacist advisers , he has an abhorrent view of women and his persistent deregulation is wreaking havoc with our environment . <p> The younger generations see Trump for exactly who he is , a big step backwards .
