<p> Dec 5 ( Reuters ) - A Black Hawk helicopter , with three soldiers aboard , went down in central Minnesota on Thursday after the Minnesota National Guard lost contact with it during a maintenance test flight , the Guard said on social media . Emergency crews were at the scene of the downed UH-60 Black Hawk helicopter south of St. Cloud . The Minnesota National Guard said in a tweet it lost communication with the aircraft at about 2 p.m. local time . <p> " Our first priority is the safety and well-being of our Guardsmen and our thoughts are with our soldiers and their families at this time , " the Guard said in an earlier tweet .
