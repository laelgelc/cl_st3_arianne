If you see comments in violation of our community guidelines , please report them . <p> Time magazine named Greta Thunberg its 2019 Person of the Year on Wednesday . <p> This is the first time the magazine has honored a teenager , which makes the 16-year-old Swedish climate change activist the youngest person to ever be named . The youngest recipient before Thunberg was 25-year-old Charles Lindbergh in 1927 . <p> The accolade goes to the person or persons who " most influenced the news and the world " in the past year . <p> On a short list of finalists were President Donald Trump , House Speaker Nancy Pelosi , the anonymous whistleblower whose complaint sparked the impeachment inquiry , and protesters confronting the Chinese government in Hong Kong . <p> Thunberg , who spoke before the United Nations Climate Action Summit this year , has become a symbol for young people across the world protesting climate change inaction . <p> Time said it named Thunberg for " sounding the alarm about humanity 's predatory relationship with the only home we have , for bringing to a fragmented world a voice that transcends background and borders " and " for showing us all what it might look like when a new generation leads . " <p> This is n't the first time the magazine chose its Person of the Year as a nod to climate change . Time named Endangered Earth as Person of the Year in 1988 . <p> Thunberg took the world by storm by organizing the Global Climate Strike in September , in which millions participated in a worldwide strike . <p> Also in September , Greta gave an impassioned speech before the United Nations Climate Action Summit in which she scolded world leaders for not acting quickly and decisively to combat climate change . <p> " This is all wrong . I should n't be up here . I should be back in school on the other side of the ocean , " she said . <p> " People are suffering , people are dying , entire ecosystems are collapsing ... and all you talk about is money and eternal fairy tales of economic growth . How dare you ?
