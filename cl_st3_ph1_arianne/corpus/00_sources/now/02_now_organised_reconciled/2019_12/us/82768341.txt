And other top moments from the December Democratic debate in Los Angeles <p> Seven candidates took the stage for the sixth debate round of the Democratic primary , just one day after the House voted to impeach President Donald Trump . It was the smallest debate stage in the primary thus far . <p> The final debate of the year started off with measured responses to policy questions but heated up as candidates reverted to speaking over and attacking one another . <p> Here are some of the top moments from Thursday evening : <h> Spotlight put on decrease in diversity on the debate stage <p> Increasingly strict criteria to qualify for the stage has also narrowed the diversity of candidates in the debate , edging out candidates like New Jersey Sen. " I miss Sen. Kamala Harris and I miss Cory , although I think Cory will be back . " <p> Yang touted his plan to give Americans a " freedom dividend " of $1,000 a month as an attempt to bolster the campaigns of candidates of color . <p> " You know what you need to donate to political campaigns ? Disposable income , " Yang said . <p> The candidates signed onto a letter sent to the Democratic National Committee by Booker , arguing that the debate qualification standards should be changed for the early 2020 debates because of the shrinking diversity on stage . <p> In a tense exchange between Vermont Sen. Bernie Sanders and PBS moderator Amna Nawaz , Sanders tried to pivot from the question about diversity to a point about climate change . <p> " Senator , with all due respect , this question is about race . Can you answer the question that was asked ? " Nawaz said . <p> ? He went on to explain that climate change hits people of color the hardest and highlighted the importance of members of affected communities giving input in policy discussions . Candidates were asked how they can convince Americans to support the impeachment . <p> Yang hit the news media , saying the reason Americans are divided on impeachment is because of the media they consume . " <p> " We have to stop being obsessed over impeachment , " Yang said . <p> Massachusetts Sen.
