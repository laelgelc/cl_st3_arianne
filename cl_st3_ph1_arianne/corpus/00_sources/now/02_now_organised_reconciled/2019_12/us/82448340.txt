Today ( Monday , Nov. 25 ) at around 4 p.m. my boyfriend and I were walking out from the Mesilla Valley Mall and we saw this young man picking up the trash that was on the floor on his way to his car . He was accompanied by a gentleman in military uniform and a lady . As he was picking up the trash , he said to the gentleman and the lady that he was doing it to save the animals . Needless to say , my boyfriend and I were baffled and we even went back on our tracks to pick up the trash that we had just passed . <p> As small as his gesture may seem to some , I think he 's doing something amazing because he 's doing his part on helping the planet just by keeping his surroundings clean , and if we all followed his example , we 'd have a cleaner , nicer environment . Cooper , Las Cruces Public Schools Board of Education member <h> More views on minimum wage <p> The recent meeting at Lynn Middle school and commentary by Phil San Filippo in the Las Cruces Sun-News about potential impacts of increases in minimum wage addresses only one side of the debate -- the effect on business owners . By the way it only goes from $10.10 an hour to $10.25 an hour on Jan. 1 , 2020 -- big deal . <p> Are people aware that only amounts to about $21,000 a year for a full-time worker . With the current cost of housing , medical bills , food , education and transportation who can afford to exist on that salary ? That is below the poverty line . Do n't tell me that is only what early entry workers earn . Fewer than 10% are young people that just entered the work force , whereas more than half are prime age adults who must support a family . <p> In Las Cruces , 70% to 80% of retail workers are associated with out-of-state corporations like Wal-Mart , Home Depot , Dillard 's , Call Centers , McDonalds , Olive Garden , Village Inn , etc . They can well afford a decent wage for their workers . The minimum wage in Colorado , Arizona and Oregon is higher than here and they are doing just fine -- as well as locally owned businesses . <p> Some speakers at the Lynn Middle school meeting said that El Paso is booming , and workers are doing well at $7.25 an hour ( really ) . They should ask them about health care , because Texas ranks last nationally for health care access and affordability . Besides , patrons in Las Cruces are not going to travel there for food and services for a few cents ' differences in cost . <p> When are we going to learn that when you pay workers a decent salary the economy does better , because they spend their money locally and have more commitment to their job ? Also , businesses compete on an equal level , because they all must pay a similar wage . <p> Paul O ' Connell , Las Cruces <p> *** <p> The actual value of the federal minimum wage has declined 24% since 1968 . The nonpartisan Congressional Budget Office this year said that increasing the minimum wage would lift some 1.5 million families out of poverty . As for would-be job losses created by higher minimum wages , over 100 empirical studies have been published over the past 20 years , and this body of research points to a very limited effect of minimum wages on levels of employment . <p> What is the attitude of the American business community ? 79 percent of business executives around the country support raising the minimum wage . That 's according to a leaked survey of 1,000 executives of businesses of all sizes conducted by Republican pollster Frank Luntz 's firm LuntzGlobal for the Council of State Chambers . Chambers that oppose minimum wage increases do n't represent the views of most business owners . Chamber executives were told in the Luntz webinar that the survey squares with other polling , and concluded that " If you 're fighting against a minimum wage increase , you 're fighting an uphill battle , because most Americans , even most Republicans , are okay with raising the minimum wage . " <p> According to SmallBuinessMajority.org , 57% of small business owners support increasing the minimum wage . 61% of small business owners in the retail and restaurant industries support increasing minimum wages . Unfortunately , in Las Cruces the present debate focuses too much upon the complaints of small business owners determined to pay workers like they are impoverished peons . Maybe those owners need to re-train and get into some other line of work .
