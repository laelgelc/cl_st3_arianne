<h> Rep. Liz Olson Running for Re-Election <p> Rep. Liz Olson Photo : Liz Olson <p> Created : December 19 , 2019 09:16 PM <p> Rep. Liz Olson ( D-Duluth ) announced Thursday she will seek re-election . <p> Advertisement <p> She joined the Minnesota House of Representatives in 2016 and said she is proud of helping get Duluth 's street sales tax across the finish line and helping on a state budget that includes millions for Duluth 's medical district infrastructure . <p> But she said in a press release she feels there is more work to be done . <p> " Families across the state are demanding action to address senseless gun violence , " Olson wrote . " As the impacts of climate change take effect , Minnesotans -- especially youth -- have been vocal about the need to reduce our reliance on fossil fuels . " <p> Olson said she also wants to work on paid family and medical leave and earned sick and safe time .
