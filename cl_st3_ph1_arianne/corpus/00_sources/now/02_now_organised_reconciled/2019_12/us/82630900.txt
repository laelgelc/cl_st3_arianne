<h> Eutrophication remains a major problem for Europe 's seas despite some progress <p> The shared vision for Europe 's seas is a healthy marine environment where human-induced eutrophication is minimised . However , the European Environment Agency 's ( EEA ) assessment , published today , shows that eutrophication still remains a large scale problem in some of Europe 's regionals seas . The assessment shows some positive effects from better nutrient management but the overall target of healthy seas will not be met everywhere by 2020 . <p> The EEA report shows that 2,400,000 km2 of Europe 's seas have been mapped for eutrophication . However , the coverage of the assessed area varies significantly among regional seas with 99 percent coverage in the Baltic Sea , 27 percent in the North-East Atlantic , 9 percent in the Black sea , and only 4 percent in the Mediterranean Sea . <p> According to the EEA assessment , about 563,000 km2 ( or 23 percent ) of these areas have a eutrophication problem , including areas in all regional seas . The situation is worst in the Baltic Sea where 99 percent of the assessed areas suffer from eutrophication , followed by the Black Sea 's 53 percent . Based on the assessments , eutrophication is also present in parts of the North-East Atlantic ( 7percent ) and in some coastal areas in the Mediterranean Sea ( 12 percent ) , mainly close to densely populated coasts or catchments that are downstream from agricultural activities . <p> Overall , Europe 's regional seas are slowly recovering from eutrophication , mainly due to efforts to reduce nutrient inputs over the past decades . Reducing nutrient inputs is embedded in several EU policies but these targets remain unlikely to be met within the agreed time frame for all of Europe 's seas . <p> The EEA assessment states that to achieve the policy vision of a healthy marine environment in all Europe 's regional seas , further reduction of nutrient inputs is needed in the most sensitive areas , together with consideration of the effects of climate change . <p> Citation : Eutrophication remains a major problem for Europe 's seas despite some progress ( 2019 , December 11 ) retrieved 28 December 2019 from https : **39;3793;TOOLONG ...
