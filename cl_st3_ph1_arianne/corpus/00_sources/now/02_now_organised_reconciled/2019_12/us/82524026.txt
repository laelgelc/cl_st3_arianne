<p> MEXICO CITY , Dec 5 ( Reuters ) - High level security meetings between U.S. Attorney General William Barr and a host of Mexican officials on Thursday made progress addressing arms and drugs trafficking as well as money laundering , Mexico 's foreign ministry said in a statement . <p> The statement said the meetings were " cordial and respectful . " <p> Barr , along with U.S. Ambassador to Mexico Christopher Landau , met with President Andres Manuel Lopez Obrador , and later with his security and defense ministers and Navy chief , the statement added .
