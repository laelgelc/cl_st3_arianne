Courtesy of IMDB . <p> It 's the end of the year and movie fans know what that means : Top 10 lists . <p> Every publication weighs in with their favorite films of the year , but as the field of candidates widens thanks to streaming services entering the distribution game , lists have grown more diverse . So to find a true critical consensus , we compiled 25 different Top 10 lists into a spreadsheet and gave each critics ' picks a point value . <p> Number one picks earned ten points , number two picks earned nine , you get the idea . <p> Now as with any amateur statistical experiment , there are some caveats to list . First , we only chose ranked lists , because let 's be honest , an unranked list is weak sauce . And in the case of longer lists , we only used the top ten ( We 're looking at you , Thrillist and Esquire ) . <p> We also counted the number of lists each film appeared on to check for any serious outliers ( movies that punched above its weight because of a couple number one rankings ) . We 're no data scientists , but the correlation between number of list appearances and overall ranking seemed sound . <p> Over the breadth of the 25 lists , critics choose 75 different films . Although some blockbuster hits like " Once Upon a Time ... in Hollywood " and " The Irishman " placed high on the list , the number of foreign and independent films will surprise many moviegoers , especially the number one film .
