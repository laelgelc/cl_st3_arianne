<p> Police in Washington DC have arrested a 82-year-old Oscar-winning actress , Jane Fonda for a fifth time on Friday , December 20th over ongoing climate change march . <p> WITHIN NIGERIA learnt that Jane Fonda had earlier given a speech in front of a sign that read , ' Happy 82nd birthday , Jane ! ' before she was arrested by the police . <p> According to reports , Fonda has been protesting weekly during her Fire Drill Fridays since announcing she was moving to Washington " to be closer to the epicentre of the fight for our climate . " <p> Her participation has ended in multiple arrests . She was arrested alongside several other protesters Friday . Of the 138 arrested , two were kept overnight . <p> The group sang ' Happy birthday ' to Fonda as she was led away in handcuffs . Friday 's march was another celebrity-studded affair . Rosanna Arquette , Gloria Steinem and Casey Willson were arrested at the protest , too .
