<h> $2.2trn Global Economy Lost to Illicit Trade -- Analyst <p> By Kabir MuhammadEsteban Giudici , Senior Analyst , Transnational Alliance has said that economic leakages from illicit trade creates an annual drain on the global economy of 2.2 trillion dollars , equivalent to three per cent of the world 's economy . <p> Giudici said this during the 2019 Africa Security Forum ( ASF ) at Rabat , Morocco on Thursday . <p> He said data on this activity has previously been scarce , adding that there are mounting evidence of the economic , social and environmental losses it caused . <p> " If illicit trading were a country , its economy will be larger than Brazil , Italy and Canada -- and as large as Mexico and Indonesia combined . <p> " Illicit trade significantly endangers all aspects of the SDGs . <p> " The World Economic Forum estimated the value of illicit trade and transnational criminal activities at eight to 15 per cent of global Gross Domestic Product ( GDP ) in 2014 . <p> " With estimations of various illicit activities running upward of $3 trillion ; these figures rival the GDP of some G7 countries , ' ' he said . <p> Giudici said the activities create a triple threat to the finance development by depriving governments of revenues for investment in vital services . <p> He said this has continued to increase the costs of achieving the SDGs by eroding the progress already made . <p> " One of the biggest oil producers in the world loses as much as 30 per cent of its refined petroleum products through theft , diversion and smuggling into neighbouring states . <p> " To compound the problem , its government loses associated tax revenues and spends billions on curbing this illicit trade in petroleum . <p> " Illicit trade pushes endangered species to the brink of extinction and causes irreversible damage to ecosystems . <p> " For instance , illegal logging , with an estimated annual value of up to $157 billion , is the world 's most profitable crime involving natural resources , ' ' he said . <p> Giudici called for participatory approach in implementing the fight against climate change . <p> He also appealed to G7 leaders to sustain counter illicit trade in their countries . <p> Suleiman idris is a graduate of Fed Univ of Tech Minna ( BTECH Mathematics/Comp Science ) , Specialist in ICT Support Services . also proficient in use of online journalism tools &amp;
