<h> Another season of The InterswitchSPAK TV Quiz series has begun <p> Season 2 of InterswitchSPAK TV show debuted on November 2 and so far , we have aired four episodes ! The InterswitchSPAK TV Show comprises of the Innovation Challenge and TV Quiz segments . <p> During the Innovation Challenge , teams of nine students each are tasked to proffer solutions to select societal challenges , leveraging technology . The nine teams are divided into three teams with three topics to work on . <p> After the Innovation Challenge comes to the TV Quiz . During this segment , the students who had collaborated on the Innovation Challenge now compete against themselves for the coveted N12.5 million scholarship prize . <p> This forum will periodically review the InterswitchSPAK episodes - the activities , highlights and insights . From the four episodes that we have viewed , one consistent factor has been the quality of production and the outstanding prowess displayed by the students . <p> In the first episode , we were brought up to speed with all the offline activities before the TV show commenced . There were highlights of the press briefing featuring the first winner of season 1 -Akachukwu Anumudu . <p> We also saw snippets of the National Qualifying Examination conducted across Nigeria . We saw the profiling of the erstwhile participant who emerged overall best student in the 2019 WAEC examination , scoring straight A1s - Stepany Ugboaja . There was also the Masterclass , featuring three remarkable Nigerians , 81 finalists and the media . <p> Photo : InterswitchSPAKSource : UGC <p> " Can I trust you ? " was the 12.5-million-naira question thrown at the students by Mitchell Elegbe , the Group CEO/Founder of Interswitch Group . He tasked the students to build trust across the board , as it was critical to success . <p> Photo : InterswitchSPAKSource : UGC <p> Photo : InterswitchSPAKSource : UGC <p> The finalists were grouped into nine teams . Each team was mentored by two Interswitch staff , volunteering as " team captains " . <p> The Innovation Challenge kicked off in the second episode . Team Mercury gave the first presentation on : " Leveraging Technology and innovation to optimize Nigeria 's Agricultural value chain " . Tomi Ogunlesi and Omowale Jacobs were the team captains . The team gave a good account of itself with its proposed solution - Agro Market Portal System . <p> The solution posits the aggregation of key players in the Nigerian Agricultural system for easy access to goods , information and funds . <p> Photo : InterswitchSPAKSource : UGC <p> The solution included integration with Quickteller for easier payments to and from the farmers . They also considered the inclusion of NIMET onto the portal , to advise farmers on climate change , weather forecasts and other meteorological issues . <p> After the teamwork , the team members moved on to battle , competing for the star prize . The best three of the nine moved to the next stage of the competition . From Team Mercury we had : Muhammed Abuulahi , Sigmus Aimofumhe and Ahmed Adulhameed . <p> Photo : InterswitchSPAKSource : UGC <p> Episode 3 started with Team Venus 's Innovation Challenge presentation . With their mentors , Wale Micaiah and Omoede Aihe , their solution was an app called AGICKET , which is applicable to consumers and farmers . <p> They proposed the construction of centres with adequate storage and transport facilities across the country . With this , the farmers can sell their products easily and be remunerated adequately , while buyers can locate produce faster without difficulty . <p> The best of the Venus team members who made it to the next stage were Anita Alabi , Ayokunle Adeniyi and Munachi Aniagolu . <p> Photo : InterswitchSPAKSource : UGC <p> The fourth episode introduced Team Earth 's presentation . With a bit of drama to spice up the show , a team member came dressed in a farmer 's attire . Their solution , FarmEazy will be available on IOS and Android devices , USSD code and SMS for feature phones . <p> The platform has different interesting features which include the farmer 's community , farmer 's market , investor 's platform , USSD service , loans and training . This platform is expected to help the farmers and buyers connect . Investors also have a space to provide loans and utilities for farmers . Their mentors were Paul Otu and Tolulope Adesokan . <p> Photo : InterswitchSPAKSource : UGC <p> The best three of the nine-team members who made it to the next stage were : Etomike Obinna- Chukwu , Ezekiel Ekanem and Dabira Akinyemi . <p> Watching these students , increasingly reassures us that Africans can indeed solve the continent 's problems if we task ourselves hard enough .
