<h> South Africa Calls For Robust Multilateral Response To Climate Change <p> South Africa on Monday called for a robust multilateral response to climate change in a way that would assist African and other developing countries to adapt to the associated loss and damage , Concise News reports . <p> The Minister of Environment , Forestry and Fisheries , Barbara Creecy said this before leaving for Madrid , Spain to attend the ongoing Climate Change Conference under the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) . <p> Developing countries must secure the finance , technology and other support they require to combat climate change and to transition their economies to more sustainable development pathways , Creecy said . <p> She recently assumed the Presidency of the African Ministerial Conference on the Environment ( AMCEN ) and , in this capacity , has a mandate to advance Africa 's shared priorities . <p> For Africa and all UN member states , the impacts of climate change are real and most felt by the poor and vulnerable groups in society , the minister said . <p> According to Creecy , over the past year , every sub-region of Africa has experienced weather events that have caused considerable loss of life and destruction . <p> South Africa , for example , is experiencing unprecedented increases in the frequency and intensity of heatwaves , bush fires , and droughts , which are placing a tremendous burden on its stressed water resources , said Creecy . <p> " Given this reality , it is imperative for South Africa to contribute to securing a robust multilateral response to climate change , " the minister said . <p> The Madrid conference 's priority task is to finalise the implementation guidelines for the Paris Agreement , which becomes fully operational in 2020 . <p> This includes , in particular , reaching an agreement on a market mechanism to replace the current Clean Development Mechanism of the Kyoto Protocol . <p> Creecy said market mechanisms need to incentivize climate action to achieve the global goal of the Paris Agreement . <p> " The new market must benefit Africa and help finance our adaptation efforts , " Creecy said . Africa also requires the Madrid conference to recognise the special needs and circumstances of African countries and to advance work towards achieving the Paris Agreement 's global goal on adaptation , review the work of the Warsaw Mechanism on Loss and Damage and the Gender Action Plan , Creecy said . <p> The conference comes at a pivotal moment where science is sending a clear message that the world faces a climate emergency and that everybody needs to act with a renewed sense of urgency , said the minister . <p> South Africa is fully committed to contributing to the success of the conference , she said .
