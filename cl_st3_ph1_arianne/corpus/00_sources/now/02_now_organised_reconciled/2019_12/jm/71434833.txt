Fayal Williams , says Government will be revising the National Energy Policy to establish new targets for renewable energy , energy efficiency , and conservation . <p> " We have exceeded expectations in many areas and I am pleased to say that after just 10 years , we are looking at revising our National Energy Policy and our subpolicies to further develop the energy sector , " she said . <p> The Minister , who was speaking at the opening of the National Energy Policy 's 10th anniversary symposium at the AC Marriott Hotel in New Kingston on Wednesday ( November 27 ) , said that the energy sector has seen tremendous growth over the years and she is proud of the country 's accomplishments . <p> Minister of Science , Energy and Technology , Hon. Fayal Williams , says Government will be revising the National Energy Policy to establish new targets for renewable energy , energy efficiency , and conservation . <p> " We have exceeded expectations in many areas and I am pleased to say that after just 10 years , we are looking at revising our National Energy Policy and our subpolicies to further develop the energy sector , " she said . <p> The Minister , who was speaking at the opening of the National Energy Policy 's 10th anniversary symposium at the AC Marriott Hotel in New Kingston on Wednesday ( November 27 ) , said that the energy sector has seen tremendous growth over the years and she is proud of the country 's accomplishments . <p> She noted that in 2008 , petroleum accounted for 95 per cent of electricity installed capacity , while wind and hyrdo accounted for 2.4 per cent and 2.6 per cent , respectively . Ten years later , petroleum accounts for 74.1 per cent , wind 10 per cent and hydro 2.8 per cent . <p> Natural gas and solar , which were added to the mix , provide 11.1 per cent and two per cent , respectively , of energy solutions . <p> " These statistics demonstrate that we are on a mission to secure Jamaica 's energy future through diversification and the provision of alternative , sustainable and environmentally friendly sources of energy , " Mrs. Williams said . <p> Meanwhile , the Minister noted that renewables and other clean energy sources will be at the core of the development of the country 's electricity sector . <p> " We boast some of the largest solar and wind facilities in the Caribbean with the 20-megawatt Content Solar and the 37-megawatt Eight Rivers Facility , in addition to the 62.7-megawatt Wigton Windfarm and the 36-megawatt BMR Wind facilities , " she said . <p> She said the Government has set a target of 30 per cent of renewables in electricity generation by 2030 , and by 2020 is expected to generate 18 per cent of its electricity from renewable sources . <p> Permanent Secretary in the Ministry , Carol Palmer , for her part , said she is pleased to be celebrating the anniversary of the National Energy Policy , which was the first long-term strategy to ensure that the country achieves a modern , efficient , diversified and environmentally sustainable energy sector by 2030 . <p> She said that the policy must not be taken for granted , as energy plays a major role in the development of the economy . <p> " Having a policy to strategically guide our plans has become absolutely critical , especially at a time when we are battling the effects of climate change and volatility in prices for imported petroleum , " Mrs. Palmer noted . <p> " We are regional leaders in energy because of our national policy . Investors and stakeholders take us seriously because we have an energy policy , which lays out the plans and strategies for our energy sector , " she added . <p> The symposium was held as part of activities for Jamaica Energy Week , which is being observed under the theme ' Energy at your Service : Empowering People , Building Resilience '
