<h> SIDO 's Guru Planet empowering women to produce alternative charcoal <p> IN a bid to wean women from using fossil fuels as a source of energy for their households , Small Industries Development Organization through Guru Planet Limited , has taught them how to make alternative charcoal from newspapers and insulated boxes . <p> A 35 year old mother of three , Warda Omary who is based at Tabata Muslim in Dar es Salaam , said last week that as a food vendor ( Mama Lishe ) , she has been using normal charcoal to cook for many years but is now egger to change and adopt green energy sources . <p> Omary who is Chairperson of the women 's entrepreneurial group lobby , Tabata Muslim Union for Nation Building popularly known as UWAJAMJETA , said the ten months old made group has learned a lot from Sido 's GPL . <p> " SIDO taught us how to make the alternative charcoal through GPL which has almost the same intensity as timber made charcoal and can cook even beans , " said Omary stressing that through GPL which is linked to Sido , her group is now targeting to produce the alternative charcoal commercially to sell to the market . <p> According to her , alternative charcoal does not emit smoke hence is environmentally friendly compared to fossil fuel charcoal which emits carbon dioxide blamed to cause climate change . She said although UWAJAMJETA has not yet started making the alternative charcoal commercially , they future is to exploit the available market . <p> She also hinted that since when they started making the charcoal almost five months ago , the main challenge has been availability of raw materials and technology to produce massively hence only two sacks of 35 kilograms each have been produced over the period . <p> " Another challenge is we put a lot of energy to make the charcoal . Pounding wet boxes and newspapers needs a lot of energy . After that you need to mix charcoal dust with the pounded materials that causes me chest pain and sometimes coughing , " she noted . <p> Machines to produce alternative charcoal are made by Vocational Education and Training Authority but are very expensive with one unit being sold at between 3-5m/- based on capacity . " Our group does not have the financial capacity to buy such machines . It would be better if we had these machines loaned to us , " she argued . <p> Giving an expert analysis of the main challenges facing alternative energy sources to charcoal and firewood , Abraham Baisi who is a chemical engineer but also Founder and CEO of Syntroleum Petrochemical Group Limited , said that alternative charcoal has low combustion capacity but also very demanding to produce . Engineer Baisi said low combustion is due to raw materials used to make it that can not transmit heat easily . <p> He noted that for the alternative charcoal to burn , it must be mixed with wooded charcoal or must be mixed with charcoal dust when making it . Eng Baisi added that the charcoal can also be improved by blending it with Ammonium Nitrate which has harmful effects on people 's respiratory system . <p> In the 2017/18 Household Budget Survey by National Bureau of Statistics , it stated that charcoal consumption has slightly increased by 28.8 percent during the period compared to 28.2 per cent in 2011/12 period . <p> The report stated that 61 percent of urban residents use charcoal for cooking compared to 12 percent of rural residents with Dar es Salaam being market leader and had consumption increased to 59 percent in 2017/18 from 46.2 percent in 2011/12 period . Morogoro came next with 44 percent followed by Mwanza at 40 percent with Kigoma and Rukwa both 36 per cent . <p> A Medical Doctor at Magu district council hospital in Mwanza region , Neheniah Nnko told this paper that charcoal dust affects the functioning of the human body systems negatively with respiratory system , digestive system and the body 's excretory system as the most affected . <p> " The dust causes allergies to the lungs . It eventually causes asthma and nasal cancer ( Paranasal Cancer ) . It also causes cancer of the esophagus ( Nasopharynx Carcinoma ) " said Nnko . He added that charcoal dust causes allergies to the skin called Allergic Dermatitis that lowers the functioning of digestive system .
