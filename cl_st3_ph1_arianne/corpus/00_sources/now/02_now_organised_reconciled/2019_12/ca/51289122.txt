How can we marshal our resources effectively ? <p> On January 16 in Ivanhoe , the Hastings Stewardship Council kicks off its 2020 Winter Speaker Series with Professor Warren Mabee , Director of Queen 's Institute for Energy and Environmental Policy . <p> Climate change continues to have an enormous impact on Canada and the world . While governments are beginning to recognize the problem , workable solutions remain in short supply . Dr. Mabee will provide an overview of the current situation in Canada , and look at the ways in which Canadian governments are trying to respond . Controversial policies such as carbon taxes will be explored . Other alternatives may have more impact , including the use of bio-based building products . Can we use more wood in place of concrete , steel and plastics ? <p> The role of individual Canadians in the fight against climate change will also be addressed . What individual commitments can we make that can significantly contribute to our goals ? <p> Dr. Warren Mabee is Associate Dean and Director of the School of Policy Studies at Queen 's University as well as the Canada Research Chair in Renewable Energy Development and Implementation , and the Director of the Queen 's Institute for Energy and Environmental Policy . Many audiences have enjoyed Dr. Mabee 's talks on the intersection of bioenergy , forestry and environmental management . <p> Join the stewardship council on Thursday , Jan. 16 from 7 to 9 p.m. at the Community Hall , 11379 Highway 62 , in Ivanhoe . The cost is $5 ( or a donation ) ; children are free . For more information , contact Ray Wellman at 613-848-7697 or **27;3510;TOOLONG . Also visit hastingsstewardship.ca . <p> This is the first of seven evenings in the Speaker Series covering these topics : Climate Change ; the Story of Plastics ; Maple Syrup ; Grazing Animals and the Soil ; Cannabis and Hemp ; Dragonflies and Damselflies ; and Earth Sheltered Homes .
