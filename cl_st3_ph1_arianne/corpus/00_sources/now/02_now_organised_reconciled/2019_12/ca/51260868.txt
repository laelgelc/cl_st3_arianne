<p> This morning , a company press release announced that Enbridge Inc. and Enterprise Products Partners L.P. have signed a letter of intent ( LOI ) agreeing to the joint development of a deepwater crude oil terminal located in the Gulf of Mexico . <p> The companies are hoping to focus their manpower on Enterprise 's Sea Port Oil Terminal ( SPOT ) , which is able to load very large crude carriers ( VLCCs ) at approximately 85,000 barrels per hour , reaching close to 2 million barrels per day . Unlike other offshore terminals , SPOT uses a vapour control system to mitigate its emissions . <p> The LOI outlines an equity participation right agreement , where an Enbridge associate has the ability to purchase ownership interest in SPOT Terminal Services LLC if SPOT is granted a deepwater port licence . The Maritime Administration is in the process of reviewing the SPOT application in order to approve the licences needed for its construction . <p> In Canada <p> A common topic at the climate summit in Madrid is the use of LNG to mitigate global emissions that would otherwise be emitted by coal-based energy . More specifically , there is discussion about Canada using LNG exportation towards its own emission reduction targets . <p> This topic is called Article 6 and would require voluntary co-operation between countries , according to ongoing deliberation . Canadian Environment Minister Jonathan Wilkinson has said that there needs to be more clarification on whether or not LNG would be used to completely replace coal-based energy or whether it would be used in addition to coal . <p> Wilkinson told The Globe and Mail that the federal government is focused on national emission reductions and " achieving our commitments without Article 6 . " He also recognized that it is n't clear how the double-counting of emission cut credits would be prevented in the case where " big , big emissions " are created because of the liquefaction process . <p> Meanwhile , a new Nanos Research survey authorized by CTV News found that in a group of 1,010 interviewees , 42 per cent of individuals were supportive of new pipeline development and 23 per cent were sort of supportive . On the other hand , 30 per cent of those surveyed were in complete opposition to pipelines , and 4 per cent were on the fence about their construction , reported CTV News . <p> Over half of those interviewed in Quebec were in opposition to , or sort of opposed new pipeline projects , but in the Prairies , there were only 10 individuals that did not agree with new construction . There was smaller support of pipeline construction among millennials , but over half of those interviewed in the age group between 18 and 34 were supportive of or sort of supported pipeline development . <p> In the 35 to 54 age group , 7 out of 10 individuals supported pipeline construction , and 70 per cent of individuals 55 and older were in support of the infrastructure . Support for pipeline development was more prominent among men ; three-quarters of men were supportive or sort of supportive of pipeline construction , and only half of women were . <p> Internationally <p> Friends of the Earth , an environmental organization , has warned that sections of the energy charter treaty ( ECT ) may allow major fossil fuel companies to take legal action against the government when it comes to green policies . <p> According to the organization , the European green deal -- which is to be announced by Ursula von der Leyen on Wednesday -- is at risk of being weakened by this treaty and must be overhauled this week in order to avoid problems , reported The Guardian . <p> The ECT , whose implementation followed the Cold War , also puts at risk the Labour party 's plan to nationalize areas of the energy industry that are under foreign ownership . <p> " As soon as people hear about this obscure pact undermining the public interest and the fight against climate change , they will be outraged . Either the EU and member states fundamentally revise it , or pull out , " said Paul de Clerck , the economic justice co-ordinator for Friends of the Earth Europe .
