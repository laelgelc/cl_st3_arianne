<h> Walking with the Bear Clan <p> It was a busy session at the Manitoba Legislature . My colleagues and I were proud to introduce a number of very important private member 's bills that the government should support ! <p> With health care in a difficult state in our province , our nurses are being seriously overworked . Mandatory overtime was intended for emergency situations but has begun to be overused to cover staffing shortages throughout the system in the past few years . It is not fair for staff to have to frequently work double shifts unwillingly which we know is happening , and it is certainly not safe for patients . We are hoping to eliminate mandatory overtime for nurses through The Restricting Mandatory Overtime for Nurses Act . <p> As the Critic for Conservation and Climate I was proud to introduce The Climate and Green Plan Amendment Act which would improve Manitoba 's climate change targets and improve government accountability for those targets . We also introduced Bill 202 which would allow the government to follow through on the Manitoba Human Rights Commission ruling that Manitoba must allow an " X " or a " no-sex " designation on birth certificates . <p> With the House now breaking for the holidays , I look forward to being out with you all in the community , celebrating the great things going on in Wolseley . I will be attending several seasonal concerts in Wolseley area schools and will be joining in the Grade 7 and 8 holiday feast at Gordon Bell High School . I 'll also be dropping into holiday events at Art City and Broadway Neighbourhood Centre and will be delivering boxes of mandarins to all of the area daycares . <p> Throughout December I have been walking with the Bear Clan in West Broadway on Saturday evenings . I very much appreciate all of their hard work and compassionate care for our community members in need of a meal , warm clothing and kindness . Together we 've picked up needles , supported folks needing a connection to a service and by our presence in the community , increased safety for everyone .
