Editorials are written by staff within the Star 's editorial board , which is independent of the newsroom . <h> Darts and laurels : A year of havoc around the world <p> If 2019 was notable for anything , it was the havoc that can be wreaked by a mere handful of so-called " strong men " who were elected to lead democracies . <p> We 're talking here about economies threatened by trade wars set off by President Donald Trump , UN climate change treaties threatened by -- oh -- that would be Trump again , and multilateral institutions such as NATO undermined by -- you guessed it -- Trump . <p> Not that he did n't have a lot of help disrupting the world order with the help of other strong men who hold power around the world , from Turkey 's Recep Tayyip Erdogan to Brazil 's Jair Bolsonaro to Russia 's Vladimir Putin . <p> Indeed , with leaders of supposed democracies like these , who needs dictators ? <p> Still , as always , there were heroes and moments to celebrate in 2019 . <p> Here , then , the Star 's annual end-of-year Darts and Laurels , starting off thematically with the other wreaker of havoc : climate change . <p> LAUREL To Sweden 's Greta Thunberg , who inspired millions of young people to skip classes and march to demand action on climate change by asking a simple question : " Why should we be studying for a future that soon may be no more ? " <p> DART To Maxime Bernier , leader of the People 's Party of Canada . Bernier the Bully assaulted Thunberg in a Twitter tirade , calling the 16-year-old " mentally unstable " and a " threat to our prosperity and civilization . " <h> YOU MIGHT BE INTERESTED IN ... <p> LAUREL To former environment minister , now infrastructure minister , Catherine McKenna . She stood up against climate change neanderthals like premiers Doug Ford , Jason Kenney , Scott Moe and Brian Pallister -- as well as the anonymous coward who scrawled the C-word across her campaign office window . <p> DART To Brazil 's Jair Bolsonaro , who childishly turned down foreign aid to help put out raging fires in the Amazon rain forest , which provides 20 per cent of the world 's oxygen . He blamed it on a personal disagreement with France 's president , Emmanuel Macron . But in reality the spat provided cover for him to live up to his election promise to open up the rainforest to industry . <p> LAUREL To Toronto Mayor John Tory who finally pushed for a substantial increase in taxes through a " city building fund " to pay for transit and housing . <p> LAUREL To NDP leader Jagmeet Singh . Instead of using Prime Minister Justin Trudeau 's black-face scandal for his own political advantage , he struck the right note by expressing empathy for those , like him , who have felt the sting of racism . " I want you to know that you have value , you have worth , and you are loved . " <p> DART To Andrew Scheer , who threatened to undo the good work done on Canada 's new food guide , the first to be guided by science , not industry lobbying . Why ? As political payback to the dairy lobby , which helped him win the Conservative leadership campaign . <h> YOU MIGHT BE INTERESTED IN ... <p> LAUREL To Bianca Andreescu . At 19 , she became the first Canadian to win what is now the Rogers Cup and then went on to become the highest-ranked Canadian in the history of the Women 's Tennis Association rankings . <p> DART To all the people who jammed 911 's emergency lines to complain about amber alerts on their cellphones . For them , apparently , their peace and quiet trumps being disturbed because a child might be in peril . <p> LAUREL To Canada 's James Peebles , who won the 2019 Nobel Prize in physics for transforming " our understanding of the evolution of the universe and Earth 's place in the cosmos . " Heady stuff , indeed . <p> LAUREL To brave democracy protesters in Hong Kong who remind us that we can never take our freedoms for granted . <p> DART To the anti-vaxxer movement , which has endangered children by exposing them to the real and serious dangers of contracting measles and other diseases .
