<h> Cold Weather Kills 50 in Bangladesh and Freezes India <p> NEW DELHI/DHAKA ( Reuters ) -- The second coldest weather in 119 years sweeping across western and northern India has led to dense fog , killing at least six people and disrupting many parts of the country , including the capital New Delhi . <p> In Uttar Pradesh , India 's biggest state , six people were killed when their car skidded off the road and plunged into a canal . Police said heavy fog was to blame . <p> As dense fog blanketed the vast country and the sprawling city of Delhi , home to 20 million people , visibility dropped sharply . <p> Sixteen flights were diverted , four were cancelled and about 500 delayed in Delhi , local media reported . <p> " Due to dense fog and poor visibility in the northern region , our flights are impacted , " IndiGo , India 's biggest airline by market share , said in a Tweet . <p> SpiceJet Ltd and AirAsia India also cancelled flights and authorities said train services were running late . <p> The city government of Delhi has moved many homeless people to temporary shelters , but a large number of them are still sleeping outdoors . <p> Winters in western and northern India have become colder in recent years , which experts say is due to climate change and rapid urbanization , and it is common in Delhi to see families sleeping on pavements and railway platforms , and under flyovers . <p> " Keeping in mind the average December temperature , we 're on course to endure the coldest month in 119 years , " Kuldeep Srivastava , a senior scientist at the India Meteorological Department , told Reuters . <p> " We expect cold wave conditions to continue for quite some time with some temporary respite for a day or two . " <p> The minimum temperature recorded in Delhi on Sunday was 2.8 degrees Celsius ( 37.04 Fahrenheit ) , a little higher than the 2.4 degrees Celsius seen on Saturday , the lowest since 2013 . <p> " Low-hanging clouds over the western and northern parts of the country , cool winds blowing from the Himalayan regions of Jammu and Kashmir , Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand , and snowfall in the hilly areas around Delhi have led to a sharp drop in the temperature , " Srivastava said . <p> Freezing weather continued in northern Bangladesh where at least 50 people have died due to the cold . <p> The country 's lowest temperature this year was recorded at 4.5 degrees Celsius ( 40.1 ? Fahrenheit ) on Sunday in Tetulia , a border town in northern Bangladesh . <p> " The cold wave drifting down from the Himalayas has adversely affected all kinds of business establishments , including farming , restaurants , and retail shops , " said Aisha Nuri , a leading government official . <p> A prolonged cold spell in Bangladesh could hit the rice crop , said Mizanur Rahman , a senior official at the agriculture ministry . <p> In Pakistan , Prime Minister Imran Khan asked the chief ministers of Punjab and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa provinces to move homeless people to shelters .
