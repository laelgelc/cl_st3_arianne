<h> Mining Association of Canada most active lobbyist on Hill in November <p> The industry association for Canada 's mining sector was the most active lobbying body on the Hill in November , according to new statistics from the Office of the Commissioner of Lobbying . <p> The Mining Association of Canada ( MAC ) filed 24 communication reports with the commissioner 's office in November . ZYUS Life Sciences , a Saskatoon-based life sciences and biopharmaceutical firm , was the second-most active organization on the lobbying circuit , submitting 16 over that span . <p> The Federation of Canadian Municipalities finished third , submitting 16 filings last month . <p> All registered lobbyists and organizations must file reports with the commissioner 's office detailing any communications with designated public office holders regarding their advocacy activities . The communications cover in-person meetings , as well as scheduled phone calls . <p> In its in-house filing with the commissioner 's office , MAC lists a wide swath of lobbying priorities , including on transportation regulations related to the " shipping of mineral products , " environmental assessment rules , tax policy for the mineral industry and on the Canadian Environmental Protection Act " as it applies to substances produced by or used by the mineral industry . " <p> The association 's first-place showing in November is owed to a " Hill Day " it held that brought some 30-40 members to Ottawa for meetings , according to MAC spokesperson Cynthia Waldmeier . <p> While most industry associations do n't hold such events , which bring members to Ottawa for a series of meetings with public office holders , when the House is n't sitting , Waldmeier said MAC still does , with the focus for November 's Hill Day more so on government officials rather than politicians . <p> The issues discussed in these meetings were pretty diverse considering the number of people involved , she said , with some of the more prominent themes including the role mining plays in the move to a " low-carbon future , " the species at risk and impact assessment laws , and " Indigenous collaboration . " <p> MAC will host another Hill Day targeting a " political audience " in May , Waldmeier said , and then , return next November . <p> These communications were held with officials from Global Affairs Canada , Environment and Climate Change Canada , Fisheries and Oceans Canada , Natural Resources Canada , the Prime Minister 's Office , Crown-Indigenous Relations and Northern Affairs Canada , Transport Canada , Finance Canada , Employment and Social Development Canada , Privy Council Office and Export Development Canada , which provides loans , advice and other services to companies seeking international markets for their goods . <p> MAC also communicated with Senator Pat Duncan . <p> All 17 of ZYUS 's filings in November were submitted to the consultant lobbyist registered to advocate on the company 's behalf -- Douglas Richardson of McKercher LLP . The topics of all the communication reports were identical : economic development , health , research and development , science and technology , and industry . The meetings were held with officials from Public Services and Procurement Canada , Public Safety Canada and Health Canada . <p> According to Richardson 's filing , he 's lobbying to " assist in securing ZYUS 's production license with Health Canada for which ZYUS is finalizing an ethanol extraction facility for the purpose of producing cannabis based oils for medical applications , particularly high Cannabidiol ( CBD ) oils . " <p> In a statement provided to iPolitics , ZYUS CEO Brent Zettl said the company has received a Health Canada processing licence , which will allow it to " proceed with the extraction and development of phyto-therapeutics as medical solutions , including conducting scientific research that will potentially benefit patients , health care practitioners and policy-makers . " <p> " Our lobbying efforts are in support of this aim , and we hope to collaborate with the government on the development of innovative cannabinoid and protein-based formulations , maintaining Canada 's position as a leader in health-care policies and scientific advancements , " he added . <p> Collectively , there were 789 communication reports filed in November , an over 100 per cent increase from the 393 filed in October . Lobbying activity was cooled by the most recent federal election , which concluded on Oct. 21.
