Visit MyNorthMyHome.ca <p> Good evening , readers . <p> The Lead <p> The Canadian economy in November posted its biggest monthly job loss since the 2000s financial crisis , leading the unemployment rate to push closer to six per cent . <p> Statistics Canada said Friday the economy lost 71,200 jobs last month and the unemployment rate rose four-tenths of a percentage point to 5.9 per cent , its highest point since August 2018 when it hit six per cent . <p> Economists on average had expected a gain of 10,000 jobs and the unemployment rate to hold steady at 5.5 per cent , according to financial markets data firm Refinitiv . <p> The loss in jobs came in both full-time and part-time employment . The number of full-time jobs fell by 38,400 , while part-time employment fell by 32,800 . <p> Parliamentarians spent much of Friday debating the speech from the throne . <p> The speech , which was delivered by Governor General Julie Payette in the Senate Thursday , outlined the governments ' priorities for the 43rd Parliament with nods to lower taxes , a pharmacare system , and plans to tackle climate change . All three opposition leaders took issue with the speech , with some introducing amendments so the speech would better reflect their party 's priorities . <p> Conservative leader Andrew Scheer said the speech showed Prime Minister Justin Trudeau " has n't learned a thing " since the Liberals were reduced to a minority government in October 's election . <p> Alberta 's new Technology Innovation and Emissions Reduction levy will go into effect at the beginning of next year , keeping a $30 per tonne price on emissions from industrial sectors such as oil and gas , electricity , cement and agriculture . Oil sands facilities , for example , will be subject to the levy . <p> The move will prevent Ottawa 's output-based carbon pricing system from being slapped on Alberta . <p> Select high school teachers and education workers across Ontario are set to hold a one-day strike next Wednesday , amid fruitless negotiations with the provincial government that saw a one-day walkout earlier this week . <p> The Ontario Secondary School Teachers ' Federation announced the looming job action via email on Friday afternoon , minutes after provincial Education Minister Stephen Lecce pre-empted the announcement with a press conference at Queen 's Park , saying the government had heard murmurs of further job action . <p> The Brant Haldimand Norfolk Catholic , Grand Erie , Hastings and Prince Edward , Near North , Rainy River , Simcoe County , Simcoe Muskoka Catholic , Toronto and Trillium Lakelands District School Boards will each take part in a full withdrawal of services , along with colleagues employed by the Conseil scolaire de district catholoque MonAvenir , Conseil scolaire Viamonde , and Conseil scolaire des ecoles publiques de l'Est de l'Ontario . <p> The Bank of Canada says its governor Stephen Poloz will not seek a second term when his seven-year term expires next year . <p> Poloz , in a statement , said the role of Bank of Canada governor has been his dream job . <p> " My time as Governor has been the most fulfilling of my long career , " he said in a statement Friday . <p> " During my term , the bank has created the conditions for steady economic growth , low unemployment , and inflation close to target through very challenging times . " <p> Before becoming head of Canada 's central bank in 2013 , Poloz was chief executive of Export Development Canada . He replaced Mark Carney , who left the Bank of Canada to become governor of the Bank of England . Poloz 's term expires on June 2 , 2020 . <p> The federal government is lacking safeguards against money laundering in Ontario 's horse racing industry , the province 's auditor general has found . <p> " For a sector that is vulnerable to money laundering , the horse racing industry in Canada is not accountable to any regulatory body to monitor its operations for this type of crime , " Ontario Auditor General Bonnie Lysyk noted in her annual report , released this week . Lysyk offered the contrast of the Proceeds of Crime ( Money Laundering ) and Terrorist Financing Act in her report , which was noted to have covered the casino sector since 2007 . <p> Under that particular act , all casinos operating in Canada are required to report transactions that exceed $10,000 , as well as any transactions deemed suspicious , to the Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of Canada . <p> The RCMP says a 22-year-old man from southern Ontario who once stood trial on terrorism-related charges in Turkey is now facing two similar counts here in Canada . <p> Ikar Mao , of Guelph , Ont. , was previously arrested in Turkey on charges related to alleged affiliation with Islamic State militants , though he was not convicted on those charges and returned to Canada in October . <p> However , the man was arrested this morning at an RCMP detachment located near Toronto 's Pearson International Airport . He has been charged with participating in the activities of a terrorist group and leaving Canada to take part in terrorist group activity , the Canadian Press reports . <p> The person suspected of a fatal shooting at a Florida Naval station on Friday was an aviation officer in the Saudi Air Force , according to U.S. officials , with the FBI and other authorities investigating the incident to determine if it was terrorism-related . <p> The officials , who spoke on condition of anonymity to discuss an ongoing investigation , said the suspect was a second lieutenant attending the aviation school at the base . Military from around the globe attend the Naval Air Station in Pensacola for flight training . The attack at the Florida facility left four people dead , including the assailant , and multiple people wounded .
