Visit MyNorthMyHome.ca <p> Good evening , readers . <p> The Lead <p> The Trudeau government recommitted to campaign pledges to lower taxes , bring in a pharmacare system and achieve net-zero emissions by 2050 , in a largely unsurprising throne speech on Thursday to kick-off the 43rd Parliament . <p> In the speech , delivered by Governor General Julie Payette in the Senate , the governing Liberals hit familiar notes about the urgency of fighting climate change , supporting the country 's " middle class " and pursuing reconciliation with Indigenous peoples . <p> On climate , the Liberals committed in the speech , as the party had in the fall campaign , to make energy efficient homes more affordable , incentivize the purchase of zero-emission vehicles , support the development of clean technology businesses and make " clean , affordable power available in every Canadian community . " <p> Specifically , the speech echoed campaign pledges to protect 25 per cent of Canada 's land and 25 per cent of the country 's ocean mass , to further reduce plastic pollution and to plant two billion trees . <p> Unlike the last Parliament , where the Liberals held a majority of seats in the House and chamber business was much more predictable , Rota will preside over a minority House set to be full of surprises and new challenges . <p> Canada still has a shortage of organ donations despite seeing a 33 per cent increase in transplant procedures in 2018 , according to the latest numbers from the Canadian Institute for Health Information . <p> At the end of 2018 , there were 4,351 people on wait-lists for organ transplants , including 2,890 deemed active and another 1,461 on hold , meaning they can not receive a transplant for medical or other reasons for a short period of time , numbers from the Canadian Organ Replacement Register ( CORR ) show . CORR is a pan-Canadian information system for organ failure in Canada . <p> The U.S. House is pressing forward to draft articles of impeachment against President Donald Trump for allegedly pressuring Ukraine to investigate a political rival , Speaker Nancy Pelosi announced Thursday . Pelosi said the president violated the Constitution , undermined U.S. national security and is jeopardizing the integrity of U.S. elections . <p> " Our democracy is what is at stake , " Pelosi said . " The president leaves us no choice but to act . " ( Associated Press ) <p> China 's growing economic strength is seen by many of its own residents as positive , but the country 's rise as a global superpower is not making it popular with neighbours in the Asia-Pacific region , a survey released on Thursday suggests . <p> China 's evolution from an economic backwater to the world 's second largest economy is making neighbours in the Asia-Pacific nervous , according to the survey of nearly 39,000 people in 34 countries by the Pew Research Centre . It also showed that the world had an overwhelmingly negative view of Chinese President Xi Jinping .
