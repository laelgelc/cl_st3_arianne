Brendan McGrath , director of corporate risk management at Western Union , said global geopolitical tensions have caused uncertainty in the international market causing businesses to turn inward . <p> " When it comes to business-making decisions about going across border , finding suppliers and whatnot in other countries , politics and geopolitical issues have a big impact , " he said . " It 's not just about cost . " <p> Do managers of a provincial government pension plan in B.C. have to collect tax on their management fees to pay to Ottawa ? <p> The Supreme Court of Canada gave a mixed answer to that question Friday . Constitutionally , the pension fund managers do not , but under a separate reciprocal tax agreement between the province and the federal government , they do . <p> In a 6-1 decision , the top court settled the constitutional question raised in this case -- the private pension management firm , because it is a Crown corporation , does not have to collect tax for the federal government because it has immunity from taxation granted to provinces by the Constitution Act of 1867 . <p> U.S. President Donald Trump said on Friday that the U.S. has canceled plans to impose new tariffs on $160 billion worth of Chinese imports on Sunday as part of a modest interim agreement to de-escalate a 17-month trade war between the world 's two biggest economies . The U.S. is also reducing existing import taxes on about $112 billion in Chinese goods from 15 to 7.5 per cent .
