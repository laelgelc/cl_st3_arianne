<h> The Drilldown : Syncrude oilsands reduces crude production by 1.6 million barrels <p> The Canadian Syncrude oilsands have been experiencing operational issues which have led the company to reduce its December crude production by 1.6 million barrels , according to Reuters . The technical problems have yet to be specified . <p> Although Syncrude has the ability to produce up to 360,000 barrels per day , it has a history with operational complications . In October , the company reduced its oil sales by 1.4 million barrels after the maintenance to the factory was extended . <p> In Canada <p> The Canada-Newfoundland and Labrador Offshore Petroleum Board ( C-NLOPB ) had its inaugural spill prevention and response forum this week . A main topic of discussion was a required review of response , safety and prevention when it comes to spills in the offshore oil and gas sector . <p> C-NLOPB CEO Scott Tessier contacted industry operators at the beginning of October where he spoke of the need to improve environmental and safety performances by developing an action plan for the offshore industry , which the Canadian Association of Petroleum Producers agreed to . <p> " The forum provided an opportunity for regulators , governments and those ' on the water ' to undertake a collective review of lessons learned from spills in our offshore between April 2018 and August 2019 , " stated C-NLOPB chief conservation officer Jeff O'Keefe , reported the St. John 's Telegram . <p> Meanwhile , Ontario Auditor-General Bonnie Lysyk released a report on Wednesday that says the Progressive Conservative government is planning on using " double counting " and " overestimates " when it comes to developing a provincial climate change plan . <p> Findings in the report show that 2030 target of getting 30 per cent below 2005 emission levels will not be achieved with the province 's current plan . The provincial government also spent $4 million on an advertising campaign that was anti-carbon tax , the report showed , reported The Globe and Mail . <p> Internationally <p> Saudi Aramco announced that its IPO will be priced at 32 riyals , or $8.53 ( U.S. ) per share , meaning that Aramco will surpass Apple as the most valuable firm in the world , with a market value of $1.7 trillion , reported Reuters . <p> In other news , a riverbank contaminated with uranium has reportedly collapsed into the Detroit Diver . The site , which is now owned by Detroit Bulk Storage , is known as the former Revere Copper and Brass site . This area was flagged as contaminated by the U.S. Department of Energy and Environmental Protection Agency in the 1940s , reported The National Post .
