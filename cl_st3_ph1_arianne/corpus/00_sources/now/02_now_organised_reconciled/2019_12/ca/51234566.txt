<h> Singh says NDP will push Liberals on more support for cities <p> NDP leader Jagmeet Singh says his party will push the minority Liberal government to act in offering more help to municipalities fighting climate change and addressing the shortage of affordable housing . <p> In a keynote speech on Wednesday to more than 100 municipal leaders gathered in Ottawa , Singh said he will challenge the Liberals , alongside municipalities , to deliver more for cities . <p> " What we have in front of us is a Liberal government that will not act unless pushed at , " Singh told the gathering of Federation of Canadian Municipalities members . <p> " We have an incredible opportunity in the minority government . There is a great chance for us to be able to advance some of the things that people need . " <p> He said municipalities are a pivotal ally in fighting climate change , whether in reducing emissions by building more public transit or protecting its infrastructure from climate warming-induced effects . <p> Singh pointed to expanding public transit , retrofits and climate change mitigation as three areas where he will push Ottawa for more funding for cities . <p> " The challenges that our country faces right now , municipalities stand on the front lines , " he said . " That you are at the front lines of the challenges and impacts of the climate crisis . " <p> He also said Ottawa needs to beef up its efforts to expand access to affordable housing and that the current plan put forward by the Liberals will not work . He identified the issue is one of the NDP 's top six priorities . <p> " We know that the approach to backfilling programs being phased out is wholly inadequate as a way to solve the housing crisis , " he said . " What we need to do is massively fill in homes , and that means partnering directly with the federal government . " <p> The Liberal government unveiled a multibillion dollar , 10-year national housing strategy in 2017 , although critics say the level of funding has not significantly changed , and in some areas , has actually declined . <p> Singh also said he will unveil his roster of parliamentary critics on Thursday . He told the audience that B.C. MP Taylor Bachrach will be the NDP 's point person for infrastructure and communities . He was formerly the mayor of Smithers , B.C. , located in the riding previously held by Nathan Cullen .
