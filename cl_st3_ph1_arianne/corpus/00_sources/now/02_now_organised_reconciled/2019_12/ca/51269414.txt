<p> Travel writer Bob Boughner writes there 's no reason to fear the snow . Ontario offers an abundance of outdoor winter opportunities , including sledding and skating . Taken earlier this year in Sudbury , this photograph shows a father and son enjoying the white stuff . File photo/Postmedia News <p> Bring on the snow ! Are you excited for winter and all the new seasonal activities it brings ? <p> You can go sledding , skating , skiing or just have a low-key adventure seeing all the twinkling lights . If you are looking to shake things up and try something new , there are plenty of secret places in Ontario to enjoy once the province turns into a winter wonderland . <p> This winter , surprise your favourite person and discover somewhere new in Ontario together . There are plenty of stunning sights and experiences to be had , such as skating along a path through a forest illuminated by tiki torches or you could go for a hike to discover a magnificent frozen waterfall . <p> Meanwhile , if you 're looking for more relaxing ideas , the two of you could book a stay in a tiny cabin in the woods or visit an icewine festival to toast the season over a glass of sweet vino . <p> A favourite of mine is walking along a magical illuminated path at Blue Mountain . The lights dance to music along the one-kilometre path . <p> Another suggestion is taking an alpaca for a walk in the snow at Kerwood or relax at a tiny cabin in the woods at Bayfield . <p> You might want to snowshoe across a suspension bridge at Blue Mountains or dine in an igloo on Bloor Street in Toronto . At Guild Park and Gardens in Scarborough , you can visit a sculpture park , an incredible Greek-inspired theatre along with other ornate sculptures . Multiple locations in Niagara are featuring ice wine festivals in January . <p> Some of the most iconic rock bands are bringing the " stadium tour " to Comerica Park in Detroit next summer . The concert date is Aug. 20 . Tickets for the tour , featuring Def Leppard , Motley Crue , Joan Jett &amp; The Blackhearts and Poison are now on sale . <p> Ever wanted to stay in a hotel room shaped like a human anus ? <p> The very well-named Hotel CasAnus is a hotel shaped like a human colon and is located near Antwerp . <p> It 's red , veiny , totally tubular and fully furnished with a double bed , kitchen table , lots of windows and of course working plumbing . <p> CasAnus features prettier surroundings than most butts probably see . <p> The nightly cost is US$140 , which includes breakfast and admission to the nearby art park 's museum . <p> Everyone agrees that the butt room could do with a wipe and one guest noted there were ants in the shower . <p> A new vehicle extravaganza , the Toronto RV Show and Sale Jan. 16-19 is one of the biggest events of the year . Explore the latest RV trends and features and get the low-down on the latest models . The deals are great and if you order at the show your new RV will be ready n time to hit the road when the first signs of spring appear . Check out the latest leisure gear and start planning your next hiking , canoeing , bouldering , biking , fishing , birdwatching and sightseeing adventure . <p> A U.K.-based landscape photographer recently captured a stunning shot of a fogbow , a white rainbow over a moor in western Scotland . A fogbow is a rainbow that has a white band that appears in fog and is fringed with red on the outside and blue on the inside . It only lasted five minutes . <p> According to NASA the fogbow 's relative lack of colours is caused by smaller water drops than those of coloured rainbows . <p> According to Bookvip.com you can book a five-night stay in luxury accommodation in Puerto Vallarta Palace Beach for $99 . It 's worth checking out . <p> Dim lights and muted sound are one hallmark of Bright Lights Windsor this month as the city offers sensory-friendly experiences on Monday evenings . In partnership with Autism Ontario Windsor-Essex , strobe elements will be paused Mondays from 5:30 p.m. to 7 p.m. for an accommodating environment to enjoy the lights . Light-dulling glasses are available for attendees to wear . There is also a new " Around the World " zone with Ramadan , Diwali and Chinese decorations . <p> More than 100,000 people attended Bright Lights Windsor last year . <p> So who is right ? The long-established Farmer 's Almanac is forecasting a " polar coaster " winter with below normal temperatures while Environment Canada predicts the winter will be milder all across Canada including Southwestern Ontario . <p> So despite forecasts like the Farmer 's Almanac , Environment Canada is predicting that Canadians nationwide can expect a warmer-than-normal winter . <p> Climate change is one possible explanation for a warmer winter . Warmer oceans and less ice in the arctic -- two effects of climate change -- are big factors in the warmer-than-average weather forecast . <p> The forecast also calls for lake effect snowstorms well into February and March , the period when the lakes are usually frozen over . <p> The Downtown Chatham Centre is celebrating local talent and local businesses each Saturday in December . Saturday 's featured artist is DaniLou of Pain Court , a talented young and upcoming singer . She will perform in the food court from 4 p.m. to 5 p.m . Admission is free . Santa will also be in attendance and the mall is offering free hot chocolate with marshmallows . <p> Celebrate this festive season at the Royal Ontario Museum in Toronto . From Dec. 26 to Jan. 5 discover over 30,000 species that feed on blood , explore an extraordinary collection of classic horror and sci-fi movie posters and catch a glimpse into the world 's most stunning wildlife photography . There 's a lot for families and friends of all ages to explore at the museum . <p> Featured exhibitions include Bloodsuckers : Legends to Leeches and Wildlife Photographer of the year 2019 . <p> More than 100 antique snowmobiles are set to cross the Mackinac Bridge today during the fourth annual Snowmobile the Mac . What started with just 30 sleds in 2016 has grown into a yearly highlight for the region . Sleds must be at least 25 years old or older to participate and must be equipped with wheel kits to keep treads off the pavement of the bridge . <p> The Chatham Armoury Christmas market is slated for Dec. 16-22 and the Chatham Armoury Light Show will take place Dec. 20-26 . A special live light show is planned for Dec. 20-22 at 7 p.m . <p> Tilbury 's First Night New Year 's Eve celebration is scheduled for Dec. 31 and the Chatham Concert Band 's Christmas Concert at Kiwanis Theatre in Chatham will take place Dec. 18 . <p> Readers are invited to submit their questions or suggestions on travel close to home or far away to travel writer Bob Boughner at bobgboughner@gmail.com
