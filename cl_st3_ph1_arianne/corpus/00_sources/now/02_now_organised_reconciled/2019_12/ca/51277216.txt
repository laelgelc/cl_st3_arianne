<p> The U.K. is expecting Alberta Premier Jason Kenney in London this week , where he plans to " reassert investment confidence in Alberta 's economy " according to a news release . During this three-day trip , Kenney will seek to " underscore the province 's reputation as one of the world 's most stable , secure and environmentally responsible energy suppliers , " wrote the Calgary Herald . <p> According to the news release , Kenney is expected to meet with Mark Carney , the governor of the Bank of England , who was more recently given the role of UN envoy on climate change . This trip will cost $34,000 for Kenney and his special adviser David Knight Legg . <p> In Canada <p> Today is the beginning of a three-day hearing at Canada 's Federal Court of Appeal in Vancouver , where the Tsleil-Waututh Nation , the Coldwater Indian band and the Squamish Nation , along with others , will bring forward their concerns about the Trans Mountain oil pipeline expansion ( TMX ) , reported The Calgary Herald . <p> The TMX would almost triple the pipeline 's capacity , allowing 890,000 barrels of oil to be transported per day , but has continued to face serious opposition from Indigenous groups and environmental activists since it was proposed in 2013 . <p> According to the manager of market economics at the Canadian Association of Petroleum Producers , Mark Pinney , uncertainty around the expansion project has become more prominent due to legal challenges . <p> In other news , Canada 's Equinox Gold Corp announced on Monday that it will be purchasing its rival company Leagold Mining Corp for $769.3 million . There is a no-premium consideration of $2.70 per share in this purchase . Leagold will own 45 per cent of the company as a whole and receive 0.331 of every Equinox share for every share it owns , reported The Globe and Mail . <p> Internationally <p> Two weeks of discussion on climate mitigation between members of the UN ended on Sunday without a clear agreement on how to regulate global carbon markets . Together , nations decided that it would be better not to develop a carbon market program instead of developing one that was weak and could create problems within the existing regional carbon mechanisms . <p> " Thankfully , the weak rules on a market-based mechanism , promoted by Brazil and Australia , that would have undermined efforts to reduce emissions , have been shelved , " said the director of Power Shift Africa , Mohamed Adow , reported CBC News .
