<p> Greta Thunberg , the teen activist from Sweden who has urged immediate action to address climate change , said she was surprised and honoured Wednesday to be named Time magazine 's Person of the Year for 2019 . <p> Thunberg , 16 , was lauded by Time for starting an environmental campaign in August 2018 that became a global movement , initially skipping school and camping out in front of the Swedish parliament to demand action . <p> " In the 16 months since , she has addressed heads of state at the UN , met with the Pope , sparred with the president of the United States and inspired four million people to join the global climate strike on Sept. 20 , 2019 , in what was the largest climate demonstration in human history , " the magazine said . <p> " Margaret Atwood compared her to Joan of Arc . After noticing a hundredfold increase in its usage , lexicographers at Collins Dictionary named Thunberg 's pioneering idea , climate strike , the word of the year , " Time said . <p> Thunberg , who turns 17 in January , is in Madrid at a United Nations climate summit , where world leaders are wrangling over how to implement a 2015 Paris agreement designed to avert potentially catastrophic global warming . <h> ' What we have done , we have done together ' <p> As she left the conference , Thunberg told The Associated Press that she was " a bit surprised " at the recognition . <p> " I could never have imagined anything like that happening , " she said in a phone interview . <p> " I 'm of course , very grateful for that , very honoured , " Thunberg said , but added : " It should be everyone in the Fridays for Future movement because what we have done , we have done together . " <p> Thunberg said she hoped the message being pushed by her and other activists -- that governments need to drastically increase their efforts to combat climate change -- is finally getting through . The " Greta effect " has already been linked to a rise in support for environmental parties in Europe . <p> But she insisted that the media should also pay attention to other activists , particularly Indigenous people , whom she said " are hit hardest by the climate and environmental crisis . " <p> Former U.S. vice-president Al Gore , a longtime environmentalist , said the magazine made a " brilliant choice " in choosing the reluctant celebrity . <p> " Greta embodies the moral authority of the youth activist movement demanding that we act immediately to solve the climate crisis . She is an inspiration to me and to people across the world . " <p> WATCH : Greta Thunberg speaking today in Madrid <p> Speaking at a climate conference in Madrid after being named Time magazine 's Person of the Year , Greta Thunberg takes a different tack in calling out politicians and CEOs for their lack of climate action . 3:01 <p> In a speech before the Time honour was announced , she was typically blunt in her assessment of politicians ' efforts . <p> " It seems to have turned into some kind of opportunity for countries to negotiate loopholes and to avoid raising their ambition , " she said on stage , drawing applause from an audience that included dozens of supporters . <p> " I 'm sure that if people heard what was going on and what was said ... during these meetings , they would be outraged . " <p> In her speech , Thunberg cited scientific reports showing that national pledges to reduce planet-warming greenhouse gas emissions are n't enough to meet the ambitious goal set in the 2015 Paris climate accord of keeping temperatures from rising by more than 1.5 C by the end of the century . <p> " This is not leading , this is misleading , " she told officials , adding that " every fraction of a degree matters . " Thunberg said the youth climate movement , which has staged repeated worldwide protests attended by hundreds of thousands of people , has managed to spread awareness about the need to urgently reduce emissions and help those already affected by global warming . <p> " To get in a sense of urgency in the conversation that is very needed right now to be able to move forward , " she said . " That , I think , is our biggest success . " <h> Praise and pans from the powerful <p> Thunberg 's uncompromising stance has brought her into confrontation with some of the world 's most powerful people . <p> One of them is U.S. President Donald Trump , another finalist for Time 's Person of the Year . <p> A video of Thunberg giving him what media described as a " death stare " at a UN climate summit in New York in September went viral on social media . <p> Trump has questioned climate science and has challenged every major U.S. regulation aimed at fighting climate change . <p> During a speech at around the same time , Thunberg bristled with anger . <p> " This is all wrong . I should n't be up here . I should be back in school on the other side of the ocean , yet you all come to us young people for hope . How dare you ? " she told delegates . <p> " You have stolen my dreams and my childhood with your empty words . " <p> After the speech , Trump derisively tweeted she " seems like a very happy young girl looking forward to a bright and wonderful future . " <p> WATCH : Thunberg , in her own words , at the Montreal climate march <p> The Swedish teen addressed an estimated half a million demonstrators in Montreal on Friday . 14:50 <p> Brazilian President Jair Bolsonaro this week called Thunberg a " brat " after she criticized mounting violence against Indigenous people in which two Amazon tribesmen were shot dead . Bolsonaro used " pirralha , " the Portuguese word for " brat , " prompting Thunberg to change her Twitter bio to " Pirralha . " <p> Asked by AP about her immediate plans , Thunberg said : " I would like to be left alone . " <p> But before heading home to Sweden , to spend Christmas with her family and dogs , she will attend a climate protest Friday in Turin , Italy . <p> Other finalists for Time 's Person of the Year were : <p> U.S. House Speaker Nancy Pelosi . <p> The anonymous whistleblower who helped spur an investigation into Trump 's dealings with Ukraine . <p> The Hong Kong protesters who have agitated for democratic reforms and against a controversial extradition bill spearheaded by mainland China . <p> Hong Kong 's Joshua Wong , who has faced prison time related to his activism there . <p> " You inspire me and many people across the world ...
