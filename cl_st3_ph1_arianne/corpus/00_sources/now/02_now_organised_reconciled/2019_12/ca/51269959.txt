<p> Navdeep Bains , who was given the slightly revised title of innovation , science and industry in last month 's cabinet shuffle , will be responsible for engaging with business stakeholders across the country and championing their success . <p> " ( You ) will continue to help Canadian businesses innovate and grow so that they can create good quality jobs and wealth for Canadians , " the mandate letter said . <p> Bains has held his ministerial portfolio since the start of the Trudeau government , though , in last month 's cabinet shuffle , responsibility for the six regional economic development agencies was shifted to new Economic Development and Official Languages Minister M ? lanie Joly . <p> Mandate letters set out the priorities and objectives for cabinet ministers . They were first released publicly when Trudeau ushered in his first cabinet in November 2015 . <p> Minister Bains was asked to use " all available instruments " to reduce the average cost of cellular phone bills in Canada by 25 per cent . <p> " You will work with telecom companies and expand mobile virtual network operators ( MVNO ) in the market , " the letter said . " If within two years this price target is not achieved , you can expand MVNO qualifying rules and the Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission mandate on affordable pricing . " <p> Bains will also create new regulations for large digital companies to protect people 's personal data and encourage competition in the digital marketplace , in collaboration with the Minister of Canadian Heritage , Steven Guilbeault . The minister will continue development on the ethical use of data and digital tools like artificial intelligence for better government . <p> Bains and Guilbeault will team up again to review the Copyright Act . The House industry committee completed its review of the act and tabled it in the House in June , but the government has yet to respond to it as an election was called shortly after . The review included recommendations like studying the implementation of artists ' resale rights , which give artists a percentage of the sale every time their work is resold , and creating an Indigenous art registry . <p> Under the science part of the file , Bains has been charged with overseeing the Feds ' relationship with the post-secondary research community , integrating " scientific considerations " into government decisions , and transforming science into commercial products and opening new markets and supporting the Chief Science Advisor . <p> Bains has also been tasked with supporting Canadian industries like the automotive , aerospace and agri-food fields in their transition to a low-carbon economy , working with colleagues to deliver high-speed internet to 100 per cent of Canadian homes and businesses by 2030 , and introducing legislation by the end of 2020 to ensure content providers offer meaningful levels of Canadian content and contribute to the creation of Canadian content in both French and English . <p> Some other highlights include : <p> Supporting innovation ecosystems across the country , particularly those based on partnerships between businesses and post-secondary institutions , to support job creation , technology adoption , investment and scaleup . <p> Work with Guilbeault to modernize the Broadcasting Act and the Telecommunications Act , examining how best to support Canadian content in English and French and ensure quality affordable internet , mobile and media access . <p> Work with the Minister of Natural Resources and the Minister of Environment and Climate Change to position Canada as a global leader in clean technology . <p> In partnership with industry and communities , work with the Minister of Natural Resources to install up to 5,000 additional charging stations . <p> Work with the Minister of Justice and Attorney General of Canada and the Minister of Canadian Heritage to advance Canada 's Digital Charter and enhanced powers for the Privacy Commissioner , in order to establish a new set of online rights . <p> Prepare for the long-form census in 2021 , including the collection and analysis of disaggregated data , as the minister responsible for Statistics Canada .
