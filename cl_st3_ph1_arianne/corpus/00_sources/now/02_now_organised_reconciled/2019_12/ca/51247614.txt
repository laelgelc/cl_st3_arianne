<h> Vital Signs report examines quality of life in Lennox and Addington <p> Bob Childs is the executive director for the Community Foundation for Lennox and Addington , which officially released its Vital Signs report for Lennox and Addington County in Napanee on Tuesday . ( Meghan Balogh/The Whig-Standard ) jpg , KI <p> The Community Foundation for Lennox and Addington has released its latest Vital Signs report for the county . <p> Using the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals as a framework , the Vital Signs report examines quality of life factors in Lennox and Addington County through statistical data and stories collected in focus groups . <p> That information was organized following a multi-sector conversation to identify priorities for the region . <p> The report was officially released at a public event in Napanee on Tuesday . <p> " This is our first report using the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals , which ultimately is aiming to create substantial progress towards those goals by 2030 , " Bob Childs , executive director for the CFFLA , said . <p> In 2015 , Canada joined 192 other nations in adopting the Agenda for Sustainable Development at the United Nations General Assembly . That agenda identifies 17 goals to be achieved by 2030 , towards a " better and more sustainable future for all . " <p> Those 17 goals explore concepts of human health , equality , justice and sustainability , as well as environmental health . <p> Childs said the foundation decided early on to use the Sustainable Development Goals from the United Nations as the lens through which to examine the quality of life in the Lennox and Addington County region , in keeping with recommendations from Community Foundations of Canada , which is encouraging its 191 member community foundations to use this approach in producing vital signs reports . <p> " We 're early on in the process of adopting this model for doing vital signs projects , " Childs explained . " We 're really excited about this project as a starting point , and I think it needs to be emphasized that it is a work in progress . " <p> In preparing the report , from January to July in 2019 , the project team examined national , provincial and local statistics from sources such as Statistics Canada , reports from local conservation authorities , and data compiled during the Prince Edward Lennox and Addington Social Services Homeless Enumeration Report , created in 2019 . <p> They also held five focus groups with individuals and representatives from area businesses , agencies and organizations to examine five areas : the environment ; poverty , hunger and sustainable communities ; good health and well-being ; decent work and economy growth , industry , innovation and infrastructure ; and gender equality , reduced inequalities and peace , justice and strong institutions . <p> Those focus groups , and a subsequent multi-sector meeting , identified three areas of focus for Lennox and Addington County to prioritize . Those include good health and well-being , poverty reduction and climate action . <p> " The community well-being end is very important , " Childs said . " If we look at community health and well-being , and poverty , the second goal , they dovetail together . So much of the good health and well-being and poverty is relating to multiple issues that people face . It might be related to poor health , which has put them into a situation of economic distress , it can be mental health issues that they 're grappling with , it might be addiction issues or gambling issues . " <p> Childs said the report examines some of the issues that local social service agencies are dealing with . <p> " Because there 's a case load there , obviously there 's more work to be done , " he said . " All of the people at our health and wellness roundtable discussion at the end referred to the importance of finding new connections to help people more efficiently and more effectively . <p> The third priority identified was climate action , and that 's an area that refers to " life and living changes , " Childs said . <p> He pointed out that Lennox and Addington County is home to a " highly mobile workforce , " with people who commute outside the region for work , often as the sole occupant of a vehicle . <p> " Some lifestyle changes , like carpooling , would be important . Working perhaps with our neighbouring municipalities and looking at an expansion of public transit would have an impact on climate change . And , of course , our work with municipalities to influence the building code can also have an impact on that as well . We do have a lot of older housing stock . We have to make sure that the new housing stock is not only affordable but is also built with climate in mind . There 's lots of directions forward . " <p> The report tests real-life stories against statistical data from the community . <p> " The statistics are one thing , but the lived experience and the stories behind the statistics are far more important to policymakers and to the community at large , in terms of them deciding on whether or not they want to get involved on a particular issue , " Childs said . <p> While the community foundation does n't directly work to implement changes based on the report , it is hoped it will inspire action by individuals , agencies , organizations and municipalities . <p> " At the community foundation , we ca n't drive that process . We can only hope that people respond to our report and get involved , " Childs said . <p> The report does help the foundation decide how to dole out the more than $1 million in grants and bursaries it administers in the course of a year . <p> " It helps us gain an understand of our community , " Childs said . " Our role in the community is to grant the community and improve the lives of all . It really helps us set priorities in that respect . But because we 're working with a lot of social capital groups , charities , not-for-profits , it makes sense for us to share that information . Everyone needs a better understanding of the community if we 're going to find the networking opportunities to work together . " <p> Going forward , the foundation will create a new Vital Signs report every two years . <p> " There 's good information here , but we should be able to , as a group of communities , measure progress towards these outcomes over the next 10 years , as we move forward on a two-year cycle of doing this project , " Childs said .
