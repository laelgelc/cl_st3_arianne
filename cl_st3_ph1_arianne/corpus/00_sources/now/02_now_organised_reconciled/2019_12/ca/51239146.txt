<h> Why residents should cheer reelection of Sav Dhaliwal and Linda Buchanan to Metro Vancouver 's top posts <p> Lower Mainland residents who fret about the planet 's future can take comfort in what happened at Metro Vancouver on Friday ( November 29 ) . <p> That 's because two sensible " greenies " , Sav Dhaliwal and Linda Buchanan , were acclaimed for a second term as chair and vice chair of the regional government 's board of directors . <p> Coincidentally , Dhaliwal and Buchanan received a second mandate on the same day that Sustainabiliteens Vancouver were holding a #FridaysforFuture climate strike . <p> The teen climate activists set up a used-clothing swap on Granville Street to discourage consumers from buying fast-fashion items on Black Friday . <p> With Dhaliwal and Buchanan at the helm , the regional government has shone a spotlight on the harmful impact of textile production through its Think Thrice About Your Clothes campaign . <p> It urges residents to reduce , repair , and reuse clothing to curtail the large volume of textiles going to landfills . <p> " On average , we buy three times more clothes than we did in the 80s , and it is estimated that one garbage truck full of clothing is landfilled globally every second , " the campaign states on its website . " Cheaper clothing , fast fashion trends , and an overall increase in consumption is resulting in more and more clothing waste ! Did you know that in Metro Vancouver we threw away 44 million pounds of clothing last year ? " <p> " It has been estimated that there are 20 new garments manufactured per person each year and we are buying 60% more than we were in 2000 , " the researchers wrote . " Each garment is worn less before being disposed of and this shorter lifespan means higher relative manufacturing emissions . " <h> Burnaby and City of North Van focus on emissions <p> A year ago , the Straight interviewed Dhaliwal , a Burnaby councillor , about his objectives as Metro Vancouver 's chair . <p> He responded that one of his aims was to work cooperatively with other directors to reduce greenhouse gas emissions by adopting best practices from other jurisdictions . <p> " It 's part of our regional-growth-strategy goal , but the local governments have , obviously , been very busy on other things , " Dhaliwal said at the time . " We have n't really picked up on a full set of strategies and an action plan to respond to climate change . " <p> The City of Burnaby has been an environmental leader in Metro Vancouver . Its environmental sustainability strategy sets out a long-term vision based on the collection of more than 8,000 ideas from the community . <p> The strategy includes a detailed community energy and emissions plan highlighting such things as per-person emissions in the city and the various sources . There are plans for addressing these issues . <p> Buchanan is mayor of the City of North Vancouver , which is one of the region 's greenest municipalities . <p> Its district-energy system , the Lonsdale Energy Corp. , has been a model across Canada for reducing a municipality 's carbon footprint . <p> The City of North Van 's magnificent library in the Central Lonsdale area won a LEED Gold certificate from the Canadian Green Building Council . <p> Its land-use planning has concentrated development , including by being among the first to allow laneway houses and secondary suites .
