<p> Shared Services Canada is the federal agency responsible for consolidated IT services . Created by the former Conservative government in 2011 , it has drawn criticism for its slow progress in completing major projects and occasionally fractious relationships with other departments . <p> The agency told iPolitics the funding would be used to update IT infrastructure to " modern standards , " including upgrading data centre services , establishing " cloud enterprise services " and moving applications over to a " state-of-the-art enterprise data centre or cloud , or a mix of the two . " <p> " The funds will also be used to upgrade hardware , including new data centre services , secure cloud services , vendor contracts , and upgrading existing IT infrastructure , to ensure government departments are better able to serve Canadians and fulfill their mandates , " the agency said in a statement . <p> Shared Services also noted that its cloud program " supports the establishment and adoption of cloud services " within the federal government , and " provides departments with new modern and flexible alternatives to make more efficient use of information technology , while protecting the security and privacy of Canadians . " <p> Collectively , the government is seeking Parliament 's approval of $4.9 billion in spending , with $676.9 million going to benefits and support programs offered by Veterans Affairs and $466.7 million to the Treasury Board Secretariat to cover the cost of negotiated salary adjustments stemming from collective bargaining agreements reached with public service unions between April 1 and Oct. 18 , 2019 . <p> The Liberals also earmarked $296 million to the Department of Foreign Affairs , Trade and Development to help developing countries tackle climate change . It stems from a November 2015 pledge by Canada to give $2.65 billion to this effort over five years . <p> The money in the supplementary estimates will be used to contribute to the International Fund for Agricultural Development and to the World Bank to establish the Canada Clean Energy and Forest Facility . <p> There have been two estimates that have already been tabled for 2019-20 : the interim estimates in late January and the main estimates in early April . <p> A motion passed unanimously by the House on Thursday set aside a day this week to have the committee of the whole consider the supplementary estimates for no longer than four hours . When this concludes , the estimates will be deemed reported and the House will then immediately adjourn to the next sitting day , as per the motion . <p> Successful passage of a so-called supply motion to approve the supplementary estimates is necessary to let the government spend public dollars . <p> This past March , Murray was named president of the Treasury Board and Minister of Digital Government after Jane Philpott resigned from the role amid the SNC-Lavalin scandal . In the Nov. 20 shuffle , she retained the digital government portfolio but was replaced as president of the Treasury Board by Jean-Yves Duclos .
