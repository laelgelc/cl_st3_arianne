<h> Zann looking forward to a busy 2020 in Cumberland-Colchester <p> Lenore Zann ( centre ) is shown with Prime Minister Justin Trudeau ( left ) and former Cumberland-Colchester MP Bill Casey during a campaign visit to Amherst in October . Zann , who was elected as the new MP for Cumberland-Colchester on Oct. 21 , is looking ahead to a busy 2020. - Darrell Cole <p> TRURO , N.S. -- <p> Lenore Zann has only been the MP for Cumberland-Colchester for a few weeks , but that has n't stopped her from becoming active on several fronts that should benefit one end of the riding to the other in 2020 . <p> Zann , who was sworn in as the riding 's new MP on Nov. 26 , has already begun working on various projects and sees big things in the next 12 months . <p> Chief among those priorities is getting out into the riding to meet the people who elected her on Oct. 21 . <p> " We 're not going back to the House of Commons until the end of January , which means I have time to spend in the riding and I want to get out to the smaller communities in Cumberland and Colchester and have the communities get to know me better , " Zann said . <p> Moving into 2020 , Zann said one of her top priorities is the future of the RCMP operational communications centre in Truro , picking up the fight started by her predecessor Bill Casey to keep the centre in Colchester instead of consolidating operations in Halifax . <p> On Dec. 13 , she tabled a petition in the House of Commons asking Public Safety Minister Bill Blair to honour the commitment for an independent study on the risks of moving the centre out of Truro . <p> She has already spoken to Defence Minister Harjit Sajjan about the future of the Col. James Layton Ralston Armoury in Amherst . She is urging the minister and the department to give the Amherst Armouries Plus Society the opportunity to investigate taking over the building and repurposing it from DND use to a community-operated facility . <p> " I intend to continue lobbying for that building to be given to the community group that wants to find a new purpose for the building , " she said . <p> She also wants to work with Parks Canada and provincial officials in Nova Scotia regarding the potential development of an interpretive centre for Beaubassin and the Chignecto Marine Ship Railway at Fort Lawrence . That included meetings with ACOA as well as federal representatives about those projects and Debert , the site of the oldest Indigenous community in Canada . <p> She also wants to work with federal and provincial officials about the redevelopment of the TAAC Grounds in Truro and is very interest in what will come out of study being completed on the dikes along the Isthmus of Chignecto . <p> " It 's very important to me to continue working for a just and balanced climate change program going forward . We really need to make sure the Chignecto isthmus is protected so we do n't become an island , " she said . " I 'm focused on what 's going to best for future generations for the environment . " <p> Women 's issues are also important to her and she is working with a pair of women on the subject of non-state torture , including human trafficking of women and children across Canada . <p> " We need to make sure government realizes that torture is not just abuse or assault that it 's much more significant than that and much more damaging , " she said . " That needs to be addressed in law . " <p> Tourism is another passion of hers and she said wants to work with the various levels of government to attract more people to both Cumberland and Colchester counties . One of those is the Cliffs of Fundy Aspiring Geopark that 's being considered for UNESCO status . <p> She also wants to work with provincial representatives including both of Cumberland County 's MLAs , the Transportation and Infrastructure Renewal and Education departments to have Springhill 's new elementary school opened by September 2021 . <p> Zann said it has been whirlwind for her since winning the Liberal nomination earlier in 2019 and holding the seat for the Liberals in the October federal election . <p> " I turned 60 on Nov. 22 and my swearing in was like a big birthday present , " she said . " She said I could n't think of a better way to enter my 60th year than being sworn in as the new MP for Cumberland-Colchester and then getting down to the nitty gritty to find out what people need in our riding to help make their lives better . That excites me . " <p> And on the day she was scheduled to make her first speech in the House of Commons , Conservative leader Andrew Scheer announced his resignation . <p> " It was almost like it was a movie or a TV show , you ca n't make this stuff up , " she said . " It just happened right in front of my eyes as I was getting ready to stand up and make my first speech . All of sudden everything changed . "
