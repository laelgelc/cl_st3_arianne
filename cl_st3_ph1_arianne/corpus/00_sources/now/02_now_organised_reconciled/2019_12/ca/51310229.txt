<h> 2019 in review : Climate strikes <p> Climate protests are nothing new , but they certainly gained a lot of momentum in 2019 . <p> Last year , Swedish teen Greta Thunberg started protesting outside her country 's Parliament every Friday , a movement that quickly spread around the world , including here in Halifax , where students walked out of class several times throughout the year . <p> Near the end of September , around 10,000 Haligonians young and old descended upon the downtown core to march from Victoria Park to City Hall , making stops along the way at Nova Scotia Power , Province House and the Petroleum Board Office on Barrington St. <p> " If I do n't come out here , if people do n't come out here to support the cause against climate change then no real change is going to happen , " said Jakob Conrad , 18 . " We need to send a message . " <p> Earlier that week , young protestors staged a " die in " at the Halifax Shopping Centre where involved youth collapsed on the floor and pretended to be dead . <p> Citadel High School student and co-organizer Willa Fisher , says the non-violent protest was meant to disrupt business-as-usual and draw attention to the cause . <p> " I think the main effect of the die-in is to bring attention to the issue and stop the regular flow of commerce , " she said . " Doing this in the Halifax Shopping Centre will get people to stop and think about their actions and whether they should be buying new things . " <p> Fourteen-year-old Chenoah Paul attended and said she did n't want to be afraid for her future . <p> " It 's not a joke , it 's real . We need to start acting now to protect the earth and protect the water , " she said . " I 've been protecting the water at the Treaty Truck House for a few years , stopping Alton Gas with my mom . It 's been a fight . It 's been over three years now and they still have n't done anything . " <p> Just before 11 a.m. that morning , officials told the group they had 10 minutes to vacate the area or be arrested . Many cleared out , but 18 people ended up being taken into custody . <p> 2019 is also the year Halifax Regional Municipality declared a climate emergency . <p> The motion was put forward in January by councillor Richard Zurawski , who went on a hunger strike in October to highlight the impacts of the climate crisis , including the threat to food security . <p> " The first thing that goes is the ability to get food , and the inability to get food means mass migrations , it means famine , it means the complete destabilization of social structures , " he explained . " I do n't want that to happen here , I want us to start being smart about things here . " <p> We welcome your feedback and encourage you to share your thoughts on this story .
