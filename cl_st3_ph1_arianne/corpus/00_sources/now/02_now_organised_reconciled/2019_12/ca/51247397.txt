<h> Significant gaps in Ontario health-care system : AG <p> It 's enough to make you sick . A review by the Office of the Ontario Auditor General highlighted many health-care system failings , including the alarming statistic that nearly six out of every 100 patients at institutions reviewed by auditors were harmed while in hospital . " This is the second highest rate of hospital patient harm in Canada , " the auditors noted . " Current laws and practices in Ontario make it difficult for hospitals to address concerns about the safety of care provided by some nurses and doctors . " For instance , auditors found that some nurses banned or terminated for incompetence were able to find work in other hospitals . The focus on patient safety varied from excellent to poor depending on the hospital , the report says . The report emphasized that most patients receive great care in Ontario , but said there are significant gaps that need to be addressed in areas like medication , hand washing and fall prevention . The findings are in the 2019 Annual Report released Wednesday by auditor general Bonnie Lysyk , and it evaluates a time period that straddles the Kathleen Wynne and Doug Ford governments with plenty of criticism for the oversight strengths of both regimes . The legislative watchdog looked at a wide range of government programs and services to determine if public dollars and resources were used wisely . Lysyk 's staff found shortcomings in many areas including how the government addresses opioid addictions , court backlogs , climate change , road safety and court backlogs . The diet of seniors in long-term care homes got a closer look by auditors , and the findings included the sad statistic that 27 died over a 17-month period for food-related reasons such as choking . During that same time period that ended in May , facilities reported more than 660 times that food and nutrition were an issue -- a list that included missed meals and gastroenteritis outbreaks . Sometimes frail seniors had to wait for overworked staff to help feed them , an average of 43 minutes for breakfast , or were given the wrong food . One senior had to sit on a toilet for 30 minutes because staff was busy in the dining room . Auditors found food past its best-before-date being used in three of the five long-term care homes they reviewed , with one home serving some residents liquid egg that was three months ' past due . " While we found that homes ' menus had sufficient protein , they contained too much sugar ... too much sodium ... and not enough fibre , " the report says . Regarding the risk of food-related illness in the general population in Ontario , the report makes several recommendations including more follow up with the small percentage of farmers whose animals exceeded standards when tested for drug residue . The report also wants the government to rethink its view that fish processors who sell only in Ontario not be required to obtain a licence and to give a harder look at what 's labelled organic and the presence of pesticides on food for sale . Auditors found that children up to six years old could spend up to 18 months in Children 's Aid Society ( CAS ) care waiting for the courts to resolve a child protection case , and children between six and 17 years old up to 30 months . Auditors complained they were not given enough information by the justice system to fully assess wait times , but found many instances where even the maximums of 18 months and 30 months was exceeded . A 22-month-old had been in interim CAS care his whole life while awaiting a decision on his future . " The boy 's bond with his interim caregiver is now deeper than it ought to have been allowed to get , " the report quotes the Superior Court of Justice . While looking at the justice system , auditors dove into absenteeism by permanent correctional officers which they found had increased by 27% in four years to an average of 31 sick days per officer in 2018 . This average was much higher than found in B.C. , Alberta , Saskatchewan and federal institutions , the auditors noted . " We noted a concerning trend in sick leave information that was manually tracked by staff in Toronto South ... more contract employees called in sick per day in 2018 during weekends , holidays and the summer months than the rest of the year , " the report says . The auditors tried to look at other jails but could n't find that type of sick day data . In a special separate report , auditors found many issues with how the government carries out its responsibility to safeguard the environment . The Environment Ministry posted a proposal to allow a hunter to take up to 50 double-crested cormorants a day while describing the impact on sustainability as " neutral . " A key conclusion of the report was that the government 's climate change plan -- proposed as an alternative to Wynne 's cancelled carbon tax -- was based on flawed emission-reduction estimates , ignores agricultural emissions and is undermined routinely by its own decisions .
