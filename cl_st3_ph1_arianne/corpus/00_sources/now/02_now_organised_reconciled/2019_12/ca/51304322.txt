<p> Residents in the District of North Vancouver have gone to court seeking the ouster of two councillors in an escalating dispute over a recently enacted pigeon ban that the local newspaper characterized as the " pettiest of pet issues . " <p> At the core of the dispute is an allegation that the councillors failed to declare fully conflicts of interest and " conspired together " to pass the pigeon bylaw last month to benefit one of the councillors who lives next door to a pigeon coop owner . <p> " I 'm abhorred at the fact a couple of councillors might conspire for their own benefit -- using the integrity other voters have given them and potentially misused it , " said Wayne Messenger , one of 11 residents who signed a petition filed in B.C. Supreme Court on Friday seeking a declaration that the two councillors are " disqualified from holding office . " <p> " It 's what you expect car salesmen to do . " <p> If I have erred in any way , I assure council and the community that it was done inadvertently <p> The two councillors in question -- Betty Forbes and Lisa Muri -- both declined invitations by the National Post to comment on the situation , citing an ongoing review . <p> At the district 's Nov. 18 council meeting , Forbes read a statement saying : " It has always been my intention to act with integrity and in the best interest of the district . ... If I have erred in any way , I assure council and the community that it was done inadvertently and in good faith with my understanding , as a new councillor , of the conflict-of-interest rules . " <p> None of the allegations have been proven in court . <p> On Nov. 4 , the district council voted to pass a pigeon prohibition bylaw , which effectively bans anyone from owning or keeping in captivity a pigeon and sets a fine of up to $10,000 for failing to obey the bylaw . <p> Councillor Betty ForbesTwitter <p> Noting that she had a neighbour who is a keeper of pigeons , Forbes excused herself from votes related to the adoption of the new bylaw . <p> But email records uncovered by CBC News through a freedom-of-information request revealed that Forbes had earlier in the summer communicated her desire to outlaw pigeons to fellow councillors , including Muri . <p> " I have a pool that pigeons fly over , " she wrote , adding they also poop and roost on her property . " Please just pass an actual bylaw outlawing them , not rescinding our current bylaw . " <p> Prior to being elected councillor in October 2018 , Forbes had appealed to the district to review the existing pigeon bylaw , telling council that pigeon coops could affect the value of neighbouring properties and that her neighbour 's coop was an " eyesore . " <p> Days after the election , she wrote to city staff to raise the issue again . She was told that the district had investigated her complaints and carried out inspections at her neighbour 's home . <p> It 's what you expect car salesmen to do <p> According to the petition filed in court , Muri drafted a report to council in April recommending that staff be directed to prepare a bylaw that would amend the existing pigeon bylaw . In it , Muri cited " documented cases of domestic pigeons perching , roosting , feeding and straying onto private property and public lands disturbing the peaceful enjoyment of home owners and residents " and pigeon food " attracting rats and vermin . " <p> The petition alleges Muri and Forbes exchanged multiple emails regarding the pigeon bylaw and " conspired together to repeal the ( pigeon bylaw ) and enact a prohibition against keeping pigeons ... for the sole purpose or predominant purpose of benefiting the direct , or in the alternative indirect , pecuniary interests of Councillor Forbes . " <p> " At all material times , Councillor Muri was aware of the full extent of Councillor Forbes ' personal interest in this matter , " the petition states , adding that the " only complaint " the district 's ever received about pigeons is from Forbes . <p> Mayor Mike Little did not respond to a request for comment Monday . He has previously asked the district 's chief administrative officer to conduct a review of the way council handled the pigeon ban . <p> Kulwant Dulay , who is a neighbour of councillor Betty Forbes.Facebook <p> Forbes ' neighbour , Kulwant Dulay , who currently keeps 15 pigeons known as High Fliers in a coop at his residence , could not be reached for comment Monday . He filed a separate petition earlier this month in court seeking to quash the pigeon ban , arguing that there were " no other legitimate underlying reasons " to enact it other than to benefit Forbes . <p> One of Dulay 's supporters is Givo Hassko , a director of the Vancouver Poultry &amp; Fancy Pigeon Association , an organization that supports hobbyists who breed and exhibit pigeons . He started a GoFundMe page to raise money for Dulay 's legal challenge and to " end the harassment of a man with some birds who just wants to be left alone . " <p> Hassko told the Post the district council failed to adequately consult the community before enacting the pigeon ban and that his association would have been happy to give its input . <p> " There 's always two sides . What they did was they took the neighbour 's side , not Kulwant 's side , " he said . <p> A scathing editorial last month in the North Shore News community newspaper described the pigeon ban as " mean-spirited " and " arbitrary " and admonished the " multiple back-channel communications " that preceded the district 's vote . <p> How come council time is being take up by this bloody pigeon thing ? <p> The editorial suggested there were many more pressing issues -- housing , climate change and traffic -- for the council to concern itself with . <p> That 's the thing that really irks Phyllis Page , who lives across the street from Dulay . <p> " How come council time is being take up by this bloody pigeon thing ? " she said . <p> Page , who also put her name to the petition and describes Dulay 's pigeon coop as " very clean , " said she does n't understand why the district needed to go to such lengths . <p> " They do n't like something happening in their neighbourhood so they band together and pass a bylaw ?
