<h> EDITORIAL : Perhaps the trees can speak for us <p> The federal government 's pledge to plant two-billion trees in the next decade is a cost-effective way to pull greenhouse gases out of the atmosphere , according to a green economy think tank at the University of Ottawa . <p> The federal government is already talking to provinces to get the planting started next year . <p> In October , a new initiative called Team Trees was launched . It 's an initiative by a large group of internet celebrities to plant 20-million trees by the end of this year . For every dollar donated , one tree will be planted through the Arbor Day Foundation . <p> Within a week , there was a donation of one-million trees by Elon Musk , along with multiple donations of 50,000 and 100,000 tree and a stunning amount of smaller donations . <p> Planting trees alone will not solve the climate change crisis , but it 's a good start . And , as far as environmentalism goes , it 's the kind of thing pretty much everyone can get behind ( as opposed to , for example , stances on pipelines ) . <p> Two-billion trees within a decade and 20-million trees within weeks . Those are a lot of trees . <p> However , we lose plenty of trees on a regular basis . California alone has averaged more than 30-million trees lost per year for the last four years to just beetles and drought . Of course , we 're no strangers to tree loss here in B.C. . In both 2017 and 2018 , well over one-million hectares burned in wildfires , aside from that lost due to drought , beetles or other causes . <p> In May , the province , in an effort to re-establish wildlife habitat , announced 22-million trees would be planted in areas burned during the 2017 wildfires in the Plateau , Elephant Hill and Hanceville fire areas . <p> And , according to the Ministry of Forests , Lands , Natural Resource Operations and Rural Development , 259 million trees will have been planted throughout B.C. this year . <p> We can all do our part , small as it may seem , by grabbing a spade and a sapling and planting one of these carbon dioxide scrubbers.
