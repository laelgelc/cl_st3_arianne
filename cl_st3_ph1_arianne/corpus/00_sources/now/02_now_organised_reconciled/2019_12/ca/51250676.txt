Singh had a free hand to attack the Liberals , but little leverage to force them to come around to his demands <p> The sight of a minority government bypassing the NDP on the way to surviving with Bloc support may be unfamiliar to MPs first elected during a time period when the sovereigntist party was little more than a rump in the House . <p> But veterans of the last spell of federal minority rule will remember that over his first two terms , Stephen Harper often relied on the Bloc to save his bacon . <h> YOU MIGHT BE INTERESTED IN ... <p> Like Harper then , Trudeau has a choice of dance partners on the opposition benches . the Bloc , the NDP and the Greens on Thursday . <p> And that the Conservatives , at least under Andrew Scheer , are determined to continue to fight the Liberal climate-change framework every inch of Still , even for the Conservatives , it was not completely business as usual on Thursday . <p> While Scheer came out with all guns blazing , calling the Liberal throne speech an insult to Alberta and Saskatchewan , his provincial allies in Edmonton and Regina seemed uncharacteristically inclined to keep their powder dry . <p> Even as Scheer talked , Alberta Premier Jason Kenney was striking a deal calls for more financial relief for provinces that are not net equalization recipients such as Alberta and Saskatchewan under the federal fiscal stabilization program . <p> The issue is bound to be raised at the federal-provincial finance table . <p> The Liberals were also reminded this week that events that could accelerate or precipitate the demise of their minority government need not result
