" <p> " Clearly , your government 's refusal to stand up for Canada on the world stage has cost our agriculture sector , " the Alberta MP writes . The letter can be found here . <p> Canada and China have been embroiled in a largely agriculture-centred trade dispute , since Chinese officials in March pulled the canola export permits from two major Canadian grain companies , citing pest concerns . Chinese importers have also stopped purchasing Canadian canola seed . <p> Bibeau stated her commitment to ensure the success of Quebec 's farm families and rural areas in Quebec City yesterday . <p> She said the feds are meeting commitments to supply-managed sectors to ensure that dairy farmers have access to the compensation as quickly as possible . She also expressed confidence that the compensation for poultry and egg producers and processors would be finalized in the near future , Newswire reported . <p> In Canada <p> Toronto 's Ryding-Regency Meat Packers and related companies have had licenses for federal slaughter , processing and export permanently revoked after the Canadian Food Inspection Agency cited " false or misleading information " given to them during an E. coli probe . <p> Monday 's cancellation exacerbates what was already described as " critical processing capacity shortage " for the province 's cattle producers , leaving them to seek other markets and feed cattle far longer than anticipated . <p> Affected companies include Ryding-Regency Meat Packers , as well as the Canadian Select Meats and The Beef Boutique , which both operate under the St. Ann 's Foods banner . <p> The companies ' licenses were suspended separately in mid-September because they " failed to implement effective control measures " at the facilities , Food in Canada reported . <p> Meanwhile , a new food pricing report Wednesday states Canadian families on average will spend about $480 more on groceries in 2020 , as food prices are set to increase by about 4 per cent next year . <p> The forecast , driven in large part by climate change and continuing trade issues , outpaces the average food inflation rate over the past decade , which has been about 2 per cent to 2.5 per cent a year , according to Canada 's Food Price Report released from Dalhousie University and the University of Guelph . <p> Meat , fresh fruit and vegetables are expected to jump the most in price , according to The Globe and Mail . <p> And while the criminalization of cannabis in Canada is a thing of the past , the legacy of legalization is far from clear . <p> Legalization represents a major social and political shift , after years of work by activists successfully showed that criminalization was harmful and ineffective . Policy-makers and business people , keenly aware of the changing legal and cultural landscape , have seized the opportunity to turn a multi-billion-dollar black market into a regulated , taxable industry . So will it be a story of harm reduction or business creation ? Policy Options reports . <p> Internationally <p> U.S. President Donald Trump only wants a " phase one " trade deal with China that works for the United States , his Agriculture Secretary Sonny Perdue told CNBC Wednesday . <p> " Trump wants to conclude a deal that can be enforceable , that can be reliable and be consistent with what the deal says , " said Perdue . <p> This shortly after Bloomberg reported that the U.S. and China were edging closer to finalizing an agreement before new U.S. tariffs go into effect against Chinese goods on Dec. 15 . <p> To add to the confusion , Trump said Wednesday at the NATO summit that China trade talks are going well -- just one day after suggesting he may want to delay a China trade deal until after the 2020 presidential election . <p> Next , land down under is regarded as one of the more enlightened suppliers of cattle . Yet harrowing footage emerged Monday of animal abuse at Australian cattle farms that send live animals to Israel for fattening and slaughter . <p> The footage showed an animal protection manager kicking and punching a cow ; workers standing on , electrocuting and even shooting " for fun " at animals ; dying cows being left to die slowly when there were n't enough guns to finish them off . And -- in one instance -- a cow was still alive on the ground after someone who had never previously held a weapon shot her four times , reported The Times of Israel . <p> Over in Europe , the EU 's environment committee said further reducing use of pesticides and better monitoring are needed to save its bees . <p> The Environment , Public Health and Food Safety Committee on Tuesday approved a resolution highlighting weaknesses in the EU Pollinator Initiative that renders it inadequate to address the main causes of pollinators ' decline in Europe . The committee proposes that a reduction in the use of pesticides be set as a " common indicator " to evaluate how effective national measures are in protecting bees and other pollinators , according to The European Sting .
