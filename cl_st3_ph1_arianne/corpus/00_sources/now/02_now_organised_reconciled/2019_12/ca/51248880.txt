<h> Retired business owner hopes tree donations will " grow and grow " <p> Longtime Brantford resident John Monne ( left ) , with Brian Hughes , director of parks services , is donating $10,000 and 10 per cent of his estate , to the city 's parks-recreation department for the purpose of planting trees . The 89-year-old retired business owner is encouraging other seniors to do the same.Michelle Ruby / The Expositor <p> Brian Hughes , Brantford 's director of parks services , said the donation likely will be used to plant a grouping of trees somewhere in the city that will recognize its benefactor . <p> The new plantings will help replace more than 4,000 mature trees lost in the city due to the emerald ash borer , an invasive insect that has killed millions of ash trees in southwestern Ontario and the Great Lakes states , he said . <p> " It has had a significant impact on our city 's tree canopy , " said Hughes . " This will help get us back on the right track . You can never plant enough trees . " <p> A passionate outdoor enthusiast , Monne is also a Pan American masters swimming champion . He was the gold medal winner in the 200-metre breaststroke and the 400-metre individual medley in the 85-to-89 age group . <p> In his presentation to city councillors and staff , Monne likened climate change to a runaway train that 's picking up speed . He said older people must do their part to halt its progress . <p> " We have lived the longest and contributed to climate change the most . If we throw enough trees in front of that train , it will be brought to a stop . " <p> Monne said he chose Dec. 5 to make his announcement because it has a special significance for him . <p> " Growing up Dutch , I associate this date with St. Nicholas Eve , which is when St. Nicholas would give gifts to young children . I see this as a gift to make the environment better for future generations . So , today was the perfect time to share this with my community . " <p> The city will honour Monne with an event on April 22 , the 50th anniversary of Earth Day , when the first tree resulting from his donation will be planted . <p> Coun. Dan McCreary , who was acting mayor on Thursday , called Monne 's generosity overwhelming . <p> " This is the creation of a legacy that will live on and be enjoyed by five to 10 generations that follow us . It is creating something really big . "
