This unanimously passed legislation was the first private member 's bill from an opposition party , and the Greens also positioned the province as a leader nationally by being the first in the country to offer a benefit company option . <p> Protecting tenants ' rights and supporting victims <p> Renters who are victims of violence at their home can now break their fixed-term lease and seek safety after the BC Greens ' private member 's bill amending the Residential Tenancy Act garnered broad tri-party support from the NDP and Liberals . <p> Honouring a past atrocity and B.C. ' s Ukrainian community <p> The Ukrainian Famine and Genocide ( Holodomor ) Memorial Day bill officially recognizes the fourth Saturday of every November as a day of remembrance for those who died during Holodomor from 1932 to 1933 . <p> Now , more than ever , political leaders must work together to address the challenges before us and step boldly into the future .
