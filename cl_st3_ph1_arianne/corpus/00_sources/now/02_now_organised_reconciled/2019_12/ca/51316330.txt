<p> One moment , Danica and Sean Collison were midway through a game of pool-side bingo , during a rather forgettable trip to the Bahamas that had been marred by unexpected costs and delays . <p> The next , the Hanmer couple was springing into action to save the life of a young boy who had quietly slipped underwater . <p> Lounging near the resort pool on Jan. 18 , Danica happened to look over and saw the toddler , struggling in vain to reach the surface , but not making a sound . The boy 's family had not noticed his plight . <p> " I jumped up and said , ' He 's drowning , he 's drowning , ' and my husband and I just bolted into the pool , " recalled Danica , herself the mother of three children . <p> Sean got him out of the water and the boy began to cry , but was otherwise unhurt . <p> The family later thanked the couple for their help . <p> " What turned out to be one of the worst vacations turned out to be one of the best and most rewarding vacations , " Danica said . " It makes me believe that we were supposed to be there , that it was meant to be .
