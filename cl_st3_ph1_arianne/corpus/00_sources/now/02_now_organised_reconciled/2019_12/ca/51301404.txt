<h> Letters to the Editor ( 5 ) : Friday , December 20 , 2019 <p> With Tolko 's recent closure of the mill in downtown Kelowa , maybe it 's time for our city to weigh the cost/benefit of logging trucks driving on our community streets . <p> Being that there is no longer any direct economic benefit to Kelowna from the logging industry , maybe they should stick to the provincial highway system rather than taking the shortcut through my community on Rutland Road . <p> Rutland Road , between Highway 33 and Highway 97 is supposed to be a bike route , yet has incomplete bike lanes , is supposed to be a walking route , yet has incomplete sidewalks . It is a main bus route , yet has incomplete pullouts in a two-lane roadway and does not have adequate crosswalks for bus passengers to cross . <p> Now I am noticing an increase in logging truck activity on Rutland Road since the downtown mill closure . Instead of continuing on down Highway 33 to 97 , they now turn down Rutland Road on their way presumably to Vernon or Lumby . <p> I bring this up as I , just five minutes ago , witnessed a near-miss accident as a logging truck turned onto Rutland Road after coming down the hill on Highway 33 . <p> Just days ago , I had a near miss as I was passing a transit bus picking up passengers on Rutland Road at Mugford . There was a person running to catch the bus , wearing all black at night in the middle of the road . I have his terrified expression forever burned into my memory as I slammed on the brakes mere feet away from him . <p> I have brought this to the city 's attention , through the kelowna.ca service request and was told they have identified the problem , know what the solution is , but have not earmarked any money for it and have no plans to follow through with this urgent life-safety project . <p> Maybe , if Tolko wants to continue to use our community as a shortcut for their business , they should pony up some money to make it safe to do so , otherwise , take the long way down the highway and stop rumbling through our neighbourhood . <p> Jeff Frank <p> Rutland <p> Stranger performs real act of kindness <p> Dear Editor : <p> This morning , as I was putting out my garbage , I needed assistance and this car was in our cul-de-sac . <p> I motioned to the gentleman that I needed assistance .
