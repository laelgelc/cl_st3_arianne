<h> Letters to the Editor : Dec. 28 <p> The Carols , made up of Marty Kolls , Lynne Craven and Natash Roberts , sing Christmas classics on London 's Dundas Place flex street as the new sections between Richmond and Wellington streets were closed earlier this month for an event . ( Free Press file photo ) <h> Downtown tax fix <p> The new flex street downtown looks great , but it 's nothing more than a Band-Aid solution . Walk around south of Richmond Row , and you 'll be greeted by a swath of vacant shops and offices . <p> The only way to revitalize downtown is to make it a place where people live , work and play . Infrastructure , transit and social care improvements are important , but the key is to attract businesses of all sizes . <p> In 2019 , city council ended the vacancy tax rebate , which gave property managers a property tax rebate when commercial units remained empty . The rebate provided some property owners with little incentive to find new tenants . <p> With the rebate gone , the city should take the next step and create a commercial vacancy tax downtown . This tax would likely cause property managers to drop rents in a bid to increase their occupancy rate , providing businesses with a major incentive to invest in the core . <h> Do n't be a Scrooge <p> I agree downtown London is a mess . Trying to make downtown alive again is not going so well . Stop building outward . Revamp roads logically and simple . <p> I work with London 's economically underprivileged . There are many issues with London 's downtown , homeless and drug addicts . We in the middle class to upper class -- me included -- need to stop pretending we are the minority , stop throwing garbage and cigarette butts out our car windows , stop looking away from the unfortunate and introduce yourself to a person in an unfortunate situation . There are many who were just like you and me , lost their job and in an instant become " one of them . " <p> Stop complaining and do something to help . A simple ear to listen , extended hand to shake and a friendly exchange will go farther than most people realize in assisting a person just enough to show some self-$respect , to feel strong enough to try to pick themselves up . <p> It 's the Christmas season . Do n't be a Scrooge . <p> S. Simons , London <h> Flex street will work <p> John Lisowski makes some rather harsh criticisms of the flex street development on Dundas Street . A brief search on the internet would show that flex streets have been done elsewhere and have succeeded everywhere . <p> The street is very much better looking than it was before . The street is open to driving and parking as it always was . And when the warm weather returns this spring , I expect the flex will become a welcome venue for many events . <p> David Nielsen , London <h> Biogas plant raises lots of questions <p> Stratford is considering a renewable gas plant . The plan is to have organic waste trucked into our city from other municipalities . This plant would be located in the area of residences , a school , retirement living and a conservation area . <p> The environmental benefits of this project and the future of our children was discussed . We agree , but why are we overlooking the children of today , considering the waste trucks that will be passing their homes and schools , the odours that will be emitted from these trucks and the RNG plant as well as the risks of explosions or leaks ? <p> There was talk at this meeting about existing biogas facilities in London and Elmira . Both are located in industrial areas distanced from residential neighbourhoods . Also not mentioned , the London facility has been charged with 11 odour-related offences . <p> With the financial burden , the safety and environmental risks and location , why is our city even considering this project ? <p> Kirk and Robin Roberts , Stratford <h> Whom do they represent ? <p> What is our city council thinking about for even considering building a new city hall . A 50-year-old building is n't old . <p> London has a homelessness issue and these numbers , sadly , are continuing to rise , as more and more citizens struggle with the high rents or the lack of affordable housing . <p> This begs me to ask the question : whom are these people on council representing , us or themselves ? <p> Victoria Stirling , London <h> Trial balloo <p> The budget planning for a new city hall is a bogus trial balloon . Twelve million dollars in early work is grossly over budget . Does the city hall not have qualified staff to co-ordinate planning ? <p> Even in 2025 dollars , $125 million is not realistic . Look at recent projects in London . Although not big office buildings , the Bostwick Y , library and arena were $40 million and the east London arena was $22 million . <p> If this scheme gets legs , the first determination should be that the building be placed on a property the city already owns . It could be anywhere in London served by bus rapid transit and with a large parking lot , as being in the core is not necessary . <p> Robert Webb , London <h> Take care of Earth <p> I was very pleased to read the letter It 's your planet ( Dec. 17 ) by Steve Matthews . <p> I work in retail and I am required to ask if a customer wishes to purchase a bag , be it a plastic bag or a reusable one . Sadly , in the majority of cases , a plastic bag is purchased , even though the customer does mention he or she has several reusable bags in their vehicle , but has forgotten to bring one into the store . <p> Then , there are those who express their surprise and , in many instances , anger at the fact that even though they are purchasing merchandise , they are required to pay for a bag . I do my best to diplomatically explain that we also sell reusable bags for a slightly higher price , and in some instances , this angers them even more . <p> While the main focus is on climate change and how it is impacting the land and air , the tonnes of discarded plastics being used , be it bottles , wrappings and bags , has as a grave impact on the land and waters on the planet . <p> When will people start taking care of Mother Earth and stop the gluttonous use of plastics ? <p> It is n't about crop farming , safety of farmers , their families , their employees or animal stress mitigation . <p> This piece of legislation is an ag-gag ( agricultural gag ) law , also known as an anti-whistleblower law . <p> This is about industry and politicians working to keep the horrors of animal agriculture a secret . If we knew , we would n't support it with our dollars and we would demand change . <p> Trespassing by activists does n't happen on crop farms . It happens on animal farms and it 's purposeful . <p> There are horrifying ( and legal ) things happen to animals out of our sight . When this obvious cruelty reaches our eyes via whistleblowers ' photos , the effects could mean devastation for a very powerful industry and its political supporters . <p> How free and open is our society when we have no freedom to learn certain truths and no means to share that information because such actions are punishable by law ? <p> The proposed legislation is inexcusably misguided and shameful , as is any support it garners . <p> Steven Palombo , Windsor <h> Fix PM 's residence <p> Although New Year 's resolutions are often personal goals , here 's one I feel many Canadians may embrace : Fix 24 Sussex Drive .
