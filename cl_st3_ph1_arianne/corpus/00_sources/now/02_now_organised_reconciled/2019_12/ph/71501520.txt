<p> There 's no doubt South Korea is slowly taking over the world through K-Pop . No matter where you go , there will always be fans ( and haters ) of K-Pop and the numerous Korean bands and singers . One of the most popular ( not to mention the biggest ) K-Pop superstar group today is BTS . Recently , the boy band has become the latest Formula E ambassadors in an effort to raise climate change awareness . <p> If you 're wondering why a huge K-Pop group would suddenly team up with Formula E , well we 're wondering too . But , it does make sense considering the electric open-wheel series will be making its South Korean ePrix debut this season . Both BTS and Formula E hope to inspire the next generation as well as millions of fans all over the world to embrace clean energy and drive electric vehicles in the future . <p> Aside from helping spread awareness about climate change and electric vehicles , Formula E will undoubtedly benefit from this partnership as well . BTS garnered over 60 million listeners and 3 billion streams across 79 countries on Spotify this 2019 alone . Furthermore , they also have over 90 million combined followers across their social media accounts . Not to mention the South Korean group has a huge die-hard fanbase as well . As a result , K-Pop fans will likely tune in to follow the various Formula E races being held across the world . <p> Formula E 's South Korean ePrix will take place on May 3 , 2020 in Seoul . The bespoke city circuit takes in some of Seoul 's best sights with a section of track housed inside the Olympic Stadium and winding its way around the Jamsil Sports Complex . South Korean national public broadcaster KBS will also be airing the inaugural race .
