<h> Recent Posts <h> GIR rises to $86.39 B <p> The country 's gross international reserves ( GIR ) went up by $560 million to $86.39 billion as of end November 2019 from $85.83 billion previously , the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas ( BSP ) said . <p> The GIR month-on-month increase " reflects the inflows arising from the BSP 's foreign exchange operations and income from its investments abroad , and the National Government 's net foreign currency deposits , " the BSP said . <p> The current GIR level continues to be " ample external liquidity buffer " as far as the central bank is concerned . It is equivalent to 7.5 months ' worth of imports of goods and services and payments of primary income , as well as 5.6 times the country 's short-term external debt based on original maturity and 4.1 times based on residual maturity . <p> Last October , the BSP subscribed -- and it is one of the first to do so -- to the open-funded Green Bonds managed by the Bank for International Settlements ( BIS ) , and this became part of the GIR . <p> Of the $86-billion level GIR , $15.50 billion are reserve assets deposited overseas including in the BIS , and about $6.50 billion are foreign currency assets including bond investments in the BIS . <p> The BIS-managed Green Bonds , an investment pool facility , is the BSP 's signal that it is embarking on the " greening " of the finance sector , or green financing in support of an environmentally responsible finance and investment practices . <p> When the BSP first announced its Green Bond investments , it said that climate change is inevitable and the financial sector " has a significant role to play in pursuing sustainable and inclusive growth in the global economy , the environment , and society . " <p> The BIS launched its Green Bonds in late September this year in response to a " growing demand for climate-friendly investments among official institutions . " It said the BIS ' green bond fund initiative " helps central banks to incorporate environmental sustainability objectives in the management of their reserves . " <p> The BSP and other central banks also invest in the BIS ' Asian Bond Fund or ABF2 . It includes the Pan-Asia Bond Index Fund and eight single-market funds , and is managed by private-sector fund managers with the BIS as the administrator . The BSP has a floor investment of $200 million in the ABF .
