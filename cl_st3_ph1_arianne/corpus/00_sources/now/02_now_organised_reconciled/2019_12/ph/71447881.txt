<h> Duterte orders immediate repair of classrooms , infra , restoration of power <p> President Duterte directed on Thursday the Department of Education ( DepEd ) to start the repairs of damaged classrooms , the Department of Public Works and Highways ( DPWH ) to restore damaged road sections and bridges , the Department of Energy ( DOE ) to continue the restoration of power supply , and the Department of Information and Communication Technology ( DICT ) to immediately restore communication systems . <p> President Rodrigo Roa Duterte ( ALFRED FRIAS/PRESIDENTIAL PHOTO ) <p> In a situation briefing in Legazpi City , Albay Thursday evening on the effects of typhoon " Tisoy , " the President also ordered the Department of Interior and Local Government ( DILG ) to intensify preparations of evacuation centers and the enforcement of pre-emptive evacuation during times of disasters . <p> He instructed the Department of Transportation ( DOTr ) to institutionalize suspension protocols of public transport and cargo vehicles for anticipated weather disturbances . <p> Duterte also ordered the Department of Agriculture ( DA ) to provide assistance to affected farmers and fisherfolk , the Department of Social Welfare and Development ( DSWD ) to assist the affected and most vulnerable families through cash or food for work programs , and the newly-established Department of Human Settlements and Urban Development to provide housing assistance for those whose homes were damaged . <p> The President also assured assistance for the construction of 2,000 housing units where local officials can relocate the residents of foreshore areas in Albay . He also agreed with the suggestion of Albay Governor Al Francis Bichara to clear the trees along the highway that often render roads impassable when uprooted by strong winds . <p> Satisfied <p> President Duterte said he was satisfied with the way government agencies responded to the threats of typhoon " Tisoy . " <p> He congratulated the government agencies involved for a job well done and said he has a good team helping him run the country . <p> " I have a good team dito sa gobyerno ( here in the government ) . I can assure you that they are good , they are not corrupt , and they are well-versed , " he added . <p> The President said he was mostly satisfied because the government agencies already knew what to do even before the typhoon entered the country on November 30 . <p> " Apparently , the government agencies knew what to do before the typhoon hit , its landfall , what to do during the entire crisis , and after . So , I am more than satisfied with the response , " Duterte said . <p> Duterte urged the government agencies concerned to immediately submit their reports so the government can give them the budget they need to address the damages caused by the typhoon . <p> The President , in particular , mentioned the departments of Social Welfare and Development ( DSWD ) , Trade and Industry ( DTI ) , Health ( DOH ) , and Public Works and Highways ( DPWH ) . <p> " The question is , as always , when we have this kind of situation , after they made the assessment and valuations there , it 's always the money . So it would be good for the entire government agencies which are here , beginning from DSWD , ( and ) ends up with the Department of Trade , to submit your report immediately so that we can pass it on to the proper agency concerned , " Duterte said . <p> He assured government agencies that he will try to get them the money they need as soon as possible . <p> " Parating man ninyo yung report ninyo diyan ( Just submit to us the report ) , we will take over and I will advise you accordingly kung kailan darating ang pera ( when you will get the money ) . I 'm sure there is always money for that , for all these things that 's happening now . The only thing is how fast , " Duterte said . <p> " Wala kayong problema sa akin ( You wo n't have any problem with me ) because I am a very impatient person . I usually do my bidding minsan diyan sa mga ( with the ) ... not to bide their time but apurado ako , especially itong calamities ( I am very impatient , especially when it comes to calamities ) , " he added . <p> One with Bicol <p> Duterte also commiserated with the Bicolanos for the difficulties that they are going through because of the typhoon . <p> " Apparently , you are condemned to suffer this kind of crisis every now and then . You know , even before the climate change which made it really worse , you are the window of the Pacific and you are regularly pummelled with things like these , " he said .
