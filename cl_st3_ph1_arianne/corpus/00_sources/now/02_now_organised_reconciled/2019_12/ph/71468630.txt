<h> Groups call for action on climate change in Mindanao peace week <p> ZAMBOANGA CITY , Dec 5 ( PIA ) -- Representatives from the local sectors of the city voiced out their concern for society to brace for the effects of climate change and be prepared . <p> Interreligious Solidarity for Peace ( ISP ) Muslim convenor Prof. Ali Yacub , Al Haj said that the effects of the climate change all over the world is being felt , and that it has become something short of a " climate crisis " . <p> " The country , for one is experiencing all sorts of natural disasters such as typhoons and floods , " Yacub said , adding that these are manifestations of the changing climate . This happens not only in the Philippines , but is experienced all over the world , he said . <p> " We must be prepared . With fervent prayers we hope to be spared from these disasters , but we must act ( to be resilient ) , " Yacub said . " The mistakes and negligence of the past must not be repeated . " <p> Mindanao Week of Peace was celebrated from November 27 to December 4 . The theme for this year 's celebration is Environmental Care and Resilience to Climate Change Action for Peace . <p> Coupled with the advocacy for climate change adaptation and mitigation , the representatives all the while call for the persistent promotion of peace in Mindanao . <p> " As a lieutenant early in my military career , I experienced first-hand how conflict and violence affected communities . As soldiers , we experienced many sad situations . As soldiers , we are the first ones who desire for peace , " Divinagracia said . <p> He shared that peace efforts have made former enemies into friends . <p> " We are grateful for the peace advocacies . Now , I have seen former enemies who returned to the folds of the law living productive lives . Let us continue the advocacy for peace . But peace must start within ourselves first , " Divinagracia added . <p> On December 4 , the celebrations formally closed with a Peace Musical Festival at the KCC Mall de Zamboanga . The night was filled with peace-themed musical and cultural performances . <p> Divinagracia reiterated that residents must continue the advocacies of the week of peace all throughout the year .
