<h> POPCOM : PH population growth slows down to 1.5% <p> MANDALUYONG CITY , Dec. 29 -- The population growth of the Philippines recorded a decline of 0.21 percentage points ( ppt ) to 1.52% in the latter half of this decade , from the previous 1.73% between 2010 and 2015 , according to the Commission on Population and Development ( POPCOM ) . <p> Given this statistic , the Philippine Statistics Authority ( PSA ) projects the population in July 2020 to be 108.7 million Filipinos , which is lower than its earlier estimate of 109.9 million , or a difference of 1.2 million . <p> According to Undersecretary Juan Antonio Perez III , MD , MPH , the expected increase in population between 2019 and 2020 is about 1,483,828 , or an annual increase of 1.38% . <p> " Certain segments of the populace are contributing to the decline , particularly the 0-to-4 years-old group , which is showing a 1-ppt drop from 11% in 2010 , to 10.12% in 2020 . Meanwhile , the 0-to-14 age group will see a greater decrease of almost 4% : from 34% in 2010 , to 30.14% in 2020 , " POPCOM 's executive director expounded . <p> At the opposite end of the spectrum , Perez pointed out that growth is seen to be occurring in the older age groups , with 70.3 million working-age Filipinos comprising 64% of the population . They were only at 62% at the start of the decade . <p> " Filipinos 60 years and older are also increasing in number and will grow to 9,686,762 in 2020 , constituting 8.8% of the population . With a growth rate of more than 4% between 2015 and 2020 , the seniors ' numbers are growing at a faster rate than other age groups , " he stated . <p> The updated numbers from POPCOM revealed that almost two out of every five Filipinos , or 38.7% , will be residing in the mega-region of Central Luzon , the National Capital Region and Calabarzon at the start of the new decade , with almost half of the population growth between 2019 and 2020 occurring in the same three areas . On the other hand , most localities from the Visayas and Mindanao will have growth figures below the aforementioned national average of 1.52% . <p> New numbers welcomed <p> The POPCOM chief welcomed the most recent Philippine population figures from the PSA , which is lower by over 1 million than the former 's initial projection . Earlier , it estimated that the country 's entire citizenry will surpass 110 million at the start of the new decade . <p> Regarding the new population projection , Perez commented : " We welcome the new information from the PSA -- proof that our nationwide efforts on reproductive health as well as family planning ( FP ) are yielding positive results , as they are steadily being embraced by Filipinos . " <p> He however cautioned : " Although the numbers released on our total population is lower than what was previously projected , we should not fall into a state of complacency . We should keep in mind that our country still has one of the highest population growth rates in the ASEAN region . There are still lingering issues that we all have to address as we usher in the new decade : management of limited resources in the face of climate change , unrestrained internal migration leading to congestion in urban areas , as well as the disturbing rise of adolescent and teenage pregnancy nationwide , among others . " <p> " We still have a lot of work ahead of us with regard to population management and FP , " the undersecretary exhorted . " It would still take a comprehensive approach that links government efforts to nongovernment organizations and private sector -- an approach that allows population programs like FP to reach every community in all 42,000 barangays nationwide . We also enjoin our countrymen to do their part , as their decisions on FP will affect our communities .
