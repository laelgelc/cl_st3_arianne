<h> Topics <p> Teen climate activist Greta Thunberg is the Time Magazine person of the Year . <p> The Swede pipped US President Donald Trump and the Hong Kong protestors among the shortlist . <p> She was recognised for " sounding the alarm about humanity 's predatory relationship with the only home we have " and " for showing us all what it might look like when a new generation leads , " editor-in-chief Edward Felsenthal said.The 16-year-old has become the spokesperson for a generation anxious about climate change , following her solo strike against global warming outside Sweden 's parliament last year . <p> " We ca n't just continue living as if there was no tomorrow , because there is a tomorrow . That is all we are saying , " she told the magazine . <p> " The politics of climate action are as entrenched and complex as the phenomenon itself , and Thunberg has no magic solution , " the magazine wrote .
