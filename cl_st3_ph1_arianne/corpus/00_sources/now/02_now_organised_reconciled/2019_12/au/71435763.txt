<h> Developer sought government backing for new city at Little River <p> The developer at the centre of allegedly crooked land deals at the City of Casey has also sought Federal and Victorian government support for a radical plan for a multi-billion dollar , carbon-neutral satellite city at Little River , south-west of Melbourne . <p> Ferrari-driving planner-developer John Woodman is a key figure in this week 's public hearings by the Independent Broad-based Anti-corruption Commission , during which counsel assisting Michael Tovey , QC , has detailed how Mr Woodman and related companies donated lavishly to both sides of politics as part of their lobbying efforts . <p> Developer John Woodman had relationships with a number of councillors.Credit:Gary Sissons <p> The Age can now reveal that Mr Woodman and his Liberal-linked lobbyist Lorraine Wreford , recently met with federal Housing Minister Michael Sukkar to seek support for a new city of 120,000 residents built on hectares of farmland at Little River , 50 kilometres south-west of Melbourne . <p> The selling points to governments for the ambitious scheme -- which would boost the value of the land dramatically -- are carbon neutrality , high-tech transport , affordable housing and state-of-the-art composting , waste and recycling . <p> Advertisement <p> The Age has also confirmed that Luke Donnellan , Victoria 's Child Protection , Disability and Ageing Minister -- and former roads minister -- has also been briefed on the scheme by another Woodman lobbyist and Labor insider , Phil Staindl . <p> The former Casey mayor and current councillor Sam Aziz , who has allegedly received $900,000 in payments from Mr Woodman , has done paid ' ' research ' ' for the Little River project . <p> Former MP Lorraine Wreford when she was Mayor of Casey <p> Mr Tovey told the IBAC hearing that Cr Aziz was to be paid $25,000 a month for a total of $600,000 in consultancy fees for the Little River scheme . <p> Of the $25,000 , $2000 was paid in cash monthly to Cr Aziz by Ms Wreford who , since losing the seat of Mordialloc in 2014 , has worked as a conduit between Mr Woodman and Casey councillors . <p> Mr Woodman told the inquiry the payments had now ceased and Cr Aziz appears to have left the country and claims now to be in Dubai . <p> Mr Woodman , who faced hours of public grilling by IBAC on Monday and Tuesday said he had employed Cr Aziz on the Little River project because of his expertise in the planning of " Smart Cities " . <p> The land is owned by wealthy Malaysian tile mogul John Chua and the proposed satellite city would be built on land that is currently zoned for farming and as a ' ' green wedge ' ' . <p> The Age has no evidence of Woodman-linked political donations to Mr Sukkar . <p> Melbourne 's south-east is where Mr Woodman is active as a developer and planning consultant to big property interests . <p> While the federal government does not have planning authority over any site , the Coalition has taken an active interest in the hotly contested electorates around Geelong , including its pre-election promise of $2 billion to turn the Geelong rail line into a fast rail service . <p> The Geelong rail line is crucial to Mr Woodman 's Little River plan . A train station is near the land and under the Coalition 's plan would provide a fast public transport link to Melbourne . <p> A spokesman for Mr Sukkar confirmed the minister had met with ' ' proponents ' ' of the scheme . The Age has confirmed Mr Woodman and Ms Wreford were the proponents . He said Mr Sukkar had never received donations from Mr Woodman or related companies . <p> ' ' Ultimately any decision to approve such a development will be a matter for the state government , ' ' said the spokesman . <p> The spokesman also said the Geelong fast rail project in conjunction with the state government would cut travel times between Melbourne and Geelong and would ' ' completely transform this region through better connectivity ' ' . <p> It is not clear if Andrews government ministers other than Mr Donnellan have also been lobbied . <p> As the land in question is beyond Melbourne 's urban growth boundary it would require rezoning by the state government -- a decision that would inevitably be controversial . However Planning Minister Richard Wynne -- who would have to approve the Little River rezoning -- said he knew nothing about the scheme . <p> Mr Chua owns about 1000 hectares of land around Little River , though not all of it is slated to be part of the development . <p> In a written statement Mr Chua said the Little River project was a ' ' preliminary study to research and address today 's climate change , environmental protection and sustainable living with various experts in their fields ' ' . <p> Loading <p> ' ' I am currently seeking advice from various consultants in this initiative and as such , I have not approached any council or government bodies . ' ' Mr Chua said he had never donated to politicians or political parties.He refused to comment on Mr Woodman 's role in the scheme . <p> In the IBAC hearing on Tuesday Mr Woodman said his relationship with the Little River project had been ' ' terminated ' ' . <p> At the heart of the Casey scandal is the question of whether hundreds of thousands of dollars in payments to councillors , gifts and political donations have tainted planning decisions worth hundreds of millions of dollars . <p> Ben Schneiders is an investigative journalist at The Age and has reported extensively on wage theft , corruption , business , politics and the labour movement .
