<h> Major South West solar farm bid as WA plays catch-up to eastern states <p> A second major solar farm in Western Australia 's South West is up for approval on Monday , with the $200 million farm hoped to power about 35,000 homes , create about 250 jobs during construction -- and help WA catch up to other states embracing solar . <p> WA presently lags way behind in rooftop solar and has no large-scale solar farms , while by contrast Queensland , New South Wales and South Australia have five currently operating ranging from 124-220MW in capacity and five under construction between 330-1500MW . <p> And while WA 's proposed Asian Renewable Energy Hub wind and solar hybrid plant in the Pilbara will have a 15-gigawatt capacity , power local industry and develop a green hydrogen manufacturing hub for Asian export and domestic sale , it will be a few years before finance is locked in and up to a decade before production starts . <p> By comparison , South Energy 's two plans for the South West will come online much quicker , with the Waroona facility planned to be operational by 2022 . <p> The Benger farm is proposed for about 180 hectares of land just off Campbell Road about 15 kilometres out of Harvey . <p> It will have around 265,000 solar panels , each on an axis allowing them to follow the sun , and a substation including a battery energy storage system , a car park and office . <p> The soil 's poor drainage prevents horticulture , grapevine or intensive pasturing use , with the site only suitable for spring and summer sheep grazing . <p> But a ' strategic farming system ' will include sheep grazing around the panels during summer to help keep the grass down and retain the maximum usefulness for the land . <p> The energy will go into the Western Power network and wholesale electricity market and be sufficient to displace 210,000 tonnes of carbon dioxide emissions annually . <p> Sun Metals ' 124MW solar farm in **25;754;TOOLONG Chambers <p> It will provide at least 250 jobs during 12-18 months of construction in fencing and landscaping , civil works , mechanical installation , electrical and transportation and up to four permanent , full-time jobs once operating . <p> While less than a hectare of the site is vegetated , patches of native vegetation retained within 15-metre protection zones include high-quality habitat trees with breeding hollows that could be used by the Carnaby 's black cockatoo and western ringtail possum , which are classified as threatened and critically endangered , respectively . <p> A portion of the project 's revenue will be allocated for community use annually . <p> " The global challenge set by climate change demands to be met with definitive and effective action . With our passion and innovation , we are reinforcing the commitment towards a more sustainable Australia in the response against climate change , " the proponents wrote . <p> " We believe in generating clean and renewable energy for the local community that helps to reduce the carbon footprint on the environment . The abundance of solar energy enables us to produce green electricity for the lifetime of the project . " <p> The site as it appears now . Credit:Google <p> The project is recommended for approval subject to conditions including that screen planting should minimise the visual impact , including any potential glare or reflection , that any areas suitable for ongoing grazing should be identified , and that no works should be undertaken within the Benger Main Drain or the Wellesley River and its foreshore . <p> It was recognised that technological advancements would be made over the 30-year horizon of the proposal , and staff recommended a decommissioning plan that incorporated end of life-cycle measures for the removal of infrastructure and rehabilitation of land for agricultural use be included as a condition of approval . <p> An artist 's impression of the site as it will appear once screening trees have grown .
