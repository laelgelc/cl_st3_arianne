<h> Massive new koala habitat proposed for south-east Queensland <p> A koala habitat twice the size of the ACT will be created in south-east Queensland , outlined in the state government 's draft koala protection strategy . <p> The strategy , released on Sunday , sets aside more than 570,000 hectares of land as " koala priority areas " . <p> Officially releasing the draft strategy on Sunday , Premier Annastacia Palaszczuk said this was a " once in a generation " chance to set aside koala habitat . <p> 570,000 hectares would be set aside for koala habitat under a draft state government plan released on Sunday by Premier Annastacia PalaszczukCredit:Alex Ellinghausen <p> " About 300,000 hectares of this land is existing koala habitat , " the premier said . <p> Advertisement <p> " In the south-east over the years there has been a lot of development and that has been good to see people come to the southeast , but we have to think about what sort of south-east we want in the future . " <p> Key to the strategy is the creation of koala habitat corridors which allow the movement of the animals from one area to another without passing through developed areas or over roads where they are susceptible to cars and domestic animals . <p> Queensland Environment Minister Leeanne Enoch said the koala population in the south-east of the state was significantly under threat , and this plan would hopefully turn that around . <p> " Over the last two decades we 've seen between 50 to 80 per cent of our koala populations lost throughout our SEQ koala areas , " Ms Enoch said . <p> " On top of that three-quarters of koala habitat has been lost in SEQ - that 's why this strategy is so incredibly important . " <p> Ms Enoch thanked the koala expert panel that provided recommendations to the government through the Koala Advisory Council report . <p> A Queensland koala conservation strategy was promised in mid-2017 , under development in late 2018 , and was expected before state cabinet this year . <p> In July 2019 , Ms Enoch gave the Koala Advisory Council an extension until the " final quarter of 2019 " to finalise the koala conservation strategy . <p> State opposition leader Deb Frecklington criticised the government 's delay in releasing the strategy . <p> " I 'm quite disappointed that Annastacia Palaszczuk is three-and-a-half years late with this study , " Ms Frecklington said . <p> " We 'll look at the detail , but anything to protect the koala . " <p> Loading <p> The draft strategy will now be put to the public to have their say before the end of January 2020 , but Ms Palaszczuk said she did not expect much pushback from councils or developers . <p> " In those koala priority areas there will not be development allowed , " she said . <p> " For example you would have seen development happening around Coomera ; there is a population of koalas there that has been identified in this map . Those residents living next to that area will be living next to a koala priority area , and that is a good thing . " <p> In addition to logging and development of bushland areas , koala populations have come under pressure due to recent widespread bushfires in the region . <p> Loading <p> UQ ecologist Professor Jonathan Rhodes , who attended the government 's media conference on Sunday , said the strategy 's land use recommendations would help deal with that threat . <p> " In the long term , with increased potential for drought and heatwave and fires I think that is a challenge for koalas and dealing with those climate change impacts is critical , " Professor Rhodes said . <p> " But I think this strategy helps to deal with that by protecting that habitat and managing that habitat so koalas are more resilient to those threats . " <p> Conservation groups reacted positively to the announcement on Sunday , with Queensland Conservation Council director Louise Matthiesson saying it was a good step forward . <p> " If properly implemented , this strategy will ensure much stronger protection for koala habitat , and give our beloved koalas a real chance to survive and flourish in this region , " Ms Matthiesson said . <p> " However , it 's essential that stronger planning and development laws are introduced quickly to give effect to the strategy and put a stop to the rapid disappearance of vital koala habitat . "
