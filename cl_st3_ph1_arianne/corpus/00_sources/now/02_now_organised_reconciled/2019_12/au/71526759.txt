<p> Fatboy Slim will kick off his run of Australian headline shows in Brisbane next month but it is the gigs away from the east coast , culminating in Perth , which will undoubtedly be the best . <p> Why ? Because , at Whitfords Nodes Park in Perth and Glenelg Beach in Adelaide , they will be right on the coast . And Fatboy Slim , aka Norman Cook , has form when it comes to beach parties . <p> Fatboy Slim is on his way back to Australia . <p> " I think there 's something about a kind of inherent beauty of your surroundings , " Cook said . He would know , having spent nearly every summer in the Spanish clubbing Mecca of Ibiza , and once famously playing to a crowd of hundreds of thousands at Brighton in the UK . <p> " Especially when we 're playing music that came out in nightclubs and it 's real dark ... when you play in a field , you 're in a field and you 're at a festival and it 's great , but when you 're somewhere where you 've got natural beauty around you , it just lifts that atmosphere a little bit higher . <p> Advertisement <p> " It makes the evening more memorable and more beautiful ... and because I live on the beach in Brighton , I kind of feel at home as well . " <p> It is in Brighton that WAtoday finds Cook when he picks up the phone to chat about his upcoming ' World Tour of Australia ' and decades-long run of DJing and producing under the Fatboy Slim moniker . <p> It 's a run which has produced notorious earworms , big-beat anthems that rumble stadiums , memorable hits and even more memorable video clips to go with them ( a gravity-defying Christopher Walken , anyone ? ) <p> But for Cook , it was a " cheeky little hobby " started on the sidelines of other projects Pizzaman , Freak Power and the Mighty Dub Katz . <p> " It was n't just about me ; musically , there was kind of this reaction that house music had got a bit dull . <p> " Me and the Chemical Brothers , we were playing every weekend and the queues were going further and further around the block and we felt like we were part of some kind of movement . It was very exciting times ! " <p> Those early days eventually led to four studio albums and a slew of music which has consistently pumped through club speakers since , even as recently as 2013 's acid-tinged radio hit Eat , Sleep , Rave , Repeat and 2017 single Boom F -- king Boom . <p> But the chances of a new Fatboy album are , at least for the time being , well ... slim . <p> The drive to produce a new studio album is n't there , Cook said , and nor is the time . Having poured a lot of energy into his life as a touring DJ and other creative ventures ( such as curating an exhibit of ' smiley ' art at a recent gallery show ) means " there just are n't the hours in the day to do it " . <p> " I 'm 56 now , and at some point I 'll be too old to be travelling around the world and DJing and staying up late , and maybe when I have to come off the road then I 'll get bored , then I 'll want to go in the studio , if that 's my only outlet then I might ( record another album ) , " he said . <p> " I enjoying DJing so much ... because I can do it all around the world all year , it sort of takes up all your time or energy . <p> " If that was cut off , if that was taken away from me then maybe I 'd have to find another outlet . " <p> There was a flash of excitement and inspiration earlier this year , however , when young climate activist Greta Thunberg gave her passionate speech to the United Nation 's Climate Action Summit . <p> A clip pulled from the Swedish teen 's speech of her saying " right here , right now " was layered over the original vocals of Cook 's 1998 hit of the same name , which he then played to a crowd , drawing a rapturous response . <p> A clip of the gig soon went viral . <p> And for Cook , it was a magical moment . <p> Loading <p> " The first time I played that I nearly burst into tears because it was just so powerful and emotive that someone had linked a record I 'd done 20 years ago to something now , and something I passionately believe in , " he said . <p> " And I like that it kind of sticks it to people who do n't believe in climate change , or find Greta Thunberg annoying , it 's quite a defiant move for me as well . <p> " If you stick around long enough things come around again , and a whole new meaning has come into Right Here , Right Now . " <p> While there may not be time or energy for another studio album , there 's plenty of both when it comes to the DJ booth . <p> Cook has long been associated with the rise of the ' superstar DJ ' , and makes no attempt to hide his love affair with the turntables -- and the crowd . <p> " For me , DJing is about a conversation with the audience , " he said . <p> " It 's about looking at them and trying to probe and see what they want that will take them higher . " <p> And the experience is n't reserved for the audience alone . <p> " My personal life in the last few years has been a little topsy-turvy , " Cook said . <p> " And I 've found rather than me just standing there generating cathartic moments for the audience , I 've been doing it to myself as well . <p> " For two hours I forget all my worries and you escape into this world of loud music and dance . I 've been cathartic with myself as well as the crowd . " <p> Fatboy Slim will play at Whitfords Nodes Park in Hillarys on Sunday , February 2 , supported by 2ManyDJs -- brothers Stephen and David Dewaele as the genre-mashing DJ arm of Belgian band Soulwax .
