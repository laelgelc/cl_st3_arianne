<h> Greta Thunberg turns Donald Trump 's mocking jibe against him over Time Person of the Year honour <p> US President Donald Trump has appeared to mock Swedish teenager Greta Thunberg after she was named Time 's 2019 Person of the Year , only for her to then hit back with her own tongue-in-cheek jibe . <p> The 16-year-old climate activist was yesterday announced as the youngest ever recipient of the magazine 's prestigious honour . <p> She rose to global prominence last year by taking time off school to demonstrate outside Swedish parliament about the lack of action to combat climate change , and then famously shamed world leaders at a UN summit for their inaction . <p> Trump today hit out at the honour awarded to Thunberg , describing it as " so ridiculous " in a post to Twitter . <p> " Greta must work on her anger management problem , then go to a good old fashioned movie with a friend , " Trump , the 2016 recipient of the same honour , said . <p> " Chill Greta , chill ! " <p> Hours after the President tweeted that message , however , Thunberg changed her own Twitter bio in a tongue-in-cheek jibe at Trump . <p> Greta Thunberg turned US President Donald Trump 's own jibe at her against him by using it for her Twitter bio .
