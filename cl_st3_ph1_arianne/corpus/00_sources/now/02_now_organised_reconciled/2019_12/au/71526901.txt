<h> ' How my mother in law ruins Christmas every year ' <p> For the last five years my mother in law , Debbie , has caused so much trouble at Christmas lunch , I usually beg my husband Terry to make sure we 're away . But we never go away at Christmas time because he loves the tradition of lunch at his parents ' house . <p> Both his parents are huge drinkers . To be honest , it 's not only Xmas lunch that Debbie ruins , it 's pretty much any big occasion . Last Christmas , she got drunk and forgot to turn on the oven so the turkey was n't cooked . You 'd have thought she 'd have checked on the turkey at least once or twice but she was too busy drinking and arguing with her brother . They have the same argument every year about how she was furious with their father for leaving her brother the bulk of his inheritance . <p> It usually takes about three drinks for the argument to get started . Then , when she 's finished on that , she usually turns to politics , religion and sometimes they argue about climate change . It 's excruciating . But the worst thing that happened two years ago is when Debbie disappeared . She was very tipsy and told us she was having a nap after lunch . We assumed she 'd gone to her bedroom but , when my father in law went to check on her two hours later , she was nowhere to be seen . <p> We literally formed a search party . They live on a large property and , once we realised she was n't in the house , we broke up in to groups of three and searched -- eventually finding her in the garage , sleeping on a camping mattress . We still do n't know how and why she ended up in there . <p> It ruined our Christmas because it was stressful thinking something terrible had happened to her -- as much as she annoys me , I am very fond of her and do n't want anything bad to happen to her ! But her disappearance meant that we 'd spent the bulk of the afternoon worrying and searching , when we should have been relaxing and enjoying some Christmas pudding and spirits . <p> This year , she was relatively well behaved but she still managed to cause drama -- she accused my husband ( her son ) of " settling . " By this she meant that he should have married his girlfriend , the woman he 'd dated before he met me -- because she is very wealthy and I 'm from a working class background . She only ever makes that comment when she 's had a few champagnes . <p> My husband , Liam , has given up trying to stand up for me and so it 's always left to me to defend myself . This year , when she made the comment , I told her I was getting tired of that joke and that I find it very hurtful . That led to a lecture about how only " classy people " can appreciate jokes about themselves . <p> Another thing she likes to do is make annoying jokes about my father who is a retired politician . Whether we 're discussing bushfires or child care prices , Debbie always turns it into a joke , placing blame on my father . <p> It 's impossible to argue with her . I always try and fail miserably . Anyway this year I let it go and it felt quite freeing to not have an extended argument with her . Another thing that was good about this year was that Debbie was n't in charge of the turkey so it was beautifully cooked . <p> Next year , I 've suggested we go to a restaurant but then there 's always the risk Debbie will insist on joining us and bringing her dramas along too .
