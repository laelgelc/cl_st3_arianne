<h> The pure joy and pain of Christmas <p> Bill HoffmanWhether taking on developers hell-bent on destroying the Coast 's natural appeal or a Prime Minister indifferent to the plight of the poor , Bill Hoffman has never been one to mince his words . Bill 's been a journalist for 32 years , 29 of those on the Coast . Love him or hate him , he 'll get you blogging . <p> OUT and about on Christmas Day it was heartwarming to get the opportunity to speak with people who were making good use of the occasion . <p> The Coolum Hearts lunch at the civic centre was a bustle of laughter lit by smiling faces among those for whom it provided welcome respite from tough lives and those who embraced the pleasure that comes from helping others . <p> Newly arrived on the Coast , Mary Ann Nelson , of Yaroomba , was there with her husband and two children having seen a notice tacked to a board at Coolum Surf Club and deciding to help . <p> There 's no question that family will help strengthen the region 's community spirit which at times looks in danger of being overwhelmed by constant growth . <p> Diane Derby working alongside her radiated happiness and quiet efficiency . Clive Holden , who with his wife Barbara , founded both the Coolum Hearts group and launched the Christmas lunch six years ago after recognising the need was also in the room assisting service . <p> So too was Sunshine Coast police officer Detective Senior Sergeant Daren Edwards who was there for the first time having previously helped out at Christmas Day nursing home lunches . <p> Chef , food and wine commentator and author Peter Howard , now semi-retired to Coolum , controlled the show with his keen eye for detail and a wide smile from the pleasure of giving back for what he described as the very fortunate life he had lived . <p> The event was in sharp contrast with a Christmas Eve and morning where drunk drivers made idiots of themselves and violence came in the form of a stabbing , a broken jaw and sundry other examples of humans at their worst . <p> Rescuers called regularly to assist " adventurers " who misjudged or simply failed to consider the conditions as they attempted to climb in the Glass House Mountains , probably could have done without it on Christmas morning . <p> But there they were at Mount Beerwah as more than 50mm of rain fell in the area and water poured down the rock face in a torrent as they retrieved another broken body in need of immediate hospitalisation . <p> At Sunshine Coast University Hospital photographer John McCutcheon and I were privileged to share the early moments of Micah Brundle 's life with his mum Camille after walking through a hospital quietly humming with professionals going about their critical roles . <p> Micah goes home to the company of seven siblings all aged from 15 down , a family size Camille declared to be " probably just about right " . <p> Sunshine Coast police were true to their word mounting a very visible presence on the roads from Christmas afternoon . <p> Apart from posing a threat through speeding , motorists who failed to see traffic police radar operators less than a metre from the roadway on the Sunshine Motorway were giving an indication their concentration levels were n't what they should be . <p> The Coast 's top traffic cop , Senior Sergeant Shane Panoho of the Nambour Road Policing Unit , has been very detailed in explaining they will be out in number both on major roads and through towns as well as on the beach to Double Island Point and on Fraser Island over the break . <p> If you speed expect a ticket , if you drink-drive expect to be breath tested . <p> It 's not a case of the police being mean if drivers who fail to take heed face the full consequences of their own behaviour . <p> There 's been idiots on the road and drongos in the paddock with Barnaby Joyce confirming in a bizarre Christmas message the extent of his political demise . Unless we understand that 's got to be respected , then we 're just fools . <p> " We 're going to get nailed " . <p> Was he arguing that there 's nothing the Prime Minister could have done about the current climate change induced bushfire catastrophe and therefore he may as well have gone on vacation to Hawaii ? <p> In the absence of a leader able to find a narrative and vision that resonates with all Australians to address the future and its challenges , are we instead simply to stand quietly , looking skyward in hope while some preordained fate consumes us ?
