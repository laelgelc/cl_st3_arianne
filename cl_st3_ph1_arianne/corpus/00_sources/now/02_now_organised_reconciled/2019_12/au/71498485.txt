<p> When Shahirah Gardner , co-founder of financial management app startup Finch , started feeling the effects of burnout a few months ago , she made three simple changes to her routine in a bid to reconnect with the world outside her startup bubble . <p> However , speaking at LaunchVic 's Yeah Nah summit in Melbourne on Friday , Gardner said she " hit a low point " a few months ago . <p> " I felt that I was not in control of my day as I used to be , I was not as focused as I normally am , " she said . <p> She thought she had a good routine -- going to the gym in the morning and spending 10 to 12 hours in the office , before heading home to wolf down some dinner and go to bed . <p> " I thought that because I could do it , I should do it , " she said . <p> " That turns out not to be the case . " <p> When she realised her " head was n't right " , however , she reached out and starting talking about it to friends . <p> " The minute you start talking about it you recognise that you 're not the only person going through it , " she said . <p> Sometimes that 's enough to make you realise you have to change something , said Gardner , and speaking to other people also means you can get some advice on what to do about it . <p> Now , Gardner has made three simple changes to her routine . <h> 1 . Taking meditation seriously <p> Gardner now recognises the health and wellbeing benefits of yoga and meditation ; " that was something I started taking very seriously , " she said . <p> She started replacing her morning gym sessions with 20 minutes of yoga and 20 minutes of meditation every day . <p> " Some days I had to force myself to do it , but once you get into the habit you actually the feel difference , " she said . <h> 2 . Going home <p> The second thing Gardner did was to set herself a calendar invite for 7pm every evening , which tells her , in capital letters , to ' GO HOME ' . <p> The email forces her to consider whatever it is she 's working on and ask , " can it wait ? " And 90% of the time , " yes it can " , she said . <p> Occasionally she does still stay in the office later , but " most of the time at 7pm I get to go home , play with my puppy , have a glass of wine -- to make it feel like there 's a part of me that exists outside of the office " . <h> 3 . Getting some fresh air <p> Finally , Gardner committed to spending some time outdoors . <p> " Every Sunday I jump in the car and just drive for an hour in any direction , " she said . <p> " I love hiking , and for me that 's the automatic reset button that reminds me that there is a world outside of my Finch bubble , " she added . <p> She admitted it sounds " soppy " , but getting out into the countryside helps Gardner get some perspective . <p> " I 'm very lucky actually to be doing what I love doing and it 's very important to remind yourself of your connectedness to the world around you , " she said . <p> If you or someone you know is living with mental health issues , contact Lifeline on 13 11 14 or beyondblue on 1300 22 4636 . <p> SmartCompany is the leading online publication in Australia for free news , information and resources catering to Australia 's entrepreneurs , small and medium business owners and business managers . <p> Monday to Friday , SmartCompany.com.au publishes news , business trends and ideas from around the world , profiles and features , as well as all the latest business , tax , legal , marketing , politics and innovation developments occurring around the country . equity crowdfunding platform I co-founded , PledgeMe , just had the largest ever public equity crowdfunding campaign across Australia and New Zealand with Puro raising $4 million from the New Zealand public and wholesale investors . Expect that pool of businesses to broaden next year . <h> Education <p> Despite passing the two-year milestone in Australia , there is still work to be done to spread the potential for equity crowdfunding for smaller businesses . <p> You do n't need to be a tech startup aiming for 10x growth to get funding . Most of the businesses we 've seen be successful are often in niche areas and have already seen success through sustained growth . These businesses need investment to get to the next level , whether that 's acquiring assets to improve production or increase the size of the team . The biggest success factor that we see is the company has a crowd that loves what it does . <p> The industry needs to raise awareness of what is involved for investors too . And during your signup process investors are required to confirm they have read and understand the warning . Yes , we need to be clear , but why do we , as those running the campaigns , have to use up half of our Twitter limit ? <p> Extending campaigns because of technical issues is still a major headache , when it does n't need to be .
