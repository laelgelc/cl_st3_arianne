If you were interested in changes relevant to the climate , then you would not want to introduce non-climatic errors into your data . <p> Professor Neville Nicholls of Monash University has explained why these adjustments matter and how ignoring clear problems with data is n't how science is done . <p> Dr. Lisa Alexander , an associate professor at the University of New South Wales who deals with global datasets all the time , has told me : " Is the bureau fiddling the figures to fit with a global warming conspiracy ?
