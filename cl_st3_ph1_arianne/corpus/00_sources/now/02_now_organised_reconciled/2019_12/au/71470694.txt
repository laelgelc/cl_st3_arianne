<h> Strategic Projects Director <p> Environmental Leadership Australia is a high-impact , entrepreneurial NGO run by author and environmentalist Anna Rose that aims to build bipartisan support for climate action in Australia . You will work in our small but mighty team across four strategic projects to support a range of diverse and influential constituency groups including conservative voters , philanthropists , farmers , professional athletes and others to help build the movement to influence local , state and federal Government policies . The role is highly collaborative , working closely with external partners to drive transformative change in politics , business and culture . <p> Imagine seeing all corners of the Australian community standing up for climate action and reducing emissions in their own sectors -- from farmers and parents to athletes and the fashion industry . Imagine helping influential leaders find their way into the climate movement and helping them be strategic in making the biggest impact that they can . Imagine seeing bipartisan legislation to accelerate climate solutions passed by State Governments with the support of both sides of politics , and knowing you helped make it happen . <p> DUTIES INCLUDE : <p> Strategic advice and support for new and existing constituency-based climate organisations , including on organisational setup , campaigning and influencing , community organising and strategic planning <p> Writing proposals and progress updates for major donors and other key stakeholders <p> Managing projects including strategy , budget and timelines <p> Liaising and coordinating with other groups in the climate movement <p> Helping launch and grow Australia 's first climate advocacy-focused giving circle into a powerful community to catalyse more funding to climate organisations <p> This role is ideal for an early to mid-career professional with outstanding communication and relationship management skills who wants to make a big impact on climate change before it 's too late . We 're looking for someone who understands politics , thinks strategically and believes that transformational change is possible . It would be a great entry point into the climate movement for a management consultant , journalist or political staffer . <p> CORE COMPETENCIES <p> Political judgement/ thinks politically : You have a deep interest in politics , and at least a medium level understanding of how the Australian political system is structured and works in practice . You 're the kind of person who hates missing ' Insiders ' . <p> Written communication . You must be a ' natural writer ' who loves reading and writing .
