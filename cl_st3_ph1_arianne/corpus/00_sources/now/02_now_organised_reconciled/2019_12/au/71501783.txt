<h> Australia 's biggest forest fire ever rages on near Sydney <p> New South Wales where Sydney lies remains under a state of emergency as around 2000 firefighters struggle to limit around 100 wildfires . But there 's no way to put them out completely , only rain could do that . <h> Topics <p> Prime Minister Scott Morrison has apologised for holidaying in Hawaii while much of Australia burned , but has also stressed now is not the time for point-scoring but rather " a time to be kind to each other " . <p> The Liberal leader returned to Sydney on Saturday night and on Sunday morning confirmed he 'd been in Hawaii with his family on a trip that was planned six weeks ago . <p> " If you had your time over again and you had the benefit of hindsight we would have made different decisions , " Mr Morrison told reporters in Sydney . <p> " I am sure Australians are fair-minded and understand that when you make a promise to your kids you try and keep it . <p> Camera IconPrime Minister Scott Morrison has returned from his Hawaii trip after ' great anxiety ' among Australians . " <p> Mr Morrison said to the people he 'd upset by being away : " I apologise for that . " <p> The prime minister said he was n't a trained firefighter and obviously would n't be in the field holding a hose . <p> " But I 'm comforted by the fact that Australians would like me to be here just simply so I can be here , alongside them , as they are going through this terrible time . " <p> He said the focus should now be on the bushfire emergency . <p> " It 's time to be kind to each other , " he said . <p> " This is not a time for division , argument , partisanship or point-scoring . " <p> But on Sunday he said : " No-one wants to be out there fighting these fires . No- one wants these fires to be happening at this time . " <p> Mr Morrison said anxious Australians should be very proud they had the best- resourced and coordinated fire services in the world . <p> He then addressed critics who have demanded he take climate change more seriously . <p> The prime minister said he 'd always acknowledged the connection between climate change and bushfires but there were also " many other issues " at play , including drought , fuel-load management , lightning strikes and arson . <p> " There is no argument ... about the links between broader issues of global climate change and weather events around the world , " he said at the RFS headquarters in Sydney Olympic Park . <p> " But I 'm sure people equally would acknowledge that the direct connection to any single fire event - it 's not a credible suggestion to make that link .
