<p> Guardian Australia reported GHD 's executive general manager Phil Duthie announced the firm 's current contractual obligations with the mine had concluded in an email to staff . <p> The company has a long history with Adani and was its first project partner in Australia . <p> In a company statement , GHD confirmed it had no current contract with Adani and was not responsible for the design , infrastructure or construction of the mine . <p> The announcement does not rule out the company partnering again with Adani in the future . <p> " We will assess any future opportunities to work with Adani 's businesses , which include renewable energy projects , if and when these opportunities arise , " a company spokesman said . <p> " GHD recognises that ecological imperatives are steering the world towards a sustainable future . " <p> The Australian Conservation Foundation said GHD had listened to its staff . <p> " Any company that is considering saddling up with Adani should be clear : if you value your reputation , do n't associate it with new coal projects , " it said . <p> " GHD 's staff left the company 's leaders in no doubt that they want to be part of the climate solution , not the problem . <p> " Australians expect business to be constructive , not destructive , when it comes to climate change . That means not helping with the digging and burning of coal , oil and gas . " <p> Frontline Action on Coal spokesman Andy Paine said the end of GHD 's contractual obligations with Adani was a " victory " for the people who lobbied against the partnership . <p> " Like more than 60 companies before them , GHD have recognised what soon or later we must all realise -- that we have a moral responsibility to put the safety of our climate ahead of short-term profits , " Mr Paine said . <p> Resource Industry Network general manager Adrienne Rourke said the finalisation of GHD 's contract with Adani was a standard procedure of working on a major mining project . <p> " This is everyday business , " Ms Rourke said , " It 's just a normal part of the project " . <p> She said the idea that the end of the Adani contact was due to targeted activism or political pressure was an exaggeration by people " twisting the facts to their story " . <p> Ms Rourke said people involved in the resource industry were not fooled by these " rumours " . <p> " They know the devil is in the detail , " she said " and they 're just getting on with the job " . <p> Ms Rourke said residents from the Mackay , Isaac and Whitsunday region were tired of the Adani mine being used as a political football . <p> " Everyone just wants to get on with the job , " she said . <p> A #StopAdani campaign directed more than 80,000 emails and calls to GHD and inspired 150 protests calling for the company to rule out working with Adani . <p> Queensland Resources Council CEO Ian Macfarlane said the group looked forward to the completion of Adani 's Carmichael mine which was " already creating jobs for regional Queenslanders and once complete will deliver lower-emissions coal to India " . <p> " Each resources project creates more than just the mining jobs onsite . There are also flow-on jobs for regional communities , " he said . <p> " For example , in the case of Adani Mining there have already been more than $500 million in contracts awarded with benefits for local businesses including in Townsville , Mackay , Rockhampton and Toowoomba . " <p> The Guardian reported GHD planned to transition away from its fossil fuel clients as it moved into the future . <p> Mounting pressure from environmental groups has pushed more than 60 companies , including Cardno and Downer , either ruling out or publicly distancing themselves from the project . <p> Recently pressure has been strongest on GHD , Siemens and BMD with activists representing various green groups staging sit-ins or protests across the country . <p> As well as a barrage on social media , activists held protests within Siemens ' offices across the country and were accused of accused of publishing details of its employees .
