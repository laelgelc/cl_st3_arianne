<h> More <h> Nothing to see here , Porter says of phone call <p> Attorney-General Christian Porter said there was nothing untoward about him sitting in on a telephone call last week between Scott Morrison and NSW Police Commissioner Mick Fuller concerning Energy Minister Angus Taylor . <p> In doing so , Mr Porter attacked his opposition counterpart Mark Dreyfus for initiating the police investigation into Mr Taylor , saying it was the eighth time Mr Dreyfus had requested the police investigate a political opponent . <p> Mr Porter predicted this would turn up " a big fat nothing ' ' , just like the other seven . <p> " A serial offender in terms of vexatious complaints that waste the time of the AFP when the AFP could be using their time looking into things like drug dealing or terrorism , or child sex offences . What a joke , ' ' he said . <p> Mr Taylor has been under pressure in Parliament for taking pot shots at City of Sydney council over climate change using figures that were wrong . <p> Advertisement <p> The saga escalated last week when the NSW Police said they were investigating Mr Taylor , and Mr Morrison personally rang the commissioner Mr Fuller to ascertain the seriousness of the probe . <p> Based on that call , he rejected demands by Labor that Mr Taylor be stood aside . <p> This led to allegations that Mr Morrison had misused his office . On the weekend , it was revealed Mr Porter sat in on the call . <p> Mr Porter backed a statement by Mr Fuller that nothing the Prime Minister asked was inappropriate . <p> Labor continued its pursuit of Mr Taylor on Monday , this time accusing him of a failure to fully disclose his shareholdings on the pecuniary interests register . <p> Mr Taylor has disclosed shares in a company called Guffee Pty Ltd and disclosed that Guffee has a interest in Farm Partnerships Australia .
