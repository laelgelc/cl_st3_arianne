<p> If the last decade was dominated by climate change and the push-back against globalisation , then in the years to 2030 these issues will only be magnified . <p> Advancements in artificial intelligence are set to spread from the factory floor to the office cubicle , potentially displacing lower-level white-collar jobs after decimating industrial heartlands in the US , UK and to a lesser degree Australia . <p> At the same time , the 2020s will be marked by the need for climate change mitigation , along with action , as changing weather patterns deliver more frequent droughts , floods and storm surges . <p> It 's hardly a cheery outlook . <p> The list of people to watch for this decade , which is understandably younger , but also more diverse than those who dominated last decade , reflects these two mega-trends . <p> The politicians who made the list will be responsible for allocating additional money for firefighters , water pipelines and new infrastructure to climate-proof cities . At the same time , they will need to plug revenue holes from declining fossil fuel use -- petrol and diesel excise is worth $17 billion annually . <p> In parallel the labour market looks to be stagnating , as the once futuristic idea of computers taking our jobs begins to materialise . <p> That will not only breed resentment from workers frustrated at flat wages , but put more pressure on business . On the bright side , it could create jobs we have n't even thought about yet . <p> The corporate leaders on the list will be straddling these twin issues , while adjusting to a new age of tougher regulation and seeking to rebuild the sector 's reputation after a decade of scandals . <p> Away from business and politics , on the sporting field , there 's Ashleigh Barty , the world 's best female tennis player , an Indigenous role model and a low-key , soft power ambassador for Australia . <p> Advertisement <h> Scott Morrison <p> Within the portion of the Canberra bubble populated by Scott Morrison 's supporters there is chatter of the Prime Minister ruling for another two terms . <p> That would make him the dominant figure of the next decade , but well before that title is bestowed , Morrison must navigate some of the trickiest political terrain any leader has faced . <p> Scott Morrison has focused on middle Australia , but it will be the big picture issues which define his prime ministership .
