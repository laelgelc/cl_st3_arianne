<p> A handful of major polluters including Australia have seen off efforts to ramp up emissions reduction targets at the United Nations climate summit in Madrid . The talks finished two days late on Sunday afternoon with countries failing to ramp up targets in line with what is needed to achieve the objectives of the Paris Climate Accord . Australia saw off efforts to prohibit its use of a loophole that allows it to apply " carry-over credits " from the previous Kyoto agreement towards its goal of reducing emissions to 26-28 per cent below 2005 levels by 2030 . Doing so means Australia would only have to reduce emissions by 15 per cent . " If you want this carry-over it is just cheating , " Laurence Tubiana , a key architect of the Paris accord , told the Financial Times ( $ ) . " Australia was willing in a way to destroy the whole system , because that is the way to destroy the whole Paris agreement .
