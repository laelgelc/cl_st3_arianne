<h> ' I was flat broke , living week to week ' : Aussie sailor Lisa Blair on the moment that changed her life <p> When it comes to setting goals and achieving them , even surpassing them , Sunshine Coast woman Lisa Blair is up there with the best . <p> And I 'm not talking about the goals most of us set for ourselves - getting up 10 minutes earlier each day to mediate , putting our dry cleaning away as soon as we pick it up , eating less carbs - I 'm talking about breaking sailing records and winning yacht races . <p> While the rest of us are deciding on which glaze to use to bake our hams for Christmas , Blair , 35 , is overseeing last-minute adjustments to her boat ahead of the Melbourne to Hobart Yacht Race , held simultaneously with the Sydney to Hobart Yacht Race and the Launceston to Hobart Yacht Race , all three finishing at the same location on the same day . <p> Lisa Blair will race in the Melbourne to Hobart Yacht Race this December . ( Supplied ) <p> " This race can be more dangerous than the Sydney to Hobart race depending on the weather , " she tells 9Honey . " There 's all the storms in the Southern Ocean near the bottom of Tasmania and heading up the River Derwent . " <p> Not content to race in the event with the usual team , the yachtswoman and Australian adventurer has decided she 'll participate with fellow competitor Jacqui Perry as the first double-handed team . <p> That means just two sailors operating the vessel . <p> Blair and Perry will be the first all female double-handed team to enter the event in 49 years , and she 's doing it by choice , for the extra challenge . <p> It 's the kind of thinking that has landed her multi-records including being the first woman to sail solo around Antarctica , the first woman to sail solo and no-stop around Australia and the first all-female team in 16 years to race the Rolex 2017 Sydney to Hobart Yacht Race . <p> Blair 's newly re-titled yacht d'Albora/Climate Action Now is her pride and joy and it 's ready to take on the challenge . She also uses her vessel to try and promote positive actions by those concerned about climate change . <p> Blair has her sights set on new goals in 2020 . ( Supplied ) <p> " I asked people through Climate Action Now to write out notes for hull of the boat about the actions they are taking for the environment , " she says . " I see first-hand sailing around the world the rubbish in the water . <p> " Actions can be a simple as picking up rubbish at the beach , turning lights off when you leave the room or using public transport instead of driving , " she says . <p> While her mother had been a sailor , Blair did n't take it up until she was in her early twenties while working on a yacht in the Whit Sundays . <p> " I see first-hand sailing around the world the rubbish in the water . " <p> " I was there working for three months and I just fell in love with the lifestyle and the sense of adventure and being part of that world , " she says . <p> She took to the sport like a natural , thriving under the pressure that comes with it and always looking for new challenges . <p> Her strength may have come from her upbringing on the Sunshine Coat in Queensland . <p> She has been sailing since her early twenties and has set records every since . ( Supplied ) <p> " We lived on 20 acres and our home operated on solar power , " she says . " We only had enough power to watch one movie a week and we would get teased a lot at school because we did n't grow up watching the latest episodes of the shows everyone else was . " <p> Her first sailing adventure was with a friend from Samoa to Hawaii in 2004 -- that first ocean crossing left her wanting more . <p> " I met the boat in Samoa and then we sailed off across the Pacific through remote islands , " she says . " During my first night watch I fell in love with it , the perfect winds , the boat sailing beautifully on a completely clear night under the stars . <p> " I could n't see the whales but I could hear them swimming with the boat each night . " <p> Blair began working odd jobs with the sole purpose of saving up to participate in more sailing adventures . <p> She immersed herself in the lifestyle , reading books about Jesse Martin , Jessica Watson and Kay Cottee . She caught the ' solo bug ' and began planning even more challenging adventures for herself . <p> From racing to seeing the stars from the middle of the ocean . ( Supplied ) <p> Yachting is an expensive exercise , particularly the adventures Blair sets out for herself . <p> " I was flat broke , living week to week , " she says of the time before gaining sponsorship . <p> She read about an event called Clipper Round The World , described as one of the biggest challenges of the natural world and an endurance test like no other . <p> For Clipper Round The World , Blair says she spent a year fundraising and trying to get sponsorship . <p> The rest , as they say , is history . <p> Now with so many records under her belt , sponsorship is no longer a problem but the challenges remain . <p> Since the beginning of her adventures Blair says she has sailed in " the worst " conditions including hurricane-force winds , snow storms , blizzards and " waves as tall as a four-story building " . <p> The weather has n't gotten the better of her yet , and has provided her with the perfect training for this coming event , although she is n't expecting any dangerous conditions . <p> " We 're expecting fairly light conditions and we plan to go out and have a lot of fun , give it our all and try the best we can , " she says .
