<h> Harry and Meghan get to keep their royal titles <p> Prince Harry and Meghan Markle are still the Duke and Duchess of Sussex , following a failed petition to strip them of the titles . <p> Residents of Sussex county started a petition earlier this year in a bid to strip the royals of their titles , which tie them to the county , and received more than 3,800 signatures . <p> Anti-monarchist campaigner Charles Ross , who kicked off the petition , claimed that the decision to give Harry and Meghan the titles was a " symbol of oppression by the wealthy elite " and " morally wrong " . <p> Prince Harry and Meghan Markle will keep their royal titles following the failed petition . ( Sipa USA ) <p> However , the petition , which received more support than Ross expected , was shot down at a council meeting last night as not being a proper use of council time and resources . <p> " I am aware that there are passionately held views on either side of the argument about the monarchy which are legitimate , " council leader Nancy Platts said at a full council meeting on Friday . <p> " However , that is not a matter for local authorities and we obviously do not have the power to remove titles . That is a matter for the Crown . " <p> She went on to say that while she " respects " the views held by people both for and against the petition , arguing about Harry and Meghan 's titles simply was n't " proper use of council time and resources . <p> Harry and Meghan received their titles from the Queen on their wedding day . ( PA/AAP ) <p> " The council has a number of pressing issues it has to prioritise , such as homelessness , climate change , the effects of austerity , ensuring the economic and social wellbeing of the city 's residents , " she said , before moving on . <p> Harry and Meghan were given the titles of Duke and Duchess of Sussex after their wedding in 2018 , as it is customary for royal men and their wives to receive a title on their wedding day . <p> The prince also holds Scottish and Northern Irish titles as the Earl of Dumbarton and Baron Kilkeel , both of which also pass to Meghan , making her the duchess of each .
