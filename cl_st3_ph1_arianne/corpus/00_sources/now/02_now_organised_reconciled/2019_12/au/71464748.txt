<h> Voting and fires : the top searches of 2019 <p> Camera Icon'Fires Near Me ' was one of the top searches in Australia in 2019 , as many areas suffered bushfires . <p> Voting may be compulsory in Australia , but it appears many of us do n't know how to . <p> Google revealed on Wednesday the most popular search terms for 2019 , which took in both the federal and NSW elections . <p> Half of the ' how to ' questions were related to politics , with ' how to vote ' topping the list , followed by ' how to vote Labor 2019 ' , ' how to vote Liberal ' , ' how to vote in NSW State Election ' and ' how to vote early ' . <p> ' Fires near me ' was the number one trending search as Australia experienced one of its worst bushfire seasons on record , while top trending news searches also included ' NSW fires , ' ' Cyclone Oma ' and ' Townsville flooding . ' <p> The most popular Australian was tennis world No.1 Ash Barty , followed by former Senator Fraser Anning and Israel Folau . <p> Globally the biggest names were YouTube make-up blogger James Charles , 17-year-old pop star Billie Eilish and Jordyn Woods , the latest villain in the Kardashian family drama . <p> In fourth place was climate change activist Greta Thunberg , while Prince Andrew came 10th in the wake of a disastrous interview about his association with convicted sex offender Jeffrey Epstein . <p> Marvel 's blockbuster Avengers : Endgame stole not just the box office but two places in the top 10 searches . <p> Beyond our borders , Australians showed concern for the Amazon and asked why it was burning , tried to make sense of attacks in Christchurch and Sri Lanka and asked ' why Hong Kong is protesting ' . <p> The rankings are based not on total searches but on what had the highest spike in traffic over a sustained period in 2019 as compared to 2018.
