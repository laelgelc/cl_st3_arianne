<h> Labor seeks ' grown-up ' political debate <p> Anthony Albanese has called for parliament to debate any decision to go to war , as part of a broader plan to improve trust in Australia 's democracy . <p> In his third major speech since assuming the leadership , Mr Albanese set out a plan to restore integrity and accountability while improving the tone of debate . <p> The opposition leader also urged social media giants like Facebook to crack down on fake news . <p> He pledged to work with media companies and journalists to enshrine in law protections of press freedom . <p> Mr Albanese said parliament " at the very least " should debate any cabinet decision to go to war - something coalition governments have been reluctant to do . <p> " We ca n't ask people to put their lives on the line if we as legislators are too afraid to put our arguments on the line . If democracy has real meaning , it is in moments like this , " Mr Albanese told the Towards 2022 forum in Sydney on Saturday . <p> Bob Hawke allowed two days of parliamentary debate after his cabinet decided to join the first Gulf War . <p> " In a world that 's being revolutionised by science and technology , and threatened by a changing climate , what sort of country treats its scientists , educators and firefighters like enemies of the people ? " he asked . <p> " The answer is : one that will have fewer jobs , a lower standard of living and a more dangerous environment in the decades to come . " <p> Labor took an electoral hit in Queensland over the party 's confusing stance on the coal industry . <p> Mr Albanese urged those who advocate change to understand the viewpoints of " those who will feel insecure by that change " . <p> He said Labor stood with media companies in their united campaign to strengthen press freedom . <p> " Journalism is not a crime . It 's essential to preserving our democracy . We do n't need a culture of secrecy . We need a culture of disclosure , " he said . <p> Mr Albanese also took a shot at Facebook founder Mark Zuckerberg for not doing enough to combat fake news . <p> " What if Facebook 's laws of the jungle trump Australia 's laws of the land ? " he said . <p> He said constitutional reform to give indigenous people a voice to parliament was a bigger priority than making Australia a republic . <p> As well , a national integrity commission - with the powers of a standing royal commission - was needed to " root out corruption in the federal sphere " . <p> Shadow treasurer Jim Chalmers also addressed the conference , arguing the party needs to be more inclusive . <p> " When it comes to policy , we can do big things slowly or small things quickly , but not big things quickly , " he said . <p> " And starting - every time - with a focus on economic growth . " <p> ALP national president Wayne Swan said economic inequality and climate change were the defining issues of the generation .
