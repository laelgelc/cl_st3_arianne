<h> NZ launches Climate Change Commission <p> Expert , trusted and independent advice will be essential to ensuring New Zealand plays its part in solving the challenge of climate change . This week , the Minister for Climate Change , James Shaw announced the team of experts who now have the important task of providing governments with that advice . <p> " Some issues are too big for politics , and the biggest of all is the climate crisis we face . Our decision to create the Climate Change Commission was about protecting climate policy from political mood swings , meaning every future government can stay focused on the job at hand : to help solve climate change and make our communities are cleaner and healthier , " James Shaw said . <p> The creation of an independent , non-political body to monitor and advise on how governments can meet New Zealand 's climate change targets was one of the most important parts of the Zero Carbon Act . <p> " We provided a bold legislative framework for what we need to do build a climate-friendly future for New Zealand ; it is the Commission who will now advise us on how best to do that , " James Shaw said . <p> The six commissioners announced are experts in the fields of climate science , adaptation , agriculture , economics , and the Maori-Crown relationship . <p> " Looking ahead to a future where we have met our targets and done all we can to solve climate change , it will be in no small part thanks to the Climate Change Commission . <p> " Whilst it is an advisory body , I fully expect that the impartial and scientifically rigorous analysis it will provide will help keep future governments ' climate policy in check , " James Shaw said . <p> The Commission will pick up the work started by the Interim Climate Change Committee , which was appointed in May 2018 . <p> " I would like to take this opportunity to thank the interim commission for their hard work , analytical honesty and rigour . What they have achieved in such a short period of time will have a lasting impact on the future of climate policy , " James Shaw said . <p> Today 's announcement confirms that , in addition to Dr. Rod Carr , who was appointed as the Chair-designate for the Climate Change Commission in October , Ms. Lisa Tumahai will continue in her role as Deputy Chairperson . <p> In addition to Dr. Carr and Ms. Tumahai , the remaining Commissioners are : <p> Dr. Harry Clark , a New Zealand expert on agricultural greenhouse gas research <p> Dr. Judith Lawrence , a thought leader with international expertise in climate change adaptation <p> Ms. Catherine Leining , a leading New Zealand economist on climate policy and emissions pricing systems <p> Professor James Renwick , a leading climate scientist and a recipient of the Prime Minister 's Science Prize for Communication <p> Professor Nicola Shadbolt , a farmer , company director and academic , with expertise in land use and land-use change .
