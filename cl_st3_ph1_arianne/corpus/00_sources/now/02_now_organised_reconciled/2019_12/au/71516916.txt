<p> HOW does the average farmer - and local councillor for that matter - understand the science and predictions of climate change ? Spoiler alert : not real well , it seems . Following some heated debate in the Bathurst Council chamber on November 20 , John Seaman reminded us in his following weekly column of some past big drought events around Federation and World War Two . The same point was made in the chamber : that we have had bad droughts in the past , so climate change must be fake news . You know the line : the climate is always changing . In simple terms , this is true - no scientist denies it - but this simplification completely misses the mark on what science is telling us . READ ALSO : Jennings echoes climate change concerns detailed in new report The critical problem we face is that the frequency of drought is set to increase because the climate is ( already ) getting hotter and more dry largely because eight billion humans burn a heap of coal and oil ( among other things ) for energy . Citing past single weather events like drought basically tells you nothing about the warming trend in our climate that is affecting our farmers ( and firies ) earlier and harder than most Australian livelihoods . Although the point does tell you one thing for certain : any person who quotes past single events to claim climate change is fake news has just proved they do not know or understand the bare basics of the relevant science regarding the theory and empirical analysis of climate change . READ ALSO : Jennings says farmers are being let down by lack of action on climate change I can understand the general public not getting across the fundamentals of climate science partly because it 's been so horribly politicised , but also because it 's a complex topic and a rational , step-by-step presentation of scientific facts is seldom seen in the media , and then there 's fake news social media like we have never seen before . It 's amazing to think that we have a retired school teacher in Cr Graeme Hanger in our chamber who , I believe , denigrates the value of education by openly ignoring and actually rejecting the prevailing scientific knowledge by saying our climate is fine and dandy with absolutely nothing to worry about - which is again a tell-tale sign someone has not done their homework on climate science .
