<h> 56 polar bears descend on village <p> MOSCOW : More than 50 polar bears have gathered on the edge of a village in Russia 's far north , environmentalists and residents said , as weak Arctic ice leaves them unable to roam . <p> The Russian branch of the World Wildlife Fund said climate change was to blame , as unusually warm temperatures prevented coastal ice from forming . <p> The WWF said 56 polar bears had gathered in a one-square-kilometre area near the village of Ryrkaipy in Chukotka on the northeastern tip of Russia . <p> There were concerns they could enter the village , home to fewer than 1,000 people , and patrols had been set up to monitor their movements . <p> " The number of human and predator encounters in the Arctic is increasing , " the WWF said in statement . <p> This handout photo taken on December 3 , 2019 and released by the World Wildlife Fund -- Russia ( WWF-Russia ) shows polar bears outside the village of Ryrkaypiy in the Chukotka region . -- More than 50 polar bears have gathered on the edge of a village in Russia 's far north , environmentalists and residents said , as weak coastal ice leaves them unable to roam . ( Photo by Maksim DYOMINOV / World Wildlife Fund -- Russia / AFP ) <p> " The main reason is the decline of sea ice area due to the changing climate . In the absence of ice cover , animals are forced to go ashore in search of food . " <p> Residents had gathered walrus carcasses in the area to try to keep the bears from wandering into the village . <p> " We have created a feeding point with walrus carcasses that we gathered along the coast , " Tatyana Minenko of the local " Bear Patrol " told news agency RIA Novosti . <p> " As long as there is no big freeze , the sea ice will not form and the bears will stay on the coast , " she said . <p> Russia 's weather service said temperatures in the region should fall from Saturday and that coastal ice should freeze by December 11 .
