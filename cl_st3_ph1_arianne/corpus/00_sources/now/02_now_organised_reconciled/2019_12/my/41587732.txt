<h> New house from TNB , SESB , precious new year gift for a family <p> KOTA BELUD ( Dec 23 ) : There 's no other more precious gift for a husband and wife in Kampung Labuan , near here , when their dream to own a house from 10 years ago , was fulfilled through the Baiti Jannati Project ( PBJ ) . <p> The corporate social responsibility ( CSR ) project , implemented by Tenaga Nasional Bhd ( TNB ) in collaboration with Sabah Electricity Sdn Bhd ( SESB ) , had given a new ray of hope to Rusdi Karim and his wife Norijan Lamjin , both 32 , and their three children , aged between three and seven years old , to live a more secure and comfortable life . <p> Norijan , when met after the house key replica handing over ceremony , expressed her appreciation and thanked TNB and SESB at their concern to provide a new house for her family , which she regarded as a " new year gift " . <p> " Thank you to TNB and SESB . I am very grateful to God at this good fortune . I am indeed very happy and relieved , because we finally have our own home after waiting for many years . This is our family 's luck , " she said . <p> The handing over ceremony was officiated by Sabah Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Deputy Minister Isnaraissah Munirah Majilis , who is also the Kota Belud Member of Parliament . Also present was SESB Corporate Communications general manager Chendramata Sinteh . <p> The construction of the three-room house with a living room , kitchen and toilet started in mid-October at a cost of RM50,000 from TNB through a zakat ( tithe ) concept and recipients were selected based on the criteria set by the utility company . <p> Meanwhile , Isnaraissah said the initiative to build a new house for the family could to a certain extent change their living environment and conditions and hoped that TNB and SESB could continue these CSR programmes . <p> She also encouraged other government-linked companies to take similar steps to help the government implement beneficial programmes for the people . <p> In the meantime , Chendramata said Rusdi was selected to receive PBJ assistance after SESB , with the co-operation of the Community Development Leaders Unit in the area , identified him as a rural poor who had no fixed income and was listed in e-Kasih . <p> This year , Chendramata said , TNB had allocated RM300,000 to build homes for six recipients under the CSR programmes . Three were PBJ recipients from the Muslim community through the zakat concept while the rest were non-Muslims through ' Projek Mesra Rakyat .
