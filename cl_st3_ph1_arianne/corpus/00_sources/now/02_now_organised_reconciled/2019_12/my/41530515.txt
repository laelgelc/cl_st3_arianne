<p> KUALA LUMPUR -- Defence Minister Mohamad Sabu today tabled a white paper to chart Malaysia 's defence plans for the next 10 years , citing increasing terrorism threats , the military tension in the South China Sea , cybersecurity attacks , and a rise in human and drug trafficking . <p> He said while the tension between the US and China continues , Southeast Asian countries also have an impact on Malaysia . <p> " Apart from territorial disputes , Malaysia 's interests in the region are also influenced by several other bilateral issues which could see overlapping demands , the spillover effect of neighbouring internal conflicts , and the refugee crisis triggered by countries ' domestic issues , " he said in the Dewan Rakyat . <p> He said Malaysia also faces non-traditional threats across its borders including the threat of terrorism and extremism which he said is on the rise . <p> He said the magnitude and shape of the threats are in line with current developments in neighbouring countries , speaking of fighters returning to Southeast Asia and forging links with local terrorists . <p> He also spoke of efforts to spread Islamic State narratives which could influence lone wolf attacks . <p> Mohamad , popularly known as Mat Sabu , also referred to cyberspace as " the new domain of national security and geopolitics " . <p> He said space has the potential to disrupt national security without the need for perpetrators to resort to physical actions . <p> " Advances in technology such as the internet of things , cloud computing , big data , deep learning , 5G technology and artificial intelligence have exposed military operations to cyber threats , " he added . <p> Mohamad also spoke on climate change which may see Malaysia being affected by natural disasters , threatening its safety , health and development . <p> To protect the nation 's borders , he said , integrated layer areas will be observed at all times . The first includes the terrain on the mainland as well as in Sabah and Sarawak , the waterfront and the airspace . <p> More advanced areas include sovereign rights such as the maritime zone , strategic waterways , airspace and critical communications links and locations that extend beyond these areas as part of national interests . <p> He said an important part of the armed forces ' future development is long-term investment in improving assets and equipment , to have a knowledge-based workforce and relevant skills among armed forces personnel . <p> These include developing cyber electromagnetic activity capabilities , and improving intelligence and monitoring . <p> Mohamad said the government also wants to develop better operations , have better satellite communications to enhance command and control capabilities , and increase maritime combat capabilities . <p> As for land , he said the white paper seeks to improve mobility , communications and logistics capabilities . <p> He said the government will continue to look after the welfare of military veterans as a token of appreciation for their sacrifices . <p> " The government 's main focus is to enhance the socio-economic status of veterans through second career opportunities and other social needs such as healthcare and related assistance including living expenses and schooling .
