<h> Greta calls 2020 ' year of action ' against climate change <p> TURIN : Environmental activist Greta Thunberg urged young people in Italy on Friday to seize the next decade to fight climate change , and chastised world leaders for acting " as if there is no tomorrow . " <p> The Swedish 16-year-old -- who this week was named Time magazine 's 2019 Person of the Year -- reminded young activists at the " Fridays for Future " rally in the northern Italian city of Turin to make 2020 a " year of action . " <p> " In less than three weeks we will move into a new decade and ... I can not say enough how important this decade will be , " said Thunberg , bundled up against the cold in a yellow rain slicker . <p> " This is a decade that will define our future . " <p> Thunberg slammed world leaders currently attending the UN Climate Change Conference in Madrid , saying they were " still trying to run away from their responsibilities . " <p> Protesters hold signs reading " Stop fossil fuels " , " Stop pollution of rivers and seas " and " Stop destruction of forests " as they attend a Fridays for Future protest in Turin , Italy . -Reuters <p> " It is not fair that the old generations are handing over the responsibility to solve this crisis to the young people who have not started this crisis . It is not fair that we have to do all this , " she said . <p> " The adults are behaving as if there is no tomorrow , " Thunberg said , cautioning that youth can " no longer take that tomorrow for granted . " <p> The teenager rose to the global stage after launching a solo strike against global warming in mid-2018 which surged into a worldwide movement that has seen her tipped as a Nobel laureate . <p> After a months-long journey through North America to raise awareness of environmental issues , Thunberg returned to Europe earlier this month after crossing the Atlantic via catamaran . The teenager does not fly due to the heavy carbon footprint of air travel . <p> In naming her Person of the Year , Time magazine wrote that Thunberg " has offered a moral clarion call to those who are willing to act , and hurled shame on those who are not . " --AFP
