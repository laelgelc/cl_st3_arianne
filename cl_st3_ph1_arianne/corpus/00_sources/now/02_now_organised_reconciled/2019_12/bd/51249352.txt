<h> Disparity affects economic growth : Mannan <p> Planning Minister MA Mannan on Monday voiced concern over the growing disparity in different sectors , saying it can harm the wave of economic growth . <p> He made the comments while addressing the closing session of the two-day Research Almanac 2019 organised by Bangladesh Institution of Development Studies ( BIDS ) at a city hotel . <p> Describing agriculture as the backbone of Bangladesh 's economy , he said the government is working to improve the sector . " We 'll make compromise to give subsidy to the agriculture sector , " he said . <p> Mannan also said Bangladesh 's position in the ease of doing business index is very poor . <p> He , however , said the government is working to improve the index creating better business environment . <p> Acknowledging that there some loopholes in different sectors , the minister sought suggestions from experts to plug those . <p> Dr Zahid Hussain , former lead economist of World Bank , said Bangladesh is expected to achieve the sustainable development goals ( SDGs ) by 2030 and become a rich country by 2041 . <p> " There 're two separate elements such as growth and governance . Growth is a wheel while governance a driver . We have to improve the infrastructure , including transportation , information , industries , agriculture and human resources , to this end , " he added . <p> Dr Zahid underscored the need for good governance to continue the growth . <p> He said new window of development will open in the future if skilled manpower and infrastructure can be created through reforms . " Besides , intellectual-based competition is needed here . " <p> The economist , however , identified some challenges such as climate change , vulnerable population , soil , air and water pollution Bangladesh is currently facing . <p> Dr Rizwanul Islam , former special adviser ( Eemployment ) of International Labour Organization ( ILO ) , said there are challenges , including increasing disparity between rich and poor and its impact on the job market . <p> Bangladesh faces challenge for the Fourth Industrial Revolution ( 4IR ) and it should devise strategy on how to adopt the technology , he said .
