<h> PM flies to Spain tomorrow to join COP25 <p> File photo of Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina . <p> BSS , Dhaka <p> Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina will leave Dhaka for Madrid tomorrow on a three-day official visit to Spain to join the " Heads of State and Government Summit " of the 25th Annual Conference of Parties ( COP25 ) . <p> The COP25 of the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) will be held on December 2-13 in Madrid under the Presidency of Chile and with logistical support from Spain . <p> A Biman Bangladesh Airlines special flight carrying the premier and her entourage members will take off from Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport ( HSIA ) here for Madrid tomorrow morning . <p> The flight is scheduled to land at Madrid Torrejon Airport at 5:40pm ( local time ) . <p> Ambassador and Permanent Representative of Bangladesh to the World Tourism Organization Hassan Mahmood Khandker will receive the prime minister at the airport . <p> After the reception at the airport , a ceremonial motorcade will escort the prime minister to Hotel Villa Magna in Madrid where she will be staying during her visit to Spain . <p> On Monday morning , the prime minister will attend the inaugural ceremony of the COP25 at Feria de Madrid , the largest exhibition complex in Spain and one of the most important venues in Europe . <p> Later , she will join the Working Session of the COP25 and hold a bilateral meeting with Dutch Prime Minister Mark Rutte . <p> Sheikh Hasina will attend the official luncheon and will again join the Working Session in the afternoon . <p> She is expected to hold a bilateral meeting with Spanish President Pedro Sanchez . <p> The prime minister will join a reception to be hosted by the King and the Queen of Spain at Royal Palace on Monday evening . <p> Wrapping up her three-day tour , Sheikh Hasina will depart from Madrid for Dhaka by a Biman Bangladesh Airlines flight at 9am on Tuesday ( local time ) . <p> The flight is scheduled to land at Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport ( HSIA ) here at 12:40am on Wednesday . <p> The 25th United Nations Climate Change Conference , known as COP25 , will incorporate the 25th Conference of the Parties to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) , the 15th meeting of the parties for the Kyoto Protocol ( CMP15 ) , and the second meeting of the parties for the Paris Agreement ( CMA2 ) . <p> The President-Designate for the conference is Ms. Carolina Schmidt Zaldivar , Minister of Environment of Chile . <p> The conference is designed to take the next crucial steps in the UN climate change process . Following agreement on the implementation guidelines of the Paris Agreement at COP 24 in Poland last year , a key objective is to complete several matters with respect to the full operationalization of the Paris Climate Change Agreement . <p> The conference was planned to be held in Brazil in November 2019 , but a year before its planned start , newly-elected President Jair Bolsonaro withdrew the offer to host the event , citing economic reason . <p> Then Chile stepped up and became the new host , but social unrest in the lead up to the meeting forced it late October 2019 to withdraw from hosting . <p> Then by mutual agreement between the UN , Chile , and Spain , the latter became the new host .
