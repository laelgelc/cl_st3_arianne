<h> Catastrophic fire warnings are in place across the Australian state of New South Wales ( NSW ) , where firefighters are tackling more than 100 blazes . <p> Authorities have urged Christmas travellers to delay their journeys as conditions threaten to intensify the bushfire crisis . <p> Rising temperatures and strong winds worsened fires across three states on Saturday . <p> Three blazes around Sydney are burning at emergency level . <p> " We are asking everybody not to travel on roads anywhere near the vicinity of an active fire unless you absolutely have to , " Gladys Berejiklian , premier of NSW , said at a news conference . <p> A combination of temperatures above 40C , low humidity and strong winds have worsened the struggle for the almost 10,000 emergency personnel mobilised to deal with the bushfires in NSW . <p> " We are in a period of unbelievable drought and some areas have n't seen rain for more than 12 months " , NSW Rural Fire Services Inspector Ben Shepherd told the BBC . <p> " These fires are likely to continue to spread well past Christmas " , he added . <p> Two volunteer firefighters died on Thursday as they were tackling a large blaze near Sydney . Fires have also been ravaging the states of Victoria and South Australia , where two civilians died on Friday . <p> Since September , Australia 's bushfire emergency has killed eight people , destroyed more than 700 houses and scorched millions of hectares . <p> What travel warnings have been issued ? <p> The states of NSW and and South Australia are the focus of the travel warnings . <p> In NSW , several major roads and highways have been closed , including a section of the Princes Highway . <p> As of 16:30 ( 05:12 GMT ) , 24 roads and highways in NSW were affected by fire , according to a government website . <p> Roads have been closed in South Australia as well , with residents asked to monitor the government traffic website for updates . <p> " If members of the public attempt to enter the areas , they will be turned away , regardless of being property owners or business owners , due to specific safety reasons , " a police official said . <p> Who are the victims ? <p> Tributes have been paid to firefighters Geoffrey Keaton , 32 , and Andrew O'Dwyer , 36 , who died when their truck was hit by a falling tree near a fire front , causing it to roll off the road . <p> Three other firefighters who were also in the vehicle survived with minor injuries . <p> NSW fire commissioner Shane Fitzsimmons said firefighters everywhere were grieving over the " huge loss " of the two young fathers , who were caught up in " the worst imaginable set of circumstances " . <p> " They simply went out , doing a remarkable job , like all their colleagues , and like they have done year-in , year-out , and to not be coming home after their shift is a tremendous grief , " he said . <p> South Australian Premier Steven Marshall said a person had been killed in Murraylands , when the car they were driving hit a tree . Another person died in the Charleston area of the Adelaide Hills , authorities said . <p> Last year , the UN reported that Australia was not on track to meet its commitments under the Paris Agreement - the global deal to tackle rising global temperatures . <p> Mr Morrison has faced criticism for going on holiday despite the severity of the bushfires . <p> Firefighters ' union leader Leighton Drury said Australia was " seeing an absolute lack of leadership from this government and it is a disgrace " . <p> Criticism grew as a heatwave broke records across the country and exacerbated mammoth blazes , making the task even harder for exhausted firefighters - many of them volunteers . <p> In a statement , Mr Morrison apologised and said he would be " returning to Sydney as soon as can be arranged " . <p> Meanwhile , acting Prime Minister Michael McCormack has acknowledged that further action must be taken to combat climate change but said there was " a lot of hysteria " surrounding the issue . <p> " Climate change is not the only factor that has caused these fires . There has been dry lightning strikes , there has been self-combusting piles of manure , there has been a lot of arsonists out there causing fire " , he said . <p> Although climate change is not the direct cause of bushfires , scientists have long warned that a hotter , drier climate would contribute to Australia 's fires becoming more frequent and more intense .
