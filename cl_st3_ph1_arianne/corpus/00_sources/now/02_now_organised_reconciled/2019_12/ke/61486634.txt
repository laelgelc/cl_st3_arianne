The lecturers then offer free extension services and general advisory on farming and pastoralism . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> A drive along the Kitengela-Namanga road in Kajiado County reveals an idyllic countryside dotted with fields hosting many head of cattle and greenhouses . <p> In this neighbourhood , Malelo Ipani 's ranch and homestead stands out from the rest in many ways . <p> The 42-year-old community leader is the chairlady of Osupuko Women Group , an association of 50 local women who are in the business of bee keeping , buying , fattening and selling steers , and making and selling ornamental beads jewellery . <p> Members of the group do not move with their cattle from one place to the other as has been the tradition . Instead , their families keep lean stocks that graze in paddocks around homesteads . <p> Malelo has gone a notch higher and replaced all her 20 indigenous cattle with a single Freshian cow . Her face lights up as she explains why she does not regret the move . <p> " The Freisian cow is easier to manage compared to the 20 indigenous cattle that I owned initially . They required more attention and a bigger tract of grazing land , " she says , adding that the animal is more valuable . <h> Related Stories <p> The cow , which Malelo bought at Sh150,000 , grazes on a fenced and paddocked land with lush guinea , horsetail , creeping and foxtail grass . <p> The cow produces 24 litres of milk daily on average . Buoyed by her success , she plans to acquire three more Freisian cows . <p> Already , her initiative has inspired five group members to buy their own dairy cows to boost their milk production . <p> Scores look up to Malelo , whom they see as a beacon of hope in a region grappling with prolonged droughts , floods , disease outbreaks , reduced agriculture production , and other vagaries of climate change . <p> The subdivision of the sprawling 150,000-acre Elangatawas Group Ranch following a government directive has further complicated things for the herders.
