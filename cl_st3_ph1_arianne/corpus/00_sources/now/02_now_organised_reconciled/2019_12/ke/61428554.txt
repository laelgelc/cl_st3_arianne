I started reading the report page by page . <p> I could see my dream for Kenya 's future in chapter three ( 46 , bullet 1v ) of the report . <p> A call to ' respect and sustain our environment as a bequest which we received and will impart to future generations . ' <p> But it is still vague , how can I use my voice to influence climate action at the local and national level to close the 1.5 ? C while ensuring the representation of youth ? <p> Chapter 7 , 123 , B of the BBI report states that ' Secure future generations from unsustainable debt and environmental destruction by utilising genuine and transparent public participation -- through the proposed Office of the Public Participation Rapporteur -- to balance the need for greater economic growth with the need to protect our environment and biodiversity for future generations . ' <p> There is no better opportunity than this to advocate for greater economic growth at a global level while protecting our environment and biodiversity for future generations , as recommended by the report while taking into consideration climate change impacts . <p> Mr President , will you be attending CoP 25 ? I will be there . I will tell the world that the young people of Kenya are ready to tackle climate change . <p> If you care about the youth , please tell them too that we are ready to rewrite our story and heal our ailing home - that is nature because without nature we cease to exist . <p> Climate Change for us is a reality , the extreme rains this season , so soon after the long dry spell , landslides and floods should raise an alarm . <p> We need a world we can live in ; not struggle on how to survive extreme weather conditions . <p> Our natural resources are getting depleted faster than they can replenish . It is high time we ensure that we utilise them sustainably because of nature matters . <p> As young people , we have a longer shelf life left , and if we do not take action now we shall face greater consequences . <p> Let 's make nature conservation part of the Building Bridges Initiative for us and the generations to come . <p> I can make a strong addition to team Kenya in Madrid , Spain as we up our ambition , action and commitments on combating climate change .
