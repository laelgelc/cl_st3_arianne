<p> The drying up of the largest lake in Africa and the largest tropical lake in the world will have a devastating effect on millions of people depending on it as a source of livelihood . <p> " Using future climate projections , our model predicts that at current rates of temperature change and previous rates of lake level fall , Lake Victoria could have no outlet to the White Nile within 10 years , and Kenya could lose access to the lake in less than 400 years , which would significantly affect the economic resources supplied by Lake Victoria to the East African community , " the researchers noted . <p> Over the last few years , the water hyacinth weed has invaded Lake Victoria causing major fishing problems to many fishermen .
