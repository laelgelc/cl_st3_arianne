<h> Kenya , EU commit to further agreements to enhance ACP-EU partnership <p> The President Uhuru Kenyatta with EU commissioner for International Partnerships Jutta Urpilainen . <p> Kenya has committed to further agreements with the EU that will enhance integration of ACP countries into the global economy in the Economic Partnership Agreements ( EPAs ) between ACP and the European Union . <p> President Kenyatta during the 9th ACP Summit noted that under his stewardship , Kenya will seek to promote and protect the interests of the ACP group at the regional , continental and international levels . <p> " I will also champion the group 's solidarity and unity ; advocate for an enhanced intra-ACP and South-South and Triangular Cooperation ; champion a Reform Agenda for the ACP Group ; push for the integration of our States into the global economy , as well as the conclusion and implementation of the New Partnership Agreement , " said the President . <p> He said he looks forward to a more balanced trading arrangement with the European Union in the New Partnership Agreement , which gives priority to emerging opportunities especially in e-commerce and trade in services . <p> Get breaking news on your Mobile as-it-happens .
