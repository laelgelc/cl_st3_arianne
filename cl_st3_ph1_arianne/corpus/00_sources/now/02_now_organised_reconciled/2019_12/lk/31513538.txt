<h> DIMO Lumin Introduces a New Dimension for Lighting Solutions in Sri Lanka <p> December , 18 , 2019 <h> Offers Complete Range of LED Lighting Solutions <p> DIMO LED , one of the country 's most well-regarded brands offering a complete range of LED lighting solutions , was recently re-launched as DIMO Lumin with the aim of highlighting to consumers the importance of utilizing LED lighting solutions in their homes and leveraging on the superior quality of these products . <p> Following a successful entry to the local market over two years ago , the brand has expanded its portfolio in offering of world class modern lighting solutions focusing on the aspect of driving the sustainability factor forward.Since its introduction to the market , consumers have benefitted greatly from the extensive range of products available to them . Consumers can also now , opt for complete lighting solutions to light up their homes and find every type of lighting appliance under one brand , which also includes a number of unique lighting solutions on offer . <p> DIMO , as a company , believes in the importance of embracing the concept of sustainability and enhancing its awareness amongst their customer and the general public as a whole . With the world today facing the harmful effects of climate change at an alarming rate , DIMO has identified the need to embrace the importance of elevating sustainable solutions and leveraging on the advantage of leading a more environmentally-focused lifestyle and taking that message to the public through their products . <p> Having been in the market for the past two years , the product range offered through DIMO Lumin today , has successfully attained a commendable market share amongst Sri Lankan consumers in both , the light bulb market and in the overall LED category . The DIMO Lumin LED range comes with the DIMO warranty which further helps elevate the brand as a recognized entity across the nation.This core focus towards introducing a range that is viable as a sustainable addition to one 's homes ensures that the user would contribute towards the preservation of energy in the process . <p> Commenting on the relaunch , Viranga Wickramaratne , Chief Operating Officer - Retail Cluster stated , " For a brand that has been in the market for the past two years , we at DIMO are proud of what we have achieved thus far and in keeping with our approach in elevating the importance of embracing eco-friendly and quality lighting solutions for Sri Lankan households and uplifting the lifestyles of our consumers , we only saw it as fitting to relaunch our brand and continue in our campaign and encourage consumers to make DIMO Lumin their choice of LED and lighting solutions for their homes . " <p> The Emergency Lights are a very unique addition by DIMO Lumin and have been designed to be utilized during a power outage or even when being in the dark outdoors and can easily be propped or hung on a peg wherever required . The range also includes LED Sensor Lights and Sensor Night Lamps which are ideal to be installed in a child 's room for instance . The sensor light comprises of a sensor bulb which lights up once it senses movement . The night lamp is activated once the lights in the room go off . <p> There are many benefits to utilizing LED bulbs , among them being very energy-efficient and eco-friendly . LED bulbs also stay lit for longer when compared with regular bulbs . They are also manufactured to high standards as they stand the test of time , are known to be very tough and hence , durable . LED lights are also aesthetically design-friendly and therefore , can be created to fulfil many types of daily lighting requirements ; from use in work environments to more soothing and mood-focused lighting solutions . <p> LED lighting can also work in circumstances where there is low-voltage power , and are therefore , ideal for use in the outdoors , be it for one 's garden area or even when camping . DIMO Lumin 's range of LED bulbs are SLS certified , are also emission free , contain no mercury and are long-lasting . Another advantage for consumers is that these bulbs are available with a 2-year warranty period . The LED lighting products and solutions offered by DIMO Lumin have been manufactured using the highest quality materials , further ensuring the durability factor .
