<p> Christmas will be just another day on the job for many people working for Irish aid agency Concern Worldwide -- which has 3,597 staff in 25 of the world 's poorest countries . <p> The international humanitarian organisation , which is headquartered in Dublin , has teams in countries where millions of people are refugees from conflict or suffering from malnutrition , disease outbreaks or trying to survive through droughts , extreme flooding and the effects of climate change . <p> Most of Concern 's overseas aid workers are hired locally in the country they work in while 206 are expatriates and 19 are Irish . <p> Those continuing to work during Christmas include veteran aid worker Aine Fay , from Limerick , who is the country director in South Sudan in east Africa , where two million refugees have fled into neighbouring countries and another 1.74 million are displaced within the country due to conflict . <p> Bentiu A Primary School has benefited from a water network that Concern has maintained , making water collection points accessible to the wider community throughout Bentiu Town , former Unity State , South Sudan .
