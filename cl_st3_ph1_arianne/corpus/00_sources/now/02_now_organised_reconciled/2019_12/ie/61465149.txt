This includes 3 nights in a self-catering apartment , twin room for 2 people , free car parking at Ireland West Airport Knock and complimentary use of the lounge at Ireland West Airport Knock and ? 500 cheque from the Galway Advertiser . <p> An art competition , asking for action on climate change , was a challenging theme , but that was to underestimate the impact climate change is having on us all , and the awareness by young people that their future world is threatened . It is the story of the decade , and young people have marched , demonstrated , petitioned , and now painted for action . <p> There was unanimity in the very large entry we received , and judging from the variety of names ( it was quite an international entry ) , the message was clear : that climate change is an important issue : ' Do something ; we want a clean planet to live in .
