<h> RPS ' Don McQuillan appointed as 2020 president of Institution of Structural Engineers <p> 19 December 2019 <p> Engineers Ireland Fellow Prof Don McQuillan becomes the 2020 -- and 100th -- president of the Institution of Structural Engineers , and will deliver his inaugural address on Thursday , January 16 , 2020 <p> Specialising in project managing large multidisciplinary projects and in forensic engineering , McQuillan is a director at global professional services firm RPS . <p> Engineers Ireland Fellow Prof Don McQuillan becomes the 2020 -- and 100th -- president of the Institution of Structural Engineers , and will deliver his inaugural address on Thursday , January 16 , 2020 . <p> Prof McQuillan said : " It is a very special honour to be invited to be our institution 's 100th president , particularly at a time when the rate of change is unprecedented . <h> ' Paradigm shift in mindset and behaviour ' <p> " The climate change emergency , in particular , which demands a paradigm shift in mindset and behaviour , means that we will all have to adapt much more rapidly than ever before . <p> " The exponential rate of change we are already experiencing could result in the next 30-year period being equivalent to the previous 100 years in terms of its significance . " <p> Specialising in project managing large multidisciplinary projects and in forensic engineering , Prof McQuillan is a director at global professional services firm RPS . <p> First elected to the Institution of Structural Engineers Council in 1989 , Prof McQuillan has remained involved at the headquarters ever since , and has been particularly prominent in shaping the development of the journal ' The Structural Engineer ' . He still serves on the editorial advisory group today . <p> He also served as chairman of the Northern Ireland Region in 1999/2000 . <h> Highly experienced and successful structural engineer <p> A highly experienced and successful structural engineer , Prof McQuillan has authored many technical papers , some award winning , and is also an RAE visiting professor at Queen 's University Belfast . <p> He is passionate about mentoring younger engineers , providing technical support and guidance to members and promoting the institution as the world leader in structural engineering . He is also in demand as an expert witness and is currently involved in a major case in Hong Kong . <p> Engineers Ireland Fellow Prof Don McQuillan becomes the 2020 -- and 100th -- president of the Institution of Structural Engineers , and will deliver his inaugural address on Thursday , January 16 , 2020 .
