<h> Climate change facts to be discussed at Teagasc event today <p> A Teagasc sustainability event is taking place in Co . Kilkenny this morning , Tuesday , December 3 , that aims to address a number of environmental topics currently affecting agriculture . <p> The event is taking place at the Springhill Hotel , Co . Kilkenny , and will commence at 11:00am where , according to Teagasc , attendees will have the opportunity to meet one-to-one with experts on a range of different topics to discuss a range of opportunities . <p> The topics being covered today are as follows : <p> Climate change -- the facts ; <p> The role of protected Urea and Low Emission Slurry Spreading ( LESS ) ; <p> Update on derogation ; <p> Water quality and biodiversity . <p> All are welcome to attend the event . <h> Beef Market Taskforce <p> In other farming news , the much-anticipated Beef Market Taskforce is set to host its first meeting today , Tuesday , December 3 , seven weeks and a day since it was due to originally meet . <p> Advertisement <p> The taskforce was originally scheduled to meet on Monday , October 14 . However , a protest took place outside the headquarters of the Department of Agriculture , Food and the Marine , where the meeting was due to take place . <p> During the protest , the group prevented Cormac Healy , senior director of Meat Industry Ireland ( MII ) , from entering the building due to frustration that injunctions arising from the beef protests had not been lifted . <p> Farm lobby groups -- that were also due to attend -- agreed they would not sit on the taskforce until all injunctions had been lifted . <p> Following the protest at Agriculture House , that meeting was postponed until it was announced last Thursday that C &amp; D Foods would apply to have the injunctions struck out . <p> The move to lift injunctions took place just over 24 hours after the tractor protest took place in Dublin 's city centre . <p> Today 's meeting will be the first of a series of Beef Market Taskforce meetings .
