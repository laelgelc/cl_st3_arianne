<h> Kosovo to double its wind energy capacity <p> By Mike **30;3710;TOOLONG <p> Kosovo is set to more than double its installed renewable energy capacity , with the construction of a 105MW wind farm in Bajgora . <p> The project got the go-ahead following the award of a loan for ? 58 million from the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development ( EBRD ) . The loan will cover approximately half of the cost of construction , with private investors covering the remainder . <p> Currently , as much as 90% of Kosovo 's electricity comes from two ageing coal-fired power plants , which are said to be among the heaviest polluters in Europe . <p> In a statement , the EBRD said , " When complete , Bajgora will represent about 10% of the country 's installed capacity and avoid 247,000 tonnes of CO2 a year , making a significant contribution to climate change mitigation . " <p> The Bajgora wind farm will contribute towards Kosovo 's stated goal of achieving 400MW of renewable capacity by 2026 -- with hydro-electric and solar power projects also anticipated . <p> That said , Kosovo announced earlier this year that it is moving forward with a project to build a new coal-fired plant . <p> If the project comes to fruition , the plant will generate approximately 500MW of energy , enough to meet almost half of the power requirements of the country .
