<h> A Novel Way to Prevent Wildfires <p> If you have been following international news , then you know that the occurrences of wildfires have increased significantly in recent years . This is not entirely surprising to scientists who have been studying the effects of climate change , as they 've sort of been expecting it , but it is still as damaging . <p> Wildfires are uncontrolled fires in areas covered by vegetation such as forests or bushes and grasslands . They are alternately known as forest fires or bush fires . <p> The problem is that we can not anticipate these fires , and even if we could , there 's not much we could do to prevent them . But that 's about to change with this recent discovery . <p> Before we get to that , let us take a brief look at what causes wildfires and the effects of wildfires . <h> What causes wildfires ? <p> Wildfires can be natural or human-made . <p> Human-made causes are mostly as a result of carelessness and account for 90% of forest fires . An unattended campfire or an unextinguished cigarette butt can cause these fires . <p> Other common man-made causes are burning debris , fireworks , and accidental or intentional arson . <p> Naturally , a fire can rise from erupting volcanoes or lightning . When lightning strikes trees , power cables , or any other combustible material , it can lead to wildfires . <p> Once the fire starts , it spreads rapidly based on the concentration of the flammable vegetation , topography , and weather conditions . A wildfire can spread quickly , sometimes reaching speeds up to 6.7 miles an hour in forests and 14mph in grasslands . <h> Where are wildfires most common ? <p> Wildfires occur in some parts of every continent , with the exception of Antarctica . They are common in the forests of the United States and Canada , as well as Australia and South Africa globally . <p> In Europe , Portugal sees the most numbers of wildfires . Greece and Russia are also prone to fires as well . <p> These are usually areas with enough moisture and rainfall to support the growth of forests that also feature long periods of dry heat . California wildfires took the headline in 2018 . <h> The effects of wildfire <p> Wildfires can have a devastating impact on the nation . The primary loss that comes to mind is that of human lives . <p> Even though the premises in nearby areas are usually vacated , and the fire is contained , there is an imminent risk to lives . The California fires of 2018 claimed 85 human lives . <p> Advertisement <p> There is also the loss of property , which is enormous . Thousands are left homeless due to the destruction , and many more houses get damaged . <p> Also , huge capital goes into trying to control these fires . The number is as high as $2 billion annually just in the US . <p> Lastly , the loss of habitat and forest destruction is massive . 149,000 acres of forest were consumed in the forest fire . This not only destroys important natural habitats , but it also consumes thousands of trees and releases hazardous levels of pollutants into the atmosphere . <p> But wildfires are n't necessarily bad for the environment . Naturally occurring wildfires can be seen as nature 's way of returning resources trapped in the dead or diseased matter to return to earth . <p> They also kill disease-carrying plants and harmful insects . <p> Advertisement <h> How are wildfires stopped ? <p> There are two significant ways of stopping the fires from spreading outwards . One way is to use water and other retardants to douse the fire with hopes of extinguishing it . <p> Depending on the conditions , specific areas may be given priority if it is estimated that they will accelerate faster and might get difficult to control later on . <p> If the fire is too big for that , we may clear the surrounding area of the forest by removing any possible fuel source . This way , the fire gets contained within the region . <p> This cleared-out line is known as the control line . Ironically , sometimes firefighters may use fire to create a control line that is big enough to contain the fire . <p> Water bodies such as rivers can act as naturally occurring control lines . Moreover , having one around means planes and helicopters can carry water from them and drop it on top of the fire . <p> Advertisement <p> Once the fire begins to clear , the firemen make sure that there are no embers that are still burning as they may spark a fire again . The exact saying applies to wildfires as well . There have been many chemicals employed to do just this . <p> Unfortunately , they get washed away or decay into constituents . But a group of researchers at Stanford might have just made a breakthrough , as reported in a study published in Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences . <p> The newly discovered gel-like fluid can be sprayed to make the retardants last longer . It is expected to be environment-friendly , and the initial tests have returned positive results . <p> Advertisement <p> They are stable enough to last entire seasons , where chances of wildfires are at the peak .
