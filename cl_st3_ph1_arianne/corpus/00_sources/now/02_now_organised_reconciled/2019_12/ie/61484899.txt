These provided something of a lifeline for many farmers who could n't get their stock into factories during the beef protests this summer . Ella Mc Sweeney visits Oldcastle in Co . Meath where Declan Flood and his family run a thriving butchers business and abattoir . Declan provides an alternative route to market for farmers who use his services . <p> Farmyard WhiskeyBallykeefe farm is a 150 acre beef and tillage enterprise run by Morgan Ging and his family near Cuffesgrange in Co . Kilkenny . It is also home to Ireland 's only farm-based whiskey distillery , producing spirit from the barley that is grown on the land . Helen Carroll visited the farm when Morgan was busy harvesting this year 's crop , and learned first-hand how to turn barley into whiskey . <p> Carbon farmingIreland has the lowest forest cover of any country in Europe with just 11% of land area under trees . As one tree captures one tonne of carbon dioxide from the atmosphere every year , it 's clear that forests have the potential to solve Ireland 's climate change challenge . But farmers are reluctant to plant forestry , despite the incentive of generous grants and tax relief . John Sherlock , a Co . Meath farmer and chair of the North East Forestry Group has planted his land and runs a thriving firewood business . Darragh McCullough visits Sherlock 's woodland and finds out some of the challenges and opportunities that forestry presents for farmers . <p> The programme information published on this site is for promotional purposes only .
