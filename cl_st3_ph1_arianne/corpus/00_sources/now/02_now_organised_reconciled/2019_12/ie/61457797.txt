Our submissions ( available here ) emphasised the importance of effective regional development , climate action , infrastructure and housing . <h> Tracking engineering salaries and benefits <p> It is based on an annual member survey , receiving almost 2,000 responses , and detailed analysis of salaries and benefits according to discipline , seniority and much more . <p> The report shows that junior engineers received a five per cent pay boost in the past year , worth about ? 2,000 . engineers post-Brexit .
