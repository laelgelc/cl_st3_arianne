<p> Young farmers are dedicated to playing their part in facing up to the climate change challenge -- but measures need to be put in place to assure their future , according to European young farmers ' association CEJA . <p> CEJA delegates approved the organisation 's position paper on climate change during a working group and young farmer symposium at Wageningen University and Research in the Netherlands last Friday , December 13 . <p> Commenting on this , CEJA president Jannes Maes said : " Climate change is a challenge we all face together and young farmers are dedicated to playing their part . <p> To ensure a constructive and proactive debate , we need all stakeholders and policymakers to consider our socio-economic boundaries and obstacles . <p> " If we are to achieve our full potential and tackle the challenges associated with adapting to and mitigating climate change , there must be measures in place to protect our future , " he added . <p> Advertisement <p> The document states that farmers are on the front line when it comes to climate change and their role is threefold : to be innovative and find ways to adapt to the changing climate ; to reduce greenhouse gas ( GHG ) emissions from production ; and to contribute to the agricultural sector 's capacity to sequester carbon while producing safe and healthy food for a growing population . <p> Young farmers realise that climate action should be carried out while ensuring quality of life and food security , a competitive European agricultural sector , and the protection of biodiversity and water resources , among other elements , CEJA notes . <p> To achieve this , a systems approach that addresses the obstacles young farmers face will be crucial . Climate change requires ambitious , worldwide action , in which everybody has a role to play and nobody is left behind , the organisation added . <p> CEJA 's position paper enables young farmers to participate in a debate that is fundamental to ensuring generational renewal and , ultimately , the survival of the sector .
