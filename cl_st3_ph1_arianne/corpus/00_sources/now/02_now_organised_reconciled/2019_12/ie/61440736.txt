<h> Time to Shine for Kilkenny woman <h> New chair of International Institute for Environment and Development <p> Kilkenny woman , Dr Tara Shine has been appointed the new chair of IIED ( International Institute for Environment and Development ) <p> Kilkenny woman , Dr Tara Shine has been appointed as the new chair of the prestigious international conservation body , IIED . <p> The aim of the International Institute for Environment and Development ( IIED ) is to build a fairer , more sustainable world , using evidence , action and influence , working in partnership with others . <p> Dr Shine , a former Kilkenny College student will succeed former Costa Rica vice-president Rebeca Grynspan when her term ends . Dr Shine will take over on September 14 of next year . <p> A specialist on climate change and the environment and development , Dr Shine has 20 years experience advising leaders , governments , multilateral agencies and civil society . <p> She first came to prominence for a television documentary on the lost crocodiles of the Sahara . <p> She completed her PhD on ephemeral wetlands in eastern Mauritania at the University of Ulster in Coleraine , where she did her primary degree . <p> She traveled to Antarctica in January 2018 as part of a global leadership programme for women scientists called Homeward Bound . <p> Speaking about her appointment , she said it was urgently time to engage people in all walks of life -- from governments to the grassroots -- to be part of the transition to zero carbon , more resilient societies . <p> " IIED 's action research and partnerships with organisations on the ground provide evidence and a clarion call for new models of leadership . I look forward to working with the trustees and the amazing staff in IIED to help shape a better , more collaborative future and make change happen , " she said . <p> Dr Shine has worked with organisations including Irish Aid , the Irish government 's programme for overseas development ; the Swedish Development Agency ( Sida ) ; the OECD ; The Elders and the Mary Robinson Foundation -- Climate Justice . <p> She is currently director and co-founder of the award-winning social enterprise Change by Degrees , and works to educate and engage individuals , businesses and communities to live and work more sustainable . <p> Dr Shine 's appointment comes at a time when IIED 's work is more urgent than ever , as detailed in the institute 's five-year strategy launched last April . <p> Current environmental and development policies are not viable while inequality prevails . Effective research and action are crucial as the pace of climate change becomes increasingly urgent , the scale of biodiversity loss escalates and risks from rapidly growing cities and unsustainable markets are on the rise . <p> IIED director Andrew Norton said : " We are thrilled to appoint Tara as the new chair of IIED , from a strong and diverse shortlist . She will bring her deep personal commitment to climate justice , her great abilities as an analyst and communicator and her experience of many roles from scientist to social entrepreneur and activist . <p> " We are delighted to have found a candidate with her exceptional experience and commitment to take forward the great work of our current chair Rebeca Grynspan . We look forward to working with her to ensure that IIED makes the strongest contribution to addressing the great global challenges facing us , from the climate crisis to accelerating global biodiversity loss and rising inequality .
