<h> Opportunities and challenges : Maintaining European unity <p> The changing of the guard at the European Commission and the European Council offers new opportunities as well as challenges for the EU . <p> Ursula von der Leyen becomes the first woman to hold the EU 's top job as president of the European Commission -- the EU 's executive arm -- which officially began its new five-year term yesterday . <p> Succeeding Jean-Claude Juncker , she has already declared that climate change will be at the top of her agenda , as she views it as an existential threat . <p> At the same time , Charles Michel , the new head of the European Council who takes over from Donald Tusk , has promised that Europe will become more " assertive " on the world stage . <p> Ireland 's commissioner is Phil Hogan who moves from the agriculture portfolio to trade , a key position , particularly in post-Brexit trade talks . <p> Mr Juncker and Mr Tusk can share much of the credit for maintaining European unity over the past five years , weathering Brexit , a migration crisis , attacks by jihadists in several EU states , and financial tribulations . <p> Mr Hogan can also take a bow , having performed very well as agriculture commissioner . <p> Of the 27 new commissioners , 12 are women , making this the most gender-balanced European Commission to date .
