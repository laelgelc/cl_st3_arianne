Picture : Extinction Rebellion North Herts <p> Archant <h> Members of Extinction Rebellion North Herts took to Letchworth town centre on Saturday for a Christmas Carol event . <p> Extinction Rebellion North Herts and the Red Rebels held a demonstration in Letchworth town centre to remind shoppers to be mindful of the environment this Christmas . Picture : Extinction Rebellion North Herts <p> The members sang adapted Christmas Carols alongside ' Red Rebels ' who walked silently through the town . <p> Red Rebels represent the sadness about climate and ecological decline . <p> The festive themed action was held to raise awareness about the climate crisis and urge shoppers to think about the environmental impact of Christmas - we generate more waste at Christmas than at any other time of year . <p> The message the group wanted to give to shoppers was that nobody is too small to make a difference . <p> Some eco-friendly steps the group suggests are to buy less material things and opt for experiences , days out , make something or buy second hand and to buy locally and ethically . <p> Greg Knapp , one of Extinction Rebellion North Herts ' members , said : " Many people have joined our local Extinction Rebellion group to alleviate a sense of helplessness . <p> " This means participation in actions , or indirectly supporting them with artwork , their voices , promotion or just moral support . <h> You may also want to watch : <p> " We are a friendly group who offer community and an inclusive forum to talk about this uncomfortable subject . " <p> The campaigners have held a number of events throughout 2019 , and lobbied North Herts District Council to declare a climate emergency , enlisting their help in tackling to issue locally . <p> The council voted through the motion to declare a state of climate emergency in May . <p> Alongside the declaration , it pledged to do everything within its power to achieve zero carbon emissions in the district by 2030 . <p> The Climate Change Strategy is currently under review to align the strategy with latest research regarding the impact of climate change . <p> The new strategy will contain an action plan , which will set out how we aim to achieve a target of zero carbon emissions by 2030 . <p> It also passed two motions with the aim of encouraging residents to strive for a Plastic Free North Herts . <p> From January , Extinction Rebellion North Herts will hold monthly meetings at Friends Meeting House on Paynes Park in Hitchin . <p> For dates , search for the Extinction Rebellion North Herts Facebook page .
