<h> Olga Aughey <h> ' Attack ' on rural Ireland raised at environment SPC <p> A north Westmeath county councillor has hit out at the " attack " on rural Ireland in relation to transport and carbon emissions . <p> Cllr Paddy Hill said that while he agreed changes would have to be made , he did not agree with " closing down rural Ireland " . <p> " Let 's face it , rural Ireland can only do so much , Ireland itself can only do so much . There are countries much bigger than us that do n't seem to be doing anything . " <p> The Finea councillor was speaking following a progress report on the Climate Change Adaptation Process at last Thursday 's meeting of the Environment , Climate Action , Water and Emergency Services SPC . Speaking as a farmer that rises early in the morning , he said : " You look up at the sky and see these big jet engines coming out of Dublin airport , pumping thousands of litres of ' whatever ' it is , into the atmosphere , and I do n't hear anyone saying one word about that , and I do n't know why that is , " he argued . <p> " We all know a plane takes off from Dublin airport every couple of minutes , if not more , and they are doing an awful lot more harm than the poor aul farmer down the road with a jeep or tractor , " he surmised . <p> " There are thousands of jobs that are being lost on bogs right across the midlands and there is no replacement for them ... It 's practically shut down Bord na M ? na , yet we 're importing coal into the country and burning it , " stated Cllr Hill who said it makes " no sense " whatsoever . " These are the questions that need to be pondered . " <p> Chairing the meeting , Cllr Frank McDermott " vigorously " supported his colleague from North Westmeath , claiming that " If one plane was knocked out of action , it would be similar to all the ( farming ) work we could do in an entire month in Ireland . " <p> " We are faced with a climate crisis . We are in an emergency situation , we need to listen to the UN , the EPA , the IPCC here , this is not a time to move slowly , " she insisted . <p> " We are putting future generations at risk by moving slowly . Ireland is one of the worst in Europe for putting in efforts to be better at adapting to the reality that is climate change . <p> " There is no attack on rural Ireland happening here , if anything this is to protect both rural Ireland and all of Ireland , " she contended , adding that the Green Party was very mindful of introducing taxes on airlines . <p> " We have to be cognisant of the fact that we are in Westmeath ... The reality is we are in a rural county and therefore we must act to do what we can to protect ourselves and future generations here . "
