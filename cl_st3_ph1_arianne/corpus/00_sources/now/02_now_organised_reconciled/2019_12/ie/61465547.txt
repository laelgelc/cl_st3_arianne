<h> Tramore homeowners to be banned from burning smoky coal <p> Tramore homeowners are to be banned from burning smoky coal next year under an extension of a ban by Minister for Climate Action and the Environment , Richard Bruton . <p> The ban is already in place in cities and big towns across Ireland but will be extended to towns with a population greater than 10,000 people in 2020 , including Tramore . <p> Towns in Cavan , Cork , Kerry , Longford , Mayo , Meath and Offaly are also being impacted . <p> Minister Bruton says the measure is being undertaken to address immediate health concerns in these towns and tackle climate change targets . The ban in Tramore will come into effect in September 2020 . <p> Waterford City &amp; County Council will be responsible for the implementation of the ban and may issue fines to people found in breach of the ban . The ban is already in place in Waterford city . <p> Minister Bruton has shelved plans for a nationwide smoky coal ban due to potential legal challenges . <p> If you have a story or want to send a photo or video to us please contact the Derry Now editorial team on 028 7129 6600 for Derry City stories Or 028 7774 3970 for County Derry stories .
