We will have the ability to bring the world 's cultures to the North , providing a globalised education for Yukoners , " says the university . <p> For Yukon students , or ' Yukoners ' , this will mean increased opportunity for degree completion closer to home and access to credentials that are focused on the North . <p> International students with a study permit can take advantage of opportunities to learn about Indigenous self-determination , Indigenous cultures and Canada 's North . <p> Already sparking global interest , Yukon University has expanded international student recruitment and diversified the countries of origin of international students at YukonU . <p> " At the same time , we have instituted a cap so that international students do not make up more than 15% of all credit students , " notes YukonU . <p> Plus , the university will soon be developing a Bachelor of Arts in Northern Studies to launch in 2021-22 . <p> Prioritising the needs of Yukon students , the university also aims to increase opportunities for students from across Canada to experience and study in the North . <p> " Yukon University plans to recruit students from across Canada , especially for our new degree programmes and niche areas -- Indigenous self-determination and governance , sustainable resource development , technology and innovation and climate change -- society and environment , " the university states . <p> This is an exciting development on the international university scene , especially for prospective international students who are keen to study in the Canadian Arctic . <p> The plan is for Yukon University to officially launch May 8 and 9 , 2020 with a convocation ceremony for the first graduates of YukonU and an open house celebration at Ayamdigut campus in Whitehorse sponsored by @CIBC. **45;97;TOOLONG ...
