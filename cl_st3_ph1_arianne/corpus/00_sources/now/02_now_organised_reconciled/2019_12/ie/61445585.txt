<h> Saudi Aramco reaches landmark $2 trillion in second day of trading <p> Shares in Saudi Aramco have gained on the second day of trading , pushing the oil and gas company to a more than $2tn ( ? 1.79tn ) valuation . <p> Shares jumped in trading to reach up to 38.60 Saudi riyals , or ? 9.25 before noon , three hours before trading closes . <p> Aramco , which is the world 's most valuable listed company , has sold a 1.5% share to mostly Saudi investors and local Saudi Gulf-based funds . <p> With gains made from just two day of trading , Aramco sits comfortably ahead of the world 's largest companies , including Apple , the second largest company in the world valued at $1.19tn ( ? 1tn ) . Aramco is the world 's most valuable listed company ( AP/Amr Nabil ) <p> Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman is the architect of the effort to list Aramco , touting it as a way to raise capital for the kingdom 's sovereign wealth fund , which would then develop new cities and lucrative projects across the country that create jobs for young Saudis . <p> He had sought a $2tn valuation for Aramco when he first announced in 2015 plans to sell a sliver of the state-owned company . <p> International investors , however , thought the price was too high , given the relatively lower price of oil , climate change concerns and geopolitical risks associated with Aramco . <p> The company 's main crude oil processing facility and another site were targeted by missiles and drones in September , knocking out more than half of Saudi production for a while . The kingdom and the US have blamed the attack on rival Iran , which denies involvement . <p> In the lead-up to the flotation , there had been a strong push for Saudis , including princes and businessmen , to contribute to what is seen locally as a moment of national pride , and even duty . Gulf-based funds from allied countries also contributed to the IPO . <p> At a ceremony for the start of trading , Aramco chairman Yasir Al-Rumayyan , described the sale as " a proud and historic moment for Saudi Aramco and our majority shareholder , the kingdom " .
