<h> Townlands -- Weatherproof -- Repeat <p> Narrated by Niall Tobin , the programme features a diverse range of contributors , each with their own vested interest in the Irish weather : <p> west coast surfers whose idea of a perfect day is an approaching low pressure system ; <p> Noel Meade and his fellow horse trainers obsessing about the ground conditions at Punchestown racecourse ; <p> the keeper of the Blacksod Point lighthouse which played a crucial meteorological role in timing the 1944 Normandy landings ; <p> and American artist Susan Tiger , whose drawings of Mayo rain are shown in galleries all over Europe and the US . <p> Throughout the programme Met Eireann forecaster Evelyn Cusack -- followed as she prepares for the main evening weather broadcast on RTE -- gives her perspective on the vagaries of the temperate Irish climate , the hit and miss nature of forecasting , the limitations of technology , and the fact that the weather we love ( but profess to hate ) could soon fall victim to climate change . <p> The programme information published on this site is for promotional purposes only .
