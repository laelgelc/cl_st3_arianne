<h> Irish fracking ban recommendation welcomed by NGOs <p> Environmental organisations have welcomed the Oireachtas ' Environmental Committee 's recommendation to ban fracking in the Republic of Ireland . <p> The Environmental Pillar lauded the decision to support a prohibition on fracking in a report compiled by the Joint Committee on Communications , Climate Action and Environment this week . <p> The Environmental Pillar -- a coalition of 26 Irish environmental NGOs -- praised the decision as " the right thing to do to protect our water quality , natural environment and the health and well-being of communities across Ireland " . <p> The organisation stated that the report will add to the increasing amount of peer-reviewed evidence from around the world -- highlighting the " detrimental impacts and risks brought with fracking and unconventional fossil fuel extraction " . <p> The coalition said that the decision shows Ireland 's commitment to the advancement of a sustainable low-carbon system and the efforts to minimise climate change . Following on from what it described as a momentous decision , the coalition sought for the report to be put before the D ? il and passed to law as soon as possible . <p> Aedin McLoughlin , spokesperson for the Environmental Pillar , said : " The decision not to pursue fracking in Ireland is needed to protect communities from the damaging impacts of fracking , such as the contamination of water , and to meet our climate change commitments " . <p> Exploiting unconventional fossil fuels such as shale gas will only increase the world 's dependency on fossil fuels and consequently slow down the large-scale deployment of clean energy renewables and energy savings . <h> What is fracking ? <p> Fracking , also known as hydraulic fracturing , is the procedure of drilling deep into ground containing oil or gas , then directing highly-pressurized water ( mixed with ' proppants ' such as particles of sand and other additives ) into the rock to force open fissures or cracks to release the gas or oil inside . <p> Advertisement <p> This process is carried out on shale rock and other similar rock formations , first drilling vertically about 1.6km or more beneath the surface before turning horizontal for the rest of the drilling . It can either use existing openings in the rock or form new ones . <p> The cracks forced open by the pressurized water mixture are held open by additives in the mixture . <p> Fracking is used to extract oil or gas in what would be hard-to-reach locations for more traditional methods of extraction . This gives oil companies scope to access vast reserves of fossil fuels that previously would have been unrecoverable . <p> As a result , this has driven down the price of oil in recent years , particularly in the US , where the economic effects have been profound . <p> However , environmental impacts are considerable , with water and ground contamination -- as well as air and noise pollution -- seen as major concerns . <p> In addition , there are worries in some quarters that fracking can trigger small earthquakes and tremors , destabilizing the earth 's surface in places , as was suspected to be the case in the 2011 earth tremors in Lancashire , UK , according to a study into the matter by the energy company Cuadrilla . <p> There have also been suspicions of potential safety issues . According to BBC reports on fracking , environmentalists are concerned that potentially carcinogenic chemicals used in fracking could escape shale rocks and contaminate groundwater around fracking sites .
