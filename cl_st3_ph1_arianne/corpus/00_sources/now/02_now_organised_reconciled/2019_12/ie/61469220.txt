<h> Emrill partners with Dubai Chamber for new recycling campaign <h> DUBAI , 10 days ago <p> Leading UAE facilities management company , Emrill , has joined forces with Dubai Chamber of Commerce &amp; Industry for a major campaign aimed at reducing waste in the emirate . <p> As part of its Let 's Reduce and Recycle campaign , Dubai Chamber invited schools , universities and colleges , private and public sector organisations , families and individuals to collaborate in diverting waste away from landfills and raise awareness about the importance of waste segregation and recycling . <p> In 2018 , the UAE ranked as one of the world 's biggest waste producers according to World Bank data . Dubai Municipality estimates the average person in the UAE produces a total of 2.5kg of waste every day . <p> Of this , 77 per cent ends up in landfills according to UAE Ministry of Climate Change and Environment estimates . <p> One of the key aims of UAE Vision 2021 is to implement green growth plans for the long term sustainable development of the country , said the statement from Emrill . <p> Aligned to the national agenda , Dubai has committed to diverting 75 per cent of waste away from landfills , focusing instead on waste reduction , segregation and collection to recover and recycle waste . <p> Additionally , Expo 2020 Dubai has further committed to diverting 85 per cent of waste away from landfills . This ambitious target is part of its bid to be the greenest expo ever . <p> As part of the campaign , which took place as part of Dubai Chamber Sustainability Week 2019 , Emrill is promoting a zero-waste lifestyle , asking employees to participate by collecting clothes , paper , plastic bottles and e-waste . <p> In the collection period between November 7 to 26 , Emrill collected a total of 280 bottles , 51kg of paper , 115kg of clothes and 689kg of e-waste , including 28 printers and two desktops . <p> During the campaign , Emrill also ran an internal marketing campaign , encouraging employees and their families to be more mindful about waste and consciously take steps to reuse and recycle . <p> Emrill 's CEO Stuart Harrison said : " It 's been a pleasure to get involved in the Let 's Reduce and Recycle campaign . Emrill is committed to sustainability and we welcomed the opportunity to do a little bit more for such a worthy cause . " <p> " Over the past year , we have launched a sustainable vegetable garden and embarked on a water conservation programme , using condensation water to clean car parks . This initiative alone has saved over 35,000 litres of water , " he stated . <p> " We have also actively sought to reduce landfill waste in the communities we manage . For example , by fitting fabricated detachable algae skimmers to our maintenance pedal boats , we were able to push algae from the centre of the lakes to shore , expediting the removal process and allowing the regular scheduling of cleaning work regardless of weather conditions , " noted Harrison . <p> " This solution has enabled us to reduce algae waste by 25 per cent and total waste volume by 31 per cent , " he added . <p> Emrill embraces initiatives that highlight the difference every person can make in the pursuit of increased sustainability and the battle against climate change . <p> Fasiulla Shariff , Emrill 's integrated management system manager , said : " Initiatives of this type change the mindset of our people . They then go home and share this information with their friends and family , creating a ripple effect . If we all take steps to reduce waste and become more sustainable , we can have a major collective impact . "
