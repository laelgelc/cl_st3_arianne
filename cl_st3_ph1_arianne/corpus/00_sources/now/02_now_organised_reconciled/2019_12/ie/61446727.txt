<h> Greta Thunberg becomes youngest person to be named Time Person of the Year <h> An incredible achievement for the 16-year-old . <p> Greta Thunberg , the Swedish schoolgirl who is at the forefront of the fight against climate change , has been named Time magazine 's Person of the Year for 2019 . <p> She is the youngest person to ever win the award , which has been running since 1927 . <p> In Thunberg 's campaign against climate change , she has addressed heads of state at the U.N. , met with the Pope , gone toe-to-toe with the President of the United States and inspired millions of people to join the global climate strike that took place on 20 September , in what was the largest climate demonstration in human history . <p> TIME Magazine justified their decision to give her the coveted award , saying : " Where others smile to cut the tension , Thunberg is withering . Where others speak the language of hope , Thunberg repeats the unassailable science : Oceans will rise . Cities will flood . Millions of people will suffer . " <p> The work has not stopped for the 16-year-old however , as she continues to work and attempt to raise awareness of the climate emergency . <p> " Well I am telling you there is hope . I have seen it.But it does not come from governments or corporations.It comes from the people . "
