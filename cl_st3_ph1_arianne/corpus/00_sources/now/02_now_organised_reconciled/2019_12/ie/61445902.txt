<p> The European Green Deal -- launched today , Wednesday , December 11 -- contains a number of measures that could bring about significant changes for farming practices in Ireland . <p> The ' Green Deal ' sets out ambitions to make Europe the first ' climate neutral continent ' by 2050 . <p> In doing this , it aims to boost the economy , improve people 's health and quality of life while caring for nature and " leave no one behind " . <p> The European Green Deal outlined that its strategic plans " will need to reflect an increased level of ambition to reduce significantly the use and risk of chemical pesticides , as well as the use of fertilisers and antibiotics " . <p> The commission will identify the measures , including legislative , needed to bring about these reductions . <p> The deal also notes that the area under organic farming will need to increase in Europe . <p> The EU needs to develop ways to protect harvests from pests and diseases and consider the potential role of new techniques to improve food-system sustainability . <h> Commission president <p> Commenting on the green deal , President of the European Commission Ursula von der Leyen said : " The European Green Deal is our new growth strategy for a growth that gives back more than it takes away . <p> It shows how to transform our way of living and working , of producing and consuming so that we live healthier and make our businesses innovative . <p> " We can all be involved in the transition and we can all benefit from the opportunities . <p> Advertisement <p> " We will help our economy to be a global leader by moving first and moving fast . We are determined to succeed for the sake of this planet and life on it -- for Europe 's natural heritage , for biodiversity , for our forests and our seas . <p> " By showing the rest of the world how to be sustainable and competitive , we can convince other countries to move with us . ' <h> Commission vice president <p> Executive Vice President-Designate of the European Commission Frans Timmermans added : " We are in a climate and environmental emergency . <p> " The European Green Deal is an opportunity to improve the health and well-being of our people by transforming our economic model . <p> Our plan sets out how to cut emissions , restore the health of our natural environment , protect our wildlife , create new economic opportunities , and improve the quality of life of our citizens . <p> " We all have an important part to play and every industry and country will be part of this transformation . " <p> Concluding , he outlined : " The European Green Deal provides a roadmap with actions to boost the efficient use of resources by moving to a clean , circular economy and stop climate change , revert biodiversity loss and cut pollution .
