<h> Rihanna 's Fenty label is big winner at Fashion Awards 2019 <h> Rihanna 's Fenty label is big winner at Fashion Awards 2019 <p> Rihanna 's Fenty label is big winner at Fashion Awards 2019 . Barbadian Singer , Rihanna saw her fashion label Fenty win the Urban Luxe category at the Fashion Awards last evening . The 31-year old saw her fashion label honoured at the glitzy event at Royal Albert Hall in London . <p> Rihanna received the award by Tyler , the Creator and Michael Jackson 's sister and singer Janet Jackson . 53-year Jackson told the " Umbrella " singer " You are loved for your style " as she presented the singer with her accolade . Rihanna first launched her Fenty label under luxury fashion group LVMH back in May 2019 . <h> Rihanna has sold over 250 million records worldwide <p> The music star already owns her own lingerie brand named , Savage X Fenty that she launched in 2018 . Rihanna said she was thankful to the British Fashion Council and her collaborators for her award . <p> The designer was previously nominated for her Fenty x Puma collaboration back in 2017 . Other winners on the night were Super Model , Naomi Campbell who received the Fashion Icon award . In her tearful acceptance speech , Naomi said her award was a deeply humbling experience . <p> Campbell thanked fellow Super Models Linda Evangelista , Kate Moss and Christy Turlington , referring to them as her " sisters for life . " She also thanked the late Italian fashion designer Gianni Versace . <p> Naomi Campbell pointed out that she was the " first woman of colour to receive this award " before thanking women who she said had come before her , including Janet Jackson , Tina Turner , Iman , Bethann Hardison and Eartha Kitt . <p> As a staunch campaigner for inclusivity in fashion and one of the founders of the Diversity Coalition , Naomi called for " diversity in the workplace and in everything we do . " <h> Rihanna 's full name is Robyn Rihanna Fenty <p> The Fashion Awards also honoured UK fashion designer , Daniel Lee who revamped fashion label Bottega Veneta since taking the helm in 2018 . Daniel picked up four awards that included : British designer of the year womenswear , designer of the year , accessories designer of the year and brand of the year . <p> South Sudanese-Australian model , Adut Akech won model of the year . Iconic Italian fashion designer , Giorgio Armani received the outstanding achievement award . This was presented to him on the night by Julia Roberts and Cate Blanchett . <p> Sustainable fashion was n't left out on the night . The collective signatories of the fashion industry charter for climate change which included Stella McCartney , Burberry and Nike , won the positive change award , for individuals and fashion brands who are making concerted efforts to be more sustainable .
