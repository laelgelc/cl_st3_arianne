<h> Climate change activist award for employee of county Limerick factory <p> David Hughes , accepting his Outstanding Achievement Award from Dr Michael John O'Mahony of An Taisce <p> CLIMATE Ambassador David Hughes , who is employed at Kostal in Abbeyfeale , has been honoured with an Outstanding Achievement Award by An Taisce . <p> David , who is based in Listowel , was one of 130 people selected from all walks of life to be Climate Ambassadors in 2019 . But at a ceremony in Dublin , his work in that role over the past year was acknowledged as outstanding . <p> One of the Climate Ambassador Outstanding Achievement award went to David Hughes . <p> David , who lives in Listowel but works in Abbeyfeale , demonstrated his commitment both in his professional and personal life , the citation states . <p> He set up recycling and composting stations at Kostal , Abbeyfeale and also managed to remove any single-use plastic cups in his workplace . He also gave a talk to local businesses in Listowel on understanding Climate Change . David also wrote and delivered a reading of a poem on The 4 Climate theories for Listowel Writers Week . <p> He visited the local secondary school and IT Tralee to speak about climate change and helped organise local Fridays for Future events . <p> He has also been really active at a community level , founding the Listowel Climate Emergency Group and giving talks through Transition Kerry and planting trees and wildflower meadows . <p> David 's Outstanding Achievement Award was one of ten awarded at a special ceremony attended by Minister for Communications , Climate Action and Environment , Richard Bruton whose department funds the Climate Ambassador programme which is operated by the Educational Unit of An Taisce . <p> The minister acknowledged the commitment of all 130 Climate Ambassadors and their valuable work in communities all over Ireland . <p> If you have a story or want to send a photo or video to us please contact the Derry Now editorial team on 028 7129 6600 for Derry City stories Or 028 7774 3970 for County Derry stories .
