<h> Workshop for owners and leaseholders of vacant town centre properties <p> A workshop for owners and leaseholders of vacant property in Dundalk Town Centre will take place this Thursday in the County Museum in Roden Place . <p> The event will run from 10am to 2pm with a light lunch provided on the day . <p> For the last few years several staff of Louth County Council have been working with the Heritage Council on a project called the Collaborative Town Centre Health Check ( CTCHC ) for Dundalk . It 's about trying to rejuvenate town centres and find new uses for them . Without meaningful economic activity , town centres , and all the heritage that they contain , will die away . One of the ' new ' uses for vacant and redundant town centre commercial properties is housing people . <p> Many town centres in Ireland are doing very badly and becoming redundant , as business shifts out-of-town or online , so there is huge interest in this project in other towns , which are looking to do what Dundalk has been doing . <p> The aim is to get people to shop in town centres , rather than in out-of-town or edge-of-town shopping centres . It 's also envisaged that people can live over shops in town centres . <p> Louth Heritage Officer Brendan McSherry said : " We want to get young people and students living in town centres , where they can travel locally on foot or by bicycle and easily access inter-hub transport by bus or train . This would also lead to intergenerational mixing and help maintain the range of shops and other businesses for the often older and poorer people who currently live and shop in towns . <p> " Housing people in towns , where they live , shop and socialise , has to make sense from a perspective of limiting climate change and for protecting our heritage .
