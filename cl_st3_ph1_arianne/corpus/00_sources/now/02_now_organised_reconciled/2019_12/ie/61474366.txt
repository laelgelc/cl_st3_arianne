It 's inspiring . " <p> As someone who time-travels on television for a living -- and does it better than ever judging by the electrifying New Year 's Day episode , Spyfall ( Part I ) -- Whittaker offers a unique perspective on the new year . <p> She says : " However deflated we feel , throughout history , people have come together and got over massive hurdles . I mean , they 're the ones that have to deal with all this . <p> " You are never too old or too young to give advice . So you need to be able to receive it from any age group .
