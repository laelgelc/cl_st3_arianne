<h> ' Do n't give us more excuses on climate , ' say school strikers <h> The streets were colder and the numbers smaller but the opinions of school strikers in Dublin during the fourth global day of action on climate change were as strong as ever . <p> A crowd of several hundred gathered outside the Dail for the protest , with gardai moving them to nearby Kildare Place because of safety concerns over the traffic speeding by . <p> The move caused some annoyance but the speakers only shouted all the louder , determined to be heard by whoever might still be in Leinster House on a Friday lunchtime . <p> Sixteen-year-old Brooke Dwyer from Sion Hill College took part in the youth assembly a fortnight ago when young activists were given the Dail chamber to air their views . <p> Reading out the 10 demands the delegates presented that day , she told the crowd she had seen no action on them yet but she would not accept the event being used " as a PR exercise " . <p> Homework <p> " I 'm sick and tired and scared of the excuses , " she said . <p> Conor Slattery , also 16 , and from Blackrock College , took aim at corporations for their " profit before planet " ethos . <p> " I should be in school where my only fear is whether I finish my homework , " he said . " Instead I am bound by moral obligation to the planet and genuine fear about what will happen if I do not do my part . " <p> Blackrock College was criticised during the last global day of action for expressing frustration with the strikes . <p> Conor said there had been a lot of reflection since . " We 're fining anyone who turns up by car ? 2 today to use the car park and other actions are taking place . <p> " The school did n't want a situation where any student felt it was obligatory to leave class . For me , I feel it is , and the school 's OK with that . " <p> Ann Berg , from Lusk , Co Dublin , is in college in Dun Laoghaire but felt she had to support the strike . " I get a train and a Dart to college and often I have to let Darts go by because they 're too crowded . If I have an exam , I have to drive because I ca n't risk being late , " she said . <p> " The Government talks about climate action but it ca n't even get public transport right for people who want to do the right thing . " <p> The strike was organised by the Schools Climate Action Network and Fridays4Future and it was supported by Extinction Rebellion and the Stop Climate Chaos Coalition . <p> Other supporters who joined the crowd included Yummy Mummies 4 Planet , Dads Against Climate Change and Grandparents Supporting School Strikers .
