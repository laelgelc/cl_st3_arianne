<h> Australia probes theft of drinking water in drought-hit state <p> FILE PHOTO : A lone tree stands near a water trough in a drought-effected paddock located on the outskirts of Walgett , in New South Wales , Australia , July 20 , 2018 . REUTERS/David Gray/File Photo <p> MELBOURNE ( Reuters ) - Australian police are investigating the theft of tens of thousands of liters of drinking water from a council site on the far north coast of the drought- and bushfire stricken state of New South Wales . <p> The devastating bushfires have killed at least four people since November , burnt about 2.5 million acres ( 1 million hectares ) of farmland and bush , and destroyed more than 400 homes . <p> Police said offenders in a water tanker and a Toyota Hilux utility stole about 25,000 liters of drinking water from a property in the town of Murwillumbah on the state 's far northern coast . <p> The act of stealing water during a time of hardship for much of New South Wales was " shocking , " said Detective Chief Inspector Luke Arthurs . <p> " This kind of theft is not acceptable , " he added . " Police will continue to investigate this incident and are pleading with anyone with information to contact us . " <p> Sydney was blanketed in smoke this week as the state registered some of the worst air quality in the world , heightening public anger and raising political pressure on the government to do more to battle climate change . <p> While police are investigating what triggered some fires , firefighters and scientists have blamed the bulk of the blazes on soaring temperatures and three years of drought that left bushland tinder-box dry .
