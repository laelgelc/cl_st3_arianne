Right now , we are desperate for any sign of hope , " she said . <p> This was meant to be a big moment in the talks , the elixir of the " Greta effect " bringing new energy to a flagging process . <p> The teenager is almost certainly the most famous person here , attracting far more attention than other celebrities like Al Gore , and the UN badly needs a boost . <p> Her talk came over as measured , grounded in the latest research , and avoided the flash of hurt and anger she displayed in New York in September . <p> Looking around the hall , it was striking how many of the national delegations had not turned up for this morning session at the conference . <p> A snub by the big fossil fuel economies ?
