<p> ( Eds : Disclaimer : The following press release comes to you under an arrangement with PR Newswire . PTI takes no editorial responsibility for the same . ) The book will focus on creating awareness about the importance of conservation of mangroves among kids <p> MUMBAI , Dec. 12 , 2019 /PRNewswire/ -- Godrej &amp; Boyce collaborated with renowned children 's author Katie Bagli to create the book ' Many Secrets of Mangroves ' . The book , launched today , is just one of the many initiatives by the company to spread awareness about environment conservation and the importance of the Mangroves ecosystem . <p> The book , unveiled by Dr. Pheroza Godrej , an avid nature lover has been dedicated to the memory of Naval Pirojsha Godrej - a man who not only built the Indian industry but was also passionate about the environment . Dr. K. Kathiresan , a world-class scientist , also gave a talk on the diversity and importance of Indian Mangroves . He has spent most of his career studying the mangrove wetlands . <p> Mangroves are not only treasure troves of biodiversity but also are of great ecological importance . Also known as ' Oceanic Rain-forests ' , Mangroves provide the right conditions for fish to breed , thereby supporting the livelihood of fishermen . In addition , they provide firewood , timber , cattle feed , honey , medicines , recreation and scope for tourism development . They are the only tall tree forests between the land and sea in the tropical and warm temperate coasts . Mangroves are like ' Green forts ' that protect the coastal life against natural calamities such as tsunamis , storm surges , cyclones and floods . It was the mangroves that saved human life and property in India during the 1999 super-cyclone of Odisha and the 2004 Asian tsunami . <p> During the launch , author Katie Bagli laid emphasis on the importance of Mangroves in protecting the city from the increasing pollution and against natural calamities . While unveiling the book , Author Katie Bagli said , " We have only one life and we must do our best to live it the right way so that future generations may also continue to enjoy the gifts of nature . In doing so , we must also teach our children the importance of protecting the environment and the part that they can also play in a world where digital technologies and social media play a large influence in our lives . I 'm proud to have worked alongside Godrej in creating a book that will help spread such awareness and influence young minds who will play a major role in shaping our future . We hope the children have fun , learn and pass the knowledge on to their friends too . " <p> On this occasion Dr. Pheroza Godrej said , " Children are key to the progress of our nation . As parents and mentors , we must do our part to arm them with the right values and information to enable them to act responsibly . We undertook the endeavor of putting together this wonderful book along with Katie Bagli in order to educate the young minds and enable fun learning too . We hope that the children that read this book will grow fond of the characters that protect the environment . " <p> Speaking at the book launch Mr. Anup Mathew , EVP and Business Head , Godrej Construction , Godrej &amp; Boyce , said , " It is evident that many countries around the world are increasingly bearing the consequences of Climate Change . India is not an exception . Adults and children , both need to be aware about its impact . Our children will play a major role in shaping the future of the country . They need to be inspired to choose wisely - being mindful about the importance of conserving nature . We are hopeful that with the help of this book , children will be able to learn about the importance of mangroves in the ecosystem in a fun-filled manner . " <p> The book has nine different stories and ends with the pictures of the mangroves and wildlife . The book is absolutely free and will be available on the mangroves website ( mangroves.godrej.com ) . <p> On June 5th this year , on the occasion of World Environment Day , Godrej has also released the upgraded version of the ' Mangroves App ' . This app covers 67 Indian mangrove and mangrove associate species and is available in 9 Indian languages of coastal states along with Hindi and English . It is available on Android , iOS , and Windows platforms . <p> The Godrej mangroves at Vikhroli extend their silent ecosystem services much beyond their boundaries to the entire Mumbai Metropolitan Region . A two-year research study revealed that the standing stock , with its biomass and sediments , holds 6 lakh tonnes equivalent of carbon dioxide - a key greenhouse gas responsible for global warming and climate change . Additionally , around 60,000 equivalent tonnes of carbon dioxide is sequestered every year . Besides land stabilisation , the mangrove ecosystem plays a vital role in natural cycles and nutrient recycling thereby maintaining the environment balance .
