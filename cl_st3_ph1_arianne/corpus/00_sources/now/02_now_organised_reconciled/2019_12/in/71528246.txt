In India , Sub Lieutenant Shivangi ( 24 ) became the first woman pilot for the Indian Navy , fulfilling her life-long dream of becoming a pilot . Two more women pilots also got their Naval wings in December 2 , 2019 , " reads a report of the UN Women . " In commercial airlines , Captain Wendy Rexon and her daughter first-officer Kelly Rexon became the first mother-daughter pilot duo . The two shared the cockpit for a flight from New York , " it added .
