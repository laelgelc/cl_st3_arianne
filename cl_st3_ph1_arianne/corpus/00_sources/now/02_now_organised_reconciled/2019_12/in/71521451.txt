This rapid expansion of population in the last decade arises two significant questions - How can the transition to a stabilising population also be a transition to sustainability ? And can human needs be met while saving the environment ? <p> A major threat to the environment that escalated in the last decade is air pollution . As per the World Economic Forum , air pollution is an invisible killer and it is one of the leading environmental threat to the public . The World Health Organization has estimated that seven million premature deaths each year can be linked to air pollution and nearly 7,500 to 52,000 people die in the United States alone every year because of exposure to small particles due to power plant emissions . <p> Air pollution is getting worse in developing countries and while air quality is getting considerably better in some of the developed nations . An estimated 4.2 million premature deaths globally are linked to ambient air pollution , mainly from heart disease , stroke , chronic obstructive pulmonary disease , lung cancer , and acute respiratory infections in children . <p> WHO data reveals that the worldwide ambient air pollution accounts for , 29% of all deaths and disease from lung cancer , 17% of all deaths and disease from an acute lower respiratory infection and 24% of all deaths from stroke . <p> Along with air pollution , climate change is another threat , as the global temperatures have increased 1.5 degree Celsius . Greenhouse gases are one of the major causes of climate change , while the increased ocean temperature is affecting the marine life and ecosystems habituated there . Climate change has resulted in massive floods , extreme summers and winters , while the world continues to suffer irreversibly . Reducing the carbon footprint is one of the few solutions to combat global warming . More importantly , we need to educate the world on the effects and severity of global warming , change the discourse of climate change , before it 's too late . <p> In order to encounter these biggest threats , societies , political leaders should approach sustainable development not as a destination but as an ongoing , adaptive learning process .
