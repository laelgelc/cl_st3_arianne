<p> Any revolution can start with just only one person , and true to the saying , a resident of Dhayari and an engineer-turned-climate change activist , has taken up the issue of garbage burning in Dhayari and surrounding areas . To lead by example , activist Madhav Patil has been extinguishing all fires related to garbage in his neighbourhood and adjoining areas . <p> Patil calls this initiative ' Dongrala aag lagli pala pala ' , which means that the mountain has caught fire and one must run away . This is a popular outdoor game in the state where children run in all directions and one person has to catch them . However , through this movement that Patil has begun , he is defining the garbage as a mountain and is appealing to people that they should n't run away from this mountain of garbage but run towards it and put the fire off . <p> . <p> Patil , with a handful of volunteers , is taking these steps to safeguard the environment and prevent harmful effects of garbage burning on people . He started this movement a fortnight ago and is carrying out the activity of putting off fire in Dhayari , Sinhagad Road , Katraj and Ambegaon areas . <p> Speaking to Mirror , Patil said , " During winter every year , people residing in the nearby slums burn garbage like woods to feel warm . These people belong to economically weaker backgrounds and are not aware of the repercussions of this and the harm that this activity may cause in return . I have lately started reaching out to them and explaining the problems of pollution to them in a simplified form . Whenever there is a fire breakout , along with a few volunteers , I go to the spot , and put off the fire . We also try to find out who has put this fire and then counsel them to not do so . " <p> However , in case of a major fire breakout , Patil does n't take the task upon himself and immediately calls for help from the fire department , just to be on a safer side . <p> Varsha Dusane , a resident of Dhayari who is aware of Patil 's works , said , " The situation has gone from bad to worse in the past few days . We have to immediately call the fire department in case there 's a fire breakout . Patil is doing a fabulous work and is taking care of our environment . I stay in a populous society and I 'm in the process of taking all the children from the society on one of the weekends to go help Patil as there is so much that one can learn from him . " <p> On the administration 's part hardly recognising Patil 's efforts , officials said they are keeping a tab on garbage burning in the area . Santosh Tandale , ward officer of Pune Municipal Corporation 's Sinhagad Road , has assured people that very soon the whole situation will settle down and there will not be any kind of garbage burning . Speaking about the same , he said , " I am keeping a close watch on this issue and have instructed the staff to make rounds and take action against the ones who are burning the garbage .
