<h> Kerala tops in coastal erosion : Study <p> Thrissur : Kerala tops in the list of coastal states which are under the threat of coastal erosion , reports a recent study . When the waves and currents remove 41 percent of sand from coastal shores , only 21 percent sand is being dumped into the land . Union Ministry for environment , forest and climate change carried out the study . <p> The strong waves in the Arabian sea causes coastal erosion in Kerala coast . <p> In Kerala , highest soil erosion is recorded in Thiruvananthapuram . Kollam and Ernakulam districts are placed in the second position . Compared to other districts , coastal erosion is low in Alappuzha and Thrissur districts . <h> Forests under threat of soil erosion <p> At the same time , 9,06,440 hectares of forest land has vanished due to soil erosion from 1973 to 2016 . Indian Institute of Science carried out a study regarding this through remote sensing . <p> The rise in soil erosion also affected dams in the state . The capacity of dams in the state has fallen by 20 percent due to soil erosion . <p> Gujarat and Daman and Diu has a coastal erosion of 26 percent followed by Maharashtra and Goa with 24 percent .
