<h> Things to do in Ahmedabad <p> Enjoy Rajasthani Folk musicThe Manganiyars , a community of musicians hailing from the heart of the Thar Desert , are known to be the true keepers of Rajasthani folk music . Bringing their magic to the city as a part of their ' Music of the Dunes ' series , Natarani presents ' Sakar Khan Manganiyar and his troupe ' . Manganiyar music includes ballads , and Sufi poems written by various mystics . They sing songs about Alexander the Great , the local Maharajas and past battles in the region . Their songs are passed on to generations as a form of the oral history of the desert . <p> Catch classic musicalsAll-time popular Broadway shows and musicals are being brought to the city and we are sure you would n't want to miss it . The acts are being put together by the students of Drama Studio by Shreta and includes four classic plays : Shakespeare 's Hamlet , The Lion King , Aladdin and Charlie and the Chocolate Factory . Head to the show with your children and relive these classic tales . <p> A spectacular eveningTo celebrate classical dance in its purest form and spread awareness about it , an event is being organised at the banks of the beautiful Sabarmati river . ' The Classical Dance -- World of Sparkling Symmetry ' will see around 50 students of Nrityabharti showcase nine performances which include Natesh Kautvam , Shiva stuti , Ramachandra kripalu bhajamana , Ganesh vandana , Mandir taru vishwa rupalu , alarippu , Gajanan stuti and Jatiswaram . The dance concept and choreography is by Chandan Thakore . <p> The journey of a dancerBhanumathi Rao , a dancer and theatre actor in her younger days , is now in her mid-nineties , living with her two daughters . Catch ' Oh that 's Bhanu ' , a documentary which covers her journey of life and performance , through the spasms of memory . The Bharatnatyam dancer is known to lead an enigmatic , passionate , yet a simple and pragmatic life . Directed and produced by RV Ramani , the film shows how her wit and elegance make up for her hearing and memory , which have been tainted by age . The filmmaker will also be present for discussion post the screening . <p> Celebrating freedom of speechThere is no denying that poetry is a powerful form of expression . Combine it with social causes , and the winds of change are soon to follow . Believing this , ' Shaam-e-satrangi ' brings you poems for a better future , for a violence free society , for solidarity of efforts to end all forms of violence , to support the 16 Days of Activism Against Gender-Based Violence ( an international campaign to challenge violence against women and girls ) and much more . Participating poets are Deepa , Shahin Shaikh , Atish Indrekar , Shivani Shah , Shraddha , Mushtaqueali Shaikh and Hozefa Ujjaini . <p> Straight from the farmsIn a day and age when eating outside has become a norm , it is necessary to make children the importance of homemade food and the source of it . Farmer 's Market is here to bridge the gap . In the event , people can not only buy farm-fresh vegetables , seasonal fruits , hand pounded spices , naturally grown grains , cold press oils , essential oils , handmade gifting products , bamboo products , etc , but also interact with farmers to know the whole process and indulge in traditional recipes . One can even get tips of kitchen gardening from experts other than participating in the various workshops like cooking , pottery , lippan kaam . <p> The mountains are callingTo celebrate International Mountain Day 2019 ( observed on Dec 11 ) , a ' Mountain Day March ' full of hiking , trekking , and scrambling has been organised in the feral landscapes of Banaskantha in the Aravalli hills . With the theme for this year being ' Mountains Matter For Youth ' , a small informative session about protecting mountain ecologies has been arranged along with a few nature connection activities and a voluntarily clean up trail . The idea is to realise how young people can become active agents of change and the future leaders to combat climate change . <p> The city and its storiesSpend your Sunday morning in the old city , getting to know more about Ahmedabad , the secrets it has kept and the traditional and cultural insights it shelters . At the ' Manek to Manek ' heritage walk , discover interesting spots of the city including Siddik Kotval Cemetry , Old Jami Masjid , Bhadrakali Temple , Badshah no Haziro and much more . Gain insights into the 608 years ' worth of rich history and a better understanding about names -- Ahmedabad , Karnavati , Ashawal and Ashapalli . Register now and enjoy the treasures our city has to offer . <p> Taking the floral routeNot letting go of her passion is what has led Grace D'souza to continue her lover for painting florals , resulting in her solo exhibition , ' Floral Route ' . 37 paintings , all done as acrylic on canvas are on display at Kanoria art gallery . " Life is a journey and we all choose to take a different route but if given a choice I will always choose the Floral Route " shares the artist , adding how she only works on floral patterns and feels that adding a little bit of creativity to everything is her motto in life . <p> Learn a new art formThis Sunday , explore your skillset by mastering an art form . Pick between traditional Gond painting and a bit more contemporary art , charcoal painting . You will be given the tips and tricks of both the art forms and later you will be creating your own artwork . What 's more , you will be provided with all the material . <p> Learn coffee artIf the smell of caffeine gives you a high , this event is just for the likes of you . No , you are n't going to find different types of coffee here , but create an artwork using it . Coffee Essence Workshop comprises using coffee and water to make compositions on cartridge sheets . From abstracts and graphics to landscapes and figurines , everything can be created using coffee art . The workshop includes introduction to the art , its advantages and drawbacks and working on a landscape and will be conducted by Trishna Patnaik . Participants can also take their A4 size painting with them . <p> Paint with threadYou might have heard about stitching with thread to create designs on clothes . But how about creating an art using this medium on a canvas cloth ? You can learn this at Thread Expression workshop where participants will be taught to use thread in different ways and stick it on six designs . Running , continuous design , cutting the thread in small pieces and sticking it -- everything will be taught at the workshop .
