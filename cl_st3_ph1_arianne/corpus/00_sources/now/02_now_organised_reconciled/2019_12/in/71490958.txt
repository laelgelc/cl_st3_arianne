Local sales of diesel , which accounts for about two-fifths of overall fuel consumption , slipped 7.4 percent year-on-year to 6.51 mt . The annual decline was the most since January 2017 , according to PPAC . Demand for diesel in the world 's third biggest oil importer is seen as a measure of industrial vibrancy as it is used , for example , to fuel trucks transporting goods across the country . Sales of gasoline , or petrol , rose 8.9 percent in October from a year earlier , to 2.54 mt . However , demand for diesel is expected to recover in the next six months as the longer-than-usual monsoon season that affected transportation and industry has ended . Slowing economic and industrial activity has already led some global agencies to cut their Indian fuel demand forecasts . Growth in fuel demand in India is on course to fall to its lowest in at least six years as the economy slows and after heavy rains impacted gasoil consumption .
