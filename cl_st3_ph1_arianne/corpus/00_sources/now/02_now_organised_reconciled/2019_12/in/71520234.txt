<h> TEDxIIMBangalore ' LEAP into the future ' <h> Experts from diverse backgrounds shared ideas at the one-day event <p> Themed ' LEAP ( Learn , envisage , assimilate and perform ) into the future ' , IIM Bangalore hosted TEDxIIMBangalore on December 21 . The aim was to provide a window into the future by understanding how our actions in different domains would shape it . Another objective was to capture the interplay of different contemporary fields and envision how these interdependencies would evolve in the coming decades . <p> Expert talk <p> Experts from diverse backgrounds shared ideas at the one-day event . Tejasvi Surya , Member of Parliament , Lok Sabha , and lawyer , discussed urban mobility crisis and need for an exclusive legislation for Bengaluru . While Ashwarya Singh , Co-founder and CEO , Drivezy , spoke on ' The transformative power of sharing in India : the next billion users ' ; Dr Jyoti Parikh , Executive Director , Integrated Research and Action for Development ( IRADe ) , spoke on ' India and global negotiations on climate change ' . <p> Playback singer and Padmashri awardee Kavita Krishnamurthy shared her thoughts on ' How music inspires leadership ' , and film trade analyst Komal Nahta gave an insight on ' How movies teach you language of business ' . The event witnessed Pragnya Mohan , athlete and chartered accountant , speak on ' Business lessons from sports ' , and Soumya Choudhury , Co-founder and Partner , Visilogix , who is an alumnus of IIM-A and IIT-B , shared his views on ' Blockchain in supply chain ' .
