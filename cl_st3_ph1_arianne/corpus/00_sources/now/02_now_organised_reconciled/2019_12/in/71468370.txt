<h> Climate Change <h> Conference of the Parties 18 <p> Doha , Qatar 26 Nov 2012 - 07 Dec 2012 <p> Qatar hosted COP 18 which took place in Doha , Qatar , from 26 November to 7 December 2012 . The Conference produced a package of documents collectively titled The Doha Climate Gateway . The conference made little progress towards the funding of the Green Climate Fund . Russia , Belarus and Ukraine objected at the end of the session , clarification needed as they had a right to under the session 's rules .
