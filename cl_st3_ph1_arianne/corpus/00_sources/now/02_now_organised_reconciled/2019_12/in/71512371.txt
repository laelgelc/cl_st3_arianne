( Photo:File ) <p> Washington : America 's legendary civil rights leader Congressman John Lewis has introduced a bill in the House of Representatives to promote the legacy of Mahatma Gandhi and Martin Luther King Jr and sought a budgetary allocation of USD 150 million for the next five years . <p> Introduced to mark the 150th birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi , the House Bill ( HR 5517 ) affirms the friendship between the two largest democracies of the world and honours the legacy and contributions of Mahatma Gandhi and Dr Martin Luther King . <p> Among other things , the bill proposes establishing a Gandhi-King Development Foundation , which will be created by the USAID under the Indian laws . <p> The bill seeks a budgetary allocation of USD 30 million every year for the next five years to the USAID for this foundation . <p> This foundation would have a governing council convened by governments of the United States and India and would oversee grants to NGOs in the areas of health , pollution and climate change , education and women empowerment , the bill says . <p> It is being co-sponsored by six other Democratic lawmakers , three of whom are Indian-Americans -- Dr Ami Bera , Ro Khanna and Pramila Jayapal . The three other Congressmen are Brenda Lawrence , Brad Sherman and James McGovern . <p> The bill proposes the establishment of a Gandhi-King Scholarly Exchange Initiative with an allocation of USD 2 million for the next five years till 2025 . <p> It will comprise an annual educational forum for scholars from India and the US held alternately in the two countries . <p> The conference will focus on the study of the works and philosophies of Mahatma Gandhi and Martin Luther King and visits to historical sites . It also seeks to establish a Gandhi-King global academy , which would be a professional development training initiative on conflict resolution . <p> The bill proposes an allocation of USD 2 million for each fiscal year from 2020 through 2025 , implemented through the United States Institute of Peace ( USIP ) . <p> Welcoming the introduction of the bill , India 's Ambassador to the US Harsh Vardhan Shringla said it " reinforces the close cultural and ideological bonds " between India and the US . <p> Noting that Mahatma Gandhi and Martin Luther King Jr were dedicated leaders fighting for social justice and social change , peace , and civil rights , the bill says that the use of non-violent civil disobedience is a shared tactic that has played a key role in defeating social injustice in India , the United States and other parts of the world . <p> Observing that Dr King 's effective use of Mahatma Gandhi 's principles was instrumental to the American civil rights movement , the bill says that there is a long history of civil and social rights movements in the United States and in India . <p> This bill , which has the support of House Speaker Nancy Pelosi , is being considered as a significant landmark initiative by the United States Congress to commemorate the 150th birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi . <p> There are two other resolutions on Mahatma Gandhi pending in the US Congress - one by Senators Bob Menendez and Ted Cruz in the Senate and the other by several other lawmakers led by Congressman Raja Kishnamoorthi in the House of Representatives . <p> Sent to the House Foreign Affairs Committee for necessary action , the bill christened ' Gandhi-King Scholarly Exchange Initiative Act ' says the people of the United States and India have a long history of friendship and the world will benefit from a stronger United States-India partnership . <p> Mahatma Gandhi never visited the US . But Martin Luther King Jr travelled to India , which he described as a pilgrimage . <p> In February 1959 , Dr King and his wife Coretta Scott King travelled throughout India . By the end of his month-long visit , Dr King said , " I am more convinced than ever before that the method of non-violent resistance is the most potent weapon available to oppressed people in their struggle for justice and human dignity . " <p> Fifty years after Dr King 's visit , All India Radio discovered a taped message by Dr King that emphasised the intellectual harmony between messages of Dr King and Gandhi on non-violent social action . <p> " Mohandas Gandhi , who employed the principle of satyagraha or fighting with peace , has come to represent the moral force inspiring many civil and social rights movement around the world , " the bill says .
