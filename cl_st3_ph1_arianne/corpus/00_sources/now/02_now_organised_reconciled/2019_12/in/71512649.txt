<h> Metro gets forest land , now needs nod to cut trees <p> BMRCL gets custody of 44 acres of land in Kadugodi , will need BBMP 's permission to cut about 120 trees <p> Construction of two Metro stations and a depot on the Baiyappan ? ahalli - Whitefield stretch is expected to speed up as the Bangalore Metro Rail Corporation Ltd ( BMRCL ) has finally got custody of about 44 acres of forest land . The utility , however , will have to clear another barrier before work actually begins , as it requires permission from the Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike 's ( BBMP ) to cut 120 trees on the forest land . <p> The Ministry of Enviro ? nment , Forests and Climate Change , in its letter dated December 18 , approved the diversion of 18.11 hectares ( 44 acres ) of forest land in Kadugodi for the extension of East-West Metro corridor towards Whitefield ( Phase II ) . The approval was accorded after the BMRCL fulfilled all the conditions laid down by the Ministry . <p> In exchange for the forest land in Kadugodi , the BMRCL has handed over 30 acres of land near Dandeli Tiger Reserve and about 15 acres of land in Thippago ? ndanahalli village . Namma Metro is also required to bear the cost of compensatory afforestation in these places . The legal status of the forest land , however , will remain uncha ? nged . <p> With the acquisition of forest land , BMRCL has now got possession of all the land required for the construction of 15.50-km Metro work that connects the IT hub in Whitefield . The officials appear to have also acquired the Railway land next to Baiyappanahalli depot as the work related to building Metro pillars is under way here . <p> The BMRCL will also be able to start depot work in Kadugodi on land that belonged to the forest department . The permission comes right in time for Namma Metro as it had recently awarded the Rs 159.88-crore contract of constructing the depot to Vascon Engineers Ltd . <p> 120 trees <p> The expert committee -- which looks into the applications seeking permission to fell trees for infrastructure projects -- is expected to survey the forest land in Kadugodi on December 28 . " Metro work is being held up at three stations as we have no permission to fell trees . The committee might decide about cutting down trees in a month or two , " said MS Channapagoudar , General Manager , BMRCL ( Land Acquisition ) . He also said only 13 trees are fit for translocation and the remaining 120 must be cut . <p> In April 2017 , the contract to construct 15.50-km Metro corridor , consisting of 13 stations , was awarded to ITD Cementation India . While the firm has completed an average of over 50% project work on the entire stretch , construction has been hindered at stations such as Kadugodi , Ujwala Vidyalaya and Whitefield due to want of forest land and permission to fell trees .
