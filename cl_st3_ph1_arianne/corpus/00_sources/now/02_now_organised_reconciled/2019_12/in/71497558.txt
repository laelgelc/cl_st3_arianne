<h> Top 10 international newsmakers of 2019 <h> As 2019 is all set to be phased out and the new year 2020 is gearing up to be ushered in , let 's take a look at the personalities who stole the headlines in the year 2019 . <p> The faces that dominated the news in 2019. &amp;nbsp <h> Key Highlights <p> President Trump will seek a second term if he is cleared of any wrongdoing in the impeachment trial <p> At just 43 years old , Ethiopia 's current Prime Minister is the youngest head of state in Africa <p> In a 2019 Stack Overflow developer survey that saw the participation of nearly 90,000 developers , Elon Musk was voted the most inspirational leader in the tech industry <p> From one man 's unveiling of a truck that may revolutionise the four-wheel-drive industry to the demise of another regarded as among the most dangerous men in the world , headlines in 2019 have been dominated by these personalities . <h> Donald Trump <p> Donald Trump 's victory over Hillary Rodham Clinton in the 2016 US Presidential elections shocked the international community , and since then the current US President has served up headline after headline , from his decision to withdraw the US from the Paris Accord to his relentless attacks against the US mainstream media , to the $32 million Mueller report , and more recently , his impeachment in the US House of Representatives . President Trump will seek a second term if he is cleared of any wrongdoing in the impeachment trial . <h> Greta Thunberg <p> 16-year old Greta Thunberg has come a long way since skipping school to carry out climate change protests outside the Swedish Parliament . It would n't be a stretch to say the Swedish activist has become the face of the international movement to address global warming . TIME Magazine named her among the 100 most influential people in 2019 , and its ' Person of the Year . ' Thunberg has also been nominated for the 2019 Nobel Peace Prize . <h> Nancy Pelosi <p> Veteran Democrat and current US Speaker of the House Nancy Pelosi has spearheaded the impeachment drive against sitting US President Donald Trump . As the first female Speaker of the House , Pelosi is widely regarded as the most powerful woman serving in elected office . Known for her leadership and bold decisions , Pelosi 's efforts to impeach President Trump finally paid off on 18 December , when the House of Representatives voted in favour of the US president 's impeachment . <h> Abiy Ahmed <p> At just 43 years old , Ethiopia 's current Prime Minister is the youngest head of state in Africa . He made headlines in October when he was awarded the 2019 Nobel Peace Prize for his sustained efforts to achieve peace and reconciliation between Ethiopia and Eritrea -- two nations that have had long-standing border disputes . Ahmed has also instituted numerous reforms in Ethiopia from liberalising the country 's state-controlled economy to overthrowing security forces crucial to the previous ruling coalition maintaining a vice-like grip around the country . <h> Abu Bakr Al-Baghdadi <p> The infamous leader of the radical Islamist group , Islamic State of Iraq and Syria ( ISIS ) and arguably the world 's most wanted man , Abu Bakr Al-Baghdadi , reportedly , took his own life , during a US-led raid in North-Western Syria earlier this year . The self-proclaimed ' Caliph Ibrahim ' assumed leadership of ISIS in 2010 , following the death of Abu Umar al-Baghdadi , and led the group 's campaigns as it committed countless atrocities and killings including the culling of the Yazidis in Iraq . <h> Xi Jinping <p> Hong Kong 's pro-democracy protests , China 's trade war with the United States and international pressure from multi-lateral human rights organisations , seem to have done little to stop the rise of Xi Jinping to become one of the world 's most powerful men . Using an unorthodox combination of state-control and capitalism , President Xi has endeared himself to China 's working class as he seeks to realise his ' China Dream ' of bringing the country to the international centre stage . <h> Michelle Obama <p> 2019 has been a big year for the former First Lady of the United States . Obama 's memoir , ' Becoming ' , as of December this year , has sold over 11 million copies making it one of the most successful books to feature on The New York Times ' bestseller list . Obama followed this up with a ' companion journal ' called ' Becoming : A Guided Journal for Discovering Your Voice ' in November . The audiobook of her memoir has also been nominated for a Grammy award . <h> Elon Musk <p> In a 2019 Stack Overflow developer survey that saw the participation of nearly 90,000 developers , Elon Musk was voted the most inspirational leader in the tech industry . Renowned for his drive to disrupt the auto-sector with Tesla 's all-electric vehicle technology , in November , the 48-year old futurist unveiled Tesla 's latest creation , the Cybertruck -- an all-electric , all-wheel drive commercial vehicle -- as a sustainable substitute to currently used fossil-fuel powered trucks . Just over a week after the unveiling , Musk revealed to his 29 million Twitter followers that orders for the Cybertruck had already exceeded 200,000 . <h> Boris Johnson <p> Only a year and a half ago , the current UK Prime Minister had just resigned from then UK Prime Minister Theresa May 's Cabinet in protest of her Brexit policies . Johnson then began a campaign against May 's Brexit proposal to the EU , and in June 2019 , he succeeded in replacing May in the top seat . PM Johnson 's Conservative Party also swept the 2019 December elections winning a clear majority , paving the way for Brexit to finally take place on 31 January 2020 . <h> Carrie Lam <p> The Chief Executive of Hong Kong continues to endure one of the most difficult years of her career as the violent pro-democracy protests in Hong Kong , that began as far back as June , continue . The Lam-government had introduced a contentious extradition Bill that sparked fears that Hong Kong 's autonomy from China was being eroded . After months of heavy pressure , and relentless violent protests on the streets of Hong Kong , Lam finally withdrew the Bill .
