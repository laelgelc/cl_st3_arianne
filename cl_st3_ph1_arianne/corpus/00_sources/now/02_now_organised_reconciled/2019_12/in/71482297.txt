However , data from the HRD ministry shows that in these three years , the number of education loans has declined rather sharply . A senior official in the higher education department of the ministry said 6,600 crore has been allocated under the scheme . " The reason is perhaps the extreme caution being taken by banks as they are sanctioning loans for admission into only top ranked institutions , " said the official .
