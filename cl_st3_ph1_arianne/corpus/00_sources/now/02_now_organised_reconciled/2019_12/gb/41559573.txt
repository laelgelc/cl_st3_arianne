<h> General Election 2019 : Social media highs and lows <h> General Election 2019 : Social media highs and lows <p> Social media has at times taken centre stage during this election campaign , with politicians facing increasing scrutiny over their use of online platforms . <p> There have been plenty of controversies as well as some lighter moments along the way . Here is a look at the high- and low-lights of the campaign as it played out online . <p> Tories accused of ' doctoring ' Starmer video <p> Not long after the election campaign got under way , the Conservatives got themselves into hot water with a truncated clip of shadow Brexit secretary Sir Keir Starmer being interviewed on ITV 's Good Morning Britain . <p> The shortened clip ended with Sir Keir staring at the camera blankly after being asked by Piers Morgan : " Why would the EU give you a good deal if they know you are going to actively campaign against it ? " <p> However , in the original interview Sir Keir actually responded at length to the question . Despite widespread criticism , the party stood by the edit , tweeting : " Believe it or not , this car crash interview did really take place . " <p> After the misuse of Facebook data during the Brexit vote and Trump campaigns , there has been a high level of scrutiny on how parties were targeting members of the public this time round . <p> The company faced calls to ban political advertising entirely after Twitter announced plans to do so -- but Mark Zuckerberg insisted he did not believe his company should censor " political speech " . <p> Overall , the parties have spent hundreds of thousands of pounds on Facebook ads during the campaign , while thousands more were spent on adverts funded from other sources , such as supporters . <p> High-profile controversial examples included a Conservative ad that was pulled by Facebook for using edited versions of BBC content and a series of misleading graphs from the Liberal Democrats used to claim it was the only party able to beat the other major parties " in seats like yours " . <p> Lord Buckethead becomes ...
