<h> Environment group to stage demonstration in Witney <h> Extinction Rebellion to stage demonstration in Witney <p> EXTINCTION Rebellion ( XR ) Red Rebels will be dressed as ghostly figures cloaked in blood-red costumes as part of a campaign on Saturday , November 30 . <p> A local group of the national XR movement - XR Charlbury - are planning a series of actions in the lead up to the general election in order to put climate change and the environment at the top of the new government 's political agenda . <p> The Red Rebel 's procession will start at the junction of Bridge Street and the High Street at 11am . <p> They will then progress along Witney High Street with a funeral cortege and pallbearers carrying a coffin that represents the planet . <p> In the 2017 election only 8% of the British public considered the environment to be one of the top 3 issues facing the country ; in 2019 25% of Britons place it in one of their top 3 issues . <p> Jenifer Wates , 86 yr old XR Charlbury Red Rebel said : " I mourn the many wonders of nature that I have loved all my life , and grieve for the catastrophe that threatens my grandchildren and great-grandchildren , and all who will follow . " <p> Braden Sheehy , 15 , said : " We are tired of hearing about short term issues . We want people to consider the climate crisis as our primary focus . If radical change does not happen now , the adults will be gambling with our futures and the futures of later generations .
