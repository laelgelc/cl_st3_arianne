<p> Flushing previous records down the drain , the Mayor bought enough paper towels to cover the whole of Wales , Jamaica twice or over 3000 football pitches . <p> The total weight of the 4.3 million paper towels comes to 8.4 tonnes -- the equivalent of four London taxis . <p> If all of the towels were lined up , they would reach from City Hall to Zurich in Switzerland . <p> It has also flushed the previous years total of 4,136,250 , and 4,020,000 in the year before . <p> Now the Conservatives have accused Mr Khan of betraying his environmental credentials , and not doing enough for the environment . <p> They say it shows that the 49-year-old has broken his manifesto promise to " make sure City Hall and all other GLA buildings maintain the highest environmental standards . " <p> Tory Tony Devenish AM said : " The fact that Sadiq Khan is prepared to lecture Londoners on the importance of getting to grips with climate change while spending thousands of pounds of taxpayers ' money on wasteful papers towels goes to show that he is guilty of staggering hypocrisy . <p> " If the Mayor was serious about improving our environment , he 'd get his own house in order . <p> " Instead of ordering millions of paper towels every year , the Mayor should be investing in hand dryers and other , less wasteful , initiatives . "
