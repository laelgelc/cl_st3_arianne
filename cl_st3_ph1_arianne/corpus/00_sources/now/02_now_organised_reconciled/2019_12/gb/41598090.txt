<h> Local School win sustainable transport award for pounding the pavement <p> Evendons Primary School in Finchampstead is celebrating a successful evening at the Modeshift South East regional awards after being named ' Best School in Wokingham ' for going the extra mile promoting the benefits of walking , cycling and scooting to school . <p> Local primary schools Evendons , The Coombes in Arborfield and Wheatfield in Winnersh were also all nominated for the prestigious ' School of the Region ' award for their incredible efforts to educate their pupils and parents about active and sustainable travel , encouraging them to ditch the car on the school run , where possible , which is part of the council 's ' My Journey ' initiative and plays a key role in the council 's efforts to be carbon neutral by 2030 . <p> Laura Shambrook , school secretary and travel plan officer for Evendons Primary School , accepted the award with pupil Stanley Shambrook , on behalf of the school . The award was presented by David Elliot Cooper from Histrionics on behalf of Modeshift , at the prestigious event last month at the Everyman Cinema in Kings Cross , London . <p> The three schools nominated for regional awards , together with Gorse Ride Junior School , Highwood Primary School , Oaklands Junior and Walter Infants , have this year also been presented with the Modeshift STARS bronze certificates for demonstrating excellence in supporting cycling , walking and other forms of sustainable travel . <p> To achieve accreditation the schools had to ask pupils how they get to school and put in place initiatives to increase the numbers of children walking , scooting and cycling to school . They also had to consult the school leadership team , governors and Wokingham Borough Council to identify any travel or transport issues . <p> The schools have taken part in at least ten sustainable travel events including road safety shows , scooter and bikeability training , as well as teaching their pupils about the benefits of active travel and how to safely travel in the winter . <p> Evendons Primary School has continued to show great commitment to promoting sustainable travel and tackling climate change , having earned silver accreditation for their Modeshift STARS travel plan and completed 42 sustainable travel initiatives <p> NOTES <p> Modeshift stars is a national awards scheme providing recognition to the schools who demonstrate excellence in promoting active and sustainable travel . <p> My Journey encourages schools across Wokingham Borough to get involved and offers a ? 2,000 grant to schools which achieve a new Modeshift stars accreditation for spending on further promotion of active and sustainable travel to and from school .
