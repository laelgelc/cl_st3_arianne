<p> The Prime Minister and Brexit Party leader were both invited to take part in an hour-long Channel 4 News debate in which all the party leaders will discuss climate change and the environment . <p> But after they snubbed the event , Channel 4 placed two ice sculptures alongside empty podiums which , it said " represent the emergency on planet earth " and the leaders ' refusal to attend despite " our repeated invitations " . <h> Sculpture a ' reminder ' of absence <p> Boris Johnson refused to take part in the climate debate ( Photo : Getty ) <p> During the show , titled Emergency On Planet Earth , the leaders were set to be grilled on their environment policies and asked how voters ' lives would need to change in order to meet national targets for refusing carbon emissions . <p> Channel 4 said it had invited Mr Johnson but he did not attend and , instead , Tory minister and former environment secretary Michael Gove turned up at the studio , as well as Mr Johnson 's father Stanley . <p> But Channel 4 bosses refused to allow either of them to take part in Mr Johnson 's place because neither is the party leader . <h> ' Visual metaphor ' <p> Presenter Krishnan Guru-Murthy , tweeted : " It was kind of Michael Gove to turn up at Channel4News tonight offering to come on the #climatedebate but the other party leaders will only debate other party leaders . We look forward to welcoming Mr Gove another time . " <p> A second sculpture was put in place in Mr Farage 's absence ( Photo : PA Wire ) <p> Ben de Pear , editor of Channel 4 News , said : " In a last ditch attempt Boris Johnson has sent his two wing men best friend Michael Gove and dad Stanley Johnson to argue their way into a programme intended only for leaders . they were lovely and charming but neither are the leader . " <p> He added : " These two ice sculptures represent the emergency on planet earth , not in any human form but are a visual metaphor for the Conservative and Brexit parties after their leaders declined our repeated invitations to attend tonight 's vital climate debate . " <p> Invitations to Mr Johnson and Mr Farage remained open , the programme said . <p> Mr Johnson had previously said he does not want to debate SNP leader Ms Sturgeon because she can not become prime minister .
