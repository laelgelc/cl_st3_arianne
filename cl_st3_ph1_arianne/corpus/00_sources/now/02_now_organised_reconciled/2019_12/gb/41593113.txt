<p> Two thirds of people believe in the need to curb air travel to tackle climate change , a survey suggests . <p> More than a third of those quizzed are " very " or " extremely worried " about climate change -- up from just a fifth three years ago , the poll for the new Centre for Climate Change and Social Transformations ( CAST ) found . <p> Almost half of more than 2,000 adults questioned by YouGov for the centre , which is led by scientists from Cardiff University , felt they had become more worried about climate change than they were 12 months ago . <p> When asked why their views had changed , people cited reasons including extreme weather events , greater media coverage and a sense of a lack of political and society-wide action to tackle climate change . <p> More than two thirds agreed that people should limit the amount of flying they do , while just 15% thought such a move was not needed . <h> Related ... <p> More than half thought people should cut down on the amount of meat in their diets to address climate change .
