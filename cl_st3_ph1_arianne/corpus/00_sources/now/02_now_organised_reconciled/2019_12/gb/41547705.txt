They 're blue . <p> " You 're a Tory in disguise , state what you are . You dare to try and raise taxes on lower earners when the country is in austerity . How dare you . " <p> Ms Swinson -- who was part of the Tory and Lib Dem coalition government between 2010 and 2015 -- replied to her critic by saying : " And we need to end that ( austerity ) by investing in our public services and we need to be honest about how we do that . <p> " If you look at the independent Institute for Fiscal Studies , they have looked at the Labour plan and they say they are not credible . " <p> The comeback saw shadow education secretary Angela Rayner launch into an angry tirade , yelling at the former coalition minister : " You voted for the bedroom tax , you voted for austerity , you voted for tuition fees . <p> " You did that nine years ago -- apologise for tuition fees . " <p> The Lib Dem leader responded by accusing Labour of " making a list of promises that you can not deliver " . <p> The leader of five months said she had apologised for her party 's part in hiking tuition fees , from ?
