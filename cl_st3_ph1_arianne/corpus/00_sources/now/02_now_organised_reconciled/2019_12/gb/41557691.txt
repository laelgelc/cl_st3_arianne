<h> PICTURES : Volunteers plant 3,000 trees on Smithills Estate in ONE day <h> Volunteers plant 3,000 trees on Smithills Estate in ONE day <p> HUNDREDS of people joined in a mass campaign on Saturday , as two weeks ' work culminated with 3,000 trees being planted on just one day . <p> The Woodland Trust 's Big Climate Fightback campaign was launched in September and hundreds of thousands of people pledged to plant trees across the UK . <p> About 15,000 trees have been planted up in Bolton over the last two weeks as hundreds of volunteers put on their wellies to help out . <p> In total across the UK , the charity estimates that more than a million trees will be planted during the campaign . <p> Darren Moorcroft , the Woodland Trust 's chief executive , said : " Since we launched the Big Climate Fightback the response has been remarkable . <p> " We have had everyone from well-known faces such as JB Gill and Cressida Cowell pledging to plant trees , to wonderful stories of school children of just five years old being inspired to get involved . <p> " This is what the Big Climate Fightback is all about ? -- giving people of all ages , no matter where they live , a simple way to make a difference on climate change by planting a tree . <p> " Trees are the natural solution ? -- they soak up CO2 and deliver oxygen . They are of course not the only solution though , every one of us could also do our bit by recycling more and reducing our carbon footprint . " <p> Tracey Garrett , general manager at Smithills Estate Woodland Trust , said : " It was a smashing day because the weather was so good . We had about 300 people with us , their were kids running about enjoying themselves and it was brilliant . " <p> The 685 hectares of the Smithills Estate is the largest site the Woodland Trust has ever acquired in England . Other large planting events for the Trust have included some of their sites at Mead in Derbyshire , Kinclaven Bluebell Wood in Scotland , Low Burnhall in County Durham , Avoncliff in Wiltshire and Tring Park in Hertfordshire . <p> Tracey added that they are still eager for people to join in .
