<h> Dorset Council sets out proposed priorities <p> Dorset Council Cabinet has approved its draft Council Plan outlining the organisation 's aspirations and priorities for the next four years . <p> The five priorities are : <p> Economic growth <p> Unique environment <p> Staying safe and well <p> Strong , healthy communities <p> Suitable housing . <p> The Council Plan outlines proposed activity to achieve these priorities , all contributing towards making Dorset " a great place to live , work and visit " . <p> Cutting across all of these priorities is the council 's commitment to tackling climate change and reducing its carbon footprint -- work on which is currently underway through a cross-party Executive Advisory Panel , chaired by Cllr Ray Bryan , Cabinet Member for Highways , Travel and Environment . <p> Dorset Council will now engage with residents and partners throughout October , November and December to seek views and feedback on the draft Council Plan . <p> Cllr Spencer Flower , Leader of Dorset Council , said : <p> " I would like to thank councillors for their input in shaping this draft Council Plan over recent weeks . We are ambitious for the people of Dorset and this plan sets out our proposals for what we believe Dorset Council should deliver over the next four years . " <p> The final Council Plan , incorporating any feedback received from public engagement , will then be submitted to Full Council for approval alongside the council 's budget and the medium-term financial plan in February 2020 .
