<p> ALMOST 2,000 vulnerable people , living on low incomes and struggling to heat their homes , were helped last year by the Footprint Trust . <p> The figure was revealed at the Isle of Wight charity 's AGM , following outreach events and home visits throughout the year . <p> It was also reported the trust had helped people save more than ? 100,000 on their fuel bills and reduced CO2 emissions by 500 tonnes . <p> Staff were praised for making a real impact in reducing fuel poverty on the Island and helping to cut carbon emissions , an essential element in tackling climate change , and for their involvement in a number of nature conservation projects . <p> They were also commended for their fundraising achievements , despite the declining availability of suitable grants . <p> Founded in 2002 to promote sustainable living , the trust works with veteran charities , family centres , social services , health visitors , carers , social and private landlords , Foodbanks , churches and schools . <p> Over the next ten years , it hopes to reduce the Island 's ecological footprint by 50 per cent . <p> This website and associated newspapers adhere to the Independent Press Standards Organisation 's Editors ' Code of Practice .
