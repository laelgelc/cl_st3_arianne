We have to be ambitious and we can not hide away from tough decisions . <p> That is why , in recognition of the need to limit aviation emissions , we have decided not to proceed with a reduction in air passenger duty . <p> We have been clear that we will not allow licences for fracking to take place in Scotland and we have given a commitment to phase out the need for petrol and diesel cars by 2032 . <p> All of this will be backed up by a Green New Deal -- billions of pounds of investment to help deliver a net-zero future . <p> We have pledged to end Scotland 's contribution to climate change , definitively , within a generation <p> This includes more than ? 500m of investment in improved bus infrastructure , the world 's first zero emissions aviation region in the Highlands and Islands , and plans to decarbonise the railways by 2035 . <p> Scotland is already contributing more than its projected share of UK tree-planting targets , with 22 million planted in 2018-19 .
