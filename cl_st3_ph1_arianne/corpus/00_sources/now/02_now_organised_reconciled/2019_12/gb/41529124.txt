<h> Christine Lagarde <p> THE REFORMER <p> DOER #2 -- FRANCE <p> Illustration by W.Flemming for POLITICO <p> As president of the European Central Bank , Christine Lagarde will be responsible not just for keeping the eurozone from collapsing but also for shoring up its foundations . She will have plenty to do in both areas . The former French finance minister takes over just as the European economy is turning sluggish . And while her predecessor Mario Draghi is lauded for navigating the eurozone through the financial crisis , he has also left the bank bereft of ammunition . With interest rates at record lows and the ECB arguably overinvested in bonds , Lagarde would likely have to turn to untested measures were she to intervene . <p> Lagarde 's critics fret she lacks the necessary policy chops , but her boosters say it is her skills as a politician that the bank needs right now -- to win over Europe 's fiscal hawks and nudge European leaders on much-needed reforms . In her previous position as head of the International Monetary Fund , Lagarde advocated for a laxer approach to Greek debt . Since her nomination for the ECB , she has suggested she would like to see a larger eurozone budget , called for increased spending by rich countries like Germany and the Netherlands and said that tackling climate change should become one of the bank 's priorities .
