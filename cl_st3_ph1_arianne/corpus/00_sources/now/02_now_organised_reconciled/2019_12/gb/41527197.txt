<p> Carney has taken up the new role with just two months left to run of his eight-year tenure as BoE governor , which ends on 31 January 2020 . <p> However , there is a growing chance that he may have to stay on at the Bank , given that the end of his term coincides with the new Brexit date , that no successor has yet been found and that the recruitment process has been suspended ahead of the 12 December General Election . <p> The Canadian has given little indication of what he wants to do after leaving Threadneedle Street . The UN climate job suggests he will seek an active role in global affairs , however . <p> Carney has been one of the most vocal global figures on climate change , driving initiatives in the BoE to ensure the UK 's financial system confronts the issue . <p> In 2021 , the Bank will carry out groundbreaking climate stress tests that Carney has said will include a " catastrophic business as usual scenario " . <p> A key focus of the UN is to push the financial system to mobilise private finance to tackle global warming . <p> Carney 's new role will see him push for major changes in finance , including better reporting and risk management , ahead of the 26th Conference of the Parties ( COP ) meeting in Glasgow in November 2020.
