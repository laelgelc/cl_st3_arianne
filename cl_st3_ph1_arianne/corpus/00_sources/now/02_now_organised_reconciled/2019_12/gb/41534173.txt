<p> Climate-conscious pupils from across the region took to the streets of Perth last Friday calling for urgent climate reform from world leaders . <p> A number of young people took part in protests on High Street , organised by pupils from Kinross High School , for the Scottish Youth Climate Strikes . <p> The four-hour protest saw the Perth campaigners conduct speeches , wield banners and placards protesting fossil fuel consumption , and even take part in a die-in , a form of protest in which participants simulate being dead . <p> The strike was part of a global climate crisis protest which took place on Friday , November 29 . <p> Inspired by climate activist Greta Thunberg , over 100 countries worldwide took part in the demonstrations , aiming to put pressure on governments and decision makers to do more about climate issues . <p> " With it being Black Friday there are a lot of people about on the High Street . <p> " Black Friday itself is not sustainable and for Fair trade it is not ideal at all . <p> " It has been a good turn out . " <p> The Scottish Youth Climate Strike is calling the government to commit to achieve net zero greenhouse gas emissions by 2030 , to implement a Scottish green new deal and the national curriculum be reformed to address the climate crisis as an educational priority . <p> Aisla added : " More funding need to be put towards a greener arrangement and to make it easier for people to live a greener and more sustainable life . <p> " To be able to live more without plastic and the use of plant oil . <p> " And public transport 's carbon footprint is not good enough at the moment . <p> " We propose that there should be education in schools with the climate crisis included in the curriculum . <p> " Some people are now realising it climate change is already having an effect . <p> " It is an important issue in all parts of the world . " <p> Perth and Kinross Council say attendance procedures for children and young people apply during term time and is a parental responsibility .
