<h> DAVID ATTENBOROUGH was stunned after a sleuth of polar bears took on a pod of whales in a desperate attempt to avoid starvation following the damning effects of climate change on their natural habitat was exposed during the new series . <p> For the penultimate episode of his new BBC show " Seven Worlds , One Planet , " Sir David sent cameras to the continent of North America , home to some 579 million people . However , viewers soon learnt that this diverse region of the planet is also one of the biggest victims to climate change , as Canada is warming faster than any other country on Earth . This shocking global warming , accelerated by the burning of carbon fuels by humans , is leading to the melting of sea ice in the area -- a key building block for the ecosystem that helps sustain the hierarchy of the animal kingdom .
