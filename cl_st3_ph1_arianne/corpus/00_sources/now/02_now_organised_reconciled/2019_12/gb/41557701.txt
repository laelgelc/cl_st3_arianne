<h> General Election 2019 in Forest of Dean <p> Born to a French mother , he developed his love of wildlife playing out on a bombed out aerodrome in Hampshire which had returned to nature . <p> While studying zooology and biology at Swansea University he worked on a project abroad to bring a rare bird back from the brink of extinction . <p> Speaking fluent French and Spanish and some German made him a natural choice as wildlife tour guide on the Galapogos Islands and in the Amazonian rain forest . <p> During his time on the Galapogos Islands he lived on a boat leading diving expeditions . He would watch the dolphins and giant tortoises and play with sea lions that would pull his flippers off . <p> But gradually he saw the harm being done to the unique habitat by mankind and was upset by sights such as albatrosses feeding their chicks luminous cigarette lighters they had mistaken for baby squids . <p> Similarly in the Amazon he saw the damage being done to the jungle and became determined to try and protect . <p> When he came back to Britain he turned up in the doorstep of the BBC 's natural history unit asking about vacancies and within two weeks was working as a fixer for programmes such as Wildlife on One , The Natural World and The Trials of Life . <p> After visiting the Forest of Dean with his partner they fell in love with the place and eventually he moved to St Briavels and became a self-employed local handyman so he did n't have to commute . <p> He joined the parish council , became a governor at his son 's school and was voted in as first Green councillor on the Forest of Dean District Council , ousting the former Conservative leader Patrick Molyneux at the last election . <p> Patrick Molyneux ( C ) and Chris McFarling leader of the Greens at St Briavels result <p> After a lifetime working with some of the most stunning wildlife on the earth and under the sea , he says only Green politics can save the planet and the different species . <p> And that includes the feared Great White shark that shook his cage so hard it lost some teeth while he was filming off the coast of Africa . <p> " They will not go for you unless you do something stupid like cover yourself in fish blood or they mistake you for a seal , " he said saying he s watched species ranging from orcas to chimpanzees . <p> " I am more frightened of the actions of this species , the human race , and what it is doing to the world . " <h> Where do we meet ? <p> We do n't . Mr McFarling has broken his leg after falling while cutting a branch off a tree in the school grounds . <p> He says it would n't be very eco-friendly of me to drive to St Briavels when we could easily use the phone . He is on antibiotics and morphine for the pain and jokes that he is the only person standing who ca n't get on his own two feet for now . <h> How are the green politics going down on the doorstep ? <p> " There has been a green resurgance and my colleagues out canvassing say people seem to understand this is a crisis and the greatest issue facing humankind , " he said . <p> " People only have to look at the floods to see the impact of climate change and this year we have seen the hottest temperatures ever recorded in the UK . <p> He says people understand that society understands the need for radical measures like switching from gas boilers to renewable energy . <p> " There would have to be subsidies to make it more affordable but I think people understand that this is a climate emergency and we have to do something because everything else will be irrelevent once we pass the tipping point , " he said . <p> " It is accelerating out of control and there will be no stopping it once we fall off that cliff edge . " <p> However the seasoned traveller does not want to stop people going on holiday because he believes it broadens the mind and says a tax on frequent flyers is the answer . <h> What about people ? <p> " I 'm passionate about wildlife but people are important to me too . Being a school governor you see the kids whose parents can not afford to feed and clothe them properly . Their shoes do n't fit , the clothes are too small and they are clearly hungry which means they can not sit down and learn . They are fidgety until they get that free school dinner at mealtime . <p> " I volunteered at a homeless shelter and it 's gtrafic to see the ordinary people who had a normal life until they got made redundance or had an accident and could n't work . Intelligent people who had experienced misfortne . <p> " That 's why we would give everybody a universal basic income of ? 89 per week whether they were in or out of work . It would help towards poverty and could give people some space to retrain or start a business . " <h> But surely Foresters are more interested in Brexit than saving the whale ? <p> " I would say people in the Forest of Dean are divided equally between remain and leave , " he said . <p> " In 2018 Channel 4 did a survey which showed the leave vote had gone down from 58 per cent to 52 per cent in the Forest of Dean and I think this indicates the tide is turning . <p> " The Forest is still profoundly divided but it is definitely more remain and less leave than it was . <p> " As somebody who is half French , I am very pro-European and actively support the rights of people in Europe to live , work and love where they want to . " <p> He says Europe is the best way to protect social , economic and environmental standards which will come under threat if the UK leaves . <h> How would a Green solve the problems on the A40/A48 ? <p> " Over the last 20 years the Government have disinvested in the public transport system and the Greens would improve it , " he said . <p> " We would look into the possibility of another station at Elton Corner and invest in trains and buses to enable more people to get out of their cars and use public transport . " <p> He said the Greens will be investing billions of pounds in footpaths and cycleways and have publicly available electric bikes to flatten out the hills and allow people to go further . More money would be invested in car pooling and faster broadband so people can work from home . <p> " You could spend billions of pounds on a new bridge over the Severn but it would just get congested , " he said . <p> " There 's no obvious answer because every time you build a new road it just gets congested again . We have to change the culture and radically redesign communities . " <p> And the rainbow cabinet ? <p> As an ardent supporter of proportional representation the Green councillor says the two party system is pretty archaic and he is proud the party won six seats at the local election . <p> He has every respect for colleagues such as UKIP councillors on the other side of the divide . <p> " The Forest is turning green , " he said . " We have a great council because we leave politics at the door .
