<h> Queen 's speech was a first step to deliver certainty <p> The National Federation of Builders responds to the state opening of Parliament , when the Queen set out the Government 's legislative agenda . <p> Richard Beresford , chief executive of the National Federation of Builders ( NFB ) , said : " We are pleased to see the Government provide much needed Brexit certainty for our members . However , while the new immigration system is being shaped , a temporary construction VISA should be considered . <p> " We also hope to see the Government redouble its efforts to fix the broken planning process , which creates far greater business uncertainty in the construction sector than Brexit . <p> " The NFB supports the Government 's commitment to boost homeownership , invest in skills and infrastructure , tackle climate change and increase building safety . We look forward to collaborating with ministers and the civil service to help reach these targets . <p> " The only frustration was the lack of focus on crucial issues such as late payment , social housing , procurement reform and fixing the broken planning system . " <p> Nick Sangwin , NFB chair , said " A cut in business rates for small businesses is welcome news ; however the Government has missed an opportunity to tackle late payment which seriously damages and too often bankrupts SMEs .
