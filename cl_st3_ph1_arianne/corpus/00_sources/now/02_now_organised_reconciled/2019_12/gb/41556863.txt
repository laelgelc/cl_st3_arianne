<p> Activists took to the streets of a busy Weymouth road to raise awareness for high levels of air pollution in the vicinity . <p> Extinction Rebellion ( XR ) - along with residents and Rodwell councillors - staged the demonstration on Boot Hill and Rodwell Road . <p> Protestors pointed to the area 's toxic air quality . They cited levels of nitrogen dioxide from car fumes , which have been raised as a concern by council Air Quality Reports . <p> Government figures estimate that about 30,000 people die every year as a result of air pollution . <p> XR activist Caz Dennett said : " The air pollution problem here is well known and has been left unaddressed for many years . <p> " Up and down the country there are people dying from the effects of air pollution , and here in Weymouth kids on their way to school are breathing air that is unsafe , it 's got to change . This is a defining moment in our future ; we are running out of time . " <p> The activists brought out placards and banners listing their concerns about air pollution . <p> These included messages such as : " Our kids breathe deadly air pollution on this street , " and " Boot Hill has illegal levels of toxic air 85% of the time . " <p> Protester Ken Huggins said : " I support Extinction Rebellion because they have actually managed to get the climate on the agenda . <p> " When you stand here on Boot Hill you fully realise just what a problem we have . <p> " There are horrendous amounts of traffic , the fumes are appalling , you can smell it and taste it . <p> " We definitely need to take action to improve this situation , and we need to do that now . " <p> Cllr Graham Lambert , who represents the area on Weymouth Town Council , joined the protestors for their demonstration . <p> He said : " I 'm supporting this very peaceful and sensible demonstration because I share their concerns . <p> " Not just in terms of congestion and air pollution , but in terms of the Carbon Dioxide emissions and climate change . <p> " We do need to make serious changes and one of those changes is around transport locally . " <p> Extinction Rebellion is a global climate activism group that was founded in London in 2018 .
