<h> What will president Trump do ? <p> What happens after Donald Trump takes the oath and becomes president at his inauguration ? Trump vows to crackdown on immigration , slash foreign trade agreements , reduce funds for the environment and get rid of Obamacare , to say the least <p> Today Donald Trump sets out his vision for the next four years in his inaugural address . Trump 's press secretary has already explained to reporters that " It 's going to be a very personal and sincere statement about his vision for the country ... it 's going to be less of an agenda and more of a philosophical document , a vision of where he sees the country , the proper role of government , the role of citizens . " <p> Uncharitable Trump-watchers might not consider the Don capable of philosophical remarks , but in what is expected to be a short speech , Trump will apparently attempt to channel both Ronald Reagan and JFK , whose remarks in 1961 concluded with the immortal lines : " Ask not what your country can do for you ; ask what you can do for your country " . So Trump 's inaugural address is going to attempt the sort of soaring , visionary rhetoric that 's been absent from his campaigning style , and might not give much detail of his plans . <p> At an address delivered in Gettysburg last year , Donald Trump set out the details of what he called a " contract with the American people " that would begin with a " very busy first day " . He laid out his views on everything from immigration to the environment . In Trump 's victory speech the morning after he became the president-elect , Trump added to his plans . Here they are below : <p> Donald Trump will bomb IsisDonald Trump has said he wants to " just bomb those suckers ... there would be nothing left . " Although he 's attacked the organization for being ' obsolete , ' Trump said he would recruit NATO to invade strongholds in the Middle East to " knock the hell out of ISIS . " Alarmingly , Trump had to be persuaded by US generals not to use his inauguration as an opportunity to parade US military hardware in the streets of Washington DC , in the style of Russia or North Korea . <p> Donald Trump may re-introduce torture as punishmentTrump has said several times that he wants to bring back waterboarding ( which became illegal in 1929 ) as a punishment for terror suspects . He 's mellowed a little on this after talks with US military leaders , but his final aims are not clear . <p> Donald Trump may not promise to defend fellow NATO countriesTrump is against regime change , which means he is less likely to intervene in the Middle East to try and topple a government ( but he would bomb ISIS ) . This has sparked fear that Trump would also therefore be less inclined to defend countries from invasion , and that as a result Britain will have to spend more money on defence , since Britain would no longer be able to rely on the US to come to its rescue . Indeed , Trump has said he may not guarantee protection to fellow NATO countries , and has also threatened to withdraw troops from Europe and Asia if those allies fail to pay more for American protection . He 's been critical of NATO , along with the EU and the United Nations . <h> Donald Trump on women <p> **Donald Trump might improve childcare ** Trump wants to offer women more choice over whether they work or not after having children , and his daughter Ivanka , a mother of three , helped with Trump 's plan to allow parents earning less than $250,000 ( ? 201,000 ) offset the cost of childcare against their tax bills , which could allow them to potentially save hundreds of dollars a year . Stay-at-home parents could even claim the equivalent for looking after their kids themselves . <p> Donald Trump is likely to crackdown on abortionTrump is against abortion , despite having been pro-choice a while back . He argued that women who are aborting should be " punished " and has suggested he will seek make laws on abortion decided by individual states of America , rather than the country as a whole . Mike Pence , his choice for Vice President , has been accused of giving ' dog whistle ' support to ' gay conversion therapy ' too . <h> Donald Trump on the economy <p> **Trump will try to cut taxes and create jobs ** Trump will talk at some length about his vision for the American economy in his inaugural address . Trump says he will " renew the American dream " and " make America great again . " How ? By creating 25 million jobs through cutting taxes and scrapping regulations on business . " I 've spent my entire life in business , looking at the untapped potential in people and projects . That is now what I want to do for our country , " he said in his victory speech . He needs speedy economic growth to fund his expensive promises such as childcare plans . His transition team have plans to slash government , cutting federal spending by around $10.5 trillion over the next ten years . <p> Donald Trump will attempt to improve America 's infrastructureTrump is going to go big on America 's infrastructure . In his victory speech , he said : " We are going to fix our inner cities and rebuild our highways , bridges , tunnels , airports , schools , hospitals . We 're going to rebuild our infrastructure , which will become , by the way , second to none , and we will put millions of our people to work as we rebuild it . " <h> Donald Trump on immigration <p> Donald Trump may build a wall to keep out immigrantsTrump spent the campaign promising to build a 50ft concrete wall between the US and Mexico to keep out immigrants . He described immigrants as " bringing drugs . They 're bringing crime . They 're rapists . " During his address at Gettysburg , he reiterated : " the country of Mexico will be reimbursing the United States for the full cost of such a wall . " He does n't seem confident of making Mexico pay for the wall up front , but he 's going to try and make them pay later . <p> Donald Trump will force immigrants outTrump has promised to deport 11 million illegal immigrants , and his End Illegal Immigration Act would also impose an automatic two-year prison sentence on anyone illegally re-entering the US after a previous deportation . Trump has also threatened to cancel visas to any country that refuses to accept its people being returned . <p> Donald Trump has toyed with temporarily banning Muslims from entering AmericaTrump threatened to temporarily ban Muslims from entering America , and , if impossible , to introduce " extreme vetting " for Muslims , or , if extreme vetting can not occur , Trump has also said he would like to " suspend immigration from terror-prone regions . " <h> Donald Trump on the environment <p> Donald Trump will shift environmental funds to infrastructure projectsTrump plans to cancel billions of dollars in payments to the United Nations climate change programs . The funds will instead pay for America 's infrastructure projects . <p> Donald Trump is pro-frackingTrump has also promised to lift restrictions on fracking and boost American oil and natural gas production . <h> Donald Trump on healthcare <p> Donald Trump would slash ObamacareTrump plans to get rid of Obamacare -- the healthcare reform introduced by President Obama which has helped 11million more Americans to afford health insurance -- he initially stated that he would do this with the Repeal and Replace Obamacare Act . Trump is answering the complaints of those who say Obamacare is too expensive for the middle classes , who do not qualify for subsidies . While the Republicans have already voted through the blueprints for their plans to scrap Obamacare , there is no sign of a replacement . ** Donald Trump will try to cancel as much of Obama 's reforms as he can** In fact , Trump has vowed to erase Obama from American history altogether , saying he would " cancel every " executive action , memorandum and order issued by President Obama " . He will not be able to touch Obama 's pardons and commutations , such as that given to Chelsea Manning , but he 's vowed to undo as much of Obama 's work as possible . <h> Donald Trump on Foreign Trade <p> Donald Trump may abandon existing free trade dealsTrump would like to disintegrate several trade deals , particularly the recently signed Trans-Pacific Partnership agreement , which lowered tariffs with 11 other nations , including Australia and Canada , to boost growth . Trump 's people have told the Foreign Secretary that Britain is at the front of the line for a trade deal . <p> Donald Trump will introduce protectionist policiesTrump will try to impose a set of protectionist policies to close America 's economic borders , breaking from the traditional Republican commitment to free trade . <p> Donald Trump may go hard on ChinaTrump has promised to get tough on China , and to " direct the Secretary of the Treasury to label China a currency manipulator " . Trump accused of China of stealing American manufacturing jobs , and in return China has stated that they 're prepared to take the gloves off when it comes to their actions in disputed waters .
