<h> Extinction Rebellion Furness criticises outcome of UN climate talks <h> Extinction Rebellion Furness criticises outcome of UN climate talks <p> EXTINCTION Rebellion ( XR ) Furness has described the conclusion of the UN 's annual climate summit as " at best an exercise in compromise - at worst an attempt to delay the reduction of greenhouse gas emissions . " <p> The talks , held in Madrid after protracted upheaval in Chile prevented the original host city of Santiago from housing the summit , ended on a note of disappointment for many climate activists and was described as a " mixed bag " by the European Climate Foundation . <p> A compromise deal was reached by delegates , yet a number of other fractious questions including future carbon markets were put on hold until next year 's summit in Glasgow . <p> Following the outcome of the fortnight-long talks at the weekend , Dr. Steve Johnson , a spokesman for XR Furness , said : " The Climate Change Summit in Madrid was the longest ever , with two additional days and nights of negotiation , but we are entitled to ask whether it was worth it . <p> " Although Europe , Africa and others are keen to adopt stronger targets to ensure more rapid change to protect our future , the biggest polluters , those slowing the whole process , such as America and Australia , objected to more stringent targets and action . " <p> " The UN Secretary General expressed his disappointment , saying an opportunity had been lost to set new , ambitious targets . " <p> " Instead we are left with an agreement to develop new plans and present them at the next conference , to be held in Glasgow in November 2020 . " <p> He said , however : " This does , though , present us with the opportunity to encourage our new Government to show how the UK and its industry can take the lead in renewable energy , reducing emissions and cleaning up pollution . " <p> XR Furness have urged the new Conservative government to lead the way on tackling climate breakdown . New Barrow and Furness MP Simon Fell has thanked the group for raising environmental concerns with him , saying he would be " more than happy to champion " the points outlined by the group . <p> Dr Johnson says he believes that concerns about the climate emergency have intensified , arguing that climate changes are occurring faster than expected . <p> " For instance near Barrow 's namesake , a town in Alaska , there has been a massive increase in emissions of methane , which is 86 times more powerful than CO2 . It 's thought to be coming from methane hydrate in the sea . <p> " The highest methane concentration seen in the 2.2 million year ice core up to now was 800 parts per million .
