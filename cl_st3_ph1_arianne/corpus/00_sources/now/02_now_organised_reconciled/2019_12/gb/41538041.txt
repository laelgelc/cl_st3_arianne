<p> It 's going to be very tight in Stroud next week in the General Election . <p> The Westminster seat which represents the district , a spirited community partially within the Cotswold AONB , is on a wafer-thin majority of 687 . <p> For Labour 's David Drew , born in Gloucestershire and who served as a town and district councillor in Stroud before becoming the constituency MP in 1997 , a fight is on to hold the seat for another five years . <p> He is no stranger to the tightrope battle which Stroud brings - it has switched hands three times between himself and the Conservatives since 1992 - but he faces exceptional circumstances as Brexit and his party leader play a part on local voters ' minds . <p> On the ballot paper on December 12 , there will be two strong contenders who could disrupt his success : Molly Scott Cato , Green MEP for the South West , and Siobhan Baillie , the Conservative candidate . <p> The Brexit Party , with Desi Latimer , and Liberatarian Party , with Glenville Gogerly , are also contesting the seat . <p> The constituency is rural in character but has populated bulks of villages , and market towns such as Stroud itself , Dursley , Stonehouse and Nailsworth . <p> It is a politically engaged area where turnout is historically high , especially compared to the five other constituencies in Gloucestershire . <p> The Local Democracy Reporting Service joined both Mrs Baillie and Mrs Scott Cato canvassing in different wards of the district , and issues such as the NHS , Brexit and climate change repeatedly came up . <p> But the most common set of words said to Mr Drew 's opponents at the doorstep were along the lines of ' I like David Drew , he 's helped me a lot but I do n't like Jeremy Corbyn ' . <p> The Labour leader Mr Corbyn has been criticised on his party 's handling of anti-semitism over the last few years and its proposed Brexit policy , which is now to negotiate a new deal with the EU and put it on the ballot paper in a second referendum . <p> When confronted by this , Mrs Scott Cato would argue she is " the only remain candidate standing in Stroud " while Mrs Baillie would say she is " the only candidate who can stop Jeremy Corbyn and move Brexit on " . <p> Mr Drew says when voters mark a cross on the ballot next week ' they 'll be voting for me , not Jeremy Corbyn ' . <p> But those voters felt stuck in choosing which way to go , and were Marmite about Mr Corbyn as well as Conservative leader and Prime Minister Boris Johnson . <p> One of the main talking points about Stroud is the impact an electoral pact between the Greens and Liberal Democrats , which saw the latter step aside , will have on December 12 . <p> The Greens ' target voters are those who are soft Tory and Labour and support remaining in the EU , and with the Liberal Democrats omitting themselves from the competition it will centralise the remain vote in Stroud . But Mrs Scott Cato said part of the reason Mr Drew was able to win in 2017 was due to a surge of support for Jeremy Corbyn , which she added has now faded . <p> For the Conservatives , Mrs Baillie said a significant increase in Tory-emblazoned boards are being pitched than ever before , and Labour are either switching to Conservatives or to the Green Party . <p> The Tories would need just a 0.55 per cent swing to gain the seat next week . <p> Here 's everything else the LDRS learnt while on the streets with canvassers . <h> Molly Scott Cato <p> We start in the quiet streets of Bussage , typically filled with young aspirationals of a red and blue mix . <p> " I 'm pro-EU but I feel like a hypocrite if I vote for you because I drive a diesel to Bristol daily . And the reason I do that is because there 's no good public transport , " one voter said . <p> The woman said : " I 've been held up by three Extinction Rebellion protests in the car . All that did was make me run my car for longer with the air-con on on the hottest day of the year . Why would they block a main road to a hospital ? That really really p****d me off . That 's not you , I know . <p> " I do n't think you can win , but I do think Labour could . I also regard David Drew as a friend and he 's been very helpful as an MP . I 'm a little bit torn . " <p> Mrs Scott Cato replied : " What I say about Extiniction Rebellion is if we had more Green MPs in Parliament then you would n't have XR members out on the streets . " <p> This level of undecided is ' unusual ' , Mrs Scott Cato said afterwards . <p> " You 'd get 60 to 70 per cent of made-up minds already but there are many undecided this time , and that 's because they 're fed up with Labour and the Tories . " <p> One of the Green 's election pledges is to scrap the electoral system known as first past the post for proportional representation . <p> Mrs Scott Cato said : " This is the real paradox we have now , the so-called two main parties really perform badly and everybody knows it . <p> " At the same time , the first past the post system forces you to vote for these two parties and there 's this big crunch . We 're trying to say ' come on you do n't have to go back to these people ' . " <p> Proportional representation is used to elect members of the European Parliament , and in May this year the Greens had the third highest vote share in the South West . <p> This statistic is printed on the leaflet Mrs Scott Cato is pushing through letterboxes for next week 's vote , indicating that the Greens would do better under the system in the region . <p> Molly Scott Cato canvassing in Stroud ( Image : LDRS ) <p> Asked if she thought it was confusing the context of the two elections , she said : " We 're trying to say ' do n't be battled by the General Election system ' , it 's not like people have suddenly decided Labour and Tories are great now , but they believe that is their only choice in a General Election . <p> " We are very clear this is not the General Election results . We are showing people that that happened this year and that 's how people decided to vote . " <p> She said she has been confronted by abuse from people on social media , with one targeted Facebook advert to people in Scotland . <p> She said : " An awful lot of that stuff on Facebook , some of it is real people and a lot if it is not . I 'm quite worried how the election plays out on Facebook . <p> " There was a national ad that that my daughter saw of a very violent image , associating that with voting for me personally in Stroud . She got that in Glasgow . There is a national campaign before the election even started against me personally . " <h> Siobhan Baillie <p> " I ca n't vote for you , you 're too young , " an elderly gentleman said to Mrs Baillie while on the doorstep in Dursley . <p> She said this is not a normal remark she receives and adds ' maybe it 's the moisturiser ' . <p> The man initially told one of the Conservative canvassers his view , and when Mrs Baillie was alerted to it she promptly wanted to speak to the man about it . <p> " You look 22 , " the man said . " I think age should give experience . " <p> Siobahn Baillie canvassing in Stroud ( Image : LDRS ) <p> Siobhan told him she left school at 15 and worked her way up in the law profession two years later . " This is my new favourite house , even if I do n't get your vote . I have n't gone around on gap years and I did n't go to university . " <p> The man eventually told her he voted for David Drew in 2017 but said : " Jeremy Corbyn puts me off completely . " <p> He was also concerned about what might happen to his son , who lives in France , after Brexit . <p> He said : " I do n't want to lose contact , it looks like that will be inevitable . " <p> Mrs Baillie said : " There 's been a lot of scaremongering about what would happen . It 's not going to be the apocalypse . " <p> After being asked whether she is supposed to address the ' too young ' comment head-on , she said : " I am fascinated by people . I love meeting new people . <p> " If I was getting training they 'd tell me not to do that . Because politics is so broken and everyone is fed up , I feel it is my job is to have positive interactions . " <p> Later on , a leave-voting woman tells Mrs Baillie she was ' fed up ' with the lack of progress in Brexit . <p> She said : " A lot of them voted for themselves , not for their constituents which I thought was wrong . Everyone 's been talking about the trade deals saying ' how can we do it ? ' but we did it years ago . We were able to before we joined the EU . <p> " It will be hard work . We 've done it before , we did it after two world wars for goodness sake . We set up trade deals then when we did n't have the EU . We are a massive country even though we 're small . <p> Siobahn Baillie canvassing in Stroud ( Image : LDRS ) <p> Mrs Baillie is keen to tell of how she got into politics . <p> " I was getting more and more clients that either could n't be helped by the system , or getting stuck in the courts system that was making their family problems a lot worst , " she said . <p> " I started looking at the legislation and guidance for children , it is massively out of date . Then I started looking at how you change that , from that I was interested in policy and then politics . I took a year out and I never went back . " <p> However , she has been criticised for not living in the constituency , with Green 's Mrs Scott Cato commenting ' no one knows she she is ' . <p> Mrs Baillie said she will move to Stroud if she is elected and highlighted how she 's ' done a lot of work ' with the local SGS college . <p> She said : " I do know the local area . I 've spent the last 16 months traversing the constituency , spending all my working time meeting community groups . I 've done more door-knocking than any of the other parties , I have n't stopped . <p> " The other thing is Molly and David may have a postcode in Stroud but it does n't mean they live in every ward . " <p> During canvassing with Mrs Scott Cato , it was frequently heard that ' you can tell when they 're a Conservative when they do n't say anything ' . <p> Asked whether she thought this was true , Mrs Baillie said : " I 've been on a stall in Stroud High Street for the last few weeks . I have people coming up to me very quietly and whisper ' I 've already voted for you ' . Really quietly . I love it . <p> " Other parties have loads of boards , but we 've got more than 100 when there is normally 20 . It 's picking up , but it requires everybody to have confidence . " <p> She continued : " We 've never had so many Labour switchers , all over the constituency . They say that if they 're not voting for us then they 're thinking about Greens . <p> " People are saying they know David Drew but ca n't have Corbyn . These are people who probably would n't have spoken to us at length before . " <h> David Drew <p> We were unable to join Mr Drew out canvassing , but have put the following points to him .
