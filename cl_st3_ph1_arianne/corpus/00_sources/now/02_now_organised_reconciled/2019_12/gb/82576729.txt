<h> Huge climate change protest in Belgian capital <p> A huge number of people turned out on Sunday in Brussels to show their support for greater political action to save the planet from the threat posed by global warming . <p> The activists occupied several kilometers of road surrounding the city 's federal parliament building and royal palace , as they called on Belgian authorities to use their influence in Europe . <p> After China , the United States is the world 's biggest polluter . It is well known Donald Trump has previously called climate change a hoax . Needless to say , at the Brussels protest he was n't very popular . <p> We are told in 2020 a record number of climate change rallies are being organized to take place across the European Union . <p> Individual actions are of course , commendable but experts say only governments can really make a major difference on this issue . Clearly , that 's why Trump 's climate change denial is so significant .
