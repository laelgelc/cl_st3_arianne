<h> What 's the Point of Critique ? <h> 28 January 2020 6:30 pm - 8:00 pm <p> Since Kant , scholars have tended to assume that the critical method is a fundamental part of doing philosophy . But can it help us address the challenges of the twenty-first century , such as globalization and climate change ? Some scholars now suggest it is that it is domineering and Eurocentric , hopelessly outdated and overly negative . So should we embrace different ways of thinking and knowing , and what might they be ?
