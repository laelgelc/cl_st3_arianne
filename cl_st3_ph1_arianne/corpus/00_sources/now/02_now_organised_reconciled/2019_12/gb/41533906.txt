For the Lib Dems , who were pinning their hopes on being the kingmakers in a hung parliament , it has forced Jo Swinson into stepping up personal attacks on the Prime Minister . <p> For Labour , they are recalibrating their pitch to Leave voters in the midlands and the north of England , in seats where , according to the **28;5458;TOOLONG poll , the Conservatives are likely to pick up the support of traditional Labour voters . <h> Sign up to our Politics newsletter <h> The i politics newsletter cut through the noise <p> Thanks for signing up ! <p> Sorry , there seem to be some issues . Please try again later . <p> Submitting ... <h> Doubling down <p> For their part , the Tories are doubling down on what was already their strategy - to make this election all about delivering Brexit . That YouGov poll simply confirms for Conservative strategists what their electoral sweet spot should be : reminding Brexit voters wavering between Labour and Tories of the benefits of leaving the EU . <p> The Tories are modelling their campaign on the successful Vote Leave strategy , and carrying out what veteran strategists like Lynton Crosby argued for in 2017 : to make it about delivering Brexit . Theresa May famously ignored this advice and her bid to hold on to the Tories ' majority ran way off course when she allowed a party manifesto that included all sorts of high-risk policies like an overhaul of social care which was branded a " dementia tax " . <p> This time around , the advice to keep the election about Brexit , Brexit and Brexit has been adhered to . The message discipline has been tight . Yes , there have been rows about Mr Johnson 's alleged untrustworthiness , his refusal to take part in an interview with Andrew Neil or Channel 4 's debate on climate change . <h> Pitch to northern Labour voters <p> There have been embarrassing columns disinterred from the 1990s in which he expressed some shocking views on single mothers and their " ignorant " children . These are issues which will cause some of those wavering voters to pause . <p> But , from the Tory point of view , their strategy is to ignore all of this and to keep the election fixed on delivering Brexit . After the YouGov poll , this is being stepped up : they have secured the backing of a fourth former Labour MP , Gisela Stuart , one of the prominent figures of the Vote Leave campaign . <p> She appeared alongside Mr Johnson and Michael Gove , another Vote Leave chief , in a press conference on Friday to hammer home the pitch to those soft Labour voters in the midlands and northern England . <p> The message is not just about Mr Johnson delivering Brexit on time - if he wins a majority on 12 December - but a warning that the very departure from the EU is at risk if Jeremy Corbyn becomes Prime Minister , because he has promised to pave the way for a second referendum . <p> Writing in The Sun on Friday , Ms Stuart claimed - controversially - that " Brexit will not happen " if Mr Corbyn is elected to Downing Street . The former MP for Birmingham Edgbaston wrote : " I will not vote for Jeremy Corbyn , but I can vote for Brexit . This is , after all the Brexit election and a vote for Boris Johnson this time around is a vote to get Brexit done . " <p> The challenge now is for Labour to counter this narrative in the less than two weeks left of the campaign .
