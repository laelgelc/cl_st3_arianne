<h> YEAR IN REVIEW : March - council declares a big ' climate emergency ' <h> March : Council declares a big ' climate emergency ' <p> ARCHAEOLOGISTS working on Carlisle Cathedral 's Grade I-listed Fratry site discovered 50 coins and some animal bones in the first few weeks of digging . <p> Workers undertook the painstaking task using a trowel and brush to work their way down in the area where a lift pit is being dug . <p> Brazen thieves stripped lead worth tens of thousands of pounds from the roof of Kendal Parish Church . <p> Priest-in-charge , the Reverend Rob Saner-Haigh , described the raid as " saddening " . <p> Tullie House Museum in Carlisle celebrated its 125th birthday . <p> A series of events were organised for its big birthday bash . These included : an animation drop-in ; object handling ; a Victorian portrait photographer ; birthday badge making ; a gallery tour ; an archaeology tour ; plus an insight into the museum 's costume collection . <p> After a ? 1 million refit , one of Kendal 's most historic pubs , Ye Olde Fleece Inn , reopened for business . <p> On March 5 , Carlisle City Council passed a motion to declare a climate emergency . <p> They are the second local authority in Cumbria to do so , following South Lakeland District Council on February 26 . <p> Dozens of protestors gathered before the meeting , urging the council to take urgent action to prevent climate change . <p> Inside the council chamber , the debate on the motion was opened by a question from six-year-old Emily Graham , daughter of the Cumbria Action for Sustainability chief executive Hazel Graham . <p> Rally cars headed into Cockermouth for a scrutineering event before the annual Malcolm Wilson Rally . <p> M-Sport had their WRC car on display , along with various stalls . <p> In Grange-over-Sands , sadness and anger greeted the impending closure of the seaside town 's last bank , Lloyds . <p> The Dutch air force touched down in Cumbria for a mammoth training exercise . <p> Sprawling hills , mountains and weather that changes on an hourly basis were just what the Royal Netherlands Air Force needed to practice their flight skills . <p> The crews were based at Carlisle Airport for about a week . <p> Meanwhile , in Bowness-on-Windermere , residents were relieved when the much-loved Royalty Cinema was removed from the Lake District National Park Authority 's draft local plan as a potential site for development . <p> Two women from Allerdale received medals for their dogs at Crufts this year . <p> Eleanor Ellwood , from Great Clifton , and Susan Briant , of Broughton Moor , entered Crufts 2019 and their dogs walked away with three awards between them . <p> More than 100 school pupils from across South Lakeland went on " climate strike " outside Kendal Town Hall , to call for urgent action on climate change . <p> One of Cumbria 's best loved broadcasters died suddenly on March 20 . <p> A founding member of BBC Radio Cumbria , Nigel Holmes was a key figure in the station 's history , widely respected for the quality of his journalism and his extensive knowledge of local life . <p> His popularity was such that when he left the station in 1997 hundreds of people complained . <p> Residents of Kendal celebrated as the Post Office shelved unpopular plans to shut the Crown PO on Stricklandgate and open a smaller branch at WH Smith . <p> Tributes were paid to a Walney mum in March who fulfilled her final wish hours before losing cancer battle . <p> A phenomenal outpouring of grief engulfed Barrow as hundreds of people paid tribute to Stacy Woodhouse - who married her long-term partner just hours before she lost her battle with cancer at the age of 39 . <p> The significant government cash injection into the Borderlands growth deal brought fresh hopes to a railway campaign group . <p> Announced jointly by the governments in Westminster and Edinburgh , the combined pledge to the cross-border economic growth scheme totalling ? 345m sparked enthusiasm among members of the Campaign to Open Gilsland Station ( COGS ) . <p> Gilsland , east of Brampton and straddling the border between Cumbria and Northumberland , lies on the Carlisle to Newcastle line but has been without a railway station since 1967 . <p> A Barrow couple made their return to a popular ITV daytime show in March . <p> Jennifer Lawley and Carl Minnican appeared for the second time on The Jeremy Kyle Show . <p> The first time they were on the show , Mr Minnican believed Miss Lawley had been unfaithful , something she proved was n't the case by taking a lie detector test . <p> Barrow musician , Stephen Fitzpatrick , sadly lost his life in March alongside his bandmate and their manager while travelling
