<h> Greta Thunberg mobbed as she arrives in Madrid for climate change meeting <h> Greta Thunberg mobbed as she arrives in Madrid for climate change meeting <p> Climate activist Greta Thunberg arrived in Madrid to join thousands of other young people in a march to demand world leaders take real action against climate change . <p> After making it through a swarm of media cameras and microphones at the Spanish capital 's northern train station , the Swedish teenager posted an ironic tweet saying that she had " successfully managed to sneak into Madrid " . <p> Madrid is hosting two-week , United Nations-sponsored talks aimed at streamlining the rules on global carbon markets and agreeing on how poor countries should be compensated for destruction largely caused by emissions from rich nations . <p> The talks came as scientific evidence mounts about disasters that could ensue from further global warming , including a study commissioned by 14 seafaring nations due to be published Friday predicting that unchecked climate change could devastate fishery industries and coral reef tourism . <p> Greta paid a surprise visit to the venue of the talks and joined a group of some 40 teens staging a sit-in there to demand real action against climate change . <p> Holding hands , the teens sang a version of John Lennon 's Power To The People and displayed banners with the logo of Fridays for Future , the global climate movement inspired by Greta . <p> Greta did not appear unsettled by the commotion surrounding her presence . <p> " It 's absurd . I laugh at it . I do not understand why it has become like this , " the 16-year-old was quoted as saying by Swedish newspaper Aftonbladet , whose reporter rode with them in an electric car in Madrid . <p> " I do n't like being at the centre of the focus all the time , but this is a good thing , " she told Aftonbladet . <p> " As soon as the media writes about me , they also have to write about the climate crisis . <p> " If this is a way to write about the climate crisis , then I guess it is good . " <p> While Greta seemed bemused by the attention , her father Svante was startled , saying it was " total madness " . <p> " I have never seen anything like this , " he told Aftonbladet . <p> The study commissioned by seafaring nations says climate change could cause hundreds of billions of dollars in losses by 2050 , adding that limiting global warming would lessen the economic impact for coastal countries , but that they also need to adapt to ocean changes . <p> Climate activist Greta Thunberg ( Andrea Comas ? AP ) <p> The presence in Madrid of Greta is expected to shift the attention to demands for greater action by non-governmental organisations and a whole new generation of environment-minded activists . <p> Past appearances have won her plaudits from some leaders -- and criticism from others who 've taken offence at the angry tone of her speeches . <p> An advocate for carbon-free transportation , Greta travelled by train overnight from the Portuguese capital , Lisbon , where she arrived earlier this week after sailing across the Atlantic Ocean from the United States by catamaran . <p> That became necessary after a sudden change of venue for the COP25 summit following a wave of anti-government protests that hit Chile , the original host . <p> This website and associated newspapers adhere to the Independent Press Standards Organisation 's Editors ' Code of Practice .
