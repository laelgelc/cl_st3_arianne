<p> The study , which reexamined 11 individual satellite measurements from 1992 to 2018 , showed that Greenland has already lost 3.8 trillion tonnes of ice since 1992 , enough to push global sea levels up by more than 1cm . <p> It was a joint project between Nasa , the European Space Agency and Leeds University , which said it was the most comprehensive survey of Greenland ice loss so far . <p> Sled dogs wading through standing water on the sea ice during an expedition in North Western Greenland earlier this yearCredit : STEFFEN OLSEN/AFP <p> The melt is a result of both surface runoff , caused by increased air temperatures , and warming oceans leading to melting from below . <p> The average annual land surface air temperature in the Arctic between October 2018 and August 2019 was the second warmest since 1900 , according to US federal agency study published on Tuesday . <p> The Arctic Report Card also warned that thawing permafrost could mean the Arctic is a net emitter of greenhouse gases , as carbon stored in the remains of plants and animals from thousands of years ago is released into the atmosphere . <p> Sudden changes to the Greenland ice sheet has long been a particular cause for concern for polar scientists ; between 1990 and 2008 they recorded a doubling in the pace of melting . <p> Melt from the ice has led to 25 per cent of global sea level rise , twice as much as that of the larger Antarctic ice sheet . <p> Professor Andrew Shepherd from the University of Leeds , who co-authored the study said : " On current trends , Greenland ice melting will cause 100 million people to be flooded each year by the end of the century , so 400 million in total due to all sea level rise . <p> " These are not unlikely events or small impacts ; they are happening and will be devastating for coastal communities . " <p> Delegates are gathered at the COP 25 in Madrid this week to devise ways of putting the Paris plan in action , but key sticking points remain over emissions trading schemes and how the fight against climate change is funded .
