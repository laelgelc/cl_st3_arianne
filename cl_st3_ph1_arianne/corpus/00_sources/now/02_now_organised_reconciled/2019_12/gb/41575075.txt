<p> It 's the eve before polling day and in Bristol North West , Labour 's Darren Jones is out in full force . <p> The Labour candidate , who is defending a 4,000 majority in this swing seat , is today targeting every former Labour voter or constituent that could be persuaded to vote for his party , by paying them a personal visit . <p> " We always spend the eve of polling day going back to our supporters and reminding them to vote , " Mr Jones told Bristol Live . " I 'll be in with the teams doing that across the constituency from dawn until the polls close . " <p> Bristol North West Labour MP Darren Jones <p> In Bristol North West , Mr Jones certainly has his work cut out . <p> The seat , which contains affluent areas such as Westbury-on-Trym and more working class areas such as Lockleaze , has fluctuated between Labour and Tory throughout its electoral history . <p> Before Mr Jones was elected in 2017 , the seat was represented by Charlotte Leslie , a Conservative former MP who supported Brexit . <p> Because of its status as a swing seat , Mr Jones has had visits from top members of the Labour party ( though not Mr Corbyn or shadow chancellor John McDonnell ) including Angela Rayner , as well as a celebrity endorsement from actor Ross Kemp . <p> Activists have been pummelled into the marginal constituency to canvass for him , including Momentum - which some think in a seat that has a tendency to vote Tory , might be more of a hindrance than a help to Mr Jones . <h> General Election 2019 <p> One Lockleaze constituent told Bristol Live that he was approached by canvassers whom he understood were from Momentum . <p> " They did n't say they were Momentum , just Labour , " he said . " They were talking about Jeremy Corbyn constantly and how great his policies are . <p> " My wife asked to hear about the local candidate because she does n't like Corbyn - and instead of talking about Darren they spent all of their time defending Corbyn . " <p> He continued : " When my wife asked about policies that will affect the local area and new housebuilding and community projects , the canvasser said this was n't her ward so she did n't know about them . They wasted time trying to change a mind that is already made up about Corbyn but that could be persuaded to vote for Darren . MPs will be able to campaign for the Labour deal or for Remain , whichever they feel will be best for our country . " <p> Mr Jones said : " I 've always supported a People 's Vote and to ensure there 's a Remain option on the ballot , which I 'd campaign and vote for . This was my promise to my constituents in 2017 and I stuck to that promise .
