Among his concerns is that the mispricing of assets by banks could quickly deplete the value of securities . Activist hedge fund manager Christopher Hohn of TCI is demanding major corporations step up their emissions disclosure . <p> Hohn is right on the need for practical solutions . But Labour 's manifesto proposition of green transformation by creating 1m jobs and insulating every house in Britain for free is not credible . <p> The green workforce does n't exist nor does the money , even if , as proposed , North Sea oil is clobbered . <p> The International Monetary Fund argues that if global warming is to be halted , taxes on carbon need to be raised to $75-per-ton by 2030 , which in turn would lift electricity prices by an average of 43 per cent . <p> Given that the global average carbon price is $2-a-ton , the disruption would be enormous .
