<p> There will be cheers across Cheltenham today as ' long-suffering ' businesses and residents breathe a sigh of relief that cars will finally be able to drive through Boots Corner again . <p> At least , that 's what the county council wants you to think . <p> But the truth is that the decision made in the bowels of Shire Hall this morning could n't be further from what is best for Cheltenham -- and the county as a whole . <p> The Boots Corner trial has been a long 18 months in many ways , and those unfortunate few ( yes they are in the minority ) who have been fined for not reading road signs would no doubt say it has been an expensive few months . <p> Business owners nearby say that trade has been affected . Motorists loudly protest on social media their right to drive right through the town centre . <p> But for the silent majority -- those of us who dutifully follow the diversions and try not to complain while we 're sat in traffic -- it 's been a success . <h> Read More <h> Related Articles <p> Before the trial more than 10,000 vehicles a day passed through the centre of our pedestrianised high street . It was loud , busy , and you had to wait ages to cross the road -- or take your life into your own hands . <p> Yesterday , as our own pictures -- taken at rush hour -- show , the road is nearly empty of cars , there are buses parked up , people sit and enjoy the calm next to our albeit tacky fake grass . <p> Boots Corner in Cheltenham on Thursday morning <p> Rome was n't built in a day -- and Cheltenham had taken one small but mighty step to help fight climate change , to increase bus and cycle journeys and to encourage visitors to use park and ride services . <p> This had worked , and the borough council points to evidence that cycling through Boots Corner itself had risen 185% since the trial began . Stagecoach had also claimed an additional 270,000 additional bus passengers . <p> To ignore this evidence and act instead on the feelings of a few loud protesters is a travesty . <p> To take this step to make our town a nicer place to spend time , with cleaner air to breathe , and grand plans for what will happen to the green space in front of Boots , was a courageous battle for the borough council to begin . <p> The county council has ignored this courage and shattered their dreams with a unanimous decision to open the flood gates and allow emissions in our town to rise . <p> It is an unbelievable decision in the face of a climate emergency which has been declared by all councils in Gloucestershire . It is totally at odds with everything the powers that be should be doing to protect the town for future generations . <p> To reopen the route to 10,000 plus cars a day , on the eve of 2020 , when we need to do more than ever to tackle climate change , is nothing short of crazy . <p> There were flaws from the start , but nothing that could n't have been worked on . There was without doubt increased traffic on the alternative routes , especially around St James Square and St George 's Street , which turns into a car park at rush hour . This had a knock on effect to St Paul 's and yet the alternative is worse -- some feel the result of the overturned decision will be a ' disaster ' for the area . <p> It 's hard not to feel that this is a politically-motivated decision pitting the district council and the county council run by differing political parties against each other . <p> Whatever their motives , this is not the right decision for our town , for our future , for our planet or for our children .
