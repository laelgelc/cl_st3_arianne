<h> Greta Thunberg tweets from floor of train as Madrid climate talks end with delay on global carbon decision <p> A woman looks at a world globe at the COP25 climate talks congress in Madrid , Spain <p> By Frank Jordans and Aritz Parra , Associated Press <p> 15 December , 2019 16:00 <p> Swedish environmental activist Greta Thunberg in Italy . She tweeted from the floor of a train in Germany on Sunday <p> Climate activist Greta Thunberg has tweeted a photo of herself sitting on the floor of a German train surrounded by lots of bags . <p> It came as marathon international climate talks in Spain have ended with negotiators postponing a key decision on global carbon markets . <p> She posted the tweet late Saturday with the comment : " Traveling on overcrowded trains through Germany . And I 'm finally on my way home ! " <p> Some Twitter users pitied the 16-year-old Swedish activist for not being able to get a proper seat on the train for the long ride home from Madrid , where she was attending the UN climate change conference . <p> They wished her a safe trip home after months of travelling by trains and boats to different climate events in Europe and the US . <p> The activist does not fly on planes because it is considered harmful to the climate . <p> She was recently named Time magazine 's Person of the Year for her efforts to prod government and others to take faster actions in fighting climate change . <p> German railway operator Deutsche Bahn has come under fire in recent years for delays , last-minute train cancellations and expensive ticket fares . <p> Deutsche Bahn replied to the teenager 's tweet , wishing her a good trip back home , adding " we continue working hard on getting more trains , connections and seats . " <p> In the picture on Twitter , Greta is sitting on the floor at the end of a rail car with her back leaning against a suitcase , staring out of a window . <p> There 's an empty food box next to her and more suitcases and backpacks piled up by her side . <p> After two weeks of negotiations on tackling global warming in Madrid , delegates from almost 200 nations passed declarations calling for greater ambition in cutting planet-heating greenhouse gases and in helping poor countries suffering the effects of climate change . <p> But despite holding the longest climate talks ever in 25 nearly annual editions , they left one of the thorniest issues for the next summit in Glasgow , in a year 's time .
