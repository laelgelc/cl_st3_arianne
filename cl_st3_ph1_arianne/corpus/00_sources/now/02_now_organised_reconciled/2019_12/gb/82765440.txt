<h> Related Companies <h> Channels <p> This content has been selected , created and edited by the Finextra editorial team based upon its relevance and interest to our community . <h> Bank of England to model climate risks to financial firms <p> 18 December 2019 <p> 6 <p> 3 <p> 0 <p> The Bank of England is to model the scale of climate-related risks to UK financial firms over a thirty-year time horizon . <p> The central bank believes that whilst climate-related risks will materialise over decades , actions today will affect the size of those future risks . <p> The Bank of England 's Biennial Exploratory Scenario ( BES ) will cover multiple scenarios that cover climate as well as macro-variables : to test the resilience of the UK 's financial system against the physical and transition risks in three distinct climate scenarios . These range from taking early , late and no additional policy action to meet global climate goals . <p> Both banks and insurers will be subject to the tests , although the Bank will only disclose aggregate results of the financial sector 's resilience to climate-related risk rather than individual firms . <p> BofE governor Mark Carney says : " The BES is a pioneering exercise , which builds on the considerable progress in addressing climate related risks that has already been made by firms , central banks and regulators . Climate change will affect the value of virtually every financial asset ; the BES will help ensure the core of our financial system is resilient to those changes . " <p> The Bank is consulting on the design of the exercise and is calling for feedback on the feasibility and the robustness of these proposals from firms , their counterparties , climate scientists , economists and other industry experts by 18 March 2020 . <p> The final BES framework will be published in the second half of 2020 and the results of the exercise will be published in 2021.
