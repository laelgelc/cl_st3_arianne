<h> Heatwave conditions fuel bushfire in Western Australia <p> Heatwave conditions and strong winds fanned a coastal bushfire that threatened properties in Western Australia on Saturday ( December 14 ) . <p> Emergency warnings were issued for the Yanchep area , north of Perth , where a blaze began on Wednesday ( December 11 ) . Temperatures for the region hovered around 40 degrees Centigrade , challenging attempts by firefighters to contain the fire . <p> Bushfires have killed at least four people across the east coast of Australia since November , burnt about 2.5 million acres ( 1 million hectares ) of farmland and bush and destroyed more than 400 homes . <p> Bushfires are common in Australia 's hot , dry summers , but the ferocity and early arrival of the fires last month in the southern hemisphere spring is unprecedented . Experts have said climate change has left bushland tinder-dry.
