<h> Dialogues necessary to address persistent fisheries ' challenges <p> Researchers and scientists gather for the 12th Asian Fisheries and Aquaculture Forum ( AFAF ) at the Iloilo Convention Center in this city 's Mandurriao district on Tuesday ( April 9 , 2019 ) . Experts underscored the important of discussions in coming up with the ways to respond to challenges in fisheries and aquaculture . ( Photo by Gail Momblan ) <p> ILOILO CITY -- Researchers and scientists from 20 countries all over the world underscore the need for discussions to address the ongoing challenges in the field of fisheries and aquaculture . <p> Delegates from the Philippines , Malaysia , Thailand , Indonesia , Vietnam , among others , were able to share new ideas and studies as they gathered for the 12th Asian Fisheries and Aquaculture Forum ( AFAF ) at the Iloilo Convention Center in this city 's Mandurriao district on Tuesday . <p> Dr. Alice Joan Ferrer , Vice President of the Asian Fisheries Society , said the field of fisheries and aquaculture is confronted with continuing problems such as climate change , growing population , and low income of fish farmers , among @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that people should be discussing about . Discussions help us come up with solutions on how we can respond to these challenges , " she said in an interview on Tuesday . <p> She noted that the community should be resilient in combatting the challenges . <p> Ferrer added that challenges in fisheries and aquaculture vary from time to time and thus " we need to adopt " . <p> Researches , as information gathering tool , are therefore important to contribute to the policy-making and decision-making of mankind , she said . <p> " Overfishing , illegal fishing , depletion of marine resources are problems that we can not immediately give solution to .
