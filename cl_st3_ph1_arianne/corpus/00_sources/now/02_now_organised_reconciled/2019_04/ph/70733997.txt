<p> LONG before seaweed processing became a big industry in Cebu , farmers used to sell raw seaweed to multi-national companies in the US . After that , the processed seaweed got sold at a hefty price tag . <p> It took visionaries like Maximo Ricohermoso , president of MCPI Corp. , to change the way Cebu played in the seaweed ballgame . <p> Seeing the potential for employment and revenue , especially in the countryside , Ricohermoso pioneered the production of seaweed cultivation in the country and transformed it into high-value product -- carrageenan . <p> Carrageenan is a gelatinous substance used in food , cosmetics and pharmaceuticals that is derived from seaweed . <p> It is commonly used as a thickener and stabilizer in infant formula , dairy products , non-dairy " milk " analogs , meats and drink mixes . <p> Ricohermoso , who now sits as the chairman of the Seaweed Industry Association of the Philippines ( Siap ) , said working in this industry is n't new to him . <p> Born in Marinduque , Ricohermoso is a child of a farmer and trader , Daniel and Januaria .
