<h> SM Malls in Cebu switch off , show support for Earth Hour <p> SM SUPERMALLS joined the Cebuano community in the observance of the Earth Hour last March 30 . <p> At 8:30 p.m. , SM City Cebu , SM Seaside City Cebu , SM City Consolacion and SM Hypermarket Lapu-Lapu City took part in the symbolic hour-long switching off of lights . <p> Now on its 11th year with SM , Earth Hour 's worldwide theme for 2019 was " #Connect2Earth , " a global campaign that aimed to highlight the importance of nature . The theme focused on biodiversity and sought to raise awareness of climate change and to get the community involved in caring for the environment and all living species . <p> SM malls switched off 100 percent of its lights , including the SM logos , dropdown banners , billboards , pylon towers , landscapes , and 50 percent of the lights of the hallways and tenants . <p> The Department of Environment and Natural Resources and the Department of Energy lauded SM malls ' participation in the global initiative by switching off the lights during the Earth Hour . <p> As a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ responsibility programs serve as a catalyst for positive change in the communities that it supports . <p> SunStar website welcomes friendly debate , but comments posted on this site do not necessarily reflect the views of the SunStar management and its affiliates .
