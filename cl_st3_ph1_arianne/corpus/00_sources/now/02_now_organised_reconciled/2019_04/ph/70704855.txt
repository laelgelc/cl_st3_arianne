<h> Legarda calls for stronger biodiversity protection to prevent further loss of species <p> Senator Loren Legarda on Sunday called for stronger biodiversity protection as she joins this year 's Earth Hour celebration . <p> Legarda , chair of the Senate committee on climate change , said this year 's theme " Protect Our Species " was a timely reminder to all people to protect and care for the Earth 's delicate ecological balance . <p> The senator said stronger biodiversity protection efforts must be put in place to prevent loss of species especially since thousands of species are becoming extinct every year . <p> " According to Earth Day Network , normally , between one and five species will go extinct annually . However , scientists estimate that we are now losing species at 1,000 to 10,000 times the normal rate , " said Legarda , a UNISDR Global Champion for Resilience . <p> Biodiversity loss , which is mainly caused by habitat destruction and wildlife trade , is heightened because of climate change and studies indicate that 30 percent of species would be at risk of extinction due to the warming climate , Legarda said . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ delicate ecological balance .
