<h> SM Supermalls celebrates Earth Hour 2019 <h> Communities join hands for the planet <p> On its 11th year with SM , all 72 of its malls across the country took part in the symbolic hour-long switch-off last March 30 , in a bid to spark awareness in caring for the environment . Joining the Earth Hour celebrations in SM were partners from local government units , non-government organizations , community members , advocates , and mall goers , all united by a commitment to take concrete actions for the planet . <p> Earth Hour 's worldwide theme for 2019 is #Connect2Earth , a global campaign to educate people on why nature matters , latching on biodiversity and the efforts of climate change . This event aims to get the community involved in helping care for the planet and all its living species . Each SM Mall came up with their own creative execution and innovative program , to engage the community in moving towards a more sustainable future . <p> " SM recognizes the importance of protecting the environment since it is our source of life . Nature matters to every one of us . This is the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to stir widespread awareness and promote solidarity in conserving our natural resources , " said Liza Silerio , director of SM Cares Program on the Environment and Sustainability . <p> Since its launch in the Philippines in 2009 , the Earth Hour celebration has been marked in more than a thousand cities and towns . Millions of Filipinos have joined the global hour-long lights off , a gesture to symbolize commitment to the well-being of the planet . <p> SM 's other environmental and sustainability initiatives include : The monthly Trash to Cash recycling market ; the annual Green Film Festival , an information campaign geared towards high school students educating them about various environmental and climate change issues ; support and participation to coastal cleanups ; water recycling treatment for all malls wherein all recycled water is used back for non-potable and industrial needs ; solar rooftops and the automated weather stations in all SM Malls , to name a few . <p> To know more about SM Cares , visit www.smcares.com.ph , **32;2792;TOOLONG or email **27;2154;TOOLONG
