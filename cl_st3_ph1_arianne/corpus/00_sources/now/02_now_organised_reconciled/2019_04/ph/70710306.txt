<h> Angara pushes for creation of Department of Disaster Resilience <p> Reelectionist Senator Juan Edgardo " Sonny " Angara on Tuesday urged lawmakers to expedite the passage of a measure that seeks the establishment of a new department that would deal with climate change and natural disasters . <p> " This new Department will oversee a comprehensive and coordinated strategy in managing natural disasters with the main goal of saving lives and minimizing damage to property , " he stressed . <p> The bill , which he filed last year , along with other similar measures , is still pending with the Senate committee on national defense and security . Its counterpart in the House of Representatives was already passed on third and final reading in October last year . <p> Angara said it is imperative for the country to have a line agency focused on disaster resilience given that the country is considered among the countries most vulnerable to natural hazards and climate change as it is geographically located along the Pacific Ring of Fire and the Tropical Cyclone Belt . <p> " What we want to ensure is a seamless synergy among government agencies and stakeholders from @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ disasters . We need to keep every member of the family safe , " he said . <p> Under the bill , the proposed new department will be tasked to lead efforts to ensure disaster resilience by delivering on the highly critical and intertwined functions of disaster risk reduction , preparedness , and response , as well as recovery and building forward better . <p> The proposed Department of Disaster Resilience or DDR shall also be the principal government institution responsible for ensuring safe , adaptive , and disaster-resilient community . <p> " It shall lead , spearhead , initiate and integrate the crafting of plans , programs , projects , and activities to reduce the risk of natural hazards and effects of climate change and manage the impact of disasters , " according to Angara . <p> The measure also gives the DDR authority to recommend the declaration of state of calamity , which allows the exercise of remedial measures such as price ceiling , grant of interest-free loans , and tax exemptions . <p> In 2016 , Angara said the Philippines ranked third in the World Risk Index @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , it experiences almost all forms of natural disasters such as typhoons , earthquakes , volcanic eruptions , floods , and landslides . <p> He also noted that each year , the Philippines sustains immense damage and tremendous losses due to natural disasters . <p> These include loss of life or injury ; damage to infrastructure , properties and equipment ; interruption of food supply ; disruptions to critical services ; loss of livelihood , business or revenues from business interruption ; disruption of economic development ; and damage to the environment , Angara lamented .
