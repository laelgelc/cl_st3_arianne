Dominguez III . The statement said Dominguez assured Japan Deputy Prime Minister and Finance Minister Taro Aso of the Philippines 's commitment to sign the memorandum of understanding ( MOU ) on the establishment of the Southeast Asia Disaster Risk Insurance Facility ( Seadrif ) .
