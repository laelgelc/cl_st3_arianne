<h> EDC forges supply deal with Neltex <p> The newest client signed up by Lopez majority-owned Energy Development Corporation ( EDC ) for a power supply deal is Neltex Development Co . Inc. , the country 's largest manufacturer of plastic pipe solutions . <p> The company disclosed that the electricity to be supplied to the Cavite-based manufacturing company will be from its Bacon-Manito ( BacMan ) geothermal plant in the Bicol region . <p> The scale of capacity offtake ( or the volume of electricity purchase from the plant ) had not been divulged by the relevant parties -- including the cost-per-kilowatt hour of the procured power supply . <p> As stipulated , Neltex is engaged in the production of PVC pipes through the use of purely organic-based stabilizers ( OBS ) and it has been utilizing just the lead-free sustainable raw materials . <p> The company 's Cavite production site is considered to be Southeast Asia 's biggest ; and its produced pipes " are used to bring water from reservoirs to homes and businesses , " and in turn are used to carry waste water from toilets to sewer treatment plants . <p> By contracting from @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that Neltex is into aligning itself as a manufacturer of products that are into the sustainable sphere and wo n't be contributory to climate change risks . According to the power customer-firm , " with its partnership with EDC , Neltex aims to make its production sustainable and safe for the environment -- making it the first in the industry to be powered by renewable energy . " <p> As it will be leaning on a geothermal power source , Neltex noted that its need for baseload capacity or an electricity supply that affords it round-the-clock will be met -- hence , that entails uninterrupted phase on its production .
