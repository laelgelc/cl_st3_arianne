<h> SM Malls promote zero waste , hold recycling market day <p> THE SM Supermalls encourage shoppers to reduce waste and earn money at the same time with its " Trash to Cash " program on April 5-6 . <p> The event will be held in the SM City Cebu 's north covered parking area , SM Seaside City 's lower ground floor and SM City Consolacion 's open parking space . <p> It will start at 10 a.m. and end at 2 p.m . <p> The " Trash to Cash " program is an open-to-all recycling market held every first Friday and Saturday of the month in all SM Supermalls . <p> Families can take home cash by bringing in their trash like unused paper and cardboard , empty ink toner cartridges , plastic bottles and scraps , used lead batteries from cars , aluminum , tin cans and other discarded items . <p> The recycling market day is a joint project of SM Supermalls , the National Solid Waste Management Committee , Philippine Business for the Environment and other government units . <p> For its efforts to promote recycling , SM was recognized as the " Champion @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Earth Day celebration at the SM Mall of Asia Complex in 2010 . <p> In 2016 , the People Management Association of the Philippines awarded the SM Cares with the " Tanging Bayani ng Kalikasan Award ( non-government category ) " for its achievement in promoting clean air and fighting climate change .
