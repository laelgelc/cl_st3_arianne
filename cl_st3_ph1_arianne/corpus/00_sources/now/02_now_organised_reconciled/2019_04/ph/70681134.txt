<h> DENR launches search for sustainable and eco-friendly schools <p> The Department of Environment and Natural Resources ( DENR ) is encouraging schools to participate in this year 's National Search for Sustainable and Eco-friendly Schools . <p> Department of Environment and Natural Resources ( MANILA BULLETIN ) <p> The search that is already on its sixth year is organized by the DENR 's Environmental Management Bureau ( EMB ) , in collaboration with the Department of Education , Commission on Higher Education , Nestle Philippines and Landbank of the Philippines , to encourage schools/academic institutions to become more actively involved in environmental issues at a practical and local level . <p> Geared towards the adoption of behavioral solutions in addressing climate change and other environmental issues , the competition also aims to initiate active responses and raising community awareness and participation on environmental concerns among students , faculty and school administrators . <p> The competition is divided into three categories : elementary , high school and college . Both public and private institutions are invited to join . It will be judged by noted individuals in the areas of environmental education coming from the government and private sector @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ The top entry per category from every region will be forwarded to the EMB Central Office in Quezon City for national judging . <p> Winning schools in the national level will receive ? 50,000 -- 1st Prize , ? 40,000 -- 2nd Prize , and ? 30,000 -3rd Prize , as well as plaques of recognition . <p> As for the regional level , a certificate of recognition and a prize of ? 15,000 will be given to regional winners for each category . <p> Aside from the main winners , the search will also be awarding three Special Category Leadership Awards , namely : The Nestle Water Leadership Award that seeks to recognize and promote solutions , practices and initiatives of schools in water management and conservation ; The Energy Leadership Award which focuses on energy efficiency and conservation embedded in school activities ; and The Landbank Green Leadership Award will be given to the three national first prize winners per category . <p> The Nestle Water Leadership Award and the Energy Leadership Award will grant ? 20,000 to each of the winners at the national level for college @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ prize of ? 10,000 and a certificate of recognition will be awarded to each of the regional winners of all categories . <p> As for the Landbank Green Leadership Award , winners at the national level will be awarded ? 25,000 . <p> The deadline for the submission of entries is on or before April 26 , 2019 . <p> For public elementary and high school categories , three hard copies of entries shall be submitted directly to their respective DepEd Division Offices . <p> For the private elementary and high school , and college categories , entries shall be submitted to the nearest EMB Regional Office . <p> Entries that should include a one-page description of the environmental programs and projects of the school with two photos with caption , and the other required information should also be uploaded at the Eco-friendly School Facebook Page or at **28;138;TOOLONG . <p> All the winning entries for each category , together with selected entries will be displayed during the national awarding ceremonies to be held in November in time for the celebration of the National Environmental Awareness Month .
