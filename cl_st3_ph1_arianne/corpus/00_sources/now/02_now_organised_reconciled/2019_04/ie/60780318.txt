Dig the bed deeply , break down clods , rake well and add an organic fertiliser ( seaweed dust would work great ) a week before sowing . <p> Make a drill 1cm deep -- if soil is dry , dampen . Sow three seeds every 15cm in rows 30cm apart and cover it with soil . Germination takes up to three weeks . When seedlings appear , pull out the two weakest ones .
