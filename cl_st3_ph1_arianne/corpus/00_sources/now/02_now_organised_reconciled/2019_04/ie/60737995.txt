Its over-arching aim is to cut Ireland 's greenhouse gas emissions to net-zero by 2050 it has laid its report before government to consider which of its recommendations it will adopt . <p> It is a most worthy , if perhaps too ambitious target , but at this stage , if the bar is not set high , we will never catch up with our international obligations signed up to in the Paris Climate Agreement of 2016 .
