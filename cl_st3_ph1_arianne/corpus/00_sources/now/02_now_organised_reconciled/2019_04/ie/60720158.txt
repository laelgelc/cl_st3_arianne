The lithium is contained in salt water brines beneath salt flats in the three countries . This brine is extracted with pumps and directed into large pools , where the water evaporates , leaving a mixture of lithium and other minerals . These are separated using a chemical process , and then sent off and used to make batteries , among other uses . <p> The impact is already being felt .
