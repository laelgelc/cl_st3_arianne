<p> Hundreds of environmental activists gathered in front of the Spire today as part of the Extinction Rebellion protests against climate change yesterday . <p> Traffic was diverted away from O'Connell bridge by gardai as protesters made their way from the GPO to O'Connell bridge where they took part in a sit-down protest . <p> Following a week of climate protests in London , where there was some unrest , there was a peaceful atmosphere among those who turned out in the sunny weather to support the cause . <p> There was mixture of young and old at the event , all concerned over the impact of climate change on the planet . Sue Breen of Extinction Rebellion said that while protesters hoped to see moves from the government to support their action , she did n't expect it today . <p> " Unfortunately , we do n't expect it to happen today , but whatever happens today the campaign will continue with great gusto , " she said . <p> She made a powerful speech in front of those gathered yesterday , <p> " We are here to tell the truth and to ask the government to tell @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ an emergency . The truth is that there are wild fires burning in the arctic , the truth is that we are destroying 18 million acres of forest per year -- that is the equivalent of the size of Panama . <p> " We are not here to cause trouble or disrupt your day . We are here because the governments and corporations are handing a death sentence to our children and we refuse to be complicit . We will not stay silent . <p> " The system is not just broken , it is malignant , and it has poisoned every river , every stream , and has caused dioxins to be present in every mother 's breast milk and toxins to be in every breath we take . " <p> There were also performances of song , written-word , and rap , as well as a stilt walker and dancers among the crowds . <p> Some protesters had travelled to the city to support the movement , with mum Majella O'Connor ( 43 ) accompanying her 11-year-old daughter Eva to the event from Tipperary . <p> " We @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ n't stand up what chance have these guys got ? " she said of her daughter 's generation . <p> Liz Barry from Donaghmede , Dublin attended the event for the sake of her grandchildren . She said in the past , Ireland was better at re-using packaging , with less plastic in use too . <p> " When we were young you got chips in newspapers and you got your milk bottles , very little plastic , you bought your fruit loose , your cheese loose , your vegetables loose . Now everything is prepacked and it is killing the environment , " said the 73-year-old . <p> Meanwhile Sarah Pace and Paul Gaffney , who live in Wicklow , were expecting their first child together in June and feel they need to protest to ensure the health of their future family . <p> " It is about our survival so it is great to see everyone getting engaged and being a part of that , " said Sarah ( 36 ) .
