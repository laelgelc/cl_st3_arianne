It covers an area of 210km2 and is traversed by the rivers Emscher and Ruhr and the Rhine-Herne Canal . <p> It was established 1,160 years ago and for centuries was dominated by the coal and steel industry , which has had a long-lasting influence on the environment . Essen is now an example of a city that is actively transforming from ' grey ' to ' green ' . <p> In particular , Essen 's surface and ground water systems were impacted by its heavily industrialised past .
