They ranged from bishops and heads of diaconal departments to coordinators of projects such as a kindergarten , a youth bureau and an old people 's home . <p> The workshop included a visit to a local congregation in Malabon , one of the most densely populated parts of the capital , Metro Manila , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ children and their families . Through Bible lessons , regular home visits and food distributions , the church seeks to respond to the spiritual and physical needs of these marginalized people . <p> Workshop participants walk through the streets of Malabon on their way to visit the local church 's children program . <p> A workshop participant plays basketball with some of the children in Malabon . <p> The aim of the workshop was to support local churches by equipping people with the knowledge and skills to implement their diaconal work more effectively and sustainably . Participants shared internal challenges , such as a lack of staff or financial resources , as well as external factors , such as growing nationalism and extremist attitudes which have led to attacks on some congregations in Indonesia . Church leaders said they were seeking to reach out to Muslim leaders to overcome hostile attitudes in society , but often lack the knowledge and skills for constructive interfaith engagement . <p> Participants at the workshop on enhancing capacities for diakonia in Southeast Asia . <p> Rev.
