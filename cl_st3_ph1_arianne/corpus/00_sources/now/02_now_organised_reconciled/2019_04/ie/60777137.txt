<h> ' It 's madness , but I 've as good a chance as anyone ' -- Kilkenny 's sole candidate in Euro elections <p> Breda Gardner laughs away my query as to whether she stands a chance of being elected to the European Parliament next month . <p> " This is madness , " she exclaims , but then adds seriously : " I 've as good a chance as anyone else . " <p> The Thomastown-based Independent councillor revealed she only decided to throw her hat into the ring for the Euro elections six weeks ago . <p> But despite her jocular demeanour , it 's obvious Breda is taking the election very seriously . And as the only Kilkenny candidate contesting the Ireland South constituency , she 's confident of winning over many voters in her home county between now and May 24 . <p> She is , by nature -- and profession ( Breda is a self-employed health therapist ) -- a very positive person .
