<h> Abolish the House of Lords and replace it with something genuinely democratic <p> 05 April , 2019 <p> The House of Lords <p> * ARTICLE 50 was due to have taken effect on Friday . Theresa May promised us it would . It did n't . The failure is symptomatic . Something has gone wrong in British politics . It 's a lack of leadership . And it has been missing for some considerable time . <p> The UK has been a member of the EEC/EU for some 45 years or so . Not once during that period has a British prime minister been perceived as playing a leadership role in the institution . Our usual position is as a surly troublemaker on the periphery . <p> Britain is a nuclear power within Nato , the most powerful military alliance in the world . Are we perceived as a leader in this organisation ? I think not . The president of the United States is a climate change denier . Has Britain stepped in to take over a leadership role on this issue ? No , we have n't . <p> Brexit or no Brexit ? During the great Brexit debate nearly half of the electorate , including a high proportion of young people , has been represented by just the minority parties who are severely hampered under the existing electoral process . <p> * Address the outdated class bias that has held back this country for a century or more .
