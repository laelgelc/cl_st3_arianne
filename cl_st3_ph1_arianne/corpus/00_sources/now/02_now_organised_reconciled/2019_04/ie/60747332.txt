<h> Pope meets teenage climate change activist Greta Thunberg <p> The 16-year-old met with Pope Francis and carried a sign saying " Join the climate strike " . The pope has made tackling climate change a key feature of his papacy ( AP Photo/Gregorio Borgia ) <p> Ms Thunberg arrived late for Francis ' general audience and took her seat in the VIP section in St Peter 's Square . <p> Francis went up to her at the end of the audience , where Ms Thunberg showed off her sign.The pope greeted Ms Thunberg in the VIP section of St Peter 's Square ( AP Photo/Alessandra Tarantino ) <p> She is in Rome to headline Friday 's " school strike " , the growing worldwide youth movement she spearheaded demanding faster action against climate change . Ms Thunberg will also address the Italian parliament . <p> In Francis , Ms Thunberg has found a kindred spirit . The Jesuit pope has made fighting climate change and caring for God 's creation a pillar of his papacy .
