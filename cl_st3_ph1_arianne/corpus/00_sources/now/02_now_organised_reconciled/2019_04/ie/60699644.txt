' <p> However , Hoogervorst acknowledged that ' sustainability issues can already have an impact that needs to be reflected in financial reporting as it currently is ' . In such cases they will tend to escape the financial statements , which are essentially backward-looking . <p> ' But even in such cases we believe the IASB has a role to play , namely through our efforts to improve what we like to call " broader financial reporting " .
