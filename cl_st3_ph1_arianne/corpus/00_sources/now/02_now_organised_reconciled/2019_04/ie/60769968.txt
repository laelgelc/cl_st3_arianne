<h> Scientists Produce First-ever Global Coral Reef Atlas <p> Researchers have developed a new way to map coral reefs accurately and have produced the first-ever global coral reef atlas . The maps contain over 65,000 square kilometers ( 25,097 square miles ) of coral reefs and their habitats . <h> A 10-year Global Reef Expedition <p> The novel detailed maps have been designed using a combination of Earth-orbiting satellites and field observations . They are the result of a 10-year Global Reef Expedition . <p> The expedition surveyed over 1,000 remote coral reefs in 15 countries , many of which had never been studied before . The scientists used data collected from extensive SCUBA surveys . <p> They then extrapolated that information across the entire reef using ultra-high-resolution satellite imagery . To check the accuracy of their new mapping method , the scientists compared the maps with video footage from cameras along the reef . The effort is part of an attempt to conserve the world 's coral reefs . <p> " In order to conserve something , it 's imperative to know where it is located and how much of it you have , " said Sam Purkis @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of Marine Geosciences . <p> " Developing such an understanding for coral reefs is especially challenging because they are submerged underwater and therefore obscured from casual view . With this study , we demonstrate the potential to use satellite images to make coral reef maps at global scale . " <h> More cost-effective and practical <p> This new method of surveying coral reefs is more cost-effective and practical than traditional ones . Conventional coral reef surveys are expensive since they require hours of underwater surveys conducted by highly-trained scientific divers . <p> " Satellite , aircraft , and drone imaging will become an increasingly important tool for addressing the coral reef crisis at the global scale at which it 's occurring , " said Purkis , also the interim chief scientist for the Living Oceans Foundation . <p> The maps made for this study can be found on an interactive coral reef atlas called the World Reef Map .
