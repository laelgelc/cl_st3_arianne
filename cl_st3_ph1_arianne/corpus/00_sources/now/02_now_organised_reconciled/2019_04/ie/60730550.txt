<h> Porter , Herron and Grayling join Balham Literary Festival line-up <p> Max Porter , Rachel Ama , Mick Herron and A C Grayling are among the names taking part in this year 's Balham Literary Festival . <p> Max Porter , Rachel Ama , Mick Herron and A C Grayling are among the names taking part in this year 's Balham Literary Festival . <p> The south London event runs from 13th to 25th June at a range of venues with an array of discussions ranging from climate change to crime . <p> Grayling will preview his new book The History of Philosophy ( Viking ) , while YouTube star Ama will share her popular vegan recipes . <p> Elsewhere on the bill , the Sunday Times Young Writer of the Year Adam Weymouth will discuss Kings of the Yukon ( Particular ) , while MP Rachel Reeves will mark the inspirational achievements of women in parliament in conversation with broadcaster Cathy Newman . <p> Balham Literary Festival is part of the MILD group 's Live Lit series , which includes two annual literary festivals and a year-round series of in-store events at Dulwich Books .
