<h> New climate action resource for teachers launched <p> A new climate action resource for teachers is now available to all post-primary schools across the country . <p> The downloadable resource launched by the Green Schools programme contains a range of lesson plans , presentations , surveys and data on climate change and action . <p> The Minister for Climate Action Richard Bruton TD announced the release of the resource last week , stating that it will " help bring climate action into the classroom " . <p> " This is an area where we need to step up our response . Young people have shown their interest and their passion , " he said . <p> " We need to make sure that the curriculum is there so that they can learn in a more structured way . " <p> The announcement of the resource comes on the heels of the Climate Action Committee 's recommendation to make Irish students " climate-literate " . <p> The Committee found that the current curriculum did not place a strong enough focus on climate change as there are " insufficient opportunities " in the formal education system to learn about or @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Kayle Crosson <p> The new resource is intended to make climate change cross-curricular , according to Green Schools Climate Action Officer Gr ? inne Ryan . <p> " I hope this resource equips not only Geography teachers but also CSPE , Science and English teachers with the tools and guidance to explore climate through the curriculum , " Ms. Ryan said . <p> However , for student climate striker Conal O'Boyle of Muff , Co . Donegal , the resource alone is n't sufficient to meet the task at hand . <p> " I for one think that this is shooting way below what we should be doing as a country , " Conal said . <p> " Personally , I feel like climate action needs to be a compulsory subject .
