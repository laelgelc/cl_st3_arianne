<h> Notre Dame and the economics of investing in heritage <p> The Notre Dame fire has strengthened the argument in both economic and environmental terms of investing and looking after heritage buildings both here and abroad , writes Kyran Fitzgerald . <p> Not since the collapse of the Twin Towers in New York have we been treated to such chilling images . <p> The sight of Notre Dame cathedral ablaze and at risk of complete collapse will surely haunt us for many years -- if not decades . <p> Neglect of security threats paved the way for the events on 9/11 . Neglect , pure and simple -- the lamentable failure to spend decent money on the ongoing maintenance of the cathedral resulted in a conflagration .
