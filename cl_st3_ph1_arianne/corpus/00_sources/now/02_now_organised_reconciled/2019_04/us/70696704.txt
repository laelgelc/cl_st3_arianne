<h> About 22 million years ago , Simbakubwa was a fierce predator that " had lots of blades , " paleontologists report . <p> The handful of mysterious fossils sat unstudied for decades , tucked safely in a drawer at the Nairobi National Museum in Kenya . Although Simbakubwa translates to " big lion " in Swahili , this behemoth was not a big cat .
