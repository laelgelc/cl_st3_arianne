<h> Rep. Swalwell joins crowded field of Democrats vying for 2020 nod <p> Rep. Eric Swalwell , D-Calif. , is in his fourth term in the House of Representatives . File Photo by Alex Edelman/UPI License Photo <p> April 8 ( UPI ) -- U.S. Rep. Eric Swalwell on Monday added his name to the growing list of Democrats seeking the 2020 presidential nomination . <p> The representative from California made the announcement during a taping of The Late Show with Stephen Colbert . <p> " I 'm running for president of the United States , " he said in a clip the program posted to Twitter . " Boy , did it feel good to say that . " <p> Swalwell , 38 , is in his fourth term in Congress representing parts of Alameda and Contra Costa counties in central California . <p> " I see a country in quicksand , unable to solve problems and threats from abroad , unable to make life better for people , " he said . <p> " None of that is going to change until we get a leader who is willing to go big on the issues we take @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and do good in the way we govern . I 'm ready to solve these problems . " <p> Swalwell has voiced support for gun control , pay equality , healthcare for all and policies to combat climate change . He serves on the House intelligence committee , which conducted an investigation into Russian meddling in the 2016 presidential election .
