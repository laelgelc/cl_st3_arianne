<p> Let 's give President Donald Trump his due : Neither he nor any of his associates conspired or colluded with the Russian government to influence the 2016 election . <p> So said special counsel Robert Mueller . And , as Americans , we ought to be relieved that our leaders are n't in cahoots with a hostile foreign power . <p> But that 's a low threshold for judging any president 's character and conduct . And it does n't begin to address the damning conclusions in Mueller 's investigative report . <p> As anyone who takes the time to read the two-volume report will see , the special counsel delivered anything but the " total exoneration " claimed Thursday by the president .
