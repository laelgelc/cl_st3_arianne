<h> The importance of simple kindness <p> By Jane Siebert <h> Saturday <p> Apr 6 , 2019 at 1:01 AM <p> I pulled into a fast food drive-through lane , following the arrows from the front and as I was supposed to do . Another car entered from the back way ( which some would call the " wrong way " ) . We both hesitated as to who should go first . I was not in a hurry for a change and motioned for the driver to go ahead of me , even though I was thinking , I am the one in the right lane . <p> After they ordered , I followed with my order and drove to the window to pay . As I was counting out my change , the attendant said , " You do n't need to pay . The car ahead of you paid for your order . " <p> This kind act from a stranger changed my day . The Chinese fast food tasted better . I went on being courteous to drivers and store clerks as I finished my errands .
