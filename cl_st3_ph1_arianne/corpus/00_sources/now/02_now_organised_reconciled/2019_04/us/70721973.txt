<h> Joe Biden is not the Jeb Bush of 2020 <p> As analysts try to rate Joe Biden 's chances in the 2020 Democratic presidential nomination fight , a number have started to draw comparisons to Jeb Bush 's failed effort in 2016 . But this analogy does not hold , on multiple levels . <p> The most straight forward reason is that Biden 's polling is significantly better . There 's a widespread misconception that Bush was initially the front-runner in the Republican field and that was never really true . <p> Specifically , in 2016 , Bush never polled above 17.8% in the RealClearPolitics average . On the day he announced his candidacy -- June 15 , 2015 -- he was only at 10.8% in polls , essentially tied with Scott Walker , who was at 10.6% . Overall , five rival candidates were within three points of Bush on average -- and that was before Donald Trump launched his campaign . <p> In contrast , Biden has consistently led in polls by much wider margins , never dropping below 26.3% .
