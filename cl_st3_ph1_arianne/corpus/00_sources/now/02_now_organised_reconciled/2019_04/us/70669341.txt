Wade , resulting in backlash from teachers and parents across Michigan . Tuesday , the State Board of Education reviewed a new 146-page draft that restored that language and increased references to issues that were previously on the chopping block . <p> Public outrage after the 2018 draft was published caused the state to convene a broader and more inclusive group of experts , educators and parents .
