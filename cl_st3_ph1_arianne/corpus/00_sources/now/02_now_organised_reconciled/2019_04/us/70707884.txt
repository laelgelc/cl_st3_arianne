<h> Growing number of Americans think environment will be worse for next generation , CBS News poll finds <p> By Fred Backus <p> April 22 , 2019 / 7:00 AM / CBS News <p> Earth Matters : Climate change across the globe <p> Americans give the U.S. just average or low marks on the progress it has made on protecting the environment and most believe the environment will be worse for the next generation of Americans . <p> A majority thinks global warming is caused mainly by human activity , and more than half of Americans think it 's having a serious impact now . Still , about 6 in 10 think humanity can do something to stop or slow climate change . <p> When asked to grade the U.S. on protecting the environment , few Americans give the U.S. high marks . Most give the country a " C " at best , including 22 percent who give a failing grade of " F.
