<h> Festival of Faiths embraces religion and science to bring enlightenment <p> Sarah Riggs Reed is managing director of the Center for Interfaith Relations , which organizes the annual Festival of Faiths in Louisville . ( Photo : John H Nation ) <p> The terms " war on science " and " war on religion " are frequently bandied about in divisive political discourse . We read stories about anti-vaxxers , climate change deniers and creationists in the classroom , as well as sweeping claims about left-wing attempts to undermine Christianity . And although the rhetoric of vocal minorities makes for provocative headlines , more often than not , the realms of faith and science not only peacefully coexist , but overlap with the shared objective of enlightenment . <p> Thomas Merton understood the beneficial balance of both living in awe of our sacred universe while seeking answers through science . The Trappist monk , scholar and social activist wrote , " It is true that neither the ancient wisdoms nor the modern sciences are complete in themselves . They do not stand alone . They call for one another . " <p> This quote by Merton epitomizes the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Sacred Cosmos : Faith and Science . " The festival serves as an invitation to delve into the rich and nourishing vein of wisdom found at the intersection of faith and science . Often the two disciplines are held in juxtaposition , as if reason and revelation can not coexist in both . However , it is far more illuminating to shine the light of inquiry at the crossroads of the two , resulting in an even deeper appreciation for the miracle of our existence and the mystery of our interbeing with the world around us . <p> As Albert Einstein said in a talk at Union Theological Seminary , " The situation may be expressed by an image : Science without religion is lame , religion without science is blind . " It is our hope that this forum will embrace both and offer an opportunity for curiosity and intellect to inspire wonder and awe , to inform a new consciousness , and to renew purpose and hope in our day . <p> Science is advancing faster than ever before , with numerous incredible feats making headlines in recent @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ astronomers capturing the first-ever image of a black hole ( a celestial phenomenon Einstein first theorized about more than a century ago ) . <p> Despite these advancements , there is still so much we do not know for certain . But consider this : It is the unknown that begets deeper thought , both in science and in faith . We invite you to contemplate the intersection of these two disciplines at this year 's Festival of Faiths ( April 25-27 at the Kentucky Center for the Arts ) . <p> Sarah Riggs Reed is managing director of the Center for Interfaith Relations , which organizes the annual Festival of Faiths in Louisville.
