<h> After a decade of research , here 's what scientists know about the health impacts of fracking <p> Fracking has been linked to preterm births , high-risk pregnancies , asthma , migraine headaches , fatigue , nasal and sinus symptoms , and skin disorders over the last 10 years , according to a new study . <p> Fracking , also known as hydraulic fracturing , is a process of extracting oil and gas from the Earth by drilling deep wells and injecting a mixture of liquids and chemicals at high pressure . <p> The study , which was published in the Oxford Research Encyclopedia of Global PublicHealth in February , looked at several hundred scientific articles about the community and health impacts of fracking .
