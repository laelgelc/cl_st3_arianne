They follow the global strike on 15 March , when more than 1 million young people across the world took action to demand rapid action to tackle the climate crisis . <p> The movement began when Swedish student Greta Thunberg started a solo school strike in August . The protests have been widely praised , although some have criticised the students for skipping school .
