<h> News &amp; Events <h> Annual Theme " Changing Climates " Ends With Grand Finale of Events <p> BLOOMINGTON , Ill. --- As the end of the school year approaches , Illinois Wesleyan 's annual theme of Changing Climates will also come to a close . To celebrate this year 's theme , the campus will host a " grand finale " series of events to help the campus community reflect on the theme . <p> The events kick off with a film screening of Ren ? e Scheltema 's award-winning documentary Normal is Over . In addition to being a film producer , Scheltema is an investigative journalist . Following the screening will be a Q&amp;A with Scheltema . The film itself is a challenging and hopeful look into humanity 's response to climate change and the way our economic and financial systems link issues together . There will be two screenings of the documentary , the first on Thursday , April 11 at 7 p.m. at the Normal Theater . The second screening will take place Friday , April 12 at 7 p.m. in the Hansen Student Center .
