<p> Today , the Museum of Ice Cream takes its sprinkle-tastic wonderland to the next level . <p> At 12:30 p.m. , the museum -- an ice cream-themed art installation of sorts popular with the Instagram crowd -- opens its first cafe and ice cream parlor , called Cafe 1905C . It 's named after the color code for the museum 's signature shade of pink . <p> The museum also plans to add a bar to its repertoire . Together , it 's an ambitious move for the San Francisco location , signaling the founders ' devotion to remaining in the city while other locations have moved on after several months . With the expensive real estate near Union Square , co-founder Manish Vora said the cafe and bar are critical for the business 's future . <p> Given how museums usually have a cafe , it also feels like a natural evolution for the Museum of Ice Cream , which already peppers its exhibits with treats . <p> " We have this incredible museum experience , but our guests want to spend more time in the neighborhood and in our building , " Vora said .
