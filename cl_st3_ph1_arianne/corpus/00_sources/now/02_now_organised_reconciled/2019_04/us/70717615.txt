The fossil fuel industry is doing it again by faking concern for the welfare of disadvantaged residents and people of color , while simultaneously promoting practices and products that harm them , the environment and climate . <p> That is the message of the new NAACP report , " Fossil Fueled Foolery : An Illustrated Primer on the Top 10 Manipulation Tactics of the Fossil Fuel Industry . " It was released because , as Kathy Egland , Chair of the NAACP National Board Committee on Environmental and Climate Justice said , " One of the most duplicitous strategies of the fossil fuel industry is manipulating messaging which feigns concern for the welfare of low income and communities of color . " <p> According to the report , one way the industry does this is by " investing in efforts that undermine democracy . " <p> Additionally , the report said the industry funds the development of scientific reports intended to " deny or understate the negative impacts of fossil fuels " ... " discredit the practicality and value of clean and renewable energy systems " ... and refute the very existence of human-caused climate change . <p> Similarly , the industry denies their operations harm people of color , deflects responsibility for climate change , and often " shifts the blame to the very communities they pollute .
