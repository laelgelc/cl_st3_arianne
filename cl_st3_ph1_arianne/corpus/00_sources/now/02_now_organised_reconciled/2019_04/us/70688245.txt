<h> Search <h> Good News About Cover Crops from the Census of Agriculture <p> Last week USDA released the results of the 2017 Census of Agriculture . The previous 2012 Census was the first one to include a question about cover crops . While the 2012 Census provided the first nationwide overview of cover crop use , state-by-state , the 2017 Census provides the first opportunity to quantify changes in cover crop use over a five-year period . <p> That 's right folks . In 2012 , farmers planted a little over 10 million acres of cover crops . In 2017 , they planted over 15 million -- 15,390,674 acres , to be exact . That 's about a 50% increase nationwide . We have the growing soil health movement to thank for that -- a group effort on the part of farmers , soil scientists , conservation districts , state agriculture departments , USDA , conservation NGOs , seed companies , trade groups , lawmakers , journalists and food corporations who teamed up to get out the word on cover crops and cover 5 million acres of ground in 5 years . <h> Cover crop acreage increased by @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ cover crop acreage increased by 50% since 2012 , state-by-state the change in cover crop acreage varies widely .
