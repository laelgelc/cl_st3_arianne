<p> Human 's impact on nature has been revealed in a new United Nations report and experts think a " mass extinction event " is already underway . <p> The report states that up to one million species are now at risk of extinction due to the behavior of humans . <p> A draft of the report was obtained by AFP and the full version will be released next month . <p> Robert Watson , chair of the UN-mandated body behind the report , told AFP : " We need to recognize that climate change and loss of nature are equally important , not just for the environment , but as development and economic issues as well . " <p> " The way we produce our food and energy is undermining the regulating services that we get from Nature . " <p> The figures in the report come with a warning that rates of species extinction are speeding up . <p> It states that the rate of animals being lost is " tens to hundreds of times higher than it has been , on average , over the last 10 million years . " <p> Factors listed as contributing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ people in the world , their increasing ability to consume things , hunting , climate change and pollution . <p> Taking areas of wilderness and species habitats and destroying them for farming or other land purposes was also listed . <p> According to the report , 40 percent of the marine environment , 50 percent of inland waterways and 75 percent of land has been " severely altered " due to humans . <p> The full UN report will be revealed on May 6 . <p> The International Union for Conservation of Nature has a ' Red List ' where it notes down all the animals that could disappear in our lifetime and noted that 27,000 species could disappear over the next few decades . <p> Once the UN findings are officially released this number could increase .
