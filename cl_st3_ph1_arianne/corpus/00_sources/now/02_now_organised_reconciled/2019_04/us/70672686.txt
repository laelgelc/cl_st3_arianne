<h> Arctic temperatures warmest in more than 10,000 years : University of Alberta study <p> Arctic temperatures are the warmest in more than 10,000 years , speeding the thaw of permafrost in Canada 's North , shows climate research out of the University of Alberta . <p> " We 've known that the last few decades have been very warm . But we 've found that temperatures are on the order of two degrees Celsius warmer than any time in the last 10,000 years -- that was a surprise , " Duane Froese , professor in the Department of Earth and Atmospheric Science , and Canada Research Chair in Northern Environmental Change , said in a Wednesday release . <p> Froese , co-author of the study , said the resulting rise in permafrost thaw across the Arctic is destabilizing soil carbon , which may further accelerate warming . <p> The study found that previous record highs occurred during the early Holocene period -- around 9,900 and 6,400 years ago .
