Now , a team of researchers think they know what caused it . <p> The ice hole -- known as a polynya in the parlance of sea ice researchers -- was likely caused by a powerful cyclone that spun up in Antarctica 's Weddell sea before migrating eastward into the Lazarev sea in mid-September 2017 , according to research published Wednesday in the Journal of Geophysical Research : Atmospheres .
