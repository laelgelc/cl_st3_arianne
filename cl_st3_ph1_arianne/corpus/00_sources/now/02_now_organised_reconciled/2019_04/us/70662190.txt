<h> Guest Opinion : Stop enabling climate of violence <p> By Joseph E. Sundeen <h> Sunday <p> Apr 7 , 2019 at 5:08 AM <p> There is a lot of talk about climate these days . The evidence overwhelmingly supports the fact that the world 's climate has changed for the worse , and the reason for that change is tied conclusively to human activities . <p> Even with such strong correlation , however , it can not be categorically stated that any one event -- no matter how horrific -- in fact caused by this change . What the evidence strongly supports , however , is that the physical condition which enhances the severity and frequency of such an event is , in fact , a reality . <p> Almost everyone except the most ardent deniers conclude , as the physical temperature of the planet -- as measured in degrees Celsius or Fahrenheit -- has increased , so has the extremes of weather events also increased . <p> Similarly , we now are confronting an increase in our civilization 's emotional temperature , and a climate of violence is one a symptom of that increase . The president @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ platform for communication -- has had an outsized effect on the emotional temperature of not only the people in our country but of people around the world . <p> A climate for violence has been enhanced , and we are seeing more and more manifestations of this change . Just as with the weather , we can not say that any one event -- like that in New Zealand -- has been caused by this climate change , but the result of the toxic rhetoric emanating from the White House is , to say the very least , not helpful . <p> We all must implore the president -- and anyone who has gone along with him for whatever reason -- to reverse this sickening and destructive trend . It may be hard to reduce the amount of greenhouse gasses in our atmosphere , but surely stopping the release of toxic words into our emotional environment will only take the will and compassion of the people who are actively responsible for dumping hate into our environment . <p> Joseph E.
