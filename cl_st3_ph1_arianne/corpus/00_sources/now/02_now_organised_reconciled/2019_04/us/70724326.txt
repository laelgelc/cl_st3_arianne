<h> Ancient trees , like those in tropical rain forests , are crucial for slowing climate change , but they keep disappearing . It 's more important than ever that countries try to limit losses -- and work to plant new trees . <p> Old-growth rain forests are some of the greatest carbon capture tools on Earth . Because they 've never been cut , their roots extend deep into the soil , storing extra carbon .
