<p> A new network of high-power charging points for electric vehicles is to be rolled out across the country . <p> More than 50 hubs will be built on motorways and national roads while 50 existing charge locations will also be upgraded . <p> Each charging hub will be able to charge between two and eight vehicles at a time -- with six minutes of charging capable of delivering at least 100km of electric driving range . <p> The upgrades to existing charge locations will more than double their output . <p> A provisional map of the planned network .
