<h> City working on plan B climate initiatives in face of Energy Efficiency Alberta 's possible extinction <p> The city is working on a contingency plan for a climate program run through a provincial office that the United Conservative Party committed to kill if it formed government . <p> " I 'd rather not have to start a new agency , but if the province closes theirs down , we 'll have to look at either banding together as municipalities to do it , or working with the federal government to do it . We need to find partners , but clearly the work needs to be done to achieve our climate goals , " Mayor Don Iveson said Thursday . <p> Edmonton hoped to launch a pilot of the Clean Energy Improvement Program in October . It 's an Alberta version of an initiative that allows property owners to get financing for investments in renewable energy or upgrading energy efficiency , and payments are tied to property taxes .
