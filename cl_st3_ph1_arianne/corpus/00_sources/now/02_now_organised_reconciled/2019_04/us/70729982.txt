Within just four years of the discovery of the thinning ozone hole , the agreement was in force . <p> But the Montreal Protocol is also important because of what it did for the climate . Ozone-depleting gases turned out to be potent greenhouse gases . The Montreal Protocol is estimated to have prevented the equivalent of 10 gigatons of CO2 per year from reaching the atmosphere , or about a quarter of the world 's annual carbon dioxide emissions . The banks were n't insignificant either .
