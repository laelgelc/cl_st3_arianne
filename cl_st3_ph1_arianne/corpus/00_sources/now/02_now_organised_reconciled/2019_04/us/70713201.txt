<p> North Shore tributaries to Lake Superior have been too cold this month to coax the annual steelhead run , but fisheries managers this week saw a sign that the spawning push is nigh . <p> On Monday at Knife River , 41 steelhead were swimming in the Department of Natural Resources fish trap 18 miles north of Duluth . Nick Peterson , the agency 's migratory fish specialist , said the finding marked the first meaningful stream activity of the season . <p> " Males are usually the first to arrive and that 's what was in there , ' ' he said . " We had a small push over the weekend . ' ' <p> For the trend to continue , flows must remain strong and water temperatures need to climb a few digits to around 40 degrees .
