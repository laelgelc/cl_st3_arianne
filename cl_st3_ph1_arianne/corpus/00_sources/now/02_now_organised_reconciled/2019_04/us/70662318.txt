<h> Sunday <p> Apr 7 , 2019 at 6:00 AM <p> PLUMSTEAD -- The Fred Beans Family of Dealerships , through its involvement in the Pennsylvania Educational Improvement Tax Credit Program , contributed $250,000 to local organizations that provide scholarship or educational improvement . <p> " We are proud to participate in the EITC program once again and help support the educational experiences of children in our area , " said Beth Beans Gilbert , vice president of the Fred Beans Family of Dealerships . " My father , Fred Beans , deeply values education and the role it plays in helping individuals grow and achieve success . We know that the organizations benefiting from the EITC program will reach a diverse group of learners in our region and provide them with memorable and meaningful experiences . " <p> The Fred Beans Family of Dealerships contributed to 10 organizations in 2018 to support programming implemented in 2019 .
