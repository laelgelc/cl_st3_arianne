This year 's Dublin Dance Festival will highlight her skills as an archivist as well as the creator of a grim end-of-world drama . <p> In AFTER , she confronts impending environmental degradation , but rather than indulging in apocalyptic sci-fi fantasy , she concentrates on the earth 's final days and hours through the eyes of two individuals . <p> " I did n't want to ask why the end of the world was happening or how it was happening .
