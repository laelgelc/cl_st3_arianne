<h> Polar bear gets lost in Russia , hundreds of miles from home <p> MOSCOW -- Residents of a village in Russia 's far eastern Kamchatka Peninsula were stunned by the sight of a polar bear prowling for food hundreds of miles from its usual habitat . <p> Russian media reported Wednesday that the exhausted-looking animal apparently traveled from Chukotka to the village of Tilichiki on Kamchatka , some 700 kilometers ( 434 miles ) south . <p> Environmentalists said the bear could have lost its sense of direction while drifting on an ice floe . <p> " Due to climate change , the Arctic is getting warmer , hunting environment gets smaller and less convenient , " said Vladimir Chuprov of Greenpeace . " The ice is receding , and polar bears look for new ways to survive . And the easiest way is coming to people .
