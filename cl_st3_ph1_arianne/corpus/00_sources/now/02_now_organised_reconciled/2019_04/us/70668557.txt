Photograph : Matthias Huss/Cryosphere/EGU <p> Two-thirds of the ice in the glaciers of the Alps is doomed to melt by the end of the century as climate change forces up temperatures , a study has found . <p> Half of the ice in the mountain chain 's 4,000 glaciers will be gone by 2050 due to global warming already baked in by past emissions , the research shows . After that , even if carbon emissions have plummeted to zero , two-thirds of the ice will still have melted by 2100 . <p> If emissions continue to rise at the current rate , the ice tongues will have all but disappeared from Alpine valleys by the end of the century .
