And what the Pinkertons see coming are climate disasters that could make some people desperate to find food and water , according to a feature story published this week as part of a special climate issue of the New York Times Magazine . In other words , a business opportunity . <p> A century ago , Pinkerton was best known for breaking labor strikes and renting private militias to wealthy industrialists . Today it is owned by a Swedish security company and bills itself as " the world 's leading provider of corporate risk management solutions . " Pinkerton regards climate change as a " natural disaster " along with risks such as floods , droughts , and wildfires .
