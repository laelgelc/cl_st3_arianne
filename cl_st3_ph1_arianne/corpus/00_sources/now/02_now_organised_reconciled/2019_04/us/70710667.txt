Palmar <p> Erupting volcanoes , earthquakes , dust storms and meteorites smashing into the Earth 's crust are natural phenomena that can cause climate change and air pollution : dinosaurs may have met their end after a giant meteorite kicked up so much dust that it blocked out the sun for decades , reducing photosynthesis and preventing the growth of plants . <p> Adding to these potential threats , we have also been contributing to air pollution and global warming through our resource-intensive lifestyles . We 're producing and consuming more than ever before , and we 're generating more greenhouse gases as a result , as well as air pollutants in the form of chemicals and particulate matter , including " black carbon " . <p> Switching to renewable sources of energy is an important part of the solution to both climate change and air pollution .
