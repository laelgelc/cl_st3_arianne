<p> Fire officials at Rocky Mountain National Park are planning prescribed burns on a section of the park to help protect wildlife and property from wildland fires , the park said in a news release . <p> The burning will be done in the Beaver Meadows entrance area , park officials said -- although it is contingent on favorable weather and fuel conditions . The park did not identify specific dates for the prescribed burns , but said they may be conducted for several weeks in multiple segments to limit the daily smoke production . A 36-acre section of this area was burned in October . <p> Fire managers conduct prescribed burns -- also known as controlled burns -- in areas that " may increase the likelihood that firefighters could hold a fire inside the park , near roads and in strategic locations , " Kyle Patterson , spokesman for the park , said in the release .
