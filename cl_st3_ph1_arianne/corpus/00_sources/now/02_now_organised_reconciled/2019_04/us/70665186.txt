<p> Scientists and officials around the US have told the Guardian that the Trump administration has withdrawn funding for a large , successful conservation program -- in direct contradiction of instructions from Congress . <p> Unique in scale and ambition , the program comprises 22 research centers that tackle big-picture issues affecting huge swaths of the US , such as climate change , flooding and species extinction . They are known as Landscape Conservation Cooperatives -- or were , because 16 of them are now on indefinite hiatus or have dissolved . <p> " I just have n't seen anything like this in my almost 30 years of working with the federal government , " said a scientist at the Fish and Wildlife Service who worked for one of the LCCs and wished to remain anonymous , because federal employees were instructed not to speak with the Guardian for this story .
