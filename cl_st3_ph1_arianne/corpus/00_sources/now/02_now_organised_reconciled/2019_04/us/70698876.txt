<h> An early love of space exploration led ASU grad to a career in the stratosphere <p> Before Alex Miller even began kindergarten , his mom would take him to the Smithsonian National Air and Space Museum in Washington , D.C. He remembers looking up at the Saturn V rocket engine and the lunar roving vehicle and staring at the astronaut spacesuits . These early trips instilled in Miller a dream of human space exploration which later developed into a keen interest in robotic space exploration and astronomy .
