Development is encroaching on the best farmland . There are issues of climate change and access to fresh water , pest problems and soil problems , heat and humidity , and the threat of hurricanes . Labor costs are high . <p> But , according to growers , the biggest challenge is n't in Florida at all . <p> It 's what the Florida Tomato Exchange , a trade organization , alleges is an effort by Mexican growers to dump artificially low-priced tomatoes on the American market .
