But once they 've outlived their usefulness , these items become ... a bit of a problem . <p> What is e-waste ? <p> E-waste refers to electronic equipment that is no longer wanted or has reached its end of life . From refrigerators to phones to used batteries , e-waste can pile up in our homes and in our landfills . <p> Why ca n't I throw it in the garbage ? <p> E-waste is hazardous material . Over time , electronics can leak toxic elements such as mercury and lead , which can be harmful to the environment and to humans . <p> How big is the problem ? @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ BIR ) , Canada generated about 638,000 tonnes of " e-scrap " ( electronic and electrical equipment ) in 2017 . Items like reusable batteries are typically separated and melted down into various elements , such as lead and nickel .
