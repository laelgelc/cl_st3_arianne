<p> Canada 's largest program of Arctic research will receive more than $32-million in funding over the next five years , the federal government announced Tuesday . <p> Louis Fortier , head of ArcticNet , said the money will be used to help understand the challenges faced by a rapidly changing North due to climate change and industrialization . <p> " It 's really science to inform decisions , to prepare people in the North and also the government in the south , " Mr. Fortier said .
