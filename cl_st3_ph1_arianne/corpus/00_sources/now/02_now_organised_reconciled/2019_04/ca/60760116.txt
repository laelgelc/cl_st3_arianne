<p> The Oxford Dictionary defines " emergency " as " a serious , unexpected , and often dangerous situation requiring immediate action . " The Cambridge Dictionary calls it " something dangerous or serious , such as an accident , that happens suddenly or unexpectedly and needs fast action in order to avoid harmful results . " The word points to something abrupt and unexpected -- for example , a plane forced to make an emergency landing . <p> Many things that are urgent or important or pose an ongoing danger need action , to be sure , but they are not " emergencies . " Abusing the word is misleading and melodramatic . <p> So Ottawans should be dismayed that a committee of city councillors wants to declare a climate change " emergency " here . Puh-leeze . A heart attack is an emergency . A fire in the ByWard Market is an emergency . Flooding , or a tornado , is an emergency . Climate change may well be connected to many incidents that become emergencies , but in itself it is n't one . Rather , it is an ongoing and worsening problem .
