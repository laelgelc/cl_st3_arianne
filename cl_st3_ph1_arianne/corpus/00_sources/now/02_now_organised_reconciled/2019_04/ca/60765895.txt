<p> Islanders are voting today in an election campaign unlike any other in P.E.I. 's history . It marks the first three-way race in a province long beholden to a two-party system , and has the Green Party hoping to make an unprecedented breakthrough . <p> Even before a single ballot is counted , it 's already been an election campaign that will live long in Islanders ' memories . <p> Hanging over election day there is the acute pain of loss that only a tragedy in a close-knit community can bring . <p> Green Party candidate Josh Underhay and his six-year-old son died on Good Friday in a canoeing accident , just four days before voting day . Underhay , 35 , was a celebrated teacher , musician and community builder . <p> All parties cancelled campaign events for the remainder of Easter weekend and the Greens announced they were drawing an early close to their campaign . <h> Byelection to be determined <p> Underhay had been considered one of the Greens ' best possibilities to win a seat .
