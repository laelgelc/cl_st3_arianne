<h> Letters : The true costs of cost-cutting <p> Do we truly understand the costs of cost-cutting in areas of health , education and mitigation of climate change ? Take climate change , for instance . One single decision that looks good for the budget can have far-reaching effects . The recent cuts to Forest Ontario , which had plans to plant 50-million trees , is at the very least shortsighted and , to many of us who understand the important role in carbon sequestration that trees provide , it is outrageous . <p> About 40 per cent forest cover is needed to ensure forest sustainability , and the average right now in southern Ontario is 26 per cent , with some areas as low as five per cent . With climate change coming , it 's going to be more important than ever to have healthy contiguous large forests . <p> As reasonable as it seems to be " trimming the fat , " in light of the PC stance on carbon tax and given the absence of any so-called alternative climate plan , any cut to funding of environmental protections is outrageous . Forests are also essential for cleaning the air and water , protection of shorelines , reduction of erosion , cooling of air , mental health , jobs -- the list goes on . The Progressive Conservative list of reasons is short , and falls short . In the short term , money will be saved . In the long term , the cost will be immense .
