<h> Lobby Wrap : Trans Canada Trail lobbying Ottawa for funding <p> The charity responsible for developing a 24,000-kilometre multi-use national trail system that stretches to the Atlantic , Arctic and Pacific oceans is appealing for funding from Ottawa . <p> Four consultants from Toronto-based StrategyCorp Inc. have registered this month with the federal lobbying commissioner 's office to advocate on behalf of the Trans Canada Trail , the charitable group that raises money for the development of the namesake trail . <p> In their identical registrations , the lobbyists say they are " seeking funding for long-term development of the Trans Canada Trail , " targeting Infrastructure Canada and Parks Canada . According to the filings , the TCT received $7.5 million from Parks Canada and just over $940,000 from Canadian Heritage in the last year . But while it expects to receive funding this year from Parks Canada , it does not anticipate any financial support from Canadian Heritage , read the registrations . <p> Completed in 2017 , the TCT is the longest recreational , multi-use trail network in the world .
