<h> Grimes Tears Apart ' Art Angels ' and Calls It " a Piece of Crap " <p> Back in 2015 , Grimes topped our Top 20 Pop &amp; Rock Albums list with Art Angels -- a record we heralded as a " masterwork of provocative , powerful pop . " However , it appears Claire " c " Boucher has changed her tune when looking back at her 2015 record . <p> In conversation with Culturedmagazine , Boucher said of Art Angels , " The last album was a piece of crap ... I feel like people really misread it and it feels like a stain on my life . " <p> She added that as opposed to a pop record , she considered it " more of a genre exercise in which she demonstrated her range as a producer . " <p> She continued : " I just wish I could make music in a vacuum . It 's good to make people mad actually , I retract my statement .
