<h> Carbon Engineering raised $90 million , Inventys $16 million <p> Inventys CEO Claude Letourneau stands next to a carbon extraction filter that is made up of many layers of treated sheets Chung Chow <p> In the Canadian carbon-capture space , Carbon Engineering has been grabbing most of the headlines lately . <p> The B.C. company recently closed a $90 million ( US$68 million ) equity financing round and is working to build its first commercial-scale direct-air capture plant . One of the products it can make with carbon captured from the air is a carbon-neutral jet fuel . <p> But another B.C. company , Inventys Inc. , has also been landing some serious financing recently and inking multimillion-dollar partnerships with industry . <p> In February , Inventys raised $16 million in C-round financing , led by the Oil and Gas Climate Initiative ( OGCI ) , a consortium of 13 of the world 's biggest oil and gas companies .
