<h> Maggie MacDonald has assembled a who 's who of Toronto artists for her ' apocalyptic space dramedy ' <p> On February 14th of next year , the people of our planet will be given an ultimatum from an alien signal : shut down pop music radio , or the Earth will be destroyed . Of course , not enough people will listen . <p> This is the entry point of No One Receiving , an apocalyptic space dramedy of a podcast that works as a metaphor for the current climate crisis and the burnout folks fighting it are surely feeling . It follows Beth Kane , a self-described music snob who manages to escape Earth on a stolen spaceship just before the aliens blow it up .
