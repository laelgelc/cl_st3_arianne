<h> Federal government announces tough restrictions on chinook fishing <p> The federal government has announced tough new restrictions on fishing for chinook salmon in an effort to protect endangered Fraser River stocks . <p> Federal Fisheries Minister Jonathan Wilkinson said the measures are necessary to save the fish from extinction . <p> " A lack of action today could mean the extinguishment of some of these runs and ultimately of the Fraser Chinook entirely over time , " he said in an interview . <p> The federal government said Chinook stocks have been in decline for years due to multiple factors such as fishing , habitat destruction and the effects of climate change . <p> The Committee on the Status of Endangered Wildlife in Canada determined that 12 of 13 Fraser River chinook populations are at risk . <p> Wilkinson said the new measures announced Tuesday establish a recreational catch-and-release fishery until the middle of July in most areas to allow the maximum number of Chinook to return to their spawning grounds .
