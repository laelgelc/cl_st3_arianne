He has been president of the WWF UK conservation organization since 2011 . <p> " There has never been a time where more people have been more out of touch with the natural world than as now , " Attenborough told Prince William in an interview in January . <p> " If we damage the natural world , we damage ourselves . We are one coherent ecosystem , " he added .
