The California-based activist investor group As You Sow makes it its mission to file shareholder proposals aimed at promoting gender diversity , advancing good governance , and tackling climate change and plastics consumption . <p> In the case of Starbucks , As You Sow was back again this proxy season seeking support for its proposal that the beverage giant provide additional disclosure on the scale and pace of its sustainable packaging initiatives . As You Sow emphasized prior goals rescinded by the company . A decade ago , Starbucks announced that it aimed to deliver 25 per cent of its beverages in reusables by 2015 . No question the company was boldly aiming to be an industry leader in this regard and As You Sow urged competitors to match that goal . <p> Article Continued Below <p> That target , however , was subsequently slashed to 5 per cent @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ served in reusables . <p> In days gone by , shareholder resolutions such as these would garner modest support . " We used to get 10 , 12 , 14 per cent support , " says As You Sow senior vice-president Conrad MacKerron . " Now we 're getting 25 , 30 , 25 per cent . " <p> Or higher . <p> The Starbucks resolution proposed by As You Sow garnered a surprising 44.5-per-cent vote in favour . In its analysis , which advocated for shareholder support of the resolution , ISS points out that while Starbucks is transparent about its environmental initiatives , " the company has dramatically lowered its sustainable packaging targets from 2008 and it does not appear to have made much progress in reaching its original goals ... This lack of progress towards its stronger sustainable packaging goals may compromise Starbucks ' image as a leader for environmental stewardship , especially as its competitors begin disclosing more stringent recycling and recyclability targets . " <p> It would be a mistake to describe Starbucks as non-responsive . In March 2018 , As You Sow filed a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ straws phaseout . That drew 29 per cent shareholder support . Two months later Starbucks announced that it would be eliminating plastic straws by 2020 . <p> Starbucks is also a founding partner , with McDonald 's , of the NextGen Consortium rewarding innovative ideas in solving the single-use coffee cup conundrum . " I do n't know if it 's going to fix anything , but it did put some fresh energy and money into the effort , " says MacKerron . <p> Hot beverages are just part of the problem . In the past five years , cold drinks have risen to 50 per cent of Starbucks ' business from 37 per cent .
