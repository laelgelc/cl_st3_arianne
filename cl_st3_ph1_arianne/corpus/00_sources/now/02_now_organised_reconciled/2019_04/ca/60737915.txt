<p> Walls are great at protecting the things behind them -- which is why it was originally built in 1917 , to protect Stanley Park 's delicate sandstone from water erosion -- but not so good at protecting what is in front of them . <p> From an environmental perspective , this is bad news . <p> The sandy area around Stanley Park has been eroding since the sea wall was built . ( Uytae Lee/About Here ) <p> The seawall actually makes the waves worse because they are crashing onto a hard surface . <p> Those waves are washing away the sand outside the wall around Stanley Park faster than it otherwise would and , because of the wall , the sand is not being replaced from the shore . <p> This sandy area , called the intertidal zone , is home to starfish , crabs and other marine life . They ca n't survive if their environment is washed away , which is why seawalls tend to damage the land and marine life in front of them over time . <p> An illustration showing how the intertidal zone is washed away by waves and , with a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ being replaced from shore .
