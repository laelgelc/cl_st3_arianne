<h> Rolling in the deep <p> In 2005 Dr. Annie Mercier was sent a strange video : sea cucumbers rolling around at the bottom of the Atlantic Ocean . <p> Sea cucumbers -- sausage-shaped marine animals with soft , cylindrical bodies -- can usually be found on the seafloor where they gather together in large herds . Suckers on their tube feet allow the animals to stick to the ocean floor as they crawl along in search of food . <p> Sea cucumbers are a prized luxury seafood in high demand in many parts of the world . <p> Photo : Rich Blenkinsopp <p> " I thought it was weird behaviour for sea cucumbers , but figured a storm might have detached them from the seafloor , and then I forgot all about it , " said Dr. Mercier , professor , Department of Ocean Sciences , Faculty of Science . <p> " A few years later I was sent another video , this time of dozens sea cucumbers rolling around the seafloor off Nova Scotia .
