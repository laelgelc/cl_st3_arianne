<h> NDP moves motion condemning influence of corporate lobbyists <p> The federal NDP is calling on the House to condemn the pervasive influence of corporate lobbyists over the federal government . <p> NDP MP Daniel Blaikie moved a motion Monday declaring that lobbyists " have had too much access to and influence over the Government of Canada , " citing , among other activities , the SNC-Lavalin scandal , the purchase of the Trans Mountain pipeline and a recent $12 million grant to Loblaws for the company to upgrade its refrigerators . <p> " It 's not the job of government to look out for the interests of those guys , " he said in a speech to the House . <p> Blaikie 's motion also lamented corporate tax loopholes , high prices for prescription drugs and " anti-consumer " banking practices . The motion , he said , offers an opportunity " to talk about the influence of corporations here in Canada and to focus on the reality of hard-working Canadians .
