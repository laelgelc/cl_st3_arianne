shortly after releasing its climate plan -- and the results were not a ringing endorsement . <p> The Progressive Conservative government cancelled the cap-and-trade program introduced by the former Liberal government to lower greenhouse gas emissions and is fighting Ottawa 's carbon tax , which comes into effect Monday . <p> Their climate plan , unveiled in late November , establishes a fund called the Ontario Carbon Trust , which aims to entice companies to invest in initiatives that reduce greenhouse gas emissions , and proposes to make large industrial companies pay for pollution if they exceed certain emissions standards . <p> As part of monthly public polling commissioned by the government , Ipsos asked a series of questions in December on the environment , the results of which were obtained by The Canadian Press through a Freedom of Information request . <p> Just four in 10 respondents could recall reading , seeing or hearing something recently from the provincial
