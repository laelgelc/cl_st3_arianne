I was given a number of reasons . But one caused me much chagrin . Some people use these to avoid the embarrassment of having their credit or debit cards declined in the cashier-staffed line . In that line , the individual has to ask the cashier to remove items until the charge is accepted . Indeed , I 've seen folks doing this themselves in the self-checkout line . <p> I have found that the cost of groceries and the other items one purchases in large stores has increased markedly over the past couple years . And many of the markdowns , sales coupons and other methods to lower the cost have disappeared . <p> Gordon A.
