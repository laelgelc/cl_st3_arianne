<p> Notre-Dame burned down . The 850-year-old cathedral at the heart of France was one of its most significant cultural landmarks . The towering gothic spire was famous worldwide . And yet the cathedral 's decline is not recent -- it has been in bad health for some time . Its walls were crumbling and its structural integrity fading , but restoration efforts were slow . <p> French President Emmanuel Macron 's government failed to provide adequate support for the cathedral 's restoration . French business-people and billionaires turned a blind eye . Restoration funds came from crowd-sourcing efforts -- cathedral staff even began looking overseas for financial support . <p> Notre-Dame was neglected by the culture she defined , and the city functioned as usual . So it comes as no surprise that eventually catastrophe ensued . And yet , as soon as she succumbed to her injuries , the emotional outpour came . Cancelling an impending address to the nation , Macron rushed instead to the site of the fire , and gave an impassioned speech about the cathedral 's crucial role in the identity of Paris . Notre-Dame , as important as she was to her city and to the world , was left to rot until it was too late .
