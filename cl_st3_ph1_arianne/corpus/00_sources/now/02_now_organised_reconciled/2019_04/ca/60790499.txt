Rochman decided to do her graduate work at UC Davis studying how plastic pollution affects ecosystems and food webs . <p> For one UC Davis study , Rochman went to the supermarket and bought some fish . It turned out that one in four contained microplastics , a number that now seems low : Every fish in the Great Lakes has plastic in it , she says . A newly published Rochman Lab study found more than 100 synthetic microfibres in the stomach of a single fish . Many of the 23 to 36 trillion microfibres that annually end up in Lake Ontario watersheds come from our own clothes , released during laundering .
