" <p> The results of the report , therefore , make Canadians out to be bigger polluters than China , India or the United States . <p> According to the U.S National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration ( NOAA ) the global average has only gone up 0.8 Celsius since 1948 . <p> Along with higher temperatures , the " leaked " report says Canada is going to experience increases in precipitation , extreme fire weather @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ risk of coastal flooding as a result of our carbon emissions . <p> Just last year Canadians witnessed the devastating destruction of wildfires heard reports of the strain droughts have played on our local farmers . <p> And , globally , we 've seen the wrath of Mother Nature wreak havoc on any number of places with no regard to social or economical standing . <p> However , with all the mounting scientific proof of global warming , we still have skeptics that claim it is all just a hoax , a way for the government to make money off
