<h> Happy ( rush ) hour <p> Getting more people onto buses is the key . These enticements might help : Coffee in the morning ; beer in the evening . The beers should be limited to two per head . Many wonderful flavours of coffee and beer are produced in Nova Scotia . What better place to try them out than during that " wasted " time of commuting ? <p> Handling this would require hiring at least one extra employee per bus , plus having a place for supplies , etc . Coupons for drinks could be purchased at outlets along with bus tickets , so change would not be an issue . <p> This suggestion is not only for the duration of the Big Jam , but afterwards also .
