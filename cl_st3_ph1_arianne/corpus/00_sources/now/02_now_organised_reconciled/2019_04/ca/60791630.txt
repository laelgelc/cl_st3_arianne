<p> Alberta 's new Premier Jason Kenney had one message after he was sworn in at Government House in Edmonton Tuesday : The work starts now . <p> Flanked by 19 new cabinet ministers and three associate ministers , Kenney said the main focus for his team over the next four years will be economic growth , job creation , getting pipelines built and standing up for Alberta . <p> Tuesday 's event was a low-key affair compared with the public celebrations of the NDP government in 2015 , when thousands of Albertans descended on the sunny legislature grounds for politics and ice cream . <p> Instead , Tuesday saw only cabinet ministers , their friends and families , party insiders and a handful of unsuccessful UCP candidates slog through a late-spring snowstorm into Government House . <p> " I promised to Albertans we would n't waste a day , or an hour , getting to work to renew the Alberta advantage .
