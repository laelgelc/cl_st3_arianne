Fracking <p> " Five years since Stephen McNeil 's Liberal government placed a moratorium on fracking in Nova Scotia , the Progressive Conservative party seems intent on putting it back on the agenda , " reports Joan Baxter : <p> In February this year , the two PC members of the Standing Committee on Natural Resources and Economic Development -- MLAs Pat Dunn and Elizabeth Smith-McCrossin -- proposed two topics for discussion in the monthly committee meetings . One is uranium exploration . The other is " shale gas development , " a euphemism for the controversial process of high pressure hydraulic fracturing -- or fracking -- to get at onshore natural gas deposits . <p> Both practices are currently banned in Nova Scotia , and NDP committee members argued they should not be discussed by the committee . <p> But with Liberal support , the PCs managed to get both onto the agenda for future meetings . <h> 4 .
