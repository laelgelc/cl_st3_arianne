<p> WINNIPEG , April 23 , 2019/CNW/ - Environmental DNA ( e-DNA ) is an efficient and non-invasive new way to sample organisms , and it is revolutionizing biodiversity monitoring in Canada . eDNA is DNA extracted from environmental samples , like water and soil , without having to isolate the target organisms . Current biodiversity monitoring relies on visual surveying to identify species , which requires expensive , labour-intensive sampling that is , invasive to the species and unsafe for the ecosystem . <p> Today , Parliamentary Secretary Terry Duguid , Member of Parliament for Winnipeg South , on behalf of the Honourable Navdeep Bains , Minister of Innovation , Science and Economic Development and Minister responsible for Western Economic Diversification Canada ( WD ) , announced an investment of $1,126,800 towards the University of Manitoba Centre for Oil and Gas Research and Development ( COGRAD ) to establish an environmental DNA ( e-DNA ) laboratory .
