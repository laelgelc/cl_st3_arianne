<h> We need to defend Bill C-69 , says reader <p> Apr. 29 , 2019 <p> As a young Ottawa resident watching communities in the Ottawa Valley grapple with year after year of increased flooding , I am extremely worried about climate change.A couple of years ago , I tried to participate in the environmental assessment hearings for the Energy East pipeline . This project would have a huge impact on climate change : the emissions involved in producing Energy East 's oil would be equivalent to putting seven million new cars on the road . We all know that climate change will affect us all . Still , under the current environmental laws , I was denied the opportunity to participate in the hearings since , according to the National Energy Board , I 'm not ' directly affected ' .
