" <p> Small groups of demonstrators also climbed atop a train at Canary Wharf station in east London 's financial business district and blocked the road outside investment bank Goldman Sachs as they focused on the city 's financial centres on their final day of non-violent protests . <p> The group Extinction Rebellion said it was targeting the financial services industry for " funding climate and ecological destruction . " Activists held signs saying " business as usual <p> death " and " do n't jail the canaries . " The demonstrators were removed by police and taken away in vans . <p> The exchange , which has no trading floor , continued to function as normal . <p> Since April 15 , peaceful climate change demonstrations have disrupted traffic across London by targeting bridges , intersections and commuter trains . <p> More than 10,000 police officers have been deployed and more than 1,100 people have been arrested , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Rebellion says it will end its remaining blockades at Marble Arch and Parliament Square on Thursday . <p> The group thanked Londoners in a statement saying : " We know we have disrupted your lives .
