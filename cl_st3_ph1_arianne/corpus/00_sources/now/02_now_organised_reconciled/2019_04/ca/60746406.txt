But it became apparent shockingly early on Tuesday night that the United Conservative Party would secure a comfortable majority in the Alberta legislature , completing Jason Kenney 's journey from right-wing unity schemer to right-wing unity premier . The incumbent New Democrats held onto their fortress , Edmonton , and may still sweep it when absolutely all the votes are counted . But they appear to be retaining no more than a few satellite seats anywhere else . The final UCP seat total should be close to 60 of the province 's 87 . <p> It was a strange election .
