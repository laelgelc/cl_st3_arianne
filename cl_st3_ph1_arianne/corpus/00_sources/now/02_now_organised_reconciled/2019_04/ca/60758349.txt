<h> Federal government offers $5K as part of its plan to tackle climate change <p> A new network of electric charging stations is growing across Alberta and into B.C. <p> The federal government recently announced incentives for people to buy electric vehicles as part of its plans to tackle climate change . It 's offering $5,000 if you buy an electric car under a certain price point . <p> Jen Grebeldinger , the communications lead for the Community Energy Association , which facilitates the Peaks to Prairies charging network , spoke to the Calgary Eyeopener on Thursday about the growth in charging stations across southern Alberta . <p> This interview has been edited for clarity and length . <p> Q : What do you make of the announcement by the federal government ? <p> A. It 's going to be a huge boost for electric vehicle sales . It 's going to get to people who are already interested in these and those who are considering purchasing a new car and it 's going to tip them over to it being a really great option . <p> Q : Do you know how they chose which vehicles were in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ electric vehicles and some hybrids here ? <p> A. This is in response to making sure it 's not perceived that they 're buying luxury cars for people who can really afford to pay the full price , so they 've chosen full electric . <p> This is really linked to emissions . This is obviously very much tied to their climate plans and action plan . Plug-in hybrid vehicles have a lower , $2,500 incentive because , of course , they do n't remove as many emissions from the roads . <p> The Chevy Bolt has an electric range of nearly 400 kilometres .
