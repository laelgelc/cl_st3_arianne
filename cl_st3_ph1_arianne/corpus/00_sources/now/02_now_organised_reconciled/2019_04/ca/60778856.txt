<h> Victoria aims to eliminate public transit fares to encourage more riders <p> Victoria wants to eliminate public transit fares for everyone in the region to encourage more ridership and reduce the impacts of climate change . <p> Mayor Lisa Helps will bring a motion to the regional transit commission Monday , asking it to embrace a policy of phasing out user fees and expanding bus service to meet an anticipated increase in demand . <p> Coun. Ben Isitt , who introduced the motion that was passed Thursday by council , said it would begin with the elimination of fares for youth under 19 next year and the broader community would be phased in . <p> Isitt said there 's almost no more space in the city for expanding roads or parking . <p> " Driving is only going to get more challenging so I think as we make transit more convenient and cheaper that provides a further incentive for people to leave their cars at home .
