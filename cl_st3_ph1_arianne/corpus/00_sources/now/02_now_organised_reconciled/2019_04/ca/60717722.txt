<p> EXETER -- Koos and Nathalie Vermue , agricultural producers from the Bayfield area , were honoured by the Ausable Bayfield Conservation Authority ( ABCA ) with the Conservationist of the Year Award at a March 21 partner appreciation evening . <p> Doug Cook , vice-chair of the ABCA board of directors , presented the award to this year 's winners at the event at Ironwood Golf Club . The award recipients received a handcrafted award made by Bob Hutson , of Windbreak Farm Custom Woodworks near Fullarton . The engraved dual-purpose board is a piece of art on one side and a serving tray on the other and is made from reclaimed wood and select local hardwoods . In addition to the award prize , the ABCA will make a donation towards a tree and plaque at a Commemorative Woods site . The award winners also received scrolls recognizing their accomplishment from Huron-Bruce MP Ben Lobb and MPP Lisa M. Thompson . <p> ABCA healthy watersheds technician Abigail Gutteridge introduced the award winners and praised their record of stewardship of the land . <p> " On one particular visit to the Vermue farm , Koos said to me @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ will be good to you . " Any opportunity they have to improve soil health and , in turn , improve water quality they take those opportunities . We are very excited to be able to present this award to them tonight . " <p> The Vermues live on Bayfield River Road and have property in Main Bayfield and Bayfield North watersheds ( two priority areas and ' sentinel ' watersheds along Lake Huron 's southeast shore ) . These local stewards plant cover crops , and use agricultural best management practices , careful management of nutrients , erosion control measures to slow @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and a mix of no-till and conservation tillage . <p> They have created at least 15 berms in the past 15 years , planted many acres of cover crops , and completed a wetland enhancement project ( which allows sediment to settle out to reduce potential impacts on Bayfield River and Lake Huron ) . <p> Koos and Nathalie have contributed to water and soil research , since 2011 , by including automatic water samplers at a water and sediment control basin and recording of cover crops and snow . The Vermues also work to build awareness in the community of positive land stewardship and of sustainable agriculture . They have hosted tours and school classes on their farm to help others learn about farming and stewardship . Koos was a member of the Main Bayfield Watershed Plan Advisory Committee .
