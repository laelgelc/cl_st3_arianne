<h> Floods , fires and ice storms : An ex-soldier 's battle on the front line of natural disasters <p> When it comes to extreme weather and natural disasters , Josh Bowen has seen Canada at its worst . <p> As a member of the Canadian Armed Forces for 13 years , his resume reads like a catalog of Canadian catastrophes .
