<h> Feds and province commit $12.5 million each to rebuilding Highway 55 <p> Carrot River Valley MLA Fred Bradshaw speaks following an announcement by the federal government to fund improvements to 50 km of Highway 55 . ( Peter Lozinski/Daily Herald ) <p> The provincial and federal governments are working together to restore and rebuild the eastern portion of Highway 55 , which is prone to flooding and is n't able to handle primary weights . <p> As part of six announcements made Tuesday , Public Safety Minister Ralph Goodale announced $12.5 million to support the upgrading and rehabilitation work along more than 50 km of the highway to make it more resilient int eh face of chronic seasonal flooding . The provincial government will match the funding . <p> The highway serves as a primary access road to Red Earth and Shoal Lake First Nations , which have a combined population of more than 1,500 people . " <p> The funding for the project comes out of a federal government fund aimed to help respond to the consequences of climate change , including drought , fire , storms and floods . <p> " Upgrading Highway 55 will also help the region 's economy , " Bradshaw said . <p> " In my part of the province , forestry accounts for almost a third of the timber harvested in the province and supports more than 800 jobs .
