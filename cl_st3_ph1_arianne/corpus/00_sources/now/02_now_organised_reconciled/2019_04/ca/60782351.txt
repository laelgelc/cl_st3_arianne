<h> Fire crews to simulate North Shore wildfire <p> Tourists watch the August 2018 Whyte Lake fire from Horseshoe Bay . North Shore Emergency Management will be staging a mock wildfire on Thursday to test their readiness . file photo Lisa King , North Shore News <p> Where there 's smoke , there 's not always fire . <p> North Shore Emergency Management is warning residents they may see some frightening sights coming from the trees next week but it 's just a simulation and exercise in fighting forest fires . <p> Multiple agencies will be conducting a mock evacuation of two neighbourhoods , setting up a command post , using simulated smoke and using helicopters to bucket water in a simulation called Operation Dry Lightening . <p> " People should not be alarmed , " said Fiona Dercole , North Shore Emergency Management director . " It 's only practice . We do expect some calls to 911 and we 've let dispatch know that this is an exercise . " <p> Most of the action will be happening in the forested area near Cypress Falls Park in West Vancouver and Kilmer Creek Park in North Vancouver . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ municipalities will conduct a mock evacuation in those neighbourhoods , going door to door , letting people know what they should be doing in the event of the real thing and distributing some materials on what they can do to help prevent forest fires . <p> After the exercise , Dercole said all the agencies will get together and compare notes on what went well , and what would need to go better in the event of an actual fire .
