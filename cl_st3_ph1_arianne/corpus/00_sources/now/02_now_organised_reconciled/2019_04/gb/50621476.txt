<h> Only 26 percent of Germans know who Manfred Weber is <p> Only around one in four Germans ( 26 percent ) knows who Manfred Weber is , just a month ahead of a European election he hopes will help propel him to being the next European Commission president . <p> But Weber , the lead candidate for the European People 's Party ( EPP ) , is not alone in having a relatively low profile on the domestic political scene . According to a YouGov poll commissioned by dpa , 45 percent of Germans do not know any of the nine leading party candidates for the European Parliament election . <p> According to the survey , Katarina Barley , the Social Democratic Party 's ( SPD ) lead candidate , is the best known of the leaders ( 39 percent ) , arguably because she has served as justice minister in the federal government . <p> Nicola Beer , general secretary of the Free Democratic Party ( FDP ) and candidate for the European ALDE group , is also known by only 26 percent of Germans . <p> Despite recent successes at a national level in Germany , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ are relative unknowns at home , with Ska Keller also scoring 7 percent and Sven Giegold 6 percent . <p> In the poll , 23 percent of respondents said they believe the European election is less meaningful than German federal , state and council elections . <p> The key issues for Germans for the May election are climate change and the environment ( 55 percent ) , and migration ( 54 percent ) .
