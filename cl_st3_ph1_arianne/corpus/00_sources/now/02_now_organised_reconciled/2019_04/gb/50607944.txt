<h> Secretary of State for Wales Alun Cairns pays visit to Rhyl and discusses plan to strengthen flood defences <p> THE Secretary of State for Wales paid a visit to Rhyl to see how work on its flood scheme was progressing . <p> It has been six years since the floods , described as " the worst tidal surge in Wales during the last 25 years " , devastated the seaside down . About 120 properties were flooded from Splash Point eastwards to Frydd Beach at Rhyl Golf Club . <p> The project will reduce the risk of coastal flooding in the Garford Road area of East Rhyl and will build on short-term resilience measures undertaken by Denbighshire County Council following the 2013 storms . <p> Cllr Thomas said : " It was my suggestion for Mr Cairns to come and speak about the flood defence scheme . We discussed what had been done and what we needed to do . <p> " Because guidelines have changed , Denbighshire Council need to contribute more than ? 6million , 25 per cent of the overall cost of the project .
