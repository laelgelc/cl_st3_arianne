<p> What have lobsters , crabs and other crustaceans got to do with protecting crops in the fields from attack ? The answer , believe it or not , is a type of sugar called chitosan , which is the principal component of their shells ; scientists have discovered that it can actually help plants defend themselves against marauding insects . <p> Advertisement <p> It is part of a growing trend , known as biomimicry , in which biological methods that nature has developed over the ages are borrowed and adapted to solve modern-day problems .
