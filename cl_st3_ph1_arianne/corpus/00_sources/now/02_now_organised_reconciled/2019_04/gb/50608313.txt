<p> Police have closed in on climate change protesters at one of their four central London blockades , beginning the painstaking task of removing their symbolic pink boat from Oxford Circus . <p> Shortly after midday , more than 100 officers arrived and formed a double human ring around the vessel , pushing back dozens of protesters sitting on Oxford Street , normally thronging with traffic <p> About 20 protesters were glued and locked to the trailer on which the boat has been resting . Specialist police wearing " Protester removal team " bibs used debonding solution to neutralise the glue , then angle grinders to free the protesters one by one . <p> The protesters were fitted with protective masks and ear defenders as the work got underway .
