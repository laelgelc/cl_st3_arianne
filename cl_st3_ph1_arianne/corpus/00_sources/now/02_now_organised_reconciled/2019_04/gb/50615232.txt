In the book all the birds die because they eat the poisoned insects . It was heart-breaking and I could n't finish it . <p> In 1989 , I started researching into the impact of the clothing and textile industry socially and environmentally and discovered it was a living nightmare in all areas . In fact , buying more clothes , ethically-made , in organic cotton actually helps climate change . Organic cotton is a carbon sink and pulls carbon dioxide out of the atmosphere and keeps it on the ground . It uses less water than conventional as it 's mostly rain-fed . It is rotated with other crops and provides food security for farmers and their families , allowing them to feed , house , educate their children , and afford health care . It makes agriculture viable and thereby cuts immigration to the cities and further north . It benefits the biosphere and cuts desertification and contamination of the water supply , and sea , with chemical pesticides , fertilisers and herbicides . <p> Crucially , we need to start lobbying for legislation that only allows goods into our economic blocks that are made to the same standards , ethically and environmentally outside as inside .
