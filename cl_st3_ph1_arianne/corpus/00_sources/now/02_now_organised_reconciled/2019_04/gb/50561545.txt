<h> How climate change activists upstaged Brexit with buttocks <p> Visitors willing to attend parliamentary proceedings from the House of Commons ' public gallery have to undergo " airport-style " security to make sure they do n't carry any forbidden or restricted item . Said items include any kind of weapon , screwdrivers , padlocks , climbing gear , whistles , placards and anything featuring political or offensive slogans . Superglue and animal masks are not restricted ; nor are , understandably , buttocks . <p> Those security loopholes collided with Brexit 's unending , unfunny farce on Monday afternoon , when a dozen activists from environmentalist collective Extinction Rebellion stripped to their thongs in the public gallery , and proceeded to superglue their hands to its windows . Some wore elephant masks and grey body paint . Their torsos had been inscribed with warnings about climate change and an imminent ecological collapse ; their rears , tightly pressed against the windows , inevitably caught the attention of MPs below , busy debating four alternative Brexit plans -- none of which would go on to muster a majority .
