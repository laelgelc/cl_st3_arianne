<h> Generation Z : beyond social <p> Marketers wanting to to get through to Generation Z may like to start with the idea that they 're actually less different to other generations than one might imagine . <p> They generally do n't see themselves as trendsetters , they value spending time with friends and family , want a job they enjoy and want to plan for the future . And , they definitely are not obsessed with social media -- they do n't care too much how many ' likes ' they have , nor do they bother a great deal about owning the latest brands and products . <p> These are some of the findings from a new study by the Institute of Practitioners in Advertising , Generation Z : Exile on Mainstream , that looks to uncover the motivations and values of this generation , defined here as those aged between 16 and 23 years old . <p> Researchers quizzed 1,000 young people across the UK about how much mainstream cultures and trends matter to them , and about what they want from the world , with the aim of coming up with insights that brands should @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of consumers . <p> They found that Gen Z places a big priority on mental health and well-being -- an average priority scored 100 , and those questioned scored wellbeing and mental health as 168 ; being with friends and family scored 171 , and having an enjoyable job , 149 . <p> Having lots of ' likes ' on social media scored just 8 , and being up to date with the latest trends , 15 . <p> Gen Z do n't generally define themselves as one personality , with only 4% saying they like to follow the crowd ; instead they see themselves as having multiple identities according to their interests , such as foodie , feminist , bookworm , etc . <p> They want stability in their future with saving for the future rated 130 and ' securing a job for life ' , 104 . A third like to plan ahead rather than live day-to-day ( 12% ) . Starting a family is important for 38% , compared to 32% who prefer to party . <p> And , like just about all of us , they @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a big range of choice makes decisions harder , and 69% considering having a product tailored to them in the future . <p> So , brands that offer both convenience and bespoke content appeal most to Gen Z , including Netflix , Spotify , ASOS and Monzo . <p> The Generation also likes to see itself as empathetic and principled , with 64% claiming to be activists in some way .
