The Guidelines also set out an assessment list which is intended to help verify the application of each of the 7 key requirements : <p> Robustness and safety : Trustworthy AI requires algorithms to be secure , reliable and robust enough to deal with errors or inconsistencies during all life cycle phases of AI systems . <p> Privacy and data governance : Citizens @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ data concerning them will not be used to harm or discriminate against them . <p> Transparency : The traceability of AI systems should be ensured . <p> Diversity , non-discrimination and fairness : AI systems should consider the whole range of human abilities , skills and requirements , and ensure accessibility . <p> Societal and environmental well-being : AI systems should be used to enhance positive social change and enhance sustainability and ecological responsibility . <p> Accountability : Mechanisms should be put in place to ensure responsibility and accountability for AI systems and their outcomes . <p> Large-scale pilot with partners <p> In summer 2019 , the Commission will be launching a pilot phase involving a wide range of stakeholders .
