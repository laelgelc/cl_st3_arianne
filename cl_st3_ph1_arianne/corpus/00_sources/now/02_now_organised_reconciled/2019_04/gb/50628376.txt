But there appears to be another , ever-growing , justification for childlessness -- the impact of climate change , which recent reports suggest will have a dramatic effect on the lives of children born today . <p> It was after reading one such report that Blythe Pepino , 33 , set up Birth Strike -- an activist group centred around its members , who have all vowed to abstain from procreation .
