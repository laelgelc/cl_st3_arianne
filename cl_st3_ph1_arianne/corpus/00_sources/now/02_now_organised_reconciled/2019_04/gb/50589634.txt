<h> Ornellaia 's Winemaker Opens Up <p> The man behind one of Italy 's most revered wines , Axel Heinz is a force to be reckoned with . <p> Interview by Tom HylandPosted Saturday , 13-Apr-2019 <p> Great wine , no matter where it is produced , is a blend of the finest vineyards along with a dedicated winemaking team . At Ornellaia in Bolgheri in western Tuscany , Axel Heinz has been the enologist since 2005 , maintaining the outstanding quality of this iconic red while , at the same time , refining the wine over a period of notable vintages that have continued to keep this Frescobaldi-owned estate , at the top of the Italian wine quality pyramid . Indeed , Ornellaia today is recognized as one of the world 's most accomplished red wines . <p> Recently , Wine-Searcher interviewed Heinz in Chicago , where he was premiering the 2016 vintage of Ornellaia , to better understand his work as well as the specifics of this wine and why Bordeaux varieties work so well in Bolgheri . <p> Where did you study viticulture ? I did n't have Italy on the map but I knew Italian wines , and that 's probably the secret as to why at the end of the day it worked out . <p> Ornellaia already had a half-French winemaker who had trained in Bordeaux , and the owners had been looking for someone else trained in Bordeaux , and so I decided in 2005 to join Ornellaia as a winemaker . <p> I assume you had tasted several vintages of Ornellaia prior to your start at the winery .
