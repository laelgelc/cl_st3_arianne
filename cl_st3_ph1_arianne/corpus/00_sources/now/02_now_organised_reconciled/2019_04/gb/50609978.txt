<h> Letters : Why we all , young and old , need to get behind the climate change protesters <h> LETTERS <p> Yes , I am a hypocrite whose carbon footprint is far too big . But I am ageing , and will soon be gone . The protesters are mostly young , and will inherit the ecological mess we are bequeathing them . <p> I suspect it is already too late as the planet is on a trajectory of climatic heating which is above and beyond the historic fluctuation of temperature , and is built in for decades to come . That this climate change is due to human activity , releasing gases like CO2 , is accepted by the scientific community , with only a tiny proportion of sceptics ( mostly funded via rich conservative think-tanks in the United States ) regarding climate change as unfounded . The seas have so far acted as a sink for carbon , but this has a limit if marine life is to be sustained . <p> So if you are stuck in traffic , or your flight is cancelled because of protests , do n't blame the activists -- point @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . Politicians , industrialists , the media , ultimately ourselves . <p> This is bigger than Brexit : bigger than any election : bigger than independence ( whether the UK or Scotland ) . It 's the biggest thing on the planet , because it concerns the very future of life on the planet . <p> GR Weir , <p> 17 Mill Street , Ochiltree . <p> IT is good to see the Herald is out and about this week , seeing both sides of the climate change debate . While Catriona Stewart was up in the air , dreaming of big aeroplanes landing in Glasgow ( " Rebellion has far to travel if it is to become a common cause " , The Herald , April 19 ) your Chief Reporter , David Leask , was seen down on the ground , prowling through the massed ranks of protestors and police at Edinburgh 's Extinction Rebellion demonstration ( " Climate protestors jam capital in " last chance " to save the Earth " , The Herald , April 17 ) . When middle-aged flower power and pinstripes join up @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ stirring at the grass roots . OK , 29 persons were arrested , but that was well after three hours of sit-down and an announcement that anyone not wanting to be arrested should move back on to the pavements . A further two hours elapsed while each sit-down demonstrator was moved , one at a time , to the waiting transport . No rough stuff from anyone and every police officer I spoke to emphasised the right of the public to protest peacefully . A similar attitude was evident in Trondheim , Norway , in mid-March , where 3,000 school children protested about climate change , supervised by just three police officers . <p> Also this week David Attenborough , in a BBC climate change documentary , told us that one-third of the carbon being released into the atmosphere comes from the annual destruction of the world 's natural forest cover . A global prohibition on such deforestation should surely be a top priority . Maybe aeroplanes are part of the answer -- would Ms Stewart feel more comfortable , every time she snuggles down into that aircraft seat , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to help pay for forest protection ? Then , as she flies over the coniferous forests of the Siberian tundra , or the African tropical rain forest , her flight would be directly contributing to the prevention of forest destruction on the ground below . All it requires is a small percentage addition to the ticket price , be it bucket or executive class . <p> An air ticket levy to stop natural forest destruction needs to be on the agenda of Scottish politicians as they discuss the changes which arise as Air Passenger Duty devolves into Air Departure Tax . Equally important is the need for politicians to stop wasting our money on poor-value , ineffective renewable energy projects . A classic example is the prospective river hydro schemes in Glen Etive . Two offshore wind turbines will generate more renewable energy than all seven of these industrial developments in an unspoilt area of great natural beauty . A primary lesson for politicians is to get their priorities right when tackling climate change -- a key use of our money must be to stop forest destruction across the globe @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ like Glen Etive , where the primary objective is not climate change mitigation but lining the pockets of big landowners and developers , is a scandal . <p> We are retired GPs and members of Medact ( www.medact.org ) and we were arrested at the Extinction Rebellion event in Edinburgh on Tuesday . Despite the friendly and professional attitude of the police officers , it was no fun being processed through the prison system . It will be a lot less fun being alive when climate change takes even more devastating effect , which it will do very soon unless governments begin to take it seriously . <p> It was a privilege to stand alongside so many committed young people demanding governments tell the truth about the effects of climate change and take radical action to reduce emissions . Australia , Canada and the United States among other nations have stringent conditions about the entry of people with criminal convictions .
