<p> Christiana Figueres last night received the prestigious Edinburgh Medal at the Signet Library in Edinburgh with a rousing speech that urged " stubborn optimism " in the face of human-made global warming . <p> On the eve of the start of the Edinburgh Science Festival , Figueres praised Scots for being " not dour , but do-ers " and encouraged a rapid transition by people and the economy off fossil fuels . <p> The former Executive Secretary UN Convention on Climate Change ( 2010-2016 ) , and Founding Partner at Global Optimism , Christiana Figueres joins a long line of Edinburgh Medal winners judged to have made a significant contribution to the understanding and well-being of humanity . <p> Accepting the award , Figueres said : " It is a true honour to join a long list of illustrious recipients of the Edinburgh Medal , in our collective quest to advance science as the basis of policy and innovation . " <p> She urged people not to be overwhelmed by the scale of the challenge that the transition entails . <p> " No challenge has ever been won with pessimism .
