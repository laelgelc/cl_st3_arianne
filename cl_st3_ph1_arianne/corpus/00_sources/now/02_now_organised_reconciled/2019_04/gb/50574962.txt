<p> London 's tough new Ultra Low Emissions Zone ( ULEZ ) is the latest - and one of the strongest - steps yet taken to limit emissions of pollutants from cars , vans and trucks . So how does it compare with measures taken in other cities in Europe and the rest of the world ? <h> CAZ ? LEZ ? ULEZ ? What do they all mean ? <p> These are all different ways of describing geographical areas of cities or towns that have placed restrictions on the use of vehicles , with the goal of tackling air pollution . A CAZ is a clean air zone , a LEZ is a low emissions zone . Now we have London with the world 's first ULEZ , or ultra low emissions zone . <p> Image copyrightGetty Images <p> In the UK , CAZs are areas that can decide to use charging as a means of cleaning the air , but do n't have to .
