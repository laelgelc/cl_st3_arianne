<h> RBS and Landsec commit to green transport as part of EV100 <p> The Royal Bank of Scotland ( RBS ) and Landsec have signed up to the Climate Group 's EV100 initiative , a global drive to encourage big business to take up electric transport . <p> As a new member , RBS announced it will switch 300 of its vehicles to electric and upscaling its EV charging facilities at its buildings . Currently , the bank only has 12 charge points around the UK but they say they will build 450 by 2025 and 600 by 2030 . <p> Landsec , one of the largest commercial property development and investment companies in the UK , will increase the number of EV charge points across its portfolio to over 300 by the end of 2019 . <p> Both companies also signed up to the Climate Group 's energy productivity ( EP100 ) , and renewable power ( RE100 ) initiatives . <p> Landsec already consumes over 95% of its electricity from renewable sources and RBS almost 75% of the way to its 2025 commitment . The bank has already achieved 100% renewable electricity in the UK and Ireland @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Caroline Hill , Head of Sustainability and Public Affairs , Landsec , said : ' Landsec is pleased to announce our ambitions to drive up electric vehicle charging infrastructure in support of our commitment to acting on climate change . <p> ' We understand that more and more of our customers are opting to drive electric vehicles and we want to ensure they have the opportunity to charge when visiting a Landsec asset . <p> ' We are also delighted to be named as one of the first companies to join all three of The Climate Group 's business initiatives , following our previous commitments to procuring 100% renewable power and doubling our energy productivity . ' <p> In February , the Climate Group released their first report on EV100 , which said its 23 members who have signed up to EV100 are switching 145,000 vehicles to electric in 66 markets worldwide by 2030 , saving over 6.6 million metric tons CO2e -- equivalent to the carbon footprint of 1.9 million UK households .
