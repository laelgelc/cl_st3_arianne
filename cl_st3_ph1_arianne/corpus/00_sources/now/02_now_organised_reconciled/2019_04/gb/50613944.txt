<p> The protesters set up a camp on Waterloo Bridge and blocked other areas such as Parliament Square , Marble Arch , Oxford Circus and Piccadilly Circus . <p> They are demanding action to halt climate change by calling on the government to " tell the truth about climate change " ; to reduce carbon emissions to zero by 2025 ; and to create a citizens ' assembly to oversee progress . <p> In total 831 arrests were made by police since the protest began on April 15 . <p> The Metropolitan Police said in a statement : " A total of 40 people have been charged in connection with ongoing Extinction Rebellion protests . Charges have been made for various offences including breach of Section 14 Notice of the Public Order Act 1986 , obstructing a highway and obstructing police . "
