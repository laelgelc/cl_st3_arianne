<h> West Dorset joins the fight against climate change in London protests <h> Members of Extinction Rebellion Bridport protest against climate change in London <p> A HUNDRED people from Bridport have joined thousands of environmentalists demonstrating in the capital for immediate action on climate change . <p> Members of Extinction Rebellion Bridport have been taking part in peaceful protests in London , which have gained global attention . <p> The action , entering a second week , has seen Waterloo Bridge and Oxford Circus blocked and a ' die in ' at the Natural History Museum . <p> The campaigners are urging the Government to declare a climate emergency and ' listen to science ' by acting now to reduce greenhouse gas emissions to net zero by 2025 . <p> They 're also calling for the creation of a Citizens ' Assembly on climate , bringing people together to learn , deliberate and make recommendations about the issue . <p> Bridport protester Sara Hudson said efforts have been focussed and peaceful . <p> " It 's been a huge rollercoaster of emotions , " she said .
