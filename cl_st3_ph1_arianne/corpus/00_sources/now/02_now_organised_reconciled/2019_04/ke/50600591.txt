<p> On Tuesday , Kenya Meteorological Department acting deputy director Bernard Chanzu uttered the grim words that every well-meaning Kenyan hoped never to hear : The rains have failed . <p> The area of low pressure -- the Intertropical Convergence Zone -- which sucks in rain-bearing air from the oceans , is not moving between the tropics as it should . It is stuck over the southern reaches and it is highly unlikely that it will bring us rain . <p> Mr Chanzu added another telling gem : The pattern of extreme weather is symptomatic of climate change . <p> The long rains of March to May give Kenya 70 per cent of its rainfall .
