<h> UK , Ireland and Kenya join new Coalition of Finance Ministers for Climate Action <p> Finance ministers from 20 countries , including the UK , Ireland and Kenya have come together to launch the new Coalition of Finance Ministers for Climate Action . <p> The coalition , which is aiming to direct additional revenue opportunities to tackle climate change , has endorsed six common principles , known as the " Helsinki Principles , " to promote new fiscal policies on climate adaptation and mitigation . It will assist countries in mobilising the required funds to implement and reach national climate ambitions , setting out strategies for green investment and factor climate risks and vulnerabilities into economic planning . <p> " Finance ministries have a crucial role to play in accelerating the global shift to a low-carbon , climate-resilient growth model , " World Bank 's chief executive , Kristalina Georgieva , said . <p> " This Coalition demonstrates new levels of ambition from decision-makers in the fiscal policy arena and provides an important platform for Finance Ministers to share best practice on the jobs and growth benefits of the new climate economy .
