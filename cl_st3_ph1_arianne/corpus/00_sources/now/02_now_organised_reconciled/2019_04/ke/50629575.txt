<p> Kenyans seem to have engaged the reverse gear in environmental conservation . Decades ago , April , indeed the onset of the long rains season , was dedicated to tree planting . <p> The founding president , Mzee Jomo Kenyata , used to lead the nation in tree planting . He introduced a national tree planting day . Daniel arap Moi followed Mzee 's nyayo of planting trees and went further by encouraging the construction of gabions on slopy areas to save the soil from erosion . <p> There was a determined effort to protect and conserve the environment . Over time , the public understood the need to treat national resources as their own to conserve and protect . Sadly , this is no longer the case . <p> Kenya has a forest cover of 7.5 per cent , below the 10 per cent target . Not so long ago , the national leadership and other stakeholders launched a campaign to plant an additional 1.8 billion trees . <p> The message that forest cover must be increased to 10 per cent must reach everyone as deforestation has certainly accelerated climate change . It is important that their standard of living is improved for them not to engage in environmentally destructive activities . <p> We still have a chance to reverse our deforestation course . Recommitment to the national tree planting across the country is a starting point .
