<p> Several African countries are on the journey towards establishing their first nuclear plant . <p> Kenya is among these countries and has purposed that it is necessary to diversify the country 's energy mix to improve its electricity generation capacity . However , a lot of questions still linger in the public minds as to whether the country is ready for a nuclear power generating plant . <p> The existing mindset has been constructed negatively as a result of the nuclear disasters that have occurred in the past and recently . People ask of what use is there to build a nuclear power plant when the country has substantial sources to exploit energy -- wind , geothermal , solar and natural gas etc . <p> The public needs to understand that with the renewables , their energy supply is intermittent and lacks the required baseload to provide electricity supply throughout . The population , as it is , is n't going to be constant . In the next five years or so , Kenya 's population is estimated to be more than 50 million , which applies to the rest of Africa .
