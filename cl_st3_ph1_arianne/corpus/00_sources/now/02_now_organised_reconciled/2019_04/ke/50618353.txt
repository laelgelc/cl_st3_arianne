<h> Maasai community call to restore lost icecap of Mt . Kilimanjaro <p> More than 1000 people , drawn mainly from the Maasai community , will gather in Amboseli , at the foot of Mt . Kilimanjaro on Wednesday to start a journey of ecosystem regeneration on the back of recurrent droughts that has left Africa 's highest mountain without its icecap . <p> The large gathering follows a call by the Maasai community for a major collaborative action , to reverse the destructive spiral of land degradation and climate change in a community driven regeneration of landscape and livelihood . <p> The Maasai country suffers more frequent and longer periods of drought affecting human , plant and animal life alike .
