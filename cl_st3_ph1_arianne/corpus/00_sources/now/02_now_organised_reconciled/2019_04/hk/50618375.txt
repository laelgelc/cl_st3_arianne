The activities coincided with Extinction Rebellion events around the world , most notably ongoing activities in London , England . <p> At the event on Saturday , Loy and five others sat in a roadway blocking traffic until police removed them . Lisa Widdekind , who sat with Loy , commented that , " Speaking for myself , I 'd say there 's a lot of relief over the outcome mixed with joy that we accomplished what we set out to and hopefully added our voices to the outcry for the transformation that needs to happen .
