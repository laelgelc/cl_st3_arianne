" The irony , as the author of the books upon which the show is based said last week , is that " winter " -- the impending destruction of all human life by an army of the dead -- is a metaphor not for petty political power games but for the existential threat of climate change . <p> In five months ' time , the 194 nations that signed up to the 2015 Paris Agreement will gather in New York to reaffirm their commitment to restrict the global temperature increase this century to between 1.5 and 2 degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels .
