The investors will and it should be that way , " says New York-based Michael Baldinger , head of sustainable and impact investing at UBS Asset Management . <p> Baldinger also stresses that no matter how much regulation is pumped into forcing companies to take action on achieving sustainability targets , the market itself will regulate who survives and who fails . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ promote corporate sustainability , I strongly believe in that , " says Baldinger . <p> The finance sector is certainly on the cusp of real change as the momentum to act over global warming gathers pace .
