<h> Girls ' school in Abia generates electricity from wastes <p> Students of Mercy Girls High School , Mbawsi , in Isiala Ngwa North Local Government Area of Abia have converted animal , human and kitchen wastes into bio-gas meant for cooking and generation of electricity . <p> Rev.
