Hundreds of thousands of people could face hunger in the months ahead , and a new storm bringing fresh flooding will further complicate efforts to recover . <p> Cyclone Kenneth could bring heavy rains and flooding to northeastern Mozambique , which was not hit by the earlier storm , and southern Tanzania , which told coastal residents to flee . The Pacific Disaster Center has forecast that landfall will occur north of the Mozambican city of Pemba . <p> The new cyclone threatens an area of Mozambique where residents are not used to such strong storms , the United Nations humanitarian agency said .
