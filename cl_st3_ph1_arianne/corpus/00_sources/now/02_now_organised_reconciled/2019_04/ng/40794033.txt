Lekwa Ezutah gave the charge to the new executives last week in Lagos during the investiture ceremony of the 16th Lagos State Chapter Chairman , Tpl . Adebisi Adedire . <p> The event , which held at Academy Inn , Ikeja , Lagos , had in attendance some notable industrialists and other professional bodies . <p> In his speech , Ezutah , who was represented by Tpl .
