<h> Gas flaring persists as 2020 deadline nears <p> After several unmet deadlines for ending gas flaring , oil and gas firms still continue to waste natural gas resources in the country as the 2020 deadline nears , ' FEMI ASU writes <p> A significant amount of natural gas produced in the country is still being wasted despite many deadlines set by the government to end it . <p> Nigeria has Africa 's largest gas reserves of more than 190 trillion cubic feet but shortages of the commodity has continued to persist in the country over the years . <p> The government has said that gas flaring , the burning of gas that is the byproduct of oil production , costs it roughly $1bn a year in lost revenue . The gas can be used in power plants , in industry or exported . <p> Oil and gas firms operating in the country flared a total of 282.08 billion standard cubic feet of natural gas in 2018 , compared to
