<h> Cooperation : President Buhari meets Crown Prince of Abu-Dhabi <h> Cooperation : President Buhari meets Crown Prince of Abu-Dhabi <p> Nigeria and United Arab Emirates ( UAE ) on Tuesday reaffirmed the need to work together to further consolidate on the strong relationship and mutual respect between the countries . <p> Mr Femi Adesina , the Special Adviser to the President on Media and Publicity made this known in a statement in Abuja on Tuesday . <p> The presidential aide said President Muhammadu Buhari and His Highness Sheikh Mohammed bin Zayed Al Nahyan , Crown Prince of Abu Dhabi reached this agreement when they met in Abu Dhabi , UAE , on Tuesday . <p> He stated that the two leaders discussed areas of enhancing and expanding mutual cooperations between Nigeria and the UAE . <p> While acknowledging that both countries enjoyed significant trade , the leaders agreed that more could be done . <p> Adesina revealed that the Nigerian leader and the Crown Prince , who is also the Deputy Supreme Commander of UAE Forces , also discussed education , healthcare , agriculture , climate change and population growth . <p> " As both countries have a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ exchanged ideas on creating an enabling environment for future generations . <p> Furthermore , the leaders exchanged views on diversification of their economies from oil as well as the security challenges confronting both Africa and the Middle East , " Adesina added . <p> The Crown Prince congratulated the President on his re-election and prayed for a successful second tenure . <p> He also expressed the commitment of UAE to increase their investments in Nigeria .
