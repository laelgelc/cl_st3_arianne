<h> CSOs Push for Safe , Beneficial Regional Mining Terms <p> Chineme Okafor in Abuja Civil Society Organisations ( CSOs ) in West African countries have said they want the harmonised model mining law enunciated by the region 's economic platform -- the Economic Communities of West African States ( ECOWAS ) , to reflect more on safe mining of minerals , equal opportunities for indigenous operators as well as respect and protection of people and environment . <p> Working on the platform of the West African Civil Society Forum ( WACSOF ) , the CSOs also indicated they would want the Act to take into consideration the region 's challenges with climate change , as well as the place of women in the mining sector . <p> Speaking to journalists in Abuja on the sidelines of the WASCOF 's review of the ECOWAS Mining and Mineral Development Act ( EMMDA ) , the Secretary General of WASCOF , Mr. Komlan Messie , explained that unfair mining frameworks have kept the region poor despite its enormous mineral deposits . <p> Messie , noted that often , big external mining firms were favoured more than indigenous firms , adding that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to be emphasised in the EMMDA . <p> " West Africa has one of the best reserves of natural resources in the world but citizens have not seen real economic impacts of those mining resources in our countries .
