<h> FCT Minister Cautions Residents Against Bush Burning <p> In a bid to ensure that incessant fire outbreaks which has become the order of the day in the country is halted , the minister of the Federal Capital Territory ( FCT ) , Mallam Muhammad Musa Bello , has cautioned residents of the territory against indiscriminate bush burning . <p> Bello gave the warning at the flag off campaign on indiscriminate bush burning , organisedby theFCT FireService at the Etsu Garki Palace in Abuja yesterday . <p> The minister who was represented by the coordinator , Abuja Metropolitan Management Council ( AMMC ) , Umar Shuaibu , appealed to the royal father to take the warning to his subjects as people , especially farmers are set to embark on another farming season . <p> According to him , " The acts , not only have an effect on our property but also the ecosystem as it gradually build into the atmosphere thus leading to climate change . We are saying that activities of bush burning are wrong . " <p> He said , " It is not even allowed . If we must have a bush burning practice , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ sure that the area is localised . " <p> The director of the FCT Fire Service , Assistant Controller General ( ACG ) , Julius Opetunsin while urging farming communities , traditional rulers and their subjects to desist from indiscriminate bush burning , said the reason for the sensitisation is because fire outbreaks is still one of the leading disasters in the territory . <p> He said , " In case you want to adopt bush burning approach as an alternative , let it be controlled burning so as to protect your property and that of your neighbour . " <p> The Etsu Garki , Dr Usman Nga-Kupi who was represented by a retired deputy director , FCT Education Board , Jacob S. Garki , commended the Fire Service for the sensitisation and urged the service to create more sensitisation programmes in indigenous languages for the people .
