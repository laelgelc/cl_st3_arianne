<h> Climate change : Finance ministers express concern , form coalition <p> Finance ministers from 20 countries including Nigeria , have come together to drive a stronger collective action on climate change and its impacts on the environment . <p> The new group , known as Coalition of Finance Ministers for Climate Action , was formed on Saturday at the Spring Meetings of the World Bank Group and International Monetary Fund in Washington DC , United States . <p> The coalition endorsed a set of six common principles , known as the ' Helsinki Principles ' , to promote national climate action , especially through fiscal policy and the use of public finance . <p> According to a statement by the World Bank Group , the Helsinki Principles , so-called , as they were conceived by a group of governments represented at a meeting led by Finland and Chile in Helsinki in February , are designed to support finance ministers to share best practices and experiences on macro , fiscal , and public financial management policies for low-carbon and climate-resilient growth . <p> The coalition is expected to help countries mobilise and align the finance needed to implement their @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ climate budgeting and strategies for green investment and procurement ; and factor climate risks and vulnerabilities into members ' economic planning . <p> The Chief Executive Officer of World Bank , Kristalina Georgieva , said finance ministries had a crucial role to play in accelerating the global shift to a low-carbon , climate-resilient growth model . <p> " This Coalition demonstrates new levels of ambition from decision makers in the fiscal policy arena and provides an important platform for Finance Ministers to share best practices on the job and growth benefits of the new climate economy , " Geogieva said . <p> The Minister of Finance , Chile , Felipe Bascu ? ? n , said , " Climate change is a real threat nowadays but we can turn it into an opportunity .
