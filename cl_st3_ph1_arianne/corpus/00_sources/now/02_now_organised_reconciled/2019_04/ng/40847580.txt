<h> Metrology Key to Economic Devt , Says SON DG <p> James Emejo in Abuja The Director General/Chief Executive , Standards Organisation of Nigeria ( SON ) , Mr. Osita Aboloma , has said metrology otherwise known as the science of measurement had become critical for meeting the country 's development aspirations , particularly the acceptance of its exports by the international markets . <p> He said measurement plays a key role for determining the success of manufactured products in terms of precision and how well they are made . <p> Speaking at the opening of a workshop for North-west on the importance of metrology for quality assurance of products and services and industrial development , he said the SON Act covers all aspects of metrology to ensure the protection of business , safety , wealth as well as other aspects of Nigerian lives . <p> He said the SON Act No. 14 of 2015 and the Weights and Measures Act on Legal Metrology remained the applicable metrology laws in the country . <p> Represented at the occasion by the FCT Coordinator , SON , Mr. Gambo Dimka , Aboloma , among other things , said human health and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ while protection of the environment and large scale studies related to global climate change depend critically on accurate measurements often extending over long periods time . <p> He said : " All these call for measurements of the highest accuracy to reduce to minimal , the cost and issue of rejection of any product from the country .
