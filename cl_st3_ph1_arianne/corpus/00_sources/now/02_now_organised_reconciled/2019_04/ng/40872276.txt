<h> NGO urges Buhari to implement climate oriented projects , programmes <p> Abuja -- The Global Initiative for Food Security and Ecosystem Preservation ( GIFSEP ) , an NGO has urged reelected President Muhammadu Buhari to intensify the implementation of climate oriented projects and programmes to protect the planet . <p> The Executive Director of GIFSEP , Mr David Michael , gave the advice in Abuja on Tuesday against the backdrop of the 2019 World Earth Day . <p> Michael told the News Agency of Nigeria ( NAN ) that the Federal Government should intensify its sensitisation programme on the need to educate Nigerians to protect the planet . <p> " Issues of climate change affect our health and the food we are eating as well as our existence as human beings . <p> " If you waste and throw away food , you are contributing to the climate change and if you are wasting water endlessly , you are contributing to climate change because all these have negative effects on the climate , ' ' he said . <p> NAN reports that the World Earth Day is celebrated every April 22 worldwide in support of the environment and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the planet .
