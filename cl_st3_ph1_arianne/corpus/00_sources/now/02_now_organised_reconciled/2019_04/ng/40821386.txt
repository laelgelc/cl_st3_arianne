<h> Kano Effluents : NESREA Threatens To Seal Defaulting Industries <p> The National Environmental Standards and Regulations Enforcement Agency ( NESREA ) has threatened to seal off industries and firms responsible for industrial effluents in Kano State if they failed to abide by environmental rules and regulations . <p> Industrial effluents in Kano State is threatening the lives of residents of Sabuwar Gandu , Maidile , Sharada , Challawa and other communities along River Wudil who are currently taunted by water borne diseases and decreased number of fishes from the river . <p> Director- general of NESREA , Prof Aliyu Jauro , stated this in Abuja yesterday while addressing newsmen on the amendment of the agency 's Act . <p> He said though the agency has been carrying out enforcement but were handicapped before the amendment of its Act , saying that the industries must abide by environmental laws , as it wo n't be business as usual . <p> Jauro noted that the new act authorised the agency to seal and close down premises or facilities whose pollution to the environment threatens lives and properties in collaboration with security agencies before obtaining court warrant . <p> He maintained @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ impeded the smooth running of the agency , saying that NESREA was now positioned to carry out compliance , monitoring and enforcement in the industries as well as built environment . <p> The DG enjoined members of the public to familiarise with environmental laws , regulations , standards , guidelines , policies and the provisions of international environmental agreements to which Nigeria is a signatory as well as facilitate environmentally-sound disposal of solid and liquid waste . <p> Jauro urged developers to obtain environmental impact statement/ certificate from the federal ministry of environment before mobilisation to sites likewise to observe the proper use of environmental sound technologies to mitigate the effects of climate change . <p> He however called on operators of industrial facilities and the business community to carry out their activities within the scope of environmental laws of the country , as the agency has set up mechanisms to deal with defaulters.
