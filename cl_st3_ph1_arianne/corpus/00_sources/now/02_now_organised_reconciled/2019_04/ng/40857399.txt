However , the overall picture is one of the international and African seed companies falling short in delivering quality seed and new varieties to smallholder farmers . This limits the potential to address food security , nutrition and climate resilience , according to a new study by the Amsterdam-based Access to Seeds Foundation . <p> Photo credit : Syngenta <p> While there is a growing number of seed companies active in the region , both homegrown and international , less than half of the 23 companies researched conduct plant breeding in Western and Central Africa . This limits the release of new varieties adapted to the region and explains the high number of varieties that are older than five years offered in company portfolios . <p> The Access to Seeds Index 2019 -- Western and Central Africa ranks Value Seeds number one .
