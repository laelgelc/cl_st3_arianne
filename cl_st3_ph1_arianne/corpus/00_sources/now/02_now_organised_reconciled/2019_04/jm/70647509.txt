However , it takes years to decompose and with increasing climate change consequences , public and private sector interests have been encouraging Jamaicans to join the fight to reduce our carbon footprint . <p> One St Catherine man , Godfrey Bromwell heeded the call to protect the environment and has also been able to create an additional stream of income , turning what other people consider trash into cash . <p> The 68-year-old golf course caddy can be seen on a regular basis , scouring the lawns of the Caymanas Golf Club in St Catherine in the hope of finding bottles to reap rewards .
