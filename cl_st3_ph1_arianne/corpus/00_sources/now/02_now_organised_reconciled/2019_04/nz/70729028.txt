<p> Some orchards in Kerikeri have been badly affected by the new fungus , and at least one was unable to send any fruit to market this year . <p> Feijoa Growers Association president Roger Matthews said the arrival of the new strain was a concern , and it was difficult to predict if the fungus would spread . <p> " Growers further south are thinking that Kerikeri does have some warm , semi-tropical rainfall and humid conditions , and that might be what the fungus needs , and further south we might be protected from it , " he said . <p> " However facing the prospect of climate change , all bets are off . " <p> He said central to efforts to stop the spread of the fungus was to practice good orchard hygiene . <p> " This should be being done already , but any equipment or footwear going out of the orchard should be cleaned , clothing should be washed . <p> " If you have an infected orchard , you should n't be taking anything off the orchard in the way of fruit or plant material that could carry spores , so if @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ had to do - those will be destroyed .
