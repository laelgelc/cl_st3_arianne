<h> Bay of Plenty Regional Council welcomes report findings <p> Bay of Plenty Regional Council has today welcomed the release of Environment Aotearoa 2019 by the Ministry for the Environment . <p> The report highlights the most pressing environmental issues New Zealand is facing and outlines nine priority issues in need of attention . <p> These issues are : <p> Our native plants , animals and ecosystems are under threat . <p> Changes to the vegetation on our land are degrading soil and water . <p> Urban growth is reducing versatile land and native biodiversity . <p> Our waterways are polluted in farming areas . <p> Our environment is polluted in urban areas . <p> Taking water changes flows which affects our freshwater ecosystems . <p> The way we fish is affecting the health of our ocean environment . <p> New Zealand has high greenhouse gas emissions per person . <p> Climate change is already affecting Aotearoa New Zealand . <p> Regional Council 's Integrated Catchments General Manager Chris Ingle said all of these issues are already recognised by Council and many are already in our current work plan .
