<p> An increasing body of evidence suggests that a closer connection to nature is good for our heads , our hearts and our lungs . But as Auckland grows , our precious green spaces and trees are increasingly under threat . In the first part of a three-part series for OurAuckland , Elly Strang looks at the many benefits green spaces bring to the health of our people and our city . <p> Humans have long believed that being in or around nature has healing properties . But the idea was previously more intuitive than evidential -- a warm glow rather than hard science .
