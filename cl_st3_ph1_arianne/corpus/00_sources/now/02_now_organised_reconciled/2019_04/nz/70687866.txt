It will take effect on July 1 , even though the state coffers already take over $1 off every litre of petrol sold in New Zealand , when you factor in GST . <p> Then there 's the 10 cent per litre Auckland regional fuel levy , which has been spread across the country 's bowsers . I was road-tripping in the North Island last week , fuelling up in Paihia where the pump price was a startlingly low $2.03 . A day later , back home in Christchurch , the standard price per litre was $2.31 .
