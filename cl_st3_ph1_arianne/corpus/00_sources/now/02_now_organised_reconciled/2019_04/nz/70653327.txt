<h> Empowering family farmers is key <p> Empowering family farmers is key to achieving Zero Hunger in Southeast Asia <p> 04/04/2019 , Jakarta , Indonesia -- Innovation , access to rural credit -- particularly for women -- and improvements to rural social protection programmes are important tools to help family farmers in Southeast Asia improve their livelihoods and become more food secure , the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations ( FAO ) said today . <p> Representatives from countries of Southeast Asia are meeting in Jakarta to discuss ways to help smallholder farmers achieve better food security and improved livelihoods in order to achieve the Sustainable Development Goal of Zero Hunger ( SDG-2 ) before the 2030 deadline . The meeting is convened by the Government of Indonesia . <p> Family farms produce the vast majority of the food that makes it to our dinner tables . They produce 80 percent of the world 's food and are the biggest sources of employment globally , yet many family farms are themselves food insecure and extremely poor . <p> In Southeast Asia , a vast majority of farmland is owned by smallholder farmers representing less than five @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with a sizable majority of farms occupying less than one hectare of land . By extension , family farming also relates to fisher folks , herders and people relying on forests for their food and livelihoods . <p> The rural poor , especially family farmers , face considerable difficulties in accessing credit , services , technologies and markets which would allow them to improve the productivity of their natural resources and labour . Rural women are disproportionately affected by these drawbacks . <p> Most available jobs in agriculture are associated with low and unstable incomes , poor safety and health conditions , gender inequality in pay and opportunities , and limited social protection . It is based on local knowledge . It sustains productivity on , what is often , marginal land , and provides local consumers with fresh food , including poultry , livestock , fish , fruits and vegetables , along with other staple foods , which are critical to achieving good nutrition . <p> Recognizing the central roles of family farming the United Nations General Assembly has officially declared 2019 -- 2028 as the Decade of Family Farming .
