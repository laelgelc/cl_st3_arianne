<h> Warmer water drives King Salmon 's profit forecast down <p> Another warmer than usual summer has increased the mortality of New Zealand King Salmon 's fish . <p> As a consequence , the Marlborough-based fish company expected underlying profit for the year ending in June to be at the lower end of a previous guidance of between $25 and $28.5 million . <p> Last year 's underlying profit was $26.2m and net profit was $16.1m . <p> In a statement , the company said warm water temperatures had continued into April , with the full year fish harvest to be in the order 7,900 tonnes , down 9 percent from the previous 8,700-tonne estimate . <p> " Although fish performance has not met our expectations during 2019 , the company continues to meaningfully grow value within the available supply of salmon , " chair John Ryder said . <p> Managing director and chief executive Grant Rosewarne said the company was working with the government to mitigate the the effects of climate change , such as a proposal to shift existing salmon space to cooler deeper waters and enabling open ocean farming . <p> " We believe local councils @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ positively to such leadership , " he said . <p> Newsroom is powered by the generosity of readers like you , who support our mission to produce fearless , independent and provocative journalism . <h> Comments <p> Newsroom does not allow comments directly on this website .
