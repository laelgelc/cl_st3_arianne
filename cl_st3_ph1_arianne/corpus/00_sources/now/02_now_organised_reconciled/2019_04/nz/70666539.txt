<h> Calls for special fund for likely costs of climate change <p> A special fund should be set up to help pay for future costs of climate change , a conference in Palmerston North has been told . <p> File photo Photo : RNZ / Supplied <p> It would pay for resettling people from vulnerable coastal communities , and help pay for threatened seaside infrastructures , like roads , railways and ports . <p> The comments came from a Wellington academic Judy Lawrence who 's a senior research fellow at Victoria University 's Climate Change Research . <p> She was speaking at a conference of the New Zealand Agricultural Greenhouse Gas Research Centre . <p> She said the proposal for a climate change fund would be similar to the Earthquake Commission , which was set up in 1945 to pay for earthquakes , and the NZ Super Fund , which was set up in 2001 , to pay for the future costs of pensions . <p> She said a climate change fund would have to be paid for from contributions over several generations because the cost would be huge . <p> " We are talking billions of dollars , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Zealand have done a recent assessment of the costs and they are talking of $14 billion . <p> " We think that is an underestimate of the real cost because there are a whole lot of things that are not included in that .
