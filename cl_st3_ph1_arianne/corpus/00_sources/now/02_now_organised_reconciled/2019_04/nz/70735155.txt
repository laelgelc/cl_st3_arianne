<h> Native tree deaths in Nelson Marlborough <p> Native tree deaths in Nelson Marlborough thought to be caused by recent drought <p> The Department of Conservation says that the high number of dead native trees being seen across the top of the South Island is likely caused by drought stress . <p> DOC botanist , Shannel Courtney , says root stress from a very wet winter and spring in 2018 had already weakened the trees and the extremely dry conditions are proving fatal . <p> " It is a much worse event than the previous big drought in 2001 . As we are likely to have more droughts due to climate change , we can expect to see vegetation dieback like this happening more often . " <p> Nelson helicopter pilot Matt Gibb has commented to DOC staff that it is the first time he has seen this many dead native trees in 25 years of flying around Nelson . <p> " The ridges especially are speckled with dead trees , including in the Wairoa Valley and the Wangapeka area of Kahurangi National Park .
