<h> London climate-change protesters to disrupt Heathrow Airport <p> Environmental protesters who have blocked London roads and bridges for more than three days said Thursday they plan to take their civil disobedience campaign to Heathrow , Europe 's busiest airport , at the start of the Easter holiday weekend . <p> The Extinction Rebellion group said it would mount a protest at the airport on Friday . The group vowed to escalate its campaign of disruption if the British government does n't step up action against climate change . <p> London 's Metropolitan Police force urged the group to reconsider , saying the airport action could " cause further disruption and misery to thousands of travelers , many of them families , over Easter . " <p> " Protesters can expect a robust police response , " said Assistant Commissioner Nick Ephgrave . " We are determined to keep the airport operating . " <p> Heathrow said it was " working with the authorities to address any threat of protests which could disrupt the airport .
