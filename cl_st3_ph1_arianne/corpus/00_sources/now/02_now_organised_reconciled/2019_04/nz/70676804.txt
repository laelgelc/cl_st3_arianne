Wine has become an ' asset ' in which one can invest . There is the London International Vintners Exchange , which like equity stock markets , has its own indices , amongst others the ' Liv-ex Fine Wine 50 ' which ' tracks the daily price movement of the most heavily traded commodities in the fine wine market -- the Bordeaux First Growths ' . <p> Over 1900-2012 , we estimate a real financial return to wine investment ( net of storage costs ) of 4.1% , which exceeds bonds , art , and stamps . <p> However , the same study notes that investing in equities , with a return of 5.2% did beat the investment in wine , which is consistent with what other studies found . Of course , if your equity investment fails you ca n't drink it . <p> Evaluating wine quality . <p> Wine is an experience good , you need to consume it to really know whether it 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is a market for wine expertise , with various guides and experts selling their evaluations of wine to the interested wine consumer . These ratings are important : one recent study found that receiving a positive rating increases demand for that wine by 6% immediately after the publication of the rating and that effect remains for more than 20 weeks . <p> Results from the first four years of the experiment , published in the Journal of Wine Economics , showed a typical judge 's scores varied by plus or minus four points over the three blind tastings . A wine deemed to be a good 90 would be rated as an acceptable 86 by the same judge minutes later and then an excellent 94 . <p> Moreover , what wine experts like is not necessarily what ' ordinary ' wine drinkers like .
