She wanted all references to deities of any denomination removed , due to the scientific nature of the document . <p> Some councillors agreed it may not be appropriate . <p> " I 'm incredibly conflicted , I have tried to try to champion integrating Maori language and culture into our processes , but I 'm also an atheist and will not support the introduction of religion , " said Councillor Iona Pannett . She said the environmental movement have desperately tried get humanity to acknowledge they have caused global warming .
