<p> Environment Aotearoa 2019 , a three-year stocktake of the nation 's natural assets by the Ministry for the Environment ( MfE ) and Stats NZ , is merely the latest volume of grim reading we 've come to expect about the impact of climate change and human activity : thousands of species going , going , gone ; sick rivers ; productive land and habitats gobbled up by urban sprawl ; and the increasingly calamitous onset of a warming climate . <p> " As a nation , we need to make a bold plan to protect and restore nature now , " said Forest &amp; Bird chief executive Kevin Hague in response . <p> That bold plan would need to consider practically every facet of the way we live , move , eat and work . This is far bigger than a few single-use plastic bags . <p> Up to this point , agriculture has been an easy scapegoat for the great majority of us who have long since left those small country roads to contribute to the growing urban sprawl .
