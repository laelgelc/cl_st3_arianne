<h> ' Report highlights the need for action on climate change ' <p> The release of Environment Aotearoa 2019 highlights the urgent need for more action on climate change . <p> The Energy Efficiency and Conservation Authority ( EECA ) is working across the transport , business , and residential sectors to help New Zealanders decarbonise the way we move around and how we use energy . <p> EECA 's Chief Executive , Andrew Caseley says with urgent action required on climate change , it 's important for people to have alternatives to using fossil fuels . <p> ' The energy sector is one where there are a broad range of existing and emerging options to decarbonise ' he says . <p> ' In transport , which accounts for 21% of our overall greenhouse gas emissions , about a third of people would now consider an electric vehicle ( EV ) as their next vehicle purchase , and to date there are over 13,000 EVs on the road . ' <p> Environment Aotearoa highlights the impact road transport has on poor air quality in some of our major cities .
