<h> MBIE report signals that the future could be plant-based - SAFE <p> The Ministry of Business , Innovation and Employment ( MBIE ) has released a report that signals the growing demand for more sustainable and ethical foods , but AgResearch could block further research . <p> The report , prepared by Sapere Research Group , identified trends likely to affect the future of food and farming and the implications for New Zealand 's science and innovation system . In the same report , AgResearch indicated they would block any research that could disrupt the industry . <p> SAFE Eat Kind Programme Coordinator , Krysta Neve says AgResearch should take notice of the trends and help the industry adapt to a changing world . <p> " Compassionate Kiwis are demanding products that are kinder to the earth and its inhabitants . Alternative proteins such as plant-based and cellular proteins could address some of the nation 's biggest issues . This includes climate change , animal welfare and our health , " says Neve . <p> " The report shows New Zealanders want foods that are more sustainable and ethical .
