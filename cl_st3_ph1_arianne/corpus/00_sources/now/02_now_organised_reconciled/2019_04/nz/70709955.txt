Targets have been assigned to protect and restore the estuary . <p> Water quality and prioritising sites for restoration work are just two of many targets aimed at protecting and restoring the internationally important Waimea Inlet . <p> The inlet is the largest semi-enclosed coastal estuary in the South Island . It has international and national importance as a site for migratory birds . <p> The Tasman District Council has agreed to lead targets from the establishment of a list of priority sites for restoration work on freshwater ecosystems and an initial review of water quality in contributing waterways , set down for 2019 , to a 10 per cent increase -- compared with 2018 -- of naturally vegetated dune land on Moturoa/Rabbit and Rough islands by 2030 and plenty in between . <p> There are other targets marked for the council to " support in principle " subject to future funding decisions .
