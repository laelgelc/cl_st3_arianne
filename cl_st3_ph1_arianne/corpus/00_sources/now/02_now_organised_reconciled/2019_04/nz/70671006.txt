<h> E-bike ride from Waiheke Island to Wellington in support of climate change laws <p> An e-bike ride through the North Island to support proposed new climate change laws has visited Palmerston North . <p> Members of the Carbon Neutral New Zealand Trust , based in Waiheke Island and Kerikeri , have been riding the length of the North Island , a journey of about 1200 kilometres , on e-bikes and bikes . The tour is in support of the Zero Carbon Bill awaiting reading in Parliament . <p> The group left Waiheke Island on March 23 and were in Palmerston North to give a talk at the city library on Wednesday . The group will be meeting with Green Party MP Chloe Swarbrick at Parliament next week . <p> The bill will determine how New Zealand plays its part in the global effort against climate change .
