It included a section that summed up their understanding of climate change . <p> " Through his worldwide industrial civilisation , Man is unwittingly conducting a vast geophysical experiment , " wrote longtime University of California , San Diego oceanographer Roger Revelle . " The climatic changes that may be produced , " he warned , " could be deleterious from the point of view of human beings . " <p> What Revelle suggested next kicked off a debate that continues to this day : Can humans avert the worst effects of climate change with a vast geoengineering experiment ? <p> Revelle thought so .
