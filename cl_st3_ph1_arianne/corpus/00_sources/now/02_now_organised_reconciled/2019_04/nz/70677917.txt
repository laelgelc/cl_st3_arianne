<h> Future of Christchurch 's historic Taylors Mistake baches assured <p> Coastal baches have been at Taylors Mistake since the 1890s . Now , after years of wrangling , their future has been resolved . <p> After decades of uncertainty the future of Christchurch 's historic Taylors Mistake baches has been assured after councillors approved a plan to license them for 35 years . <p> Uncertainty has long surrounded the fate of the 45 privately-owned buildings , some of which are at Boulder Bay and Hobsons Bay . <p> The baches , which are on public land , previously had council permission to occupy an unformed public road , but the last licences lapsed more than 30 years ago . Authorities have since struggled to find a long-term solution . <p> SUPPLIED/BRENT GILPIN <p> Taylors Mistake Association president Brent Gilpin , outside the bach his family have owned since 1942 , has long fought for the famous baches to be saved for future generations . <p> But city councillors on Thursday granted owners new licences allowing them to be used as holiday homes and let for up to 20 nights a year . <p> Councillor Sara Templeton , who @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ solution to the longstanding problem , said the decision was a " really big step " . <p> She said : " The last licences expired in 1986 and ... the next step is getting a couple of those licences signed - that is when I 'll be celebrating .
