<h> Fooling people who want to be fooled <p> A few years ago I was sent a YouTube video depicting a " scientific " experiment showing that " more CO2 is good for earth " . <p> The video -- titled Plant Time-lapse CO2 Concentration Global Warming Experiment -- claims to show that rising atmospheric CO2 is beneficial rather than harmful . Accompanied by reassuring music , it shows the growth of two cowpea plants from seedling to harvest , at two different CO2 concentrations : 450ppm ( parts per million , somewhat above present values ) and 1270ppm . The plant given more CO2 grows faster , and at harvest 42 days later is significantly taller and has produced more leaves and roots . <p> Now for the scientifically infantile methodology . Using only one plant in each treatment , no conclusions can be drawn because two individual plants given the same treatment would have responded slightly differently anyway . Accordingly , as every high school student knows , large numbers have to be given the same treatment and the averages calculated for each group .
