<h> Government appoints National Climate Change Risk Assessment panel <p> The government has appointed an expert panel to create the framework for New Zealand 's first National Climate Change Risk Assessment ( NCCRA ) . <p> Minister for Climate Change James Shaw welcomed the appointment by the Ministry for the Environment , adding the NCCRA will provide a national overview of the impact of climate change to New Zealand . <p> " We need to be assessing now what the future risks of climate change will be , and where and how New Zealand needs to adapt , " Shaw said . " We also need to be able to anticipate what our most challenging issues are likely to be . The NCCRA will inform and help prioritise effective adaptation action . " <p> The minister said the nine members of the NCCRA expert panel come from a range of backgrounds , including research , engineering , and the public sector . The initial step for the panel is to create a risk assessment framework by the end of June , with the first risk assessment expected to be completed by the middle of next year .
