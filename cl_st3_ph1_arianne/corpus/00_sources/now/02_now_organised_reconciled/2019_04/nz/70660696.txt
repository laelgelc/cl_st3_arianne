<h> Stop defending patch and show ambition <p> The dairy and meat sectors are still defending their patch rather than embracing changes in the way we farm and the way we eat to fight climate change , writes Rod Oram . <p> Our climate change opportunities fall broadly into two equal parts . This column explores the rural half ; next week 's the urban half ; and the following week 's the synergy between the two . <p> If we want to rise to the challenge of climate change , both halves have to transform the way they work . They must significantly improve their environmental performances , including large reductions in emissions . Then they will be at the forefront of the clean technology revolution underway in the world . <p> We 're all in this together . If those responsible for one half of our emissions act and the other half do n't , we will fail to meet our climate ambitions . We will lose what 's left of our environmental reputation and become a technology and economic laggard . <p> These issues will become very intense over the next few months .
