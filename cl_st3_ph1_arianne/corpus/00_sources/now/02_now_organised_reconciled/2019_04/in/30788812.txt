A verdant , undulating patch of God 's own country bordering Tamil Nadu and Karnataka , tucked away among the rolling hills of the Western Ghats , the unassuming district at first appearance seems blessed with plenty . From Stone Age petroglyphs and ancient temple ruins to forested hills that are home to Kerala 's largest tribal communities and highest number of tigers , Wayanad always had it all .
