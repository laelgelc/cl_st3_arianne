The case is the latest to draw attention to Saudi Arabia 's strict social rules , which force women to obtain the permission of a male " guardian " if they want to work , marry or travel . <p> Human rights groups say the system can trap women and girls as prisoners of abusive families . The sisters are not the first Saudi women to seek refuge outside their homeland which is one of the world 's most gender-segregated nations .
