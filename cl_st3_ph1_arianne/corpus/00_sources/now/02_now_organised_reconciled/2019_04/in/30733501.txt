<h> Chandigarh Chapter of ' Save the Himalayas Foundation ' launched <p> STATE TIMES NEWS CHANDIGARH : The Chandigarh chapter of ' Save the Himalayas Foundation ' ( SHF ) was launched by the Founder-President of Mahabodhi International Meditation Center ( MIMC ) Leh , Bhikkhu Sanghasena at Panchkula on Wednesday . Bhikkhu Sanghasena founded SHF in 2016 with an aim of protecting , preserving and promoting land , nature , people , culture and values of Himalayan region by generating awareness at the domestic and international level about the urgent crisis regarding Himalayas . SHF focuses on three areas of work viz addressing glaciers melting due to climate change ; developing clean and renewable sources of energy , especially solar energy ; and promoting responsible , sustainable and environment-friendly tourism . Since , Chandigarh is the gateway to the Himalayas , it was considered as the best place to have a SHF chapter . While addressing a press meet at Doon Public School , Bhikkhu Sanghasena said , " I am happy to announce the launch of SHF Chandigarh Chapter , which will spread information and gather support for its activities in Western region of Himalayas .
