<p> The Home Ministry , Government of India , has released a letter stating that investments from the International Solar Association ( ISA ) will no longer be treated as a foreign source of funding . <p> This step is likely to create a positive impact on Indian solar photovoltaic ( PV ) project developers who have been struggling to find viable funding options for projects . <p> According to a PTI report , " The ISA will no longer be treated as a ' foreign source ' of funding for Indian NGOs and other entities under the Foreign Contribution Regulation Act ( FCRA ) . " <p> The report cited Union Home Ministry officials commenting that the decision is part of India 's dedicated efforts to assist and help achieve the goal of increasing the use of solar energy . <p> The government notification states : In exercise of the power conferred by clauses , and provisions of FCRA , the central government hereby specifies that the international organization , namely , the ' International Solar Alliance ' will not be treated as ' foreign source ' for the purposes of the said Act . <p> Simply put @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ entities originating from the ISA will not be governed by the FCRA . <p> At the 2nd Global RE-Invest India-ISA Partnership Renewable Energy Investors ' Meet &amp; Expo ( RE-Invest 2018 ) , leading bankers and renewable energy financing experts from multi-lateral agencies had discussed solutions about the financing of renewable energy projects , and how access to this financing could be improved to expand clean energy projects domestically and globally .
