One of the areas of study which could help us in brining solution is studying environmental science . Environmental science is an interdisciplinary academic field that integrates physical , biological and information sciences ( including ecology , biology , physics , chemistry , plant science , zoology , oceanography , soil science , geology and physical geography , atmospheric science , etc ) to the study of the environment , and the solution of environmental problems . Environmental science emerged from the fields of natural history and medicine during the Enlightenment . Today it provides an integrated , quantitative , and interdisciplinary approach to the study of environmental systems . Students can become environmental scientist after completing courses related to environmental science .
