For days , I entered the park looking around hopefully for the first signs of basant -- the first peony and snapdragon ; the shahtoot tree dropping the green mulberry flowers on the walking path or the starry white flowers sparkling among the leaves of the tree with tiny sour oranges . Then one sunny , mellow morning , the ring doves welcomed me with a triumphant double-toned chorus and the squirrels joined in with their ticking call ; everyone was singing , " Basant is here !
