<h> Services <h> Papanashini points to eco crisis in Wayanad <p> TNN Updated : Apr 18 , 2019 , 11:14 IST <p> Papanashini ( pic for rajeev " s story ) Photo By E Gokul <p> KOZHIKODE : The once-perennial Papanashini stream near Thirunelli temple where Congress president Rahul Gandhi performed bali tharpanam on Wednesday has now been reduced to a trickle . The water availability , critical to the beliefs and rituals associated with the temple , is currently being ensured by diverting water from a distance stream to it through a 3km-long pipe . <p> The almost drying up of Papanashini during summer points to the severe damage Wayanad 's ecology has suffered in the recent past . " Though water level in the stream used to dip considerably during summer earlier , the situation has become more severe in recent years . To avoid the total drying up of the stream , a pipeline has been laid from a stream at Chooralmala to Papanashini , " said temple executive officer K C Sadanandan . Papanashini originates from the hills of the Brahmagiri mountain ranges which has shola grasslands on top followed by forests below .
