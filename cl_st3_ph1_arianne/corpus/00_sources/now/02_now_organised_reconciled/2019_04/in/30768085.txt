<p> The material ( fossil fuels , biomass , metal ores and non-metal ores ) consumption in India has increased more than six times - from 1.18 billion tonnes in 1970 to more than 7 billion tonnes in 2017 - which is 7.2% of globally extracted raw materials . <p> Globally , India is the second largest consumers of materials ( at 7.42 billion tonnes ) after China ( 35.19 billion tonnes ) . <p> Noting how the country 's development need will further increase this consumption , the TERI 's report pitched for enhancing " resource efficiency " and use of secondary raw materials ( SRM ) . <p> " Given its current pattern of material consumption , India may in the near future not have access to virgin resources for driving its economy .
