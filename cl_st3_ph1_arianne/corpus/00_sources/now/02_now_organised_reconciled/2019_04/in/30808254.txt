<h> World 's forests ' in emergency room ' after years of losses <p> LONDON , April 25 ( Thomson Reuters Foundation ) - The world lost 12 million hectares ( 30 million acres ) of tropical tree cover last year - the equivalent of 30 football pitches a minute - researchers said on Thursday , warning the planet 's health was at stake . <p> It was the fourth highest annual decline since records began in 2001 , according to new data from Global Forest Watch , which uses satellite imagery and remote sensing to monitor tree cover losses from Brazil to Ghana . <p> " The world 's forests are now in the emergency room , " said Frances Seymour , senior fellow at the U.S.-based World Resources Institute ( WRI ) , which led the research . <p> " It 's death by a thousand cuts - the health of the planet is at stake and band aid responses are not enough .
