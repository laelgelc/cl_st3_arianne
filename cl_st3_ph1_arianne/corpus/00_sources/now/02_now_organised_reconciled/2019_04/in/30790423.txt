USA , Apr 19 ( ANI ) : Climate change is a factor that might affect the Galapagos or giant tortoise 's ability to migrate at the right time , reveals a study . The creatures are known to take the migration routes to look for the right temperature and the right food quality , the study added.The study was published in the Journal ' Ecology ' . The researchers kept a track of the timings and patterns the tortoise followed over years , using GPS technology . " We had three main goals in the study . One was determining if tortoises adjust their timing of migration to current environmental conditions . Two , if so , what clues do they use to adjust the timing , and three , what are the energetic consequences of migration mis-timing for tortoises ?
