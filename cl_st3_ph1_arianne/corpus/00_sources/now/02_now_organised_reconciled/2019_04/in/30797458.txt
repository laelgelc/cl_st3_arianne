Bringing an extinct species back into a rapidly warming 21st-century habitat may entail many unintended consequences . The revived species may face another extinction or may turn into an invasive species , destroying the habitats of existing ones . To minimise such impacts , the International Union for Conservation of Nature ( IUCN ) has come out with guiding principles on how to create and translocate proxy species . <p> The suffering of individual animals involved in such attempts , including new individuals and surrogates , still awaits a just solution .
