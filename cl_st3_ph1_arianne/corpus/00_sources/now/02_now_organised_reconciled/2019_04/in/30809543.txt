But almost none have been there since 2016 , according to a study in Wednesday 's Antarctic Science . <p> The breeding pair population has increased significantly at a nearby breeding ground , but the study 's author said it is nowhere near the amount missing at Halley Bay . <p> " We 've never seen a breeding failure on a scale like this in 60 years , " said study author Phil Trathan , head of conservation biology at the British Antarctic Survey .
