<p> The Congress on Sunday formally adopted a holistic security vision document that aims to protect the nation as well as citizens from multi-dimensional threats and coercion . <p> The document , prepared by a task force headed by Lt Gen. ( retired ) D.S. Hooda , popularly known as the surgical strike hero , deals not only with external military threat and transnational terrorism but also with internal strife , Left-wing extremism and militancy , threats to data and privacy of citizens along with other concerns like economic , environmental and climate change . <p> The five pillars of " National Security Strategy " have been identified as : assuming India 's rightful place in global affairs , achieving a secure neighbourhood , peaceful resolution of internal conflicts , protecting the people ( Jan Suraksha ) and strengthening the country 's capabilities . <p> The report was released on Sunday by senior party leaders P. Chidambaram and Jairam Ramesh and Gen. Hooda himself . <p> Hooda headed the Northern Command of the Indian Army when the much-publicised surgical strike took place across the border in September 2016 . <p> The Congress stressed the need for a comprehensive review @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the security apparatus , military capability , gathering of intelligence and cyber safety . <p> " This is more relevant today than ever before as the last five years have witnessed an alarming erosion in our military capabilities and defence preparedness , even as political rhetoric on national security has scaled new heights , " the report said . <p> The BJP has been citing the Balakot air strikes to canvass votes while Uttar Pradesh chief minister Yogi Adityanath had referred to the armed forces as " Modiji ki sena " . <p> The report pointed out that the defence budget had reached a 57-year low in 2019 in terms of percentage of the GDP and suggests the absence of critical initiatives to enhance capabilities coupled with short-sighted diplomacy had severely compromised national security . <p> It gives a detailed roadmap , saying : " To secure land &amp; maritime borders , we will adopt the principle of ' one border , one force ' , put in place a ' smart fence ' on the Myanmar border that combines technology with a physical barrier , operationalise a ' Comprehensive @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ speed up infrastructure development across the border , set up a ' National Maritime Authority ' to remove jurisdiction issues in coastal security .
