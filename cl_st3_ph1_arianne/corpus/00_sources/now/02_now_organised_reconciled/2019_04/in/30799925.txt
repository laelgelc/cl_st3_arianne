<h> TNAU to study impact of climate change on crops , find solutions <p> COIMBATORE : The Directorate of Crop Management at the Tamil Nadu Agricultural University ( TNAU ) has undertaken a project to analyze how climate change would affect agriculture in the state and to develop adaptation options . The inter-disciplinary project is titled - Building Resilience to Climate Change and Improving Food Security through Climate Smart Solutions ( BRIFS ) . It seeks to investigate a wide range of aspects related to climate change and agriculture from soil conditions to gene make-up of crops to economic factors of climate change and its impact . The project supported by the department of science and technology ( DST ) , under the ministry of science and technology . The financial outlay for the project is Rs2.42 crore , a TNAU official said . <p> Speaking of the project 's scope , director of crop management at TNAU and the principal investigator and coordinator of the project V Geethalakshmi said by the mid-21st century , the temperature in the state would go up by 1.4 ? C to 1.5 ? C. Also , the rainfall would increase by 5% @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ decrease . This would lead to extremities in climactic conditions , she said , adding that the project seeks to find adaptation methods under such conditions . The university is in the process of creating facilities for the research by acquiring equipment and setting them up . " We would set up facilities such as climate control chambers which would maintain specific levels of temperature , humidity , light and CO2 levels . We will set up the climactic conditions expected in 2040s and 2050s in the chamber and grow plants in it to study the impact , " Geethalakshmi said . The university is in the process of purchasing micro-climactic instruments such as devices to measure photosynthesis and leaf area , at a cost of Rs50lakh , and would soon set them up on the campus . " For the next two years we would conduct continuous and intensified research in this line , " Geethalakshmi said . <p> Some of the important parameters the researchers would probe into were the effect of temperature on the microbial and organic content in soil and the genes responsible for temperature tolerance @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ genes and upregulate them in crops , " she said . Similarly , the effect of temperature on the lifecycle of pests would be studied . The additional costs and benefits of implementing climate smart solutions and the losses due to not implementing them would be studied in an economic perspective , she said . The research would be carried out in the state-level for major crops such as paddy , maize , millets and sorghum . From May , TNAU would also conduct capacity building programmes on climate research for personnel from institutions across the country .
