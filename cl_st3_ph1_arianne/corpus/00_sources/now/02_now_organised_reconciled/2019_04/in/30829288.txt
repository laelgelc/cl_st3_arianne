<h> 64 IFS officers graduate from Forest Academy <p> Dehradun : As many as 64 Indian Forest Service probationers graduated from Indira Gandhi National Forest Academy ( IGNFA ) here on Tuesday after undergoing two years of intense forestry studies and rigorous training in conserving wildlife . The batch of year 2019 had 66 trainees , out of which two were foreign officer trainees from the government of Bhutan . Eight women were also part of the batch . <p> The highest probationers were from Maharashtra ( 9 ) followed by Chhattisgarh ( 6 ) , Orissa ( 6 ) and Uttarakhand ( 5 ) . Sharing more information about the batch , Omkar Singh , director of IGNFA , said , " Interestingly , 44 officers passed the training with distinction . I am elated to share that this was one of the best batches where 44 of 66 secured over 75% marks . " Director general of forests Siddhanta Das reminded the officers that their focus should be on mitigating climate change . " Forget not , you all have to contribute immensely in creating an additional carbon sink of 2.5 to 3 billion tonnes of carbon @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to meet our targets of the Paris Agreement . India has committed three promises in the agreement and this is most significant for the foresters , " he added . Meanwhile , Jagtap Kiran Suresh from Maharashtra received the All Round Outstanding Performance and Vikal Yadav from Himachal Pradesh secured the Best All-round Forester award . Rahul Kumar from Jharkhand became the topper in core forestry subjects while Magar Dhanaji Raoso from Odisha secured the Sanjay Singh Memorial Prize . Similarly , 19 other awards were also distributed to the new officers . <p> Speaking about upcoming amendments in the Indian Forest Act , Das said , " At present , we have the Indian Forest Act , 1927 . It is time we make amendments for better conservation of the wildlife , water resources and trees which is only possible by way of protecting forests . " Similarly , governor Baby Rani Maurya urged the young officers to devote time for out-of-the-box ventures to safeguard the planet and environment . " You might have already understood the man-made and natural challenges in this field while undergoing training . However @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ensure development as well as conservation of natural resources as both are important for humanity . "
