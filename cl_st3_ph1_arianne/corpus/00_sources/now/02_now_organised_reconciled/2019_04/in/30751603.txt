<h> AIADMK , DMK stand for nationalisation of inter-state rivers <p> CHENNAI : The BJP has promised constitution of a separate authority for linking of rivers in its manifesto , but the Dravidian majors - the AIADMK and the DMK -- want the rivers to be nationalised to put an end to all inter-state water disputes . The two parties are united also in demanding linking of Godavari and Cauvery . They speak in one voice in raising the water storage in Mullaperiyar dam to 152 ft and stalling the proposed Mekedatu dam in Karnataka . The state has been feeling the heat of climate change . With prolonged legal battles delaying resolution of inter-state water disputes , it is imperative to conserve every drop of rain water it receives . <p> The DMK has said linking of rivers across the country was a prerequisite to increasing agricultural production . To begin with , it wants peninsular rivers to be interlinked to save farmers in Tamil Nadu . " The party will urge the Centre to enact a separate law or bring in a constitutional amendment to link inter-state rivers . It will settle the inter-state disputes as well , " said DMK MP and party manifesto committee member , Trichy Siva . The AIADMK government has been consistently reiterating its demand before successive Union governments that all inter-state rivers be nationalized and Godavari ( at Akinepalli ) and Cauvery ( at Grand Anicut ) be linked to ensure a minimum of 125tmcft of water is available for Tamil Nadu . " If nationalization of rivers is done , Tamil Nadu will be one of the biggest beneficiaries . We have suffered heavily owing to water scarcity , because our rivers rely on neighbouring states for inflow . Our legal battles continue as there is no uniform policy on water conservation in the country , " law minister and AIADMK manifesto committee member , C Ve Shanmugam said . He said a strong Union government was needed to interlink rivers . States can not do it on their own . <p> Tamil Nadu is pinning hopes on transferring surplus flow from Mahanadi to Godavari and finally from Somasila reservoir @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that water reaches Kattalai barrage , upstream of Grand Anicut . The state has been making futile attempts to get Kerala to agree to the Pamba-Achankoil-Vaippar link . Kerala cooperated with Tamil Nadu in the preparation of a feasibility report earlier , but shows reluctance now for a detailed project report . The project will help divert 22tmcft water from Kerala rivers , which is only 20% of the neighbouring state 's surplus , to Tamil Nadu . Interestingly , the MDMK has allotted a chunk of its manifesto for water management . Vaiko is credited with introducing a private member bill , The Nationalisation of Inter-State Rivers Bill , 1999 , in Parliament . The PMK has promised a separate commission with experts to evolve a new action plan for linking rivers .
