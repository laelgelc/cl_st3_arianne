<h> Services <h> People 's charter sent to Rahul Gandhi focuses on ecology of Wayanad <p> TNN Updated : Apr 13 , 2019 , 11:50 IST <p> KOZHIKODE : Aiming to utilize the current national focus on Wayanad following Congress president Rahul Gandhi 's candidature in the constituency , environmentalists and diverse group of sustainable farmers have prepared a people 's charter drawing Rahul 's attention to the threats faced by the district 's fragile ecology and suggesting a broad action plan for ecological revival and sustainable development of the district . The five-page people 's charter with the tag line ' Wayanad : Where ecology defines progress and development ' has been sent to Rahul and the signatories have also conveyed their desire to organize a workshop to chart out a roadmap for sustainable development of Wayanad with his support . <p> The initiative has been made by lead organizations Wayanad Prakruthi Samrakshana Samithi ( WPSS ) and Wayanad Karshaka Koottayma , a collective of farmer organizations in the district . " We have reached out to Rahul with the people 's charter as he is a prominent candidate in the constituency with chances of winning the polls @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ by the high-altitude hill district on the environmental front due to anthropogenic pressures and climate change so that they can be factored in the development vision for the constituency , " said N Badusha , president of WPSS who is a signatory to the charter along with Rajesh Krishnan , convener of Wayanad Karshaka Koottayma . He said that election debate especially after the candidature of Rahul had been focused on promises of mega infrastructure projects while the reality is that the projects would not solve the livelihood issues that 99% of the people in Wayanad face but would only add to it . The charter suggests a five- point action plan for environmental and agrarian revival of Wayanad including conservation and increase in area of natural forests , regulated tourism and land use management , steps to assure sustainable income for farmers and ensuring freedom from debt , enabling growth of agro-based industries and promotion of bamboo cultivation in farmlands . On reviving the forests , the charter calls for adoption of strategy of avoidance to ensure that no more natural forests are converted for any non-forestry purposes . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for a mission mode work towards making the district fully organic in the next five years . It also draws Rahul 's attention to the initiative of late Prime Minister Indira Gandhi in framing conservation related legislations like Water Act , Air Act , Wildlife Protection Act and Forest Conservation Act .
