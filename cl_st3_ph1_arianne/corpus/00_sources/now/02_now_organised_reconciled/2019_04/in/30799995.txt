<h> Govt asks colleges to remove plastic from campuses <p> Bhubaneswar : The higher education department of the state has asked its degree colleges and universities to take necessary steps to remove single-use plastic from the campuses . It has issued the letter ahead of the World Environment Day which is to be celebrated on June 5 . The department in its letter , issued last Friday , said the Union ministry of environment , forest and climate change ( MoEFCC ) had taken the first step to prohibit the use of single-use plastic items in the headquarters of the ministry . The forest and environment department of the state has also imposed restrictions on the indiscriminate use , manufacture , sale and storage of polythene carry bags . <p> Rajashree Mohanty , additional secretary of the higher education department , urged the registrars of all universities and principals of all government and non-government degree colleges functioning under the department to take steps to remove single-use plastic from their campuses . " After implementing the order , the institutions will submit an action report to our department . The state government took this action after getting a letter from the MoEFCC on March 26 concerning the same . The ministry urged the state government to encourage employees to refuse , reduce , reuse and recycle plastic products on a daily basis . <p> " Prohibit single-use plastic products including water bottles , take-away coffee cups , lunch wrapped in disposable plastic packaging , plastic bags , disposable food containers , plates , containers made of polystyrene foam and plastic straws . Avoid plastic office supplies , especially plastic folders and tapes , " said the ministry in its latest letter . In October 2 last year , the state government prohibited the use and sale of certain plastic products in Bhubaneswar , Cuttack , Berhampur , Rourkela , Sambalpur and Puri .
