<p> The energy sector is one of the mainstays of India 's growth and development . From infrastructure to economy everything is highly dependent on the nation 's ability to produce and supply adequate and reliable power to all . With its commitment to the Paris Agreement signed in 2015 , India is at the forefront of championing the cause of sustainable development . While concerted efforts are being made to combat climate change by way of curtailing emissions , a lot more needs to be done to overcome the problem of air pollution . <p> For the last couple of years , the quality of air in Indian cities has deteriorated to the extent that it has put the lives of its citizens at risk . Global air pollution database of the World Health Organization ( WHO ) corroborates this fact , revealing that India has 14 of the 15 most polluted cities in the world . Findings of the satellite data indicate that areas in Delhi-NCR , UP , MP and Odisha are the biggest contributors of nitrogen pollution . Since thermal is considered to be a backbone in India 's energy mix for the next 30 years , emission control in this industry could accelerate the country 's fight against air pollution , as well . Noting the deteriorating air quality in Indian cities the Union ministry of Environment has expanded the ambit of emissions standard norms of Sulphur dioxide ( SO2 ) and nitrogen oxides ( NOx ) in five industries -- ceramic , foundry , furnace based on fuel , glass , lime , kiln andreheating furnace . <p> Though retrofitting works are under preparation and even in progress in some power plants under the private and government sectors , the emission scenario would further improve if emission norms for boilers and technology that are available and friendly to emission reduction are implemented to their fullest potential . The use of innovative technology will not only give an impetus to India 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ power plants , but also be instrumental for reducing air pollution . <p> To comply with the latest NOx emission regulation , which requires all utility boilers as well as industrial and captive plant boilers to implement NOx abatement technologies , India needs a time-saving and cost-effective solution . The answer lies in the implementation of advanced low-NOx technology at the primary source of emissions i.e. at the boiler combustion . Besides reducing emissions , it will enhance plant performance through optimization of the boiler operations and , with lower operating and maintenance costs compared to secondary NOx reduction methods , the overall life-cycle costs are kept at minimum . <p> This technology helps to reduce nitrogen oxide emissions at thermal power plants in a manner that the formation of nitrogen compounds is reduced by air and fuel staging .
