<h> Researchers work on tomato 's immunity <p> Tomato was chosen from a host of vegetables because of its global presence and wild varieties <p> In order to mitigate the threat posed by climate change to plants - where they are often rendered vulnerable to diseases - and cater to global demands , city-based researchers are working to identify genes in the wild relatives of tomato varieties . The team , comprising experts from Savitribai Phule Pune University ( SPPU ) , National Chemical Laboratory ( NCL ) and Weizmann Institute of Science ( WIS ) , Israel , are working on improving immunity of tomatoes . <p> " Humans have domesticated the wild varieties of plants over many decades now and worked to improve its yield , colour , taste , size and other characteristics for commercial benefits , " said professor Avinash Kamble at the department of botany in SPPU . <p> The process of domestication , he added , did not prioritise plant health , leading to the loss of defence mechanism among hybrid or popular cultivated varieties .
