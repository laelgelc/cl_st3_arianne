<h> " If we neglect higher education , we neglect growth of the nation as a whole because higher education contributes to the nation 's growth , " NITI Aayog official V K Saraswat said <p> NITI Aayog official laid emphasis on the grim situation of higher education in India and how there is a need of reformation . There is a need to grow human expertise in the field of science , technology , engineering and mathematics . While releasing a task force report on ' Emergence of STEM education in India ' , NITI Aayog member VK Saraswat commented saying that the country may have a large number of graduated , post graduates and PhDs , but their quality is questionable due to the shortcomings of the higher education system . <p> " If we neglect higher education , we neglect growth of the nation as a whole because higher education contributes to the nation 's growth . If we have to transform our demography , we have to take a look at the higher education in a big way because if you take an overall view of it , the situation is grim and not a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ People say a large number of graduates and PhDs are coming out but we know the quality of those products /graduates/PhDs , " Saraswat said . The government official compared the investment in higher education in India to that in China and said the situation is very serious . <p> " Four percent of GDP has been spent by India on overall education while China is spending around 565 billion dollars , out of which 145 billion dollars is spent on higher education . However , India spent only 12.5 billion dollars in overall education out of which 4.5 billion dollars were spent on higher education . This is the ratio in which we are working vis-a-vis China , " he said . <p> The report has been prepared by the Vivekananda International Foundation ( VIF ) with collaboration with several other professors across the country . It highlights the issues in STEM , Science , Technology , Education and Mathematics , and made recommendations to reform them . <p> The report recommended expanding the diversity in STEM education on National priority and noting that women and racial minorities are @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> " Call for a focus on ' diversity , equity and inclusion ' in STEM education . It needs to be made public friendly as private institutions are far behind in enrolling Scheduled Castes , Scheduled Tribes , Other Backward Classes and special category students in comparison to public universities . " The report stated <p> Moreover , it also stated that the GDP on education should be increased to 1.5 percent from one percent and economics ( analysis of cost and benefit ) of higher education needs to be discussed in more details . <p> Other recommendations of the report included planning national-level specialized institutions in areas where the impact is expected to be the highest and for the long term , for example , artificial intelligence , cyber security , climate change , event-driven business ecosystem and internet of things etc . <p> It also suggested setting up of institution for popularisation of science in one year . The report was released by Sarawat along with Arvind Gupta , Director , VIF , and members of the task force at the VIF , Chanakyapuri.
