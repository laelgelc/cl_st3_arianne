Four years ago , an ISRO report based on analysis of satellite data from 2004 to 2014 pointed out that 9,990 hectares of land mass in the delta had been lost due to erosion . Ghoramara island , for instance , has shrunk from 8.51 square kilometres in 1975 to 5.11 square kilometres in 2001 and 4.43 square kilometres in 2012 . <p> Ghoramara was originally a cluster of three mauzas ( ' mauza ' is a Bengali term widely used for a rural locality ) , the other two being Khasimara , and Lohachara . Khasimara had a land mass of 873.30 hectares , while Lohachara had 910 hectares with 374 families ( according to 1991 data ) residing there . Both mauzas were swallowed by the waters , forcing the families to move elsewhere . <p> The changing course of the Hooghly river to the east is also adding to the islanders ' woes .
