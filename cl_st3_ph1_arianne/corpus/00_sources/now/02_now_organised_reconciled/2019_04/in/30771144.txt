<h> Brazil to auction offshore oil fields despite environmental warnings <p> RIO DE JANEIRO : The administration of President Jair Bolsonaro plans to auction seven offshore oil fields in the northeast despite contrary advice from analysts of Brazil 's main environmental body , according to documents obtained by the Associated Press . <p> Environmentalists say it 's the latest example of how Bolsonaro , who campaigned on promises to revive Latin America 's largest economy by cutting red tape , is pushing aside warnings and scientific evidence in the name of progress . It comes at a time when Brazil , one of the world 's largest oil producers and energy consumers , is moving toward the privatization of several industries , including offshore exploration . <p> " There is no need to explore these areas , " said Carlos Rittl , executive secretary of environmental group Climate Observatory . " This decision is in line with a government that sees the environment as an obstacle .
