<p> ( Reuters ) - U.S. presidential candidate Beto O'Rourke on Monday laid out one of the most detailed climate policies so far in the crowded Democratic field , calling for a $5 trillion investment first major policy announcement , unveiling the plan ahead of a campaign stop meeting with scientists at the iconic Yosemite National Park in California . <p> " The greatest threat we face -- which will test our country , our democracy , every single one of us -- is climate change , " O'Rourke said in a statement . <p> The plan lays out a series of executive actions that would reverse the "
