<h> Film-making workshop at VIT concludes <h> A workshop on documentary film making was organised by the VIT- AP School of Business on its campus here on Monday and Tuesday . <p> Vijayawada : A workshop on documentary film making was organised by the VIT- AP School of Business on its campus here on Monday and Tuesday . Snehasis Das , director- film maker and Jyoti Prakash Das , visual editor from Naked Eye Film , Bhubaneswar were the resource persons for the workshop . <p> Snehasis Das is a national and international award winner for making films on social issues such as climate change , maternal mortality , right to education and disability.One of his recent music video on ' The Stories Untold ' related to super cyclone and migration has been given the best music video award at the Los Angeles film awards in USA . <p> The team demonstrated BBA students on making video and documentary film on social issues . As part of the liberal arts foundation in the BBA programme , the students are expected to go for rural immersion programme to document rural village lifestyle .
