<h> Flooding begins in Mozambique in wake of Cyclone Kenneth <p> Pemba ( Mozambique ) : Flooding began on Sunday in parts of northern Mozambique that were hit by Cyclone Kenneth three days ago . The government has urged many people to immediately seek higher ground , with hundreds of thousands of people at risk . <p> Authorities have said at least five people died after the storm arrived Thursday evening with the force of a Category 4 hurricane , stunning residents of a region where a cyclone had not been recorded in the modern era . Kenneth came just six weeks after Cyclone Idai ripped into central Mozambique and killed more than 600 people . <p> The remnants of Kenneth could dump twice as much rain as Idai did , the UN World Program has said . It was the flooding after Idai that caused most of the deaths . <p> Heavy rain was falling in the main city of Pemba , which had lost power . The storms we sometimes have , but such rains , never , " said a 35-year old resident , Michael Fernando . <p> Aerial photos taken on Saturday showed several coastal communities flattened by the storm in Mozambique 's northernmost Cabo Delgado province .
