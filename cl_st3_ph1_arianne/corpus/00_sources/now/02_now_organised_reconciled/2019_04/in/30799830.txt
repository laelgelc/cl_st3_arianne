<h> World Earth Day : Students vow to protect environment <p> TNN Updated : Apr 23 , 2019 , 6:35 IST <p> Picture used for representational purpose only <p> PATNA : Principal secretary of forest , environment and climate change department Dipak Kumar Singh on Monday expressed concern over the fact that plants and ecosystem are being destroyed in the name of development . Addressing college and school students at a function organised to mark the World Earth Day on the premises of Tarumitra here , Singh said , " For infrastructural development , we cut the trees and make dams on rivers to regulate its flow and such activities affect the ecology and inhabitants of aquatic life . We never see the second side of the coin . People only see the short-term gains of development and forget about the long-term difficulties , " he said . <p> Singh called upon the students not to use plastic products . " Use paper and cloth bags as plastic is not disposable , " he added . Several other officials of forest , environment and climate change department were also present at Tarumitra to celebrate the World Earth Day . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ city ' . More than 300 students from several educational institutions in the city , such as Carmel High School , Don Bosco Academy , The Tribhuvan School , Indirapuram Public School for Girls , St M G Public School , S R Vidyapeeth , Global School of Education , St Xavier 's College , J D Women 's College and Shri Ganesh Dutt College , participated in the programme . The event started with a pilgrimage march led by the students , which was followed by a prayer service . A one-minute silence was observed by the participants for the trees cut down in the city recently for widening of roads and other development work . Student choir presented the Earth song and dance . They also performed a skit and displayed posters based on the theme of the event . Students handed over a signature campaign letter to principal secretary Singh and , emphasising on creation of a green capital . <p> Singh planted two rare saplings from Nepal at Tarumitra .
