<p> The ominous instructions to reach the Dr Salim Ali Bird Sanctuary in Pune perhaps foreshadow the fate that is looming for this green patch . <p> Located off the busy Nagar Road in Pune , on the banks of the Mula Mutha river , the inconspicuous sanctuary shares an entrance with a crematorium and a garbage dump -- not the most appealing welcome for visiting birds or birdwatchers . The slice of green , however , has been quietly attracting several species of birds and butterflies and houses a variety of trees and shrubs . <p> But Pune 's booming infrastructure is likely to swallow it up , to accommodate the city 's growing population and its needs . <p> For years now , citizens have been pushing the local authorities to take action to protect the small but significant bird haven .
