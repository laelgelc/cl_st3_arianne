<h> U.N . slams Brunei 's Islamic laws as violation of human rights <p> FILE PHOTO:The Secretary General of the United Nations Antonio Guterres attends a press conference about climate change in New York , New York , U.S. , March 28 , 2019 . REUTERS/Carlo Allegri <p> UNITED NATIONS ( Reuters ) - Brunei is violating human rights by implementing Islamic laws that would allow death by stoning for adultery and homosexuality , the United Nations said on Wednesday . <p> Brunei , a Muslim-majority former British protectorate with a population of around 400,000 , on Wednesday began implementing Sharia laws , which punish sodomy , adultery and rape with the death penalty - including by stoning , and theft with amputation . <p> U.N . Secretary-General Antonio Guterres " believes that human rights are to be upheld in relation to every person everywhere without any kind of discrimination , " said U.N . spokesman Stephane Dujarric . <p> " The legislation approved is in clear violation with the principles expressed , " he said . " So long as people face criminalisation , bias and violence based on their sexual orientation , gender identity or sex characteristics @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . " <p> " Everyone is entitled to live free and equal in dignity and rights , " Dujarric said . <p> Brunei has defended its right to implement the laws , elements of which were first adopted in 2014 and which have been rolled out in phases since then . <p> Brunei 's Sultan Hassanal Bolkiah , 72 , is the world 's second-longest reigning monarch and is prime minister of the oil-rich country . He ranks as one of the world 's wealthiest people . <p> Oscar-winning actor George Clooney has called for a boycott of luxury hotels owned by The Brunei Investment Company , such as the Beverly Hills Hotel , the Dorchester in London and the Plaza Athenee in Paris . <p> The United States on Tuesday criticized Brunei 's decision to implement the laws and urged it to ratify and implement the United Nations Convention Against Torture . <p> Reporting by Michelle Nichols at the United Nations ; Editing by James Dalgleish
