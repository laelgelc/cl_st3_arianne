<h> Cold comfort : Countries get help to cool down in a warming world <p> LONDON , April 24 ( Thomson Reuters Foundation ) - Developing countries facing rising temperatures as a result of climate change will get help to cool down in potentially deadly heat , under a World Bank programme announced on Wednesday . <p> Around the world , people are facing more regular and severe heatwaves and droughts , largely as a result of climate change , while energy-intensive air conditioning and refrigerants threaten to further drive up emissions as the world warms . <p> " A sustainable approach to cooling is central to addressing climate change " both in terms of holding the line on emissions and cutting the risks from increasing heat , said Marc Sadler , from the World Bank 's Climate Funds Management Unit . <p> " This programme is a way to accelerate collaborative solutions and raise finance to meet the demand for cooling , " he said . <p> Heat is often neglected as a threat because it is both an invisible and hard-to-document disaster that claims lives largely behind closed doors , climate experts have said . <p> But @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ worsening food waste and significant losses by growing populations , urbanisation and rising income levels in developing countries , it said , as climate change also pushes up demand . <p> By 2050 , energy use for cooling is projected to triple
