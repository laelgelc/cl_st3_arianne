<p> New Delhi , Apr 2 ( UNI ) Congress in its manifesto released for general elections 2019 , on Tuesday promised to constitute , by law , an independent , empowered and transparent Environment Protection Authority ( EPA ) to establish , monitor and enforce environmental standards and regulations . ' ' The EPA will replace all other bodies that currently exercise jurisdiction and powers , ' ' it said . ' ' Congress promises an action agenda that will place India at the forefront of the battle against global warming and for the protection of the environment . ' ' We will significantly strengthen the National Clean Air Programme in order to urgently tackle the problem of pollution .
