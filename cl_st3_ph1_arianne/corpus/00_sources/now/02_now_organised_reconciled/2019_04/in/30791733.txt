<p> Climate change can affect the food , water and energy security of a region , Ministry of Earth Sciences Secretary M. Rajeevan said here on Friday . " Climate is changing and global warming is happening due to the release of greenhouse gases . In many parts of the world , including India , the effects of climate change are being seen especially in mountain regions like Mandi , " he said . <p> He was speaking at the Indian Institute of Technology-Mandi that hosted an International Workshop on Climate Change and Extreme Events in the Indian Himalayan Region . The workshop was aimed at understanding the effects of climate change , melting of glaciers , increased frequency of extreme events , atmospheric pollution and pollution due to the burning of crop residue in the Himalayan region and applications of remote sensing . <p> Lauding IIT-Mandi for hosting the workshop , Rajeevan said the Himalayas were one of the worlds sensitive hotspots to climate change along with the Artic region . " The Himalayan region is experiencing increasing variability in weather in the last many years .
