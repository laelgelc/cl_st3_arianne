USF has also reduced campus water use by 30 percent since 2014 , through water conservation initiatives , new pool filtration and kitchen equipment , and upgraded , high-efficiency water fixtures . <p> Another major component of achieving carbon neutrality is USF 's purchase of carbon offsets , which financially support projects that reduce emissions . After calculating the total metric tons of carbon that USF produces each year , the University purchases enough offsets to account for carbon emissions that can not be eliminated by its other actions . <p> An early champion of renewable energy , USF first installed solar thermal hot water systems in 1981 and added solar panels in the early 2000s -- which now generate five percent of the main campus electricity . USF was also an early adopter of cogeneration technology , installing its system in 1987 .
