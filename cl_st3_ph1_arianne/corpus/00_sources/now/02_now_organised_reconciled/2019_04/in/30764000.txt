<p> New Delhi : The Supreme Court allowed Thiruvambady Devaswom and Paramekkavu Devaswom to display fireworks during the ' Thrissur Pooram ' festival . The annual festival , celebrated since 1798 , will be take place between May 7-14 this year . <p> The top court directed the Petroleum and Explosive Safety Organization ( PESO ) to grant the approval for fireworks within three days from the date on which the temples move application . <p> A bench of Justices S A Bobde , M Shantanagoudar and Indira Banerjee said that displaying of fireworks has been an integral part of the celebration of this festival . <p> " Hence , we see no reason why the festivity should be suspended . It appears that the applicants-temples get the fireworks manufactured through their own licensees . They may do so subject to the written approval to be granted by the PESO recording the new formulation that can be used in the firecrackers , " the bench said . <p> It said the fireworks will be displayed according to the usual customs of the temples but they would ensure that barium is not used in the fireworks . <p> The apex @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ green crackers after the PESO formulates the new composition , to enable their manufacturing . <p> It allowed the fire crackers manufacturers to apply for requisite approvals from the concerned authorities after PESO approved the new formulation of fireworks by April 30 . <p> The apex court had last year banned the manufacture , sale and use of joined firecrackers ( series crackers or ' laris ' ) saying that they cause " huge air , noise and solid waste problems " . <p> It had said that noise and smoke emission limits of the crackers will have to be approved by PESO , a body under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry . <p> The Supreme Court directed the Centre to approve the composition of green crackers after it is formulated by PESO so as to enable their manufacturing . <p> It allowed the firecracker manufacturers to apply for approvals from the concerned authorities after Petroleum and Explosive Safety organization ( PESO ) approves the new formulation of fireworks by April 30 . <p> A bench of Justices S A Bobde , M Shantanagoudar and Indira Banerjee said that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ approval of Union of India should also be taken so that manufacturing can start . <p> " Any licensed manufacturer may apply for requisite approval in accordance with the relevant rules and orders .
