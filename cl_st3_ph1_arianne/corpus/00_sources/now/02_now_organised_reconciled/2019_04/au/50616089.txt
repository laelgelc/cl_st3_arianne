But voting in the state could say a lot about the future of the Liberal Party . <p> Andy Marks <p> Apr 23 , 2019 -- 12.00am <p> Like reality TV , NSW will be high drama but low impact when it comes to deciding the federal election . If the Coalition is to be voted off the show , then recent state electoral twists , and plain old marginality says it will be Victorian and Queensland voters who extinguish the flame . The show-stopping moments , however , will be in NSW.The federal leadership malaise did n't really rate at the March NSW poll . The few seats that turned , did so on demonstrably local issues : the fish kill , water management and infrastructure headaches . Liberal premier Gladys Berejiklian did n't exactly romp home , but she certainly did n't endure the " very cold , deadly silence " , that moderate Liberal Andrew Wilson noticed among voters at the November 2018 , Victorian election .
