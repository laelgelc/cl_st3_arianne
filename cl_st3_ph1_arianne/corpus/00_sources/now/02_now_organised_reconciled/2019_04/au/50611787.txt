<h> London police arrest 750 climate activists <p> British police arrested 750 climate change activists over six days during protests at various landmarks in central London , as Oxford Circus opened to traffic . <p> UpdatedUpdated 21 April <p> Share <p> More than 750 climate change activists blocking roads at some of London 's most famous landmarks have been arrested over the past six days , police say . <p> The protests , organised by climate group Extinction Rebellion , have for several days disrupted travel through parts of central London , including at Marble Arch and Waterloo Bridge . <p> The activists had also blocked the Oxford Circus shopping district , but by 5pm local time on Saturday , the roads had been cleared by police and traffic was flowing again . <p> Extinction Rebellion has called for non-violent civil disobedience to force the British government to reduce net greenhouse gas emissions to zero by 2025 and stop what it calls a global climate crisis . <p> Twenty-eight of those arrested had been charged with offences , London 's Metropolitan Police said in a statement on Saturday . <p> Metropolitan Police Commissioner Cressida Dick told BBC News that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ said there were now 1500 police officers , up from 1000 previously , working to clear the roads . <p> On Waterloo Bridge , which connects south and central London , a Reuters journalist said police had cleared away banners and obstructions such as bales of hay and even a sofa . <p> But as of 5.20pm local time , the area was still packed with activists , who remained good-humoured . <p> The police reiterated that protests were only allowed to continue at Marble Arch . <p> " I 've got one message for the protesters and that is : Please , go to Marble Arch where you can protest lawfully . Stop your unlawful protest , and if you do n't want to go to Marble Arch , then go home , " Dick said . <p> On Friday , well-known actor Emma Thompson joined activists at Oxford Circus , at the heart of one of the capital 's most popular shopping districts , to read poetry praising earth 's bounties.
