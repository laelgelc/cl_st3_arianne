<p> The Australian Conservation Foundation ( ACF ) scorecard of the parties ' climate and nature policies has given the Coalition a fail , Labor a pass and the Greens a high distinction . <p> ACF has independently assessed the parties ' policies on 50 key tests across four broad areas -- ramping up renewables , phasing out coal , stopping Adani 's coal mine and protecting nature -- and has liaised with the parties before giving each a score out of 100 . <p> " Stopping climate change and protecting nature are top issues for Australians at this election , according to numerous opinion polls and the ABC 's Vote Compass , " said ACF 's Chief Executive Officer , Kelly O'Shanassy . <p> " Australians are already being hurt by climate damage and they want to be represented by politicians who take the issue seriously and will act with urgency . <p> " The Coalition 's signature climate policy -- the emissions reduction fund -- has not curbed Australia 's climate pollution .
