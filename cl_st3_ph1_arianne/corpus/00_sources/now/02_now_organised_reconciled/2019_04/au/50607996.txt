<p> A Newcastle-based company has developed a new technology that has the potential to replace coal-fired power and change the world . The technology - which is named HERO Hydrogen Energy Release Optimiser - produces no pollution . It 'll dramatically change the Australian and world economy , " Mr Horvath said . He said the technology could make a massive contribution towards tackling climate change .
