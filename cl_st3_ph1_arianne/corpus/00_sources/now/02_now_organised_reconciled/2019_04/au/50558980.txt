It was a total sleight of hand that in fact increased the effective subsidy to the industry . It increased the tax payable by everyone on higher incomes ( insured people lost the rebate and the uninsured paid a higher tax penalty ) , made the subsidy more opaque and allowed even more distortions in the market ( for example , encouraging more people to take out insurnace for tax purposes only , or to take out minimum cover while still presenting as public patients in public hospitals ) . <p> Under both parties ' policies , there is no reason to believe the industry is genuinely taking pressure off the public system and every reason to believe it is encouraging higher costs and increased waiting times for the uninsured .
