<h> Victorian Socialists : a socialist voice in federal politics <p> Liz Walsh <p> 12 April 2019 <p> The Victorian Socialists are a new political party . We stand for the interests of working people against the corporate interests that dominate mainstream politics . <p> In last year 's Victorian state election , we gained nearly 19,000 first preference votes , a remarkable achievement for a party funded entirely by supporters and powered by volunteers . Our message -- that society should be run for people , not the powerful -- struck a chord in the northern metropolitan area of Melbourne , where people are fed up with years of privatisation , under-funded services and the pro-corporate priorities of both major parties . <p> In the federal election , Victorian Socialists will again provide a voice for working people , contesting three lower house seats in Melbourne 's north : Calwell , Cooper and Wills .
