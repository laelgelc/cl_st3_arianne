If the environment suffers , so will the economy . <p> The Reserve Bank of Australia has acknowledged this point . <p> In a major speech last month , RBA deputy governor Guy Debelle warned Australia could face severe economic shocks that threaten its financial stability if it does n't prepare adequately for climate change . <p> Traditionally , economics students have been trained to think of ' the economy ' as a separate entity from the environment .
