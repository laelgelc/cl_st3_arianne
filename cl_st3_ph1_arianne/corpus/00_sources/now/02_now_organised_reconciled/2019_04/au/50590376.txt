<h> Smart Farms Grant success for Sapphire Coast Wilderness Oysters <p> Sapphire Coast Wilderness Oysters has been a big winner through the Smart Farms Small Grants program with $194,812 on its way to grow the business and strengthen ecosystems . <p> Made up of oyster farmers from Wapengo , Merimbula , Pambula , and Wonboyn , Sapphire Coast Wilderness Oysters aims to ensure the protection of the environment and the sustainability of the oyster industry . <p> The funding will allow for an " industry-led coordination of updating and implementing Environmental Management Systems . " And " will continue to build the capacity of 60 farmers in six grower groups to adopt best-management farming practices and to work in partnership with key stakeholders to address catchment-based threats .
