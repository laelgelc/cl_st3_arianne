<h> Relentless focus on local issues misses the bigger picture <p> The frenetic pace of the election campaign reached a point this week when even Bill Shorten 's team was surprised at the level of activity during what was meant to be a drowsy rest stop on the ride to May 18 . <p> The timing of the election meant there would always be a natural pause in the intensity of the campaign around Easter and Anzac Day , but that has not stopped both sides campaigning hard at a local level . <p> This is just one of the contrasts of the campaign .
