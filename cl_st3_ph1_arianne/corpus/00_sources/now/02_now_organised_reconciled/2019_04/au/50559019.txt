<p> I am pleased to see more coverage of electric vehicles in the Drive section , particularly the ' Ready to go electric ? ' article ( canberratimes.com.au , March 30 ) . However , I disagree with its statement " ... you 'll need to invest in a high-voltage wall box " . Home charging rarely needs to be fast . I would avoid the extra couple of thousand dollars to buy and install a high current ( not high voltage ) wall box . A portable ' EVSE ' charging cable with a 10A or 15A plug is typically supplied with a car and most people find it is sufficient . Our household has driven electric cars for the last 10 years using no more than an ordinary 240VAC/10A power point at home . Faster charging is really only needed on longer trips away from home .
