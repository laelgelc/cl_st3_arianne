<h> Why Warringah is ready to break its Liberal habit and Abbott 's reign <h> By Peter Macdonald <p> April 19 , 2019 -- 12.00am <p> History shows that large parts of the Warringah electorate have been prepared to vote against the Liberal Party . Between 1991 and 2007 , the state seat of Manly was independent . <p> I was the independent state member for eight of those years , and locals told me they enjoyed having a representative who could work without fear or favour and not be under the influence of the party machine . <p> Tony Abbott with independent Warringah challenger Zali Steggall at a forum last month.Credit:AAP <p> Now , after 97 years , the federal seat of Warringah may be on the verge of voting out its long-running Liberal member , former prime minister Tony Abbott , despite his technically safe margin of 11 per cent . <p> I ran against Abbott -- unsuccessfully -- in 2001 . That was the Tampa election , when the swing was to the Coalition . Even without Tampa and the spectre of boat arrivals , the mood was different . Liberals were not clamouring for change @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ around these neighbourhoods often and I talk to long-term , rusted-on Liberal voters who tell me they wo n't be voting for Abbott this time . Their concern about climate change is the common thread . <p> Loading <p> Abbott and his campaign team know he 's in trouble . At the 2001 election , to their credit , I experienced no dirty campaigning tactics . This time they are desperate and we 're confronted by their negative campaign against his independent rival for the seat , Zali Steggall , such as the billboards trying to link her to Labor and Bill Shorten , or false claims that she is funded by GetUp . <p> While Warringah has long voted Liberal , it is not capital-C conservative . Seventy-five per cent of Warringah voted " yes " in the same-sex marriage poll , the fourth highest yes vote in NSW . Yet our member , who claims credit for introducing the poll , ignored them once the matter reached the Parliament , where he failed to vote at all . <p> The same-sex marriage vote and the ousting of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the camel 's back in Warringah . <p> A number of grassroots organisations emerged , such as Voices of Warringah . Vote Tony Out , Think Twice Warringah , People of Warringah and Stop Adani . Later , after all these had helped to change the conversation , GetUp entered Warringah . It did not come to fund or endorse Zali Steggall -- GetUp does not fund particular candidates -- but it did come to urge voters to oust Abbott . <p> Dr Peter Macdonald is a medical practitioner , former Independent NSW MP and mayor of Manly .
