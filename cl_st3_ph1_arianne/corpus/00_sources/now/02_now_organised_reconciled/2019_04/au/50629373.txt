<p> Members of the Ballarat community will have the opportunity to raise their concerns directly with local candidates at a pre-election forum on Monday evening . Each candidate will be allowed three minutes to explain their policies for a ' compassion and a just society ' before taking questions from the public . Ballarat residents Elissa and Andy are planning to attend the forum to hear local candidates policies and positions , with particular concerns about the environment , cost of living , education , mental health and public transport . The couple moved to Ballarat with their two young children , 10-year-old Amelie and 12-year-old Noah , from London in 2010 . Elissa works as a teacher in the western suburbs of Melbourne , leaving early and arriving home late from work , while Andy works part-time as a youth worker in Ballarat and takes care of the school runs , cooking and housework . Elissa said public transport was an issue she would like to be informed about in the lead up to the federal election .
