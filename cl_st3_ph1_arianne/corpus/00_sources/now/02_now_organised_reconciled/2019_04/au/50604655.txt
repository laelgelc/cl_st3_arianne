<h> Greens Put NSW Environment at Centre of Election Campaign <p> Comments Off on Greens Put NSW Environment at Centre of Election Campaign <p> The Australian Greens MPs <p> Australian Greens Senator for NSW , Dr Mehreen Faruqi , has announced the Greens Environment Plan for New South Wales . The centrepiece of the Greens environment policy this election is a new Nature Fund , which would invest $2bn a year across the country to protect Australian flora and fauna and fund the development of recovery plans for at-risk species . The fund will also pay for qualified " environment managers " , and boost the number of Indigenous rangers . The plan is funded through a carbon price and making big polluters pay . <p> In New South Wales , the plan consists of : <p> $8.9m for conservation of koalas including habitat restoration , support for community based programs and research . <p> $17m a year to go to local councils through the Local Greening Program <p> $6.8m a year from additional Landcare funding <p> $2m a year to NSW Nature Conservation Council <p> More Indigenous rangers -- boosting the number of NSW rangers to 240 <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ : <p> " Nature has intrinsic value , it is not just a commodity to be used and abused .
