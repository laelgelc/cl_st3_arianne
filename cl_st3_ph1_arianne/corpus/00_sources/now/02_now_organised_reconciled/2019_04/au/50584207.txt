<h> The key issues of the election <h> Wages and cost of living <p> Bill Shorten has declared the election to be a referendum on wages and wants to see a " living wage " introduced in Australia . Business is on edge over the plan , which Treasurer Josh Frydenberg says is a rush to fuel class warfare . The Fair Work Commission would be required to take into account more factors than it currently does when setting the minimum wage . Labor plans to hand down a budget update later in 2019 if it wins the election . <h> Negative gearing <p> Labor plans to restrict any future negative gearing to new homes , favouring first homebuyers over investors . The Opposition took the proposal , which is grandfathered for existing investments , to the 2016 election but it has come under sustained attack from the government this year with about 1.3 million Australians negatively gearing investment properties . The policy would apply to an investor 's total portfolio value of assets , including shares , and kick in from January 1 , 2020 .
