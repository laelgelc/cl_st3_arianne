<p> Out in Canberra 's east , mountain biker Paul Cole and and ACT assistant forestry director Peter Langdon stand on a top of a hill watching a wedge-tail eagle glide over logged pine trees in Kowen Forest . The forest is the forestry department 's commercial plantation , but running through it is 70 kilometres of mountain bike track built and maintained by Mr Cole and his mates . Mr Langdon and Mr Cole are preparing to run a tour of Kowen 's bike tracks on Sunday to mark the start of " Tree Week " , a week of events aimed at appreciating Canberra 's tallest mates . The " beer garden " track runs down the flank of the hill the pair stand on ; once surrounded by 30-year-old pine trees , the section was logged and the track shredded in the process . But Mr Cole came back and rebuilt it last year with the help of Mr Langdon 's department , and beer garden lives on , snaking through logged pine and surrounded by green replanted saplings . Mr Langdon said the tour would highlight his department 's work , while Mr Cole hoped Sunday 's tour would help encourage Canberrans to get out to Kowen more . Not just home to mountain bikers , it hosted sled dog races in the winter and trail runs throughout the year . " It 's a hidden gem , " he said . " It 's an area right on Canberra 's doorstep with a really good network of tracks . " He said people come as far as Sydney , Albury and the South Coast to use Kowen . " I met two blokes from Newcastle on the weekend who got up at half past four and came down here , " Mr Cole said . He 's been helping out at Kowen since 1999 , organising volunteer crews and taking his own time to chainsaw out fallen pines or improve jumps . " Imagine a city without trees , " Ms Ning said . " Cleaning the air . Shade in summer . Just the beautification of the city . " Ms Ning and her late colleague Adam Burgess came up with the idea six years ago and it took off . Other events included " hug a tree day " at the National Arboretum , tree climbing races , or a talk on what tree species will best cope with climate change . Mr Langdon and Mr Cole 's Kowen tour starts at 8:30am on Sunday , May 5 and there are options to tag along for one , two or three hours . Bookings are essential .
