<p> On behalf of Can Assist volunteers , I would like to take the opportunity to thank members of the Boorowa and Young communities for their generous support of our recent Songs of Hope fundraiser . The concert and raffle , which was conducted at Boorowa and Young , raised an approximate net total of $2,800 . The generosity of the public will ensure the continuation of the valuable charitable work of Can Assist throughout the community . We must especially thank all choir members , the St Patrick 's Parish Council , and our generous sponsors , Bendigo Community Bank . It has been a wonderful community effort .
