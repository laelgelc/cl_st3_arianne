<p> Australians will elect 151 MPs ( one from each seat ) to the lower house and 40 senators ( six per state and two per territory ) to the upper house . Senators have six year terms , so a regular election - such as this one - means only half of the Senate is up for re-election . You can find a list of the senators whose terms have expired here . A seat that was last won on 60 per cent or more of the two-party-preferred vote is considered " safe " by the Australian Electoral Commission . Between 56 to 60 per cent is " fairly safe " and under 56 per cent is " marginal " . You will also hear election commentators talk excitedly of " ultra marginals , " which are seats held on less than one per cent . The most marginal seat in the country is the northern Queensland seat of Herbert , held by Labor on just 0.02 per cent , but hotly contested by the Liberal National Party .
