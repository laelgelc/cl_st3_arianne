<h> Vertical farming , blockchain and the circular economy are bringing ' farm to table ' into the 21st century <h> Your groceries are getting a 21st-century reboot thanks to a new research centre focused on the future of food . <p> Australia 's population is forecast to hit 30 million by 2029 and reach almost 50 million -- double the current population -- by 2066 . That 's a lot of mouths to feed , and with most of this growth concentrated in cities , the question needs to be asked : how can food production keep pace ? <p> A new Future Food Systems Cooperative Research Centre ( CRC ) has received funding to the tune of $35 million to help answer that question over the course of the next decade . <p> The new CRC will focus on advanced food manufacturing , as well as how the sector can be more sustainable as Australia 's population continues to grow . <h> A cornucopia of challenges <p> Modern food production faces problems on many fronts .
