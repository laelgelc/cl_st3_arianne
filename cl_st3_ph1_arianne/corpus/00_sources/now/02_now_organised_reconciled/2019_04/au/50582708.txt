Prior to the Queensland election in November 2017 , the situation looked good . The Queensland government supported it , the major regulatory hoops had been overcome and it was now a private commercial question of whether or not Adani could secure funding . In part it was waiting on a potential $1 billion from the Northern Australia Infrastructure Facility for a new rail line . Suddenly scared of losing inner city seats to the Greens , Queensland Labor went cool on the deal , announcing it would not sign off on federal subsidies for Adani .
