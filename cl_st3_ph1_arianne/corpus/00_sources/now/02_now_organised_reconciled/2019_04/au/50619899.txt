<p> In 1938 , John Job Crew ( JJC ) Bradfield , engineer and principal designer of the Sydney Harbour Bridge , proposed an ambitious water infrastructure plan that became known as the Bradfield Scheme . <p> According to a 1947 report by William Nimmo , the chief engineer of the Stanley River Works Board , Bradfield 's 10-page typed plan had been submitted to the office of then-Queensland Premier William Forgan Smith . <p> It was titled : " Queensland : The Conservation and Utilization of Her Water Resources " . <p> Studying rainfall patterns , water resources and the impact of drought , Bradfield envisaged a complex hydraulic system using dams , pumps and pipes to divert floodwaters from the coastal rivers of north Queensland inland across the Great Dividing Range . <p> Bradfield 's revamped scheme -- titled Watering Inland Australia -- was published in the Australian illustrated magazine Walkabout in June of that year , and in Rydge 's Business Journal the following October . <p> Again , the scheme was rejected . <h> Would it improve the climate ?
