<p> A global climate change expert will team with leading eco-artists for the 3rd National EcoArts Australis Conference . Using the Visual and Performing Arts to Encourage Pro-environmental Behaviour conference will be held at the University of Wollongong Innovation Campus on May 26-28 . Dr David Curtis , conference convenor and president of Wollongong-based not for profit EcoArts Australis , was especially pleased that Climate Change Institute director Professor Mark Howden from the Australian National University was speaking at the event . " Mark will be able to share at the conference his extraordinary expertise on a range of related climate change issues . " This will ensure participants at the conference will gain reliable , current and in-depth insight into this global issue , " Dr Curtis said .
