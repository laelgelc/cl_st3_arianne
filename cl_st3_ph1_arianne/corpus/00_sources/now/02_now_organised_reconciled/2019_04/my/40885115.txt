Ikon was originally seen by many skiers as a useful and necessary counterpoint to the corporatised Vail empire and its Epic Pass . Introduced in 2008 , Epic gives holders unlimited access to Vail 's 18 mountains and to an ever-expanding selection of partner mountains . As sales approached one million passes annually , independent resorts outside the Vail ecosystem seemed to face an existential threat : Give up their independence and a bit of their character to join forces with Vail , or risk losing out on skiers . <p> Aspen Snowmass .
