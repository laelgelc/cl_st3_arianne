" We hope that by doing this , we are encouraging the governments to do facilitating , arrangements for the countries to move from coal to renewable . " <p> At least 100 major lenders have put restrictions in the past five years on mines that produce coal and power plants that burn it , according to a February report from the Institute for Energy Economics &amp; Financial Analysis .
