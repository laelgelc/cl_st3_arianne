<p> Anil Netto reports on a new wave of environmental awareness sweeping among the youth of southern Penang . <p> A wave of environmental consciousness is sweeping across young people in southern Penang Island . <p> Pupils from two schools in Malaysia created history this morning by becoming the first Malaysian schoolchildren to participate in the Youth Strike for Climate global campaign , which is taking places in a series of Friday protests around the world . <p> The Sungai Ara Tamil primary school organised a " postcard-making competition against climate change " this morning with over 100 pupils participating . Five winners of the postcard competition were awarded prizes and lots of small consolation prizes too . <p> Half way through the event , heads turned when another hundred pupils from the nearby Al-Itqan primary and secondary schools , accompanied by teachers , trooped in . <p> They had come to join in the youth climate event bearing the theme " Climate Strike for Better Future " in a rare but welcome show of multi-ethnic unity of purpose . <p> At first the pupils from the two schools , seated on the floor , chanted slogans in separate @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ pupils were joined by pupils from the Al-Itqan School nearby <p> But then they came together and enthusiastically chanted , " Climate justice !
