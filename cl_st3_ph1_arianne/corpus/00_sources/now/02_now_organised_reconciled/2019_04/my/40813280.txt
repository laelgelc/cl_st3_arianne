<h> Langkawi to focus on sustainable development <p> GEORGE TOWN : Langkawi will focus on sustainable development with the right balance between growth and conservation , newly appointed Langkawi Development Authority ( Lada ) CEO Dr Hezri Adnan said today . <p> " It is our hope to see Langkawi as an example for other townships to emulate in terms of green policies , " he said after assuming the position left vacant for three months after the retirement of Datuk Azizan Nordin . <p> The Kedah-born academician was formerly the senior director ( research ) of the Institute of Strategic and International Studies ( ISIS ) . <p> He said tourism in Langkawi would be upgraded with sustainable practices to ensure the ecology and pristine conditions of the island were preserved . <p> " Sustainable development is our priority , " Azizan said , adding that the status of Langkawi as a Unesco geological park and its " Natural Langkawi " tag are part of the branding of the island resort . <p> Langkawi Malaysian Nature Society chairman Eric R.
