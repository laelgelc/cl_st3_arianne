<p> Hamid remarked that the occasion was a special one as it helped create awareness of climate change . <p> He also commented that the programme had brought friends and families together . <p> A press statement by Pustaka stated that the programme was aimed at fostering appreciation of environmental greenery besides serving to educate the general public on the importance of safekeeping and appreciating the environment , especially among the younger generations . <p> " Aside from that , it will also provide aesthetical value and enliven the surrounding area of the library 's garden as well as providing green shade to the patrons and various species of flora and fauna coexisting in the garden . " <p> It is learnt that 119 stems of 19 different species of trees were planted on that day , among them were ' Engkabang ' , ' Pokok Ubah ' , ' Kembang Semangkuk ' and ' Bintangor Putih ' . <p> " The organising of such programme is expected to strengthen the relationship and spirit of cooperation as well as mutual assistance among users , smart partners and collaborators , " the statement added .
