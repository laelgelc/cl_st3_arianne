<h> 9 M'sian Unis Make the List of University Impact Rankings 2019 &amp; USM is Part of the Top 50 ! <p> It 's a great feeling whenever our local universities make it to global ' Best Of ' lists , and this one should be no exception ! Times Higher Education has released its first ever University Impact Rankings , and we 've made the cut with not one , not two , but NINE institutions ! <p> Better yet , we even broke into the Top 50 , thanks to Universiti Sains Malaysia ( USM ) , which shot to 49th place with 84.1 marks . <p> The University Impact Rankings differ from Times Higher Education 's other list -- World University Rankings -- as they are self-described as " the only global performance tables that assess universities against the United Nations ' Sustainable Development Goals " . <p> In other words , instead of quality of education , these rankings focus on the work that universities do to change society for the better , and how they address front line issues such as gender equality , climate change and sustainable communities . Metrics include the institutions @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ contracts and the number of senior female academic staff . We like this approach ! <p> The Star reported a statement by UTAR President Professor Datuk Dr Chuah Hean Teik , who expressed his happiness at being ranked within the 101-200 bracket . " We are glad to be informed that our strengths are in quality of education ( ranked 45 ) , reduced inequality ( ranked 70 ) and gender equality ( 101-200 ) . " More impressively , he added that a recent survey by the university showed that 50% of its undergraduates are the first in their families to enter a university . <p> The Rankings ' editor , Ellie Bothwell expressed that the results have changed the idea of what conventional academic excellence looks like , as universities in New Zealand , Canada and UK -- which are traditionally ranked highly -- are now placed along those in Iran , Malaysia and Saudi Arabia . <p> Who sits at number 1 then ? The honour goes to New Zealand 's University of Auckland , which is trailed by Canada 's McMaster University and University of British @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , while United States and Russia are the second and third most-represented countries overall . <p> Altogether , 550 universities from 80 countries across six continents were featured . <p> We 're glowing with pride for our universities ' achievements , and we 're also inspired by the innovative approach of these new rankings ! Let 's hope this motivates our tertiary education industry to go higher and further .
