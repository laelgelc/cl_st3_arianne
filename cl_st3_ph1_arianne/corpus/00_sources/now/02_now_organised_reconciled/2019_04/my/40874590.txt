<h> 24 containers of dirty plastic from Spain smuggled into Port Klang <p> The Energy , Science , Technology , Climate Change and Environment Ministry has uncovered a company smuggling prohibited plastic waste into the country through shipping containers by making false declarations . <p> Its minister Yeo Bee Yin said this was discovered following a series of checks by the ministry in Port Klang since last month . <p> " The containers are declared containing clean used-plastic , but actually they contain dirty plastic . According to regulations , only clean used-plastic is allowed to be imported into the country for recycling . <p> " We have detected a company that exports this dirty plastic and an investigation is in progress . The company will be charged soon , " she told reporters during an enforcement operation at the west port in Port Klang yesterday . <p> It is learnt that 24 containers , believed to be from Spain , were found to contain dirty plastic waste at the port . <p> Yeo said the ministry would order the containers to be sent back to their country of origin by the shipping companies that brought them in . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ checks had been conducted on 300 factories , with 148 of them ordered to either cease or suspend operations .
