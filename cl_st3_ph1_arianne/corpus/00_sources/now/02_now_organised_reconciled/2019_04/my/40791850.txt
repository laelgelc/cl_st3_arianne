<h> Niosh : Formation of toxic waste management panel timely <p> PETALING JAYA : The formation of the Toxic Waste Management and Scheduled Waste National Committee -- announced by the Department of Environment ( DOE ) last weekend -- is timely , says Tan Sri Lee Lam Thye . <p> The National Institute of Occupational Safety and Health ( Niosh ) chairman said everyone should support the committee , as it would help tackle the indiscriminate disposal of hazardous waste . <p> The committee , he said , would be the highest platform that could decide on how to better manage toxic waste , so that the Sungai Kim Kim incident in Pasir Gudang would not recur . <p> Lee said that besides the DOE and other relevant agencies , the committee should include experts from the institutions of higher learning and private sector . <p> " They must study how toxic and scheduled wastes are being handled in developed countries , " he said in a statement . <p> DOE director-general Datuk Dr Ahmad Kamarulnajuib could also originate from rubbish dumps and landfills . <p> Most of the country 's landfills and dumpsites , he said , were not properly designed with the proper collection and treatment of gases and
