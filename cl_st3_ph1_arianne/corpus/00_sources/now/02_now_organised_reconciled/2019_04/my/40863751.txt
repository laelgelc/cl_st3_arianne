<h> Green group urges govt to do more about climate change <p> KUALA LUMPUR : An environmental group has urged the government to step up its efforts in addressing climate change . <p> Klima Action Malaysia spokesman Reza Abedi said several steps could be taken , including improving climate literacy , imposing stricter environmental regulations and enforcement , and having more robust climate assessments as a pre-condition for approval of new infrastructure projects . <p> " We have less than 11 years to take climate action to limit a temperature rise of 1.5 ? C , or face worsening droughts , floods , extreme heat waves , declining food production and increased rates of extinction , " he told theSun today . <p> " Sadly , a Merdeka Centre survey in 2016 found that only 32% of Malaysians are concerned about climate change . Inaction now means a bleak future marred by conflict , famine and mass migration ; all things that Malaysia is not prepared for and that we hope to avert . " <p> He said the group would send a memorandum on this to Energy , Technology , Science , Climate Change , and Environment Minister @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Minister Dr Xavier Jayakumar and Primary Industries Minister Teresa Kok before the next Parliament sitting . <p> The group organised a climate change rally today in conjunction with Earth Day , which falls tomorrow .
