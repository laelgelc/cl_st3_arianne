<h> Malaysian honoured for his work in marine conservation <p> PETALING JAYA : A Malaysian marine biodiversity conservationist has been honoured by the US-based Society for Conservation Biology ( SCB ) for his work . <p> The SCB , a global community of conservation professionals with its headquarters in Washington DC , has announced its 2019 award winners and Kevin Hiew Wai Phang is among six from across the globe honoured for their contributions to the field of biodiversity protection . <p> Hiew was given the Edward T. LaRoe III Memorial Award for his pioneering marine biodiversity conservation efforts in Malaysia , particularly in the establishment of marine parks and championing the Coral Triangle Initiative ( CTI ) . <p> He previously was the Department of Fisheries Malaysia head of marine parks and WWF-Malaysia 's senior adviser for CTI . <p> The initiative comprises six nations working to " sustain marine and coastal resources by addressing issues such as food security , climate change and marine biodiversity " . <p> The Edward T. LaRoe III Memorial Award is given to individuals who have been " a leader in translating principles of conservation biology into real-world conservation " . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ National Climate Change policy adviser Dr Mark Shaffer was also honoured in the same category for his contribution to develop and implement policies and programmes to conserve biodiversity and promote climate adaptation in the United States and around the world . <p> In the Distinguished Service Award , University of Oxford 's Prof E.J. Milner-Gulland and University of New South Wales ' Prof Richard Kingsford were hailed for their work in innovation in conservation science , and conservation of freshwater and wetland ecosystems , respectively . <p> National Autonomous University of Mexico associate research Prof Dr Tuyeni Heita Mwampamba was awarded the Conservation Beacon Award , while conservation scientist Dr Aerin Jacob was given the Early Career Conservationist Award . <p> The society is expected to present the awards at the 2019 International Congress for Conservation Biology in Kuala Lumpur in July .
