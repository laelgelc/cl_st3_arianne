<p> Wan Azizah said Datuk Seri Mohd Redzuan Yusof was " entitled to comment " on the issue because investment matters came under his ministry 's purview . <p> The bickering began on Monday when Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Deputy Minister Isnaraissah Munirah Majilis said Malaysia would cooperate with Australia to ensure that the wastes from Lynas 's operations in Gebeng , Kuantan , were removed from the country before its operating licence expires on Sept 2 . <p> She said if Lynas failed to remove the wastes as agreed , the company should not have its operating licence renewed . <p> Lynas , a mining company based in Australia , processes its ores into rare earth metals at its Gebeng plant . Environmentalists claim that the process poses a danger to the local community because the waste is radioactive . <p> Redzuan then lambasted Isnaraissah 's boss Yeo Bee Yin for suggesting that Lynas should be compelled to ship its waste out of the country , claiming her stand was not endorsed by the Cabinet . <p> He had also said earlier that the company should be allowed to operate in Malaysia @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ welcomed in the country . <p> Later , Deputy Minister in the Prime Minister 's Department Fuziah Salleh said Redzuan was not in a position to make comments on Lynas as the matter came under Yeo 's purview . <p> Wan Azizah pointed out that both investments and economic empowerment , as well as pollution , were the issues . <p> " Pollution matters come under Yeo but investments are Redzuan 's responsibilities , " she told reporters at the Parliament lobby today . <p> In any case , she said , the spat should be seen as " healthy debate " among ministers to look for the best solution for the country .
