<h> Role of forwarding agency in handling of cargo <p> WE refer to the reports headlined " China 's ban , Malaysia 's bane " and " When trash is cash " ( The Star , April 24 ) . In the second report , it is stated that 111 containers of contaminated plastic waste unfit for recycling at Westports and 18 containers at Northport were still unclaimed . <p> Selangor Freight Forwarders and Logistics Association , the largest association in Selangor representing the forwarding industry , wishes to comment on these reports . Our association and the Federation of Malaysian Freight Forwarders ( national association ) fully support the government 's initiative to stop the illegal import of plastic waste into this country . In fact , we gave our full support when this issue was brought up by a member of the Council of Eminent Persons ( CEP ) during our session with them in June last year . Our associations maintain our commitment and support for the government 's initiative on banning the import of unrecyclable plastic waste . <p> The above report quoted Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ clients importing plastics , adding that the government had the right to revoke the agencies ' licences if their clients made false customs declaration " . <p> We wish to advise that forwarding agencies are only intermediaries acting on behalf of importers and exporters to bring in or ship out goods . Any information declared by the forwarding agencies are given by the importer or exporter or obtained from the bill of lading ( BL ) or manifest provided by the shipping lines . The forwarding agent is not present when the cargo is loaded at the point of origin and does not know who the shipper is until he is engaged by the importer to declare the customs form . <p> Shipping lines have access to information about the shipper and cargo . This information is in the BL and shipping manifest . In some countries , for example those in the European Union , the Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System ( HS code ) of the cargo is required in the BL and manifest . The forwarding agency is only given the information by the importer through @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to declare the customs form on behalf of customers . <p> Hence , forwarding agents are in no position to verify the information provided to them by the importer or the shipping lines , contrary to the allegation of collusion between forwarding agencies and their clients as reported . <p> Where no HS code is provided , the forwarding agency may propose for one to be declared to customs based on the cargo description given by the importer . However , customs may amend or change the tariff code or require examination of the cargo before determining the correct HS code . Thus , a forwarding agent declares the goods based only on the information he gets , and he is in no position to verify the accuracy of the information . <p> The report also says " The forwarding companies , she said , had the right to open up the containers should they have suspicions about the contents . " We wish to clarify that pending release by customs or any relevant government agency , the forwarding agent has no right to open the container . Customs would also have queries if a container is opened and its seal does not tally with the original seal number that was declared . <p> While it is still possible for a forwarding agent to open a container , he would have little information other than that provided to him by the importer to arouse his suspicion to get the said container opened . <p> The report also says " They are falsely declaring it as ( HS Code ) 3920 , which does n't need a permit and can enter the country ...
