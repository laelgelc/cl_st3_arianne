<h> Healthy debate needed on Lynas , says Saifuddin <p> The Indera Mahkota MP said he understood the main contention was about the water leach purification ( WLP ) and neutralisation underflow ( NUF ) residues produced from processing rare earth at the Lynas refinery in Gebeng here . <p> " There 's about 450,000 tonnes of WLP and some 1.5 million tonnes of NUF now . <p> " The debate is whether it 's safe or not ? Whether it has high or low radioactivity ? <p> " The benefits of rare earth is not being questioned because it is widely known . The question is on the possibility of something undesirable occurring , " he said after visiting the Lynas Advanced Materials Plant here Saturday ( April 20 ) . <p> Saifuddin said his interest was for the two parties involved in the scientific argument on Lynas ' residues to have a healthy debate . <p> " After that , we can all evaluate the weight of the arguments , " he said . <p> Lynas Malaysia radiation safety , regulations and compliance general manager Dr Ismail Bahari said the company was always open to healthy discussions @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ since the beginning . We do not have any restrictions and will meet with anyone with only one intention , to find out the truth . <p> " If there 's no agreement , we can work towards that , " said Dr Ismail . <p> Saifuddin said he did not have the mandate to comment on development in the Lynas issue as this was under the jurisdiction of Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin . <p> " I can only say that we give this issue serious attention , like any other issue . <p> " Of course , I discuss this matter as a member of the Cabinet . Furthermore , as the Indera Mahkota MP , I am involved on the administrative side of things , looking at this issue and giving my attention to the debate , process and the voices of the people . <p> " The Cabinet is taking every factor into consideration , " he said when askedthe Cabinet 's current position on Lynas ' waste management was . <p> However , Saifuddin said as @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ he had asked Yeo whether she would visit the plant . <p> " She answered positively that she will come and visit . But I do not know when , " he said . <p> Saifuddin added that his priority was the welfare of Lynas workers . He said the workers told him they were not in any trade union . <p> To this , Lynas vice-president of people and culture Mimi Afzan Afza said that they had an internal focus group . <p> " The staff have regular discussions with the management on any issue that may arise such as things that need improvement . Then we settle it amicably , " she said after the press conference .
