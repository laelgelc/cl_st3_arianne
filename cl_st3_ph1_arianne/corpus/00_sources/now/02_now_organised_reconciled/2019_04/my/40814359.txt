<p> The scientists suggest that after the Earth underwent a period of rapid warming , environmental changes limited the available prey for Neanderthals . Facing starvation , our ancestor cousins started eating one another . <p> Some 100,000 years ago , six of our Neanderthal cousins met a grisly end at the hands of their compatriots . <p> In a cave in southeastern France , two adults , two adolescents between the ages of 12 and 15 , and two children as young as 4 were eaten by other Neanderthals . <p> Archaeologists have long accepted that Neanderthals were occasional cannibals .
