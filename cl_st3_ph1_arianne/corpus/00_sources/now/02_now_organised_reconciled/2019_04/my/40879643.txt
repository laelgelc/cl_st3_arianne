<h> M'sian plastics industry supports government drive for environmental protection , development <p> 25 Apr 2019 / 13:04 H. <p> Prime Minister Tun Dr Mahathir Mohamad <p> KUALA LUMPUR : The Malaysian plastics industry is supporting the government 's drive to ensure environmental protection is in sync with the country 's physical development . <p> This was stated by Prime Minister Tun Dr Mahathir Mohamad at the Taman Tugu World Earth Day celebrations recently . <p> Members of Malaysian Plastics Manufacturers Association ( MPMA ) and Malaysia Plastic Recyclers Association ( MPRA ) are also supporting the Circular Economy model , where the redeployment and reuse of resources is a key organising principle . <p> In a joint statement , MPMA and MPRA said recycling played an important role in protecting the environment and for proper management and disposal of waste . <p> " We take the stand that Malaysia has no place for other countries ' ( dumping of ) rubbish and we should not become the developed world 's dumping ground , " stressed MPMA and MPRA . <p> Local and international coverages of Earth Day have highlighted that developed countries such as the United States and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ wastes to South East Asia - Malaysia , in particular . <p> The government has clamped down on the rampant smuggling of overseas household and municipal wastes into Malaysia , as well as illegal operators who process smuggled rubbish . <p> The Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Ministry , headed by Yeo Bee Yin , has closed down over 150 of these illegal operations since last July . -- Bernama
