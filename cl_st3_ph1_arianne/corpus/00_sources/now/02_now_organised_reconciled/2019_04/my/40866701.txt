<h> Climate change emergency -- use solar panels now <p> LETTER In 2015 , Malaysia 's net Green House Gas ( GHG ) emissions stood at 50,479,060 tons while we pretended to hide behind our status as a developing nation . While Malaysia has pledged to reduce GHG intensity per GDP by 35 percent by 2030 to the UN Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) , our pledge will remain rhetoric if we do n't take urgent measures to control our GHG output . <p> This is because we rely heavily on private transport consuming 14.4 billion litres of petrol per annum and we generate 86 percent of our electricity from fossil fuel . Malaysia needs to declare a climate change emergency in order to reorganise our priorities to combat climate change . How can we achieve our UNFCC commitments if we fail to tackle the following issues ? <p> Forest cover areas nationally are being depleted at great speed . Lacking other means of revenue , state governments are cutting down their forest areas at a disturbing rate . <p> Prioritising private transport by the construction of new highways leading to deforestation particularly in Selangor and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ car instead of improving public transport . <p> Poor enforcement paves the way for illegal factories to continue to dump their waste into our rivers and seas ( e.g. Sungai Kim Kim in Johor ) and clearing of forested hills with no one taking responsibility ( e.g. Bukit Kledang in Perak ) . <p> We continue to strive for GDP growth without fully understanding its implications on the climate as economic plans are structured and executed independently of environmental concerns . <p> Over-development causing landslides and siltation of rivers and dams ( e.g in Cameron Highlands ) . <p> Thus , PSM asks , is Putrajaya really serious about climate change ? <p> Malaysia needs to put a moratorium on new fossil fuel power plants and have definite deadlines to phase out our coal and diesel/oil power plants . Renewable energy and energy efficiency need to be expanded aggressively to replace fossil fuels . In 2015 , one-sixth of all the electric for the commercial sector was consumed by the government . Here is what landed residential owners in Peninsular Malaysia need to know about solar panels . <p> Understanding the NEM <p> Net Energy Metering ( NEM ) is the policy utilised for residential rooftop solar panels . Under NEM , homes with solar panels can store electricity generated during the day and withdraw the electricity at night . In short , the TNB grid acts as a battery to store and withdraw the electricity for homeowners . <p> If consumption is greater than generation , homeowners have to pay for the additional electricity units based on TNB 's residential tariff . If generation is greater than consumption , the excess electricity units are stored as credit that can be carried forward for the next 24 months . TNB does not pay homeowners for additional electricity generated by solar . <p> Electricity Reduction and Efficiency ( ERE ) <p> ERE will reduce the amount of electricity needed to be generated by solar panels . Good electricity practices such as setting air-conditioner temperature to 24'C , using the timer and switching off fans , lights , the Astro decoder and the wifi modem can reduce electricity demand . <p> Cultivate the habit of unplugging electrical appliances when not in use as electrical appliances attached to a socket still suck power although switched off . This phenomenon is known as " phantom power " and contributes to a higher electricity bill . <p> Electricity efficiency requires a change of obsolete equipment which requires some investment . Refrigerators , air-conditioners , kettles , electric water heaters and fans collectively known as heating , ventilation , and air conditioning ( HVAC ) appliance are the biggest electricity suckers at home . Replace old appliances with five-star energy rating appliances . The Energy Efficiency label is issued by the Energy Commission ( ST ) . <p> The higher the star , the higher the efficiency . The higher starred appliances may be pricier but the additional cost will be recouped with lower electricity consumption .
