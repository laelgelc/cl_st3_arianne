<h> Canadian capital of Ottawa declares state of emergency as waters swell <h> Canadian capital of Ottawa declares state of emergency as waters swell <p> Written by Reuters <p> 26 Apr , 2019 8:53 am <p> Written by Reuters 26 Apr , 2019 8:53 am <p> Share : <p> Reuters -- Rising waters were prompting further evacuations in central Canada on Thursday ( April 25 ) , with the mayor of the country 's capital , Ottawa , declaring a state of emergency and Quebec authorities warning that a hydroelectric dam was at risk of breaking . <p> Prime Minister Justin Trudeau visited with flood victims in Gatineau , Quebec , saying communities have to " reflect that with climate change we are going to see more and more of these extreme weather events more regularly " . <p> According to a government website , spring flooding had killed one person and forced more than 900 people from their homes in Canada 's Quebec province as of 1 p.m. on Thursday ( April 25 ) .
