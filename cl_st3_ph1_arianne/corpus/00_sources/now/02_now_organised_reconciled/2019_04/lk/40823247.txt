<p> Internationally endorsed once again for its genuine Social and Environmental Sustainability orientation , Alliance Finance Company PLC - Sri Lanka ( AFC ) has been recognised with a Merit award at the ADFIAP Outstanding Development Projects Awards 2019 , organised by the Association of Development Financing Institutions in Asia and the Pacific ( ADFIAP ) , headquartered in the Philippines . <p> It was awarded under the category of Local Economic Development for AFC 's contribution towards developing Social Entrepreneurship in Sri Lanka by partnering with the Ath Pavura reality TV programme . <p> ADFIAP is the industry body representing the development banks and other financial institutions engaged in the financing of development in the Asia-Pacific region . Its mission is to advance sustainable development through its members . Founded in 1976 , ADFIAP has currently 131 member-institutions in 45 countries including the Asian Development Bank as a special member . <p> In July 2018 , the Deputy Chairman and Managing Director Romani de Silva received the Merit award for the Sustainability Leader of the Year at the Karlsruhe Sustainability Finance Awards , held in Karlsruhe , Germany , organised by the European Organisation for Sustainable Development ; @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ sustainability efforts of the company . <p> Upon receiving the ADFIAP award , Sustainability Director Mahinda Gunasekara , dedicated the award to the highly committed AFC team which has made possible these achievements and said : " AFC believes that true triple bottom line value creation is a process that identifies significant social and environmental issues that are prevailing and finds innovative as well as effective business solutions that ensure a positive outcome for society and the environment in a holistic manner . This process is key to sustainable development . " <p> AFC 's sustainability mandate in the context of its core business is largely focused on battling the climate change and developing sustainable communities using finance as a catalyst . In 2017 , AFC has pledged its commitment to this goal as a signatory to the Karlsruhe Resolution at the Global Sustainable Finance Conference held in Germany ; organised by the European Organisation for Sustainable Development . <p> This is a declaration of institutional support for the UN Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) and the Paris Climate Agreement ( COP21 ) , collectively referred to as ' @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ biggest project AFC conducted in the year 2018 , planting well over 214,000 plants by the year end of which 176,518 were planted on World Environment Day , with the participation of over 370,000 school children from 991 schools in all districts of Sri Lanka . <p> This is a programme scheduled for three years with the objective of compensating the carbon dioxide emitted by the motor vehicles leased by customers of the company .
