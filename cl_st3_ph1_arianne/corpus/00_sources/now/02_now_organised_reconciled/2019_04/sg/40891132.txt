<h> BJP leader Harsh Vardhan attacks AAP : How can full statehood be given to Kejriwal with ' dubious ' character <p> Refuting the ruling AAP 's demand for full statehood to Delhi , Union minister and BJP 's Chandni Chowk candidate Harsh Vardhan wondered whether statehood could be given to a chief minister like Arvind Kejriwal with " dubious and questionable character " . <p> In an interview to PTI , Vardhan , who is contesting for the second time from Chandni Chowk Lok Sabha seat , asserted that the national capital was suffering due to the AAP government 's " negative mentality " . <p> He said the Kejriwal-led government has done nothing for the people , except " wasting time in abusing the Prime Minister " . " Delhi is suffering because of the negative mentality of its government and the chief minister in particular . They have hardly done anything in the last four years . <p> You can see their 70 promises and find there is hardly any promise that has been delivered , " the minister said . The AAP supremo had during a protest on January 20 , 2014 in the city called himself an " anarchist " . <p> He had raised the demand of bringing Delhi police under the city government .
