<h> Oxford University researcher claims aliens are interbreeding with humans to produce hybrids <p> Several conspiracy theorists strongly believe that aliens are secretly living in the earth on confidential bases , and governments all across the world are aware of their existence . Adding heat to these claims , a top Oxford University researcher has now suggested that aliens might be actually interbreeding with humans to produce hybrids . <p> This bizarre theory is presented by Dr Young-hae Chi , a Korean lecturer who teaches at the Oxford Oriental Institute . The researcher also added that alien hybrids born from mixed parentage will survive climate change easily , and thus they may emerge as a dominant species on the planet . <p> Young-hae Chi who is a strong believer of alien existence made these remarks in the book ' Alien Visitations and the End of Humanity ' , Express.co.uk reports . <p> " One possibility is that they find our DNA valuable for the preservation of the stock . Secondly , to create species which can survive in the future climate conditions . Thirdly , some abductees report that these hybrids are of very high intelligence , so are @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ leader , " said Chi . <p> Chi also believes that there are actually four types of aliens living on the earth . As per Chi , the major alien types are extraterrestrials with scales and snake eyes , small aliens and tall and bold aliens . <p> Chi also added that these aliens have reached the earth just to assure their survival . <p> " So , they come not for the sake of us , but for the sake of them , their survival , but their survival is actually our survival as well , the survival of the entire biosphere . That is where I progressed in developing my theory and I 'm still looking for more evidence to support my view , " added Chi . <p> A few days back , a team of researchers who attended the METI ( Messaging Extraterrestrial Intelligence ) International Meeting suggested that humans might be living in a galactic zoo , and aliens are monitoring our activities . Researchers also revealed that aliens are hiding themselves to avoid cultural conflicts .
