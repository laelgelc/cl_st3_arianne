Against a backdrop of the US 's withdrawal from the 2015 Paris Agreement and Mr Trump 's promise to bring back coal , China is emerging as an unlikely champion of the environment . But as the planet 's largest polluter , responsible for about 30 per cent of global greenhouse gas emissions , its critics would argue that it is about time China took a more prominent role to tackle the climate challenge . <p> It would be fair to say that China 's motives serve its own interests , as well as the rest of the world .
