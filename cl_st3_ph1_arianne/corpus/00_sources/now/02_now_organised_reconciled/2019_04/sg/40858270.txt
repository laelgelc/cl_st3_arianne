<h> Greta Thunberg joins Italian students to demand action on climate change in Rome <p> ROME ( Reuters ) - Swedish climate activist Greta Thunberg wants to fix climate change and hopes for world peace , but acknowledges that she might be being " very naive " . <p> Thunberg , 16 , has brought her campaign against rising global temperatures to Rome this week , meeting Pope Francis , addressing Italian parliamentarians and joining a student protest on Friday in a city-centre square . <p> " I think what I want for the future is just that we fix everything and that we fix the climate and the ecological crisis so that everyone lives in peace , I guess , very naive , " she told Reuters , shrugging her shoulders . <p> Thunberg shot to prominence last August with weekly sit-ins on the cobblestones in front of Stockholm 's Parliament House with her " school strike for climate " sign . <p> Thousands of students around the world have since copied her and youth organisations have
