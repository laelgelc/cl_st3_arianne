" <p> In a letter ahead of a White House meeting with Trump on Tuesday , House Speaker Nancy Pelosi and Senate Democratic Leader Charles Schumer said the deal must be " comprehensive and include clean energy and resiliency priorities . " <p> Any deal must " go beyond transportation and into broadband , water , energy , schools , housing and other initiatives .
