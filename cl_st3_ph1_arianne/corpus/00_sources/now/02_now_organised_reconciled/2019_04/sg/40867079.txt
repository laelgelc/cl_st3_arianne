<h> Lil Dicky 's Epic ' Earth ' Music Video Has Gone Viral <p> Rapper and comedian Lil Dicky has released an animated music video centered on climate change . Featuring the voices of Ariana Grande , Justin Bieber , Halsey , Miley Cyrus , Sia , Snoop Dogg and many other celebrities , the video sees animated animals championing the environmental movement . <p> A portion of the proceeds will go to the Leonardo DiCaprio Foundation , which endeavours to protect threatened ecosystems and wildlife communities around the world . DiCaprio , committed to tackling the climate crisis , signs off as an animated version of himself towards the end of the clip . <p> " What started as a silly joke of an idea along the way became the most important thing I 'll ever do , " said Dicky , aka David Burd . <p> The music video has amassed more than 23 million views since its premiere on 18 April .
