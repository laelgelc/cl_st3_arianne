<h> Naked truth : climate change protesters cause a stir in Brexit debate <p> LONDON ( Reuters ) - A group of semi-naked climate change protesters created a stir in Britain 's parliament on Monday , pressing themselves up against the glass screen of a public gallery overlooking the chamber while lawmakers attempted to debate Brexit . <p> As lawmakers started yet another day of lengthy debate on how or even whether Britain should leave the European Union , around a dozen protesters stripped to their underpants to show slogans painted on their backs , including " Climate justice now " . <p> The incident lasted around 25 minutes ; some glued their hands to the glass and had to be removed by police , who later said 12 people had been arrested for " outraging public decency " . <p> Several lawmakers could not stop themselves looking up at the gallery , some giggling as Labour lawmaker Peter Kyle argued that the government must hold a confirmatory vote on any Brexit deal by pointing out what he called the " naked truth " . <p> The activists were from a group named Extinction Rebellion , according to a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ they wanted to " draw politicians ' attention to the Climate and Ecological Crisis " . <p> ( Reporting by William James and Kylie MacLellan , writing by Elizabeth Piper ; Editing
