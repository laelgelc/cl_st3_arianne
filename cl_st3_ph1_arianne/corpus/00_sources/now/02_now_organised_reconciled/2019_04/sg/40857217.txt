<h> Extinction Rebellion protest in London <p> LONDON ( Reuters ) - Film star Emma Thompson joined climate change activists in a central London shopping district on Friday to read poetry praising Earth 's bounties , part of five days of protests which clogged the British capital with transport snarl-ups . <p> Organisers Extinction Rebellion have called for non-violent civil disobedience to force the British government to reduce net greenhouse gas emissions to zero by 2025 and stop what they call a global climate crisis . <p> The protests did not cause major travel disruption on Friday during one of Britain 's biggest holiday weekends , but police said they have now arrested more than 682 people . <p> Extinction Rebellion blocked several locations in central London this week after staging a semi-nude protest in parliament earlier this month . <p> " Our planet is in serious trouble , " Thompson told reporters amid a crowd of about 300 activists , according to eye witnesses .
