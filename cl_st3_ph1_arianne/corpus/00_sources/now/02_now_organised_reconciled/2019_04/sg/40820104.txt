Those losses would be offset by higher returns on renewable energy investments . <p> " On the two-degree scenario , our broad view is that the impact overall on GDP is pretty negligible , " Steven Sowden , a principal at Mercer and one of the report 's authors , said in a phone interview . <p> If warming is allowed to exceed that level , however , investors would have few good options . <p> Three degrees of warming would spare most of the energy sector from significant losses , Mercer found , with the exception of coal .
