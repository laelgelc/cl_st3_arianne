<p> President Xi Jinping did not pledge fresh capital to fund China 's ambitious " Belt and Road Initiative " and focused instead on " co-development " as he delivered a keynote speech at the second Belt and Road Forum in Beijing on Friday . <p> Compared with the inaugural 2017 event , Xi 's speech was shorter and contained fewer concrete proposals . Instead , he seems to have focused more on deflecting criticism and doubts about the multibillion-dollar initiative . <p> Even though he did not name the United States -- with which China is having a trade war -- or Europe , Xi 's speech appeared to be directed at their concerns over the belt and road plan . <p> His speech was delivered just hours after an announcement by US President Donald Trump that Xi would visit the White House " soon " .
