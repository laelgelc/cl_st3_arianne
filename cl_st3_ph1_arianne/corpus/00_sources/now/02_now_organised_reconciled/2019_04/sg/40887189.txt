<h> Working within such multilateral frameworks only way to solve transnational challenges , he says at BRI forum <p> As a strategy that promotes trade and connectivity , the Belt and Road Initiative ( BRI ) can play a crucial role in strengthening cooperation between countries , Prime Minister Lee Hsien Loong said yesterday . <p> It is coming at a time when the world is seeing a pushback against globalisation , and growing distrust of governments and public institutions , making it harder for countries to work together , he said . <p> But working within such multilateral frameworks is the only way countries can solve the many complex transnational challenges they face , from terrorism to climate change , PM Lee said at a high-level meeting during the second Belt and Road Forum in Beijing . <p> " Both countries and their problems have become so interconnected and interdependent , " he added . <p> " All countries , big and small , rely on a stable global order on which we can cooperate productively , resolve disputes peacefully and work together on new areas .
