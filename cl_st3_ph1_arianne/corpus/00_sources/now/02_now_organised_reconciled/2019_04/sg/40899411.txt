<h> Rains torment cyclone-hit communities in Mozambique <h> A villager carries wood through maize fields flooded in the aftermath of Cyclone Kenneth , along the Mieze river near Pemba <p> A villager carries wood through maize fields flooded in the aftermath of Cyclone Kenneth , along the Mieze river near Pemba , Mozambique , April 30 , 2019 . REUTERS/Mike Hutchings <p> By Mike Hutchings and Shafiek Tassiem <p> PEMBA , Mozambique ( Reuters ) - Torrential rain battered northern Mozambique on Tuesday , grounding aid flights and further swamping a region where a second cyclone in six weeks has killed at least 41 people . <p> Cyclone Kenneth slammed into Mozambique 's Cabo Delgado province on Thursday with winds of up to 280 km per hour ( 173 mph ) , flattening villages , destroying crops and turning streets of the Indian Ocean port city of Pemba into raging rivers . <p> It followed last month 's Cyclone Idai , which struck further south , destroying the port of Beira and killing more than 1,000 people across Mozambique , Zimbabwe and Malawi . <p> Tuesday 's heavy rains swelled already bursting rivers and interrupted aid operations for a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ take off in the morning , meaning some of the worst-hit communities remain cut off with limited supplies . <p> The United Nations ' World Food Programme ( WFP ) said it was particularly worried about people on Ibo island , who had little food and were in the open after most homes were destroyed . <p> " For us it 's a frustrating day ... There is not much we can do to reach these islands now , " said WFP spokeswoman Deborah Nguyen . <p> More flooding may be coming , with thousands of people living in areas at risk . In Pemba , 35,000 houses were completely or partially destroyed , authorities said , while outside the city crops were destroyed and villagers waded through flooded fields with wood , salvaged maize and other products on their heads . <p> The confirmed death toll from Cyclone Kenneth rose on Tuesday from 38 to 41 , a spokesperson for the Council of Ministers told a news conference in the capital Maputo . <p> It is likely to rise as officials reach all affected areas . <p> Pemba 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ landslide at a rubbish dump on Sunday had killed at least five people , possibly in addition to the official toll . <p> The people were all members of the same family and several other houses had also been buried , STV reported . <p> Kenneth killed four people in the Indian Ocean island nation of Comoros to the north before barrelling into Mozambique , the first time on record two powerful storms had hit the southern African country in such a short space of time . <p> Aid agencies and others have blamed climate change . <p> Preliminary government assessments suggest 31,000 hectares of crops have been lost in an area already vulnerable to food shortages . Fisheries and other key sources of sustenance like coconut trees were also damaged . <p> " The short- , mid- and long-term availability of food is worrisome , " senior WFP spokesman Herve Verhoosel said in Geneva . <p> ( Reporting by Mike Hutchings and Shafiek Tassiem in Pemba , Emma Rumney in Johannesburg , Manuel Mucari in Maputo and Stephanie Nebehay in Geneva ; Writing by Emma Rumney ; Editing by @ @ @ @ @ @
