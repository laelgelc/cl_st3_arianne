Put together , those companies made nearly US$106 billion last year , according to CNBC . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ but also tightly bound to one country and the price of oil . <p> Aramco issued the financial data in a prospectus as it prepares to borrow up to US$15 billion through a bond sale , in what could signal a more aggressive approach to capital raising for the company and Saudi Arabia , which is seeking to cut its dependence on oil and gas revenue . <p> The money will help finance Aramco 's US$69 billion purchase of most of a state-owned petrochemical company from Saudi Arabia 's sovereign wealth fund , whose chairman is Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman . <p> The Crown Prince wants to diversify Saudi Arabia 's economy , and the sovereign wealth fund has been investing in tech companies such as Uber and Tesla as a way of doing so . A planned stock sale last year by Aramco was expected to raise money for that purpose , but it was postponed . <p> The sale of a stake in petrochemical company Saudi Basic Industries appears to be an alternative way to raise the funds . <p> While the Crown Prince pursues new investments @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the killing of Saudi journalist Jamal Khashoggi last year , Aramco also appears to be trying to make itself into a broader energy producer and , thus , more attractive if the government decides once again to try to sell a slice of the company . <p> Aramco chief executive Amin Nasser has said the company is pursuing international acquisitions in areas such as liquefied natural gas , a chilled fuel that , like oil , can be transported globally on ships . <p> The financial results also show how the company 's fortunes rise and fall with the price of oil . For instance , in 2016 , a time of low prices , the company reported only US$13.3 billion in net income . <p> For investors , Aramco 's ties to the Saudi government are also a persistent concern . Last year , Aramco paid about US$160 billion to the government in dividends , taxes and royalties . <p> Moody 's attributed Aramco 's profitability in part to economies of scale stemming from enormous production volumes extracted from oil and gas assets of unmatched size . Aramco has some of the world 's largest oil fields , leading to very low costs . " Aramco 's scale of production , in combination with its vast hydrocarbon resources , is a very strong competitive advantage , " wrote Moody 's analysts . <p> The prospectus reveals some long-hidden details about the size of Saudi Arabia 's oil fields .
