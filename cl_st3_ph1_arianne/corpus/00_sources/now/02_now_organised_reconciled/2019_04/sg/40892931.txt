<p> Amid geopolitical and economic uncertainties , it is critical for countries and international organisations to work closely together towards long-term , sustainable development , said Finance Minister Heng Swee Keat yesterday . <p> He called on local and foreign labour ministers and tripartite leaders attending a conference on work this week to grow and develop their citizens , create different learning pathways , and utilise labour productively so workers can lead better lives . <p> The world is facing many complex and transnational challenges such as terrorism , climate change and food security , said Mr Heng . <p> " As we become more interdependent and interconnected , it is even more important for countries and international organisations to work closely together to tackle these challenges within a rules-based , multilateral framework , and to build a more peaceful and prosperous world . " <p> This is among the ways Singapore is preparing for the future of work , he said .
