<h> Share this content <h> Bookmark <p> PARIS : The second largest Emperor penguin colony on Earth has suffered a " catastrophic " breeding failure after nearly all chicks born over three years died as their icy Antarctic habitat shrinks , researchers said on Thursday ( Apr 25 ) . <p> The British Antarctic Survey ( BAS ) used satellite imagery to study the behaviour of the Halley Bay colony in the Weddell Sea due south of Cape Hope , which normally sees up to 25,000 penguin pairs mate each year . <p> Advertisement <p> They found that in 2016 , when abnormally warm and stormy weather broke up the sea-ice on which the penguins normally raise their young , almost all the chicks died . This pattern was repeated in 2017 and 2018 . <p> The BAS said the colony at Halley Bay has " all but disappeared " . <p> " We have been tracking the population of this , and other colonies in the
