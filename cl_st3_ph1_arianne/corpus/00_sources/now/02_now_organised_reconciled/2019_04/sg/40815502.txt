<h> Chile wants more ambitious action at U.N . climate change conference <p> SANTIAGO ( Reuters ) - Chile will urge countries at the United Nations climate change conference in Santiago at the end of the year to adopt more ambitious commitments to deal with global warming , President Sebastian Pinera told reporters on Monday . <p> The U.N . Paris Agreement was not strong enough " to prevent the world from continuing on a path that could end in tragedy , " the president told a news conference . <p> " We will have to move forward so that countries assume more ambitious commitments , " he said . The summit will be held on Dec. 2-13 . <p> Chile , fronting the Pacific Ocean at the southern tip of South America , will also advocate for the protection of Antarctica and the oceans , Pinera said .
