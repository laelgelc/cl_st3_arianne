<h> Oil industry under pressure to respond to climate change <p> The oil industry says it is committed to reducing its environmental impact , but critics charge it is moving too slow <p> The oil industry , under mounting pressure from environmental activists to react more quickly to counter climate change , has begun to adapt its strategy but is struggling to convince critics it is doing enough . <p> Last week activists , responding to a call from the Extinction Rebellion group , laid siege to Shell 's London headquarters whose windows were smashed . <p> Several days later hundreds of activists blocked several symbolic sites in France , including the headquarters of oil giant Total . <p> " They are mastodons whose business model is based on fossil fuels and which do n't want to shift one iota because up until now they 've made money that way , " said Cecile Marchand , who heads up climate activities of the French branch of the Friends of the Earth network of environmental protection groups . <p> " The activities of these multinationals are not at all regulated today , and they should be if we are to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 1.5 degrees " Celsius , she added , referring to the goal laid out in the 2015 Paris climate deal . <p> The pressure is n't only coming from environmental activists . <p> " Investment funds are now requiring oil and gas companies to explain how climate change might affect a company 's value , " said David Elmes , a professor at Warwick Business School . <p> " This is driving some companies to be much more transparent about the emissions they cause and their plans to reduce them , " he added . <p> -
