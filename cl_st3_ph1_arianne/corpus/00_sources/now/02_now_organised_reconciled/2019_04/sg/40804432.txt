For years , a cousin of mine has been on treatment for major depression and mild bipolar , which has affected his employment situation . There 's also the old uncle living in my block at Marine Crescent who was depressed from the death of his wife and started sleeping on the bench in the void deck at night . <p> Just the other day , a young taxi driver shared with me about his 87-year-old grandfather jumping to his death -- his whole family was having their breakfast inside their flat when they heard a thud .
