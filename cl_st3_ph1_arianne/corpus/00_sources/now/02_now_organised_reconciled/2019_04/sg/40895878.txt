opposition Labor party would risk setting back the economy amid looming problems . <p> Advertisement <p> " We are facing stiff headwinds into the future for our economy , " Morrison told an audience Australians to fund more spending on health and education . <p> Shorten 's debate performance will be scrutinised as Australia 's preeminent political poll showed his party 's lead was fast
