<h> Seals , caviar and oil : Caspian Sea faces pollution threat <p> Seals that were once a common sight on Baku 's waterfront have been declared endangered <p> Seals waddling along the waterfront were once a common sight in Baku Bay , the Caspian Sea home of Azerbaijan 's capital . <p> Not anymore . Of the more than one million seals which inhabited the shores and islands of the Caspian a century ago fewer than 10 percent remain , and the species has been declared endangered . <p> Azer Garayev , the head of the Azerbaijan Society for the Protection of Animals , says the seals have for decades been suffering from over-hunting and the effects of industrial pollution . <p> In 2003 , his group found 750 seal carcasses in just one month . <p> " It was not normal , " but no one looked into the issue , the 57-year-old activist said . " The seal is a sign of all the major environmental problems ( in the Caspian ) .
