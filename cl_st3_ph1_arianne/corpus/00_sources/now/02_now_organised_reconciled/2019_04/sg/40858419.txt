<p> Hundreds of French activists on Friday staged a sit-in outside top energy companies in Paris , describing France as the " republic of polluters " , as part of a international civil disobedience campaign on climate change . <p> Some two thousand environmental activists in total assembled at the La Defense business district in a bid to block access to to the headquarters of energy giant Total , electricity firm EDF as well as bank Societe Generale and France 's Ministry for Ecological Transition . <p> Protestors rallied around the slogan , " Block the Republic of Polluters " and held banners bearing the face of President Emmanuel Macron and the message , " Macron , President of polluters " . <p> Some lay on the ground , in what they said was an action symbolising a " climate crime scene " . <p> The demonstration , a joint initiative of ANV-COP21 , Greenpeace and Les Amis de la Terre , comes at the end of the " International Week of Rebellion " launched by Extinction Rebellion , the climate change pressure group using civil disobedience to demand action over climate change from politicians and governments worldwide . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ protest outside Total was symbolic as the company was the " factory of climate change " . <p> The French multinational ranks among the world 's top 20 CO2-emitting companies . <p> " We have come out to denounce the policy of Macron and show that politics in France happens here and not at the ministry , " Cecile Marchand , of Les Amis de la Terre , told the crowd . <p> But in a message on Twitter , France 's minister for ecological transition Francois de Rugy said the activists had " got the enemy wrong ... we are acting !
