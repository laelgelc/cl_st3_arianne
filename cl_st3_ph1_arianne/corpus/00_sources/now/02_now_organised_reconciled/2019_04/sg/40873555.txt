<h> Senior leaders will help next generation of leaders to succeed , says outgoing DPM <p> Relinquishing his appointment as Deputy Prime Minister ( DPM ) while continuing in Cabinet to support the new generation of leaders is the " Singapore way of ensuring smooth leadership transition , continuity and stability " , DPM Teo Chee Hean said yesterday . <p> " Senior leaders make way in good time for the next generation , share their experience and help the next generation of leaders to succeed , " added Mr Teo , 64 , in a statement on the upcoming Cabinet changes . <p> He also said Mr Heng Swee Keat 's promotion to DPM is " another important step in our leadership renewal " . <p> From May 1 , Mr Heng will be DPM while remaining as Finance Minister .
