<p> SINGAPORE - Drones which locate termites in hard-to-access spots in buildings and sensors that capture the movement of rats are among new high-tech weapons being adopted by pest control companies to solve age-old problems . <p> Innovative solutions like these are also helping to overcome issues plaguing the pest control industry such as manpower shortages and low productivity . <p> Minister for the Environment and Water Resources Masagos Zulkifli said on Wednesday ( April 24 ) that the sector will be the latest to come under the Environmental Services Industry Transformation Map , which was launched in December 2017 to drive innovation and raise productivity in the cleaning and waste management sectors . <p> He was speaking as guest of honour at the two-day Singapore Urban Pest Management Forum , organised by Singapore Pest Management Association ( SPMA ) . <p> Mr Masagos added that the three sectors share similar challenges , and that the integration will allow businesses to achieve greater efficiency . <p> " As our economy and consumption continue to grow , service demands and public expectations over performance standards will increase , " he said . <p> " Climate change may also lead to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a higher incidence of vector-borne diseases such as dengue . " <p> " Pest management businesses and employees must therefore innovate continuously , adopt new technologies , and improve efficiency and productivity to meet new challenges . " <p> Mr Gavin Raj , managing director of pest control service Verminator , said that using technology like thermal imaging is more effective in identifying infestations than " primitive " methods such as looking for them with the naked eye . <p> Besides boosting efficiency , technology can also act as a draw for younger , tech-savvy talent to the labour-intensive industry , according to PMA president Andrew Chan . <p> " If you want fresh graduates to go out on the field spraying ( pesticides ) - I think that 's totally impossible , " he said . <p> " But if you have new technologies , or smart systems , where the data collected can be sent back to the office for them to study , they will be more interested .
