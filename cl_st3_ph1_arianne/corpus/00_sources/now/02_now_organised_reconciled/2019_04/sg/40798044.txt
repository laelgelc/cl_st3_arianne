<h> Bleaching hits corals in far south : Scientists <p> SYDNEY * The world 's southernmost coral reef has been hit by bleaching this summer , Australian scientists said yesterday , as they warned that rising sea temperatures from climate change were affecting even the most isolated ecosystems . <p> The corals off Lord Howe Island - about 600km from Sydney - were affected by elevated temperatures this summer despite escaping severe bleaching that damaged the Great Barrier Reef in 2016 and 2017 . <p> " It 's a canary in the coal mine that we are seeing bleaching at this very isolated southernmost reef , which is worrying , " Associate Professor Bill Leggat of the University of Newcastle told Agence France-Presse . <p> " It 's just another indicator that climate change is affecting everywhere around the world . Here is a reef that is 600km from the mainland and we are seeing bleaching there in a beautiful ecosystem .
