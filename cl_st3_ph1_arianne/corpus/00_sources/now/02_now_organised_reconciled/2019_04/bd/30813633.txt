<h> Connecting the nation <p> There are tremendous benefits -- monetary and non-monetary -- to be derived from investing in the railway system connecting a nation . <p> On that front , Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina inaugurating the first non-stop intercity train service on the Dhaka-Rajshahi route should be welcome news , one that will hopefully serve as encouragement of similar initiatives moving forward . <p> The high-speed train -- which has 12 coaches -- will be running on the 343-kilometre route without any stoppage between Dhaka and Rajshahi , and cut down the travel time to four hours and 45 minutes . <p> A proper and efficiently functioning railway system will , in addition to giving the economy a boost by cutting down travel time between destinations , which helps transport both passengers and freight faster , also help reduce the untenable congestion on our national highways . <p> Additionally , with Bangladesh already feeling the effects of climate change , and the need for environmentally sustainable practices a more pressing need than ever before , investment into a more efficient railway network is the most environmentally and economically sustainable approach to addressing the country 's connectivity issues @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ tourism would get a boost , as tourists would no longer have to brave the traffic in order to visit different parts of the country , and this will also boost knowledge-sharing among the different regions , leading to more well-rounded , informative , and conscious citizens . <p> Ultimately , this will improve the overall quality of life of every Bangladeshi . <p> No nation can progress with sub-par connectivity , and there is hope that the government of Bangladesh realizes this , and continues to invest in the country
