<h> Traditional brick kilns to be closed down <h> Says environment minister <p> Environment , Forest and Climate Change minister Md Shahab Uddin yesterday said traditional brick kilns that are currently operating across the country will be shut down , owing to environmental hazards . <p> He said modern technology should replace existing brick kilns . <p> Shahab Uddin said this to reporters while visiting Department of Environment 's ( DoE ) Chattogram Lab at Khulshi , for the first time after assuming office . <p> " We will close down traditional brick kilns as it is harming the environment badly . For brick kilns we will formulate new guidelines so that they can be operated in an environment-friendly way , " the minister said . <p> He ordered DoE officials to take stern action against the kilns that pollute the environment by releasing black smoke . <p> " Only filing cases against them wo n't work . We have to water down the kilns if necessary , " he said . <p> Shahab Uddin asked officials to visit the factories on a regular basis to make sure they are not polluting rivers and water bodies . <p> He @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> Deputy minister Habibun Nahar and secretary Abdullah Al Mohsin Chowdhury of the ministry were also present .
