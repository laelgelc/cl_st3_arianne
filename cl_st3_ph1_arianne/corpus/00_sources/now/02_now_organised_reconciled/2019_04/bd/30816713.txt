<h> PM Hasina " flag bearer " of multilateralism , says Ambassador Masud <p> Dhaka , April 27 ( UNB ) - Permanent Representative ( PR ) of Bangladesh to the UN Ambassador Masud Bin Momen has said the world needs multilateralism more than ever before noting that Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina too is a " flag bearer " of multilateralism . <p> The Prime Minister has been attending the high-level week of the UNGA for consecutive 10 years now and is perhaps among the very few world leaders to have participated in adoption of both the MDGs and SDGs , he said . <p> The diplomat was addressing at the high-level plenary meeting of the International Day of Multilateralism and Diplomacy for Peace at the UN headquarters recently , said the Bangladesh Mission on Saturday . <p> Ambassador Masud said multilateralism can only happen if United Nations and the multilateral system as a whole embrace all stakeholders , business community , civil society and youth in particular ;
