<p> Rising inequality , increasing effects of natural disasters and high level of bribery and crimes are pushing SDG indicators in the wrong direction and will obstruct Bangladesh from achieving at least three sustainable development goals by 2030 . <p> The goals are SDG 10 , 13 and 16 , which aim at reducing inequality , combating climate change and ensuring peace and justice in society respectively . <p> The observations came in the " Overview report on four years of SDGs in Bangladesh " unveiled by the Citizen 's Platform for SDGs , Bangladesh , a forum of civil society , NGOs and the private sector . <p> The platform organised the day-long conclave to discuss and track Bangladesh 's progress in six SDGs since 2015 , at Bangabandhu International Conference Center in Dhaka yesterday . <p> The indicators for SDG 4 , 8 and 17 are moving in the right direction but require some policy interventions to steer them towards achievement by 2030 , Fahmida Khatun , executive director of the Centre for Policy Dialogue , said while presenting the report . <p> " On the other hand , the SDGs 10 , 13 and 16 are @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ changes and significant efforts from all stakeholders in order to reverse their trajectories , " she said at the opening of the conclave . <p> The meet took place ahead of the High-Level Political Forum ( HLPF ) on sustainable development to be held from July 9-18 in New York .
