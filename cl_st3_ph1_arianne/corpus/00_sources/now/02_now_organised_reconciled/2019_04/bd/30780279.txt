<h> BUP organizes environment festival <p> Department of Environmental Science of Bangladesh University of Professionals ( BUP ) , is going to have an environmental festival on Thursday , April 18 . <p> The department will organize the environmental festival , focusing on students of different schools , colleges , and universities to provide them guidance in building awareness for the conservation of nature . <p> The theme of the festival will be " Green Revolution , Best Solution . " <p> The program will be held on the university premises , with a variety of programs like a photography competition , an eco-olympiad , project models , poster presentations , and a seminar . <p> The seminar will be on " Achieving Sustainable Development Goals : Affordable and Clean Energy . " The Environment , Forest and Climate Change Minister , Md Shahab Uddin , will speak as chief guest at the seminar .
