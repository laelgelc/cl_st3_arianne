<h> Farm machinery fair : Local entrepreneurs flex muscles <p> A fair on farm machinery , that ended on Saturday in the capital , drew a large number of visitors Rajib Dhar/Dhaka tribune <p> Of the farm equipment , 79% is locally produced and the rest 21% imported <p> Agriculture equipment manufacturers are helping the country 's farmers mechanize and increase crop production by supplying quality machinery at lower prices . <p> They can make 50 to 60 varieties of agriculture equipment , enough to supply 70% of the machinery needed by the farmers , manufacturers say . <p> Of the farm equipment , 79% is locally produced and the rest 21% imported , according to Professor Monjurul Alam of Bangladesh Agriculture University . <p> The sales of machinery are on the rise as awareness among the farmers is rising , " says RK Metal proprietor Paritosh Kumar Malo . <p> " Agriculture equipment manufacturers are directly helping the country with higher crop production as well as mechanization of the traditional cultivation , " he tells the Dhaka Tribune . <p> Paritosh 's company took part in the National Farm Fair organized by the Ministry of Agriculture and Department @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Bangladesh ( KIB ) in Dhaka . <p> About 29 companies participated in the three-day fair , which began Thursday . <p> Another entrepreneur Kamal Mia , who is also secretary of Agriculture Machinery Manufacturers ' Association , started making farm equipment in 2009 with a small capital in Bogra but now his products are selling across country . <p> He now has 17 employees and annual earnings from the agro machinery stood at around Tk1 crore last year , he informs . <p> " If any imported equipment sells at Tk1 lakh local , the local one sells between Tk90,000 and Tk80,000 . If the government reduces duty on all raw materials , local entrepreneurs can produce all farm equipment . This will also create jobs , " he says . <p> Kamal Mia gives his technicians the credit for developing quality products and manufacturing new items based on their own ideas despite having no academic background . <p> Rafed-ul-Islam , director of Uttaron Engineering Workshop , says the government has given priority to the agriculture equipment industry to promote local manufacturers and mechanize the agriculture sector in order @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the quality of our equipment steadily , " he states , adding that mechanization is helping the agriculture sector produce more and reduce production cost . <p> Over 12 lakh farmers applied new technologies in their farms in 2018 , he mentioned . <p> The firm makes 48 types of agriculture equipment with prices ranging between Tk2,500 to Tk3 lakh . <p> Uttaron Engineering Workshop began producing agriculture equipment in 1997 and its earnings reached Tk3 crore to Tk4 crore last year . <p> Impediments to mechanization <p> Lack of the soft loan , decreasing number of farmers and depleting farmlands , adverse impacts of climate change are major hurdles in the way of farm mechanization . <p> Moreover , the sector needs financial support such as reduced duty and VAT for its expansion . <p> Steps needed for expansion of mechanization <p> The government should create mechanism so that farmers can purchase equipment without facing any hassle . Centres should be set up to extend services to the farmers .
