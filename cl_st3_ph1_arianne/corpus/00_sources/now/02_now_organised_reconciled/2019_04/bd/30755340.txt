<h> Nor'wester at 85 kph rips up trees in Dhaka <h> A nor'wester with a speed of 85 kilometres per hour hit Dhaka on Tuesday evening by the end of the Bengali month of Chaitra . <p> It lashed the capital at the highest speed this season , senior meteorologist Abdur Rahman told bdnews24.com . <p> The storm lasted for several minutes with the maximum speed for a minute from 6:04pm , according to the meteorologist . <p> He advised cautionary measures saying that such storms are likely to hit the capital in the beginning of Baishakh , the first month on the Bangla calendar , which starts on Apr 14 . <p> No casualties were reported in the storm as trees were uprooted at different places on Tuesday , an official at the Fire Service and Civil Defence control room said . <p> Similar storms on Mar 31 pulled up trees , sank a boat , and blew away bricks from buildings , killing at least six people in the city . <p> Locally known as Kaal Baishakhi , nor'westers are thunderstorms that generally blows over Bangladesh usually in April-May from a northwesterly direction . <p> Cumulonimbus , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ causes the thunderheads along with lightning strikes , heavy rains , rock storm , gusty wind and tornado in this season . <p> Scientists say warmer conditions associated with climate change are causing more water evaporation from the land and ocean , increasing clouds and rainfall and the potential for lightning storms . <p> Meteorologists advise people to stay indoors for at least half an hour from the beginning of storms in this season . <p> In Wednesday 's forecast , the Met Office said rain or thunder showers accompanied by temporary gusty or squally wind is likely to occur at many places over Barishal , Dhaka , Khulna and Chattogram divisions and at a few places over Rajshahi , Rangpur , Mymensingh and Sylhet divisions with hails at isolated places .
