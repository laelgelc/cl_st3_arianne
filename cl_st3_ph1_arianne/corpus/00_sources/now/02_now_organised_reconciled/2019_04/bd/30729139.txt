<h> ADB says Bangladesh can look up to Germany as a ' possible model ' for decentralisation <h> The Asian Development Bank , or ADB , has found Germany a " possible model " for the equitable development across Bangladesh through decentralisation . <p> Country Director of this Manila-based development bank Manmohan Parkash says Bangladesh needs a development model that promotes " equitable development " in the country -- a model which is " inclusive , sustainable and resilient " . <p> " We researched and found that Germany is a possible model , " he said on Tuesday as the ADB organised a seminar on " spreading equitable development in the country ' bringing German expert Ralf Schuster , head of Bank Non-OECD Countries , Helaba , Germany . <p> Schuster shared German development model which was achieved by decentralised demographic and industrial development , expanding SME financing and providing education , infrastructure and digitisation in local areas . <p> He said Germany manages to create job opportunities all over the country . <p> " You will find only four cities -- Berlin , Hamburg , Munich and Cologne -- with more than 1 million inhabitants , " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ only in big companies , but also in SMEs " . <p> " Even in small cities you will find good education and people need not to go big cities . " Schuster added that they have also dual education system -- both apprenticeship and vocational education together . <p> " I am the product of dual education , " he said , among many other aspects of German development model . <p> On being asked about the feasibility of German model in Bangladesh , he said it took 200 years for Germany to come to this stage . " Each and every country needs its own model . You have to find your own model . " <p> The ABD country director said Bangladesh has made rapid development and it is also facing developmental challenges . <p> " The higher economic growth may not necessarily be benefitting all regions and sections of the society . The Gini coefficient is increasing and so is the disparity , " Parkash said . <p> " Almost 2 million new workers get added to the society annually . Spreading industries including SMEs in the countryside will also help develop lagging regions . <p> " Also the country needs to create a population with a higher employment potential by providing quality education and developing and upgrading the skills of the labour force . " <p> At this moment , Parkash said Bangladesh needs policy directions , infrastructure development , giving thrust on innovation and skills development , access to affordable finance and capacity building of institutions . <p> Salman Rahman , however , said Bangladesh can not wait for 200 years for development and that there are many things that Bangladesh is already doing will ultimately lead to a German-style development . <p> " The process has already started .
