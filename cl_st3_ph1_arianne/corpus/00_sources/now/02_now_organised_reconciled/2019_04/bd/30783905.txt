<h> Why Kalboishakhi came early this year <p> The Kalboishakhi storm is one of the main indicators of the arrival of the Bengali New Year , or " Pohela Boishakh " in Bangladesh . <p> Pohela Boishakh marks the beginning of the New Year , and the beginning of summer . The Kalboishakhi storm happening earlier may very well indicate that summer is arriving earlier in Bangladesh . The scientific research on Bangladesh is sparse , but climate change being a global phenomenon , a lot can be inferred from the research that is based on other areas of the world . <p> According to scientific records and media reports , summer is arriving earlier every year in Europe as well . The early occurrence of the Kalboishakhi this year in Bangladesh may indicate the same thing happening here . The implications of this are major -- it means that climate change is no longer the big bad thing in the future , but something that is happening right now .
