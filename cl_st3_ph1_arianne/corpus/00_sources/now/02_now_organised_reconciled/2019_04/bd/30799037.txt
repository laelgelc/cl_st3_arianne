<h> Rohingya refugee youths celebrate storytelling success <p> The youths were trained in storytelling techniques including photography and videography <p> Thirty Rohingya refugees have graduated from the United Nations World Food Program ( WFP ) Storytellers program . <p> A graduation ceremony , on Saturday , featured Raba Khan as a special guest . <p> For the first time , the WFP -- with support from BRAC -- trained some Rohingya refugees in digital storytelling so that they could share their stories with the world . <p> WFP Country Director for Bangladesh Richard Ragan said : " There has been a huge media focus on the Rohingya crisis and the lives of those affected living in Cox 's Bazar . However , it is very rare that these stories are told by the people who have lived them . <p> " Through WFP Storytellers , young Rohingya men and women can share their stories with the world . We are delighted to unearth and celebrate some of the storytelling talent that lies right here in the Rohingya community , " he added . <p> The 30 youths learned storytelling techniques , how to take photos , and the basics @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ skills to act as communicators with , and on behalf of , their community . <p> Head of Operations , BRAC Humanitarian Crisis Management Programme Khaled Morshed said : " The WFP Storytellers project has been instrumental in giving the Rohingya refugees their own voice . <p> " I can think of no better way to empower them , " he added . <p> During the two-week training the WFP storytellers shared their stories of : loss , tragedy , hope , and everyday life living in the world 's largest refugee camp . <p> Hafsa Akter , 22 , one of the participants said : " We as Rohingyas have seen so much , and been through so much , and I want this to be understood from my own words and photos . <p> " Through WFP Storytellers I can see photography is very important for us to capture who we are , and to express our feelings , " she added . <p> Raba Khan from The Jhakanaka Project presented certificates to the graduates and also did a session , inspiring them to pursue their storytelling passions @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ its launch , WFP Storytellers has trained Syrian refugees and vulnerable men and women from : host communities in Jordan , Sudanese refugees in Chad , youths from remote areas affected by climate change in Guatemala , and South Sudanese refugees in Uganda .
