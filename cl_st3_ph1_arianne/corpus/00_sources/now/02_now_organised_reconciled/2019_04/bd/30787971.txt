While the Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change estimates that Bangladesh currently has 17 percent forestland , according to the Food and Agriculture Organisation ( FAO ) , it is only 13 percent . There are other organisations that have found our forest coverage to be much lower -- less than 10 percent . Shuknachhari forest is a sanctuary for wild animals including the Asian elephant , listed as an endangered species by the International Union of Conservation of Nature ( IUCN ) . At least 12 species of trees and shrubs , various species of birds , wild monkeys , wild hogs , pythons and other snakes , among other animals , live in the forest . <p> The forest was declared an ecologically critical area in 1999 by the environment and forest ministry . It is not possible that the public administration ministry is not aware of this .
