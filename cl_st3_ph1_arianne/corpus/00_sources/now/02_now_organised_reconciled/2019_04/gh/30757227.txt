. <p> The virtual trade hub is intended to make them adopt sustainable practices to make their products meet the standards of the international market . <p> Dubbed : Trade for Sustainability Development ( T4SD ) , the hub -- which is being implemented in five other countries -- focuses on the use of modern technology in the production value chain to ensure sustainable use of natural resources . <p> It is being implemented over a two-year period through a collaboration between the GPA and the International Trade Centre ( ITC ) , with funding support from the Swiss government . <p>
