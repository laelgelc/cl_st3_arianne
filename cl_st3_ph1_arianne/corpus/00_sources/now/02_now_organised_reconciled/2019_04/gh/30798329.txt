An easy brine for carrots , green beans , or zucchini ? In a small pot , mix 1 cups distilled white vinegar , 1 cup water , 4 cloves peeled and crushed garlic , 3 Tbsp sugar , and 2 Tbsp kosher salt . Heat on medium , stirring , until sugar dissolves . Pour this mixture into a 32-oz jar along with 1 lb desired vegetables , quartered lengthwise , and 3 sprigs of dill . Let cool slightly , then replace the lid and refrigerate at least 4 hours or up to 2 weeks .
