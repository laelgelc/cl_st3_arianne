<p> The Environmental Protection Agency ( EPA ) on Friday signalled its readiness to sanction individuals and firms who flout environmental laws as well as those operating without permits . <p> The Agency has , therefore , put in place proactive , effective and efficient systems including the training of senior officers on prosecution to deal with offenders . <p> Mr Kingsley Ekow Guray-Sey , the EPA Chief Programme Officer , announced this at a two-day training the Agency organised for Environmental Journalists in Accra . <p> The participants would be schooled on the EPA 's Mandate , Regulations on Waste , Chemicals , Mining and Climate Change to enable them to understand and better educate the public on the need to protect the environment . <p> Mr Guray-Sey said as part of the systems to ensure compliance was a proactive monitoring of companies in the country , especially those in the big cities . <p> " In the past , we have sanctioned culprits in the districts ... now we are in the process of prosecuting persons and companies , " he said . <p> Mr Guray-Sey recounted that prosecution in the past had not been as expected @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> The Agency , he said , was teaming up with the various metropolitan , municipal and district assemblies and the Ghana Police Service to form a tax force to deal with industrial effluent and noise pollution . <p> Mrs Angelina Ama Tutuah Mensah , the Head of Public Relations , EPA , said environmental protection was a shared responsibility and the public needed to support the Agency by volunteering information to the appropriate officers . <p> She noted that even though it was EPA 's statutory function to co-ordinate the activities of bodies concerned with the technical aspect of the environment , the public had a role to play in making this a success . <p> Mrs Mensah said the Agency would continue to initiate and pursue formal and non-formal education programmes for the creation of awareness on the
