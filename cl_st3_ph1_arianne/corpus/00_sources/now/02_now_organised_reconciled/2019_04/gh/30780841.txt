<p> The 2019 World Construction Forum has ended in Ljubljana , Slovenia with a call for heightened support for international development agreements . <p> Engineers worldwide have been urged to support deals such as the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development , the 2018 Paris Agreement on Climate Change and the Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction . <p> The Forum was convened in collaboration with the World Federation of Engineering ( WFEO ) in commemoration of the 100th anniversary of both the Slovenian Chamber of Engineers and the University of Ljubljana . <p> Participants pointed out that Engineering-related activity is an important driver in different economic environments directly contributing to GDP . <p> Participants further pledged to support the strategic goals of WFEO to advance the UN SDGs through engineering highlighting SDG#6 - Clean water and sanitation ; SDG#7 - Affordable and clean energy ; SDG#9- industry innovation and infrastructure ; SDG#11- Sustainable cities and communities , SDG#13 - Climate action and SDG#17 -- Partnerships for the goals . <p> The Declaration adopted after the conference made the following recommendations ; <p> - That to cover increasing global energy demand , smart power grids from more renewable and environmentally @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ fuels . <p> - Construction should embrace the digital revolution by creating high quality inexpensive , sustainable , smart interconnected and customized products for the end user . The backbone of a digitally engineered built environment is Building Information Modelling BIM that supports the evolution of real-time sensing and analysis of structural and environmental data to improve the capacity and resilience of buildings and infrastructure . <p> - Developing structural design codes , integrated tools for quantitative risk and resilient assessment and policies for retrofitting and upgrading the built environment . <p> - STEM education at the primary and secondary level is vital for creative and breakthrough thinking of the young generation and must provide a solid foundation for lifelong continued education to respond to the fast-paced digital revolution the world is experiencing . <p> The Ljubljana Declaration was signed by Dr Marlene Kanga WFEO President , Dr. Peggy Oti-Boateng UNESCO , Prof Matyaz Mikos University of Ljubljana and Ing . Crtomir Remec President Slovenia Chamber of Engineers .
