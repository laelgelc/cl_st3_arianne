<p> Speaker National Assembly Asad Qaiser has said that under the 18th Constitutional Amendment , the provinces should play their due role to uplift the living standard and status of the common people by making comprehensive public policies . <p> " The Constitution of the Islamic republic of Pakistan ensures the protection of the basic rights and provision of equal opportunities for progress and development of its entire citizens in the country . It is the duty of the government to provide basic facilities of education and health to the people . The 18th amendment in the Constitution has enhanced the importance of federating units and now provinces can play their vital role for uplifting the status of the common people , " the speaker expressed these views while addressing in inaugural session of two-day national parliamentary consultation on Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) here at Pakistan Institute of Parliamentary Services ( PIPS ) on Tuesday . <p> The speaker said that the incumbent government is striving hard to eliminate poverty , unemployment and illiteracy from the country . He said that he gifted one thousand kanals of personal land for establishment of Model Villages in KPK . He stressed on the parliamentarians to specially concentrate on the welfare of the people in their constituencies . <p> The speaker appreciated the efforts of Riaz Fatyana , convener of SDGs Task Force in National Assembly , for supporting the cause of SDGs in Pakistan .
