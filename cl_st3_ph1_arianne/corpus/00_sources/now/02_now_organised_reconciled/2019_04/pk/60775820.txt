<h> PM urges people to people contact , climate change awareness <p> Prime Minister Imran Khan made five suggestions to leaders attending the Belt and Road Forum in Beijing on Friday while assuring Pakistan 's support for the initiative . <p> He suggested that the members of the Belt and Road Initiative ( BRI ) undertake joint efforts to mitigate the effects of climate change . In five years we planted five billion trees in one province of Pakistan ( Khyber Pakhtunkhwa ) and we have now launched a nationwide project to plant 10 billion trees , he said , urging the leaders to make it a joint , ambitious project so that we can mitigate the effects of climate change . <p> His second suggestion was the establishment of a BRI tourism corridor to promote people to people contact and inter-cultural understanding . Another suggestion was to establish an office of anti-corruption cooperation to tackle white collar crime , which he described as a ' menace ' . <p> We must also set up a poverty alleviation fund that supplements national efforts to combat poverty and malnutrition , he suggested .
