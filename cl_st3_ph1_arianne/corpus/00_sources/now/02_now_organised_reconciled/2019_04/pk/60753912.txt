<h> Poster Competition On The Theme Of Environment Organised At GCU <p> A poster exhibition was organized at the Government College University , Lahore on the theme of environment by the GCU Biological society , on Thursday . Students and art lovers , from different universities , participated in the activity.GCU Vice Chancellor Prof Dr Hassan Amir Shah was the chief guest at the exhibition . Students from 45 colleges and universities displayed more than 83 posters at the GCU Abdus Salam Hall highlighting pertinent issues including water scarcity , climate change , plastic waste and threats to biodiversity . <p> Addressing the participants , the vice chancellor said the role of art in education and raising awareness in society was critical , adding that it enabled them to see the world and the human condition differently . <p> " Artists raise questions and compel us to think , " he added . The VC also talked about the importance of animal protection , green energy , organic food and recycling . Prof Dr Khalid Pervez lone also addressed the possible threats to biodiversity at the prize distribution ceremony .
