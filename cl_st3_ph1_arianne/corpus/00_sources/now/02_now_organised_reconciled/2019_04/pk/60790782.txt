<p> Senate Subcommittee on Climate Change , on Tuesday , launched a campaign to make Islamabad plastic-free by August 14 . <p> This pledge to make the federal capital " clean and green " was taken by Senator Mushahid Hussain Sayed as presided over the meeting held at COMSATS . <p> Committee Chairman also addressed the students and the faculty of the COMSATS ; seeking their support for this cause . <p> Senator Sayed further announced that the committee would seek to finalise a landfill site for garbage disposal in the capital . He also talked about fields visits during the month of Ramazan for cleaning the nullahs and other encroached sites in Islamabad . <p> The chairman asked COMSATS to prepare a comprehensive media awareness plan to achieve the noble cause of " Clean and Green " Islamabad with the support of students , media , civil society and the concerned citizens . <p> The event was attended by faculty and students of COMSATS Islamabad , Vehari Lahore and Abbottabad campuses . <p> Referring to his recent visit to China , Sayed said the Belt and Road Initiative ( BRI ) and its flagship project CPEC were announced @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Clean and Green " Silk Road . <p> He talked about a special focus on " Green Development .
