<p> The second Belt and Road Forum for International Cooperation is being held in Beijing from April 25 to 27 , and leaders including heads of state and government from nearly 40 countries will attend the forum 's roundtable meeting . <p> The Belt and Road ( B&amp;R ) Initiative , proposed by China in 2013 , aims to build a trade and infrastructure network connecting Asia with Europe and Africa along the ancient Silk Road trade routes to seek common development and prosperity . <p> A total of 126 countries , including developed and developing nations , and 29 international organisations have signed cooperation documents with China on the initiative . <p> Prime Minister Imran Khan , in his keynote speech at the opening ceremony , called for greater attention to tackling climate change and poverty as Pakistan and China enter the next phase of the China-Pakistan Economic Corridor ( CPEC ) . <p> The prime minister appreciated the significance of China 's Belt and Road Initiative ( BRI ) ,
