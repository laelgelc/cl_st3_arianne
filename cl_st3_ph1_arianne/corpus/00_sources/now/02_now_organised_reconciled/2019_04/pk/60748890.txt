It is a powerful and sobering call to arms , to save not only our planet but also our humanity . <p> In a review published in The New York Times , Jared Diamond said McKibben 's book " is much more about grounds for fear , which take up some 18 chapters , than about grounds for hope , which take up five . " Diamond added : " Fear will motivate some people who are currently undecided , and increase the motivation of others already convinced . But in my experience most people need a strong dose of hope to be spurred to action .
