<p> During the course of proceedings , the bench expressed concern over the federal and provincial governments ' efforts in the environmental pollution case , asking whether the government was a sleep as judicial orders were not being acted upon . <p> Justice Umar Ata Bandial remarked that environmental pollution was an important issue , but the federal and provincial governments were not cooperating with the environmental commission . <p> He remarked : The poisoning from factories is spreading ; there is no arrangement for toxic water reservoirs ; judicial orders are not being complied with ; is the government sleeping ? , " Justice Ata stated . <p> He observed that toxicity was increasing , owing to smokes that excreted from chimneys of factories . No proper measure has been taken to get rid of poisonous water , he added . <p> The court summoned the Secretary Climate Change for the next hearing and adjourned the hearing .
