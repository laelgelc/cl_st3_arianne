<h> ' Fearmongering is impacting government 's ability to make objective decision on GMO ' <p> CropLife Pakistan is an association of multi-national biotech firms working on seed technology in the country . While CropLife members in the past have been responsible for bringing hybrid seed technology to Pakistan , they have recently been advocating the introduction of Genetically Modified seeds , specifically GM maize . <p> While GMO technology suffers from a public perception problem , technical and academic voices have been virtually missing from the discourse . To address the knowledge gap , BR Research sat down for an extensive conversation with Muhammad Asim , chairperson of CropLife 's Seed subcommittee . The first part of this interview focused on developing an understanding of the science behind GMOs ; and was published in this section on Monday April 15 , 2019 , and can be accessed here : <p> The second part , reproduced below , focuses on the environmental safety and regulatory aspects . For it to be active against any other living organism , it needs two things .
