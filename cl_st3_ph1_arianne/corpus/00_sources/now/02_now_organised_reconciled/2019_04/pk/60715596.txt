" Wakker said before the car was modified , it would have used 6,785 litres ( 1,800 US gallons ) of petrol to complete the journey . <p> The modified vehicle can travel 200 kilometres on a single charge , with Wakker saying he spent just $300 on electricity , much of it in the remote desert Outback of Australia .
