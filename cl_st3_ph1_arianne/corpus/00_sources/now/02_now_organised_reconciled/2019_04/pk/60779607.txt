<p> The Employers ' Federation of Pakistan is proud of the fact that by observing the International Day on Health and Safety at Work almost religiously on 28th April every year for the last 14 years , the Federation has made a considerable impact in inspiring among its member organizations in particular and among business community in general to develop health and safety culture which is a prerequisite for productive and efficient performance of any organization today . <p> EFP have not only been observing the International Day on Health and Safety but have also initiated the EFP Best Practices in OSH Award to recognize companies whose best practices go under strenuous scrutiny and evaluation by an independent Jury to qualify for the winning awards on their best practices in different categories and sectors chosen for the competition . <p> During the last 14 years , more than 300 companies have participated in the EFP Best Practices in OSH Award Contest with an average of 25-30 companies every year . The number of such participating companies is increasing every year . This year 32 companies enrolled themselves for participation in this contest . What is necessary is not only to maintain this momentum but to increase it . <p> EFP is the pioneer in introducing internationally certified training course in OSH&amp;E in collaboration with ILO-ITC-Turin in English and Urdu language so that the corporate managers , supervisors , as well as workers can avail the benefit of this training . This certification course in English and Urdu has received a very good response from our member organizations . <p> As employers ' only body in Pakistan mandated in its role of advocacy , among others , in the area of social compliance , it is our resolve that the Federation will continue not only to maintain the momentum created by our efforts in this particular area of business concern but to increase the same with passing of every year . <p> " Health and Safety and Future of Work " is the theme of this year 's International Day on Health and Safety .
