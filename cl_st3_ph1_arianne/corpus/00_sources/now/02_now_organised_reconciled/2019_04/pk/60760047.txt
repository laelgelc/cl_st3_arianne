<h> China provides technical support to Pakistan in building infrastructure , mitigating natural disasters <p> Chinese Academy of Sciences has provided technical support to assist Pakistan in building its infrastructure and mitigating natural disasters , Cao Jinghua , director of international cooperation at the academy said . <p> The Belt and Road Initiative ( BRI ) has provided ample opportunity for scientists around the world to collaborate and use science and technology to solve challenges related to people 's livelihoods . <p> According to details , the Chinese Academy of Sciences has trained nearly 5,000 high-level scientific and technology students from countries and regions involved the Belt and Road Initiative since 2013 , including more than 1,500 doctoral and master 's degree students . <p> More than 700 foreign scientists from BRI countries have come to China for their postdoctoral work or visiting research , said Bai Chunli , the academy 's president . <p> They studied genetics , biodiversity , medicine , climate change , green engineering , new materials and other fields in which their expertise is highly valued . <p> " Many of these talented people have returned to their home countries and had a huge @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ than 1,000 administrative officials and staff members in the science and technology fields , and some currently , hold key government or research posts in their home countries . <p> Some examples include water management , public health and maritime environmental protection bureaus in Sri Lanka ;
