<p> ISLAMABAD , Pakistan : A high profile French Parliamentary Delegation headed by the Chairman of the Pakistan France Friendship Group in French Senate Senator Pascal Allizard met with the Pakistan Senate Parliamentary Friendship Group of the Senate headed by Senator Faisal Javed Khan at the Parliament House in Islamabad . <p> Senator Faisal Javed welcomed the delegation and observed that the bilateral relationship between Pakistan and France has grown over the years and the huge potential on both sides especially among the young population can take it to new heights . <p> Senator Faisal Javed remarked that more than 65 percent of Pakistani population comes under the category of youth and it can change the tide for the country . He said that after the new government has come into power , there is an enhanced focus on trade and business with different countries and our orientation towards France is no different . <p> Pakistani side expressed hope that France will explore investment in agriculture research , climate change , railway , surgical instruments , and technical training centres among other sectors .
