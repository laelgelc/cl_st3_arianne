<h> Clean , green highways provide friendly environment <p> KARACHI : Federal Secretary for Communications Shoaib Ahmad Siddiqui has said that clean &amp; green Motorways and National Highways provide friendly environment to the travelers . <p> " Clean &amp; Green Pakistan " drive initiated by the Government is a noble cause and we are to move forward hand in hand to make it a success , he said this while talking on the occasion of planting tree in the Service Area at Karachi -- Hyderabad Motorway ( M-9 ) on Tuesday . <p> Emphasizing upon the afforestation drive Shoaib Ahmad Siddiqui recalled lasting relation between human beings and trees . Lush Green Pakistan will not only provide pleasant environment to the people but trees also guard against calamities occurring due to climate change . Hence , he said , planting more and more trees is a national requirement . <p> The Ministry of Communications and its attached departments are making practical advancement to realize the vision of Clean and Green Pakistan , given by Prime Minister Imran Khan .
