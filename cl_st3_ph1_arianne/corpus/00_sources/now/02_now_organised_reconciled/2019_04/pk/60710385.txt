<h> Go native when it comes to plants and trees <p> People should think before planting trees and they should try to plant native trees as much as possible . <p> Shahzad Qureshi , the founder of Urban Forest , thinks that people need to start focusing on native trees . " We have to plant our native trees . They are a part of the eco-system , " he said on SAMAA TV programme Naya Din . <p> Native trees are those species have grown in a particular area after going through a number of climatic changes . They adapt themselves to the local systems and many fauna then become dependent on them . Unfortunately , most of the native trees are victims of deforestation and have been reduced to the verge of extinction . <p> " Find native trees and plant them like there is no tomorrow , " he advised the people . <p> Photo : Urban Forest <p> Some of the native species of Pakistan are neem ( azadirachta indica ) , the golden shower tree ( locally known as amaltas ) , almond tree , and peepal ( sacred fig ) . <p> Eucalyptus @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , is a native of Australia , he said . <p> He remarked that there is no fixed number of trees that can be planted to stop the effects of climate change . " The environment will start changing after we have dense forests . " <p> People , however , should n't just plant trees . " You have to take care of a tree for at least five years , " said Qureshi . <p> He said that his organisation takes care of plants . " We want to more forests in the country and we focus on dense forestation . " When you have a forest , it gives out 30% more oxygen and absorbs 30% more carbon dioxide , he added .
