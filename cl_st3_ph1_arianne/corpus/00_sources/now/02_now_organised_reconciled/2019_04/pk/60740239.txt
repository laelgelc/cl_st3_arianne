<h> Campaign launched to make Islamabad smoke free city <p> ISLAMABAD : The Federal government on Monday launched a campaign to make Islamabad a smoke free city . <p> In the first phase , Shakarparian and Pakistan Monument have been declared smoke free areas . <p> Speaking to journalists on the occasion , Minister for National Health Services Aamer Mehmood Kiani said the smoke-free campaign has been launched with a view to make Islamabad a model city not only for Pakistan but also the region and the world . <p> Referring to the adverse impact of smoking on the lives of the people , he said about one hundred and sixty thousand people die annually in Pakistan due to smoking . He said there is the law which prohibits smoking at public places but this was never implemented . <p> Kiani said that the smoking will now be banned at all the public places including the premises of National Assembly and the Senate .
