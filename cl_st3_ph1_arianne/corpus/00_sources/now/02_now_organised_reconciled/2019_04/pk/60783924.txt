<h> Pakistan Navy launches tree plantation drive in Manora <p> The Pakistan Navy on Sunday launched a cleanliness and tree plantation drive on coastal areas of Manora . Students and teachers of Dow Medical University also participated in the tree plantation campaign , said the Pakistan Navy spokesperson . He said the purpose of the campaign was to spread awareness about cleanliness and tree plantation . <p> Guest of honor on this occasion was Commandant Pakistan Naval Academy Commodore Waqar Mohammed . Commodore Waqar said the Pakistan Navy had taken responsibility of plantation on entire coastal line . " Forest plays crucial role in tackling the issue of climate change , " he said . The commodore said forest also played an important role in protection of sea environment . <p> On April 27 , Special Assistant to Prime Minister on Information and Broadcasting Firdous Ashiq Awan had rejected the opposition parties report on billion tree Tsunami as contrary to facts . <p> " This so called report is a political conspiracy and yet another drama on the part of the opposition , " Firdous Ashiq Awan had said this in a statement .
