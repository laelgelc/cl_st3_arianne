<p> Provincial Minister for Environment , Climate Change and Coastal Development Mohammad Taimoor Talpur said that a dedicated department to address climate change related issues would be established soon . " Climate change never been considered an issue in our country and now a dedicated department will be established to address and respond to climate change related issues , " he said , speaking at a meeting of Korangi Association of Trade and Industry ( KATI ) held to mark the " Earth Day . " <p> Taimoor Talpur said Sindh Government was working to improve solid waste management in Karachi . He said that requirements of staff would be provided to Sindh Environmental Protection Authority ( SEPA ) . <p> KATI president Danish Khan said that all institution , including KSWB , KMC and DMCs should collectively work to improve environment . He expressed concern over increasing pollution in the sea . He said government has asked to establish an effluent treatment plant for each industrial unit .
