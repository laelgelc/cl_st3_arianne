<h> UAF Walk Highlights Need For Climate Change Action <p> Experts at the University of Agriculture Faisalabad ( UAF ) have called for efforts to fight environmental hazards , preserve the nature and save the world from the devastating effects of climate change . The Institute of Soil and Environmental Sciences , University of Agriculture Faisalabad , took out a rally to mark the Earth Day . The UAF walk began at the institute and ended at varsity 's Iqbal Auditorium . <p> Institute Director Dr Javed Akhtar said it was need of the hour to sensitize the people about the environmental issues . He said we " have to create public momentum to mitigate greenhouse emissions , increase tree cover , reduce pollution and protect endangered species " . He added that the government 's initiative of Green and Clean Pakistan was a hallmark step that would bring tangible results . <p> Environment Protection Agency Director Shaukat Hayat said Earth Day was celebrated on April 22 every year .
