<h> ADB chief says bank will continue to grant loans to China <p> MANILA : The Asian Development Bank sees value in continuing to lend to China , its president said on Thursday , in response to calls for the institution to stop granting loans to the world 's second-largest economy . <p> ADB President Takehiko Nakao also said the multilateral financial institution 's lending to China " is not huge " so it will not crowd out borrowers from poorer countries . <p> " There is merit in lending to China . One we can have influence over such policies like climate change and the environment , which might have a positive impact on developing countries and to the region , " Nakao told reporters . <p> The Japanese government , which is a founding member of the ADB , has urged the Manila-based lender to stop lending to China on the grounds that it is rich enough to " graduate " from aid , the Nikkei has reported . <p> But while China 's share in ADB lending has been declining , Nakao said there are no plans of " letting China graduate immediately " . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ this income could also be used to support its operations in poorer countries . <p> China has been the bank 's second-largest sovereign borrower and is a major contributor to the institution 's development finance and knowledge sharing initiatives , the ADB said . <p> ADB 's committed loans to China have fallen to 12 percent of its total in 2018 from 19 percent in 2013 , Nakao said . <p> Founded in 1966 with a mandate to lift hundreds of millions of Asians out of poverty , the Japanese-led ADB has 67 member countries ranging from struggling Bangladesh and Pakistan to booming China and India , with its largest donors Japan and the United States .
