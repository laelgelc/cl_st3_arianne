<h> #EarthDay : The link between colonialism and over-exploitation of natural resources <p> People carry baskets of coal scavenged illegally at an open-cast mine in the village of Bokapahari in the eastern Indian state of Jharkhand . Mostly to blame are anthropogenic , or human-generated factors , including the burning of fossil fuels like coal and oil . <p> Other industries like gem and mineral mining also destroy the world 's ecological sustainability , leading to deforestation and the destruction of natural habitats . Much of this traumatic exploitation of natural resources traces its origins to early colonialism . <p> Colonialists saw " new " territories as places with unlimited resources to exploit , with little consideration for the long-term impacts .
