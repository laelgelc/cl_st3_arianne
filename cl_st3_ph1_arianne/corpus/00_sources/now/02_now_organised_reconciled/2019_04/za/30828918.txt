<p> This handout picture provided on April 26 , 2019 by the World Food Programme shows a destroyed house in Macomia district , Mozambique . - The back-to-back cyclones that have ravaged Mozambique are unprecedented in recorded history , the UN said on April 26 , 2109 , as it planned to examine the country 's defences against extreme weather in the light of climate change . Cyclone Kenneth , which smashed into northern Mozambique late on April 25 , hit " an area where no tropical cyclone has been observed since the satellite era , " the World Meteorological Organization ( WMO ) said in a statement . The government described the situation as ' critical ' in other centres of Cabo Delgado province , which are unreachable by road , and said the death toll could rise due to ' worrying ' rains , " according to IFRC . <p> Last month , Cyclone Idai destroyed the port city of Beira and submerged entire villages , vast swathes of land and 700,000ha of crops . It killed more than 1,000 people across Mozambique , Malawi and Zimbabwe . <p> The South African Red Cross is seeking donations in line with the IFRC emergency appeal to support 200,000 people with food , water , sanitation and hygiene ; shelter , health , livelihoods and First Aid and protection services over the next 24 months . <p> Activities will be implemented by Mozambique Red Cross , supported by ICRC , IFRC , and Red Cross Societies in Comores , Tanzania , Zimbabwe , South Africa and Malawi .
