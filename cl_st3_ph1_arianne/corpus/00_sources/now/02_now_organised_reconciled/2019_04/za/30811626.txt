<h> Editorial : With no life , there are no voters <p> Our climate is collapsing . At least 70 people died in KwaZulu-Natal this week , as tonnes of rain smashed into the ground and water tore through poorly built infrastructure . Hundreds died in Mozambique as Cyclone Idai ripped an entire city apart . The maize crop is staggering under the effects of last year 's drought , intense heat and now vicious hail . <p> All of this was predicted . This is the inevitable outcome of a global system in which we do n't pay the true cost for the things we use . Shopping centres and mines are built in the wetlands that used to slow down floodwaters . Oil refineries dump poisoned air into the lungs of children , denying them the chance to create a better future for all of us . Power plants pump carbon dioxide into the atmosphere , trapping heat and warming the planet that we live on .
