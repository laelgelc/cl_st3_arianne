Andrew Digby , a science adviser to New Zealand 's kakapo recovery operation , said . <p> Digby oversees a breeding programme so precisely monitored that scientists can state the last of 249 eggs laid will hatch on Friday . <p> It will significantly boost the population , which has grown to 147 adults since a small number of the plump green , yellow and black birds were discovered in 1970 . <p>
