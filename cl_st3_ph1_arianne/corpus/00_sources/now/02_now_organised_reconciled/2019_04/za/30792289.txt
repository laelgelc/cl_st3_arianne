<p> A polar bear has ended up pretty far from home - and he 's giving locals quite the spook . <p> This week , the estimated 2-year-old bear was spotted in the Russian village of Tilichiki on the country 's Kamchatka Peninsula . The bear has been seen roaming around the village , which is 700 kilometres from his home in Chukotka , CNN reports . <p> Experts think the bear traveled such a far way by drifting there on an ice floe . <p> Experts say the bear , who at this stage in development would typically be near its mother , ended up in Tilichiki because of climate change . <p> " Due to climate change , the Arctic is getting warmer , hunting environment gets smaller and less convenient , " Vladimir Chuprov of Greenpeace told the Associated Press . " The ice is receding , and polar bears look for new ways to survive . And the easiest way is coming to people . " <p> A plan has been set in motion to execute a rescue mission this week .
