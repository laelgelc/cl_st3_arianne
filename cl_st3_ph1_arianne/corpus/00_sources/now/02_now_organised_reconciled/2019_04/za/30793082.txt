Now most scientists agree that it really is happening and that a lot of the changes they have recorded over the past 50 years have happened because of what humans are doing . <p> " Global warming is affecting us in many ways that we can see and feel -- heat waves , droughts , flooding , storms , decrease in crop yields , rising sea levels , to list a few .
