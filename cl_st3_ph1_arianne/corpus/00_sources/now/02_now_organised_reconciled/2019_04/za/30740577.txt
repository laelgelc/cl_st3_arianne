<h> Google disbands AI ethics board after one week of existence <p> A week ago Google announced that it had created an ethics board to monitor how the tech giant utilised artificial intelligence ( AI ) , but the board 's lifetime is rather short-lived , with it recently being dissolved , as per a report from Vox . <p> Ever since it was first announced , the AI ethics board ( called the Advanced Technology External Advisory Council ) was mired in controversy , not regarding the subject matter that they would be handling , but rather as a result of the people it choose to assign to this panel . <p> More specifically Google was criticised for the appointment of Kay Coles James to the panel .
