The Zionist Federation would have your readers believe that the South African government 's towards Israel is the result of the ANC 's stupidity , and that somehow the ANCs political strategists are too dense to realise that the " hating the Middle Eastern Country has never garnered the ANC votes or won it any international favour " . <p> The claim is plainly absurd : for a start SA 's Muslim voters are 20 times the size of the country 's Jewish populations so at least along ethno-religious lines , there are very real incentives for the ANC to be seen @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , the core of the ANC and its brand is as a liberation movement ; the cost of protecting that brand is worth the spend , because as a liberation movement its voters remain loyal and forgive its failures and excesses . <p> Frankly it is impossible to understand how the liberation narrative applies to the Israel-Palestine conflict except for siding with the Palestinians . And , considering the current global consensus on the need for an end to the occupation , if SA wants to be friends with any other foreign power it is much easier to do so as part of the conversation about protecting Palestinians than through arguing against Israel being victimised . <p> Much to the disdain of many pundits , the ANC is actually an incredibly astute political strategist and its decisions tend to be extremely well thought out through a real politics lens . It has remained in power now for 25 years and it has done so through careful alliance building . It has punted well above its weight on the international stage such as in getting access to the Brics even though @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ other members . <p> Nothing demonstrates SA 's diplomatic prowess more than the fact that while the SA government lambastes Israel in public to get votes , it courts Israeli investment , trade and co-operation in private . There are no SA against Israel , both Israel and SA afford their respective citizens visa free access and organisations such as the Zionist federation are allowed to operate in SA freely and with the protection of the government . <p> That is the crux . The job of the Zionist Federation is to protect Israel 's image abroad . The federation is a club of Israeli political actors who task the organisation with protecting their interests abroad . While the Zionist Federation of SA continues to do a fantastic job of sustaining Jewish support for Israel and raising money from SA Jews for Israel , it is a fundamental failure in its core mandate . Instead of recognising that the public 's distaste for Israel is due to the federation 's own crappy public relations strategies , it tries to convince readers that the fault is its audience is stupid . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is objectively stupid in this debate . <p> If the Zionist Federation wants SA to be more supportive of Israel , it needs a better strategy . It needs to start building alliances rather than telling people that disagree with it that they are stupid or antiSemitic or self-loathing Jews . It could also start a conversation about why the international community should like Israel any more than any other country . <p> It is not like Israel deigns to contribute aid to global goods even though the country is now richer per capita than the UK . It is not like Israel carries its weight in absorbing refugees : actually , Israel is less generous to refugees than many dictatorships and its multiyear campaign to expel African ( not European or Asian migrants ) migrants has garnered it an ugly association with bald-faced racism . <p> Israel could , for example , get involved globally in climate change : it certainly has positive stories to tell when it comes to renewable energy such as solar . It could also do what other developed countries do and turn criticism into @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ excesses , invite the international community in , honestly ask for advice and support . After all there is no army on the planet that is immune from excesses that destroy innocent lives : frankly it is an obligation of every democracy to constantly check the excesses of their militaries . <p> Israel 's plummeting reputation in SA diplomatic circle is at least in part due to the Israeli government and the Zionist Federation fundamentally failing to make a case for why Israel is a positive force for good . Blaming that failure on other people 's stupidity is more a sign of executive incompetence than ANC stupidity .
