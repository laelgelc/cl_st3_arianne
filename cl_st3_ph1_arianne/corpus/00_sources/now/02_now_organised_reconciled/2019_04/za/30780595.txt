Thousands of classrooms were destroyed , and children thrown out of school . <p> Massive food shortages are projected . <p> " This natural disaster once more shows the importance of building climate-resilient economies in the African continent , " said African Development Bank ( AfDB ) President , Akinwumi Adesina . <p> The financial institution is leading reconstruction efforts in Malawi , Mozambique and Zimbabwe in the aftermath of the deadly cyclone . <p> AfDB noted in the wake of natural disasters like cyclones , initial efforts concentrate on humanitarian relief @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> However , in addition to immediate relief support , reconstruction and economic recovery are paramount . <p> " Indeed , while aid is trickling in , a big push is needed to help rebuild the three countries , " Adesina said . <p> AfDB is leading fundraising efforts from private sector sources for the affected countries . <p> Bank experts are currently assessing key reconstruction priorities and working to mobilise resources from the international community , global philanthropic organisations and the general public . <p> To kick-start proceedings , a high-level AfDB delegation , headed by Vice President for Corporate Services and Human Resources , Mateus Magala , visited Mozambique in the first week of April to assess firsthand the massive devastation to crops , villages , infrastructure , businesses and social amenities . <p> Mozambique President , Filipe Nyusi , assured the delegation the bank 's assistance would be key to help victims restart their lives . <p> Sebastian Rhodes Stampa , the Deputy Head of the United Nations Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs in Mozambique , described the rebuilding stage as critical . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ organisations like the AfDB to lead reconstruction efforts .
