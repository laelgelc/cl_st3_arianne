<h> Govt hails research scientists <p> Government through the Ministry of Agriculture , Irrigation and Water development has saluted the commendable job agricultural research scientists are doing in the country to address challenges slapping productivity in the agriculture sector . <p> The remarks were made at Chitedze Research Station where the 61st annual Chitedze agricultural research field day was held with the message " Promoting Agriculture Innovation for Improved Food Security , Nutrition and Economic Stability . " <p> Speaking to Malawi24 , principal secretary in the Ministry of Agriculture Irrigation and Water development Grey Nyandule Phiri said government is impressed with the path research scientists have taken in cracking down barriers that have been standing against the wish of government to ensure food security in the country . <p> According to Nyandule , government will not sit and watch but will join and use all formulas from the book to make sure that the development reaches even down to the smallholder farmer . <p> " As government we are very happy that we have such activities in the country because this has shown that we are responding to the challenges Malawi is facing in agricultural productivity .
