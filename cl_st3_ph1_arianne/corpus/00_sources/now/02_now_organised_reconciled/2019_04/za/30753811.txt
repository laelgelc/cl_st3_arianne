<h> Political Analysis South Africa <h> South African minister : Stop undocumented migrants from travelling to South Africa <h> South African minister : Stop undocumented migrants from travelling to South Africa <p> Published on <p> 9 April , 2019 <h> Undocumented foreign nationals are not welcome in South Africa and should stay " home to control human displacements , " Small Business Development Minister Lindiwe Zulu has said . <p> The minister was speaking to the media during the ruling African National Congress ( ANC ) party 's manifesto briefing on international relations and cooperation on Sunday , 7 April 2019 . <p> Zulu , who is the chairperson of the international relations committee in the ANC , urged neighbouring countries to control their borders to stop undocumented citizens from travelling to South Africa . <p> " This is in line with the Paris Climate Change Agreement of recommitting South Africa to its responsibility in the fight against climate change , and working with other countries to incentivise their population to stay within the borders of their countries of origin , " Zulu was quoted as saying by the Sowetan newspaper . <p> According to the paper , the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ integration and cooperation policies included a resolution of immigration matters , especially where there were concerns of human displacements and undocumented immigrants . <p> " The ANC-led government is working closely with neighbouring countries as well as the global community to ensure better management and coordination of people 's movement from different countries , " she said . <p> Zulu 's comments follow the xenophobic attacks on African immigrants three weeks ago where their shops were looted in the port city of Durban . <p> Some 100 repatriated Malawians were expected to arrive home Monday afternoon , 8 April 2019 , with the aid of the International Migration Office and the Malawi diplomatic mission .
