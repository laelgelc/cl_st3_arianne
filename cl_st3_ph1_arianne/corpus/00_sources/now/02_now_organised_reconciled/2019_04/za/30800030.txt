But perhaps the question is wrong-headed . For to ask " what " is to invite the same answer year after year . The more poignant question is this : Why Davos ? <p> When the WEF was founded by German economist Klaus Schwab in 1971 as a not-for-profit organisation and then reinvented in 1987 as a platform for " improving the state of the world by engaging business , political , academic , and other leaders of society to shape global , regional , and industry agendas " , it seemed timely . <p> By @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ new world order " had left deep scars . As the Bretton Woods system -- represented by the International Monetary Fund ( IMF ) , World Bank , and later the World Trade Organisation ( WTO ) -- that has underpinned global governance , trade and development for close on eight decades has come under increasing strain , the need for a fresh approach has been self-evident . <p> Against a backdrop of what is now perceived to be malfunctioning global governance institutions and stalled international policymaking , the reinvention of the WEF in January 2015 under the Swiss Host-State Act , as an International Institution for Public-Private Co-operation , was an acknowledgement that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ requirement of the emerging global order . <p> To be fair , after years of warnings , most business leaders , politicians and economists seem to have got the message . Even the WEF 's Schwab conceded at this year 's meeting that : <p> " Globalisation produces winners and losers and there are many more winners in the last 24 , 25 , 30 years . Despite a broad-based recovery in the global economy during 2018 , the latest IMF data in this area has shown that rising inequality poses downside risks to the strength and durability of a more inclusive economic growth path . <p> The wealthy are getting wealthier and the poor poorer . In advanced economies , the incomes of the top 1% have grown three times faster than those of the rest of the population and considerably more than populations in developing economies . <p> What is frequently underestimated is just how concentrated world economic growth is in only a few countries .
