<h> Cape Town 's R8m Lotus Park electrification project progressing well <h> The city says it has shifted a large part of its focus to the formalisation of informal settlements . <p> The city of Cape Town says its R8 million project to electrify 700 homes in Gugulethu is progressing well and should be completed by the end of June . <p> The city 's mayoral committee member for energy and climate change , Phindile Maxiti , visited residents in Lotus Park in Gugulethu to check on the project for the electrification of the informal structures which are located on land belonging to the Passenger Rail Agency of South Africa ( Prasa ) , which has given the city permission to complete the electrification work . <p> " The work of this department must be seen as part of a wider approach by the city to enhance basic services to residents , " Maxiti said . We remain committed to improving the lives of our residents in informal settlements .
