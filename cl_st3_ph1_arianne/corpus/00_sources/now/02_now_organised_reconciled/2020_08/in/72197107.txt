<h> Can Iceland survive another crisis ? <p> Iceland is a small Nordic nation with a population of about 350,000 . Historically , the country has been largely dependent on fishing . It was n't until the late twentieth century that aluminium smelting took off in the country , which in 2008 accounted for 39 percent of exports and 17.5 percent of GDP . However , from the early 2000s , the financial sector of the country began to dominate its economy , so much so that the assets owned by the banks exceeded US $100 billion , about ten times the size of the Icelandic economy . Hence , when the Global Financial Crisis hit in 2008 , it hit the Icelandic economy hard . Its banks were burdened with toxic assets due to their exposure to the subprime mortgages , the leading cause of the crisis . In addition to this , such investments were made through debt borrowed from the Dutch and English depositors , which made things even worse .
