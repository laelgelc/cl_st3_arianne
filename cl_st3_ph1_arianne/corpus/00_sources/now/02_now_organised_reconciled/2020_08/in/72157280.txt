<p> The government on Tuesday tightened norms for import of certain chemicals , which have the potential to adversely affect the environment . <p> The conditions have been tightened for about 18 chemicals , including carbon tetrachloride , chlorodifluoromethane and **25;1877;TOOLONG , the Directorate General of Foreign Trade ( DGFT ) said in a notification . <p> " The notification amends the policy conditions for imports of various chemicals , " it said . <p> The Directorate added that importers of the certain chemicals have to submit a copy of the bill of entry within 30 days to the ozone cell in the Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change . <p> It added that the import of **28;1904;TOOLONG is prohibited except for feedstock application . This chemical is used by foam manufacturing enterprises and it is one of the most potent ozone-depleting chemicals after chlorofluorocarbons . <p> Carbon tetrachloride is used in some industries and dry-cleaning units .
