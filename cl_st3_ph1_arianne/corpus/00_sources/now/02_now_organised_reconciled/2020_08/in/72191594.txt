<h> Unleashing the potential of data analytics to solve global challenges for humanity <p> It is only when we channelize the potential of data analytics in connecting people , finding ways to measure and control , and bringing about new discoveries that it can help in multifarious ways to address global issues <p> We live in a data-driven world where data is being generated at a staggering volume . With over 3 billion active social media users worldwide -- which isalmost half of the earth 's population -- the Internet is chock-full with an incredible amount of data . Each day around 500 million tweets are shared ; 4 petabytes of data is created on Facebook ; and more than 5 billion searches are made on the World Wide Web.This is just a small portion of the global data sphere , which according to IDC is expected to reach 59 Zettabytes for 2020 . <p> One of the main reasons that data is growing at an exponential pace is because of the varied methodsof passive data collection , which is made possible through inexpensive information-sensing devices . With such a vast amount of data being generated and stored , there is potential to convert this data into valuable information . This information can then be used to achieve significant breakthroughs for the betterment of society and the natural environment . <p> Opportunities hidden within data for solving global issues <p> Business organizations across the world are using emerging intelligent technologies , such as artificialintelligence ( AI ) , machine learning ( ML ) , cloud , edge , and more to harness the potential of structured ( logs , sensors etc. ) and unstructured data ( images , videos etc. ) to bring out actionable insights . By leveraging such insights , businesses are becoming more proficient and their conversations with consumers are becoming increasingly personalized . <p> However , the potential of data science still remainslargely untapped . In 2015 , the United Nations identified17 Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) that impact the global community and the environment . Emerging technologies and data analytics can help effectively address most of these global issues , making the world a better place to live in . And steps are already underway . <p> Take for instance the case of healthcare where remote monitoring is now a reality . Additionally , predictive healthcare , personal fitness and genome research are advancing at great lengths . The machine-generated data is either stored on a device or in the cloud . The compute runs standard algorithms on the data and then triggers input actions , such as call an ambulance , alert the doctor or prompt a threat alert . <p> Changing the world one kilobyte at a time <p> Some of the key areas where enterprises can utilize the capabilities of data analytics to create solutions for tackling existing global challenges are : <p> Climate change : The existing storage mechanisms have made it possible to store large volumes of data garnered from populations across socio-economic strata , historic temperature measurements , and flood and drought statistics and so on . Machine learning tools can further analyze and share deep insights to understand global climate patterns that can be leveraged to addressglobal environmental issues , such as pollution , deforestation and food scarcity . <p> Deep learning can help us sift through clusters of data and use statistics to identify critical weather patterns and detect anomalies . Machines can then use this data to structure probability models that can predict the next drought , hurricane , or cyclone in the upcoming year . In addition , such models can be used to manage natural resources and save endangered flora and fauna . <p> Eradicating poverty : Complex data mining algorithms hold great potential for predicting individual behavior in a given environment . Big Data collected across from satellites , social media , and other sources can be used to supplement traditional techniques for collecting data along socio-economic parameters . For instance , satellite imagery is being used to determine the concentration of poverty by analyzing night-time light measures across geographical areas . <p> More recently , a team of researchers from Stanford University used satellite imagery from day and night time with AI to compute the level of poverty in villages across Africa and analyze the changes in development over time . Such analysis of past and current data can better inform the economic policies of the government for alleviating poverty . Accurate , timely and structured baseline data is essential when determining ways to reduce the effects of poverty . However , most current studies are limited to a handful of data sources . A computational framework must be set in concreteto derive data from multiple sources to provide actionable insights . It can even be used to ensure sustainable production and consumption patterns in the agriculture industry , by adapting farming practices , boosting crop yields and tackling hunger . <p> Improving public safety : Predictive analytics can be used as a key tool for ensuring public safety -- thanks to analytics tools that can be used for running through vast volumes of data to identify patterns and maintain law and order . Be it creating smart homes with IoT applications , efficient surveillance systems , or firewalls for cyber security , data analytics has a key role to play everywhere.City police and fire departments can collate data from various sources andleverage smart analytics to manage public safety . For instance , in many countries , such as the United States , specialized mobile crisis teams are integrated into the local public safety systems that use evidence-based analytics instead of anecdotal reasoning to address issues such as public intoxication , substance abuse and dispute resolution . <p> Data can be best described as a high-value strategic input that can bring about meaningful changes in today 's world . There is likely not a kilobyte of data we create that is worthless . By building effective means for data collation ; data storage ; data analysis ; and designing unbiased algorithms , we can unlock the true potential of predictive analytics .
