Millions lost their livelihoods practically overnight , and with it the capacity to feed their families . But what served as a societal shock absorber of sorts was the huge foodgrain stocks held by the state-run Food Corporation of India . <h> An estimated 81 crore beneficiaries are being provided 5 kg of rice/wheat per person free over and above their monthly entitlement under the Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana . <h> Such largescale distribution of free foodgrains was possible only because the country had attained self-sufficiency in food production over the previous decades . About 70 per cent of rural households depend on agriculture for livelihood , and 82 per cent of farmers are small and marginal . <h> Access to food <h> While farm output has kept pace with population growth over seven decades , the country still faces food insecurity : millions do not have access to adequate and nutritious food .
