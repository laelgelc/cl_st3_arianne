As it becomes increasingly clear that a quick and sustained V-shaped recovery is not on cards anymore , it appears that only an earnest investment in finances , ideas and efforts for a long-term revival can pull us out of this void today . With sharp and sensible planning , this recovery could be resilient and sustainable -- one which helps us build back better . <p> For a truly holistic and informed revival , we must first admit that the pandemic has erupted due to a dysfunctional relationship between humans and nature . To reverse this predicament , we must invest in natural capital , primarily through the pathways of nature-based solutions ( NBS ) . Strong measures to mitigate climate change , halt biodiversity destruction and land degradation will pave the way to a cleaner and healthier environment , better economic turnaround and sounder living conditions for the people . <p> Globally too , nations are identifying green recovery through NBS as the way forward to stimulate the economy and build a sustainable , inclusive , cost-effective society . Studies by UNEP and other research organizations have suggested a great potential for NBS in facilitating economic revival , specifically through the restoration of the land . For the urban poor and the rural populations , landscape restoration efforts could offer direct benefits . Recently , a new tool developed by the National Biodiversity Authority called the biodiversity mainstreaming index ( BMI ) shows that five major public finance schemes ( namely , MGNREGA , National Rural Drinking Water Mission , Swachh Bharat Mission , Integrated Management Program and Atal Mission of Rejuvenation and Urban Transformation ) have maximum linkages with respect to conserving biodiversity . Investments in such nature-centric programs that are labour-intensive and employment-generating , can bolster jobs and enhance financial security for the youth while safeguarding our natural resources . <p> 3 ) Better jobs in villages , better benefits in cities : There are estimates of over 140 million job losses due to COVID-19 in India . Technological interventions can improve the internet , digital access and communication to support remote absorption of labour . <p> 4 ) Innovative finances : Smart mechanisms like blended finance should be tapped to fund NBS . Private sector involvement and contributions have great potential in powering the nation 's environmental and climate commitments . Corporate social responsibility ( CSR ) , a mandatory contribution made by companies under the Companies Act , 2013 , stipulates that businesses should invest a share of their profits for social causes such as environmental protection , education , poverty alleviation etc . However , only 5-6% of a company 's funds are diverted for environmental protection or nature conservation . While such schemes have a great potential to enforce corporate accountability , clarity in calculations and implementation is key to ensure a steady flow of corporate funds for environmental protection and conservation . In the recently published WEF report ' The Future of Nature and Business ' UNEP Chief Inger Andersen has said that restoration of land to achieve land degradation neutrality by 2030 is cost-effective , and will help reduce poverty and inequality . Indeed , for a sustainable economic recovery , alongside immediate measures like opening up the green job market for our youths , cataloguing funds flow for NBS and bolstering policies , a robust long-term natural recovery model must be built .
