<h> 03 THINGS TO DO TODAY <p> Join Duke Farm 's own federally permitted bird bander , Clifford Berek , as he discusses this unique field . Bird banding is the process of attaching a small band or tag around a bird 's leg in order to identify individual birds from the band 's unique number . During this process , ornithologists collect measurements such as age , weight , feather molt and other information . In this webinar called Behind the Scenes : Bird Banding , you will explore the history and current methods of bird banding . August 11 , 11.30 pm .
