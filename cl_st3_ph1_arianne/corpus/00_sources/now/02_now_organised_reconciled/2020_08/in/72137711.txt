<h> About nature <p> 29-Jul-2020 <p> <p> IMPHAL , Jul 29 : Irengbam Momocha Singh has been declared the winner of a photography contest held on the theme ' All about nature ' . The contest was held yesterday celebrating the World Nature Conservation Day , organised by the Wildlife Institute of India , Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change and Deputy Conservator of Forest ( Park and Sanctuary ) . Irengbam Momocha , a member of Wildlife Explorers Manipur from Sandankhong , Bishnupur secured the first position while Aklanta Madhav Kalita and Thuanriulung Gonmei secured second and third position respectively.Staff and publisher of The Sangai Express , Laishram Shamungou Singh bagged the 4th place while Ayan Dutta secured 5th position.The winners of the contest were announced during a webinar held to celebrate the World Nature Conservation Day.Wildlife Institute of India representative , Dr Chongpi Tuboi , Wildlife Habitat Protection Society , founder , Moirangthem Loiyangamba and others attended the webinar.
