<h> 32 species of ' bat community ' found in Himalaya in U'khand <h> Share <h> 32 species of ' bat community ' found in Himalaya in U'khand <p> Tuesday , 25 August 2020 Archana Jyoti New Delhi <p> At a time when the debate over the linkage of bats with the novel coronavirus pandemic continues , scientists have identified as many as 32 species of the flying mammals in the picturesque western Himalayan region in Uttarakhand . The finding is important given that the ultrasonic echolocation calls of nine bats were recorded for the first time in the world while one was documented for the first time in South Asia . <p> It is the first study to document echolocation calls of bats in Himalayas . The finding has been published in the recent edition of journal Acta Chiropterologica . <p> Additionally , the researchers also sequenced genes belonging to 13 species , of which eight had never been sequenced before in the region . <p> There are over 1,400 species of bats worldwide while in India at least 128 species of bats have been recorded , belonging to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the recorded bat species in India , according to the Zoological Survey of India . <p> The identification of bats species in Himalayan region will help the scientists to monitor populations , understand behaviour , and better understand natural history . <p> The study has been conducted jointly by researchers from the Nature Science Initiative in Dehradun , the Indian Institute of Science in Bengaluru , and the Natural History Museum of Geneva . <p> Individuals from a total of 35 species were humanely trapped and studied before being released back into the wild , according to the researchers . The study was conducted much before the Covid-19 happened . <p> Bats play a crucial role in a number of varied ecosystems besides being as indicator of the impact of climate change and human activity on the environment , as their sensitivity to environmental changes is very high . This group of mammals in the past several decades , have been repeatedly linked to the emergence of viral diseases , including SARS , MERS , and most recently , Covid-19 which has swept the world . <p> " In the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ bats and virus linkages , we are receiving calls for removing bats from trees and also cutting trees , " R. Sharath Babu , wildlife advisor to Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike said , as per reports . <p> However , in a statement issued a few months ago , 64 chiropterologists from six South Asian nations have debunked myths on Covid-19 and bats .
