This includes the requirement for respect for the knowledge and rights of indigenous peoples and members of local communities and the full and effective participation of relevant stakeholders . <p> Additionally , in order to receive results-based finances from REDD+ implementation , the Warsaw Framework requires a recipient country to develop a system for providing information on how safeguards are addressed and respected . <p> Additional safeguard requirements may be required to be followed by developing countries .
