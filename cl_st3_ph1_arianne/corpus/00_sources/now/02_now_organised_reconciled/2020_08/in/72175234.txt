<h> Trees vs roads in city 's periphery <p> On Monday , a eucalyptus tree fell from the DG 's residence premises on to the KG Road , and had to be cleared out <p> Three major infrastructure projects are expected to swallow up hectares of cultivated land and fell several trees <p> The peripheral areas of the City are likely to witness a dramatic change in human settlements and land use pattern with massive impact on the environment over the next 5-10 years . Three major road infrastructure projects such as Peripheral Ring Road ( PRR ) , Satellite Town Ring Road ( STRR ) and widening of six two-lane roads are expected to eat up hectares of cultivated land and nearly 60,000 trees . Environmentalists warn of dire consequences . <p> A few kilometres after the Outer Ring Road , the Karnataka Road Development Corporation Ltd ( KRDCL ) is building a four-lane road around the City , totalling around 130 km . The road-widening project has proposed to axe 8,561 trees . Further ahead , the 65-km PRR project is heading closer to execution .
