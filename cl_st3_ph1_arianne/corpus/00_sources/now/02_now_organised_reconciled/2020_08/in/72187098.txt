<h> Moments of solitude with Mother Nature <p> Oswald Fernandes <p> World over , Nature is reverently referred to as Mother Nature and her very first act of service is to provide the soul with a body . Like a caring mother , she gives us the food that nourishes and sustains our bodies as also water that cleanses and gives us life . It also provides fresh air to supply us with oxygen , which is vital to keep bodily functions going . <p> It also provides materials -- from hay , firewood and timber to rubber , gum , minerals and countless other things -- that have made our lives easier . <p> When one thinks about how much humanity is still served by Nature in spite of man 's depredations that have brought about the phenomenon of climate change , one begins to wonder how marvellous Nature must have been in its original , unpolluted state . <p> The law of Nature is a pattern of ceaseless motion and change in which every natural process is irreversible and leads towards a state of decay or disorder , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ try to restore balance and minimise pollution and disorder . <p> The fact that the environment is polluted and abused today is because we have forgotten that we are intimately connected to Nature , as is any baby to its mother . <p> Arrogantly treating her as an object for reckless exploitation in the name of our perceived , but often only imaginary needs , as well as desecrating the original beauty of our planet , we have fouled and blocked our life-giving umbilical cord . <p> Hence , as humans , we must accept the responsibility for our ill behaviour towards Mother Nature and change our way of thinking as well as our way of life for the common good of all . This would be the first and fundamental step to genuine partnership between humans and Nature . <p> We must put hard efforts to develop new insights and inspiration on how to protect our home by developing a deep understanding that by destroying environment , we are destroying ourselves .
