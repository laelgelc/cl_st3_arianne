<h> Demand for withdrawal of EIA 2020 <p> Authors and environmentalists have written to environment minister demanding withdrawal of the Environment Impact Assessment 2020 draft . The draft is open for public comment till August 11 . Author and conservationist Prerna Singh Bindra , along with writerconservationist Vaishali Rawat in a letter have listed down several flaws and concerns to the environment in the draft . <p> In a media statement , they claimed support of over 100 reputed researchers , conservationists , ecologists and allied professionals . The letter mentioned , " The EIA 2020 draft notification directly contradicts the basic precautionary principle of environmental governance . It legitimises post-facto clearance , which means that even in cases where a project has come up without environment clearance , the project can continue to run . <p> " Thus , the notification proposes to regularise violations that may occur , when instead it needs to take stringent action against the defaulting project proponent and shut down violating projects .
