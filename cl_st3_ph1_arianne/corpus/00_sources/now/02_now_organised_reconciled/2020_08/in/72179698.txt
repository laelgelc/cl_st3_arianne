<h> Tezpur University generates electricity from solar power <p> At a time when the world is fighting Climate Change and exploring alternative ways to generate energy , Tezpur University is prioritizing the use of renewable energy for generating electricity for its buildings and classrooms as a part of its sustainable campus initiatives . <p> The University considers five thematic areas i.e. energy , water , waste , natural ecosystems and sustainable building for its mission towards sustainable campus , varsity authority said a statement . <p> In order to develop Sustainable buildings ( also known as green buildings ) , the University has installed 1000 kWp rooftop grid connected solar power plant under Solar Energy Corporation of India Limited ( SECI ) scheme for government buildings under RESCO ( Renewable Energy Service Company ) mode and has effectively replaced 24% of the monthly electricity consumption of the University during October 2018-July 2020 period . <p> " The University purchases electricity from Assam Power Distribution Company Limited ( APDCL ) at the rate of Rs. 6.60/kWh , whereas the solar power plant generated electricity cost is Rs. 2.42/kWh " , it @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ from commercial rates " , said Sadhan Mahapatra , associate professor , Department of Energy , who is associated with installation of rooftop grid at the University as the coordinator . <p> Currently , the power plant is installed at 15 building roof spaces at the University . The total electricity fed into the University grid during October 2018 to July 2020 is 1968345 kWh . <p> The monthly average electricity generation from the plant is 89470 kWh . The maximum generation in one day is 5938 kWh/day and the average daily generation during this period is 2949 kWh/day . The maximum generation was 112149 kWh in the month of August 2019 . <p> The University also reduces 1614 tons of Carbon Dioxide ( CO2 ) emission due to solar power generation during this period . <p> " Solar roof top projects are focused to solar systems installations on rooftops of residential , commercial , institutional and industrial buildings .
