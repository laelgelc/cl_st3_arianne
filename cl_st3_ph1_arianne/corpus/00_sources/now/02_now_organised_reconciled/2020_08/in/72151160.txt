<p> New Delhi : Environmental activists and civil society groups are up in arms against the Tamil Nadu state government allegedly for pushing through a new hydroelectric project in the ecologically fragile Nilgiris . It has been alleged that the state-run Tamil Nadu Generation and Distribution Corporation ( TANGEDCO ) has gone ahead with the proposal for the power project without conducting a public hearing or an environment impact study . <p> However , as per TANGEDCO , no rule of law is being flouted in the execution of the project and a public hearing will be conducted in due course of time .
