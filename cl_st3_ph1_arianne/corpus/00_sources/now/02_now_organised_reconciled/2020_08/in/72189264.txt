<h> Last Updated : Wednesday 19 August 2020 <p> About 70 per cent data on compensatory afforestation was " incorrect or incomplete " , according to a letter Inspector General of Forest , AK Mohanty . The letter , dated August 10 , 2020 , was sent to principal secretaries for forests of all states and Union territories . <p> The data in question was for work done under the Compensatory Afforestation Fund Act , 2016 and uploaded on the ministry 's e-Green portal . <p> The letter stated : <p> It is being continuously observed that despite repeated requests from the Forest Survey of India ( FSI ) , a significant percentage of data being uploaded , particularly the polygons , is either incorrect or incomplete . For example , only 30 per cent of the uploaded polygons have been found to be correct as of now . <p> The Compensatory Afforestation Fund Act aims to restore forest cover , lost due to diversion of forest land for non-forest purposes such as mining and industrial use , in the country . <p> Development and industrial projects such as erection @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ require diversion of forest land . Any project proponent -- government or private -- must apply for forest clearance from Ministry of Environment , Forests &amp; Climate Change ( MoEF&amp;CC ) before the conversion of land can take place . <p> This proposal is submitted through the state 's forest department concerned . If clearance is given , compensation for the lost forest land is decided by the environment ministry and regulators . <p> However , due to discrepancies in the implementation of compensatory afforestation , the Supreme Court ( SC ) on July 10 , 2009 ordered a Compensatory Afforestation Fund Management and Planning Authority ( CAMPA ) as the national advisory council under the chairmanship of the MoEF&amp;CC . Its aim was to monitor technical assistance and evaluate compensatory afforestation activities . <p> The same was institutionalised under the Compensatory Afforestation Fund Act , 2016 . <p> The SC in its 2009 order also talked about the need for an information system to monitor the compensatory afforestation activities , following which MoEF&amp;CC created e-Green Watch in 2012 . <p> The portal
