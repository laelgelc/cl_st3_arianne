The ' Commonwealth Advantage ' enables member countries to trade up to 20 per cent more with each other than other non-Commonwealth countries , at a 21 per cent lower cost , on average . Members also invest up to 27 per cent more within the Commonwealth than outside of it -- almost triple the estimates from five years ago . This investment effect is particularly strong in Africa .
