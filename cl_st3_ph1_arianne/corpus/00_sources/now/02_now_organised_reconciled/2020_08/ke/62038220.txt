<h> British Council raises Sh3.5mn to support fashion designers in the wake of COVID-19 pandemic <p> NAIROBI , Kenya , Jul 29-The British Council is working with local fashion designers to boost the growth of a sustainable creative industry in the face of the Covid-19 pandemic through a programme aimed at developing their skills , knowledge and global networks . <p> In response to the COVID-19 pandemic and the challenges it pose on creative businesses in Kenya , the Creative DNA programme will make available 11 seed grants valued at Sh3,580,000 from the initial Pre -- COVID 19 allocation of five seed grants that were each valued at Sh655,000 . <p> This has been done to increase the ability of all participants to innovate and chart a new path for their businesses . <p> Led by Kenyan partner , Metta Nairobi , and supported by UK based partner Fashion Scout , 11 fashion businesses are undergoing a 12-month incubation programme between February 2020 and February 2021 that includes a bootcamp , a digital fashion showcase , competitive seed funding and a UK study visit scheduled for 2021 . <p> Through @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ( CDNA ) programme explores three areas of work namely , research and insight , policy and advocacy and business support . <p> Through the focus on research and insight the British Council will publish a needs analysis report for the Kenyan fashion sector produced by Collective RW and independent UK researcher Jan Miller ; a case study on one of Kenya 's oldest markets , Uhuru Market , in partnership with HEVA Fund ; as well as an analysis of the potential use for alternative fibers and raw materials in Kenya led by the Kenyan chapter of Fashion Revolutionaries . <p> In response to the potential risks associated with a physical fashion showcase the programme will be presenting an innovative , digital fashion showcase scheduled for an online launch in August 2020 . <p> With support from a Creative Director , each designer will have a curated digital room that highlights their work and inspiration . <p> The showcase will increase the visibility of the 11 designers and ideally grow their customer base in Kenya and the UK . <p> Advertisement .
