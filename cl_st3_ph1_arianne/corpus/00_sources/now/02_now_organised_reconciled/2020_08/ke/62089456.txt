<h> The Allure of Vertical Forests <p> NEW ORLEANS -- The Vertical Forest buildings of the architect and urban planner Stefano Boeri make the most of the often overlooked and profound contributions of a single tree . <p> The Vertical Forest prototype was first constructed four years ago in Milan , Italy , where a pair of apartment buildings also housed 21,000 plants and 20 species of birds . <p> Cities cover only about 3 percent of the Earth 's land , Boeri said during his presentation at the conference . But they are responsible for 70 percent of the world 's energy consumption and 75 percent of the carbon dioxide emissions that contribute to climate change , which threatens to significantly alter New Orleans and the Southeast , according to a federal National Climate Assessment report released last month . <p> The beauty of incorporating more trees and plant life in urban architecture is that the trees both assist in absorbing carbon dioxide and in producing more oxygen . <p> The process begins by selecting plants that would be suited for the environment of the city .
