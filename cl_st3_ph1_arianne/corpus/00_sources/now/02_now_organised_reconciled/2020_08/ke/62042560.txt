<h> Transmission of COVID-19 to newborns via breast milk highly unlikely <p> Transmission of COVID-19 to newborns ( neonates ) is thought to occur primarily through respiratory droplets during the postnatal period when they are exposed to infected mothers or other caregivers . <p> What this means is that transmission of the virus to the newborn via breast milk is highly unlikely . <p> Generally , COVID-19 infections in neonates are uncommon , if they do get infected postnatally , the majority are either asymptomatic or with mild disease and they recover . <p> Severe COVID-19 in neonates appears to be rare although the ones with underlying medical conditions and preterm infants may be at a higher risk of severe illness . <p> Get breaking news on your Mobile as-it-happens .
