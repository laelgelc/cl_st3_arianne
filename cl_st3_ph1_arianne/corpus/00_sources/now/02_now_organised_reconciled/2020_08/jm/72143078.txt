<p> Amid the challenges being experienced with the current pandemic , Jamaica , through the Ministry of Finance and the Public Service ( MoFPS ) is becoming a leader in the global conversations to ensure that climate change is at the forefront of the economic recovery . <p> Jamaica announced its official membership to the Coalition of Finance Ministers for Climate Action a year ago , which currently includes 52 Finance Ministries worldwide . <p> In a recently published report , " Ministries of Finance and Nationally Determined Contributions -- Stepping Up for Climate Action , " the MoFPS articulated the role of Ministries of Finance to engage in the preparation , update and implementation of Nationally Determined Contributions ( NDCs ) . The report was produced by the MoFPS in collaboration with the Ugandan Ministry of Finance , NDC Partnership and the World Bank . <p> Case studies in the report show that Finance Ministries are contributing to the costing , macroeconomic and macro-fiscal assessments , and the mainstreaming of efforts to reduce national carbon emissions . <p> The report also shows that countries have used multiple entry points @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to the core of economic and financial decisions . <p> To achieve faster progress , public finance systems and economic policy tools , including macro-modelling and fiscal planning , should be improved to better capture climate targets and integrate them in the designing of long-term financial strategies , in tandem with the work under NDCs . <p> Minister of Finance and the Public Service , Dr. Nigel Clarke noted , " Jamaica , like many other countries , is grappling with the economic impact of the COVID-19 pandemic .
