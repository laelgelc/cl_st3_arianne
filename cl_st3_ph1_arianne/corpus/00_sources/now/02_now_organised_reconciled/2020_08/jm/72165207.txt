<p> USD$100,000 has been allocated to Jamaica and will be used to support immediate needs such as access to food and hygiene supplies and provide income and livelihood support including psychosocial support for some of the most vulnerable including women and girls , the elderly , persons living with disabilities and individuals who have lost their jobs due to the COVID-19 . <p> UNDP , who will be administering the funding on behalf of Canada , will be partnering with the Government of Jamaica through the Bureau of Gender Affairs and the Climate Change Division , UN Women and Civil Society Groups to carry out this work . <p> More broadly , EnGenDER is a key initiative that seeks to address the issue of climate change impacts particularly on vulnerable groups such as women , children , youth , persons living with disabilities , and indigenous and tribal populations . <p> In light of COVID-19 , a portion of the project funding has been tailored to meet the needs of the host-countries ' response to the pandemic . <p> " Canada continues to be a supportive and responsive partner in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ work through the EnGenDER project is yet another example of that commitment -- in this case by delivering timely and targeted support to allow Jamaica to strengthen disaster recovery mechanisms , " stated Laurie Peters , High Commissioner for Canada to Jamaica . <p> She further noted : " As a strong advocate when it comes to ensuring the health and safety of women and girls , Canada recognizes the considerable impact of this Covid-19 Pandemic and is pleased to work with UNDP and other partners as Jamaica seeks to advance its Vision 2030 national development agenda in these uncertain times .
