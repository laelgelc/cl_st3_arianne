<h> ActionAid : Livelihood the main driver of internal migration <p> The findings of the study , " Addressing climate induced migration in Bangladesh : Taking a human rights-based approach , " were disclosed on a webinar on Tuesday <p> Livelihood is the primary driver of internal migration in Bangladesh , although climate change is also a factor , according to a study by ActionAid Bangladesh . <p> The findings of the study , " Addressing climate induced migration in Bangladesh : Taking a human rights-based approach , " were disclosed on a webinar on Tuesday . <p> 254 people from 13 communities in five districts participated in the study . Of the five districts , Sunamganj , Khulna and Naogaon were source areas while Dhaka and Chittagong were destinations for migrants . <p> The study found that livelihood superseded river erosion and natural disasters as reasons for migration of the sample population . <p> It also found a lack of diversified skills in migrants , and that debt is a common reason for both seasonal and permanent migration . <p> A lack of local government mechanisms or information systems @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to the study . <p> " Cities such as Khulna and Sunamganj are not ready to host mass migration . This is why people are heading to Dhaka and Chittagong . But these two big cities and other cities are not adequately ready as safe , environmentally friendly , and rightful hosts for migrants , " the study said . <p> After migrating to another place , many families or communities are often forced to migrate again due to development interventions taking place . <p> In urban spaces , fear of eviction remains a constant threat and migrant families do not receive local government services as they are not voters in the area . <p> Migrant families in urban slums face environmental challenges as well as challenges related to basic services such as water , sanitation , and lack of social security . Migrant families also have no guarantee of work at their destinations . <p> In source areas , women reported that they face sexual and mental harassment from men in the community when male household members migrate . <p> Prof Dr Saleemul Huq of the International Centre for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ I hope that in the future , development plans , policies , and the means of addressing migration will be more focused . It could be economic or climate induced . <p> " We need to address climate induced migration properly , " he added . <p> Farah Kabir , country director of ActionAid , said : " Policy recommendations have to be more comprehensive .
