<p> Dhaka , one of the most densely populated cities in the world , has been grappling with air pollution for a long time . The air quality generally worsens during summer and shows signs of improvement in monsoon when the dust settles . <p> Environment , Forests and Climate Change Minister Md Shahab Uddin had admitted that the air pollution in Dhaka reached an intolerable level while the High Court issued a nine-point directive to bring down air pollution in the capital . <p> In an interview with UNB , he said brick kilns were responsible for 58 percent air pollution in the capital . <p> He said the Department of Environment ( DoE ) and the World Bank published a research report on the sources of air pollution in Bangladesh in March last year which identified brick kilns , fumes of vehicles and dust from construction sites as three main sources . <p> On January 13 , the High Court issued nine-point directive following a writ petition filed by Human Rights and Peace for Bangladesh ( HRPB ) .
