Utilities in countries which have invested heavily in wind and solar are burning fossil fuels ( as backup power ) 70% of the time . <p> Nuclear power is the best option to replace fossil fuels . China is building its first molten salt reactor ( MSR ) at Wuwei in Gansu Province . Coolant circulation is needed even after the reactor is shut down because of " residual radioactivity " ; after the chain reaction has been stopped , unstable fission products in the fuel continue to decay and generate heat . <p> At Fukushima , the reactors were automatically shut down when the earthquake hit ; however , coolant circulation was still needed to remove heat generated by residual radioactivity . In a conventional ( LWR ) reactor , the fuel is ( expensive ) uranium . In a MSR , the fuel is uranium mixed with ( very cheap ) thorium .
