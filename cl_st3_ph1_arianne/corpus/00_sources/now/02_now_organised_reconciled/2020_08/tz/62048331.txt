When levels of hydrogen or aluminum become too high -- and the soil becomes too acid -- the soil 's negatively charged cation exchange capacity ( CEC ) becomes " clogged " with the positively charged hydrogen and aluminum , and the nutrients needed for plant growth are pushed out .
