This involves using farming techniques that protect the environment . Sustainable agriculture has grown out of concerns over the industrialization of agriculture that began in the 20th century . <p> Although industrial agriculture has the ability to produce abundant amounts of food at affordable prices , the method of farming can be harmful to the environment . These methods are heavily reliant on chemical fertilizers and pesticides and put high demands on soil and water resources . <p> The methods can lead to water pollution when chemicals run off into waterways and deplete the water resources due to overuse . They can also cause soil erosion and poor soil quality due to intensive planting . With sustainable agriculture , farmers minimize water use and lower the dependence on chemical pesticides and fertilizers . They also minimize tillage of the soil and rotate crop planting each year to ensure higher soil quality . <p> Sustainable forestry also promotes environmental sustainability .
