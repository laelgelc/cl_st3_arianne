International Youth Day gives an opportunity to celebrate and mainstream young peoples ' voices , actions and initiatives , - <p> -as well as their meaningful , universal and equitable engagement . . <p> The theme of International Youth Day 2020 , " Youth Engagement for Global Action " seeks to highlight the ways in which the engagement of young people at the local , national and global levels is enriching national and multilateral institutions and processes , as well as draw lessons on how their representation and engagement in formal institutional politics can be significantly enhanced . <p> As the United Nations turns 75 , and with only 10 years remaining to make the 2030 Agenda a reality for all , trust in public institutions is eroding .
