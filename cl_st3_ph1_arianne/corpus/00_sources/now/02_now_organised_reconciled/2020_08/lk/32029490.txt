<p> Sri Lanka 's most respected tea and rubber producer , Hayleys Plantations has become the first plantations business sector of its kind in the world , and the first company in the island to officially commit to the globally recognised Science Based Targets initiative ( SBTi ) for cutting Greenhouse Gas ( GHG ) emissions . <p> The SBTi is the product of a collaboration between CDP Worldwide , World Resources Institute ( WRI ) , World Wide Fund for Nature ( WWF ) , and the United Nations Global Compact ( UNGC ) . The initiative is a well-recognised commitment that companies can make as part of the ' We Mean Business Coalition 's Take Action ' platform on climate change . <p> Moreover , SBTi specifies how much and how quickly a company needs to reduce its greenhouse gas emissions , in line with the Paris Agreement goals to limit global warming keeping it at 1.5 ?
