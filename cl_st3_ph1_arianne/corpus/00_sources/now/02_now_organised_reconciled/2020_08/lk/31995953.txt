<h> Floods may cost 20% of global GDP by 2100 : Study <p> PARIS AFP July30 , 2020- Coastal flooding and storm surges could threaten assets worth up to 20 percent of global wealth , according to research published Thursday highlighting the potential economic catastrophe that climate change may bring . <p> The team found that under a high-emissions scenario , in which humanity fails to curb its carbon pollution , up to 287 million people would be at risk from extreme floods by the end of the century . <p> Given the large number of megacities and financial capitals on the coastline globally , they calculated the value of assets under threat to be more than $14 trillion -- around a fifth of global GDP . <p> Researchers in Australia combined data on global sea levels during extreme storms with projections of sea level rises under different greenhouse gas emission scenarios .
