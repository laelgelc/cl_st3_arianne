We are so proud of what we have been able to achieve , and very excited about the future of our company . It is fantastic to have the Clean Energy Council 's support in our vision to unlock rooftop solar for those missing out . " <p> Allume Energy challenged itself to develop a product that solves the huge market gap of supplying solar energy to multi-unit buildings .
