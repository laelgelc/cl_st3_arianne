Those winds force ocean water to spin down into the water column , creating vertical circulation in the ocean below . Once the hurricane reaches shallow coastal waters , the ocean bottom interrupts this cycling . Unable to go down , the water pushes out onto land . <h> Storm surge can be deadlier than hurricane winds <p> While hurricane-force winds can rip roofs off of homes and take down trees and power lines , the surge of ocean water rushing inland often causes more damage .
