Examine almost any contemporary political problem , from Australia 's growing economic inequality to the declining performance of our school students relative to the rest of the world , to our dying coral reefs , and you will find the fingerprints of John Winston Howard . <p> Howard was the most right-wing prime minister we ever had , at least until Tony Abbott . Unlike Abbott , as ineffectual in office as Billy McMahon , the other usual nominee for worst-ever PM , Howard was highly effectual but in a regressive sense . <p> Let 's get down to examples , starting with the economy . <p> Saul Eslake , former big bank economist , has memorised a long list of the worst economic decisions of the past 20 years , and almost all the items on it are down to Howard and his treasurer , Peter Costello . <p> Eslake recites , rapid fire : " The halving of the capital gains @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is if you are over 65 you pay less tax on a given income than if you are under 65 . " <p> There was no rhyme or reason to it , except as an example of class prejudice and to benefit those on whom the government depended for support : the wealthy , the older and the self-employed . <p> " Self-funded retirees were a protected species really , " Eslake says . <p> The Howard government could afford it at the time because it was rolling in cash , courtesy of the mining boom . <p> A few prominent economists -- Eslake nominates himself , Ross Garnaut and Chris Richardson -- warned at the time it was unsustainable in the long run . <p> " Chris , Ross and I all used to say that what they were doing was converting a temporary windfall revenue gain into a permanent erosion of the tax base and a permanent elevation of the welfare spending base . Then came the global financial crisis -- a year after Howard 's defeat -- and Australia 's debt and government deficits have ballooned ever since . <p> Among the many ways in which the Howard government robbed the future , one standout is education policy , notably the way in which it damaged school education . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ scores comparing the performance of students in different countries , Australia ranked fourth for reading , sixth for maths and eighth for science , among 41 countries . Under the new model introduced by David Kemp , Howard 's right-wing education minister , the amount directed to private schools , particularly elite schools , was greatly increased . And so the inequity remains entrenched , and results continue to slide . <p> There are other ways in which Howardism inflicted lasting damage . <p> One of the first things he did on gaining power was to sack one-third of the heads of the public service .
