Staying below the upper objective of 2oC requires a halt to the burning of fossil fuels today . <p> That will obviously not happen , but the more carbon that is pushed into the atmosphere , the greater the overshoot beyond 2oC and the greater the catastrophic conditions we create for ourselves . <p> For Australia , as one of the hottest and driest continents , this is extremely dangerous , particularly for our rural communities and for Northern Australia . <p> The climate impact of carbon emitted @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ In these circumstances , to seize upon " backloading " as a key policy plank , deliberately encouraging Adani and other Galilee Basin coal mines , the expansion of domestic coal-fired power , and CSG which is worse than coal from a warming perspective , is the height of irresponsibility , for it would automatically lock-in catastrophic outcomes . <p> Emissions have to be reduced now , not a decade hence .
