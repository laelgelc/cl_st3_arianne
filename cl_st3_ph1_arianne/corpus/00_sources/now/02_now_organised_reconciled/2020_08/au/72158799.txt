<h> Rights of indigenous peoples continue to be trampled on , with equality a distant goal <p> The United Nations has declared 9 August the International Day of the World 's Indigenous Peoples , highlighting the diversity and uniqueness of indigenous cultures . <p> Rani-Henrik Andersson , university lecturer in North American studies at the University of Helsinki , points out that the majority of the world 's languages are those spoken by indigenous peoples . Furthermore , indigenous peoples inhabit some of the richest areas in terms of biodiversity in the world . Peoples classified as indigenous live on every continent . <p> " In certain areas , centuries-old colonialism has nearly eradicated their cultures , while in other areas indigenous peoples still have a strong presence . The definition of indigenous peoples has been difficult , an issue linked to questions of land ownership , culture , language as well as identity , " Andersson says . <h> Equality yet to be achieved , UN gives a voice <p> For decades , indigenous peoples have demanded an equal role among international operators .
