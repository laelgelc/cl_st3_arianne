We do n't need any more evidence that the Earth is warming to the point of mass instability , but this latest discovery is unsettling , to say the least . <p> Scientists at the National Snow and Ice Data Centre ( NSIDC ) said last week that the St. Patrick Bay ice caps in the northeastern Ellesmere Island in Nunavut , Canada , are nowhere to be seen on satellite imagery . Researchers long suspected that this would be possible , but the prediction has come a bit sooner than expected . <p> An ice cap is a sort of mini-glacier . These formations stretch less than 49,987 , so they 're smaller than ice sheets . The St. Patrick Bay ice caps were truly tiny : In 1959 , the largest one was merely 8 , and the smaller one was only 3 .
