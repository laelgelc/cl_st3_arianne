<p> For University of Wollongong student Tim Wall , appearing in an ABC TV series seemed a great way to get his housemates thinking more about the environment . Mr Wall and his four housemates met in their first year at university and all moved into a Gywnneville sharehouse a year later . In 2019 he saw an ad for Craig Reucassel 's new TV show Fight for Planet A , which was looking for households to take part .
