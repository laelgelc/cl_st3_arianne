<p> The " mother of all murals " which created a stir at Thirroul pool has been resurrected by climate activists , led by Bulli 's own Orange is the New Black star Yael Stone who wants to undo a huge loophole in the city 's carbon emissions calculations . The mural , a mother earth figure nestled against the escarpment , was painted by northern Illawarra Extinction Rebellion activists last month and received a warm reception from the public before it was painted over by Wollongong City Council because it had not been approved . It was part of the group 's " dilemma " installation strategy - prompting people to ask questions about why something apparently beneficial would be destroyed . Now Stone and fellow activists are repurposing the scene into a flyer urging residents to have their say about the council 's draft Climate Climate Change Mitigation Action Plan before August 12 .
