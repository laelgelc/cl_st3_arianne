<p> Australian advisory firm Pollination Group will partner with global banking giant HSBC to facilitate up to US$3 billion ( A$ 4.15 billion ) of investment in ' natural capital projects ' , that will project the environment and cut greenhouse emissions . <p> It 's a major deal for the Australian based Pollination Group , which is headed by Martijn Wilder , who has substantial experience in the development of energy and climate change policy both in Australia and internationally , and most recently was chair of the Australian Renewable Energy Agency , which , ironically , has been starved of funds by the Australian government . <p> Pollination Group was co-founded by Wilder and investment banker Tony O'Sullivan to provide specialist climate change advisory services , facilitating climate friendly investment opportunities .
