<h> COVID and the rise of renewables make summer blackouts less likely <p> The rapid development of large-scale renewable energy projects and record popularity of rooftop solar panels have boosted the reliability of Australia 's power supply , reducing the likelihood of blackouts this summer . <p> The coronavirus pandemic is also expected to reduce energy consumption in the short term because of scaled-back commercial demand , according to the Australian Energy Market Operator , which published its annual 10-year reliability forecast on Thursday . <p> The operator also predicts an ongoing decline in daytime electricity demand in all areas of the national energy market because of the continued strong uptake in rooftop solar installations . <p> The summer forecast reflects the lowest risk of forced rolling blackouts -- known as " load shedding " -- across the nation 's main power grid in recent years . <p> Advertisement <p> Loading <p> Last year , a string of coal or gas power plant outages and bushfire impacts left parts of the grid facing outages across several scorching days . <p> However , Thursday 's forecast warned Victoria -- the state @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to blackouts in the event of unexpected generator failures , transmission-line breakdowns , or if heatwaves coincide with overcast or still days that curb renewable power generation . <p> And AEMO said it would still seek bids for emergency power reserves this summer in order to reduce those risks .
