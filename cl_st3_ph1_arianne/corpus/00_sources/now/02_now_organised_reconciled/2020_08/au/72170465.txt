<p> Many species have declined sharply in recent decades , and as many as 90 of Australia 's 315 freshwater fish species may now meet international criteria as threatened . <p> No Australian fish species is yet listed officially as extinct , but some have almost certainly been lost before scientists even knew they existed . With so many species at risk , understanding which are in greatest peril is a vital first step in preventing extinctions . <p> This is what our new research has done . We 've identified 20 freshwater fish species with a 50 per cent or greater probability of extinction within the next two decades , and a further two with a 40-50% chance -- unless there 's new targeted conservation action . <h> Slipping through the conservation cracks <p> Many small-bodied species , including Australia 's smallest fish the red-finned blue-eye , look likely to be lost within a single human generation .
