<p> The dark clouds of low investments over the Indian startup ecosystem continues in the second week of August , as not much momentum was seen in the funding activity as compared to the first week of the month . <p> The total equity investment in the second week of August stood at $12 million , a very negligible change when compared to the $9 million in the comparable previous week . <p> In fact , since the start of the current financial year , this is the first time that venture investments into startups has fallen to a level below $15 million on a weekly basis . <p> For the second week of August , there were a total of nine equity deals , with a majority of the transactions concentrated in the early-stage deals .
