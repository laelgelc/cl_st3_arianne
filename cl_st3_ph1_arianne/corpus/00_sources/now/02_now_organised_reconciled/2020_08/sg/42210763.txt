Our earnings have dropped and we spend every waking hour trying to think of how to get new clients . " <p> The 14-year-old laundry was set up as social enterprise , one of many businesses with a mission to help society in India by providing economic opportunities for survivors of trafficking . <p> But like many businesses the laundry has been hit by the multiple lockdowns as India 's coronavirus cases crossed two million this month . India 's lockdown has left millions jobless and crippled businesses , including start-ups and social enterprises that have pioneered solutions aimed at improving services from health , education and housing , to providing sustainable livelihoods . <p> Providing work and financial security is seen as a crucial link in the rehabilitation of trafficking survivors . <p> " Like everyone else , we are also struggling but we can not afford to fail , " said @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Vimukthi Survivors Collective in southern Andhra Pradesh state . <p> " Their business of selling vadams ( homemade snacks ) to local hotels and messes along the busy highways has been severely hit .
