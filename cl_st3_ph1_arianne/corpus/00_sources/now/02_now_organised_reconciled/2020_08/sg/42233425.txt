<h> More than 150 people walked across one of the world 's largest islands during coronavirus to highlight a land dispute . <h> Share <p> More than 100 Indonesian farmers gathered outside the presidential palace in Jakarta on Monday , having walked about 1,800 km ( 1,118 miles ) from the island of Sumatra to protest against forced evictions from their lands . <p> About 170 farmers began the trek from Deli Serdang in northern Sumatra on June 25 to raise awareness of a long-running land conflict with a state-owned plantation company , PT Perkebunan Nusantara II ( PTPN II ) . <p> The group of 43 women and 127 men walked every day for about 45 days , starting at dawn and often late into the night , before arriving in Jakarta on August 7 . <p> " We have walked a long way , " farmer Imam Wayudi told the Thomson Reuters Foundation . " Today it has been 60 days since we left the homes of our wives and children .
