The economic cost of this ecological damage goes mostly uncounted . But it is prohibitively high , eroding the value of goods and services that nature produces . One million species are now at risk of extinction . <p> Fortunately , there is a relatively simple initiative underway to curb some of these losses and solve our looming conservation crises . Under the heading of " 30x30 , " it aims to protect 30 per cent of our planet 's land and oceans by 2030 through effective , permanent measures .
