<h> You are here <h> Draft of Singapore 's carbon pricing bill released for public consultation <p> THE government is conducting a second round of public consultation on the carbon tax as it releases its draft of the carbon pricing bill . <p> The bill sets out the overall carbon tax framework and obligations for large greenhouse gas emitters , including the measurement , reporting and verification requirements . <p> It will also give the National Environment Agency ( NEA ) the powers to make and amend regulations on related matters . <p> The tax will take the form of a credits-based mechanism , under which facilities pay the tax by buying and surrendering to NEA the number of carbon credits corresponding to their greenhouse gas emissions . <p> The draft bill was launched on the website of government feedback unit Reach on Tuesday morning . The consultation period for the draft bill will last for six weeks , till Dec 8 . This follows public consultation that the National Climate Change Secretariat ( NCCS ) had conducted earlier in March , on the plan to introduce a carbon tax @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ The government announced plans to implement a carbon tax of between S$10-S$20 on each tonne of greenhouse gas emissions from 2019 during the Budget session earlier this year . <p> The tax will be applied on power stations and other large direct emitters which produce over 25,000 tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent of greenhouse gases a year .
