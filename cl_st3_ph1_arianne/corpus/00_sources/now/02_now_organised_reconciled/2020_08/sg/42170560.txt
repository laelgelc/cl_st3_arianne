Just based on that , the correlation is very clear , " Rhita Simorangkir , a researcher at the National University of Singapore ( NUS ) and one of the authors of the study published in Science Advances , told Mongabay . " We already know the correlation , but we wanted to find the direction of the causation . The country lost more than 6 million hectares ( 15 million acres ) of natural forest between 2000 and 2012 ; by the end of that period , Indonesia 's annual deforestation rate had surpassed Brazil 's for the first time . <p> Rhita and her co-author , Paul Ferraro from Johns Hopkins University , began their research five years ago . They analyzed 7,468 rural forested villages across 15 provinces that participated in the PKH programme between 2008 and 2012 .
