<h> Greening sovereign-debt restructuring <h> The Covid-19 crisis has made painful sovereign-debt restructurings inevitable for many countries . Countries should offer more favourable terms to debtors that improve protection of natural assets such as rainforests , oceans and biodiversity . <h> Share <p> Many developing countries have so far avoided the high Covid-19 infection and mortality rates that are being experienced elsewhere . While that may be good news , at least in the short term , the bad news is that these countries are set to be among the hardest hit in economic terms . <p> The World Bank estimates that as many as 100 million people could fall into extreme poverty as a direct result of the crisis . This is only the tip of the iceberg , as developing-country exports collapse , and small businesses , communities , and livelihoods implode . <p> The fiscal implications are equally dire , with tax revenues in freefall and extraordinary increases in public spending . From Bangladesh to Brazil , developing countries are trying to keep their economies afloat through debt-financed public spending .
