<p> MONTREAL , Aug. 19 , 2020 ( GLOBE NEWSWIRE ) -- Bombardier ( TSX : BBD.B ) announced today that it has successfully closed the previously announced three-year $1.0 billion senior secured term loan facility ( the " Facility " ) with HPS Investment Partners , LLC , acting as administrative agent , collateral agent and the lead lender for a group that included investment funds and accounts managed by HPS Investment Partners , LLC and Apollo Capital Management , L.P. , or their respective affiliates , and Special Opportunities and Direct Lending funds managed by Ares Management LLC . <p> The Facility will have a minimum utilization of $750 million and a term of three years . Bombardier will have the right to voluntarily prepay the outstanding amount of the Facility .
