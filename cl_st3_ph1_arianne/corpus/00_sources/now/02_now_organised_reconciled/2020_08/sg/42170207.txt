During the last three months , private cars stayed parked , factories curtailed operations , and power plants stopped burning coal . <p> As a result , carbon dioxide emissions have fallen dramatically -- even returning to 2010 levels . But , even if true , the respite will be brief , and do little to change our long-term climate trajectory . <p> In fact , the same researchers who reported that Covid-19 lockdowns had caused emissions to drop also reported that , when lockdowns have been eased , emissions immediately rose . They predict that the pandemic will reduce total emissions in 2020 by 13 per cent , at most , and only if some restrictions remain in place worldwide until the end of the year . Once we return to the pre-pandemic " normal , " so will emissions . <p> Sustaining lockdowns -- which disproportionately hurt the poorest and most vulnerable -- is not the answer .
