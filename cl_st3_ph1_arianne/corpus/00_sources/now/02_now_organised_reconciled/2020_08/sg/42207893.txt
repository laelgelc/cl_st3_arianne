<p> An aircraft hanger at Changi Air Base using solar panels , and retrofitting work at One Raffles Link that will save the equivalent of $120,000 a year in power costs , were among those given a coveted award by the Building and Construction Authority ( BCA ) . <p> The BCA Green Mark was conferred on 376 projects , including 16 overseas , this year , the authority said yesterday . Green Mark certifications were launched in 2005 to evaluate a building 's environmental impact and performance . <p> The aircraft hangar and One Raffles Link were among 22 of the 376 projects that attained the top Green Mark that designates a super-low energy building . Only 39 projects involving 50 industry players have received this coveted award . <p> The BCA said : " The trend of building owners going for higher tier Green Mark certifications ( Green Mark Platinum and above ) is indicative of the industry 's awareness of the positive impact of green buildings and recognising them as key in our efforts to fight climate change . " Major Singapore developers , such as CapitaLand , City Developments and Keppel Land , have also been setting targets for reducing energy use and carbon footprint in their building portfolios , " the BCA added . <p> CapitaLand won this year 's Green Mark Platinum Champion Award , which recognises developers that demonstrate strong commitment towards corporate social responsibility and have achieved a substantial number of Green Mark Gold buildings or higher . <p> The developer installed 21,000 solar panels atop logistics building LogisTech and five other industrial properties here . This will enable its corporate offices in three locations to be totally powered by renewable energy by the end of the year . This will help the firm reduce over 700 tonnes of greenhouse gas emissions each year , equivalent to emissions generated annually from 400 four-room Housing Board flats . <p> CapitaLand has secured $1.2 billion in green loans that will be used to accelerate its efforts towards greening its portfolio by 2030 . <p> The BCA also collaborated with the Health Promotion Board to develop a Green Mark @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ provider Space Lab One won the award this year for a project with designs supporting mental well-being and energy-efficient lighting , as well as programmes that help employees in areas such as healthy eating , and chronic disease management . <p> BCA chief executive Hugh Lim said : " Aside from environmental performance throughout the building life cycle , the Green Mark scheme also takes into consideration building occupants ' health and well-being - an immediate concern now with the pandemic .
