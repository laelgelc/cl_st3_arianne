A Zoom Link will be sent closer to the Event . " <p> Here in Singapore , we love our food and we can not lie . With a plethora of dishes and flavors from all corners of the world , It 's no wonder eating is the ( unofficial ) Singapore 's favorite pastime . <p> As Singapore imports over 90% of our food supply , we are exposed to the volatilities of the global food market , which include sudden disruptions to transport routes , export bands , climate change and most recently , the pandemic . To combat this and ensure food security , Singapore has set an ambitious 30-by-30 goal . In other words , by 2030 , the nation state aims to produce at least 30 per cent of its food needs . But to that very ambitious end , we need to rethink our approach to how we feed our country . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the boundaries of innovation in what we consume . Our lineup of speakers are creating plant-based seafood alternatives , creating sustainable and safe methods for milk production , and getting you to eat your veggies by turning them into delicious snack . <p> Our panelists will share the breakdown of the complexities to overcome in order to drive innovation in this sector .
