<h> Indonesia 's new emissions target actually a step back : Greenpeace <h> The new target might seem like an improvement over Indonesia 's previous commitment to cut emissions growth by 26 per cent over projected business-as-usual levels by 2020 , but it actually represents a regression , Greenpeace says . <p> A rainforest in Bali , Indonesia . Indonesia is a leading carbon polluter , mainly due to deforestation and peatlands degradation . <p> Greenpeace says while the " 29 per cent by 2030 " target is vague and still needs be cleared up , it appears to actually be a step back from the earlier commitment . <p> That 's because moving the temporal goalposts back by so many years more than negates the slight uptick in the growth percentage to be reduced . <p> According to Greenpeace , " 26 per cent by 2020 " would have seen a reduction in real emissions from around nearly 1,500 million metric tons of carbon dioxide equivalent ( MtCO2e ) in 2010 to around 1,400 MtCO2e in 2020 .
