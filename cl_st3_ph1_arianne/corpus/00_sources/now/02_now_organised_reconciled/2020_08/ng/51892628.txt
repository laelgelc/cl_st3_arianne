<p> The National Biosafety Management Agency has assured Nigerians of a holistic and sound National Biosecurity Policy that would safeguard the health and environment from harmful biological agents . <p> Dr. Rufus Ebegba , the Director-General of NBMA gave the assurance during the Presentation of the Preliminary Draft of the National Biosecurity Policy by the Committee in Abuja on Monday . <p> It could be recalled that the NBMA Act was amended in 2019 and its scope expanded to include emerging aspects of modern biotechnology and bio-security . <p> Ebegba said the issue of biosecurity had become more eminent , especially with the outbreak of the novel COVID-19 , which he said was a major biosecurity issue . <p> " We must now as a nation start from this policy so all the sectors of the economy can stand up to ensure we have a biosecurity system that can contest with world best standards . <p> " Nigeria as a country , we will not wait until another pandemic , we will be ready to combat , defend and protect our environment from any harmful biological agents that will cause @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ this policy and other documents that will emanate from this policy , we assure Nigerians that the Federal Government will take responsibility to protect the country , humans and the environment <p> " As well as ensure stability and the growth of our national psyche . We must protect our environment from the use of harmful biological agents . " <p> The D-G commended President Muhammadu Buhari for giving his assent to the National Biosafety Act as amended by the National Assembly to include biosecurity into the mandate of the agency . <p> According to him , this is one of the best bills passed by the national assembly because it affects human health and the environment and COVID-19 attest to it . <p> The NBMA boss said the document would also be presented to stakeholders for their input and recommendations . <p> Ebegba said the document would be reviewed thoroughly so that the agency would come up with a final document that would be presented to the Federal Executive Council for approval . <p> He congratulated the in-house committee for a job well done . <p> Ms. Chinyere Nzeduru @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the committee said the amended Act gave the NBMA the power to coordinate activities in various agencies handling issues of biosecurity . <p> According to her , the National biosecurity policy will be through a one health approach- agriculture , environment and human with the full knowledge that disease and pest control requires effective collaboration between multi-sectoral stakeholders . <p> She said the committee had so far been able to produce a fine document tailored to suit the demands of the country on biosecurity . <p> Nzeduru , who said biosecurity existed in various activities in life , added that the document focused on 22 thematic areas . <p> She listed some of the areas to include agriculture , plant and human health , climate change , border control , waste management , transport security , research and development , bioterrorism , bioethics , industrial governance and coordination , and financing and resource mobilisation .
