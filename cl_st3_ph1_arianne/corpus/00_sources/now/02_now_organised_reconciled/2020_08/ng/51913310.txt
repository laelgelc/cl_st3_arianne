<p> The Minister of Humanitarian Affairs , Disaster Management and Social Development , Sadiya Umar Farouq has presented 100 trucks of assorted grain as palliative to cushion the effect COVID-19 in Adamawa state <p> Farouq said she was in Yola on behalf of President Muhammadu Buhari to present the assorted grains from the National Food Reserve , approved as palliative for distribution to vulnerable persons affected by the recent restriction of movement to curtail the spread of COVID-19 pandemic . <p> She said the outbreak of the pandemic has brought about changes and challenges to the normal ways of living of the people . <p> " Most obvious , is the vulnerable persons that depend largely on others for their daily survival . <p> " Such persons are the aged , people living with disabilities and persons of concerns that deserve support in terms of basic necessities including foods . " <p> " A total of 10,142 farmers have been verified to benefit from the fertilizer for having been affected by the flood and 11 , 400 affected by conflicts .
