<p> The World Health Organisation says there is no guarantee the discovery of a vaccine will end the COVID-19 pandemic currently ravaging the world . <p> The Director-General of WHO , Dr. Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus disclosed this during the virtual media briefing on COVID-19 on Friday . <p> He noted that everyone must learn to control and manage the virus using the tools that are presently available , adding that everyone must make the needed adjustments to stay safe and keep others safe . <p> According to him , several countries around the world are now experiencing fresh outbreaks after a long period with little or no transmission . <p> " These countries are a cautionary tale for those that are now seeing a downward trend in cases . Progress does not mean victory . <p> " The fact remains that most people remain susceptible to this virus . That 's why it 's vital that countries are able to quickly identify and prevent clusters , to prevent community transmission and the possibility of new restrictions . <p> " No country can just ride this out until we have a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and we hope that we will have one as soon as possible . But there 's no guarantee that we will , and even if we do have a vaccine , it wo n't end the pandemic on its own . <p> " We must all learn to control and manage this virus using the tools we have now and to make the adjustments to our daily lives that are needed to keep ourselves and each other safe . <p> " So-called lockdowns enabled many countries to suppress transmission and take the pressure off their health systems . But lockdowns are not a long-term solution for any country . We do not need to choose between lives and livelihoods , or between health and the economy . That 's a false choice . <p> " On the contrary , the pandemic is a reminder that health and the economy are inseparable , " Ghebreyesus said . <p> He added that the UN agency is committed to working with all countries to move into a new stage of opening their economies , societies , schools and businesses safely . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . Every single person can make a difference . Every person , family , community and nation must make their own decisions , based on the level of risk where they live . <p> " That means every person and family has a responsibility to know the level of transmission locally and to understand what they can do to protect themselves and others . <p> " At the same time , we will not -- we can not -- go back to the way things were . Throughout history , outbreaks and pandemics have changed economies and societies . This one will be no different . <p> " In particular , the pandemic has given new impetus to the need to accelerate efforts to respond to climate change . The pandemic has given us a glimpse of our world as it could be : cleaner skies and rivers . Building back better means building back greener , " the UN chief said . <p> According to him , there are now more than 22 million reported cases of COVID-19 , and 780,000 deaths , globally .
