<p> Joe Biden vowed to " end the chapter of American darkness " by defeating President Trump in the presidential election as he capped the virtual Democratic convention with a vow to work hard for supporters and opponents alike if elected to the White House . <p> Mr Biden , 77 , contrasted his cross-party credentials with the president , whose own convention is next week , delivering a passionate 25-minute speech that defied his caricature by the Trump campaign as a has-been in his dotage . <p> Delivering his speech in an empty auditorium because the pandemic caused the cancellation of the traditional party jamboree , Mr Biden told America that he had plans to tackle four urgent crises facing the country : the coronavirus pandemic , the shattered economy , racial justice and climate change .
