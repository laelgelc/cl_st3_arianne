<h> Gucci 's Off The Grid sneakers sex up sustainability <p> Sustainability is a bit of a buzz word in the world of fashion right now . With the environmental crisis looming , an increasing number of brands have come to the realisation that the carbon footprints they create by producing and peddling endless product for a global audience is no longer ecologically tenable . <p> Gucci , under the aegis of all-seeing creative director Alessandro Michele , has been one of the first global super brands to take the issue of sustainability truly seriously . <p> In 2017 , the Kering-owned label banned real fur from all of its product lines and at the end of last year the label 's CEO Marco Bizzarri announced that the brand he helms alongside Michele had achieved the much-coveted status of carbon neutrality for the first time in its 99-year history . <p> Now , in its latest step in the fight against climate change , Gucci has launched the genderless Off The Grid collection .
