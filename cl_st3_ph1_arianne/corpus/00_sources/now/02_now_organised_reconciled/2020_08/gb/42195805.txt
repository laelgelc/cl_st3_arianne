<p> Ocean waters are rising because of global warming . They are rising for two reasons . First , and perhaps most obvious , ice is melting . There is a tremendous amount of ice locked away in Greenland , Antarctica , and in glaciers . As the world warms , that ice melts and the liquid water flows to the oceans . <p> The other reason why water is rising is that warmer water is less dense -- it expands . This expansion causes the surface of the water to rise . <p> Rising ocean waters . Illustration : NASA <p> Rising oceans are a big deal . A great example is Superstorm Sandy , which hit the US East Coast in 2012 . It cost approximately $65 bn of damage . The cost was higher because of sea level rise caused by global warming . <p> Climate scientists do their best to project how much and how fast oceans will rise in the future .
