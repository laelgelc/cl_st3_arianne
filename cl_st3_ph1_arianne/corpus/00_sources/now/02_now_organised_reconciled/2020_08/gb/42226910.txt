<p> The sighting of the Dark Crimson Underwing was the first ever recorded in Wales . <p> While we slept badly in our beds during the tropical nights of early August , the record-breaking weather provided ideal conditions for native moths to thrive and encouraged rare visitors from continental Europe . <p> The Dark Crimson and Light Crimson Underwings are largely confined to the New Forest in Hampshire and nearby woodlands - but there are some signs of recent spread into new sites . <p> And recent weather has seen the Dark Crimson Underwing moving into new areas . <p> Founder of Moth Night - an annual celebration which will be held between August 27 and 29 - Mark Tunmore said : " Already we have been receiving widespread reports of Dark and Light Crimson Underwing moths in southern England , indicating that the range expansion of these species is continuing , induced by climate change . <p> " Particularly exciting has been a recent sighting of Dark Crimson Underwing in Gwent , which represents the first ever record for Wales , and just a few days ago we received @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " So we ca n't wait to see what happens with so much interest focused on these stunning species over the Moth Night event .
