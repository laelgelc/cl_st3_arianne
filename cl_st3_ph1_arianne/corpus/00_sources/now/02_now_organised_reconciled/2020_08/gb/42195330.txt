<p> Imagine your favourite stretch of coastline -- whitesand beaches , rocky tide pools , the cliffs of Dover , what have you . Now transport yourself ahead two decades , after plastic production and waste have continued to skyrocket . Humanity is now unloading 29 million metric tons of bottles , bags , and microplastics ( little bits smaller than 5 millimeters ) into the oceans annually . That means for every meter of your favourite coastline , 50 kilograms -- that 's 110 pounds -- of plastic is entering the sea every year . <p> " Now imagine that 's happening for every meter of coastline around the world , " says Richard Bailey , who studies environmental systems at Oxford University .
