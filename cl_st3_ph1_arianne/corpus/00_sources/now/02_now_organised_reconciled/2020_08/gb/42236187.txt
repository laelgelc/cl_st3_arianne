<h> ON TIME <p> 6.00 -- 7.15pm , Tuesday 6 October <p> ' Time is a big invisible thing that will kill you ' , wrote philosopher Craig Callendar . It also affects all aspects of our lives , from the political to the very nature of our experience . As lockdown made clear , it can be agonizing to be in a state of limbo , to have routines disrupted , unable to plan for the future . Paradoxically , when it comes to things like climate change , we prioritize living the present . Some people 's time is valued more highly than others , and time is used to both punish and reward .
