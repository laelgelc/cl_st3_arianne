<h> Where to find Bluebells this year <p> * Foxley Wood -- The largest ancient woodland in Norfolk a mile north-east of Foxley village on the A1067 . In the past couple of decades the Norfolk Wildlife Trust has been busy removing the conifers and replanting with native deciduous trees and bluebells have come back in their droves . <p> * Blickling -- Blickling Hall has a five-mile waymarked estate walk and if you wander from the path in the Great Wood you 'll come across plenty of wonderful displays of bluebells . <p> * Wayland Wood -- Believed to be the site of the legend " Babes in the Wood " , this actively managed wild wood , near Watton , still instils a sense of history for which it is locally renowned . As well as bluebells , at the moment you 'll see purple orchids , wood anemone and nightingales . <p> * Burlingham Woods -- Owned and managed by Norfolk County Council , this wood , at North Burlingham sign-posted just off the A47 , has several circular walks offering a glimpse of bluebells . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ garden designed in 1812 by Humphry Repton and one of his most outstanding achievements .
