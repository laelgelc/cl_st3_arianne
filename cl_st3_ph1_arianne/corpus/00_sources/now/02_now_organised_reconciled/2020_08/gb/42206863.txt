<h> Environmental Photographer of the Year 2016 : Winners and shortlisted images <p> The winners of the the Atkins CIWEM Environmental Photographer of the Year 2016 competition have been announced . This contest provides an international showcase for the very best in environmental photography , by both amateurs and professionals .
