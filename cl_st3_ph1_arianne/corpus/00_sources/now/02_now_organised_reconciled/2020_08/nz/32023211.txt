<h> EVs save lives and money in US study <p> Words : NZ Autocar <p> 19 Aug 2020 <p> Yes , electric vehicles are more expensive than ICE power alternatives currently , but a new US study indicates EV adoption could save lives and billions of dollars annually . <p> The analysis included the effects of traffic pollution on public health and found EVs could counteract a good proportion of the heart , lung and brain damage from pollution , as well as preventing many premature deaths from related diseases . <p> Premature death from air pollution caused by burning petrol is a major issue in big American cities . Diesel burning is also an important contributor to pollution-induced illness . NOx liberated as the fuel is burned reacts with compounds in the atmosphere to create lasting clouds of pollution . <p> Tiny particles , the so-called PM2.5s , that the lungs can not filter are absorbed into the bloodstream and can cause long-term health consequences , including stroke , heart disease and lung cancer , as well as obstructive lung disease .
