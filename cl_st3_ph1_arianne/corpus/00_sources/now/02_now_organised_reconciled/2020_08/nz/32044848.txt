Healthy forests are a critical nature-based solution for the climate crisis and these pledges mark the launch of the US chapter of 1t.org , the global trillion trees movement . <p> The US is home to the first regional chapter of 1t.org . Pledges demonstrate an advancement of conservation , restoration , and reforestation goals within the US and internationally , a significant effort and achievement toward the trillion trees goal . Studies have shown trees can reduce temperatures by 9 degrees and energy costs by $7.8 billion a year .
