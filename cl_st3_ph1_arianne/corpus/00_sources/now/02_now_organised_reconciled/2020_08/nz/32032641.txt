<h> Warning for Pacific governments gambling on deepsea mining <p> As the pandemic dries up traditional revenue sources , some Pacific Island governments are considering diving into the unknown waters of deep sea mining . <p> Mining advocates say the world badly needs polymetallic nodules on the seabed for materials to make batteries that will drive the carbon-free societies of the future , but it 's a gamble which Pacific countries are being warned is too risky . <p> The fledgling deep sea mining sector is moving quickly , and much of its interest centres on the world 's biggest ocean .
