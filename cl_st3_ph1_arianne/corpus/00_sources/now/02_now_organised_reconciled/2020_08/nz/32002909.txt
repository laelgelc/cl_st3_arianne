<h> Welcoming wheels of all sizes - Council <p> Plans are underway to increase transport choices for Hamiltonians with the development of a city-wide programme to encourage biking and micro-mobility ( that 's electric scooters and skateboards ) . <p> " We know that investing in biking and micro-mobility not only increases transport choices for people ; it also increases the overall efficiency of our existing transport network and supports our city 's steady growth , " says Hamilton City Council 's Infrastructure Operations General Manager Eeva-Liisa Wright . <p> " The biking and micro-mobility programme is likely to include a wide range of actions that enable our vision of being a 20-minute city , such as new cycleways , safety and education initiatives , supporting policy and regulations and better end-of-trip facilities , " she says . <p> The Council will be seeking community feedback on the projects and initiatives in the biking and micro-mobility programme around September this year , with a final plan expected in late 2020 or early 2021 . <p> " We know from our cycleway counts that people got out on their bikes and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , " says Councillor Angela O'Leary . <p> " We heard stories about kids learning to ride their bikes and people having fun exploring their neighbourhoods . We want people to continue to do this and for Hamilton to be a city that 's safe , easy and enjoyable to get around . " <p> The biking and micro-mobility programme is just one area that will support Hamilton in achieving ambitious transport , safety and climate change targets . <p> The programme is also another next step for the Council 's Biking Plan , which projects such as the Western Rail Trail were delivered under . <p> People can find out more information about the biking and micro-mobility programme on the website and expect to hear and see more about this in the coming months . <p> All articles and comments on Voxy.co.nz have been submitted by our community of users .
