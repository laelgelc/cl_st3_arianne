<h> Scientific thought behind hydro idea <p> The Government announced late last month it would invest $30million investigating a proposal to store water in the Onslow-Manorburn depression above Roxburgh , to be used to generate electricity when existing hydro storage dams are running low . <p> Enough water could be stored to generate at least 10,200 GWh of electricity , about a quarter of New Zealand 's annual electricity consumption . <p> The water would be pumped from the Clutha River below the Roxburgh Dam , uphill through a 15km rock tunnel , to the storage lake . <p> It would be released back down the tunnel , when required , for the pumps to then become electricity generators . <p> The multibillion-dollar scheme was first envisaged by University of Waikato associate professor Earl Bardsley . <p> Mark Price put a series of questions to Prof Bardsley this week . <p> The Upper Manorburn Dam .
