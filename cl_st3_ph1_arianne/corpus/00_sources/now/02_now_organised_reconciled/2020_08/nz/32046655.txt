<h> Funding for energy-efficiency infrastructure <p> Photo : Whakatane District Council Facebook . <p> Whakatane District Council has successfully secured funding to the tune of $63,000 from the Low Emission Vehicles Contestable Fund . <p> Designed to enable projects that encourage the uptake of electric vehicles and build confidence in the purchase of e-bikes , the Central Government fund is administered by the Energy Efficiency and Conservation Authority . <p> Whakatane District Council will use the fund toward the purchase of further electric vehicles , the installation of five on-site double charge stations to support an electric pool fleet , visitors and the public , and also a secure lockup and charging site for ten staff e-bikes . <p> Council General Manager Finance and Corporate Services , Helen Barnes , says the government co-funding supports the Council 's climate change principles and follows other initiatives already implemented . <p> " An energy audit identified that Council 's petrol and diesel consumption was contributing to more than 12 per cent of our carbon footprint , so the decision was made to begin replacing the Council fleet with electric vehicles . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ energy-efficient infrastructure is higher so attaining this funding is hugely valuable . " <p> Sixty-seven applications to the fund were received , and Whakatane District Council 's project was one of 24 that were approved . <p> Minister of Energy and Resources , Dr Megan Woods says it was the first time organisations could apply for co-funding to install secure e-bike storage facilities to encourage their uptake . <p> " Demand for e-bikes is high , but we know one barrier to regular e-bike commuting is having somewhere safe to store the bike . <p> " Decarbonising the transport sector represents a huge opportunity to reduce the country 's emissions , and enabling people to shift to a different transport mode plays a part in that . " <p> While announcing the successful projects , Dr Woods noted that a number of previously-funded ventures and spin-offs have hit the road in the last few months , including Foodstuffs launching the first fully electric refrigerated truck in the country , a NZ Post scheme to assist its contract drivers to buy electric delivery vans , and Asthma New Zealand buying EVs @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> " The Contestable Fund encourages innovation and investment to accelerate the uptake of low emission vehicles in New Zealand . The success of previously-funded projects shows that we can meet New Zealand 's transport needs across a variety of sectors , giving other organisations the confidence to make their own investments . " <p> Barnes says installation of the charging stations and the e-bike facility will begin next month . <p> " Now we 've received the funding , we 're eager to progress this project quickly as possible , " she says . <p> " We 'll certainly advise our stakeholders when the charging stations are operational , so everyone can benefit from the new technology .
