<p> The results from respondents in Nelson/Marlborough show Labour is in the box seat , just over a month away from the election . Of course , Marlborough on its own has long been a blue stronghold . The last time a Labour Party candidate was successful in the Kaikoura electorate was in 1970 , when Ian Brooks won a by-election . <p> Labour was the single most popular party in the survey , with 44.7 per cent of respondents saying they would be likely to vote for them if the election were held tomorrow , with National trailing behind on 35.6 per cent . <p> The next most popular were the ACT Party on 8.8 per cent , with the Greens at 5.7 per cent the only other party above the five per cent threshold .
