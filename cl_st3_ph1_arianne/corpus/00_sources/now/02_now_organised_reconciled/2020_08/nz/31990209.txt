<p> We have a Prime Minister describing climate change as " my generation 's nuclear free moment " ; and a Climate Change Minister who not only happens to co-lead the Greens but who sees climate change " as a once-in-a-generation opportunity to reinvent parts of our economy and society for the better " . <p> And this new Labour-Greens-NZ First Government is forthright in its green-leaning tendencies and policies . <p> Last month , in a historic move , it announced that no new exploration permits for offshore oil and gas fields will be issued , in support of its commitment to action on climate change . <p> We do n't think the oil and gas industry expected such a strong move against them ; and dairy could similarly be blind-sided by the Government . <p> Federated Farmers leader Andrew Hoggard , a dairy farmer in Manawatu , says this sudden move on oil and gas has farmers spooked .
