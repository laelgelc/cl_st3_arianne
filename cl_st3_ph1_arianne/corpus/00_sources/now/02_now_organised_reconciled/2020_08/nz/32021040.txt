" <p> Slattery and her husband Justin own and operate a 300 cow , 106ha dairy farm near Te Aroha . She is an associate with a local chartered accountant firm . The Slatterys won the NZ Dairy Industry Awards 2015 Sharemilker/Equity Farmer of the Year title . <p> The Dairy Environment Leaders programme is run by DairyNZ .
