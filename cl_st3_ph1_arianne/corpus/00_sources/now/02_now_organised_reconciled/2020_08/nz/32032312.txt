<h> Hinahina Road Bridge Replacement - council <p> Work starts this week on the replacement of the Hina Hina Road bridge . <p> The current bridge was constructed in the 1950s and has reached the end of its design life : future disruption , routine maintenance and repairs costs will become significant . <p> The bridge will be closed for approximately 10 months , and a detour will be clearly signposted . <p> Jules Witt , Group Manager Service Delivery said " There was a real possibility that this bridge may not have been renewed which would have made the ' detour ' permanent . In the event , we appreciate the assistance of Waka Kotahi NZTA , our investment partners who are funding 59% of the replacement costs - giving us a total budget of $3.4million . " <p> The residents at approximately 60 properties will have a longer journey if travelling from Owaka , however , many of these properties are only occupied on a part time basis .
