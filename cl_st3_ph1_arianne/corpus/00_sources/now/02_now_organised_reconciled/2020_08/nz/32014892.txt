<p> Te Mana o te Taiao -- The Aotearoa New Zealand Biodiversity Strategy sets five key goals to reach by 2050 , with specific objectives and goals for 2025 , 2030 , and 2050 . These include " getting the system right " , by improving science , data , and innovation systems , and incorporating matauranga Maori in biodiversity research and management . <h> The SMC asked experts to comment on the strategy . <p> " Te Mana o te Taiao , the New Zealand Biodiversity Strategy , was developed with input from a wide range of people , and it shows .
