<h> Councils set to vote on 11 remits at LGNZ AGM <p> Eleven remits have been set down for debate and voting at the 2020 Local Government New Zealand ( LGNZ ) Annual General Meeting ( AGM ) to be held this Friday 21 August . <p> The AGM , postponed from July , will be held remotely and will enable all 78 member councils to vote on both the remits , as well as a new LGNZ president and vice-president . Due to the Covid crisis , LGNZ 's 2020 Conference , usually held over the same period as the AGM , has been postponed until 2021 . <p> LGNZ President Dave Cull says that the remit process is important as it enables ground-up policy making to address a range of issues affecting New Zealand 's communities . <p> " Remits are the lifeblood of any membership organisation as they enable members to directly inform advocacy , " says Mr Cull . <p> " LGNZ believes in ground-up policy making that allows local people to deliver their own local initiatives and solutions , and remits are a great way @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ areas including public transport , housing , climate change and rates . While some remits are completely new , others confirm and update existing policy . <p> New remits include : <p> - Returning GST on rates to councils to spend on infrastructure ; <p> - Extending the local government electoral cycle from three years to four ; <p> - Placement of a moratorium on water bottling ; <p> - Extending emergency legislation that allowed councillors to be included in the quorum when connecting remotely to a council meeting ; and <p> - Development of an annual regional balance of transfers , to show how much each region contributes in taxes , and how much each region receives in government funding . <p> The LGNZ AGM is open to members only .
