<h> Greens have the solutions <p> The Green Party has a beautiful answer to Chris Brown 's concern about two-tier welfare , where those who lost jobs through the Covid lockdown are paid twice as much as other beneficiaries . The Green poverty-action plan guarantees every unemployed person $325 a week , with no conditions of employment history or private life , plus $100 for every child . So a family of two parents with two kids gets $850 a week . A single parent receives $435 , plus $100 per child . <p> This breakthrough has been a long time coming . While being fair to people is hardly rocket-science , it has taken careful budgeting . Most of those who will pay extra tax can see the sense , and many will willingly . Amazingly , the capital tax is only 1 or 2 percent , for freehold equity , per person in ownership , over $1m or $2m ; the top income tax is still lower than in Britain and Australia . <p> While the Government worked brilliantly through the pandemic crisis , daily consulting , calculating @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in compensating people through their employers , and in proportion to their previous incomes .
