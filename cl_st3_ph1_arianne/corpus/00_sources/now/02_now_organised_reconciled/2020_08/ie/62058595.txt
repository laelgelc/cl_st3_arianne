We know that Europe is not always the most ambitious continent in the world , for example in the field of innovation , entrepreneurship and economic growth . But in the field of climate change we can be proud of what Europe is doing . <p> If all the satellites envisaged within the Copernicus programme become operational , Europe will have the world 's most developed observation capacity .
