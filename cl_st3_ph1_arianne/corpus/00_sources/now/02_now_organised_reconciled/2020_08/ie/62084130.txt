To achieve this , the primary task is to develop a brown-minus-green ( or BMG ) risk factor , similar to Fama and French ( 1992 ) . Secondly , we must estimate the carbon beta using a multi-factor model . <p> While G ? rgen et al . Moreover , we test several specifications of the BMG factor to understand which climate change-related dimensions are priced in by the stock market .
