<h> Carbon Capture Coalition welcomes Calpine as a participant <p> The Carbon Capture Coalition has welcomed Calpine Corporation as a Coalition Participant . <p> Headquartered in Texas , Calpine is one of America 's largest generators of electricity from natural gas and geothermal resources with operation in competitive power markets . <p> The company 's fleet of 77 power plants , including one under construction , represents over 26,000 megawatts of generation capacity . Through its wholesale power operations and retail businesses , the company serves customers in 23 states , Canada and Mexico . <p> Calpine 's fleet uses advanced technologies to generate power in a low-carbon and environmentally responsible manner . They are supportive of carbon capture technology as an important part of a set of market-based solutions to address climate change . <p> " We are happy to welcome Calpine as a new participant in the Carbon Capture Coalition , " said Jessie Stolark , Coalition Public Policy and Member Relations Manager . <p> " Calpine 's interest in collaborating with the more than 80 organisations in the Coalition to promote carbon capture technology deployment reflects the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as the growing support for this technology across the economy .
