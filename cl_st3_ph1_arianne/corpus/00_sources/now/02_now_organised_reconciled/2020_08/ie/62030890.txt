the Met Office has said . <p> The latest annual State of the UK Climate review compiled by the meteorological experts shows how the country continues to warm , with 2019 's average temperature 1.1C above long-term 1961-1990 levels . <p> The most recent decade has been 0.9C warmer across the UK than the 1961-1990 average , the report said . <p> Last year was most notable for breaking records ,
