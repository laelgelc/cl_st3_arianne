<p> The Edeka Group and Apeel have announced that they are further extending their joint commitment against food waste . <p> The outcome of an extensive pilot test in about 2,900 selected Edeka and Netto Marken-Discount stores proved successful , Apeel said , with a 50 per cent reduction in avocado food waste , along with a significant increase in sales and greater customer satisfaction . <p> As a result , Edeka now offers Apeel 's longer-lasting avocados and citrus in more than 11,000 Edeka and Netto stores throughout Germany , and Edeka and Apeel are planning to introduce additional categories in the future , subject to approval by the EU authorities . <p> " With the nationwide launch of Apeel in our stores , we are reaching a new milestone in our goal of reducing food waste and the use of plastic packaging step-by-step , " said Markus Mosa , chief executive of Hamburg-based Edeka AG , " We also support our shoppers to make an active contribution themselves to prevent valuable food in the household from ending up in the garbage bin . " <p> The pilot test with Apeel was conducted at about 2,900 Edeka and Netto Marken-Discount stores in the first half of 2020 , with avocados from Chile and Peru treated with Apeel . <p> Over a period of twelve weeks , write-off rates ( shrink and food loss ) , sales and turnover values were tracked . <p> Figures were compared with those of a control group of non-Apeel treated produce , and results showed that in the produce departments that used Apeel , a total of 50 per cent fewer avocados were wasted , while Apeel helped pilot stores achieve 20 per cent growth in avocado sales , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ gone to waste as well as increased customer demand . <p> In addition , avocados treated with Apeel proved to be superior in quality thanks to its plant-derived technology that retained moisture while reducing oxidation , which ensured longer-lasting freshness throughout the natural ripening process and longer overall shelf-life . <p> In Germany , some 12 tonnes of food are disposed of each year -- which corresponds to about 75kg per person . Studies show that more than half of food losses occur in private households , and fruit and vegetables are discarded most of all , accounting for 34 per cent .
