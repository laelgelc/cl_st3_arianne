<h> L&amp;T Hydrocarbon and NTPC to construct CO2 to methanol plant <p> L&amp;T Hydrocarbon Engineering ( LTHE ) , a wholly owned subsidiary of Larsen &amp; Toubro ( L&amp;T ) , has signed a Memorandum of Understanding ( MoU ) with Indian power utility NTPC to build a CO2 to methanol demonstration plant . <p> Located at NTPC Power Station , the CO2 to methanol plant will be formed of three sub-units : CO2 capture from flue gas , hydrogen production by electrolysis and catalytic conversion of CO2 and hydrogen the methanol . <p> " We are delighted to be a part of this initiative of NTPC in the field of clean energy , " said Subramanian Sarma , Whole Time Director and Senior Executive Vice-President of L&amp;T . <p> " This development towards establishing CO2 to methanol plants is an important step towards India 's commitment to combat climate change .
