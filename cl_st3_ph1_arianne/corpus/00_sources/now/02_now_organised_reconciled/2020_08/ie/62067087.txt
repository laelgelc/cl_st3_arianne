<p> Parliament will soon head to Bonn on an official mission to COP 23 . This delegation , which I will lead as the Chair of the environment , public health and food safety committee , will participate in the negotiations and contribute to the solutions for designing the facilitative dialogue and securing more climate finance . <p> Stakeholders and the public are very eager to grasp what will actually be decided in Bonn and what the EU will do in order to achieve its objectives in this changing global landscape . <p> Their expectations are legitimate given the climate of uncertainty regarding the future of COP and the level of involvement of certain parties .
