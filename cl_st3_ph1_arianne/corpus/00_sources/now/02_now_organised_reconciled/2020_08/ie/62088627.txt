<p> Many of the radical environmental measures being proposed for agriculture are completely at odds with farmers ' strongly held beliefs on what constitutes good farming . And policy-makers risk wasting massive amounts of public money if they do n't resolve this dilemma . <p> " IF a farm was going downhill , what are the things you would notice ? , " UCD researcher Louise Burns asked a number of farmers as part of a wider land-use study she was working on . <p> Struck by the response which emphasised traditional concepts of ' tidy ' and ' good ' farming , Louise decided to write a separate paper examining the concept of the ' tidy farmer ' in Ireland and how this may affect the uptake of agri-environmental schemes . <p> In the coming years , billions is set to be invested by the State and the EU to improve Irish agriculture 's environmental performance .
