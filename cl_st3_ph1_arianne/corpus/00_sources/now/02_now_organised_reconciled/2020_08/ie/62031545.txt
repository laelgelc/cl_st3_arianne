<h> 20,000-Acre Fire in California Causes Thousand of Evacuations <p> With climate change causing unnatural heat waves , fires seem to be showing up all over the U.S. even causing the deaths of two firefighting pilots last Thursday in Nevada . Now , it seems California has gone up in flames . <p> The fire reported to be in Cherry Valley has already devastated more than 20,516 acres of land and is currently only 5% contained . <p> " Much of the northern and eastern edge of the fire is in very steep , rugged hillsides which are not accessible to firefighting vehicles . Firefighters continue to work to slow the spread of the fire using all resources available including helicopters , fixed-wing air tankers , ground equipment , and hand-crews , " wrote Forest Service authorities . <h> Raging on since Friday <p> The fire , referred to as the Apple Fire , is located in Riverside County , about 75 miles east of Los Angeles .
