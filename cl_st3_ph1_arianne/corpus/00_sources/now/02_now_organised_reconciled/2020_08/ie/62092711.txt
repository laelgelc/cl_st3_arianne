<p> Visually light yet structurally strong and sustainable , glass has long been favoured by architects as a means of opening a building to its environment while still protecting the inhabitants from the elements . There seems little reason then why we could not explore the material 's many applications , treatments and finishes to create a yacht that elevates our basic need of shelter whilst bringing us even closer to nature . <p> The 68 metre Estrela do Mar , a world expedition yacht , is a striking and innovative design that strives to maintain a relationship between inside and outside , to galvanize passengers to watch the power and beauty of nature from the comfort and safety of a bespoke , luxurious interior . <p> Glass is a sustainable , fully recyclable material which provides great environmental benefits such as contributing to mitigating climate change and saving precious natural resources . This conceptual build is our contribution to being more eco-conscious .
