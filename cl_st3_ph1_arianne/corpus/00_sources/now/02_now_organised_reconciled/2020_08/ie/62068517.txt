<p> Fruitnet is teaming up with the World Citrus Organisation to host the first-ever Global Citrus Congress Live . <p> It takes place online on 5 November 2020 and is targeted at everyone in the global supply chain of fresh citrus from the farm gate to the supermarket shelf . More information can be found at www.citruscongress.com . <p> The free-to-register online conference focuses on some of the big developments across the citrus category and features a number of expert presentations and panel discussions . <p> Fresh citrus categories are one of the main global fruit commodities , but will have to face substantial challenges in the next years , ranging from increased competition with other categories , to the impacts of climate change , which may impact production , quality and biosecurity .
