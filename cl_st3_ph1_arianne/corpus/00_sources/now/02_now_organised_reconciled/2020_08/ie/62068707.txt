<h> Aurivo shortlisted for three Green Awards <p> AURIVO has been shortlisted as a finalist in the Green Food &amp; Beverage Producer Awards 2020 . <p> The awards recognise outstanding contributions to sustainability and the environment and Aurivo has been shortlisted in three categories ; Best Green Dairy Producer and Best Sustainable Packaging Strategy and CSR Initiative of the Year . <p> They has been shortlisted in the Best Green Dairy Producer category for its recent sustainability investments which include two projects undertaken at its Killygordon , Co . Donegal and Ballaghaderreen , Co . Roscommon sites for energy efficient improvements . <p> In the Best Sustainable Packaging Strategy entry , the co-op highlighted that it was the first dairy co-operative in Ireland to launch the larger format 1.75 litre fully renewable plant-based carton across major Irish retailers . Sustainability and enhancing the lives of our members , customers and colleagues across the North West region is the essence of our mission .
