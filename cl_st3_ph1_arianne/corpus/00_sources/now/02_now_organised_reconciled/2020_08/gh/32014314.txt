These farmers have also embraced scientific tools including tissue culture and hybrid to enhance crop production . <p> In recent times , scientific tools applicable to the agricultural sector that have been embraced by some countries around the globe include biotechnology ( BT ) . It involves the genetic engineering ( GE ) or genetic modification ( GM ) of a crop to produce traits , which hitherto were non-existent in that crop .
