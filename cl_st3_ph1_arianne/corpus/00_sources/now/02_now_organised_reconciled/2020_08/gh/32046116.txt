<h> Cocoa production in Ghana is expected to increase with the completion of automated drip irrigation schemes . <p> The 28.3 hectares automated drip irrigation schemes were developed by the Mennonite Economic Development Associates ( MEDA ) . <p> The Project was funded by the Global Affairs Canada ( GAC ) , a Canadian government Agency , at cost of 756,000 Canadian dollars . <p> On Wednesday , August 26 , 2020 , MEDA officially handed over the project to the Ghana Cocoa Board ( COCOBOD ) in Accra . <p> MEDA , an international economic development organization whose mission is to create business solutions to eradicate poverty is implementing the Farmers ' Economic Advancement Through Seedlings ( FEATS ) Project in Ghana . <p> The FEATS activities are in four major tree crop industries namely cashew , cocoa , rubber and Shea . <p> MEDA identified the Cocoa Seed Garden as a critical plank in the cocoa value chain in Ghana , which led to MEDA 's partnership with COCOBOD .
