<h> US announces $1 billion research push for AI and quantum computing <p> The US government is announcing $1 billion in new funding for multidisciplinary AI and quantum computing research hubs today , **26;965;TOOLONG . A total of 12 hubs will be funded , each embedded within different agencies of the federal government . Their work will span a diverse range of topics , from using machine learning for atmospheric and ocean science , to speeding up high-energy physics simulations with quantum systems . <p> The investment is part of a slow push from the White House to fund emerging technologies .
