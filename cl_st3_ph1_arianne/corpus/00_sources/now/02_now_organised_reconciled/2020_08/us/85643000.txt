She and her assistants found several different bumble bee species among roughly 1,000 bees on the properties . Of those , only five were the rusty patched . <p> " We catch every bumble bee we see . A federal permit is required to capture bumble bees , and they must be released within minutes so they will not be harmed , she said . <p> " I 've been very eager to find Bombus affinis ( the Latin name for the rusty patched bumble bee ) at Pine Dunes , " Schrage said .
