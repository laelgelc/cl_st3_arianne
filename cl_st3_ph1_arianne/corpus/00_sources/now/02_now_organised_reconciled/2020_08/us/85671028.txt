<h> Representative Jones Serving On Climate Bill Conference Committee <h> Local legislator will help finalize state plan for reaching net-zero carbon emissions by 2050 <h> Aug 13 , 2020 9:45 pm ET <p> House Minority Leader Bradley H. Jones , Jr . ( R-North Reading ) is now serving on a six-member legislative conference committee that is reviewing two major climate change bills approved by the House and the Senate in an attempt to develop a final compromise proposal that can be sent to Governor Charlie Baker . <p> Senate Bill 2500 , An Act setting next-generation climate policy , was engrossed by the Senate on January 30 , while House Bill 4933 , An Act creating a 2050 road map to a clean and thriving Commonwealth , was passed by the House on July 31 . Both bills set a goal of achieving net-zero carbon emissions in Massachusetts by 2050 , but differ in their approach . <p> Under current law , Massachusetts is already working to reduce its carbon emissions to 80% of 1990 levels by 2050 .
