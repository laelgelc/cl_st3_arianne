<h> Australian government sued over climate change silence <p> A 23-year-old Australian law student , Katta O'Donnell , is suing her country 's federal government for failing to take action on climate change . As part of a class-action lawsuit , she is accusing her country of failing to disclose the financial risks of climate change , an unusual legal tactic .
