We call on the commission to recommend the establishment of a national fire monitoring agency . We propose that such an agency should collect the following information using nationally consistent criteria . <p> Causes . Fire scientists , criminologists , social scientists and state fire agencies14 must work together to fully understand the relative importance of lightning , climate change and arson , among other natural and human factors , in causing bush fires . Such collaborative research can build on a 2008 national pilot study14,18 , which revealed that it is impossible to report to what extent fires are lit deliberately in the absence of a nationally consistent collection , classification and analysis of the causes of fires . <p> Frequency , extent and severity .
