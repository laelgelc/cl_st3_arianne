<h> Spain sets temperature records , U.K. sees hottest day of 2020 <p> MADRID -- As parts of Spain set record temperatures during a heatwave , people in Britain sweltered and flocked to beaches Friday on the country 's hottest day this year . <p> San Sebastian on Spain 's northern coast witnessed 107 degrees Fahrenheit on Thursday -- the hottest temperature there since records began in 1955 , the national weather agency said . <p> The city of Palma , on Spain 's Mediterranean island of Mallorca , set a local record of 105 degrees Fahrenheit on Tuesday . <p> The Spanish weather agency , Aemet , said tropical nights -- when temperatures do not fall below 68 degrees -- were also frequent in many parts of Spain in July . <p> " Climate change is increasing the frequency of heatwaves , " it said , adding that the annual number of days in heatwave conditions has doubled since the 1980s . <p> To the north , U.K. residents sought shade as temperatures rose above 98.6 degrees .
