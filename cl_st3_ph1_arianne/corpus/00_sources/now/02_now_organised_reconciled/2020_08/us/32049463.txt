Blame it on climate change , rising infrastructure costs , or simple supply and demand . In a number of major cities , water prices are on the rise . <p> A new report from Circle of Blue , a network of journalists and scientists that covers the global freshwater crisis , details 30 such cities where residents are facing substantial water price increases .
