While there were no crowds , handshakes , or applause to demonstrate excitement , the new format allowed for Americans across the country to participate . <p> Each night consisted of live and taped speeches where voters implored those watching to vote for Joe Biden . They spoke about President Trump 's failure to address climate change , structural racism , gun violence , economic insecurity , and the coronavirus that has killed more than 170,000 Americans .
