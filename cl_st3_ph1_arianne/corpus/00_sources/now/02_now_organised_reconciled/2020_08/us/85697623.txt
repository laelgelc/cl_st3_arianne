These QTP-Au veins form steeply northwest plunging " Main Zone " envelopes within quartz-eye porphyry host rocks on the hanging-wall ( NW ) side of the Valentine Lake Shear Zone . The extent of mineralization appears related to the size and frequency of sheared mafic dykes which extend NE-SW within the hanging-wall , parallel to the shear zone . <p> The nine drill holes released today represent both step out and in-fill drilling over a broad length of the Berry Zone .
