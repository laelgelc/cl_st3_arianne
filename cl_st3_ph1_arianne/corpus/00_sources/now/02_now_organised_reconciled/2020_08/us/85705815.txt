<h> Stories from immigrants who risked their lives to come here , and of immigrants whose lives the Trump administration ripped apart . <p> Emotional , human-centered issue montages dominated the opening 30 minutes of the Democratic National Convention 's third night . A segment on gun control concluded with an address from former Rep. Gabby Giffords , while a climate change segment included scientists who resigned from the administration . But the issue where it was apparent Democrats have come the farthest in four years was immigration -- the policy area that might be least hospitable to abstractions after four years of Donald Trump . <p> The immigration portion of the evening featured two families recounting how their lives had fallen to pieces as a result of the Trump administration 's hardline immigration policies . The first segment featured a prerecorded speech from 11-year-old Estela Juarez , whose father Temo is a Marine and National Guard veteran , and whose mother Alejandra came to the U.S. without documents in 1998 .
