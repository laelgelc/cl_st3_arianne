<p> Flooding has killed at least 45 people and led to the displacement of more than 226,000 from their homes in Niger this week , officials said Saturday . <p> Days of heavy rain in Niger 's western region caused the Niger River to overflow , practically shutting down the capital , Niamey . <p> Mud huts along the river have collapsed and rice fields are submerged . <p> Niger 's prime minister , Brigi Rafini , visited affected neighborhoods and families . He was quoted as saying that with river dike rehabilitation work completed just before the rainy season , flooding should not have happened . <p> Rafini said additional measures will be taken to protect other threatened areas , adding that with climate change , " we are never safe from floods . " <p> Last year , floods in Niger killed at least 57 people and forced more than 132,500 to abandon their homes , according to government figures .
