<h> Letter : Long on right side of issues <p> By Mark Moulton , Vancouver <p> Published : August 4 , 2020 , 6:00am <p> Share : <p> Carolyn Long is challenging Jaime Herrera Beutler in District 3 . Both candidates are smart , decent and personable . But on my two big issues -- health care and climate change -- they could not be more different . Carolyn backs the Affordable Care Act ( " Obamacare " ) plus public option , which my wife and I depend on . Jaime keeps voting to repeal or undermine it . Carolyn supports strong legislation to fight climate change , such as The Energy Innovation and Carbon Dividend Act . Jaime 's website does not even mention climate change .
