<p> Amazon has announced that it has placed orders for 1,200 Mercedes-Benz eSprinter zero-emission vans and 600 eVito mid-sized electric vans . The new vehicles will make up part of its European delivery fleet and bring the company closer to achieving The Climate Pledge . <p> As part of the arrangement , Mercedes-Benz has also announced that it is becoming a member of The Climate Pledge which calls on signatories to become net-zero carbon across their business ten years before the Paris Agreement goal of 2050 . <p> Commenting on the new vans , Amazon CEO Jeff Bezos , said : <p> " Amazon is adding 1,800 electric delivery vehicles from Mercedes-Benz as part of our journey to build the most sustainable transportation fleet in the world , and we will be moving fast to get these vans on the road this year . " <p> Regarding Mercedes-Benz joining The Climate Pledge , Bezos said : <p> " We welcome the bold leadership demonstrated by Mercedes-Benz by signing up to The Climate Pledge and committing to ambitious action to address climate change . We need continued innovation and partnership from @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ tackle the climate crisis . " <p> Amazon 's purchase of the vehicles marks a milestone for Mercedes-Benz Vans as this is its largest single order of electric vehicles to date . Amazon 's Delivery Service Partners will begin to use the new vehicles later this year , ultimately reducing their carbon emissions by thousands of metric tonnes .
