<h> Coal is burning out <p> It 's Tuesday , August 4 , and the capacity of the world 's coal plants decreased this year for the first time ever . <p> More coal-fired power plants closed than opened globally in the first six months of 2020 for the first time on record , according to Global Energy Monitor . Between January and June , 18.3 gigawatts of coal power was commissioned so far in 2020 , while 21.2 gigawatts was decommissioned -- a difference of nearly 150 percent the energy capacity of the Hoover Dam . Most of the closures occurred in the U.S. and Europe , while many of the new plants are in Asia . <p> According to Christine Shearer , the Global Energy Monitor 's coal program director , the decline was due to both the economic downturn from the COVID-19 pandemic and tightening regulations on pollution and carbon pricing in the E.U.
