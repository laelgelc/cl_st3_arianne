<p> Not all arguments are worth your time and energy . In some cases -- like when someone fundamentally disagrees with you -- there 's nothing you can say or do to change their mind . But in other instances , it 's entirely possible to make your case and get someone to agree with ( or at least understand ) your point of view . Instinctually , you may rely on facts or stats to do your convincing , but statistics can be easily manipulated , and not everyone responds well to information being thrown at them . As it turns out , telling a good story may be the key to winning people over .
