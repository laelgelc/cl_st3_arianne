<p> The tracker is a powerful interactive tool that allows Houston Chronicle subscribers to see not only rainfall location and intensity , but real-time , continuously updated flooding alerts and street closures as storms sweep through the area . <p> The tracker also has utility during non-weather events , as a source of long-term flood-risk data for neighborhoods across the Houston region and state of Texas . <p> To use the flood risk map , enter a property address in the search bar or click/pinch to zoom and drag the map to the area that interests you . Locations are color-coded to the percentage of properties within a given census tract that are at risk of flooding in the next 30 years due to high-intensity rainfall , overflowing rivers , streams and bayous , high tides and/or coastal storm surge . <p> The risk data is provided by FloodFactor , a free online tool created by First Street Foundation , a nonprofit research and technology group that uses historical environmental data and climate change projections to calculate the likelihood of floods . <p> Live weather updates are provided by RainViewer @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the precipitation and converts it to rainfall rate -- blue for light rainfall of at least 0.01 inches per hour , red at 1 inch per hour , and pink at 8 inches per hour or more . <p> Both the live weather and flood risk pages will automatically update with " likely " or " possible " flooding alerts from Harris County Flood Control District gauges , as well as Houston Chronicle and Houston Community Newspaper staff reports of street closures and flooding across the Houston region .
