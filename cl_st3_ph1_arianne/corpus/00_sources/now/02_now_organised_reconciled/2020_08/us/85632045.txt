<h> Letters : Face masks work like furnace filters to stop germs from getting far <p> Chicago Tribune <p> Aug 09 , 2020 at 5:00 AM <p> Here 's why you should wear a face mask . It works like a furnace filter . Suppose it 's mid-June , and you check the mantle over the fireplace . You find a thin layer of dust because it has n't been dusted since the middle of April . When you change the furnace filter on the first of July , you see the filter has a lot of dust on it . The filter did n't catch all the dust that floats in the air , but it caught much of the dust and allergens that otherwise you would have been breathing in . <p> A face mask works the same way with COVID-19 . When you breathe , your exhalations include a fine mist , and it is in that mist that a pathogen can travel . When you speak , you release the same fine mist . If you cough , you are expelling more of that mist , and , because of the force behind the cough , the mist travels farther . If you sneeze , again there is more of the mist , and the force of the sneeze makes it travel even farther . <p> Advertisement <p> So what does the face mask do ? It catches some of that mist -- not all of it , but some of it . And it reduces the velocity of your exhalations , whether it is normal breathing or coughing or sneezing , so the mist does not travel as far . When you breathe in , it catches some of the mist that is in the air , and if some of that mist carries pathogens , it reduces your risk of infection . <p> A face mask is n't complete protection , but it certainly helps . <p> Advertisement <p> -- Fred Lierman , Rockford <h> Visualizing the toll of COVID-19 <p> A number of years ago , I visited the Normandy American Cemetery in France . It is quite understandable that this crisis is absorbing our time , our attention and our resources . Yet , at this same time , the Earth is also headed toward a longer-term challenge that threatens to change the habitability of the planet and cause misery and suffering , even death , to millions of the world 's inhabitants . Global climate change is unfolding . While its effects sometimes seem to advance at a glacial pace , they are actually accelerating . <p> Coastal regions are threatened by rising oceans ; hundreds of thousands of animals and plant species are threatened by increasing temperatures and less consistent rainfall ; arable land is threatened by the encroachment of expanding deserts ; and our ocean seafood population is threatened by an increase in the temperature of the water .
