<p> Right now , on the brushy tundra of northern Alaska , grizzly bears are gathering at quiet streams and rivers , attracted by the largest calorie reward they can find -- spawning salmon . <p> Until recently , scientists did not know salmon swam up some of these waterways , nor that grizzlies were fattening up on them before entering hibernation . <p> Mathew Sorum is one of several biologists for Gates of the Arctic National Park and Preserve who tracked northern grizzly bears . He and his team found the bears were catching and eating chum salmon in places researchers had not documented as salmon streams . <p> Starting in about 2008 , the scientists , including Sorum 's colleagues Kyle Joly and Matt Cameron , heard from pilots who flew over the Brooks Range that they had noticed grizzly bears congregating along arctic rivers in August and September .
