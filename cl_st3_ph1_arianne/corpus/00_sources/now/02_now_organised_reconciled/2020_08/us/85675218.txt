First , he adopted his predecessor 's approach to DACA by simply refusing to enforce a law . Citizens rule themselves principally through the legislature . <p> For its part , the Supreme Court has added fuel to the fire by not only tolerating but also institutionalizing the idea that presidents can make law on their own . In its recent decision in DHS v. Regents , the court made President Obama 's approach to DACA -- refusal to execute a set of laws -- binding on his successors with respect to that policy . In doing that , the justices have confirmed presidents ' ability to make law -- or refusal to enforce laws -- and expanded it by occluding possible remedies . <p> Democrats were happy enough about the policy outcome that they did n't care that the court had dramatically expanded the powers of the executive at the expense of the legislature .
