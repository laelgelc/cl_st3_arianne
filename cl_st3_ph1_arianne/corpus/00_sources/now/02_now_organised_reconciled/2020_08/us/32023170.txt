<h> Pension fund investing is not the place to solve the ills of the world . <p> Getty Images/iStockphoto <p> On June 23 , the Department of Labor announced a proposed rule strongly limiting ESG ( or " social " ) investing for private pension plans covered by the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 ( ERISA ) . <p> I applaud this rule . <p> Pension fund investing is not the place to solve the ills of the world . ESG investing -- which involves environmental , social , and governance factors -- is a diversion that enriches financial managers , reduces participants ' retirement investment returns , and makes people think they are addressing a problem without doing anything substantial . No one can seriously think that stock selection is going to fix climate change . <p> The DOL takes a strong stand : " It is unlawful for a fiduciary to sacrifice return or accept additional risk to promote a public policy , political , or any other nonpecuniary goal .
