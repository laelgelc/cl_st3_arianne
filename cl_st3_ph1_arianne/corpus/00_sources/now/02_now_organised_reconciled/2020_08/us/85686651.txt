<h> Downloads <h> Overview <p> Globally , 23% of all deaths could be prevented through healthier environments -- and scaled-up action is required . This document presents an overview of sectoral actions that can be taken by various actors -- and the support that is being offered by the World Health Organization -- to create healthier environments , including in priority settings such as workplaces , cities , dwellings , health care facilities , and emergency settings . Key risk areas are addressed , such as air pollution ; water , sanitation and hygiene ; chemical safety and radiation ; and climate change . <p> The actions presented provide an initial overview and overall policy directions and refer to more detailed information for the next steps . They focus on intersectoral collaboration between a wide range of partners -- international organizations , governments , and national and subnational actors -- to create safe , enabling and equitable environments for better health and a more sustainable future .
