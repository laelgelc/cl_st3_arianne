The online research firm Civiqs found in June that voters approved of the movement by a 28-point margin . Rasmussen found 62% of likely voters viewed it favorably and 32% very favorably . <p> This demonstrates that there is a national consensus that the lives of black fellow citizens matter , which has not always been the case in our history .
