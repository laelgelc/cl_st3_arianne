Iowa energy companies had been required to reinvest about 7 percent of their revenue back into the state 's energy efficiency programs , but a 2018 bill supported by MidAmerican and Alliant capped their contributions at just 2 percent of their revenue . As this bill moved through the legislature , a group of residents in Decorah began campaigning for a municipal electric utility that would allow their community to continue investing in renewable energy . Linn County has seen some of the storm 's harshest damage and has suffered the longest power outages . At the storm 's peak , 97 percent of Linn County was without power .
