The article also indicated that Phoenix , Ariz. will experience Baghdad-like temperatures by mid-century . The Union of Concerned Scientists , a 50-year-old nonprofit organization founded at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology , analyzed climate projections to find out where and how often in the United States the heat index could top 105 degrees , if no action is taken to reduce carbon emissions . <p> Historically , Pinellas County has averaged one day a year when the heat index was at least 105 degrees . The analysis shows that by the mid-century , Pinellas County will average 77 days a year , and by the late century , 123 days a year .
