<p> Never has a presidential nominating convention been held like the one the Democrats wrapped up Thursday . No live crowds . No traditional roll call . No after-parties with delegates and fundraisers . <p> But the 2020 virtual gathering accomplished its main purpose : nominating Joe Biden and his running mate , Kamala Harris , as the Democratic candidates for the Nov. 3 election to face Republican President Donald Trump and Vice President Mike Pence in a contest that Biden Thursday called " a battle for the soul of this nation . " <p> For many Americans , the Democratic National Convention offered the first chance to see Biden live or hear from him directly since the coronavirus pandemic took hold of the nation in March and relegated the former vice president to his home base of Delaware . <p> For even more viewers , it was their first glimpse of Harris , the California senator who is making history as the first Black woman and the first Asian-American person on a major party 's presidential ticket . <p> The Republicans will have their four days of fanfare starting @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Trump and he gives his acceptance speech Thursday .
