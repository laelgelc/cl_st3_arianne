<h> CA Fires 2020 : 1.46M Acres Burn ; Containment Progress Grows <p> CALIFORNIA -- First responders battling the monstrous lightning complex fires over the weekend finally started to make significant progress on suppressing the firestorm that seized Northern California in early August . The two largest fires , the LNU and SCU Lightning Complex Fires , were more than halfway contained Monday . <p> As crews gained some control over the largest fires , many evacuation orders were lifted ; however , a near 40,000 people were still displaced from their homes . <p> California lawmakers also worked over the weekend to consider a last minute bill , AB 1659 , that could have raised $ billion for wildfires and climate change projects by imposing a fee on some utility customers . The bill , however , faced opposition from both parties . <p> More than 16,000 firefighters were active Monday , battling 18 major fires across the state , Cal Fire said . Since a wave of thunderstorms brought 14,000 lightning strikes that started Aug. 15 , more than 875 wildfires have cropped up around the Golden State @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 2,800 structures . There have been seven fatalities related to the fires . <p> A heat advisory is in effect for many of the northern counties as well as a Red Flag Warning for the inland areas of Del Norte County and Northeast Humboldt counties . The warning will remain in effect through 11 a.m. due to high winds and arid conditions . <p> Warmer weather is expected to persist toward the end of the week and through the weekend , and although the lightning strikes that ravaged the state last week have dissipated , they could make a resurgence this week in the upper portion of Northern California and the Sierra Nevada Mountain range . <p> Damage inspection teams worked to assess the devastation in Northern California throughout the weekend . <p> According to Cal Fire , 95 percent of wildfires are sparked by human activity .
