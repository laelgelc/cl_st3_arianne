<h> Sunday <p> Aug 30 , 2020 at 5:45 AM <p> I find it odd that some people still express skepticism or outright denial of climate change science . <p> Physicists derived one of the fundamental insights into climate change by 1850 : Air holds 7% more moisture for every 1 degree Celsius ( 1.8 degrees Fahrenheit ) it rises . The ability of air to hold more moisture rises exponentially with its temperature . <p> This relationship between air temperature and its ability to hold moisture derives directly from basic thermodynamics , the fundamental laws of physics . No scientist , organization or policymaker , even those supported by the fossil fuel industry , expresses doubts about -- much less denial of -- basic thermodynamics . <p> Search for the Clausius-Clapeyron relation on the web to find derivations and graphs of this relationship between air temperature and its ability to hold moisture .
