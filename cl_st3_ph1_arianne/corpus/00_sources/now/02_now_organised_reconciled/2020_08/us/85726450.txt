<p> An ancient stand of the world 's tallest trees has fallen victim to California 's raging wildfires . The CZU and SCU complex fires near Santa Cruz have ravaged Big Basin State Park . Big Basin is California 's oldest state park and home to the largest continuous stand of ancient coast redwoods south of San Francisco . <p> Some of the giant redwoods there are more than 50 feet around and as tall as the Statue of Liberty . At 1,000 to 1,800 years old , some may predate the Roman Empire . <p> More recently , Big Basin 's ancient trees provided the setting of a forest walk that James Stewart and Kim Novak take in Alfred Hitchcock 's Vertigo . <p> Ethan Barron , a business/tech reporter at the Mercury News in San Jose , CA , posted photos to his Twitter account today showing the devistation the fire has caused among the majestic trees . <p> The CZU and SCU complex fires have blackened 229,000 acres thus far , making it the 7th largest fire in the state 's history .
