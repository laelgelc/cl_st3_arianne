<h> Canada 's Trudeau plans sweeping social welfare reform , sources say <p> OTTAWA ( Reuters ) - Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau is weighing sweeping changes to the country 's social welfare system and a series of economic measures that will align Canada with ambitious climate goals , according to people familiar with the matter . <p> The plan to bolster the social safety net will especially help those hurt most by the pandemic and come after Trudeau replaced a fiscally cautious finance minister . <p> " The prime minister wants to go big , " said a government source , adding that Trudeau , 48 , sees the moves as part of his legacy . <p> Trudeau replaced former Finance Minister Bill Morneau , who resigned on Monday , with close ally Chrystia Freeland . <p> Trudeau said the pandemic had unmasked " fundamental gaps " in society and the country needed a long-term recovery plan that addressed issues head on . <p> " This is our chance to build a more resilient Canada , a Canada that is healthier and safer , greener and more competitive , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " he said after the cabinet shuffle . <p> Trudeau said he would seek a confidence vote in parliament over his plan when the body reconvenes in September . <p> Cabinet minister and close Trudeau ally Dominic LeBlanc said the plan " will contain a number of positive ideas that opposition parties have been talking about . " <p> These include measures on childcare , access to quality housing and an expanded employment insurance program , " he told the Canadian Broadcasting Corp on Wednesday . <p> Trudeau took office in 2015 and was re-elected last year , with a minority government . The son of former Prime Minister Pierre Trudeau , Trudeau has focused on boosting social programs , raising taxes on the highest earners and stressing the need to fight climate change . <p> Canada 's economy has been crippled by the COVID-19 pandemic , and Trudeau 's government has spent billions on support and other measures to alleviate the worst of the pandemic lockdown .
