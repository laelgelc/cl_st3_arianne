<p> The Arizona Corporation Commission has reached an impasse on making clean energy requirements for regulated electricity utilities even more stringent . <p> Meanwhile , a recent Arizona Supreme Court decision fundamentally alters the relationship between the commission and the Legislature on such matters . In fact , it raises a serious question as to whether the commission had the authority to adopt the clean energy requirements currently in place , much less ones that further dictate the future energy mix for regulated utilities . <p> The question immediately before the court was whether the commission had the authority to suspend the ability of Johnson Utilities to manage its water and wastewater services in Pinal County and appoint a substitute interim manager . <p> The court , by a 6-1 margin , decided that it did . But before reaching that conclusion , the court pretty much rewrote the case law on the commission 's authority in ways with far-reaching consequences . <h> Commission has 2 kinds of authority <p> The commission is a constitutional body . It 's core authority is set forth in a prolix run-on sentence that would make any English teacher faint , assuming English teachers still try to impart writing skills these days . <p> The sentence says that the commission " shall prescribe ... just and reasonable rates . " And it says that the commission " may ... make and enforce reasonable rules , regulations , and orders for the convenience , comfort , and safety , and the preservation of the health , of the employees and patrons of such corporations . " <p> Looking for the other side of the story ? The first is called the commission 's ratemaking authority . This authority is exclusive to the commission . The Legislature can not dictate outcomes or processes . <p> The second is called the commission 's permissive authority . It can act , but so can the Legislature . That 's old law . What is new in the Johnson decision is the finding that , if the commission and the Legislature conflict , the Legislature wins . The Legislature 's police-powers authority on matters of public safety and health trumps the commission 's permissive authority . <h> Court scaled back its ratemaking power <p> The Johnson decision also sharply circumscribes what falls within the commission 's exclusive ratemaking authority . Beginning in the early 1990s , previous justices had begun expanding this writ beyond just actual rate-setting to encompass virtually anything that could be argued to affect rates now or in the future . <p> This reached its apogee with a 2011 Court of Appeals decision , Miller . At issue was the commission 's current clean energy requirements . Regulated electric utilities had to produce certain percentages of their power from renewable sources , principally solar , by certain dates . And a certain percentage of that power had to come from rooftop systems . <p> The Miller court upheld these requirements as within the commission 's exclusive ratemaking authority , although it did n't involve any setting of rates . The argument was that a utility 's energy mix affects future rates so the commission dictating them had a " sufficient nexus " with setting rates to sweep them under the commission 's exclusive ratemaking authority . <p> The Supreme Court at the time declined to hear an appeal of Miller . But this court , in Johnson , flatly states that Miller was incorrectly decided . That ratemaking means setting rates , period . It does n't include the broad ambit of anything that could be argued to affect rates in the future . <h> Clean energy requirements have no legs <p> That knocks the legal legs out from under the current clean energy requirements . They can no longer be argued to be part of the commission 's exclusive ratemaking authority . <p> And it would be quite a stretch to argue that they come under the commission 's permissive authority . The commission has authority to adopt regulations to protect health and safety . But what is clearly contemplated is doing such things as take action to stop raw sewage leaks , as was done with respect to Johnson Utilities . Not to take on global environmental issues such as climate change . <p> And even if it can be argued that clean energy requirements are within the commission 's permissive authority , Johnson makes it clear that the Legislature can supersede them . That 's been contemplated at the Legislature in the past , but there was considerable doubt at the time about whether it had the authority to do so . <p> It 's always been foolish to believe that a committee of politicians has the clairvoyance to discern optimal energy mixes decades into the future . <p> Solar energy is becoming more efficient and price competitive . It will gain market share without now legally questionable mandates and subsidies .
