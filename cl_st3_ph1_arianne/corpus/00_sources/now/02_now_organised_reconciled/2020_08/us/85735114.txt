The apparel industry is the third-largest guzzler of water globally and its carbon emissions are off the charts . Something like $120 billion worth of new fabric goes to waste every year , winding up in a landfill or ( gulp ) burned . Enter Stephanie Benedetto , a 2020 Grist 50 Fixer , who is helping to create a solution to all that waste . Her company , Queen of Raw , gives textile sellers a new market to offer up excess fabrics , at a discount , to interested buyers . <p> With around 130,000 users on the platform , Benedetto says that Queen of Raw saved more than 1 billion gallons of water that would have gone into making new material , in just one three-month period recently .
