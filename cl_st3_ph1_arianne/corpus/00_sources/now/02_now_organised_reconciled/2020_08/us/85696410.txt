<h> Login to your account <h> Climate change still perceived as the most pressing problem of our time according to Vattenfall report <p> LONDON , Aug. 17 , 2020 /PRNewswire/ -- The new survey complements a report by Vattenfall carried out in December 2019 , which explored people 's attitudes and emotional response to the conversation on climate change . This first round of research found that climate change was seen as the most serious problem in the world -- ahead of any other global issue such as poverty , wars and conflict , or economic recession . The follow-up study , undertaken in June 2020 , was designed to find out if and how views on climate change have evolved in the wake of the global health pandemic . <p> While there is a sharp increase in people 's concern about epidemics and economic recession , nearly a third ( 28% ) of people in Sweden , Germany , the Netherlands , Denmark , Finland , France and the United Kingdom indicate that they see climate change as the most pressing global issue in the world today .
