Before the pandemic the Trump policy mix of tax reform , deregulation and unleashing domestic energy production produced the best wage gains in two decades for the lowest-skilled Americans . Among black workers the jobless rate hit 5.4% , the lowest since that statistic started being kept in the 1970s .
