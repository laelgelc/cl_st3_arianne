<h> Big Oil Asset Write-Downs Are Not The End Of The Oil Age <p> Climate change activists have long lobbied for divestment from fossil fuel-producing companies . They have largely failed in this quest . This year , the steep falls in the value of the large oil and gas companies , however , occurred with a rapidity that astonished market watchers . Within weeks , the coronavirus pandemic and the oil market-share battle between Saudi Arabia and Russia launched after the collapse of the OPEC+ talks in early March led to unprecedented falls in Big Oil equity . <p> The S&amp;P Global Oil Index , measuring the performance of 120 of the largest , publicly-traded companies engaged in oil &amp; gas fell by 45% in the month to March 18th when Brent crude was trading at $25/barrel .
