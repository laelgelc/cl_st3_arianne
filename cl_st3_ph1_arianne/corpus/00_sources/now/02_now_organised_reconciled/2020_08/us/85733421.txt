<p> The greater sage-grouse has a very hard life . Aside from facing predators like foxes , bobcats , and coyotes , the ground-dwelling western bird is also threatened by oil and gas development , miners , ranchers , hunters , climate change , invasive grasses , and , now , Kanye West . <p> This spring the rapper and presidential hopeful , who purchased a sprawling ranch in Cody , Wyoming , in 2019 , got the go-ahead to expand development on the property , which happens to be situated in key sage-grouse habitat . And while West is n't likely to single-handedly wipe out the sage-grouse with a few ( albeit big ) ranch upgrades , the episode illustrates how a lack of protection under the Trump administration -- which has aggressively sought to dismantle safeguards for the species -- has further threatened the already vulnerable bird . <p> According to permits shared with Mother Jones by officials from Park County , Wyoming , and as first reported by TMZ last month , West plans to add to his ranch several new structures : a two-bedroom lake house @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a 10-bedroom , 52,000 square-foot residence , which coincidentally would be about the size of the White House .
