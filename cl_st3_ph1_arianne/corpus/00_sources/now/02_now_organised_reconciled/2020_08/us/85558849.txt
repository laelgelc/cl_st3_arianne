( Lori Van Buren/Times Union ) <p> Lori Van Buren/Albany Times Union <p> It 's not looking good for the red , white and blue these days . We are hopelessly divided down party lines on the following issues that would be no-brainers for most well-educated societies : <p> * Should we wear masks and limit movement of the public during a deadly ( 150,000-plus deaths and 4 million-plus infected ) viral pandemic fueled by contact transmission ? * Should we encourage or discourage gun ownership for all in a society that is off the charts for gun violence and allows an association of gun manufacturers ( National Rifle Association ) to dictate public policy ? * Should we , or should we not , seek justice and make reparations to American citizens who have been horribly mistreated through our history because of their race ? * Is climate change a big hoax despite @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ or allowed to pursue their profit motives with no constraints on their impact to human health or the environment ? * Do we make abortions illegal and force folks to add a couple million more kids each year to a society that already has more neglected and abused children ( 400,000 kids in foster care and 100,000 waiting to be adopted ) than we can take care of ? * Do we continue to let public policy be determined by the national religion that defies science and believes in holy ghosts , devils , angels and is 4 billion years off the mark for when the planet Earth was formed ? <p> We know where the party of President Donald Trump comes down on these issues . It is shameful . That party has clearly chosen greed and scape-goating over the welfare of our citizens and the planet . All great societies usually implode from within . We are on the precipice of that implosion and we probably deserve it as we all have wallowed in our comforts until it may be too late .
