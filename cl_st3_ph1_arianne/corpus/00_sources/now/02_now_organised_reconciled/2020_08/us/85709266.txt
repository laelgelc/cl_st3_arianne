<h> BoA : Replacing Chinese Suppliers Comes With $1T Price Tag <p> U.S. and European companies will have to spend as much as $1 trillion to replace Chinese suppliers , but the expense will be worth it , according to Bank of America research cited by CNBC on Tuesday ( Aug. 18 ) . <p> The bank 's researchers , based on a survey of analysts around the world , concluded that Western companies had already started contacting supply chains when COVID-19 accelerated the process . According to the report , 75 percent of companies whose supply chains included China had expanded their efforts to source materials and components closer to home . <p> The CNBC listed four reasons cited by the BoA researchers for the move toward more local sourcing : trade fights , national security issues , climate change and increased automation . <p> BofA 's Candace Browning said , according to CNBC : " While COVID has acted as a catalyst to accelerate this change , the underlying reasons are grounded in a shift to ' stakeholder capitalism , ' concluding relocation favors a broader community of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> The BofA report states that companies are most likely to bring operations from China to their home countries , but absent that , to bring them to countries that are eyed as " allies .
