<h> Dead dolphins have been washing up on Mauritius shores weeks after an oil spill disaster <p> On the morning of Aug. 26 , residents of Mauritius 's Southeast region discovered several stranded dolphins close to the site of the oil spill from the Japanese vessel MV Wakashio , which ran aground on July 25 causing what some scientists have described as the worst ecological disaster in the Indian Ocean . <p> Greenpeace has confirmed four dolphins were found dead and says four others are in critical health . Wireagencies reported up to 17 dead dolphins were found and the environmental group fears more marine life could be at risk . <p> " It is very likely that there are more who have died and we expect more to be found dead and ailing , " said Tal Harris , International Communications Coordinator for Greenpeace Africa .
