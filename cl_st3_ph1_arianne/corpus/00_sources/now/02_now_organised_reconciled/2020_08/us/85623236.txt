<p> In an opinion piece , Paul Gallay of Riverkeeper described past incidents at Indian Point as " major malfunctions . " Yet , none of those events , or the other plant 's operations he paints as " safety problems , " have put the public in any danger . <p> Tritium levels measured in test wells under Indian Point do not represent concentrations in the Hudson River or drinking water supplies . Levels in the river and beyond are essentially undetectable . Furthermore , this year federal regulators reported that bolts at all seven similar Westinghouse reactors--including Indian Point--were replaced . The Nuclear Regulatory Commission determined that their condition did not warrant shutdown or constitute a significant safety concern . While the 2003 report cited by Gallay made recommendations about outreach , monitoring and coordination , it did not find that estimates for safe , timely evacuation in an emergency were inaccurate .
