Following the Great Depression , New Deal policies transformed the economy , putting people to work in public projects that still benefit us today . In 2018 , the National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health found that 1 in 10 miners who worked in underground mines for at least 25 years have black lung disease -- 1 in 5 in central Appalachia . Just as black lung claims are going up and the coronavirus wreaks havoc on people with lung conditions , coal companies have asked policymakers to cut the tax that funds the Black Lung Trust . <p> Burning coal takes a toll on the surrounding community too .
