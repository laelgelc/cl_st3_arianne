<h> A Closer Look at Who 's Protecting South Florida 's Beaches , Reefs and Wildlife <p> The beaches of Miami-Dade and Broward counties are a cherished resource , where the biodiversity of the ocean meets the cultural diversity of South Florida . Making sure the beach is healthy for future generations actually begins in the ocean itself , with corals . <p> In 2014 , the town of Lauderdale-By-The-Sea began working with NOVA Southeastern University to help restore and conserve the corals just off-shore . David Gilliam , an associate professor at NOVA , said that " Corals are the living organism that creates our reefs . Reefs are created by the growth of corals , one on top of each other , for hundreds and hundreds of years , and if you lose the corals that live on top , that reef growth will eventually stop . " <p> Reefs around the world are dying because of climate change and the warming oceans .
