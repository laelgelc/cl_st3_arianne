FFO is a financial measure that Inter Pipeline uses in managing its business and in assessing future cash requirements that impact the determination of future dividends to shareholders . Inter Pipeline expresses FFO as cash provided by operating activities less net changes in non-cash working capital . The impact of net change in non-cash working capital is excluded in the calculation of FFO primarily to compensate for the seasonality of working capital throughout the year . Certain Inter Pipeline revenue contracts dictate an exchange of cash that differs , on a monthly basis , from the recognition of revenue . Within a 12-month calendar year , there is minimal variation between revenue recognized and cash exchanged . Inter Pipeline therefore excludes the net change in non-cash working capital in its calculation of FFO to mitigate its quarterly impact . The intent is to not skew the results of Inter Pipeline in any quarter for exchanges of cash , but to focus the results on cash that is generated in any reporting period .
