<p> Today , the Trump Administration announced that it would allow oil drilling in Alaska 's Arctic National Wildlife Refuge . The Trump Administration advanced this endeavor , while many European producers have been backing off their own extraction efforts as the coronavirus pandemic has caused oil prices to drop . <p> The nearly 20 million acres of land and water within the Refuge have been protected from oil and natural gas development since 1980 , when the Alaska National Interest Lands Conservation Act ( ANILCA ) was passed . As a result , it has remained virtually untouched by human activity for the past four decades ( the indirect effects of climate change and pollution , notwithstanding ) . <p> The Refuge carries enormous cultural to the Gwich'in and Inupiaq tribes that have subsisted off these lands for millenia . Additionally , it is habitat to over 270 species , including musk oxen , Porcupine caribou that migrate across these lands , and endangered polar bears that den there .
