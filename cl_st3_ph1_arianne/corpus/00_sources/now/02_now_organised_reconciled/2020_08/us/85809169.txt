Or , at least , they can mean whatever you want them to mean . <p> Some of those memes get traction -- " zero waste " and " net zero " are two relatively recent examples having their moment . But it 's not always what it seems . <p> So , what does " climate risk " actually mean ? <h> Minimize or manage ? <p> First , it 's important to understand that " risk " means different things in business than it does in our personal lives . For most individuals , the word is synonymous with " danger " -- the risk that we might be infected with coronavirus , for example , or that we could fall into financial distress because of a job loss or some other event . Or that something we do n't want others to know gets found out . <p> Risk , in that context , is something to be minimized or avoided altogether . <p> Not so in business .
