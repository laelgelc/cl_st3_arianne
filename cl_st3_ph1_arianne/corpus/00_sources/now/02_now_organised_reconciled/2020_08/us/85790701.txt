<h> Just a quick reminder that the planet is on fire Sheneman <p> With the social upheaval gripping the nation you 'd be forgiven for not noticing that mother nature is currently rampaging all over our exceptional American hind quarters . Derecho wind storms in the midwest , enormous California wildfires and a massive Category 4 hurricane ravaging Louisiana all within the space of a few weeks are an ill harbinger of a future shaped by climate change . <p> Climate change suffers from a branding problem . Early on we referred to man-made climate change as " global warming " a much more palatable way of describing an existential threat to humanity . The global warming moniker allows deniers to crow loudly every time it dips below 30 degrees and gives bad faith politicians cause to throw snowballs on the Senate floor as proof that everything 's just dandy . Global warming as a label just does n't capture the depth and breadth of the issues we 're facing . <p> Some of the consequences of climate change are now inevitable . It 's not a question of if @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ weather events are a reality we are going to have to live with for decades to come . Droughts , wildfires and massive superstorms are the new normal . Somehow , we still have a chance to mitigate the ravaging effects climate change will have on us . And this is all about us as a species , not the planet . The sun has about 10 billion years of fuel left to burn before it expands into a white dwarf and swallows everything in its path , until then the planet will continue circling the star at the center of our solar system , we just may not be on it .
