<h> Texas Flood Map &amp; Tracker <p> Interact with the map for real-time flood updates in the Houston region , long-term flood risk assessments for the state of Texas , and live weather radar . <h> About the Data <p> Flood Risk : Enter a property address in the search bar or zoom and drag the map to see the percentage of properties in a given area that are at risk of flooding in the next 30 years due to high-intensity rainfall , overflowing rivers , streams and bayous , high tides and/or coastal storm surge . The data is provided by FloodFactor , a free online tool created by the First Street Foundation , a nonprofit that uses historical environmental data and climate change projections to calculate the likelihood of floods . <p> Flood Warnings : The flood map will automatically update with " likely " or " possible " flooding alerts from Harris County Flood Control District gauges ; National Weather Service flood warnings ; National Hurricane Center storm surge predictions ; and staff reports of street closures and area flooding across the Houston region . <p> Live @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ every 10 minutes .
