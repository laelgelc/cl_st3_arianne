<h> Hurricanes Like Laura Are More Likely Because Of Climate Change <p> Hurricane Laura approaches the Gulf Coast of the U.S. on Wednesday . The storm rapidly intensified before it made landfall . ( NOAA via AP ) <p> Hurricane Laura 's top wind speeds nearly doubled in just 24 hours as it approached the border between Texas and Louisiana . The wall of water it pushed in front of it grew until forecasters warned that it would produce " unsurvivable " storm surge . <p> Laura 's rapid intensification is one hallmark of climate change . As the Earth warms up , the water on the surface of the ocean gets hotter . Hot water is like a battery charger for hurricanes ; it send energy and moisture into the storm as it forms and helps it grow more powerful and deadly . <p> A study last year found that storms are more likely to become major hurricanes very quickly .
