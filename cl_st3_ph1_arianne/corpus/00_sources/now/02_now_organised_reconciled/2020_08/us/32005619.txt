<p> WASHINGTON -- Joe Biden personally intervened to keep the word " occupation " out of the official Democratic platform , according to a new report . <p> Foreign Policy reported Thursday that Biden intervened after pro-Israel groups appealed to him , citing three sources , including Jason Isaacson , the chief policy and political affairs officer at the American Jewish Committee . <p> " The question of whether to allow the text to refer to ' occupation ' or use the phrase ' end the occupation ' was taken to the vice president and he said ' no , ' " Isaacson told Foreign Policy . <p> Prior to Biden 's intervention , progressives in the party had secured agreement to include the word for the first time in the Democratic platform . <p> Ultimately , the section on Israel included more robust language defending the rights of the Palestinians to a state . It also condemned the boycott Israel movement . <p> The platform included a number of victories for progressives on domestic issues , including closing the wage gap and climate change . Vermont Sen.
