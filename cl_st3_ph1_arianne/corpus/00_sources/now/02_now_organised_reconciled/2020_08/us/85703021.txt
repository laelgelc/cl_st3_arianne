He 's used them to study the sounds of the marine environment , why sperm whales collide with ferries in the Canary Islands , and the acoustic problems that pink dolphins face in the Amazon river . <p> Last year , he and his team from the Laboratory of Applied Bioacoustics from Polytechnic University of Catalonia ( UPC ) -BarcelonaTech in Vilanova i la Geltr ? were called in solve a problem in India . <p> Records show that the Siliguri-Jalapaiguri railway line has the largest number of fatal collisions involving elephants in the country . Over the past 10 years , trains on this line have struck and killed more than 200 elephants . <p> The growth of human activity in India , with the consequent development of the railway network and the expansion of cities and plantations , increasingly brings humans and elephants into conflict . <p> Elephants enter the plantations that have replaced areas of forest and feed on crops .
