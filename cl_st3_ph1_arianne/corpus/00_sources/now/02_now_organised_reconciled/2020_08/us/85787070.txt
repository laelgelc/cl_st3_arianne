For instance , if the tropical Pacific is warmer and trade winds are weaker than usual -- an El Ni ? o event -flooding in California typically occurs and monsoon failures in India and East Asia are detrimental to local rice production . In contrast , during a La Ni ? a the global weather patterns reverse with cooler temperatures and stronger trade winds in the tropical Pacific .
