As a newly minted doctor , beginning the job as COVID-19 ravaged New York in April , I felt entitled to my anger : I lost five of the first 10 patients for whom I cared . As cases began to ramp up again across the country , I wondered , had the people going to pool parties and brunches learned nothing from what we had gone through ? <p> But also , as a doctor , I knew I had to reframe my thinking .
