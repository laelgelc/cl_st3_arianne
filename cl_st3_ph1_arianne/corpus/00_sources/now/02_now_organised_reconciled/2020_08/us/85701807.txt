<p> The Trump administration said Monday it will open up the Arctic National Wildlife Refuge to drilling , a move that will allow oil and gas rights to be auctioned off in the heart of one of the nation 's most iconic wild places . Achieving a goal Republicans have sought for 40 years , the action marks a capstone for an administration that has ignored calls to reduce fossil fuel consumption in the face of climate change . <p> The move will allow leasing on the 1.6 million-acre coastal plain , part of a nearly pristine wilderness that is home to migrating caribou and waterfowl as well as polar bears and foxes that live there year-round .
