<p> Aug. 19 , 2020 , marks one year since the Business Roundtable released a new statement about the purpose of corporations , choosing so-called " stakeholders " over shareholders in pursuit of an " economy to serve all Americans . " It was a bold move that jolted the business press to life in the sultry days of August and offered some welcome news to those who believe we need business to make progress on significant challenges , from climate change to inequality . It felt long overdue . <p> Business executives interviewed at the time said the old mantra of " shareholder value " was out of touch with the complexity of managing a global enterprise .
