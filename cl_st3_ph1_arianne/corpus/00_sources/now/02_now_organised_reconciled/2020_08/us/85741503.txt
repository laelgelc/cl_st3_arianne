<h> Letter : Facing our problems makes America greater <p> The leader of the free world , formerly leading the world in fostering democracy , leading the world in science , medical research and technological innovation , the country with the largest economy , is now approaching a quarter million American deaths due to the pandemic-causing Covid-19 virus . <p> We are now exceptional by leading the world in Covid-19 deaths and the largest crumbling economy . <p> Should n't we be ashamed at how the world now views us in decline ? <p> Our country had no plans for a pandemic . Our country , our government leadership , never responded quick enough , smart enough nor thoroughly enough to the threat . We never had enough personal protective equipment , testing , hospital beds or medical workers . <p> For those who believe it is unpatriotic to not believe in American exceptionalism , please consider what it should take for us to merit that title . We are not currently honoring that title . <p> We are not the leader in climate change , we are not the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ nor the protector of liberty in the form of civil rights for our country nor others . But we lead in Covid-19 deaths . <p> Are n't you ashamed ? Should n't this pandemic be enough of an impetus to demand and fight for change ? <p> I am trying to be a patriot . Ignoring our problems serves no one except our enemies . Acknowledging our problems helps us work on that which will make us greater . Let 's do that .
