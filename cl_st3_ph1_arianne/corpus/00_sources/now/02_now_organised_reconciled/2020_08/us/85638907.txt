<p> HYDERABAD , India , Aug. 10 , 2020 /PRNewswire/ -- Atal Incubation Centre at International Institute of Information Technology ( AIC-IIITH ) has selected 10 startups for its flagship Akash incubation program for tech-based social enterprises working on Sustainable Development goals . <p> AIC-IIITH received 100 applications from 17 states , out of which 17 were selected for the final round .
