<h> Fossils reveal diversity of animal life roaming Europe 2 million years ago <p> IMAGE : Research team members Marius Robu , Alexandru Petculescu and Claire Terhune discuss where to look for fossils in the Olte ? River Valley . view more <p> Credit : University of Arkansas <p> A re-analysis of fossils from one of Europe 's most significant paleontological sites reveals a wide diversity of animal species , including a large terrestrial monkey , short-necked giraffe , rhinos and saber-toothed cats . <p> These and other species roamed the open grasslands of Eastern Europe during the early Pleistocene , approximately 2 million years ago . Ultimately , the researchers hope the fossils will provide clues about how and when early humans migrated to Eurasia from Africa . Reconstructions of past environments like this also could help researchers better understand future climate change . <p> " My colleagues and I are excited to draw attention back to the fossil site of Gr ? unceanu and the fossil potential of the Olte ? River Valley of Romania , " said Claire Terhune , associate professor of anthropology at the University @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ community . We found multiple animals that had n't been clearly identified in the area before , and many that are no longer found in Europe at all . Of course , we think these findings alone are interesting , but they also have important implications for early humans moving into the continent at that time . " <p> About 124 miles west of the Romanian capital of Bucharest , the Olte ? River Valley , including the the important site of Gr ? unceanu , is one of Eastern Europe 's richest fossil deposits . Many Olte ? Valley fossil sites , including Gr ? unceanu , were discovered in the 1960s after landslides caused in part by deforestation due to increased agricultural activity in the area . <p> Archeologists and paleontologists from the Emil Racovi ? ? Institute of Speleology in Bucharest excavated the sites soon after they were discovered . Fossils were recovered and stored at the institute , and scholarly publications about the sites flourished in the 1970s and 1980s . But interest in these fossils and sites waned over the past 20 to 30 years @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ fossils were lost . <p> Since 2012 , the international team , including Terhune and researchers from Romania , the United States , Sweden and France , has focused on this important fossil region . Their work has included extensive identification of fossils at the institute and additional field work . <p> In addition to the species mentioned above , the researchers identified fossil remains of animals similar to modern-day moose , bison , deer , horse , ostrich , pig and many others . They also identified a fossil species of pangolin , which were thought to have existed in Europe during the early Pleistocene but had not been solidly confirmed until now .
