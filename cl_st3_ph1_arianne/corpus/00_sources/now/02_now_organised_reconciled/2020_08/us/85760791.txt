<h> Sales of California pollution permits rebound in 3rd quarter <p> SACRAMENTO , Calif . ( AP ) -- Sales of permits that California businesses need to emit pollution rebounded in recent months after a dip in May due to the pandemic , according to state figures released Tuesday . <p> California auctions off carbon allowances through its cap-and-trade program , which aims to reduce greenhouse gas emissions . Proceeds of the program go to projects aimed at fighting climate change . Advocates had been worried those programs would suffer as businesses shut down due to coronavirus and needed fewer permits . <p> The state sold about 52 million of the 59 million carbon allowances for sale this quarter , according to numbers from the California Air Resources Board . That 's more than double what it sold in May . <p> California also sold all 8.6 million permits it was auctioning for the year 2023 , allowing companies to get a cheaper price for future pollution allowances .
