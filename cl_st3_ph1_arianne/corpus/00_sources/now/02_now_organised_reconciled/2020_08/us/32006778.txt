Questions are being asked about how this happened , could it have been prevented , and more importantly what steps should now be taken to prevent a similar tragedy from occurring anywhere else in the world . <h> A revolution from space <p> Part of the answer to these questions may come from space . The last ten years have seen a technological revolution in small cube and nano satellites . There are now over 50 private operators in space , operating satellites that track vessel movement , imagery , synthetic aperture radar through clouds and the night sky , as well as a range of environmental data that is critical for climate change . <p> In 2020 , there are 2666 satellites in space ( and growing each month ) , over 70% of which are operated ... + by private companies . <p> getty <p> Advances in data science has meant that these satellites can also be used forensically - effectively to go back in time to track the activities on the ocean . <p> One leading company in this field is Israeli-based satellite analytics company , Windward , who recently appointed former BP CEO Lord Browne as Chairman . <p> Using their data analytics platform , they have been able to trace the movement of the ' MV Wakashio ' over the past week of its travels , including the critical last 2 days prior to impact .
