<h> Drills wo n't come near Utah 's iconic parks <p> It 's Thursday , August 13 , and the Bureau of Land Management will not be selling out Utah 's national parks to the oil industry . <p> Earlier this year , land as close as half a mile away from Utah 's prized Canyonlands and Arches National Parks was going to be offered to oil and gas companies in the Bureau of Land Management 's ( BLM ) September lease auction . But on Tuesday , the agency reversed course and agreed to take 87,000 acres near the two parks off the table . <p> The BLM had planned to lease more than 114,000 acres of public land in southern Utah for drilling . But the Moab and Grand County councils , which govern the region outside the parks , fought the proposal , arguing that the addition of roads , rigs , and pipelines to the iconic landscape would threaten the local tourism and recreation economy .
