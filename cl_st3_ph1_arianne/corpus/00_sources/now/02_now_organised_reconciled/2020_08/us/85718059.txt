Kamala Harris , who officially accepted the vice presidential nomination . <p> The VERIFY team investigated the claims they made during their speeches . <p> Actress Kerry Washington claimed that " Joe Biden introduced one of the first climate bills in Congress . " <p> This claim accurately represents the findings of a widely shared report by the Institute for Policy Studies , but the number is a bit misleading . <p> The institute monitored the collective wealth of billionaires in the U.S. and found that their total net worth grew from $2.948 trillion to $3.531 trillion during the pandemic .
