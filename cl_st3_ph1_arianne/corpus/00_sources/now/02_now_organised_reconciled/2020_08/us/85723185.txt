<h> The derecho did n't have to knock out power for days . Here 's how we can improve the grid . <p> For decades , scientists have been warning us that our deteriorating electric grid would fail us when we needed it most . Now with the latest extreme weather event , a derecho , Iowa is paying the price of inaction . <p> It does not have to be this way . There are many ways to upgrade the grid and make it more resilient . The use of storage is one type of improvement that will not only increase the resiliency in the grid , but reduce the need to burn fossil fuels , which causes climate change and violent weather . If storage systems were used , the grid could be decentralized and less subject to widespread outages . <p> If these storage systems were coupled with renewable energy sources , such as solar panels and wind turbines , these micro-grids could provide days of energy in emergencies . In normal times , the storage systems could store energy when there is an abundance of electricity @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ be used during peak-use periods . By reducing the electricity production needed in peak times and storing excess renewable energy produced in low-need times , the burning of fossil fuel for electricity production could be decreased and ultimately reduced to zero . <p> For years , individual homes and essential facilities have added generators in order to be able to endure power outages that happen during thunderstorms and ice storms . These emergency generators are determined cost-effective due to the potential for loss of life and loss of vital resources when electrical service is interrupted . Most emergency generators are operated using fossil fuels . Now with great strides in battery storage , there are battery systems available for individual homeowners as well as larger facilities such as hospitals or even neighborhoods . In many scenarios , it is now cost-effective to implement battery storage projects to reduce peak load without factoring in any losses associated with power outages . This is because electricity from renewable energy has become so much cheaper than electricity produced from coal . <p> The need is here and the technology is available .
