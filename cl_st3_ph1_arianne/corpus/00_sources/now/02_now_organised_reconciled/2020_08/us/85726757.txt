<h> Nobel Prize Winner Dr. Sheldon Glashow Endorses Dr. Natalia Linos <h> Aug 21 , 2020 5:32 pm ETUpdated Aug 21 , 2020 5:34 pm ET <p> ( Nobel aureate Dr. Sheldon L Glashow ) <p> Brookline , MA - Today , Nobel Prize-winning American theoretical physicist and Brookline resident Dr. Sheldon Glashow announced his endorsement of Dr. Natalia Linos to represent Massachusetts ' 4th Congressional District . Dr. Glashow was the Metcalf Professor of Mathematics and Physics at Boston University and Eugene Higgins Professor of Physics at Harvard University . Dr. Glashow will hold a campaign event for Dr. Linos on Tuesday August 25 to discuss the importance of science in government . <p> " Congress must confront critical dangers - many demanding scientific literacy - such as COVID-19 along with other pandemics sure to follow , issues related to nuclear weaponry and power , the widespread pollution of our air , water and land , and the truly existential threat of climate change . There are far too few scientists within our government . As an expert epidemiologist experienced in matters of public health and natural resources , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ play an essential role as our caring and concerned Congresswoman , " said Dr. Glashow . <p> " I 'm thrilled to have the support of Nobel Laureate Dr. Glashow as I campaign to be a voice for science and equity in Congress , " said Dr. Linos . " He 's dedicated his career to the pursuit of scientific research and discovery , and understands the crucial need for data-driven policy leadership on complex issues impacting our environment and health . I 'm proud to have his support as we build momentum heading into the September 1 primary .
