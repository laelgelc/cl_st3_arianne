They also found declines in lifetime wealth from the upper to lower-middle classes . <p> To gauge the wealth tax 's impact , Diamond and Zodrow had to make assumptions about how the money would be used . Paying down the national debt , for instance , has different implications for capital allocation than beefing up welfare programs .
