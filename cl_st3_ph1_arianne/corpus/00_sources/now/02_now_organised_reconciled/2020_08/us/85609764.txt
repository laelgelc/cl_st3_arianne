<h> Golden Shoals Release Self-Titled Full-Length Album <p> The road to Golden Shoals has been a long , fruitful journey for Amy Alvey and Mark Kilianski . The duo has toured on foot-gig to gig with backpacks and instrument cases-for weeks at a time ; called Asheville , Boston , California , and New Jersey home ; and lived in various moving vehicles on the road for the past seven years under different names and incarnations . After all of that , their new self-titled record , Golden Shoals , represents a fresh start for Alvey and Kilianski ; one that is more inclusive of the inspirations they 've taken in since beginning their musical journey . <p> Out today , Golden Shoals contains twelve new songs which examine love and loss , personal growth , and political strife ; all through an inward-facing lens and void of pretense or preachiness .
