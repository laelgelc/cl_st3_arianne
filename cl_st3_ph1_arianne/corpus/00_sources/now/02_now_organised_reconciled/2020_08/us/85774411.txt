<h> TurfMutt 's Mutt Mulligan Celebrates National Dog Day <p> ALEXANDRIA , Va. , Aug. 26 , 2020 /PRNewswire/ -- Today is National Dog Day , and there 's no better time to celebrate humankind 's best friend with a walk outside . Mutt Mulligan , a rescue dog and the spokesdog for the TurfMutt Foundation , reminds everyone that getting out into some green space is great for your dog and for you ! <p> During the COVID-19 pandemic , dogs provide companionship , joy and consistency for many people . That 's why so many have adopted new pets from animal rescues in 2020 , and why so many people are trying to adopt new pets now . <p> Mutt Mulligan ( or " Mo-Mo " ) , the new TurfMutt spokesdog , with her human , Kris Kiser , President of the TurfMutt Foundation . <p> Mulligan was adopted at Lucky 's Mutt Madness in 2019 by Kris Kiser . <p> Mutt Mulligan enjoying her yard . <p> For a decade , the TurfMutt Foundation has advocated the importance of managed landscapes and other green space as @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ these green spaces for wildlife food and habitat .
