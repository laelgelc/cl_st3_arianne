<p> Hurricane season is in full swing , and there 's no greater time than the present to make sure you 're up on all the terminology you may encounter over the next several months . It has been a record-setting hurricane season so far , and now Hurricane Laura is bearing down on the coasts of Texas and Louisiana . <h> Storm designations <p> Tropical wave : An area of disturbed weather that meanders east through the tropics and is accompanied by disorganized shower and thunderstorm activity . The majority of tropical waves that create Atlantic hurricanes are African easterly waves that exit off the west coast of Africa . <p> Tropical depression : A tropical wave that has begun to organize and has a " closed circulation , " or discernible spin . Winds in a tropical depression are less than 39 mph . <p> AD <p> AD <p> Tropical cyclone : A catchall term that includes tropical storms and hurricanes . Tropical cyclones do not have fronts . They spin counterclockwise in the Northern Hemisphere and clockwise in the Southern Hemisphere . They can not cross the equator . <p> Tropical storm : A tropical cyclone that has one-minute sustained winds between 39 mph and 73 mph . Tropical storms have persistent , organized thunderstorms that orbit around a well-defined center . <p> Major hurricane : A hurricane rated at Category 3 strength or greater on the Saffir-Simpson hurricane scale , with maximum sustained winds of 111 mph or greater . <h> Storm phrases <p> Typhoon : A hurricane-strength tropical cyclone in the West Pacific Ocean . <p> AD <p> Squall : Strictly speaking , a squall is characterized by an abrupt increase in wind speed of at least 18 mph , with sustained breezes at 25 mph for more than two minutes . In the rain bands , air is generally rising , with pockets of air cooling and condensing into heavy downpours . When more air is present over a location , a column of atmosphere has more weight , resulting in high pressure . But when air is removed from the column , low pressure results . Hurricanes are a powerful source of suction because of their low pressure center . The eyewall boasts the strongest winds , greatest surge and most rapidly rising air inside a storm . These simulations are produced by computer models , and each individual simulation is the result of tweaks to the initial data put into the model to capture the range of possible track outcomes . <p> Rapid intensification : The process through which a cyclone strengthens and its maximum sustained winds increase by 35 mph or more in 24 hours .
