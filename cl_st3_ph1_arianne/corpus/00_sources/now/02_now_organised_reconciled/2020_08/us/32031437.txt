Today 's weather is expected to start out with rain , easing up a bit to showers , with the risk of a thunderstorm in the afternoon . Environment and Climate Change Canada is forecasting up to 10 millimetres of rain in the region Thursday , with a high of 21 C. Overnight , expect more showers and there 's a chance of a thunderstorm . On Friday , the wet weather continues , with up to 20 millimetres of rain expected , 21 C , and a risk of a thunderstorm in the morning . The rain is expected to clear up in time for the weekend , with a mix of sun and cloud on Saturday , sunshine on Sunday , and highs of 21 C and 22 C , respectively .
