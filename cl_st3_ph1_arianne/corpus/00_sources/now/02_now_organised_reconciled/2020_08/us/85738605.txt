<h> Heat warning issued for Toronto for Sunday and Monday <p> Environment Canada says it has declared a heat warning for Toronto on Sunday and Monday as temperatures soar into the 30s . <p> The weather agency said it " issues a heat warning when it forecasts two or more consecutive days with daytime maximum temperatures of 31 C or warmer , together with minimum nighttime temperatures of 20 C or warmer , or when there is a forecast of two or more consecutive days with humidex values expected to reach 40 or higher . " <p> According to Environment Canada , Sunday is expected to see a high of 32 C with the humidex at 40 , and a low at night of 21 C. <p> Monday is expected to see a high of 30 C and a low of 19 C in Toronto . <p> City officials said a modified " heat relief network " will be in place due the warning which includes pools , wading pools , splash pads , 47 community centres and emergency cooling centres for the public . <p> Emergency cooling centres ( @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and receive a cool drink , the city said . <p> The following three ECCs will be open : <p> Metro Hall at 55 John St. -- open 11 a.m. on Sunday and remains open overnight until 11 a.m. on Tuesday , Aug. 25 . <p> Mimico-Coronation Club House at 10 Judson St. -- open on Sunday only from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m . <p> Masaryk-Cowan Community Recreation Centre at 220 Cowan Ave . -- open on Sunday only from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m . <p> Environment and Climate Change Canada ( ECCC ) has declared a #HeatWarning for the #CityofTO on Sunday , August 23 and Monday , August 24.
