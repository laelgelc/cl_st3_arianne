<p> Carlsbad International School , an IB boarding school in Europe , presents a holistic approach to education to help international high school students prepare for uncertain times . <p> Carlsbad International School , a prestigious boys and girls boarding school in Europe , has taken upon itself the task of redefining college preparatory education in the time of Covid-19 . Just seven years since its founding in 2013 , this international school in Karlovy Vary is already recognized as one of the most prestigious boarding schools in the Czech Republic . This is no doubt due to the skill and experience of its teachers , its luxurious campus amenities , and its status as an International Baccalaureate ( IB ) World School , able to award the coveted IB Diploma to high performing students . <p> When the school first opened its doors to students in 2014 , opening day boasted 21 students from 11 different countries , along with a mission and vision to guide young people from all over the world to become leaders for a rapidly changing global society .
