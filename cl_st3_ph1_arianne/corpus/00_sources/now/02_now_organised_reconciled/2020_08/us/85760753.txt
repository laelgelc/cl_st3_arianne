<p> What 's the best way to help your city thrive after a global pandemic ? For Mark Gannon , director of business change and information solutions at Sheffield City Council , the answer might lie in big data . <p> " We want to give the organisation and the city the greatest opportunity to bounce back from COVID . Part of that focus is around using digital and data , " he says . <p> Gannon aims to create an organisation that 's driven by data . Working alongside counterparts in other public sector organisations , he wants to combine public data sources and create new insight into some of Sheffield 's most-pressing health and wellbeing issues , helping the city 's councillors make timely and cost-effective decisions in a post-COVID age . <p> " So when we make policy decisions , they 're based on intelligence rather than gut instinct .
