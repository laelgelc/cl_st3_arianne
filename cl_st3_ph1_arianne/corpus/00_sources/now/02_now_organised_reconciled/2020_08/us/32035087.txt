But we are currently not able to manually analyse more than a fraction of that data . This research shows AI is a promising tool but our AI classifier would still be wrong one out of five times , if it was used to identify animals in our images . <p> " This makes it an important step forward in dealing with the huge amounts of data being generated from the ocean floor , and shows it can help speed up analysis when used for detecting some species .
