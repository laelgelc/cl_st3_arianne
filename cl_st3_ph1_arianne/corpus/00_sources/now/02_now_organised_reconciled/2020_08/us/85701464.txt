<h> Scorching temperature in US 's Death Valley could be global high <p> by Ivan Couronne <p> A new scorching temperature of 130 degrees Fahrenheit ( 54.4 degrees Celsius ) recorded in California 's Death Valley could be the highest ever recorded , US officials say <p> A temperature of 130 degrees Fahrenheit ( 54.4 degrees Celsius ) recorded in California 's Death Valley on Sunday by the US National Weather Service could be the hottest ever measured with modern instruments , officials say . <p> The reading was registered at 3:41 pm at the Furnace Creek Visitor Center in the Death Valley national park by an automated observation system -- an electronic thermometer encased inside a box in the shade . <p> In 1913 , a weather station half an hour 's walk away recorded what officially remains the world record of 134 degrees Fahrenheit ( 56.7 degrees Celsius ) .
