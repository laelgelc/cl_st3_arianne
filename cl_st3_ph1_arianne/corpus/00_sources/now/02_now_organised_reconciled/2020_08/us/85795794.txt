The awards will be handed out in September . <p> Fast facts <p> About BubbleRunning time : 5 minutesBubble takes us to Montreal in 2050 , in a world where no action has been taken to curb climate change . Using delicate illustrations and an understated ambient soundtrack , the scenes are intercut with lyrical commentary and contemplative animation depicting disaster scenarios playing out in Montreal.From newborn to senior , your avatar passes through the stages of life , and in each interactive scene must keep its head encased in a bubble in order to survive in this hostile environment . By moving your finger across the screen , you take care of your bubble -- cleaning it or patching it up -- to make sure you stay alive . The experience ends with an animated video in which the now badly damaged bubble is removed from your avatar and given to another newborn .
