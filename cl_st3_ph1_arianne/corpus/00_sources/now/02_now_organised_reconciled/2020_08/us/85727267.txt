His strategy -- by design or happenstance -- of remaining relatively out of sight this year has resulted in six straight months of national polls in which Donald Trump has never had a lead . <p> A political convention is the biggest opportunity of an election year to communicate to the public the values and policies of a party 's candidate . Presidential candidates generally receive a polling bounce after their convention , and they offer a struggling campaign a chance to reframe its message . <p> But it 's not clear Biden needed any of that .
