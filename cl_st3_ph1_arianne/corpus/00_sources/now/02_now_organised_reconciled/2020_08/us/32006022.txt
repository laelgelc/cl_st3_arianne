<h> Amazon fires rage in early August as fears of mass blazes mount <p> Data from Brazil 's space agency suggests fires in the Amazon are on track for a seven percent increase over last year . <p> 08 Aug 2020 00:37 GMT <p> Smoke billows during a fire in an area of the Amazon rainforest near Porto Velho , Rondonia State , Brazil File : Bruno Kelly/Reuters <p> Fires in the Brazilian Amazon have surged far in August , government data on Friday showed , outstripping the same period of 2019 and renewing concerns about the destruction of the world 's largest rainforest , which is a critical bulwark against climate change . <p> Brazilian space research agency INPE recorded 5,860 fires in the Amazon in the first six days of August , a seven percent increase from the same period of last year . The data set is small and daily variation can be considerable , but the trend suggests this month may be on par with a year ago - the worst August in nine years . <p> Witnesses driving on Thursday night along the BR-319 highway @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the state of Amazonas , spotted small fires crackling along the side of the road , with smoke hanging heavy over the asphalt . <p> Some of the areas burned last year were now being used for cattle raising . Fire is commonly used across the Amazon to clear land for pasture . <p> The Brazilian Amazon lost 9,205sq km ( 3,554sq miles ) of vegetation in the 12 months ending in July , according to data from the country 's space agency . That was largely due to a surge of fires in August and September last year . <p> The overall loss was 34 percent greater than that of the prior 12-month period . <p> The data was produced by Brazil 's DETER monitoring system , which provides daily deforestation alerts based on satellite images .
