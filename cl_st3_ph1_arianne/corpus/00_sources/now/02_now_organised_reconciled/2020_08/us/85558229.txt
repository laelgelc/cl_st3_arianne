The mere mention of it brings back fond memories of warm , lush , tropical places . Without rehashing the pro and cons of the mimosa , I 'll just say that invasive species upset the natural balance and impact the natives , which are adapted to the area . They often have the ability to take over and change the dynamic , because in their new environment , they may lack natural predators that natives must battle . Diversity is important in ecosystems , and invasive species tend to decrease this . <p> It is preferable to have our native species mitigate the effects of climate change rather than have invasive species mucking up the natural order of things . I do n't think we can have too much green -- our native green , that is.
