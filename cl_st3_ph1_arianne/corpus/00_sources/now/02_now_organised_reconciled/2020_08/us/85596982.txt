<p> Common ragweed , that pestilent inflamer of sinuses everywhere , shows a remarkable ability to rapidly evolve under climate change , according to a new study . <p> Scientists expect the invasive plant , Ambrosia artemisiifolia , to spread across Europe and North America as the climate warms , vastly increasing the range of its irritating pollen . <p> " Our findings demonstrate that ragweed populations can rapidly evolve in response to climate change within a single generation , " the scientists write in the journal Global Change Biology . <p> Led by biologist Yan Sun of the University of Fribourg , the scientists subjected genetically identical ragweed plants to simulated climate warming . They examined the DNA of the plants ' offspring and found convergent evolution of seven out of nine plant traits , including larger biomass and later flowering , in a single generation . <p> " The experimental populations not only evolved at the level of DNA sequence , but also at the level of phenotype .
