<h> East Brunswick : Public Library To Host Talk On Climate Change <p> EAST BRUNSWICK -- As climate change brings about extreme weather conditions across the globe , the call to make lifestyle changes has grown in the past few years . Tackling the issue of climate change is the East Brunswick Public Library . <p> The library , in association with the Raritan Valley Group of the New Jersey Chapter of the Sierra Club , is hosting a community discussion on climate change in New Jersey . The discussion will center around the environmental impact on the quality of life in the state , and is set to take place on Sep . 2 , 7 p.m. -8:30 p.m . <p> The organizers have not yet announced the names of the panelists . <p> In this discussion , speakers will talk on topics like the impact of climate change on New Jersey ; environmental factors affecting health , wildlife , energy availability , flooding in New Jersey ; and practical steps community members can take to address those issues .
