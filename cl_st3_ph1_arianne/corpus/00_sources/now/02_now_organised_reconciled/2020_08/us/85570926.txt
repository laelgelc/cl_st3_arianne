To which the addendum that we never reason from a manipulated price is something we should remember . <p> Prices are indeed information but if someone has manipulated that price then the information is , at best , somewhat garbled . <p> Please note that price manipulation here does n't need to be something evil , or something people should n't be doing . It 's just an observation that people do sometimes intervene to change prices .
