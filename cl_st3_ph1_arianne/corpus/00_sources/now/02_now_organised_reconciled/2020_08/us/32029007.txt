<h> Sabah 's takeover of SESB put on hold by PN govt , claims Shafie <p> KOTA KINABALU : Sabah caretaker chief minister Shafie Apdal says the return of utility company Sabah Electricity Sdn Bhd ( SESB ) was disrupted after Perikatan Nasional ( PN ) took over power at the federal level . <p> Speaking to reporters after an event in the Kunak east coast district yesterday , he said negotiations began during Dr Mahathir Mohamad 's tenure as prime minister . <p> He said everything was going well until the change of government earlier this year . <p> " There have been continued electricity disruptions and villages without any power at all . That is why I discussed with Mahathir -- I told him I wanted to take back SESB , " he said . <p> Shafie Apdal <p> Adding that it was better for Sabah to handle the matter itself , he said Mahathir had agreed to the plan . <p> " Unfortunately , I was made to understand yesterday that the present federal government has put it on hold . " <p> The state surrendered its power over the company to Tenaga Nasional Bhd 34 years ago . <p> In January last year , Yeo Bee Yin who was energy , science , technology , environment and climate change minister at the time said the federal Cabinet had agreed to start the process of returning ownership of SESB to the Sabah government . She also said the process would take about two years to complete . <p> In September , Yeo 's deputy minister Isnaraissah Munirah Majilis said the process was going well , and that due diligence was being done . She also said most of the issues that needed addressing were technical matters . <p> Speaking today , Shafie said the state would continue pursuing the matter . <p> " We hope this can be continued to ensure that the takeover ( is completed ) , although it would cost a lot , because I do n't want our people to be in darkness , " he said .
