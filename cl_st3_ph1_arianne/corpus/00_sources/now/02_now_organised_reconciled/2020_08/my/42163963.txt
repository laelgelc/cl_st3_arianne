<h> Govt urged to promote , subsidise production of fabric face masks <p> KUALA LUMPUR : An Opposition MP has urged the government to subsidise the production of fabric face masks to ease the financial burden of Malaysians . <p> Klang MP Charles Santiago said this is to ensure that face masks prices can be affordable and production meets local demands . <p> " A family of four using six pieces of single-use face masks a day ( 180 pieces a month and paying RM1.20 per piece ) will fork out about RM216 a month , and this constitutes about 20% of the country 's minimum wage of RM1,200 , " he told a press conference at the Parliament here on Monday ( Aug 3 ) . <p> Santiago said by subsidising the production of fabric face masks , it would create opportunities for those who have lost their jobs due to the movement control order ( MCO ) imposed in March . <p> " I 'm talking about a cottage industry , which could spur entrepreneurship and employment for people who have lost their jobs during the MCO period , " he added . <p> He also said the government could cooperate with the Technology Vocational Education Training ( TVET ) , Mara and other agencies offering design and tailoring classes to start production of fabric face masks for the market . <p> He said funds should be pumped towards research and development to ensure that these masks will pass the bacterial filtration efficiency ( BFE ) and particle filtration efficiency ( PFE ) tests , among others . <p> Santiago also said other countries such as Hong Kong , South Korea and Vietnam were already creating face masks using innovative methods . <p> He said reusable face masks are more environmentally friendly and help mitigate climate change . <p> " It could also help to reduce the 10 million masks we dispose of on a daily basis in the country . <p> " We can do all this by incorporating beautiful batik fabric and prints , " he added .
