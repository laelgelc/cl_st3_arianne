<h> PSO starts import of Euro-V fuel <p> ISLAMABAD : As per the directives of Prime Minister Imran Khan and Energy Minister Omar Ayub Khan , Pakistan State Oil has started to import Euro-V Standard Fuel into the country , said the Petroleum Division spokesperson in his official statement on Saturday . <p> PSO is the first oil marketing company to initiate the process of importing Euro-V petrol . <p> " This move will enable the country to import better quality fuel and primarily aim at protecting the environment from climate change and global warming , " the spokesperson said , adding that the division has formulated policy guidelines for switching of petrol imports to Euro-V specifications from August 2020 . <p> " All imports of diesel will be conformed to Euro-V standard by January 2021 in order to revolutionise the fuel mix of the country . The government is encouraging private OMCs to introduce better grade fuel to protect the environment . <p> PSO also took the lead in providing electric charging facility at one of its fuel stations in the federal capital which was inaugurated by Omar Ayub Khan last month . <p> In addition , the Hydrocarbon Development Institute of Pakistan ( HDIP ) has also set up testing labs for the examination of Euro-V fuel specifications before OMCs to begin the import of upgraded version of petrol and diesel in the country .
