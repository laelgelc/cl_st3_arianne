<h> Glaciers in New Zealand 's Southern Alps have lost over half of their ice in the last 400 years <p> The Southern Alps in New Zealand are the latest icy region to be devastated by global warming . A study finds the mountains have lost up to 62 percent of their glaciers since the end of the Little Ice Age , around 400 years ago . <p> This , according to the study , equates to a maximum ice loss of 73 square km , an area half the size of Liechtenstein . <p> By contrast , Patagonia , which is home to the largest body of ice in the southern hemisphere outside of Antarctica , has seen just 11 percent of its Little Ice Age volume vanish . <p> New Zealand 's Southern Alps are best known for being the set of many scenes from The Lord of the Rings and the Hobbit movies . <p> The Southern Alps is the highest mountain range in Australasia , with Mount Cook 's summit standing at 12,218ft above sea level . <p> The study analysed volume changes in the Southern @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 1978 to 2009 and 2009 to 2019 .
