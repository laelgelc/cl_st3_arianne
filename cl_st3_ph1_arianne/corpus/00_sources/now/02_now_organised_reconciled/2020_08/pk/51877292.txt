<h> All of God 's creatures <p> As if the pain of the animals , trapped by us in our zoos with scant care , was not enough , these speechless creatures have been made pawns in the hands of those who should be rather taking the best care of them . Two of these animals , a lion and a lioness , recently perished at the hands of untrained zoo staff who were trying to shift them from the Capital 's Marghazar Zoo . The Islamabad High Court took notice of the deaths and directed fixing responsibility . But the authorities dragged their feet over the matter , instead filing a case against unidentified people . <p> The court has now decided to proceed against Minister of State for Climate Change Zartaj Gul , Special Assistant to the Prime Minister on Climate Change Malik Amin Aslam , the climate change secretary and officials from the Islamabad Wildlife Management Board ( IWMB ) who were part of a board responsible for the relocation of the zoo animals to various sanctuaries within and outside the country on court orders . The court had further remarked that the various agencies of the city -- including the IWMB , the Capital Development Authority , the Islamabad Metropolitan Corporation and even the Climate Change Ministry -- had been vying for control of the zoo and claiming credit , but no one was ready to do anything for the animals there . In effect , the court suggested that the zoo and its animals had been turned into a political pawn . <p> The entire case has highlighted the abject regard we have for animals , especially the exotic kind . Whether it is the authorities responsible for the zoo or the visitors , a few actually care about the well-being of the animals beyond their own need for entertainment . The court is right in its observation that this attitude of certain quarters is reflective of the deeper malaise plaguing us as a country . Perhaps it is time for the honourable court to take a stern view of the matter and jolt those in power to become cognizant of their responsibility towards all of God 's creatures .
