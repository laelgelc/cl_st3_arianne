<h> Menace of Fast Fashion <p> In an increasingly apparel conscious society , the concept of fast fashion has become extremely popular . It is a concept that depends on the streamlined distribution of lower quality clothes at cheaper costs that tend to sell faster . Every season calls for new clothes . Brands release new collections several times a year from brightly coloured florals in spring to elaborately designed wedding clothes in winter . Consumers are able to purchase new and trendy clothes without straining their pockets while brands continue to make quick profits from metabolised trends . <p> Fast fashion is a trend that reaps some short-term benefits , including abundant quantities of cheap clothes but raises generations of problems i.e. the devastating impact on our environment . Much of the underwater invasion of microfibers stems from the clothing made from synthetic fabrics . It is interesting to note that regular washing cycles of bulk loads release 500,000 tons of microfibers into the sea annually which is roughly equal to releasing 50 billion plastic bottles into the sea . The International Union for Conservation of Nature reported that these microfibers contribute to about 35 per cent of the microplastic pollution in the oceans . <p> Despite such a hazardous impact of fast fashion , Pakistan 's fashion industry releases multiple collections each year just to maximise profit . In this money-making race , designers produce clothes that sell fast but end up being discarded after a few wears .
