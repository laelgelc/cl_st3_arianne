<h> Court irked at authorities over deaths of animals at Islamabad Zoo <p> The Islamabad High Court ( IHC ) summoned the chairperson of the Wildlife Management Board and the secretary of ministry of climate change , sought names of wildlife officials , and hinted at contempt of court proeedings against Climate Change Minister Zartaj Gul and Special Assistant to the Prime Minister on Climate Change Malik Amin Aslam , on Monday . <p> IHC Chief Justice Athar Minallah was hearing the case pertaining to deaths of animals at Islamabad Zoo . The court expressed its annoyance at the federal government . <p> It appears that the animals died due to incompetence of the Wildlife Management Board , observed the court . <p> According to the May 21 ruling , the board was to be responsible for the transfer of animals , remarked the court , adding that on March 16 the notification of the wildlife management board was submitted with approval of the federal cabinet . <p> The court was informed that the lions are being shifted to Lahore instead of a sanctuary , recalled the court .
