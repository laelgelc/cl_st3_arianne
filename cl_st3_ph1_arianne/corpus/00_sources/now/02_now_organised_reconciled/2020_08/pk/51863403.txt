It will maintain a data-driven , growth-oriented team to further the interests of the State of Pakistan locally and internationally . <p> It will strengthen the government of Pakistan for the 21st century with robust , authentic and high-quality communication platforms . <p> DMW will also be responsible for curating the digital content for official social media assets of government .
