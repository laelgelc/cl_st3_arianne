<h> Verdant Pakistan <p> Pakistan is now counted among 10 countries in the world most vulnerable to effects of climate change <p> August 10 , 2020 <p> We could soon see January becoming hot as June , considering the seemingly unstoppable increasing heat with every passing year . So it was in the fitness of things that soon after coming to power , Prime Minister Imran Khan announced the launch of Billion Tree Tsunami to arrest the process of growing deforestation . The announcement is being sincerely translated into action and so far , more than a billion trees have been planted all over the country . On Sunday , a nation-wide tree plantation campaign was launched as part of the larger programme to make Pakistan more verdant . <p> In Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa , 2 million samplings on were planted by the provincial government on the first day alone , according to Chief Minister Mahmood Khan . The Chief Minister has taken notice of the incident and ordered action against those involved in violating the law . <p> Underscoring the vital importance of trees in maintaining ecological balance and reversing the process of climate change , Prime Minister Imran Khan rightly said a green future was sine qua non for survival while inaugurating the national tree plantation drive . Over the years , the timber mafia has mercilessly felled trees denuding large tracts of land of greenery . As a result , Pakistan is now counted among 10 countries in the world most vulnerable to effects of climate change . Trees exhale oxygen that we inhale and they inhale carbon dioxide which we exhale . This is one of the countless benefits of trees .
