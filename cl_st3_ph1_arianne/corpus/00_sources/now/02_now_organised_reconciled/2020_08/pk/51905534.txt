<p> ISLAMABAD : The Islamabad High Court ( IHC ) on Saturday has ordered implementation on the law pertaining to elimination of the environmental pollution . <p> IHC Chief Justice Athar Minallah conducted hearing on the case and summoned report from the federal government as well as the National Disaster Management Authority ( NDMA ) chairman within three weeks . <p> Special Assistant to the Prime Minister on Climate Change Amin Aslam and Capital Development Authority Chairman Amir Ali appeared before the court . <p> While addressing Amin Aslam , Justice Athar Minallah said the court has many expectations from you and it supports your initiatives , but the entire National Park has been destroyed . <p> " Irreparable damage has been caused to the atmosphere . It is not your fault ; it has been ongoing for seventy years . Jurisdiction of regulator is not more than 1,400 square miles after the 18th amendment . <p> " There seems no writ of the law in 1,400 square miles in every case which is interrogated . "
