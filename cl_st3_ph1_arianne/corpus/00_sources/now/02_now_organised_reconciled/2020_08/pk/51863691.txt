<p> Ministry of Human Rights to launch Film Festival titled ' Reels for Rights ' <p> July 30 , 2020 <p> Ministry of Human Rights is launching a Human Rights Film Festival titled ' Reels for Rights ' to increase awareness and understanding of human rights issues in Pakistan . <p> This drive will be held online between 4 to 25th of next month as a part of the Huqooq-e-Pakistan collaborative project with the European Union . <p> The Reels for Rights Festival will feature a collection of 14-15 pre-selected films on themes including child rights , female empowerment , transgender rights , minority rights , access to justice , death penalty and climate change . The Ministry of Human Rights will host ' Live Watch Parties ' across social media platforms to promote interaction and engagement . <p> The festival will also include multiple virtual panel discussions to facilitate and encourage dialogue and discourse on various human rights issues that have been explored through the selected films . <p> In a statement today Secretary for Human Rights , Rabiya Javeri Agha , said that this Festival is an initiative @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on human rights issues in Pakistan .
