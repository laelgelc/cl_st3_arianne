In an article he published on his website titled , " COVID-19 is awful . Climate change could be worse " , Mr. Gates outlined the urgent need for governments to address climate change as intensely as they have the pandemic , or else face dire consequences in the future . <p> " If you want to understand the kind of damage that climate change will inflict , look at COVID-19 and spread the pain out over a much longer period of time .
