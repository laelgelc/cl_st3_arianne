<h> " Do or die " -day for COP17 <h> Warning : <p> By Richard Davies It is " do or die " day for negotiators at the COP17 climate change conference in Durban , Africa Group chair Tosi Mpanu-Mpanu said on Friday . <p> The head of the 54-nation bloc , which includes South Africa , told journalists the pace of negotiations to stop the world slipping into a situation where global temperatures rise more than two degrees Celsius was " not the best " . <p> Scientists warn that if greenhouse gas emissions continue as they are , the world faces catastrophic consequences in the form of extreme weather , soaring temperatures , and rising sea levels . <p> Politicians and senior officials at the international high-level talks reportedly haggled into the early hours of Friday morning . <p> So far , they have failed to strike a deal on either an extension to the Kyoto Protocol , or the formal establishment of the Green Climate Fund , two items on the Africa Group 's bottom line for the conference . <p> A senior United Nations Framework Convention @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to be named , told Sapa at noon on Friday that the talks might go on well into Saturday . <p> Mpanu-Mpanu said his group wished to see the Durban conference become a " milestone " in the climate change process . <p> Striking an optimistic tone , he said the Africa Group believed it was " more than possible " for the conference to deliver concrete results . <p> Asked whether it had been " hard enough " on countries that were hanging back , he said : " If you are too rigid , you become irrelevant .
