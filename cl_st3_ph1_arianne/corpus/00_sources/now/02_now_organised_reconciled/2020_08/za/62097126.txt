<p> There are a number of friendly , furry faces that call the Cape home . While sightings of caracals and whales are fairly common these days , another rare creature recently took centre stage . <p> Amy and Bremner Bruwer from Cape Town were lucky enough to spot a Cape clawless otter while on the way to Smitswinkel Bay on July 19 . They stopped along the main road just past Simon 's Town when the otter popped up right in front of them . <p> " It seemed fearless and tried coming to us , but we clapped our hands so that it did n't . It lay down and stayed for a while and then run back down to the sea and swam away , " Amy explains . <p> It was an exciting encounter for the pair , as they had never seen a Cape clawless otter before . <p> " The otter stayed in front of us for about three minutes or so .
