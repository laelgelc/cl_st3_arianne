<h> Dr Dhananjaya Regmi appointed CEO of Nepal Tourism Board <p> KATHMANDU : Dr Dhananjaya Regmi has been appointed the Chief Executive Officer of Nepal Tourism Board . The executive committee of the NTB on Tuesday appointed Dr Regmi for a term of four years . Dr Regmi holds a PhD in climate change and is currently teaching at the Central Department of Geography and Environment under the Tribhuvan University .
