What to do ? <p> Since the days of Greenpeace in the 1970s and 80s , there has been a generation of Canadians who demand governments address environmental issues . This groundswell of public opinion towards the sustainable use of our natural resources will continue unabated until governments at all levels adopt policies that focus on these concerns . <p> Governments are caught between a rock and hard place .
