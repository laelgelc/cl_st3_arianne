" Large floating ice features are no longer viable . " <p> An ice shelf consists of a vast expanse of ice that is many tens of metres thick . While it is buoyed by ocean waters , it is fixed to the shore , creating landscapes and marine ecosystems that are unique to the world 's polar regions . <p> Story continues below advertisement <p> The north coast of Ellesmere Island in Nunavut once featured several such ice shelves that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ most have fractured and all but vanished due to rising Arctic temperatures . Their rapid demise has sent enormous city-sized blocks of ice drifting out to sea . <p> Prior to this week , the Milne Ice Shelf was the exception . It extends from a narrow fjord that bends away from the coastline , providing extra stability . But an unusually warm summer combined with a lack of sea ice that would otherwise push back and protect the edge of the ice shelf appears to have sent the entire formation past a crucial tipping point . <p> " I did n't expect this one to go , " said Dr. White , who was the first to spot the collapse of the shelf using satellite imagery and who has worked on the Milne Ice Shelf as part of her PhD research . <p> BREAKING AWAY <p> The Milne Ice Shelf on the northwest coast of Ellesmere Island was the last intact ice shelf in the Canadian Arctic until July 31 , when several large pieces broke away .
