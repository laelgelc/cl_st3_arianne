The latest departures provide a final exclamation point to a brutal decade of media cutbacks that have ripped like a hacksaw across all traditional media platforms and left the communities they serve the poorer for it . &lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt; These latest departures &amp;mdash ; all from CTV Ottawa ( formerly CJOH ) &amp;mdash ; seem especially poignant as they impact veteran TV personalities who became household names over a generation as we welcomed them into our living rooms during local TV newscasts . They also willingly embraced leading roles beyond the TV lights in support of countless charitable and other community endeavours . For all of that , they will be missed . &lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt; It seems only fitting then that Terry Marcotte , Catherine Lathem , Joanne Schnurr and Michael O&amp;rsquo ; Byrne should wind down their careers together in the dying days of this decade , if only because they went through so much together during careers spanning nearly 40 years . They developed their skills together and enjoyed countless successes under the leadership of their mentor , the late Max Keeping , who had a knack for moulding unvarnished talent . &lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt; They endured their share of terrible tragedies too , none worse than the senseless , cold-blooded shooting death of their colleague , Brian Smith , who was gunned down in 1995 by a psychotic in the parking lot of the old CJOH building on Merivale Road . &lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt; I remember vividly how that CJOH news team pulled together to cover the story and how their grief became the grief of an entire community . &lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt; The era of CJOH dominance seemed to crest about the time its Merivale offices and studios burned down in 2010 , forcing the entire crew into an uncomfortable early marriage with their crosstown CTV affiliate , The NewRO . The fire destroyed decades of film and audio material . The station never seemed the same after that . &lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt; But in their heyday , the CJOH team dominated the local airways , crushing the local CBC station , sometimes 10:1 in ratings . &amp;nbsp ; While most big city local TV newsrooms depended on pretty faces and hairspray for their ratings , Max Keeping&amp;rsquo ; s team banked on a much different formula . &lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt; Keeping&amp;rsquo ; s secret was finding people with whom the community could identify ; often local people who could create compelling narratives , not just 40-second news clips , and who understood that with elevated public profiles came certain responsibilities to give back to the community . &lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt; Often his young charges lacked the polish , the big hair and giant egos of big city stations . But it didn&amp;rsquo ; t seem to matter . &lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt; Marcotte admitted in interviews this week he was not exactly the perfect TV stereotype and wasn&amp;rsquo ; t even a great broadcaster . Yet that was exactly what endeared him to TV viewers &amp;mdash ; an honest , authentic storyteller who found value in telling the stories of local athletes who developed their skills at community rinks or in local parks and who often beat all odds to enjoy their share of success . &lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt; In addition to his news anchor job , O&amp;rsquo ; Byrne has also played a huge role in helping charities raise money and expand their reach . He has received numerous honours in the process , including a Governor General&amp;rsquo ; s Award , for his community work . &lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt; Some organizers in the charitable sector worry about losing this type of community asset who could bring their energy and high profile to help organizations in need . The team also happened to produce good journalists , too . Catherine Lathem and Joanne Schnurr will be sorely missed in that regard . &lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt; The media landscape has changed dramatically over the decade , thanks to a digital age and the new competition for advertising dollars that came with it . Local TV newscasts neatly packaged to serve a dinnertime audience no longer carry the same punch in a world with never-ending news cycles and an insatiable demand for immediate news . But in their day , they reached large audiences and , in the case of CJOH , could bring the entire community together . &lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt; These latest CTV Ottawa departures follow a decision announced last spring to change the traditional job of reporting staff . In future , reporters will become video journalists who will not only report , but operate cameras , too , and even edit their work .
