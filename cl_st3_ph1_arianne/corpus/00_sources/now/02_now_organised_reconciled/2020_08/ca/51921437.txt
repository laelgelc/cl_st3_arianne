<h> ' On its last legs ' : Remnants of Hurricane Laura make its way to Eastern Canada <p> A satellite image shows the remnants of Hurricane Laura as it moves over Canada . ( National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration ) <p> SHARE <p> TORONTO --As the remnants of Hurricane Laura move out of the United States , Environment and Climate Change Canada says the system will bring much needed rain to parts of the country . <p> Drought conditions have intensified in eastern Quebec and across much of the Maritimes this summer . The dry conditions across the Maritimes have even forced some farms to conserve their water supply and reduce their crop irrigation schedule . <p> Environment Canada senior climatologist David Phillips suggests that the remnants of Hurricane Laura could help improve conditions . <p> " Sometimes some good can come from hurricanes , " Phillips told CTVNews.ca in a phone interview . " The winds do n't bring any good , but the rains can reverse long dry periods . " <p> According to the federal government 's latest Agroclimate National Risk Report , the most significant climate-related risk to agriculture in Canada is drought and abnormally warm conditions in the Maritimes . <p> The Canadian Drought Monitor indicates that many parts of New Brunswick and P.E.I are experiencing a severe drought while most of Nova Scotia is considered to be abnormally dry . <p> " It 's a worrisome situation in Atlantic Canada because they 've had such little rain ... you want to have a balance of weather , " Phillips said . " This is a God-send for anybody that depends on water , like farmers and firefighters . " <p> Phillips suggests that the incoming rainfall from Laura will not only benefit agriculture systems in the east , but will also help to prevent potential forest fires . <p> Torrential rains were felt over southwestern Ontario on Friday night and into Saturday morning , as Laura crossed the northern U.S. <p> " When we look at this clearly , we can see the storm is not like Hurricane Dorian which created havoc over Nova Scotia last year , " Phillips said . " This could turn out to be good news . " <p> Environment Canada has announced a special weather alert for Nova Scotia and parts of Newfoundland and Labrador . Up to 50 millimetres of rain are expected , lasting until Sunday night . Residents have been warned that heavy rain could lead to significant water pooling on roadways and minor flooding in low lying areas . <p> The death toll from Hurricane Laura is now at 16 in the U.S. Laura was a Category 4 storm when it made landfall in Louisiana on Thursday . <p> Hundreds of thousands across the state are without power and water service .
