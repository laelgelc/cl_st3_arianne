Staying " in the bubble " -- especially a fortified bubble -- is a prescription for isolation . Pre-clearance was necessary to leave the premises and military escorts were often required . Roaring around the city and surrounding countryside in heavily armed convoys -- and arriving at meetings in the company of uniformed guards bristling with weapons -- did not make for the initiation of open , free-flowing exchanges . <p> Under such constraints , non-military members of the mission did not spend as much time in the field as they might have needed to .
