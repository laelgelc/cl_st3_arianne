<h> Friday climate event to mark two years of school strikes <p> Fridays For Future climate activists Maggie Fu , left , and Sophia Mathur take part in a shoe strike with Bollywood dancing on Friday , July 24 , at the corner of Brady and Paris streets . A similar event will be held this Friday at Tom Davies Square.John Lappa/Sudbury Star <p> Fridays For Future members will be rallying this week in conjunction with the two-year anniversary of school strikes launched by Swedish activist Greta Thunberg . <p> The local youths will hold a shoe strike -- arranging rows of footwear in lieu of people , in order to respect limits on proximity and gathering size -- along with a Parachutes for the Planet display , Bollywood dancing and a Zoom broadcast . <p> There will also be speeches from politicians ( to be announced on Wednesday ) and Sudbury 's newest climate reality leaders , Jane Walker , Maggie Fu and Sophia Mathur . <p> The trio was recently trained by Al Gore and this will be their official debut speech since assuming their new roles . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ PA system . <p> COVID precautions will be in place as per the provincial Stage 3 reopening -- mask wearing , keeping two metres apart , fewer than 50 people , and frequent hand-washing -- and any other directions from city hall . <p> The event begins on Friday at 10 a.m. in the courtyard outside Tom Davies Square . <p> If there is inclement weather , the displays of shoes and parachutes will be moved under the overhang in the courtyard . <p> The theme of the event is " Build Back Better/Just Recovery For Everyone . " <p> The take-home message , according to the organizers , is that each of us should listen to the scientific experts and do their part to counteract climate change . <p> " This has been their message since Nov. 2 , 2018 , " a release states . " The experts include the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change , the Paris Agreement , Nobel Prize economists and the Lancet Countdown on Health . " <p> Fu , Mathur and Walker want everyone to know that they " demand a just recovery @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ build back better , " they urge . " We can not go back to the way it was before . To do that , we all must listen to the experts and co-operate . "
