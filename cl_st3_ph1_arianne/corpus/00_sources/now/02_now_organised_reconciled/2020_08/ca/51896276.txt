<p> Minimum daily temperatures in Calgary have been trending upward for more than a century , according to historical data maintained by Environment Canada . <p> Through Aug. 17 , there have been 42 days this year where the overnight low has stayed above 10 C. <p> During the same period from 1901 to 1910 , there were just 11 days per year , on average , that saw such warm nights . <p> By the 1960s , that was up to an average of 23 days . <p> In the 2000s , it was up to 30 days . <p> And the past decade has been the warmest on record , in these terms , with an average of 43 days . <p> " This is a trend we 're seeing all around the world , but most dramatically in places where we usually get , on average , cooler overnight lows -- like most of Alberta , " said CBC senior meteorologist Johanna Wagstaffe . <p> " The boundary layer -- or that lower level of the atmosphere that warms up and cools down -- is much thinner @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ afternoon temperatures .
