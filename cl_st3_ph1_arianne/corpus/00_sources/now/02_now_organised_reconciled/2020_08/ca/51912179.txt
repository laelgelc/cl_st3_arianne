<h> Welfare of Polar Bears <p> Conservation Coordinator Dylan McCart is with PhD student Stephanie Penk developing a method to measure polar bears that is voluntary for the bear . Photo provided by the Polar Bear Habitat.TP.jpegjpeg , TP <p> Tourism has been slowly ramping up at the Habitat since we reopened on July 1 , with some days looking almost like a typical summer day -- besides the masks , sanitizer , and social distancing , of course . <p> We have been fortunate to be able to welcome some of our volunteers back to the Habitat , too . We are up to four dedicated people helping out to prepare the bear diets on a weekly basis , and we are so grateful for their valuable contributions . With the hot temperatures , the bears have been spending plenty of time in the water . Each enclosure has at least one water source at all times , and of course the bears always have access to indoor air-conditioned rooms , too . <p> Eddy in particular has developed a habit of laying in the shallow waters of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on end . He will be able to continue this favourite activity for the rest of the summer , as his return home to the Quebec Aquarium has once again been delayed due to construction . <p> Dylan McCart , the Habitat 's Conservation Coordinator , was recently featured by the International Association for Bear Research and Management ( IBA ) for his Masters study , which is looking at the hormone patterns of male polar bears in different social conditions . With help from a grant from the IBA , he is investigating whether testosterone levels in males differ in the presence and absence of females . The results could improve the housing conditions for all polar bears in human care . <p> Another study , published in Nature Climate Change by respected polar bear scientist Peter Molnar and his team , received a lot of media attention this past month , too . It predicted that polar bears in some regions -- like those closest to Cochrane -- would not have enough resources to be able to reproduce by the end of this decade . One of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ collaborating with the Habitat for data collection related to this body of research . Penk visited the Habitat and worked with our team to develop a new method to measure polar bears . The measurements taken at the Habitat help understand the relationship between a polar bear 's size and his energy needs and will contribute to science 's understanding of polar bear populations in a changing climate . The data collected here are the most consistent and robust in the world -- and it 's all done voluntarily . <p> These are excellent examples of the Habitat 's contribution to the welfare of polar bears and underscore the importance of our incredible facility . Our Board of Directors is continuing to work on securing funding solutions for the Habitat to ensure this important work can continue . Thank you for your ongoing support !
