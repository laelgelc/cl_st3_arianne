<h> ONGA announces the appointment of Jamie Ellerton <p> TORONTO , March 21 , 2016 /CNW/ - The Ontario Natural Gas Alliance ( ONGA ) is pleased to announce that it has appointed veteran public affairs practitioner Jamie Ellerton as primary spokesperson . Mr. Ellerton brings a wealth of public affairs and communications experience to the role , including past advocacy work on behalf of Canada 's petroleum industry . <p> Keith Boulton , Director of Public Affairs at Union Gas Limited , described the appointment of Mr. Ellerton as " an important step in the evolution of ONGA , " as the sector works to collaborate with the government of Ontario on the coming implementation of a cap-and-trade regime . <p> Chris Wray , Manager of Government Relations of Enbridge Gas Distribution , pointed to Mr. Ellerton 's past experience working at both The House of Commons and Queen 's Park as " vital to the process of engaging decision makers and public leaders at Queen 's Park in our shared goal of addressing climate change . " <p> Mr. Ellerton , whose previous work and commentary has been @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Toronto Star and Huffington Post Canada , said today : <p> " I 'm delighted to be joining the Ontario Natural Gas Alliance at this important time . Natural Gas is an ally in the fight against climate change . The largest source of emissions in Ontario is the transportation sector and natural gas is 30 per cent cleaner than traditional transportation fuels . By embracing natural gas , Ontario can continue to be a North American leader in the fight against climate change , while growing the economy and creating jobs .
