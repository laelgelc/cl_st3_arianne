The federal government has announced it will join in an environmental assessment of a major expansion to proposed southern British Columbia coal mine . A spokeswoman for Environment Minister Jonathan Wilkinson says the decision was made after analyzing the potential of Teck Resources ' Castle project to cause adverse effects within areas of federal jurisdiction .
