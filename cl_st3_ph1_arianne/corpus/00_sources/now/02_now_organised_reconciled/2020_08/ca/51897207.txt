<h> Councillor chosen for climate leadership course <p> Timmins Coun. Kristin Murray has been chosen for the Federation of Canadian Municipalities climate leadership course . She 's seen here with Ecole secondaire catholique Theriault student Jessica Kim , Timmins MPP Gilles Bisson , and manager of environmental services and public utilities Scott Tam at a Climate Crisis town hall earlier this year . Maija Hoggett/TimminsToday <p> A Timmins councillor known for advocating for a more environmentally-friendly city has been chosen to take part in a climate leadership course . <p> Coun. Kristin Murray is one of 93 people chosen for the Federation of Canadian Municipalities ( FCM ) first-ever online course for elected officials . <p> " This is an excellent opportunity for additional learning from other climate change leaders across Canada , " said Murray in a City of Timmins news release . " As I move through the course , I intend to work with council , administration and our environmental services team in implementing additional climate change strategies and initiatives . The course includes information on tools and best practices for incorporating a climate lens into municipal initiatives . Participants will also learn about opportunities , resources and funding for local climate action , " reads the FCM news release . <p> For the course , there are three assignments and three live chats from September to November . An in-person summit is being planned for Ottawa in January 2021 for select course participants . FCM says the advice of local public health authorities will be considered before finalizing travel and event plans . <p> " I am confident that Kristin will represent us well with FCM , take in everything the Climate Leadership program has to offer , and share that knowledge with her colleagues here in Timmins , " said Timmins Mayor George Pirie .
