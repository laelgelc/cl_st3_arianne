<p> Wiikwemkoon 's Kaella-Marie Earle with her chemical engineering diploma from Laurentian University ( LinkedIn photo ) <p> An aspiring Indigenous leader from northeastern Ontario is ready to make her voice heard in a national forum . <p> Kaella-Marie Earle , an engineer-in-training with Enbridge Gas and member of Wiikwemkoong Unceded Territory on Manitoulin Island , is taking a seat with the inaugural Indigenous Advisory Committee of the Canada Energy Regulator ( CER ) . <p> The CER was formed in August 2019 to replace the former National Energy Board , the federal regulator of pipelines , power lines and imports and exports of energy products . The updated mandate of the Crown organization is to transition Canada to a low-carbon economy and bring about greater participation for Indigenous people in the energy sector . <p> Earl is one of eight people recently named to the newly formed committee .
