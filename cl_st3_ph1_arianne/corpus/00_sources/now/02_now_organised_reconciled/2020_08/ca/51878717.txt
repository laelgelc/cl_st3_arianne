<h> Lower Trent in a level two low water condition <p> Rainfall continues to be in short supply across the Trent River watershed forcing Lower Trent Conservation to declare Level II Low Water conditions ( moderate drought ) are now being experienced across the region which stretches from Grafton to Quinte West and from Lake Ontario to Rice Lake . <p> Under synch conditions , residents , businesses , industries , and municipalities are asked to voluntarily reduce water usage by 20 per cent . Lower Trent Conservation encourages everyone to use water wisely and apply water conservation measures . Non-essential water uses should be suspended until natural water supplies recover . Those who hold a permit to take water from the Ministry of the Environment and Climate Change are asked to reduce water taking by 20 per cent as well . <p> Residents can help conserve water by using rain barrels to capture rain for watering lawns and gardens , limiting vehicle washing , etc . Lower Trent Conservation staff urge rural residents to be wary of their water use . <p> " Water is a precious resource -- @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , " the conservation authority stated in a media release .
