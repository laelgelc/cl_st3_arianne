It harmlessly records valuable information . <p> THUNDER BAY - If the spring and summer season continue to get longer due to climate change , anglers may be enjoying smaller shore lunches as fish struggle to adapt . <p> A new study has found that climate change is having an impact on lake trout by changing their behavior , forcing the cold-water species into deeper water for longer periods to look for food during long summers . <p> " There is some resiliency there , " said Michael Rennie , an assistant professor in the department of biology at Lakehead University and Canada research chair in fresh water ecology and fisheries . <p> " They are not just going to hang out in warm water and die . They will seek out colder water and tough out the summer .
