<p> OTTAWA , ON , Aug. 10 , 2020 /CNW/ - Today the Canadian Medical Association ( CMA ) is pleased to announce its 2020 CMA Award recipients . The 16 award recipients will be officially recognized during a virtual ceremony later this year . <p> " Throughout this pandemic , we 've seen the impact health workers have in our lives .
