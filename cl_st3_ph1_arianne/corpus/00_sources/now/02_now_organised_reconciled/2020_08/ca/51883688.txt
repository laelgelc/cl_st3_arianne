<p> Kamala Harris is Democratic presidential nominee Joe Biden 's running mate . She is the first Black woman to run on any major political party 's presidential ballot , and if elected , she will be the first Black woman to serve as vice president . <p> The California senator and former presidential hopeful has rested her candidacy on her record : first as San Francisco 's District Attorney , then California 's Attorney General , and now as her home state 's senator . However , her record is also what 's attracting the most flak from critics , especially those eager to point out her harsh prosecutorial record . <p> She fought truancy , for instance , by supporting a statewide policy of arresting the parents of children who habitually missed school . Her opponents argue that the law , rather than encouraging kids to go to school , trapped already disadvantaged communities into a cycle wavering between poverty and the criminal justice system . <p> Scroll down to catch up on what Harris is saying about today 's most pressing issues .
