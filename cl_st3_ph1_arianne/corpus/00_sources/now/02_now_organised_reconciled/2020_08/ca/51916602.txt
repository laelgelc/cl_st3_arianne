<h> Twenty years of effort paying off with improving marine health in Howe Sound <p> Twenty years ago , Howe Sound was still sick from decades of pollution from pulp mills and the Britannia copper mine , which shut down in 1974 , over-fishing of certain species , and the general pressures of urban development . <p> Today , the sound is lot healthier . After a long absence , various species of whales can be seen in the sound and herring -- also absent for many years -- have begun spawning again in the sound . <p> " There 's a lot more big cetaceans being seen back in the sound , " said Aroha Miller , manager of Oceanwatch , which is part of the Ocean Wise Conservation Association . <p> " So killer whales are being seen in the sound much more often . And because killer whales are top-level predators in the food chain , when you start seeing them , you know that the levels below them are beginning to heal and repair themselves .
