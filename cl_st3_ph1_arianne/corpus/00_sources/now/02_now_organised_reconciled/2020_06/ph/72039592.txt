" <p> " With the alarming spread of COVID-19 and the sudden stringent health and safety measures being implemented across the country to contain the spread of virus , people have been reported to be suffering from anxiety , stress , and even depression , " House Deputy Speaker and Antique Representative Loren Legarda said .
