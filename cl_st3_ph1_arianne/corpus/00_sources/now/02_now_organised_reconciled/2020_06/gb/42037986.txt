<h> Richard Threlfall leads KPMG 's ESG practice KPMG Impact <p> KPMG has appointed a new leader of its ESG business . Partner Richard Threlfall will assume the position of Global Head of KPMG IMPACT alongside his previous role as Global Head of Infrastructure . <p> Environmental , Social , and Governance ( ESG ) work refers to the three central factors in measuring the sustainability and societal impact of an investment in a company or business . These criteria help to better determine the future financial performance of companies . <p> KPMG IMPACT brings together KPMG 's expertise and knowledge from around the world to help businesses , governments , and other organisations address the pressing ESG issues that they and the world in general are facing . As the Big Four professional services firm looks to boost its ESG business ahead of a series of systemic crises -- from climate , to economics , to healthcare -- it has installed a new leader of KPMG IMPACT . <p> Richard Threlfall , KPMG 's Global Head of Infrastructure , takes up the new role having spent 16 years with KPMG in the UK .
