<p> The guidelines were developed by a forum founded by the Bank of England and Financial Conduct Authority ( via Getty Images ) <p> The Bank of England and UK financial regulator today told banks and insurers to press ahead with analysing the impact of climate change on their operations . <p> The Climate Financial Risk Forum ( CFRF ) -- established by the BoE and Financial Conduct Authority ( FCA ) last year -- published a guide setting out how financial firms can analyse and disclose risks from climate change and identify opportunities for new consumer products . <p> The CFRF 's guide , the first of its kind , sets out what the Bank of England expects from the firms it regulates but is not a set of mandatory rules . The forum said its guidance would be followed by targets on progress next year . <p> Sarah Breeden , the Bank of England 's co-chair of the forum , said firms should begin their analysis straight away .
