<p> A Carlisle school headteacher has spoken of the scale of the challenge posed by Covid-19 to the wellbeing of children , as supermarket giant Tesco reveals it has donated more than 360,000 meals to schoolchildren across the county . <p> Norman Street Primary School in Carlisle is one of a number of schools across Cumbria that has benefitted from a share of the 364,360 free meals donated by Tesco to those in need throughout Cumbria . <p> The school has been able to launch a food parcel donations service for vulnerable families , much needed throughout the current period of disruption and hardship caused by coronavirus . <p> " I have been overwhelmed by the generosity of Tesco customers and colleagues alike in their dedication to ensure that those most in need within our community have what they need during these trying times . " <p> The food has been donated through the Community Food Connection scheme it operates with food redistribution charity FareShare . <p> Every Tesco store in the UK participates in the scheme , which is celebrating five years of helping communities across the country .
