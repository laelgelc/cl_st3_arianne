In Bradford , pollution levels have risen 116 per cent from their April low . <p> Even when adjusted for the role of weather , it 's clear nitrogen dioxide levels are rising , Mr Chakraborty , an air quality researcher , said . <p> " As the lockdown started easing there has been more traffic , " he told i . " With increasing traffic we have seen NO2 levels rising gradually everywhere in the UK . " <h> Other pollutants <p> Yet air pollution has not disappeared during lockdown , he stressed .
