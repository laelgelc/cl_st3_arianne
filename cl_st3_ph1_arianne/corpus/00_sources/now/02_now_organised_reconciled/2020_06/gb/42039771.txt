<p> Pedigree is a funny thing in the video game industry . On the one hand , if you 're familiar with a developer and have enjoyed their previous work , odds are that you 'll like the next game they put out . But pedigree also means that a new IP can carry some heavy expectations that are very difficult , if not impossible , to meet . <p> As the inaugural title from V1 Interactive , a small studio founded by Halo co-creator Marcus Lehto and other former Bungie staffers , Disintegration is leaning hard on its pedigree to make a name for itself in the crowded first-person shooter genre .
