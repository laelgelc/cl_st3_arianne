<p> The UK has suffered a deep recession . The combination of the scale of the collapse in income , demand and output so far , alongside the unlocking that is currently underway , suggests that the economy will pick up in the second half of this year . <p> However , it is unlikely to be until the end of next year that we will return to pre-crisis levels . And even then , there will have been some permanent scarring in terms of lost jobs . <p> The wait for a vaccine will continue severely to impair the business models of the hospitality and creative sectors . Travel constraints and social distancing will have a wider impact . <p> Thus , continued policy stimulus will be needed in the recovery phase . Counter-cyclical policy measures should include cuts in VAT to boost demand and in stamp duty to help the housing sector . <p> Similarly , measures will be needed to support the supply side of the economy .
