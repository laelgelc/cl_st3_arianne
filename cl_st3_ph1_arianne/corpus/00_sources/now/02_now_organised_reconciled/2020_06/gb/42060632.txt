Facilitated by businesses and enabled by government , racism is part of society here - it 's a key British export . In WWII , Brits were rightly horrified by the racism shown in the segregated US Army , with some publicans reacting to the demands from white officers to enforce a colour bar by banning white soldiers . The same solidarity was not seen with Britain 's own Empire troops , who were paid according to their ethnicity , with black soldiers paid a third of what white soldiers were . This is not just a relic of a bygone era . Racist policies are in place today .
