<h> Colourful celebration of new cycle route on main road <h> Celebration of Old Shoreham Road cycling route <p> MORE than a hundred people took part in a colourful cycle ride to celebrate a new route . <p> The Sunday celebration was to mark the introduction of the new Old Shoreham Road cycle lane in Hove . <p> The cyclists and their families had brightly coloured flags attached to their bicycles and were accompanied along the route by two " disco bikes " with sound systems mounted on them . <p> The ride , organised by Extinction Rebellion Brighton , began at Hove Park and did two loops along the cycle route , going west as far as Portslade and east to The Drive . Paul Taylor , 60 , from Seven Dials , is a spokesman for Extinction Rebellion Brighton . <p> He said : " It was amazing to see so many people , young and old , out riding in a colourful cycle convoy . Brighton residents dearly want to be able to cycle safely in the city and this route is a positive step towards that .
