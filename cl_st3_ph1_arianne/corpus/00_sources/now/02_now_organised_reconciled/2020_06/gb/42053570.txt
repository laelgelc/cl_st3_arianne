Yet she stressed that there are still opportunities for amendments . <p> Quoting Building magazine , Berger said that , just two providers and seven assessors have signed up to the Green Deal so far . <p> On the cost of the finance , she said interest rates expected at a level of 7.5% will " severely limit the range of measures a house can adopt if it 's going to adopt it all " . <p> " A ? 10,000 Green Deal package will be @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ over ? 22,000 .
