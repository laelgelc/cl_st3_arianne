<p> Any hay fever sufferer will be able to tell you when spring has arrived . The change in season is often accompanied by a runny nose , itchy eyes , and for those who have it , worsening asthma . From spring right up until the end of summer , an estimated 13 million people in the UK with a variety of hay fever allergy triggers will feel the assault of pollen on their systems . But many sufferers are saying that they 're struggling more this year than they have before . <p> Could botanical sexism be to blame ?
