But we can only continue to grow with your support . <p> The coronavirus pandemic has hit the United Kingdom worse than most other countries . The raw numbers are staggering . Last week the number of confirmed deaths passed 40,000 , and if we take estimated excess deaths from the virus -- the difference between ' usual ' deaths at this time of year , and those we might reasonably attribute to Covid-19 -- those are might be some way over 60,000 . <p> At the moment , the UK has the second-highest number of recorded deaths in the world , after the United States ( though Brazil is catching the UK very quickly ) . Things have undoubtedly gone very wrong indeed . <p> The question then arises : what should the political reaction be ? To split hairs between them is unwise -- and inaccurate . <p> Social media pundits want to say that everything is clear . That the situation is cut and dried . Academics are here to tell you that things are not so simple . To what extent can it work , and which elements are most efficacious ? What is the role , if any , of seasonality ? Are some people immune to the disease , and why ? Might that perform some sort of barrier effect on its spread ? <p> There is no doubt this crisis has shed a harsh light on British public policy . The state is too weak . It is disorganised .
