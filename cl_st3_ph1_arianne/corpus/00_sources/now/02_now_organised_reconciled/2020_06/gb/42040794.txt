<h> Policy to decarbonise home heating must be fairer in wake of Covid-19 crisis , says OFTEC <p> As the UK begins to emerge from the Covid-19 crisis , government must not waste the opportunity to ensure future heat policy is greener and fairer , says OFTEC . <p> The coronavirus pandemic has hit the UK economy hard . Spending cuts and tax hikes to cover the ? 300 billion coronavirus bill are predicted , alongside further rises in unemployment . The result is an increasingly bleak outlook for household incomes and Treasury finances . <p> In response OFTEC warns that unless measures to cut carbon emissions from home heating are made affordable for many more households , climate change targets will not be met . <p> For the past six years , the domestic Renewable Heat Incentive ( RHI ) has offered financial support to encourage homeowners , particularly those off-the gas grid , to install green heating technologies . <p> However , the scheme 's success has been limited .
