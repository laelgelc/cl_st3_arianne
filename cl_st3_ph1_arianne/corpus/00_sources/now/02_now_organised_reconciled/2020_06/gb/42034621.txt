Equally , it is almost certainly the area where Covid 19 has most dramatically changed the story . Decisions are made by the public sector and improvements are generally not charged for directly . Equally , major infrastructure investment has massive positive externalities ( and some negative ones ) which get reflected in land and property values . Increased residential values do not generate higher revenues through the council tax system and so provide windfall gains to those who benefit from infrastructure ; the supplementary business rate on the other hand does , in time , reflect these higher values .
