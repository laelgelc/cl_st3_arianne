Some are expected to join us during the construction phase and stay with us during the operation of the power station . <p> When we have completed construction we will need 900 people to operate Sizewell C. The station will run for at least 60 years : that means decades of skilled , sustainable work in well paid jobs for local people . <p> I know some people have claimed that people in Suffolk wo n't benefit from jobs created during construction . Colleges in Suffolk are preparing the pathways into these jobs now . <p> The project will provide opportunities in a wide range of professions for people to grow and progress .
