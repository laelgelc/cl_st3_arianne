<h> Calls are being made for more to be done to provide electric vehicle charging points in Mid Suffolk as latest figures reveal just nine across the whole district . <p> Mid Suffolk Green councillor Andrew Stringer , who owns an electric car , said the authority should be leading on EV charge points not lagging behind .
