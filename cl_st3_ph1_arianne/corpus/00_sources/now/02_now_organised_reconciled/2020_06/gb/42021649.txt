<p> The creator of a controversial electromagnetic space propulsion technology called EmDrive has finally had a paper peer reviewed and accepted by the International Academy of Astronautics ( IAA ) . <p> The paper , Second Generation EmDrive Propulsion Applied To SSTO Launcher And Interstellar Probe , by British scientist Roger Shawyer was published in the journal Acta Astronautica and made available online on 10 July . <p> Shawyer conceptualised and developed the space propulsion technology EmDrive and first presented this in 1999 .
