But it would be dangerously counterproductive to let the crisis response become a gateway for interest groups to weaken the very laws and regulations that keep us safe . The government 's insistence on carrying out trade negotiations with both the EU and the US during a pandemic , combined with political pressure to secure deals before the US elections and the end of transition , seems destined to result in knee-jerk decisions and set a worryingly low bar on oversight . <p> If a pandemic were @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , responding would be instinctive for all of us . But entering the race to the bottom would simply pile new crises upon the pandemic , like dealing with the burning house that is the climate emergency by settling light to the fire extinguisher . <p> The starting gun on this race has already been fired around the world .
