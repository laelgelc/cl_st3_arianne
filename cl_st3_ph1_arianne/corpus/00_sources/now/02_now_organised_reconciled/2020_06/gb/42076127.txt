<h> Future of Queen 's Park Golf Course will be ' looked at ' in council review <h> Future of Queen 's Park Golf Course to be reviewed <p> THE future of an historic Bournemouth golf course could be put in doubt by a review of open spaces across the conurbation . <p> Council chiefs have revealed closing Queen 's Park Golf Course will be considered as part of a wide-ranging review , which is due to be carried out over the next 12 months . <p> The course was opened in 1905 and went on to establish a reputation as a high-standard municipal course . <p> Over the years , it hosted several European Tour events , welcoming golf legend Seve Ballesteros and fellow major winners Bobby Locke and Henry Cotton . <p> At present three golf courses are located in the space of a five-mile stretch on land both sides of the A338 , with Meyrick Park , Queen 's Park and Playgolf Bournemouth off Riverside Avenue . <p> This subject was raised by a resident to BCP Council leader Vikki Slade on Twitter .
