<h> Fund for York students in memory of academic who died in climbing accident <h> Fund in memory of York lecturer who died in climbing accident <p> A FUND has been set up to help York students in memory of a world-leading environmental scientist who died in a climbing accident . <p> University of York academic Dr Richard Payne was killed on May 26 last year , while attempting to climb Peak 6477 , a previously unclimbed subsidiary peak of one of India 's highest mountains , Nanda Devi . <p> A senior lecturer in the department of environment and geography , Richard joined the University of York in 2015 . His areas of expertise included climate change and peatland environments . <p> The bursary will celebrate Richard 's life by offering undergraduate students the chance to apply for financial support for field work associated with research topics including ; atmospheric pollution , natural hazards , climate change , soil carbon , plant biodiversity and bogs , mires and peats . <p> The bursary has already raised over ? 12,000 via the university 's fund raising pages . He often encouraged undergraduates to join his research projects , resulting in exciting dissertation topics for his students . He also created summer internships for students so they could work with him and learn from him . Richard was an excellent field scientist and all these projects were field-based . <p> " Richard 's students were mentored and supervised to a very high standard , but Richard treated them as collaborators . Indeed , some students co-authored research papers with him and many pursued Masters and PhD degrees to become scientists themselves . The purpose of these bursaries is to inspire students in similar ways . The funds will allow students to conduct fieldwork in subjects that were close to Richard 's heart .
