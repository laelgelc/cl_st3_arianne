<p> A quarter of English league football grounds are at risk from flooding over the next three decades , a new report into the impact of climate change on sport reveals . <p> The report , Playing against the clock , published by the environmental group Rapid Transition Alliance , outlines how a myriad of sports face major threats due to weather associated with climate change . One in three Open Championship golf courses are expected to be damaged by rising sea levels . Around the world , half of previous Winter Olympic cities will be unreliable hosts of winter sports . <p> Twenty-three clubs in the English Football League or Premier League can expect partial or total annual flooding of their stadiums by 2050 . In the Premier League , Chelsea 's Stamford Bridge , West Ham 's Olympic Stadium , Southampton 's St Mary 's and Norwich 's Carrow Road are all stadiums under threat . In the Championship , both Hull City and Cardiff City 's grounds face being entirely under water by 2050 . <p> Even some grounds that are not directly affected face huge challenges .
