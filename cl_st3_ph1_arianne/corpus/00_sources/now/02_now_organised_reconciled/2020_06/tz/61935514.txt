<h> Global awareness can prevent desertification and drought <p> The World Day to Combat Desertification and Drought is a United Nations observance each June 17 . Its purpose is to raise awareness of the presence of desertification and drought , highlighting methods of preventing desertification and recovering from drought . <p> Each year 's global celebration has a unique , novel emphasis that had not been developed previously . <p> Desertification is not cheap . It has social , cultural , environmental and of course economic costs to reverse what it destroys .
