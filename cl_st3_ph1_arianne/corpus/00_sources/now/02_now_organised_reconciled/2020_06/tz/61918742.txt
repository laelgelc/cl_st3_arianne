The domestic biomass stove has been a common cooking stove used in Tanzania . The qualities of these products have been varying and therefore the level of their performance . Basing on this fact and issues of protecting the users against inferior products and assisting manufacturers to produce quality products , this Tanzania Standard was revised in 2010 and it only used charcoal as a fuel . <p> This was done to ensure the availability of quality domestic charcoal cooking stoves . The standard has been revised to take into consideration , all types of biomass fuels , durability , safety and emission issues that were not included in the previous version . <p> This Tanzania Standard is a revision of the first version finalized in 2010 .
