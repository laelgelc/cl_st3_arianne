The dengue viruses ( DENVs ) which thrive in Aedes aegypti mosquitoes that are highly prevalent in Tanzania and other parts of the world are continually spreading and causing dengue fever , yet many cases of the disease may have gone unnoticed in the country as attention was turned to Covid-19 pandemic.Outbreaks of dengue fever have previously been widely reported especially in the months of March to May in Dar es Salaam , a city that is generally hot and humid with small seasonal and daily variations in temperature .
