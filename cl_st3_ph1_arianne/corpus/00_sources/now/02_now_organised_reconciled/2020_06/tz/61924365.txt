<h> Dr Mpango : Cash crops production up by 43 per cent in 2018/19 <h> Thursday June 11 2020 <p> The Finance and Planning Minister Dr Philip Mpango <h> In Summary <p> Finance and Planning minister Phillip Mpango has told the Parliament that cash crops production has increased to 1.145 million tonnes in 2018/19 fiscal year up from 796,502 tonnes in 2015/16 financial year . <p> By Louis Kolumbia @Collouis1999 **28;1608;TOOLONG <p> Dar es Salaam . Production of major cash crops has increased by 348,129 tonnes from 796,502 tonnes in 2015/16 to 1,144,631 tonnes in 2018/19 , according to Finance and Planning minister Philip Mpango . <p> Dr Mpango announced some of the main achievements recorded in the production of major cash crops in Parliament on June 11 when tabling the country 's budget for the 2020/21 fiscal year . The increment is equivalent to 43 per cent . <p> Presenting the 2020/21 budget , Dr Mpango said between 2015/16 fiscal year and 2018/19 financial year , government recorded significant achievement in agriculture including increased production of major cash crops . <p> According to Dr Mpango during the period in review , the National Food Reserve Agency ( NFRA @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ) have increased storage capabilities of cereal crops from 371,000 tonnes in 2015 to 621,000 tonnes in 2020 . <p> " During this period we have successfully introduced crops insurance in order to shield farmers from natural disasters , climate change and enable them to secure loans from financial institutions , " he said . <p> He told Parliament that the other achievement in the agricultural sector includes increased area of irrigation from 461,326 hectors in 2015 to 694,715 in 2020.
