<h> DNCC Mayor Atiqul elected C40 steering committee chairman <p> UNB News <p> Dhaka <p> Publish- June 10 , 2020 , 09:25 PM <p> UNB NEWS - UNB NEWS <p> 377 Views <p> Dhaka North City Corporation ( DNCC ) Mayor Atiqul Islam has been elected the chairman of Steering Committee for South and West Asian region of C40 -- a network of the world 's megacities committed to addressing climate change . <p> Eric Garcetti , mayor of Los Angeles informed the news through an email , according to a press release . <p> Eric also congratulated Atiq for being elected chairman of C40 Steering Committee for the South and West Asian region . <p> Last year , Mayor Atiq attended C40 conference in Denmark from September 9-12 and delivered speech on various topics , including creating healthy environment in the cities and decreasing carbon emission by tree plantation . <p> Around the world , C40 Cities connects 96 of the world 's cities to take bold climate action , leading the way towards a healthier and more sustainable future .
