<p> With efforts to improve environmental sustainability at a corporate level , JAT Holdings , Sri Lanka 's trusted household name for wood coating products , currently stands as the first paint company in Sri Lanka to calculate its direct and indirect carbon footprint of all 3 scopes . Identifying 2018/19 as the base year for carbon footprint calculation , JAT Holdings is officially authorized to use the Responsible Care international logo on its products by complying with international standards in relation to the organization 's commitment to environment and social sustainability . <p> Demonstrating environmental stewardship by Carbon Foot Print disclosure , JAT Holdings succeeded with the introduction of more reliable products along with greater , long term profitability . Adhering to guidelines and conversion factors of the United Kingdom Government Green House Gas Conversion Factors for Company Reports -- DEFRA 2019 , JAT completed the calculations under all three scopes .
