Presently , only 17.8% of households in Africa have internet at home and the continent accounts for only 21% of worldwide internet users . <p> It is estimated that over a quarter of a billion school children in Africa have been affected by COVID-19 and most of them lack the digital tools to continue their education online . <p> " This is very concerning and conveys a strong message that broadband connectivity is absolutely crucial for educational institutions and businesses to continue to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " reliable , high-speed internet is key to ensuring that hospitals and medical institutions can access the global information network and resources necessary to fight COVID-19 . " <p> In terms of bandwidth , Mr. Adam said many African countries still havebandwidth as low as 64 kilobits .
