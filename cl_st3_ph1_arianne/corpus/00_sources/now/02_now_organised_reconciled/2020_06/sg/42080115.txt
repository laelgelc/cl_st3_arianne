Many of you have reached out to us . We want to assure you that your concerns will not go in vain . Justice will prevail . An investigation is underway , focusing on three suspects , " the Chief Minister posted on Twitter and Facebook . He also added , " Having said that , we are saddened by the fact some have used this tragedy to unleash a hate campaign .
