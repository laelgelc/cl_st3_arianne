Car-free neighbourhoods <p> Jakarta under partial lockdown offered a rare respite from the city 's notorious traffic . This glimpse into a Jakarta with free-flowing traffic is a powerful image and an opportunity to rethink the city 's reliance on cars and its attitudes towards vehicle ownership . <p> Starting small could be a big step into quieting the city 's infamous traffic problems . <p> At a neighbourhood level , authorities could free streets of cars , through interventions that emphasise active mobility , such as walking , cycling and personal mobility device infrastructure , integrated with public transportation . <p> Each of these car-free neighbourhoods can in turn be connected via protected corridors for commuters .
