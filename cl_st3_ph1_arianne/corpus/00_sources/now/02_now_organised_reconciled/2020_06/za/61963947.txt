<p> A survey of French , German and US opinion released by the German Marshall Fund of the United States found significant increases in perceptions of Chinese influence since the outbreak of COVID-19 -- in which Beijing has alternately been portrayed as a culprit and an aid provider . <p> The proportion of people who said China was the most influential global player shot up from 13 to 28 percent in France between surveys in January to May , from 12 to 20 percent in Germany and from six to 14 percent in the United States . <p> " Chinese influence in the world was kind of an abstract idea before the crisis , " said Martin Quencez , deputy director of the German Marshall Fund 's Paris office . <p> " When you think about the dependency on China for mask and medical equipment , for instance , this has become very concrete , " he said . <p> Quencez expected a lasting impact , saying that the changes in perceptions were seen across generational and political lines . <p> " It seems more structural than just a quick response to the crisis , " he said . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the United States was the most influential nation but less overwhelmingly . <p> In France , 55 percent of people said the United States was the top global player in May , down from 67 percent in January .
