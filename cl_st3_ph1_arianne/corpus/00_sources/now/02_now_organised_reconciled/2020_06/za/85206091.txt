<h> UN charts new territory with project to track all Myanmar 's forests <p> KUALA LUMPUR , June 18 ( Thomson Reuters Foundation ) - A new five-year project in Myanmar will for the first time document all forests in the Southeast Asian nation - including places affected by ethnic tensions - to pinpoint deforestation risks and boost conservation , the United Nations said . <p> The joint Myanmar-Finland project , launched this week with funding of 8 million euros ( $9 million ) , will monitor all types of forests in an exercise aimed at helping the country reduce emissions that fuel climate change and adapt to warming impacts . <p> It will also serve as a basis to develop global guidelines for tracking and protecting forests in conflict zones . <p> " For a lot of people , Myanmar is a country with still a lot of unknowns , " said Julian Fox , team leader for national forest monitoring at the United Nations ' Food and Agriculture Organization ( FAO ) in Rome , which is managing the project . <p> " There are huge areas of forests that have never been measured , " Fox @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 70% of Myanmar 's population living in rural areas rely on its estimated 29 million hectares ( 72 million acres ) of forests to provide for their basic needs and services . <p> But Myanmar also has the third-highest deforestation rate in the world -
