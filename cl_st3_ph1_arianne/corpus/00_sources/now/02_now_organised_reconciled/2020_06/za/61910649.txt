<p> Other pollinators essential to the health of plants and humans include moths , flies , wasps , butterflies and beetles , as well as bats , many species of monkeys , rodents and birds . <p> Pollination , or the transfer of pollen from the male anther of a plant to the female stigma , which allows plants to be fertilised , is how seeds are produced , and creates offspring for future plant generations . <p> Without bees and other pollinators , plants that we rely on for food and goods would no longer grow . <p> Bees are more important than ever , with the world 's population numbers continuing to soar .
