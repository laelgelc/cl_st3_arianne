<h> Researchers find the world 's cleanest air at the tip of the planet <h> Antarctica , the windiest and driest place on Earth , now has another record : the cleanest air . <p> The world 's cleanest air , free from particles , more specifically , aerosols , caused by human activity was found over the Southern Ocean close to both Antarctica and Australia according to a study published this past week titled , Airborne bacteria confirm the pristine nature of the Southern Ocean boundary layer . <h> The first study of its kind <p> In an ever-evolving world , it is certainly difficult to find parts of the world that are untouched by human impact . This was the first study to measure the bio aerosol composition of the Southern Ocean , where the lower clouds over the Southern Ocean were found to be free of human activity and air pollution . <p> Co-author of the study , Thomas Hill stated , " The Southern Ocean is one of very few places on Earth that has been minimally affected by anthropogenic activities .
