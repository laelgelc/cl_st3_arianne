<h> The Italian oil and gas firm wants a clear , long-term direction ' to deliver different kinds of energy products ' to its clients <p> Milan/London -- Eni CEO Claudio Descalzi wants to turn the 67-year-old Italian oil and gas firm into a greener business , focusing on clean , low-carbon products to build a huge customer base that he believes can steady the ship and attract investors more than ever . <p> The veteran oil executive , reappointed as Eni CEO for a third time in May , said he wants to ensure Eni will play a role in the energy transition that he believes has accelerated amid the coronavirus crisis . " In these next three years I want to trace a completely irreversible path for the group , " Descalzi said . <p> He was in charge of upstream business at Eni before taking over the top spot in 2014 , and is looking to create a slimmer and more flexible company that can switch very rapidly to meet market conditions but which has a clear , long-term direction . <p> " What 's clear is we need to deliver different kinds of energy products @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ February , Eni pledged to slash its greenhouse gas ( GHG ) emissions by 80% in one of the most ambitious clean-up drives in an industry under pressure from investors to go green .
