Thanks to technology , millions will still access the event based in Colombia through virtual means . The theme for 2020 is Celebrate Biodiversity . This day is marked annually to bring to the attention of world citizens the impacts of environmental degradation and drivers of biodiversity loss ( species threats and extinctions ) .
