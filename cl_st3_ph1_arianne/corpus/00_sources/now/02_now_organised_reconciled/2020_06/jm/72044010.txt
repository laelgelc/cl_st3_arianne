<p> The Forestry Department will be embarking on the reforestation of 150 hectares of land and the maintenance of parcels comprising 588 hectares , during the 2020/21 financial year . <p> This was announced by Minister without Portfolio in the Ministry of Economic Growth and Job Creation , Hon. Daryl Vaz , during his contribution to the 2020/21 Sectoral Debate in the House of Representatives on Tuesday ( June 23 ) . <p> " It ( the Department ) will also assess 2,500 hectares of Mangrove forest and complete the Bill to amend the Forest Act ( 1996 ) , " Mr. Vaz informed . <p> He further noted that 109 hectares of denuded lands have been reforested across the island , surpassing the target of 100 hectares . <p> In the meantime , Mr. Vaz said that for the current financial year , a total of 671 employment opportunities were provided for 667 casual workers from rural areas/communities close to forests , in the areas of plantation maintenance , reforestation , biophysical inventory and volume assessments . <p> This , he noted , represents an 18 per cent increase over the 569 casual employment opportunities provided last financial @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Department will continue to provide support to the tree-planting programme to meet the targeted three million trees over three years . <p> " Under the Initiative , the Agency will be coordinating the provision of seedlings to meet the needs of the government-led planting programme as well as general distribution to the public . There will also be some training for HOPE interns over the period , " he said . <p> Following a change to Ministerial arrangements in the Cabinet , announced by Prime Minister , the Most Hon. Andrew Holness , on Wednesday ( June 24 ) , Minister Vaz is now responsible for the Water and Housing portfolios and subject matters relating to certain special economic development projects . <p> Newly appointed Minister without Portfolio in the Economic Growth and Job Creation Ministry , Hon. Leslie Campbell , has been assigned to deal with Land , Environment and Climate Change .
