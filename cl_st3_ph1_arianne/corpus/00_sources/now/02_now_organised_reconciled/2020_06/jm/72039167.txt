<h> Farming communities get a helping hand through COVID <p> The dislocation caused by the spreading coronavirus ( COVID-19 ) , was nowhere as marked as in the farming sector in rural Jamaica , where men and women saw their earnings contract and some altogether disappear . <p> And as their earnings shrank , they also lost the ability to buy necessary inputs to plant again . Inputs like water , without which it is impossible to cultivate new crops . <p> The intervention of the Adaptation Programme and Financing Mechanism ( AP&amp;FM ) Project , a Pilot Programme for Climate Resilience ( PPCR ) has been critical in helping farmers in five Clarendon communities to resolve these issues , stepping in to provide equipment and water for aquaponics farming . <p> Community Animator Dr Bridgette Barrett explains , " Given that primary beneficiaries of the AP&amp;FM project are farmers and some of them lost produce and markets due to COVID , we decided to assist with water to help to ' save ' some of the crops that were left . They did n't lose jobs but lost earnings . " <p> " Now they will be better @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ capacity . " <p> In March the communities of Aenon Town , Ritchies , Alston , Johns Hall and James Hill , with a combined population of over 10 , 333 persons , were introduced to new aquaponics systems operated by their Community Development Committees . <p> Aquaponics is an innovative farming technique that combines aquaculture ( fish farming ) with hydroponics ( soilless crop production ) into a closed system that is resilient to destructive climate change events . <p> It is a method that boosts crop production up to 10 times higher than traditionally cultivated plots of equivalent size . It uses 85 to 90 per cent less water and uses no chemical fertilizers or pesticides . <p> It is low energy consumption providing year-round crop production and uses less labour than traditional farming , making it accessible to individuals with disabilities , beneficial for female-led households and attractive to youth . <p> This aquaponics system is one of five recently launched in Clarenon . <p> Barrett outlines that the AP&amp;FM COVID-19 response project has two components .
