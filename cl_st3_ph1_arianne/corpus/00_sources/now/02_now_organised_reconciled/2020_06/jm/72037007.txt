<h> 4-H Launches Family Backyard Garden Challenge <p> The Jamaica 4-H Clubs is inviting young people to register for its family backyard garden challenge . <p> The competition , which started in May , will run over a three-month period and is open to Jamaica 4-H Clubs members , volunteers as well as members of the public aged nine and over . <p> Executive Director of the Jamaica 4-H Clubs , Dr. Ronald Blake , says the challenge has became necessary , in light of the coronavirus ( COVID-19 ) pandemic , which has brought into greater focus the need for increased food security . <p> " We are ... just coming off the heels of making adjustments based on the threat that climate change presents for food security , and so this is almost a double impact . So it is very clear to us that one of the strategies that we will have to implement to secure food for our people is to ensure that we broaden the production base , " he outlines . <p> He notes that " not only can we rely on our few farmers and investors who are producing food , we @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ who are physically able and with the resources , to play a key role in food production " . <p> Dr. Blake says that the challenge will continue outdoor activities in a fun and engaging way , while maintaining social distance . <p> He notes that the activity , while targeted at the youth , aims to involve the entire family . <p> " While the young person will be the applicant , it does not exclude grandma , parent , baby sister , baby brother , uncle or whoever is in that setting . We are saying it is a very healthy activity for the entire family ... not only for physical health but you are growing your own food , " he points out . <p> The family backyard garden challenge , under the theme ' Plot to Plate ' , runs from May 28 to August 31 . <p> The gardens can be a small plot of land , one 's backyard , or containers used to plant two or more cash crops such as cucumber , callaloo , lettuce , pak-choi , cabbage , corn and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a three-minute video in mp4 format or six high-quality photographs of the garden area at the beginning , during and end ( harvesting ) stages of the challenge . <p> " We will be able to assess the garden based on the submission of photographs and videos through our online platform .
