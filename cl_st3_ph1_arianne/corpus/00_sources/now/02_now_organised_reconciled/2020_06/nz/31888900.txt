When these contaminants end up in the bales of mixed plastic or mixed paper and cardboard , it reduces the value of the materials . Soft plastics also jam up the sorting machines , making them a health and safety risk . These materials can be dropped off for free at retail shops to be made into new local products . <p> Hazardous waste including gas cooker aerosol canisters , gas cylinders , and batteries are an explosion waiting to happen , yet people still put them in their kerbside bins .
