<h> Further water storage for Northland announced <p> IrrigationNZ is commending the Government for funding further water storage as part of its post-COVID infrastructure fast-track . <p> Yesterday , the Government announced eleven infrastructure projects to be fast-tracked under a new law to help rebuild the economy after the COVID-19 pandemic . <p> One of the earmarked projects is the Kaikohe water storage facility in Northland , providing water for agricultural and horticultural use , as well as drinking water for the area . <p> The project is expected to create 70 jobs in the drought-hit region . <p> Last week , the Government announced it would be investing a further $37.5 million into water storage at a site in Ngawha , Northland . <p> Shortly after the announcement , IrrigationNZ announced that it wants the prioritisation of water storage in Northland extended to other dry regions . <p> " It is very promising to see our country move toward embracing the right mix of regulatory and environmental frameworks to allow water infrastructure to develop for the benefit of all - including the environment and Iwi , " says IrrigationNZ chief executive Elizabeth Soal . <p> " The slow @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ block for progressing pragmatic and necessary water infrastructure in areas where it is sorely needed by the community , " says Soal . <p> Soal says reliable water is integral to the ongoing sustainability of New Zealand . <p> " The next step is an overarching water strategy to guide planning and ensure further water storage development is in the right place , for the right reasons , with the right funding . " <p> This morning , Agriculture Minister Damien O'Connor said the primary industry is in a good position to help re-boot the economy , with primary exports on track to grow by $1.7 billion on last year . <p> " Access to reliable water is key to this growth , " says Soal . <p> Environment Minister David Parker says the eleven infrastructure projects included in the COVID-19 Recovery ( Fast Track ) Bill will be introduced in the House later this week . <p> Parker says that the principles of the Treaty of Waitangi , and Treaty Settlement obligations will apply to all projects under the Bill . <p> Once the Bill passes , the projects @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ will set appropriate conditions on the projects before they can proceed . <p> Parker has encouraged further applications to be sent to him which will be assessed as part of the Fast Track Bill to stimulate the economy . <p> IrrigationNZ is encouraging communities to come together to identify water storage options for their area to ensure themselves and the country be more resilient to economic shocks like COVID-19 and future implications such as climate change .
