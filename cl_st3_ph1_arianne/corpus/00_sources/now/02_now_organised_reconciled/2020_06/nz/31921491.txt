<p> " This is a great thing to do with your whanau , friends and tamariki ( children ) and is an excellent activity out in nature that benefits your wellbeing , " a Manaaki Whenua-Landcare Research spokesperson said . <p> The results from last year 's survey showed positive signs for four native species . There have been increases in piwakawaka/fantail , tui and kereru over the past 10 years and a acceleration of numbers in the past five years . <p> Past data indicates a moderate decline nationally in tauhou/silvereye , although that had slowed in the past five years . <p> Retired researcher Eric Spurr , who started the Garden Bird Survey 14 years ago , says the decline in tauhou/silvereye could be due to climate change raising the temperatures in gardens , meaning the birds are choosing to stay in the forest or bush where it is cooler . <p> Combined information from surveys completed across New Zealand help to paint a picture of birds in gardens . <p> Data is used in a variety of ways , including by regional councils for predator control and biodiversity programmes . <p> * More information is available @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ FLIGHT : Results from garden bird surveys over the past 10 years have shown an increase in piwakawaka/fantail numbers .
