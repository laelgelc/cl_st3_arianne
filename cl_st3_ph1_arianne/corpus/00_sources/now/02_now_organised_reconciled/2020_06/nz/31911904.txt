The solution is to educate farmers that maintaining nutrients on-farm rather than letting it run off is valuable to them , as well as to local waterways , " he told Dairy News . " That carbon and biology rich soils with deep rooted plants will mitigate droughts ... that they can be part of the solution to climate change rather than the villains .
