<h> On doctors , unions and competing interests <p> We in the medical profession can learn something from the new chief of police in Minneapolis , writes Dr David Galler . <p> Following the death of George Floyd the spotlight has rightly settled on a close examination of police behaviour , as well as on the role of the police unions in setting and maintaining a culture that seems to promote violence against citizens of colour and in protecting officers from being held accountable for it . Unionism for good , or for bad , falls into that category . <p> Given this antipathy , the strength of the police unions and their pervasive influence over police culture might seem out of place , but it is n't .
