It also heralded the Bill 's progress through Parliament where it is now awaiting its third reading . <p> Minister for Climate Change , James Shaw said adding the cap in what was always intended to be a cap and trade scheme , finally brings into place a mechanism to lower the lid on emissions . <h> Cap details <p> The cap on the ETS will be 160m tonnes of CO2 equivalent greenhouse gases over @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ period of 354m tonnes of CO2 equivalent greenhouse gasses . This reflects half of all NZ emissions are agricultural and for the time being remain outside the ETS . <p> Of these 160m NZ Units ( NZUs ) , 43m will be allocated for free to emissions-intensive trade-exposed industries . While the proposal to ratchet down the free allocation over time is still in place . Another 90m will be auctioned and the remaining 27m will have to come from NZU stockpiles . More detail on the auction system are elsewhere in this week 's Energy and Environment . <p> The provisional budget represents 15 Mt CO2e in reductions over the five-year period from previous forecasts . <h> Emissions budget review delayed <p> The Climate Change Commission will provide advice next year on future emissions budgets . Papers released around the Bill show this has been delayed . The Commission was initially required to recommend the first three emissions budgets and advice on the first emissions reduction plan by 1 February 2021 . <p> The Commission has said these statutory deadlines are challenging and this has been exacerbated by covid-19 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to extend its deadlines by six months , from 1 February 2021 to 30 July 2021 . <p> As a result changes to the law have been made to allow the Minister to delay deadlines for up to six months if he agrees after a request from the Commission . <p> Then the Commission will propose a revised emissions budget for 2021-25 that could supersede the provisional emissions budget . This is likely as the current budget is not sufficient to meet commitments under the Paris Agreement to cut NZ 's emissions by 30% below 2005 levels , and 11% below 1990 levels by 2030 . Shaw is amongst those who say those commitments are insufficient to meet the wider Paris goal of limiting warming to 1.5C above pre-industrial levels . <p> The budget allows for 354 Mt CO2e in net emissions over the next five years , the Government 's commitment is to only emit around 601 Mt CO2e in net emissions by 2030 . <p> Shaw also acknowledged the reality that to achieve this NZ will have to purchase carbon credits .
