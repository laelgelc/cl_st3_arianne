The legislation fixes ... <p> New Zealand now has one of the world 's most ambitious frameworks for tackling the climate crisis , the Minister for Climate Change James Shaw said after the Emissions Trading Reform Bill passed through Parliament this afternoon . <p> The legislation fixes the Emissions Trading Scheme and turns it into one of the most effective tools New Zealand has for reducing climate-polluting emissions . <p> " In a single term we have taken more action on climate change than the past 30 years of governments combined . Today we have reached another significant milestone in our plan to reduce New Zealand 's emissions . <p> " There is a long way to go , but we are starting to bend the curve towards a climate-friendly future in New Zealand . Alongside the Zero Carbon Bill , which was passed unanimously by Parliament last year , this Bill means that we now have the foundations in place @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " James Shaw said . <p> The changes made by the Bill include setting a cap on the total emissions allowed within the ETS . This is something the previous government had failed to do , leaving the scheme too weak to have any real impact on reducing our emissions . <p> The Bill also includes a range of necessary rule changes that will incentivise New Zealand 's biggest polluters to invest in the transition to a clean , climate-friendly economy . <p> " Until now , the ETS has not fulfilled its primary purpose , which is to reduce emissions .
