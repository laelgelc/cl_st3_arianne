<h> Cap finally added to NZ 's cap-and-trade scheme <p> The decade-in-the-making strengthening of the ETS is finally upon us , but now questions are being raised over whether the scheme will actually make a difference , Marc Daalder reports <p> The Emissions Trading Scheme ( ETS ) , New Zealand 's take on a cap-and-trade carbon market , will finally have the eponymous cap on the number of units in the market . <p> On Tuesday , Climate Change Minister James Shaw announced the final version of regulations that Cabinet will approve after a bill strengthening the ETS passes through Parliament in the coming days . <p> " An effective Emissions Trading Scheme is a key part of what needs to be done . Unfortunately the rules set by previous governments left the scheme too weak to have any real impact on reducing our emissions , " Shaw said . <p> " A good example of this is the fact that until now the ETS has been a cap and trade system without a cap . This has meant that emissions permitted under the scheme were , in effect , unlimited . I am delighted to say we @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ today will better translate our emission reduction targets into a predictable emission price , which will incentivise our biggest polluters to invest in the transition to a clean , climate-friendly economy . " <p> New regulations for ETS <p> The regulations set out a provisional emissions budget of 354 million tonnes of CO2 , or the equivalent amount of another greenhouse gas , to be emitted over the five years from 2021 to 2025 . This budget will be replaced by an official emissions budget for 2022-2025 that will be released by the Climate Change Commission in February . <p> The provisional budget will cap the number of carbon credits in the market at 160 million over the next five years , with each unit representing the right to emit one tonne of CO2-equivalent ( CO2e ) . <p> Of these 160 million New Zealand Units ( NZUs ) , 43 million will be freely allocated to emissions-intensive trade-exposed industries like New Zealand Steel or the Tiwai Point Aluminium Smelter . The floor will be $20 per tonne and the ceiling will be $50 . Both will increase by 2 percent a year to account for inflation . <p> In the year until the new regulations come into effect , the fixed price at which emitters can purchase unlimited NZUs from the Government will rise $10 to $35 a tonne . <p> Significant reforms to the forestry sector 's involvement in the ETS have been pushed off by a year as the industry grapples with Covid-19 .
