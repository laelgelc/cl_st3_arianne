<h> Paula Bennett quits politics after ' a hell of a ride ' <p> After being rolled as deputy leader and losing the spot running the campaign , the National MP and former deputy prime minister is quitting parliament after 15 years . <p> In August 2019 , when it was announced that Paula Bennett would be going list-only and taking on the role of chairing the National Party campaign , it was a safe bet that this provided a smooth exit strategy : were National to fail to make up the numbers and form a government , she could slide out of parliamentary politics without triggering a byelection . <p> At the end of last month , however , as Todd Muller deposed Simon Bridges as leader , Bennett lost not just the deputy leadership to Nikki Kaye , but her position running the campaign to Gerry Brownlee . She was bumped down the shadow cabinet ladder from second to 13th . And with less than 12 weeks to go , she 's had enough .
