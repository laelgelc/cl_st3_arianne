Vector and Sustainable Coastlines invite you on Sunday 21 June 2020 to celebrate International Climate Change day at the Puhinui Reserve . We ca n't think of a better way to mark the occasion than by getting your hands dirty and planting some beautiful native trees for our Papatuanuku . So far , Vector 's ' Urban Forest ' has planted 31,000 trees in the Puhinui catchment , stretching from Totara Park to the Puhinui Reserve . This catchment is one of the most significant tributaries of the Manukau Harbour , impacting both environmental and social outcomes , flowing through farmland , reserve , industrial areas , communities , and the Manukau town centre . Planting in the Puhinui catchment is helping to improve water quality , reduce tree inequality , and increase the visual amenity of the area .
