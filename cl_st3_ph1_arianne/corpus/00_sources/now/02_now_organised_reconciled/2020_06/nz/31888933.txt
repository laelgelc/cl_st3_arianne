<h> Four focus areas <p> Rau Tipu Rau Ora , our regional Covid-19 response and recovery plan released on Monday , says on its cover : " Not simply restoring the good things we had before Covid-19 , it is also about future-proofing and re-establishing an even better region with a whole-of-wellbeing approach . " <p> The plan has four focus areas , with the region 's workforce at the centre of this framework . In summary they are : <p> " Supporting our whanau and community -- Our people must have confidence in their health , social and emergency services to cope with a repeat of the pandemic , while maintaining core services and support for those in need . Our ongoing responses will build on services , partnerships and approaches that worked well during Alert Level 4 ; address key gaps in our services and support for those most affected by Covid-19 and its impact ; and increase local decision-making across health , emergency services and the social sector in support of Tairawhiti households , whanau and communities . <p> " GETTING OUR ECONOMY MOVING -- Stimulate our regional economy by delivering large-scale capital works projects , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and support for our Tairawhiti businesses through enhanced procurement processes will enable the creation of further employment and training opportunities . Opportunities for growth through the recovery plan will include a focus on meeting housing and infrastructure needs . <p> " OUR ENVIRONMENT -- As part of our broader interests in managing and protecting our natural environment we unlock immediate opportunities for ' green ' employment and training . This will include initiatives to sustain our local waterways and riparian lands , and endangered ecosystems . As well as those initiatives , we practise good kaitiakitanga ( guardianship ) : which means improving biodiversity , fresh water and clean rivers , sustainable land management , and climate change mitigation . <p> " OUR WORKFORCE -- To improve our economic recovery , and ensure our households and whanau have greater resilience against future shocks , we boost the opportunities for local training , qualifications and jobs . We partner with industry and employers , iwi , tertiary education providers and government , to include a strong focus on re-deployment .
