<h> Document dump : What 's in the latest round of Covid-19 papers ? <p> The government has proactively released a whole bunch of Covid-19-related documents . Alex Braae has been trawling through them to bring you the highlights . <p> The last time a whole lot of Covid-19 papers were released , it was controversial . They came out late on Friday afternoon , and seemed to be a case of overwhelming the nation 's journalists and watchdogs with information at an inconvenient time . <p> This time , the release happened very differently . The government 's press people gave quiet warnings that it would be coming out on Friday morning , and then it did -- right on time . <p> So what 's in the latest round of files ? By our count , there are 256 documents in total , so it 's fair to say this is only a small selection of interesting points . But we 'll keep adding to the list over the course of the day as new details come up .
