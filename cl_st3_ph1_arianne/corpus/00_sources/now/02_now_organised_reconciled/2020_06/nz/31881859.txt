<p> On World Environment Day ( June 5 ) , UN Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) and the Google Arts &amp; Culture Lab residency program announce " Heartbeat of the Earth " , a series of experimental artworks inspired by climate data . <p> What we eat : Data artist Laurie Frick 's work examines the impact of individual foods on the environment using hand-drawn data visualisations , colour coded and sized by CO2 output . <p> Coastline paradox : Pekka Niittyvirta and Timo Aho visualize the predicted sea level rise -- and the number of people likely to be displaced -- in more than 200 different locations between the years 2000 and 2300 . <p> Timelines : Experimental photographer Fabian Oefner explores the retreat of the Rhone and Trift glaciers in Switzerland over the last 140 years by using data from ETH Zurich and drone captures . <p> Ovais Sarmad , Deputy Executive Secretary of UN Climate Change said " We may represent different groups and different cultures , but science is our common language .
