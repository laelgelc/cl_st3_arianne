" <p> But are we risking missing out on the benefits of flexible working arrangements , just so Wellington can keep the global crown of most cafes per capita ? <p> Public sector ordered back to work <p> Saving the CBDs would begin with the public sector , Ardern said . The Government has instructed the State Services Commission " to issue new workplace guidance to make it clear that every public sector worker should return to their usual place of work , taking into account extant flexible work policies " . <p> This is despite a strong preference from many public sector workers for working from home .
