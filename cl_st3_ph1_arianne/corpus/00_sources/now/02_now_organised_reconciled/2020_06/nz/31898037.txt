" Threatened kauri trees can not wait , species that are being destroyed by pests can not wait , and new biosecurity threats will not wait . These are things we must act on now . " <p> Changes to Auckland 's budget as a result of the pandemic include a drop from $17 million to $7 million for capital expenditure in Environmental Services , including the Natural Environmental Targeted Rate ( NETR ) .
