<h> Metlink to boost electric buses from 2 to 22 per cent of its fleet - Greater Wellington Council <p> The ink has dried on contracts for 98 new electric buses , which will take Metlink 's fleet of electric buses to 108 , significantly reducing its carbon footprint and taking Greater Wellington a step closer on its target of carbon neutrality by 2030 . <p> Metlink currently has 450 buses in its active fleet in the Wellington region 10 of which are EVs , making up two percent of the fleet . Once the 98 buses are added to the fleet the proportion of EVs will rise to 22 per cent which , excluding market leader China , is high by international standards . <p> Seventy-three of the buses will be used on current scheduled services with a further 25 to be progressively added to routes to meet future network growth .
