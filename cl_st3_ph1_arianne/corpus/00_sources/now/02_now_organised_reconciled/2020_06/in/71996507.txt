<h> Daily Court Digest : Major environment orders ( June 3 , 2020 ) <h> Last Updated : Wednesday 03 June 2020 <p> Santragachi Jheel pollution <p> Inaction and inordinate delay by the authorities in saving the Santragachi Jheel from pollution and encroachment earned the ire of Justice Sonam Phintso Wangdi of the Kolkata ( Eastern Zone Bench ) of the National Green Tribunal ( NGT ) on June 1 , 2020 . <p> The case was instituted on the allegation that the water body -- a notified bird sanctuary in Howrah , West Bengal , was being polluted due to dumping of municipal and plastic wastes and building waste materials . <p> Besides , it was alleged that constructions were being undertaken on the northern side of the jheel by filling up portions of the water body . <p> The NGT noted that even after the passage of two-and-a-half years and orders to settle the issue , nothing had been done . <p> The railways had to provide for the land for setting up the sewage treatment plant for treatment of the sewage diverted away from the water body and the cost was to be shared between the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the " ratio of the contribution of sewage " . Also , all the encroachments had to be removed . <p> The tribunal was appraised of the fact that the HMC and the railway authorities were responsible for the inordinate delay , whether it was the question of lease of the land , the release of funds towards cost of the projects and removal of encroachers . Counsel for the applicant had submitted that all statutory clearances were required to be obtained afresh by the transferees . <p> The NGT disposed of the application saying it was not tenable to say that it was a new expansion of business and thus would require fresh environmental clearance , as there was no expansion .
