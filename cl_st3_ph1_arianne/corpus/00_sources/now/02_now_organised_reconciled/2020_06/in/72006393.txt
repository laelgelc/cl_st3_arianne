<h> Environment Minister Prakash Javadekar has said that India has been able to maintain 8 per cent of the world 's biodiversity because " our ethos is to live with the nature " <p> Despite a huge human and animal population on a limited habitation area , India has been able to maintain 8 per cent of the world 's biodiversity because " our ethos is to live with the nature " , Environment Minister Prakash Javadekar said Thursday . <p> In a video message on the eve of the World Environment Day , Javadekar said there are many constraints for India in conserving the biodiversity , like 16 per cent of world 's human population and 16 per cent of cattle population living on 2.5 per cent of world 's land . " Still we have maintained biodiversity of 8 per cent of the world , " he said . <p> " India 's culture is to be with the nature . We are the only country which worships trees , animals , birds everybody . We love nature .
