( Photo : Xinhua/ Ammar Safarjalani ) <p> In its annual " Global Trends " report released on June 18 , Thursday , the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees ( UNHCR ) noted that the total number of displaced people in the world rose by a record 9 million in 2019 , bringing the global total to a staggering 79.5 million , which is more than 1% of the world population . <p> Though most of them are internally displaced , the number of people who are forced out of their countries -- identified as refugees -- is also rising . Of these , 5.6 million are Palestinian refugees displaced in the 1948 Nakba and during the successive Israeli expansions into Palestinian territory . <p> According to UNHCR data , more than two-thirds of the non-Palestinian refugees come from only five countries -- Syria , Venezuela , Afghanistan , South Sudan and Myanmar . <p> The destruction of the political , economic and social infrastructure of such countries by imperialist aggression , both conventional and non-conventional , remains the primary reason for the displacement of millions globally . The prolonged wars in most of these countries have also destroyed the chances of return of the refugees , who are forced to live in camps and face various forms of harassment . It says , " in the 1990s , on average 1.5 million refugees were able to return home each year . Over the past decade that number has fallen to around 385,000 . " <p> Busting the popular myth and propaganda about the so-called ' refugee problem ' in developed countries , the UNHCR data establishes that around 85% of the total refugees in the world are hosted by neighboring developing countries . In fact , apart from Germany , no other developed country figures in the list of top five host countries . The top five host countries are Turkey , Columbia , Pakistan , Uganda , Germany and Bangladesh .
