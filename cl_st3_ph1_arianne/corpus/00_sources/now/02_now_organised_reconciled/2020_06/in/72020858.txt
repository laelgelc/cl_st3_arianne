<h> Last Updated : Wednesday 17 June 2020 <p> Culling can not be an option to reduce human-crocodile conflict in India , experts said on June 17 , 2020 -- the World Crocodile Day . <p> India is home to three crocodilian species : <p> The mugger or marsh crocodile ( Crocodylus palustris ) <p> The estuarine or saltwater crocodile ( Crocodylus porosus ) <p> The gharial ( Gavialis gangeticus ) <p> The mugger is the most widespread , found in other South Asian countries too . The gharial is found mostly in Himalayan rivers . The estuarine crocodile is found in Odisha 's Bhitarkanika National Park , the Sundarbans in West Bengal and the Andamans and Nicobar Islands . It is also found across Southeast Asia and northern Australia . <p> The estuarine crocodile is infamous globally as a known maneater . The mugger is also known to be dangerous . The gharial is known to be a relatively harmless , fish-eating species . <p> The crocodiles , in general , keep hitting headlines due to conflicts with humans , often resulting in the loss of life or limb . The shallow , sluggish Vishwamitri river that flows through the heart of the city , is home to over 200 muggers . Every monsoon , the media is rife with reports of crocodiles entering city localities and being rescued . There have been attacks on humans , fatal as well as non-fatal , in the city and the surrounding district . <p> " Please do n't make the crocodile the villain here . Attacks usually increase when humans approach river banks , " herpetologist Raju Vyas , who has studied the crocodiles in the region for two decades , told DTE . <p> " In 2019 , there eight attacks in the whole of Gujarat -- three fatal and five not . In 2017 , there were 12 attacks and in 2018 , 10 . " People here are very tolerant of these animals and will not harm them at any cost , " Vyas said . <p> Culling is something that Tapeshwar Singh Bhati would also never recommend .
