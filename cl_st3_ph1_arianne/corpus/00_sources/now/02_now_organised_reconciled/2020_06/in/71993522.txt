The EC dated September 3 , 2019 was granted to M / s Adani Power ( Jharkhand ) Ltd by the MoEF&amp;CC , which permitted withdrawl of water from the Ganga for a proposed thermal power project . <p> According to the earlier EC , water was to be drawn from the Chir river . <p> The complainant said the project proponent had changed the scope of a crucial aspect of the project relating to drawing of water from the Ganga to meet the water requirements of the thermal power plant though the same was never mentioned in environment impact assessment or placed before the public during public hearings . <p> Further , the impact on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ where there was presence of Gangetic dolphins , otters and many other wildlife species , had not been considered in the Source Sustainability Study report .
