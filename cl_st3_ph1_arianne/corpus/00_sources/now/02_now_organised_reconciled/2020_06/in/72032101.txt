<h> Protests mar Bengaluru consultation on draft solid waste rules <h> Last Updated : Wednesday 08 July 2015 <p> The Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change ( MoEFCC ) was forced to call off the national consultation on the draft Municipal Solid Waste ( Handling &amp;Management ) Rules , 2015 , in Bengaluru on May 22 amidst protests . <p> Environmental activists and civil society groups opposed the convention as they felt it lacked transparency . The consultation was meant to cover all the five southern states -- Andhra Pradesh , Kerala , Karnataka , Telangana and Tamil Nadu . <p> The environment ministry organised the " Stakeholders ' Consultation on Draft Waste Management Rules , 2015 " in partnership with the Central Pollution Control Board ( CPCB ) , the Federation of Indian Chambers of Commerce and Industry ( FICCI ) and the Confederation of Indian Industry ( CII ) . <p> " The consultation was unconstitutional , undemocratic and illegal from the very beginning , " Leo F Saldanha of Environment Support Group ( ESG ) , a Bengaluru-based non-profit , said . <p> The consultation should have been conducted either by the State Pollution @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Environment and Climate Change . Instead , the ministry outsourced the essential executive function of formation of rules to corporates , he added . <p> People kept out <p> In April , the MoEFCC put up a release on the draft solid waste management rules on its website for public viewing and comment . <p> " It was not clear from the release whether the draft rules were notified in the gazette within the 60-day timeframe as per the rules before public consultation , " Saldanha said . <p> However , it was categorically stated on the website that the ministry was planning national consultations on the issue . The release also said that such programmes would be organised on May 1 in New Delhi , May 8 in Mumbai , May 22 in Bengaluru and June 5 in Kolkata , in partnership with CPCB , FICCI and CII . <p> The ministry said that with " the government 's special impetus on Clean India Mission in consonance with Make in India and ease of doing business , these amendments are timely endeavours towards meeting these initiatives of the government @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , the release read . <p> " Subsequent to this release , the ministry did not issue any public advertisements inviting people to engage in the consultations , " Saldanha added . <p> " Instead , the CII and the FICCI selectively invited industry representatives or pro-industry lobby groups to the consultations held in New Delhi and Mumbai . Also , nowhere on the ministry 's website there was any mention that other consultations would also be held , barring those organised by CII and FICCI . <p> Consultation in five-star hotel <p> The Bengaluru consultation was organised at Le Meridian , a five-star hotel . E-mail invitations were sent out and the participation was restricted to invitees who had registered themselves in advance . <p> " This invitation exercise was undertaken with such secrecy that not even the Solid Waste Management Expert Committee members constituted by Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike ( BBMP ) , the municipal corporation , were invited , " Bhargavi S Rao of ESG said . <p> Petitioners , who had @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ progressive , just and environmentally-viable waste management strategies , were also not invited . <p> " CII officials resisted my entry to the forum even after my informing them that I would be participating as a petitioner in the waste management case before the Karnataka High Court , " Rao added . <p> The programme started smoothly when Shekar Viswanath , the chairperson of Karnataka CII , welcomed Vaman Acharya , the chairperson of Karnataka SPCB and Biswanath Sinha , MoEFCC joint secretary , to preside over the consultation . <p> However , activists raised their voice soon after and questioned the ministry 's action of making industry bodies partners in organising the consultation . <p> " Nowhere in the constitution or in the Environment ( Protection ) Act , there is any mention that the responsibility of formulating rules and legislations can be outsourced to bodies such as the CII .
