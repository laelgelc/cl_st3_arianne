<h> India Underreports Heatwave Deaths . Here 's Why This Must Change <p> New Delhi : India counts among heat stroke deaths only those deaths medically certified as having been caused by direct exposure to the sun , thereby capturing only 10% of the real figure , leaving out deaths due to high ambient temperature . Accurate reporting is essential to create informed policies to build resilience , at a time when India is grappling with increasingly intense and frequent heat waves and is vulnerable to rising temperatures due to climate change , experts say . <p> Heat waves killed about 6,167 people in India over eight years to 2018 .
