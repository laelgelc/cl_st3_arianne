<p> The way the pandemic has been handled across the globe , especially by authoritarian populist regimes , gives us very valuable clues into what politics and democracy will look like after Covid-19 has subsided . The United States under Donald Trump was in denial of the pandemic for close to six months and demonstrated its indifference to the growing causalities , most of whom were African-Americans . Brazil under Jair Bolsonaro followed a similar path of denial , which has resulted in a growing number of infections , chiefly among the elderly . One of his aides reportedly even said that the concentration of deaths among the old will reduce Brazil 's pension deficit . <p> India under Narendra Modi demonstrated a similar stoic indifference to the migrant crisis , turning it into a low-key spectacle . It neither prepared more medical facilities during the lockdown nor allotted more resources to states , but simply passed the buck to state governments after having announced a lockdown without consultation .
