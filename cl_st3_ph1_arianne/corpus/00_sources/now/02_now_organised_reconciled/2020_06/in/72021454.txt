<p> Gond painter Venkat Raman Singh Shyam 's new work takes a sharp look at the fallout of the lockdown <p> TWO intricately painted airplanes fly across a stark red background , while a swarm of people trudge along , on foot or on bicycles , lugging their belongings with them . ' Journey on Mars ' is Pardhan Gond artist Venkat Raman Singh Shyam 's comment on how the government wanted to repatriate Indians stuck abroad , but let down the migrants who came to cities for work . " Hum apne logo ke liye kuch nahi kar sakte ? ( Ca n't we do anything for our own people ? ) " he asks . " The news channels are full of stories about the hardships labourers have faced , but there are many stories we have n't even heard about . " <p> Through his art , the 50-year-old artist hopes to narrate their tales to shed light on the current crisis .
