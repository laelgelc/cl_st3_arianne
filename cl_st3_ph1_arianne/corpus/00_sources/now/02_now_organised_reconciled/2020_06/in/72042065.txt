<h> PCBA fails to make OIL accountable for Baghjan catastrophe <p> In a dramatic turn of events the Pollution Control Board of Assam ( PCBA ) has recalled its closure notice to Oil India Limited ( OIL ) to shut operations at the Baghjan oil field in Tinsukia district of Assam . <p> It took the PCBA just 48 hours to overturn its own decision to slap a closure notice to OIL charging the PSU of ' nonchalantly violating ' provisions of law . <p> On June 19 PCBA issued a closure notice to OIL accusing the PSU of carrying out operations at the Baghjan Oil field without PCBA 's consent . <p> According to PCBA sources , the notice also accused OIL of violating important laws like the Water ( Prevention and Control of Pollution ) Act of 1974 and The Air ( prevention and Control of Pollution ) Act of 1981 . <p> On June 22 PCBA 's recall letter ( **29;4733;TOOLONG ) was received by OIL .
