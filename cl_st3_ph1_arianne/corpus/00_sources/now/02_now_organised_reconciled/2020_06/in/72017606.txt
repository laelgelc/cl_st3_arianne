<h> State to seek extension of 2 years to Mhadei tribunal <p> The state cabinet on Friday passed two resolutions linked to the Mhadei river issue , as regards declaration of eco-sensitive areas around wildlife sanctuaries and extension of the term to the Mhadei Water Disputes Tribunal , which would be soon forwarded to Prime Minister Narendra Modi , Union Minister for Water Resources , River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation Uma Bharati and Union Minister of State for Environment , Forest and Climate Change ( independent charge ) Anil Dave . <p> Stating that Goa has the largest geological area of 78 per cent in terms of the existence of river Mhadei , followed by Karnataka at 18 per cent and Maharashtra with 4 per cent , the Chief Minister said that Karnataka , by way of its major ongoing works , has violated and affected the eco-sensitive areas of Western Ghats , considered as hottest of hotspots of biodiversity . <p> " These works have badly damaged the flora and fauna , and biodiversity of the area , " Parsekar added , pointing out that the state of Goa has taken steps to declare one km eco-sensitive zones @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ taken by the ministry of environment and forests to declare eco-sensitive areas in Karnataka , thereby permitting such heavy environmental damage and similarly damaging wildlife sanctuaries and Western Ghats . " Therefore , the council of ministers today resolved to request the Prime Minister , Union Minister for Water Resources , River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation , and Union Minister of State for Environment , Forest and Climate Change to forthwith take steps to stop such degradation of wildlife sanctuaries , as also to restore the area and undue harm done by the works of Karnataka . " <p> The Chief Minister said the state cabinet also resolved to request the Prime Minister and other two ministers to extend the period of the Mhadai Water Disputes Tribunal for two years , as the tribunal is seized with the matter pertaining to the Mhadei river issue , and hearing is going on before it on day-to-day basis . <p> " The resolution maintains that the term of the tribunal be extended by two years , which is expiring in August 2016 , " he added .
