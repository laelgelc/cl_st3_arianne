In India , 50 per cent of the labour force is engaged in the agriculture sector , which accounts for 18 per cent of the gross domestic product ( GDP ) . <p> Similarly , Pakistan 's agriculture sector employs 45 per cent of the country 's labour force and contributes around 18 per cent of the GDP . In landlocked and mostly mountainous Nepal , 66 per cent of the population is engaged in agriculture , which accounts for 36 per cent of the GDP . <p> Agriculture in the mountains -- mostly subsistence and fraught with risk -- remains the mainstay of the economy in the HKH region , despite economic diversification , growth of industry , trade and the service sector . <p> In addition to physical constraints , climate change , increased outmigration , land abandonment , crop depredation by wildlife and market failures have added to farmers ' vulnerabilities in Nepal . There are news reports of vegetables rotting in fields because of transport restrictions . <p> Farmers fear for their survival as their reliance on one large market has backfired in the most unexpected manner .
