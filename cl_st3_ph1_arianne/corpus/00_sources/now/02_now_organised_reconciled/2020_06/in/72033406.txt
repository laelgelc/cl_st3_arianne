The money is used for conservation and to provide livelihood to local communities . Hunting advocates insist that trophy hunting can create incentives for conservation . However , given the widespread illegal hunting across the globe , the activity has received criticism . Changes in reproductive pattern of species , reduction in genetic diversity and adverse impact on ecosystem are some of the negative aspects associated with hunting . In fact , many countries such as Kenya , Botswana and Zambia have already banned trophy hunting .
