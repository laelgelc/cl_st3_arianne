<h> Have We Attained Swaraj <h> Have We Attained Swaraj <p> Omkar Dattatray <p> Swaraj is a very old Vedic word but comes into the vocabulary of modern India in the nineteenth century . Swaraj may loosely mean self rule but it is much more than this . In fact swaraj is not a contemporary concept but age old Vedic idea going back to ancient times . Though we have completed seventy two years of freedom but we are yet far from the swaraj . In strict sense of the term we have not attained swaraj because swaraj is not political freedom only but much more than that . Swaraj in fact is more than the political independence and therefore we have not achieved the total swaraj in Gandhian sense Swaraj is much more than self rule . It was a household name during the struggle for independence and still it is referred by all the politicians in post independent India . We got independence on august 15 , 1947 but the pertinent question is have we attained swaraj . This question may perplex manyas we have tuned to equate political independence to swaraj and it is biggest sign @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of Gandhi not only domestically but globally and we should conceptualise about him to address the contemporary issues and problems . It is very unfortunate that in the post independent India we have used , misused and even abused the Gandhi s name without following his message to a large extent and this is the tragedy . When we are able to play a constructive role at the global level , we should revisit the most influential son and messenger of India and address the contemporary issues and challenges . Here two of his things are important which are his civilisational concept of swaraj and his innovative tool of satyagraha . Satyagraha for Gandhiji was not only the tool to fight for the freedom from British but the larger concept of preparing the masses for larger reconstruction . The soul of Gandhiji 's satyagrah was preparing individuals for the larger cause of reconstruction with values of dignity of labour , sacrifice and collectivism . Swaraj was not just the replacement of the white British rulers with the Indian ones but preparing every individual to act as per his or her @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and non-violence . Satyagraha has moral physical and spiritual basis and the true satyagraha is not possible without these qualities but this message of Gandhi we forgotten In the world when we are following the path of materialistic progress and when we see progress only in terms of GDP growth the message of Gandhi is more relevant as the enlightenment of individuals becomes more important . Besides civil disobedience , non-cooperation and other methods of fighting the evil , Gandhi 's constructive programme was central for revitalizing India at the collective level . The constructive dimension of his satyagraha includes village industry and economy , removal of un-touchability and his scheme of providing education to all and Gaw sevaor charkha were crucial for inculcating a sense of equality and dignity . Community and not the state should take care of the vital functions of the society to attain complete independence . But unfortunately by injecting foreign state centric socialistic ideas in our government machinery , which has percolated down in the society has robed us of our soul-societal self sufficiency and autonomy based on voluntarism . We are still grappling @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on British paradigm . Freeing our self of this approach is the most critical part of attaining intellectual swaraj . Gandhian Swaraj can not be confined to India only but it was for whole humanity of life . But at the same time , there is no such thing as Gandhism as per the Gandhi himself . Gandhi never wanted to create a new sect but just wanted to apply the eternal truths to the problems facing the society . Taking the Gandhian legacy forward we need to apply Gandhian methods to the find solutions to the persisting problems of climate change , terrorism and growing inequalities . We should resolve to reinvest satyaghra for swaraj that takes us beyond independence and paves the way for self rule in the true sense and this will be the true tribute to Gandhi and his philosophy . Today swaraj means augmentation of India 's hard power through military prowess , economic empowerment and determined diplomacy on the one hand combined with soft power of culture , spirituality , yoga , cuisine and so on . Gandhiji 's ideas can be kept alive @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ cow conservation and protection , social equality and harmony , imparting education in one 's own language and swadeshi economy and life styles and some organisations are working on this . In brief we can say that we have to go a long way to attain swaraj in the true sense of the word as Gandhi used and meant it .
