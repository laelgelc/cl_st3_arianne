<h> Last Updated : Friday 26 June 2020 <p> The Hubballi-Ankola railway line , approved by the Karnataka government March 20 , 2020 , will lead to desertification and a water crisis , experts have warned . <p> The proposed project has already raised fears of massive damage to environment and biodiversity in the ecologically fragile Western Ghats . <p> About 80 per cent of the proposed 164-kilometre railway project cuts through the core forest lands , according to the site inspection report presented to the Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change ( MoEF&amp;CC ) . At least 995.64 hectares will be given for the project , of which 595.64 ha will involve forest land ; 184 ha wetland ; and about 190 ha dry land . <p> The amount of land that will have to be deforested will amount to aproximately 1,337 ha , according to the report . <p> " The proposed railway cuts through the heart of Sahyadri mountains that comprise half of the watershed area . This will impact the surrounding regions , " he added . <p> The ecologist said the work would probably continue for 8-10 years and break the water veins in the region . " The area receives 1,800- 2,500 mm rainfall in about two months . Heavy rains will cause flash floods and increase surface run-off of water , " he said . <p> Reddy added that as the water holding capacity of the area will drop , it will adversely affect percolation of underground water .
