The roots are immersed in water and nutrients , instead of soil . <p> It is a system where water runs in cycles in a fixed quantity . The water needs to be changed every 15 days , a feature that eliminates the need to water the plants every day . Thus , both efforts and water resources are saved in the process . <p> " There are multiple methods of hydroponic systems , but the main aim is to pump the right amount of nutrients and water directly to the roots . Compared to conventional farming , one can cut down almost 90 per cent of water usage due to water recirculation in hydroponics , " says Dhakad . <p> Controlled farming , feeding , and watering increase the yield significantly than what is grown in soil in the same area . Macro and micronutrients in the water are directly fed to the plant , helping it to grow faster . <p> One of the most significant advantages of hydroponics , says Dhakad , is the elimination of chemicals .
