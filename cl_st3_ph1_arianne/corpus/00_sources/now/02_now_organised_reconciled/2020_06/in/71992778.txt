<h> Environment ministry issues advisory to import live exotic animals <h> Last Updated : Friday 05 June 2020 <p> The Union Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change ( MoEF&amp;CC ) has issued an advisory to streamline and formalise the process of importing live exotic animals . <p> Exotic animals are those species that are found in a particular country . The advisory has defined them as those that are mentioned under the Appendices of the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora ( CITES ) , but not under the schedules of the Wild Life ( Protection ) Act , 1972 . <p> Many exotic species of birds , reptiles and amphibians are imported into India for commercial purposes . <p> " This is the first time we have given the issue proper thought , " Soumitra Dasgupta , inspector general of the MoEF&amp;CC 's wildlife division told Down to Earth . <p> " These imports were happening though the Director General of Foreign Trade ( DGFT ) , but they were beyond the purview of the forest departments and the chief wildlife wardens were n't aware about them . However , if the declaration is made after six months , documents will have to be submitted . <p> " The major reason to do this is to regulate the trade because the issue of zoonotic diseases is also linked to wildlife .
