<h> Last Updated : Monday 19 March 2018 <p> Africa accounts for 25 per cent of the world 's disease burden , but only 2 per cent of the research is focused on the continent . Only two countries in Africaallocate 15 per cent of their total budget to health care in line with the Abuja Declaration . <p> However , according to Mustapha Sidiki Kaloko , the ex-Commissioner of Social Affairs at the African Union , deficient fund is not the only problem plaguing health care sector . " You go to basement of any hospital that is operating for more than 20 years or so and there you will see CT scans lying defunct because of lack of maintenance contract with the donor agency , " says Mustapha at the Aid &amp; International Development Forum Africa Summit in Nairobi today . <p> Delving into the aspect of private-public partnership ( PPP ) in healthcare sector in Africa , Gabriel Rugalema , FAO Representative in Kenya cited example of how Sierra Leone fought Ebola crisis through partnerships . There is a huge scope for such partnerships , he says . However , there is a caveat .
