The International Energy Agency ( IEA ) published a report in 2019 titled ' Global Status Report for Buildings and Construction ' . The findings revealed that the construction sector accounted for 36 per cent of energy use and is responsible for 39 per cent of carbon emissions . <p> Additionally , other reports show that about 50 per cent of climate change and landfill waste comes from conventional construction techniques . <p> If these numbers highlight anything , it is the need for sustainable alternatives that can offset the environmental damage attributed to these sectors .
