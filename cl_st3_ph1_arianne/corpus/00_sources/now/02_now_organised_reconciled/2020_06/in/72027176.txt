As human beings we are the last to have come into existence through the phenomenon of natural evolution . This natural evolution is not static , but conscious , vibrant and laden with the virtues of renewability and enrichment . The continuous enrichment the natural evolution brings to life is reflected in the stunning variety of life in the biosphere . This is eco-yoga -- a benevolence of natural evolution to life . <p> The dazzling number of species prevailing on Earth as of today has not been the same throughout the evolutionary history of life . <p> It is assumed that 99.99 per cent of all the species that ever existed in the historical passage of natural evolution have vanished .
