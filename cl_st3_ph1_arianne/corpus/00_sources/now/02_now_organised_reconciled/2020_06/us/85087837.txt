Few would join a battle of the United States against China . But if we make it about democracy vs. kleptocracy , and the rule of law vs. the law of the jungle , the United States would have allies to spare , including among the overwhelming share of Chinese people who believe in those values , too . <p> This is a fight the United States can win , hopefully without firing a shot .
