<h> Teaching climate change ; Time to take off the masks ? ; Cultured meat is coming Letters <p> NJ recently made a historic move . We became the first state in the nation to require teaching climate change in public schools , for kindergarten through 12th grade . Young people will be learning about climate change as part of their core curriculum along with math and English . This is critical because NJ is one of the most vulnerable states to climate change in the country . We need to make sure that the education is n't just about science but the impacts of climate change and how to mitigate or adapt . <p> This is the first generation whose whole lives will be affected by climate change . Integrating it into the curriculum will help them better understand the implications and come up with solutions . This could lead to developing the next generation of climate leaders and activists . <p> We could have hundreds of Greta Thunbergs in NJ , maybe more . This is like when Earth Science was added to the core curriculum after the first Earth Day .
