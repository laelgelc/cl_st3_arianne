<h> In the Democratic primary runoff for the 24th Congressional District , here 's our recommendation <h> Kim Olson and Candace Valenzuela square off for the open seat <p> Two good candidates emerged from a crowded field for the Democratic primary runoff to replace Kenny Marchant in the 24th Congressional District . <p> We recommend retired U.S. Air Force Col. Kim Olson , 62 , over former Carrollton-Farmers Branch ISD trustee Candace Valenzuela , 36 . While they are aligned on most policy issues , Olson most exhibits the leadership experience required to find bipartisan solutions to chip away at the country 's most pressing problems . <p> The seat is open for the first time in years because Marchant , an eight-term GOP congressman , announced his retirement last year . The Democratic nominee will face former Irving mayor and Republican Beth Van Duyne in November . <p> Olson , who after retirement led the nonprofit Grace After Fire , which helps women veterans transition to civilian life , calls for incremental steps to address issues that divide even the Democratic Party , such as health care , climate change and tax code reforms . <p> For example @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ care is the ultimate goal , Olson offered a levelheaded suggestion that Congress take incremental steps such as lowering the age for Medicare to 55 , and allowing Medicare to negotiate drug prices as the Department of Veterans Affairs does . <p> The race since March has not been without controversy . At a virtual candidates ' event this month , Olson came under fire for her remarks while discussing her concerns over the militarization of police departments during recent protests over police brutality . Some perceived her strident remarks as a dismissal of concerns about looters and accused her of inciting violence . <p> We 'd caution her of the need to take a more tempered and careful approach when discussing such volatile issues . <p> Olson is a frequent Democratic candidate ; she lost the race for agriculture commissioner to incumbent Sid Miller in the last election .
