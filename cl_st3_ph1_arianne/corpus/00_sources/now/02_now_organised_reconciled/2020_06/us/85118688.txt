<p> Crews planted trees last week in St. Paul 's Payne-Phalen neighborhood using a new science-based mapping tool that specifies which areas would most benefit from more greenery . <p> " We 've brought together data to inform where we should plant , " said Marya McIntosh , conservation specialist with the Nature Conservancy , which developed the tool . <p> " There are huge discrepancies where there are trees and where there are not . We are planting in areas where the benefits of trees are most needed . " <p> The mapping tool will be made available to local governments and organizations to identify sites in the metro area most in need of tree planting . <p> The tool looks at factors such as existing tree canopy , air quality and tree loss from pestilence and disease , such as an emerald ash borer infestation .
