The agency is tasked with making maps of areas exposed to different levels of flooding . But many of the agency 's maps are outdated , and FEMA has n't measured the risks for large swaths of the U.S. <p> First Street created one national model to look at all the flood risks for a property , whether it comes from high tides , rainfall , rivers or hurricane storm surge .
