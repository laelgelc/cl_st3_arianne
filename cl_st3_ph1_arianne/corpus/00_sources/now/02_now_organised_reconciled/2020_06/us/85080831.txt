The plan includes a greenhouse gas inventory of Travis County operations and revealed that the county 's largest sources of emissions are its buildings and employee commutes . <p> " It 's a significant day for the county , " Travis County Commissioner Brigid Shea said . " The county has taken some really far-sighted steps to change course and conserve our resources more wisely and better prepare our community to be more resilient . " <p> Streamlining telecommuting and providing more support for working from home was among the top short-term priorities identified by the plan .
