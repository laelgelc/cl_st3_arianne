<h> House Democrats released their climate-change recommendations , which include incentives to encourage use of green energy . <h> Andrew Duehren <p> WASHINGTON -- A House panel dedicated to studying climate change called on Congress to adopt policies that would make the U.S. economy carbon-neutral by 2050 , recommending steps Democrats say would head off the worst consequences of a warming planet . <p> The wide-ranging plan proposes Congress mandate that every car sold by 2035 does n't emit carbon , overhaul the electricity grid by 2040 and limit fossil-fuel production on public lands , among other measures . The 547-page report is a product of the Democratic members of the House Select Committee on the Climate Crisis , which had delayed the release of its report because of the coronavirus pandemic . <p> The recommendations , which also include strengthening labor protections and updating building codes , put the House Democratic plan roughly in line with presumptive Democratic presidential nominee Joe Biden 's vision to make U.S. carbon-neutral by 2050 .
