<p> Economidis , who was appointed vice president of operations at Illawarra in 2018 , will also manage the company 's Australian manganese operations . <p> He joined from Melbourne-headquartered commercial explosives firm Orica and has previously held senior positions such as COO of Vale Coal Australia and general manager of BHP Group 's Goonyella-Riverside and Caval Ridge mines . <p> Economidis will succeed Paul Harvey who is leaving to pursue other opportunities , South32 said . <p> The Perth-based miner said Mike Fraser will remain in his role as joint COO and will be responsible for Cerro Matoso , Hillside Aluminium , Mozal Aluminium mines and its South African energy coal and manganese divisons . <p> The miner in November last year said it would sell its South Africa thermal coal business , the latest company to exit energy coal at a time when investor pressure and climate change concerns are prompting businesses to limit their exposure to fossil fuels .
