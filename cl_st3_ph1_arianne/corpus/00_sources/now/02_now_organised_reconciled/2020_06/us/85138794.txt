<p> The wildlife charity had spotted 53 of the UK 's 59 resident and regular migrant butterfly species by the end of May -- the first time that so many have been seen by the end of spring in 100 years . <p> These butterfly sightings , recorded by members of the public , show some particularly early dates for various species . The first Ringlet butterfly , for example , was reported on 24th May , but would not normally be seen before 8th June , while the White-letter Hairstreak was spotted on 29th May but typically does n't appear until 11th June . <p> Elsewhere , the Silver-washed Fritillary and White Admiral were both seen on 30th May , two weeks earlier than usual . Recent research shows that emerging earlier leads to larger populations of species that have more than one generation each year .
