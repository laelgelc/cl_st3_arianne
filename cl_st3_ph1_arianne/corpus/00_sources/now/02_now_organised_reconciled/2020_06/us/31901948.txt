Thanks in large part to strong economic conditions in 2019 , our team found total giving rose 2.4% . <p> Giving dipped slightly in 2018 , but taken together , the past three years have been the three highest years on record . When the financial markets fare well , the assets foundations hold grow ' " and that 5% of their total value gets larger too . <p> The opposite happens during downturns . <p> During the Great Recession , which officially lasted from December 2007 until June 2009 , the endowments at some foundations declined by as much as 20% .
