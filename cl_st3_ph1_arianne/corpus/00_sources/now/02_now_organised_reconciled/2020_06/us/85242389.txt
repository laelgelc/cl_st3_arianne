Coaches would create different game scenarios and we would rehearse how to react . Each player had a role and an assignment , whether it was blocking an opposing player or running a specific route . Regardless of how well or poorly a given country has responded to the crisis , it is worth noting that the entire world is dealing with the pandemic at the same time .
