<h> Planting trees helps climate change and creates jobs <p> Rarely in our nation 's history have we simultaneously managed two crises at the scale of COVID-19 and climate change . This perfect storm demands quick-start solutions that address both simultaneously . <p> One such solution is something both political parties , as well as many corporations and nonprofit organizations , supported even before the storm hit -- planting billions of trees across the United States to naturally capture carbon emissions while generating thousands of new jobs . <p> With 40 million Americans filing unemployment claims over recent weeks , this kind of federal leadership has an urgency not seen since the Great Depression . In those dark times , my organization , American Forests , worked with President Franklin Delano Roosevelt to establish the Civilian Conservation Corps , which planted 3 billion trees and employed 3 million Americans , primarily in our national forests . <p> It 's more apparent than ever that we , once again , need to turn trees into jobs . <p> Trees in America 's vast rural landscapes and densely-populated cities already are a powerful engine for our economy .
