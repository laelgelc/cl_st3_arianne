<h> How to know if she 's marriage material <p> We hear plenty about eligible men , or men who are " marriage material . " But what makes a woman " marriage material " ? This is a more pertinent question since women are the sex that bows out of marriage the most . <p> Last week , pop star Kelly Clarkson filed for divorce from her husband , Brandon Blackstock , citing " irreconcilable differences . " That may get a big yawn from those who do n't expect celebrity marriages to last , but the framework of Clarkson 's divorce is emblematic of most divorces today : The overwhelming majority are filed by wives who use the vague umbrella of " irreconcilable differences " to get out of an abuse-free marriage . <p> How can young men avoid this fate ? By paying close attention to the things a woman says and does while dating .
