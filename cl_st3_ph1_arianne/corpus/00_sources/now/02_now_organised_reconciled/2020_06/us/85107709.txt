( Luigi Costantini/Associated Press ) <p> By Associated Press <p> June 5 , 2020 at 5:27 AM EDT <p> ROME -- A quarter of Venice has been submerged by a near-record high tide for June , a time of year when such flooding is rare . <p> The water level in Italy 's lagoon city reached 116 centimeters ( 3 feet , 9.7 inches ) late Thursday , the third-highest mark for June . That level indicates that around a quarter of Venice has been flooded . <p> Venice 's sea monitoring agency blamed the unusually high late spring tide on a storm in the Atlantic that brought heavy winds and rain to northern Italy .
