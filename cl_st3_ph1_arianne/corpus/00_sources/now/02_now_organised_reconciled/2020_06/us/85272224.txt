<h> The Fed Turned to Fink 's Firm to Help Manage its Bond Buying <p> Few firms are as ubiquitous in the U.S. financial landscape as BlackRock . The world 's largest money manager , with $6.5 trillion in assets , it has the largest exchange-traded fund platform , iShares . It also has one of Wall Street 's most well-known risk-management systems , Aladdin . <p> As a measure of its influence , when the coronavirus arrived on U.S. shores . BlackRock found itself lending the Federal Reserve its expertise by running the purchase of corporate bonds and mortgages for the central bank 's economic-stimulus program . ( In the 2008 financial crisis , BlackRock helped the Fed manage the assets it had acquired from AIG and Bear Stearns . ) <h> Return to the Top CEOs of 2020 <p> Garrulous and personable , CEO Larry Fink , 67 , believes that companies should have a purpose , and he has n't been afraid to take a social stand . This year , he declared that he would put sustainability and climate change at the center of BlackRock 's investment strategy and analysis .
