<h> BFC Sets Positive Fashion Site , Partners With Parley for the Oceans <p> LONDON -- A few days ahead of staging the first all-digital London fashion showcase , the British Fashion Council is moving with the times , broadening its sustainability platform to include a social and human rights agenda that reflects the dramatic impact of the coronavirus and the anti-racism protests in the U.S. and the U.K. <p> The BFC will today unveil the web site for its Institute of Positive Fashion . It was meant to be a resource for businesses looking to ramp up their sustainability efforts , urging them to meld global collaboration with local action . The site includes information and campaigns aimed at helping fashion businesses increase their knowledge of environmental issues and to embrace sustainable best practices . <p> Given the world-altering events that have unfolded since the launch of the institute , the BFC wants to help businesses address the impact of the COVID-19 crisis and " to end prejudice , racism and injustices " against human rights .
