I have learned the history of imperialism from the perspective of a former colony . <p> At school , I was taught that Brazil was " discovered " in 1500 by the Portuguese . The fact that several million people lived there prior to that was barely mentioned in our books . We were told of a long history of brutal exploitation of our natural resources , including vast amounts of gold , rubber and timber . All this was achieved through the exploitation of our native people and African slaves -- including my own ancestors . <p> Kew Gardens .
