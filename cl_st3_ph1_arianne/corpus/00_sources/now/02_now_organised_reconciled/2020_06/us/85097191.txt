<h> Russia declares state of emergency in Siberia after oil spill <p> By <p> Daniel Uria <p> Russian President Vladimir Putin declared a state of emergency in Siberia after a fuel tank from a power plant leaked 20,000 tons of diesel fuel into the Ambarnaya River . Photo by Russian Marine Rescue Service/EPA-EFE <p> June 4 ( UPI ) -- Russian President Vladimir Putin declared a state of emergency for a region in northern Siberia after oil spilled from a power plant into a river . <p> A fuel tank at the plant in Norilsk ruptured on Friday , leaking 20,000 tons of diesel fuel into the nearby Ambarnaya River , contaminating portions of the waterway as far as 7 miles away from the site . <p> Advertisement <p> The plant 's parent company , Norilsk Nickel , said it believes thawing permafrost caused one of the tank 's pillars to collapse . <p> Permafrost thawing is linked to climate change and has caused issues including damage to roads and homes as well as disruptions to herding and agriculture throughout Siberia . <p> Greenpeace Russia issued a statement describing the incident as the largest oil spill in the history @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Valdez oil spill in 1989 . <p> The Russian Investigative Committee opened a criminal inquiry into the spill and detained the plant 's manager , Vyacheslav Starostin . <p> Putin said he would ask investigators to look into the spill and assess how officials responded . <p> He also criticized the company in a videoconference , questioning why he only learned of the spill Sunday . <p> " Why did the government agencies only find out about this two days after the fact ? " Putin said . " Are we going to learn about emergency situations from social media ? " <p> Norilsk Nickel , the world 's leading producer of nickel and palladium , was responsible for another oil spill in 2016 . <p> The company has dispatched hundreds of personnel to clean up the most recent spill , gathering 340 tons of oil .
