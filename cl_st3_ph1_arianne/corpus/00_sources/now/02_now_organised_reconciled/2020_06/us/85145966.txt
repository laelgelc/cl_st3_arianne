<h> Representative Andy Kim Endorses Evelyn Farkas for Congress <h> " From her work in the Situation Room to her leadership around the globe fighting ... Evelyn would provide a much needed voice in Congress " <h> Jun 10 , 2020 9:17 am ET <p> Kim Andy , Member Portrait ( Franmarie Metzler ) <p> Elmsford , NY -- Today , Evelyn Farkas , a senior national security appointee under President Obama , announced that Congressman Andy Kim ( D-NJ ) has endorsed her campaign to represent New York 's 17th Congressional District . Before his election to the U.S. House of Representatives in 2018 , Andy served as a foreign policy and national security advisor to General David Petreus and President Obama . " I 'm proud to endorse Evelyn Farkas for Congress , " Andy said . " Evelyn is a career public servant who knows that good government starts with good people in government . From her work in the White House Situation Room to her leadership around the globe fighting for human rights , defending democracy , and protecting against terrorism , Evelyn would provide a much needed voice in Congress . As a proud @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ refugees , Evelyn understands what is at stake at home and around the globe right now . As she has done her entire career , she would serve her constituents and her country with honor and integrity . " " Andy is part of the wave of experienced national security professionals who , like me , decided to run for Congress in response to the unprecedented assault on our liberties under Trump 's Republican party , " Farkas said . " Andy has fought effectively for his district and understands firsthand we need experienced leaders like myself in Congress to address America 's most pressing challenges , from dismantling systemically racist systems to reversing the trend of climate change and rebuilding in the wake of the coronavirus pandemic . " <p> Andy Kim is the latest to join a double-digit group of sitting congressional representatives , including Reps . Swalwell , Escobar , and Moulton , to endorse Farkas .
