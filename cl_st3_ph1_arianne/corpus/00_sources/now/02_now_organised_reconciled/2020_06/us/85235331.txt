<h> Jun 22 , 2020 5:25 pm ET <p> Green Sanctuary , Climate Justice Group of the Unitarian Universalist Church of Reading , encourages any customer of the Reading Municipal Light Department ( RMLD ) considering an investment in rooftop solar panels to apply for an RMLD solar rebate by Dec 30 , 2020 . <p> Why ? <p> RMLD is currently offering unusually generous solar rebates , for which you must apply by 12/30/2020 . Installation must be complete by 12/30/2021 . <p> The Federal tax credit for a solar install is a whopping 26% this year , but is scheduled to decrease in 2021 . <p> Massachusetts tax credit for a solar install is 15% , up to a max of $1,000 . <p> RMLD has partnered with the Massachusetts Department of Energy Resources ( DOER ) to offer customers a limited-time rebate for the installation of solar systems that are 25 kilowatts ( kW ) or less in size . The amount of the rebate will be based on the size of the system installed , and equates to $1.20 per watt .
