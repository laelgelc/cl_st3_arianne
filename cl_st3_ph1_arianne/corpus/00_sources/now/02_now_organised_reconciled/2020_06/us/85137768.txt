<h> How to Get Involved , Politically <p> The protests over the death of George Floyd in Minneapolis , as well as other recent high-profile killings of African-Americans , have strengthened a mood of political activism in the United States . <p> Right now , protests against racism and police brutality are taking place across the country . But you might want to act on climate change , gun violence or other issues that feel urgent and important to you . There are many ways to get involved politically , and taking to the streets is just one of them .
