All the work gravitates around the question of how biodiversity originated and how it is reshaped by changes in the environment . Things like climate change , sea level rise , deforestation , colonization and events like hurricanes and tornadoes -- they 're all changing the environment . We think about what the environment looked like before a change happened , including the species that were there and the traits they possessed , and how those things changed after an environmental perturbation . <p> What I 'm really excited about with this research is understanding how our present day ecosystems emerged by looking at the fossil record . The fossil record gives us information about past changes but also gives us insight into how those communities might change in the future .
