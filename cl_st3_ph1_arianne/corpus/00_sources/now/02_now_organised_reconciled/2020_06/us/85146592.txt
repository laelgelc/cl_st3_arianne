<h> Who Is Joe Biden ? <p> Former Vice President Joe Biden is the presumptive Democratic nominee to take on President Trump in November . <p> Biden , the eldest of four children , was born in 1942 . He is 77 years old . Until ten years old , Biden lived in Scranton , Pennsylvania . His family moved to Claymont , Delaware in the early 1950s to look for better work ; Biden considers Delaware his home state . <p> About his youth , Biden has said , " During my adolescent and college years , men and women were changing the country -- Martin Luther King , Jr. , John F. Kennedy , Robert Kennedy -- and I was swept up in their eloquence , their conviction , the sheer size of their improbable dreams . " <p> Biden studied , history , political science , and law . At just 29 years old , he became one of the youngest people ever elected to the United States Senate . <p> His life is often said to be marred by tragedy . His wife and daughter died in a car crash but his two sons , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Biden began a daily 120-mile pilgrimage to Washington DC and from Wilmington , Delaware , where he lived and raised his sons . <p> Biden is a six-term Senator . He also served as the Vice President of the United States from 2008 to 2016 . <p> The former VP is running on a moderate Democratic platform where his main issues are health care , climate change , and the economy . He often talks about returning to America during the Obama-era.
