The news comes as the Environmental Protection Agency ( EPA ) moves to rollback safeguards against methane emissions . <p> Canadian company GHGSat has been collaborating with the European Space Agency ( ESA ) since early 2019 to investigate possible methane emission hotspots by using data from the Copernicus Sentinel-5P satellite .
