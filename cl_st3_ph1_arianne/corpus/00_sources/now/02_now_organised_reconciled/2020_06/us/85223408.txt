<h> AOC and The Justice Democrats : The Future Is Now for the Democratic Party ? <p> In 2017 , a group of staffers from Bernie Sanders 's 2016 campaign started the Justice Democrats , a progressive group loosely aligned with the Democratic Party . Since then , the Justice Democrats -- along with an array of other left-leaning progressive groups -- have become a significant presence in Democratic primary contests , reflecting both generational and establishment-insurgent fissures within the party . <p> The Justice Democrats recruited 12 candidates to run for office in 2018 and endorsed 66 . Only one recruit , Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez ( AOC ) , won election to Congress from New York , toppling the third-ranking Democrat in the House , Joe Crowley . Today , seven members in Congress identify as Justice Democrats , including five prominent women -- Ayanna Pressley ( MA-7 ) , Rashida Tlaib ( MI-13 ) , Pramila Jayapal ( WA-7 ) , Ilhan Omar ( MN-5 ) , and Ocasio-Cortez ( NY-14 ) . At the congressional level thus far this cycle , only one Justice Democrats-endorsed candidate , Marie Newman , has defeated a Democratic incumbent -- Dan Lipinski in Illinois . <p> These groups are benefitting from a substantial advantage Democrats have over Republicans in party identification among the young . In Pew 's latest cumulative summary of data collected in 2018 and 2019 , 54 percent of millennials identified as or leaned to the Democratic Party , while only 38 percent identified or leaned to the GOP . <p> Neither party is unfamiliar with challenges from its extremes , and the Justice Democrats and its sister organization , the Sunrise Democrats , are pulling the Democratic Party leftward . The Justice Democrats focus on a broader platform than the climate-centered Sunrise Democrats , including the Green New Deal , Medicare for All , free public college , campaign finance , and criminal justice reform -- including abolishing ICE and the death penalty . There are many cross endorsements between the two groups . Brand New Congress , founded in 2016 , also claims Ocasio-Cortez . Another group , Our Revolution , concentrates on local races . <p> The Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee 's vow to break ties with pollsters and political consultants who worked for primary challengers has n't slowed the momentum of challenges from the left .
