<p> Democrats on a House committee voted late Thursday to send a far-reaching transportation bill to the full House , a key milestone in a process that faces an uncertain future . <p> The five-year , nearly $500 billion funding bill passed the House Transportation Committee after months of drafting and two arduous days of debate and amendments , with some members present at the Capitol and others patched in via video conference because of coronavirus concerns . <p> The measure , the Invest in America Act , is meant to replace the law authorizing spending on highways , transit and other transportation programs , which expires at the end of September . <p> Backers said the bill that emerged from the committee represents a departure from previous surface transportation reauthorization legislation .
