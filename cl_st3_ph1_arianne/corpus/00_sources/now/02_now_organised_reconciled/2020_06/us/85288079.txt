And in both Brazil and the United States , it 's to extract more minerals and fossil fuels . <p> These are some of the reasons that governments have given for shrinking or eliminating protected areas in recent months , even as a global pandemic has limited the public 's ability to participate in these decisions . <p> The cruel irony is that , by scaling back protected areas , governments are silently weakening policies that can help minimize the risk of future pandemics . <p> Governments must reverse course on these rollbacks , and instead invest in scaling up protections , for the environment and our health . <p> Protected areas are some of the best investments for nature and people that we can make . They can safeguard biodiversity , reduce deforestation and sequester carbon . They can also help to improve incomes and health outcomes in local communities .
