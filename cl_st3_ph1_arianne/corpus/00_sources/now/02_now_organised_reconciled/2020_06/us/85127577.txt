The artist scorned the New Deal art programs of the thirties as sops to mediocrity . Interrupting a vacation on Cape Cod in 1940 , he returned to New York to register to vote in order to cast his ballot against Franklin Roosevelt . The orientation leaves no mark in his work that I can detect -- Hopper 's artistic passion disallowed the trivia of opinionating -- but it chimes with a wary individualism that could seem to refuse agreement about practically everything with almost everybody except his painter wife , Josephine Nivison .
