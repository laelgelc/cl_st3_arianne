They had no expectations of becoming pioneers . <p> The Stallmers ' flock has grown by eight chickens . <p> It puts the family at the forefront of the city 's " Cleaner Greener Cohoes " initiative , living life as urban farmers with a chicken coop in the side yard and eager anticipation of the first fresh eggs for a brood that ca n't get enough eggs to eat . <p> " We fell in love with the idea , " Jeff Stallmer said @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the new city code regulating chickens , bees and community gardens . <p> After seeing the shortage of some food items , including eggs , during the early days of the coronavirus pandemic , Caroline Stallmer said , " The idea of having your own food supply is good . " <p> The Stallmer chicken flock has captured the hearts of the three youngest children , Josie , Oliver and Oscar , who are fascinated the the birds . They 're still waiting for the chickens they 've raised from chicks to lay their first eggs . <p> Getting residents interested in urban farming is part of the " Cleaner Greener Cohoes " program Mayor Bill Keeler unveiled in February . It aims to make this **28;1502;TOOLONG hub at the confluence of the Hudson and Mohawk rivers more environmentally prepared to deal with climate change . <p> The new law adopted by the City Council in April set the guidelines for chickens , bees and community gardens in an effort to promote green space and healthy eating . It is also intended to provide an insight into farming and nature @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ need a 5,000-square-foot lot , which is slightly more than a tenth of an acre . So far , five applications have been filed for chickens and two for bees . <p> The lot-size requirements mean some areas in the city such as downtown will not be big enough for bees or chickens . <p> Other communities are allowing similar efforts . Last year , Albany 's Common Council backed a measure to let residents raise chickens in their yards . <p> " I always thought they were interesting . I grew up across from a farm in Cohoes , " said Keeler . He has 14 chickens in two coops and prefers his eggs scrambled . <p> Besides the space , homeowners have to have a covered , ventilated , predator-proof chicken house . The coops must be placed at least 10 feet from the property lines and the chickens must be kept inside from sunset to sunrise . Owners are required to make sure the chickens always have food and water and that the food is kept @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ live in a healthy , clean environment . <p> The program has a cardinal rule and it is written in boldface on the city 's " Farming in Cohoes " website page : " and remember , no roosters ! " Crowing roosters lead to noise complaints , which the city wants to avoid . <p> The city is working on the selection of spots for community gardens . Plans call for transforming vacant lots that are too small to build on into garden spots . They will also serve as pollinator gardens for bees , hummingbirds , butterflies and other creatures that spur pollination . <p> The Stallmers like the lessons their children are learning from having the chickens outside the house . Caroline Stallmer said it keeps them active , supplies interaction with living things and provides an alternative to being online .
