But these policies also have dire global implications . With its abundant plant life , the Arctic , for tens of thousands of years , was a carbon sink for the rest of the planet . Permafrost across the Arctic and boreal regions contains between 1.46 trillion and 1.6 trillion tons of organic carbon , which is almost twice the amount present in the atmosphere today . This carbon includes hidden pouches of ancient methane , plus long-frozen organic matter ( akin to a frozen compost pile ) that can release carbon and methane once microbial life awakens in the warming ground .
