The fund will help Amazon and other companies adhere to The Climate Pledge initiative it started in September 2019 . That pledge committed the company , and others that sign onto it , to becoming carbon neutral by 2040 . <p> To become carbon neutral , companies need tools to be able to reduce , offset , or capture all the planet-heating pollution they produce . The $2 billion will go toward advancements in transportation and logistics , energy generation and storage , manufacturing and materials , food and agriculture , and reducing or reusing waste . That 's compared to just over 44 million metric tons it emitted in 2018 , which was the first time it disclosed its carbon footprint.
