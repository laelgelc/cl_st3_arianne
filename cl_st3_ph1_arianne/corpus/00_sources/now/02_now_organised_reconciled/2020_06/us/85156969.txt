<h> US Agency Proposes Financing of Nuclear Power Exports <p> WASHINGTON - A U.S. development agency proposed lifting restrictions that bar the financing of advanced nuclear energy projects abroad , a move the Trump administration hopes will help the industry compete with state-owned companies in China and Russia . <p> The U.S. International Development Finance Corp. , or DFC , late Wednesday opened a 30-day comment period on the proposal . The idea was included in the Trump administration 's Nuclear Fuel Working Group report , released in April , on ways to modernize nuclear energy policy . <p> The DFC said the proposed change could help deliver secure power to developing countries and that new U.S. technologies , such as small modular reactors and microreactors , may be well-suited for them . <p> The DFC , which replaced the Overseas Private Investment Corp. , launched in January with a $60 billion budget . But nonproliferation experts caution that the plants and their supply chains could become targets of attack .
