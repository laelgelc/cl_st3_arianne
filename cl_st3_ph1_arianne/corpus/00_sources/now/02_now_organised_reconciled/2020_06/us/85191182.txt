Houston will soon be the energy transition capital of the world , Greentown Labs CEO Emily Reichert said during the announcement downtown . <p> The lab , which grew from a small group of startups nine years ago near the Massachusetts Institute of Technology in Cambridge , will provide space and funding to startups on the road to commercialization . The nascent ventures are expected to focus on carbon capture , hydrogen fuel technology , battery storage and other clean energy sources . The Massachusetts lab has helped launch 280 companies . <p> A site for Greentown Labs Houston was n't disclosed , but it will be near downtown , Reichert said .
