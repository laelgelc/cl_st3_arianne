In the 19th century , rail was the most practical way to travel overland between distant cities ( horse-pulled vehicles being far slower ) . After the Civil War , the network of tracks grew to span the contiguous United States and territories , with stops in every major settlement . But those railroads , including the tracks themselves , were privately owned .
