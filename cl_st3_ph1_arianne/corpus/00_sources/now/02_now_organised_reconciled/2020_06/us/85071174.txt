<p> States have less say in issuing Clean Water Act permits under an EPA rule finalized Monday as the Trump administration and conservative lawmakers seek to speed energy projects . <p> The rule limits the scope of environmental reviews that states conduct before issuing permits for projects such as pipelines and hydropower plants . It directs states to narrow their reviews to water quality issues and creates a one-year deadline for states to make decisions . <p> EPA proposed the rule in August following directions from President Donald Trump 's " Promoting Energy Infrastructure and Economic Growth " executive order .
