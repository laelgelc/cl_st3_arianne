They will need a big victory there to take control -- and a gigantic landslide if they want to be able to break a filibuster , which requires 60 votes . Each state gets two senators regardless of population -- thus a resident of Wyoming has nearly 70 times the Senate representation as California -- and less populated , over-represented states have trended Republican over the last couple decades . The median state in Senate terms , North Carolina , leans 3 points to the GOP . <p> The Senate filibuster further entrenches this bias . Senators from just the 21 most conservative states can bottle up almost all legislation -- the major exception for this being reconciliation bills , a complex procedure that can only be done once a year , and contains limits on what can be included .
