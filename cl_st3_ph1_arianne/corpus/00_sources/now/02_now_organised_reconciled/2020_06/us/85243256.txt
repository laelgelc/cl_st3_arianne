Their record turnout in the 2018 midterm elections , signs of political activism , and a handful of issues being used as a rallying cry , including soaring college debt , health care and climate change , stand as evidence . <p> Millennial voting nearly doubled between 2014 and 2018 -- from 22% to 42% -- according to demographer Richard Fry at the Pew Research Center in Washington .
