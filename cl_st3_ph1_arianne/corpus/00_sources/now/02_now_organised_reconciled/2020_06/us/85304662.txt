These design principles -- social , short and positive- are useful for all ages and for many difficult conversations , including climate change . <p> Social means focused on people or places we care about . Short means focusing on a human-scale time frame ( e.g. , 2-3 generations or 50-75 years ) . Positive means showing people actions that they can take once they have identified a concern . <h> It Sounds Good But Does It Work ? <p> Using these principles to frame a conversation sounds good , but does it work ? <p> In a recent study , we tested out two strategies for framing the climate conversation , compared to a control condition , for adults 18 and older ( Wickersham et al. , 2020 ) . The second strategy asked participants a simple question : How do you want to be remembered ?
