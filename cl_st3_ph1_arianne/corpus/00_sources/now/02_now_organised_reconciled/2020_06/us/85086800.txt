<h> Rivers ensure one-third of carbon from fires is stored in the ocean <p> Rivers help carry black carbon from forest fires into the oceans , where the carbon is stored for long periods of time . Photo by UEA <p> June 3 ( UPI ) -- Rivers help carry as much as a third of the black carbon generated by wildfires to the ocean , according to new research . <p> For the study , scientists analyzed the dissolved carbon flowing through 78 rivers located all over the world .
