In the 1890s , a trickle of intrepid homesteaders began arriving here . They battled the heat and mosquitoes to carve farmland out of limestone , dense tropical forests , thickets of saw grass and marshes . The Everglades had to be drained , canals had to be dug , the ground scarified to break up the limestone . <p> In other words , farming here has never been easy . There are pests , hurricanes , too much or too little rain and cheap imports to compete against . <p> But today , Ms. No ? told me , it 's almost impossible for small farmers to make a living and keep up with the efficiency of factory farms .
