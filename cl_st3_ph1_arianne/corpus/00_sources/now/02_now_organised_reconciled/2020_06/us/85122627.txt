<p> Throughout the COVID-19 pandemic , experts and observers have predicted there will be some type of " new normal " on the other side . <p> Exactly how that will look is unclear , but an undeniable trend so far is the acceleration around e-commerce . <p> For fashion brands trying to reach the lucrative Generation Z market , which is on course to be the largest consumer population in the next decade , that particular trend , along with a few others , is something they would or should have been pivoting to for some time . <p> Gen Zers , generally defined as anyone born from 1995 to 2012 , are digital natives . They are coming of age with access to an advanced internet-based global marketplace for products and services , and many are thinking about being entrepreneurs themselves or have parents nudging them in that direction . <p> At a time when conversations about climate change and racism are at the forefront , they also have an innate sense of social justice . <p> You might say it is Gen Z 's world and brands are just living in it .
