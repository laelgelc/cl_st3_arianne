<h> Letter : Climate action is the opposite of sacrifice <p> I applaud Galen Heins'attempt to understand the back-to-work protesters ( " Selfishness hinders social responsibility , " May 10 ) . In these disputatious times , we could all use more understanding . <p> I would like to expand on what Heins says in order to illustrate ways we can move beyond this time of individual sacrifice for the collective good to slow the spread of COVID-19 , and continue to work together for the good of our planet . Shutting down the economy has entailed great sacrifice . Retooling our society to escape the tyranny of the fossil fuel industry is the opposite of sacrifice . <p> Consider the new jobs created by the construction and maintenance of renewable energy facilities . Consider also the benefits of cleaner air -- benefits most of us began to notice as the shutdown went into effect . Consider enhanced public transportation , more electric vehicle charging stations , pedestrian-friendly towns and cities , bike paths , local and sustainable farming . Just about every step we need to take to address climate change will in some way enrich our lives @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Cuomo must continue to hold fast to his climate goals . Sen. Charles Schumer must act to oppose oil bailouts in Congress . Our leaders must focus on measures that protect and sustain our communities while also mitigating the upcoming climate disasters . This is not sacrifice but enlightened self-interest .
