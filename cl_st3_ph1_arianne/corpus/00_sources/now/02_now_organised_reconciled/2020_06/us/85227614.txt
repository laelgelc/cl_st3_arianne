<h> Brothas Be Heard offers a platform for Black men to share their stories and correct misconceptions <p> Andre Keitt , a storyteller and teaching artist from Hartford , will participate in Brothas Be Heard , a Zoom event geared toward empowering men in the Black community . ( Courtesy Andre Keitt ) <p> Andre Keitt is a Black storyteller and teaching artist -- not a doctor -- but he 's using his art to address what he calls the three pandemics facing the United States : " the White House , police and domestic brutality of the Black race , and COVID-19 , " Keitt said . " Every chance I can find to dispel ... negative vs. positive Black male imagery , is a rich opportunity . " <p> Keitt , who has entertained and educated children throughout Hartford , regionally and nationally , under his professional name , the Greatheart Griot , is one of several speakers who will participate in Brothas Be Heard : Honoring Black Men on June 20 . The Zoom event encourages men to " speak your mind , share your story , express yourself in any form .
