<p> It 's time once again for Mad Cow Theatre 's annual Science Play Festival -- can you believe it will be the seventh time the downtown Orlando theater celebrates this wonderful intersection of art and science ? <p> Advertisement <p> This year , as medical scientists the world over work on finding ways to control the coronavirus , the theater will appropriately use the science of technology to present its festival .
