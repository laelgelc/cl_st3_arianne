<p> The UK has been left " dangerously dependent " on just two EU countries for its fresh vegetable imports , a new study on the impact of the coronavirus pandemic on the UK food system has revealed . <p> The study , published in Nature Food , also reveals that insufficient capacity in domestic food production , just-in-time supply chains and Brexit-related labour market challenges have all resulted in a weakened UK food system . <p> Building diversity and collaboration in the food system is essential for resilience in the COVID-19 recovery , the authors of the University of York study say . <p> The UK imports almost half of its food and 84% of its fresh fruit , and is heavily reliant on EU countries for vegetables and salads . <p> In particular , the UK is reliant on the Dover Strait maritime route and ferry services between Dover and Calais .
