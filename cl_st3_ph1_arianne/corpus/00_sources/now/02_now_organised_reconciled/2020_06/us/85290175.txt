<h> The View from Here : Revolution on Bastille Day ? <p> The last time Maine Democrats went to the polls they were not ready for a revolution . <p> On March 3 , moderate Joe Biden won the Maine presidential primary without ever setting foot in the state or spending a dime on advertising . He beat progressives Bernie Sanders and Elizabeth Warren , who did have well-organized campaigns here , saying their proposals on health care and the economy were too costly and would turn off voters in a general election . <h> Related <p> But March 3 seems like a long time ago . It was a pandemic ago . An economic collapse ago . A massive anti-racist uprising ago . <p> The next time that Maine Democrats vote will be July 14 , Bastille Day , and we 'll find out whether the appetite for revolution has changed . <p> On the ballot is a three-way primary for U.S. Senate among Maine House Speaker Sara Gideon of Freeport ; Betsy Sweet of Hallowell , an activist and social services lobbyist , and Bre Kidman of Saco , a criminal defense attorney . <p> At stake @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Susan Collins , who is more vulnerable than at any time in her career . She has all the outward signs of success -- she raised a record $14 million by the March reporting deadline , and has the heavyweight endorsements of Planned Parenthood , EMILY 's List and the Democratic Senatorial Campaign Committee . <p> There may not be any public polls , but the Republicans must have some , and the way that they are attacking Gideon indicates that they see her as the likely nominee .
