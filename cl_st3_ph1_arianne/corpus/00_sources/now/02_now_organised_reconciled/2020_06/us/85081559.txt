<p> In a shift that significantly expands public access to some of California 's most scenic places , parking lots at half of California 's 280 state parks , beaches and historic sites have now reopened after being closed for more than two months due to the coronavirus pandemic . <p> Meanwhile , several high profile state parks that had been completely closed also have reopened in recent days , including Big Basin Redwoods and Castle Rock in the Santa Cruz Mountains , which began welcoming visitors again on Friday . <p> Big Basin , set aside as California 's first state park in 1902 , is known for its old-growth redwood trees that tower more than 300 feet tall . About 150 of the 300 parking spaces at the park are now available for daily use . If they fill , no more cars will be allowed in , rangers say . <p> Campgrounds and the visitor center remain closed at Big Basin , as they are at all state parks still . But restrooms are open , along with trails , and the park store . Rangers are wearing masks . " We are working on a phased approach to increase access , but only when compliance with state and local health ordinances can be achieved .
