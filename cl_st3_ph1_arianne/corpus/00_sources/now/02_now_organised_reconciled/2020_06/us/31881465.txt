<p> To make climate scenarios work for decision-makers , an international team of researchers developed a comprehensive interactive online platform . It is the first of its kind to provide the tools to use those scenarios - from climate impacts to mitigation and energy options - to a broader public beyond science . The scenarios help policy makers and businesses , finance actors and civil society alike to assess the threat of global warming and ways to limit it . <p> " Climate scenarios are powerful tools that allow us to explore possible climate futures and how they are shaped by our collective actions - which is why we want to enable all kinds of decision-makers to actually make use of them , " says Elmar Kriegler from the Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research ( PIK ) who leads the SENSES consortium that collectively developed the online platform . " Science has been developing and using climate scenarios for many years , based on computer simulations , yet they 're admittedly a rather complicated matter and results are scattered in all too many scientific publications .
