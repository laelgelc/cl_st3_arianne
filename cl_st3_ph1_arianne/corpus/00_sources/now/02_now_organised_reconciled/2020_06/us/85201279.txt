<p> Gucci had the perfect person in mind when launching their new sustainable line : Jane Fonda . <p> The climate change activist is the new face of the luxury brand 's latest campaign . Gucci also announced Tuesday that accompanying Fonda , is " Old Town Road " artist and fashion icon Lil Nas X. <p> The campaign is dubbed " Gucci Off The Grid , " and it boasts products made from recyclable and organic materials in order to cut down on pollution . The collection is the first line produced by the brand 's new sustainable arm , Gucci Circular Lines . <p> The ad featuring the new products shows Lil Nas X and Fonda shunning city life in order to build their own home made out of twigs , leaves and other sustainable objects .
