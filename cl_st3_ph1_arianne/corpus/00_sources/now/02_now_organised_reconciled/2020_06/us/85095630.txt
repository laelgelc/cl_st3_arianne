<h> The Perfection of Things as They Are <h> A Conversation With Poet Jane Hirshfield <p> Jane Hirshfield is a triple threat . Internationally renowned as an award-winning poet , she is also a stunning prose stylist and lay-ordained Zen practitioner whose brings decades of intensive dharma practice to her prodigious oeuvre . Her work has appeared in The New Yorker , The Atlantic , The New York Times , The New York Review of Books , Orion , Tricycle , and Poetry , as well as ten editions of the Best American Poetry . Among Hirshfield 's many honors , she was inducted in 2019 into the American Academy of Arts &amp; Sciences and has received fellowships from the Guggenheim and Rockefeller Foundations . A lay-ordained Zen practitioner , she has just published her ninth book of poems , Ledger ( Knopf , 2020 ) and has seen a number of her recent poems become anthems of activism , both in climate and in political realms .
