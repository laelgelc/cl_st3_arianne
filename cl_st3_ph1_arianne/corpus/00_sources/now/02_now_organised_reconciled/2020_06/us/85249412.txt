In her book The Entrepreneurial State , Mazzucato looked at examples of technology like the smartphone and the fact that core tech that enabled it -- the internet , touchscreens , GPS , voice control -- came from public investments and scientific research , not entrepreneurs . She argues that governments need to see their role differently , focusing on co-creating value with businesses , rather than only intervening if the market fails , and investing more in innovation . Investments could be structured differently so the government can benefit rather than only taking risk , for instance .
