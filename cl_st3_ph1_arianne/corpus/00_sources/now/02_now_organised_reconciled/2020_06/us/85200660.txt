<p> I made a point of getting to know Philippe and his wife Ashlan after I had watched them in interviews and on their TV shows . I was impressed by their profound passion for saving the environment and their unique way of involving not just the general public but , more important , the youth . <p> They know that our best chance for reversing the terrible course humanity is on with regard to climate change and the ocean is to get the next generation involved through their schools and educators . <p> I am proud to call them friends . Their commitment to positive environmental change and the incredible difference they are making for the health of our planet and our children 's future is an inspiration to us all . <p> Cary Elwes is an actor and producer . <p> This story appears in the Summer 2020 issue of Town &amp; Country .
