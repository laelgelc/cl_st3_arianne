<h> TMC NEWS <h> TMCNET eNEWSLETTER SIGNUP <p> " Nationscape Insights " Launched by Democracy Fund and UCLA in Partnership with USA TODAY <p> June 08 , 2020 <h> " Nationscape Insights " Launched by Democracy Fund and UCLA in Partnership with USA TODAY <p> It is more critical than ever that the diverse views and voices of all Americans are heard . In an effort to better understand opinions across the country on the most important policy issues , Democracy Fund and UCLA are launching " Nationscape Insights " in partnership with USA TODAY . The interactive data dashboard will be updated weekly with findings from the 500,000 interviews conducted by the Democracy Fund + UCLA Nationscape project leading up to the 2020 election . The dashboard builds on the group 's successful collaboration covering issues including the presidential primaries , COVID-19 , the economic crisis , and recent protests across the country . The survey is fielded by Lucid . <p> Nationscape Insights offers immediate , in-depth access to the Democracy Fund + UCLA Nationscape project , one of the largest public opinion surveys ever conducted .
