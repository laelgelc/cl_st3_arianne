<p> HOOFDDORP , Netherlands , June 10 , 2020 /PRNewswire/ -- Delta , a global leader in power and thermal management solutions , announced today a collaboration with the Groupe PSA to foster e-mobility across Europe and beyond . Delta 's EV charging infrastructure solutions will be made available via the Group 's specially selected EV charger installation partner list .
