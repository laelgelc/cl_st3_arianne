Spring trainings , World Series and all-star games -- I was there , the envy of the other kids on the playground . I enjoyed every minute of it , attending many , many Cavaliers games at the old Richfield Coliseum near my hometown of Cleveland . <p> I rooted against the Bulls and White Sox in particular and still do to this day . I was there when Michael Jordan and company eliminated the Cavs from the 1992 Eastern Conference finals , a series mentioned in " The Last Dance " documentary .
