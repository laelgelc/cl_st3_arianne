Jenkins , Jr . <p> Most of what left-wing film producer Michael Moore has to say about green energy in his latest film , " Planet of the Humans , " is right : These technologies have nasty environmental impacts as do all products of industrial civilization . They are not going to make a dent in CO2 . <p> A parade of experts is tapped to reinforce a point known to anybody who has looked at the electric grid . Because of their intermittency , wind and solar require backup from coal and gas . An engineer introducing a solar array in Lansing , Mich. , is heard saying it will support 10 houses -- to support the whole city would require 15 square miles of panels , or nearly half the town , and still need the town 's local coal plant . <p> Neither Netflix nor any paid streaming service picked up the film , so Mr. Moore offered it free on YouTube , until YouTube took it down under an onslaught of green complaint and one flimsy copyright gripe about a week ago .
