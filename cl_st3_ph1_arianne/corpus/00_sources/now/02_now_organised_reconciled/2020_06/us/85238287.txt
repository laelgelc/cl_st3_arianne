<h> Students for Melanie : Youth get involved in local politics <h> Young people support D'Arrigo 's platform on health care , Green New Deal , and Black Lives Matter <h> Jun 22 , 2020 2:28 pm ET Updated Jun 22 , 2020 2:57 pm ET <p> ( Democratic congressional candidate Melanie D'Arrigo ) <p> Youth participation in politics is a potent instrument for change . In recent years , young people collectively have risen up against various injustices and thereby instigated reforms that are much-needed in our society . <p> When 17 people were killed in the 2018 shooting at Marjory Stoneman Douglas High School , young people across the country coalesced in the March for Our Lives Movement . This national push for gun control reform , organized by survivors from the Parkland , FL high school , has been the most robust yet . In response to the imminent perils of climate change , young people banded together and formed the Sunrise Movement , an organization that has brought the Green New Deal into the mainstream of U.S. politics . The Black Lives Matter movement , whose young founders were galvanized by the acquittal of George @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Trayvon Martin , is especially salient today . Spurred by the egregious murders of black Americans like Ahmaud Arbery , Breonna Taylor , and George Floyd , the Black Lives Matter movement has gained unprecedented national traction and has already engendered a spate of policy reforms . <p> Young people are getting involved here on Long Island , too . I am a high school student from Port Washington and a member of the youth coalition Students for Melanie , an organization founded by rising Holy Trinity High School senior Leo Cronan . The group was formed to bring young voices into local politics as well as support the campaign of Congressional candidate Melanie D'Arrigo who is competing in the Democratic primary tomorrow , June 23rd . I joined the group after learning about Melanie 's compelling policy platform . She is a progressive candidate who supports equitable policies like Medicare for All , the Green New Deal , reforming ICE , repeal of the Hyde Amendment , and ending qualified immunity for police officers , among others . These policies will help uplift marginalized people and affirm the gospel @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ commitment to her ideals and constituents through her rejection of corporate PAC donations . Melanie stands in contrast to our current Representative Tom Suozzi who has taken over one million dollars in corporate PAC donations and has voted against the Democratic caucus to roll back Obama-era banking regulations and expand ICE detention centers at the border . <p> Other young people have likewise flocked to support Melanie 's campaign . Students for Melanie boasts membership by more than fifty students across NY 's 3rd Congressional district . Our group is effective in mobilizing young supporters of Melanie , who constitute the heart of her base . We participate in collective phone banking , which inspires a sense of camaraderie that is heartening as we cold-call strangers to spread the word about Melanie 's candidacy ; we make engagement in politics energizing and fun through lighthearted events like virtual campaign-related bingos and proms ; we keep abreast of current events by hosting virtual forums with Melanie and other special guests , as well as chapter meetings where we discuss the latest developments in politics among ourselves ; our social media presence @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 's campaign . <p> The vigorous participation that Students for Melanie has amassed is even more affecting considering the backdrop of a pandemic that has at large dampened political action , especially in local races . I joined partly because I wanted to become more civically active , and I am gladdened by the other young people who Melanie 's candidacy impelled to feel the same . Youth activism is not only healthy for our public discourse , but represents an urgent call to do better in a moment we know we can .
