<h> Planting New Forests Can Do More Harm Than Good , New Study Warns <h> KEY POINTS <p> Studies revealed that programs that aim to plant entire forests might be harmful <p> Planting entire forests can affect the biodiversity of native trees <p> The amount of carbon trees can absorb has been overestimated <p> New studies have revealed that programs aimed at encouraging people to plant more trees or entire forests can actually do more harm than good . Aside from reducing the biodiversity of native trees , these kinds of programs also tend to overestimate the amount of carbon trees can absorb . <p> The studies were conducted by two different international teams of researchers . Both were published on Monday ( June 22 ) in the journal Nature Sustainability . <p> Planting trees has always been regarded as a low-cost solution to global warming . This idea is mainly due to studies highlighting the ability to absorb carbon in the air . <p> As a result , various programs were established to urge groups and even entire countries to plant trees . One of these , known as the Bonn Challenge , is urging countries to plant @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of deforested land by 2030 . <p> In most cases , these kinds of programs and challenges offer financial incentives to encourage people to plant more trees . For instance , the authors of one of the studies learned that in Chile , landowners were subsided from 1974 to 2012 to fill their lands with new trees in order to curb the effects of deforestation . <p> Although the program was able to increase the number of trees in the country , researchers noticed a decrease in biodiversity . Due to the financial incentives involved , many landowners replaced native forests in the lands with their own tree plantations . Most of the trees in these plantations came from the same species . <p> The researchers also learned that the decrease in biodiversity also lessened the ability of the trees in the country to absorb more carbon . <p> " If policies to incentivize tree plantations are poorly designed or poorly enforced , there is a high risk of not only wasting public money but also releasing more carbon and losing biodiversity , " Eric Lmbin of Stanford University , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to BBC . <p> " That 's the exact opposite of what these policies are aiming for , " he added . <p> Aside from its effect on biodiversity , the researchers also learned that the programs tend to overestimate the amount of carbon new tree plantations can absorb . <p> As noted by the researchers , this data would depend on the amount of carbon already present in the soil where the trees will be planted . <p> Based on their findings , the researchers noted that relying solely on planting entire forests might not be enough to solve the issues related to climate change . <p> " We hope that people can understand that afforestation practices are not one single thing , " Anping Chen from the Colorado State University said .
