<p> DALLAS &amp; BRISBANE , Australia-- ( BUSINESS WIRE ) --Adani Green Energy Limited ( AGEL , NSE : ADANIGREEN ) has won the first of its kind manufacturing linked solar agreement from the Solar Energy Corporation of India ( SECI ) . <p> As a part of the award , AGEL will build 8 GW of solar projects along with a commitment that will see Adani Solar establish 2 GW of additional solar cell and module manufacturing capacity . This award , the largest of its type ever in the world , will entail a single investment of US $6 billion and will create 400,000 direct and indirect jobs . It will also displace 900 million tonnes of carbon dioxide over its lifetime . <p> With this win , AGEL will now have 15 GW capacity under operation , construction or under contract thereby accelerating its journey towards becoming the world 's largest renewables company by 2025 .
