<h> D.C. punks Bad Moves expand their sound but keep their focus on ' Untenable ' <p> Two years ago , Washington was in the throes of the debate about Initiative 77 , which would have phased out the minimum-wage exemption for tipped employees . David Combs , guitarist in the D.C. band Bad Moves , felt the controversy acutely , having worked in restaurants for over a decade , but was shocked at how hostility over the bill had been internalized by the people it was intended to help . He even spoke about it on " The Kojo Nnamdi Show " on WAMU -- but felt he had to do so anonymously . The experience stayed with him . <p> " It was a very focused point of feeling like capitalism is so well-designed to screw over working people to the point that it can get working people on board with screwing over each other , " he says . ( Initiative 77 was approved by voters but repealed by the D.C. Council . ) <p> That experience and ones like it inform Bad Moves ' new album , " Untenable .
