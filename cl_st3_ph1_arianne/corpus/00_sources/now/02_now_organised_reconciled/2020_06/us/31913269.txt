Insects that live nowhere else thrive in the worlds within worlds of old-growth canopies . Bears den in the cavities of massive gnarly old trees , and birds , including pileated woodpeckers , nest and feed in their branches . Inland rainforests host lichen that are a primary food of mountain caribou , now pushed to the brink of extinction by loss of the forests they depend on . <p> The Grey Ghost herd in the south Selkirks , the only mountain caribou in the Lower 48 , is already functionally extinct . <p> Cutting and replanting the old-growth forests that supported caribou and other wildlife will produce fiber , but not the ecological web of life that was lost , said Karen Price , another author on the report .
