<h> SAP launches product to help firms track supply chain emissions <p> BERLIN ( Reuters ) - Software group SAP launched a product on Tuesday to help firms track greenhouse gas emissions in their supply chains , backing a view that being transparent about their carbon footprint will be good for business . <p> The German company , which counts 440,000 customers and touches 77% of global transaction revenue , sees itself as uniquely positioned to track carbon dioxide emissions that are a major cause of climate change . <p> Taking a more holistic approach towards the inputs that go into making everything from cars to food will become more important as customers weigh the environmental impact of their purchasing decisions . <p> " CO2 efficiency will become a differentiator for companies , " Thomas Saueressig , the SAP executive board member in charge of product engineering , told reporters . <p> Saueressig is launching the CO2 tracking product at SAP 's annual Sapphire Now user conference , being held online this year because of the coronavirus pandemic .
