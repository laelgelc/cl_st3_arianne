Photo by Jon Gitchoff <p> HAZELWOOD -- Car horns replaced air horns when members of the McCluer North High School class of 2020 walked across the stage and accepted their diplomas Sunday in a drive-in style ceremony . <p> The nontraditional ceremony in the parking lot of the POWERplex site at the former St. Louis Outlet Mall drew thousands of well-wishers , grateful to have something resembling tradition during the coronavirus pandemic . <p> " It 's different , but I 'm happy I get to see him walk across the stage , " said Bridgett Swanson of Florissant , whose son Keaton Coger plans to join the military . <p> The virus shut down the last two months of classroom instruction , senior prom , spring @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ leaders across the area scrambled to find alternative plans for graduations . Some have held virtual ceremonies . Many have hosted parades for seniors to drive past teachers and supporters in school parking lots or through neighborhoods . <p> " It seemed like we were compromising and there could be a better way , " said Dan Buck , president of POWERplex . " They can always play more ball games , go to more dances , but they only graduate once . " <p> More than 2,700 students from nine high schools are expected to receive their diplomas in the mall parking lot in ceremonies through June 10 . Graduates in caps , gowns and masks sit in front of the stage on folding chairs set 6 feet apart . The ceremonies are broadcast to large screens in the parking lot , where families listen on the radio . Confetti cannons and fireworks top off the celebrations . <p> The Rockwood School District had originally reserved Chaifetz Arena for its graduation ceremonies but was able to recoup its deposit and switch to POWERplex .
