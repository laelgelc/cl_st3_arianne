<h> Why is Washington leaving sportsmen out in the cold ? <p> Like many Americans , I grew up with the unforgettable aroma of trout sizzling in an old cast-iron frying pan , the smell of Hoppe 's #9 gun oil and the scent of Sno-Seal rubbed into old leather boots . I remember watching my grandfather clean his shotgun at the kitchen table , following my dad through the November deer woods and all the endless hours I spent fishing the tiny creek that ran past our house . Simply put : Hunting &amp; fishing have always been an integral part of my life , and that 's not likely to change . <p> Here in Montana there 's an enduring connection between the landscapes that support us and the people who spend their time afield with rod and gun . While it might seem like we 're unique , it turns out that our passion for the outdoors is actually mirrored all over the country .
