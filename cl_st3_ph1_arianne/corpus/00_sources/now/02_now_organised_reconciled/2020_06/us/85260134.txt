<h> Endorsement : Michael Kroske is the better Republican contender for U.S. House , District 23 <p> Sun Sentinel Editorial Board interview with U.S. House of Representatives , District 23 candidate Michael Kroske . <p> Michael Kroske and Carla Spalding are the two Republicans seeking their party 's nomination in Congressional District 23 , which serves south Broward and part of eastern Miami-Dade . Neither candidate appears to be realistically grounded in current national issues , but there is a significant difference in their personas . <p> Kroske impresses us as having a mind more open to cross-party cooperation . Both of them are partisan and supportive of Donald Trump 's misused presidency , but Spalding is more so . That would make her the weaker nominee in a heavily Democratic district that re-elected Debbie Wasserman Schultz with 58.5 percent of the vote two years ago . <p> Advertisement <p> We think Kroske would make a more competitive nominee for the Republicans and , if elected , a more responsible member of Congress . <p> Michael Kroske is a candidate in the August 2020 Republican primary , U.S. House , District 23 . <p> Kroske , 60 , lives @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ .
