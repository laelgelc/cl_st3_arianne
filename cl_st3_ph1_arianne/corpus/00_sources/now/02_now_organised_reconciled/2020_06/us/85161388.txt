Much harder will be making sure global economies build back together . A hyper-green Europe will have little impact on the climate unless the better technology and business practices nurtured at home can spread widely to the places that cause most emissions . Only 9 percent of world emissions come from Europe , a share that has dropped steadily and will decline even faster the more Europe invests in weaning its economies off of fossil fuels .
