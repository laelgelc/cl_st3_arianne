<h> New spider species named after Greta <p> Swedish climate campaigner Greta Thunberg can add to her list of personal accolades , including TIME magazine 's Person of the Year , a group of spiders named after her . <p> Thunberga gen. nov. is a new genus of huntsman spiders from Madagascar , described by German arachnologist Peter Jager , and named after the wunderkind in honour of her commitment to tackling climate change . <p> " The rising temperatures affect all areas of nature - -- including the endemic diversity and spider fauna of Madagascar , " said Jager , who has participated in several protests inspired by Thunberg 's School Strike for Climate . <p> In a study printed in the scientific journal Zootaxa , Jager explained that the new genus differed from other huntsmans in their eye arrangement and unique dotted patterns on their backs . <p> Jager , who has discovered several new spider species in a career spanning 20 years , said he hoped that by naming the creepy crawlies after Thunberg he could draw attention to the issue of biodiversity loss in Madagascar . <p> He named a previous discovery of Southeast Asian @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ type and by far the largest species of the entire genus .
