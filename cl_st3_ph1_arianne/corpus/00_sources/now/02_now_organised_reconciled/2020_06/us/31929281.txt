<h> Florida has thousands more properties with high flood risk than FEMA says , according to new study <p> Tampa is the second-most exposed city in the state , the disaster modeling found . <p> About 114,000 more Florida properties are at risk of flooding in a 100-year storm than the Federal Emergency Management Agency currently estimates , according to a model released Monday by a nonprofit arguing the country has undersold its vulnerability to disasters . <p> Tampa is the second-most exposed city in the state , says the First Street Foundation , with 43,111 properties that could flood in such an event -- the seventh most at-risk in the country .
