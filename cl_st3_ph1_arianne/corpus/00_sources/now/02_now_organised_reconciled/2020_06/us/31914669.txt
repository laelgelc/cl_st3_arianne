<h> A Reading List On Race For Allies Who Want To Do Better <p> A reading list on race for people who want to be and do better . ( Arielle Gray/WBUR ) <p> There have been a lot of reading lists floating around the internet that provide titles on how to learn and talk about racism and current racial tensions in America . This list certainly includes some of those titles , including personal favorites of mine like " So You Want To Talk About Race " by Ijeoma Oluo and " How To Be An Antiracist " by Ibram X. Kendi . But an important part of learning about racism is realizing that no reading list can do the work for you . Learning and excommunicating your internalized racism is a lifelong process that requires intense self-study and determination . I want to be clear : this list is really for allies looking to be and do better . <p> This reading list ( hopefully ) provides suggestions that 'll help you develop a more comprehensive view of race .
