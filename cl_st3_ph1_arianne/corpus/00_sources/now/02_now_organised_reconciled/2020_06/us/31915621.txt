<h> Review : ' The Politician ' is a perfect mix of dry humor and political satire <p> " The Politician " is a soapy comedy-drama that surrounds Payton Hobart ( portrayed by Ben Platt ) , an upper-class kid with dreams of becoming President of the United States . <p> Season one focused on Hobart 's run for Student Body President at his Santa Barbara high school . The first season has a feel of satire and fantasy and puts into perspective how out of hand politics can get . <p> It forced watchers to come to terms with the fact that we often vote for candidates because of their likability rather than policy . The season also had subplots of sexuality , self-identity and privilege . The first season ended with a time jump and set-up for another election , State Senator , and season two picked up a few months after we left off . <p> The second season began weakly and felt like you were being thrown into the action without any setup .
