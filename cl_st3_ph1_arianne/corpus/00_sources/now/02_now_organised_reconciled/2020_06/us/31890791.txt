<h> Brazil deforested 10,000sqkm of Amazon rainforest in 2019 <p> In this Nov 23 , 2019 photo , a burned area of the Amazon rainforest is seen in Prainha , Brazil . ( AP pic ) <p> BRASILIA : Deforestation of Brazil 's Amazon rainforest was worse than previously reported in 2019 , revised government data showed on Tuesday , during the first year of President Jair Bolsonaro 's administration . <p> Bolsonaro is keen to develop the rainforest that is crucial to curbing global warming . <p> Brazil 's space research agency INPE recorded 10,129sqkm of deforestation for its benchmark annual period from August 2018 to July 2019 . That 's an area about the size of Lebanon and a 34.4% rise from the same period a year earlier . <p> The revision is higher than INPE 's initial report of 9,762sqkm of forest destroyed during that period , an increase of 29.5% . <p> The 2019 data remains the highest level of deforestation seen in Brazil 's Amazon since 2008 , a level it had already hit prior to the revision . <p> INPE generally revises the data every year for accuracy as a standard practice . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ right-wing Bolsonaro for emboldening illegal loggers , ranchers and land speculators to clear the forest . <p> Bolsonaro has urged the development of the Amazon , including protected areas , as a way to lift the region 's poor residents out of poverty . <p> Brazil is home to roughly 60% of the Amazon , the world 's largest rainforest . Protection of the Amazon is vital to curbing climate change , scientists say , because of the vast amount of carbon dioxide it absorbs . <p> The revised data is based on a system called PRODES that is only released once annually , which is more accurate than monthly data reported using a rapid system known as DETER . <p> Monthly data shows that deforestation has continued to worsen in 2020 , rising 55% for January to April , as compared to the same period in 2019 . <p> Bolsonaro deployed the armed forces to combat rising deforestation in May , using a decree that expires Wednesday but which officials say they expect to be renewed for another 30 days .
