<h> The Potential Pitfalls of Sucking Carbon From the Atmosphere <p> Last week , Canadian company Carbon Engineering published research findings that show how carbon dioxide could be sucked up from the atmosphere for less than $100 per ton . In 2017 , the world emitted some 32.5 gigatons of the stuff . But hey -- baby steps . <p> Scientists have long speculated that so-called " negative emissions " technologies like CO2 removal could not only slow the accumulation of carbon in the air , but even reverse it . Before last week , though , all that speculation was , well , largely speculative ; nobody had convincingly demonstrated how to pull off negative emissions at scale . Previous estimates had pegged the cost of sucking carbon from the skies , for instance , at $600 per ton -- way too pricey to qualify as a viable cleanup solution .
