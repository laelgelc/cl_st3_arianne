<p> " In the midst of every crisis , lies great opportunity , " Albert Einstein said . If the size of the crisis determines the size of the opportunity , the coronavirus pandemic and the recession it drove have given Tasmania plenty to consider . Immigration is pretty much dead , for now at least , so the supply of imported workers and , particularly , new residents can be expected to slow significantly . At the same time , many Tasmanians have been thrown out of work and are looking for opportunities . Demographer Lisa Denny sees big opportunities there , but does not believe it will just happen . Rather , she sees the crisis as an opportunity to boost and broaden economic recovery through industry policy going " hand in hand " with education and training policy , to not only help more Tasmanians into work , but to create better and more secure jobs .
