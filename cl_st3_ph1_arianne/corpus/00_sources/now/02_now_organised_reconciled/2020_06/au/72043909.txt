<h> Security and defence <p> Concern about non-traditional security threats appears to have eclipsed that of traditional security threats for Australians in 2020 . COVID-19 and ' drought and water shortages ' are seen as the top threats to Australia 's vital interests in the next ten years . Traditional threats such as international terrorism and Iran 's nuclear program have moved from the forefront of the Australian public 's mind , whereas concern about ' a severe downturn in the global economy ' has risen by 20 points , with 71% saying that it poses a critical threat . Following the bushfire crisis over the Australian summer , six in ten Australians see ' environmental disasters such as bushfires and floods ' ( 67% ) and climate change ( 59% ) as critical threats .
