<p> Creating jobs , ensuring the Tamar River is in good health and helping businesses rebuild are shaping up to be key priorities for candidates in the upcoming Legislative Council election for the seat of Rosevears . The election was originally due to take place in May , but COVID-19 delayed it and now voters will go to the polls on August 1 to elect a new represenative . To mark the restarting of the campaign , Premier Peter Gutwein joined Liberal candidate Jo Palmer on the husting at Riverside on Saturday . Ms Palmer said the state government she sought to be part of had a plan to help Tasmanians regain jobs and recover from the COVID-19 crisis .
