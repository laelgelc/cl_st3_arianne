Rise Up Australia <p> Rise Up Australia national president Daniel Nalliah , who is an evangelical pastor , claimed he had a discussion with Bernardi in the period leading up to the leadership spill , but only in regard to Nalliah 's efforts to lobby the government to prioritise persecuted Christian minorities in the Syrian refugee intake . <p> " Who knows , if he was part of a breakaway party , we could work together , " Nalliah said , also nominating Pauline Hanson 's One Nation as a conservative force with which he would like to better co-ordinate . <p> Rise Up Australia was n't far behind Family First in plotting how to make the most of the new political @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ prime ministership , Nalliah met with his deputy , Rosalie Crestani , to discuss how to reach out to Abbott supporters , and the two have met repeatedly since to refine a strategy . " We 've had five prime ministers in five years , and people are being turned off by the major parties . They are not pleased . ' A setback for conservatism ' <p> Before the leadership coup , the polls indicated a Turnbull prime ministership could leave the Liberal Party exposed on the political right . This dramatic turnaround could be attributed to a number of factors : the honeymoon period for a new prime minister , a preference to fall in behind whoever the actual leader happens to be , and perhaps Turnbull 's assurances he wo n't tear up the Coalition 's policies on climate change and same-sex marriage . <p> Sydney Traditionalist Forum convenor Edwin Dyga said Abbott 's loss was more a symbolic setback for conservatism than anything , and ground needed to be gained in the " culture war " outside the halls of parliament to generate an authentic conservative political comeback within it . <p> " As the term suggests , this is a cultural struggle , " he said . " Certainly , it has political consequences , but you can not change the hearts and minds of people by legislative , judicial or executive fiat alone . " They rode in on a populist wave and shouted out their positions on policy without an intellectual basis . " <p> Torrisi offered Bernardi 's controversial 2013 book The Conservative Revolution as an example of a fully fleshed-out conservative manifesto , but wonders whether the senator would actually leave the Liberal party . <p> " He is future leadership material , but leadership of what I do n't know , " he said . <p> Torrisi believes it will take some kind of crisis to attract people back to old-school conservatism and thus see the rise of a competitive alternative to the Liberals on the right , but for now he 's doing what he can as a foot soldier of the culture war .
