<h> Summary <p> Under Matt Kean , NSW has started to plan more seriously for a lower-carbon future . It 's possible to see the current policy as just a statement of intention and that , over time , more will follow . As it stands , though , there is pretty much a complete lack of teeth , a lack of skin in the game . NSW consumers pay more for electricity than in other states and arguably that is at least partly due to a lack of government commitment . <p> NSW 's emissions are very largely sourced from coal ( 50% ) and land transport ( 18% ) .
