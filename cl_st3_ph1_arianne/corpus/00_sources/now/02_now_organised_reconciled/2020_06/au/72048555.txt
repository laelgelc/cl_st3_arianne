<p> The picture of economic recovery painted by Prime Minister Scott Morrison is looking like a mirage . <p> The 22 leading economists polled by The Conversation from 16 universities in seven states on average expect historically weak economic growth in all but one of the next five years , with growth dwindling over time . <p> Growth one percentage point above trend would average almost 4% per year . <p> Instead , The Conversation 's economic panel is forecasting annual growth averaging 2.4% over the next four years , much less than the long-term trend , tailing off over time . <p> The results imply living standards 5% lower than the prime minister expects by 2025 . <p> The panel expects unemployment to peak at around 10% and to still be above 7% by the end of 2021 . <p> It expects wages to barely climb at all , by just 0.9% in 2020 , the lowest increase on record and even less than the rate of inflation , which it expects to be only 1.2% .
