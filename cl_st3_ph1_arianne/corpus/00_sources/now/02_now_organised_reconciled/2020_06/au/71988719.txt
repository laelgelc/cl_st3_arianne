It 's big . Spend-a-whole-day-there big . <p> Multiply that by 170 and you get around 6500 hectares . That 's how big Melbourne 's new series of parks and trails will be ( or would be , if you squished them all together ) . <p> The new green spaces are part of the recently announced $154 million Suburban Parks Program , which includes " pocket parks " ( up to 2000 square metres in size ) and off-leash dog parks in built-up areas , plus walking and bike trails . <p> The program means substantially more open , green @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ friend ) to get out and about . And the announcement comes at a time -- in the wake of the coronavirus lockdown -- when we 've never been more appreciative of a leg-stretching stroll . <p> The 24 new parks were announced as part of the program 's first stage -- 15 pocket parks and nine dog parks . They 'll start opening from next year . <p> The dog parks will be located in South Yarra , Maribyrnong , Werribee , Carrum , Cranbourne East , Rowville , Cairnlea , Eltham North and Mickleham . <p> Ease of access played a major part when settling on each location , according to a press release by the Minister for Energy , Environment and Climate Change , Lily D'Ambrosio . The new parks had to be easily accessible by car and public transport , and well-linked to existing walking and bike trails . <p> In stage two of the program , a further 10 pocket parks and seven dog parks will be created .
