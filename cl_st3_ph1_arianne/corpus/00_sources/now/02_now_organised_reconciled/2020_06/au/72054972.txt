But a new report predicts it could actually make or break civilisation as we know it . <p> The idea that humanity is facing a major shake-up this century is not new . The Fourth Industrial Revolution being brought about by technologies like AI , gene editing , robotics , and 3D printing is predicted to cause dramatic social , political , and economic upheaval in the coming decades . <p> But according to think tank RethinkX , thinking about the coming transition as just another industrial revolution is too simplistic .
