<h> Topics <p> FREMANTLE and Cockburn environmental groups have received a combined $150,000 in funding to aid their conservation efforts . <p> 13 groups have shared in the funding from the Communities Environment Program to undertake works around the federal electorate of Fremantle . <p> From Rottnest Island 's salt marshes to pockets of Banksia and Tuart woodlands across Cockburn , there will be numerous on-ground works focused on native plant revegetation , invasive weed removal and habitat expansion for native fauna . <p> One of the groups to receive funding was the Friends of Clontarf Hill , and convenor Kelli Featherstone said they needed to protect the grounds and one of the last remaining old-growth Tuart trees in the region . <p> " This forms part of essential habitat linkages for native flora , fauna and birds but over time , weeds have taken over preventing native plants to thrive , " she said . <p> " These weeds need to be controlled as a matter of priority if the reserve is to recover its original vegetation and biological values . " <p> Fremantle MP Josh Wilson said the dedication of volunteers involved in the conservation and rehabilitation @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ projects will expand community-level conservation efforts , which of course is critical to the restoration of our environment and protection of threatened species , but also to the return of community participation and confidence following the COVID-19 crisis and our first national climate change catastrophe -- the summer of bushfires , " he said . <p> There are 55 threatened species in the Fremantle electorate , such as the fairy tern , three species of black cockatoo , the western ringtail possum and Rottnest Island 's quokkas . <p> In addition , it is home to two threatened ecological communities ( Tuart Woodlands and Forests , and Banksia Woodlands ) , and four wetlands of national importance .
