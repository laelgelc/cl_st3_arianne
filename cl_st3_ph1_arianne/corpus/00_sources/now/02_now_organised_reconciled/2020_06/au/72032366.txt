<h> Government 's COVID-19 commission accused of shutting out green groups <p> The Morrison government 's COVID-19 commission has been accused of shutting out peak environmental groups , despite claiming to have included them in consultation . <p> In a letter obtained by 9News , Greenpeace , the Wilderness Society , the Sunrise Project , the Australian Marine Conservation Society and the Australasian Centre for Corporate Responsibility have protested directly to the Prime Minister . <p> They complain they are listed on the commission 's website " as organisations with which the Commission has engaged " . <p> Environmental groups say they have not been consulted by the government 's COVID-19 commission . ( Getty ) <p> " We write to inform you that our organisations have not been consulted by the National COVID-19 Coordination Commission , and to express our disappointment at the Commission 's lack of meaningful engagement with the environment and climate change sectors , " they write . <p> On June 4 , the Chair of the National COVID-19 Coordination Commission , Neville Power , told a Senate committee around 1000 organisations had been consulted . <p> " Some of them have been in very @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , and some of them have been small groups , " he said . <p> " We 've put all of those that we 've noted on the website , so they 're available there to see the range of the people that we 've engaged in . " <p> Greenpeace Australia Pacific Campaigner Jonathan Moylan told 9News he participated in a large Zoom conference in May , but did not get the chance to ask questions . <p> " We managed to listen in to what was billed as a chance to hear from Nev Power - it certainly was n't consultation , " he said . <p> " Consultation involves a deliberative process where everyone has an opportunity to express their concerns and then those concerns are dealt with in a meaningful way . " <p> The organisations have raised several concerns about the Commission , including its gas interests and lack of representation of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islanders and sectors most impacted by COVID-19.
