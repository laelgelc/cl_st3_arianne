<p> New analysis has shown that a lifting of Victoria 's gas exploration moratorium is unnecessary because investments in energy efficiency and reductions in gas use are a more effective way to bridge a predicted gas supply shortfall . <p> The findings have been outlined in an analysis commissioned by Environment Victoria , after the Australian Energy Market Operator predicted there could be a shortfall of gas supplies from 2024 in the last 2019 Gas Statement of Opportunities . <p> The analysis , completed by consultants Northmore Gordon , found that Victoria could successfully avoid a potential shortfall in supply by investing in measures to reduce overall demand for gas , and that increasing gas production was unnecessary . <p> " Victorians are the biggest gas consumers in the country -- half of our gas usage is in residential heating and hot water alone . We need to shift our thinking : the question is how do we shift our energy needs away from gas , not how we get more of it out of the ground .
