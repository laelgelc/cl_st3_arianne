<h> Statement on launch of UK-New Zealand FTA <p> Today we are very pleased to announce the launch of free trade agreement ( FTA ) negotiations between the United Kingdom and New Zealand . An FTA between our two nations , the closest of friends with deep historic and family ties rooted within New Zealand 's founding Treaty of Waitangi , will strengthen our economies and set the stage for an exciting new chapter in our relationship . It is a top priority for both countries and will play an important role in our COVID-19 economic recovery strategies . <p> Our FTA will be ambitious , comprehensive , future-focused , and will support sustainable and inclusive trade . By removing tariffs and tackling non-tariff barriers , the agreement will reduce costs for exporters and consumers , support job creation , innovation and collaboration , and boost two-way trade in goods and services worth ? 2.9bn in 2019 . It will also encourage more two-way investment , with the UK already the fifth largest investor in New Zealand , and the UK the fourth largest recipient of New Zealand 's direct investment in 2018 .
