<h> Save Lands Gives Back to the Environment with Each Item Sold <p> Fun apparel and accessories with a serious mission to plant 12 trees for each item sold <p> ( PRUnderground ) June 14th , 2020 <p> Deforestation remains one of the leading threats to nature . With 80% of land animals and plants living in forests , deforestation is a bigger problem than most think . 80,000 acres are destroyed everyday . This is what a clothing brand based in Ohio is trying to help solve by planting trees and spreading awareness about this environmental issue . <p> Save Lands is dedicated to ensuring future generations can continue to enjoy nature . With this mission , the company has partnered with Trees.org to help plant as many trees as possible to deflect climate change . <p> It has launched a program that aims to save the planet in style . For every item they sell on their website , 12 trees are planted . Each item from Save Lands has been made with a reminder of mother nature , whether through an inspirational phrase , image , or other design element . <p> " We believe that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ preserves the environment . Their short-term goal is to hit the 1,000,000 tree mark , and they 're not too far from reaching this . With the help of customers on Earth Day this year , the company was able to plant 320,000 trees . This marked a total of 750,000 trees planted since they started the program . <p> Save Lands is not only helping the environment , but also farmers and their families .
