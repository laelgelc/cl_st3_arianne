<h> This is why you should visit Australia 's Great Barrier Reef <p> Australia 's biggest tourism draw stretches 2,300 kilometres off the coast of Queensland . On any given day , large boats and catamarans depart the coastal marinas for the ride to several fixed points along the reef where tourists can dive and snorkel . <p> This is why visitors come here : to view one of the easiest natural structures that can be viewed from space and which is more than 69 million football fields -- Aussie rules , naturally -- in size . It is spectacular and everyone should see it at least once in their lifetime . Here are a few reasons that may entice you to visit . <h> 1 . Help the reef <p> Every visitor to the world 's largest marine park with a permitted tour operator is charged $6.50 , which is part of your ticket price and is paid toward ongoing park management .
