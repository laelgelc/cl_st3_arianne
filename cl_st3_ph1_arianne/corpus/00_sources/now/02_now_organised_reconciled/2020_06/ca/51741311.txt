<h> The World 's Biggest Threat is Social Media <h> Repairing social relations in the age of uncertainty . <p> News and opinions -- be they medical , scientific , or political -- have spread and shifted with manic speed since the novel coronavirus took over our lives and attention in late February 2020 . The wave of protests following the murder of George Floyd in Minneapolis has accelerated this trend . As our already fragile world faces another divise issue , I write to propose a pause and a truce . First , allow me to share a counter-intuitive observation from the perpective of a cognitive anthropologist who studies cultural evolution in the digital age . The biggest , most immediate threat to our health , peace , and integrity is not COVID or systemic racism . Climate change ( at least immediately ) is not it either .
