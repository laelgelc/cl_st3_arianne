<p> The Global Green Mining market is expected to reach $16.68 billion by 2026 growing at a CAGR of 9.0% from 2018 to 2026 . The goal of green mining is to minimize the adverse environmental and social influences in all stages of the operations . <p> At the same time , the operations strive to make the most of social and local benefits . The increase of the middle class in most developing economies and the rapid growth of technologies have created growing interest in mineral resources . Mining has become more and more difficult for societal and environmental reasons all over the globe . <p> Factors such as the need for the sustainable and cost-effective mining , climate change and its effects on the mining industry and increasing concern about the environment are driving the market growth . Though , financing green mining procedures are restraining the market growth .
