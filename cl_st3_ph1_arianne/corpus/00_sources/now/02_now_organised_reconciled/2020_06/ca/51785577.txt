<h> Heat wave a sign of summers to come <p> The signs of the current heat wave gripping the city are all around -- people sweating through their shirts just walking down the street , others seeking shelter from the oppressive heat wherever there is shade and a bit of breeze . <p> Environment Canada has issued a heat warning in Winnipeg , advising that a " stagnant weather pattern " has brought with it a week full of temperature highs each day between 29C and 34C , with expected humidex values around 40C . <p> This heat wave is a sign of things to come as Winnipeg is set to be one of the cities in Canada that sees the most dramatic increase in the number of extreme heat days in years to come , due to climate change . <p> RUTH BONNEVILLE / WINNIPEG FREE PRESS <p> Luc Labelle cools himself while spending time with his godchild at Provencher Park Spray Pad during the heat wave on Monday . <p> Between 1976 and 2005 , Winnipeg saw an average of 14.3 days per year where the high was above 30C .
