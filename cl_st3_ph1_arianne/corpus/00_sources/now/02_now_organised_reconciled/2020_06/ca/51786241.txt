<h> Corporation of Metro Vancouver now carbon neutral <p> DELTA , B.C. - The regional district of Metro Vancouver says it has achieved corporate carbon neutrality , balancing its carbon output with its removal from the atmosphere . <p> Board chairman Sav Dhaliwal said Monday that the regional district became carbon neutral in 2019 and is poised to remain so at least through 2022 . <p> The achievement applies to the corporate organization of Metro Vancouver , not to the broader population of residents or businesses within the region . <p> The regional government has set a goal of expanding carbon neutrality across Metro Vancouver by 2050 , but Dhaliwal said it will require buy-in from everyone . <p> " Unless we are all committed to it , it ca n't be done , " he said during a news conference at Burns Bog , a protected area in Delta , B.C. <p> Metro Vancouver 's carbon emissions have been offset through initiatives that include parkland acquisitions , the installation of energy efficient boilers and an increase in electric vehicles for staff use , it said in a news release . <p> Its use of trenchless tunnel construction for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and caused fewer disruptions .
