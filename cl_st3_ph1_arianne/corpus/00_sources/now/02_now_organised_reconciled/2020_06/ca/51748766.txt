<h> No exemption for carbon tax on grain drying <p> It does not appear the federal government is keen on helping grain farmers deal with the added cost of the carbon tax on grain drying . <p> Agriculture and Agri-Food Minister Marie-Claude Bibeau was speaking to reporters this morning to announce a second round of applications for the government 's local food infrastructure fund . <p> Bibeau was asked about the federal governments ' analysis of the impact of the carbon tax on grain drying . <p> The provinces were asked to submit their estimates of the cost of the carbon tax on grain drying back in November . Bibeau said the data the government received and analysed shows the impact is a very small percentage of operating costs . <p> " The estimates ranged from $210 to $819 per farm and 0.05 per cent to 0.42 per cent of total farm operating expenses , " Bibeau said . " When you compare it to greenhouse heating , which is a significant portion of operating costs , if I 'm not mistaken , it 's about seven per cent . There 's a significant difference .
