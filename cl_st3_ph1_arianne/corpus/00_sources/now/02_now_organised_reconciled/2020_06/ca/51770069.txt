<h> The Weather Network joins the search to find all of Canada 's tornadoes <p> The Weather Network joins the search to find all of Canada 's tornadoes <p> To reach their ambitious goal of locating every tornado that occurs in Canada , the Northern Tornadoes Project has now teamed up with The Weather Network , which gives the Project unprecedented access to a vast resource of severe weather content . Their search is about to kick into overdrive ! <p> The Northern Tornadoes Project began at Western University , in 2017 , using a unique new method of locating tornadoes in Northern Ontario . Since then , the project has grown as the organizers formed partnerships , allowing them to expand their search , coast to coast to coast . <p> The latest partnership is possibly the most exciting , especially for the public . The Weather Network and the Northern Tornadoes Project will combine reach and resources to track down tornado and damage reports across Canada . Safety first ! <h> CANADIAN TORNADOES ? <p> When we think of tornadoes , it 's easy to envision somewhere in the United States , with its classic ' tornado alley ' , but Canada sees its fair share of twisters too . <p> " If the United States was n't there , we 'd be right near the top of the list , in the world , for tornado activity , " Sills said in a Weather Network interview in 2019 . <p> " The activity that happens in the northern United States extends into parts of Canada , " Sills explained at the time . " The big tornado alley through the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and the the midwest tornadoes extend into southern Ontario and Quebec , and even New Brunswick . " <p> According to Environment and Climate Change Canada , there is an average of around 60 tornadoes per year across the country . Although tornadoes have been reported in every province and territory of Canada , the majority occur in Alberta , Saskatchewan , Manitoba , Ontario and Quebec . <p> These events end up costing Canadians millions of dollars . Taking just one occurrence as an example , the six confirmed tornadoes that swept through Ottawa on September 21 , 2018 - one EF-3 , one EF-2 and four EF-1 - are estimated to have caused close to $300 million in damages . <p> Having a full account how many tornadoes are actually occurring across Canada , including those that would not normally be reported due to their remoteness , will benefit the insurance industry and those researching into Canadian tornadoes . Most importantly , though , it will help to better protect the public during severe weather events .
