It was consolidated after centuries of warfare around 1478 by Ivan the Great , creating the largest political unit on Earth ( which it still is ) . The expansion was continued by his militaristic grandson , Ivan the Terrible . The legacy of his brutality , plus the fact that he died without an heir , ending the line of Russia 's founding House of Rurik , triggered a horrific 15-year " time of troubles " ( 1598-1613 ) . It was marked by anarchy , uprooted populations , bloody uprisings , and the brief reign of the quickly assassinated " False Dimitry " ( who claimed to be the son of Ivan the Terrible , but who was dismissed by his enemies as a maverick monk ) -- all made worse by the plague and crop failures . <p> In the Ottoman Empire , things started to fall apart when a certain @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ classic civilization topped by the Han Dynasty , with the passage of time , suddenly a band of adventurers known as the " Posterior Han " ( i.e. , the fake Han ) were in charge . In 96 AD , the Roman factions compromised , temporarily they thought , on an aged ( 65 -- considered too old to cause trouble in those days ) senator named Marcus Cocceius Nerva . He did die two years later , but pulled off a world-changing move in the meantime . He formally adopted his best general , Trajan , who legally and peacefully succeeded him . The practice continued from one emperor to another and the next century became known as Rome 's golden age , or " Nerva 's golden chain , " before the empire took its final plunge . <p> In Russia , in 1613 , the factions calmed down enough to agree on a young @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ settled ( although the serfs , temporarily free , became serfs again ) and the Romanov dynasty lasted until the Bolshevik Revolution in 1917 . <p> Civilizations did not decline in a straight line , Toynbee found -- there was always a " rout-and-rally " effect , although every " rally " was weaker than the last , as the society 's founding principles gradually eroded . Not that civilizations needed to decline and fall , he said -- essentially it 's all up to its citizens . <p> In this light , the prospects are that the U.S. , and perhaps the entire Western world with it , will rally from the Trump calamity , perhaps even generate some optimism and take on the real problems in the world -- notably social justice and climate change .
