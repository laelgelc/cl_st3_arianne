Then COVID-19 hit . The homeless were suddenly seen as threats to middle-class health , and society moved rapidly to house them to reduce the chances of shelters becoming vector sites for illness . Cities such as Toronto and Vancouver rented entire hotels to house the homeless . <p> Doctors had more " pull , " it seems , than scholars -- they mobilized and actualized a change we 'd been after for years . And do n't think politicians did this from the goodness of their hearts -- they did it out of fear and to protect taxpayers .
