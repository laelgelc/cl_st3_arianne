<h> You can make a difference . <p> One of the world 's great rivers , the Fraser River in B.C. , is home to millions of salmon that swim downstream to feed and grow in the Fraser estuary before beginning their ocean journey . <p> But this rearing ground is threatened . And it needs our attention and concern . <p> The Fraser River has historically produced more salmon than any other place in North America . It is the rearing ground for fish species that play a crucial role in a food web that links fish , birds and marine mammals across thousands of kilometres of the Pacific Ocean . <p> However , in the last 100 years , salmon habitat in the lower Fraser has been dramatically changed . <p> The Fraser River is the rearing ground for millions of salmon .
