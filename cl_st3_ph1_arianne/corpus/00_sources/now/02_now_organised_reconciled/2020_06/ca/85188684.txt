As renewable energy is dependent on intermittent sources , reliable and affordable energy storage is an essential component of solidifying this progress and eradicating dependence on fossil fuels . <p> Energy Vault 's specially designed crane uses proprietary technology and machine vision software to autonomously orchestrate the lifting and lowering of massive composite bricks made from locally sourced soil or waste materials .
