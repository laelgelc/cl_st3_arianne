Environment Canada is doing fewer inspections , investigations and prosecutions to enforce its law protecting people from toxic chemicals and air pollution . THE CANADIAN PRESS/Adrian Wyld <p> OTTAWA -- Environment Canada has been doing fewer inspections , investigations and prosecutions over the last five years to enforce a law protecting people from toxic chemicals and air pollution . <p> According to figures provided last month in response to a written question submitted in the House of Commons , the department investigated 43 companies for violations of the Canadian Environmental Protection Act in 2015-16 . There were 22 prosecutions and convictions . <p> In each of the last two fiscal years , however , the department investigated 12 companies .
