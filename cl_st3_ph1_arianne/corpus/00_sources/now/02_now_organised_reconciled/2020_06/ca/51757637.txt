<p> MOSCOW ( Reuters ) - State prosecutors have charged a prominent Russian scientist with treason after accusing him of passing state secrets to China , his lawyer said on Monday , adding that his client could serve up to 20 years in prison if found guilty . <p> Valery Mitko , president of the Arctic Academy of Sciences in St. Petersburg , has been under house arrest since authorities charged him with high treason in February . <p> Ivan Pavlov , his lawyer , said the 78-year-old academic travelled to China twice a year to speak as a guest lecturer and denied handing state secrets to the Chinese authorities . <p> Mitko did travel to China with documents for his lectures , but these contained academic and open source information only , Pavlov said . <p> " There were no state secrets in there at all . We consider these accusations to be absurd , " Pavlov said , saying he hoped the case would be dismissed before trial . <p> A source told the Interfax news agency that Mitko has been accused of having given China information pertaining to methods used to detect submarines . <p> Mitko @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Pacific Fleet , according to an association of Russian navy veterans . <p> A number of Russian scientists have been arrested and charged with treason in recent years for allegedly handing sensitive material to foreigners .
