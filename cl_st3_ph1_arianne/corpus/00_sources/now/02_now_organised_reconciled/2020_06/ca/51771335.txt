<p> Dramatic weather can happen in the blink of an eye . Meteorologist Sara Hoffman breaks down the different kinds of watches and alerts Environment Canada issues . <p> " A watch of any kind is issued when all the ingredients are present for active weather and the potential for impact weather . Quite frequently , we have every ingredient we would need for a severe weather event to take place . However , it 's just not come together in the right way to create that weather event . " <p> When a watch is issued , it 's time for you to think about an action plan and how the weather may affect your daily activities .
