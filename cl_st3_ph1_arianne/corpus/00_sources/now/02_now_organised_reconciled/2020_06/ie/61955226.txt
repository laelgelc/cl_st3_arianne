<h> Green light for Limerick solar farm plan <p> PLANS for a solar farm on the edge of Abbeyfeale have been given the go-head by Limerick City and County Council . <p> Local farmer Michael Harnett has secured planning permission to construct a solar farm on his land at Knockbrack . <p> The proposals will see approximately 13.63 hectares of solar panels on ground mounted steel frames , as well as a single-storey substation , four inverter units , a temporary construction compound , access tracks , CCTV and security fencing . <p> Mr Harnett , whose family have farmed in Abbeyfeale for generations , says he wants to use the farm as an opportunity to support local economic activities in terms of " job creation , benefiting local businesses and encouraging further sustainable initiatives . " <p> Speaking to the Limerick Leader , Mr Harnett said he has become quite interested in renewable energy schemes in recent years . <p> " Climate change is something I do think about . Locally , the State will now end support for the Shannon LNG project at Ballylongford . <p> Mr Harnett hopes more farmers might follow in his footsteps . <p> " If this is successful for me , other farmers might look and say , I have 40-acres here .
