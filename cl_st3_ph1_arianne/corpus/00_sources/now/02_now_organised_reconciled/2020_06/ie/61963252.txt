<p> According to the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations , world food production will need to increase by an estimated 70 percent to feed everyone by 2050 . To achieve this , our food systems will need to become more sustainable to meet the declining availability of land , water and energy resources . <p> Remarkably , while food insecurity remains an issue for many , about 20 percent of the food produced in Europe today is wasted . This is compounded by the fact that Europe has the second highest proportion of overweight people behind America , with 51.6 percent of its adult population overweight or obese . <p> In this context , the EU must deliver on its promise to build a fairer , healthier , and more environmentally friendly food system , and its recently published Farm to Fork strategy is the first step towards achieving this .
