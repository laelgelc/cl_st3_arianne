<h> EC opens for applications to new sustainable finance advisory body <p> The European Commission has launched a call for applications to form the group that will be advising it on the further development of the sustainability taxonomy , its review , and on sustainable finance more broadly . <p> The move came hot on the heels of the European Parliament yesterday adopting the taxonomy regulation , which provides for the creation of the body , known as the Platform on Sustainable Finance . <p> It is the successor to the technical expert group ( TEG ) that has been advising the Commission in connection with its 2018 sustainable finance " action plan " measures . The TEG 's mandate is due to run out at the end of September , unless the new group is formally established before then . <p> The Platform on Sustainable Finance , whose members will have a two-year mandate , will have four main tasks , " some of which are particularly urgent " , according to the Commission . <p> The tasks the Platform will be asked to prioritise during the two-year term to Q3 2022 are : <p> to advise on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ taxonomy ; <p> to advise on the review of the taxonomy regulation ; and <p> to advise on " the possible need to develop further measures to improve data availability and quality " . <p> The focus of the first task will be on criteria for screening economic activities against the four specified environmental objectives that go beyond climate change . <p> The TEG already came up with criteria for assessing whether economic activites make a substantial contribution to the objectives of climate change mitigation or adaptation , including an assessment of significant harm to other environmental objectives . <p> The second task will include advising the Commission on the possible need to amend the taxonomy regulation , and on addressing other sustainability objectives besides those focussed on the natural environment . <p> The topic of data availability and quality links to that of the review of the Non-Financial Reporting Directive , on which the Commission is currently consulting . <p> The Platform will also be asked to monitor and report regularly to the Commission on EU and member state-level trends in capital flows towards sustainable investment . <p> However @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ by public entities such as the European Environment Agency and the European Investment Bank , seven representatives of which it would appoint directly to the body . <p> Valdis Dombrovskis , Commission executive vice president , said : " This Platform will play a crucial role in the development of the EU Taxonomy and our sustainable finance strategy over the coming years . <p> " The adoption of the taxonomy regulation marks a milestone in our green agenda .
