<h> THE LYRIC CONCERT WITH PAUL HERRIOTT <p> Join Paul Herriott for a very special live concert coming from WDR Broadcasting House in Cologne via the European Broadcasting Union to celebrate Beethoven Pastoral Day . <p> Join Paul Herriott for a very special live concert coming from WDR Broadcasting House in Cologne via the European Broadcasting Union to celebrate Beethoven Pastoral Day . We join many countries around Europe to hear a socially distant arrangement of Beethoven 's ' Pastoral ' Symphony- arranged for two violins , two violas and two cellos . In The Tears of Nature , Tan Dun commemorates natural disasters in Sichuan , Fukushima and Hurricane Sandy in New York . The concert closes with the radio broadcast premiere of Tan Dun 's Prayer and Blessing , a work referring to the corona pandemic . <p> The destruction of Nature by human beings and its consequences are the focus of this concert by members of the WDR Symphony Orchestra in conjunction with International World Environment Day . As a contrast with Beethoven 's ' Pastoral ' Symphony , they look back at times when people and Nature were still in harmony . <p> Beethoven : @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Pastoral ' ) arranged for two violins , two violas and two cellos . <p> Tan Dun : The Tears of Nature ( chamber version for two pianos and three percussionists ) <p> Tan Dun : Prayer and Blessing <p> Performers : Members of the WDR Symphony Orchestra <p> Notes for Editors : <p> Beethoven 's sixth symphony is titled ' Pastoral ' and is a musical portrayal of the interrelationship between human beings and nature .
