<p> In uncertain times , one should look for stable allies and long-term partners . In a North African region marked by political and economic turmoil , the Kingdom of Morocco stands out as a nation enjoying institutional and macro-economic stability , a liberal economy as well as an educated workforce . <p> Moreover , under the leadership of His Majesty King Mohammed VI , Morocco has , over the last decade , become one of North West Africa 's leading countries , a catalyst for economic growth and a financial hub granting access to a market of over a billion consumers . <p> But this tells only half the story .
