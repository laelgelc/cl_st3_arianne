<h> Two Tipperary students predicted world economic depression <h> ROCKWELL COLLEGE STUDENTS SHINE <p> Rockwell College , Cashel , claimed a clean sweep of gold medals in 6th Year , 5th Year and TY in the national Young Economist of the Year competition 2020 . <p> This builds on last year 's top awards for 5th and 6th year . <p> Teacher Pat Egan expressed pride in the achievement of his students but he was more happy with the fact that ' all projects had a distinctly local and environmentally sustainable theme ' <p> Leah McGarry , Clerihan , and Padraic Clark , Dualla , 6th Year gold medal winners ( last year 's 5th year winners ) continued where they left off with a project entitled ' A Positive Look at Climate Change ' . <p> While the project was submitted on February 27 it predicted the global depression that we are now experiencing as a result of the Covid-19 pandemic . <p> In doing so they called for fossil fuels to be phased out and appealed for renewable energy to replace it as demand for energy begins to rise post Covid .
