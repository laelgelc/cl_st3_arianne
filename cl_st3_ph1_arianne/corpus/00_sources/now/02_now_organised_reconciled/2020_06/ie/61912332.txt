<h> An inside view of Ireland 's largest beef producer group <p> The Angus breed arrived in Ireland in the 19th century and has played a pivotal role in Irish farming ever since -- both in suckler and dairy farming circles . <p> With the ability to thrive and reach finishing potential -- on a predominately grass-based diet -- the popularity of the breed has increased over the years . Also , as the traditional breed naturally matures earlier , this results in Angus cattle producing fewer carbon emissions . <p> Additionally , traits such as easy calving and short gestation have allowed the breed to successfully grow into one of the most popular beef breeds for use on the Irish dairy herd . <p> Calls for more producer groups have echoed in the Irish beef industry for some time now , with these groups in a position to deliver for Irish farmers , while also providing a traceable , top-quality product for the end user -- the consumer . <p> One such organisation -- the Certified Irish Angus Producer Group -- has been excelling in this space since its onset in 1995 .
