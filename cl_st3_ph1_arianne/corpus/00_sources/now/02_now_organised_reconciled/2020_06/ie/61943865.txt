<h> Irish consumers under 35 increase dairy consumption during lockdown <p> Irish consumers under the age of 35 increased their consumption of dairy produce during the Covid-19 lockdown , according to new research . <p> This research , based on a survey of over 2,000 Irish adults and released today , Monday , June 22 , by the European Milk Forum , also shows that price and nutritional issues rank above climate concerns when it comes to purchasing food . <p> Almost 40% of Irish consumers under the age of 35 increased their milk , cheese and yogurt consumption since the onset of Covid-19 restrictions , with families and households seeking more nutritional value ; local produce ; and assurance of quality during " uncertain times " . <p> The research shows that the carbon footprint of food and sustainable packaging were only listed as a factor in dairy product purchases for 10% and 19% respectively of the respondents .
