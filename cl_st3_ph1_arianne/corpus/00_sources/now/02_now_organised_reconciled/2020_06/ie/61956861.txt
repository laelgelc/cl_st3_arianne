<p> Nanotechnology , energy from manure , and insect pheromones that lure pests away from precious fruit crops , are three exciting new horticulture technologies to receive backing from a new ? 3.5 million climate change fund . <p> Three up-and-coming agri-tech ventures were awarded ? 140,000 each from the SHAKE Climate Change Programme , to help agriculture go carbon neutral . <p> SHAKE funds and trains entrepreneurs and start-ups who have innovative solutions to tackle climate change linked to the agri-food sector , which contributes more than a quarter of global emissions . <p> The three tech firms were chosen from an initial 17 applicants last summer . Of this initial cohort , ten were chosen for a further three-months of intense training to build their ventures .
