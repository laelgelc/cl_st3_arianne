<p> Russian roulette is played by firing at your head a revolver that has six chambers , five empty and one containing a bullet , presumed to be lethal . <p> The odds are not attractive and there are no living addicts . The game is too risky . But you at least know the odds so it is a game of risk rather than one of uncertainty . <p> If there were 99 empty chambers and just one bullet , the risk would be much lower and the game more popular , although not for everyone . <p> Managing the epidemic out of lockdown is a game of uncertainty rather than one of calculable risks . <p> We know there is a bullet - the resurgence of the virus and a second lockdown - and we fear it would be catastrophic for an already-weakened economy .
