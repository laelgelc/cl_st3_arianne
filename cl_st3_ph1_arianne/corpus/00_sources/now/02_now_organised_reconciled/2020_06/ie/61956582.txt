<h> Tepid reaction from organisations to programme for government <p> IFA President Tim Cullinan said the Programme for Government has positive aspects but lacks clear commitments on budgets and spending . <p> " Overall , while there is lots of rhetoric , it lacks hard figures , " he said . <p> " In implementing the programme , farmers will be looking to this Government to support farming and bring forward climate action measures that are practical , achievable and that properly reward farmers , " he said . <p> " Farmers must be viewed as part of the climate solution , " he said . <p> " Any Climate Change Bill will have to take account of the benefits that farmers and agriculture provide in carbon sequestration . All carbon sinks including grassland , hedgerows , crops , peatlands and forestry must be fully accounted for with the most up to date science .
