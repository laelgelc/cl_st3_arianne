Imagine it lobbies as hard , but for pandemic recovery programmes to include a carbon price . Totally unrealistic ? Exactly my point . In banking , there is a big difference even in Europe between , say BNP Paribas or Lloyds Banking Group versus Intesa Sao Paulo or Danske Bank according to ShareAction . <p> But for real change to happen , we need to go beyond this superficial blaming and consider the system these financial behemoths find themselves in .
