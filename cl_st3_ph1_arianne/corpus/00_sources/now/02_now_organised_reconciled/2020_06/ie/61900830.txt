The Economist argues that the sort of " collective capitalism " espoused by the US Business Roundtable suffers from two pitfalls -- a lack of accountability and a lack of dynamism . In the case of accountability , the Economist makes the fair point that it is not clear how CEOs should know what ' society ' wants from their companies . The domination of the economy by large firms means that a small number of unrepresentative business leaders " will end up with immense power to set goals for society that range far beyond the immediate interests of their company " . It justifies private gains at the expense of public losses .
