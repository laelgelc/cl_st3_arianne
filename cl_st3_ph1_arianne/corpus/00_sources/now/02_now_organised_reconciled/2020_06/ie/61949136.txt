<h> 9 Ways to Prevent Biodiversity Loss and What Causes It <p> There is so much life on this planet that it 's estimated 86 percent of species are still undiscovered . Thanks to humans , though , we may never get the chance to find them all . Biodiversity loss is becoming a bigger problem than we ever thought it could be . It 's estimated that half of all the species on the planet could go extinct by 2050 -- only 32 years from now . What is biodiversity loss , and why is it such a big problem ? <h> What is biodiversity loss ? <p> Biodiversity is defined as the totality of genes , species , and ecosystems in a defined area . Everything from the smallest single-celled organism to the largest apex predator makes up the biodiversity of a given area . <p> Biodiversity loss , on the other hand , is the death of those ecosystems . Forest fires , floods , and volcanic eruptions all have the potential to destroy an ecosystem in a given area . However , these natural types of biodiversity loss are normal -- and the planet has a plan in place to restore them once the damage has passed . Some seeds , like those from a number of pine trees , wo n't even germinate unless their parent tree has burned in a wildfire . <p> Those are n't the kinds of biodiversity loss we should be concerned about . <p> Human intervention has caused the majority of biodiversity loss in the last few decades .
