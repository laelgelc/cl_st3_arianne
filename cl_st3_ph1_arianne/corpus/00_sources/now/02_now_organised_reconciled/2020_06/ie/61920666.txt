<h> EC presented with plan for capital markets union ' game-changers ' <p> The European Commission has been presented with a plan for " game-changer " measures to take to realise the creation of a capital markets union ( CMU ) in the EU , according to the High-Level Forum ( HLF ) behind the recommendations , with " remarkable " attention seen being paid to pensions in the group 's report . <p> Established by the European Commission last year , the HLF on the CMU today set out 17 clusters of recommendations in its final report , describing them as " the game-changers " , because they were " what the EU needs to implement urgently in order to tackle the most serious barriers in its capital markets " . <p> The recommendations span the full spectrum of capital market activities , grouped by the HLF under the headings of : financing business ; market infrastructure ; individual investors ' engagement ; and obstacles to cross-border investment . <p> " The report does not contain abstract ideas or high level principles that should be achieved , " said Thomas Wieser , chair of the Forum .
