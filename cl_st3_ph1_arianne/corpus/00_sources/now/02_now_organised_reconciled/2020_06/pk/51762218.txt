<h> Highlights of PSDP 2019-20 <p> Following are the highlights of allocations under the Public Sector Development Programme ( PSDP ) for various ministries and divisions for the fiscal year 2020-21 announced by the Federal Government here Friday : <p> -- Total outlay of the PSDP for the fiscal year 2020-21 is Rs 1.324 trillion <p> -- The share of federal PSDP is Rs 650 billion while that of provincial PSDP is Rs 674 billion . <p> -- Rs 1320 million has been earmarked for Aviation Division . <p> -- Board of Investment will get Rs 80 million . <p> -- Rs 47,802 million has been earmarked for Cabinet Division . <p> -- The Climate Change Division will get Rs 5,000 million . <p> -- Rs 103 million has been set aside for Commerce Division . <p> -- Rs 254 million are allocated for Communication Division ( other than NHA ) .
