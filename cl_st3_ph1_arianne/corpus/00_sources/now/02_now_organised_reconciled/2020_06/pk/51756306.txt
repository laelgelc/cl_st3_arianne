<h> Climate change in KP leads to 30pc drop in wheat production <p> PESHAWAR : Climate change in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa has led to a 30 per cent drop in wheat production , creating fears of food shortage in the province . <p> Experts said heavy rains and hailstorms this year had adversely impacted the wheat crop , with yields reducing by 25pc to 30pc . <p> Talking to this scribe , KP Agriculture Dept Director-General Abid Kamal said that the province had set a target of 1.35 million tonnes of wheat for this year , but owing to climate-related problems , it failed to achieve the desired results . <p> " The province now estimates the production of wheat at 1.25 million tonnes , " he added . <p> Meanwhile , the Federal Ministry of National Food Security &amp; Research had estimated wheat production at 27 million tonnes for FY20 .
