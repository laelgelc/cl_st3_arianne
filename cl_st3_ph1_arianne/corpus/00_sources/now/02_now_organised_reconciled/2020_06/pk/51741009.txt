<h> ' All green areas of capital part of MHNP ' <p> ISLAMABAD : In a landmark move , the federal government has decided to identify all green areas in the federal capital and include them in the Margalla Hills National Park ( MHNP ) . <p> Moreover , Prime Minister Imran Khan has taken notice of cars being parked in the no-parking zones of the city -- including on greenbelts . <p> The decision came after the Adviser to the PM on Climate Change Malik Amin Aslam met with the premier on Tuesday . <p> During the meeting , PM Imran directed the adviser , the Capital Development Authority ( CDA ) and the Islamabad Capital Territory Administration ( ICTA ) to take steps to end parking on the green belts and other areas of the city .
