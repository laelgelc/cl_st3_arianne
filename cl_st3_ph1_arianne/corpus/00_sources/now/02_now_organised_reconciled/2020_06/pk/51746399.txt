<h> Incentives for Electric Vehicles in Pakistan <p> The Prime Minister 's Committee on Climate Change in its first meeting held on May 17 , 2019 , decided that " Climate Change Division shall , in consultation with all relevant stakeholders , examine the practical and financial viability of the " Incentive Package " to introduce Electric Vehicles in Pakistan and policy on EVs shall be placed before the Cabinet " . <p> In line with the above-mentioned directions , the Ministry of Climate Change undertook a number of consultative sessions with the relevant stakeholders ( including FBR , Petroleum Division , Oil refineries/OMCs , and EDB , M/o Industries ) to formulate the National Electric Vehicle Policy . <p> The Electric Vehicle Policy was submitted to the Cabinet after intensive consultations with relevant stakeholders . <p> The Cabinet approved the National Electric Vehicle Policy in its meeting held on November 5 , 2019 . <p> These EV penetration targets are as follows :
