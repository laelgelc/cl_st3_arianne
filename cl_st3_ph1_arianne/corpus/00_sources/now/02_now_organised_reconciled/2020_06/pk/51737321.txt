<h> Omar Ayub inaugurates Euro V Petroleum Products Testing Lab <p> In his remarks on the occasion , the minister said that the Euro IV and V Petrol will be available in the Country in accordance with Prime Minister Imran Khan 's vision for a clean and green environment . <p> Minister for Power Division Omar Ayub Khan <p> The Minister for Power Division said that clean fuel is need of the hour to protect the Country from adverse impacts of the Climate Change . <p> Omar Ayub said that the Testing Lab Project has been prepared at a cost of Rs 304 million under which such facilities will be established in Islamabad , Lahore , Multan , Peshawar , and Quetta . <p> The federal minister said that the Petroleum Testing Lab established in Islamabad has the International Organisation for Standardization ( ISO ) Certification .
