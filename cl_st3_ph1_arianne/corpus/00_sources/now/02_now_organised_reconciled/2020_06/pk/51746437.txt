<p> ISLAMABAD , Pakistan : A totalnumber of 243.76 million trees were planted from Monsoon 2018 to Monsoon 2019 under the Ten Billion Tree Tsunami Programme as part of Prime Minister 's GreenProgramme Initiative , the National Assembly was told . <p> Replying to a question in the House on Friday , the Minister of State for Climate Change Zartaj Gul told that 147.32 million trees were planted in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa , 38.69 in Punjab , 32.50 in Sindh , and 13.92 in Azad Jammu and Kashmir ( AJK ) during the said period . <p> The minister also told that the plantation target for Spring 2020 was fixed as 240 million plants . <p> Zartaj Gul said that an amount of Rs 7.146 billion was released to provinces/territories under the Ten Billion Tree Tsunami Programme . <p> It included an amount of Rs 3.750 billion released from the federal public sector development programme ( PSDP ) whereas Rs 3.396 billion were released from the provincial annual development programme ( ADP ) , she said . <p> Apprising about the criteria of selection fixed for the areas for planting trees , the minister told that the provinces/territories selected sites @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> Conservation and enhancement of natural forests through assisted natural regeneration : <p> Road and canal side plantation ; <p> Rehabilitation and re-stocking of historical plantations <p> Restoration and improvement of scrub forests ; <p> Increase in existing cover of mangrove forests : <p> Watershed and soil conservation in hilly and river catchment areas ( reserved as well as community forests ) ;
