" <p> " As regards to sustainability of the system , Nigeria ranks also in the bottom third . It is advised that the harmonization of the retirement ages of the various professions and adjusting the retirement age in line with future increase in life expectancy would improve the long-term sustainability of the pension system further , " the report noted . <p> Furthermore , the report stated that among the analysed countries , Nigeria has by far the most comfortable starting position especially due the fact that it has one of the youngest populations worldwide . <p> " The number of people aged 65 and older is set to increase from 5.6 million today to around 16 million in 2050 . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a pension system with a broad coverage and for further improvement on the access to financial services . <p> " Retirement age is an important lever .
