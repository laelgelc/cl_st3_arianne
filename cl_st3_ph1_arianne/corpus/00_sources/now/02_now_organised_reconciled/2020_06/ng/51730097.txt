<h> Making Waste Management More Effective , Profitable In Enugu State <p> As the world marks the Environment Day on June 5 , 2020 , there is need to re-examine our various practices in relation with the environment . As David Attenborough , the English broadcaster and historian , remarked , " None will protect what they do n't care about , and no one will care about what they have never experienced . " <p> What we experience as citizens of the state , country and the world deserves more care and attention from us than ever before . Climate change is turning the world on its head , and we must not shy away from the realities . Every society is desirous of development . But improvement on the quality of human lives and resources across societies has shown to be a product of passion and commitment of visionary leadership . <p> Among indices of development , environmental sustainability seems one of the less focused concerns of some societies , although it forms a vital fabric of societal existence . And the attitude of some politicians and policy-makers , particularly in developing countries , clearly shows that they do not understand the gravity of environmental crisis facing society . <p> However , it is commendable that some state governments are demonstrating visionary leadership in this regard by empowering under their watch government agencies , non-governmental organizations , and activists having stakes in environmental concerns . The Enugu State government has demonstrated an appreciable level of effort , collaborative spirit and innovative strategy in delivering environmental sustainability . Through its environmental agency , Enugu State Waste Management ( ESWAMA ) , a public agency saddled with the responsibility of promoting waste management in the state , the state has recorded a commendable progress in maintaining environmental best practices both in rural and urban settlements . <p> In 2014 , the operational efficiency of Enugu State Waste @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ carried out by Benjamin Uwadiegwu and Edmund Iyi , which was published in the European-American Journals . The governor of the state , the Rt Hon Ifeanyi Ugwuanyi , using a mitigation plan , ordered the constitution of a task force to assist ESWAMA . The task force is headed by the chief of staff to the governor , Dr Festus Uzor , and the senior special assistant to the governor on special duties , Mr Sam Ugwuozor , and supervised by Oscar Ukwume , an SSA to the governor and certified environmental consultant . <p> This development succeeded in laying the roadmap for the massive reforms as recorded today in the waste and sanitation management system at the state and local government areas . In Enugu South Local Government Area , for instance , an Environmental Responsibility Marshal Committee was recently inaugurated by the SSA to the governor , Hon Oscar Okwume , with directive from the state governor . More sustainability education is needed to enable citizens to understand the impact of environmental degradation , especially in the form of littering , inappropriate waste disposal and other forms of pollution . <p> The state government and its agency ( ESWAMA ) must do well to leverage public private partnership to effectively deliver robust environmental education to Enugu residents . Civil society organisations ( CSOs ) , sustainability advocates and other concerned individuals in the state should be drafted into the environmental sustainability project . Governments across the world are turning wastes into gold mines . Robust partnership with recycling companies can help improve waste management practice in the state .
