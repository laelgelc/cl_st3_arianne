<h> Murtala Muhammed Airport achieves milestone <p> The Murtala Muhammed Airport in Lagos State , operated by the Federal Airports Authority of Nigeria ( FAAN ) has achieved a milestone . <p> TheNewsGuru.com ( TNG ) reports the airport has joined the community of climate smart airports within Airport Carbon Accreditation as the first in Nigeria . <p> The airport received a level 1 ' Mapping ' recognition for creating a complete and independently verified carbon inventory , and outlining plans on how to reduce the emissions . <p> In June 2008 , the annual assembly of ACI EUROPE adopted a landmark resolution on Climate Change when its member airports committed to reduce carbon emissions from their operations , with the ultimate goal of becoming carbon neutral . <p> One year later , at the 2009 annual assembly , ACI EUROPE launched Airport Carbon Accreditation , allowing the assessment and recognition of participating airports ' efforts to manage and reduce their CO2 emissions . <p> Airport Carbon Accreditation is an independent , voluntary programme administered by WSP , an international consultancy appointed by ACI EUROPE to enforce the accreditation criteria for airports on an annual basis .
