<h> Vinayaki was her name <h> Share this article <p> I WOULD like to dedicate this article to the pregnant elephant that was abused beyond imagination by locals in Kerala 's Palakkad district . She was later named Vinayaki . She was only 15 years old . <p> Vinayaki had left the national park in the Silent Valley and meandered into a nearby village in search of food . It is believed that she had eaten an explosive-laden pineapple , as farmers in India 's south often use firecrackers and explosives in fruits to protect their land and crops from wild animals . <p> So powerful was the explosion that her tongue and mouth were destroyed , and according to forestry officer Mohan Krishan , she must have walked around in the village for days , in pain and hunger . She was unable to eat anything because of her injuries . He added that " she must have been more worried about the child inside her than her own hunger " . <p> On May 27 , Vinayaki succumbed to her debilitating injuries .
