<h> " First of its kind " memorial tree installed at Altrincham Crematorium <p> A new " memorial tree " -- the first of its kind in the country -- has been installed in Altrincham Crematorium 's Garden of Remembrance . <p> The weeping willow-style creation stands one and a half metres tall and was designed by Hull-based Columbaria with families in mind . <p> Councillor Stephen Adshead , Executive Member for Environment , Air Quality and Climate Change , said : " This tree is the first of its kind in the country so it is very special to everyone at the Crematorium . <p> " It gives people a chance to have a permanent memorial to their loved one . It is a lovely setting to sit , relax and reflect . " <p> The unique memorial tree was designed with families in mind <p> The granite leaves provide space for people to inscribe the names and dates of loved ones . <p> There is also the opportunity to have cremated remains buried in the surrounding area with wild flower seeds to help create a colourful area where families can sit and remember .
