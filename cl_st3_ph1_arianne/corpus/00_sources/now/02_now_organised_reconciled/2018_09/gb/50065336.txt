<h> LETTER : Water is the main cause of erosion on the fells <h> LETTER : Water is the main cause of erosion on the fells <p> I write the report ' Call for 4x4s ban in Lakes ' ( Gazette , August 16 ) , which included the subhead , referring to the view of World Heritage Watch , ' Off-roading dubbed inappropriate for WHS ' . <p> They are obviously unaware that off-roading without legal authority is a criminal offence in the UK and police have powers to seize vehicles from offenders . <p> What we are talking about is the legal use of an unsealed road by vehicles . The argument is more about the sustainability of the route and/or the lack of suitable maintenance given the current level of use . <p> The WHW says the issue caused by 4x4 using the routes is soil compaction and erosion yet the photograph used with the report shows the bedrock beneath a vehicle 's wheels ! <p> The single greatest erosive force is simply water and 50 years of neglect by local authorities has allowed drains to fail and rainwater run-off destroy routes .
