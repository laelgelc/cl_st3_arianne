But none of the natural causes of climate variation , from the Sun 's output , the tilt of the Earth , volcanic activity or emissions from rotting vegetation , can account for the warming we observe today . There is only one thing that can : the emissions from fossil fuels caused by human activities over the last two hundred years . The concentrations of carbon dioxide in our atmosphere have increased by nearly 30% since the beginning of the industrial revolution . <h> It 's not as bad as you think -- it 's worse <p> Here 's another inconvenient truth .
