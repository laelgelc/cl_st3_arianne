However , Sander van der Linden and Cameron Brick , two researchers at the department of Psychology , argue that the climate change challenge is primarily a social and psychological one : to stop the earth from warming , we need a change of heart . <p> " Climate change is the largest social dilemma in history , " says van der Linden . " It 's amazing how powerful just five pence is , " says Brick , " people really want to avoid paying it .
