Both Richard and Andrew are friends , they have both been my editor and I admire them both as superlative journalists -- so the post was rightly an intimidating one , and one which absorbed every waking moment of my life . <p> It was strange to take the helm of a paper I had joined nearly 20 years earlier at its birth and rose up through its ranks -- and difficult to go from being part of the team , albeit second-in-command , to captain of the ship . Just the other day , I was looking through a cardboard box filled with the first batch of notes and studies laying out the vision for the Sunday Herald .
