<h> Growing numbers of Scots concerned about climate change <h> Growing numbers of Scots concerned about climate change <p> THE number of adults in Scotland who believe climate change is an urgent problem has risen by one-third since 2013 , statistics show . <p> Six in 10 adults ( 61% ) in 2017 said that they viewed it as an immediate issue , an increase of 6% in one year , according to the annual Scottish Household Survey published by Scotland 's chief statistician . <p> The proportion of 16-24 year olds who view climate change as an immediate problem increased by more than half between 2013 and 2017 , from 38% to 58% , the largest increase of any age group . <p> Dr Sam Gardner , acting director of WWF Scotland , said : " It 's no surprise a growing number of people are concerned about climate change .
