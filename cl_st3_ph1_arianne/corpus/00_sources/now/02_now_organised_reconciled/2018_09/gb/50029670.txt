<p> The heatwave in the UK may have caused hundreds of extra deaths over the summer , new figures reveal . <p> Between June 21 and June 22 , there were 214 fewer deaths than the five-year average for the same period , a quarterly report by the Office for National Statistics ( ONS ) shows . <p> However , there were 259 more deaths than average in England between June 25 and 26 , coinciding with hot weather which triggered a Public Health England ( PHE ) heatwave alert . <p> A high of 30.1C was recorded in London on June 25 , making it the UK 's hottest day of the year so far at that point . <p> Maximum temperatures exceeded 24.5C until the end of June , above the level at which Public Health England ( PHE ) warns heat-related deaths may occur . <p> During that period , there were 382 more
