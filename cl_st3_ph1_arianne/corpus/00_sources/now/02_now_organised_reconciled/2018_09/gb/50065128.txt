<h> People of faith ' must act over climate change ' <p> Sunday , 2nd September 2018 <p> A Christian climate change charity is urging the Church to speak out on the issue and " make its moral voice heard " . <p> Nicky Bull , CEO at Operation Noah said : " Women , children and the poor make up the vast majority of those already bearing the consequences of climate change today , and for low-lying island nations , climate change increasingly threatens the lives and livelihoods of those least responsible for today 's climate crisis . <p> " On this most pressing injustice , the Church today can , and must , make its moral voice heard . " Ms Bull 's call comes as the charity launched a thought-provoking animated film , Salote , to help Christians around the world recognise the human cost of a changing climate , and its particular impact on women and children . <p> While most adults in the UK think that climate change is real and caused primarily by human activity , many Christians do n't yet see this as a faith issue .
