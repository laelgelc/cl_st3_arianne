It is three years since the Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) were agreed : a set of transformative targets to reshape our world by 2030 . With these goals , every year really does count . The clock ticks . <p> We can be optimistic . Important progress is relentlessly happening - often not reported , but remarkable . In Africa , fewer mothers are dying in childbirth than ever before , and in Asia fewer children are being forced into early marriage . <p> But a new report by the Overseas Development Institute and International Rescue Committee is alarming . The key finding is that people who are caught up in conflict or disaster are being left behind . One in seven of the world 's population now live in fragile or conflict-affected countries , and 68.5 million people are unable to live in their homes .
