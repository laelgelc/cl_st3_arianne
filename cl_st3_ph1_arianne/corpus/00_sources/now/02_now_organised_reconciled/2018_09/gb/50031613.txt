<h> Number of dirty diesels on road still growing , report shows <p> The number of dirty diesel vehicles pumping out toxic emissions on Europe 's streets is still rising three years after the Dieselgate scandal began , according to a new report . More than seven million such cars and vans remain on UK roads alone . <p> Car manufacturers were fined billions in the US and Germany , but have yet to pay any significant financial penalty in the UK for selling polluting vehicles <p> There were 29 million diesel vehicles whose emissions on the road were significantly higher than official laboratory-based EU limits when the diesel scandal erupted in September 2015 . But that has now risen by to 43 million , according to analysis by the group Transport &amp; Environment ( T&amp;E ) . France has the dirtiest diesels on the road , with 8.7 million , followed by Germany 's 8.2 million and the UK 's 7.3 million . <p> Experts say the number of polluting diesel cars will continue rising until September 2019 , when new EU regulations force older models out of showrooms .
