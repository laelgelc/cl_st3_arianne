In July 2018 , the company pledged to stop offering cover to companies and projects which rely on coal for more than 30% of their revenues or more than 30% of the power they generate . <p> The paper claims that Swiss Re is the only reinsurer of the five to restrict cover for coal companies as well as coal projects . None of the groups currently restrict cover based on the absolute scale and development plans of companies ' coal activities , the report states . <p> " Reinsurers have warned about the risks of runaway climate change for decades , " Unfriend Coal 's European co-ordinator Lucie Pinson said .
