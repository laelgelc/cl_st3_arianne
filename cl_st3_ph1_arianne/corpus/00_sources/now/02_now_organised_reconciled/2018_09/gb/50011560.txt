<h> Water supply in South East set to be half of demand by 2030 <p> Action needs to be taken to prevent water supply in the South East only matching 50 per cent of demand by 2030 , Southern Water has warned . <p> The water company , which operates throughout Sussex , cited climate change and population growth as key factors that would affect its operations in years to come . <p> To protect against a future shortfall , the company 's business plan for 2020-2025 outlines strategies to reduce water consumption such as encouraging the use of smart meters and reducing leakage by 15 per cent . <p> An introduction to the plan read : " Twenty-five years from now we will have lost a third of our water sources through climate change , seen a reduction in the amount of water we are allowed to take from rivers and underground sources , and our population will have grown by 15 per cent . <p> " Without action , we predict a supply and demand deficit by 2030 equivalent to around 50 per cent of our current supply .
