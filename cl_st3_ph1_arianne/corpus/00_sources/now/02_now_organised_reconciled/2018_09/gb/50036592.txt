<h> Cyclists demand a tenth of new transport budget <p> Campaigners say that increased investment in cycling would would be beneficial for people 's health , traffic congestion and the **25;761;TOOLONG <p> Cycling safety campaigners have called for a tenth of transport funding to be allocated to cycling . <p> Cyclist.ie , a national lobby group , said in a pre-budget submission that investing in cycling would help to deal with climate change , improve the health of citizens and tackle congestion . It said more people would take up cycling if there was better infrastructure . <p> Between 2 and 4 per cent of the transport budget is spent on cycling and walking projects . Cyclist.ie called for this to be increased to 10 per cent , which it said would bring Ireland in line with other European countries . The United Nations recommends spending 20 per cent of transport budgets on non-motorised or " active " travel .
