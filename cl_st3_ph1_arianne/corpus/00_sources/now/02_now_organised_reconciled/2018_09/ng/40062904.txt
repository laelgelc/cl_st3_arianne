<h> Buhari , Wife Depart For UN General Assembly <p> President Muhammadu Buhari will today depart for New York , U.S.A. , to participate in the 73rd Session of United Nations General Assembly ( UNGA73 ) which officially opened September 18 , 2018 . The theme for this year 's session is : " Making the United Nations relevant to all People : Global Leadership and Shared Responsibilities for Peaceful , Equitable and Sustainable Societies . " The president 's wife , Aisha , will also depart for New York to participate in the Organisation of African First Ladies ( OAFLA ) against HIV/AIDS meeting . According to a statement by his special adviser on media , Femi Adesina , the highpoint of President Buhari 's participation would be his address to the General Assembly on the opening day of the General Debate on Tuesday . <p> The president 's presentation of Nigeria 's National Statement is expected to reaffirm the nation 's commitment to international peace and security , sustainable socio-economic development , disarmament and denuclearisation , youth and women empowerment , climate change , rule of law and human rights , among others .
