<h> Saving the ozone layer <p> Yesterday , Nigeria joined the rest of the global community in celebrating this year 's World Ozone Day . The theme for this year 's commemoration is " Keep Cool and Carry On . " In the 1980s and 1990s , the gaping holes in the ozone layer threw down a scary phenomenon . The scary prospect was discovered by the scientists precisely in 1985 as the fallout of human activities such as ozone-depleting substances ( ODS ) widely used in refrigeration and air-conditioning aerosols . The ozone layer hangs high up in the atmosphere between 10 and 40 kilometres above the earth surface . The region is called stratosphere and it contains about 90 per cent of all ozone in the atmosphere with the capacity to absorb some of the sun 's biologically harmful ultraviolet radiation . Disturbed by the emerging man-made enemy , policymakers and scientists from around the world scrambled to Montreal , Canada , to hammer out a Protocol which was adopted in 1987 . Under the agreement , the use of ozonedepleting substances or potent global-warming gases was phased out around the world and huge successes were recorded @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 98 per cent of identified ODS have now been eliminated . Scientists have also predicted that the " healing " might return the ozone layer and the global climate to the 1980 levels by around 2050 if the protocol is not breached , thus averting the catastrophe that may result in over two million cases of skin cancer yearly by 2030 . On December 19 , 1994 , the United Nations General Assembly proclaimed September 16 every year as International Day for the Prevention of the Ozone Layer to commemorate the same date on which the Montreal Protocol on Ozone Depleting Substances ( ODS ) was signed . The protocol which has been substantially ratified between 1990 and 1992 stipulates that the production and consumption of compounds that deplete ozone in the stratosphere are to be phased out in a specified period of time . The ODS are widely used in refrigerators , air-conditioners , in dry cleaning , as solvents for cleaning electronic equipment and as agricultural fumigants . However , experts say it would take between 50 and 100 years for the holes to disappear completely if the rules are strictly followed . It is sad to note that the recourse to the usage of hydro-fluorocarbons ( HFCs ) as substitutes for ODS has also been discovered to be more extremely deadly climate-warming gases that can be thousands of times more potent than carbon dioxide in contributing to climate change . The new reality triggered off the Kigali Amendment to the Protocol , to be effective from January 1 , next year , aimed at getting ratifying countries to cut their production and consumption of HFCs by more than 30 per cent over the next 30 years . Recent studies have also shown that preserving peat lands is also very necessary not only for keeping carbon dioxide in the ground but also to prevent the emission of nitro oxide which has turned out to be one of the biggest contributors to ozone depletion all over the world . This necessitated the first summit on Climate Change in 1992 under the auspices of the United Nations held in Rio de Janeiro , Brazil . More than 160 countries attended the Conference which aimed at averting the global catastrophe that the phenomenon portended for humanity . Similar conferences have been held in countries like Denmark and the United States at various times to review and ratify the Rio de Janeiro Protocols as the man-made condition presents frightening challenges to the global community . The race against climate change , however , suffered a terrible setback recently when President Donald Trump of the United States pulled out of the race , describing the phenomenon as a hoax despite the potent dangers it presents . It is within man 's power to reduce the emissions that are causing climate change and to catalyse a new era of global greenhouse growth .
