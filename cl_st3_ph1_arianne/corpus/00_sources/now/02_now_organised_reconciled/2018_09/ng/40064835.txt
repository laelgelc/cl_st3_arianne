<h> Tackling climate change to be key talking point at UN summit <p> BERLIN ( AP ) -- Global temperatures are rising . Superstorms are taking their deadly toll . There 's a year-end deadline to firm up the Paris climate deal . So leaders at this year 's U.N . General Assembly are feeling some urgency to keep up the momentum on combating climate change . <p> That 's why they 'll be devoting substantial time in New York this week to the question of global warming and how to rein it in . <p> There 'll be talk of emissions targets and the need to adapt to the inevitable changes already underway when small island states take the floor . <p> Ministers from major economies , meanwhile , will be meeting behind closed doors to discuss who will pay to help poor countries avoid the worst effects of global warming -- and prevent a wave of climate refugees in future .
