<p> The United Nations Development Organisation ( UNIDO ) has unveiled a four-year science-oriented development plan , covering 2018 to 2022 , to promote industrial expansion in Nigeria . <p> During the launch in Abuja , Reuben Bamidele of the global agency listed the areas of interest to include poverty reduction , metal and minerals , trade capacity building , sustainable energy and agribusiness . <p> Others are industrial governance , research and statistics , special economic zones , such as industrial parks , as well as private sector development and environmental management . <p> " UNIDO is collaborating with the Nigerian government on a number of projects , including ' Global Clean Technology Innovation ' for micro small medium entrepreneurs and MSMEs ' development , " he stated . <p> Bamidele added that his organisation would provide support to the Federal Government during the technology needs assessment process as well as develop a roadmap and strategy for climate change management .
