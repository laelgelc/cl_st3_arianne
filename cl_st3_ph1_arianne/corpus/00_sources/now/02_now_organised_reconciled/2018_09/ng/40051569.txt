<h> California moves to stop hackers from taking control of smart gadgets <p> The state of California has been a pioneer when it comes to shaping policies to tackle everything from climate change to consumer privacy . Now it could take the lead in yet another area : cybersecurity for online gadgets . <p> The state 's lawmakers have just sent California 's governor , Jerry Brown , draft legislation that aims to tighten the security of web-connected devices . <p> If he approves it , California will become the first US state with a law specifically tailored for the internet of things ( IoT ) . <p> It 's not hard to see why such legislation is needed . Barely a day goes by without some new report of hackers compromising all kinds of products , from web-connected dolls to security cameras .
