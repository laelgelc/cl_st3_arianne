<h> UN Agency , Nigeria Signs Regional Host Right Agreement <p> The Global Meteorological Organisation ( WMO ) , Wednesday , signed a Memorandum of Understanding ( MoU ) with Nigeria to host its regional office for West , Central and North Africa . WMO is the United Nations ' specialised agency for meteorology ( weather and climate ) operational hydrology and related geophysical sciences . It has more than 191 Member States and Territories . The Minister of Foreign Affairs , Geoffrey Onyeama , signed on behalf of Nigeria while the WMO Secretary General , Petteri Taalas , signed on behalf of the organisation . According to Taalas , with the MOU , Nigeria will serve the interest of 27 countries by boosting early warning capacity and addressing the challenge of climate change in affected regions . He lamented that climate change has increased disasters of most African countries which he said had affected their agriculture and economy .
