Out the window , massive chunks of broken ice looked like salt flakes on the water . <p> The March mission was part of NASAs Oceans Melting Greenland ( OMG ) project , a five-year , $30 million effort aimed at improving sea level rise projections by understanding how warming oceans are melting ice sheets from below -- the most ambitious research on the subject to date . <p> Rising seas threaten low-lying cities , islands and industries worldwide . A rate of 1.5 meters would swamp the homes of more than 150 million people worldwide , the study estimated . <p> A 2016 study published in a journal of the U.S. National Academy of Sciences predicted that an event comparable to Superstorm Sandy , which flooded large swaths of the New York region in 2012 , would be up to 17 times more likely by 2100 if seas rise by 1 meter . <p> Higher seas are already creating more dangerous storm surges , the IPCC says , exacerbating flooding or coastal @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ China .
