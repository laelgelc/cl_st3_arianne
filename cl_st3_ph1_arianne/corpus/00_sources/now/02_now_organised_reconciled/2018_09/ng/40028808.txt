<h> Global Hunger Continues to Rise , Says New UN Report <p> New evidence continues to signal that the number of hungry people in the world is growing , reaching 821 million in 2017 or one in every nine people , according to the State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World 2018 released on Tuesday . <p> Limited progress is also being made in addressing the multiple forms of malnutrition , ranging from child stunting to adult obesity , putting the health of hundreds of millions of people at risk . <p> Hunger has been on the rise over the past three years , returning to levels from a decade ago . <p> This reversal in progress sends a clear warning that more must be done and urgently if the Sustainable Development Goal of Zero Hunger is to be achieved by 2030 . <p> The situation is worsening in South America and most regions of Africa , while the decreasing trend in undernourishment that characterised Asia seems to be slowing down significantly .
