<h> A disaster long foretold <p> The Igbo have a proverb , when translated loosely , which speaks to the need to take needful action after being forewarned in order to avert a seeming misfortune or tragedy . It says , " O bu nani osisi ka ana-agwa na aga-egbutu , o noro ebe ono . " Meaning , it is only a tree that refuses to make a move when told it would be mowed down . <p> But as events show daily , it is obvious it is not only a tree that remains unperturbed when told tragedy is lurking in the horizon , Nigeria also does . This we can glean from the raging floods that have ravaged many parts of the country in recent weeks , leading to the declaration of national disaster in four states by the Federal Government on Monday . We were forewarned , but we were not forearmed . <p> On May 11 , the Nigeria Hydrological Services Agency released the 2018 Annual Flood Outlook in 35 states of the country , which projected that Sokoto , Niger , Benue , Anambra , Delta , Anambra , Ogun , Osun , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ flooding . The outlook further warned that Lagos , Bayelsa , Rivers , Delta , Ondo states might likely experience coastal flooding . <p> The acting Director-General of NiHSA , Olayinka Ogunwale , reportedly said that out of the 35 states , 318 local government areas would be affected , while about 78 of them would have high risk of floods . The Minister of Water Resources , Suleiman Adamu , while presenting the outlook , noted that the River Basins , which are the custodians of the dams , have provided necessary warning . <p> The projected flooding , the agency indicated , was consequent upon a " likely rise in the sea level and tidal surge , which would impact fishing and coastal transportation . " Flash and urban flood , it also predicted , were expected to occur in Port Harcourt , Sokoto , Lagos , Ibadan , Kaduna , Yola , Abuja , Maiduguri , Makurdi , Calabar , Jos , Owerri , Osogbo and Ilorin . He cautioned that some dams in the country were getting silted up , with the storage capacity also reducing , causing a lot of the water to be spilled through the waterways . <p> The NiHSA had , on its website , updated " the general public , especially the riparian states and communities by the banks of Rivers Niger and Benue and their floodplains " , that " there has been systematic daily rise in the water levels on both rivers Niger and Benue . Records from our measuring stations in Lokoja ( River Niger ) and Makurdi ( River Benue ) clearly show this . This situation calls for watchfulness on the part of the riparian states as there is still likelihood of occurrence of river flooding . " <p> On September 13 , the National Emergency Management Agency , raised the alarm on imminent flooding disasters in the country , calling for " adequate preparation " . <p> Speaking at a stakeholders ' forum in Lagos , the Zonal Coordinator of NEMA , South West , Suleiman Yakubu , reiterated that with the " current rise in water level in the River Niger area and increased persistent rainfall , Nigeria may experience flood disasters higher than that of 2012 . " <p> He said , " Both Kainji and Jebba dams are already spilling water downstream with the level of water in Lokoja downstream of the confluence standing at 8.69m . This value has exceeded the corresponding value of 8.57m that occurred on August 29 , 2012 . " <p> For those who did not know , the 2012 flooding , NEMA said , led to the death of 363 people , displaced 2.1 million residents and affected 7.7 million people in 30 of the 36 states of the country while the economic losses were put at N2.5tn @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ two million people unaccounted for , it was reported . <p> This was enough to send any responsible government scampering for measures in preparation for the doomsday . But , none was done , apparently . We are used to , as a people , not taking decisive actions , timeously . <p> Characteristic of the way we run things in Nigeria , the floods came as predicted , but unlike the Biblical Noah who built an ark when forewarned , we went about our normal ways , unprepared . At best politicking . And frolicking . And if at all we prepared , the dysfunctionality of our half-hearted measures was brought to the fore . If at all we learnt anything from the 2012 disaster , our actions this time round never displayed such knowledge . The result is what is on our lap right now : Another unmitigated disaster across the country ! According to an official , " the four states are in a very bad condition ; the remaining states are being monitored , but if the conditions of the remaining eight states ( deteriorate ) , they would also be declared a national disaster . " Disturbingly , there is no sign of abatement . <p> On Tuesday , the agency advised " residents in seven flood-prone communities in Ohaji/Egbema and Oguta Local Government Areas of Imo State to quit their homes to prevent any calamity . Last year , Governor Rochas Okorocha was more interested in creating a Ministry of Happiness , with his sister , Ogechi , in charge , than applying his mind to the forewarned disaster by creating a Ministry of Climate Change . As they say , that is the way we roll . <p> While Okorocha did not take any action of note after a series of early warning signals from the relevant agencies , his Kogi State counterpart , Yahaya Bello , who rushed to Abuja to seek the President 's help this week , resorted to " praying that the situation will not exceed " the 2012 experience , after admitting that " between nine and10 local governments are under water " ! And this is even after @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . To him , prayer is the key to unlocking the mystery of climate change . Even Noah did not think that way . Instead , he built an ark to save himself and his family . <p> In the aftermath of the Monday declaration of national disaster in the aforementioned states , the Director-General of NEMA , Mustapha Maihaja , noted that following a presidential directive , the agency had " inaugurated five Emergency Operation Centres to facilitate prompt search and rescue operations as well as humanitarian support in the 12 states worst affected by flooding . " We are always wont to react in national matters . <p> Notwithstanding these desperate reactions , what is instructive is that communities and government agencies in the country have not been taking the yearly flood outlooks seriously , a situation that has led to loss of lives and property . <p> It is petrifying that we have ensconced ourselves in the thought that climate change is a distant reality . As a result , we have failed or neglected to mainstream it in our contemporary social realities . Raging flooding in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ region are living realities that should nudge us into behavioural change , both at the personal , policy and political levels . <p> To mitigate the devastating effects of climate change , government at all levels should proactively initiate and streamline its adaptation strategies . People should stop building in flood-prone areas , or dumping on waterways . Government on its part needs to construct drainage , clean up storm water drains on an annual basis to allow water to flow , and create artificial flood plains . <p> Sadly , the rural poor who are more vulnerable are mainly at sea with issues about climate change . This should not be so . <p> We ca n't pray away the floods . The climate has changed , indeed .
