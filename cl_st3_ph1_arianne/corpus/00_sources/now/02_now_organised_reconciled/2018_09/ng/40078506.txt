<p> The African Development Bank ( www.AfDB.org ) called on global partners to join hands to lift one billion people worldwide out of hunger and said it was leading the way by investing US$24 billion in African agriculture over the next 10 years in the largest such effort ever . <p> " We are not winning the war against global hunger , " Bank President Akinwumi Adesina told an agriculture conference at Purdue University in Indianapolis on Tuesday , 25 September . <p> " We must not get carried away , " he added , referring to statistics showing a decline in the global population living on less than two dollars per day . In reality , the number of hungry people in the world had increased from 777 million in 2015 to 815 million in 2016 , he said citing the latest World Food Security and Nutrition data . <p> Adesina told the audience that included researchers , implementing organizations , business leaders , policymakers and donors that simple technical and scientific methods were already making a whole difference to farm yields and income in Africa .
