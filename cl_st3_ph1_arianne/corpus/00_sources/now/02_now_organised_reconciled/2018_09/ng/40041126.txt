<h> NEPAD To Partner S/Korea On Capacity Building , Indigenous Products <p> The Peoples ' Republic of South Korea and the New Economic Partnership for African Development ( NEPAD ) in Nigeria have agreed to enter into a partnership agreement that will allow Korean private sector entities in building capacity and promotion of indigenous products with Nigerian manufacturers . The aim is to create employment and wealth for local manufacturers in Nigeria . <p> Speaking during a courtesy visit of the Korean Ambassador to Nigeria , Major Gen. Lee In Tae ( rtd ) NEPAD 's national coordinator for Nigeria , Princess Gloria Akobundu said the collaboration is to get development partners , donors and funding agencies to key into NEPAD Nigeria programmes which cut across all sectors of the nation 's economy in line with NEPAD 's mandate . <p> This is to " create jobs for unemployed Nigerians , women/youth/vulnerable groups through skills acquisition , empowerment and capacity building , " she said , recalling that NEPAD Nigeria has been conducting advocacy on violence free elections , women participation in politics , sensitisation on the effect of climate change among other areas . He said Korea is ready to expand its presence in ICT and vocational training , with a determination to create jobs for the youths and partnering with small-scale business operators . <p> After the agreement on industrialisation and trade , four-man joint committee of the Korean embassy and NEPAD was set up to come up with a framework for the partnership . They have 14 days to produce implementation plan for the partnership .
