<h> EU Commends Nigeria 's Efforts On Climate Change <p> The European Union , EU , has commended Nigeria for its efforts at combating climate change . The EU Head of Delegation to Nigeria and ECOWAS , Kertil Karlsen made this disclosure , yesterday , at the opening of the EU Climate Change Diplomatic Week at the International Conference Centre , Abuja . <p> According to Karsen , " In the sense of trying to combat climate change , the European Union wants to commend Nigeria for taking ambitious challenges and goals . <p> " Nigeria is in a vulnerable situation due to the conflicts already mentioned , population growth and ecosystem . " <p> On what the European Union is doing to combat climate change , Karlsen said , " We are trying to take on our shoulders the responsibility of climate change . However , Europe is only a part of the world , 10 per cent of the world population . And , of course , it is important to remind ourselves that all countries , no matter the background , contribute to the best of their abilities . " <p> Speaking on why the world @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that , " Climate Change is something that , without a doubt should be on our minds because it is what defines , to a very large extent , the future of our children and their own children too . So , it is a defining matter in terms of a better world for our children . " <p> Karlsen reiterated that " What has become evidence to us is that climate change is engaging in all aspect of the society . It defines the way we run business . <p> It is directly and indirectly influencing the security . Right now , if we look at conflicts in Nigeria 's North East , we know how intrinsically this is connected to climate change issues . " <p> " The emerging food security in Nigeria is a challenge . Population growth is also a challenge . Nigeria is adding approximately , the size of my country , Denmark , every year to her population . There are examples that go beyond Nigeria to the entire world " , he added . <p> The representative of the Ministry of Environment , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ stone unturned to combat the effects of climate change .
