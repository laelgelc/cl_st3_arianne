<p> President Muhammadu Buhari on Tuesday drew the attention of world leaders to conflicts around the world and their devastating effects . <p> The President called on leaders to take urgent action to bring the conflicts to an end , warning that the situation has become worse than it was last year . <p> He made the call while addressing the United Nations General Assembly in New York . <p> Beyond the conflicts , especially in the Middle East , and the Rohingya crisis , President Buhari drew the attention of the world leaders to the effects of climate change and the need for concerted efforts to check corruption and stop illicit financial flows . <p> The President said Nigeria supports efforts by the United Nations and other partners to protect the dignity of the Rohingya and to help restore peace around the world . <p> He commended the government and people of Bangladesh and other countries that have come to the aid of the Rohingya . <p> As for the conflicts in Syria , Yemen and other countries in the Middle East , President Buhari urged the UN to push for dialogue and peaceful solutions . <p> He @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , and France for hosting the millions of refugees fleeing these brutal conflicts . <p> Beyond calling for action to end conflicts and bring peace to the world , President Buhari rebuked the abuse of power , which has caused or worsened conflicts . <p> " The deteriorating humanitarian crisis in Gaza is an appalling result of the unrestrained use of power , " he said .
