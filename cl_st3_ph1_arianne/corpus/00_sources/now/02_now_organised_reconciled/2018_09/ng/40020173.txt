<h> Climate change responsible for farmers , herders ' clashes -- Onu <p> The Minister of Science and Technology , Dr Ogbonnaya Onu , has attributed frequent clashes between farmers and herders to climate change . <p> Onu said this while declaring open the Inception Workshop on Technology Needs Assessment for Climate Change Mitigation and Adaptation in Nigeria on Monday in Abuja , according to a statement made available to one of our correspondents by the Head , Press and Public Relations Unit of the ministry , AbdulGaniyu Aminu . <p> The minister cited herdsmen/farmers ' clashes as a fallout of climate change resulting from depleting arable land . <p> He stressed the need to fight desert encroachment and other problems arising from depleting arable land . <p> Failing to do this , he said , there would be nothing to present to the future generation . <p> Onu cited China and Israel as countries that had been able to fight desertification by converting dry land to arable land , adding that they succeeded thereby in creating job opportunities and reducing poverty . <p> He said , " Climate change is not something to fear . " <p> Onu tasked the National Steering Committee on Technology Needs Assessment on Climate Change to come up with practical solutions to combat the challenges of the desert and coastal encroachment in Nigeria . <p> He also called on research institutes across the country to apply science , technology and innovation to address the challenges of climate change in the country . <p> The representative of the United Nations Industrial Development Organisation , Dr Reuben Bamidele , said that UNIDO would be available to provide support to Nigeria during the technology needs assessment process and in developing a road map and strategy for climate change technology management in Nigeria . <p> Meanwhile , the Minister of Information and Culture , Alhaji Lai Mohammed , has said that clashes between farmers and herders have now reduced in the country . <p> He said he was glad that several measures put in place by the Federal Government were responsible for the reduction . <p> " Unfortunately , this has not received the kind of media coverage that was given to the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ .
