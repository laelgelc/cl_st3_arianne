<h> NNPC records 48% reduction in pipeline vandalism <p> The Nigerian National Petroleum Corporation says it has recorded a 48.21 per cent reduction in the rate of pipeline vandalism in the month of April 2018 . The corporation disclosed this in its monthly Financial Report , released in Abuja , on Thursday . It said that vandalism fell to 166 from 224 vandalised points in the previous month . According to the report , the Aba-Enugu pipeline segment accounted for 78 vandalised points , representing 84.78 per cent of total vandalized points on the nation 's network of products pipelines . The report , the 33rd edition since NNPC commenced the monthly publication was part of efforts to instill a culture of transparency and keep stakeholders and the general public informed of its activities . It noted that the NNPC had consolidated on its operational performance with a trading surplus of ? 17.16 billion in the month under review . The report noted that the surplus indicated a ? 5.43 billion improvement representing 46.29 per cent on the trading surplus recorded in the previous month of March , 2018 .
