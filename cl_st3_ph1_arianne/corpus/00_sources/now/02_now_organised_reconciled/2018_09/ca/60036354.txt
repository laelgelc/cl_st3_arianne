<h> The Boomers and the world <h> Environmental devastation was caused by the boomer generation <p> In 1945 , the world changed . World War Two had officially ended , the ink of the politicians ' signatures had dried . Dozens of cities lay smouldering . Two were nuclear wastes . The United States had emerged as a superpower and the Soviet Union was readying as a foil . Sixty million people were dead who should not have died . <p> Between 1945 and 1965 , there was a huge spike in births worldwide . Soldiers returned home , got married , and began to reproduce in rapid numbers . The global population skyrocketed , and the children who represented this rise grew up , grew old , and took over the world while they were doing it . For decades , the Boomers have had undue influence in the world , mostly because of their demographic power .
