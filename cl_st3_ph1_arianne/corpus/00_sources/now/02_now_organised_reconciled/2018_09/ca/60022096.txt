<h> New cities may make millions more vulnerable to climate change <h> News <p> A forthcoming study of over a hundred new cities being built around the world suggests developers and planning authorities are doing very little to make their projects resilient to climate change . On the contrary , a boom in new city projects in coastal areas -- including some on reclaimed land in the sea -- appears to fly in the face of the danger of rising sea levels and more frequent extreme weather events . <p> When McGill geography professor Sarah Moser mapped 120 new cities under construction across Asia , Latin America , Africa and the Middle East , she was struck by how many of them were in vulnerable coastal areas . <p> " I think this has to do with the fact that a lot of these projects are real estate projects .
