<h> 42 B.C.-based Order of Canada recipients want the pipeline cancelled for the sake of climate change <p> 42 B.C.-based members of the Order of Canada have signed an open letter asking the federal government to cancel the Trans Mountain Pipeline expansion project . ( desirebetter.org ) <p> Filmmaker Bonnie Sherr Klein says she is turning heartbreak into action by joining 41 other B.C.-based Order of Canada recipients who have signed a letter calling on the federal government to cancel the Trans Mountain pipeline expansion project . <p> " I hesitate to use the word heartbroken but that 's how I 've been feeling after those unbreathable weeks in our city caused by the wildfires , " said Sherr Klein from her Vancouver home . <p> Steel pipe to be used in the construction of the Trans Mountain pipeline expansion project sit on rail cars in Kamloops , B.C. The plan to twin the existing 1,150 kilometre-long pipeline has been indefinitely suspended .
