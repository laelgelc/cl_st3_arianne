<h> " You Can See Almost Everything " In Antarctica , The Best-Mapped Continent on the Planet <p> Earth has a lot of good maps , even in the polar regions . There are maps that even show the thickness of the ice sheet in Antarctica . There is also a map showing the hidden bedrock in Greenland , and it is very detailed . <p> But the most amazingly detailed map on Earth , with the highest resolution ever , is the one representing Antarctica ! This continent might not be your average tourist destination , but if you plan on visiting , there is a detailed one just released by the scientists at Ohio State University and the University of Minnesota . <p> The team created this map , making Antarctica the best-mapped continent on the planet . <h> The Reference Elevation Model of Antarctica ( REMA ) <p> According to Earther , the map is called REMA , and it was constructed with hundreds of thousands of satellite images from a period between 2009 and 2017 . The team of researchers needed a supercomputer to assemble the data , to add the elevation of the land @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ most detailed topographical map ever . <p> An Ohio State press release explained that the map has a resolution of 2 to 8 meters . It 's like being able to look down and see a car , compared to the old map that allowed you to only see an area as big as Central Park . <p> At the moment , with this map that has an error margin of a few feet , scientists can know the elevation of all points on the frozen continent . <p> The head of the REMA project , glaciologist Ian Howat ( Ohio State University ) stated : <p> " Up until now , we 've had a better map of Mars than we 've had of Antarctica . At this resolution , you can see almost everything . We can actually see variations in the snow in some places . We will be able to measure changes in the surface of the continent over time . " <p> Tom Wagner , a NASA cryosphere program scientist , stated in an email to Earther that : <p> " This is AMAZING . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ granularity never before possible . " <p> This map will be the perfect tool for research projects that will need data on snow cover , the motion of ice , thinning events , river or volcano activity and monitoring of climate change effects .
