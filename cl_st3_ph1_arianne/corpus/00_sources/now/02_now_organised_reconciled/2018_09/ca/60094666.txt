<h> New Billboard Celebrates Forests and Environmental Stewardship <p> Sudbury , September 27 , 2018 -- To mark National Forest Week , representatives of Domtar Inc. , EACOM Timber Corporation and Forests Ontario gathered in Northeastern Ontario to unveil a new billboard as the most recent development in the province-wide It Takes A Forest initiative . The billboard promotes Ontario 's world-class forest management standards , as well as the importance of our forests and forest sector to the economic , social and environmental well-being of the province . <p> The It Takes A Forest initiative is a collaborative effort of over 40 organizations , designed to increase public awareness of Ontario 's forests through the dissemination of fact-based information . Whether living in big cities or rural communities , forests are the centre of our lives . They purify the air and water , they provide habitat for animals , they help fight climate change and they offer endless playgrounds to enjoy nature . Forests also provide the wood we need to build homes and make many products that make our lives better . <p> The new billboard is located just outside of Webbwood , Ontario , approximately @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ focuses on wildlife and the important role that sustainable forest management practices play in ensuring the long-term provision of wildlife habitat . <p> " We are pleased to support this initiative ; we believe it is important for every Ontarian to understand and appreciate this fantastic resource " said Bonny Skene , regional public affairs manager for Domtar . " The forests support jobs , clean our air and provide an amazing venue for so many outdoor activities . " <p> " Canadian forests are a great public treasure . Our team at EACOM is proud to help showcase how responsible forestry practices not only ensure that this renewable resource is maintained into the future , but also work to protect wildlife habitat and social values , " explained Jennifer Tallman , R.P.F. , Chief Forester , Ontario at EACOM .
