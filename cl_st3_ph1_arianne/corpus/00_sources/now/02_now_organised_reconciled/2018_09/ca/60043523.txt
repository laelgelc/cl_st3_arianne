<p> A Canadian animal welfare group has made their way to the G7 meetings in Halifax to shed some light on a little-known issue impacting oceans worldwide : ghost gear . <p> Ghost gear refers to fishing gear that has been lost or abandoned from fisheries around the world . The United Nations Environment Programme estimates 640,000 tons of fishing gear is lost each year , resulting in the deaths of more than 136,000 seals , sea lions and small whales annually . <p> The organization says the animals get trapped or entangled in the lost gear , which often leads to a slow and painful death . <p> " Once the gear becomes detached ... it continues to do what it is made to do which is to fish and as a result of that , it 's incredibly harmful to marine animals , " Josey Kitson , executive director of World Animal Protection Canada , told CTVNews.ca on Wednesday . <p> World Animal Protection Canada is in Halifax this week for the G7 meetings on climate change , oceans and clean energy .
