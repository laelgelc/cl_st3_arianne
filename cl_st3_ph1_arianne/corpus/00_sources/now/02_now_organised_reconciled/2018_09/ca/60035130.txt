<h> Letters to the Editor : Sept. 18 <h> Democracy can be better served <p> International Day of Democracy was Sept. 15 , and it starts Canada 's Democracy Week . Canada is a wonderful place to live but far from a shining example of how democracy should work . <p> This is starkly illustrated by Canada 's first past the post electoral system , which not only elects false " majority " governments that are opposed by the majority of voters , but also ensures that the vast majority of voters have no impact on election results . <p> First past the post awarded false " majority " power to the current federal Liberal and Ontario Conservative parties , which each received around 40 per cent of the popular vote . Over the united objections of opposition parties representing 60 per cent of voters , these two parties respectively spent $4.5 billion taxpayer dollars on a public stake in the fossil fuel industry and exercised the notwithstanding clause to override a court ruling regarding the constitutionality of a provincial bill . Claims that these governments have mandates from a majority of voters are clearly untrue . <p> First @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ while electoral systems based on proportional representation ( PR ) ensure that , to a close approximation , all parties are treated equally . They win the same percentage of seats as their percentage of votes , and all votes and voters are equal and effective . A party can not win majority power without a majority of vote , s so PR encourages parties to seek opportunities to co-operate with their competitors instead of attacking them , and to focus on policies that have broad voter appeal . In order to win a place in a " majority " government , a party can not just rely on its base but will almost always need one or more other parties as allies so that collectively they have a majority of votes . <p> Having an equal opportunity to vote has dominated many human rights struggles in the past . Unfortunately , winning the right to vote becomes a meaningless victory if the vote is ineffective . Time is well overdue to make all votes effective and equal through PR , thereby filling a glaring deficiency in Canada 's democracy @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to help achieve that . <p> Electoral reform is often mistakenly regarded by voters as a lower priority issue far less important than critical issues such as health care , education , economy or climate change . Please remember , however , that the electoral system provides the only avenue by which you can influence who gets to make the key policy decisions on the issues that are most critical to you . For most voters , that critical avenue is blocked by first past the post .
