<p> TORONTO , Sept. 27 , 2018 -- CPA Canada is continuing to raise its profile on sustainability issues with the signing of $1.45 million climate change awareness campaign with Natural Resources Canada ( NRCan ) . With a review underway of the CPA Competency Map , the announcement of the joint funding agreement counters criticism that sustainability training is missing from the CPA professional education program ( CPA PEP ) . <p> CPA Canada and NRCan will contribute $725,000 over two years for training , research and guidance . The purpose of the pact is to help Canadian business leaders recognize and anticipate the emerging financial impacts of climate change and to help their organizations better respond to the increasing demand for enhanced climate-related disclosure . <p> CPA Canada will focus on two projects related to awareness and reporting . It will provide climate change training through three training programs to build capacity within the private sector , targeting boards of directors , executives and CPAs working in business and professional services . <p> Its second project will focus on climate change disclosure and decision-making through research and best practice guidance , by providing research and outreach with @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures ( TCFD ) recommendations . <p> " Climate change has wide-ranging business , economic and social impacts , " stated Joy Thomas , president and CEO , CPA Canada in a press release announcing the pact . " Driving awareness about the risks and opportunities associated with climate change is a strategic priority for CPA Canada . These initiatives will build upon our legacy of leadership and training in this area and will help promote transparent financial reporting , sound business practices and sustainable economic growth .
