<p> Nickel Belt MP Marc Serre has met with numerous organizations such as the Citizens ' Climate Lobby Canada and large natural resources companies to discuss the proposed federal approach to carbon pricing . <p> " It 's the right thing to do not only for our kids and grandkids but also to protect the land , air , waterways and wildlife that surrounds Nickel Belt-Greater Sudbury , " Serre said in a statement . " We are so fortunate to live in a community where we can enjoy the great outdoors and benefit from the growing innovative mining , forestry and clean-tech sectors in the area . Greater Sudbury is a shining example for its rich experience of environmental recovery and restoration while contributing to the creation of well-paying jobs in the industry . This sustainable model has been at the forefront of the Sudbury Protocol project led by Laurentian University which actively reinforces and shares our positive experience with the rest of the world . " <p> Serre believes the " pan-Canadian approach " to carbon pricing proposed by the federal government is a " practical and cost-effective way to address climate change and will contribute to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ jobs for the middle class . " <p> " Implementing a price on pollution across the country is a matter of fairness for all , " he added . " It 's time to change the conversation ; partisan political agendas will not benefit the sustainability of our environment which needs to be protected . " <p> Carbon pricing is a critical part of the Canada 's climate action plan , which the federal government says will make a significant contribution towards meeting the national greenhouse gas reduction target . <p> " Carbon pricing is not a new concept and was adopted by many jurisdictions around the world including our own country . British Columbia introduced their carbon pricing model in 2008 and many industry organizations agree with carbon pricing and stand fully behind this system , " Serre said .
