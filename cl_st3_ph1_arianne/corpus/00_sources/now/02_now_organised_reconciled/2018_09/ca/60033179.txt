The province estimates the carbon tax will bring in $248 million in its first year , but reducing the PST will cost $300 million per year . Indexing the BPE costs $178 million and other small-business tax deductions $1.8 million . The Canadian Centre for Policy Alternatives -- BC found tax cuts and credits introduced with the carbon tax reduced government expenditures more than the tax brought in , making the B.C. carbon tax " revenue-negative .
