<p> The North Saskatchewan Watershed Alliance is in the process of studying the health of watershed infrastructure and the best way to go about ensuring the health of riparian areas and aquatic ecosystems . File photo <p> The North Saskatchewan Watershed Alliance announced a new project to study the most cost-effective ways to maintain and repair riparian setbacks . <p> The new project stems from a recently completed study conducted by the Headwaters Alliance that looked at the health of riparian setbacks within the Modeste Watershed , which includes Parkland County . <p> The two studies were presented at the Parkland County council meeting on Sept. 25 .
