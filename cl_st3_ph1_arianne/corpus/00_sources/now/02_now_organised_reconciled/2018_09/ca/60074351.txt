<p> MANILA -- ? The Presidential Commission for the Urban Poor ( PCUP ) and the Climate Change Commission ( CCC ) are partnering to help the country 's urban poor build resilience to climate change . <p> Such resilience-building is important , so the urban poor can better cope with and recover from climate change 's impacts , PCUP Chairperson Noel Felongco said on Friday . <p> " The urban poor is highly vulnerable to climate change , " he said on the sidelines of the National Urban Poor Summit in Metro Manila . <p> He said it is urgent to help the sector deal with the impacts of climatic changes . <p> The PCUP refers to urban poor as " individuals or families in urban areas with incomes below the poverty line , as defined by National Statistical Coordinating Board . " <p> Poverty is among the factors that raise this sector 's vulnerability , as it lacks resources to fend themselves against flooding , drought , and other events caused by the onslaught of extreme
