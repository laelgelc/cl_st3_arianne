<h> Northern families see green under Pallister 's carbon tax <p> Saturday , Sept. 1 was supposed to be the first day of Brian Pallister 's carbon tax on northern families and businesses . It 's not being implemented yet , because my NDP colleagues and I stopped him , at least for a few months . I know some of you may be thinking , " The NDP giving us a tax break ? What , is it going to snow today too ? " We did it because it was the right thing to do , and there were lots of reasons to do it . One of the most important was that it saved Manitobans $60 million in taxes between now and December . <p> I want Manitoba families to have the support they need to make green choices , keep life affordable and protect our environment . But we wo n't get any of those things with Pallister 's carbon tax grab -- life will get less affordable without programs to help the average family reduce their carbon footprint or help the environment .
