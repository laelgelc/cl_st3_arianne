<p> The mother of a man who went missing in Kelowna almost 30 years ago has applied to have her son presumed dead , but she is not giving up the search . <p> Charles Horvath was 20 years old when he hitchhiked across Canada in 1988 . <p> In the spring of 1989 , he was tenting at the Tiny Town Campground in Kelowna and working at Flintstones Bedrock City . <p> He was close with his mother and maintained contact with his family throughout his travels . <p> When he failed to finalize arrangements to meet his mom in Hong Kong as planned , she reported him missing . <p> Horvath faxed his mother on May 11 , 1989 , but was never heard from again . He cashed his Flintstones paycheque at the Royal Bank near Orchard Park mall on May 26 . <p> His mother , Denise Horvath-Allen , travelled to Kelowna from England several times in search of him . <p> " There are times when I have not wanted to be here because it is too painful to live each day , " Horvath-Allen told the media in 2010 .
