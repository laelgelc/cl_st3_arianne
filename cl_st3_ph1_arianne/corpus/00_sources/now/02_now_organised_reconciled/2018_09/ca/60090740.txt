<h> Article text size <h> Globe editorial : Bill C-69 kills the National Energy Board but keeps all the problems <p> Published September 26 , 2018Updated September 26 , 2018 <p> In April of this year , a spokesperson for Rachel Notley said the Alberta Premier was working closely with Ottawa to soften the contentious parts of a bill that redefines how pipelines and other resource projects are assessed and approved in Canada . <p> Six months later , Bill C-69 has been adopted by the House of Commons and sent to the Senate , and Ms. Notley is angry . She criticized the legislation on Tuesday and announced she will send two ministers to appear before Senate hearings on the bill , the dates of which have not been set . " We have n't seen the movement we had hoped to see , " she said . <p> From that , it would appear the Trudeau government has dug in it heels on the bill , which kills the National Energy Board and replaces it with the Canadian Energy Regulator . " <p> That reference to " economic well-being " is one of very few in the law .
