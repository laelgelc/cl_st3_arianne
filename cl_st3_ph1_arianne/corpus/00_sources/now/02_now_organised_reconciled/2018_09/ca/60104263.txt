<p> Prime Minister Justin Trudeau briefed Canada 's top bankers in the days leading up to an 11th-hour breakthrough in talks that sealed the terms of a new , long-awaited U.S.-Canada trade deal . <p> A series of calls the Prime Minister made to the chief executive officers of Canada 's five largest banks to provide updates and seek advice marked the culmination of months of back-channel communications between banks and government officials . <p> Mr. Trudeau has had occasional contact with bank CEOs throughout Canada 's long-running negotiations with the U.S. and Mexico to revamp the North American free-trade agreement .
