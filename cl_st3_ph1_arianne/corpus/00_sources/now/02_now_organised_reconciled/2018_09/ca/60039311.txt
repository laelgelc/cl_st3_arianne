( FILE ) <p> This week Halifax will be inundated with government ministers , dignitaries and leaders from Canadian and international fisheries and oceans organizations who are gathering as part of the G7 Ministerial Meetings to advance the Charlevoix Oceans Blueprint adopted earlier this summer . <p> In addition to the ministerial meetings , there is an Oceans Partnership Summit , a place for thought-leaders , innovators , business , and other groups can discuss ways to improve the health of our oceans , and to make our fisheries more sustainable today and for future generations -- it 's a conversation that Canada 's commercial fisheries are eager to have . <p> The United Nations Food &amp; Agriculture Organization ( FAO ) is the epicentre for international collaboration on fisheries issues .
