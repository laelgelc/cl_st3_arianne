I did n't feel the need to complain publically . <p> The poor air quality in Victoria in recent weeks coincided with visits from both of my new grandchildren , one from Vancouver and one from Nova Scotia . The realization that the " new normal " will be theirs really struck home . What future are we leaving them ? <p> I wo n't attempt to argue the impact of climate change . Emma Gilchrist has done an excellent job of that in her commentary , as have many other experts . <p> Is there a more pressing problem that our leaders need to focus on ? Ultimately , not .
