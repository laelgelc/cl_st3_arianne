The fly 's larvae travel through the animal itself before erupting through the skin , leaving holes in the flesh behind -- a menace to the cattle industry as they can damage the value of leather and meat . <p> In the spring , in particular , evidence of warble flies can be seen in many caribou hides . Researchers working in the 1980s and 1990s , counting the number of warble flies under the skin of caribou in the Beverly herd , reported as many as 220 per animal in some older males . <h> Advertisement .
