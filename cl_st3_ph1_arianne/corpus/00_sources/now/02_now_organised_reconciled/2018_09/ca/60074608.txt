It was designed to give people thinking of running for local councils an idea of what the job actually entails . <p> The event attracted candidates from Creston , Warfield , Rossland , Nelson , and Castlegar for the event . <p> " I have been thinking of running myself , " said Ida Price of Castlegar . " I am here today to find out more about what I can do to improve Castlegar . This is a wonderful city and I want to see what I can do . " <p> " I think it is important that anyone running for any public office understand governance , " said Dallas Magrum of Creston .
