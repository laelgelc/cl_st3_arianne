<h> ' Massive destruction is on the way ' : Typhoon Mangkhut hits the Philippines <p> Typhoon Mangkhut slammed into the northern coast of the Phillipines early Saturday , with witnesses saying the storm 's ferocious winds and rain ripped the roofs off buildings and knocked out power . <p> Nearly 15,300 people have already evacuated the northeastern corner of the island , the Phillipine Office of Civil Defence said . An estimated five million people , including just under 3,500 Canadians , are still in the path of the storm and are being told to stay indoors . So far there have been no reports of casualties or major damage in the affected region . <p> The Hawaii-based Joint Typhoon Warning Center has categorized it as a super typhoon with powerful winds and gusts equivalent to a category five Atlantic hurricane . <p> Joy Maluyo , field and emergency communications officer at World Vision Philippines , told CTV News Channel on Friday that her family and colleagues there were already experiencing heavy rains and strong winds . <p> " It 's a little scary both for the residents in Cagayan and ( the country 's second largest province @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ We 're worried about the children who might be affected by this massive typhoon .
