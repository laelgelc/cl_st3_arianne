Citing the elimination of the Canadian Wheat Board in 2012 , which dried up grain shipments through the north , the U.S. company shuttered the port in 2016 . <p> Then spring flooding in 2017 damaged the rail line and the company said it could n't afford to fix it . Fuel and food had to be flown into Churchill at skyrocketing costs , creating a huge burden for businesses that cater to tourists who visit Churchill to see polar bears , beluga whales and the northern lights . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Murad Al-Katib , president and CEO of AGT , said the purchase of the line and port assets is an opportunity to develop an Arctic gateway . <p> " We ca n't ignore the potential of the only rail link to an Arctic port in North America , " Mr. Al-Katib said Saturday . <p> " That gives the opportunity for this to be a natural resources corridor . And grain happens to be one of the abundant natural resources that we have in Western Canada , " he added . <p> Hugh Stephens , an executive fellow with the University of Calgary School of Public Policy who wrote a paper on northern sea routes , has doubts about the financial viability of a northern port like Churchill . <p> Mr. Stephens noted the closest Canadian shipping port to Asia is Prince Rupert , B.C. , and the closest to Europe is Montreal .
