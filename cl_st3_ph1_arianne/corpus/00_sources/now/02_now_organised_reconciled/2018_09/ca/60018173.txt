<p> EDMONTON -- Glen Murray , the former Ontario cabinet minister who left government to pursue a job he " dreamed of having " with the Pembina Institute , has resigned as its executive director . <p> In an announcement posted online Monday , the Calgary-based environmental think-tank said Murray was leaving . " While I 'm looking forward to a new challenge , the Pembina Institute is a very hard place and team to leave , " said Murray in a statement . <p> David Runnalls , chair of the board of directors , thanked Murray for " the leadership he has shown . <p> " In his time here , Glen has been a great accelerator for the organization and its staff , " Runnalls said . <p> Murray , 60 , came to the job after a long career in politics . Before announcing , last July , that he was joining Pembina , he was the minister for environment and climate change in then Ontario Premier Kathleen Wynne 's Liberal government . He was a longtime ally of Wynne 's . <p> " It 's a job I 've dreamed of having and it 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ one of the easiest decisions I ever made in my life was filling out the application form for the job at Pembina , " Murray told the National Observer last summer . <p> A former Winnipeg city councillor and mayor , Murray jumped into provincial politics , winning an Ontario byelection in February 2010 . He ran against Wynne for party leadership in 2012 , before dropping out and endorsing her . <p> While in government , he served as minister of transportation and infrastructure , and later as environment minister , leading the development of Ontario 's cap-and-trade system . He left for the Pembina Institute in advance of the election that saw Doug Ford 's Progressive Conservatives sweep to victory in Ontario last May . <p> " I ultimately have made the difficult decision , with the support of my partner Rick , to transition from this chapter on to the next chapter of tirelessly working to mobilize to fight climate change at the national level , " said Murray 's statement last year when he left politics . <p> Staff with Pembina , reached by the Post @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> " I will continue to be one of Pembina 's biggest cheerleaders , and wish the organization continued success , " concluded Murray 's statement .
