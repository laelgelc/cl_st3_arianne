<h> Canada 's libraries and museums prepare for disasters <p> With Brazil still reeling from the loss of its national museum to fire , Canada 's own museums and libraries have taken the opportunity to review their risk management plans in the event something similar threatens their collections . <p> Calamities such as earthquakes , flooding , and forest fires have made emergency preparedness a top priority for archival institutions in Canada , but University of Toronto museum studies program director Cara Krmpotich admitted that funding for infrastructure is difficult to come by . <p> " Could what happened in Brazil happen here ? In some ways , absolutely , " Krmpotich told The Canadian Press . <p> " If we think about shifting weather and climate change , absolutely this is something we should be thinking about in Canada . " <p> Associate chief librarian Loryl MacDonald of the Thomas Fisher Rare Book Library at the University of Toronto said the institution receives enough support from donors .
