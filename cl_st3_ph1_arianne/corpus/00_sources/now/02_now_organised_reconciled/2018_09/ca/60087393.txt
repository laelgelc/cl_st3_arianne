<h> Ontario 's environmental watchdog keeps an eye on Sarnia <p> The Aamjiwnaang First Nation is home to the only kindergarten in Canada with a display flag that notifies students and families when the air is safe to breathe , says Ontario 's Environmental Commissioner . <p> " This is one example of a historical , shameful problem and it 's high time for us to fix it , " Dianne Saxe said . <p> As Ontario 's environmental watchdog , Saxe wrote extensively about Sarnia in her annual report one year ago . She accused the provincial government of turning a blind eye for too long to pollution problems at Aamjiwnaang and other Indigenous communities . <p> Dianne Saxe is the Environmental Commissioner of Ontario.Submitted Photo <p> " It was very discouraging to see that the province let four decades go by without updating sulphur dioxide regulations , " she told The Journal . <p> " In the meantime , there was no adequate monitoring and no health study for the people in Aamjiwnaang . " <p> Saxe is coming to Sarnia on Oct. 3 to join the next Green Drinks roundtable discussion .
