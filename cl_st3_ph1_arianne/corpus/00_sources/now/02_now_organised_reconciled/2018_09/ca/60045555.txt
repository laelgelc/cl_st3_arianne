<p> A film currently in the works looks to shine a light on the impact climate change is having on the people of Grey-Bruce . In Resilience : Transforming our Community , producers look to not only explain the science of climate change , but also tell the stories of how it will impact those in Grey-Bruce and provide some insight into how people can respond to help make a difference themselves . Retired marine scientist John Anderson , whose talks about climate change and the changing world form the background of the documentary , said the film is different because they are emphasizing solutions . " I think more and more people are tending to do that because the picture , when you really start to understand it , can get quite overwhelming , " said Anderson .
