For the local bodies will soon have a working group for biodiversity management , climate change , environmental protection and disaster management . <p> According to the Local Self-Government Department ( LSGD ) , local bodies have been instructed to constitute such a new working group by September 28 . " In that time-frame , they can also reconstitute their respective planning committees or working groups , if needed , " said an officer . " The back-to-back natural disasters , especially the devastating floods , have necessitated the formation of a working group for biodiversity management , climate change , environmental protection and disaster management . The group will bolster relevant activities in this regard , " said the officer . <p> The working group , to be chaired by respective local body heads , should have experts in the aforementioned fields as members .
