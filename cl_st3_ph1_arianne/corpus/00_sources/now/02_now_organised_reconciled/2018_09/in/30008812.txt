<p> Prime Minister Narendra ModiFriday called for clean energy and " clean kilometres " as he unveiled a mobility road map that seeks investments in manufacturing electric vehicles and increased use of public transport . He said that clean mobility powered by clean energy is the most powerful weapon to fight climate change . And , congestion-free mobility is critical to check economic and environmental costs of congestion , he said while addressing the Global Mobility Summit ' MOVE ' . <p> " This means a pollution-free clean drive , leading to clean air and better living standards for our people . We should champion the idea of ' clean kilometres ' , " Modi said . <p> The country needs to go beyond cars to other vehicles such as scooters and rickshaws , Modi said . <p> " Common public transport must be the cornerstone of our mobility initiatives ... We need to ensure that public transport is preferred to private modes of travel , " he said , urging people to utilise the full potential for vehicle pooling . <p> " The Internet-enabled Connected Sharing Economy is emerging as the fulcrum of mobility , " he said @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ driver of the economy , and it should be safe , affordable and accessible for all sections of the society . It is already a major employer and can create the next generation of jobs , " Modi said , adding that mobility is the key forward .
