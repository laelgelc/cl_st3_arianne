<h> Sabarmati turns black near Indira Bridge <p> TNN Updated : Sep 22 , 2018 , 05:04 IST <p> AHMEDABAD : After the Kharicut Canal , it is now the Sabarmati river where chemical waste is being dumped suspect officials . On Friday , people visiting the riverbank found black and red water flowing in the river . <p> AMC officials said they did n't realize before this that polluted water was flowing into the river and had formed a big pool . The incident occurred near Indira Bridge where there 's only a single rivulet . <p> The officials said that someone could have dumped industrial waste in the river . It is worth recalling that according to Union ministry of environment , forest and climate change ( MoEF ) reply in Lok Sabha in January , Sabarmati is among Gujarat 's 20 most polluted rivers . Further , Gujarat ranks fourth among top five states with highly polluted rivers . <p> Environment experts believe that industrial effluents are the most common reason for pollution of Sabarmati river . They say industrial effluents are being released in big rivers including Sabarmati without being treated .
