<p> PUDUCHERRY : Climate change , destination degradation and disruption are the three critical issues that concern the travel and tourism sectors in the world , said director of strategic partnerships , Global sustainable tourism council ( GSTC ) , Kathleen Pessolano . Speaking on sustainable tourism while participating in a one-day seminar on " Challenges and opportunities on Puducherry tourism " organized by the department of tourism on Thursday , Pessolano advocated for public-private partnership initiatives to overcome the critical issues concerning the two sectors . <p> Stating that market demand for sustianble travel has been on the rise she cited an US travel portal 's survey that nearly a third of travelers choose **25;2049;TOOLONG tourist destinations . <p> " New tourists , who are highly educated , mature , affluent , well-travelled care about the environment , social and cultural traditions of the destinations , " she said . <p> Outlining the complementary trends in business travel , she said 65% of corporate travel executives reponsible for more than $10 million in annual travel budget are implementing ' green ' business travel guidelines . <p> She said the four pillars of sustainable tourism include effective management , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and environment and natural resources . <p> GSTC is the world 's leading standard-setting body for tourism sustainability .
