Other fires clear out underbrush , open the forest floor to sunlight and stimulate growth . <p> Government agencies in recent decades effectively upended that cycle of destruction and rebirth . Fire suppression policies allowed fuels to build up in many Western forests , making them more susceptible to major fires . <p> Those influences are magnified as development creeps ever deeper into forests and climate change brings hotter temperatures .
