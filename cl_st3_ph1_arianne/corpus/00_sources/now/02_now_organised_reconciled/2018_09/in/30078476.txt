The first four are part of common discourse in Kerala . By redevelopment , however , we are alluding to using the context of a disaster to re-envision a change in the composition , pace and direction of the development process . <p> In Aceh , reconstruction was coordinated by a specially created agency , decreed by the President of the Republic of Indonesia .
