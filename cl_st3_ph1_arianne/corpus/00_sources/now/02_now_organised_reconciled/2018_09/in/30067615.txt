<h> Land given to Quaid-e-Milleth college surveyed <p> CHENNAI : A team of officials led by Kancheepuram district collector P Ponniah on Monday surveyed the land which was given to Quaid-e-Milleth following a Madras High Court order which asked the district administration to survey the land and submit a report in this regard to the court on Wednesday . A senior forest official said the forest department assigned 40 acres of land in Nanmangalam forests to Quaid-e-Milleth Educational and School Trust functioning on Medavakkam Koot Road in 1975 . A government order was issued to the college . The land was given to the college on a condition that the entire portion of the allocated space has to be utilised by the trust . In case the trust fails to do so , the forest department can take back the land . Only on this condition the land was allocated . <p> Subsequently as per the agreement between forest department and the trust , the entire trees , bushes and plantations had to be either cleared by the forest or by the trust . The forest department said clearly that the de-reservation of the land could not be done as the Forest Conservation Act 1980 has come into place . For doing so , clearance from the Union Ministry of Environment Forests and Climate Change has to be obtained . <p> Forest officials said the college had used only 10.67 acres of the allocated land . The remaining 29.33 acres of land has a lot of wild growth and the college management did not use this space . The Forest department asked the trust not to do any non-forestry activity in the 29.33 acres of land , which is prohibited under the Forest Conservation Act . <p> The government also issued an order to take over the unused land from the trust in 2012 . However , the trust approached the court and the court only directed for a survey and subsequently a report was asked to be submitted in this regard to them . <p> As per the directions of the Madras High Court , the survey was conducted on Monday .
