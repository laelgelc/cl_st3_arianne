<h> National Water Mission : State forms 2 panels <p> Aurangabad : State government has formed two important committees for effective implementation of National Water Mission ( NWM ) , which was launched by the Centre under the National Action Plan for Climate Change . Every state is required to form the State Specific Action Plan ( SSAP ) for water as a part of NWM . The two newly formed panels have roped in top authorities from different government departments . <p> Since SSAP ( Water ) is a collective exercise of numerous departments and agencies to be completed within a specific time period , it is necessary to constitute two committees -- state level steering committee and state level monitoring committee . They are to review and monitor the progress of work . <p> The first component of SSAP is preparing the state water budget for water security , safety and sustainability , which needs to be duly approved by the state steering committee before submitting it to the NWM . <p> The main objective of the NWM is conservation of water , minimising waste and ensuring equitable distribution across the state . It constitutes as many as 43 other members . It includes 25 top officials from key state departments , eight others from the Centre , four resource people from expert institutions and private experts among others . The steering committee is expected to meet quarterly . <p> The monitoring committee will comprise of 27 top officials from different government departments . It is to be headed by principal secretary of water resource department . The panel is expected to meet once in a month . The panel will report to the steering committee and is expected to monitor the preparation of SSAP . Retired water resource department secretary C A Birajdar has been appointed as the expert to the steering committee .
