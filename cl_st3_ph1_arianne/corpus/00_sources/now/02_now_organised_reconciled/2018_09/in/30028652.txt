And then when the prayers are done and rituals over , the clay form dissolves into the earth and the embedded seeds and grains sprout as flowering plants . It is known that divinity stays with you forever but when you align your earthly rituals with that thought , you get a conscientious society . <p> Mukesh Goyal of Kriti Creations in Khan Market has been doing eco-friendly Ganeshas for the past three-four years . Their sizes vary from four inches to 30 inches and they do not use any kind of resin . The paints used are eco-friendly too . <p> The idol makers are based out of Calcutta and Maharashtra and since these idols are not baked , they break easily during transport .
