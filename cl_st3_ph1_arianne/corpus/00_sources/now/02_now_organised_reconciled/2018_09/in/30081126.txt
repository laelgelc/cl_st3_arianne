Read More <p> GURUGRAM : Residents of Sector 23 have moved the court alleging that the developer has sold off a green belt in the area as a school site to a private party . They raised an alarm and informed police after noticing that construction material had been dumped on the green belt on Thursday evening . The 150mX50m green belt , which is located near Rotary Club , has more than 30 fully-grown trees of neem , papri and babul . According to the initial layout plan of 1985 , the site is a green belt , but the developer , Ansal API , sold it off as a school site , residents alleged .
