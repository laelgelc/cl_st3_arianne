<h> New coastal plan promises better livelihood for fishers <p> he state 's fishermen will have better livelihood protection , housing and roads , besides ice plants , fish-drying platforms and net-mending facilities as per the new draft of the Coastal Zone Management Plan . The revised policy also ensures that the ecology of the coastal areas is preserved . As per the final draft of the Coastal Zone Management Plan , the 1,076km-long coastline in the state has been divided into six categories . The state plan was recently submitted to the Union ministry of environment , forests and climate change . <p> It also includes detailed plans for long-term housing needs of coastal fishermen communities , road networks , ice plants , fish-drying platforms and infrastructure facil-ities of fishing communities such as net mending yards . <p> Sharing details with TOI , R Ramesh , director of the National Centre for Sustainable Coastal Management ( NCSCM ) , said the six categories are ecologically sensitive areas , intertidal zones , developed areas , under or undeveloped areas , water area and water area of tidal-influenced water bodies .
