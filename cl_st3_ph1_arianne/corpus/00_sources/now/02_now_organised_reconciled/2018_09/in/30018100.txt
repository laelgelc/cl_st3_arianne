<h> Climate-change fizzle <p> Editorial September 11 , 2018 2:32 am <p> Representational Image . <p> The Bangkok meeting on climate change has floundered on the rock of discord over the formulations and funding , adopted at the Paris conference of December 2015 . The international meeting , which concluded in the Thai capital on Sunday , has fallen short of its aim to complete preparations towards an agreement this December for implementing the tentative Paris pact . <p> From Paris to Bangkok , the essay towards resolving a deeply contentious issue has been reduced to a fizzle . The six-day meeting was scheduled to lend an impetus to the battle against rising global carbon emissions by adopting a completed text that could be presented at the COP24 conference in Katowice , Poland , three months from now . <p> In the net , the deadlock persists , as it did in Copenhagen and Cancun . The road from Paris has neither been paved with good intentions nor an agreeable roadmap . Donald Trump , who withdrew from the deal some months ago , might just be enjoying a sense of Schadenfreude . An agreement remains ever so elusive @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , to which 190 nations including Australia subscribe , is to limit the increase in global temperature by 2100 to less than 2 degrees Celsius and as close as possible to 1.5 deg C , which is vital for the survival of island nations that are threatened by rising seas . But the absence of guidelines for meeting that objective would suggest that adequate action is not being taken . The major differences relate to what they call " fair financing " for implementation of the rules by developing countries , and the technical details of their reporting on the progress made . <p> The issue of how funds are made available to developing countries has emerged as a key sticking point . Despite the withdrawal by President Trump , there is little doubt that Washington does have a certain leverage over the climate-change talks , whether in Bangkok or the next round in Poland . It would be pertinent to recall that the Paris deal was hailed as a game-changer as it promised $100 billion annually from 2020 to poor nations that are coping with floods , heatwaves , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> But the system to provide that money as well as how donor-nations would source their contributions were left delightfully vague . As it now turns out , given the absence of a consensus over basics , the Bangkok conference prolonged to six days was bound to fail . The meeting was attended by representatives of most of the countries that are party to the Paris agreement , as well as the United States , which has announced that it is pulling out of the pact . <p> The pattern of funding is no less a contentious issue as a roadmap towards climate change , decrease in sea levels , a cleaner environment , and a habitable world . Every international effort has been overshadowed by the carbon emissions , so to speak .
