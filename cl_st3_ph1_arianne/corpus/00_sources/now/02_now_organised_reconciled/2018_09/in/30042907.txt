<h> Central ministry seeks to fund earth crust studies <p> PANAJI : Due to the paucity of data on tectonic plate movement , the Union ministry of earth sciences plans to lay extra emphasis on research and monitoring of tectonic plate movement in the Indian peninsular and the Indian Ocean region . Given the concerns over the occurrence of earthquakes and tsunamis , the ministry is keen to fund research projects that will study the Andaman Sea area where one tectonic plate is moving below another tectonic plate . <p> " India is willing to spend , to fund good scientific drilling proposals if it will focus on India-specific proposals that can give more data on monsoon patterns , tectonic plate consequences such as tsunami and earthquakes , " director , National Center of Antarctica and Ocean Research ( NCAOR ) , M Ravichandran , told TOI . <p> As part of the international ocean discovery programme ( IODP ) , there is a proposal to bring the international research vessel ' JOIDES Resolution ( JR ) ' to the Andaman Sea in 2023 to bore into the earth 's upper crust . Surface water salinity , temperature and currents are strongly affected by seasonal variations in monsoon . The region also witnessed a powerful tsunami and earthquake in 2004 , which killed thousands , prompting the government to take a closer look at the region . <p> " We are already focusing on solid earth sciences , we now want to strengthen this . We want to strengthen the monitoring network and observation , " advisor for the Union ministry of earth sciences , Brijesh K Bansal said . <p> Once the earth 's mantle is drilled and the drill core removed for research studies , scientific instruments will be inserted into the drilled hole , which could be a depth of approximately 2km . <p> " Monitoring of deformations is important , especially in the drill hole .
