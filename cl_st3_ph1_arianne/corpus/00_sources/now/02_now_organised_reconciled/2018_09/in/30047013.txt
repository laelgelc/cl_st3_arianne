<h> Drastic change in city 's rain pattern <p> Vadodara : Have you noticed rain playing spoilsport during Navratri festivities in the last couple of years ? Or times when the city gets completely pounded with heavy rains for a couple of days and then witnesses a full dry patch . The monsoon that we know is n't the same that we had witnessed a decade ago . A study conducted by researchers of MS University has revealed how the monsoon pattern has changed , especially in the last 40 years thanks to the impact of urbanization on climate . <p> As the city has expanded in all directions and vegetation cover reduced , urban pockets in Vadodara district are witnessing more rain nowadays compared to rural areas of the district .
