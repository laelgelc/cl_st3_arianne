Bengaluru and Lucknow get 95 per cent of their monsoon rain in just over five days . <p> Twelve of the 22 cities have seen a partial decline in southwest monsoon rain hours from 1969 to the early to mid-2000s , the data show . <p> " This is very important for city and town planning .
