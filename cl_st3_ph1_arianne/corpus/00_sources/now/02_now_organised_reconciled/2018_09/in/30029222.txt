<h> Govt : Contract staff to have uniform service conditions <p> TNN Sep 14 , 2018 , 02:30 IST <p> PATNA : The state cabinet on Thursday approved uniform service conditions for the contractual staff working in various departments , except the data entry operators from Beltron . This was in consonance with the cabinet 's approval to the recommendations made by a high-level committee headed by former chief secretary Ashok Sinha . <p> Principal secretary ( cabinet secretariat ) Sanjay Kumar said the performance of the contractual staff would also be annually assessed . Further , the contractual staff not needed by their parent department after a certain period can be adjusted in another department . <p> As for the data entry operators , Kumar said , the departments concerned have been asked to review their cases and report to the committee for consideration of their terms and conditions . <p> The cabinet also approved a proposal to do away with the interview of the candidates after they clear examinations for appointment on the posts of medical officer , assistant engineer and veterinary doctor . The merit list will now be prepared on the basis of the marks @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Commission . <p> In pursuance of the Supreme Court 's order , the cabinet cleared a proposal for compensation to lynch mob victims . While the overall compensation to the next kin of a person lynched by a mob would be Rs 3 lakh , a sum of Rs 1 lakh of this would be paid as interim compensation within a month of the incident and the balance after the resolution of the case by fast track court . <p> A seriously injured person rendered unemployable will now get a compensation of Rs 2 lakh , and an injured person Rs 25,000 . <p> The cabinet also approved change in the name of environment and forest department . It will now become the ' environment , forest and climate change ' department . <p> As part of the 150th birth anniversary celebrations of Mahatma Gandhi next year , the state government will give remission in punishment to women and inmates above 60 years of age .
