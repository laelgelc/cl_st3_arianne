Over 180 participants from 16 ballet dance schools from all-over India and abroad are registered to attend the three- day event in October 27 , 28 and 29 . <p> The level and significance that the festival has reached in 3 years is amazing ! The geography of the event covers New Delhi and NCR , Pune , Mumbai , Bangalore , Ahmedabad , Chennai and other cities of India . Furthermore , the nature of the festival has become truly international with participants being of Indian , Russian , French , German backgrounds . <p> October 14-15 , 2017 saw an abundance of vibrancy , talent and technique . Prior to opening her own school , Galina was teaching at Russian Cultural Centre for 15 years and it is her passion and dedication that made her own ballet school famous across India for its high-quality ballet education , setting high standards and planting seeds of love and passion for this beautiful form of dance . <p> Mosaic of Dance was born not only with the idea of promoting ballet in India , but with the purpose of bringing together dancers from different schools all over India , giving them a platform where they could showcase their talent , learn from others , gain new exposure and experience . No such ballet competitions have ever been organized in India which makes it very unique and the first of its kind .
