<h> Rainfall deficit widens to 10% below normal levels <p> NEW DELHI : The country 's rainfall deficit in the ongoing monsoon season widened to 10% , hovering on borderline drought conditions , following below-normal showers every month -- a pattern of consistent shortfall not seen since 2004 . <p> After a vigorous start , the southwest monsoon was patchy through the June-to-September season so far , although the deficit may narrow in the days ahead as more bursts of rainfall are forecast . <p> The government , however , is confident that this year 's farm output will top last year 's record . Officials said the biggest shortfall in rainfall has been in the eastern and northeastern parts of the country . <p> Rainfall was 5% below normal in June , followed by a 6% shortfall each in July and August . Rainfall so far in September has been 30% below normal . <p> The southwest monsoon normally begins to withdraw September 1 onwards , but the process has n't yet started this year and northwest and central India are expected to receive a heavy spell of rainfall towards the end of this week , government and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ withdrawal of monsoon have not been met so far as many regions have been witnessing brief spells of rainfall lately , " said K Sathi Devi , head of the national weather forecasting centre at the India Meteorological Department . This should ease concerns of a drought . <p> The weather office declares a drought if the full-season rainfall deficit exceeds 10% and spans 20-40% of the area of the country . <p> The withdrawal of monsoon is expected to begin towards the fag end of this month or even in the first week of October .
