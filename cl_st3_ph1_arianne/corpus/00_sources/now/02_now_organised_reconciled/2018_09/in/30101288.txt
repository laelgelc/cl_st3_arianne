<h> UDC performs Puja to celebrate order on sanctioning of Devika Project work <p> Puja being performed admist drum beats and sound of bugle for celebrating Govt order of sanctioning work on Devika river in Udhampur . <p> Excelsior Correspondent UDHAMPUR , Sept 30 : To celebrate the historic decision of Union Ministry of Environment , Forest &amp; Climate Change passing written order of sanctioning work on river Devika at Udhampur under National River Conservation Plan(NRCP) , members of Udhampur Development Council ( UDC ) today performing Puja in a traditional way on the bank of sacred river Devika along with devotees and citizens of Udhampur . Briefing media persons about the Devika Project , Anil Paba , president UDC , showed gratitude to Dr Jitendra Singh , MP Kathua- Udhampur-Doda constituency and MoS in PMO for getting the Devika river project sanctioned and work order issued . Paba said that Devika river along with other rivers like Saryu , Kuhu , Gomati , Gandaki and Lohia etc was existing during Ramaayan period without any shadow of doubt . He appealed to the Centre and district administration to clarify stand on the issue of illegal encroachment and demarcation of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the illegal encouragement was still going on thus hurting the sentiments of Udhampur people . Mr Paba said that blue print of the Devika Project should be made public . He also appealed the Central Government to invite global tendering to implement the NRCP project .
