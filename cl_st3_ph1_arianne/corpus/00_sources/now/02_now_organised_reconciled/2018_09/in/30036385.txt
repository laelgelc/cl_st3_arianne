<h> Gujarat a degree away from climate calamity <p> After a hot summer beset by record temperatures , skewed rainfall and a neardrought situation , Gujarat can no more ignore the harsh reality of climate change . In June , the World Bank in its report , " South Asia 's Hotspots : The Impact of Temperature and Precipitation Changes on Living Standards , " had warned that 19 districts of Gujarat are poised to become climate change hotspots by 2050 in the " moderate and mild " category . <p> An Energy Policy Institute at the University of Chicago ( EPIC ) report warns that productivity of workers in the state may drop by up to 8% , if Gujarat continues to drag its feet on climate change action plans . <p> Temperatures in the three coastal districts , namely Junagadh , Gir-Somnath and Porbandar , have risen by 1-1.5 degrees in the time period spanning from 1950 to 2010 . In the rest of Gujarat , temperatures rose by an average of 0.5 to 1 degrees .
