The numbers do n't lie . <p> Despite early signs of improvement in February this year , matters took a turn for the worse in June when dust storms kicked up in nearby Rajasthan state created a blanket of smog . <p> In parts of the city , air quality index ( AQI ) readings had hit 999 -- the equivalent of smoking 45 cigarettes a day . According to the World Health Organization , Delhi has the worst air quality in the world alongside other Indian cities like Gwalior , Allahabad , and Ludhiana .
