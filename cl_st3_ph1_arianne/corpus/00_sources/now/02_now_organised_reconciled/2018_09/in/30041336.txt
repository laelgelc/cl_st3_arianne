<p> There are many movements around the world encouraging people to reuse , upcycle and recycle their waste . However , the growing piles of garbage that choke our landfills and oceans remind us of the Herculean challenge ahead of us . Human ingenuity , however , offers hope . Researchers are continually developing solutions to deal with mankind 's waste problem . Here are some creative solutions that could help the world turn waste into wealth . <p> It is common knowledge that carbon dioxide is a wasteful by-product of human activities . We know that global warming and climate change are both consequences of increased carbon dioxide emissions . India has also been witnessing the effects of rising CO2 emissions -- from extreme weather events to variations in the monsoon -- first hand for many years now . <p> However , there are many industries that actually use carbon dioxide for purposes like aerating beverages or producing alternative fuels .
