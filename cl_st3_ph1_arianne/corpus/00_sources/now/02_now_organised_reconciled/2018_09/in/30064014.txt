<h> ONGC gets nod for development drilling of 72 wells in KG-Basin <h> Share <h> ONGC gets nod for development drilling of 72 wells in KG-Basin <p> Monday , 24 September 2018 PTI Hyderabad <p> The Expert Appraisal Committee ( EAC ) under the Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change , has given the green signal to State-owned ONGC limited for Additional Development Drilling of 72 wells in the Krishna-Godavari basin . <p> The Oil and natural gas PSU submitted a proposal to the Environment Ministry for environmental clearance ( EC ) to the project , related to the additional Development Drilling of 72 wells in East Godavari , West Godavari and Krishna districts of Andhra Pradesh , with an investment outlay of Rs 792 crore . <p> " The EAC , after deliberations , recommended the project for grant of environmental clearance , subject to compliance of terms and conditions as under , " the EAC said in the minutes of meeting held on August 30 . <p> The standard Terms of Reference for the project was granted on January 29 , 2016.
