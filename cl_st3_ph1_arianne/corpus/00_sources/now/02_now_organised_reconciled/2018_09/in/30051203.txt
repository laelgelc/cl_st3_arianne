<h> Land registrations declined by 50% in August <p> KOCHI : Several parts of Ernakulam were ravaged by floods in August and the district witnessed a 50% decline in property registrations compared to the the same period last year . <p> As per the data available from district registration office , as many as 4,654 registrations of documents were made in August 2018 . Last year , in the same month , the district witnessed 7,859 registrations of land documents . <p> But the decline in the number of registrations does not fully reflect in the revenue collection of Ernkaulam , since the state government revises stamp duty in every budget . When the total revenue collection touched Rs 68 crore in August last year , it touched Rs 44 crore during the same month this year . <p> The district managed to collect 38% of the targeted amount in stamp duty category and 41% of the targeted registration fee . <p> The government raises 10% target every year in revenue collection for each district .
