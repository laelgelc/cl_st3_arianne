<h> Himalaya Day celebrated across U'khand <p> TNN Sep 9 , 2018 , 21:55 IST <p> Rishikesh/Dehradun : Himalaya Diwas ( Himalaya Day ) -- dedicated to the protection and conservation of the Himalayas -- was celebrated throughout Uttarakhand on Sunday with the main event taking place at Parmarth Niketan , the largest ashram in Rishikesh . Hundreds of seers , inter-faith leaders , activists , environmentalists , government officials as well as members of the public along with governor Baby Rani Maurya took a pledge on the banks of the Ganga to safeguard the Himalayan ecosystem . Participants at the event expressed concern on how the Himalayas have been deeply impacted by human-induced threats , including climate change , deforestation , encroachment and pollution as a result of which natural disasters were on the upswing <p> A ' Himalaya declaration ' was also handed over to the governor outlining immediate steps needed to be taken to protect the Himalayas . " The future of the Himalayas is the future of India . If the Himalayas die , India dies . If the Himalayas survive , then India thrives , " the declaration said .
