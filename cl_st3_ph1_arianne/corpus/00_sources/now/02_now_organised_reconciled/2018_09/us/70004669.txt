<p> Cheryl Dodson sometimes wonders what John B. McLemore would think about his legacy in the place he famously called " S-Town . Visitors take selfies at the Little Caesar 's Pizza on Alabama 5 that he frequented . Some still try to get a look at the maze he made on his place . <p> It 's the peculiar legacy of the podcast that told the story of McLemore , a clock restorer who killed himself in 2015 , and the strange , gothic life he carved out for himself in this rural community . Since then , " S-Town , " the product of creator Brian Reed , has won a Peabody Award , and there is talk of a movie to retell the story . <p> For many who visit , there are two Woodstocks .
