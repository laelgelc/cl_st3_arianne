<h> Poetry for the sake of creation <p> It is the strangest of times . Alarming effects of climate change are being felt all over the world , while many , including those in power , consider climate science conspiratorial fiction . Even the believers , the data analysts warn , fail to appreciate the scale of the catastrophes that await us . <p> And then there 's poetry . Which I suggest is a great gift for such a time as this . <p> How can this be ? It 's not intuitive that poetry , so mystifying to many , has any material value for the work necessary to confront the unprecedented assaults on planet Earth by its human inhabitants .
