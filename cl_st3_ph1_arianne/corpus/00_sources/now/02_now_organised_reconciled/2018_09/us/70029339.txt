<p> North Carolina was a leader in coastal management , said Stanley Riggs , a distinguished professor of geology at East Carolina University . It heeded the advice of a " tremendous team of scientists " studying the origins and evolution of the Eastern Seaboard , of which the Tar Heel State has a particularly broad swath . As a result , lawmakers adopted policies in the 1970s and 1980s to safeguard the natural environment and protect human lives , he said . No hardening of the shoreline . Setbacks from the coast . Guidelines about inlets .
