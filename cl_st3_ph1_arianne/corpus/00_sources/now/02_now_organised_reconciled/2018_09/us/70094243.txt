<p> At a time that faith is being pushed out of the public square , religious leaders from around the world assembled in Argentina to discuss ways they can work with each other and with government organizations to impact a wide array of global issues . <p> Deseret News in-depth team reporter Kelsey Dallas was in Argentina to cover last week 's G-20 Interfaith Forum . The event is an international gathering of religious leaders and faith-based social activists from the world 's 20 leading economies . <p> Dallas reported , " The fifth annual G-20 Interfaith Forum features three days of panel discussions during which speakers share their on-the-ground experiences addressing issues like starvation and violence , as well as their suggestions for G-20 political leaders . This year 's discussion topics included environmental protection , religious freedom law , women 's rights and workplace policies . " <p> Elder D.
