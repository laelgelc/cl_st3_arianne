<p> We have all kinds of stats that confirm what is obvious to Washingtonians : This month is one of the cloudiest and wettest Septembers the District has ever seen . The month is doing more than its fair share to put 2018 in the running for Washington 's wettest year on record . <h> September by the numbers <p> Through Sunday , Washington had posted 8.25 inches of rain for the month , which is 5.44 inches above normal and ranks fifth-most on record ( through Sept. 23 ) . <p> Rain falling early this week could easily push that number over nine or even 10 inches . <p> Washington 's rainiest September on record occurred in 1934 , when an astonishing 17.45 inches fell . That record is safe , and the second-place total of 12.36 inches from 1975 probably is , too . But a top-five finish above 8.84 inches ( from 2011 ) seems like a good bet this year . <p> The rain this month has been both persistent and , at times , heavy . We 've registered at least a trace of rain on 17 of 24 days and measurable amounts on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ times ( Sept. 7 , 9 and 17 ) . <p> Even on days when it has n't rained , stuck weather systems have frequently cast a bleak shadow over the region . Through Sept. 24 , we 've compiled 14 cloudy days , seven partly cloudy days and just three sunny days . <p> Cloudy skies have prevailed on 13 of the past 16 days , and , no , this is not normal . <p> The top panel on the chart below shows it has been overcast at noon 70 percent of the time this September , way above the long-term average ( climatology ) of 27 percent . It ranks as the third-greatest amount on record year to date , trailing only 1886 and 1889 . <p> Since April , Washington has picked up 40.70 inches of rain , which is the most on record . During this stretch , we 've observed at least an inch of rain on 15 days , also the most on record . The 23.17 inches of rain we 've seen since July 1 is the second-most on record . <p> With more than three months left this calendar year , Washington already has sailed past its average annual total of 39.74 inches . If no more rain fell the rest of the year after Sunday , 2018 would still rank as 23rd-wettest on record . <p> The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 1889 , is perhaps not out of reach .
