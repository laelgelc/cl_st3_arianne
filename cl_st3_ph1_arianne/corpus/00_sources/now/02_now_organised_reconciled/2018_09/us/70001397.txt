<h> Opinion : This is one way Donald Trump has been good for the environment <h> MeredithJones <p> Depending on your political views , President Trump could represent any number of things to you . Swamp drainer or swamp filler . Make America Great Again or Make America Hate Again . But interestingly , it is becoming increasingly clear that Donald Trump may be one thing in particular : Good for the environment . <p> If you 're scratching your head on that one , you 're not alone . Given longtime climate change denier Trump 's almost abnormal affinity for coal and seeming total aversion to environmental regulations , it seems improbable , if not impossible , that the current administration would create positive momentum for eco-friendly causes and companies . <p> But as counterintuitive as it may seem , Mother Earth may owe The Donald for its own Trump Bump . <p> In fact , since the November 2016 election , an increasing number of investors seem to be sticking up for the environment , and for social and governance issues , creating a " green wave " of assets into both responsible investment and ESG ( @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and ETFs . In 2015 , there was an anemic $650 million increase in ESG ETF assets under management ( AUM ) , according to figures from JPMorgan Chase -- an amount eclipsed by the rise in AUM in just three months post-election in a mix of new money and price gains . <p> From November 2016 to January 2017 , $740 million poured into ETFs focused on environmental , social and governance issues . In addition , the number of funds increased by nearly 8% , from 104 to 112 , according to the same JPMorgan data . <p> In addition to growth in the number of funds and assets under management , proxy votes related to environmental and social issues are on the rise . In the 2017 proxy vote season ( January 2017 to June 2017 ) , 43.2% of the proposals brought to a vote for Russell 3000 companies involved environmental and social issues , according to the Conference Board . How will ESG investors fare in an economic environment geared more toward free markets than Free Willy ? <p> While there is n't a definitive answer , it looks like the do-gooders may , in fact , do good .
