Spread primarily by Ixodes scapularis -- the black-legged tick , aka the deer tick -- Lyme is now the most commonvector-borne illness in the United States . Not a bad showing for a disease that was only identified , from an initial handful of cases , a half century ago . The arrival of Europeans triggered a chain of events that unlocked its infection potential . Colonists and their descendants , once they got to America , undertook a series of tick-friendly activities : they downed forests and eradicated native predators of tick hosts , and later , filled the atmosphere with so much carbon that the planet warmed .
