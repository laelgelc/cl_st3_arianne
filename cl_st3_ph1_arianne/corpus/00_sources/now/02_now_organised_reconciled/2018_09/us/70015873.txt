<h> What will Hurricane Florence mean for the Washington area ? <p> Washington is waterlogged , and the last thing it needs is a hurricane deluge and damaging winds . Unfortunately , this will be a possibility Friday and into the weekend . But there are scenarios in which we would avoid serious effects . <p> Flooding rain and winds strong enough to bring down trees are the biggest concerns . But other storm hazards could affect the region , as well . <p> Depending on the track of Category 4 Hurricane Florence , a storm surge could come up the tidal Potomac and Chesapeake like a bulldozer , pushing water multiple feet above normally dry land along their shores . <p> In addition , quick-hitting tornadoes could form in some of the storm bands that pass through the region . <p> " The potential for a disaster is high , " said Jim Lee , meteorologist in charge at the National Weather Service serving the Washington area . But he stressed uncertainties in the specific outcomes , noting that storm effects are still several days away . " Time will tell as the week unfolds .
