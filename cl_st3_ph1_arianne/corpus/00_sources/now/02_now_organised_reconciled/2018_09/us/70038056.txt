<h> Fern Ridge draw-down a sign of changing climate <h> Monday <p> Sep 17 , 2018 at 12:01 AM <p> The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers has started to draw-down Fern Ridge Reservoir earlier than usual this year . After a dry hot summer , that makes sense , and it could be the new normal in a warmer world . But the biggest surprise has been the lack of controversy . The new climate normal no longer surprises . <p> When the Corps built the Fern Ridge Dam on Long Tom River in 1941 , the authorizing legislation listed flood control , irrigation and navigation as the primary purposes for the dam . Navigation become less essential now that roads crisscross that part of Lane County , but flood control and irrigation remain priorities . <p> The legislation also encouraged secondary uses such as recreation and fish and wildlife management . <p> The need for the dam back then was clear . During the rainy winter and spring , the Long Tom often flooded . If federal estimates are even remotely correct , the dam has been a resounding success .
