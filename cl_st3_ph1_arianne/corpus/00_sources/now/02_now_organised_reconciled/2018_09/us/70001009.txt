When a colleague mentioned he 'd experienced a similar rattlesnake sighting , Lipman got to thinking . <p> " I wondered if there are more snakebites during droughts , " said Lipman , clinical associate professor of emergency medicine at the Stanford School of Medicine , who routinely treats patients with venomous snakebites . <p> Lipman launched a study with a team of researchers to investigate the question .
