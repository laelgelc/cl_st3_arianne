<h> My celebration of National Estuaries Week <p> Sunday <p> Sep 23 , 2018 at 9:16 AMSep 23 , 2018 at 9:16 AM <p> Sept. 20 -- To the Editor : <p> Last week was National Estuaries week . Thanks to the Op Ed by Corey Riley and Rachel Rouillard on Sunday Sept. 16 , we were invited to join the celebration . Their Op Ed provides us with both opportunities to savor our treasured estuaries and the imperative that we save and restore them . This letter reflects my celebration in response their invitation . <p> When I think of estuaries , I think of the large and growing number of people who care about and for them . I think of the major threat to our estuaries from climate change . And I go sit someplace like North Mill Pond knowing that it will restore me even as I consider what is wanted and needed to steward it . <p> I am inspired by the emergence of a community of people committed to their preservation and restoration . This includes ordinary citizens who now are making informed choices as to the snow melt product they select as @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Rouillard who are doing the challenging work of research , design , implementation of practices to protect and restore our estuaries while providing education necessary to empower ordinary citizens like myself . <p> These professionals and ordinary citizens work side by side inside of many organizations like the Conservation Law Foundation , Blue Ocean Society and Great Bay Stewards . <p> Quite simply , estuaries are at extreme risk from climate change which is bringing sea level rise and storm surges . <p> What is needed is a revenue neutral , bipartisan solution called Carbon Fee and Dividend ( CFD ) that will put a fee on fossil fuels and distribute that fee to households as a dividend for challenging climate change . <p> Over just 20 years CFD can drive emissions down 50 percent , spur business growth adding 3 million jobs and protect consumers from the higher cost of fossil fuel based products like oil , gasoline and plastic . <p> The route to CFD is communication , communication to elected officials , especially members of Congress , communication with one another to bring the problem of and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to go both ways ; we need to listen to people like the scientists , 97 percent of whom are clear that climate change is a function of fossil fuel consumption , national security experts who have been saying for years that climate change is a threat multiplier and business leaders who are calling for federal action to drive down fossil fuel consumption . <p> I am proud of and inspired by organizations like Citizens ' Climate Lobby(cclusa.org) and the Climate Leadership Council(clc.org) who are taking the lead in empowering the public to act effectively . <p> On that note , I am off to have a beer at Great Rhythm Brewery , marvel over the natural beauty of North Mill Pond and contemplate how we will restore it . <p> Wes Tator <p> Portsmouth <h> Never miss a story <p> Choose the plan that 's right for you .
