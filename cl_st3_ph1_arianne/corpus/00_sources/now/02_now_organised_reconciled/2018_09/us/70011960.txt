<h> Share this : <p> Climate change is the poster child for what happens when government retreats from truth . Unwilling to face facts and grapple with the reality of a rapidly warming planet , the current regime in Washington D.C. is rolling back real-world solutions to the climate crisis -- clean power plants , clean air rules and vehicle mileage standards that reduce fuel costs and pollution . <p> Last year , abandoning American global leadership and 21st century economic prospects , the White House withdrew from the United Nations Paris Climate Agreement , through which all other nations are working together to solve this existential threat . <p> That 's when Marin 's Time to Lead on Climate coalition of community groups decided to ask Christiana Figueres , the architect of the Paris Agreement , to come to Marin to discuss the path forward . With the abdication of national climate leadership , what could we do ? <p> Figueres graciously accepted our invitation , in part because of Marin 's ongoing commitment to climate action . It 's an affiliate event of the Global Climate Action Summit earlier that week in San Francisco . <p> In a special part of the evening , over 40 local organizations will kick things off at 5:30 p.m. with a Marin Climate Action Showcase , focused on sampling local wines , tastes and climate solutions . All these steps are available and affordable .
