<h> Flooded neighbors will need our help <p> It was n't that big a bump . I mean , come on , even if you 're a short , 83-pound , white-haired senior citizen , you should be able to take more than that . Those old people are such whiners -- and I even helped pick up her crutches . <p> The main thing is , I got what I was after -- those last two super-size containers of bottled water . Determination and a sharp elbow won out again . It was less of a problem getting a five-year supply of batteries and extra flashlights . <p> Shirley was on her own quest , hauling home enough food to feed the neighborhood for a very long time and enough toilet paper to last us through 2025 . <p> The thing is , you ca n't be too careful . Rain from Florence hit us starting early last week , piling on what had been a steady diet of rain this late summer season . <p> I must have been born in a storm . I worry even when we 're this far away . It was even worse in 1962 . Everyone thought we were headed for a nuclear war with Russia over missiles in Cuba . I flew back to Baltimore from Denver to dig a basement fallout shelter for my mom and two younger brothers who were still at home . Fortunately , we did n't have to test it out . <p> You really ca n't prepare for a major storm like Hurricane Florence , or any of the hurricanes that seem to come with more frequency lately . It 's way past time for our leaders to take man-made climate change seriously -- the change that heats up our oceans and increases the intensity of hurricanes .
