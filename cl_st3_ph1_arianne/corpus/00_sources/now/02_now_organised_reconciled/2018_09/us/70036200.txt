<h> SPOTLIGHT : Kashmir downpour heralds arrival of autumn <p> Two days of intermittent rainfall resulting in sharp dip in temperatures has sent people in Kashmir looking not only for their shelved woolens but also ushered in autumn in all its resplendent colours . <p> With a considerable nip in the air during mornings and evenings , orchards dotted with apples , walnuts , grapes and fields of rice offer the perfect setting for farmers to step-out into their fields to reap the benefits of a yearlong hard work . <p> The change in season has also resulted in nomads returning with their flock of sheep and goats to warmer pastures in Jammu region . Like every season of change , the nomads carry their children , tents , day-to-day goods and other essentials which make their stay in the alpine pastures possible . <p> " I have never seen such a picture-perfect setting ever in my life . I am in Kashmir for a week and loving every moment of it , " said Rajesh Kumar , a tourist . <p> The autumn has also made Valley 's hinterland come to life as womenfolk and men in different @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ grain-laden paddy fields . Agriculturists say a rich paddy yield is expected this year , contrary to predictions earlier . <p> " Even though there were advisories issued by the agriculture department for restraining paddy sowing to only well-irrigated areas , it seems the yield will be quite rich , " said Dr Farooq Ahmad Lone , group leader of Center for Climate Change and Mountain Agriculture , Sher-e-Kashmir University of Agricultural Sciences and Technology , Kashmir . " There was apprehension that less than average rainfall during summer would be a spoilsport but I believe we have overcome that fear " . <p> In the city-centre of summer capital , the weekly Sunday market which has become a regular shopping place , is seen attracting shoppers from across the city as brisk sales of woolens has picked up here . <p> " I visited LalChowk to make purchases for some jackets and windcheaters today as I go for a morning walk daily . This weather is a respite from the scorching heat , " said Jasim Ahmad , a shopper . <p> Fruit growers and traders at this time @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ apples with different varieties are being supplied to mandis and markets outside the state . <p> Bashir Ahmad Basheer , president of Kashmir Valley Growers and Dealers Union says that Kashmiri apple is fetching better rates in outside mandis than the last season . The apple trader says local Kashmiri apple is facing intense competition from neighboring Himachal Pradesh where marketing of Himachal Apple has seen a spurt . <p> Arrival of autumn has also come as a ray of hope for tourism sector in Kashmir . The early snowfall in upper reaches of the Valley is seen by tourism stakeholders in Kashmir as an opportunity to save the remaining tourist season , hoping that increased tourist influx will provide a much-needed boost to the ailing sector .
