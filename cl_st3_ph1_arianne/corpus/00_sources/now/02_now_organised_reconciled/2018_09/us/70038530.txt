Once you subscribe to that argument , it becomes nearly impossible to think of a noble pursuit for a person . Doctors save lives -- firefighters too . Teachers hope toinspire the next great genius , maybe someone like Norman Borlaug , whose agricultural breakthroughs allowed our population to balloon on a planet with only so much arable land .
