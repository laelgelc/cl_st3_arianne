" But these giant towers , looming over the skyline of Delhi , India , land with benevolent intentions . <p> " The Smog Project , " designed by Dubai-based architecture firm Znera Space , is an ambitious proposal to clean the air in one of the world 's most pollution-blighted cities . <h> Related content <p> Delhi 's citizens are on the frontlines of a smog crisis .
