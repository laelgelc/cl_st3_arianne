<p> The Royal Academy of Arts in London will host the first major survey of Oceanic Art in the UK . " Oceania " will open on September 29 and will be on view through December 10 , 2018 . <p> The exhibition celebrates the art of Melanesia , Micronesia and Polynesia . It also encompasses the vast Pacific region from New Guinea to Easter Island , Hawaii to New Zealand . According to the RA , " the show brings together around 200 exceptional works from public and private collections worldwide , and will span over 500 years . It will be a rare opportunity to be immersed in the art and culture of an area that represents nearly a third of the world 's surface , a region rich in history , ritual and ceremony . The exhibition marks the 250th anniversary of the Royal Academy , founded in 1768 , the same year Captain James Cook set sail on his first expedition to the Pacific on the ' Endeavour .
