She was placed on administrative leave late Tuesday and asked to hand over her badge , keys and cellphone , according to an E.P.A . official familiar with the decision who was not authorized to discuss the move and who asked not to be identified . <p> The official said Dr. Etzel was not facing disciplinary action and would continue to receive pay and benefits .
