He will meet with each atoll 's Taupulega ( village council ) and community members , and see progress on New Zealand-funded projects . <p> The visit is part of the Government 's recently announced shift in New Zealand 's strategic approach to the Pacific , known as the Pacific Reset . <p> " We want to build deeper partnerships in the Pacific , and work more closely with our Pacific neighbours on issues we all face , such as climate change resilience , good governance , health , education , gender , youth and human rights , " said Mr Faafoi . <p> " It 's really a special honour for me to undertake this trip , both as a member of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ reset , and as the son of proud Tokelauan parents -- I am truly honoured .
