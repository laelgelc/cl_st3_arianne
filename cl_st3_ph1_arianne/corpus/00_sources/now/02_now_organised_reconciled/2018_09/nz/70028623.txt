Why , it 's almost as if all these countries are completely different countries ! <p> Africa has supported human life since human life began , and birthed cultures that know how to cultivate and survive . The need is almost always because people are being prevented from providing for their families by poverty , war , displacement , or disaster . <p> In Yemen , for example , a blockade of the country 's main port threatens to cut off the population from incoming aid .
