According to the commission , significant changes in land use are required to achieve this goal . This includes making marginal farmland , whether publicly or privately owned , available for planting as forests . <p> One of the report 's recommendations is that " the Ministry for Primary Industries , working with Land Information New Zealand ( LINZ ) and Landcare Research , should undertake a complete audit of the availability of government controlled land suited for afforestation ( whether native or exotic ) @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on such land as is available " . <p> KENT BLECHYNDEN <p> John Perkins ' farm was purchased by the government in 2012 . Straightaway , residents in the neighbouring town of Paekakariki saw potential uses for the land that was surplus to the motorway requirements . <p> The time is ripe to update the aged Public Works Act to ensure that the process of land disposal following the completion of large public ventures -- a process controlled by LINZ -- also supports this low-emissions transition . <p> The Public Works Act allows the compulsory purchase of privately owned land for public works , directly affecting not only people whose properties are needed but also , potentially , wider communities . In the Wellington region , such acquisitions have been necessitated by the current Transmission Gully , as well as by the recently completed Mackays to Peka Expressway and Peka Peka to Otaki projects . While many purchases involve small urban or rural sections , large areas are , at times , acquired . Consultation tends to focus on the actual route of particular highways ; the location of on and off ramps ; and the desirable features of associated cycleways and walkways . <p> Because these major ventures clearly have a significant short-term and , potentially , long-term impact on local communities , early stage consultation is regarded as vital . Yet , by contrast , on project completion , there is no requirement for public consultation on what to do with any excess land . Instead , the land is disposed through the provisions of the Public Works Act , which sets out several stages for the disposal of surplus land , including offering it back to the original owners . If the public knows that a sale is imminent , they can lobby both local and national government about future land use . Straightaway , residents in the neighbouring town of Paekakariki saw potential uses for the land that was surplus to the motorway requirements . Since the sale , community members have held numerous meetings with public agencies to discuss the future possible use of the farm . <p> There have also been a number of community meetings and various reports regarding possible usage of this land . These include : forest restoration on the steep erosion prone areas of the farm to create carbon sinks ; creation of stop banks and wetlands to reduce flooding threats to the village ; improving the health of local streams ; the building of a community owned windfarm ; and the development of community gardens . Most of these potential land use changes support the transition to a low-emissions economy . <p> While the Department of Conservation is one of the parties needing to be consulted in a sale process , other land uses which may not meet the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , developing wetlands for carbon sinks or creating community gardens , are currently not considered . <p> A number of reports produced by the Paekakariki community have indicated that planting -- or , at least , natural revegetation -- of the steep , erosion-prone Perkins ' land would be useful both for creating a local carbon sink and increasing biodiversity . Already the farm is at the centre of a newly created 2000 ha " Kapiti Mainland Island " : a predator control project that joins up three neighbouring restoration projects .
