<h> Positive returns for farmers but dark clouds ahead <p> OPINION : Calving and lambing are well and truly under way now with farmers working flat out and looking forward to another season forecast for very good returns . <p> It is pleasing to see that most primary sectors will benefit from continued growth in our exports according to MPI 's Situation Outlook report . <p> Red meat returns are strong and milk prices appear solid . <p> The softening US exchange rate will help these prices along , although farmer returns could be impacted by tariff wars . <p> The policies of the Labour-NZ First Government that negatively impact upon our agricultural sector continue to rightly concern farmers . <p> Results from spring milk testing are awaited to measure the spread of M Bovis with most farmers backing the eradication plan . <p> However , the uneasiness among farmers goes far beyond M Bovis . <p> On top of that they also have to worry about regulations around climate change , obligations under the Emissions Trading Scheme , ever-increasing petrol taxes , slowing overseas investment , reforms to industrial relations and a wide-ranging dairy industry review . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ but we think this is best dealt with in a scientific , evidence-based , pragmatic way , and opposes slap-happy taxes . <p> It is crucial that we help solve our methane emissions using technology and addressing the wider impacts on incomes , jobs and the economy .
