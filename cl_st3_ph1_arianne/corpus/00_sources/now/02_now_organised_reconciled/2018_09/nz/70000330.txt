<h> Pacific leaders expected to sign new security agreement in Nauru <p> NICK PERRY <p> Last updated 14:23 , September 5 2018 <p> JASON OXENHAM/POOL <p> The Pacific leaders gather for a formal photo opportunity during the Pacific Islands Forum in Nauru . They are expected to sign an agreement addressing climate change and crimes such as drug smuggling and illegal fishing that cross borders . <p> Pacific leaders meeting in Nauru are expected to sign a security agreement addressing climate change and crimes such as drug smuggling and illegal fishing that cross borders . <p> The declaration signing Wednesday is the centrepiece of the three-day Pacific Islands Forum . Prime Minister Jacinda Ardern arrived early on Wednesday to attend a leader 's retreat and the signing ceremony . <p> The declaration also addresses cybercrime and health issues such as communicable diseases and pandemics . <p> Under the Pacific-European Union Marine Partnership , the EU will provide 35 million euros ( $62 million ) and Sweden will provide 10 million euros ( $18 million ) over five years . The program will provide direct assistance to regional organisations .
