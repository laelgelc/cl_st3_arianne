Or the Labour-New Zealand First Coalition . Thirty years forward . <p> As with all 30-year plans it was virtuous and nebulous . Everything to agree with , nothing to oppose . And it was un-detailed and unspecific and unformed and unknown . <p> Winston Peters , Jacinda Ardern and James Shaw front the media at the launch of the Government 's 30-year plan . <p> Imagine if Peters had done a 30-year plan 30 years ago when : he was in the National Party , happy to be the Member for Tauranga , yet to be sacked and stood down from various ministries - and so far from being an international statesman he could @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Ronald Reagan or not . <p> The atmosphere in the room was warm , but a furnace away from the sort of heat Ardern produced a year ago on the election campaign trail .
