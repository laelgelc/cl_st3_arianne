<h> Otari Native Botanic Garden launches a laboratory <p> Otari Native Botanic Garden and Wilton 's Bush Reserve has taken a major step forward in its history , taking on scientific research aimed at saving native plant species . <p> The Lions Otari Plant Conservation Laboratory is now in operation on the site , focusing on seed germination and long term storage , including how seeds can be stored at -196degC in liquid nitrogen and then germinated into normal plants . <p> Once this process has been perfected , more species can be stored safe in the knowledge it can be germinated when needed for restoration or research . <p> The laboratory , a small prefabricated building , was set up with $72,000 raised by the Lions Club of Karori and more than $50,000 worth of work from Wellington City Council . <p> " Otari is the only public botanic garden in New Zealand dedicated solely to native plants .
