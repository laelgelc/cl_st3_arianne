<h> GE ban jeopardises farm emissions reductions : Peter Gluckman <p> New Zealand 's moratorium on genetic modification is a significant barrier to achieving reductions in greenhouse gas emissions from the farming sector , says Peter Gluckman , New Zealand 's outgoing chief science advisor . Gluckman , in his final report , has joined a growing pro-GE scientific lobbycalling for the government to reconsider the country 's anti-GE stance . <p> He says many of the most promising innovations to reduce agricultural greenhouse gas emissions rely on genetic engineering or genetic modification methods , which face restrictions in New Zealand . <p> The July paper to the Prime Minister , Gluckman says farmers can take a number of immediate steps to start reducing agricultural emissions , but for New Zealand to make meaningful steps it will need to embrace technological innovations . <p> " Clearly all social licence for these technologies does not exist in New Zealand , " Gluckman 's report says .
