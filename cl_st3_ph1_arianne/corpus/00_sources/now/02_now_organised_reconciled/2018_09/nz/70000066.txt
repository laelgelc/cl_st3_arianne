<p> " We are pleased to cooperate with the Government of Japan on the development of this key regional institution , " Mr Peters said . <p> " The Climate Change Centre will contribute to efforts to address the needs of Pacific Island Countries to combat the impacts of climate change over the coming decades . " <p> The Pacific Climate Change Centre is being built on the headquarters of the Secretariat of the Pacific Community Environment Programme ( SPREP ) in Apia , Samoa . <p> " New Zealand is pleased to be contributing up to NZ$3 million for the Climate Change Centre . This builds on Japan 's significant support , " Mr Peters said . <p> " We recognise that partnership is vital to development success and are look forward to cooperating with Japan on this project over the coming years . " <p> The PCCC is expected to open in mid-2019 , and will be a pre-eminent regional hub and centre of excellence for climate change issues .
