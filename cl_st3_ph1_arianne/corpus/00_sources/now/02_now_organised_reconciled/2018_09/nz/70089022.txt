Cyclone Liua has developed from a tropical storm , and is currently over the Solomon Islands . While it is only forecast to develop to a category 2 , that can still mean sustained winds well above 100kmh , and it is expected to also bring heavy rains to the Solomons . This one is n't looking likely to be a monster , but whatever comes next might . <p> Radio NZ reports that it 's the first time a cyclone has formed in September in the South Pacific for almost 70 years .
