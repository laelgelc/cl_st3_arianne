<h> National Portrait : Max Rashbrooke <p> Researcher and writer Max Rashbrooke has found that government is good but could be better . <p> You could try this some time . Head out to a busy pedestrian street and ask passers-by if they like government and want more of it . What would happen ? <p> " Few people would say we need more government , " Max Rashbrooke agrees . <p> Government is a hard sell . But ask people if they want more or better public services , and the answer would be very different . Polling tells Rashbrooke that two thirds of us would be prepared to pay more tax to fund public services . <p> " Which is really the same thing , and you would get a warmer response than if you said government , " he says . " That 's the challenge I 'm up against . " <p> Rashbrooke , 37 , is a respected writer and researcher based in Wellington .
