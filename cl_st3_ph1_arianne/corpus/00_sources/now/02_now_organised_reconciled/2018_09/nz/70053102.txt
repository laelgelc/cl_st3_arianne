<h> Popular McKee reserve campground tipped to reopen for Labour Day <p> Campers will soon once again be able to pitch their tents at McKee Memorial Recreation Reserve on the shores of Tasman Bay , near Nelson . <p> Time to air out that tent -- the popular McKee Memorial Recreation Reserve , near Nelson , is on track to reopen to campers in October . <p> " We 're hoping to be open for Labour Day , " Tasman District Council horticultural officer Richard Hilton on Thursday told councillors on the community development committee . " We should be all set to go for this summer . " <p> Closed since just before ex-Tropical Cyclone Fehi hit the Nelson-Tasman region on February 1 , the reserve is a prized camping spot , picnic area and low-tide walking area for visitors and residents of the region .
