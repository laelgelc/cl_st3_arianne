New incursions are more likely to take hold . <p> Niwa specifically investigates and models climate-change effects on rivers . Average river flows are expected to increase in the west and south of the South Island and decrease in the east and north of the North Island . Low flows are projected to become even lower and dry conditions to arrive earlier following the wet season over most of the country , with the exception of the West Coast . This means that water availability for irrigation will be less reliable . <p> Floods , by contrast , are expected to become larger everywhere , particularly in the south . <p> Soil conditions are projected to become drier over much of the country during spring and summer , with an earlier onset of dry conditions . By contrast , soils will be wetter during winter . <p> While water demand for agriculture is projected to increase , less will be available .
