<h> Focus on ' the positive ' for Maori <p> MAORI ASPIRATIONS AND DEVELOPMENT : Minister of Maori Development Nanaia Mahuta was the opening speaker of the Pae Tawhiti Maori Leadership Conference : " Pae Tawhiti - Beyond the horizons " , which began yesterday . Ms Mahuta spoke of how Maori knowledge can help shape the Government to make better informed decisions to achieve better outcomes for Maori , and ultimately New Zealand .
