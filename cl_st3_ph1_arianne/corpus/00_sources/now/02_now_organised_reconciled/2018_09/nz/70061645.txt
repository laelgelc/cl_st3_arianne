<h> Rally decision , Titirangi summit plans <p> This week will see a meeting of the council 's Future Tarawhiti committee on Thursday . <p> There is a request for the use of the western road network for the Silver Fern Rally on December 1 . This event requires temporary road closures of Pehiri , Wharekopae , Tahora , Rakauroa , Ruakaka , Parikanapa and Taumata Roads . Council officers advise in the paper that these roads are subject to a number of overdue maintenance requests which , if the rally were to go ahead , would be deferred till afterwards . Some are school bus routes so the buses would be using these roads on the Monday following . <p> The event organisers have visited Gisborne regularly and used some of these routes previously . They have contacted by letter all residents on the routes and some have expressed concerns that they are currently living with substandard roads and this event may make them worse until the overdue maintenance is completed , which would then be after the rally . <p> Councillors will have to weigh up the local concerns with the interest of the community in being @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ bond towards maintenance after the rally .
