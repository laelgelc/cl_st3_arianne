<h> UK to ban plastics from all its Pacific overseas missions <p> The United Kingdom has announced plans to ban all single-use plastics in its South Pacific missions <p> Britain currently has missions in Fiji , Papua New Guinea , Solomon Islands , Samoa , Tonga and Vanuatu . <p> It said banning the plastics will be part of global efforts to combat plastic pollution in the world 's oceans . <p> PACNEWS reported the ban is part of a wider commitment , with the UK Foreign Office having already announced its intention to cut single-use plastics from its UK operations by the end of this year and from its global operations by 2020 . <p> The UK said it would contribute about $US7 billion to developing countries before 2020 to help them mitigate and adapt to the challenges of climate change . <p> Its Department for Environment will provide an additional $US8 million to the Solomon Islands and Vanuatu from its Commonwealth Litter Programme .
