<h> FSM warns countries could disappear without climate action <p> The Federated States of Micronesia President says islands in the Pacific will be the first to disappear unless countries get behind the implentation of climate change agreements . <p> Peter Christian made the statement while addressing the United Nations General Assembly in New York . <p> He said the existential threat of climate change is becoming more real with every hurricane , wildfire , heat wave , and centimetre of sea-level rise . <p> Mr Christian said the reality is that islands like the FSM , Kiribati , the Marshall Islands and Tuvalu will be among the first to , literally , disappear . <p> President of the Federated States of Micronesia , Peter Christian .
