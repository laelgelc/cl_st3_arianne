<h> Coral bleaching impacts even deep reefs - study <p> New evidence has been found to suggest that coral reefs deep in the ocean are also affected by coral bleaching . <p> Deep reefs have long been considered a refuge from the thermal anomalies caused by climate change . <p> But a team who studied the 2016 mass bleaching in the Great Barrier Reef found that it was substantial even at great depths , 40 percent of deep coral was bleached in some areas . <p> Great Barrier Reef Photo : AFP / FILE <p> The University of Queensland 's Professor Ove Hoegh-Guldberg , who was part of the team , said while bleaching does lessen over depth , record water temperatures were still found as deep as 100 metres . <p> The scientists said the study , which is published in Nature Communications , added an extra limitation to the idea that deep reefs could act as a refuge for species from shallower reefs affected by bleaching.
