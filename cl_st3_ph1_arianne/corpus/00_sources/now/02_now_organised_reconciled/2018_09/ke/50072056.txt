<h> Mango value addition to mitigate Kitui farmers ' post-harvest losses <p> Over 800 mango farmers in Kitui County have been trained on reducing post-harvest losses of their fruits through value addition . <p> The training is spearheaded by the Kitui Enterprise Promotion Company ( KEPC ) in collaboration with the National Environment Trust Fund . <p> Grace Kiteme , a smallholder mango farmer has benefited from KEPC initiative . " I started with 85 mango trees back in the year 2011 and currently I have 0ver 400 mango trees , " she revealed . <p> " Previously , I used to experience close to 70% post-harvest losses which have now reduced to 25% as a result of value addition of the mango fruit , " said Mrs Kiteme . <p> Speaking to Kenya News Agency in Kitui on Tuesday , she disclosed that the mangoes have enabled a diversified revenue streams as a result of product range including mango powder , juices , fortified flours and mango flakes acquired from the mango processing . <p> Kitui County has a population of about 1.1 million people and it is categorized as an arid and semi- arid region . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ However , mangoes are the only trees that remain green when the area is dry . <p> The region
