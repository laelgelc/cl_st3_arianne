<h> Machakos County no longer on relief food <p> The Machakos County Executive for Agriculture and Food Security , Urbanus Musyoka ( in white shirt ) and the Machakos County Director of Agriculture , James Kariuki during a farmers ' training in Machakos ATC on Friday September 21 , 2018 . Photo by KNA . <p> The Machakos County is food secure and no longer in the list of counties receiving relief food , thanks to the uptake of innovation and modern farming technologies employed by farmers through the support of both the county and national governments . <p> Speaking during a two days farmers training at Machakos ATC on Friday , the County Executive for Agriculture and Food Security , Urbanus Musyoka said the county government has laid meticulous plans since 2013 which have enabled farmers to harvest maximally . <p> He noted that agriculture forms the main economic activity , source of livelihoods and employs 87% of the population in Machakos . <p> " As the key driver of the county 's economy , the government has put @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ modern farming technologies which continue to yield good results , " added Musyoka . <p> Ambitious plans by the Machakos government has improvised a quarter acre farming model designed to give a farmer multiple crop varieties which when well executed , the farmer would have continuous farm production and harvest throughout the year . <p> The quarter acre farming model for income is an effective and simple model of income generation with an ecosystem dam , capsicum garden , 10 beehives , a dairy unit and chicken where all the four make around Sh.63 , 000 per month . <p> The county executive said the government would select model farmers in each ward in two months for demonstration saying account opening would be mandatory for the farmers to benefit and for accountability purposes . <p> He said the county government would promote the quarter-acre model to farmers as a start-up then expand to large scale when the farmers gain experience after starting small . <p> As an example , Musyoka said if a farmer plants coriander ( dhania ) , black night shade ( managu ) , capsicum ( hoho @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ saumu ) , he would have revolving crops for the market throughout the year . <p> In the first month after planting , coriander is ready for the market while black night shade matures in two months with capsicum coming in three months . <p> He said through this strategic farming , farmers would be enriched due to the demand for the crops at local market since they matured at different times . <p> He defended Mutua 's record saying since 2013 , they have ploughed 12 , 814 hectares of farm land for an equivalent of 7 , 335 households , distributed over 619 tons of free seeds to over 139 , 000 households , given 58 , 358 chicks to 4 , 163 farmers and erected 250 green houses across the 40 wards . <p> In 2013 in collaboration with the national government , the county gave subsidized fertilizers and free vaccinations to 600 , 000 livestock , grass seeds to 528 farmers , restocked two dams one in Machakos and the other in Yatta with 10 , 500 fish fingerlings and improved extension activities . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ farmers in accessing quality farm inputs seeds , subsidized fertilizer so as to improve food security , " said Musyoka <p> The executive said water and climate change posed a challenge to farming in the area with rain fed agriculture no longer reliable due to its unpredictability . <p> He reiterated the government 's commitment towards rain water harvesting , dam construction and drilling of boreholes and called on farmers to have water harvesting structures at homestead levels for micro irrigation . <p> According to meteorological office , rains have been forecast to be of above normal beginning mid-October .
