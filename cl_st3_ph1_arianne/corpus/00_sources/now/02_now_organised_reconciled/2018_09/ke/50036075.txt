<h> Green bonds will spur investment in climate technology <p> Most additive and extractive industrial pursuits , that are often the go-to solution for most government development agenda , are the same ones that also carry a huge negative environmental effect . <p> While these still dominate the leading portfolios in terms of revenue and return in the market , the move towards smarter ways of work and living , riding on the back of advanced technology are starting to change the shape of business . <p> Unfortunately the track that many of these new businesses are subscribed to , is social enterprise , which is limiting in many ways as the purveyors of this flavour of capital often dictate overall direction . <p> The challenge comes when looking to raise money at a for-profit organistaion that wants to do good while doing well with a model that will see returns in the mid to long term .
