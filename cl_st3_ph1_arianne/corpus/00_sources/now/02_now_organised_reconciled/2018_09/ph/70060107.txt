<h> Trump rolls back pollution rules for drilling on US lands <p> BILLINGS , Montana -- The Trump administration recently rolled back an Obama-era rule that forced energy companies to capture methane -- a key contributor to climate change that 's released in huge amounts during drilling on US and tribal lands . <p> A replacement rule from the Interior Department rescinds mandates for companies to reduce gas pollution , which Trump administration officials say already is required by some states . <p> Within hours of the announcement , attorneys general for California and New Mexico filed a lawsuit in federal court seeking to reinstate the 2016 rule . <p> " We 've sued the administration before over the illegal delay and suspension of this rule and will continue doing everything in our power to hold them accountable to our people and planet , " California Attorney General Xavier Becerra said . <p> The change by Trump could save companies as much as $2 billion in compliance costs over the next decade .
