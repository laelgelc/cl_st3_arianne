<h> Gov't wants to maintain good ties with water utilities to ensure supply <p> Metropolitan Waterworks and Sewerage System ( MWSS ) wants to maintain a good relationship with its concessionaires to ensure that Metro Manila will not run out of clean water . <p> Reynaldo Velasco <p> In a statement , MWSS Administrator Reynaldo V. Velasco reiterated anew the need for teamwork among all stakeholders and strategic planning to ensure water security and to further address challenges posed by climate change . <p> Speaking at the 25th PWWA International Conference and Exhibition in Tacloban City the other day , Velasco assured the public of a continuing and fruitful partnership with its concessionaires Maynilad Water Services , Inc. , Manila Water Company , Inc. , and San Miguel Corp. -led Bulacan Bulk Water project . <p> This , after MWSS and Manila Water disagree on the need to build another water treatment plant in Laguna Lake , which the latter is proposing . Velasco , for his part , said the project is expensive to build and could mean higher cost of water for consumers once built . <p> During the PWWA conference , Velasco told delegates of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ concessionaires to make sure that safe water will be available and sustained under the present administration . <p> He also said there is no looming water crisis in the near future in Metro Manila . <p> He made the statement even as the country already falls below the international " water stress " threshold of 1,700 m3 per year and fast approaching the " water scarcity " threshold of 1,000 m3 per year . <p> Using the 2018 population of 107 million against the fresh water supply of 146 BCM , per capita availability stands at 1,553 m3 per year . <p> The **29;147;TOOLONG situation in the country represents the country 's water security issue . <p> Nevertheless , he said MWSS is now pursuing various projects to sustain the supply . <p> Velasco also unveiled about his proposed Angat , Bayabas and Candaba water source projects or A-B-C projects , which have a potential of producing a combined 1,950 million liters per day ( mld ) capacity to complement the existing capacity of Angat Dam . <p> These projects could address the increasing demand for water in the next @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ humble representation together with the MWSS Board of Trustees and our three concessionaires can all be proud of as part of our legacy to water security and sustainability , " Velasco said .
