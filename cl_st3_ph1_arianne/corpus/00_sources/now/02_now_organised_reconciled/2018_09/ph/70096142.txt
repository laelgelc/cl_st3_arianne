The blast hurt scores and killed two . Among the injured were several LP senatorial candidates . It led President Ferdinand Marcos to suspend the writ of habeas corpus , which essentially justified arbitrary arrests . Several protest leaders were imprisoned . Others went underground . A surge in mass protests and opposition activities ensued . <p> On September 23 , 1972 , Marcos declared martial law ; closed down the Senate , the House of Representatives , major newspapers , and radio/TV stations ; and arrested opposition and protest leaders . The exodus of activists to the countryside rejuvenated the armed struggle for power by the Communist Party of the Philippines . This armed struggle has smoldered ever since . <p> On August 21 , 1983 , former Senator Benigno Aquino Jr . arrived in the Philippines , after deciding to come home from his US exile @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , right on the tarmac . The ensuing massive protests culminated in the 1986 EDSA Revolution and installed the slain senator 's widow , Cory Aquino , as the new president . <p> Every year , these events are recalled by the generations of Filipinos that went through them , triggering similar debates between pro- and anti-Marcos sides among the millennial generation . <p> In the coming years , these martial law debates will rise in intensity , as the 50th anniversaries of events that happened in 1969 to 1972 are commemorated . <p> 2019 will be the 50th anniversary of the 1969 presidential elections that saw Marcos reelected for a second term . It was an election marked by the widespread deployment of " guns , goons , and gold " .
