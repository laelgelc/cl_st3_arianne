<h> Western Visayas Is 123% Fish Sufficient <p> Western Visayas is unaffected by the lack of fish supply as the region has 123% fish sufficiency rate . <p> " We are not affected by the lack of fish supply , " assured Remia Aparri , regional director of the Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources in Western Visayas ( BFAR-6 ) , on Thursday . <p> She said that last year the region has 398,000 metric tons production for aquaculture , commercial and municipal . <p> Although there was a decline in the municipal fishing figures , it was compensated by the commercial fishing , whose production increased by 9 percent , she added . <p> The decline in municipal production was attributed to climate change , less supply of bangus fry and the higher cost of production input that limits operators to increase their production . Also , some fishpond owners have yet to recover from their losses due to Super Typhoon Yolanda in 2013 . <p> Minus seaweeds , she said milkfish remains to be the top produce in Western Visayas when it comes to aquaculture .
