<p> They were chosen to " promote ESG investment for the purpose of improving the long-term return of the whole asset by reducing the negative externality to the environment and society " , GPIF says in a September 25 statement posted on its website , adding that it hopes the indexes " will provide an opportunity for companies to work on carbon efficiency and disclosure " . <p> The pension fund says the benchmarks " can include small listed companies that were not covered by other ESG indices , which goes along with GPIF 's idea to improve the sustainability of the overall market " . <p> According to S&amp;P Dow Jones Indices Chief Executive Officer Alex Matturri , the two benchmarks are the only carbon indexes selected by GPIF . He says the reweighting methodology of the indexes " helps encourage Japanese listed companies to adopt better carbon practices and increase data visibility " . Index constituents are weighted according to their carbon scores . <p> " As the large pensions and institutional investors have become increasingly aware of the need to understand , measure , and manage the environmental impacts of their investments , S&amp;P Dow Jones @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ contribute to the growth of sustainable economies , " Mr. Matturri says in a separate statement on September 25 . <p> GPIF 's move sends a strong signal to the market by acknowledging that climate change represents a threat to the long-term return of its assets , according to Loic Dujardin , managing director for Japan at Amsterdam-based ESG rating firm Sustainalytics . <p> " It also creates a strong incentive for Japanese asset managers to engage systematically with their portfolio companies on climate change-related risks and to improve their risk-adjusted return , " Mr. Dujardin tells Asia Asset Management . <p> GPIF , the world 's largest pension fund , was one of the first Asian signatories three years ago of the United Nations-supported Principles for Responsible Investment , a set of six guidelines on incorporating and implementing ESG principles into investment decisions and analysis . <p> GPIF had approximately 158.58 trillion yen of total assets under management at the end of June 2018.
