<h> Construction of SEA Games facilities uninterrupted by storm <p> MANILA -- Typhoon Ompong failed to hold back ongoing projects within Clark , including the building of sports facilities for the 2019 Southeast Asian ( SEA ) Games , Bases Conversion and Development Authority ( BCDA ) President and Chief Executive Officer Vivencio Dizon said . <p> In a press conference during the Philippine Economic Briefing Tuesday , Dizon said the recent typhoon only brought " very minimal damages " to the area . <p> Soon to rise within Clark is the administration 's pet infrastructure project , the 9,450-hectare New Clark City , which is intended to be the country 's next metropolis and which will help decongest Metro Manila . <p> Inside the New Clark City , construction of sports facilities for the 2019 Southeast Asian ( SEA ) Games is ongoing . <p> Dizon told the Philippine News Agency that despite weather disturbances experienced in Luzon in the past months , the developer of the SEA Games facilities is set to deliver the project on time . <p> Malaysian firm MTD Capital Berhad is the BCDA 's partner for the construction of the SEA Games facilities @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the sports
