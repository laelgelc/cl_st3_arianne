<p> QUEZON CITY , Sept. 21 ( PIA ) -- The Agricultural Communities International Foundation , Inc . ( AGREA ) , will host the first-ever Leaders and Entrepreneurs in Agriculture Forum ( LEAF ) at the Taal Vista Hotel , in Tagaytay from September 29 to 30 , 2018 . <p> The two-day forum with the theme " Redefining and Revitalizing Agriculture " aims to cultivate the newest generation of agri-champion by demonstrating agriculture as a field with abundant possibilities on and off the farm . <p> It also aims to bridge gaps in innovation and cultivate the newest generation of agri-champions by addressing the following topics : <p> Agripreneurship -- turning farming into a creative and lucrative business <p> Agritourism -- integrating farms into leisure and lifestyle to give a better understanding of food sources and connecting the public to producers . <p> AGREA founder and chief operation officer , Cherrie Atilano said the ultimate goal is to build a global community of agricultural innovation across different geographies , discipline , and levels of experience . <p> Atilano said agriculture is not a one-way ticket to get out of poverty and she wanted to change the current @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for future generations through agripreneurship , agritech , and agritourism . <p> She added , AGREA hopes to help in eradicating poverty among families involved in farming and fishing , alleviate the effects of climate change , and help establish food security in the Philippines , which is also an objective of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 2 . <p> Attending the two-day forum are scientists , techies , farmers , restaurateurs , and other agricultural advocates who will share their insights , find mentors , and take their pursuits to the next level . <p> AGREA hopes to reach a diverse audience of up to 500 delegates . <p> Included in the first day 's activities are keynote speeches and group sessions led by high-caliber speakers . It will emphasize on systems , design-thinking , mentorship , and interactive learning .
