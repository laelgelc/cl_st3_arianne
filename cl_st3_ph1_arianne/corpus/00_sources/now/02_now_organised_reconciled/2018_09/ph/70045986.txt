<h> EDC vows to continue environmental awareness drive among youth <p> DUMAGUETE CITY -- A key official of geothermal power leader Energy Development Corporation ( EDC ) based in Negros Oriental has assured that they will continue their advocacy to empower the youth to actively participate and gain more knowledge on the preservation of the environment . <p> The assurance came following the successful staging of this year 's science quiz bee among Negros Island senior high school students . <p> " We are happy to see the interest among our youth to start initiating changes in how we take care of the environment , " said Norreen Bautista , head of EDC 's Community Partnerships , External Relations and Watershed Management ( CREW ) Group on Tuesday afternoon . <p> " I think this has been strengthened by the fact that they have seen the effects of the onslaught of Typhoon Ompong , " she pointed out . <p> The EDC , along with its partners , has carried out for three years already
