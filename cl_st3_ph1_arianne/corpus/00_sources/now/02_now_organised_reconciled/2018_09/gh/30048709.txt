<h> Let 's explore pension funds for housing -- Atta Akyea <h> The Minister of Works and Housing , Mr Samuel Atta Akyea , has called for a stakeholders ' engagement on the construction of housing projects for government workers through a mortgage system that will leverage pension funds . <p> He explained that the pension fund , as a pool of resource backed by legislation , could see about 30 per cent of contributors ' funds invested in housing projects . <p> " In about 15 to 20 years , the one who did n't have money to buy property at a go will be able to say that ' I 'm retiring and I have a two-bedroom house .
