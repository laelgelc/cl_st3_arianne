<h> UN Chief says will continue to support Sri Lanka <p> President briefs UN Secretary General about Sri Lanka 's progress in democracy , rule of law and reconciliation <p> President Maithripala Sirisena briefed United Nations Secretary General Antonio Guterres about Sri Lanka 's progress in strengthening democracy , rule of law , media freedom , and reconciliation . He said this during a meeting at the UNSG 's office on the 27th Floor of the United Nations Headquarters in New York . <p> The President said that during the last three and half years , the country has made progress in many fronts , and his Government would continue to work towards reconciliation . He urged the international community to look at Sri Lanka with a fresh perspective , taking into consideration the progress made so far . <p> Secretary General Guterres warmly greeted President Maithripala Sirisena and the delegation , comprising Ministers Tilak Marapana , Champika Ranawaka , Mano Ganeshan , and Permanent Representative to UN Dr Amrith Rohan Perera .
