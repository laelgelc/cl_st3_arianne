<h> CHINA FACTOIDSDid you know that ... <p> The 2018 Beijing Summit of the Forum on China-Africa Cooperation ( FOCAC ) successfully concluded earlier this month . During the Summit , leaders from FOCAC countries gathered under the theme of ' China and Africa : Toward an Even Stronger Community with a Shared Future through Win-Win Cooperation ' , reviewed the implementation of the outcomes from the 2015 FOCAC Johannesburg Summit and agreed on plans for the future growth of China-Africa cooperation . <p> The Summit issued the Beijing Declaration Toward an Even Stronger China-Africa Community with a Shared Future and adopted the Forum on China-Africa Cooperation Beijing Action Plan , which sends out a strong message that China and Africa will work hand-in-hand for years to come . <p> China is the world 's largest developing country , and Africa is the continent with the largest number of developing countries . With a similar past and common mission , China and Africa have helped each other over the course of many years . In 2000 , the Forum on China-Africa Cooperation was established to answer the call of the times .
