<h> Sony to run on 100% renewable energy by 2040 <p> On Monday , the company announced that they aim to be 100 per cent renewable across all of their business sites by 2040 . <p> Sony 's European sites have already achieved this goal , but by 2040 they promise the same globally . <p> They want to install solar panels at their manufacturing sites in Japan and Thailand . Japan sites have the largest amount of energy consumption and therefore there will be a focus within the country to work directly with RE100 to ensure the target is met . <p> Kenichiro Yoshida , President and CEO of Sony Corporation , said : " For many years , Sony has been an industry leader in actively addressing climate change and other environmental issues . As part of our " Road to Zero " initiative to eliminate our environmental footprint , we are pleased to join RE100 and contribute to the realization of a society that operates on fully renewable energy . " <p> The RE100 are a group of over 140 members worldwide who are committed to promoting and utilising renewable energy . <p> Helen Clarkson , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ are expected to welcome Sony aboard RE100 .
