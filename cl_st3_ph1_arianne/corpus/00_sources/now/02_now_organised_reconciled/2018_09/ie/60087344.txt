<h> Villagers must contribute to reforestation , official tells district meeting <p> Luke Rawalai <p> 26 September , 2018 , 5:10 pm <p> Village headmen of Lekutu at their district meeting held at Votua Village on Tuesday , September 25 , 2018 . Picture : LUKE RAWALAI <p> VILLAGERS in the district of Lekutu in Bua , Vanua Levu , have been urged to take advantage of the Ministry of Forests free tree seedling distribution under Fiji 's Reforestation of Degraded Forests ( RDF ) program . <p> The ministry is distributing free seedlings of native trees to villages , including mahogany and pine . <p> Speaking at the Lekutu district meeting at Votua Village yesterday , ministry official Ilaisa Mataqeledra reminded the villagers that they too must contribute in re-growing their forests , at the same time contribute to efforts of combating climate change . <p> " Villages can submit the minute of their meeting with their request letter to their nearest forestry office if they are interested to join the RDF program , " Mr Mataqeledra said .
