<h> COP24 : Talanoa dialogue to generate awareness <p> Talebula Kate <p> 29 September , 2018 , 5:27 pm <p> COP24 chief negotiator Luke Daunivalu . Picture : FT FILE <p> THE intention of the Talanoa Dialogue to take place in the lead-up to and during the COP24 meeting in Poland is to generate greater awareness of what is happening tangibly and concretely on the ground across the different regions . <p> Chief negotiator Luke Daunivalu said this at a session titled ' Financing Climate Futures : Rethinking Infrastructure ' , which was organised by the Organisation for Economic Cooperation and Development , UN Environment and the World Bank Group . <p> Mr Daunivalu said this would allow everyone to " harness and learn from " those experiences " and be inspired to make greater commitments " as national governments compile their next round of NDCs ( Nationally Determined Contributions ) . <p> He was asked to share how the COP23 Presidency contributes to the low-emission , resilient transformation that is needed to ensure sustainable development in infrastructure . <p> Mr Daunivalu said the talanoa dialogue was an effective tool for generating greater awareness of the actions governments , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ greater resiliency to the effects of climate change . <p> The forum was held on the margins of the United Nations General Assembly . <p> Other panelists at the forum were ministerial-level participants from Ethiopia , Japan and Poland and senior climate and environmental officials of the United Nations and World Bank .
