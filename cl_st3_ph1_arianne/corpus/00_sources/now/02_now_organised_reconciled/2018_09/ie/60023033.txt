Photo : Fibonacci Blue ( CC BY 2.0 ) <p> PINE RIDGE , USA/GENEVA -- I was born and raised on the Pine Ridge Indian Reservation during a time when families were still living off the land . Many of the people still gathered their food from the land , plants and wildlife but this way of life was quickly fading . The land of my reservation home was not land that was vibrant and productive .
