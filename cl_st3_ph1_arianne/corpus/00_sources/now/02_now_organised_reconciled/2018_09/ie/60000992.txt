<p> FIJIAN Prime Minister Voreqe Bainimarama has called on international climate negotiators to urgently agree on implementation guidelines of the Paris Agreement . <p> Speaking to climate negotiators additional COP23 negotiating session in Bangkok , Mr Bainimarama did not mince his words when he stated that Governments were not ready for COP24 , or the 24th annual Conference of the Parties to the 1992 United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change -- a crucial climate change meeting in Katowice , Poland later this year . <p> " In three months ' time , we will be in Katowice , and frankly , we are not ready . I do n't think that statement should surprise anyone in this room , " Mr Bainimarama said . <p> " Our work in COP23 and throughout this year is essential to that goal . It is our responsibility-and I use that term purposely-to agree on implementation guidelines . " <p> The Implementation Guidelines , referred to by some as the " Paris Rulebook , " are the details that will determine how the Paris Agreement works in practice . <p> They address how countries will communicate , report , review , and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a way that is transparent and accountable to the international community . <p> In 2015 , countries agreed that they would be completed by COP24 . <p> However , that deadline is quickly approaching with much work still to do to produce a draft negotiating text that can be reviewed and finalised by ministers . <p> " Without Implementation Guidelines that everyone can live with , we risk chaos at Katowice and the possibility of yet another delay in the urgent work of combatting climate change , " Mr Bainimarama said . <p> " We have been talking since Rio , a full quarter-century ago . In that time , nine new nations have been created , and we have experienced a digital revolution . And also in that time , the warming of the earth has accelerated , and the seas have continued to rise . " <p> The COP 23 president said that in our effort to confront climate change , tens of millions of words have been spoken , and nearly as many promises have been made to the people leaders represented . <p> " If all @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ built this process since 1992 could be converted to clean energy , we could resolve the crisis now . But intentions are not actions . " <p> Mr Bainimarama also called on leaders for more ambition , and not just that but more ambitious action . <p> " Ambition means total commitment -- commitment to persevere and do all that must be done , here and back in your capitals . It means being focussed and energetic , " he said . <p> " Our main task at COP24 is to agree on a package of decisions that will ensure the full implementation of the Paris Agreement . " <p> Mr Bainimarama concluded his remarks with words of encouragement , urging the negotiators to rediscover the spirit of leadership , cooperation and compromise that led to the historic Paris Agreement in 2015 . <p> " Work in good faith with each other . Reach out across natural divides . Find common ground . Let us keep our eyes firmly on the common good . And then let us come together to reach consensus . " <p> Prior to the opening session on Monday , September 3 , Mr Bainimarama had also chaired the COP Bureau meeting in his capacity as COP president . <p> Meetings of the Bureau are convened by the COP President and are usually held on a regular basis during COPs and two or three times between to discuss important matters related to the Conference of Parties . <p> During its meeting in Bangkok , the Bureau discussed strategy , joint efforts and initiatives to help reach a coherent and balanced draft negotiating text by the end of the session . <p> On the sidelines of the meeting , the Prime Minister met with the incoming President of COP24 Michal Kurtyka . <p> Mr Bainimarama also extended his support to the COP24 President designate and said that the COP23 Presidency stands ready to assist in any wat it can . <p> He also met the Executive Secretary of the UNFCCC , Patricia Espinosa , where the two agreed on the importance of the work ahead .
