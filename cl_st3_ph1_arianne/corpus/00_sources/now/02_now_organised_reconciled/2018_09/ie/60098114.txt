<h> Risk of ? 693 million worth of damage if Shannon 's flood defences are n't improved <p> SHANNON 'S flood defences are not adequate , and unless ? 14.6 million is invested , a massive ? 693 million worth of damage could result . <p> That is the conclusion of the report on the Shannon Town and Airport embankments , carried out on behalf of Clare County Council , the Office of Public Works and Shannon Group . <p> The report warns , " There are considerable concerns regarding the adequacy of the existing embankment flood defences fronting both Shannon Town and Airport . The findings of the investigations undertaken for this report confirm that these concerns are justified . The embankments do not provide an adequate standard of protection from flooding from the Shannon Estuary . " <p> It said that because of the hydraulic connectivity between the town and the airport , and the common flood risk , measures should be taken jointly by the three bodies . Regarding what works should be carried out , the report says , " The preferred method of providing an appropriate standard of defence to the two frontages is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ faces and crest of all the embankments along the frontage , and the armouring of the front face of the Airport embankments and E6 ( a name given to one of the embankments in the report ) fronting Shannon town . This method is estimated to cost in the region of ? 14.6 million including an allowance for a mid range climate change scenario . This reduces flood risk relating to water levels overflowing and waves overtopping the embankments , and , more importantly , the risk of breaches in the embankments due to erosion of the front , crest and back face of the embankments . " <p> Explaining the benefits of the solution proposed , the report says , " It is considered to be an option that satisfies the requirements of reducing overflowing and overtopping volumes , reducing breach risk due to erosion of the crest and back face ; and reducing breach risk due to wave action on the front face . <p> " This option should be combined with monitoring , maintenance , flood warning , development restrictions and other non structural means of managing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ easily adaptable to increases in sea levels and storminess in the future . Other options considered either do not provide the necessary standard of protection , or are extremely costly . All options relating to holding the line of the existing embankments are likely to have a similar range of ecological and environmental impacts . " <p> If nothing is done , the consequences will be serious , the report warns . " Sections of embankments do not provide an appropriate standard of protection . In the event of failure in the do nothing case there will be a loss of the Airport , the Town and adjacent industrial areas . Residential properties within the Town are likely to have a value in excess of ? 375 million ( based on a population of 10,000 , four people per house and a value of ? 150,000 per house ) . In this regard it is agreed that the protection of the town and airport embankments should proceed as one project .
