<h> Irish Rail takes TD 's call for Limerick to Castleconnell train to leave later on board <p> IF A person works from 9pm to 5pm in Limerick city centre and wants to take the train home to Castleconnell it will take them two hours and 57 minutes . <p> The journey takes 20 minutes . This is because the last direct train leaves at 4.55pm . After that you could take the 5.50pm train from Colbert Station to Limerick Junction . Change trains to go to Ballybrophy , then get out and take another train back to Castleconnell . Arrival time is 8.47pm . <p> Deputy Jan O'Sullivan is calling on Irish Rail to give the evening Limerick to Nenagh train a later departure time . The Labour TD was one of many guests on a special train to Castleconnell to mark the 160th anniversary of the line . <p> " My Labour colleague Virginia O'Dowd , in conjunction with the Nenagh Rail Partnership , fought hard for a commuter service from Nenagh to Limerick .
