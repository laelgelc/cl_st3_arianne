<h> How TEDx and Cork Combined To Create #CorkLovesMusic <p> It was once written by researcher John Seely Brown that " Conversation is the catalyst for innovation " and nowhere is that more prominent than with the development of the TED talk series . TED ( Technology , Entertainment and Design ) , a non-profit organisation determined to spreading ideas across the world , was first launched as a conference in 1984 . <p> The conference , made up of short talks on a vast array of topics , has been a huge success and the concept has since been developed across the world . Scientists and other academics have educated the public about various aspects of everyday life , be it the continuing issue of climate change , or the psychological reasoning behind our choice of ice-cream , all under the TED name . <p> Local communities can hold their own events under the name TEDx allowing people to come together to discuss the issues and developments ongoing in their community . <p> In 2016 , a number of like-minded musical individuals got together in Cork to host an event about Cork 's musical landscape .
