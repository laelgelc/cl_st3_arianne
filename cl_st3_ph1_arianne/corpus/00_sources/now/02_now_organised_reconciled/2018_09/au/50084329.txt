<h> What Does Fuel Emissions and Consumption Really Mean ? <h> When fuel ignites inside an engine , some people think that an explosion occurs . <p> It might sound like semantics , but it is actually a controlled combustion -- the difference between throwing together ingredients for a dinner meal , and carefully measuring each one out . <p> Just as an engine knows how to blend the right quantities of fuel , air and spark , fuel companies are given the recipe that they must meet in order for their refineries to turn barrels of crude oil into an unleaded or diesel brew .
