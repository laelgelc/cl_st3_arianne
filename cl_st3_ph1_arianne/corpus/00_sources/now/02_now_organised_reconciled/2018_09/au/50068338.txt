<h> The tiny country town set to become suburban Perth <p> You might know it today as a picturesque agricultural settlement established in 1893 , but the Waroona of the future will be the new outer edge of Perth 's sprawling suburbia , a mere 1.5-hour drive from the CBD . <p> This is Waroona looking north along the Darling Scarp toward Perth .
