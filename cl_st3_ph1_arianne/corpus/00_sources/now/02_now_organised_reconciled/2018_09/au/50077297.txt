Apply now for a Regional Collaborations grant <p> September 28 , 2018 <p> Dr Sarah Hamylton and Dr Nani Hendiart <p> Applications are now open for eligible Australian research organisations and businesses who wish to apply for funding for projects and/or workshops under round two of the Regional Collaborations Programme . <p> The programme , administered by the Australian Academy of Science , will fund Australian participants from eligible organisations to collaborate with regional and international science , research and innovation partners on solutions to shared regional challenges within the Asia -- Pacific region . The latest funding round was launched by the Minister for Industry , Science and Technology Karen Andrews . <p> Funding of up to $1.38 million is available for collaborative , multi-partner projects . Projects are expected to commence after 1 April 2019 and must be completed by 31 December 2020 . <p> Funding of up to a total of $250,000 is available for non-project aligned , multi-partner workshops . Up to $100,000 per workshop is available for workshops held between 1 April 2019 and 31 December 2020 . <p> The first funding round provided five research @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ $2.5 million of matched funds by project partners was invested in the projects , close to a 3:1 return . <p> Dr Sarah Hamylton from the University of Wollongong was awarded $45,000 over three years to develop institutional capacity for regional monitoring of costal climate change impacts through remote sensing technologies . <p> " To be neighbourly is to be helpful , kind and supportive to those in your vicinity .
