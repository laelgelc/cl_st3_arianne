<h> Globe-trotting Barnett returns to her roots and wows Melbourne crowd <p> In April last year , Courtney Barnett played Festival Hall supporting Patti Smith , and joined her on stage for one song . <p> Fast forward a year and a half and it 's Barnett who is the main attraction , fresh off the release of her second studio album , Tell Me How You Really Feel , ads for which have been plastered on billboards around her stomping ground in High Street , Northcote . <p> Barnett has been on the road with a punishing schedule since May , which has seen her take in the US , Europe , Canada , New Zealand and Australia , with Melbourne her final stop before she hits the road again at the end of the month . <p> It 's no wonder then that Barnett , who has long been a firecracker live , is running such a tight ship . Her deadpan account of having an asthma attack and being rushed to hospital takes on added weight in the wake of Melbourne 's deadly " thunderstorm asthma " event . <p> Barnett 's sound has the kind of fresh-faced appeal of late ' 90s indie pop that we apparently never stopped loving . Yet it 's undeniably music for today . <p> She took her audience through Kim 's Caravan - a frustrated admonishment to inaction on climate change - and Depreston , a " misleadingly titled " song about house hunting in Coburg , which long before Bernard Salt penned his " smashed avo " column became something of an anthem for a generation of Australians locked out of home ownership . We should feel proud that her music , while quintessentially " Melbourne " , resonates with people all over the world .
