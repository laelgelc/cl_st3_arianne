<h> Hopes of royal wake-up for Fraser Island conservation <p> FRASER Island 's leading defender hopes Prince Harry and Duchess Meghan 's pending visit will " wake the Federal Government " up to their environmental responsibilities to protect the landmark . <p> Conservationist Dr John Sinclair AO , who has devoted more than 45 years to defending Fraser Island , said it was important there was a focus on the natural rainforests growing there , claiming they are its " heart and soul " . <p> Dr Sinclair told the Chronicle said it was time the Federal Government was made more aware of its responsibilities . <p> Over the last decade , the amount of money given by the Commonwealth for the protection of Fraser Island has remained at about $170,000 , which is being eroded away year by year , " Dr Sinclair said . <p> " It 's very poor in comparison to the protection of heritage sites all over the world , which receive millions of dollars . " <p> In February , Fraser Island was added to the Queen 's Commonwealth Canopy Initiative by Prince Charles during his visit to Bundaberg . <p> This is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ site during their royal tour which is built around the Invictus Games in Sydney . <p> The pair is expected to participate in engagements focussing on the environment , local history and sustainable tourism during their visit on October 22 . <p> Dr Sinclair said the island 's forests were facing two ominous threats in the near future , climate change and myrtle rust , a disease caused by an exotic fungus . <p> " Climate change is slower and more insidious , but myrtle rust affects most of the plants on the island now , " he said . <p> " It affects all plants in the Myrtaceae plant family , which comprise of the largest group of family trees on Fraser Island . <p> " I 'd suggest the Commonwealth make a bigger contribution to try and preserve this section of the Queen 's canopy . " <p> The island visit is built around the schedule of the Invictus Games , an international sporting event for injured and ill veterans founded by Prince Harry which will be held in Sydney .
