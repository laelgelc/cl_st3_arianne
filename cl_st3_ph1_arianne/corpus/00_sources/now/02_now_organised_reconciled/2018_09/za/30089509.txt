Archive ( ANA ) <p> In some schools , the only meal that learners are going to have for the day will be from a feeding scheme . <p> And this is what pushed Inmed Partnerships for Children to come up with innovative ways to ensure that learners are not only fed but are healthy . <p> Inmed Partnerships for Children , with the help of the Mondelez International Foundation , run aquaponics plants in the Eastern Cape and Gauteng which reach up to 100 000 primary pupils a year . <p> A TEAM build a small aquaponics system in Port Elizabeth . The harvest goes to schools for use in their feeding schemes . Archive ( ANA ) <p> Sihlahla said the aquaponics units are up to 10 times as productive as equivalently sized plots that are traditionally cultivated and use about 90% less water than traditional agriculture . <p> Speaking about the Port Elizabeth plant , Sihlahla said the great thing is that you are growing all your vegetables without using soil as a medium . We have two units here - the first is a greenhouse structure where we keep our fish . It has five tanks with a total of 4 000 fish , either tilapia or catfish . We then have nine grow-beds for vegetables . These grow about 130 heads of lettuce and 110 tomato plants . " <p> A TEAM build a small aquaponics system in Port Elizabeth .
