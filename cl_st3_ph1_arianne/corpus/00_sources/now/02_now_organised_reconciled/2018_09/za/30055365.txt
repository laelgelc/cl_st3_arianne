<h> Most Read <h> Most Read <h> CARBON-REDUCTION VENTURES <h> US oil majors in U-turn on industry climate initiative <h> After refusing to do so for years , Exxon Mobil , Chevron and Occidental Petroleum will become members of the Oil and Gas Climate Initiative soon <p> 21 September 2018 - 05:05 Agency Staff <p> Picture : ISTOCK <p> London -- US oil companies Exxon Mobil , Chevron and Occidental Petroleum will join an oil industry climate-change group led by their European rivals , after refusing to do so for years . <p> The companies will become members of the Oil and Gas Climate Initiative ( OGCI ) on September 24 , the group said . <p> The OGCI was founded in 2014 by the heads of BP , Royal Dutch Shell , Saudi Aramco and seven others based in Europe and Asia . It invests in carbon-reduction ventures . <p> The U-turn by the US oil majors puts them at odds with their country 's president , Donald Trump , who is trying to relax rules governing greenhouse-gas emissions and last year vowed to pull the US out of the landmark Paris climate agreement . <p> In 2015 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Europe broke ranks with their US counterparts , calling on governments to agree on carbon pricing at a climate summit in Paris . <p> Exxon and Chevron are now under new leadership , and have come closer to the views of their European competitors . <p> " It will take the collective efforts of many in the energy industry and society to develop scalable , affordable solutions that will be needed to address the risks of climate change , " Exxon CEO Darren Woods said . " This dual challenge is one of the most important issues facing society and our company . " <p> With the three US firms , the OGCI will have 13 members . Together , they represent about 30% of global oil and gas production .
