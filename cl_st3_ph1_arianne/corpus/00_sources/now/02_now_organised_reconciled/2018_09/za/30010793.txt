<h> Those in public service must serve honestly <p> This month is designated as a Public Service Month to recognise the selflessness of those men and women who are serving the South African public with distinction across the three spheres of government . <p> However , this should also be a month in which we reflect on our role as public servants in the realisation of the vision of the National Development Plan ( NDP ) to eliminate poverty and reduce inequality by 2030 . <p> It is an opportune time for introspection on the extent to which we are contributing to hampering these noble goals , leading to public frustration and casting the government as unresponsive to their plight . We need to look hard at our conduct to see if we are doing enough to change the material conditions of the marginalised . <p> This theme should be a clarion call to public servants to be agents of change . Through the resources at our disposal , we must work tirelessly to make tomorrow better than today for millions of people who still languish in despair and hopelessness . <p> As we pay homage to those of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the needy , we should work to raise the bar in terms of our ethical conduct . The public service should not be a cash cow for rogue elements bent on milking it dry . <p> Serious gatekeeping must be exercised through rigorous processes to ensure that only those with the interest of the public genuinely at heart join the ranks of government <p> The vision of the NDP requires nothing less than a committed public service that exhibits the highest standards of ethical behaviour . <p> It is in light of this vision that the Department of Water and Sanitation , which is dealing with a myriad challenges , including water shortages , climate change , toxic waste and water pollution , seeks to attract not only appropriate skills , but public servants who pursue ethical conduct with unerring vigour . <p> The magnitude of these and other challenges cry out for solutions that require not only the kind of skills that are needed in the sector , but conduct towards which all those who are part of the department should strive . <p> In the face of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the department requires and encourages public servants to uphold values that are fundamental to our Constitution , and frowns on anything that resembles any tendency that exhibits , even to the slightest degree , illegal conduct that involves moral turpitude . <p> So , apart from technical skills and hard work , the department is putting emphasis on the impeccable integrity of its officials . Judging by the scale of work the department is entrusted to carry out , almost everyone agrees that officials should be able to withstand the temptation to do something that is irregular or dishonest for purposes of self-enrichment . Taking resources meant for the public is tantamount to taking food out of the mouths of the poor and is an act of treason . <p> Appropriately qualified and ethical public servants are central to the transformation of the South African water and sanitation sector . <p> Any effort aimed at changing the landscape of the sector calls for incorruptible and accountable public servants . <p> As we celebrate Public Service Month , we should stop for a moment to think about the dire situation in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ time , it should be our foremost priority to become the champions and the voices of the voiceless . <p> Ours must be to ensure that we do not in any way impair their dignity , which we are enjoined to promote , protect , respect and fulfil .
