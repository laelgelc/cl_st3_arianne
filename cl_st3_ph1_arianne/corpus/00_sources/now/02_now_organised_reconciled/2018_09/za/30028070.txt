and that is in all our interests , " said Ban . <p> The commission , which will be supported by the Netherlands-based Global Center on Adaptation and the World Resources Institute in Washington D.C. , plans to deliver a flagship report at a U.N . climate summit next September . <p> Its roughly 20 commissioners -
