<p> Foreign Minister Vivian Balakrishnan has held a series of meetings on the sidelines of the United Nations General Assembly in New York , according to a statement by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs ( MFA ) . <p> At a bilateral meeting with German Foreign Minister Heiko Maas yesterday , they reaffirmed the longstanding and excellent relations between Singapore and Germany . <p> They welcomed the upcoming signing of the Free Trade Agreement , Investment Protection Agreement , as well as the Partnership and Cooperation Agreement between the European Union and Singapore next month in Brussels . These agreements will strengthen ties between both countries and the two regions , the MFA said . <p> Following their meeting , Dr Balakrishnan and Mr Maas issued a joint declaration to reaffirm the commitment of both Singapore and Germany to free trade , multilateralism and a rules-based global order , and to deepen cooperation at all levels .
