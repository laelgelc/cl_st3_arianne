<h> India 's focus on solar power may earn it a place in the sun by 2022 <p> India , the world 's third-largest energy consumer after the US and China , is working to meet 100 GW solar power target by 2022 as part of its global climate change commitments . <p> The sun 's shining bright on India 's solar energy plans . The world 's third largest consumer of energy after the US and China , India has crossed 25 GW in terms of total installed solar capacity , and is moving closer to the 2022 target of 100 GW . <p> In the first half of 2018 , about 5 GW of solar capacity was installed across the country including both large-scale and rooftop solar . <p> " We are well on track to achieving 175 GW target of installed renewable energy capacity . Trends suggest that the target will not only be achieved , but exceeded , " a statement released by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy said . <p> Solar energy capacity has gone up from 2.63 GW in 2014 to 22 GW currently , an eight-fold increase .
