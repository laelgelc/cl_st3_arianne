<h> Private foundations pledge US$450 million to save forests , climate <p> SAN FRANCISCO ( THOMSON REUTERS FOUNDATION ) - Leading philanthropists pledged hundreds of millions of dollars to rescue shrinking tropical forests that suck heat-trapping carbon dioxide from the atmosphere , on the eve of a global climate change summit in San Francisco . <p> Nine foundations announced the US$459 million ( S$632 million ) commitment , to be delivered over the next four years , a day ahead of the Global Climate Action Summit , which is expected to draw about 4,500 delegates from city and regional governments . <p> " While the world heats up , many of our governments have been slow to act .
