" <p> He said : " Building bigger and bigger drains to deal with more and more intense rain is not a good method . The SDT and SDC are better ways . By detaining , delaying and diverting storm runoff before it can do serious harm , the SDT and SDC offer long-term flood protection for Orchard Road .
