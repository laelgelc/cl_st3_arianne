Instead , it could be repaired or recycled . <p> Advertisement <p> Filling landfill with clothing and textiles costs the UK alone an estimated ? 82 million ( US$106 million ) every year . But on the flip side , the consumption of clothing is hugely important to the economies of many countries , too . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ found that global fashion is a US$2.4 trillion industry . <p> Despite this , materialistic values and a widespread desire for having new things , twinned with fashion 's premise to create and sell different styles , has reduced the functional value of clothing , making it easily disposable . A staggering 100 billion items of clothing are being produced annually , and 50 per cent of fast fashion pieces are disposed of within a year . <p> In fact , recent figures show that one rubbish truck of textiles is thrown away every second globally . Little wonder , then , that fashion has been dubbed " incredibly wasteful " -- even by insiders . <p> Advertisement <p> Advertisement <p> THE PROBLEM WITH FASHION <p> Fashion and sustainability have historically had an uncomfortable relationship . The 2013 Rana Plaza disaster in Bangladesh , along with growing concerns over sweatshop labour , have seen fashion companies overhaul their social and environmental footprint . <p> ( Photo : AFP ) <p> Consumers , meanwhile , have grown increasingly concerned about where and how garments are made . Some 35 per cent of the global total of microfibres in the oceans comes from clothes and textiles , meaning fashion is a major contributor to this pollution . <p> By 2050 , it is anticipated , the fashion industry will use up 25 per cent of the world 's carbon budget . <p> RECYCLING ? <p> So what 's the solution ? A circular economy seeks to move beyond fashion 's linear model of take , make and waste , to close the loop , designing out waste and minimising environmental impact . <p> While fashion brands work to limit their polluting practices through the creation of organic , environmentally conscious collections , there is still a need to limit the sheer volume of waste that fashion creates . <p> Recycling has become an important initiative to address this . <p> H&amp;M , for example , has a successful garment collection scheme @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , meanwhile , are using recycled materials to create clothing . Outdoor clothing brand Patagonia has made polyester fleece out of recycled plastic bottles . <p> A woman shops at an H&amp;M store in New York City , U.S. December 23 , 2017 . ( File Photo : REUTERS/Stephanie Keith ) <p> While recycling could achieve circulatory by designing out waste , it is problematic environmentally . Recycling is energy intensive and may require use of further virgin materials . <p> Additionally , while it resolves some of fashion 's sustainability issues , it does not adequately address the problem that consumers buy too much , and that the average number of times a garment is worn has declined by 36 per cent since 2000 . <p> We must reconsider how fashion is sold , encouraging consumers to waste less , and ensure that garments have a longer life span . <p> RENT , DO N'T BUY <p> One UK resource efficiency agency , WRAP , has identified leasing as an innovative business model that gives clothes a longer service life , while reducing material use and carbon dioxide emissions @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in London also suggested that clothing rental may be a growing key trend in retail . <p> The possible value of the clothing rental market in the UK is predicted to be ? 923 million and the model is already well-established for certain items , such as dinner jackets and wedding suits for men . <p> Despite this , there are currently just a handful of fashion companies that have adopted a leasing model . <p> At Mud Jeans , for example , consumers can lease a pair of organic jeans , and after a year can keep , swap or return them . Girls Meets Dress , meanwhile , was founded in the UK in 2009 , under the ethos that in a sharing economy ownership will become obsolete . <p> In America , Rent the Runway has become a significant player in the fashion industry . However , there are issues to consider from transportation to dry cleaning . <p> Clothing rental has the potential to reduce waste and increase the lifespan of garments , but to achieve a more sustainable industry , a systemic change in business practice and consumer behaviour is needed . <p> Naomi Braithwaite is a senior lecturer in Fashion Marketing and Branding at Nottingham Trent University .
