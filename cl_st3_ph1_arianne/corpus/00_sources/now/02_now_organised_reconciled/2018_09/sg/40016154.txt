<h> ' Win-win for all ' as Chinese invest in Africa <p> The Investing in Africa forum held in Changsha city , Hunan province , on Thursday ( Sept 6 ) and Friday saw lively discussions on development issues and business opportunities in Africa including agriculture , energy and climate change , skills development and the digital economy .
