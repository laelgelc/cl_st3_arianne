<h> Malaysia to get rid of single-use plastic by 2030 , says minister <p> Energy , Technology , Science , Climate Change and Environment Minister Yeo Bee Yin said the ministry is working on ways to fully abolish single use of plastic by 2030 . ? Picture by Sayuti Zainudin <p> PUTRAJAYA , Sept 24 -- The Ministry of Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change ( MESTECC ) is working on ways to fully abolish single use of plastic by 2030 , its minister Yeo Bee Yin said today . <p> " Plastic contamination has become a national issue and it bears negative implications to the environment , " Yeo told reporters after chairing a town hall session on the issues surrounding single-use plastics at Putrajaya . <p> The town hall session was attended by more than 500 stakeholders from various disciplines . <p> Yeo said plastic itself was not the problem , but rather its handling and disposal were hazardous to the environment . <p> She said the government was studying the possibility of using alternatives to plastic such as hydrocarbon-based plastics , which were more environmentally friendly .
