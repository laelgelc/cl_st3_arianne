<h> Sabah to seek Ramsar status for Klias Wetlands <p> KOTA KINABALU : Sabah is expecting to attain international recognition for Klias Wetlands in the near future . <p> The state now has Lower Kinabatangan-Segama Wetlands and Kota Kinabalu Wetlands of international importance , where both had been bestowed with Ramsar status . The status is accorded under the Ramsar Convention , an inter-governmental treaty that provides the framework for national action and international cooperation for the conservation and wise use of wetlands and their resources . State Wetlands Conservation Society president Datuk Zainie Abdul Aucasa said by having more areas in Sabah to be recognised as Ramsar sites , it would mean there will be more protection for the habitat . " It took about eight years for previous sites to be recognised , but we hope that for Klias it can be done in shorter time . " This time is shorter , it could be five years , ( Klias ) is a good site and we hope to make it a reality , " he said , adding the Society could help to initiate the paperwork to be submitted to Sabah government . Meanwhile , the society vice president Dr Rahimatsah Amat said the nomination process of Klias wetlands as a Ramsar site could only be done upon completion of paperwork , endorsement from Sabah government and later submission of the proposal from Water , Land and Natural Resources ministry at federal level to the Secretariat of Ramsar Convention . " For now , the boundary of the proposed Klias wetlands is still in discussion with the state government as those areas include forest reserves and private land , " he said . Earlier , Sabah Tourism , Culture and Environment minister Christina Liew said wetlands were among the most threatened ecosystems in the world . " Many wetlands have been significantly destroyed in order to make way for development . " However , if managed well , wetland ecosystems can play a vital role in the mitigation of climate change which helps humans counter its effects based on its role in ensuring water and food security , " she said .
