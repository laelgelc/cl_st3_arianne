We should champion the idea of ' clean kilometres ' . <p> " My vision for the future of mobility in India is based on 7 Cs : common , connected , convenient , congestion-free , charged , clean , cutting-edge , " he said . <p> The focus must be to go beyond cars to other vehicles such as scooters and rickshaws , Modi said .
