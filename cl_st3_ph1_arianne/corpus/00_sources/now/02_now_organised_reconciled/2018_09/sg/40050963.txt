<h> Lynas plant safe , let it operate freely : Pahang assemblyman <p> KUANTAN : After years of being on the receiving end of criticism for the state 's controversial rare-earth business here , Beserah assemblyman Andansura Rabu hopes that Australia-based Lynas Corporation Sdn Bhd ( Lynas ) will now be allowed to operate just like any other factory in the country . <p> Andansura , who is also the state Pas deputy commissioner , said that the plant has been operating in Gebeng here for six years and by now , the people should be able to judge whether its presence is harmful , based on facts and not emotion . <p> He admitted that in the early stages when Lynas began its operations here , many people were concerned about various issues and if Lynas might be a threat . <p> " I admit that we did oppose the plant ... this was due to bad experience in the past and wanting to prevent issues like the Bukit Merah incident from recurring . <p> " Now , after six years , Lynas has strong proof and data to ... prove that it is safe and has created @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ past ( when opposing Lynas ) , we might have been a little emotional , but now , we have to think rationally and there is no need for double standards . People no longer need to have negative impressions about the rare earth plant , " he said . <p> On Tuesday , Andansura attended a high-tea session with Lynas management staff members and the village headman from Beserah here in conjunction with the visit of the Australian High Commissioner to Malaysia , Andrew Goledzinowski . <p> Andansura said he visited Lynas some three weeks ago with Bentong Member of Parliament Wong Tack , who is among those leading the committee set up to review the Lynas project . <p> He said during the visit , they were briefed on the plant 's waste management , which prompted him to send a letter with three recommendations to Energy , Green Technology , Science , Climate Change and Environment Minister Yeo Bee Yin on Sept 6 . <p> " I noticed the amount of waste has been increasing , and although Lynas is managing them well , there might be @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ has plans to recycle the waste into a soil enhancer for agricultural use , I suggest the ministry should consider approving it . <p> " Secondly , I recommended that the Department of Environment and Atomic Energy Licensing Board 's reports on Lynas be made public . This includes their findings over the past six years so that the people will have better knowledge of the plant and its business , " he said . <p> Andansura also proposed that all parties , including non-governmental organisations , who want to gather information on Lynas must obtain them from the government and not stir trouble with Lynas . <p> " Lynas has to be treated fairly as an investor in our country and people must not simply vent their frustration at the plant , but gather genuine information through the proper channel , " he said .
