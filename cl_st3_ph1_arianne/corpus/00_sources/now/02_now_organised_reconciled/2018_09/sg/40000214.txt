<p> SINGAPORE - Solar shades , natural ventilation and renewable energy panels are some of the prospective energy-saving features the national regulator wants to see in more Singapore buildings . <p> To intensify the move to a greener future , the Building and Construction Authority ( BCA ) has launched a new energy rating for buildings that are at least 60 per cent more energy efficient compared with 2005 building codes . This was announced on Wednesday ( Sept 5 ) , the first day of the Singapore Green Building Week ( SGBW ) at Marina Bay Sands . <p> Under the new rating , called Green Mark for Super Low Energy , office buildings can not use more than 100 kilowatt hour ( kwh ) per sq m a year . <p> To help more buildings qualify for the voluntary rating , the BCA is working with industry professionals , including property developers , to build such super low energy buildings , said its chief executive , Mr Hugh Lim . <p> The BCA is also doing more rigorous research and innovation to further push the frontier for green buildings , he added . <p> " By setting @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ role in mitigating climate change , " Mr Lim said . <p> Previously , the highest class under the Green Mark - the BCA 's benchmarking scheme - was the Green Mark Platinum , awarded to buildings that save at least half of the energy listed in the 2005 building codes . <p> More than 10 organisations , including Defence Science and Technology Agency , Singapore Management University and City Developments , have pledged to achieve at least one super low energy project in the next five years , a BCA spokesman said . <p> The BCA is now working with property developer Keppel Land to convert Keppel Bay Tower , a Green Mark Platinum building , into a super low energy building . <p> In 2017 , Keppel Land was awarded $1.28 million from the BCA 's Green Buildings Innovation Cluster to conduct a pilot on super low energy technologies at its building in HarbourFront . <p> Five technologies to be tested in the 18-storey building later this month include : a smart lighting system , a fresh air intake system that ?
