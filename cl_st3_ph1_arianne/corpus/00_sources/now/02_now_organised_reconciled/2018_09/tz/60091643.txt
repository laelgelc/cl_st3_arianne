<p> A new research released on Tuesday says serious and rapid investment in sustainable upgrades and alternatives is needed to limit climate change . <p> With high-carbon infrastructure responsible for the majority of global greenhouse gas emissions , a summary of an upcoming UN Environment , OECD and World Bank Group report , Financing Climate Futures : Rethinking Infrastructure , lays out six ways to bring public and private financial flows in line with the Paris Agreement goals , in particular in infrastructure finance . <p> The report says governments must adopt a more transformative agenda on financing for a low-carbon future to meet the Paris temperature goals by peaking CO2 emissions as soon as possible and then bringing them down to net zero or lower in the second half of the century . <p> Solutions in the report include better planning and foresight , integrating climate concerns into all budgetary decisions and leveraging public procurement into low-emission infrastructure . <p> " Building climate-compatible infrastructure is a cornerstone for the success of the Paris Agreement and broader sustainability goals , and we have seen encouraging momentum in this direction .
