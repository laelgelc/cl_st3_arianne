In Malawi , the analysis has found that the national water policy , national climate change adaptation programme of action , and agricultural policies are not consistent with efforts to address climate change . <p> The SDGs and the Paris Agreement on climate change call for coherent policies across sectors such as water , agriculture and energy to help address climate change impacts including severe droughts and flooding . <p> The authors of the policy brief resulting from the analysis recommend policy coherence across such sectors . <p> " There are many barriers to a lack of policy coherence . Some are common throughout all countries , including lack of buy-in , conflicting mandates for sectoral ministries or the limited resources to enable the processes necessary to support coherence , " says Patrick Curran , co-author of the policy brief and a policy analyst at the UK-based Grantham Research Institute on Climate Change and the Environment , London School of Economics and Political Science . <p> Other barriers include limited institutional @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to undertake regular reviews or institute a process to update and align national and sectoral policies . <p> The researchers analysed climate change policies and related policies such as those that focus on water , agriculture , energy , climate change and national development plans in Malawi , Tanzania and Zambia by using a scoring system to assess the extent to which the policies were consistent in addressing climate change impacts . <p> Andy Dougill , a co-author of the policy brief and a professor of environmental sustainability at UK 's University of Leeds , tells SciDev.Net , " The research was conducted to gain a greater understanding of the political economy affecting climate change and sustainable development decisions being taken in the region .
