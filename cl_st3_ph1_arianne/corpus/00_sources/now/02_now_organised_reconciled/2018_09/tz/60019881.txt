<h> Local organisations team up to support cleanliness efforts <p> LOCAL organisations team up to support Ilala District 's cleanliness efforts aiming at improving hygiene and ensure clean environments at various areas in the city . <p> Ilala District Commissioner , Sophia Mjema . <p> The cleanliness exercise to be done at different places within Ilala District will be marked on September 15th in collaboration with the office of the Ilala District Commissioner . <p> Addressing journalists recently in Dar es Salaam , Ilala District Commissioner , Sophia Mjema mentioned the local organisations that will take part in the exercise as , the Forum CC , United Nations Environment ( UN Environment ) , Nipe Fagio , and Lions Club with the support from European Union ( EU ) . <p> She said the exercise is important since apart from ensuring clean environments , it targets to emphasise and build a culture of cleanliness amongst petty traders , road users and environment officers . <p> The Ilala DC noted that the exercise will involve 1,000 people from Ilala municipality and that it will be conducted in selected areas including Selander Bridge , areas surrounding the Ocean Road Cancer Institute @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Segerea and Jangwani areas . <p> " The purpose of the exercise is to sensitise citizens on the importance of hygiene and to initiate a sustainable schedule for the exercise , " she said adding the organisations have decided to support the campaign by providing required and tools equipment such as dustbins . <p> According to Mjema , the government will not spare anyone disobeying cleanliness regulations . She said stern measures will be taken against those violating the laws . <p> Mjema directed local government officials to allow citizens to collect garbage and take them to dumping sites if contractors have failed to properly execute their jobs . <p> Faizal Issa , project manager from Forum CC , an organisation committed to work on climate change , said they have teamed-up with other partners to join efforts towards ensuring better hygiene in the city . <p> During the World Hygiene Day , all people will participate in their areas , while in the country there are about 80 areas earmarked for the cleanliness on that day , he said . <p> " We will work to educate citizens on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and simple garbage , in order to protect the environment effectively , " he said . <p> Issa said that since hygiene is important , manufacturers of plastics bags should also take part on September 15th 2018 so that they contribute in making sure that their environments are safe and clean .
