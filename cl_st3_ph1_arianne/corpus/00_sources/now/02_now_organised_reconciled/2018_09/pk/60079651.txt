<h> Fighting the timber mafia <p> The assailants of Muhammad Asif -- aPunjab Forest Department guard , who was abducted and tortured by the timber mafia in 2015 when he attempted to stop some of its members from illicit logging-were finally arrested this past Saturday . The victim filed the first FIR regarding this incident on January 20 , 2015 but it was only after another FIR that the law took its course . Footage of this harrowing incident also went viral on social media three years ago . Later , it emerged that that the perpetrators had connections with a Pakistan Muslim League-Nawaz MPA.As such , this is a case that has exposed how criminal organisations are able to operate with impunity in Pakistan because of their political patrons . <p> Connections between criminals and politicians are detrimental to the state of law and order throughout the country , but the consequences Pakistan will have to bear on the environmental front could be absolutely disastrous if this state-of-affairs does not change . As such , the provincial and federal governments will have to do something about these problems . <p> But these are other issues as well .
