<h> Senate delegation participates in ASEP10 <p> ISLAMABAD : A three-member delegation of parliamentarians from Senate has reached Brussels to participate in 10th Asia-Europe Parliamentary Partnership Meeting ( ASEP10 ) hosted by the European Parliament in the Belgian capital . <p> According to a press release issued on Thursday , the delegation comprises Senators Najma Hameed , Shafiq Tareen and Faisal Javed . <p> Senators have attended plenary sessions on 27th and participated in discussion panels of three thematic sessions pertaining to environment and climate change . The first panel discussed impact of climate change and environment on the economy . <p> The second panel looked into the impact on migration ; whereas the third panel deliberated on the impact of climate change and environment on the security . <p> The delegation would also visit House of European History and have an interaction session with Pakistani community and the media based in Belgium , the press release said .
