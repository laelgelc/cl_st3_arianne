<p> ISLAMABAD : Advisor to the Prime Minister on Climate Change Malik Amin Aslam Khan has said that the initial response to our tree plantation drive #Plant4Pakistan was phenomenal . <p> People from all walks of life overwhelmingly participated in the plantation drive . It was more encouraging that the youth of Pakistan actively participated in this initiative . <p> We initially set the target of planting 1.5 million trees in a day but due to active and massive participation , we ended up with more than 2.5 million which is a highly remarkable achievement . Therefore , we have decided to start the next phase of this plantation drive by coming January . <p> He stated this while chairing a high level meeting at the Ministry of Climate Change , Islamabad on Tuesday . Climate Change Federal Secretary Khizar Hayat Khan and senior officials from the ministry were also present in the meeting .
