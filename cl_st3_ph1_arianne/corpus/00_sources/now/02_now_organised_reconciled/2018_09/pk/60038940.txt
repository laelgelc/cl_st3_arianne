<p> The Chinese envoy congratulated the advisor on climate change for launching 10 billion tree programme and said this is a landmark initiative which reflects seriousness of the government towards addressing the country 's present environmental and climate change issues . <p> During the meeting China ambassador lauded the Ministry of Climate Change 's efforts to boost Pakistan 's forest cover and control deforestation as a part of climate resilience , adaptation and mitigation measures . <p> Malik Amin Aslam shared with the Chinese ambassador information about various measures taken by the present government to address the environmental risks and climate change issues . <p> The Chinese ambassador informed that Prime Minister Imran Khan is visiting China during the first week of November this year . a Memorandum of Understanding will be signed between the countries to boost collaboration in Climate Change promotion of forest management sectors .
