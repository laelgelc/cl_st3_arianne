" We are in a crisis , " Martha Rojas Urrego , head of the Ramsar Convention on Wetlands , told reporters in Geneva , warning of the potential devastating impact of wetland loss , including on climate change . <p> The convention , adopted in the Iranian city of Ramsar nearly a half-century ago , on Thursday issued its first-ever global report on the state of the world 's wetlands .
