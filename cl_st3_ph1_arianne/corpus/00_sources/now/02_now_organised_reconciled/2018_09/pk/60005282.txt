<h> Two new Climate Change bodies to be constituted <p> ISLAMABAD : ? Ministry of Climate Change ( MCC ) will constitute two new climate change bodies called the Climate Change Council ( CCC ) and the Climate Change Authority ( CCA ) under the Pakistan Climate Change Act , 2017 . <p> MCC Secretary Khizer Hayat Khan informed the Senate Climate Change Caucus about the decision in a ? recent meeting held at the Parliament House . <p> The CCC would be constituted as the apex decision-making body . <p> Both the Council and Authority will coordinate , monitor and supervise the implementation of the National Climate Change Policy and the Sustainable Development Goals . <p> To make the CCA functional , the ministry has demanded 282 posts whereas the Management Services Wing has agreed to 124 posts . <p> The case related to the creation of 124 posts has been submitted to the ? Ministry of Finance .
