<h> WEF applauds success of ' Plant for Pakistan ' campaign <p> World Economic Forum ( WEF ) has endorsed efforts of Pakistan government to plant 1.5 million trees in one day under ' Plant for Pakistan ' campaign , which was initiated by Prime Minister Imran Khan as part of a mega 10 billion trees over the next five years . <p> The World Economic Forum ( WEF ) reposted a short video documentary on Twitter stated that Pakistan planted 1.5 million trees in a single day to counter the effects of climate change and pollution , said a statement issued here on Friday . <p> Pakistan 's tree-plating is part of a nationwide drive to plant 10 billion trees . WEF also urged the world to follow this plantation model to counter the hazards of global warming and other climate changes . <p> Pakistan Tehreek-i-Insaaf government has practically launched its first ever programme and that too in the area of environmental protection to save the country from unusual and extreme climate patterns . <p> The first phase of this campaign titled ' Plant for Pakistan ' on September 2 undoubtedly is a step in the right direction @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ distribution of free saplings at about two hundred distribution points across the country . <p> The response on the campaign was phenomenal initially the target of planting 1.5 million trees was crossed with the number 2.5 million trees in on day . World Economic Forum recognizes the start of biggest tree plantation drive in Pakistan .
