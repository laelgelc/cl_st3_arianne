<p> ISLAMABAD -- Adviser to Prime Minister on climate change , Malik Amin Aslam said Monday first-ever National Wildlife policy draft would be soon submitted to the cabinet for approval . <p> He said this in a meeting of Climate Change Caucus held here at Parliament House . The meeting was chaired by Chairperson of the Committee Senator Sherry Rahman and attended by other members including the leader of the House in the Senate Shibli Faraz , Senator Faisal Javed , Senator Sitara Ayaz , Senator Muhammad Usman Khan Kakar , Senator Dr Jahanzeb Jamaldini , Senator Muhammad Akram and Senator Nuzhat Sadia . <p> Advisor to PM said that the government is committed to working on the climate change issues . <p> He said " In Khyber Pakhtunkhwa , we successfully carried Billion Tree Tsunami project , " adding that it will be replicated on a national level . <p> Chairperson Sherry Rahman said that Pakistan is the victim of Climate Change , adding that Climate Caucus would make efforts to formulate suggestions and recommendations on climate change issues to the ministry . <p> She said that Caucus also issue recommendations to the provinces to coordinate with the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ take a briefing on Climate Change policy in next meeting . The Chairperson said that the Caucus will also discuss climate change issues with other national and international organizations . <p> The senators expressed concerns over the drought and water scarcity issues in Balochistan . <p> The meeting was also informed that the ministry would soon initiate a new project under Green Climate Fund . <p> The leader of the House in the Senate Shibli Faraz said : " We need to change ways of irrigation in the country to compete for issues like drought .
