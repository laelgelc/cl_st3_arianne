<h> Ministry of Climate Change organises awareness seminar on mercury <p> The Ministry of Climate Change in collaboration with United Nations Environment and the Global Environment Facility ( GEF ) organised an awareness seminar on mercury and its impacts on human health and the Pakistani environment . <p> The Ministry of Climate Change with development partners is implementing a project on Development of Minamata Initial Assessment in Pakistan which is aimed to strengthening the baselines on mercury management , development of national mercury release inventories , piloting of sectoral action plans as a follow up of prioritisation , including indicative sampling and development of national mercury management plans . <p> The seminar was also aimed to highlight the harmful impacts of mercury and briefing on phasing out of mercury use in the country . The use of mercury poses risks to environment and human health , especially the health of children .
