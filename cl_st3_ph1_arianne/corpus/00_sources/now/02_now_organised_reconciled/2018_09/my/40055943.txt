<h> Govt to scrutinise feedback before implementing PIL1 <p> GEORGE TOWN ( September 21 ) : The federal government has not come to a decision regarding the implementation of the proposed Pan Island Link ( PIL1 ) in Penang island , under the Penang Transport Master Plan ( PTMP ) . <p> Minister of Energy , Technology , Science , Climate Change and Environment Yeo Bee Yin said that her department would scrutinise all feedback from the public , both in support and in opposition to the project , and conduct a discussion with the state government before making a decision . <p> " We want the best outcome for all involved parties , including considerations of factors like traffic , the environment , social effects , and the like . We are open to ideas and I see that some of the people support the project , and others do not . <p> " But hopefully , it will be a middle road between development and the environment , and those who want a traffic solution versus those who think the other way is better .
