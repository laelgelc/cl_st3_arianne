<h> NZ for holistic education <p> WE 'VE all heard of the awe-inspiring landscapes and breathtaking views New Zealand has to offer , but did you know that the country has as much to extend in terms of a good solid education and quality of life ? <p> New Zealand is known to provide students with a comprehensive all-rounded education filled with valuable learning experiences . At a media gathering here in Malaysia organised by Education New Zealand ( ENZ ) , the country 's government agency in promoting international education - its Southeast Asia regional manager Ben Burrowes said that Malaysia remains an important market for New Zealand 's international education sector . <p> Even till today , more than 60 years since both countries forged diplomatic relations , Malaysia still is a key partner of New Zealand 's when it comes to education . <p> According to Burrowes , the number of Malaysian students choosing to further their education in New Zealand has risen . ENZ statistics taken in June this year reported a 21% increase in the number of first-time student visas from Malaysia compared to the same period last year . <p> " And with @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ are aiming to provide more Malaysian students with not only high-quality education that is recognised internationally , but also a welcoming and safe environment to study in , " he said . <p> Touching a little on the education blueprint , Burrowes informs that there is an International Student Wellbeing Strategy constituent that was developed , that sees to the wellbeing of international students , and ensures they are made to feel inclusive , welcomed and valued for their contributions . <p> " More importantly , we hope to develop students to become global citizens , equipped with skills needed to tackle global issues of today such as sustainability and climate change , " he concluded .
