<h> Plastic pollution is destroying the world <p> LETTER Plastic is everywhere . It can be found in the oceans , forests and landfills . Our world has been massively polluted by plastic bags . And this over usage of plastic bags is actually killing our Earth silently . <p> A massive island of garbage floating in the Pacific Ocean has causes approximately 100,000 marine animals to die each year from suffocation on or by ingesting bags . <p> Shockingly , the usage of plastic bags could also contribute to the build-up of carbon dioxide in the atmosphere .
