<h> TNB starts pilot project for National Fiberisation and Connectivity Plan <p> PETALING JAYA : Tenaga Nasional Bhd ( TNB ) today said it will start a pilot project this month to assess the technical , safety and commercial viability of using its electrical infrastructure for the National Fiberisation and Connectivity Plan ( NFCP ) . <p> Chairman Tan Sri Leo Moggie said in a statement , the pilot project will cover 1,100 out of 4,300 houses in three areas , namely Taman Merbau , Taman Maju and Felda Kemendor in Jasin , Malacca . The pilot project will be completed by the end of 2018 . <p> The households will gain access to high speed broadband ( HSBB ) network that will be made available through TNB 's owned fibre optics which forms part of the company 's existing telecommunication network . <p> " Telecommunications system has always been an integral part of the utility industry .
