<h> Trending Now <h> Land conservation steps into limelight as key climate change fix <p> SAN FRANCISCO ( Sept 14 ) ( Thomson Reuters Foundation ) : From U.S. states to retail giant Walmart , governments and businesses pledged to protect natural lands that help brake global warming at an international climate summit this week . <p> Important ecosystems like Brazil 's Cerrado savanna and Malaysia 's Borneo rainforest also got a boost from donors allocating new money for their conservation . <p> Cutting down trees to grow crops or for timber , and other uses of land for food production and forestry , contribute about a quarter of global climate-changing emissions each year . <p> But efforts to keep peatlands , forests , mangroves and other vegetation intact receive only 3% of global climate finance , according to conservation group WWF . <p> " Land has emerged as a priority topic , " WWF 's climate lead Manuel Pulgar-Vidal , former Peruvian environment minister , told the Thomson Reuters Foundation on the sidelines of the meeting . <p> At the Global Climate Action Summit , which ends Friday , 17 U.S. states pledged to measure the carbon storage @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ their greenhouse gas reduction plans by 2020 . <p> States like Hawaii have already begun to restore native forests on former farmland , in order to capture more carbon , like the 5,500-acre ( 2,225-hectare ) Pu'u Mali Forest . <p> " We have more agricultural land than we need to produce more of the food that we want to eat , " Hawaii Governor David Ige said by telephone from Honolulu . <p> Other states view conservation of natural land as essential , but they flagged the lack of financial incentives to discourage landowners from selling up to real estate developers . <p> " The greatest threat to the Washington state environment is the conversion of working forests and lands , " Washington 's Commissioner of Public Lands Hilary Franz said in an interview . <p> Globally , 18.8 million acres ( 7.6 million hectares ) of forests are converted for agriculture , livestock or human infrastructure use annually , WWF says . <p> Franz also highlighted the threat posed to forests by wildfires , which new programme to help farmers and ranchers increase their yields without encroaching on more forest land . <p> And the Governors ' Climate and Forests Task Force , composed of state leaders from Brazil , Indonesia and the United States , announced US$25 million in grants for forest restoration . <p> U.S. retailer Walmart Inc adopted " science-based targets " to reduce emissions from its supply chain , and said it would hire environmental groups to vet the forest-friendly credentials of its suppliers for materials like palm oil and wood pulp . <p> These pledges will contribute to a new goal announced at the summit of providing 30% of the planet 's needed climate change solutions through land-based measures by 2030 . <p> " Countries must advance more of these conversations to set
