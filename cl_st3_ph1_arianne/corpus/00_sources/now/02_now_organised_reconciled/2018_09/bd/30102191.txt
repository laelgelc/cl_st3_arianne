<h> Hasina returns after joining UN General Assembly <h> Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina has returned to Bangladesh after taking part in the 73rd UN General Assembly , where she expressed her frustration that the Rohingya crisis had not been resolved in a year . <p> The prime minister and the rest of the delegation returned from the 10-day trip aboard a special Biman Bangladesh flight from London at 9:10am on Monday . <p> She had departed New York aboard a British Airways flight for London on Saturday afternoon . She boarded the special flight there after a two-hour layover . <p> The prime minister spent six busy days in New York .
