<h> ADB lending to cross $2b this year <p> Asian Development Bank 's lending to Bangladesh is likely to exceed $2 billion this year , said a top official yesterday . <p> The ADB has already approved over $1.6 billion , of which $1,535 million is sovereign , $60 million non-sovereign and $8.7 million technical assistance . <p> " More projects worth over $600 million are likely to be approved by year-end , " said Manmohan Parkash , country director of the ADB . <p> He said $988 million was approved for different projects since January , of which $586 million has been disbursed as of yesterday -- the amount being 46 percent higher compared to that in the same period in 2017 . <p> Parkash was addressing a ceremony at Economic Relations Division ( ERD ) in the capital 's Sher-e-Bangla Nagar on the ADB and the ERD signing loan agreements . <p> The deals comprised $350 million in loan and $7.5 million in grant for a project to develop two power lines . <p> Kazi Shofiqul Azam , secretary of " Bangladesh 's power sector has rapidly grown in recent years , but more investments are needed to meet the rising electricity demand in a fast-growing economy , " said Parkash . <p> The ADB assistance aims to underpin the current impressive pace of growth and development by making power generation , transmission and distribution systems more efficient , reliable , and environmentally friendly . <p> " The project will promote business expansion , and create employment opportunities for local communities , including poor and socially disadvantaged people in southern and western zones of Bangladesh , " he added . <p> Azam said there was no crisis of foreign funds for development work in Bangladesh . <p> He , however , emphasised expediting project implementation to reduce cost overruns due to implementation delays . The Southwest Transmission Grid Expansion Project builds on the ADB 's previous work in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to southern and western load centres . <p> This includes the recently approved Rupsha 800 megawatt combined-cycle power plant in Khulna . <p> The project will develop a 126 kilometre-long 230 kilovolt ( kV ) transmission line from Barisal to Faridpur and a 104km 400 kV transmission line from Bogra to Rohanpur , along with substations , transformers and associated extensions and connections . <p> The transmission lines will have a new type of high temperature conductor to allow more power transfer at lower energy losses .
