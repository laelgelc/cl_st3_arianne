<h> Malta celebrates National Day in Dhaka <h> The Consulate of Republic of Malta in Dhaka has hosted a reception marking the 54th National Day of the European nation . <p> National anthems of Bangladesh and Malta were played at the beginning of the programme at a hotel in the capital on Tuesday . <p> Consul of Malta Shoeb Chowdhury gave the welcome speech at the programme , according to a press release from the consulate . <p> He greeted and extended compliments to the guests on behalf of Malta President Marie-Louise Coleiro Preca and Prime Minister Joseph Muscat . <p> Chowdhury congratulated the government and people of Malta on the occasion of the Independence Day of Malta . <p> He called upon Bangladeshi tourists and entrepreneurs to visit Malta for exploring the beauty , resources , business prospects and cultural heritage of his country . <p> He also told the audiences about Maltese history in brief . <p> Information Minister Hasanul Haq Inu , who attended the event as chief guest , felicitated the Maltese government on its 54th Independence Day . <p> He said Bangladesh and Malta can work together for utilising the commercial potentialities existing between @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the adverse effects of climate change and other environmental challenges . <p> Top government officials , diplomats from different foreign missions , politicians , business leaders , journalists , academics and other dignitaries joined the occasion .
