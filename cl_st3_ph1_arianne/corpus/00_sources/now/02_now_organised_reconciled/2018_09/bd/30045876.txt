<h> Bangladesh 's largest solar power plant begins commercial operations <h> Bangladesh 's largest solar power plant has gone into operations . <p> The power plant in Teknaf 's Hnila has a capacity to produce 28 megawatts ( MW ) and will feed 20MW to the local substation during sunlight hours . <p> The Bangladesh Power Development Board or BPDB had previously signed a 20-year deal for 20MW of power with the plant 's owner , Joules Power subsidiary Technaf Solartech Energy Ltd or TSEL , said TSEL in a statement . <p> The solar plant is the first step towards the government 's target of producing 2,000MW of solar power by 2021 . <p> Solar photovoltaic cells use panels to absorb sunlight and convert it directly into electricity . <p> The plant emits 20,000 fewer tonnes of carbon dioxide each year than a similar sized diesel-run plant does . <p> " It is truly a pleasure to have the opportunity to aid Bangladesh in contributing to the global clean energy revolution to battle the threats of global warming and climate change , " said TSEL Director of Operations Rayan Moyeen . <p> " We at TSEL wholeheartedly support @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ energy mix towards more renewable and sustainable sources such as solar power . Our team has worked relentlessly to bring this project to life and we are proud of our achievement . " <p> Nuher Latif Khan , managing director of TSEL , thanked the Prime Minister 's Office , the Power Division and the BPDB for their support on the project . <p> " We would be grateful for the opportunity to work with them on future projects in renewable energy to help advance our countries sustainable development efforts . " <p> In the statement , TSEL said the plant would help to meet power shortages in the Teknaf region , which often suffers from rolling outages . <p> The project was co-financed by One Bank Ltd and Standard Chartered Bank . <p> Chief Coordinator for Sustainable Development Goals ( SDG ) Affairs at the Prime Minister 's Office Abul Kalam Azad told benews24.com , " It 's very good that a new solar power plant has been set up . It was launched very fast . " <p> In October last year , Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina inaugurated @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Sarishabarhi . <p> Asked if 10 percent renewable energy production will be possible in line with the SDGs , Azad said , " We are very optimistic . We are moving forward with an aggressive plan . "
