<h> PM Hasina wants specific steps to put pressure on Myanmar to take back Rohingyas <h> Bangladesh Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina says she expects specific steps from the international community to increase pressure on Myanmar to take back Rohingyas from Bangladesh according to their repatriation agreement . <p> The prime minister repeated the call at the inaugural programme of the Islamic Development Bank Group Regional Hub at Hotel Radisson Blu in Dhaka on Sunday . <p> " We have given shelter to them on humanitarian grounds , but Myanmar has to take them back , " she said . <p> " We are standing strong in the face of this crisis despite the negative impact on own resources , ecology and local population . We have opened our border to give shelter to a huge number of Rohingya Muslims . " <p> About 700,000 Muslim Rohingya refugees have fled to Bangladesh since the military launched a crackdown in Myanmar 's western Rakhine state in response to insurgent attacks on security forces last August . <p> About 400,000 Rohingyas had come to Bangladesh at various points in the past few decades before 2017 's army crackdown began . <p> Under @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ agreed to sign an agreement with Bangladesh last December to repatriate hundreds of thousands Rohingyas .
