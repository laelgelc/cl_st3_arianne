<h> Samuda urges regional co-operation to protect food security <p> Jamaica 's Minister of Industry , Commerce , Agriculture and Fisheries , Karl Samuda , is urging countries in the Latin America and Caribbean ( LAC ) region to collaborate more to address the pressing problem of food security . <p> Samuda said food security in Latin America and the Caribbean can only be addressed by collaboration among the countries of the region . <p> He said the " going it alone " approach has never worked , and needs to be replaced by a spirit of unity and co-operation . <p> " It is critical that we co-operate with each other so we can find common ground on issues that have caused problems in the past , " he indicated . <p> " Climate change is one of the biggest threats we face in the region today . We must educate our farmers on best practices so we will not be as susceptible to this problem as we are now , " he added . <p> Samuda was addressing the final press conference of the Food and Agriculture Organisation @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ at the Montego Bay Convention Centre in St James . <p> He said there must be a concerted effort to attract and increase investment in climate-smart agriculture , noting that countries in the region have been calling for greater access to grants to support such initiatives . <p> " This is absolutely critical , " he argued , noting that the FAO has agreed to work with member states to guide them through the process of accessing grants . <p> Samuda said the Mexican Government has signed a letter of intent with the FAO to strengthen the capacities of small island developing states in the Caribbean to strengthen their resilience to climate change . <p> " This , indeed , is a welcome initiative , " he declared . <p> Samuda said he was pleased with the spirit of the discussions at the conference , adding that Jamaica was happy to have hosted the event . <p> The 35th FAO Conference for LAC was held from March 5-8 . It included four days of intense discussions on matters such as hunger and malnutrition , rural poverty and change climate . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ diplomats , ministers and other officials of the 33 LAC member countries , attended .
