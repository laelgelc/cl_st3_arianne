But sometimes it 's hard for these instruments to differentiate clouds from other Earthly things . For example , NASA says that thin , wispy cirrus clouds and snow can easily be confused because they 're both cold and bright . Differentiation gets even more difficult when these clouds hang out above patches of snow .
