<h> MU 's Canada Days to feature Consul General of Canada , film screening and a play <p> Saturday , March 10 , 2018 - 2:56 AM <h> Brandon Hill <p> MU 's Canada Days celebration , which begins next week , will feature a visit from Consul General of Canada in Chicago John Cruickshank . <p> Hosted by MU 's Canadian Studiesdepartment and its director , James Endersby , Canada Days is a yearly celebration of the relationship between the U.S. and Canada . With a variety of guest speakers , Canada Days provides ample opportunity to learn more about this relationship . <p> Canada Days serves as a way to highlight why Canada matters to Missouri . In fact , Canada is Missouri 's largest partner in trade . Missouri sells more goods to Canada than its next five largest foreign markets combined .
