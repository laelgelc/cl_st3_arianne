<p> Populations of sturgeon are listed as threatened in the Gulf of Maine and endangered in New York Bight , the Chesapeake Bay and off the Carolinas and South Atlantic . <p> The National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration says the Endangered Species Act requires the agency to conduct the review to ensure the listings are still accurate . The listings are intended to be based on the best available scientific data . <p> The Atlantic States Marine Fisheries Commission said last year that a sturgeon stock assessment indicated the population is still very low compared to its historical abundance .
