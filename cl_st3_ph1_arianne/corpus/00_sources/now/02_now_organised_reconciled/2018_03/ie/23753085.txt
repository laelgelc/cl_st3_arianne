<p> Environmental and farming groups have opposed all three deals , worried about their power and their advantage in digital farming data , which can tell farmers how and when to till , sow , spray , fertilise and pick crops based on algorithms . <p> The European Commission said Bayer addressed its concerns with its offer to sell a swathe of assets to boost rival BASF , confirming a Reuters story last month . <p> " Our decision ensures that there will be effective competition and innovation in seeds , pesticides and digital agriculture markets also after this merger , " European Competition Commissioner Margrethe Vestager said in a statement . <p> " In particular , we have made sure that the number of global players actively competing in these markets stays the same . " <p> Vestager said the Commission , which received more than a million petitions concerning the deal , had been thorough by examining more than 2,000 different product markets and 2.7 million internal documents to produce a 1,285-page ruling . <p> Bayer has already reached a deal to sell certain seed and herbicide assets for ? 5.9 billion to BASF and to give it a licence to its global digital farming data . It will also divest its vegetable seeds business to BASF . <p> The Commission is due to rule on the BASF deal by April 16 . <p> Online campaigns group Avaaz criticised the EU approval . <p> " This is a marriage made in hell .
