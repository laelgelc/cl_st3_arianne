made from the same material found in coral skeletons - could cover the entire 348,000 square-kilometre reef . <p> " But it could be deployed on a smaller , local level to protect high value or high-risk areas of reef , " she added . <p> " The concept needs more work and testing before it gets to that stage , but it 's an exciting development at a time when we need to explore all possible options to ensure we have a Great Barrier Reef for future generations .
