<h> ' 10% of CAP budget must be earmarked for young farmers ' <p> Ambitious changes to CAP post-2020 have been proposed by Macra na Feirme to address structural , competitive and generational challenges in Irish farming -- with calls for 10% of overall CAP funding to be earmarked for young farmers . <p> To address the lack of young farmers in the industry , Macra na Feirme is calling for a minimum of 10% of the total CAP budget to be dedicated towards generational renewal and young farmer measures . <p> Macra na Feirme National President James Healy said : " If we are to be successful in tackling the demographic and structural challenges in Irish agriculture , a paradigm shift in CAP post-2020 is required , including young farmer proofing of all aspects of the CAP . " <p> The young farmers ' association has also recommended a budgetary increase for CAP . <h> Policy paper <p> The Macra na Feirme policy paper contains proposed changes to the young farmer and active farmer definitions including the abolition of the ' five-year-rule ' for young farmers and suggested changes to the active farmer definition to ensure payments are targeted at active farmers . <p> On CAP direct payments Macra na Feirme does not favour the " outdated " method of historical reference year payments -- which are a barrier to young farmers , it says . <p> Advertisement <p> The organisation proposes a new four-way budgetary split for direct payments . <p> This would consist of : <p> 40% targeted at economic viability ; <p> 30% at climate change and environment ; <p> 20% at farm business development measures ; and <p> 10% at young farmers . <p> For young farmers to offset establishment costs , the Macra na Feirme policy contains details of a mandatory start-up aid package . <p> Start-up aid would be available for vouched expenditure and necessary capital inputs and investments at establishment . <p> Once established , Macra na Feirme is calling for mandatory young farmer top-ups for all those up to the age of 40 , that meet the young farmer definition criteria as well as a continuously-funded National Reserve to provide young farmers under the age of 40 with national average payments . <p> The organisation 's policy paper also makes suggestions on : <p> the introduction of financial instruments to make alternative sources of credit available ;
