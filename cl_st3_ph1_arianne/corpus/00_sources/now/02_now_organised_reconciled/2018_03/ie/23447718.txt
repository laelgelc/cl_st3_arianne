Many issues around the adoption of new technologies within the financial services are implicitly addressed by rule books such as the FCA 's Senior Management Regime ( SMR ) , which replaced the Approved Persons Regime ( APR ) in 2016 . <p> The goal of the SMR is to increase the accountability of senior management within financial services .
