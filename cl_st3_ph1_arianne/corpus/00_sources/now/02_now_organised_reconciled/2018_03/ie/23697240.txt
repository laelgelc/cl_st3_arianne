<p> A new report from the UN 's food agency has highlighted how an increased prevalence of natural disasters is causing billions of dollars in agricultural losses around the developing world . <p> The study , released by the Food and Agriculture Organization ( FAO ) , details how between 2005 and 2015 natural disasters cost $96 billion of damages to the agricultural sectors around the world . <p> Asia is bearing the heaviest brunt of this impact with $48 billion in losses , followed by Africa with $26 billion and Latin America and the Caribbean on $22 billion . <p> Drought was the main cause of damages , followed by floods and earthquakes .
