<h> World Water Day being celebrated locally <h> The importance of clean freshwater <p> World Water Day is celebrated today , March 22 right across the world . Its aim is to focus attention on the importance of clean freshwater for our lives and our livelihoods . <p> The United Nations Educational , Scientific and Cultural Organisation ( UNESCO ) promotes World Water Day to raise awareness of the need to manage water resources locally , nationally and globally . <p> Although Ireland is a water-rich county , in that we get lots of rainfall , we rely heavily on a steady supply of clean water as is evidenced by the recent rationing of water in and around Dublin during storm Emma . <p> Globally agricultural production accounts for 70% of water usage and we have a large agricultural sector in Ireland that depends on a reliable source of clean water . We are the 5th largest exporter of beef in the world and it takes approximately 15,000 litres of water to produce 1kg of beef . <p> Regardless of the amount of water we have at our disposal , the real challenge is to keep it clean . Normal activities we take for granted can impact negatively on water quality including simple things like washing our clothes and cars to flushing our toilets . In Ireland , nearly 50% of our rivers and over 50% of lakes are below the good quality standard required by the EU Water Framework Directive ( WFD ) . <p> In recent years we have experienced fierce and frequent storms that many associate with climate change . These severe weather events have caused widespread flooding and devastation to our countryside and urban areas impacting massively on people 's lives and livelihoods . The impact on farms and livestock has been particularly severe resulting in lack of fodder and reduced farm incomes . <p> The Waters and Communities Office in Carrick-on-Shannon was set up to support local communities develop ideas and projects to improve water quality in our streams , rivers and lakes . <p> If you would like to find out more about ' green infrastructure ' or would like to get involved in protecting your local waterbody or to organising an event or activity you can contact your local community water officer , see **27;568;TOOLONG for more information . <p> If you wish , you can contact us using any of the methods below : Leitrim Observer , 3 Hartley Business Park , Carrick-on-shannon , Co LeitrimEmail : **34;597;TOOLONG : 071 9620025 <p> This website and its associated newspaper are full participating members of the Press Council of Ireland and supports the Office of the Press Ombudsman .
