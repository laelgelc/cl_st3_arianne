<h> Taoiseach 's White House visit - Can Leo charm The Donald ? <p> Thursday , March 15 , 2018 - 12:00 AM <p> TAOISEACH Leo Varadkar will meet US President Donald Trump at the White House today for the traditional St Patrick 's Day visit . <p> On the same day last year , Enda Kenny made headlines for stressing the importance of immigrants . This time , Varadkar will have other uncomfortable realities with which to confront Trump : His apparent dislike of LGBT people and his determination to start a trade war with the EU . <p> As a gay man and a head of government , the Taoiseach is uniquely placed to put the cause of the LGBT community on Trump 's agenda . He is also well-positioned to argue against trade protectionism and to act as an honest broker between the EU and the US in trade negotiations . <p> He has warned that America and Europe may " drift apart " , because of growing divisions on trade , tax , climate change , and other areas . His language is Trumpish -- a mixture of buffoonery and stream of unconsciousness . <p> The Taoiseach will have to dig deep to persuade him that the advance of human rights is essential , that climate change is real and must be addressed , and that trade tarriffs are in nobody 's interests , least of all America's.
