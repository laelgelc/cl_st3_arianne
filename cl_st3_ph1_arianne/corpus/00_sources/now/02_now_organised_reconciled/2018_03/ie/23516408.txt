<p> JAPANESE CAR MAKER Toyota has said that it will phase out production of all diesel engine cars by the end of this year . <p> With sales of diesel cars falling in Ireland , the company said it would help to contribute to a zero emissions society with its decision . <p> Toyota will now focus on making self-charging hybrid cars that it says are being purchased more and more in the Irish market . <p> It said that diesel passenger car sales have seen a 17% year-on-year decline between January 2017 and 2018 , with sales of diesel cars only 20% of its overall sales , compared to 60% just a few years ago . <p> Toyota Ireland said that sales of hybrid cars now makes up 50% of its Irish market . <p> Its CEO here , Steve Tormey said that the move will " help deliver cleaner air quality for our future and that of our children 's children " . <p> Commenting on the decision , Minister for the Environment Denis Naughten said : " As a country we have no option but to move @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ environment , improve our health and to ensure the next generation does n't suffer from complacency or inaction now . <p> Climate change requires all of us to make changes and Toyota 's initiative will significantly help lead us on our low-carbon journey . <p> Ireland has already said it will seek to curtail the sale of diesel cars in the future and a number of other car manufacturers have already begun moving to no- or low-emission vehicles . <p> Volkswagen says it will invest ? 34 billion in new technologies by 2022 .
