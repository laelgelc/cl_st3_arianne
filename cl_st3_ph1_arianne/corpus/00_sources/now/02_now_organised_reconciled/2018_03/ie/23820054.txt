<h> Why you need to turn your lights off at 8:30pm tonight <h> We all need to do our bit . <p> The battle against climate change is getting tougher by the day , and we should all do what we can to limit our carbon footprint , as hard as that can be . <p> Switching off lights when they 're not being used , recycling plastics , little things like that can go a long way if we all make an effort . With this in mind , the World Wide Fund for Nature ( WWF ) want everyone to turn their lights off and put all electronic devices away for an hour starting at 8:30pm on Saturday night as part of their Earth Hour campaign . <p> The idea is obviously to save energy and make people wary of how much energy they normally use . The event started in Sydney , Australia , back in 2007 . Since then the movement has gained momentum and now includes more than 7,000 towns and cities worldwide . <p> It 's easy to think that you ca n't make a difference , but according to WWF Thailand , Bangkok 's electricity usage reduced by 73.34 megawatts during the hour - saving 41.6 tonnes of carbon monoxide from being produced . <h> Advertisement <p> Dublin Airport also announced that it will be dimming and switching off lights tonight :
