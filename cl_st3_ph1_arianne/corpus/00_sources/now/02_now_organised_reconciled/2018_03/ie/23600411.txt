<h> Farming is ' write ' up Longford man 's street <p> A Longford man has documented a slice of family farming life in a new book . <p> Journalist , writer , film maker and farmer John Connell puts his artistic eye to good use , recording the rhythm of everyday activity as well as contemplating the bigger picture in ' The Cow Book ' , published by Granta . <p> Cow-inspired books are having a moment , it seems , with ' The Secret Life of Cows ' attracting a lot of attention recently . Its author , Rosamund Young , describes this latest publication as a piece of " living breathing agricultural history by an insider who has the rare quality of being able to see it from the outside too " . <p> Connell has the benefit of perspective , through his travels in Australia and Canada .
