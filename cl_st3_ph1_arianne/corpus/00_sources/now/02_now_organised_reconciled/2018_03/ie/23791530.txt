<h> What should an Irish farmer do for a living ? See what the public thinks <p> Almost 60% of the general public think the main responsibility of farmers in Irish society is to " provide safe , healthy food of high quality " , a new EU survey has found . <p> The data -- compiled as part of the EU 's Special Eurobarometer -- also found that nearly 30% of Irish citizens think the chief responsibility of Irish farmers is to " ensure the welfare of farmed animals " . <p> Meanwhile , 15% said the duty of farming should be to " encourage and improve life in the countryside " . <p> The survey -- commissioned by the Directorate-General for Agriculture and Rural Development -- explores public opinion on agriculture , rural areas and the Common Agricultural Policy ( CAP ) in each of the 28 member states . <p> Although completed just last month , the fieldwork for the survey commenced in early December 2017 -- shortly after the publication of the European Commission 's draft CAP communication on ' The Future of Food and Farming ' . <p> The survey was carried out through face-to-face interviews with a sample group of 1,000 citizens , from different social and demographic backgrounds , in each individual member state . <p> The interviews were carried out at the home of each respondent and communicated through his/her native language on behalf of the Directorate-General for Agriculture and Rural Development . <p> One of the 12 questions asked in the survey , conducted by institutes within the TNS Opinion and Social network , was : ' What do you think should be the two main responsibilities of farmers in our society ? ' <p> Advertisement <p> In addition to the above findings , the results for Ireland -- which were obtained by AgriLand -- reveal that other widely held views are that 23% of the general public believes the main obligation of Irish farmers is to " protect the environment and tackle climate change " . <p> However , 23% also ticked " creating growth in rural areas " and 16% ticked " supplying the population with a diversity of quality products " . <p> Other opinions collected in the Special Eurobarometer include : <p> Opinions about the importance of agriculture and rural areas and how things have changed over the last decade ; <p> Awareness of the CAP ; <p> Financial support for farmers now and in the future -- including justifications for the CAP 's budget ; <p> Views on who benefits most from CAP . <h> Overall EU results <p> After compiling the data from all the member states , it was found that the majority of EU citizens -- 28,031 contributed to the survey -- share views similar to the Irish . <p> Overall , 55% of EU citizens say " providing safe , healthy food of high quality " is the main responsibility of farmers . <p> Respondents in Cyprus ( 67% ) , Sweden ( 65% ) , the Netherlands and Slovenia ( both 64% ) are most likely to say this ; compared to 42% in Slovakia and Romania and 43% in Croatia .
