<h> The Buen Vivir Fund : Radical Shifting of Ownership in Investment <p> The Buen Vivir Fund , established by Thousand Currents and a global network of partners , is a transformative approach to impact investing that aims to shift the ownership of investment in the impact space . Core to this idea is investing in projects that grassroots groups have already proved effective and to uplift and elevate these practices to the level of a global investment fund . <h> Buen Vivir : ' Right Living ' <p> Buen Vivir loosely translates to " well living " or " right living " , though neither of these phrases does justice to the complexity and depth of the concept . The Latin American social philosophy implies life in balance with community , natural systems and future generations , and has inspired social movements and governments across Latin and South America . Buen Vivir has been supported as an alternative beyond the consumerist and ecologically damaging components of neoliberalism , the current dominant global macroeconomic paradigm . <h> How the Buen Vivir Fund is Different <p> The Buen Vivir Fund aims to transcend the current duality paradigm of " investors " and " investees . " In contrast to a Board of Directors , the Fund has established a Members ' Assembly where those with on-the-ground expertise have equal voting rights in decision making . This shift in ownership is more practical than the traditional structure in which relevant expertise is equated to those holding more wealth . In this way , the investment process is democratised and characterised by a shift and expansion of ownership much like the alternative business ownership shifts of worker cooperatives . Worker-owned cooperatives are owned by their members , transcending the paradigm of " workers " and " owners " and democratising capital in the process . Such firms prove more productive than normal companies , and building evidence supports the viability of worker-owned cooperatives in generating higher sales , lower employee turnover , and greater employee loyalty . <p> The Buen Vivir Fund has created a system , referred to as Aportes , whereby borrowers make a solidarity contribution of their choosing that will remain in the fund for current and future projects . The structure of Aportes contrasts with the conventional investment operation of interest , in which money is paid at regular intervals at a particular rate and accrues earnings for the purpose of increasing return on investment to investors . The functioning of Aportes echoes the concept of Non-Extractive Finance pioneered by The Working World , an investment vehicle for cooperatives in low-income communities . Non-Extractive Finance prioritises the stability and growth of businesses and does n't force companies to put down collateral or take on a burden of debt . The Working World 's model is scaling fast and has proved very successful over the past decade , with a 98% repayment rate from over 1,000 investments in worker and community-owned companies . <h> The First Investments <p> In seeking to promote financial models that build wealth , community power , and wellbeing , the Buen Vivir Fund has launched in early 2018 to provide funding for nine projects across Latin America , North America , Southern Africa and South Asia . The total investment includes $427,700 in loan capital and $113,000 in grant capital as well as business support via advising , learning-exchange , and the sharing of best practices . All of the investments are characterised by close relationships with the local communities , and the majority are focused on women 's rights as well as environmental protection and efforts to address global climate change . <p> The initial investments of the first round of the Fund include the Mayan Medical Clinic , located in Sacatep ? quez and Chimaltenango , Guatemala , which seeks to preserve Mayan traditions and provide high-quality natural prevention , treatment , and healing services to individuals seeking care . The use of the of grants and funds provided by the Buen Vivir Fund is to build additional medical facilities , strengthen the service offerings for the natural clinics and provide physical equipment in the clinic space . The Ubunye Foundation , located in the Eastern Cape , South Africa , is also supported by the Buen Vivir Fund as one of the initial nine investments . Ubunye , meaning " togetherness " in isiXhosa embodies the services offered by The Ubunye Foundation , including leadership development , savings groups and financial education , and integrated early education and health . Investments from the Buen Vivir Fund will support the expansion of Ubunye 's technical support to local NGOs in the establishment of the Ubunye Technical Assistance and Support Service . <h> Conclusion <p> The Buen Vivir Fund is a transformative approach to shifting ownership in the impact investing space on a global scale . The Fund is working to provide a practical , financially viable and community-oriented model for the impact investing sector at large to incorporate within current funding flows . In essence , the creators of the Buen Vivir Fund have dared to vision-make an investment process in which the duality of " investor " and " investee " is transcended in an ownership shift in which those with on-the-ground knowledge are equal voting members in decision making . In addition , earnings are not just channelled to increase the rate of return for investors but also contributed to a fund to ensure the stability of current and future projects . Though still in very early days , the Buen Vivir Fund provides a powerful " reimagining of global finance " that intends to shift mainstream investment paradigms to better serve the needs and expertise of local communities .
