<h> Bishop of Oxford wants to spread hope at Easter and through the year <p> I 'll be in Christ Church Cathedral on Easter Eve at 8pm . The service will begin in darkness as we look back down the years and tell the story of salvation . Then the great Easter candle is lit and we proclaim together : ' Alleluia ! Christ is Risen ! ' <p> It 's vital for Christians to have this long and deep perspective at this time of year . Six hundred years before Christ , a prophet called Ezekiel had a dramatic vision of hope . He found himself in a valley full of dry bones bleached white by the sun . There was no life in the place whatsoever : a picture of desolation . <p> Ezekiel is told to speak to the bones and pray for them . Slowly there is a rattling and a rustling and the bones come together . Flesh and sinew and muscle and skin grow too . But still there is no life . <p> God tells Ezekiel to speak again and pray for God 's Spirit , the breath of life , to come . Life returns . The mighty army rises up . There is a new beginning . <p> The world this Easter faces many perils : war and climate change , cruelty and abuse , poverty and mass migration . <p> As people and families we will each face our own challenges : some facing illness or death , loneliness or uncertainty . Ezekiel 's vision is a sign of hope for every generation : things do n't get much bleaker than a valley of dry bones . <p> No matter how bad or difficult life can seem -- or the world can seem -- there is space for hope , for change and for renewal and new life : the green shoots of spring follow the harshest winter . <p> As I light the great Easter candle on Easter Eve -- and as Easter candles are lit across the world -- Christians will be remembering the day of resurrection . Ezekiel has a vision of people rising from the dead in the future . <p> But Christians believe that Jesus did rise from the death . He was crucified on Good Friday and laid in a burial cave . Early on Sunday morning the stone was rolled away , the cave was empty , the grave clothes were folded neatly by . He appeared to his disciples for 40 days . He sent his Spirit of new life to them and promised that all who trusted and believed in him would share his resurrection . <p> The Church tells this Easter story every Sunday as we gather to break bread and drink wine , to share the meal Jesus gives to us . <p> Every Sunday is a day of hope and joy , no matter what the circumstances of our lives . But that is especially so on Easter Day , the first Sunday , the day of resurrection . <p> The first Christians were all of Jewish faith , of course . The great Jewish Sabbath was ( and is ) Saturday . But so great was the impact of the resurrection that the special day for Christian worship became Sunday , the day on which Jesus Christ rose from the dead . <p> There is one more part to the service on Easter Eve . Easter faith is personal . There is to be a baptism and confirmation . Again , this will be happening in many different churches and cathedrals across the world . We will journey to the font . The candidates will make a confession of faith in Christ . <p> They will be baptised in water as a powerful sign that they are washed clean and forgiven on the inside as well as the outside . Baptism is a sign of dying to our old ways and our old self and rising with Christ to new life . <p> The symbolism is clearer when the candidates are immersed fully in water and rise up again but that 's not always practical . We will then pray , as Ezekiel prayed , for the Holy Spirit of God to come and bring new life and strength to the candidates as they are confirmed . <p> Despair turns to hope . Darkness becomes light . Death becomes life . These are the great themes of Easter Christians celebrate in this season of the year . <h> Ipsoregulated <p> This website and associated newspapers adhere to the Independent Press Standards Organisation 's Editors ' Code of Practice .
