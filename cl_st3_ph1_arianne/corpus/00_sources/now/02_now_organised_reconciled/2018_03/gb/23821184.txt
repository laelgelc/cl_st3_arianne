<h> Consultation looking at future of farming after Brexit <p> A consultation is underway on the impact of Brexit on the environment and farming . Picture by Andrew Diamond . <p> Published:18:16Saturday 24 March 2018 <p> Share this article <p> A 10-week consultation is under way looking at the future of agriculture and land management in a ' green ' Brexit . <p> The consultation , called Health And Harmony : The Future For Food , Farming And The Environment , will inform the Government 's forthcoming Agriculture Bill . <p> It comes as the 46 Wildlife Trusts believe the UK 's wildlife is in crisis , with only a tiny proportion of the previous abundance of wildflowers , birds and sealife now remaining . <p> They say farming has a huge role to play in securing high quality water , preserving healthy soils , reducing the contribution made to climate change and adapting to it , and , critically , restoring the abundance of wildlife . <p> " However , it is important the same or increased levels of funding be channelled into conservation linked to land management . Whilst the higher level and basic Countryside Stewardship funds have produced very good benefits for wildlife and people , landscape improvements can be made to better target funds and action when a new scheme is devised . "
