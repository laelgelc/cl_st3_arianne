<p> Graham Readfearn reports on Professor Peter Ridd , the new hero of " free speech " for conservative media and climate change deniers . <p> CLIMATE SCIENCE DENIERS and conservative media have found themselves a new " free speech " hero -- an academic who is suing his own university and thinks the multiple human threats to Australia 's Great Barrier Reef are overblown . <p> Professor Peter Ridd might be a new name to some , but the marine geophysicist has a long association with groups pushing denial of the well-established links between human activity and dangerous climate change . <p> Outlets including Breitbart and Fox News have joined a steady flow of columns and interviews across Australia 's conservative media landscape covering Ridd 's case , sometimes handing over space to him in their column pages . <p> Each time , Ridd , of Australia 's James Cook University , has been painted as a bastion of truth pushing back against the establishment . But how does that image hold up to scrutiny ? <p> Climate science denier James Delingpole of the hyperpartisan Breitbartwrote that the ' gagged professor ... has plenty of solid scientific evidence to prove ' that the reef was ' doing just fine ' . <h> Examining Ridd 's case <p> So what 's actually been happening ? Another , less hysterical way of looking at the case , is this . <p> For more than a decade , Ridd has been happily criticising the science linking dangerous climate change to greenhouse gas emissions and the science showing the impacts of humans on corals . <p> Ridd has also repeatedly , over many years , said that the impact of agricultural runoff and water quality on the health and growth rate of corals is overstated . <p> But his employer , James Cook University , initiated its own action against Ridd after he had criticised specific organisations at his own university in media interviews , saying they could not be trusted . This , the university alleged , went against the university 's code of conduct . <p> So this is not about Ridd 's " freedom " to say what he wants but is about an alleged breach of the university 's code of conduct -- whether you agree with that code or not . <p> The university alleged this constituted further " serious misconduct " , so Ridd took the issue to his lawyers and a case is proceeding . <p> To help fund his legal bills , Ridd got some help from the IPA ( a key organisation pushing climate science denial in Australia for two decades ) to set up a crowdfunding campaign that raised the necessary $95,000 in just 49 hours . <p> Many of Ridd 's cheerleaders have taken his scientific claims without scepticism and have not entertained the idea that he might be wrong . <h> Ridd 's marine pollution ? <p> But Ridd repeated in detail several of his criticisms in a November 2017 " viewpoint " article in the journal Marine Pollution Bulletin -- opening up his arguments for scrutiny . <p> Now , as reported in The Guardian Australia , a team of nine scientists , many based at the Australian Institute of Marine Science and the James Cook University centre Ridd has attacked , have issued a response through the same journal . Their assessment of Ridd 's claims is sharp . <p> They say Ridd 's criticisms are based on ' misinterpretation , selective use of data and over-simplification ' and that they ignore ' formal responses to previously published critiques ' . <p> While Ridd and his colleague Piers Larcombe argue their critiques are ' largely ignored ' , these researchers point out that , in fact , many of Ridd 's arguments have been directly addressed in the scientific literature . <p> The Australian Institute of Marine Science researchers wrote : <p> To republish previous claims that have been addressed and refuted appears to be selecting information to support their statements and an example of the very issue Larcombe and Ridd ( 2018 ) are criticising . <h> Ideological bent ? <p> Also in the 2017 Sky News interview , Ridd accused other scientists of lacking objectivity and suggested another problem was that " we also potentially have scientists with an ideological bent " . <p> This is the time to look at Ridd 's own " ideological bent " and his long history associating with climate science deniers whose " theories " are rejected by every major scientific academy on the planet , as well as governments around the world . <p> Ridd has been affiliated with several groups that reject the science linking human emissions of carbon dioxide to dangerous climate change . <p> The AEF , which emerged from a 2004 meeting organised by the IPA , promotes the idea that wind turbines make people sick and that human-caused climate change is unproven . <p> Ridd is also listed as an adviser to the Galileo Movement -- an Australian group that for many years was run by former One Nation Senator and climate science denier Malcolm Roberts . <p> Ridd joined a list of well-known climate science deniers in 2009 when he co-signed an open letter coordinated by the Cato Institute -- a U.S. " think tank " funded by petrochemical billionaires Charles and David Koch . <p> The letter ran as a full-page ad in several newspapers , including The New York Times , and claimed that recent temperature changes were ' modest ' , that the ' case for alarm regarding climate change is grossly overstated ' and that global warming had stopped . <p> In 2011 , Ridd called for a ' Scientific Challengers Office ' that ' should start on Global Warming ' and ' the supposed threats to the Great Barrier Reef ' . <p> Ridd is based at the Marine Geophysics Laboratory and , according to his university research profile , he ' ... raises almost all of his research funds from the profits of consultancy work which is usually associated with monitoring of marine dredging operation ' . <p> Several major coal and gas projects are listed as former clients of the lab , which was also home for the late Dr Bob Carter who was , at one time , associated with ten or more different climate science denial groups around the globe . <p> Ridd says he stands for ' truth and honesty ' and has spent his ' whole life fighting for scientific truth ' . <p> The problem is that the version of the " truth " he has been standing alongside , including his own arguments , have been repeatedly shot down by the world 's leading scientific institutions . <p> Despite this , and despite the howls of his supporters , Ridd remains free to voice his " truth " . <h> Ashbygate Book <h> Support IA <h> Recent
