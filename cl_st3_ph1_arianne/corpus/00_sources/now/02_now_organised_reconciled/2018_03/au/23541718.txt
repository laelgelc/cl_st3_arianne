<h> Snowy Hydro sale should fully fund Eden-Monaro projects , says Kelly <p> Debate has begun over where the money from the federal government 's multi-billion dollar purchase of the state 's share in the Snowy Hydro scheme should be invested across regional NSW . <p> Last week , NSW Premier Gladys Berejiklian announced funds from the sale of NSW shares in the scheme , valued at $4.15billion , will " create an investment bonanza in regional NSW " , with " every cent " to be spent in rural and regional areas . <p> Eden-Monaro MP Mike Kelly said the money should be used to fully fund an East/West link road from the Monaro to the Port of Eden and establishing the Bundian Way as a key tourism draw card and " Indigenous icon " . <p> Dr Kelly also said Labor would not support " anything that includes a plan to privatise the iconic Snowy Hydro scheme " . <p> " The Turnbull government needs to commit to keeping the Snowy in the hands of the Australian people , " Dr Kelly said . <p>
