<h> Tim Flannery , chief councillor of the Climate Council <h> Australia 's leading apostle on climate change is targeting the role of cities . <p> The planet is heating up at a precipitous rate . Glaciers are thawing , sea levels are rising and coral reefs are expiring . None of this is news to Professor Tim Flannery . The Melbourne native has been at the forefront of the global warming phenomenon for decades . Raising the profile of environmental issues was key to his being named Australian of the Year in 2007 . <p> Flannery , an internationally acclaimed scientist , conservationist and author , has long argued for measures to reduce heat-generating carbon dioxide emissions . Frustrated by federal government inaction , his current focus is encouraging renewable energy and zero emissions at a city level .
