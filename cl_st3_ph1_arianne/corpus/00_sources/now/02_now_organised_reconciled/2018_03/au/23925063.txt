<h> Letters to the editor : Saturday March 31 2018 <p> I 'VE had a house built . I have had a conservative count of the number of tradies and others who at some stage of the building were on site over the course of the job , plus those people who delivered stuff to the job , and those who prefabricated stuff to come to the site . <p> I came up with 35 . So if I was a politician or a developer , I could claim to have " created 35 jobs " . However , as far as I know , none of the people who were involved in this project were unemployed . Yes , I provided work to these people for a short length of time , but I did n't get them off the unemployed list . They were already working and I had to wait until they could get here . <p> Lately I have heard it said that the government of Australia has created over 400,000 jobs , I would be interested to know how many of those jobs were going to unemployed people . I think not many at all . <h> Fred McInerney , Karuah <h> A LACK OF TRUE CONFIDENCE <p> WHAT does ' commercial in confidence ' mean ? In my view it means that politicians and private businesses conspire together at the taxpayer 's expense . Much is made of one foreign leader colluding with another , but I believe every day at every level of government our leaders are speaking to their wealthy mates and telling us we have no right to know what is being done with our money . Until this situation changes , anyone who reveals the expense and arrangements involved is a true public servant . <h> Peter Ronne , Woodberry <h> FIXES FAR FROM LIGHT SPEED <p> IT IS now over 10 weeks since I spoke to a shop assistant in a store in Kurri Kurri regarding an issue with flickering lights in the store . She made a note of the problem and said it would be added to the list of matters to be addressed by their contract electrician . <p> The reason I raised the issue is because I have been informed in the past that flickering lights can cause serious health issues for some people . <p> The light has now stopped flickering , but it has not been replaced . Today , I took a brief look around the store and was amazed at the number of lights that were not working . The staff have to be remarkably patient to work under those conditions , but probably would not be game to complain for fear of losing their job . It would be over two months since I spoke to the store manager about the matter and he tried to explain to me the protocol they have to go through just to get the lights fixed , and that they ca n't just call in a local electrician to do the job . <p> As a former safety officer , I am gobsmacked by the lack of action in this matter . Surely there has to be a better work practice that can be implemented to ensure that no member of the public suffers as a result . <p> When I was eight years old we had a chook in the back yard ( that 's a chicken on legs , not a barbecued one ) . I had to feed it every second day with pellets as every other day it had scraps . So one day I gave it one hand full instead of two , trying to save pellets , so the chook was looking for the extra pellets and kept pecking at my feet . I often wondered years later who was the smartest , me by saving pellets or the chook who knew he had been cheated ? <p> The moral to this story is , even though you think you are clever and try to pull the wool over people 's eyes , you are n't . <p> In my opinion the words used in regards to Jeff McCloy and the structure he put in place , which have been condemned by council , is not a true account of the facts . I believe Jeff would have built a structure that worked for all Newcastle ratepayers and , more importantly , for Newcastle itself . <p> Mr McCloy renovated the Lucky Country Hotel , built the Telstra building and worked on many others , all on Hunter Street . Did we ever hear so much about roads being shut down , businesses closing and people not knowing where to park in the course of construction ? No , you did n't . So that tells you the calibre of this man and the structure he put in place . <p> No chook was harmed in the writing of this letter , and he received two handfuls a couple of days later .
