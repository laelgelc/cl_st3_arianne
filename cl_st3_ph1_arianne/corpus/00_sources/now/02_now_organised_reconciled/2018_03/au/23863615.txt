<p> The Victorian Government has vowed to protect the Kuark Forest in eastern Victoria after announcing a two-year extension of three of the state 's Regional Forest Agreements . <p> A total of 2,500 hectares of the Kuark Forest , north-west of Orbost , will be set aside as a special protection zone where logging is banned . <p> The Government will eventually move to legislate the area for permanent protection as part of the Errinundra National Park . <p> Environment Minister Lily D'Ambrosio said all native trees across the state greater than 2.5 metres in diameter would also be protected , whether they stood in forests or along roadsides . <p> " We will also introduce more mandatory , independent surveys of threatened and endangered plants and animals before approving logging in native forest , " Ms D'Ambrosio said . <p> Goongerah Environment Centre welcomed the move to protect some old growth trees in the Kuark Forest , but raised concerns about the continued logging of environmentally sensitive forests under the forest agreements . <p> " Today 's announcement does not provide full protection for the Kuark Forest . Some areas have not been included , " spokesman Ed Hill said . <h> Agreements rolled over for two years <p> Regional Forest Agreements ( RFA ) , which have set the terms for native logging and forest management in East Gippsland , Central Highlands and north-east Victoria for the past 20 years , have been extended for another two years . <p> They were due to expire today . <p> The Victorian and Federal Governments signed a deal late yesterday to extend them until March 31 , 2020 when new , longer-term RFAs will take effect for all five Victorian RFAs , including West Victoria and Gippsland . <p> " What 's important here is that we rejected the Federal Government 's view to simply roll over these RFAs for another 20 years without any scientific studies or data collection of what exists in the forests right now , and also through the lens of climate change , impacts on our water and bushfire , " Ms D'Ambrosio said . <p> " For the next two years our Government will undertake a thorough analysis of the science to determine the look of the new RFAs from 2020 . <p> " We need to modernise these RFAs . They 're 20 years old . <p> " We need to make sure the evidence is examined so that when we finalise the longer term RFAs , they include the right environmental safeguards . " <p> Federal Assistant Minister for Agriculture and Forestry Anne Ruston said forestry practices had improved greatly since the original RFAs were put in place . <p> " There are new considerations now that were n't considered when RFAs were first put into place 20 years ago , " she said . <p> " But equally we 've got great sustainable practice in forestry now . Forestry now does not look like forestry 20 years ago , so I think there 's a whole new set of considerations . " <h> Timber industry ' cautiously welcomes ' RFA move <p> Bairnsdale sawmiller Brian Donchi welcomed the extension of the RFA for East Gippsland as a way of providing desperately needed certainty of log supply . <p> " The problem with the Victorian timber industry is that we have a small area of state forest that is sustainable over an 80 -- 100 -- year cycle , " he said . <p> " But unfortunately because of some of the issues , including the Leadbeater 's possum , there 's a lot of good high-value timber going into reserves , and the timber industry is left with nowhere to go . " <p> The Victorian Association of Forest Industries is calling on the Victorian Government to ensure that all RFAs from 2020 guarantee quantity and quality of log supply to ensure ongoing investment in the timber industry . <p> " In the period since we 've had RFAs , more than 13 million hectares of forest in Australia has been added to the conservation reserve system , " chief executive Tim Johnston said . <p> Debate around forestry and the environmental impact of logging is now expected to dominate the state election campaign in regional Victoria . <p> When Takata airbags were recalled , the manufacturers were able to tell motorists which models had the faulty safety device .
