Picture : AFP PHOTOSource:AFP <p> A THRIVING " hotspot " of 1.5 million Adelie penguins , a species fast declining in parts of the world , has been discovered on remote islands off the Antarctic Peninsula . <p> Scientists were surprised to discover that the first bird census of the Danger Islands unearthed over 750,000 Adelie breeding pairs , more than the rest of the area combined , the team reported in the journal Scientific Reports . <p> The group of nine rocky islands , which lie off the northern tip nearest South America , in the northwest Weddell Sea , housed the third- and fourth-largest Adelie penguin colonies in the world , they found . <p> " It is certainly surprising and it has real consequences for how we manage this region , " study co-author Heather Lynch of Stony Brook University told AFP . <p> A thriving " hotspot " of Adelie penguins has been discovered on the remote Danger Islands @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Michael PolitoSource:AFP <p> Just 160 kilometres away on the west of the peninsula , which juts out of West Antarctica , Adelie numbers have dropped about 70 per cent in recent decades due to sea ice melt blamed on global warming . <p> " One of the ways in which this is good news is that other studies have shown this area ( the eastern side of the Antarctic Peninsula ) is likely to remain more stable under climate change than the western Antarctic Peninsula , " said Ms Lynch . <p> " So we end up with a large population of Adelie penguins in a region likely to remain suitable to them for some time . " <p> Adelies are one of five penguin species that live in and around the Antarctic continent . <p> A medium-sized penguin , they grow to about 70 centimetres tall , and weigh three to six kilogrammes . They are identified by a white ring around the eye . <p> They are carnivores , and feed on krill -- shrimp-like creatures that are commercially fished in the area . <p> The Danger Islands @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ research team from America , Britain and France . <p> " This is called the Danger Islands for a reason , " said Ms Lynch . " The area is covered by heavy sea ice most of the year , and even in the height of summer it is difficult to get into this region to do surveys . " <p> Even the most visited of the isles , Heroina Island at the chain 's northeastern tip , receives only about one ship landing per year . <p> Evidence of the previously-unknown penguin colony first emerged in data from the Landsat Earth-monitoring satellites run by NASA and the US Geological Survey . <p> The Danger Islands , said the team , has felt the ravages of climate change less than the western peninsula , and knew very little human activity . <p> Now it turns out , the area may need stronger protection from overfishing . <p> " The most important implication of this work is related to the design of Marine Protected Areas in the region , " said Ms Lynch . <p> Adelie penguins leaping off an iceberg @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Brook University and Louisiana State University/Rachael HermanSource:AFP <p> In addition to Adelies , the team also found about 100 nests of Gentoo penguins , and about 27 nests of Chinstrap penguins . <p> But it is not known how long this tiny island group will remain protected , especially from fishing , as the polar regions are warming more rapidly than the rest of Earth as heat-trapping greenhouse gasses from fossil fuel-burning build up in the atmosphere . <p> Researchers survey a colony of Adelie penguin on the remote Danger Islands in the east Antarctic .
