<h> Port of Newcastle could be slumped with $166m bill to prepare for climate change , according to report <p> PRICE OF CHANGE : The Port of Newcastle faces costs of up to $166 million to deal with the effects of climate change , according to a new report . <p> THE Port of Newcastle is facing an estimated bill of up to $166 million to deal with the effects of climate change , according to a new report comparing the costs of environmental damage on ports . <p> The Asia Research and Engagement report , Climate Costs for the Asia Pacific , explored the impact on port infrastructure of increased sea levels , wave heights and storm intensity expected between 2070 and 2100 . <p> In the first scenario , the report assumed an increase in temperature between 1.1 degrees and 2.6 degrees , a sea level rise between 0.3 and 0.6 metres , an average increase in storm intensity between 10 to 20 per cent and an increase in storm surges by 0.5 and 5 metres . <p> The second scenario estimated likely temperature rises between 2.6 and 4.8 degrees , which would have more severe consequences on sea levels and storm intensity . <p> " Safeguarding ports is critical since up to 60 percent of global GDP is reliant on trade , " report author Ben McCarron said . <p> " The costs of adapting this essential infrastructure are already onerous , but the key lesson for stakeholders , from port owners to investors , is that planning climate resilience from the outset is key to avoiding greater expense in years to come . " <p> A spokeswoman for the Port of Newcastle said : " Climate change and the severity of weather events will continue to be important issues taken into consideration in planning port infrastructure . "
