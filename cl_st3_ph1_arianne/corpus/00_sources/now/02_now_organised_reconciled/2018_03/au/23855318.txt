Picture : Xavier GalianaSource:AFP <p> LAND degradation will unleash a mass migration of at least 50 million people by 2050 -- as many as 700 million -- unless humans stop depleting the life-giving resource , more than 100 scientists have warned . <p> Already , land decay caused by unsustainable farming , mining , pollution , and city expansion is undermining the wellbeing of some 3.2 billion people -- 40 per cent of the global population , they said on Monday , in the first comprehensive assessment of land health undertaken . <p> Land depletion threatens food security for the entire global population , as well as access to clean water and breathable air regulated by the soil and the plants that grow on it . <p> The condition of land is " critical " , according to the Intergovernmental Science-Policy Platform on Biodiversity and Ecosystem Services ( IPBES ) . <p> " We 've converted large amounts of our forests , we 've converted large amounts of our grasslands , we 've lost 87 per cent of our wetlands ... we 've really changed our land surface in the last several hundred years , " IPBES chairman Robert Watson said . <p> " Land degradation , loss of productivity of those soils and those vegetations will force people to move . It will be no longer viable to live on those lands , " he told AFP . <p> The lowest number of 50 million migrants is a best-case scenario . It assumes " we 've really tried hard to have sustainable agricultural practices , sustainable forestry , we 've tried to minimise climate change , " Prof Watson said . <p> The Borneo Forest cleared This means not only a loss of soil , but also populations of wild plants and animals , and forests that suck up planet-warming carbon dioxide and produce oxygen . <p> " Biodiversity loss is projected to reach 38-46 per cent by 2050 , " said the report , warning that Earth is in the beginnings of a sixth mass extinction -- the first since the demise of the dinosaurs 65 million years ago . <p> The IPBES assessment took global experts three years to compile , analysing all available scientific data . <p> Australia has the worst mammal extinction rate in the world : 30 native mammals extinct since colonisation . To put this in a global context , 1 out of 3 mammal extinctions in the last 400 years were in Australia .
