<h> World 's Iconic Buildings Go Dark For Earth Hour <p> The bright green lights that typically illuminate the Petronas Towers in Kuala Lumpur -- the tallest twin buildings in the world -- were turned off for a recent Earth Hour -- an annual event aimed at raising awareness about energy consumption . <p> Dozens of other iconic landmarks around the world , including the Parthenon in Greece , the Golden Gate Bridge in California , and Australia 's Sydney Harbour Bridge , have participated in the annual event , which is sponsored by the World Wildlife Fund ( WWF ) . <p> The first Earth Hour took place in Sydney in March 2007 , with the goal of raising money to help protect the Great Barrier Reef . <p> Since then , Earth Hour has grown and spread around the world . Thousands of cities now switch off many lights for the hour in a symbol of environmental awareness . More than 170 countries take part , along with dozens of UNESCO World Heritage Sites . <p> Connecting the event to a wider message , then-UN Secretary General Ban Ki-Moon said in 2015 : " Earth Hour is a way for the citizens of the world to send a clear message that they want action on climate change . " <p> Earth Hour takes place from 8:30 to 9:30 pm , in each time zone around the planet . <p> Here are some pictures of famous monuments observing the darkness of Earth Hour in recent years . <h> Acropolis <p> Dating to 447 B.C. , the Parthenon in Athens is one of the most famous surviving buildings of ancient Greece . The city of Athens has turned off its buildings ' lights for Earth Hour and asked locals to follow suit . <p> PHOTOGRAPH BY KOSTAS TSIRONIS , ASSOCIATED PRESS <h> Sydney <p> Sydney Harbour Bridge is the largest steel arch bridge in the world , connecting its city 's north and south harbours . Thousands of houses and businesses in Sydney go dark on Earth Hour . <p> PHOTOGRAPH BY WILLIAM WEST/AFP/GETTY IMAGES <h> Eiffel Tower <p> Built as an archway for an international world fair in 1889 , the Eiffel Tower got some help from Spiderman in turning its lights off on Earth Hour 2014 . <p> PHOTOGRAPH BY ETIENNE LAURENT , EPA <h> The Big Apple <p> Times Square is the busiest commercial intersection in New York City and the world 's most visited tourist attraction . For the first time ever , in 2014 the city called in a specialist to turn off its New Year 's Eve ball , which typically remains on throughout the year . <p> PHOTOGRAPH BY EDUARDO MUNOZ , REUTERS <h> Minsk , Belarus <p> In the Belarusian capital of Minsk , the monument to humanist Francysk Skaryna is usually lit by the bright purple lights from the National Library . Big department stores and universities also turned off their lights in Minsk for the event . <p> PHOTOGRAPH BY SERGEI GAPON/AFP/GETTY IMAGES <h> Brandenburg Gate <p> The Brandenburg Gate was built in 1791 as an entryway to Berlin . It survived World War II but with extensive damage . The Earth Hour motto in Germany in 2014 : " You do n't need to be a superhero to take action on climate change . "
