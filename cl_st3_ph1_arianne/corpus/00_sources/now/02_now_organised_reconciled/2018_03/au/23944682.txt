<h> Demand for justice program to tackle core issues : Greens MP <p> A project to help address core social problems , like the justice reinvestment initiative in Bourke , is needed in Dubbo , says NSW Greens MP David Shoebridge . <p> Mr Shoebridge visited Dubbo recently as part of a trip visiting communities across NSW and hearing their concerns . One year out from the state elections , Mr Showbridge said he was focusing on renewable energy , public services and the needs of the Aboriginal population . <p> A justice reinvestment program would have significant benefits , Mr Shoebridge said . The trial program began in Bourke in 2013 . It was developed with local community groups and key stakeholders like the police to create long term change . <p> " Rather than just arresting their way out of social problems , to try ad address the core social problems whether it 's housing , problems at school , health issue and that 's seen terrific results , " Mr Shoebridge said . <p> As well as reducing the level of domestic violence in the town , Mr Shoebridge said school holiday programs , which run from 10am to 10pm every day , had " completely extinguished " juvenile offending during the break from school and had also boosted the number of students attending their classes . <p> " The fact that that 's been driven by the Aboriginal community in partnerships with the police and FACS in Bourke as a pilot project is great but we would love to see that pilot project run in more significant areas like Dubbo , " the MP said . <p> Mr Shoebridge said a significant amount of his trip had focused on Aboriginal people and their concerns . <p> " Too many of tier kids are being stolen . Their kids are ten times more likely to be taken than non-Aboriginal kids and that is a crisis and a tragedy and we need to empower and support Aboriginal families to allow them to keep their kids at home , " he said . <p> The Greens were also focused on renewable energy , Mr Shoebridge said . Not only because it was the best energy option environmentally and socially , he said , but because it would create jobs for the regions . <p> " The Greens have been at the forefront of pushing for those renewable energy projects in the regions . Not only does renewable energy provide local jobs it provides far greater energy security because you 're producing the energy locally and we think it 's an important part of combating climate change . "
