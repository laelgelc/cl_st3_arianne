<h> Australia 's greenest energy companies leave big retailers behind <h> By Cole Latimer <p> Powershop and Diamond Energy have been voted as Australia 's most green energy retailers in Greenpeace 's electricity guide . <p> The two retailers were the only companies to win a five out of five star rating in the third annual Green Electricity Guide from Greenpeace and the Total Environment Centre . <p> Ed McManus , CEO Meridian Energy Australia and Powershop <p> Photo : Georgina Safe <p> " In taking the top two places for the third time running , five-star retailers Powershop and Diamond Energy continue to be the guiding lights in the retail energy space with their investment in renewable energy projects and refusal to buy power from coal or gas plants , " Greenpeace Australia Pacific climate and energy campaigner Nikola Casule said . <p> " They show that a rapid decarbonisation of Australia 's energy sector is possible , and can be cheaper for consumers with retailers recently passing on savings to their customers . " <p> However , Powershop was criticised for its higher than average cost for green power while Diamond Energy fell down due to its lack of demand management programs and carbon offsets . <p> Advertisement <p> Energy retailers were ranked on emissions intensity of their power stations , their investments in and policy positions on renewables and fossil fuels , deals for solar consumers , their energy efficiency , and corporate transparency and sustainability reporting . <p> Energy Locals and Enova Energy were rated third and fourth respectively , while AGL was ranked fifth , was the top-rated retailer out of the big three -- which includes Origin and EnergyAustralia -- and also received the Most Improved Retailer award . <p> AGL was the top rated major retailer and generator in Australia . <p> Photo : Bloomberg <p> Total Environment Centre 's energy market advocate Mark Byrne said AGL won the award for its push towards more renewable generation despite issues surrounding the planned closure of its Liddell coal-fired power station . <p> " AGL has been recognised as this year 's Most Improved Retailer for its work around decarbonisation and renewables and we commend the company for standing up to the Federal Government 's bullying to try to keep Liddell power station open past 2022 , " Mr Byrne said . <p> Sumo Energy , CovaU Energy , and 1st Energy were ranked at the bottom of the table . <p> Mr Byrne said this year 's guide has seen a number of smaller retailers making their way up the charts . <p> Loading <p> " What is most interesting and different about this year 's guide is the emergence of a new breed of small retailers with a strong commitment to supporting local communities as well as renewable energy , " he said . <p> This latest report comes as the energy sector pushes for higher emissions targets in the National Energy Guarantee policy . <p> The country 's largest electricity generators urged the government to be more ambitious with carbon emissions reductions , saying they could play more of a role in achieving Australia 's international climate change targets . <p> " Origin supports more ambitious emission reduction targets for the electricity sector , " the company said . <p> " The electricity sector can do more than its pro rata share of the target as it has cost-effective abatement options available to it which could be unlocked given the right policy settings . "
