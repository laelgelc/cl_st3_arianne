We both love the Vivid Light Festival , which , as spruiked by Premier Gladys Berejiklian this week - to announce the lineup for its 10th annual edition , starting on May 25 and going for three weeks -- is no less than " the world 's largest festival of light , music and ideas " . Long forgotten is that it was started by our erstwhile premier Nathan Rees , who at the time publicly eschewed Melbourne 's notion of paying a fortune to have Tiger Woods make an appearance at its golf tournament , and instead sunk the state 's money into the creative idea that has gathered strength every year since , returning the investment many times over . <p> Rees is long gone from politics and is back with the union movement , but when I talked to him on Friday about it , he acknowledged a little pride in the festival 's success . <p> " Politically , " he said , " it was very costly to us , but there is no argument about it now . When I walk around and you see the so many families , so many international tourists , it is great to think we got it right !
