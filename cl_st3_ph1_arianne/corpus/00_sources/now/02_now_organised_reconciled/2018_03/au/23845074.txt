<p> The ' Time to Choose ' protest not only marked one year until the next election , but also the two year anniversary of Bingara farmer Glenn Morris ' historic one man horse ride protest over the Harbour Bridge . <p> While the protest was led by a large group of First Nations people , including Gomeroi , Mr Morris led the horse brigade at the back as the march moved down Elizabeth Street to Parliament House . <p> Mr Morris called for a ban on coal seam gas and mining . <p> " There is devastation because of climate change all across NSW , " he said . <p> " We ca n't afford to destroy any more healthy land . " <p> Georgina Woods from the Lock The Gate Alliance said that the protest was putting members of the NSW Government on notice . <p> " NSW is at a crossroads , " she said . <p> " We can have a future of productive land , clean and secure water and air , reliable clean and affordable energy , but that bright future is at risk from coal and coal seam gas mining that damages farmland - It 's time to choose . " <p> " We have one of the most coal dependent energy systems in the world , with 79% of our electricity coming from coal , " she said . <p> " The transition from coal and gas to solar , wind and storage will attract $25 billion of investment , the construction of about 2,500 wind turbines and installation of more 42 million solar panels across the state . " <p> " It 's a big job , but making the NSW electricity system 100% renewable is 100% doable.
