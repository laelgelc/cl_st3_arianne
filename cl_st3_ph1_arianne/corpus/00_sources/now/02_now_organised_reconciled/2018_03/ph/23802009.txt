<h> What will you do on Earth Hour and beyond <h> What will you do on Earth Hour and beyond <p> This was the challenge that Nida Cabrera , head of Cebu City 's Community Environment and Natural Resources Office , raised this week for all households and offices . <p> Start by switching off lights and electrical appliances from 8:30 p.m. to 9:30 p.m. on Saturday , March 24 . <p> Earth Hour is usually celebrated every last Saturday of March , but because March 30 , 2018 falls within Holy Week , this year 's campaign is being held a week early . <p> " This is a global campaign to showcase what we can do to combat climate change . This is a worldwide initiative to have individuals , communities , businesses , and governments participate in activities against global warming , " Cabrera said . <p> " We will campaign to reduce power consumption not only during Earth Hour but all throughout the year , to decrease pollution , " Cabrera added . <p> Under her leadership , the Cebu City Government has revived its policy not to collect trash unless this is segregated ; this is one way to encourage recycling and reduction of waste at source . <p> " At that time ( when implementation began in 2011 ) , we were able to reduce our waste by 30 percent . It was stopped when we transferred and dumped our garbage in Consolacion town . But we have had to revive it now , " Cabrera said . <p> " We are asking the people in our communities to follow the law , " Cabrera said , pointing out that barangays should be more involved since the primary responsibility of collecting garbage is theirs . <p> Cabrera strictly implemented the recycling of garbage to reduce solid waste and at the same time earn income when she was the village chief of Barangay Luz , Cebu City .
