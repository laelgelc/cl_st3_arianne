<h> Milton Baptist Church hosts World Day of Prayer <p> The Republic of Suriname lies in the northeastern part of South America , bordering the Atlantic Ocean in the north , French Guiana in the east , Guyana in the west and Brazil in the South . During the afternoon of Friday , March 3 the dozens of people gathered in the Milton Baptist Church in Queens County shared something with this small country located thousands of kilometres away -- The 2018 World Day of Prayer . <p> The World Day of Prayer is a global ecumenical movement which brings Christians of many traditions together to observe a common day of prayer which is traditionally celebrated in an estimated 170 countries on the first Friday of March . Each year , a different participating country writes the service and this year a committee from Suriname provided the material . This year marks 100 years that the World Day of Prayer has been observed in Canada . <p> The theme for the 2018 World Day of Prayer was All God 's Creation is Very Good . Suriname is described as a beautiful country with incredible flora and fauna , a country where people from different ethnicities may live in peace with each other . However , mining of gold and bauxite has not been environmentally friendly and has polluted the water supplies . The eggs of the green sea turtles have been harvested causing their endangerment . The forests are at risk of over harvesting and depletion by the logging industry . Unfortunately , many countries , including Canada , have examples of environmental issues and concerns within their borders . The material presented served as a reminder that there are similar concerns in many countries both near and great distances away . <p> Each person attending the World Day of Prayer service in Milton was given a small paper sea turtle . Everyone was asked to write on the turtle , one thing that they could and would do to improve the environment and to place the small turtles in the collection plates . Before the closing prayer a few of the ideas were read aloud . The commitments included eating more foods grown locally and decreasing the use of plastics particularly plastic bags . <p> The program started with a video about the country of Suriname showing the diversity of the landscape and giving insights into the way of life of the residents . It outlined a brief history of the country and explained some of the current environmental challenges facing the citizens . This was followed by a few words of welcome from members of the hosting church , Milton Baptist Church . <p> Mary White was a special guest speaker . She moved to the region in 2012 and is an active volunteer with several community organizations including : Planning Advisory Committee , Plant to Plate Community Garden , Edible Landscapes in Queens County and the Queens Garden Club . She is involved as a community activist and she would like to see the region become a better place for everyone , now and in the future . She is passionate about reducing food insecurity and the barriers that prevent food security for certain individuals and groups . She is also very passionate about environmental issues that we are facing now and those that will occur in the future and she is very interested in reducing global climate change . Her presentation was based on the Plant to Plate Community Garden and the importance of community gardens in our area . <p> White grew up in rural Nova Scotia and learned at a young age just how important locally grown farm products are . Many people do not have a place where they can grow their own vegetables . Community gardens give people a chance to grow their own foods at reduced costs . They also give people control over the quality of the food they are eating . White also commented that when people have the opportunity of growing their own foods there has been an increase in consumption of healthier foods . <p> Ten churches in the Liverpool area took part in the service : the host church the Milton Baptist Church , Milton Christian Church , St. Gregory 's Roman Catholic Church , Liverpool United Baptist Church , Brooklyn Baptist Church , Trinity Anglican Church , St. Andrews Church in Brooklyn , Zion United Church , Salvation Army and the Pentecostal Church . Following the service , a lunch was enjoyed by those in attendance . Next year 's service will be hosted by the Zion United Church . <p> Come , Everything is Ready will be the theme for the 2019 World Day of Prayer . The program and materials will be prepared by representatives from Slovenia . The 2020 materials will be prepared by Zimbabwe and in 2021 Vanuatu will prepare materials based on the theme of Build on a Strong Foundation . Canada prepared the program for the 1931 World Day of Prayer and used the theme Ye Shall Be My Witnesses . Canada also prepared the 1978 program with the theme of Community Spirit in Modern Living .
