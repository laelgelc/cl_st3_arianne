<p> As an Ontario resident , I am keenly following the leadership race for the Ontario Progressive Conservatives . I am also anxiously waiting for a " progressive " candidate to emerge . So far , I have been profoundly disappointed . <p> With each passing day I become more concerned about the future of my children , grandchildren and our planet . There is no longer any doubt that " climate change " is the most urgent issue of our time . <p> Without realistically addressing " global warming " all other political issues become irrelevant . If we no longer have a livable planet then economic growth , jobs and health care are of no relevance . <p> All citizens , but particularly those who seek to lead , have a moral responsibility to take up the challenge of mitigating climate change . At present none of the current candidates has even addressed this challenge except to condemn the idea of pricing carbon . Shame on you for such blind and self-serving statements . <p> So many economists and think-tanks agree that pricing carbon is the most effective @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ many positive reports on this idea that I can not believe you are ignoring them . Not one of you has proposed an alternative plan . Do you have no concern for the future of your own children ? Surely you must realize that eventually we will all pay for our failure to act against climate change . <p> When will politicians start to act in the best interests of their constituents and make bold moves to deal with carbon emissions ? I realize this is a tough issue that has been ignored for too long but a " carbon fee and dividend " policy is the perfect conservative plan to reduce emissions without hurting middle- and low-income people . <p> Until one of the candidates shows the guts to tackle climate change head on , I will continue to be disgusted with the Conservatives and my future votes will reflect this . Shame on all of you . <p> Sue Braiden <p> Erin Ont . <p> What 's on your mind ? <p> Got something on your mind ? Care to share it with our readers ?
