The director of the Institute of Geophysics of the National Autonomous University of Mexico ( UNAM ) , Hugo Delgado , warned that the glaciers thaw in Mexico is extreme and is due to climate change and global warming . <p> Delgado said that if the global warming continues to advance , it will not be possible to stop the disappearance of the glaciers in the country , as it already happened in the Popocatepetl volcano , although it still has ice at the summit . <p> He also stated that in the case of the Iztaccihuatl volcano , " very likely in five or ten years they will only see themselves as ice bodies and also declare themselves extinct . " <p> " The freezing temperature that allows the ice to remain on the snowy peaks of Mexican volcanoes is reaching a level that will soon exceed 0 , which means that Mexican glaciers will have no way of surviving , " said the specialist . <p> However , not all the Mexican glaciers thaw <p> For example , the one located in Pico de Orizaba , the highest mountain in Mexico , presents a different situation , as it is not surrounded by industrial zones as in the case of the others . <p> " Being at 5,570 meters above sea level , it makes its glacial feeding system survive maybe a few more decades , " the professor explained . <p> One of the consequences of the disappearance of the glaciers is that the water reserves decrease , something of great importance because during the dry seasons the glaciers melt rapidly and feed the rivers and aquifers . <p> In the absence of water recharge , there is a negative impact on the availability of water resources in the cities that surround the glacier mountains of Mexico . <p> Globally , climate change and global warming are leading to glaciers thaw in the mountains in Europe , South America , Asia , as well as in the Alaska and Canada . <h> Ad <h> Subscribe to Canadian Homesteading via Email <p> Enter your email address to subscribe to Canadian Homesteading and receive notifications of new posts by email . <p> Join 4 other subscribers <p> Email Address <p> The goal of the website is provide up-to-date information and news on all things related to homesteading .
