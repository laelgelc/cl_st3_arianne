<h> Sam Roberts Band headlines first Global Citizen Live in Vancouver <p> Sam Roberts Band will be headlining the first ever Global Citizen Live in Vancouver this April . <p> Global Citizen , which started in New York in 2012 , highlights issues that affect women and girls around the world , such as global health and climate change . <p> Tickets to the event are free and can be earned by emailing world leaders about issues , signing petitions and using social media to generate commitments in support of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals . <p> For the Vancouver event , issue-specific goals include girls ' and women 's health and calling on the Canadian government to become a leader on these issues .
