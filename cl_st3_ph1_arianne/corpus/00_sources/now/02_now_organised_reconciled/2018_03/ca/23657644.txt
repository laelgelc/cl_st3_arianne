<p> There will be a new fund for Indigenous filmmakers to access across the circumpolar North . <p> The Arctic Indigenous Film Fund launched last week at the Indigenous Film Conference in Norway . It 's an international collaboration between film organizations in Canada , Alaska , Greenland , Norway , Finland , Sweden and Russia . <p> Eirug said the purpose of the fund is to support more production of Indigenous films , help youth to work in the media industry from their own communities , and encourage collaboration between Indigenous people around the world . <p> " We might end up in a situation where we have an Inuit writer for example ... and it 's directed by a Sami director , " he said , adding it 's an opportunity for Inuit to influence others , and to be influenced . <p> Huw Eirug is the CEO of Nunavut Film Development Corporation .
