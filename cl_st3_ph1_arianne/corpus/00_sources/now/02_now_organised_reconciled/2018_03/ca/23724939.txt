<h> News Local <h> Dyer on Donald Trump , war , poverty , climate change <p> Dr. Gwynne Dyer talks Donald Trump , North Korea , war , poverty and climate change at a talk held at the Timmins Museum on Saturday evening . The journalist and historian presented " The State of the World in 2018 " to a sold-out crowd . <p> The sold-out audience may not have agreed with everything Dr. Gwynne Dyer said , but all came out to his talk with the same purpose -- to learn more about the state of the world in 2018 . <p> Before presenting the guest -- a renowned journalist and historian - Karen Bachmann , director of the Timmins Museum , explained that events like these happen in the building because it offers a safe , interesting place for discussion . <p> Bachmann said audience members might agree or disagree with some or all of Dyer 's talk -- something the journalist alluded to in a pre-speech interview with The Daily Press . <p> Dyer said the average person can be well-informed -- if they devote the necessary time to go through various media sources . <p> " That takes a lot of time -- and most people do n't have that time , " said Dyer . " Which is why , among other things , bringing in somebody who does do it all the time , like me , helps . Here it is in a package : You do n't have to accept the package , I could be wrong -- hard to imagine , but it 's possible -- but at least , at the end of this evening , we will have touched all the things we need to touch on , I hope . " <p> Dyer 's talk included war , poverty , climate change and Donald Trump . <p> He said the American president " is a legitimate cause for a bit of fear and trepidation . " <p> But he said Trump has done the world a favour . <p> " The fact that he got elected suddenly woke everybody up to the notion that things are going wrong , and in the system , not just individuals , random things . <p> " Basically what is going wrong is much bigger unemployment than the official statistics say , in every country , and America probably amounts to ... 17% of men of prime working age not working , " Dyer said . " That 's depression-level unemployment . " <p> All the same , Dyer is looking forward to the end of Trump 's term in office . <p> " As long as he goes away again , then I 'll be quite happy . " <p> He hesitated to label the American president a fascist , " because he does n't have the concentration . " <p> Dyer added , " People who support him are worrisome people . " <p> The speaker told the group of about 75 attendees that it 's been 73 years since two great powers have fought each other directly -- though proxy wars have been taking place in their stead . <p> " That is an accomplishment not sufficiently celebrated , " he said . <p> Dyer said the tension between North Korea and the United States could , in a worst case scenario , escalate to a " catastrophic " and ironic nuclear confrontation -- about the use and possession of nuclear weapons . <p> That conflict , he argued , would limit casualties to the Korean peninsula -- though those casualties would be " horrendous . " <p> Still , the journalist was n't convinced the conflict would occur . <p> " I do n't think it 's all that likely , " Dyer said , pointing to the " reasonable grown-ups " in the White House who would prevent an escalation . <p> Dyer said he believes North Korea 's supreme leader , Kim Jong-Un , when he argues the nuclear weapons are for deterrence only . <p> The speaker also touched on poverty -- the world is doing " spectacularly well " on that front , with the exception of Africa , where Dyer is concerned about an unchecked population boom that could lead to famine , massive out-migration , and war .
