<h> Ontario Building Fully Electric Ferries for Wolfe and Amherst Islands <h> News Release <h> Ontario Building Fully Electric Ferries for Wolfe and Amherst Islands <h> Province Fighting Climate Change with New , Clean Ferries <p> Ontario is building the first fully electric non-cable vessels in Canada with two new ferries to connect the mainland with Amherst Island and Wolfe Island . <p> In November 2017 , the province awarded the design-build contract for new , larger ferries that will support more people , vehicles and goods in travelling to and from the Islands . Those ferries will now be electrified , reducing greenhouse gas emissions by an estimated 7.4 million kilograms of carbon dioxide per year , the same as taking 1,357 cars off the road , compared to conventional diesel ferries . <p> Jointly funded by Canada and Ontario , the ferries are expected to be operational in early 2020 for Amherst Island and early 2021 at Wolfe Island .
