<h> Willis Towers Watson aims to build ecosystem resilience <p> Willis Towers Watson has announced the development of a global insurance facility aimed at building the resilience of ecosystems and the communities they support . <p> The Global Ecosystem Resilience Facility ( GERF ) was launched Friday at the Economist 's World Ocean Summit in Mexico by Willis Towers Watson CEO John Haley . <p> With marine ecosystems at risk , the economies in coastal communities in developing and emerging countries are harmed by damage to natural capital like coral , mangroves and fisheries . Global climate change also threatens the lives and livelihoods of residents of those communities as storms grow more intense and sea levels rise . <p> According to Willis Towers Watson , the GERF will respond to these risks by delivering powerful analytics , incentivizing environmental stewardship and providing insurance protection .
