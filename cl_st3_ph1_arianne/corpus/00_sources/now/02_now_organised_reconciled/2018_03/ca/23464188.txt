BIOVIA <p> " When they want you to buy something they will call you . " Poet Wendell Berry 's words from 1973 were prescient for the last decades of the 20th century . Driven mostly by curiosity , researchers and inventors used ' what if ' experimentation to create new products , and then sales and marketing professionals would build consumer desire for them and push them out to customers . Today , companies create things because customers ask for them . This is known as pull manufacturing , in which production is based on demand .
