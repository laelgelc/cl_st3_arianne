It brought together the science , policy and practice communities to help determine current and future sources of greenhouse gas ( GHG ) emissions , and pathways for cities to pursue emissions reduction and resilience strategies . The conference also focused on implementation of the Paris Agreement on climate change , the SDGs , the New Urban Agenda ( NUA ) and the Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction ( DRR ) , all of which require cities for successful sustainable development plans to adapt and respond to climate change . <p> More specifically , the conference sought to take stock of scientific literature , data and other sources of knowledge on cities and climate change since the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) Fifth Assessment Report ( AR5 ) , and to build ongoing work under AR6 to be completed in 2022 .
