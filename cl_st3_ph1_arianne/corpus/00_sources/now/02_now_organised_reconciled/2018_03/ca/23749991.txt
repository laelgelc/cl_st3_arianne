<h> Pipeline not in national interest <p> Times Colonist <p> March 21 , 2018 12:04 AM <p> Re : " Government ' determined ' to expand Trans Mountain , " March 16 . <p> The infamous Nazi propagandist Josef Goebbels is reputed to have said that if a lie is repeated often enough people will come to believe it as fact . Unfortunately our federal government appears to be taking the same approach . <p> They do not clearly define what they mean by this , and the statement is clearly ridiculous unless one equates national interest with the financial interest of a foreign oil company . <p> How can it be in the national interest to press forward with a project that might be already creating a constitutional crisis between provinces and will severely undermine the credibility of a genuine reconciliation process with First Nations ? A recent poll indicates considerable support for civil disobedience if legal challenges do not succeed . <p> Kinder Morgan projects only about 2,500 jobs during construction , falling off to 50 . Is this really enough to balance the risk from an oil spill to thousands of jobs in coastal B.C. and possible wiping out of First Nations food resources indefinitely ? <p> The additional CO2 emissions from this project will not only make it impossible for Canada to reach its internationally agreed climate change targets but will further incrementally exacerbate our ever-more-destructive forest fires and flood events . <p> The only way to have an expanding economy compatible with our environment and climate goals is to focus on job creation in sustainable industries such as alternative energy . Already far more jobs are being created in this area than currently exist in the fossil-fuel industry . It 's time to phase out , not expand , the oilsands and retool the Alberta economy away from what will become stranded assets .
