<h> NDP on wrong side of history with LNG <p> Times Colonist <p> March 31 , 2018 12:57 AM <p> Re : " LNG plans at odds with clean growth , " letter , March 25 . <p> The NDP government appears to have a problem with basic climate-change arithmetic . If this single LNG Canada plant goes ahead , it will add about eight megatonnes of greenhouse gases to B.C. 's output . That can not possibly be made up by any combination of other reductions in the rest of the economy , thus blowing B.C. 's carbon budget and any hope of meeting our targets . <p> Climate change is the worst crisis facing humans in the past 10,000 years , requiring us all to step up and do our part . Premier John Horgan 's failure to show leadership in this situation when he has the chance is unforgivable . <p> Exemption of this plant from carbon tax increases might well scuttle the social licence for the carbon tax , as who in their right mind will support an increase in carbon tax on small industries and the public when big oil and the gas industry go untaxed ? <p> This is exactly the opposite of what the carbon tax is intended to accomplish , which is to make the polluter pay in proportion to the amount of pollution . <p> The government should move to the right side of history and reverse this move .
