" But we do n't need a war on the car . What we need is an intervention . <p> We need a serious conversation about our collective , structural addiction to this substance , which is toxic in high doses . And we need a strategy for real , lasting change . <p> First , let 's add up the toll on our well-being . <p> Death and injury : In the 10-year span of 2007-2016 in the City of Toronto , there were 531 deaths from auto collisions , most of them pedestrians .
