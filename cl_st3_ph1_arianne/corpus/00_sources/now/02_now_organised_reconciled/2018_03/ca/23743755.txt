<p> Canada Kuwait Petrochemical Corp . has hired a ninth lobbyist to help them secure federal funding for " the development of two processing facilities " in Alberta -- one for propane dehydrogenation and the second for polypropylene . <p> The corporation , which is listed as a subsidiary of Calgary-based Pembina Pipeline Corporation and Kuwait City 's Petrochemical Industries Company K.S.C. , registered Lise Jolicoeur of StrategyCorp Inc. with the federal lobbying registry earlier this month . Jolicoeur joins eight other colleagues on the file . <p> The proposed production facilities would be integrated and located in Sturgeon County , Alta. , according to a May 2017 news release . The online lobbying registry indicates the corporation has not received any government funding to date . <p> Jolicoeur 's filing was one of 21 new registrations posted between March 11 and March 17 -- representing less than half the normal number of new weekly filings . Several organizations hired two lobbyists each -- Emblem Corp. , Opus One Solutions and the Ottawa Art Gallery . Global Public Affairs won the week with five new clients , followed by Sussex Strategy Group with three . Only two lobbyists signed multiple clients -- Carl Baltare ( for Emblem Corp . and Pharma &amp; Biopharma Outsourcing Association ) and Lise Jolicouer ( for Canada Kuwait Petrochemical Corp . and the Greater Toronto Airport Authority ) . <p> In recent agriculture and trade registrations : <p> The Canadian Turkey MarketingAgency , wholly owned by the Turkey Farmers of Canada , hired Darren Ference to lobby about supply management and tariffs . Ference is based in Alberta . <p> And the Grain Farmers of Ontario registered board member Scott Persall to lobby about agriculture policy , trade , pest management regulations and crop research . The organization received about $620,000 in funding from the Department of Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada last year . <p> In recent energy and environment registrations : <p> The Co-operators Group Limited registered board member Hazel Corcoran to lobby about climate change adaption , fire safety , the establishment of a " national co-operative development strategy " and a number of other issues related to the co-op sector . <p> Meanwhile , Opus One Solutions signed Liam Daly and Chris Benedetti of Sussex Strategy Group to look for funding for their ' smart grid ' technologies as well carbon displacement initiatives and " matters related to energy conservation ... climate change action , and electricity utility regulation . " The company received $1.8 million in funding last year from Sustainable Development Technology Canada . Benedetti worked in the Chr ? tien government for a few years . <p> NextBridge Energy Transmission hired David Timm of Sussex Strategy to lobby the departments of Environment and Climate Change , Indigenous and Northern Affairs , and Parks Canada about transmission lines in parks and protected areas . <p> And the Electric Power Industry of Quebec hired Marc Desmarais of National Public Relations to get them some funding for a green electrical technology cluster in Quebec . Desmarais worked for various Liberal parliamentarians in the 1980s and 1990s and ran for the Grits in the 2015 federal election . <p> Emblem Corp . wants Carl Baltare and Roberta Kramchynsky of Global Public Affairs to lobby government to get different delivery methods for medical cannabis into the legalized pot regulations . <p> In recent transport registrations : <p> Proterra is a California-based manufacturer of zero-emission electric buses . They hired Matthew Larventz of Gowling WLG ( Canada ) LLP to introduce the company to stakeholders and seek out " related policies , programs and incentives that may be available and pertinent to Proterra 's customers . " Larventz worked in government for a few years but from November 2015 to November 2017 was a legislative assistant to B.C. Liberal member of Parliament Randeep Sarai . <p> The Greater Toronto Airport Authority also signed Strategy Corp 's Lise Jolicouer discuss a long grocery list of issues with an even longer list of departments and agencies . <p> Of note : <p> Sleep Performance Inc. dba Fatigue Science hired Ben Parsons of Counsel Public Affairs to lobby government about sleep -- specifically how fatigue might lead to problems in some work environments ( military , Coast Guard , space , transport industry ) . They have a product that tracks sleep patterns with an app so they want Parsons to educate and look for procurement opportunities . Before becoming a lobbyist last fall , Parsons did two stints working in the Liberal Research Bureau -- between 2009 and 2011 , and again from 2016 to 2017 . <p> The Ottawa Art Gallery is set to open next month so it hired Evan Sweet and Tara Mazurk of Global Public Affairs to promote the new " revitalized " facility to the Prime Minister 's Office , Canadian Heritage and the Canada Council for the Arts , as well as parliamentarians . The Gallery received $1.3 million from Canadian Heritage to build the new facility last year , in addition to $1.28 million in regular funding from three levels of government . <p> Monthly communication reports <p> Thanks to the two-week March break , the lobby firms had all the time in the world to file communications reports , with 2,605 filed last week . Only 15 reports date from 2017 with the rest from 2018 -- the vast majority from February .
