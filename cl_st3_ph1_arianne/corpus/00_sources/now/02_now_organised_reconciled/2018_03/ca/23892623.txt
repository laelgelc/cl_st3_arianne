" It was a good meeting . . . I do n't think we satisfied some of the residents , but most of the people were in agreement . " <p> It was very late in the fall when it became clear that more of the Moulinette Island paving and drainage project would go forward in 2018 . <p> A request by local landowner David Petepiece , trying to achieve a more comprehensive environmental process by having the Township of South Stormont prepare an individual environmental assessment for the Moulinette Island road reconstruction project , was turned down by Ontario Minister of the Environment and Climate Change Chris Ballard . <p> But , the minister did also address township engineers regarding the constructing of areas along the side of the reconstructed and paved roads that should be designated for recreational purposes . <p> And , the township was required to hold another public meeting before the project could resume . <p> Petepiece was n't able to attend the Tuesday meeting , but a letter he had written regarding the project had been circulated by email by Moulinette Island resident Chris Emard . <p> In an email to residents of Moulinette Island , in which the Petepiece letter was distributed , Emard encouraged that it be read before Tuesday 's meeting . <p> " ( It ) hits on many important and relevant points regarding the proposed paved road , " Emard said , praising Petepiece for his thoughts and insight regarding the road project . <p> Emard said he wanted to distribute the letter " so that all residents could understand the reasoning behind ( Petepiece 's ) bump-up application ( with the ministry ) , as they were supported by many of us . . . Dave is a true friend to the island and its residents . " <p> As per the ministry request , Bancroft on Wednesday said that the recreational area work " will be part of the tendered document . . . tendered documents will be going out soon -- within the next two weeks . <p> " Hopefully we 'll get a good result from that , a reasonable , good price . " <p> Bancroft said the Sunset Drive portion of the project would include the removal of some trees , upgrading the road with more gravel before asphalt is applied , and ditching work that will have sod used on the property side of the ditch .
