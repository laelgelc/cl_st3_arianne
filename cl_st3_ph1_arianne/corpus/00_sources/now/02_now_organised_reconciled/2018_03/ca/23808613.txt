<h> Group protests proposed pipeline at MP 's office <h> Citizens United for a Sustainable Planet say Trans Mountain pipeline expansion would be a ' climate disaster ' <p> Paul Berger , along with about 40 fellow members of Citizens United for a Sustainable Planet , gathered at MP Patty Hajdu 's office on Friday to show support for those resisting the proposed Kinder Morgan Trans Mountain pipeline expansion . ( CBC News ) <h> Related Stories <p> More than 40 members of Citizens United for a Sustainable Planet ( CUSP ) gathered at a Northwestern Ontario MP 's office on Friday to call for the cancellation of a proposed pipeline expansion on Canada 's west coast . <p> The CUSP members also delivered a sample of water from British Columbia 's Burrard Inlet to staff at Thunder Bay-Superior North MP , and Minister of Employment , Workforce Development and Labour Patty Hajdu 's office . <p> It was one of dozens of similar actions that took place Friday across Canada , in opposition to the proposed pipeline , said Paul Berger , CUSP member . <p> " It threatens the land and the water , but even moreso , it threatens the climate , " Berger said . " It 's another pipeline that would allow the Alberta tar sands to expand , and we simply ca n't do that at this point . " <p> " It 's 2018 , we know we 're in the middle of a climate crisis , and we have to stop expansion . " <p> Berger said even though the proposed pipeline would run a route between Alberta and B.C. , it 's not just a western Canada issue . <p> " Climate change is much broader than any one province or territory , " he said . " It 's incumbent upon all Canadians and citizens to stand up for what they believe in . " <p> Hajdu was n't present , as she was in Ottawa during the CUSP gathering . <p> Later Friday , however , she released a written statement : <p> " Thank you to the constituents of Thunder Bay-Superior North who came to my office today to share their concerns , " Hajdu stated . " I am truly disappointed that I was unavoidably delayed in Ottawa and not able to personally hear from the group and accept the water sample directly , as I had planned . "
