David Ake ) <p> The Associated Press Published Saturday , March 31 , 2018 7:00AM EDT <p> DETROIT -- The Trump administration is expected to announce that it will roll back automobile gas mileage and pollution standards . <p> Automakers wanted the government to relax the current standards , which were imposed by the Obama administration to combat climate change . They say the rules will cost the industry billions of dollars and raise vehicle prices . <p> But environmentalists say the right technology is available to increase a car 's gas mileage and building it into the car saves money at the pump . <p> Any change is likely to set up a lengthy legal showdown with California , which currently has the power to set its own pollution and gas mileage standards and does n't want them to change . <p> EPA spokeswoman Liz Bowman said in an email Friday that the standards are still being reviewed .
