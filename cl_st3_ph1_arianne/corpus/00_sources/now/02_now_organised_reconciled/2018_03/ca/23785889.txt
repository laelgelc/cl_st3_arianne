<h> Ontario Increasing Affordable Housing in Oakville <h> News Release <h> Ontario Increasing Affordable Housing in Oakville <h> Proposed Land Transfer Includes Commitment for New Housing Units <p> Ontario is transferring 128 hectares of provincially held lands to the Town of Oakville , supporting the creation of affordable housing and protecting access to green space . <p> Lands being transferred include a portion currently leased to the Deerfield Golf Course as well as the protected green space known as the 14 Mile Creek conservation lands . As part of the transfer , the province will require the protection of recreation and conservation areas , in addition to supporting the building of affordable housing units within the Town of Oakville . <p> Building new affordable housing and protecting green space are part of Ontario 's plan to support care , create opportunity and make life more affordable during this period of rapid economic change . The plan includes a higher minimum wage and better working conditions , free tuition for hundreds of thousands of students , easier access to affordable child care , and free prescription drugs for everyone under 25 , and 65 or over , through the biggest expansion of medicare in a generation . <h> Quick Facts <p> As part of this land transfer , the Town of Oakville is required to commit $29 million for affordable housing units within the town 's boundaries . The transfer is anticipated to take place in the coming year . <p> Ontario 's Fair Housing Plan includes a comprehensive package of measures to help more people find affordable homes , increase housing supply , protect buyers and renters and bring stability to the real estate market . <p> Protecting green space is part of the Climate Change Action Plan , Ontario 's strategy to cut greenhouse gas pollution to 15 per cent below 1990 levels by 2020 , 37 per cent by 2030 and 80 per cent by 2050 . <h> Additional Resources <h> Quotes <p> " Infrastructure investments are key to improving quality of life and building affordable , liveable communities . Our government is leveraging surplus public properties to ensure more affordable housing is built in communities across the province . " <p> " Unlocking surplus provincial land for affordable housing is part of Ontario 's Fair Housing Plan . Our priority is to make sure that everyone has safe , affordable and suitable housing . This action by our government will mean more families in Oakville will have a place they can call home . " <p> " Protecting green space is a top priority especially the conservation lands near 14 Mile Creek in Oakville , which will ensure future generations will have wonderful trails and recreation areas to enjoy for years to come .
