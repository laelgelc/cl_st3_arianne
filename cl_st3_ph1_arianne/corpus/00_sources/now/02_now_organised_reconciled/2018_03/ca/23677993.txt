<h> Nunavut 's failing grade on climate change sign of greater problem <p> Kent Driscoll APTN News Nunavut is not doing enough to prepare for climate change according to the latest report by the Auditor General of Canada . <p> " The impacts of climate change are visible across Nunavut . We found that the Government of Nunavut was not adequately prepared to respond to these impacts , " said Jim McKenzie from the auditor general 's office . <p> If Nunavut is not ready , then why not ? After all , the government put a plan in place in 2014 . <p> " Human resource capacity challenges within the government and those make it difficult to sustain effort and attention , " said McKenzie . <p> This is nothing new for Nunavut residents . <p> In the last decade the auditor general 's office has issued reports on social workers , child safety , justice and jails . All of them found major problems . <p> And each time the Nunavut government agreed and promised to make changes . <p> But those changes do n't happen . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the jobs done with 27 per cent of government jobs currently vacant . <p> The auditor general 's office did suggest a way to get out of the understaffing cycle : pick fewer things and do them right . <p> " I think it 's important , because resources are limited , that they try not to do everything , " said McKenzie.
