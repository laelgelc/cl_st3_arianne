<h> Opinion Letters <h> In praise of good deeds of women <p> I am saddened and sickened by this letter . For how many years have we praised men ? Our culture is male dominant . For centuries , when we talk history , there is always an over emphasis on men . What is wrong with praising the good deeds of women for once ? <p> It has nothing to do with Dr. Frederick Banting not rating recognition . It has to do with acknowledging women for once who have made a difference . <p> Christine Beach <p> Brantford <p> PCs should become progressive <p> This year , the first voters born in the 21st century will cast their ballots . These voters are not socially conservative . They support the rights of LGBTQ+ individuals , they support the right of a woman to seek an abortion and they support seeking a solution to climate change . Doug Ford has come out opposed to these issues and many others , which the voters of tomorrow hold very dearly . The Progressive Conservative need to adopt 21st-century policies on these issues and deal with members who support the existing regressive policies . I strongly suspect that the Ontario PCs will be unsuccessful for upcoming elections if they fail to modernize , especially as millennials start to outnumber the baby boomers . We all want a more capable leader in Queen 's Park , but the PCs really are n't helping themselves by alienating the current generation . <p> Henry Tyson <p> Brantford <p> Market Brantford 's historic sites <p> Brantford and area have a wealth of historic sites and other places of interest to tourists . <p> I believe we can do a better job of marketing these and other sites in the area . <p> It is most unfortunate the telecommunications museum , of a few years ago , did not come to reality . This would have been the crown jewel to attract a wide variety of tourists and their families . <p> Something as constructive and doable as that should have never been abandoned . <p> Surely there are enough of us who believe in Brantford 's potential for such an attraction to rekindle the concept . This time let 's move it forward .
