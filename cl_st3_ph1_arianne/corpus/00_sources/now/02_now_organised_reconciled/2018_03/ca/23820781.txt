Earth Hour , which started in Australia in 2007 , is set to be observed by millions of supporters in 187 countries , who will turn off their lights at 8:30pm local time in what organizers describe as the world 's " largest grassroots movement for climate change " . ( GIUSEPPE CACACE / AFP/GETTY IMAGES ) <p> A vendor sells light-up toys in front of the Quezon Memorial Shrine after the switching off lights for the Earth Hour environmental campaign in Manila . ( NOEL CELIS / AFP/GETTY IMAGES ) <p> This combination of photos shows the Marina Bay Sands hotel in Singapore and resort lit up and with the lights turned off during Earth Hour . ( ROSLAN RAHMAN / AFP/GETTY IMAGES ) <p> Toronto plunged into darkness on Saturday in observance of Earth Hour . <p> The event began in 2007 in Sydney , Australia , and ran from 8:30 p.m. to 9:30 p.m. local time worldwide . More than 180 countries take part in the lights-out movement each year . <p> Social media users across Toronto used the hashtag #EarthHour to commemorate the event , resulting in it trending on Twitter throughout the night . <p> The World Wildlife Foundation ( WWF ) reported more than 3.5 billion impressions were recorded on the #EarthHour social media hashtag , and trended in more than 30 countries last year . <p> Research from the WWF shows the planet is on pace to have lost 67 per cent of wildlife populations by 2020 . The projection comes from reports showing that global populations of mammals , birds , fish , amphibians and reptiles had already declined by 58 per cent between 1970 and 2012 . The population decline is due to habitat losses and pollution , as well as climate change , which puts one in six species at risk for extinction , the conservation group said . <p> Article Continued Below <p> While Toronto Hydro typically tracks the movement in relation to how much electricity is used , spokesperson Tori Gass told the Star in an email that they would not be reporting the electricity drop this year . " It seems Torontonians have been listening to the message about conserving electricity in their everyday lives . . . " Gass wrote . <p> The drop was recorded at about 2.8 per cent last year , which is too low to attribute to Earth Hour alone , Gass said . <p> The CN Tower could be seen without lights from the streets of Toronto , as all exterior lights were shut off in honour of the movement . The Toronto sign at Nathan Phillips Square also shut off for the duration of the event . <p> Mill St. Brewery also turned their lights off , and donated 50 cents from each pint sold at participating bars until Earth Hour began on Saturday evening . Live acoustic performances were featured during the evening , hosted at the Mill Street Beerhal . The Toronto-based brewery has been participating in the event since 2011 , and asks partner restaurants to light candles in lieu of keeping the lights on . <p> The Toronto Star and thestar.com , each property of Toronto Star Newspapers Limited , One Yonge Street , 4th Floor , Toronto , ON , M5E 1E6 .
