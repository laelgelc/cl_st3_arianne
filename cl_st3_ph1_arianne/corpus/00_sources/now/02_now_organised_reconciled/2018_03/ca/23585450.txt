Even as global oil consumption has soared and sea lanes have become increasingly packed with tankers , the amount of oil spilling into ocean has plummeted . In 1974 there were nearly 3,000 oil spills of more than seven tonnes , according to data maintained by the International Tanker Owners Pollution Federation . In 2016 there were fewer than 100 . The decrease closely mirrors a similar decrease in commercial aviation crashes over the same period .
