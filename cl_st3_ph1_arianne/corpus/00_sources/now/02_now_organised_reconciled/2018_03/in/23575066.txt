The report focused on the flood-prone regions of Velachery and Mudichur Road , which were worst affected in the 2015 deluge . <p> The study has suggested that storm water drains in these areas should be redesigned to cope with future flood events . Usually , the runoff from Velachery zone discharges into Pallikaranai marshland . Pallikaranai marshland receives run-off from adjoining catchment , including Velachery , Madipakkam , Perumbakkam etc .
