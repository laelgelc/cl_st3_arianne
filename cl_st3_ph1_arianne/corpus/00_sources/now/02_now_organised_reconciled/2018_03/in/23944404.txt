<h> Indian Medical Association : Use but avoid abuse of antibiotics <h> HIGHLIGHTS <p> IMA has begun campaigns to sensitize people about overuse of antibiotics . <p> Association has found that overuse of antibiotics leads to ' antibiotic resistance ' . <p> The association will also urge doctors to prescribe antibiotics only when needed <p> Indian Medical Association <h> HIGHLIGHTS <p> IMA has begun campaigns to sensitize people about overuse of antibiotics . <p> Association has found that overuse of antibiotics leads to ' antibiotic resistance ' . <p> The association will also urge doctors to prescribe antibiotics only when needed <p> Medical forums in India are getting wary of increased ' Antibiotic Resistance ' ( ABR ) cases surfacing and issuing guidelines to doctors , chemists and patients on their careful usage . The Indian Medical Association ( IMA ) has begun four campaigns to tackle this public health threat - ' Jaroorat Bhi Hai Kya ' , ' 3A Avoid Antibiotic Abuse ' , ' Use Wisely not Widely ' and ' Think Before you Ink ' - to sensitise healthcare professionals on the issue . <p> While the Indian Council of Medical Research ( ICMR ) has initiated an ' Anti-microbial Stewardship Programme ' in 20 private and public hospitals on ' rational antibiotic use ' , other panels of doctors like the Confederation of Medical Associations in Asia and Oceania ( CMAAO ) are conversing with the government to put H1 antibiotics in Schedule X and strictly regulate their over-the-counter sale . ABR occurs when ' superbugs ' --strains of bacteria , virus or parasites that are immune to existing classes of antibiotics -- develop . <p> Since the 1930s , when Penicillin was first discovered in London , a wide range of earlier-fatal sinus , meningitis , pneumonias and bladder and kidney infections have been successfully cured with antibiotics . <p> However , researchers say that due to their overusage and residual presence in food , water and environment , ' superbug ' bacterial strains have evolved . This is not just from direct consumption of antibiotics , but also through their illegal abuse as ' manure additives ' to crops in agriculture and ' growth promoters ' in chicken supplements in the poultry sector Besides , their disposal on the ground and in water has also been a concern . <p> Dr. KK Aggarwal , vice-president of CMAAO , said , " Antibiotic resistance is a growing concern for the world . It prolongs hospitalization , increases cost of treatment and the risk of death . As with climate change , there may be an unknown tipping point , and this could herald a future without effective antibiotics . " " For this reason , we are all taking steps to sensitization . The IMA has proposed several initiatives to tackle this public health threat such ' Jaroorat Bhi Hai Kya ' , ' 3A Avoid Antibiotic Abuse ' , Use Wisely not Widely and ' Think Before you Ink ' . Doctors as well as patients should be aware about judicious antibiotics use . Over prescription and self-prescription , both , need to be checked , " he said . <p> A recent study in the Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences ( PNAS ) showed that antibiotic use more than doubled in India between 2000 and 2015 --from 3.2 billion defined daily doses ( DDD ) to 6.5 billion -- fuelling antibiotic resistance . <p> This has made common infections such as E.coli , pneumonia and tuberculosis more difficult to treat . Dr Kamini Walia , senior scientist and programme officer ( AMR ) at ICMR , said , " Few years back , we initiated the ' Antimicrobial Stewardship Programme ' at 20 hospitals in our network and even set up committees to head these programmes . They would monitor antibiotic prescription and consumption through bills and frame their own policies in this regard . " <p> " For example , they could lay down rules such as only the head of the neo-natal unit can authorize administering a patient with Colistin -- a last-resort antibiotic , avoid board-spectrum antibiotics , etc .. " she added .
