Photo : AFP <p> New Delhi : President Emmanuel Macron of France will visit India on a four-day trip starting 9 March , the foreign ministry said in a statement . The centrepiece of the visit will be his co-chairing of the International Solar Alliance ( ISA ) with Prime Minister Narendra Modi . <p> Macron 's visit comes two years after the last visit by a French President , Francois Hollande , who was chief guest at the Republic Day parade in 2016 . <p> Modi visited France in June 2017 and held talks with Macron , just a month after the latter 's election .
