We remain confident about our ability to emulate Praj 's success in the continent with our 2nd gen technology . <p> The techno-socio-economic model of 2nd gen ethanol technology is already progressing in India and can be replicated in the developed region like Europe . In addition to mitigation of climate change , 2nd gen technology is also expected to enable rural job creation .
