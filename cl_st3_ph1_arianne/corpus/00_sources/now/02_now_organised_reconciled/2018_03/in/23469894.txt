Even if countries just carry out their current climate change action plans -- which together is not adequate to meet the 2-degree target -- there will be a 5-per cent reduction in air pollution-related deaths by 2050 , the study said . <p> Monetary benefits of improved health in a lesser polluted environment would be evident most prominently in India and China , which together will gain nearly 90 per cent of the benefits , the study said . <p> In India , health benefits far outweigh the costs of reducing emissions in all scenarios assessed in the study .
