<p> Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Friday night received his counterpart French President Emmanuel Macron with his trademark hug at Palam 's Air Force Station in New Delhi . On Saturday , India and France inked 14 pacts and pledged to deepen economic , security and defence ties between the two countries . Macron , who is in India on a four-day visit , was accompanied by his wife Brigitte Marie-Claude Macron , besides senior ministers from his cabinet . Macron and his wife on Saturday also paid tribute to Mahatma Gandhi at Rajghat . A joint statement issued after the meeting of Narendra Modi and Emmanuel Macron said India and France stressed on the importance of holding annual dialogue at the ministerial level to deepen cooperation in the economic and financial sectors .
