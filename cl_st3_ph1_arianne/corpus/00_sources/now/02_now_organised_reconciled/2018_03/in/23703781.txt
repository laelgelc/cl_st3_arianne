Photo : Pradeep Gaur/Mint <p> New Delhi : India 's environment ministry has unveiled a draft of the new National Forest Policy ( ) that proposes to restrict " schemes and projects which interfere with forests that cover steep slopes , catchments of rivers , lakes , and reservoirs , geologically unstable terrain and such other ecologically sensitive areas " . <p> " The ecologically sensitive catchment areas shall be stabilized with suitable soil and water conservation measures , and also by planting suitable trees and grass like bamboo , " the draft suggests . <p> It also suggests setting up of two national-level bodies -- National Community Forest Management ( CFM ) Mission and National Board of Forestry ( NBF ) -- for better management of the country 's forests . <p> In an explanation , it says NBF needs to be headed by the central minister in charge of forests .
