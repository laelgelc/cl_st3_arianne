<h> African forests-lungs of the world needs to be saved <h> The most population of the Democratic Republic of Congo depends on this forest for its needs of food , medicine , and shelter . <p> Devdiscourse News DeskViews : 55 <p> Forests of Africa ( Image credit : twitter ) <p> Tropical forests are essential for sustainable growth around the world because of their economic , environmental , and social implications . <p> About 20 percent of the world rainforests grow in Africa 's Congo River basin . This area is earth 's second largest tropical rainforest . It covers nearly 1.5 million square miles . <p> It 's incredibly rich and diverse ecosystem provides food , fresh water , shelter , and medicine for tens of millions of people , and is home to many critically endangered species including forest elephants , gorillas , bonobos and okapis . <p> The most population of the Democratic Republic of Congo depends on this forest for its needs of food , medicine , and shelter . The Congo Basin rainforest plays a critical role in regulating the global climate and halting runaway climate change , for the benefit of the entire biosphere . <p> Africa 's rural poor are particularly dependent on its forests . Although forest products , primarily unfinished logs , account for only about 2 percent of sub-Saharan Africa 's exports , forests generate an average of 6 percent of the region 's gross domestic product -- triple the world average . Eighteen African countries , including Cameroon and Ghana , are among the 24 countries worldwide that rely on forests for 10 percent or more of their economies . <p> Kenya is similarly reliant on the forests for electricity , over 70 percent of which is generated by hydroelectric dams fed by mountain forest watersheds . <p> Despite their economic , social , and environmental importance , Africa 's tropical forests are under threat . Almost 90 percent of West Africa 's coastal rainforest has already been destroyed and deforestation in the Congo Basin has doubled since 1990 . <p> Last but not the least , forests are very crucial for the maintenance of the ecological balance of Planet Earth so saving them from the chainsaw and deforestation is essential to the health and productivity of the continent 's economy and world 's as well .
