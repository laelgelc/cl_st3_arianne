<h> 23 Heads Of State Hail International Solar Alliance <p> Heads of the states from 23 nations have hailed the efforts of International Solar Alliance ( ISA ) at its founding summit in the capital for providing them a common platform to work towards achieving the ambitious targets for clean energy . <p> These heads of state underlined the importance of clean energy in coming day , particularly for developing countries who want to save huge fuel cost and vying for cleaner planet in future . While addressing at the ISA Summit here , President of Seychelles Danny Antoine said the nation has planned to have 100 per cent renewable energy by 2050 despite the fact that it is an island nation . <p> Other countries also unveiled plans to have substantial share of renewables like solar and wind power in their energy mix and hailed the ISA , which would help to reduce carbon foot print in the coming days in view of climate change .
