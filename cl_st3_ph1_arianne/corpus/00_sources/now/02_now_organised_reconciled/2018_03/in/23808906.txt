<p> Besides Bhigwan , bird numbers have also reportedly dropped in the Little Rann of Kutch <p> Number of birds dips from 3,000 in 2015 to 150-200 this year ; experts consider intensive research on development <p> Birdwatchers from around the country are baffled this year by a drastic dip in the number of flamingos visiting the wetlands of Bhigwan , which is usually a well-known destination for this feathered species . In fact , in 2015 , some 3,000 flamingos reportedly landed at Bhigwan , transforming it into a sea of pink and white -- this year , barely 150-200 birds have been sighted in peak season . <p> So unnerving is the development that the Bombay Natural History Society ( BNHS ) is also considering the possibility of studying this unexpected drop in numbers . <p> Dr Nita Shah , a scientist working on flamingos at BNHS , explained , " It is very difficult to pinpoint one reason for this decline -- it is only after observing and analysing for two or three consecutive years that we may be able to figure it out . Nesting has also been impacted by heavy rains this year . A study may be conducted by BNHS by ringing these birds , to understand why the numbers change . Intensive research is required in this case , which could become a reference point to understand the bird better . " <p> Regulars at Bhigwan and other birding spots feel the exact reason may not be known , but the phenomenon certainly needs to be observed and the reasons for it analysed by experts . Some have also attributed it to rising development in and around these wetlands . <p> An environmentalist from NGO Jeevidha , Rajiv Pandit , told Mirror , " It was appalling to see this sudden dip this time . I do n't think climate change can be blamed -- but it is true that water levels at the location are quite high this year . As flamingoes are waders , they need shallow water to walk and find food . So , there is very little space for them to land . " <p> He added , " It is their nesting period at the moment , so they will soon move towards the Little Rann of Kutch for the same . Interestingly , as per data with us , bird sightings have gone down at that location as well . People are saying only a flock of 25 flamingos has visited this year . " <p> Deepak Laddha , an enthusiastic birdwatcher who has been visiting Bhigwan for the last five years , said , " Just a few years ago , in 2015 , I went to the spot for some nature photography , and saw thousands of flamingos on the wetland . This year , there were n't even hundreds as such . God knows if it is climate change or pollution . " <p> The dip has also impacted business for organisers of nature walks and birdwatching visits , which are on the down-low at the moment . Dhanraj Malik , who organises events of this nature in the Little Rann of Kutch , said , " Seeing the scanty number of flamingos , no major walks are being held . The drop in sightings is alarming , as this dip was very sudden . Even we are baffled by it . Lack of food could be one reason , but this is just speculation .
