The objective of International Solar Alliance is to promote solar power generation and utilisation globally to fight climate change and protect the environment . International Solar Alliance brings together 121 countries that fall between the Tropic of Cancer and Tropic of Capricorn to give a push to solar power generation . <p> The international government organisation has got recognition from the United Nations . New Delhi will host the Founding Conference of International Solar alliance on 11th March . While International Solar Alliance is a coalition of 121 prospective member countries , 60 countries are signatories to its framework agreement.and 30 have ratified it so far . Leaders of 47 countries will be participating in the Founding Conference . <p> International Solar Alliance 's Interim Secretariat has been set up in Gurugram .
