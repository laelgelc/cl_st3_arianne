The International Solar Alliance was unveiled by Prime Minister Narendra Modi and then French President Francois Hollande . The idea was to form a coalition of solar resource-rich countries to collaborate on addressing the identified gaps in their energy requirements through a common approach . <p> What are the goals and focus areas of the International Solar Alliance ? <p> The ISA has set a target of 1 TW of solar energy by 2030 , which would require $1 trillion to achieve .
