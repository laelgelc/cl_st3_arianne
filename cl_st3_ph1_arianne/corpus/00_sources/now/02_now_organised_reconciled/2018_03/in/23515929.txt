<h> Share Via Email <p> NEW DELHI : The headquarters of the Animal Welfare Board of India ( AWBI ) has been shifted from Chennai to Haryana 's Ballabhgarh for " better coordination " between the environment ministry and the the board , the Rajya Sabha was informed today . <p> Union Minister of State for Environment , Forest and Climate Change Mahesh Sharma said this in a written reply to a question . <p> " Rule 3 of the Animal Welfare Board ( Administrative ) Rules , 1962 reads as -- ' The Headquarter of the Board hall be at New Delhi or at such other place as the central government , may , after consultation with the Board direct ' , " he said . <p> After consultation with the board , the headquarters of the AWBI has been shifted to Haryana for " better coordination between the ministry and the AWBI for carrying out the animal welfare activities , " the minister said .
