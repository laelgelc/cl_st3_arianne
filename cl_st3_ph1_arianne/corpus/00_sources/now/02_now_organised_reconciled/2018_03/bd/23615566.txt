<p> 122 of 127 critically vulnerable closures of 37 haors under the Water Development Board in Sunamganj have been reinforced with soil . <p> Five other closures remain vulnerable due to a lack of available soil nearby . <p> Reinforcement work is under way at the 122 closures using packed soil , bamboos and sandbags . Local farmers say that if the closures are not protected , the 1,500 kilometre under-construction beribadh might fail . <p> This year , the water receded a month later than usual due to climate change . As a result , work on the crop-protecting dam was greatly delayed .
