<p> President Abdul Hamid will leave Dhaka for India on Thursday on a five-day official visit . <p> He will participate in the founding ceremony of International Solar Alliance ( ISA ) and Solar Summit on March 11 , jointly hosted by India and France in New Delhi , said Foreign Minister AH Mahmood Ali while briefing reporters at his ministry on Wednesday . <p> Presidents , prime ministers , ministers from 23 countries will participate in the summit . <p> After the summit , joint statement will be made from participant countries . <p> Alongside joining the summit , President Abdul Hamid is likely to pay courtesy calls on presidents of India , Sri Lanka and France . <p> On November 30 , 2015 , India and France , at the UN Climate Change Conference in Paris , have launched an International Solar Alliance to boost solar energy in developing countries .
