<h> PM Modi suggests turning to Vedas to combat climate change <p> Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Sunday at the Founding Conference of the International Solar Alliance , suggested looking back to the Vedas in a bid to combat climate change . <p> " Vedas consider the Sun as the soul of the world , it has been considered as a life nurturer . Today , for combating climate change , we need to look at this ancient idea to find a way , " the Prime Minister said here . <p> He also suggested measures to increase the consumption of solar energy . <p> " To promote the use of solar energy , the availability of technology and development , economic resources , reduction in prices , development of storage technology , mass manufacturing , and innovation require a complete ecosystem , " PM Modi said . <p> Prime Minister Modi and French President Emmanuel Macron on Sunday co-chaired the first founding conference of the ISA in New Delhi 's Rashtrapati Bhavan . <p> The summit was attended by United Nations Secretary-General Antonio Guterres , External Affairs Minister Sushma @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Hamid , among other dignitaries from over 50 countries .
