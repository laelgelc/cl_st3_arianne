<h> Asean military chiefs reaffirm security pledge <p> The Asean defence forces chiefs having breakfast with Defence Minister Ng Eng Hen ( in blue shirt and tie ) yesterday , during which they discussed regional security issues and the Asean militaries ' agenda for the year ahead.PHOTO : MINDEF <h> They discuss boosting grouping 's collective ability to counter various threats at annual meeting <p> Against a backdrop of rising terrorism and the growing sophistication of terrorists ' tactics and capabilities , Asean defence forces chiefs reaffirmed their commitment to work together to maintain peace , security and stability in the region yesterday . <p> They were in Singapore for the annual 15th Asean Chiefs of Defence Forces ' Informal Meeting ( ACDFIM ) , chaired by the Chief of Defence Force , Lieutenant-General Perry Lim . <p> A key focus of the discussion was enhancing regional counter-terrorism and cooperation . In particular , they discussed improving Asean 's collective capability to respond to chemical , biological and radiological threats from terrorist groups and rogue states , the Ministry of Defence ( Mindef ) said .
