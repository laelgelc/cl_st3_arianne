also agreed to implement the Action Programme for 2017-2020 in a drastic and effective manner . <p> They will soon organise the sixth meeting of the Joint Committee on economic and trade cooperation framework . <p> In terms of economics and trade , both sides expressed their satisfaction with positive bilateral trade growth which hit US$1.24 billion last year . <p> They agreed on numerous trade
