<p> China 's economic planning agency , a legacy of the command economy days that gained new power during the era of rapid state-led growth , will be significantly weaker under a government reshuffle announced on Tuesday . <p> The National Development and Reform Commission , known as the " little cabinet " , has been a formidable power centre in the world 's second-biggest economy , with its portfolio ranging from approving high-speed railway projects to the electricity rates for every household . It will remain one of the 26 bodies under the State Council , but many of its functions will be taken over by other agencies , according to the plan presented to the National People 's Congress . <p> Under the revamp , which is expected to be endorsed by the legislature later this week , the NDRC will lose responsibility for creating development zones to a new natural resources ministry , while its climate change unit will be transferred to the environment ministry .
