<h> How to tackle wicked problems with spatial analysis <p> For every challenge governments face today , location can answer difficult questions . Whether it is climate change or ageing population , officials must understand where and how it is happening . <p> Geospatial company Esri has worked with cities across the globe , using location to tackle wicked problems -- complex issues that cut across multiple aspects of government 's work . <p> Spatial analysis is helping officials predict crimes , finding patterns in events that may seem unrelated at first glance , an e-book by Esri shows . Meanwhile , in Atlanta , it is helping transport officials understand how effectively commuters are being served . And in Texas , it is helping the government understand the extent and impact of droughts . <p> In all these cases , officials are analysing maps and geospatial data to understand trends and make decisions .
