<p> SINGAPORE - An annual drive to collect " pre-loved " goods such as clothing , toys , books and accessories pulled in its biggest haul yet this year . <p> This weekend , the items went on sale to the public at City Square Mall with the aim of benefiting disadvantaged children - and the environment . <p> EcoBank collected more than 17,500kg of second-hand goods which , had they been sent to the incinerator , would have produced 7,012 tonnes of carbon emissions - equal to what is produced from 1,121 homes in one year . <p> The total was up from 10,770kg last year and 6,100kg in 2016 , when the initiative began . <p> The public donated the items at eight collection points across Singapore . <p> All proceeds from the bazaar will be donated to the six charities under The Children 's Charities Association of Singapore , while unsold items will be passed on to the association and other non-governmental organisations ( NGOs ) for their fund-raising efforts . <p> Singapore produced 7.81 million tonnes of rubbish in 2016 , up from 7.67 million tonnes @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ NEA ) . <p> Giving a speech at the bazaar 's official opening ceremony on Saturday ( March 3 ) , Senior Minister of State for the Environment and Water Resources Amy Khor , the guest of honour , said reducing waste will help to decrease emissions and combat climate change . <p> She added that initiatives such as EcoBank are important and support the Year of Climate Action 2018 , a national awareness campaign . <p> Fashion 3R , a sister initiative that aims to spread awareness about sustainable fashion and the true cost of consumption , was also launched at the opening ceremony . <p> Jointly organised by CDL , the NEA , Eco-Business , NGO Connected Threads Asia and the Raffles College of Higher Education , the initiative has conducted several activities , including a showcase and sale of outfits " upcycled " by students of the Raffles College of Higher Education . <p> Fashion 3R will also hold an upcycling workshop by local textile artist Agatha Lee on Sunday at the Singapore Sustainability Academy .
