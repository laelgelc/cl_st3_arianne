After changing his habits and practising various other energy-saving tips , such as making it a habit to switch off the lights and fans whenever he leaves a room , Mr Vishnu said he saves about $40 per month in electricity bills . <p> " My electricity bill used to be $200 plus a month . By practising these and other energy-saving tips , I have reduced my bill by 20 per cent , " he says . <p> As a low-lying islandstate , Singapore is vulnerable to the effects of climate change , such as rising sea levels and temperatures , as well as more pronounced wet and dry seasons . <p> The production of electricity from fossil fuels is one of the main contributors to greenhouse gas emissions . <p> Like Mr Vishnu , we will be able to help mitigate climate change , if everyone does his or her part to conserve electricity . <p> PHOTO : THE MINISTRY OF THE ENVIRONMENT AND WATER RESOURCES <p> Practise these energy-saving tips today ! <p> Pick the fan over the airconditioner when you can . This translates to almost $300 in savings each year . <p> When using the airconditioner , set it at 25 deg C. Every degree raised saves up to 1 per cent of your total electricity bill . <p> Reduce your air-conditioner usage by one hour per day , and save up to 3 per cent of your total electricity bill . <p> Even if you switch off appliances , energy is being consumed . Switch off appliances at the power socket , and unplug your fully charged laptops and phones . You 'll save up to 2 per cent of your total electricity bill . <p> Use energy labels to choose energy efficient appliances . As a basic guide , the more ticks there are on the label , the more energy efficient the appliance is . A 5-tick air-conditioner , for example , can save you up about $200 a year compared to one with just two ticks . Similarly , using a 3-tick refrigerator instead of a 1-tick refrigerator will save you about $35 a year . <p> Similarly , using a three-tick refrigerator instead of a one-tick refrigerator will save you about $35 a year . <p> Light up with LED . Choose LED bulbs instead of incandescent bulbs and save about $9 annually . <p> Switch off the storage water heater after use .
