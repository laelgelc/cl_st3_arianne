<h> Health savings outweigh costs of limiting global warming : Study <p> PARIS ( AFP ) - The estimated cost of measures to limit Earth-warming greenhouse gas emissions can be more than offset by reductions in deaths and disease from air pollution , researchers said on Saturday ( March 3 ) . <p> It would cost US$22.1 trillion ( S$29 trillion ) to US$41.6 trillion between 2020 and 2050 for the world to hold average global warming under 2 deg C , a team projected in The Lancet Planetary Health journal . <p> For the lower , aspirational limit of 1.5 deg C , the cost would be between US$39.7 trillion and US$56.1 trillion , they estimated . <p> But air pollution deaths could be reduced by 21 to 27 per cent to about 100 million between 2020 and 2050 under the 2 deg C scenario , the team estimated , and by 28 to 32 per cent to about 90 million at 1.5 deg C. <p> " Depending on the strategy used to mitigate climate change , estimates suggest that the health savings from reduced air pollution could be @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ mitigation , globally , " they wrote . <p> Health costs from air pollution include medical treatment , patient care , and lost productivity . <p> The countries likely to see the biggest health savings were air pollution-ridden India and China , said the researchers , who used computer models to project future emissions , the costs of different scenarios for curbing them , and the tally in pollution-related deaths . <p> " The health savings are exclusively those related to curbing air pollution , " study co-author Anil Markandya of the Basque Centre for Climate Change in Spain told AFP . <p> " Other health benefits are not included , which of course makes our figures underestimates of the total benefits .
