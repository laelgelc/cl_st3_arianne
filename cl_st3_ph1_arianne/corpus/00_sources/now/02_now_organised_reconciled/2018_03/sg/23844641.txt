<h> Highlights from Art Basel Hong Kong <h> Ahead of the fair taking place from 29 to 31 March , we take a look at some notable pieces to look out for . <p> AFP Relaxnews <p> VR works from Anish Kapoor , Marina Abramovic <p> Marina Abramovic and Anish Kapoor will present their first virtual reality works at the fair through a partnership with HTC Vive . Abramovic will present the work " Rising , " confronting viewers with the realities of climate change as they enter a virtual space where the artist will appear in a glass tank that is slowly filling with water . <p> Kapoor will bring his work " Into Yourself , Fall , " which is described as a journey through the human body . Visitors will be able to explore the two VR works both at the fair -- in the HTC Vive Lounge -- and at home via the VR app store Viveport . <p> Five recent works by Jeff Koons , presented by David Zwirner , are sure to be among the most buzzed-about . On view will be " Bluebird Planter " ( 2010-2016 ) , a large-scale work in mirror-polished stainless steel ; " Swan ( Inflatable ) " ( 2011-2015 ) , also in mirror-polished stainless steel and giving the appearance of a Mylar balloon ; and three works from Koons 's " Gazing Ball " series , begun in 2013 . Koons will also be present at the gallery 's booth . <p> For those at home , David Zwirner will offer an online Viewing Room featuring prints that span the artist 's career . <p> Art Basel Hong Kong 's Encounters sector is dedicated to large-scale installations and site-specific projects and is behind some of the fair 's most eye-catching works . The sector is presenting 12 projects this year , including nine created just for the fair . Highlights include a work by Taiwanese artist Chou Yu-Cheng featuring giant-sized dishware , and " Start . Stop " by Subodh Gupta ( pictured in the cover image ) , an installation consisting of a large sushi belt carrying stacks of tiffin boxes and gleaming pots . <p> Harbour Arts Sculpture Park <p> This brand-new space on the Hong Kong landscape opened last month with a collection of works by 19 local and international artists , including Yayoi Kusama , Tracey Emin , Jenny Holzer , Antony Gormley and Zhan Wang .
