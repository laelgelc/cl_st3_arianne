<p> In the 1980s , Prosopis juliflora , a shrub popularly known as " mathenge " , was introduced in arid and semi-arid areas to halt desertification but proved invasive , leading to its classification as a weed . <p> Benefits aside , Mathenge has been blamed for damage to livestock and humans in areas where it grows . <p> Whereas its introduction was laudable , the research for more sustainable wood substitutes ought to have continued immediately complaints against it arose . <p> It took a herding community in Baringo to present a toothless goat in court to show the detrimental effects of Mathenge on livelihoods . <p> LOGGINGMeanwhile , runaway deforestation in Kenya has taken a turn for the worse , compounded with unmitigated climate change phenomena . <p> Environmental disasters such as landslides , drought and drying up of water bodies are imminent and their effects will bite . <p> Kenya 's first Nobel Prize laureate , Prof Wangari Maathai , dedicated her life to protecting forests and woodlands in the country . <p> But with the rampant tree-felling , whether on wetlands or water towers , her legacy has , sadly , gone up in smoke . <p> The recent ban on logging is a typical knee-jerk reaction to the underlying issue . That alone wo n't remedy the situation as the damage is already done . <p> TREE PLANTINGFor real change , planting more trees , which will take quite some time and requires cooperation from residents , must be institutionalised as a national duty . <p> It took nearly two and a half decades for the impact of South Korea 's national restoration of forest cover to be felt . <p> Consequently , it has taken patriotism to keep the forest cover growing by girth and twigs . <p> What we urgently need is short-term or hypodermic solutions to manage our forests while we work on the long-term or surgical ones . <p> Most of the trees in Kenya are cut down primarily for wood fuel . <p> What is the solution that can curb that demand while we plant more trees that will , in truth , take close to 10 years to grow ? We must grow hemp . <p> CONVENIENTHemp , part of the Cannabis sativa L. plant species , is a multi-purpose plant chiefly grown for its fibre , seed and intoxicating resin . <p> The latter type of Cannabis sativa is what is called marijuana , a narcotic drug illegal in most parts of the world . <p> Hemp , however , is not marijuana -- even though it looks similar . It lacks enough Tetrahydrocannabinol ( THC ) to cause intoxication . <p> This family association and off-shoot stigma is probably why many governments in developing countries have shunned this plant that offers more than 1,000 solutions in eco-friendly products . <p> A fast-growing wood substitute , hemp matures in between five and seven months -- much faster than Mathenge , which takes three to four years . <p> Hemp will grow anywhere without pesticides or herbicides and does n't compete for soil nutrients with other plants . <p> NUTRITION <p> It is known to repel pests and birds from invading crops such as corn and sorghum as its seeds are much more nutritious compared to those cereals . <p> In fact , hemp restores the soil by breaking down harmful metals and consumes three times the carbon dioxide in the air . <p> Its longer and stronger fibre makes products that are firmer but lighter than those made of wood . <p> Owing to its natural constituent , hemp-made products last longer than those of wood as termites and molds tend not to attack them . <p> FIREWOODHemp is more viable than wood . You need just an acre of hemp to give you enough fibre pulp you would otherwise get from four acres of trees . <p> Hemp briquettes have more energy than charcoal and burn longer with less smoke . The briquettes are also light in weight and can be transported with ease . <p> Paper made of hemp is of the best quality . It 's so good that the Declaration of Independence of the United States of America was written on it , as was the first Bible printed by Gutenberg and most of the currencies in the 19th Century . <p> I urge Pan-Paper Mill in Webuye to start producing hemp paper if they want to be profitable . <p> The paper can be made into strong brown bags that can replace the polythene recently banned in Kenya and Rwanda . <p> PLASTICSAnything made of plastic can be fashioned out of hemp . <p> This being a plastic-free environment movement era , the National Environment Management Authority ought to offer hemp-related alternatives to most Kenyans to dull the impact of the looming ban on plastic bottles and straws . <p> Hemp cellulose can be moulded into containers such as bottles , plates and cups or even poles and blocks . <p> Finally , products made out of hemp are biodegradable -- a big plus to the people , planet and profit cycle . <p> For that , hemp should be given a chance to salvage a world choking on plastic .
