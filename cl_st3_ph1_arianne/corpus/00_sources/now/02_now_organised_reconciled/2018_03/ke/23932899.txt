<p> The combined cost of interventions was Sh78 million , inclusive of human feed supplements , drugs , hay and transportation of water to hard-hit areas in Kajiado West . <p> This amount did not include figures spent on fuel subsidies for boreholes during the drought period . <p> On Friday , Centre for Minority Rights Development and pastoralists community representatives held a press conference in Kajiado town after a three-day seminar on droughts and shocks associated with climate change . <p> The representatives of NGOs and community-based organisations noted the need for counties to fast-track the issuance of county adaptation funds to cushion pastoralists during droughts . <p> While reading a statement at the end of the seminar , Juliana Nasaru said pastoralists are at a critical state in their lives and are unsure of how they and their livelihoods will evolve in light of climate change . <p> " Relying on mobility , pastoralists follow their essential but fleeting resources such as pasture and water to make ends meet . They depend on scarce resources that are slowly being depleted by climate change and national projects , " Nasaru noted . <p> She said lack of harmonious policies and commitment by the national and county governments , to protect indigenous communities and their critical resources , exposes them to peril . <p> Pastoralists who occupy arid and semi-arid lands suffer disproportionately , Nasaru said , adding that with ongoing droughts , they must move to areas with plenty of rainfall and vegetation . <p> " The increasedprobability of droughts that occur once every three years has the potential to decrease herd sizes due to increased mortality and poorer reproductive performance of the livestock , " the statement said . <p> It added that Kenya has seen more frequent cycles of droughts , increasing the need for support to improve the resilience levels of pastoralists to anticipat . <p> This is so they can absorb and recover from the effects of climate change shocks . <p> The convener of the seminar , Nyangori Ohenjo , said there has been a noticeable increase in the need for programmes , policy and development actions that will increase resilience levels . <p> Thank you for participating in discussions on The Star , Kenya website .
