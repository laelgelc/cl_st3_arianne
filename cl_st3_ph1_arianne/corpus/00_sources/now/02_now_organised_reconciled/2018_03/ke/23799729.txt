<h> Call to include pastoralists in tree planting projects <p> A forest lobby wants the reintroduction of customary laws to train the community in environment conservation . <p> Northeastern Forest Conservancy chairman Ahmed Mohammed yesterday said the county government should , through the county assembly , come up with relevant legislation . He said anyone cutting down a tree should be fined three goats . <p> The community , primarilymade up of pastoralists , moves from one point to another . Mohammed said they should be given seeds to plant in the grazing fields . <p> Dr Mohammed said cutting down a 300-year-old tree should not go unpunished . Some of the indigineous trees are used for charcoal , which Mohammed says needs to be controlled . <p> The regional conservancy coordinator said such projects in Garissa Township and Ijara may lead to more than 2,000 trees being planted .
