<h> Pastoralists count losses as drought , diseases bite <h> share <p> James Leposo is a livestock farmer in Oloitokitok , Kajiado County . Last year , he lost 106 cattle worth Sh4.5 million to a deadly disease and the 2016/17 prolonged drought . His animals were almost swept by Contagious Bovine Pleuropneumonia ( CBPP ) , a highly contagious bacterial disease that afflicts the lungs of cattle , buffalo , and yaks . <p> The cows , he explained , succumbed to death after developing swollen feet and excessive diarrhoea . Identification of the disease followed laboratory testing of some samples at the Kenya Agricultural and Livestock Research Organisation ( KALRO ) ' s Muguga centre . <p> To Leposo the disease was a big blow as everyday he could lose 15 cows , leading to a near depletion of his herd of cattle .
