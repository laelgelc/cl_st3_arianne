<h> Kenyan resort village hits back at plastic waste <p> JOHANNESBURG - Like many places in the world , Kenya 's coastline faces challenges with waste plastic , from used shopping bags that block drains to throw-away water bottles that litter streets and wash into the sea . <p> Now the Indian Ocean resort village of Watamu , best known for its tropical beaches and Swahili history , is taking on plastic waste , turning it into homes and furniture - and maybe even a ship capable of sailing all the way to South Africa to raise awareness about plastic pollution . <p> Sammy Baya , a resident of the coastal community , now owns a house with walls made of stacked glass and plastic bottles . <p> " It 's just like living in any other house , but this one , unlike other ordinary houses , allows more light to enter , and therefore I do n't use my solar ( panels ) for lighting when there is a full moon , " he said .
