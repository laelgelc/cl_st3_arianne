<h> ' Goronyo dam 's water level depletes by 90% , worst in 25 years <p> Managing Director of Sokoto Rima River Basin Development Authority , Engineer Buhari Bature has said the depletion of Goronyo dam 's water level is the worst seen in the dam in over 25 years . <p> The water level is noted to have depleted by 90 per cent of its installed one billion cubic meter capacity . <p> Speaking when he conducted Governor Aminu Tambuwal round the dam site , the SRRBDA MD attributed the prevailing situation to the shortage of rainfall in 2017 , climate change and lack of desalting of the dam . <p> Bature noted that the problem had affected water supply to water board and irrigation farming in Sokoto and Kebbi states . <p> In a remark after inspecting water levels at the dam , Governor Aminu Tambuwal described the depletion as alarming . <p> " The reservoir of Goronyo Dam was constructed to hold one billion cubic meter of water but as we 've seen today , the water in it is just about 100 million cubic meter . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and in effect , we had to resort to rationing water to the people , " he stated . <p> He however said proactive measures had been taken to boost water supply to the people pending when the rains would come and increase the volume of water in the dam . <p> If you are happy to be contacted by a Daily Trust journalist please leave a telephone number that we can contact you on .
