<h> DAPCHI : ' As a mother , I share the sorrow and agony of the parents ' - Aisha Buhari <p> President Muhammad Buhari 's wife , Aisha Buhari , has empathized with parents of the abducted Dapchi school girls , expressing her hope that ongoing efforts by government would lead to their release soon . <p> She was speaking during an event to mark the 2018 International Women 's Day , on Thursday , March 8 , 2018 at the National Center for Women Development , Abuja . <p> Mrs Buhari observed that for Nigeria , the day must be marked with a difference considering the sad incident of the abduction of girls , especially the case of Chibok and Dapchi , which must take the national spotlight . <p> " As a mother , I share the sorrow and agony of the parents at this time , it is my sincere hope that efforts by government will soon lead to their release , " she said . <p> She introduced a local theme tagged , " Leave our daughters alone " which she said , is a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ media to own the campaign and spread message . <p> She also charged wives of the Governors to propagate the campaign in their various states , stating that the message must echo through all corners of Nigeria and be on everyone 's lips . <p> Speaking earlier , wife of the Vice President , Dolapo Osinbajo , while congratulating women as they celebrate the special day , said it is sad that Nigerian women were " witnessing the worst of times " . <p> She however noted that at the same time , they ( women ) are also witnessing the best of times in many respects . " This is because there are many good stories happening around women , " she said . <p> Director-General of the National Center for Women Development , Mary Ekpere-Eta , said this year , there was an unprecedented global movement for women 's rights , equality and justice . <p> She said sexual harassment , violence and discrimination against women has captured public discourse , propelled by a rising determination for change , which she noted were instrumental to the declaration by @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ women and girls in Nigeria . <p> She drew attention to the plight of rural women , who despite their hard work lack basic infrastructure and services , decent work and social protection , and are left more vulnerable to the effects of climate change . <p> She called for urgent action to reverse this trend and ensure better life for the rural women . <p> Goodwill messages were delivered by UNICEF , UN Women , UNFPA and a host of others . <p> Highlight of the event was an exhibition of women 's business products .
