surrounded by 12 nautical miles of marine reserve . <p> Maha and the other students learnt about the Kermadec Ocean Sanctuary Bill that proposes the area be turned into an ocean sanctuary . <p> According to the Ministry for the Environment , at 620,000 square kilometres , it will be one of the world 's largest and most significant fully protected areas . <p> " The trip was really cool
