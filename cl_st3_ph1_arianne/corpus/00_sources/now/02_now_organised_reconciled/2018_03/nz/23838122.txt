<h> Former Mahurangi rugby captain wins leadership award in Antarctica <p> North Auckland 's Winston Cowie has returned home from Antarctica with an experience like no other . <p> Cowie spent this month at the Sir Robert Swan International Antarctic Expedition and was given the prestigious Sir Robert Swan Leadership Inspiration Award . <p> The former Mahurangi Rugby Club captain , and now marine policy manager at the Environment Agency Abu Dhabi ( EAAD ) , represented the EAAD and Dr Jane Goodall 's Roots and Shoots at the expedition and was one of two who was given the award for outstanding contributions to the expedition . <p> Cowie was one of 90 environmental ambassadors from more than 20 countries who took part in the expedition led by Sir Robert Swan , the first explorer to walk to the North and South Poles . <p> The expedition was a chance for the ambassadors to learn about climate change and witness the impact of global warming on Antarctica . <p> Supplied <p> The group had an unforgettable experience . <p> As a part of the expedition Cowie had to brave the freezing conditions to take part in the Zayed Solar Lights Show . He and his team lit up the Antarctic night using 100 solar lights , sending out a message of unity , hope and action on climate change .
