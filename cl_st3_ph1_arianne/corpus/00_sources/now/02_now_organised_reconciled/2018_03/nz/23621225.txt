Some of New Zealand 's top Maori leaders , scientists , and economic development experts are set to speak at an emergency climate change summit . <p> At Waitangi in February 2018 , the Iwi Chairs Forum agreed to convene the first Maori Leaders ' Climate Change Summit to be held in Wellington on 24-25 March 2018 . <p> With a line-up of expert presenters and interactive workshops , the Summit aims to update Maori leaders about the state of our climate . Speakers include New Zealand 's top climate change expert , Dr James Renwick ; senior meteorologist at New Zealand MetService , Erick Brenstrum ; Canterbury health leader and former kaiwhakahaere ( chairman ) of Ngai Tahu , Sir Mark Solomon ( Ngai Tahu , Ngati Kuri ) ; public health specialist , Dr Rhys Jones ( Ngati Kahungunu ) ; and senior business journalist , Rod Oram .
