<p> Located between mainland Norway and the North Pole , on the archipelago Svalbard , the vault was intended to be safe from the effects of climate change . <p> DAVID UNWIN/ STUFF <p> Seeds from the Margot Forde Germplasm Centre in Palmerston North are now stored deep underground in the Arctic . <p> In October 2016 the vault suffered from flooding , despite being built to last more than 100 years , when the surrounding area experienced unseasonably high temperatures which melted the permafrost around it . <p> The vault was built 10 years ago , and its concept was proven successful in 2015 when seeds were withdrawn to re-establish crops wiped out by civil war in Syria .
