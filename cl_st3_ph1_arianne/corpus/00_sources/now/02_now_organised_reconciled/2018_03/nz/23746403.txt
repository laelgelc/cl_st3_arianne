<h> Lack of sea ice surprises scientists <h> Scientists have been surprised at the absence of sea ice near Antarctica during a research expedition . <p> 21 March 2018 <p> The absence of sea ice near Antarctica has surprised scientists who have spent the past six weeks carrying out research in the Southern Ocean . <p> The NIWA vessel Tangaroa returned to Wellington on Wednesday . <p> NIWA marine ecologist David Bowden says the voyage was planned late in the season knowing that they may not be able to get where they wanted to go because of ice , but it was almost completely ice-free . <p> Sea ice forms when the ocean surrounding Antarctica freezes , which plays a crucial role in the global climate system , but this summer coverage has dropped to its second-lowest on record . <p> " It has been an extraordinary year from that aspect but has allowed us to go to places we thought would probably be inaccessible , " Dr Bowden said . <p> The voyage lost three days to bad weather but managed to achieve what they set out to do , he said . <p> " We have installed long-term experiments and collected a wide range of samples and data that will help to improve understanding of climate and oceanographic processes in the region , and how these influence the marine ecosystem . " <p> Data gathered during the voyage would also be valuable in helping determine the effects a lack of sea ice has on the Antarctic ecosystem . <p> The voyage looked at : <p> * Climate change effects on oceanographic processes <p> * Marine microbial community structure and function <p> * Influence of marine aerosols on cloud formation and properties of Antarctic clouds
