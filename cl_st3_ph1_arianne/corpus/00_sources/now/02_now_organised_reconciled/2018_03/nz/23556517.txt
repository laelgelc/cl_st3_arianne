<h> Celebrating International Women 's Day <p> International Women 's Day is an opportunity for everyone to celebrate the social , economic , cultural and political achievements of women . <p> Across Auckland Council we 're celebrating the contribution our women and their many successes play in making our organisation a diverse and vibrant place to work . <p> Today we shine the spotlight on Parul Sood , Waste Solutions Planning Manager who is trail blazing in the world of waste . She represented Auckland at the global C40 Cities Awards 2017 in Chicago , which recognises the world 's most inspiring and innovative cities tackling climate change . <p> The Zero Waste Award recognised the leadership and the amazing work done by her team and community partners for striving towards our goal of zero-waste for Auckland by 2040 . <h> What does International Women 's Day mean to you ? <p> For me it 's a way to recognise and celebrate women and their achievements , regardless of their ethnic , economic or political preferences . <p> Every woman has something special to add to the world and the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ difference within their sphere of influence -- whether it 's taking the time to support another woman in her development or leadership journey or being a strong role model and inspiring others . <h> Why is diversity in the workplace important ? <p> Diversity of people represents diversity of thought -- people who have different experiences and backgrounds enhance a business . Those differences shape how we think and help boost creative problem-solving to find the most innovative solutions to complex challenges . <p> The challenge is how we can make sure we incorporate diversity across all areas and levels of our organisation . <h> What 's your biggest wish for Auckland 's future ? <p> To inspire everyone to take better care of Mother Earth and our environment . It worries me that we fail to recognise long term benefits of our investments today . There is no waste in nature -- so having waste in our society represents great inefficiencies . <p> A world-class city is a zero-waste city ; we all need to take care of Mother Earth and our environment for future generations . <p> We @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ proud to work with an amazing team who are all equally passionate to turn our waste into valuable resources and strive towards a more circular economy . <h> Ideas to celebrate International Women 's Day <p> Take the time to acknowledge your fellow female colleagues and the contribution they make . <p> Wear the official colours : green , white and purple . <p> Tonight head along to 2018 Writers &amp; Readers opening night event Women Changing the World in celebration of women , their words and their power to change -- marking 125 years of New Zealand suffrage . <p> Got any unwanted shoes , bags , jewellery , make-up or clothes in new or near-new condition ?
