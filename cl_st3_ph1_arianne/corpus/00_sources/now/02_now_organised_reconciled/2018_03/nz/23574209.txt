<p> A University of Otago health expert is calling for an independent assessment of the Trans-Pacific Partnership trade pacts potential impacts on climate change and health.Trade treaty threatens health if climate change not addressed : University of Otago expert <p> A University of Otago health expert is calling for an independent assessment of the Trans-Pacific Partnership trade pact 's potential impacts on climate change and health . <p> Senior Clinical Lecturer at the University of Otago 's Christchurch School of Medicine , Dr Erik Monasterio , is concerned about the potential consequences of the Comprehensive and Progressive Agreement for Trans-Pacific Partnership ( CPTPP ) , an economic treaty , originally called the Trans-Pacific Partnership Agreement . <p> Dr Monasterio argues the CPTPP , which is set to be signed by 11 countries in Chile today , is geared fundamentally towards the interests of transnational corporations and foreign investors at the expense of concerns about human and environmental health . <p> Dr Monasterio , a consultant in forensic psychiatry , is one of the authors in an editorial Climate change , human health and the CPTPP in the latest issue of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ calls for an independent assessment of parts of the treaty . <p> An assessment of the CPTPP 's anticipated impacts on climate disruption , and on mitigation strategies , should be undertaken and released for public discussion before the treaty is ratified , the editorial states . <p> It says the Labour-led Government has launched into its first term with bold plans to align New Zealand 's economy with priorities dictated by the urgency of the climate crisis , yet its ambitions would be undermined by signing the treaty . <p> " Ironically , the Government 's ambition in this regard would be seriously undercut by signing a treaty that underwrites the economic status quo and creates strong legal headwinds for essential regulatory action . " <p> They say there is a considerable body of scientific evidence on the harmful impacts of climate change on health . So much so , that climate change has been identified as the most serious threat to global public health this century .
