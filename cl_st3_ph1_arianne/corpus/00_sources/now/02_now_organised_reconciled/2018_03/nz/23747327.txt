Later that month , ex-cyclone Gita brought more rain , causing heavy flooding in some areas . <p> The Insurance Council said there had been about 3,300 claims for damage done by ex-cyclone Fehi , with the bulk of those domestic claims . <p> " We 've had $65.2 million in insured losses from extreme weather events already this year , off the back of two storms in the first quarter of 2018 , and we 're yet to receive the data for Cyclone Gita , " said chief executive Tim Grafton .
