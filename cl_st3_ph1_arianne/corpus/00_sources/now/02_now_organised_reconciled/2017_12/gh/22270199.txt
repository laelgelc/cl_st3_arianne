<h> Government 's plans to embark on solar programme is in right direction - Minister <p> Mr Mustapha Abdul Hamid , Minister of Information , has said Government 's plan to embark on an MDA Solar Rooftop Programme from 2018 was in the right direction . <p> He said programme dubbed : " Government Goes Solar " will ensure that government institutions switch to renewable energy to reduce Government 's expenditure on utilities . <p> The " Government Goes Solar " project also seeks to address the huge expenditure of state institutions to purchase power into prepaid meters . Mr Abdul Hamid said this when Photon Energy , a Private Solar Energy Firm , handed over a 15 Kilowatts Solar Power Panel to Ministry of Information in Accra . <p> The Solar Panel is being donated to the Ministry as a test system to complement their efforts to reduce the Ministry 's cost of electricity payment .
