<p> President Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo , Wednesday said addressing the root causes of conflicts was critical in promoting inclusive development and the consolidation of peace on the African Continent . <p> He said , to reduce the likelihood of conflicts , and the relapse of violence in post conflict countries , it was essential that the issues of inequality , unemployment , poverty , human rights abuses , climate change , governance , weak law enforcement , illicit national and transnational crime , and the issue of inequitable resource allocation , are tackled holistically . <p> President Akufo-Addo made this known on Wednesday , when he addressed 77 graduands , who had completed the Master of Arts in Conflict , Peace and Security Studies , Master of Arts in Gender , Peace &amp; Security , and Executive Master of Arts in Conflict , Peace &amp; Security Studies programmes at the Kofi Annan International Peace Keeping and Training Centre ( KAIPTC ) in Accra . <p> Urging the graduands of the KAIPTC ) to help address the root causes of conflicts on the continent , the President reminded them that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ changed , demanding new approaches and increased
