The Foreign Editor of the Daily Graphic , Kate Baaba Hudson , sought insights into the visit and the President 's view on some current topical issues . <p> We publish below the answers to our Foreign Editor 's ( FE ) questions from the German President ( GP ) . <p> FE : Sir , you just attended the African Union-European Union ( AU-EU ) Summit in Abidjan , La Cote d'Ivoire . Could you please share with the Daily Graphic some insights into the summit and some of the key decisions that were taken there ? <p> GP : Europe is Africa 's neighbour and Africa 's most important partner with regard to trade , investment and development cooperation . A key message sent by the summit was that we must consult with one another more , and on all levels , because in future , our destinies will be even more interlinked than they are today . Encounters between Europe and Africa now occur under a new heading , namely that development cooperation in Africa can not provide sustainable growth but it could also effectively support Africa 's own efforts . That is why the German G20 Presidency promoted the Compact with Africa . It addresses both sides : Africa 's own motivation to spur development across the continent , as well as changes in Europe , so that more European investment reaches Africa . <p> FE : There have been international concerns about the issue of illegal immigrants , drug trafficking , terrorism and its financing .
