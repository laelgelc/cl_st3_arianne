<h> Flat earthers and the rise of conspiracy theorists <p> Right now in California , an American is preparing to launch himself into the atmosphere with a homemade rocket . <p> His mission is to prove that the Earth is not round -- but actually flat . <p> In the US , conspiracy theorists are becoming increasingly prominent -- with even the current President Donald Trump questioning the authenticity of his predecessor Barack Obama 's birth certificate and the official story about the assassination of John F. Kennedy . <p> Critics say it 's all part of a growing movement against the validity of science , from everything on climate change to the Moon landing , and the shape of the Earth .
