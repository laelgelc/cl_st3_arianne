<h> Janus Metz Pedersen 's sports drama revolves around the 1980 Wimbledon final clash between Bjorn Borg and John McEnroe . <p> Janus Metz Pedersen 's Borg McEnroe aims to be both a tennis movie and a psychological study of two players at the midway point between descent and ascent . In the metaphorical centre are Swedish champion Bjorn Borg ( Sverrir Gudnason ) , who is aiming to make history with his fifth straight Wimbledon win , and American prodigy John McEnroe ( Shia Labeouf ) , whose dazzling talent has made him the player most likely to ruin Borg 's dream . <p> The movie is based on one of the most storied contests in tennis history : the men 's single final that was played at Wimbledon on July 5 , 1980 . The contrasting personalities of the contenders -- Borg famously ice-cool and McEnroe a profanity-sputtering hothead -- provides for an easy contrast in the early sections , but the film hits its stride in the later portions , which suggest that the players enter Centre Court in 1980 after having swapped their personalities . Largely ignored in measures to control vehicular pollution , experts say two-wheelers too need to be brought in the ambit of pollution control as they contribute across most factors determining vehicular pollution - engine technology , total number of vehicles , structure and age of vehicles and fuel quality .
