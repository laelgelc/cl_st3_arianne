<h> You are here <h> Will global warming affect Wind Energy Generation ? <p> Wind energy contributes to 12% of installed power in India . At present , most of this comes from the wind farms located on land . However , the country has ambitious plans of expanding wind energy generation by building wind farms on the sea in the next 5 years . How feasible is that at a time when our oceans are warming and the sea levels are rising ? In a study , researchers from the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay have developed a mechanism to study how climate change could impact wind energy harnessed from wind farms in the sea . <p> Wind energy farms constructed on the sea bed are called offshore wind farms . Studies show that harnessing wind power offshore is more advantageous than doing so on land .
