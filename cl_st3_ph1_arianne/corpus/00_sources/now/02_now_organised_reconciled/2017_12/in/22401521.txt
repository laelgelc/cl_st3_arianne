<h> Joy of giving back : Mumbai housing society creates 10,000 sqft garden <h> Residents of a housing society in Kandivli created a 10,000 sqft garden to restore lost biodiversity . ( HT Photo ) <p> Christmas celebrations began on an unconventional note at Tirupati Tower in Thakur Complex , Kandivli . <p> The residents have created a 10,000 sqft garden , with fruit and flowering plants that will attract birds and butterflies . <p> From children to senior citizens , 80 residents got together on Sunday to recreate a green area that was lost when a drainage channel flowing along the building compound was widened . The residents planted five-foot-tall saplings of mango , pomegranate , tamarind , and neem , to ensure they have a higher survival rate . They plan to plant another 300 native trees in the compound . <p> " Our efforts are aimed at protecting the biodiversity of this area , which was home to countless birds , butterflies , and squirrels .
