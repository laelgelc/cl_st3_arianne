<h> Climate change to up migration of asylum-seekers to Europe <p> New York , Dec 27 ( PTI ) Migrants applying for asylum in the European Union will nearly triple over the average of the last 15 years by 2100 , if carbon emissions continue on their current path , according to a study . <p> The study , published in the journal Science , suggests that cutting emissions could partially stem the tide , but even under an optimistic scenario , Europe could see asylum applications rise by at least a quarter . <p> " Europe is already conflicted about how many refugees to admit , " said Wolfram Schlenker , an economist at Columbia University in the US . <p> " Though poorer countries in hotter regions are most vulnerable to climate change , our findings highlight the extent to which countries are interlinked , and Europe will see increasing numbers of desperate people fleeing their home countries , " said Schlenker . <p> Researchers compared asylum applications to the EU filed from 103 countries between 2000 and 2014 , with temperature variations in the applicants home countries @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ countrys agricultural region deviated from 20 degrees Celsius during its growing season , the more likely people were to seek refuge abroad . <p> Crops grow best at an average temperature of 20 degrees Celsius , and hotter than normal temperatures increased asylum applications in hotter places , such as Iraq and Pakistan , and lowered them in colder places such as Serbia and Peru , researchers said . <p> Combining the asylum-application data with projections of future warming , they found that an increase of average global temperatures of 1.8 degrees Celsius - an optimistic scenario in which carbon emissions flatten globally in the next few decades and then decline - would increase applications by 28 per cent by 2100 , translating into 98,000 extra applications to the EU each year . <p> If carbon emissions continue on their current trajectory , with global temperatures rising by 2.6 to 4.8 degrees Celsius by 2100 , applications could increase by 188 per cent , leading to an extra 660,000 applications filed each year . <p> The research adds to a growing body of evidence that weather shocks can destabilise societies @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ countries . <p> In a widely-cited 2011 study in the journal Nature , researchers linked modern El Nino drought cycles to increased violence and war globally . <p> More recently , researchers have highlighted the connection between the drying of the Middle East and ongoing conflict there . <p> In a 2015 study in the journal PNAS , researchers made the case that climate change made Syrias 2006-2010 drought two to three times more likely , and that the drought was a catalyst for Syrias 2011 uprising .
