<h> Parrikar says no to expansion of coal handling facility <p> PANAJI : Reiterating his government 's stand as regards " no expansion " of existing coal handling facility at the Mormugao Port Trust , Chief Minister Manohar Parrikar on Thursday said that his earlier government had rejected all the proposals in 2002 for setting up of coal-run power generation plant in Goa , and this stand of the government would continue in the future . <p> The Chief Minister also stated that major quantity of coal is necessary to any state for two purposes namely either running steel units or operating power generating plant . <p> " Goa does not require coal for any of these purposes and therefore , we will not allow any expansion of the existing coal handling facility in the state , " he added . <p> The Parrikar government has already written to the Union ministry of environment , forest and climate change , way back in August 2017 , as regards no expansion desired by his government for the coal handling facility , at the MPT . <p> Presently , two companies , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ already handling coal operations at the MPT.
