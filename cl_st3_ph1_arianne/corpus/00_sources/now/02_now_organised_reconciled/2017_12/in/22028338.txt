C40 has 17 networks organised under 5 initiative areas covering the mitigation , adaptation and sustainability topics of highest priority to C40 cities and with the potential for the greatest climate change impact . C40 networks help cities replicate , improve and accelerate climate action . These city only working groups provide for honest knowledge exchange , enabling cities to tap into the global expertise of their peers as well as connect with technical partners . Through networks , cities find opportunities to undertake joint projects in areas of mutual interest and benefit . C40 networks also amplify individual city solutions by providing a global platform for showcasing city successes . <p> Benefits of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ networks are focused on the needs and priorities of the cities in the network and have a high level of city accountability , they are chaired by the lead city 's mayor and led by appointed city staff . The lead city contact will work closely with the C40 Initiative Head or Network Manager to shape the strategic direction of the network and collaborate with the participating cities to implement the network work plan . <p> The lead city contact should expect to contribute approximately 5% of his/her time to the network , although the commitment will vary throughout the year depending on network events and activities .
