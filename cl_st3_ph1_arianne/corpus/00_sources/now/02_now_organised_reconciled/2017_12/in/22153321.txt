<p> Railway projects passing through forest lands , national parks and eco-sensitive zones will not be exempted from the forest clearance process , the Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change ( MoEF&amp;CC ) told the Railways Ministry and state forest departments . The Environment Ministry 's clarification on the matter comes after the Railways had argued that Railways Act , 1989 , gives it the power to acquire land , including forests , falling in its right of way . MoEF&amp;CC clarified its position on the issue after referring the matter to the Ministry of Law and Justice , who seconded the Environment Ministry 's opinion . <p> DNA has reviewed the MoEF&amp;CC letter to Railways Ministry and state forest departments . <p> The MoEF&amp;CC told Railways , that as per the Law Ministry , despite provisions of the Railways Act , 1989 , allowing Railways to acquire any land for its projects , forest land falling in the right of way attract provisions of the Forest Conservation Act , 1980 .
