Two-wheelers have also allowed more women to commute independently with the advent of gearless scooters and mopeds . Largely ignored in measures to control vehicular pollution , experts say two-wheelers too need to be brought in the ambit of pollution control as they contribute across most factors determining vehicular pollution - engine technology , total number of vehicles , structure and age of vehicles and fuel quality . In fact , in major Indian cities , two-thirds of pollution load is due to two-wheelers . They give out 30% of the particulate matter load , 10 percentage points more than the contribution from cars .
