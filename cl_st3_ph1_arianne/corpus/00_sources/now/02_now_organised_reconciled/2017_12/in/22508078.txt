With unprecedented intensity and frequency , a series of hurricanes laid waste to Caribbean countries , Houston and the Gulf Coast of Texas , and large parts of Florida . In southern Europe , Australia , and the American West , wildfires tore across the countryside , claiming lives and causing extensive property damage . In South America , the Indian subcontinent , and other regions , heat waves , crop failures , and flooding reached crisis levels .
