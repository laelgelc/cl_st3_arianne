<h> Supreme Court eases ban on use of petcoke , furnace oil <p> The bench also exempted the lime stone industries from the purview of the October 24 order in respect of the use of petroleum coke . <p> Supreme Court of India ( Photo : PTI ) <p> New Delhi : The Supreme Court on Wednesday relaxed the notification banning use of petcoke and furnace oil in Uttar Pradesh , Rajasthan and Haryana for industries like cement , lime stone and thermal power plants in Rajasthan , Uttar Pradesh and Haryana . <p> While cement and lime industries use petcoke , thermal power plants use furnace oil . <p> A bench of Justices Madan B. Lokur and Deepak Gupta also issued a direction to the ministry of environment , forest affairs and climate change ( MoEF &amp; CC ) to frame guidelines for regulating the use of petcoke . <p> The bench also exempted the lime stone industries from the purview of the October 24 order in respect of the use of petroleum coke . <p> The Centre had earlier informed the top court that the Central Pollution Control @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ use of petcoke and furnace oil until further orders in the states of Uttar Pradesh , Rajasthan and Haryana . <p> The use of petcoke and furnace oil is already prohibited in Delhi . The top court had on October 24 banned use of petcoke and furnace oil in these three states from November 1 in view of the pollution levels in Delhi-national capital region ( NCR ) and noted that these states had no objection to it . <p> On December 4 , the Centre filed an affidavit pleading for lifting the ban as petcoke and furnace oil are used in negligible quantities by the cement industry and power plants , respectively , and that too for non-fuel purposes . <p> Additional solicitor-general ( ASG ) Tushar Mehta had submitted that the National Thermal Power Corporation ( NTPC ) utilises furnace oil of the amount of a mere 0.03 percent . By virtue of the same , the relaxation of the prohibition with respect to the said industrial units for a definite period of time had been suggested , allowing for the arrangement of an alternative and cleaner source @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for MoEF , told the court that the system of regulation on sale of petcoke would be brought into force within four weeks . <p> On the use of pet coke in lime industries , the bench asked the government to come out with emission standards . <p> Regarding the use of furnace oil in thermal power plants , the amicus told the bench that Ministry of Power has requested allowing the use of furnace oil for a period of one year till the thermal power plants switch over to alternate fuel . <p> The bench accepted timeline given in this regard by the MoEF and said that thermal power plants would have to switch over to alternative fuel within a year . <p> Applications for modification of the said order were filed by industrial units engaged in cement manufacturing , paper manufacturing , malting , production of agro-foods , etc , in addition to the power generation company NTPC and aluminum manufacturer Hindalco Industries Ltd . The Bench relaxed the order in the PIL filed by environmental activist MC Mehta in 1985 on the issue of air pollution .
