<h> How Bangkok plans to tackle traffic jams <p> Tantikom is Chief Resilience Officer of Bangkok , advising the city 's Governor on how to prepare for future challenges . The city has seen " unprecedented change " in the last decade , he says , becoming a major centre of commerce and culture in Asia . It has brought about massive migration of people from surrounding areas of Thailand to the city . <p> But the development has " not been universal " . It has created congested streets , poor air quality , frequent flooding and stressful lifestyles . " Our natural environment and the health of our residents have suffered with the expanding development , " Tantikom says . Bangkok needs to " set out a more deliberate course to ensure our city is resilient " , he adds . <p> Improving public transport <p> Bangkok 's reputation for bad traffic precedes itself .
