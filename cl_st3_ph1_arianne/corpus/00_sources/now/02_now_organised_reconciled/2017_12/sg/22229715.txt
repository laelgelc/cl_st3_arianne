<h> Singapore should step up environmentally friendly efforts <p> Singapore needs a nationwide effort to show that we are serious about environmental change . <p> As a metropolitan country without local crops or a diverse population of animals , Singaporeans naturally feel detached from the idea of " real " climate change . <p> We hear of winters getting shorter , droughts lasting longer , crops not harvesting well and the endangerment of animals , but we do n't experience it . <p> However , in the age of the Internet where information is just a click away , we simply have no excuse to be ignorant . <p> Being an affluent and fast-moving nation , we have come to value convenience over all else . <p> Many food centres still give out plastic packaging and utensils to reduce the number of items to be washed . <p> On our phones , cheap private-hire rides are just a few scrolls away - never mind the carbon emissions . <p> In supermarkets , pristine , bruise-free fruits are packed in plastic to create the idea of cleanliness and premium quality . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the idea that imperfect foods not in plastic packaging are " unclean " . <p> It has been seven years since Penang implemented its " No Plastic Bags " programme . <p> We trail shockingly behind . <p> If Singaporeans are made to pay for their carbon footprint , we will think twice about polluting the environment . <p> Singaporeans should also be shown the impact and consequences of plastic waste and pollution . <p> We should not worry about upsetting people with real images . <p> Change is difficult , and many will complain about it , but our children will thank us for doing our part in protecting their future . <p> We need to put the environment and our future above mere convenience . <p> Tan Shu Shu Ethel ( Miss ) <p> A version of this article appeared in the print edition of The Straits Times on December 15 , 2017 , with the headline ' Singapore should step up environmentally friendly efforts ' .
