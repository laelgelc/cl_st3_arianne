<h> DMO to list green bond for easy trading and liquidity <p> Investors staking their funds in the Federal Government maiden N10.69 billion Green Bond have been assured of liquidity as the Debt Management Office ( DMO ) said the bond would be listed on the Nigerian Stock Exchange ( NSE ) and FMDQ OTC Securities Exchange . <p> Green Bonds are debt instruments tied to environmental projects to address climate change .
