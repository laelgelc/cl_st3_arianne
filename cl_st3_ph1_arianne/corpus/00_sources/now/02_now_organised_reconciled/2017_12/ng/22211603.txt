<h> Buhari goes to One Planet <p> the picture of President Muhammadu Buhari arriving Paris last Monday for the One Planet Summit taking place the next day , December 12 , was a stunner . Because of the cold , he wore a light coloured plain trenchcoat , striding a few feet ahead of his entourage , who were all adorned in different types of dark-coloured anti-cold cladding . With the dark sky diluting the airport lighting to an ominous blue glow , and the Nigerian Air Force jet parked just behind them , Buhari and his men ( and woman ) looked every inch like an extraction crew on a high-priority French mission . <p> Perhaps , Providence manufactured that black humour tableau -- which actually went viral -- for people to appreciate the weight of the assignment that is before the Nigerian delegation to the One Planet Summit . It is not as if Buhari had never attended any climate change conference before . No . But this is the first time a climate change conference is specifically tailored to address issues of climate finance .
