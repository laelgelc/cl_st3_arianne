<h> Lafarge Africa unveils 2030 sustainability plan <p> Lafarge Africa Plc has unveiled its 2030 Plan , with which it intends to tackle the world 's biggest issues for the next decade , set new standards and be the leading example of sustainability . <p> According to the company , the 2030 plan with the theme , ' Building for tomorrow ' , is supportive of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals and consists of the four pillars of climate , circular economy , water and nature , and people and communities . <p> The firm noted that each target would come with a set of quantitative targets such as reducing emissions , deriving energy from waste and developing initiatives that would impact 75 million people across the world . <p> The Director of Communications , Public Affairs and Sustainable Development , Lafarge Africa , Mrs. Folashade Ambrose-Medebem , said sustainability was a core value and business strategy of the company . <p> " It 's part of what we do wherever we operate . Some of the world 's biggest challenges like urbanisation , housing and climate change are @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of providing sustainable solutions to these challenges within and outside our operations , " she explained . <p> The Chief Executive Officer , Lafarge Africa , Michel Pucherchos , said LafargeHolcim , the parent company , was not only the world 's largest building solutions firm , but one with a rich history of sustainability . <p> " This enables us to adapt quickly and proffer sustainable and innovative solutions in markets like Nigeria where the growth potential is immense but must be sustained for future generations , " he added . <p> The company said of late , it had either adopted or sponsored sustainable innovations within and outside its operations , such as the use of alternative fuel and the Lafarge National Literacy Competition . <p> It added that as part of its alternative fuel strategy , it was using local alternative energy sources at its plants across the country . <p> Copyright PUNCH.All rights reserved .
