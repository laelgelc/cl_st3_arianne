<h> FG pledges to restore 4m hectares of degraded land by 2030 <p> The Federal Government has reiterated its determination to reclaim four million hectares of degraded land by 2030 so as to improve agricultural production . <p> Bala Gukut , the Deputy Director , Drought and Desertification Amelioration Department , Federal Ministry of Environment , conveyed the government 's commitment in an interview with News Agency of Nigeria in Abuja on Wednesday . <p> Gukut said that the government intended to achieve land degradation neutrality . <p> He said that land degradation neutrality was a state whereby the amount of healthy and productive land resources , necessary to support ecosystem services , remained stable or increased within specified temporal and spatial scales . <p> He said that the government 's efforts were part of the African Forest Landscape Restoration Initiative ( AFR100 Initiative ) and Bonn Challenge , adding that AFR100 Initiative indicated a continuing drive across Africa to restore the productivity of lands . <p> Gukut said that the Bonn Challenge , on the other hand , was a global effort to restore 150 million hectares of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ million hectares by 2030 . <p> According to him , 26 African nations are now committed to restoring more than 84 million hectares of degraded land under the AFR100 Initiative . <p> He said : " Also , the Bonn Challenge , a global effort , aims at restoring 150 million hectares of land by 2020 and 350 million hectares by 2030 ; spurring climate resilience , job growth and other benefits . <p> " Restoring land is critical to Africa in the face of climate change , as the latest research finds that land restoration holds the greatest potential for storing carbon in any land-based climate solution . <p> " Forest Landscape Restoration is a major priority for Nigeria and Nigeria is happy to be associated with AFR100 Initiative and Bonn Challenge .
