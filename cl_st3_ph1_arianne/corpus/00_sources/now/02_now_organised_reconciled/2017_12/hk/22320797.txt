And yet , while energy giants like Exxon and Shell have drawn fire for their role in fueling climate change , the corporate meat and dairy industries have largely avoided scrutiny . If we are to avert environmental disaster , this double standard must change . <p> To bring attention to this issue , the Institute for Agriculture and Trade Policy , GRAIN , and Germany 's Heinrich B ? ll Foundation recently teamed up to study the " supersized climate footprint " of the global livestock trade .
