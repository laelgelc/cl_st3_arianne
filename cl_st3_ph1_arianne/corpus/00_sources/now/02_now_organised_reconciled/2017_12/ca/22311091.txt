<h> National parks offer free admission for youth in 2018 <h> Free admission to all Parks Canada sites for youth under 17 <p> In 2018 , admission to all Parks Canada sites will be free for youth 17 years of age and under . For a better camping experience , Pukaskwa National Park ( photo ) will offer five new cabin style units with wood stoves , while other national parks will host new events .
