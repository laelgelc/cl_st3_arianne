<h> Public Asked to Report Oiled Birds Off Coast of Fogo Island <p> The federal government is asking the public to report any instances of sheens or oiled birds in the area off the coast of Fogo Island . <p> The advisory comes after Fogo Island resident Barry Payne shot an oiled duck near Pound Rocks off Fogo late last month . <p> The discovery of the oiled bird followed reports of another sheen in the area of the Manolis L , a massive bulk paper carrier which sank in the area back in 1985 . <p> Residents of Fogo Island and Twillingate want a permanent solution to what they see as an environmental ticking time bomb . <p> The Department of Environment and Climate Change has issued an advisory to all bird hunters in the area not to consume oiled bird , and to deliver them to a drop-off location . Birds should be placed in a plastic bag and labeled with the date and location where they were shot . <p> Drop off locations are located throughout the Notre Dame , Bonavista and Trinity Bay areas .
