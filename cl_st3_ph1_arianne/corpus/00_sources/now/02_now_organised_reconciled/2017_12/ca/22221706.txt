<h> Chamber praises Premier Wall <p> Recently , Premier Brad Wall stood in the Legislative Assembly for the last time . After 18 years as the MLA for Swift Current , and the past 10 also as Premier of Saskatchewan , Brad Wall will be remembered as one of the best premiers in Canadian history . <p> Over the course of his term as premier , Wall 's government accomplished a great deal . <p> Instead of being the place to be from , Saskatchewan became the place to be . Our province became the " it " province for investment , jobs and people . Population , GDP and employment growth became the norm , and along with growth came higher government revenues for health care , infrastructure , schools and more . However , most importantly , growth provided the people of the province with a better quality of life , which in most cases included higher household incomes and access to burgeoning opportunities . <p> At the Regina &amp; District Chamber of Commerce , we will miss Premier Wall . <p> We will miss his oration skills @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ his steadfast adherence to the importance of growth not being the end , but rather the means to achieve ends like reducing the doctor and nursing shortages , ensuring that those who are most in need are eliminated from provincial income tax rolls , and the list goes on . We will also miss his tenacity at standing up for the interests of the people of Saskatchewan against all comers . <p> On behalf of the members of the Regina &amp; District Chamber of Commerce , the board of directors and the staff , I want to express our sincere appreciation for your years of service to the people of Saskatchewan , and wish you nothing but success in the future . <p> Thank you , Brad ! <p> John Hopkins , CEO , Regina &amp; District Chamber of Commerce <h> Citizen not fond of Wall <p> Yes , we will remember Brad Wall . <p> He has left us with a huge debt for a carbon capture project that cost $1.5 billion , does n't work and no one else in the world wants .
