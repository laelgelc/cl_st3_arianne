Now , Harris is the alarmist by making absolutely ridiculous and unfounded @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ prediction of massive starvation is unfounded . Crops grew without any problem even before we polluted the atmosphere . Additionally , even if we completely stopped emitting carbon dioxide today , it would take centuries to remove just the CO2 we have already added . There is no threat in the least degree that the atmosphere will ever run out of CO2 . Knowing that , it is easy to see why no one attended the conference in Houston or paid it any attention . <p> Harris ' claims indicate just how desperate the anti-science fossil fuel advocates are becoming . The public is rapidly becoming aware of their tactics and is increasingly convinced that the threat of global warming is real and we need to address it . <p> Dr. Christopher Keating is a professor of physics who does research in planetary geophysics , including climate change .
