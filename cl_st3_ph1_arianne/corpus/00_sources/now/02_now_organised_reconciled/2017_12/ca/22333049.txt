<h> Abacus announces grant of stock options <p> VANCOUVER , British Columbia , Dec. 20 , 2017 ( GLOBE NEWSWIRE ) -- Abacus Mining &amp; Exploration Corporation ( " Abacus " or the " Company " ) ( TSXV:AME ) announces that pursuant to an agreement with an officer , and as provided under the Company 's approved stock option plan and subject to regulatory policies , the Board of Directors has approved the issuance of stock options allowing for the acquisition of up to , in the aggregate , 150,000 shares in the capital stock of the Company at a price of $0.25 per share for a period of five years from the date of grant . Insiders and directors of the Company had been on a trading blackout until receipt of notice from the British Columbia Minister of Environment and Climate Change Strategy and Minister of Energy , Mines and Petroleum Resources on the issuance of an Environmental Assessment Certificate ( " EAC " ) for the Ajax project .
