<p> The whitebark pines cut down by Lake Louise ski area workers are the only endangered tree species in Banff National Park , a court exhibit shows . <p> An agreed statement of facts was filed Thursday to support guilty pleas entered earlier this week by the resort . <p> Lawyer Alain Hepner entered guilty pleas on behalf of the ski area to charges under the Species at Risk Act and the Canada National Parks Act . <p> Under those two acts , the combined maximum fine , per tree , that Lake Louise is facing is $550,000 . <p> Outside court , Hepner said the main issue when sentencing submissions are made next July is the exact number of trees felled . <p> According to the agreed facts , whitebark pines are one of three species listed as endangered in the national park , the others being the Banff Springs snail and little brown myotis bat . <p> " The species is in serious decline due to four primary factors , " says the court exhibit , drafted by Hepner and Crown prosecutor Erin Eacott . The regeneration of the trees is dependent on avalanches and fires , which clear space for the shade intolerant whitebark pines , the exhibit says . <p> The decline rate of the trees , which are found in the Rockies in Alberta and B.C. in Canada , is estimated to be about one per cent annually . That would see the estimated 200 million whitebark pines in Canada reduced by 78 per cent in the next century . <p> " Whitebark pine is a keystone species at the centre of a high-elevation network of plants and animals , enabling increased biodiversity , " the agreed statement says . <p> Keystone species are species " so closely involved with other organisms that if it becomes extinct , or even seriously depleted , the effects will ramify throughout the ecosystem , " says a report by the Committee on the Status of Endangered Wildlife in Canada . <p> " Whitebark pine provides food and habitat for numerous birds and mammals , including bears . <p> " It facilitates the establishment and growth @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and helps regulate snowpack and runoff , providing watershed stability . On Sept. 24 , 2013 , trail crew employees attended Ptarmigan Ridge and cut down flora , including whitebark pines . The resort did not have either a Restricted Activity Permit , which would allow them to cut down any trees , or a Species at Risk Act permit . <p> Evidence as part of the sentencing hearing before provincial court Judge Heather Lamoureux will commence July 9.
