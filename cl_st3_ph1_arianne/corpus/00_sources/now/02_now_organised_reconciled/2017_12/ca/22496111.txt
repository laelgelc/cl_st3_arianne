<h> Arctic freeze curtails New Years Eve events and freezes sharks <p> As climate change ushers in another year of extreme global temperatures , Canada and much of the United States has been hit with below freezing temperatures and heavy snowfall . It has been so cold that even sharks have washed up on beaches in Cape Cod . <p> In Canada , the federal weather agency has issued an extreme cold warning for Sunday morning as another blast of Arctic air moves across the provinces plummeting temperatures well below the seasonal mark . A wind chill of -30 degrees Centigrade ( -22 degrees Fahrenheit ) will make it feel much colder , according to Environment Canada . <p> Needless to say , but quite a few New Years Eve events have been canceled , including many of the events in the New Year 's Eve party on Parliament Hill in downtown Ottawa , which also was going to be the end of Canada 's sesquicentennial celebrations . The five-foot snowfall in Erie , Pennsylvania is now solidly frozen in place , leaving many residents marooned in their homes . <p> Airports in New York City recorded record low temperatures , with the icy conditions resulting in cancellations of flights and many traffic accidents on the roadways .
