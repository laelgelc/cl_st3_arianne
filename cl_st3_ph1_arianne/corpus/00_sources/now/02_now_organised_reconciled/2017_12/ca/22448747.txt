<p> Efforts are currently underway to raise a privately-owned vessel which sank in the frigid waters at Bellevue Marina . <p> A diver was observed at 3 p.m . Wednesday , his head just above a hole in the ice . <p> The road to the marina , situated at 48 Pine Street , was closed as a Canadian Coast Guard crew , Ontario 's Ministry of the Environment and Climate Change ( MOECC ) officials and private contractors worked to raise the vessel . <p> The Canadian Coast Guard was alerted to the sinking of the privately-owned 13.7 metre ( 45-foot ) vessel , known as the Dispatch II , at approximately 1 p.m .
