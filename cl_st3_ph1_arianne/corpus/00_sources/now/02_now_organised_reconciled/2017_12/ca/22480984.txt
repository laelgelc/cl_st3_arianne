<p> With global warming records being broken regularly these days , 2017 is on track to be yet another year of record-setting heat , and with disturbing signs of human-caused climate change like ferocious storms , rising oceans , disintegrating ice shelves , bleached coral reefs and unusually fierce wild fires reported regularly , many Canadians are worried about the Alberta tar sands . <p> Some are concerned that by allowing the unrestricted exploitation of the 165.4 billion barrels of thick , dirty bitumen ( the tar in tar sands ) currently believed to lie under the Athabasca , Cold Lake and Peace River areas of northern Alberta , and by allowing new pipelines to be built to take the heavy oil to tidewater , Canada is complicit in creating a climate change catastrophe . <p> Other Canadians say that someone will continue to supply the world 's addiction to fossil fuel energy and it might as well be a jurisdiction like Canada that imposes some serious environmental controls on resource extraction .
