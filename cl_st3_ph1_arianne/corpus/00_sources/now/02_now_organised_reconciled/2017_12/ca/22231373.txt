<h> Province rejects Ajax mine application <p> The provincial government has rejected the KGHM Ajax mine application , meaning the proposed project dies as it required approval from both the provincial and federal governments . <p> B.C. Environment and Climate Change Strategy Minister George Heyman and Energy , Mines and Petroleum Resources Minister Michelle Mungall decided on Thursday not to issue an environmental assessment certificate to KGHM Ajax Mining Inc. for the Ajax project in Kamloops . <p> The ministers had until Jan. 3 to make a decision .
