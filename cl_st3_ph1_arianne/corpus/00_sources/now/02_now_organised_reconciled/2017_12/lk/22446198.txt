<h> NDB goes paper-free during this festive season <p> 2017-12-28 00:00:35 <p> 0 <p> 90 <p> The holiday season is all about sending greeting cards , good wishes and gifts . However , the impact that these simple gestures have on the environment is profound . During a time where climate change is having a massive impact on Planet Earth , NDB decided to go paper-free during the festive season . The usage of massive amounts of paper is one of the key factors contributing towards climate change . While deforestation is a tremendous issue that contributes towards climate change , paper accounts for nearly 40 percent of the trees cut down each year . Statistics show that a ton of paper costs a total of 17 trees . <p> NDB has always been in the forefront in taking new and improved initiatives in championing environmental friendly activities . In an effort to lessen the burden caused on Earth due to the usage of paper - especially during the holiday season - NDB will be sending e-cards to all clients , staff and others affiliated with the bank .
