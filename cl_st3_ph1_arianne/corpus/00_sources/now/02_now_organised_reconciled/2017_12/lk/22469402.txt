" In that piece , I stuck to generalities and some trends without going into specifics , in a conceptual essay that discussed the possible direction 2017 would take . It did not offer political prognostications , analyses or solutions . In other words , a world of populism and liberalism . I need not go into the details of events of 2017 of which the reader is aware . For decades , the world has been comforted by the Truman Doctrine which is a quintessential foreign policy approach of the United States that started with the announcement in Congress on 12 Marc 1947 by President Harry S. Truman . The announcement , although calculated to counter Soviet geopolitical expansion during the Cold War at that time , was further developed on July 12 , 1948 when President Truman pledged to contain threats to Greece and Turkey . The Truman Doctrine , which effectively changed the foreign policy direction of the United States , was an undertaking by the United States to provide political , military and economic assistance and protection to all democratic nations threatened by external or internal authoritarian forces . This policy has stayed with the United States as well as the rest of the world and has culminated in what Richard Haas , President of the Council on Foreign Relations , in his excellent essay in Foreign Affairs @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ : Hass says : " Such a concept of sovereign obligation , it is worth pointing out , differs from the notion of sovereign responsibility ... It stems from a need to expand and adapt the traditional principles of international order for a highly interconnected world . Sovereign obligation thus retains a respect for borders and an opposition to their being changed through coercion or force " . <p> Senator John McCain 's statement which appears at the commencement of this article , is given a further dimension by Naomi Klein , in her book No is Not Enough : " For decades , elites have been using the power of shock to impose nightmares " .
