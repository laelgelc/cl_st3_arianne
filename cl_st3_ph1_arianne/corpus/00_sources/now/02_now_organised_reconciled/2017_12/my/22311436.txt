<h> Top polluter China unveils nationwide carbon market <p> BEIJING : China , the world 's biggest polluter , unveiled plans on Tuesday for a national carbon market likely to become the world 's largest exchange for emissions credits . <p> China burns more coal than any other country and bears the ignominious title of top greenhouse gas emitter . <p> The dirty fuel generates most of the nation 's power but the country has moved rapidly this winter to limit its use in northern China . <p> The launch of the long-delayed carbon exchange scheme is a sign that China is taking its war on pollution to new levels . <p> The country is the largest investor in renewable energy but has faced an uphill battle transitioning from coal , which is used to generate roughly three-quarters of its power according to the International Energy Agency . <p> " The launch of the Chinese carbon market shows that there is increased commitment around the world to price pollution and direct investments into clean technologies , " said Femke de Jong , policy director at Carbon Market Watch . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ against climate change after the US retreated from the Paris accord struck in 2015 . <p> The emission exchange outlined by officials from China 's National Development and Reform Commission may slowly change the calculus for utilities and other coal-burners . <p> " The purpose of this programme is to reduce greenhouse gas emissions , " said commission vice chairman Zhang Yong . <p> " We aim to reduce emissions through market-based mechanisms . " <p> The immediate focus for establishing the carbon credits exchange is on the power generation industry , Zhang said . <p> The project expands on the lessons learned from seven provincial and city carbon exchanges . <p> " With just emissions trading in the power generation industry , the scale will exceed that of every country in the world , " said Li Gao , an official in the National Development and Reform Commission 's climate change department . -- AFP
