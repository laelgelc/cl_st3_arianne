<h> Call for government , NGOs to help create ' climate change ' awareness <p> KUALA LUMPUR : The government and non-governmental organisations can play a useful role in helping to create greater awareness of climate change , said St John Ambulance Sarawak chairman and commander Datuk Seri Ang Lai Soon . <p> He said it was everyone 's responsibility to help in their own little way and within their own surrounding areas to protect and conserve the exceedingly fragile environment . <p> " Those more able can , of course , do more by helping to educate others on how preserving the environment is very much about their own self-preservation . <p> " We must all be more conscious about leading a more sustainable way of life , and choose only products that are most friendly to the environment and it can be as simple as insisting on paper or reusable bags rather than plastic bags , " said Ang in his New Year message today . <p> He said indiscriminate jungle burning by plantations and by individuals in both rural and urban areas must be stopped , so @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ particularly , did not have to breathe the most polluted air known to man , and damaging human and animal health . <p> Ang said that if each person could plant a tree a year , it could help make urban areas greener and healthier . <p> " Instead of seeing trees being mercilessly cut down and hills flattened in the name of progress and development , we must preserve trees where possible , and the hills as well . <p> " Attempts to create concrete jungles must not be encouraged at all , " he said . -- Bernama
