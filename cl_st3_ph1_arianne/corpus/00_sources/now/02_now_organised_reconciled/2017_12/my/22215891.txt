<p> As world leaders gather in a Paris suburb for the " One Planet Summit , " one artist is showing her support by donning her latest creation . <p> Hong Kong-based artist and environmental activist Kong Ning calls this dress The Little Blue Man , and is meant to symbolise the 190 countries that adhere to the Paris climate agreement . <p> " The dress is made of biodegradable material and I 'll let it disintegrate naturally , because all the materials that we use come from nature and must return to nature , " Hong Kong-based artist and environment activist Kong Ning said . <p> While Kong 's work is a visual symbolisation of the pact , nearby a group of protesters were more blunt about showing their discontent . <p> France announced a raft of 12 non-binding commitments at the summit organised to breathe life back into climate action after US President Donald Trump withdrew the United States from the climate accord . <p> But there was no headline promise likely to reassure developing nations on the sharp end of climate change , that they will be better able to cope . -- Reuters
