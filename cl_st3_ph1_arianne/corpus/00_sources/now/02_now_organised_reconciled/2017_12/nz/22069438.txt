<h> More than 750,000 Kiwis exposed to potentially unsafe drinking water <p> TRACY WATKINS <p> Last updated 18:20 , December 6 2017 <p> Complacency , inept officials -- a Government inquiry paints a frightening picture of the state of New Zealand 's drinking water , with at least 750,000 of us drinking from supplies that are " not demonstrably safe " - a figure described as likely to be a " significant underestimate " . <p> The inquiry was sparked by the 2016 Havelock North gastro outbreak , which has now been linked to four deaths , and calls for a major overhaul of water supplies , including mandatory treatment . <p> The Government has now written urgently to all mayors and district health boards asking to check the water they are supplying meets current standards after the inquiry revealed 20 per cent of water supplies were not up to standard . <p> inthehouseNZ/YouTube <p> David Parker makes his Ministerial Statement about the report in Parliament . <p> That 20 per cent affects 759,000 people , of which 92,000 are at risk of bacterial infection , 681,000 of protozoal infection @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ exposure to chemicals through their water supply . <p> But that figure was likely to understate the problem , as it did not include more than 600,000 people who drink water from self-suppliers or temporary suppliers , or tourists to places like Punakaiki on the West Coast , which is under a permanent " boil water " notice . <p> Mangateretere Stream , which is near one of three bores that supplied Havelock North 's water , was identified as a likely source of contamination . Sheep faeces from a nearby paddock likely entered the stream . <p> The inquiry found that complacency about the state of New Zealand 's drinking water was common , yet the evidence showed that in many cases it was safer to drink tap water overseas than here . <p> But its most damning findings related to the Ministry of Health , which it described as inept and negligent in its oversight of a system in which non compliance with safe standards was high . <p> The risks for contamination of the water supplies were detailed by the inquiry including damaged pipes , a huge @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ proximity of sewerage to drinking water assets , a factor that caused surprise among overseas experts . <p> Other risks were earthquake damage , climate change , disused landfill sites -- as many as 1000 -- and deteriorating infrastructure , a recent example being collapsed asbestos cement pipes in Marton , affecting drinking water supplies .
