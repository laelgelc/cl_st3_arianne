<h> World famous in New Zealand : Gumdiggers Park , Awanui , Northland <p> A bug captured for all time in a blob of kauri gum , at Gumdiggers Park in Northland . <p> WHERE/WHAT IS IT ? <p> Behind a fence hung with gumboots , just 20 minutes north of Kaitaia , lie the remnants of the country 's biggest gumfield . <p> There 's a 30-minute self-guided loop track to follow through some of the park 's 26 hectares of passionately preserved history .
