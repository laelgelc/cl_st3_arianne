<h> Rainfall data shows Auckland locked in a decade of dry , hot summers <p> Scientists believe that the summers in Auckland will be increasingly drought prone . <p> Despite the occasional days of relief , this summer in Auckland is tracking to fit within a decade-long run of dry summer months . <p> More than 46 years of regional data collected by University of Auckland climate change scientist Anthony Fowler showed a clear trend of drier summer months in the last decade . <p> Compared to the overall data from 46 years , the last 10 summers have seen less rain than usual . Seven of those 10 years have been dry compared to the average , with two or three of these considered very dry . <p> This long period of dry weather fitted within projected climate change scenarios , said Fowler . <p> " Water managers should be alert to what this might mean for the future .
