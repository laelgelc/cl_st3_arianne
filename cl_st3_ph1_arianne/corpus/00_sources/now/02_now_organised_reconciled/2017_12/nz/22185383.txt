<p> Ian Taylor looks at the possibilities.I note that Dunedin City councillor Lee Vandervis has voiced his concerns about the harbourside development and its potential cost to ratepayers . Before this turns into the black and white debate that always seems to arise , with solid lines being drawn between people holding differing views , could I just say that Lee is right to raise these issues . It is important that we debate this freely and be sure in ourselves that it is not the " frivolity " that Lee currently views it as . I have asked people to view this opportunity and simply ask the question : " What if ... ? " <p> One of the questions that has to be asked is : What if doing this meant we handed our grandchildren a debt that was impossible to sustain ? It 's a question that must be asked and answered satisfactorily before we proceed . But also , alongside that question there are many others .
