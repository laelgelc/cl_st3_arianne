<h> Fonterra to divest all its coal interests by 2025 <p> A coal-powered boiler at Fonterra 's Clandeboye site , near Timaru . Picture/Supplied . <p> Fonterra , one of New Zealand 's biggest coal users , plans to divest all its coal interests by 2025 . <p> The co-operative has a direct investment in coal mining through its wholly-owned subsidiary , Glencoal Energy , which has mines in the Waikato . <p> " With our raised ambition in climate change , we have committed to divesting any coal mining interest by 2025 , " Fonterra said in its first sustainability report . <p> Glencoal has historically provided coal for local Fonterra factories . <p> The company 's last mine , Kopako 3 , finished producing for Fonterra 's sites last season , and is now in the process of being rehabilitated . <p> A new site at Mangatangi , close to the existing mine , was granted resource consent in 2013 . <p> Most of the site has
