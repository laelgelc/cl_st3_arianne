<h> ' ' But in the grand scheme of things , our Earth is too hot and it 's getting even hotter . ' ' <p> Liam Gallagher has narrated an " alternative Christmas advert " from the Climate Coalition as part of their ' Show The Love ' campaign . <p> The advert depicts a snowman melting , showcasing the effects of climate change . <p> " It 's winter , and it 's cold this time of year is n't it ? " Gallagher says in the clip . <p> " Maybe you want to snuggle up with a hot chocolate or wrap up warm and wear your biggest coat . But in the grand scheme of things , our Earth is too hot , and it 's getting even hotter . " <p> The short film was produced by famed photographer Rankin and animated by Jelly London . Rankin says : " We were over the moon to have the iconic voice of Liam to give the piece a twist and really get it noticed . "
