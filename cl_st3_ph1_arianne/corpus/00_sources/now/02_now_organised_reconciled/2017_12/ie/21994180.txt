<h> How is the tech diversity pledge doing , 12 months later ? <p> A number of US tech multinationals signed on to the White House tech inclusion pledge in the summer of 2016 . How is it doing now ? <p> In the summer of 2016 , 30 tech companies signed a White House tech inclusion pledge in a bid to " fuel American innovation and economic growth by increasing the diversity of the technology workforce " . <p> It was essentially non-binding , but the willingness of tech behemoths -- such as Medium , Pinterest , Spotify and GitHub -- to agree to , among other things , " annually publish data and progress metrics on the diversity of each company 's technology workforce " and to " implement and publish company-specific goals to recruit , retain and advance diverse technology talent " was extremely heartening . <p> Since this point , the number of companies that have gotten on board with the pledge has ballooned to almost 80 . <h> Dearth of diversity <p> The tech industry has a diversity issue , something that is so readily @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ observation and morphed into a tautology .
