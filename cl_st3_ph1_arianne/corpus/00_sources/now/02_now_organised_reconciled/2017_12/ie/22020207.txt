<h> Could you bring yourself to eat seal meat ? <p> Whether they 're flippering about and playing in the water , honking happily or doing tricks for a fish supper , it 's hard to argue with the majestic loveliness of seals . <p> The slippery sea-dwelling mammals live in both arctic and temperate oceans , and provide food for sharks , polar bears and killer whales , amongst others .
