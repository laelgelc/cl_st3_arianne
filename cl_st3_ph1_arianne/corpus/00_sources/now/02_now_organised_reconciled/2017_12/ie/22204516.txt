Globally , the species is thought to have declined by about 40 per cent since the 1970s , leading to its reclassification as " vulnerable " on the IUCN Red List . <p> Although there is no national seabird monitoring scheme in Ireland , some colonies are monitored annually by BWI staff , committed volunteers and National Parks &amp; Wildlife Service staff .
