As a nation we have accepted the need articulated by the IPCC panel of experts that we must cut anthropogenic CO2 emissions to avoid devastating the planet by temperature induced climate change . <p> Anthropogenic means that which is caused by human activity . The calculus is plain ; the totality of CO2 emissions is the product of the CO2 emission intensity of each activity @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> People of course are you and me . We have the option to curb our CO2 emissions . It 's called taking personal responsibility . <p> This demands sacrifices by each and every one of us , and the question is whether we are prepared to make these in the interests of the collective sustainability of the planet . Some examples , however unpalatable , include : <p> Smaller families reducing the amount of human activity ; <p> Cooking with local produce to reduce transport emissions ; <p> Holidays at home to avoid aircraft emissions on foreign holidays ; <p> Ceasing the use of smart phones and tablets ( data centre emissions ) . <p> The rise of Irish CO2 emissions by 3.5 percent over the past year have been attributed by the Environmental Protection Agency ( EPA ) to increased activity in the dairy and energy industries and the transport sector . This is a direct consequence of the Irish CO2 mitigation strategy to date of focussing all effort into increasing wind generation and largely ignoring all other factors . <p> It is the easy option , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ profit . The profit is guaranteed by the Renewable Energy Feed-In Tariff ( REFIT ) which subsidises the wind farm for a 15 year contract paid out of the PSO Levy on each electricity bill . But electricity generation ( the energy industry ) only accounts for 19.6 per cent of our CO2 emissions . <p> Even if all Irish electricity was to be generated by wind farms , we still have to tackle the remaining 80 per cent of CO2 emissions from other human activities . And that does not take into account that the wind does not always blow , and when it does it may not coincide with electricity demand . <p> We must take personal responsibility , and we must do so right now . The alternative is to pass the legacy of unsustainable living to the next generation . The issue is too important to allow it to be trivialised by nuclear and big oil conspiracy theories , or subsidy-milking commercial entities .
