<h> Artists represent Creative Cities Council <h> Artists represent Creative Cities Council <p> ARTIST representation , as well as plans to make the annual flower festival attune to culture and creativity is being planned by the newly formed Creative Cities Council . <p> Department of Tourism -- Cordillera Director Marie Venus Tan with University of the Philippines Baguio Chancellor Raymundo Rovillos convened the first technical working group meeting gathering artists and artisans to rally the implementation of an action plan for the next four years . <p> Plans include a unified creative cities arts festival , hosting international events and a souvenir redesign project , as well as creating a line of wearable items that will represent the city . <p> The creative economy of the city is also being studied , with plans to measure the economic contribution of the creative industries under arts and crafts and set an agenda for growth . <p> The group was facilitated to choose sectoral representatives , which include Laida Lim for the weaving industry , which includes cloth and basketry ;
