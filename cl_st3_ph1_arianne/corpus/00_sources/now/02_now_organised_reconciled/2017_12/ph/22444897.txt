Edd Gumban <p> BOCAUE , Bulacan , Philippines -- Sparklers , " fountains " and other pyrotechnics can still be lit at home to greet the New Year . <p> The Philippine Fireworks Association ( PFA ) is clarifying misconceptions in the interpretation of the provisions of Executive Order 28 signed by President Duterte to regulate the use of firecrackers and other pyrotechnics . <p> The PFA , headed by Joven Ong , said EO 28 allows consumer pyrotechnics like fountains , sparklers , luces , aerial display cakes or devices , etc. to be used outside a citizen 's residence . <p> Ong noted that " only firecrackers like labintador or kwitis would be used in designated areas for firecrackers identified by local government units ( LGUs ) .
