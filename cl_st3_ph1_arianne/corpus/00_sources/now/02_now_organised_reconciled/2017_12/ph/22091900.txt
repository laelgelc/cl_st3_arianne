<h> Consumer group calls for fair and equitable new taxes <p> A consumer group is urging the bicameral conference committee tasked to scrutinize the proposed Tax Reform for Acceleration and Inclusion ( TRAIN ) to further conduct in-depth study and look into the economic impact of the revenue-generating measure before passing it . <p> In a letter to Davao City Rep. Karlo Alexei Nograles , chairman of the House Committee on Appropriations dated December 5 , Alyansa ng mga Grupong Haligi ng Agham at Teknolohiya para sa Mamamayan , Inc. or AGHAM ( Filipino for " Science " ) reminded the bicameral panel to ensure that new taxes that will be imposed should be " fair and equitable . " <p> " AGHAM believes the Honorable Bicameral Conference Committee should conduct further research and verify the economic impact of the proposed tax reform before the proposed TRAIN tax reform is passed , " AGHAM President Angelo B. Palmones said in his two-page letter to Nograles . <p> " The consumer group holds that the imposition of drastic increase in taxes should be fair and equitable to the taxpayers , " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Senate . <p> Palmones , a former congressman , expressed concern that all the members of AGHAM , who are consumers of electricity " will be directly affected financially " by the passage of Senate Bill No. 1592 and House Bill No. 5636 , also known as the TRAIN . <p> " Particularly , in the Senate Bill 1592 , while intended to be a revenue acceleration measure , it is actually replete with taxation rates and provisions that seem to be surreptitious insertions without proper and legal basis , " he said . <p> The group noted that the proposal for the amendment of the excise tax imposed on coal as a " mineral and mineral product(s) , " imposing a nearly 3,000% hike in three tranches from 2018 to 2020 , is " overtly excessive .
