<h> Making R&amp;D available to all <h> Making R&amp;D available to all <p> DAVAO . The once leading export industry in Mindanao is now threatened and may lose all its markets within two to five years , as the banana industry has been clobbered by different issues that include disease and pestilence . ( Stella A. Estremera ) <p> IT 'S the first approved project proposal in the Philippines under the Department of Science and Technology 's Collaborative Research and Development to Leverage Philippine Economy ( Cradle ) Program , and it intends to break the walls that have kept players of the banana industry confined to their own individual fields , which could be one of the factors that contributed to the decline of this giant industry . <p> Fact : Studies made about the diseases that plague the banana industry in the Philippines have been treated as top secret by the industry player that has conducted it . These were never shared . <p> Consequently , the growers could not access these , and now suffer the consequences of the absence of science research and development as it @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ No. 2 producer in the world and the main source of bananas of quality-conscious prime Japan market , the Philippines is now at No. 6 and is fast losing the Japan market , among many other markets , to Ecuador . <p> Thus , the DOST and the academe welcomed the initiative to bring about a technology that will help the growers immediately identify threats of Sigatoka Disease and the Bunchy Top virus as proposed by the Hijo Resources Corporation . <p> As explained by DOST , Cradle is a program that seeks to " create a synergistic relationship between the academe as producer of knowledge and manpower , and the industry as the entity which translates technologies to real world applications to generate new opportunities for Filipinos in the form of new industries , enterprises , jobs and solutions to pressing community and national problems . <p> Under the Cradle Program , the private sector industry will identify the problem ; and the HEI or RDI will undertake the research and development , with funding from DOST . " <p> Hijo has proposed to " Synergize Academe-Industry Research Undertakings @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ System " . <p> What this means is that it will partner with the University of Southeastern Philippines ( Usep ) for a research to develop an early warning system for banana diseases Sigatoka and Bunchy Top . <p> The project , the first under Cradle , will be getting a P5-million fund from DOST and has a duration of two years . <p> As the implementing agency , Usep commits to come up with one publication on banana diseases early warning system , one disease visualization application software ( DVA ) , one IEC material using the DVA , and a patent for the DVA , said Val A. Quimno , the project leader . <p> " This is an opportunity to reinvent the banana industry , " said Hijo Resources CEO Rosanna Tuason Fores in an earlier interview , as this will see the collaboration of the industry and the academe with the DOST , with the ultimate goal of benefitting the banana farmers . <p> Fores said that the technology to be developed will be made accessible to all . <p> " I 'm willing to share @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ should be developed as one whole industry , " she said . <p> This is contrary to how the banana industry has been investing in its research and development ( R&amp;D ) . <p> " ( Banana companies ) do not give out any data . They keep these as a company secret , " said Dr. Gilbert A. Importante , Usep Research Director . <p> But he is not surprised that Hijo Resources has initiated this and intends to make this accessible to the public since the company has a history of investing on R&amp;D and making these available to all . <p> " DOST has partnered with them since it was the Twin River Research Center , Hijo was well known for that , " Importante recalled . It was in this Research Center where tissue culture of bananas was discovered and developed . While before , banana companies used the suckers for replanting after the bananas are cut down for harvest . When tissue culture was developed , all plantations used this . <p> In the memorandum of agreement signing and inception meeting for the project held @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ House in Madaum , Tagum City , Fores stressed the importance of collaboration to move the industry forward . <p> Importante said that the University of the Philippines in Los Banos and the Tokyo University have indicated their interest to become part of the research development , thus he looks forward to a longer partnership with Hijo . <p> There for the signing of the MOA last December 1 aside from Fores , Importante , and Quimno were Armela K. Razo , DOST 's chief of Special Projects Division , Dr. Danilo Pacoy , vice president of Usep , and Rodolfo C. Ilao , the Director of the Agricultural Resources Management Research Division of the Philippine Council for Agriculture , Aquatic and Natural Resources Research and Development , and DOST 11 Assistant Regional Director Elsie Mae Solidum . <p> The Pilipino Banana Growers and Exporters Association ( PBGEA ) had earlier warned that the banana industry can disappear in two to five years if government will not come to its rescue . <p> The banana industry identify the factors that are contributing to the decline of this once robust industry @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ; disease and pestilence ; climate change ; speed and interconnectivity of markets , and inconsistencies of government policies . <p> But industry players are saying that disease and pestilence are the ones driving away the market as quality standards are no longer met . <p> The industry suffered a major blow in 2012 after the typhoon Pablo hit and deluged thousands of hectares of banana plantations , and in the process spread diseases from farms that have been isolated because of Sigatoka , bunchy top , and the most dreaded fusarium wilt or Panama disease . <p> Those who attended the MOA signing are hopeful that the research can soon extend to fusarium wilt , which wiped out Panama 's banana industry in the 1950s . <p> Latest issues of SunStar Davao also available on your mobile phones , laptops , and tablets .
