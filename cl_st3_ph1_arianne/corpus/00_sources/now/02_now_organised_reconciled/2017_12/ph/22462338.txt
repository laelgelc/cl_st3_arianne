<h> Firecracker-free new year <p> As we have traditionally celebrated many of our new year 's eves past , we look forward to ending the year 2017 in much lesser noise and smoggy air . This we hope to see as a direct result of the implementation of Executive Order ( EO ) 28 that President Rodrigo Duterte issued in a bid to implement a nationwide ban on the use of deadly firecrackers . We have seen so much loss of limbs , if not lives of Filipinos due to the use of deadly firecrackers while celebrating New Year 's eve . <p> President Duterte signed EO 28 in June of this year , regulating the use of firecrackers and other pyrotechnic devices and limiting " the use of firecrackers to community fireworks display authorized by local governments " to minimize the risk of injuries and casualties . It took a long while before President Duterte came up with a firecracker ban amid strong resistance of the affected fireworks and pyrotechnics industry , mostly those located in Bulacan . <p> The President cited how he successfully implemented the firecrackers ban @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . While taking into account the potential dislocation of the local fireworks and pyrotechnics industry sector , President Duterte ordered concerned government agencies to look into how the government finds common grounds that would not stop Filipinos from celebrating New Year with the usual bang but that would not cause injury nor death to revelers . <p> Hence , it took almost a year before EO 28 came out with a " happy new year compromise " to all concerned . <p> More than an accident-free celebration of the new year , a staunch advocate for climate change warned about the adverse effects of the chemical haze and air pollution caused by firecrackers and pyrotechnics . <p> May I share a statement of support to EO 28 that was sent to us by former Senator Heherson Alvarez who also served once as Secretary of the Department of Environment and Natural Resources ( DENR ) and the first Secretary of the Climate Change Commission during the administration of former president Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo .
