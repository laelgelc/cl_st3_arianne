<h> Classes cancelled in parts of Laoag for ASEAN forum <p> LAOAG CITY -- As part of an intensified security protocol , classes were cancelled in parts of Laoag where delegates of the Association of South East Asian Nations ( ASEAN ) are expected to pass by along the Laoag International Airport road to Gabu and Paoay . <p> Thru an Executive Order signed by Ilocos Norte Governor Ma . Imelda Josefa Marcos on December 3 , she ordered the cancellation of classes for Balacad Elementary School and the Balatong Elementary school , including pre-schools along the airport road on Dec. 4 and 8 . <p> Motorists passing through established checkpoints within this area are also advised to bring with them pertinent documents such as driver 's license , OR/CR , etc. for security purposes . <p> Around 150 ASEAN delegates are in the province this week to attend a forum on urban resilience to climate change and disaster risk reduction and management strategies . <p> The delegates started to arrive at the Laoag airport on Sunday evening and today , December 4 .
