<p> Kenyan farmers will benefit from Sh64 billion funding to develop new crops that can withstand harsh weather and resist attacks from pests and diseases . <p> The commitment , made by the Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation and the European Union , will be spread over three years . <p> Gates Foundation is to donate Sh31 billion to complement various projects funded by the European Commission worth Sh33 billion . <p> The funds will also support research to help farmers in Africa and Asia better adapt to rising temperatures , extreme weather patterns , diseases , poor soil fertility and attacks from crop pests . <p> " The disproportionate impact of climate change on the world 's poorest people means that there is a more urgent need to help the farmers improve their productivity in the increasingly tough conditions that they continue to face , " Bill Gates said . He is the co-chair of the Bill &amp; Melinda Gates Foundation . <p> Gates spoke on Tuesday during the One Planet Summit in Paris to mark the second anniversary of the Paris climate accord .
