All of our economic indicators were headed in the correct direction , and we had secured a reduction in the primary surplus in order to increase capital expenditure in growth , inducing projects in road infrastructure , agriculture , housing , climate-change adaptation and initiatives to boost competitiveness . That process would no doubt have continued had the People 's National Party formed the Government in 2016 and beyond . <p> Since then , we have transitioned a new government and to a new programme .
