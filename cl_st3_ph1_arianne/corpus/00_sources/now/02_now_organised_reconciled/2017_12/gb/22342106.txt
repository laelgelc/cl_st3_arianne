<h> MP arranges public meeting over flood defence fears <p> AN MP has arranged a public meeting in response concerns from residents about a lack of flood defences in Clementhorpe . <p> Rachael Maskell , MP for York Central , says that since the 2015 floods residents in the area have been pressing for flood defences , with homes in River Street , Collenso Street , Lower Darnborough Street and Lower Ebor Street at risk . <p> Ms Maskell said : " I have been contacted by a number of residents from the Clementhorpe area who tell me that , two years on from the Boxing Day floods of 2015 , there are still no flood defences in place . It is no surprise to learn that the residents are extremely worried about this . <p> " The residents were told that flood protection would be in place by this winter and it is unacceptable to learn that it could be another year before the flood defences are ready . <p> " If we were to have another flood in the meantime hundreds of homes could be affected .
