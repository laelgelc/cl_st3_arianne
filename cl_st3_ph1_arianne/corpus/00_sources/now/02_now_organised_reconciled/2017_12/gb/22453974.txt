<p> Each day increasingly dangerous hurricanes , wildfires , and floods betray the influence of climate change . We are appalled at the accruing losses of life and property . The arguments to address climate change at the recent UN climate conference in Bonn focused most often on these more concrete risks . However , the worst effects of climate change will come not from severe weather but from the irreversible loss of species and ecosystems . <p> Moulded over millions of years by natural selection , the diversity of species on Earth does more than just inspire awe .
