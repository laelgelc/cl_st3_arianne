<p> Thousands of animal species are at critical risk of going extinct due to unsustainable farming and fishing methods and climate change , a conservation group has warned as it released the latest red list of endangered species . <p> In a rare piece of good news , the International Union for Conservation of Nature IUCN praised New Zealand for its success in turning around the fortunes of two species of kiwi , prompting it to upgrade them from endangered to vulnerable . <p> The struggle for survival among at-risk animals -- and now crops -- has intensified as a result of rising human populations , economic development and drastic changes in the natural environment caused by global warming , the IUCN said . <p> Craig Hilton-Taylor , who heads the group 's red list unit , said species were going extinct at a faster rate than at any time in human history . <p> But he drew encouragement from New Zealand 's example .
