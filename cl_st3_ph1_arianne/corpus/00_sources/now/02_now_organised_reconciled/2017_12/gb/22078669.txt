<p> Ocean waves in the sky above Granary Square , flamingos flying through Chinatown , singing see-saws in South Molton Street , Westminster Abbey bathed in painterly colours and a recreation of the aurora borealis in Grosvenor Square are just a few of the art installations planned for the Lumiere Festival , an event commissioned by the Mayor of London that will turn nocturnal , mid-January London from dreary darkness into a vibrant spectacle of light , colour and sound . <p> Building on the success of the first Lumiere London in 2016 -- which attracted 1.3 million visitors -- the four-day event takes place from January 18 . Forty specially-commissioned installations designed by artists from all over Europe will light up buildings and public spaces across the city , from King 's Cross to the Southbank , and the West End to Waterloo , from 5.30pm-10.30pm each night . " It 's like a theatre show : five hours of drama . Curtain up , curtain down , " says the festival 's organiser , the formidable Helen Marriage .
