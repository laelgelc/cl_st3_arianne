<h> Luyolo Mngonyama represents SA in Miss Africa Calabar <p> Miss South Africa 2016 top 12 finalist , Luyolo Mngonyama of Mthatha , Eastern Cape is at Nigeria to participate in Miss Africa Calabar 2017 , representing South Africa . <p> Miss Africa Calabar is a beauty pageant which seeks to promote green economy whilst merging beauty and nature . Thirsty ladies between the ages of 18-25 years representing 40 African countries are set to compete , eventual winner will be the Face of the Christmas Festival/Carnival Calabar brand as well as the Cross River State Tourism Brand Ambassador on climate change and the Destination Cross River , Africa 's warmest welcome brand . <p> Luyolo Mngonyama representing South Africa . Picture : Supplied . <p> The main objectives of the pageant is to reshape climate change and human civilization . <p> " How we respond will determine the future of human race . Our food system , our economies , our cities and communities are all adapting to the climate change globally .
