<h> Canada boosts protection for endangered whales <p> AFP <p> A Beluga whale plays with a tourist at an aquarium <h> The Canadian government said Wednesday it will boost protection for two species of endangered whales . <p> Since June , 12 North Atlantic right whales have been found dead in the Gulf of St. Lawrence , which is home to nearly a quarter of the last 458 specimens of this kind of whale . Canadian environmentalists say it is among the most threatened in the world . <p> The Ministry of Fisheries said it would set up " critical habitat " protection areas for these whales off the coast of Nova Scotia and for beluga whales in the Gulf of St. Lawrence . <p> The goal is to safeguard " specific geographic locations and conditions essential for the survival and recovery of the species , such as where they give birth , hatch , feed or raise their young , " the ministry said . <p> The government is especially worried about the North Atlantic right whales because their mortality rate in 2017 has been higher than the average @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ death after what were likely collisions with ships or getting caught up in fishing nets . <p> Fisheries Minister Dominique LeBlanc wants officials to update identified critical habitats because of " changing population distributions as a result of climate change . " <p> The government already took measures last year to protect beluga whales and white sturgeon . It denied TransCanada permission to build an oil facility on the coast of the Gulf of St. Lawrence , where female belugas give birth in the spring .
