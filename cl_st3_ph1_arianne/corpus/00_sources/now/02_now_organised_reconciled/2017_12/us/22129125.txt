<h> John Andersen : The good and bad of Christmas music <p> I simply can not abide the Christmas song " A Grown Up Christmas List " . If you do n't know the whole song I will give you the best known part : " No more lives torn apart , then wars would never start , and time would heal all hearts . And every one would have a friend , and right would always win , and love would never end , this is my grown-up Christmas list " composed by David Foster ( music ) and Linda Thompson-Jenner ( lyrics ) in 1990 . Yes I give it 10 thumbs down , the razz berry , a Bah Humbug and a stake of Christmas holly through the heart of the sheet music . <p> The two singers most associated with " A Grown Up Christmas List " are Amy Grant and Kelly Clarkson . It presents wishes that are so vague and impossible to achieve that it defies life as it has existed since the cave men walked the earth with or without dinosaurs , take your pick . Yes I know every piece of musical literature can not be Beethoven 's " Ode to Joy , " but come on . Since man learned to whistle , we can do better than " A Grown Up Christmas List " <p> " A Grown Up Christmas List " is just one song that comes along that you just know is written to make some money and pull at people 's heart strings .
