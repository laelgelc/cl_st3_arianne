<h> Time to limit number of Outside hunters <p> It has come to my attention that a growing number of nonresident big game hunters are coming to Alaska . Our renewable resources are getting more and more precious and sought after by many who cherish the Alaska outdoors experience , and the strain is beginning to show on Alaskans . Whether a hunter be rural or urban , we should look at the growing number of nonresident hunters . From 2015 to 2016 , the number grew from 14,985 to more than 20,000 . <p> I admit that I -- a Class A assistant guide -- am part of the guiding industry , so I have a vested interest in the health of our animals we hunt . But I also am a Koyukon from the Upper Koyukuk River . I have hunted , fished and trapped most of my life , and have the same vested interest as the guiding industry .
