But a Swedish electric car startup argues that in the context of modern city life , the entire idea of a car needs more of a reinvention . <p> advertisement <p> Uniti , which launched a prototype of its tiny electric car today , started by rethinking size : If most people living in cities commute to work alone , over short distances , it does n't make sense to make an oversized , overpowered vehicle . <p> Lewis Horne Photo : Uniti " The shift to electric cars is a positive one , " Lewis Horne , Uniti CEO and founder , tells Fast Company .
