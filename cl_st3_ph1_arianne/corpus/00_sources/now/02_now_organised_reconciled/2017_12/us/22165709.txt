But a new study predicts that a changing climate will weaken winds that blow across much of the Northern hemisphere , possibly leading to big drops in clean wind energy . <p> That 's because the temperature difference between the North Pole and the equator , which drives atmospheric energy in the form of winds and storm systems , is shrinking as the Arctic warms . A warmer Arctic means less of a temperature difference and therefore weaker winds across the central United States , the United Kingdom , the northern Middle East , and parts of Asia .
