<p> Video footage captured in Canada 's Arctic region showing a starving wild polar bear roaming around dry land looking for food has offered a devastating glimpse of the impact climate change is having on polar bears in the region . The video has gone viral since it was posted on social media by photographer Paul Nicklen . <p> The scene was recorded by the conservation group named " Sea Legacy " during a late summer expedition in Baffin Island , Canada .
