<h> Bozeman should invest in future of electric cars <p> A new city report shows that transportation is now Bozeman 's largest source of greenhouse gas emissions . Transportation accounts for 42 percent of emissions in Bozeman and 27 percent of emissions nationally . After a devastating wildfire season , it is clear that we need to address the issue of climate change . <p> Electrifying our transportation is a key component in the solution . A 2017 Chevy Bolt charged on Bozeman 's grid has one-third the lifetime emissions of a gasoline vehicle . Electric vehicle ( EV ) charging infrastructure is essential in making EVs viable and Bozeman desperately needs more of it . <p> Advertisement <p> Smart planning and investment now will save the city and taxpayers money opposed to waiting until EVs proliferate . There are over half a million EVs on the roads in the U.S. , but as of October 2017 , only 475 registered in Montana . Gallatin County had 48 EVs , ranking behind Missoula and Flathead counties .
