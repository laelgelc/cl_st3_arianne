<h> U.S. pulls out of a global pact on the treatment of migrants . Here 's why <p> The U.S. is stepping away from a U.N . agreement aimed at addressing the needs of migrants , saying it could " undermine the sovereign right of the United States to enforce our immigration laws and secure our borders , " according to the State Department . <p> The U.S. joined the global compact on migration in September 2016 under then-President Barack Obama . Under that declaration , countries aim to " protect the human rights of all refugees and migrants , regardless of status . " <p> About 65.6 million people have been displaced worldwide , the highest number of displaced people since World War II , because of conflict and other hardships . Of them , about 22.5 million are refugees , according to the U.N . refugee agency . <p> The nearly 200 participating countries -- now minus the United States -- are negotiating a comprehensive approach to helping migrants . The final framework is slated for adoption next year .
