This is n't a " best of " list for 2017 , but rather the things I read that stayed with me and that I 'd recommend for gift giving or reading yourself if you have n't yet . <p> Topping my personal list is " Threadbare " by Mary Kudenov , a collection of previously published essays that form a memoir about life in the part of Alaska one does n't find on TV shows or in promotional material for tourism . <p> Kudenov was born to an alcoholic mother and grew up poor in Haines and Moose Pass . As an adult she washed up in Anchorage where she lived in one of that city 's roughest neighborhoods . The fate of the two ships and 129 men lost in a futile effort at discovering the Northwest Passage after leaving Britain in 1845 has obsessed thousands and launched endless searches -- both public and private -- that finally culminated in 2014 and 2016 respectively when the sunken Erebus and Terror were located . <p> Paul Watson 's " Ice Ghosts " details the early searches for the ships and then jumps to their discovery , noting that after more than a century-and-a-half of looking in the wrong place , the ships were found precisely where Inuit oral history had said all along they were . <p> " Finding Franklin " by Russel Potter covers similar territory adding accounts of searches by Americans and Canadians that Watson skips @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the vexing questions it raises .
