<h> Korane threatens lawsuit against KenGen for damage caused by dams <p> Garissa county has threatened to sue KenGen for compensation for residents whose property was destroyed by flooding caused by the firm 's overflowing dams . <p> Governor Ali Korane on Saturday said it was wrong for KenGen and other government agencies to release excess water from their dams without a care for residents ' livelihoods . <p> Last week Masinga Dam , Kenya 's largest hydropower dam , overflowed after it reached full capacity . The Kenya Electricity Generating Company early last week warned that the dam was nearing full capacity and would overflow anytime before Friday . <p> The dam started overflowing on Wednesday evening . The company estimated that the water would reach low lying areas in three or four days . <p> " We are glad that most residents heeded an earlier warning by authorities and moved to safer grounds to avoid possible disasters , " managing director and CEO Rebecca Miano said . <p> Korane last month threatened to take KenGen to court for causing loss of livelihoods , lives and property when it released huge volumes of water from its dams . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the upstream dams need to be decommissioned immediately . <p> On Saturday he signed four Bills , two of which will help the country fight perennial droughts and climate change . <p> The Garissa County Environmental Management and Coordination Act regulates the use of resources , settlement and environment management in general .
