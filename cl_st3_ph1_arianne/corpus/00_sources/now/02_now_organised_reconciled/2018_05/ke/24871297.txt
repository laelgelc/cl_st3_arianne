<h> The real import of Uhuru and Raila handshake <p> Unbeknown to some , the context to which our politics operate is defined in large measure by events that occurred far back in our history . <p> The present situation is the product of a series of events in our past . However , not all events in our history have equal significance at any given time . The significance of a certain period of history in current affairs raises its head when certain things occur . <p> The current rapprochement between the two scions of the leading political families in Kenya is of significance because it attempts to bridge a rift dating back to the Jaramogi Oginga Odinga-Jomo Kenyatta disagreements of the early post-independence years . What was initially a fall-out between allies quickly degenerated into tribal mistrust and hatred that has remained long after the two protagonists left the scene . <p> The ground-shaking disagreement has reverberated across time to infect our politics and social relations , and in the recent past , our business relations . Recent calls for cessation and the boycott of certain brands and products in some regions highlight the gravity of the problem .
