<h> Lupita 's virtual reality film puts remote Samburu in global spotlight <p> Elephant conservation efforts by a community in Kenya 's Samburu County is expected to bask in global spotlight after a virtual reality film about it was released worldwide on Monday . <p> Narrated by Kenyan-born film star Lupita Nyong'o , the film , " My Africa " lets viewers experience life in Reteti Elephant Sanctuary , which rescues abandoned or orphaned baby elephants before returning them to the wild when they are strong enough to live on their own . <p> The sanctuary is the first elephant orphanage in Africa owned and operated by the local community in a region where conservation has traditionally been pursued by wealthy outsiders . <p> The film has so far attracted attention after it was shortlisted for screening at the prestigious Tribeca Film Festival in New York from April 18 to 29 . <p> The annual festival attracts millions of visitors and showcases compelling but relatively unknown film talent or stories to global audiences . <p> The film , produced for global environment NGO Conservation International which supports the elephant sanctuary , is available for viewing online in seven languages @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Spanish and Swahili . <p> The NGO has a regional office in Nairobi with headquarters in the US and supports nature protection globally . <p> At the heart of the film is a young Samburu woman named Naltwasha Leripe , who takes viewers through her community 's daily life , tending livestock , digging " singing " wells deep into dry riverbanks and rescuing a baby elephant orphaned by a poacher 's gun . <p> " Today , conservation is not about building fences but rather breaking down barriers , so that local communities benefit when nature thrives , " said project Executive Producer and Conservation International CEO Dr. M. Sanjayan . <p> He added : " If we are going to save nature in a crowded world we need to entirely rethink how we go about it . This film shows a new way , one that unites , rather than divides , people from wildlife .
