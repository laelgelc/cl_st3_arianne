<p> Amon Kimeli , 33 , is a professional geological and physical oceanographer with an experience spanning over eight years . <p> He tells the story of his interesting career as he reminisces being part of a research crew aboard Langseth of Lamont Doherty Earth Observatory vessel in 2015 where the team was involved in sub bottom data collection off the coast of Massachusetts in the USA . <p> This work was part of the U.S. Law of the sea cruise to map the foot of the slope of the Northeast U.S. Atlantic Continental Margin . <p> Currently , he is working at Kenya Marine and Fisheries Research Institute aboard Kenya 's only marine research vessel christened RV Mtafiti . <p> Why did you want to become an oceanographer ?
