<h> President Kenyatta unveils strategic water storage program covering entire country <p> President Uhuru Kenyatta Thursday unveiled government plans to implement the strategic water storage program that will increase the number of Kenyans connected to safe piped water by 9 million people by 2022 . <p> The massive country-wide program will additionally increase the proportion of households with access to safe drinking water from 60 -- 80 per cent in the next five years-with a special focus on informal settlements and arid areas , said the President . <p> He spoke at the Kenyatta International Convention Centre ( KICC ) Nairobi , where he launched the Ministry of Water and Sanitation 's Rapid Results Initiative ( RRI ) cycle 2018 . <p> The President said provision of water to Kenyans was an underlying enabler for the attainment of his Administration 's Big Four development agenda " which is the future I see .
