<h> ESB signs four-year industry partnership with UL <p> ESB has agreed new partnership with the University of Limerick to support energy researcher over the next four years . <p> In addition , ESB will be an industry partner to Science Foundation Ireland-funded projects with the Centre for Marine and Renewable Energy at UL , with focus on real-time remote monitoring of substations and the remote operation of drones for inspection of transmission lines and wind turbines . <p> ESB will also fund an annual visiting lecture which will bring international experts to UL to share insights and the latest international thinking on research to tackle climate change . <p> Dr Desmond Fitzgerald , President of UL , said : " In UL we are involved in a race against time to develop novel , more sustainable , more efficient means of energy generation and use . The fact that ESB is supporting three PhD student scholarships in energy and sustainable development is testament to the urgency of this research . It is also a vote of confidence in UL , particularly our people in the world class Bernal Institute who are well placed to lead in this field @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , added : " The Turbine Installation at University of Limerick is a fitting reminder of the important role played by the Shannon Scheme in triggering major social and economic change in Ireland . <p> " One of the most visionary projects of the last century , it helped to change a way of life that had existed for generations . We at ESB continue to see electricity as a force for positive change , particularly in the context of tackling climate change . <p> " The transition to a low carbon future will require creative and innovative solutions , and the work currently being carried out in the Bernal Institute at UL will support this . "
