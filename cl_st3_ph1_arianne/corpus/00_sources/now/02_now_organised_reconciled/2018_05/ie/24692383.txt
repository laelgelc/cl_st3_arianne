<h> Helen Clarkson responds to the news that less than 2% of girls take A-Level physics <p> Helen Clarkson , CEO of The Climate Group , has responded to the news that less than 2% of girls in England take A-Level physics . <p> " It 's the engineers , scientists and coders who will deliver the solutions to climate change , but with less than 2% of girls taking A-Level physics , and nearly half of schools not seeing any studying the subject at all , the problem is only going to get worse if we do n't do something about it . We have a huge opportunity here if we inspire the next generation to take on the biggest scientific challenge facing the world today . <p> " It 's vital that we give young women the right opportunities to succeed in science and engineering so that we have the best minds tackling climate change . Universities , businesses , and charities need to amplify the work women in science already do to inspire greater participation in these subjects at school . "
