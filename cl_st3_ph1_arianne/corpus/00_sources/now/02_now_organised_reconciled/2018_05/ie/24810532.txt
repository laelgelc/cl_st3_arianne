<h> England facing water shortages due to environmental threats <p> England is facing increased water pressures due to the combined challenges of a growing population , climate change , and unsustainable land use . <p> These are the key findings from a new report from the government 's Environment Agency , which looked at the state of the country 's water resources . <p> An astonishing 3 billion litres of water are also lost every day simply from leaks , equivalent to the amount used by 20 million people . <p> In 2016 , 9.5 trillion litres of freshwater were extracted from the country 's rivers , lakes , and reservoirs , among others . However , both groundwater and surface water usage was seen to be at levels which were n't sustainable in the long run . <p> Population in England alone ( not including the rest of the UK ) is expected to hit 58.5 million people by 2026 , creating new pressures in areas where water availability is already under strain . <p> " Projections suggest that if no action is taken to reduce demand and increase supply of water , most areas will not meet @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> " Even low population growth and modest climate change scenarios suggest significant water supply deficits by the 2050s , particularly in the south-east .
