<h> News <h> US team examines greenhouse gas emissions meal by meal <p> Individual meal choices have a profound effect on our environmental footprint , but much of the analysis has been based on aggregated or stereotyped diets . Now researchers have examined the food choices of more than 16,000 US residents on a single day . <p> The study shows that an environmental saving equivalent to cutting 66.1 million passenger vehicle miles could occur if just the top 20% of most intensively emitting meals on a given day were replaced with a diet with the average impact -- 4.7 kg of carbon dioxide equivalent . <p> What 's more , these savings soon add up . The group reported that such a hypothetical diet shift -- if implemented every day -- could bring the US 9.6% closer to achieving UN Framework Convention on Climate Change targets for 2025 . <p> " One of the key outcomes is being able to represent the greenhouse gas emissions and energy impacts associated with the US diet as a distribution across the population , " said Martin Heller of the University of Michigan . <p> To examine greenhouse gas emissions and energy @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ life cycle assessment studies to the US National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey ( NHANES ) .
