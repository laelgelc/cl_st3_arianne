The government also confirmed that the MRFF remains on track to reach its capital target of A$20 billion by 2020 -- 21 , and announced a list of MRFF-backed projects over the next decade , including A$240 million over four years to develop innovative medical ideas , research devices and treatments , and A$500 million for a major new genomics initiative to develop new ' precision medicine ' approaches to a wide range of diseases . <p> A 12 year research infrastructure investment plan will commit A$1.9 billion to critical national research infrastructure . Initial investments include A$140 million upgrades to two supercomputing facilities . Investment will be guided by a roadmap developed by Australia 's chief scientist , Ian Chubb , and will require co-investment by industry and other institutions .
