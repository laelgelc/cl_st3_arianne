<p> On a day of glorious sunshine , Birdhill celebrated its National Tidy Towns award in equally glorious fashion last Saturday with a visit to the village by President Michael D Higgins . <p> President Higgins and his wife , Sabina , mingled with residents , listened to the wonderful entertainment provided by local Comhaltas musicians and took a stroll in Birdhill 's fabulous park following a guard of honour by pupils from Birdhil National School who had made their Holy Communion that morning . <p> President Higgins told the gathering that he knew the village well from his travels to and from Glenstal Abbey , and he could see there was " no doubt why you won " . <p> He was delighted to see at first hand the fruits of their labour , he said . <p> He knew their reward had been built up over a number of years and , he said , " to achieve what you have achieved requires spirit and community . When you see people work together it is very impressive and is good for the future of your community .
