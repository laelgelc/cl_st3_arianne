<h> UEFA calls attention to the ' significant issue ' of making sport sustainable <p> UEFA , football 's governing body in Europe , has released a new report emphasising the urgent need to make sport more sustainable . <p> The report , called Playing for our Planet , was produced with the World Wildlife Fund ( WWF ) and contains examples of how making the transition to sustainability is both necessary and comes with major benefits . <p> The upsides of embedding sustainability include reduced costs from efficiency savings ; reduced impacts from climate change ; and greater innovation in infrastructure and operations . <p> Sport has a unique ability to engage tens of millions of people around the world ; the report makes clear that if clubs and institutions accelerate their efforts then their reach will be " unparalleled " . <p> However , as the report makes clear few sports bodies " can truly say they are tackling sustainability in full . " World Sailing is one of them , and it has created a sustainability strategy with clear objectives . These are underlined by three pillars : environmental , social and economic .
