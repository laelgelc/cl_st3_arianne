<h> Japan 's NYK Line sells first green bonds <p> Japan 's largest shipping company , NYK Line , has sold 10 billion yen ( US$913 million ) of five-year green bonds priced at a coupon rate of 0.29% per annum . <p> Issued on May 24 , they are the first green bonds sold by a global shipping company , NYK Line says in a statement on May 21 . <p> Green bonds are debt where the proceeds will be exclusively applied to finance eligible green projects . <p> The NYK Line bonds are only available within the Japanese domestic market . Investors in the offering include Nagoya Broadcasting Network Co Ltd , Hiratsuka Shinkin Bank , and Sumitomo Mitsui Trust Bank Ltd . NYK Line did not provide the subscription details . <p> The company says the bonds have been approved by the Japanese Ministry of Environment as " a model case for green bond issuance in fiscal year 2018 " . <p> Nomura Securities Co Ltd ( Nomura ) , a whole owned subsidiary of Nomura Holdings Inc , was the bookrunner for the bond offering . <p> The proceeds are to help NYK Line invest @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ gas ( LNG ) -fuelled ships , LNG bunkering vessels , and ballast water treatment equipment , according to Nomura . <p> " The green bond offering is intended to diversify NYK Line 's funding resources and help a wide range of stakeholders better understand the company 's proactive approach to environmental investment , " Nomura says in a statement on May 21 . <p> It adds that NYK Line is also interested to incorporate environmental , social and governance ( ESG ) perspectives into its management strategies . <p> With ESG trending in recent years , Nomura notes that green bonds have been attracting attention globally as a means to address environmental problems . <p> " By underwriting these types of bonds , Nomura aims to serve as a bridge between investors who want to contribute to society and projects with capital needs that aim to resolve social issues such as climate change and water , " the company says .
