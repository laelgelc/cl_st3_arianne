<h> Global warming may have ' devastating ' effects on rice : study <h> Agence France-Presse <p> As carbon dioxide rises due to the burning of fossil fuels , rice will lose some of its protein and vitamin content , putting millions of people at risk of malnutrition , scientists warned on Wednesday . <p> The change could be particularly dire in southeast Asia where rice is a major part of the daily diet , said the report in the journal Science Advances . <p> " We are showing that global warming , climate change and particularly greenhouse gases -- carbon dioxide -- can have an impact on the nutrient content of plants we eat , " said co-author Adam Drewnowski , a professor of epidemiology at the University of Washington . <p> " This can have devastating effects on the rice-consuming countries where about 70 percent of the calories and most of the nutrients come from rice . " <p> Protein and vitamin deficiencies can lead to growth-stunting , birth defects , diarrhea , infections and early death . <p> Countries at most risk include those that consume the most rice and have the lowest gross domestic product @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Cambodia , Drewnowksi said . <p> The findings were based on field studies in Japan and China , simulating the amount of CO2 expected in the atmosphere by the second half of this century -- 568 to 590 parts per million .
