" <p> BSP Governor Nestor A. Espenilla Jr . one of the panelists at the seminar , said the central bank has been encouraging the use of innovation , digital finance and financial technology firms or fintech in promoting and improving financial inclusion . <p> Espenila said that it has been a balancing act encouraging fintech to expand its reach and services , but also ensuring consumer protection grows at pace with innovation . " There will always be a trade-off or a healthy tension between security and convenience as well as efficiency and financial integrity . That is why the BSP has established a regulatory environment that allows innovations to flourish , at the same time ensures that risks are effectively managed . " <p> The region has made important progress in expanding and deepening its financial systems , but now it " must make further progress to improve financial inclusion using fintechs , " according to the seminar panelists . <p> The seminar also tackled issues such as new innovations that would further improve financial inclusion like distributed ledger technologies @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> The lack of access to financial services is widely viewed as a key challenge for Asia 's poor households and smaller firms , according to a statement from ADB .
