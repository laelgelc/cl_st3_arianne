The procedure was to have two vessels working together -- one loaded with dynamite , the other towing streamers or geophones . The dynamite vessel would toss tons of dynamite over the side , which was programmed to explode at certain depths . <p> The seismic vessel would capture and record the sounds emitted from deep within the ocean floor .
