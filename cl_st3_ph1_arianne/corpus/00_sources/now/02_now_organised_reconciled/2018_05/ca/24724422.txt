<p> It may not have been a tornado , but it was strong enough to carry a trampoline through the air in Azilda on Thursday afternoon . <p> Jacques Vincent told The Star he was driving on Notre Dame Street around 3:50 p.m. when the brief weather event struck . He said he was coming around a bend past a gas station when he saw what looked like a hot air balloon flying over a set of trees , which are around 100 feet high . <p> " Then I saw a funnel carrying the object , which turned out to be a trampoline , " Vincent said . <p> The winds dropped the trampoline in the middle of Notre Dame Street , he said . <p> " I saw the funnel going on Landry Street and gravel from the side of the road going up the funnel , " Vincent recalled . " It kept going and went away . It did not last for more than 15 seconds .
