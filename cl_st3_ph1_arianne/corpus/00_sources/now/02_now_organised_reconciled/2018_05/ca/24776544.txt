During a recent news conference to raise awareness of the residents ' plight , a chef from Toronto poached eggs in the sediment-rich water along with a dash of vinegar , turning them black . " I watched them cook it , and holy crow , it bound the black shale , all of those sediments , " says Kevin Jakubec , a spokesperson for Water Wells First .
