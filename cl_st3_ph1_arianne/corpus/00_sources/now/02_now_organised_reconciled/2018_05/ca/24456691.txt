<h> Economical reinforces its support of the Broker Identity Program <h> Economical reinforces its support of the Broker Identity Program Insurance Business <p> The Insurance Brokers Association of Canada ( IBAC ) has revealed that Economical Insurance is maintaining its support of the Broker Identity Program ( BIP ) at the Full Partner level . <p> Since its creation in 1988 , it has been the BIP 's mission to " raise awareness of the insurance broker distribution network , " and to " promote the value and professionalism of brokers to consumers , insurers and governments , " a release said . <p> " We 're pleased to partner with the Broker Identity Program to support the value and insight of our broker partners , which is especially valuable today as our industry is impacted by climate change , technological innovation and changing customer expectations , " said Economical Insurance president and CEO Rowan Saunders .
