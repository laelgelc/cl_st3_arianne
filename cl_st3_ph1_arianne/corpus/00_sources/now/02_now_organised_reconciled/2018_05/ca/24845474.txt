Here , Harmer is less successful . In addition to switching perspectives and locations , and sometimes times , just when events begin to seem urgent , the characters ' inclinations toward nostalgia makes events taking place in the novel seem remote , and events in the past seem disproportionately consequential . <p> More importantly , though , is that the novel 's end undermines itself . Jason , Marie 's ex-husband and the only character to return through a Port , sums this up just before he re-enters to join his family : " Port is sport . This is a game , Marie . None of it has consequence . None of it is real . However , the questions about the purpose of humanity and the nature of a good life , as well as the way our longings either propel or distract us from living , are brought into sharp relief . <p> The setting and precipitating events of The Amateurs are vivid and might have been taken from recent headlines -- global climate change , the pernicious effects of capitalism , hegemonic technology and information companies -- and the questions asked by Harmer are , in their own right , urgent .
