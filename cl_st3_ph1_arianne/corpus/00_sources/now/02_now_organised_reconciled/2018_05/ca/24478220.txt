( Sebastian Bathiany/Wageningen University ) <p> Poor countries are expected to see the greatest swings in temperature as a result of climate change , a new study suggests . <p> Using models for the last report of the United Nations Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change , European researchers analyzed 37 climate-model simulations to calculate which regions would likely experience the higher highs and lowest lows until the year 2100 . <p> The European study of 37 climate models revealed that most of the increased temperature fluctuations expected in the coming decades could be in the tropics , threatening food and water supplies . ( Jayanta Shaw/Reuters ) <p> The researchers said they discovered an " unfair pattern " as they tried to predict how weather extremes such as heat waves and cold snaps might change in the future .
