<p> HALIFAX - More than 2,000 municipal politicians and staffers have descended upon Halifax for the Federation of Canadian Municipalities annual conference that kicks off Thursday and runs through Sunday at the Halifax Convention Centre . <p> A meeting of the Big City Mayors ' Caucus , which includes mayors of Canada 's largest cities , will open the festivities Thursday with a wide range of issues on the agenda . <p> In a news release , the caucus said issues " from growth to climate change " would be discussed and Halifax Mayor Mike Savage says overall the group will be looking to " replicate their success in the last election . " <p> In 2015 , Savage said " all parties came to the Big City Mayors ' Caucus and talked about what they thought they could do for municipalities and the government that got elected , got elected on a platform of investing in infrastructure , much of it municipal .
