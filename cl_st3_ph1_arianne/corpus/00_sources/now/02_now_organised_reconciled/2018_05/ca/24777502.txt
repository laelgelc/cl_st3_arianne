<p> As Pacific Gas and Electric Company ( PG&amp;E ) prepares to meet the " new normal " of climate change , working with state and local officials to find a comprehensive solution to California 's approach to wildfire liability remains a top priority , the company said today at the joint annual shareholders meeting of PG&amp;E and its parent , PG&amp;E Corporation . <p> " This is about more than PG&amp;E . And it 's about more than one bad fire season . It 's about a growing threat to our communities , our economy , and our environment as climate change makes wildfires and other extreme weather events more likely , " said PG&amp;E Corporation CEO and President Geisha Williams . <p> Williams stressed that PG&amp;E is committed to meeting California 's clean energy goals , further improving the safety and reliability of its system , and hardening its infrastructure against climate effects , but the company 's ability to make the necessary investments will depend upon reforming the state 's " broken " wildfire liability rules . <p> Community Wildfire Safety Program <p> PG&amp;E President and Chief Operating Officer Nick Stavropoulos presented the company 's new @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ major focus areas , implemented in light of the wildfires throughout the state last year : <p> Bolstering PG&amp;E 's wildfire prevention and emergency response efforts in coordination with first responders , public safety agencies , and other community partners using the company 's own Wildfire Safety Operations Center , along with enhanced weather forecasting , monitoring and modeling . <p> Reducing fire threats by increasing the distance between overhead electric lines and surrounding vegetation , and developing protocols for proactively shutting off power when extreme fire conditions are occurring . <p> Hardening the electric system and integrating new technologies , including stronger power lines , non-wood poles in high-threat areas , and microgrids to establish energy resilience zones around hospitals and schools . <p> " All of this work reflects our continued commitment to our communities and , in particular , to their safety .
