<h> Hundreds of scholars gather for country 's largest academic conference <p> On Saturday , hundreds of scholars and researchers from around the country gathered at the University of Regina for the 87th Congress of the Humanities and Social Sciences . <p> The annual congress brings together more than 70 scholarly organizations to build partnerships , spread ideas and discuss ways to improve the country . It 's the largest academic conference in the country . <p> One of the key topics at the event was sustainable energy , which Melina Laboucan-Massimo talked about in her speech . <p> " Currently we have over 11,000 small-scale renewable energy projects across Canada that are indigenous- led , and over 150 large-scale renewable energy projects that are indigenous community owned and led , so indigenous communities are a part of this charge and a part of implementing solutions , " Laboucan-Massimo , who is a speaker and fellow at the David Suzuki Foundation , said . <p> Laboucan-Massimo also hopes to see more students being educated about sustainable energy . <p> " If we start young , you know , just like anything , the more that we bring about energy @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ more we will understand the impacts of climate change and the more we will also understand our part in solving the issue , " she said . <p> First Nations National Chief , Perry Bellegarde , said another way the country can prosper is through understanding the past and present lives of indigenous peoples . <p> " This is a very key audience that I spoke to today and I do n't believe they get enough treaty awareness treaty education , I do n't believe they get enough about impacts of the residential school and colonization and oppression .
