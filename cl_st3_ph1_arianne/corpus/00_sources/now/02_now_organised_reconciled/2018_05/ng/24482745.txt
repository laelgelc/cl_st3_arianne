<h> Climate resolution at Rio meeting heralds coming ... <p> LAUNCESTON , Australia , May 3 ( Reuters ) -- It may not sound like a lot , but the 18 percent of Rio Tinto shareholders that voted in favour of a resolution that effectively called on the company to do more to address climate change have launched a snowball at the top of a large mountain . <p> The vote at Rio 's May 3 annual general meeting called upon the world 's second-largest mining company to review its membership of the Minerals Council of Australia ( MCA ) and other lobby groups . <p> While the vote was easily defeated , it sends a very clear message to natural resource companies that investors are going to be paying more attention to environmental credentials in the future . <p> The 18 percent support was said by the resolution 's lead sponsor , Brynn O'Brien of the Australasian Centre for Corporate Responsibility , to be the " highest vote ever on a similar issue , " the Sydney Morning Herald reported . <p> It 's
