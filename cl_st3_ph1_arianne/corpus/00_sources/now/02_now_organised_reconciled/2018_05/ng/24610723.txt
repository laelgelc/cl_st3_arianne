<h> Kew 's native tree protection project collects 10 ... <p> Conservationists have collected 10 million seeds from trees and shrubs across the UK as part of efforts to protect the country 's native species . <p> The National Tree Seed Project by the Royal Botanic Gardens , Kew , was launched to protect the nation 's woodlands in the face of spreading diseases and pests such as ash dieback which threaten to wipe out millions of trees . <p> Seeds have been collected from 7,623 individual trees of 60 different species including ash , juniper , Scots pine , alder , birch and yew , since the project was launched in 2013 . <p> Seeds from 60 different UK species have been collected as part of the project ( RGB Kew/PA ) <p> The seeds have been banked at Kew 's Millennium Seed Bank at Wakehurst Place , in West Sussex , where they will be saved for decades and provide a resource for scientific research to help understand and tackle threats to trees . <p> To reach the 10 million seed milestone , more than 950 individual collections of seeds were made across the UK , from @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to Northern Ireland , Kew said . <p> " These collections will be available for many decades and are a great resource to better understand all kinds of traits associated with trees which may prove vital as our environment and economy changes in the future .
