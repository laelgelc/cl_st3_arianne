<h> Red Cross plans to fight disasters with fast funding <p> YAOUNDE , May 8 ( Thomson Reuters Foundation ) -- A fund launched by the Red Cross aims to deliver life-saving humanitarian aid before rather than after floods , droughts and other natural hazards hit , so as to reduce damage and trauma in some of the world 's most disaster-prone areas . <p> The " Forecast-based Action Fund " , set up by the International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies ( IFRC ) , uses weather predictions and historical data to trigger the distribution of money when a natural disaster looms . <p> The German government has committed 3.6 million Swiss francs ( $3.6 million ) to the new forecast-based fund . <p> It will sit within another of the IFRC 's disaster relief funds that is worth 25 million-30 million Swiss francs annually , and can draw more from that pot of money . <p> " It means that life-saving action can now take place before anyone is in immediate danger , which will save lives and reduce the need for more costly emergency response and recovery efforts , " said Pascale Meige @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , response and recovery . <p> Fifteen Red Cross/Red Crescent societies in Africa , the Americas and Asia-Pacific are readying to tap into the fund over the next two years , if needed . Other branches may also use it . <p> The model has been tested in communities in Peru , Togo , Uganda , Bangladesh , Mozambique and Mongolia since 2014 . <p> " Waiting for disasters to happen should not be an option any more , " Peter Felten , head of humanitarian assistance at the German Federal Foreign Office , said in a statement . <p> " As soon as the forecast reaches that level of severity , the mechanism can release the funds for pre-specified early actions , " he said in emailed comments . <p> For example , in a community-based scheme in Bangladesh , predictions that the Brahmaputra river would overflow in 2017 sparked cash grants for local people who could then use the money to support their families during the emergency , he said . <p> The mechanism could change the way agencies tackle disasters , providing an incentive to act when @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ disaster has struck , he noted . <p> Governments and aid agencies have been calling for more investment in disaster prevention for years , to cut the rising bill for helping people hit by crises . <p> In 2016 , former U.N . Secretary-General Ban Ki-moon called on states to dedicate at least 1 percent of international development assistance by 2020 to reducing the risks of disasters and preparing for them . <p> This March , Zambian officials said the forecast-based financing model could be used as part of a national strategy for managing flood risk , and would strengthen preparedness.
