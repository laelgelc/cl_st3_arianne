<h> Buhari departs for London with few aides <p> Only few of President Muhammadu Buhari 's aides travelled with him to the United Kingdom where he is billed to meet his doctor . <p> Buhari left Abuja on Tuesday afternoon . <p> Muhammad Bello , minister of federal capital territory ; Ibrahim Idris , inspector-general of police ; and Abba Kyari , chief of staff to the president ; were among those at the Abuja airport to bid him farewell . <p> Buhari in a handshake with Ibrahim Idris , inspector-general of police <p> Garba Shehu , presidential spokesman , had said the president would return to the country after four days . <p> He had also said the president 's trip to the UK is at the instance of his doctor . <p> Before leaving Abuja , Buhari received letters of credence from ambassadors of Netherlands , Republic of Botswana and Lebanon to Nigeria , where he stressed the need for sustained financial and capacity building to mitigate the effects of climate change in the country .
