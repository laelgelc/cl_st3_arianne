<h> FEATURE-Rebels with a cause -- the women bikers saving lives in Nigeria <p> ABUJA , May 17 ( Thomson Reuters Foundation ) -- Whenever the all-female Nigerian biker group D'Angels hit the streets , people would stare in amazement at the sight of women on motorbikes . So they made up their minds to use the attention for a good cause . <p> This August , D'Angels and another female biker group in Lagos , Amazon Motorcycle Club , plan to provide free screening to 5,000 women -- a significant undertaking in a country where many lack access to proper healthcare . <p> " Some asked if the bikes really belonged to us . Some asked if they could sit on our bikes . We decided to use the opportunity to do something to touch women 's lives . " <p> Breast and cervical cancer are huge killers in Nigeria , accounting for half the 100,000 cancer deaths each year , according to the World Health Organization . <p> Screening and early detection can dramatically reduce the mortality rate for cervical cancer in particular . <p> But oncologist Omolola Salako , whose Lagos charity partnered with the FBI @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the need for screening . <p> " Among the 600-plus women we have screened since October , about 60 percent were being screened for the first time , " said Dr. Salako , executive director of Sebeccly Cancer Care .
