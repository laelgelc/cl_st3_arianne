<p> Singapore 's household electricity consumption has increased by about 17 per cent over the past decade , the National Environment Agency ( NEA ) said yesterday . <p> Households here consumed a total of 7,295 GWh ( gigawatt hours ) last year , which is the equivalent of each home spending about $1,000 a year on electricity . <p> A 2017 NEA survey of 550 households found that air-conditioners were largely to blame , accounting for about 24 per cent of the consumption of a typical home . <p> Refrigerators accounted for 17 per cent , while water heaters consumed about 11 per cent of electricity used . <p> Four in five households said cost savings would motivate them to take steps to save energy , but not if such measures inconvenience them . <p> For example , while 84 per cent of those surveyed agreed that using a fan instead of an air-conditioner would save energy , only 71 per cent did so , with the others saying they valued personal comfort more . <p> " The increase in electricity consumption could be due to greater adoption of household appliances and electronic gadgets , " said Professor @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 's Energy Research Institute . <p> While appliances such as refrigerators are required to be labelled according to energy efficiency , this may not have much of an impact , he said . He noted that people are likely to switch to more energy-saving appliances only when such devices need to be replaced . <p> One way to reduce energy consumption is by increasing prices , similar to the upcoming hike in water prices , he said . <p> In the long run , however , a behavioural change is needed . <p> " A personal commitment to sustainability has to be taught in the schools , " he said . <p> As part of efforts to get people here to save energy , the NEA launched the second annual Energy-Saving Challenge yesterday . <p> The challenge , which will run from May to August this year , will award prizes to households here which manage to cut electricity use by at least 1 per cent . <p> Last year 's challenge saw almost 7,000 participants achieving savings of about 330,000 kWh - the electricity needed to power 1,000 three-room @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Environment and Water Resources Minister Masagos Zulkifli , who launched this year 's challenge , said cutting energy consumption will help reduce Singapore 's carbon footprint as well as the impact of climate change . <p> As a low-lying island , Singapore is particularly vulnerable to rising sea levels , he said . <p> A version of this article appeared in the print edition of The Sunday Times on May 06 , 2018 , with the headline ' Local household electricity usage up 17% over past decade ' .
