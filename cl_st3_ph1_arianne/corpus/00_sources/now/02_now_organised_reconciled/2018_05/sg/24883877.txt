<p> The Asian Development Bank ( ADB ) on Thursday announced a project-finance assistance of about $40 million to Indonesia 's first utility-scale solar photovoltaic ( PV ) plant , developed by Vena Energy . <p> The loan package is part of a two-phased portfolio financing totaling approximately $160 million for new renewable energy projects being developed by Vena Energy , formerly Equis Energy , an independent power producer in the Asia Pacific region , with 11 gigawatts in operation , under construction , and in development , it said . <p> The investment will help construct , operate , and maintain a portfolio of energy projects , including a wind farm and four solar PV plants in eastern Indonesia . <p> " By supporting a sector-changing financing for renewable energy , with an innovative portfolio approach , ADB and Vena Energy have been able to add over 114 MW of clean energy to Indonesia 's electricity grid , while helping reduce the country 's dependence on fossil fuels and promoting renewable energy development , " said Infrastructure Finance Division Director for Southeast Asia , East Asia , and the Pacific at ADB 's Private Sector Operations Department Jackie @ @
