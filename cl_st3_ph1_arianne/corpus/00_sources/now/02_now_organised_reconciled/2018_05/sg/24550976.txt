<h> PacificLight provides wider opportunities for young Singaporeans to support sustainability efforts <p> Published byPacificLight <p> Monday 7 May 2018 <h> Share <p> Singapore , 7 May 2018 -- PacificLight has launched its 2018 Crea8 Sustainability Competition , which aims to engage and champion Singaporean youths on sustainability and environmental conservation . <p> The focus of this year 's Crea8 Sustainability Competition is climate footprint , in support of Singapore 's Year of Climate Action in 2018 . Through drawing , writing and video production , participants are encouraged to generate ideas on the actions needed to reduce our society 's environmental footprint , expected outcomes , and how these can be achieved . <p> " We want to inspire Singaporean youths to be more involved in building a sustainable environment for themselves and future generations . This competition provides audio and visual avenues for students to share their innovative solutions for addressing climate challenges and to raise public awareness , " said Mr. Yu Tat Ming , CEO of PacificLight . <p> Formerly known as the Sustainability Singapore School Art Competition , the competition has been rebranded in its third edition as the Crea8 Sustainability Competition to reflect @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of students .
