It said it was banning fresh produce , including mangoes , dates and bananas - the bats ' preferred fruits.PHOTO : AFP <p> DUBAI ( REUTERS ) - The United Arab Emirates ( UAE ) has banned imports of fresh vegetables and fruits from the southern Indian state of Kerala where 13 people have died due to an outbreak of the rare brain-damaging Nipah virus , the Gulf state said on Tuesday ( May 29 ) . <p> The UAE 's Ministry of Climate Change and Environment also notified other local authorities , including the Abu Dhabi Food Control Authority and the municipalities of its emirates , to prevent the entry of any fresh produce from Kerala , it said in a statement . <p> The ministry suspects that fruit bats are the source of the virus .
