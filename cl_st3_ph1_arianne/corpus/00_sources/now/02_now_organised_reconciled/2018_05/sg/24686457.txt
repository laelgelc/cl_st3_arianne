<h> Warming planet faces cooling crisis <h> As climate change warms the Earth , one significant concern is the cooling crisis , the quest for energy-hungry artificial ways to keep ourselves cool . <p> A new study finds the world is headed towards a " cooling crunch , " with key cities in Asia contributing the most to the environmental impacts of air conditioning use .
