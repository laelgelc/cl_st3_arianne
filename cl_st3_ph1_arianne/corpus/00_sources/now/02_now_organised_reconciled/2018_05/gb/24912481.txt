<h> " I wish I was part of this family " <p> Tom Saater is one of Nigeria 's top photographers . He 's the ' go to ' for big international titles like the New York Times , Huffington Post and Bloomberg amongst many others . But he did n't learn his trade at a prestigious college , he learned it while living on the streets . Tom 's covered stories from the insurgency in northern Nigeria to elections and climate change , but one of his most emotional projects has involved using his camera to find himself the family he never had .
