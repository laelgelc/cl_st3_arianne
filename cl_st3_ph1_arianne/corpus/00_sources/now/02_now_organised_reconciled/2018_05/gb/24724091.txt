<p> An estimated 300 people took part in the newly-formed Cycle Winchester 's Mass Ride . <p> A ' bike train ' pedalled around the city picking people up at various points including Kings Worthy , Headbourne Worthy , Harestock , Weeke , Badger Farm and Stanmore <p> The riders lapped the city centre one-way system before gathering in Abbey Gardens for a rally . <p> Cycle Winchester is campaigning to make Winchester Better By Bike with an aim to show the city and county councils the potential for cycling to help alleviate congestion and air pollution . Civic chiefs have been drawing up a new Movement Strategy consultation . <p> Ride organiser Hazel Agombar , of Egbert Road , Hyde , said : " We were thrilled with the turnout - it shows the huge appetite for cycling that there is in Winchester .
