It 's growing at around 4% per annum . <p> Previous estimates of the impact of all this travel on carbon suggested that tourism accounted for 2.5-3% of emissions . <p> However in what is claimed to be the most comprehensive assessment to date , this new study examines the global carbon flows between 160 countries between 2009 and 2013 .
