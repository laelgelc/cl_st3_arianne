<p> The Bank of England has hired an expert on financial technologies to help it navigate issues thrown up by digital developments such as blockchain . <p> Huw van Steenis will now act as a senior adviser to Mark Carney , Governor of Threadneedle Street . Mr van Steenis worked as head of strategy at asset management firm Schroders before leaving in February , and previously held a senior role at US investment bank Morgan Stanley . <p> According to the Bank , Mr van Steenis will " deepen understanding of a number of major issues " in his new specially created role , and have a particular focus on new financial technologies . <p> Blockchain , the technology behind cryptoassets such as Bitcoin , allows for encrypted information to be transferred within a closed network . <p> It is also known as distributive ledger technology ( DLT ) and has an increasingly wide range of potential applications in the financial sector . Mr van Steenis is understood to be an expert in this field . <p> The role will also involve examining how technology can be used to ensure emerging economies have sufficiently robust banking systems in order @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Long term challenges such as how the UK can best adapt to become a low carbon economy will be another area of focus for Mr van Steenis . <p> Mr Carney has long sought to champion environmental concerns in his tenure at the Bank . Climate change could have a " catastrophic impact " on the banking and insurance sector , the Governor has warned . Mr Carney has also called for these business to share publicly the risks posed to them by the issue . <p> Mr Carney said that Mr van Steenis will be developing the Bank 's vision of the " financial system of tomorrow " . <p> Payment for the appointment will be revealed along with other salary information in the Bank 's annual report . Mr van Steenis is expected to work at the Bank for a minimum of six months , but the precise length of his tenure has not be decided .
