<h> New bat species discovered in the Soutpansberg <p> According to a new study published in the Zoological Journal of the Linnean Society , the diversity of the southern African horseshoe bats has been increased by three species . <p> These species were detected with the help of Prof Peter Taylor and his students at the University of Venda . They made use of modern technology such as bat detectors that recorded the echolocation calls of bats . <p> " My students and I were recording this strange bat in the Soutpansberg Mountains with a peak echolocation call frequency of 100 kHz , which is not known to be associated with any described species , " said Taylor . " The species with the closest call is Swinny 's horseshoe bats , but this species echolocates in a different frequency range of around 106 kHz , " he further explained . <p> This led researchers to investigate further . More bats were captured from two caves in Mozambique to establish how this 100kHz-frequency bat is distinct in its own way .
