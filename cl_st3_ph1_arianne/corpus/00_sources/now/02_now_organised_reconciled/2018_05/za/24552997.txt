As a result the significant and growing pollutants in the transport sector like sulfur dioxide and nitrogen dioxide concentration shows a few areas of concern , " Ngcobo told The Witness . <p> The report stipulates that the air quality measured at three monitoring stations in the city shows that sulfur dioxide exceeds the safety limits .
