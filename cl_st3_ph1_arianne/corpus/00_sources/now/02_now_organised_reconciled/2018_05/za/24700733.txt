<h> An open letter to the SA born-frees <p> Across the earth , nations watch us with bated breath . For while we are mighty , we sleep . They pray we never wake up ! For all of the pomp and pleasantries they greet us with , they have never shown us what their true feeling is : fear . <p> They fear this giant that sleeps . They fear the day the Azanian machine awakes from her slumber . Her slumber of despair . Her slumber of hopelessness . <p> This land is richer than all others . Her soil carries the fertility of Eden . Her gold runs into the very mantle of this planet . Her seas crash against a continent filled with the greatest resource of all : the South African people . Never before in human history has such a pantheon of humanity been assembled . <p> I do not say this lightly . We have done the impossible . The first South Africans tamed a land where the mountains scraped the sky , and the deserts crossed an endless plane . <p> They fought for a place among the beasts and built @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ people of this land fought back Western invaders without end . South Africans do not surrender . When their land was stolen , they did not bite their tongues . South Africans do not surrender . They were forced down into the earth to dig out its riches as little more than slaves , but they refused to let the dream of freedom die . South Africans do not surrender . <p> They fought other men 's wars on continents over the seas , while their own people suffered . South Africans do not surrender . Our people suffered murder , humiliation , segregation , torture and tyranny . South Africans do not surrender . <p> We invent , we create and we change the world . I dare say that we live in the greatest nation on earth because we have overcome the impossible . <p> Every generation has its own great struggle . Every generation of this land has added chapters to the masterwork that is South Africa - the masterwork that is the universal quest for freedom . <p> Sadly , we as collective authors of our @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ we have many chapters in need of closing ( income inequality , climate change , violence against women , crime , racism , etc. ) , but we are thus far unable to pick up the pen . <p> For reasons that escape me , we born-frees are unable to pick up the pen and continue the next chapter in the book of freedom . Yes , we have some daring authors finishing some pages , and #FeesMustFall is certainly a chapter in its own right . However , they are outnumbered by an alarming group seeking not to write new pages , but rather to tear them out . <p> We born-frees are poisoned by politics . Be it along race , gender or whatever other divisionary line , we tear our nation apart . Line by line . Page by page . <p> A white child refuses to see the lingering legacy of apartheid and their inherent privilege . Instead , he cries reverse racism . <p> A young man refuses to see how the women of our country suffer . Instead , he longs for the old @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ oppressors refuse to admit guilt . The children of the oppressed refuse to engage with the few that do . What is the result ? <p> We are left with a book unfinished . <p> We are left standing as that generation that took the torch , dropped it and carried on . <p> Instead of young South Africans wanting to turn their country into the great power it deserves to be , they take their skills and give them freely to foreign lands that gave them nothing . <p> Why would you want to serve any other country , when you could be a part of the greatest story of innovation , liberation and freedom that the world has ever seen ? <p> I implore you , young South Africans , have faith in your country . Have faith in your countrymen . Forget the politics . Remember instead , the great journey that we all share . No other nation is abounding with such opportunity as ours . <p> South Africa is destined to be more than that country that became democratic in 1994 . That was a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ can not take credit for our parents ' work . We can not sit by and reap from what we have not sown . <p> South Africa is destined to be more . We are destined to be a great power among the cast of nations . We are meant to move out of the wings and into the spotlight . <p> We are destined to be a beacon for all humanity to gaze upon and say : that nation is great . <p> This can happen . This will happen . That is our story to write . <p> All we have to do is come together , and realise that you do not save a sinking ship by ignoring each other or jumping overboard : <p> Please , just pick up the pen ! <p> Matthew Field is a Grade 12 pupil from St Alban 's College in Pretoria .
