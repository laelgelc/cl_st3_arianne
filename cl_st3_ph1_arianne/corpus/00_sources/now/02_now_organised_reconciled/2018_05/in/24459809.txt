Scientists highlight that their growing abundance is a cause of concern , because they adversely affect fish , which do not thrive in such waters . <p> " Less dense water comes to the surface because of the warming of oceans , encouraging these intense blooms , which has an adverse impact on fisheries . Currently , the western coast , Persian Gulf , and Oman are largely affected , but if it keeps on increasing , it will have drastic effects on fisheries along the Indian coast . " This discourages growth of diatoms , which need silicate and sunlight . It instead leads to flourishing of Noctiluca which preys on diatoms , " said the study . <p> " The present findings show no evidence that cultural eutrophication has contributed to the decadal scale shifts in plankton ( algae ) composition in the north- eastern Arabian Sea .
