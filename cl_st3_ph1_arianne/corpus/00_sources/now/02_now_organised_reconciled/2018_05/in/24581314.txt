<h> Pondy bans party poppers using harmful chemicals <p> Puducherry : Puducherry pollution control committee ( PPCC ) has banned the use of party poppers that use low-intensity explosive chemicals that are harmful to human health and environment . <p> The low-intensity explosive chemicals -- red phosphorous , potassium chlorate and potassium perchlorate -- when exploded cause severe eye trauma and facial injuries . PPCC had banned the use of these party poppers following a direction from the Union environment , forest and climate change ministry . <p> However , the party poppers that use compressed air as the charge material and soft papers as streams have not been banned . Party poppers commonly used at parties are generally charged with an explosive which is activated by pulling a string .
