<h> Mexico is strengthening tools to adapt the effects of climate change <p> Fund for the Prevention of Natural Disasters , which aims to revitalize initiatives aimed at preventing disasters . ( Image Credit : Flickr ) <p> Mexico is strengthening both governmental and technological tools to improve its adaptation to the effects of climate change , informed the Ministry of Environment and Natural Resources . <p> The Secretary of Environment and Natural Resources highlighted in a statement the implementation of programs such as the National Atlas of Risks , the Digital Atlas of Water and the Fund for the Prevention of Natural Disasters , among other tools that protect the country , located in one of the areas of the planet most vulnerable to this problem . <p> The fundamental objective of the nation when adapting to the global increase in temperature is to be able to reduce vulnerability in 160 municipalities , strengthen the capacities of local communities , as well as increase protection and restoration of ecosystems , as per Infobae . <p> Faced with the natural phenomena that have arisen in recent decades , the National Center for Disaster Prevention , the National Water Commission @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ among other instances , carry out studies on the conditions of danger . These include the Diagnosis of Hazards and Identification of Disaster Risks in Mexico and the Digital Atlas of Water . <p> Regarding conservation , the National Commission of Protected Natural Areas promotes the development and implementation of Programs of Adaptation to Climate Change ( PACC ) in protected areas , in their areas of influence and in priority regions for conservation . <p> The Secretary of Environment and Natural Resources emphasized that each state and each municipality is also developing its own risk atlas . In the financial field , the Mexican Government meets needs through the Trust Fund for Natural Disasters , which provides relief supplies and assistance in emergency and disaster situations . <p> With this , according to the institution , the possibility of responding immediately and timely to urgent needs for the protection of life and health of the population before the occurrence of a disturbing natural phenomenon is guaranteed . <p> In parallel , there is the Fund for the Prevention of Natural Disasters , which aims to revitalize initiatives aimed at @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Early Warning System for Tropical Cyclones and the Digital Climatic Atlas .
