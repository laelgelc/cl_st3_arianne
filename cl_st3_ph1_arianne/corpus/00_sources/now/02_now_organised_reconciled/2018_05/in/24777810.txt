<h> Today 's Newspaper <h> Floating Varanasi museum to revivify Ganga <p> A first-of-its kind floating museum will soon sail on the river Ganga in Prime Minister Narendra Modi 's constituency Varanasi , moving from ghats to ghats to create awareness about the importance and need for conservation of the water body -- one of the most revered rivers in the world and also one of the most polluted . <p> The project is part of the Government 's " Biodiversity Conservation and Ganga Rejuvenation " programme under the National Mission for Clean Ganga ( NMCG ) with an objective to ensure the river 's rejuvenation by restoring its ecological integrity . <p> An official from the Wildlife Institute of India ( WII ) , under the Union Environment Ministry , which is executing the project , told The Pioneer that an exhibition has been proposed on bajras -- the magnificent boats meant to be floating on river waters -- showcasing detailed information on Ganga 's prestine history , ecology and riverine ecosystem , its route -- from origin to destination -- biodiversity like wild animals and plant species found in and around the river . <p> Typically , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ boat with a cabin , deck and a large rooftop to sit and enjoy the river sights . <p> The floating exhibition on bajra will be open for the local communities and tourists visiting the city . <p> The museum will also highlight threats and possible solutions to overcome the threats to the river . Another important feature of the museum will be focus on climate change and how it is affecting the river , said the official . <p> " The objective is to educate all stakeholders , particularly the local communities , about the values of aquatic biodiversity of the river and natural processes through establishment of knowledge centres , " he added . <p> It is said that Ganga still has about 2,000 aquatic species . In fact , during the rapid biodiversity assessment made under the project , the WII has observed species , such as Siebold 's smooth scaled water snake ( Enhydris sieboldii ) , after a gap of 70 years in river Ganga . <p> The biodiversity assessment team also reported new nesting ground of Indian Skimmer ( Rhynchops albicolis ) , a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ signs for the river 's biodiversity . <p> On the lines of the floating exhibition in Varanasi , the Government has also plans to set up an interpretation centre at the Turtle Rescue and Breeding Centre in Sarnath . In future , science-based aquatic species restoration plan for Ganga river is on the cards . <p> Modi , who represents the Varanasi constituency on the banks of Ganga , has made a clean-up of the river , one of his key campaign promises in the 2014 general elections .
