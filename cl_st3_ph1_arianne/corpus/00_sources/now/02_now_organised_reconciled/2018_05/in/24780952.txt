<h> CERC readying paper on tariff norms for 2019-24 <p> He said during the hearing of the petitions , it appeared that there was a general consensus among power generating companies and distribution companies on the need for early resolution of the issue . <p> NEW DELHI : The Central Electricity Regulatory Commission ( CERC ) will kick-start in the next 10 days the process of setting tariff regulations for the next five years of tariff control period , starting 2019 , said its chairperson PK Pujari . <p> A mechanism to address the recovery of additional capital expenditures by power companies on eco-friendly equipment needs to be worked out urgently , Pujari said . He said during the hearing of the petitions , it appeared that there was a general consensus among power generating companies and distribution companies on the need for early resolution of the issue . <p> The Central Electricity Authority ( CEA ) is working on calculating a benchmark cost for such installations , he said . The ministry of environment , forest and climate change had in December 2015 notified revised standards for coal-based power plants in the country to curb emissions . The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ has prepared a project-wise plan to retrofit the equipment . <p> " CEA is working to benchmark the equipment retrofitting costs depending on the recent tenders of NTPC . NTPC tenders have discovered a lower than expected cost for the equipment supply . The cost for such equipment shall not vary much between tenders of NTPC and private power producers , " said a senior government official , who did not wish to be identified . <p> The power ministry has already advised that such costs will not be included during preparation of dispatch order of electricity to ensure these plants remain competitive . Power producers had sought a clarification from the ministry and the regulator to incur the costs for meeting the environment norms . <p> NTPC has filed a petition with the CERC under Section 79 of the Electricity Act 2003 for approval of expenditure on installation of various emission control systems in compliance with the revised environmental norms for thermal power stations . The CERC is close to finalising the approach paper for framing the tariff regulations for the period 2019-24 . <p> The paper is likely @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ days , Pujari told ET . " We are trying to widen the scope of deliberations for the tariff regulations . Our teams reached out at zonal levels during preparation of the approach paper . We have tried to include every debatable aspect on the electricity sector in the paper . The paper does not reflect views of the commission , " he said . <p> Consultations on the approach paper with stakeholders will be followed by issuance of draft tariff regulations for the five years . " We plan extensive interactions of senior members of the commission with various sets of stakeholders over the next few months , " he said , adding that the final regulations will be ready by December .
