With this wide range of products , ASL continues its expansion into the Indian market . ASL products are exported to over 80 countries across the globe . <p> The Indian subsidiary of the company is set to help farmers meet growing demand for quality , natural biostimulants and bio-nutritional products that increase crop yield , quality and farmers ' return on investment in a sustainable way .
