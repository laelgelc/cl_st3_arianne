This is significant given the role of government subsidies in some countries which subsidised overfishing and the depletion of global fish stocks . <p> The OECD is supporting work to stop harmful fossil fuel subsidies . New Zealand supports this work and we are working with like-minded countries to advance discussions at the WTO . <p> Every year , governments spend more than US$425 billion making it cheaper to buy fossil fuel .
