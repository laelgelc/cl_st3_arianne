That 's 4% higher than the same time last year , representing our most rapid growth rate in three years.Our annual growth also outpaced that of the wider Waikato region ( 3% ) and New Zealand as a whole ( 2.7% ) . Our Mayor Sandra Goudie welcomes the new figures - representing an economy that 's firing on all cylinders . " It 's no surprise the summer months are always the strongest for our economy , when we have our huge swell in visitors , " Mayor Sandra says .
