<h> Drop in puffin numbers for 2018 census causes concern <p> Scientists are concerned about a sudden drop in the number of puffins on Britain 's Farne Islands . <p> They 're blaming it on climate change and fear the species could be wiped out completely if the trend continues , ITV News reports . <p> The Farne Islands are known for their seal and seabird colonies , and tens of thousands visit the coast every year . <p> Puffins are one of the most iconic of the animals on the island , but early results from 2018 puffin census suggest the population has dropped by around 12 percent . <p> The last time a census was conducted was around 2013 , when 40,000 birds were counted .
