At the moment , treaty-making is governed by something called the royal prerogative , a source of legal authority that was described by a leading English judge in the 1960s as " a relic of a past age " . The prerogative is basically a set of powers that the executive government and its agencies have because ( mostly British ) courts in the past have said they are legitimate .
