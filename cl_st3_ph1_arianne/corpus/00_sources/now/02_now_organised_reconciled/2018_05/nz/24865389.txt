<h> Approaching crisis in District Court requires intervention <p> NZBA President Clive Elliott QC , says it is time to acknowledge that District Court resourcing has reached a critical point where immediate intervention by the Government is needed . He was responding to a column written by the Chief District Court Judge , Jan-Marie Doogue , in which the Judge advised that there would be a redeployment in judicial resource from the criminal jurisdiction to the Family Court , to meet a backlog in that court . <p> Previous changes in legislation which restricted who can serve as an acting judge have created an untenable situation for the court . A serious backlog has arisen in the Family Court , one of the areas of pressure , where there are around 8000 Care of Children Act cases waiting to be heard .
