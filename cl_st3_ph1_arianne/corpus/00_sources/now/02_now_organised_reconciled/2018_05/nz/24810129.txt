<h> EU-NZ trade deal a bright spot in gloomy times <p> Opinion - Trade is frequently in the news these days , often with gloomy stories about trade wars . <p> Photo : 123RF <p> But in a welcome contrast , this week has seen European Union ministers decide to proceed with negotiations for a high-quality , comprehensive and ambitious new trade agreement with New Zealand . <p> Trade Commissioner Cecelia Malmstrom will visit in June to launch the process officially . Both sides are keen to wrap up the talks quickly ( at least in trade negotiations terms ) , ideally within a couple of years . <p> The decision builds on years of effort by New Zealand governments , diplomats and business representatives to make the case for a modern trade agreement with the EU . This week 's decision is a welcome turbo-charging of that process . <p> New Zealand exporters have been at a growing competitive disadvantage in Europe : the rules governing our trade are now thirty years old , while most of the EU 's other trading partners have already negotiated or are in the process of negotiating preferential trade agreements .
