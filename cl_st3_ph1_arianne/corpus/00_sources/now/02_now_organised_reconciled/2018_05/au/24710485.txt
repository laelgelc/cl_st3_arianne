<h> Birds are dropping dead off Australia 's coast , and it 's all our fault <h> Confronting new images have revealed the cost of Australia 's obsession with plastic , with appalled scientists recovering hundreds of fragments from the stomachs of fledgling chicks . <h> By Nicole Hasham <p> 18 May 2018 -- 7:26pm <p> Deep in their burrows , hungry shearwater chicks on Lord Howe Island await a meal . Their parents have been scouring the sea in search of fish and squid . Instead , they return to feed their babies clothes pegs , bottle tops and Lego pieces . <p> After 90 days the fledglings emerge from their burrows , stomachs bulging with plastic . They prepare for their first flight . Many are so malnourished they die outside the nest . Others make it to the beach , but their undeveloped wings flap in vain and waves engulf them . <p> Ian Hutton , a naturalist and museum curator on Lord Howe Island , pulls the bodies off the beach . Researchers slice open their stomachs to confirm the cause of death .
