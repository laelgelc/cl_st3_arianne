In the third of this multi-part series ( read part one and two ) , Elena Garcia provides some answers . <p> There are profits to be made from dairy . Murray Goulburn 's new owner Saputo is one of the top 10 dairy processors in the world . The Montreal-based company is worth more than $15 billion and processes more than 8 billion litres of milk per year into a variety of dairy products , which are sold in more than 40 countries . <p> Outgoing Murray Goulburn farmer director Craig Dwyer told the April 27 AGM that voted to sell the cooperative : " Now it is time to turn to the future . And Australia also had the capability to supply the international markets , because there was excess production .
