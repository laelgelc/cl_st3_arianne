<h> The Delaneys of Delhi <p> It 's so simple to be happy , but it 's difficult to be simple . But Aussie couple Mark and Cathy Delaney , and their two children , Tom and Oscar have definitely disproved what Rabindranath Tagore once said . <p> For the Brisbane-based couple , who moved to Barapullah ( Delhi ) years ago and now call a modest one-room accommodation in home , simplicity is a way of life . Mark and Tom 's book , Low Carbon and Loving It is a testament to that . <p> Based on sustainable living , their observation of Australian lifestyle and personal lived experiences in Indian slums , the book is a collection of their unique perspectives . The Delaneys hope that it would contribute in some way to reverse the devastating impact of climate change . Only in their case , they practise what they preach . <p> " We share a fairly small room with one fan , one light , and a few electrical devices using little electricity .
