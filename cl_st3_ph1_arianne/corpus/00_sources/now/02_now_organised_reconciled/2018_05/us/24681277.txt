Emma studied Environmental Analysis at Pomona College . <p> In a world where wind and solar resources make up 40 to 50 percent of generation , wholesale energy prices will drop by as much as $16 per megawatt-hour , according to a study released Wednesday from a group of researchers at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory . <p> Modeling 2030 scenarios in which CAISO , NYISO , SPP and ERCOT reach combined wind and solar penetration at or above 40 percent , the researchers found electricity prices will fall -- but price fluctuations may increase and the number of peak net-load hours will spread to a greater number of days . <p> According to co-author Joachim Seel , the study offers a " holistic " analysis of price formation in a decarbonizing market . He said that data is not generally available to the public . <p> Researchers hope the results , part of a three-part study stretching into the next several years , will offer policymakers , utilities and grid authorities @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ future ability to cope with changes in the electric sector . Seel said it should help them " make sure their decisions are robust in a high wind and solar future .
