<p> It 's runoff season and Sourdough Creek , bloated and brown from sediment dragged down by snow from nearby peaks , emerges torrential from the mouth of the Gallatin Range . <p> Watching from its bank , it 's difficult to imagine the creek , which provides nearly half the drinking water to Bozeman 's some 48,000 residents , as anything less than infinite . <p> But in the Gallatin Valley , where sources like Sourdough have been largely shut off to new extraction for the last 25 years , water is both limited and coveted .
