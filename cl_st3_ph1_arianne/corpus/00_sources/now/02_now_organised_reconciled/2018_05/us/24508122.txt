People who read the Bible literally have created some unnecessary conflicts . But scientifically inclined people may make a similar mistake : read the Bible literally and then dismiss it . The writer of Genesis related Israel 's faith in God to everything they ( thought they ) knew . So should we . <p> Anthropologist Claude Levi-Strauss said that for traditional peoples it 's impossible to understand anything without understanding everything -- i.e. , one of the deepest needs of human beings is orientation , the need for a sense of direction . Without some sense of the lay of the land , how would you know to go east or west ? Or even straight down ? Having no sense of a " big picture " is crazy making . <p> The writer of Genesis located all of the experiences of Israel , both triumphs and defeats , within the vast geography of God .
