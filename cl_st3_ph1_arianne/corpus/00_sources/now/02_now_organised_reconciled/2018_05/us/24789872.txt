Photograph : Gill Allen/REX/Shutterstock <p> People need to use less water and companies must curb leaks to prevent future water shortages and damage to rivers and wildlife , the Environment Agency ( EA ) has warned . <p> Many sources of water supplies are already overstretched and , with climate change and a growing population , much of England could see significant supply shortages by the 2050s -- particularly in the south-east . <p> In England , almost 9,500bn litres of freshwater were abstracted in 2016 , which is enough to cover the whole of Greater London in nearly 6m ( 20ft ) of water , the EA said in a new report . <p> But 3bn litres a day are lost through leaks from pipes -- equivalent to the amount of water used by more than 20 million people in an average day . <p> Large amounts of water are also wasted by households , where average use is 140 litres per person a day .
