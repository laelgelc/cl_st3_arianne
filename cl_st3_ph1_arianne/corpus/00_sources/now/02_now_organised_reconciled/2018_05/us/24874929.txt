It is committed to the principle of Credible Minimum Deterrence and has persistently sought deterrence stability in the region . <p> This objective has driven Pakistan to offer and conclude several Confidence Building Measures ( CBMs ) in the nuclear and conventional domains , including the 2004 Pakistan-India Joint Statement which recognised the respective nuclear capabilities of the two countries as a factor for stability . Pakistan has consistently signaled its willingness to consider further measures for risk reduction and avoidance of arms race in the region , said the Foreign Office . <p> " At the same time , Pakistan remains confident of its ability to deny space for any misadventure against the backdrop of rapidly expanding nuclear and conventional forces in its neighbourhood , deployment of BMD and pursuit of aggressive security doctrines and developing force postures , " it said , adding , " The nuclearisation of the Indian Ocean and canesterisation of ballistic missiles in our neighbourhood should be a matter of concern for the international community as well , since these developments have extra-regional ramifications .
