<h> Solar energy could lead to plenty of savings , too <p> The Chronicle created a special publication celebrating Earth Day 2018 , including a section entitled , " The Bozeman Energy Project . " Emphasis is overwhelmingly on energy savings by businesses that have switched to LED lighting ; a good idea . But what about the energy savings by businesses and families that switch to solar energy ? Bozeman gets an average of 188 sunny days a year , enough to justify solar . <p> Installing 2.8 KW of solar capacity on the roof of our first home on South Black Avenue largely met our electricity needs . We also had switched to LED lighting . On a larger scale , Bozeman , through building code provisions , could incentivize developers , architects , and individuals to orient building roofs to the south at approximately a 45 degree angle . This would facilitate solar installation in the future , even if current owners are not interested .
