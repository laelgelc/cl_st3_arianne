<p> A new series from the excellent Longreads website and podcast explores a story that goes to the heart of much current American pathology . Cliven Bundy is a Nevada rancher whose standoff with the federal authorities ( ostensibly over changes to grazing laws ) came within a whisker of armed confrontation . How did Bundy 's brand of extreme anti-state belligerence take hold ? And is Bundy a domestic terrorist or just a proud upholder of the increasingly tattered American dream ?
