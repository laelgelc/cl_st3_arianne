<h> Letter was not from a climate ' expert ' <p> The April 30 letter " Do n't pay attention to climate alarmists " is misleading at best . The writer , from the " International Climate Science Coalition , " starts by presenting himself as an involved environmentalist , and then , with the implied authority of his position as executive director of a " climate science " organization , strongly suggests that the concern expressed by the majority of climate scientists is " alarmist " and unwarranted . <p> The writer is a mechanical engineer and public relations specialist who has worked as a lobbyist for the electric and gas power industries and for Canada 's largest nuclear power company .
