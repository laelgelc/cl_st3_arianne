It was an economic issue . <p> Most Canadians saw building a pipeline to the west coast as a natural thing to do . A pipeline would better utilize oil which had been discovered in large quantities in Alberta in 1947 . Trans Mountain was built to supply west coast refineries with crude oil . Three of those , the Standard Oil , Shell and Imperial Oil refineries on Burrard Inlet , had received their supply of crude oil by tanker until that time . Most of that oil came from California .
