" For the past 12 years , I have been planting eucalyptus and poplar species of trees and supplying timber to particleboard , matchsticks and shuttering industry . The profits are far more as compared to what I used to earn by growing sugarcane and wheat , " he said . <p> He explained the mathematics involved too .
