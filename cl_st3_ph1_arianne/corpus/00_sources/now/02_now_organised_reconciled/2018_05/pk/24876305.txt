<h> Pakistan is Celebrating 20th Youm-e-Takbeer Today <h> ISLAMABAD : Pakistan is Celebrating Youm-e-Takbeer , on Monday , with patriotism and zeal . <p> 28th of May , also called Youm-e-Takbeer , is the day when Pakistan first successfully tested its nuclear capabilities in 1998 . The tests were in response after India had successfully tested their nuclear bombs and had posed a threat to Pakistan . It was an attempt at self defense from a threat posing India who had just become a nuclear power . <p> In a release by Foreign Office of Pakistan , it stated that Pakistan was a long pursuer of belief of Nuclear free South Asia . However ; after the nuclear tests , that prospect was ended there . But Pakistan is still committed to nuclear non-proliferation and global peace strategy . <p> The release also stated that Pakistan has shown restraint and responsibility being a nuclear power . Pakistan has sought deterrence stability in South-Asian region from the very beginning . For this Pakistan has offered many Confidence Building Measures ( CBMs ) since then . One of them being the 2004 Pakistan-India Joint Statement which recognized both countries ' nuclear @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ avoiding risks of an arms race in the region . <p> While Pakistan is always moving ahead for maintaining peaceful relations , its also confident in its nuclear capabilities . The statement said that nuclearization of the region should also be of concern to the international community . <p> Pakistan 's security of its nuclear resources has been internationally recognized . While the country 's nuclear harnessing capabilities are also up-to date . The statement read that Pakistan is one of the biggest victims of climate change . So its planning on using nuclear energy as a clean alternative for fossil fuel to produce 40,000 Megawatts by 2050.
