<h> ' Govt has allocated Rs100b for Diamer-Basha , Dasu dams ' <p> ISLAMABAD : State Minister for Finance Rana Muhammad Afzal Khan on Friday informed the Senate that an amount of Rs100 billion has been allocated in the budget 2018-19 for Diamer-Basha and Dasu dams . <p> Responding to a query raised by Opposition Leader Sherry Rehman , he said the country is wasting huge amounts of water due to non-construction of reservoirs . <p> " We have allocated Rs100 billion for the Diamer-Basha and Dasu dams and it is up to the next government to decide about their construction , " he added . <p> He suggested inviting experts from universities and other departments to advise on how to handle the shortage of water . <p> He said that members of the water caucus should come up with an open mind to give their recommendations . <p> Earlier , Rehman demanded setting up parliamentarians ' water caucus to discuss recommendations to address the issue . <p> She said all four provinces have come to an understanding and signed the country 's first water policy . <p> The new policy is essential for better management of Pakistan 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ deprived due to global warming and climate change , she added . <p> According to the policy , administrative bodies will be established at the provincial as well as federal levels to effectively manage water supplies better , she said , adding this includes the national water council and provincial water authorities for the purpose .
