Hiding the data mix What is distinctive about this latest effort at claiming unusual -century warming and implying a human contribution is their presentation. As a reviewer of IPCC reports, I well remember efforts to hide the mixing of proxy and thermometer data: IPCC’s showed the proxy temperature record with a black line and the temperatures with a blue line. I complained that these were very hard to distinguish — especially in a black-and-white Xerox copy. Since then, the IPCC and everyone else have used a distinctive red color for the instrumental data. That kind of distinction, however, is missing in the present OSU-Harvard paper. To use a current analogy: it’s like putting horsemeat into Swedish meatballs that advertise beef. In the case of the meatballs, the DNA evidence betrayed the addition of horsemeat.
